My mom got a call from a lady in the afternoon . She said that she had recharged my mother's Jio number by mistake(one digit was pressed wrong while she input her own number) . My mom got scared because she thought this was another scam call targeting old people. She gave me the phone and listened to that lady . I checked My Jio app on mom's phone. I recharge my mom's number every month (₹209 ). Found that there was another plan of ₹239 upcoming. The lady was very polite in her mannerisms and explained she was tensed for 3 days because she thought she had recharged her number but today she got to know from customer care that she had given wrong number while recharging. I didn't send her any money via UPI because I was still skeptical idk why. I told her I would recharge her number instead of sending money and she was quite happy to hear that.

It feels so good after helping a complete stranger 😊. I don't check my mom's phone usually so had no idea someone recharged her number. Even if i knew I couldn't have helped her. She told me she worked in Insurance Company and her name n all even asked me if I had anyone needing insurance to contact her as it would be of great help to her. 

Thanks for reading. Have a good day all. Has something similar happened with you guys? Let me know in the comments.
