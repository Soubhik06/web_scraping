My sister and her classmates enrolled in an Internship Training Program offered by a company named INTRAINZ. They had to pay 1k to book their slots and an additional 2k before the training starts in December. The students could choose from HR, Stock market, or accounting, and they were promised one month of training and project work in the second month.

 
I was checking the legibility of this program as this feels like a scam, but I am unaware if it is common practice in BCom/Finance fields. Most people said their training is only substandard pre-recorded videos and useless when it comes to job scenarios.

Have any of you availed their service before? Is it worth paying 3k for just a certificate to submit to college? Is there any other way BCom students can get some real industry exposure/internship experience?
