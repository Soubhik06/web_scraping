Welcome to "India Expose"
*An Anonymous Platform to Expose Corruption in India*

India Expose is a safe space for individuals to anonymously report and discuss corruption cases within India. Our mission is to shine a light on the injustices, malpractice, and unethical practices in government, corporations, and other institutions. We believe in the power of collective action to bring about change by holding those in power accountable.

 **What We Do:**
- Share and discuss cases of corruption, bribery, fraud, and abuse of power.
- Allow whistleblowers to anonymously reveal critical information about corruption.
- Provide resources and support for those seeking to expose unethical activities.
- Foster discussions about how to combat corruption at all levels in India.

### **Guidelines:**
1. **Anonymity is Key**: Ensure you protect your identity at all times. Avoid sharing personal details that may compromise your safety.
2. **Verify Facts**: When posting, share as much verifiable information as possible (documents, links, etc.). False accusations harm the credibility of the group.
3. **No Doxxing**: Do not share private information (such as addresses or personal contact details) of individuals. We expose corruption, not people’s private lives.
4. **Respect**: Maintain respectful dialogue. Hate speech, threats, or abuse will not be tolerated.
5. **No Self-Promotion**: Posts promoting personal interests, services, or unrelated content will be removed.

Why Join?
- Share your experience and stories of corruption without fear of retribution.
- Connect with like-minded individuals committed to justice and transparency.
- Help bring about change by contributing to the exposure of corruption.
