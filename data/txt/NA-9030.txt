Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and learned

 APP for the State. 2. In the present case, the petitioner is

 apprehending his arrest in connection with Phulwariya P.S. Case

 No. 60 of 2023, registered on 16.02.2023 for the offences under Sections 341 , 323 , 379 , 498A / 34 of the Indian Penal Code. 3. As per prosecution case, the petitioner

 solemnized marriage with the informant and allegation against

 the petitioner and other co-accused persons is that they assaulted

 the informant making her unconscious and took away all the

 household articles from the house of the informant. 4. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.14626 of 2024(2) dt.14-03-2024 2/3 the informant is not the wife of the petitioner and she is wife of

 one Nand Kumar, Resident of Village - Bishunpur, P.S. -

 Kateya, District - Gopalganj. The informant is not a lady of

 good character and she has been having illicit relationship with

 many persons and she used to blackmail them and extort money

 from them. The petitioner extended some loan to the informant

 and when he asked for return of his money, he has been falsely

 implicated in this case. Learned counsel further submits that the

 petitioner stays abroad and when notice under Section 41A Cr.P.C. was sent to him, he could not join the investigation and

 thus arose apprehension of his arrest. Learned counsel further

 submits that the petitioner has lodged Complaint Case No. 248

 of 2023 against the informant in the month of October, 2023 and

 thereafter, this false case has been lodged by the informant. The

 petitioner is having clean antecedent. 5. Learned APP opposes the submission made on

 behalf of the petitioner. 6. Having regard to the facts and circumstances

 and submissions made on behalf of the parties and considering

 the possibility of false implication in the background of

 facts so enumerated, let the petitioner above named, in the

 event of his arrest or surrender before the court concerned
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.14626 of 2024(2) dt.14-03-2024 3/3 within a period of eight weeks from today, be released on bail,

 Only) with two sureties of the like amount each to the

 Gopalganj/concerned court in connection with Phulwariya P.S.
