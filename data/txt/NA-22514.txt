Hi everyone,
My uncle recently fell victim to a scam involving a fake website design company. He owns a small electronics business and was looking to get a website created. A company named "Digital India Solutions" contacted him, claiming to be a website design service. They initially asked for a 1000 INR advance and promised the remaining amount would be paid after the website was created.
They used WordPress to create a basic website and then demanded the remaining payment. Shortly after, they claimed they needed to buy an SSL certificate and charged him 4999 INR. They also agreed to integrate a payment gateway without extra charges but later called back asking for 4999 INR for verification, which they said was refundable. Following this, they asked for 3240 INR as GST and then another 4999 INR as a deposit for the payment gateway.
It’s clear this was a scam. They’ve already collected over 20,000 INR from him. They also provided a GSTIN, which seems to be part of their fraudulent act.
What steps can we take to report this company and take action against them? Any advice or guidance would be greatly appreciated.
Thanks in advance!
