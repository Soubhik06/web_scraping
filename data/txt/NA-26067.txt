I got a call from a number claiming to be from DHL, saying that my parcel was returned to their office. They asked me to press 1 to talk to a customer executive.

 I did, and some guy named Mahesh started telling me that my parcel was stuck with customs in Mumbai. I told him I don't remember ordering anything or expecting a parcel, and that’s when I realized something was off. 

I asked for his name, which he hadn’t mentioned before. I started speaking in Hindi, and he got startled and cut the call. 

When I called back, a woman answered, blaming her kids for misdialing my number. Obviously, it’s a scam. Stay alert, everyone!
