--- POST TEXT ---

I'm from a small city in northeast india. Recently moved to west bengal to live with relatives and I'm having a very hard time adapting to life over here.

First of all, the entire place in incomprehensibly big to me. You need to be able to navigate public transport, buses, trains, autos, to get nearly anywhere. Back home I can easily walk anywhere I need to. I'm filled with dread and anxiety everytime I need to catch a train. Getting on and off trains, punching and kicking off crowds is insanity.

And there is so many people everywhere. Its overwhelming. There is more peace and quiet back home. I feel like I can never relax here. People are loud, rude and very busy. Having been able to live at a slower, intentional pace, the contrast here is very confusing. Are people happy with living such fast-paced lives? Also, people here are consumed by their work it seems. People seem more ambitious and want to earn more, etc. Its good if they're happy, but I can't relate.

The thing that upsets me most is I feel city life is dulling away my brain. Life is so overwhelming and exhausting I don't have time to contemplate my life, to read or spend time on hobbies or learn new things. There's almost no natural spaces around where I live. There's a fog in my brain that makes any thinking difficult and I feel intense rage everytime I'm losing time in traffic, or strangers are rude for no reason or trying to scam you just because.

There are cool things about cities. Accessible healthcare, endless entertainment options, interesting food, but it all feels empty somehow. I had relatively less creature comforts back home but I was happy.

I don't understand. Everyone wants to move to cities. I too wanted this. Yet I'm miserable now. I don't know if I should just go back home or smth.
