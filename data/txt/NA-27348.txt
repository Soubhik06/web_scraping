they replied with this reply 

\-----------------

Dear Sir /Madam,

 

 

Re your inquiry, please note the following:

 
1) We can categorically confirm that EEB is currently recruiting Independent Trade Finance (Executives/Consultants/Professionals).

 
2) You have been contacted by and have received the communication from an employee of \*TRDFIN Services Limited in India, \*a third-party recruiter through its Nextgen recruitment portal. Please follow the instructions provided to you to progress the process of onboarding by Nextgen. 
 
3) EEB is a Class A bank registered and headquartered in St. Lucia and has a representative office in London, UK. We do not have any operations in India. 
 
TRDFIN is EEB's \*contracted service provider \*in India. It supports EEB with several back-office functions, including onboarding and KYC services. \*It is not EEB's global back office\*. 
 
4) For clarity, please note that the opportunity being offered by EEB as communicated by the sender (member of the contracted service provider's team) is \*genuine\*. The request for documents (if any), is also legitimate.

 

 
To be clear, this is for your appointment as an \*Independent Sales Professionals \*of the bank and is \*not a job\*. The role will entitle you to market the bank's trade finance products and make introductions of the prospective clients to EEB in consideration of commissions. Aside from the contractual obligations, which would be clearly set out in your \*Independent Sales Consultancy Contract \*with the bank, there shall be no agency/ association/ affiliation between the bank and you.

 

We hope this clarifies. 
 
Kind Regards,
