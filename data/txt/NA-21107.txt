--- POST TEXT ---

I received an IVR call from a +1 unknown number telling me that my personal details have been found in relation to illegal activities in the dark web. It told me to press 9 in order to talk to the Hyderabad Cyber Crime Unit, which unsuspectingly, I did. After informing them of my cluelessness, they confirmed it was a case of identity theft and asked me to prove my innocence to the Hyderabad Police Headquarters, whom they forwarded the call to again. 

My naivety still had not let go and I was on a video call with them (with their face hidden), for 40 minutes. I was pleading my innocence and unfortunately giving them info about my life (college, work, parents, etc.). The guy was supremely convincing as a police officer and lecturing me on what would happen if I got arrested, yet reminding me that he believed I was innocent and I needed make a statement which convinces the court, because apparently, that's where he'd send the video to. 

I was scared out of my wits at this point so when he asked for my Aadhar card, I sent it in as well. Finally, he made a shady call to some crime department who seemed to be talking over a walkie talkie. The person from the supposed crime department shouted "Arrest this guy immediately". 

The person who was "investigating" me then gave me two options, a) he will execute my arrest warrant, or b) I would have to bail myself out. My doubts of it being a scam then finally heightened when he told me he would forward my number to the court and I have to make a payment of Rs. 7500, and I would have to share my screen while doing so. I did none of that and hung up on him.

What should my next steps be? I have already blocked my Aadhar Card and I haven't given them any financial information.
