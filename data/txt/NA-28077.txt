--- POST TEXT ---

I submitted a porting request from Vodafone to Airtel and was going to switch on couple of days after. On the day of porting, I got an sms from 5676775 which said that you need to do your KYC again for Airtel and asked to call a cell number. I tried calling the mentioned number but it was busy.

&amp;#x200B;

By the following day, I had ported over to Airtel. Early morning I got a call with True caller name as Airtel KYC. He mentioned that he will need to take some steps on my phone for Sim activation.

&amp;#x200B;

First thing he did was to look up an application in Playstore named Airtel Quicksupport. I could not find any app with that name, to that asked if I see TeamViewer quick support. I installed the app and he started controlling my phone. For those who don't know, team viewer is a popular support tool using which someone can see your screen and guide you or fix any issue on your phone.

&amp;#x200B;

Immediately then I got an sms with a link. The link looked very closer to Airtel site and had a activation page. He asked me that there is a activation fee of INR 10 and tax of 3.5 so a total of INR 13.5. The activation page asked my number and then led me to a payment page. It had pretty much all options that a typical site would have like UPI, Debit/credit cards, NetBanking etc. He persisted that I pay via Netbanking or Debit/credit cards and not thru UPI as they are having an issue with it.

&amp;#x200B;

During the call, he kept asking me what I was doing. When I asked that he can see my screen, doesn't he know what I am doing. He got a bit pissed and said that he was not looking at my screen and its connected to support server. I was not ready to pay anything or access net banking while he was looking at my screen so I stopped the TeamViewer session. That got him fuming on why I stopped the connection and my Airtel Sim will get deactivated within 1 hour. And I disconnected the call.

**This is a cautionary tale in case if you get any similar calls.**
