--- POST TEXT ---

Justice AK Sikri has authored judgment on behalf of himself and CJI Dipak Misra and Justice AM Khanwilkar

 **(Majority Judgement)**

* It is better to be unique than the best: Justice Sikri

* There is no possibility of obtaining duplicate Aadhaar card, Sikri J.

* **Section 57 struck down. Private companies cannot insist on Aadhaar**

* **SC strikes down the National Security Exception. This means, investigating agencies would now require a judicial warrant to access data of a person under investigation.**

* **Aadhaar Act can be passed as Money Bill, hold Supreme Court; Turns down challenge on the ground that it could not have been classified as a Money bill.** :(

* Whether Aadhaar act violates right to privacy. Justice Sikri says they have examined only Section 7 and 8 for this purpose.

* **Authentication records cannot be stored for more than six months.**

* **A data protection law is needed as soon as possible.**

* CBSE NET cannot make Aadhaar mandatory - Sikri said: **"Enrolment of children only with parental consent. They should be given an option to exit on attaining majority."**

* Supreme Court upholds Section 139AA **mandating linkage with PAN**

* Aadhaar **cannot be mandated for opening of bank accounts,** Supreme Court.

* Aadhaar **cannot be mandatory for mobile connections;** DoT notification to that effect unconstitutional

Sikri J concludes pronouncement of majority judgment; DY 
Chandrachud J. starts pronouncement of his judgment.

* **DY Chandrachud J. dissents on Aadhaar Act as money bill. Holds Aadhaar Act cannot be treated as Money bill.** :)

* **Passing of bill as money bill when it does not qualify as a money bill is a fraud on Constitution, violates Basic Structure, DY Chandrachud dissents.**

* Aadhaar violates Informational privacy, data protection, Constitutional guarantees cannot be compromised by vicissitudes of technology: Chandrachud

* Aadhaar program in it's entirety is unconstitutional, Rights of 1.2 billion citizens cannot be tested as a mere contract with UIDAI: Chandrachud

* **Allowing private players to use Aadhaar will lead to profiling which could be used in ascertaining political views etc of citizens, Chandrachud J.**

* Mandating Aadhaar for benefits and services under Section 7 would lead to a situation in which citizens will not be able to live without Aadhaar. Hence, Section 7 arbitrary and unconstitutional, Chandrachud J.

* 139AA Of Income Tax Act Unconstitutional: Chandrachud dissents with majority. It is based on the premise of constitutionality of Aadhaar. 139 AA is also unconstitutional.

* Aadhaar numbers shall be deleted forthwith by the telecom companies: Chandrachud.J

Justice Ashok Bhushan commences reading his Judgment

* Ashok Bhushan J concurs with majority judgment of AK Sikri but differs on three issues.

* There is No violation of privacy by Aadhaar. Aadhaar Act Passes the three fold test in Right To Privacy Judgment: Ashok Bhushan

* Justice Bhushan also struck down Section 57 to the extent it includes "contract"

TLDR: **Pronouncement concludes; 4-1 in favour of Aadhaar.**
