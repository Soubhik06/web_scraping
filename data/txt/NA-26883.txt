**My Story:**

**(i) Scenario Prior to the Scam:** From Nov 2021 till Dec 2023, I helped my NRI friend 
from my university days in India (currently staying in Europe) monetarily during his 
difficult times (for medical expenses, flight tickets to visit their dying father &amp; grand- 
father, tuition fees etc.). I believed in mutual support &amp; cooperation among NRIs. 
Unfortunately, that same friend later orchestrated an "Arrest &amp; Detention Scam" while 
feigning his helplessness under the arrest of impersonated law enforcement &amp; public 
officials. 

**(ii) Unfolding of the Scam:**

* **False Arrest Claims &amp; Urgent Demands:** In January 2024, a third party—claiming to be law enforcement from Europe—contacted me on WhatsApp, warning that my friend was about to be arrested and that urgent payments were needed to avoid deportation and even physical harm. The friend verified this on video call. 
* **Exploiting International Jurisdictions:** This scam involved impersonated law enforcement &amp; fraudulent public officials operating from **Slovakia, Estonia, Lithuania, Latvia, Austria and India** using multiple phone numbers and fake identities to coerce me into sending money under the pretext of arrest, physical assault &amp; detention of this friend. 
* **Involvement of Family Members:** When the tactics of these impersonated officials were collapsing, the supposed "family members" of this friend in tandem with the impersonated officials got involved to extort money - Where **Fabricated Photos &amp; Event timelines** were presented to validate this friend's arrests. 
* **Direct Financial Impact:**
 * **7,416 EURO** extorted during the initial “arrest scam" into a bank in Slovakia and through Wise Transfer
 * An additional **9,678 EURO** forced into a bank account in Slovakia between **January 2024 and April 2024.**
 * Prior payments from May 2023 to December 2023 totaled **13,207.38 EURO** under false pretenses such as emergency deportation, eviction, and lawyer fees for divorce settlements.
* **Legal &amp; Emotional Fallout:** Beyond the severe financial loss, I have faced significant legal complications and emotional distress. False complaint emails and fraudulent legal notices were sent to my workplace and local authorities all aimed to destroy my reputation &amp; employment in a foreign country. 

**(iii) Current Status of the Case:**

* Filed police cases in Singapore and India for fraud, cheating, and cyber harassment - have been closed- due to challenges in overseas jurisdiction investigations.
* Request to Asset recovery agencies in Singapore- received NO effective response. 
* Lawyers from Slovakia dropped my case- due to mandate issues.

**(iv) Checklist Warnings for NRIs:**

* **Always Verify Urgent Demands:** If you receive sudden, pressured requests for money—especially those citing legal emergencies—always seek independent verification from trusted professionals.
* **Question Inconsistencies:** Be wary if a familiar contact unexpectedly resorts to coercion, impersonation, or conflicting narratives involving international jurisdictions.
* **Protect Your Finances &amp; Reputation:** Secure all communications and evidence. Consult legal or financial experts immediately if something seems off.
