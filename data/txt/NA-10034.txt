1. This Original Application (OA) was registered suo-moto on the basis

of the news item titled "On revamp priority list Satpula Lake being filled up

with raw sewage from drain" appearing in the Times of India dated

06.04.2024. 2. The news item had revealed that there was pumping of raw sewage

into Satpula Lake from a large drain in Khirki near Saket Court in South

Delhi. It has also disclosed that Satpula Lake was listed in 2021 as one of

the 1,040 wetlands to be rejuvenated by Delhi Government's wetland

authority. This Lake has an area of 3.06 acres as per official record and is

situated inside a park of Delhi Development Authority (DDA). 3. Tribunal by the order dated 16.05.2024 had impleaded the

concerned authorities and had issued notices to them. 4. The reports dated 24.07.2024, 17.01.2025 and 26.06.2025 were

filed by Delhi Pollution Control Committee (DPCC). In the report

24.07.2024 following status of the Lake was disclosed:- 1 "xxx xxx xxx 2. That, in compliance to the order dated 16.05.2024, an
 inspection of the site was carried out on 24.07.2024 by the DPCC
 officials. During the inspection, it was observed that: 3. That, in view of the above findings of the inspection and
 eutrophic conditions, DPCC issued a letter on 29.07.2024 to DDA to
 repair the dysfunctional parts of the STP so as to make it functional
 and submit the treated water analysis report of STP from DPCC lab or
 any other NABL accredited laboratory within 15 days. Copy of the
 letter dated 29.07.2024 issued to DDA is attached herewith as
 ANNEXURE-2. 4. That, no reply has been received from DDA so far. The present
 status taken report may kindly be taken on record." 5. In the subsequent report dated 21.04.2025, DPCC had stated about

the improvement in the condition of Lake. The latest report of the DPCC is

 inspection the samples collected from the Lake were found to be meeting

 the norms and STP treated water is being discharged in the Lake for

 rejuvenation. 3 7. So far as ownership and possession of the Lake is concerned,

Counsel for the DDA does not dispute it, hence, primary responsibility of

maintaining the Lake is with the DDA. 8. It has been pointed out that the work of revival of Lake was issued

to International Rotary Club and NOC dated 23.06.2022 has been placed

on record by DDA along with its report dated 28.09.2024. 9. In such circumstances, DDA is required to ensure that Lake is

properly maintained, its rejuvenation channels are not blocked and water

quality of the Lake meets the prescribed notified standards. It is also

required to be ensured that the STP from which the treated water is

discharged in the Lake performs within prescribed parameter and no

untreated sewage is discharged in the Lake. 10. The DPCC will carry out monthly inspection of the Lake and

bimonthly during the monsoon season. The DPCC will submit the report of

water quality of lake and STP before the Registrar General of the Tribunal

within eight months from today to ascertain the compliance of the

consideration before the bench again. 11. The OA is disposed of accordingly. Prakash Shrivastava, CP
