Okay, let's just vent about Delhi for a sec. Look, I've globe-trotted a bit, and every city has its quirks, right? But Delhi? Man, it's like they collected every urban nightmare and put it in one place. I spent over five years there, dipping in and out, so I'm not just talking out of my hat. And yeah, I can get by in Hindi and understand Punjabi, so it's not a language barrier thing.
First off, the air. I mean, do I even need to elaborate? The AQI is a joke, and not a funny one. Then there's the traffic, a special kind of hell. You think you're going somewhere, but you're really just participating in a slow-motion parking lot.
And don't even get me started on women's safety. My female friends have had some truly awful experiences. It's just…pathetic. The racism and discrimination? Real. And let's not pretend crime isn't rampant. People sweep it under the rug, but it's there.
Weather-wise? Meh. Nothing to write home about. Dating? A minefield of scams, honestly. Forget romance, you're lucky if you don't get fleeced. Culture? I'm searching, but coming up empty. Food's decent, I'll give them that. And yeah, the cost of living is relatively manageable compared to other Indian metros, but what's the point when you're breathing in smog? All that "healthy lifestyle" stuff goes out the window when the air is trying to kill you.
Seriously, my first Delhi experience was a cabbie stealing my DSLR while I dozed off. And that was just the beginning of the petty theft saga.
And this might sound harsh, but a lot of Delhiites seem to have a geography blind spot. They mash states together like it's a culinary fusion, which, it's not. 

Delhi people are not bade Dill wale I am sorry. 

Honestly, I think it's time we considered moving the capital. Delhi just feels broken.
