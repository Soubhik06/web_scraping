I received a call today saying she was HR from Agenda technologies.
She asked me if am looking for job and said that your shortlisted for our company and have interview tomorrow.
I told can I attend it on Monday, she told no u have to attend tomorrow only.
I said her okay and she asked me are you sure? I said yes.
She asked me where do u stay, do u know Marathalli.(I got a doubt that its fake because I received same call more then 3 times but with different company name)
Then I asked her to send me location on whatsapp.
She sent me location when I opened it was Baskin robbins location
I was confirmed that its a scam
Checked in google for the company but there were none.
Then I asked her this is Baskin robbins shop location, she was like yes its the landmark once u reach there call me i'll guide u the way.
I asked her for company website she sent fake one.
I scolded her and blocked her.

Guys, We are freshers who are struggling to get a job. And these scammers play with students Life, 
Everyone please be aware that none of the companies asks you to pay for interviews or anything and also many scammers call and tell your shortlisted and you have to attend interview, look for the company's official website, and 
Proper location.
And Sometimes there will be a real company but scammers will claim that they are calling from it.
In that case, if you find company's official website just call or send an email to confirm if your getting call from company really or not.

And the scammers numbers in truecaller or whatsapp will be HR name(ex:HR Ankita, HR Patil etc)

Please be aware of scams.
