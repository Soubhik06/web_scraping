I was scammed by a scammer in AIG Hospital Gachibowli (the one near Ikea) sitting in a waiting area after an outpatient procedure (the anesthesia was almost off and i was sent outside in the larger open sitting area, in the billing cum waiting area) and my relative went off to get some reports. and this guy randomly comes up all sweating and looking tense. Asks if i know telugu and i said yea and then he started his story saying that he came from kurnool and his grandfather is in a major heart surgery. He has made too many bill payments and is maxed out on UPI and out of cash. Asked me if i can give some 1200 bucks in cash for a emergency bill payment and that he'll repay me by evening or as soon as his UPI works or once he could get some cash. I fell for that and gave him money and he said he'll be back soon and said his name is Karthik vaidya. I was anyway waiting till evening for my treatment and never saw him around again. 

I am so stupid i fell for that, I thought he looked like some genuine guy and was in need of help but after i thought about it i realized he had nothing on him, no reports nothing, it was obviously a scam, even the name was generic lol. I should have also realized what could cost so little as 1200 bucks in a super speciality hospital and an emergency operation never gets delayed just because some guy doesn't pay the bill in the next few minutes, it doesnt work that way and i wasnt even sitting at the heart speciality floor lol. I was so stupid, the scammers have evolved and i learned a lesson. Shit people like these make me not help some real person in the future out of trust issue. F*** these people!!!!
