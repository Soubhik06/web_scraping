Hello, r/india today I've found myself in a situation worthy of an Indian film. I have introduced my mother to the internet years ago and she has her own email that she operates and runs a small business. But she is ~~so culpable~~ gullible to online scams and has a history of falling for them even after my repeated warnings. Today she came to me to discuss a "business opportunity” and I started rolling my eyes from the get go. 

She says she has been in contact with a person who is willing to buy a "product" from her. This person is from the UK and s/he will arrange for the supplier of "the product" from India itself. This raised so many red flags in my mind and I could feel my anger welling up inside. I guess she could see it on my face so she quickly said this is why I didn’t tell you all this, you never take these seriously *picardfacepalm.jpeg* after another session on internet safety she agreed to not mail or respond to anyone again.

So anyway I checked her mail and went through the email chain, it was so surreal I had to put it up here so that it might help others.
