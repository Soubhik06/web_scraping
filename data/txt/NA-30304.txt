Jamshedpur, Mar 20 (PTI) A patient at a state-run hospital in Jharkhand’s Jamshedpur died after allegedly jumping from the fourth floor of the building in the early hours of Friday, police said.
The HIV patient, a resident of Parsudih area of Jamshedpur, was suffering from tuberculosis and admitted to MGM Hospital on March 16, family members said.
