India’s telecom regulator wants to stop call management apps like Truecaller from blocking, filtering, tagging, or restricting calls from designated commercial number series, 140 (promotional) and 1600 (transactional). MediaNama sent Truecaller a set of questions on the proposed amendment and the company declined to comment on this.

Apps that do not comply risk losing Section 79 safe harbour under the Information Technology (IT) Act, the legal protection that shields platforms from liability for third-party content.

The Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) released the draft third amendment to the Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations (TCCCPR) on March 13, 2026, with public comments open until April 12 and counter-comments are due by April 27.
