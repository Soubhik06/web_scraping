A couple of times in the past few months I have received calls from an American sounding woman called Jessica who launches into a spiel about back taxes, I cut her off both times before she could go into detail. I am assuming that she is going to pitch some scheme along the lines of helping to pay any back taxes I might owe and will fish around for my personal information. The first time I called her a fucking scammer and hung up. She did not address me by name like so many of these creeps do. It is all very puzzling as I do not owe back taxes and it would be no business of some stranger on the phone if I did. My taxes are done every year by one of the well known tax services and by the same guy every year as he knows me well and knows what he is doing, may he never retire. I have never been audited or had any trouble with the IRS. My income over the years has been not too large. So, has anyone else here experienced anything like this? I am retired now so maybe I am assumed to be senile or something and I am on one of the old people lists, like the ones who go phishing for my Medicare# who are clearly calling from India. I just find it bizarre.
