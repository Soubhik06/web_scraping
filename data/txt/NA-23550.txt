We (five of us) booked two rooms for two nights (18-20th Nov) about 2 months back and like always chose Pay at hotel and paid an advance amount with balance around 4k to be paid when we checked-in. 
Around 9 AM of 18th I received a call from someone saying that he’s from the hotel and asked me when I’ll be checking in. We were reaching the hotel around 8 PM, so I told him between 7 and 8. He told me there’s some hotel policy that if we didn’t check-in before 12 PM they’d cancel our booking. I told him we had to travel a very long distance before we could reach the place. So he suggested we could pay the remaining balance to keep the booking. Worried that we won’t have a place to stay I agreed and I told him I’ll pay on the app. He started explaining to me how they don’t get money from OYO and if we paid directly to them they’ll get the whole payment and wanted me to pay to the owner’s UPI. I agreed and asked for the ID. It was hotelpayment6@okaxis. Kinda sus, yeah. But here’s the twist. When I got suspicious and told him I’ll come to hotel and then pay, he said something that made me believe it was actually someone from the hotel. 
He went like “I know so many scams happen, but to confirm that we’re from the hotel, I’ll tell the booking ID and the remaining balance, please open your OYO app and verify.” I checked the app and it was exactly what he said. I didn’t give a second thought, I paid immediately and sent him the screenshot like he asked, to the same number. 
Then he says I had to mention the booking ID as a Note. He asked me to resend the whole amount again, this time mentioning the booking ID. I realised this was a scam when he asked me to pay again and wouldn’t refund the money that was sent initially without the booking ID. 

Complained regarding this to my bank and OYO within 2 hours. Met bank manager yesterday, said they can’t do anything and asked me to file a cyber crime complaint even though I received a mail from the bank asking me to go to the home branch to submit necessary documents. OYO asked me to submit all the details through email. I did and was told I’ll get a reply within 24-48 hours. It’s been 6 days, still nothing. Drafted a cyber crime complaint on the official site which is very buggy. It logged me out several times while entering the details. I cant see the drafted complaint nor can I create a new one cause the transaction ID can’t be duplicate (same as the one mentioned in previous complaint which was never filed). 

Not sure how the booking ID was leaked but here are some theories. 
• Someone who works for OYO and has access to all the information is running this scam. They knew when to call, hotel name and address, booking ID and balance amount. 
• I had received two mails two weeks apart from OYO about telephone conversation with them for confirming the booking. The first one mentioned the booking ID and balance. The second one mentioned booking ID and hotel name. I did not make these calls. I’m not sure if someone else received them. 
• Heard from the hotel manager that this has happened before (a month before we went there) and has closed OYO booking because of the scam. So someone who worked at the hotel before who might still have access to the hotel bookings has done this. 

TL;DR: got a call from someone who knew the exact OYO booking ID on the check-in day and got UPI scammed. 

Lessons learnt: 
• Always pay through the app or call the hotel and confirm before doing any payment. 
• Nobody will help you get your money back after UPI scams.
