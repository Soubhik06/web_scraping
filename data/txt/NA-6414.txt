CORAM : HON'BLE MR. JUSTICE GAUTAM KUMAR CHOUDHARY
 ......... The petitioner who is in custody in connection with Cyber P.S. Case No. 21
 of 2023 for the offence registered under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 120B of
 the I.P.C., Sections 66(B) , 66(C) , 66(D) and 84(C) of the Information Technology
 Act pending in the Court of Additional Sessions Judge-II, Deoghar. It is submitted by the learned counsel on behalf of petitioner that he has been
 implicated in this case only on the basis of seizure of four mobile sets, but there is
 no further material to show that these mobile sets were used in any way in
 commission of cyber fraud. The seizure of mobile sets cannot per se attract the
 provisions under the I.T. Act or under IPC . There is no further material to show that
 there was any victim of the cyber fraud committed by this petitioner. Learned S.P.P. opposed the prayer for bail. It is submitted that the mobile
 sets which were seized from the petitioner having Sim Cards in the name of some
 other persons. It has come in Para-71 of the Case diary that the IMEI number of the
 mobile sets which were seized from the petitioner was used by different sim cards.
 Suspicious e-wallets were created from the mobile seized from him and series of
 call had been made and there is specific reference to one of the victims in Para-101
 of the case diary. Considering the nature of allegation and materials on record, I am not
 inclined to enlarge the petitioner on regular bail and accordingly, the same is
 rejected. (Gautam Kumar Choudhary, J.)
