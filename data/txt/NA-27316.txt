^(This is one scam that comes like a lead generator scheme for startups and businesses. I was hoping it to be genuine lead generation scheme. I registered for a seminar to learn lead generation for my software business, and the seminar was supposed to be a exclusive seminar but to my surprise "I got a call in less than 5 mins" after registering. That is lightning fast reflexes! I answered the call in proper professional manner. The free seminar turned out to be a money sucking scheme where the service i.e. lead generation was offered at 3.5 Lakh INR per session, to which they provide a mentor, marketing ad budget and there is no success gurantee. The session that was sold in this scheme was just for 2 hrs.) 

Lesson Learnt: 

1. Be as quick as these folks to approach your clients. They have excellent time to reach back to customer. Imagine, you register for a webinar at some tech giant and the CEO or the country head calls you up, with the utmost care.
2. There is no free lunch, you got to pay or put in your time and sweat some place or other.
