--- POST TEXT ---

I thought I'd save myself some money and hassle by purchasing from an online pharmaceutical company without a prescription. Following a brief Google search, I came across a site that seemed legitimate and offered a reasonable deal. However, the lack of third-party information or reviews about this website raised some doubts. Even so, seeing a Google credibility ranking associated with the site, I decided to risk purchasing something from them. This was the start of what turned out to be a scam.

Here's how it unfolded:

1. They called me, assuring me it wasn't a scam. They suggested a test transaction where I could pay 50% upfront and the remaining 50% upon arrival of the product. The total cost was roughly $100.
2. The next day, they claimed they had shipped the product and required the rest of the payment at that time. They had switched the agreed payment method and foolishly, I paid the balance. They asked for the payment in Bitcoin.
3. The following day, they told me the shipping company needed proof that I was authorized to purchase the product or a different "insurance" fee of another $100, all of which would be refunded upon delivery. This is when I realized I had fallen victim to a scam.
4. The original guy (supposedly in NY, but probably in India) again called me and offered to share his personal information, so I would be confident enough to pay the insurance fee. (I didn't pay it, but these guys are definitely professionals at this, and lie without hesitation and know how to address buyer concerns in a way that gets money out of them)

It's probably a common scam, but I assumed they wouldn't risk their entire reputation since they had a Google ranking. I figured a simple Reddit post with their URLs would warn future potential customers and help stop the scam. Hopefully others will do a Google search and find this post...

The URLs they used were:

* [**https://goodmanpharmaceuticals.com/**](https://goodmanpharmaceuticals.com/)
* [**https://dmasupplyusa.online**](https://dmasupplyusa.online/) (the alleged shipping company)
