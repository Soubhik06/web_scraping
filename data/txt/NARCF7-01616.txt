If someone in Hitech City comes to you selling airpods, Apple watch, or airpods Max, however genuine the product looks and feel, however genuine the packaging feels, however convincing the background story of the guy sounds... do NOT buy it, it is fake.

Bought airpods for 5k 😂, Apple website shows the serial number is legit, on opening the case the airpods pop up on my iPhone screen, it charges wirelessly, it sounds good, the touch controls on the stem work, build quality is good... But it is fake (the ANC, the transparency do not work)

For 5k I won't complain that it is a bad product with all these features + it looks exactly like airpods, but try to negotiate it within 1k-2k range
