A warning post if anyone has purchased Paytm Fasttag. Periodically keep checking you can still access your Paytm wallet, or at the time of a long trip when you go to recharge your FastTag balance like I did today, you will get a surprise "Wallet Unactivated", and can't activate it because "We are no longer boarding new customers". I am one of the very early adopters of Paytm, although I stopped using their app once they started doing "everything" on the app and deploying a ton of dark patterns.

Now I am off getting a lot of cash/change to be paying on the toll booths because paytm customer care will take 24-48 hours to resolve the issue.

And they don't actually tell you your wallet is unactivated and what not, they just won't allow you to add money. Instead, they show forms to enter your Adhaar, PAN, Lisence and what not, and even after that won't even give you an error. It feels like a phishing campaign to harvest very private data, and despite being usually wary of this shit, I fell for it in the hour of desperation. 

First thing I am doing after my trip is burning their shitty FastTag and get one from some bank instead. At least their FastTags are a separate thing which can be recharged separtely, even if I end up locking some money away, at least it won't get locked because Paytm decided you are not an old customer anymore.
