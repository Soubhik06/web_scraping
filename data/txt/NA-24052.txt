Settl is a FRAUD company. I stayed at Altea Property, Domlur, Bangalore, from October 24 to November 17, 2024. I moved in due to a family emergency—my father was admitted in critical condition at Hospital which was near to the property. Unfortunately, I lost him during my tenancy.

Before signing the contract, I confirmed with the management that if I vacated before the agreed period, I would receive a full refund of my security deposit and any remaining rent. I vacated the property on November 17, ensuring all necessary clearances were completed, and the property manager provided a some kind of move-out summary from the property manager confirming the satisfactory condition of the property.

However, despite multiple follow-ups over calls, emails from the past two months, I have not received my almost ₹30,000/- security deposit. The management has failed to provide any valid justification for the delay and has been unresponsive to my inquiries. This lack of accountability is deeply disappointing.

I urge potential tenants to exercise caution and ensure that all terms are clearly documented and honored. I hope this review serves as a cautionary tale for others considering this property.
