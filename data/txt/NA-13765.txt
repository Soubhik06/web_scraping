Fintech company MobiKwik’s payment gateway subsidiary Zaakpay has announced an integration with Meta that will enable users to make payments through WhatsApp. The partnership allows customers to purchase a service or a product and businesses to accept payments within a WhatsApp chat.

The integration offers payments through Credit Card, Debit Card, UPI, and Wallet payments. Users also have the option to make payments using MobiKwik Wallet, ZIP, and Pocket UPI. The transactions are centralized on one interface and encrypted for security purposes. ZaakPay aims for this to be a financial inclusion product, stating that it will allow businesses to leverage Whatsapp and cater to a customer base in Tier 2 and 3 cities.

Currently, WhatsApp offers payment services through UPI. It allows users to transfer amounts through BHIM UPI and has partnered with 4 banks: ICICI Bank, HDFC Bank, Axis Bank and State Bank of India.

Zaakpay received in principal approval from the RBI to operate as a payment aggregator in 2023. Reportedly , MobiKwik first applied for a PA license in 2021 but was rejected by the RBI. MobiKwik announced its plans once again to go public in 2024, after deciding to forego its plans in 2021 due to weak market conditions. According to its Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) it has invested Rs. 8.9 crore in Zaakpay and has earned Rs. 51.7 lakh from consumer payments made through it, as of March 31, 2023. Zaakpay had an income of Rs 1.6 crore.

It also stated that it depended on ZaakPay for its payment services and financial services business and any disruption in its functioning could adversely affect the operations of its payments and Financial Services products.

Mobikwik reported a profit for the first time this year, of Rs 14.08 crore in the financial year 2024 (FY24), Co-founder and CFO Upasana Taku shared on LinkedIn. This was a notable turnaround from the Rs 83.8 crore loss recorded in FY23. Its payment gateway revenue grew by 18.4% to Rs 201 crore in FY24, according to data from the Registrar of Companies.

Mobikwik Co-Founder Bipin Preet Singh told NDTV Profit at the Global Fintech Fest 2024 in Mumbai that it was aiming for 10% market share in the entire UPI ecosystem. He said, “We are the largest wallet and largest in terms of transactions on UPI from a PPI (prepaid payment instruments) point of view. We are doing 2,00,000 transactions every day. If 100% transactions today are happening via the bank from the UPI, we want to take 10% from that and move it to digital wallet.”

Mobikwik have also reportedly applied to join the Reserve Bank of India’s digital currency pilot and offer transactions via e-rupee, according to Reuters . This came after the RBI shared its plans to include non-bank payment system operators in offering Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) wallets earlier this year in April .
