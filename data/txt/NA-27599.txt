Hello everyone, in March 2023, a couple of weeks after losing my job, I was contacted via WhatsApp for a work-from-home opportunity, which turned out to be a scam. Despite successfully avoiding various scams over the years, such as phone calls with Indian accents, fake bank calls, and others, I fell victim this time due to negligence and arrogance, thinking I was good at avoiding scams. This was a wrong assumption. I should mention that in the past, I also struggled with gambling problems. The total loss amounts to nearly 20,000, and now I suffer from PTSD, with a continuous cycle of thoughts for a year. The main problem is that I can't understand why I desired more earnings. Was it due to being tired of living in a shared house? Stress from too many working hours? Low self-esteem? Dissatisfaction? Wanting to return to my country? Or do I simply have low cognitive abilities? Should I really blame myself, at least partially? These are all questions spinning in my head, and it's burning my brain. Anyway, to anyone else who has experienced something similar, I hope your lives are going better.
