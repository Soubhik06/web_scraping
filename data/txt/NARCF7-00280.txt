--- POST TEXT ---

Yesterday, my girlfriend and I (British’s tourists) were looking for a mobile phone shop. It had been closed down. As we stood figuring out what to do next, a guy stopped us and asked if he could help. He took us to a nearby shop, and then we exchanged numbers and went for a chai. 

He seemed great. We had to go for a tour shortly after we met, but we agreed to meet after the tour. 

When we met again, we went for another chai. He explained that people in Rajasthan, where he is from and where we intend to visit, respect tourists who wear the local clothes more. 

He suggested we go to a tailor to get some custom clothes (kurta, PJ, etc.) made. We agreed it could be useful. After all, we trusted this guy.

He led us into a back street and into a very small tailor shop. The owner sat us three down on stools and asked mine and my girlfriends names. This guy then began talking for ever about all his UK clients, including (he claims) cricketer Ben Stokes. He talked forever.

Eventually we talked price. He tried to charge 7,000 rupees for one Kurta with PJs. 

Then we realised it was odd that he didn’t ask for our new friends name. After all, as far as he knew, he was a customer too, right? 

When I raised this concern, they both looked sheepish. The owner denied it casually while the “friend” stayed silent. 

At that point, we walked out. The owner said “you know, this is very rude.” At the door, we shook hands and said a quick goodbye to our new friend - who showed no objection.

Was this a scam?
