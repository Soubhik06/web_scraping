Heard counsel for the parties. The applicant has been arrested on 5.4.2015 in
connection with Crime No.266/2013 registered with Police
Station Civil Lines, Rewa District Rewa (M.P.) - commonly
known as VYAPAM Examination Scam cases - for the
offences punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 466 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 109 , 120-B of IPC and under Section 3/4 of M.P.
Recognized Examination Act, 1937. The role ascribed to the applicant by the prosecution is
that of an impersonator. The applicant, at the instance of
racketeer - Vikas Dixit, facilitated the original candidate -
Anoop Singh Baghel by appearing in his place in the PMT
Examination 2009 conducted by VYAPAM. It is not not disputed that all the co-accused linked to the
applicant have already been arrested. It is, fairly, stated by the learned counsel for the State, on
instructions, that custodial interrogation of the applicant is no
more required. The charge sheet has been filed against the
applicant on 20.6.2015. Further, the investigation against the 2 M.Cr.C. No.7396/2015 applicant is complete in all respects as of now and, therefore,
the presence of applicant is no more required. In that sense, the
link between the accused persons is already complete. Further,
the applicant is not involved in any other crime of similar type.
Besides, the applicant through counsel undertakes to appear
before the Investigating Officer as and when called for further
investigation including against him as also to punctually
appear before the trial Court to ensure early disposal of the
criminal trial. In the interest of justice, taking overall view of the
matter, we deem it appropriate to grant bail to the applicant on
following strict conditions:- The bail application stands disposed of accordingly.
