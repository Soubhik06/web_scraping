ಎಲ್ಲರಿಗು ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ 

Couple of weeks ago, my friends and I decided to go to Socials for food and drinks. We drank a lot and are comparatively lesser food. Socials has the order online option so we placed orders through that. Once we were done, the person got us a bill. We didn't check thoroughly and paid, after going home I realised we got scammed. 2 beers (500 rs) and 3 G&amp;T (1700) were added on the bill. I called them and asked them to inquire into this which they said they'll talk to the server. I didn't hear from them again and I called them after two days asking what is the update, they said you placed orders offline (we had asked him to repeat one beer offline). I asked them to check CCTV footage to see if we've ordered extra, the guy gave different reasons like we've come to him two days late and CCTV footage is stored somewhere else and that they cannot go checking for it now plus also that we placed some orders offline. We gave up because there was no definite proof that we could find for it.

Adre naavu ivaga yavude socials ge hogalla. Neevu kuda swalpa kottiro bill na channage check madi payment madi. Scam ide anta gothadre avarna name and shame madi!
