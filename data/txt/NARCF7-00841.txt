I just wanted to raise awareness about the recent scam circulating on social media, where they claim that you can earn a certain amount by completing simple tasks at home like “watching a certain youtube video” or “following certain instagram pages”

Just wanted to tell alert everyone **DO NOT engage in these so called tasks.**

Initially, it might seem genuine that an agency is paying for someone to watch a video or follow someone on Instagram, but there's a lot more going on

The individuals behind this scam will approach you through WhatsApp or Instagram and then add you to a Telegram group where they give you tasks and claim to pay you for completing them. However, they will only pay you a small, negligible amount, such as 5 or 10 rupees, if you don't complete the "prepaid task."

The prepaid task is the true scam, as they will tell you that after completing it, the task price will increase to 70-100 rupees. In the prepaid task, they will offer you different money brackets and promise to double the money you invest.

Once you agree to the prepaid task, they will add you to a group with 4-6 other individuals and ask you to register on a shady cryptocurrency website where they claim to be able to double your money. In the group, they will tell you that there are two more sub-tasks to complete and that after completing them, you will receive the money back with increased profits. 

However, for each sub-task, the amount you previously paid will be doubled until you are not willing to pay more and after that they will simply block you from every group and you will no way to recover the money.

**Please be cautious and stay alert.**
