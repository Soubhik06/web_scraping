I thought i had found a good website while i was looking for a PS5. I read through some small print and saw it had a chat facility, so i used it to ask if PS5 were in stock, as i didnt want to order one and then be on a waiting list for stock to arrive. The "customer support" told me they were in stock. I filled in my details and gave my card details. I was presented with a page that said my order was confirmed. I didnt screenshot it but went to my emails for my confirmation email. There wasnt one. I assumed because it was evening that it would arrive the next day. It didnt. I tried the phone numbers supplied, one didnt exist, one went to a voicemail and another went to india or somewhere. The money has gone out of my bank and the bank can not cancel until after 30 days when i can dispute it. I sent an email to try to cancel my order but of course it was returned as it doesnt exist.
