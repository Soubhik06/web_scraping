I posted my dining table for sale on OLX and within a minute I received a text from someone who was interested in buying the same. It was a lady, we checked her profile and found that the profile was created the same day only, so that was the first red flag. She straight up called as well. We missed her call but then we called back. She just asked about the condition of the furniture, that’s it, no bargain, no query regarding exact location, nothing at all. After that she asked us to just send her our QR code to scan and make the payment which sounded fishy to us. So we told her that we will take payment only when she will come to receive the furniture. Post that she said she doesn’t want it and hung up the call.
I am aware about the scams where buyer sends you the qr code and then scams you but this was something different. Does anyone have any idea about this kind of scam where the buyer asks you for your QR code ?
