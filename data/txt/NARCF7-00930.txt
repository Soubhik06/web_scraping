Hi everyone

I am a doctor and had given out my number and CV at a few places while looking for a job a while back. 

In June 2022, I got a call from someone claiming to be Suhana Gupta and informed me of a vacancy. When inquired further, she said she is recruiting for Fortis and Apollo. I had visited the sites for both and submitted my resume at both the places. I vaguely remembered Fortis website warning about possible scammers and that their recruitment is through their HR only. 

But I was a fresher with limited options, so I asked her for further details nevertheless. She sent me a date, time, documents to bring and the address (Netaji Subhash Place). I was living with my grandparents, who were going to take me for the interview there the next day. I am a suspicious and paranoid person however (RIP my future spouse), and I looked up the number on TrueCaller and it was a proper, reported fraud. [https://imgur.com/a/tfv8MJr](https://imgur.com/a/tfv8MJr) 

Despite my grandparents insisting otherwise, I did not go. Because they are caring and simple people who would have 100% given any money asked for to ensure I got a good job. 

Fine, crisis averted. 

Two days back, I get a call from someone claiming to be Roshan. He told about recruitment at Sir Ganga Ram Hospital. I spoke to him twice. His phone number wasn't flagged by TrueCaller. He sent me the details, and its again at the same building (Netaji Subhas Place). I spoke to my old roommate who is training at Ganga Ram to speak to their HR and they said they aren't recruiting for any JRs. 

Some light reading about the building and multiple people have filed reports about getting duped out of tens of thousands of rupees. They even arrested two people a while back. And the job offer scam isn't limited to the medical field. 

If this is so widespread and common, how are they still operating? I mean their base seems to be the same in all the cases?

Has anyone else dealt with a similar case during their job hunt?
