Heard learned counsel for the petitioner. The petitioner apprehends his arrest in Daudnagar P.S.

 Case No.304 of 2018 registered under Section 420 , 409 and 34 of Indian Penal Code. The gist of the allegation against the petitioner is that

 he being the Chairman of Malwar PACS purchased 1401

 quintals paddy from farmers on payment of money given by the

 Co-operative Bank and the petitioner had to supply the entire

 paddy to the mill for milling. The miller had to supply 938.67

 quintals C.M.R. to the S.F.C. but no amount of C.M.R. was

 supplied to the S.F.C. Petitioner has not annexed any papers

 showing that he had supplied 1401 quintals of paddy to any

 miller entrusted by the S.F.C. for milling the paddy. Learned counsel for the petitioner prayed for time to
 Patna High Court Cr.Misc. No.74483 of 2018(3) dt.09-01-2019 2/2 file the documents showing supply of paddy to the miller. As prayed for, put up on 18.01.2019. The learned counsel for the petitioner is also directed

 to serve a copy of this bail petition to the learned counsel

 appearing on behalf of S.F.C.

 (Prabhat Kumar Jha, J)
