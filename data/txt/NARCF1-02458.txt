A scam is currently underway by imposters posing as Maharashtra State Electricity Board (MSEB) officials. They are contacting people through WhatsApp, demanding immediate payment for overdue electricity bills, or threatening to cut off the power within a specified timeframe. Today I got a message from the scammer I was nearly convinced by that message then I tried to call that number no one picked up then a call came from the other side saying I haven't paid my bill and the penalty of my bill. I was previously having an issue with mseb about our residential connection. Even I have got a notice about it. I have also applied a complaint to them about my penalty. This message kinda convinced me. I thought my application got rejected and I have to pay my penalty, but when the call came he was saying you have to pay via credit card and all the so-called scam techniques I got alerted by that scam. Lastly, I messaged that scammer He instantly blocked me which made my doubt sure about the scam. Stay vigilant, report any suspicious messages to MSEB and local authorities, and help spread the word to protect others. (I think this scam is going around for years)
