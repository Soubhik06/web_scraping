Mumbai, Apr 1 (PTI) Maharashtra NCP (SP) president Shashikant Shinde on Wednesday dismissed as “baseless and childish” the allegations that the Sharad Pawar-led party had given a “contract” to run a smear campaign against the ruling NCP.
He was reacting to allegations raised by Nationalist Congress Party (NCP) leader Suraj Chavan that NCP (SP) MLA Rohit Pawar had provided Rs 5 crore to an agency to defame leaders of the NCP, a member of the Mahayuti alliance, and create internal rifts.
“These are baseless and childish allegations. Those raising such claims should instead verify with their allies to identify who was actually creating trouble for them,” Shinde told reporters.

Referring to the air crash that killed then Deputy Chief Minister Ajit Pawar in January, the NCP (SP) leader said the black box report was still awaited, and findings of the CID probe had not been made public.
