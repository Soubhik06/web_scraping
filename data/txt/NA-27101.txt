Hey, I am 26 year old from India and had recently completed my TESOL course and was looking for jobs in the field of ESL when I came across an ad by a party disguised as a recruiter for a "newly opened language center" at the University of Hamburg. Seeing it as a genuine opportunity, I quickly applied and was contacted soon for a written and an oral interview. After completing my interviews, which looked very genuine, I was offered a job after a month. I was sent a job contract, which I signed and was promised an air ticket along with a visa and a decent salary. As I thought it was a genuine offer, as the domain name on the email was .de, I signed the contract and submitted the needed documents. After a few days I was shown an apartment and was asked for a payment of EUR 2550 to secure the apartment. As the proceedings that they told me about matched the Indian proceedings I did not doubt them for long and sent the amount. Little did I know what fate awaited me. Today I was asked to send them a sum of EUR 2800 to register myself as a teacher in Germany. This fee also included a lawyers fee and a fee for a courier service. I was taken aback for a moment because the contract did not mention any payments of that nature. That is when I searched the internet extensively and found out about this scam and about this specific email id. I want to share my story here because not much information about this scam is available online and I believe people should be aware of this. Also please share stories of yourself or anyone you know of have been scammed by these people or parties like these.
