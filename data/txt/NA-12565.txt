by Rule 7 of Cenvat Credit Rules, 2004. The rule prescribes that, firstly,

the distribution of input service tax cannot be more than the Service

Tax paid and secondly no input tax credit availed on the services used

exclusively in a unit manufacturing exempt goods can be distributed to

the other units manufacturing dutiable goods or providing taxable

services. I find in this case the appellant has distributed the input tax

credit on 'Management Consultancy Services' to the 2(two) units

manufacturing dutiable goods. The department has confirmed the

demand of Cenvat credit attributable to the exempt unit, which has

been distributed to the other two dutiable goods manufacturing units. I

find that the distribution of the input tax credit by the appellant is

legally sustainable and the demand of the Cenvat credit along with the

interest and is not tenable. The appellant further contended that they

are an Input Service Distributor (ISD) and hence they merely distribute

the input tax credit and they neither avail or utilize the input tax credit,

therefore the provisions of penalty under Rule 15 of the Cenvat Credit

Rules pre and post amendment to Rule 15 is not tenable. I find for the

period prior to 26.02.2010, penalty can be levied only for wrong

availment of Cenvat credit on inputs or capital goods and there is no

provision for invoking penalty provisions under Rule 15 with regard to

wrong availment of Cenvat credit on input services. However, for the Page 18 of 19 Excise Appeal Nos. E/26512, 26522/2013

period after 26.02.2010, penalty can be levied under rule 15 for wrong

utilization of Cenvat credit on input services. I find in this case the

period involves pre and post amendment to Rule 15 of Cenvat Credit

Rules, 2004. Therefore, imposition of penalty for the period prior to

launch 26.02.2010 is legally not sustainable. Further it is seen that the

availment of the distribution of credit to the 2(two) units manufacturing

dutiable goods is legally tenable and hence the confirmation of demand

with interest and imposition of penalty are not sustainable.

Consequently, irrespective of the period no penalty can be imposed

under Rule 15 of Cenvat Credit Rules, 2004 more so when the demand

of irregular availment of Cenvat credit, itself is not tenable. 32. In view of the above discussion, Appeal No. E/26522/2013 is
