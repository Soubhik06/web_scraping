So I have been getting calls from this number in the link, almost twice or thrice a day sometimes even at 2-3am . The first time I picked up the call the person on the other side apparently couldn't hear me. So I hung up. Other times I couldn't hear anything. When I checked Truecaller it was shown as some "Nhai fastag". But when I checked online I couldn't find anything related to it and FASTag had different numbers. According to Truecaller, many users has reported as a scam. And according to this [article](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/gadgets-news/dont-fall-for-this-fastag-fraud/articleshow/73465367.cms) , cases like this has happened before. I blocked the number after this. Have you guys ever received calls from this or a similar number ? Do you guys think it's a scam or a legitimate FASTag in-charge worker?
