traversed,   it   would   be   necessary   for   the 
plaintiff to prove in order to support his right 
to a judgment of the court. In other words, it is 
a   bundle   of   facts   which   taken   with   the   law 
applicable to them gives the plaintiff a right to 
relief   against   the   defendant.   It   must   include 
some   act   done   by   the   defendant   since   in   the 
absence   of   such   an   act   no   cause   of   action   can 
possibly accrue. It is not limited to the actual 
infringement   of   the   right   sued   on   but   includes 
all the material facts on which it is founded. It 
does   not   comprise   evidence   necessary   to   prove 
such   facts,   but   every   fact   necessary   for   the 
plaintiff   to   prove   to   enable   him   to   obtain   a Page 26 of 29 C/FA/863/2015 CAV JUDGMENT decree. Everything which if not proved would give 
the defendant a right to immediate judgment must 
be   part   of   the   cause   of   action.   But   it   has   no 
relation whatever to the defence which may be set 
up by the defendant nor does it depend upon the 
character   of   the   relief   prayed   for   by   the 
plaintiff.   (See  : A.B.C.   Laminart   Pvt.   Ltd.   & 
Anr. v. A.P. Agencies, Salem (1989) 2 SCC 163. 19. We   are   of   the   view   that   the   learned   trial
