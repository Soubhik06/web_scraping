I had read a lot about these scam calls online. Just few days back I had to send a courier to Chennai &amp; 2 days later I received a call from this number - +919779570374 saying this is an emergency call from customs &amp; there is an problem with my parcel. 

Thought for a second of I should play with these guys, but was super busy with work so just reported &amp; blocked the number. 

While I have received other scam texts or calls that you read about everyday, I always wondered why I never got this particular scam call &amp; I got it just few days after I sent this courier. 

Correlation is causation in this case?
