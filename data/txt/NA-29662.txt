Balasore, Mar 23 (PTI) Two crew members of a mechanised boat died on Monday after allegedly inhaling toxic fumes while unloading fish at a jetty near Chandipur beach in Odisha’s Balasore district, police said.
The incident occurred at Naupalgadi fishing jetty at Balaramgadi. The deceased were identified as Pravakar Mallick and Sashina Mallick, residents of Aruha village in Bhadrak district.
The two men fell unconscious after entering the fish storage chamber of the boat soon after it anchored at the jetty, police said. They were rushed to the Balasore District Headquarters Hospital, where doctors declared them dead.
