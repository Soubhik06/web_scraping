Those who have watched the movie The Big Short will probably remember Michael Burry pointing out that before every recession fraud rates go up, this has been true for every recession/depression starting from 1929.

The same has already started at least for UK and India. Financial fraud rates are at an all time high. But due to excessive quantitative easing by central banks the markets are totally immune as of now, I am kind of worried what is going to happen, if those indicators turn out to be true. I just started earning and have already started investing in stocks.
