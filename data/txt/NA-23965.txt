WhatsApp Scams unfortunately are picking up and more often than not, even digital natives get caught in such scams. Can't even think of the efforts required by an elderly person :(

Anyway here is an easy way to protect yourself from scamsters who use OTP to access your WhatsApp: WhatsApp has a Multi Factor Authentication. It can be enabled via whatsapp &gt; account&gt; 2 step verification.

**How it helps?**

1. If someone gets OTP from you with a fraudulent method, they still won't be able to login to your WhatsApp since the second PIN required has to be entered from the memory.
2. Incase someone else gets your discarded phone no, they won't be able to login to WhatsApp either.

This ensures a higher degree of safety from scamsters.

**Another PIN to remember?! 😢😢** 

Well WhatsApp has made this easier as well. It prompts you once in a while to make sure you remember your PIN.

**Is it 100% full proof?🤦‍♂️** 

Well there are difficult workarounds to get into your WhatsApp account without the second OTP but again they are very difficult.

Having said that please still beware of the scamsters. This protects you against one type of scam. Not all.
