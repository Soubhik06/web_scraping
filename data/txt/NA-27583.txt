--- POST TEXT ---

I asked Google to give me the Apple Support number. The number that came up I now realize was part of a scam site. Just be careful if you try to contact Apple Support through a Google Assistant search.

I called and explained that there were fraudulent charges on my card that I needed to be refunded. They proceeded to ask me "identity verification questions" which included the last 4 of my social (I'm such an idiot...), where I do my banking, and my zip code. Since I was the one who called the number initially, I wasn't in a suspicious as I should have been. She then asked me to download Team Viewer. This is when I realized something was wrong. I started to get suspicious, and the call dropped.

When I called back, I explained that I didn't believe they were actually Apple Support and asked if they could verify their identity. She said yes, but needed to know the reason for my call. I explained, and she asked for my DOB. I refused, said it wasn't relevant and to please verify her identity. She then asked for my Apple ID so they could send me a verification code. I told her, and a 2FA code popped up as a text. She then asked me to read it to her and I refused. She tried to explain that this was the only way she could verify her identity, but that made no sense and it was enough to confirm it was a scam. I immediately called my bank and set up a verbal password (instead of my mother's maiden name which can be googled for most people).

I have no other information other than the fact that she called herself Jennifer and she sounded Indian. I can't even find the site that Google sent me to anymore so I'm quite shaken, but I created a Fraud Alert through Equifax and signed up for their identity protection service. If you guys have any other tips to keep my identity safe, please let me know. 😥
