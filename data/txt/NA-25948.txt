--- POST TEXT ---

We visited Kondapochamma reservoir this weekend. Unfortunately by the time we reached, it was past the closing time (5PM) so us and many others just parked our cars on the side of the road and walked up to take a view. One policeman who was at the gate asked one of my friend about where the vehicle is parked. He replied far away. The policeman mumbled "Toh kya faayda (what is the benefit in that)". At that time, we did not think much about that comment. 

Later when we were on the way back, I get an SMS that I have been issued a challan for ₹535 for "Disobedience of order under section 179(1)". Now there were at-least 15-20 cars parked on the side of the road in a row. And I am sure that they challaned each one of them. Imagine if they are doing it every weekend! Further this does not add up since there is no Section 179(1) in Telangana MV Act and the maximum penalty for "Disobeying a traffic police officer in uniform" is ₹200 (which we did not do BTW, since we were not ordered by any police officer to do anything). 
[https://www.htp.gov.in/MV-Act.html](https://www.htp.gov.in/MV-Act.html)

So how can I challenge this challan? I think they are using this opportunity to scam money from desperate people who after a long lockdown are going for some fresh air. I am thinking of filing an RTI to ask them to explain the nature of the offence.
