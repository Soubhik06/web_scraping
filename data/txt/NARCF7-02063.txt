When I was talking with my parents about the fraud in NEET exams, they said that the Pokkho stepped in to issue that only the Bengali NEET aspirants should get jobs since they didn't do any fraud unlike Punjab, Bihar, UP and more...

When I questioned this, they reiterated why Bengal isn't great today (because apparently the people in UP and Bihar have a "will do job" mindset, to name a few). They started to reiterate what 12th Fail showed about the board exams there.

I told them that the fraud was done on more than 5 states or such, how can you know that the ones who have got rankings lower than 1st (but single/double digit rankings) and from Bengal haven't done any কারচুপি?

I am not saying that everyone did it. I am just showing the bias in people these days.

Now, when something (possibly) spreads through Internet, only then can we say that so many people got exposure to it. Bengalis were no exception. There can remain some outliers.

See, the fraud has happened nationwide. Everyone needs to talk about it. But when the Pokkho steps in regarding these issues, it just devolves to discrimination against Bengalis. What we want is to inform that frauds from Internet can happen all around India.

Even NTA released a circular which clearly stated that the toppers weren't restricted to one state only.

Let us not get blinded by this Us vs Them game.
