I'm absolutely fuming and wanted to warn everyone about a terrible experience I had with a vendor called Prime Meats (phone number 7328880920).

This wasn't a random vendor I found online. They had been messaging me on WhatsApp for several months, advertising their products. Earlier I've used other online meat delivery platforms in Bhubaneswar without any issues, so I thought I'd give them a try.

I placed an online order for fish and mutton and prepaid Rs.2470 via UPI. I have the transaction ID/screenshot as proof.

After the payment went through, I waited for the delivery... and waited. Nothing. I tried calling their number but no answer. After multiple attempts, I realized they had blocked my number!

Initially, I wanted to do Cash on Delivery (COD). However, the vendor insisted that they only accept advance payments due to frequent order cancellations by customers. Since I was busy and they had been messaging for a while, I decided to pay upfront. Big mistake!

So, to recap:

* **Ordered and prepaid INR 2470 for meat and fish.**
* **Vendor refused COD, insisting on advance payment.**
* **No delivery.**
* **Vendor is unresponsive and has blocked my number.**

I have filed a dispute abut transaction with my bank and also filed online complaint with Odisha police (don't know if FIR has been registered or not).

Honestly, 2470 isn't a huge amount, but I'm just so tired of all these small-scale scams popping up everywhere.

A Truecaller search of the number (7328880920) reveals that other users have reported similar scam experiences. It seems this is a pattern of behavior, not an isolated incident.

Though I've little faith in recovering my loss, I desperately want to stop this from happening to other people. 

**(TL;DR: Prime Meats (7328880920) scammed me out of INR 2470, took my money and blocked me. Beware! They contacted me via whatsapp for months, and insisted on advance payment only.)**
