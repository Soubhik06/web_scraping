I became a victim of an online fraud which happened after contacting the owner of a property listed on Housing.com.
The owner shared a QR code with me and along with one more person continuously asked me to make transactions in order to visit the property. He scammed me by saying that the amount is for visiting fee or housing fee etc and said the amount is refundable. In the hope getting the refund of my money I was asked to tranfer more money every time.
A fraud of total Rs 52,200 has been done with me. I have done a complaint in Cyber Cell regarding the same.
I have the scammer's contact number, UPI id and even a photo of the aadhar card and I presented the same to the police but they didn't take any action does anyone know what can I do to get my money back? Can anyone please help?
