Like many Indian citizens I too get bombarded with spam and scam calls all the time. From people asking if I wanted loans, credit cards, and even trying to scam. 

Even DND fails or only mitigates it to an extent. Truecaller is a must have app now.

It really kills one’s productivity when working and peace of mind. When it’s spam I usually am stern but not rude as they are doing they’re jobs. But scammers..well screw them. 

I believe the more older you get, have a bigger income, better credit scores and patterns, this menace keeps multiplying.

I’ve completely stopped sharing my mobile number to anyone who asks at malls, shoppers stop, pantaloons, Grocery stores, More outlets, restaurants (yea even if it’s for waiting list as I’m usually standing right there waiting) unless there’s a heavy purchase involved and for invoice reasons. But that this too is restricted. Everywhere they ask “sir contact number?” I say “no that’s ok”. I’ve never been questioned on that though ready to shut any one down who may insist for my number. I mean the so called loyalty benefits if any are pretty much useless anyway.

Only on online stores are we forced to give it for account OTP related reasons. Although I’m very sure they out that too. Recently purchased some products from Nykaa and a scamster called saying she’s from Nykaa and I’ve won appliances and stuff and she knew my entire order history on Nykaa.

Beware folks. Take care of your privacy and protect it.

Edit- My point about Truecaller being necessary is to identify these unwanted calls. 

Those who helpfully pointed out why it ain’t the best out there- thank you.

Those who cynically pointed it out - screw you.
