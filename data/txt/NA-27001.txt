--- POST TEXT ---

I received an Instagram ad that Lucy &amp; Yak were having a huge sale where everything was $3.99. I should have known it was too good to be true! I placed a $65 order, then noticed that it is shipping from China. This is what tipped me off to the scam - Lucy &amp; Yak make all of their clothing in the UK/India. I looked at the website and receipt - the order wasn't placed on the official Lucy &amp; Yak website, it's from a website called GloPast. It was hard to find information about them online, but I eventually found a Reddit thread (lost the link!), which confirmed my suspicions. Apparently, this happens a lot with Instagram ads and Lucy &amp; Yak, but with various "business" names (not always GloPast). Filing a transaction dispute and freezing my card until I can get a new one. Be careful! (Although I'm sure most of you are smarter than me...I just couldn't resist those damn dungarees!)
