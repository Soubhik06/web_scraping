Hello everyone will explain you a scam by these mobile phone repair stores. A shop named Truebot in Barkatpura, I went there to repair my speakers. The Google reviews are very good of this store. With trust I went there and explained my issue with the speakers. The owner of the store said to me that my speakers will need to be replaced because due to use they weren't working properly. 

He said that he will replace them and add new parts. I agreed paid 6000 amount as said too.
Guess what my speakers once again are not working so I took it to a different shop. 
Said them that why the new part of speaker isn't working. The other shop owner said that no new part is installed, there is no change in the speaker.

I was shocked and completely in disbelief. I got to know that the Truebot owners has only cleaned my speakers and gave me in return. 
For that he took 6k from me.
Do not ever visit this shop ever.
