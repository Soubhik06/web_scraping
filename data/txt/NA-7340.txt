Heard counsel for the parties. This application for bail is filed by the applicant who has
been named as accused in Crime No.9/2014 for the offence
punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 and 120-B of
I.P.C. and Section 3(D)(1)(2) read with section 4 of M.P.
Recognised Examination Act, 1937, registered with S.T.F. Police
Station, Bhopal, commonly known as VYAPAM scam cases. The role ascribed to the applicant by the prosecution is that
of an Impersonator. It is, however, common ground that the
applicant was arrested on 2.5.2014 and thereafter investigation
has been completed against the present applicant on 25.6.2014.
As a result, the Investigating Officer has filed final police report
against the applicant. It is stated across the Bar by the counsel for the State, on
instructions, that the custody of the present applicant is no more
necessary in connection with Crime No.9/2014. Further, other
accused, namely, the Middleman and Beneficiary, are already in
custody. Hence, there is no possibility of tampering of evidence
by the applicant. Taking over all view of the matter, therefore, the applicant is
ordered to be released on bail on strict conditions keeping in mind
that the applicant is non-resident of the State of Madhya Pradesh
and has assured through counsel that he will cooperate with the
Investigating Officer and also regularly appear before the trial
Court, as and when required, for early disposal of the trial :- the sum of Rs.5,00,000/- (Rupees Five Lacs) with
 two sureties in the like amount to the satisfaction of
 the Trial Court for his regular appearance during the
 trial in connection with Crime No.9/2014 in Police
 Station S.T.F. Bhopal; (2) The applicant shall also comply with the
 conditions enumerated under Section 437 (3) of
 Cr.P.C. meticulously; and
 (3) Additionally, the applicant will report to Police
 Station Virole, District Darbhanga, Bihar, once a
 week on every Sunday between 10:00 a.m. to 12:00
 noon unless required to attend the office of the
 Investigating Officer at Bhopal on that day.
 The bail application stands disposed of accordingly.
