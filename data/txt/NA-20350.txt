A junior scientist at a top government agency, who was kidnapped two days ago after he got honey-trapped, has been rescued from a hotel room in Noida and three people, including a woman, have been arrested, police said on Monday.
Official sources confirmed to PTI that the 45-year-old man worked as a junior scientist at a top government organisation in Delhi.
