--- POST TEXT ---

An envelope was at the gate.

Went and checked.

Shocked to see it had an original aadhar card. Of an old neighbour who'd shifted some 5-6 years ago.

The postman hadn't asked us to sign anything. It looks like it's not even a registered post, just a normal post. 

Postman had simply thrown it in inside our gate like any other spam post.

The neighbour is lucky I found it. I'm not in the [business of misusing other people's aadhar](https://www.indiatoday.in/technology/news/story/how-to-save-yourself-from-aadhaar-scams-1416910-2018-12-25). 

Lucky too because I recognized his name. My family members likely wouldn't have. Called him and told him to come collect it. 

He's an idiot too for not updating his address despite being educated and shifting years ago.

RS Sharma and Nilekani talk about 5 feet thick 13 feet high walls. What use is a 5 feet wall made of poor quality sand?!
