**TL;DR - OpIndia failed an international accreditation test for fact-checking websites on multiple grounds. Altnews and Boomlive have passed. Be careful about the fact-checking sources that you use. Do your homework on the organisations and the accrediting bodies.** 

**Background**: 

* The International Fact Checking Network is an international group of fact checkers in journalism, associated with the Poynter Institute in USA. More information about what they do, who works for them, and who funds them, can be found here: https://www.poynter.org/ifcn/

* Organisations that have accreditation from IFCN get access to more funding and collaborate on international fact-checking initiatives. See here for more details: https://www.poynter.org/ifcn/

* In India, Facebook has started using IFCN accredited organisations to confirm news stories, in an effort to tackle fake news in the political sphere. More information and background on this here: https://www.businesstoday.in/top-story/facebook-expands-fact-checking-network-in-india-adds-5-more-partners-to-spot-fake-news/story/318468.html
 
* Some Indian news organisations already have accreditation. The full list is here: https://ifcncodeofprinciples.poynter.org/signatories

**OPIndia, Altnews, Boomlive's applications:**

Recently three Indian organisations have applied for fact-checking accreditation. You can see the reports of their assesments here: 

**Altnews:** - https://ifcncodeofprinciples.poynter.org/application/public/pravda-media-foundation/D27BB43D-D8FC-F85B-1C25-2AF73DF3A12C
Recommendation: Accept

**Boomlive:** - https://ifcncodeofprinciples.poynter.org/application/public/boom/BEE99226-33F7-4B33-9B78-F0F98F51E991
Recommendation: Accept. 

**OPIndia:** https://ifcncodeofprinciples.poynter.org/application/public/opindiacom/EED18C9F-C8B2-258A-BB43-7E90FA57C26C
Recommendation: Reject 

In the case of **Altnews** and **Boomlive**, while accepting their applications, the assessor has made some recommendations for them to improve. If you look at those links, they have said **Altnews** has a grievance mechanism, but "It would help for the applicant to invite readers more proactively to send claims to fact check." Similarly, for **Boomlive**, they have said that while they do make some funding details clear, they can be more transparent about the specific sources of funding. 

When it comes to Opindia, it is worth reading the entire report, but I'll just quote this bit: 

&gt; Though the applicant has not officially endorsed any political group and has a stated non-partisianship policy, a quick look at their work indicates otherwise. Also, in the page where the website invites contributions from readers, it clearly indicates its bias. I quote from the website: “We won’t entertain the usual left-liberal narrative.”
&gt; 
&gt; The applicant links to the sources of the claim it is fact-checking and evidence it uses to fact check it. However, more often than not, the evidence that is used is usually from speeches made by political parties or the government, which are merely countering the claims. Data is rarely used, and then only to counter data claims made by political or other organizations.
&gt; 
&gt; Additionally, most claims are countered by making disparaging comments (for instance, calling the portals ‘habitual offender and regularly publishes fake stories’) about the organizations that published the claim, or by quoting someone with little credibility who generally agrees with their point of view.
&gt; 
&gt; The applicant lists its owner but offers no further details on funding. All key team members are clearly listed, with their biographies.

OPIndia has published a response to this failure to get accreditation here: https://www.opindia.com/2019/03/opindia-response-to-ifcn-international-fact-checking-networks-rejection-of-its-application-for-accreditation/
If you don't want to give them clicks, you can use this alternative link: https://web.archive.org/web/20190309142846/https://www.opindia.com/2019/03/opindia-response-to-ifcn-international-fact-checking-networks-rejection-of-its-application-for-accreditation/ 
Amongst other things they say, "...we had applied for accreditation by IFCN ourselves to check if the institution itself was biased." They do not deny that they are partisan, rather they justify it by saying there's nothing wrong with being partisan. They have also not denied the accreditor's assessment that they do not use data sufficiently in their fact checks, asking, "the argument about ‘data’ is bizarre. If the Fake News is being spread about data and statistics about a certain thing, only then can we use official data to prove that it is ‘Fake News’. How do we insert ‘data’ where it is not relevant?"

People may also be interested in this interview with Pratik Sinha, where he talks about IFCN accreditation, and the Indian organisations that have it, including one Republic News affiliated organisation. https://caravanmagazine.in/politics/alt-news-pratik-sinha-fake-news-election
