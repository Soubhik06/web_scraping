1. The present application is filed under Section 439 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973, for
 regular bail in connection with the FIR being C.R.
 No.11213022230118/2023 registered with the Jetpur
 City Police Station, Rajkot for the offence
 punishable under Sections 306 , 384 and 114 of the
 Indian Penal Code. 2. Learned advocate for the applicant has submitted
 that the applicant is arrested on 07.02.2023 and
 since then, he is in judicial custody and now the
 investigation is over and the chargesheet is
 filed. It is submitted that the allegations
 leveled against the applicant are that he aided
 and instigated the deceased to commit suicide as
 also the allegations of honey trap and extortion
