* Of all the cases tried in 2013, over 40% dealt with consensual sex, usually involving the elopement of a young couple and the girl’s parents subsequently charging the boy with rape. 

* Another 25% dealt with “breach of promise to marry”. Couple were into physical relationship. Guy did not marry the girl hence, the girl filed an FIR of rape.

* 35% of all the cases tried ACTUALLY fall in the actual rape category as we know it where there is no consensus between both the parties.

* Of all the ACTUAL rape cases, accused is a family member in 20% of the cases. In 70% of the cases the person was neighbor. Only 7% of the cases, the accused was an outsider.
