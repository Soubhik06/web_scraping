Story: I need an EC to apply for a loan. I applied for an encumbrance certificate(EC) online on the Kaveri Online Services website as instructed by the govt official at a BDA office. I filled in all the required info, searched the property and then applied for an E-signed copy. Had to pay like Rs.250 for this. (I had to give consent that this is non-refundable under any circumstance LOL)

2 days later, I get a message saying your application number JPN-EC-A-xxxxxxxx-2022-23 has been signed by SDA and uploaded. Well, I log in, and guess what? There is nothing there except the initial search application which is watermarked and useless anyway. 

So now I will have to go in person to the govt office again to get the EC and end up paying again + the customary bribe charges to get it done. 

Also, I did some searching cuz of the problems and I read that online EC's aren't accepted by banks for loans. If they don't accept it for loans, no way can it even be used for buying/selling too. Do they offer online services for govt documents that don't even hold any value and then don't even deliver on it just to scam people's money?
