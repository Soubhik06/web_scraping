This happened today near Pacific Mall in Tagore Garden. 

I was walking towards the mall and a guy approached me with a bike which had few broken parts. He asked for help as he had an accident and the people who helped him on the accident spot took out his wallet so he has no money. 

I saw the bike, it did have few things broken like headlight, gear knob, etc. I told him I don't have cash, if he uses UPI, I could give him something. I thought UPI will atleast give me his identity as he was ensuring to return the cash once he reach home. But who can trust anyone with cash!! On asked for UPI, he took out a phone from his pocket which had a cracked screen. He said its not working. He was super quick to take it forward by asking a nearby shopkeeper if he could give some cash if I pay to them via UPI. 

The shopkeeper denied saying he don't use online payments. I had my doubts about the legitimacy of this entire story and I asked him if has any bruises or injuries from the accident so I could take him to a doctor. To my surprise, he was extremely casual on this and said its just a small bruise on his knee, he pointed towards his right knee. 

And that's when I lost it. I was now 90% sure in my head that it's all a scam. The gear knob on his bike was on the left which according to him was broken. Head light was broken. It must have been a big crash or a bad fall. How come his hands or clothes are all clean!!! 

I politely suggested him to visit a mechanic and ask them to make a call to someone in your family who could pay the cost of repair via UPI to that mechanic and left from there.

P. S. Thank you for reading this long post. Just wanted to share if in case something similar happen to you, you can be aware.
