A delivery boy arrived in the afternoon claiming to have a parcel addressed to my brother. Since my brother was at work and couldn't recall ordering anything, he told me to accept the package and pay via UPI.

However, during his break, he checked his Amazon account and confirmed he hadn't placed any such order. He contacted customer care, who requested a bill. Unfortunately, there was only one bill stuck on the outside of the package.

They also sent a message with an OTP and a link to track the order status. Thankfully, my brother didn't click the link.

We lost around ₹1,000 in this scam. Apparently, some online sources suggest they might return and request a "wrong order" return.

Has anyone encountered a similar scam? Should I report it to the police or cybercrime?

The message originated from the number 57575022. I only used Google Pay for the payment.
