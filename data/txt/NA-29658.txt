Bhopal, Mar 25 (PTI) Prime Minister Narendra Modi, Union Home Minister Amit Shah and BJP president Nitin Nabin wished Madhya Pradesh Chief Minister Mohan Yadav on his 61st birthday on Wednesday.
Extending greetings to Yadav, PM Modi wished him a long and healthy life.
“Chief Minister Dr Mohan Yadav is taking several important initiatives for the all-round development of Madhya Pradesh, and under his leadership, the state is steadily progressing,” he said in a statement.

Shah prayed to Lord Mahakal for the chief minister’s good health and long life.
He said under the guidance and leadership of PM Modi, Madhya Pradesh is scaling new heights of development and public welfare.
BJP chief Nabin also extended birthday greetings to Yadav.
In Bhopal, MP BJP president Hemant Khandelwal, several ministers, bureaucrats and a large number of people turned up at Yadav’s residence to wish him on his birthday.
Born on March 25, 1965 in Ujjain, Yadav was sworn in as the chief minister of Madhya Pradesh on December 13, 2023. PTI MAS GK
