--- POST TEXT ---

My Xfinity bill went up unexpectedly, so I decided to call the worthless-bastards to find out why. I forgot the number so Googled Xfinity. I dialed the first number that came up. Why I didn't just call 1-800-XFINITY is beyond me. Brain fart.

First red flag; they answered immediately. Second red flag; it was an Indian guy calling himself Max. However he kept sending me text-code verification messages just like Infinity would, so it seemed slightly legit.

Max told me my bill went up because of taxes. I called bullshit on that. Taxes aren't twenty-dollars. Then he told me I switched to a more expensive plan on my own. I asked him to show me when I did that, and he quickly moved away from that topic.

Finally he offered me December for thirty-dollars and January and February free. I just had to pay thirty-dollars up front. Then he sent me a PayPal link with a crazy URL. 

At this point I knew it was definitely a scam, so I asked Max what the weather was like in Bangalore? He hung up. 

Immediately I attempted to call Xfinity Fraud Department. I had taken screen shots of everything and wanted to get this guy. But I was passed around a phone tree and put on hold forever. Eventually I hung up in disgust. 

This was an interesting con because "I called them" from a number "listed as Xfinity" from a "trusted source of information (Google)." Even more interesting is Xfinity could care less.
