This writ petition has been filed seeking issuance of a Writ of

 Mandamus directing the 2nd respondent to consider the petitioner’s

 representation dated 10.10.2025 and to pass orders forthwith for dissolving

 the unregistered Chit Fund Scheme said to be run in the name of “The Senai

 Thalaivar Community Society, Veeravanallur, Tirunelveli District”. 2. The petitioner states that he is a member of the Senai Thalaivar

 Community Society, which consists of around 600 members. According to

 the petitioner, the society has no valid registration and the so-called office

 bearers are only “letter-pad office bearers”. It is further alleged that the

 President has appointed himself and others without proper election or 2/6 https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis ( Uploaded on: 09/12/2025 08:47:32 pm ) W.P(MD)No.29728 of 2025 authorization and that, in spite of having no registration, the society has

 members of the public, including the petitioner, have raised objections and

 issued legal notices and representations to the authorities. However, no
