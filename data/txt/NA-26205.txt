I know most of you guys have heard about it. And I thought by this time almost everyone was aware that this is a scam but something unexpected happened today. So this is for the people who are unaware.

My Friend who is aware of almost all scams, had a call from dhl. They stated that there was a parcel in his name and verified his number. The scam is well setup, first there is an automated call which asks you about your parcel, press one if you want it delivered two if you want to cancel and such.

Once you press something, you get connected to a person who then explained that the parcel is in his name and that they found drugs in it. They asked for his skype id so that an officer from the mimbai police can investigate about it through video chat.

I happened to visit his home and saw him panicking about the situation. I told him it was a scam and not to attend the skype call. 

I explained the situation and well he's pissed at himself for even believing it. This can happen to anyone so kindly educate your friends and family.
