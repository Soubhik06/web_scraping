I recently received a random call on my phone at work some days back. It was from a lady who knew my name and she started speaking in Marathi saying she was calling from XYZ bank. 

She said I was eligible for a free credit card and that she wanted me to confirm the address for delivering my card. She even knew my work and home address and email ID. 

Usually I'm an alert person and with the ongoing scam scenes I would have just ended the call, but for some particular reason I was careless and my gaurd was low as I was in the midst of work. Plus she knew a lot of details about me. 

She then asked me to open a link she had shared via message and asked me tick on two boxes to confirm the delivery address. Thats when I was jolted and immediately disconnected the call thinking it was a scam. 

I blocked her number and deleted those messages. She called me from another number and even messaged me on WhatsApp from a third number. 

I'm certain this was a scam..Has anyone experienced this ? Also how did she have my addresses and email ID? Is our data sold?
