--- POST TEXT ---

I remember when I was a toddler, maybe 3 or 4, my Grandma's house phone would ring at least once a day. Sometimes she would answer it, and sometimes she would let me answer it. Almost all the time, it was just silent on the other end, or it was some Indian dude rambling on and on about some product no one wanted. 

Telemarketers.

I never thought anything of it though.

Once I got a little bit older, around 8 or 9, I got my first phone. I NEVER got any random number texts or anything, I would just play games after school all the time. I can remember my Mom getting SOOO much spammy/scammy emails, but she never made a fuss and just deleted them.

Now, after discovering this Reddit forum, I see all the little scams and tricks. They're everywhere. I see plenty of fake emails from 'Apple' (air quotes) to my Mom saying things like 'Your account has been deactivated!' and shit. I get telemarketer calls all the time, and almost all of my friends get those stupid hot chick texts.

It just makes me question humanity sometimes. Why do people make our lives harder by making scams?

The world is confusing.
