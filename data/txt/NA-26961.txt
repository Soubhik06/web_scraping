--- POST TEXT ---

I live in a Scandinavian country, and answered a call this morning from a Belgian number I didn’t recognise. Yesterday morning another Belgian number called, with the same first 4 digits in the number. I answered this time since this was 2 mornings in a row, although my gut instinct said I should just have ignored it.

When I answered, a woman with a strong Indian accent, seemingly reading from a script, said their “cyber security department” were being contacted through my Microsoft Office account. When I pressed what company she was representing,if her department was part of Microsoft, she ignored the question and asked if I have my computer in front of me. She kept interrupting, saying “do you understand? Do you know what hacking is?”

After less than 2 minutes I just said “I don’t trust this call” and hung up. 

Anyone else have this experience? I’m a little surprised over how they found my number, although in my country your address and phone number is open public information.

Seemed like the worst scam attempt ever lol - I should just have not answered

Edit: after this happened I told my brother about it, and he [recommended this podcast](https://gimletmedia.com/amp/shows/reply-all/6nh3wk) from Gimlet Media about this. A guy who gets. a similar tech support call, calls the scammer back, builds a friendly relationship with the caller, investigates the big call center the caller works at and confronts their boss, it’s so funny and interesting
