1. Heard learned Counsel for the petitioners and learned

 Additional Public Prosecutor for the State. 2. This application, for grant of anticipatory bail, arises out

 of Giriyak Police Station Case No. 379 of 2024, dated

 02.10.2024, disclosing offences punishable under

 Sections 342 (1)/316(2)/318(4)/319(2)/336(3)/338/339/

 340(2)/341(1)/3(5)/61(2)/111(2)(b) of the Bhartiya Nyaya

 Sanhita, 2023, Sections 66 / 66C / 66D of the Information

 Technology (Amendment) Act, 2008 and Sections 25 (1-

 B)a/26/35 of the Arms Act . 3. The prosecution case, as per the First Information Report,
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.8814 of 2025(3) dt.14-05-2025 2/3 is that the informant, on receiving information from the

 Station House Officer that the accused persons in the

 form of organized gang are indulged in cyber fraud and

 are also in possession of fire arms, raided the joint house

 of accused persons, namely, Shailendra Ravidas,

 Ramanand Ravidas, Shivnandan Ravidas and Sakendra

 Ravidas and recovered a large number of debit cards,

 passbooks, cheque books, deposit slips and stamps of

 different banks, mobile phones, arms and ammunition and

 arrested the said four accused persons. The police also

 recovered many other incriminating materials, including

 diary and copy containing mobile numbers of customers.

 The arrested accused persons disclosed that they, with the

 help of the petitioners, committed cyber fraud in the name

 of providing loan. 4. Learned Counsel for the petitioners submits that the

 petitioners are innocent and has falsely been implicated in

 this case on the basis of confessional statements of

 arrested co-accused persons. He further submits that the

 petitioners have got no criminal antecedent and no

 incriminating material has been recovered from the

 premises belonging to them. Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.8814 of 2025(3) dt.14-05-2025 3/3 5. I have heard learned counsel for the parties and perused

 the materials on record. 6. The Police apprehended four accused persons with

 incriminating articles, which appears to be used in cyber

 crime. The apprehended accused persons have disclosed

 that they, along with the petitioners, operate an organized

 gang for committing cyber crime. The Police recovered

 debit cards, pass books, deposit slips, stamps and other

 materials from the arrested accused persons. It appears

 that all accused persons have formed a gang to defraud

 the innocent public and the phishing/cyber fraud is

 rampant in the area, where the attackers try to trick

 individuals into providing user names, password and

 other details and are indulged in e-mail phishing, voice

 phishing, SMS phishing etc. and the case is still under

 investigation, for which custodial interrogation of the

 petitioners may be required. Hence, I am not inclined to

 grant the petitioners privilege of anticipatory bail. 7. This application is, accordingly, dismissed. (Anil Kumar Sinha, J.)
