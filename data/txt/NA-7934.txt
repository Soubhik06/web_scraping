 trend of use of social media to influence the people. Main
 accused, namely Yogendra @ Bakro, who is a social media
 influencer, posted a video, as can be seen from the
 impugned FIR and as it is alleged that he has made
 hundreds of such videos, which he allegedly used to extort
 money from the respectable persons of the society, high
 rank public servants, including police personnel. Therefore,
 the misuse of social media is required to be curbed and
 when such incidents have come to the notice of the
 authorities and when the FIR is filed, such offences are
 bound to be investigated seriously and thoroughly.
 Generally, when the claim of a person is found to be
 genuine, then, at the stage of investigation, itself, the IO
 exercises his discretion, on the basis of the material
 collected during the course of investigation and can always
 file a summary report, but, without even investigation
 taking place, when allegations are of serious nature and it
 prima facie constitutes an offence, neither the investigation
 can be stayed nor the FIR can be quashed. 5.6 In the present case, there are 13 antecedents at the
 discredit of the present petitioner and when the same are
 examined in the light of 243 calls between the petitioner and
 the main accused Yogendra @ Bakro, it prima facie appears
 that the petitioner made derogatory remarks against the
