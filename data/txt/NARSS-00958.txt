I went to Karol bagh today with my cousins to visit a restaurant for dinner that someone recommended us.
After that when we were heading back to the metro we stopped at a food shop very small, 2 shops are there both side by side and we thought meh lets eat momos and have some juice so we told them panner momos and 4 juice 2 annar and 2 mosmi ( I know weird combo) and when we tasted it understood that they mixed something in this drink its not pure after that when we asked how much they told us 600! 600 for 4 juice? And 1 plate panner momos this shouldn't cost more than 250 ruppe 300 max!! 
Not to mention all four of us starting having stomach pain just after we entered the metro. 
 
 
We didn't asked for pricing before so we had to pay then we noticed that on their menu board price is not mentioned and when we entered the shop they were being super nice but still. 
 
Now some of you might ask why didn't we asked for price because it never happened to us before in our entire life and never to our friends as well. 
 
 
We visit a lot of places in our hometowns as well as delhi and other cities and it never costed more than 40-50 ruppe on a street shop untill unless told to make big one of around 80 ruppes but today and because of them we will have to ask everytime. They broke our trust and we will never trust another shopkeeper 

TLDR:- Street food shop at Karol Bagh selling overpriced 3rd quality food and drinks. 
Please be aware and ask price for everything beforehand you just cant trust shopkeepers in delhi.This incident reminds me of New Delhi Station Restaurant scam
