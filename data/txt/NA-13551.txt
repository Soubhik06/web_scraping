Enforcement Directorate (ED) Director, Rahul Navin asked his zonal heads to enhance the use of the National Intelligence Grid (NATGRID) and the Financial Intelligence Network (FINnet) at a high-level meeting in Guwahati last week, as per a Times of India (TOI) report .
