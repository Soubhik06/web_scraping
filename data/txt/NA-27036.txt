I was at my parents' house, and they got a call from an unknown number. Of course, they answered it, which I tell them never to do. The guy on the other end had a thick Indian accent and my mom, who is hard of hearing, and a bit racist, couldn't understand him, so she passes the phone to me.

Anyway, the guy says he's from CVS and he needs to update my dad's "profile". He proceeds to read his name and DOB, except he has my mom's DOB. Then he asks for my dad's Medicare number.

We had literally just picked up my dad's prescription at CVS, so I thought it might be a legitimate call. But I told the guy I wasn't comfortable giving the number over the phone. I asked if I could update the profile online, and he said no. I asked if I could do it in the store, and he said no. Then he got angry with me, telling me they wouldn't be able to process the benefits and that he's not a scammer, otherwise he'd be asking for bank accounts and social security numbers. Regardless, I politely declined to give him the medicare number and eventually hung up.

Was this a scam? Could you do any damage with someone's Medicare number?
