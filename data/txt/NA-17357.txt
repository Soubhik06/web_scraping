So recently I got an SMS saying that there is no KYC done on my Vi sim Card and services would be stopped, and that I should contact the given number. At the time I just ignored the sms, thinking I'd see what'll happen when my number gets blocked. Two days later I got a phonecall from the number mentioned in the sms. He said that my KYC wasn't done and that my number would be blocked and asked if I wanted to retain my phone services. I obviously answered yes. At the time I had to give an exam so told him to call later. He again asked me if I wanted service or else it would be blocked when I said "Aise thodi na hota hai"
To which he replied, "Kaise nahi hota hai" 

Well at this point I thought something was fishy and told him to go ahead and block my number. Well my number seems to still be functioning. If anyone has been through this, or knows about this new type of scam, please enlighten me.
