 Deepak and Pummy are facing the trial for the offence under Section 427 / 34 IPC. Since the application of Section 34 IPC

 are three types of witnesses i.e. (a) Wholly reliable. (b) Wholly unreliable. (c) Neither wholly reliable nor wholly unreliable. When the witness is wholly reliable, the Court should
 not have any difficulty in as much as conviction or acquittal
 could be based on the testimony of such single witness.

 unreliable, there would be no difficulty in as much as neither

 It is only in the third category of witnesses that the
 Court has to circumspect and has to look for corroboration in
