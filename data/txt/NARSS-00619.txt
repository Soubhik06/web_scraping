Hi,
I was traveling from Bangalore to Delhi via flight. I landed at terminal 1 and booked an Ola from airport till nizamuddin station. It was showing 425 but when the ride ended, the driver charged 200 extra from me. He gave the reason that " These charges are not added in Ola" and it cuts from his pocket so I need to pay." He collected his fellow drivers when I refused. But since I haven't heard good things about Delhi from my friends in Bangalore, I gave in and paid the scamster. 

I wanted to know whether it's common or is it a scam? Isn't these charges already added to the fare?

Edit: Hi everyone, I appreciate your concerns and suggestions. I complained to Ola from their support section. Ola has refunded me the excess 200 rupees as a voucher and also they promised to take serious action against the driver. I had paid via UPI so I had a receipt of payment which came in handy. Thank you everyone once again!
