So i landed at Hyderabad airport this morning. Luckily, I exchanged some cash but none of the cab companies that are waiting outside the airport accept foreign credit cards. They ask me to pay via UPI or Gpay. The what? Now i know what those are. But i asked them what a non Indian tourist would do. They said, and I quote, " valla saavu vallu sasthar. Em cheyyale?" 

What the fuck is wrong with this country? Slowly killing off tourism is what is happening. My credit card don't work online. At least they used to work on Uber. Today two different credit cards were not accepted. Uber forced me to use cash. Forget about OLA, I cannot even book a taxi through that. 

Why does using a credit card feel like rocket science? I understand the need of 2FA. But, if they can identify that a credit card is foreign issued, they are as they block the transaction, they might as well let it through leaving fraud to those who use it. Send OTP to Indian issue cards and i couldn't care less. 

I don't want to carry wads of cash with me.
