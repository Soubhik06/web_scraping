Five months ago, someone on LinkedIn contacted me about a direct selling opportunity. Suspecting it might be a scam, I decided to back out. The person who introduced me emphasized that I’d get a session with a senior whose time was extremely valuable and that I was lucky to have the chance.

Fast forward to yesterday—this same "senior," whose time was supposedly precious, sent me a connection request on LinkedIn. Not only that, but he even called me to pitch a new business opportunity. Since I’m not entirely sure whether they are scammers, I’m not revealing their LinkedIn profiles here.

I also frequently receive WhatsApp messages about review/rating schemes, which seem quite common. Three of my friends have come across the same scam. It starts with a request to review three restaurants, offering ₹150 for the first two rounds. The real trap begins when they introduce an investment scheme, claiming that ₹10,000 will yield ₹12,000 in returns. Unfortunately, one of my friend's sisters fell for it and lost ₹10,000.

What scams have you guys encountered?
