 For the Petitioner : Mr. Arvind Kumar Choudhary, Advocate
 For the State : Ms. Ruby Pandey, A.P.P. ------ Order No.04 Dated : 07.02.2023
 Heard both the sides. The petitioner- Buchkun Kumar Das is in custody since 18.08.2022
 in connection with Cyber P.S. Case No. 54 of 2022 for the offence registered
 under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 120B of the I.P.C. and Sections 66B , 66C , 66D , 84C of the I.T. Act pending in the Court of learned Additional
 Sessions Judge-II cum Special Judge, Deoghar. It is submitted by the learned counsel on behalf of petitioner that
 there is no material against this petitioner except recovery of mobile and SIM
 from. There is no reference on whom cyber fraud was committed or who was
 cheated by this petitioner. It is further submitted that similarly situated co-
 accused have been granted bail. Learned A.P.P. opposed the prayer for bail. It is submitted that during
 investigation SIM that was recovered from this petitioner, in para 91 and 104
 of the case diary, suspicious e-wallet and series of calls were made by the SIM
 seized from this petitioner. Considering the facts and circumstances of the case, the prayer for
 bail of the petitioner, is hereby, rejected. Learned Court below is directed to expedite the trial. (Gautam Kumar Choudhary, J.)
