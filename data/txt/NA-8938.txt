1. Heard the parties. 2. This application, for grant of anticipatory bail,

 arises out of Complaint Case No. 1001 of 2023, registered under Sections 341 , 323 , 326 , 307 and 504 / 34 of the Indian Penal

 Code. 3. As per complaint case, petitioner nos. 1 and 2 are

 the Branch Manager and Marketing Executive, respectively, of
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.20909 of 2025(3) dt.14-08-2025 2/4 the Sri Ram Finance, Balua Chowk Branch, Motihari. In the

 year 2022, the petitioner no. 2 arrived at the shop of the

 complainant and persuaded him to grow business. The

 complainant agreed to take loan of Rs. 13 Lakhs from the Sri

 Ram Finance Company and petitioner no. 2 told that the loan

 has been sanctioned @ 7% interest annually. Further allegation

 is that the petitioner no. 2 demanded bribe of Rs. 39,000/- for

 sanctioning the loan amount. Thereafter, complainant paid Rs.

 35,000/- through Phone Pay and Rs. 4,000/- as cash. 4. Learned counsel for the petitioners submits that

 petitioners are innocent and they have falsely been implicated in

 this case. Petitioners are the Officers of the Sri Ram Finance

 Company Limited duly registered under the Companies Act ,

 having branch office at Balua Chowk, Motihari. The allegation

 against the petitioners are false and fabricated and have been

 alleged with malafide intention by the complainant to evade

 from his liability. The complainant is not paying the

 installment/EMI of loan regularly and when the petitioner no. 2

 visited the shop of the complainant and demanded the EMI of

 loan amount, the present complaint has been filed in order to

 harass the petitioners. Insofar as allegation regarding payment

 of Rs. 50,000/- to the petitioner no. 1 is concerned, learned
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.20909 of 2025(3) dt.14-08-2025 3/4 counsel submits that petitioner no. 1 neither demanded any

 money nor such transaction, as alleged by the complainant, ever

 took place between the petitioner no. 1 and the complainant.

 However, complainant was required to pay the processing fee

 and charges against the Stamp duty etc. which is part of the

 processing/legal fee which he paid through account transfer to

 the petitioner no. 2 to the tune of Rs. 35,000/- only. 5. Despite service of notice, no one appeared for the

 complainant/opposite party no. 2. 6. Learned APP for the State opposes the prayer for

 anticipatory bail of the petitioners. 7. Having regard to the submissions made on behalf

 of the parties and taking into consideration the nature of

 allegation and the fact that the petitioners are the officers of the

 Sri Ram Finance Limited Company, I am inclined to grant

 privilege of anticipatory bail to the petitioners. 8. This application is, accordingly, allowed. 9. Let the petitioners, above named, in the event of

 their arrest or surrender before the Court below within four

 weeks, be released on bail on furnishing bail bond of Rs.

 10,000/- (Ten Thousand) each with two sureties of the like

 amount each to the satisfaction of learned Judicial Magistrate,
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.20909 of 2025(3) dt.14-08-2025 4/4 1st Class, East Champaran at Motihari, in connection with

 Complaint Case No. 1001 of 2023, subject to the condition laid

 down under Section 438 (2) of the Code of Criminal Procedure. (Anil Kumar Sinha, J)
