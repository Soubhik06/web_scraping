--- POST TEXT ---

I listed my 2bhk flat for rent on 99 acres this morning, and received a message that some Mr Deepak Bajrang Pawar was interested in renting it. 

Right when I received the message, I also got a call from Mr Deepak stating that he’s an army man from pune and wants to rent my flat. He asked me to share pictures with him through WhatsApp, so that he could show them to his family and finalize. I asked him to visit once but he said they really like the flat and want to pay the token amount. This made me suspicious. After sending the pictures, he told me they love the flat and it’s ‘beautiful’. I asked him to share his aadhar and pan for a police verification thinking that he will refuse. To my surprise, he sent all those documents right away. 

At this point I was thoroughly confused as the documents seemed genuine. Upon googling his name I found out it’s a big scam! The scammer claims to be an army person and expresses an urgent need to shift into a flat due to a transfer. Then instead of sending a deposit, scams the owners through pay request links or QR codes. 

The sad part is that even after so many complaints and press coverage since 2022, this scam is still up and running.

What can be done to stop this? I am still in conversation with him as I haven’t expressed any doubts. I know police won’t help because there have been multiple reports and the same IDs are still valid and circulating. 

TL:DR serial tenant scammer posing as army man using real IDs and pics to scam house owners since 2022. Help me put an end to it.
