I have been getting a few of those "lower your interest rate" scammer calls on a regular basis. Doing a lot of busy work today, so I thought I would actually answer them. I've done this in the past using a US-based fake CC generator. But I found one that I though would inflict a little more pain, because they'd immediately realize that they in fact weren't talking to their next victim. Using [https://www.coolgenerator.com/credit-card-generator-india](https://www.coolgenerator.com/credit-card-generator-india) I generate fake Visa numbers to give them associated with specific Indian banks. 

Wow! This really pisses them off. After they get the number, there is a few moments of confusion on their part when they check the issuer. When it comes back as an Indian bank, the tone of the call changes. Several of them start swearing at me, and they all get incredibly pissed. Every time one of them starts going "fuck you American asshole" and hangs up I feel like I've had just a tiny little victory. And I am wasting their time - time they aren't able to spend ripping someone off.
