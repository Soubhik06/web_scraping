--- POST TEXT ---

Two days ago, I was coming back from office on my scooter and had to fill her up. I was going through TC Palya main road and saw an Indian Oil pump and decided to stop there. It was near that Nippu Kodi place. While the guy was filling my scooter's tank I noticed the air machine and I thought to check the air in my tyres too. The guy filling up the air first did my front tire. Then he went to the back tire and there was no response in the machine. It was not showing anything and I couldn't hear the filling sound as well. Then he said, "puncture sir". I was a little sceptical because even if the tire is punctured the air should go into the tire, right? The guy then pointed to the right side and said there's a tire shop there. Fortunately, I didn't go there and I went forward towards home because even if it was punctured, I just rode 9 kms and there wasn't any apparent issue. Then I saw a tire shop and I thought to check there as well just to make sure. The guy checked the tire and there was no issue. Then the store owner asked me who told me that there is an issue. I said the pump guy and then he said, "they do this often sir. They have their own tire shop and say this to make people go there. If you go there they'll make their own hole and fix it and take the money". I felt happy that I trusted my instinct in this case. It's a small time scam but still beware of such practices guys!
