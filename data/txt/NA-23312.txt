In my Tier-3 Town in state of Rajasthan a Post Official Official allegedly took deposits of people and gave them fake sealed depository receipts but never deposited the amount in their account.

Most of the individuals take it as a secure and safe bank and hence don't use Internet banking or UPI with it generally.

The fraud recently came to light , the estimated amount is said to be around 2Cr+ (Grapevine amount). Majority of people have Post Office Account and use it as a safe and secure emergency/fixed goal fund.

No media-house is covering it despite it being known by whole town , the amount may be less but the process lapse is real.

It shows how flawed the Internal Controls are of Government Agencies , and such lower level executive is able to pull off this.

I really want to name my town but my family advised me against it , hence refraining.
