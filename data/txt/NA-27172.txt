So i'm starting to lead more and more to the "Scam" camp on this one, but i wanted to run it by the hivemind first, and see if anyone has had experience with something like this.

So my Fiance has been looking for a job as an administrative assistant. She put her resume on a few sites, and has had a few interviews, but this latest one seems off.

Its for a company called Biofil Chemicals. They're based out of India (red flag #1) and seem to be a legitimate company. She was contacted first by a local person calling themselves Jeff. Who told her she'd been selected for an online interview, via Yahoo Messenger. So she downloaded messenger, and had her "Interview" and she was "Hired" on the spot (red flag #2). She's supposedly going to be Working from Home, managing Schedules, and doing some kind of accounting work? They're sending her the needed software, and sending her a check to buy a printer/scanner/fax (red flag #3). She was supposed to be contacted at 8:15 this morning, but finally heard from someone at 945. They have sent the check.

So what do you guys think? Whats the scam here? the Check? The "Accounting Software"? The whole thing? None of it? Help me out.

EDIT: Yea, this shit's completely a scam. We received the "check" for 4 grand today:

Postmarked from New York

With a return adress based in Chicago (that doesn't exist)

From an AC repair shop in New Jersey

From a bank based in the EU

Which was supposed to be from a company based in india

...
