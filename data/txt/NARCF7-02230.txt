To give a little background, I woke up with a shock today - my grandpa had fallen down from his bed he is a patient of Parkinson’s and i rushed downstairs. He is fine btw; Work was hectic and then later in the morning i got an IVR call, it was auto generated and sounded very genuine. I usually do not even pick unknown numbers but I was expecting a parcel so i picked up the call.

They say “There is a parcel booked by your name in Delhi airport customs department booked under dhl to Reza Malik in Taiwan, it contains certain illegal items and hence has been blocked to be sent out, press 1 to talk to a DHL executive.”

I pressed 1 - honestly my brain was in a haze since morning 

The scamster pretended to be a DHL woman employee and gave me her emoloyee ID and spoke in vv good english. She said apparently the parcel contained mdma and other illegal drugs, Said if I am denying the parcel as mine I have to report it to karol bagh police station asap. They didn’t ask for me money and thats what made it seems so genuine, while on call I googled ‘scam courier’ and a recent article came up with this new scamming tactic and I immediately hung up. Beware and be alert guys! Inform elders and your vulnerable friends lol!
