My friend had put up a macbook for sale on olx. Instantly got a call from a guy saying he's interested and wanted to buy it immediately. I was mediating because he could only speak hindi. Insisted on sending money to me even before receiving product which was fishy already. 

 He wanted to send money through an "Army merchant account" controlled by "Colonel" saab. The "Colonel" called, a very stern sounding guy and was short on time and wanted to make the process real quick. Told me to open Phonepe while on phone and send a message saying "You have received X amount from merchant.card " . Made it look like a phonepe's own confirmation message. And wanted me to type the amount back on the send space to send the money from said Army merchant account to mine. 

Cut the call and blocked him. It is an elegant scam they are making us send them money without having to give them PIN or OTP that would raise suspicion in common people. 

What do we do people using Indian Army to build trust and scam people?
