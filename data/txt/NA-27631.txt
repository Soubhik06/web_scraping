--- POST TEXT ---

Hey everyone,

A few days ago, someone followed me on **social media.** His face looked familiar, so I followed him back. We started texting normally, and I was fascinated by his posts—they were all about business, pictures of him in five-star hotels, expensive cars, and traveling abroad.

After a while, he asked me to share my number so we could talk, and I did. At first, our conversations were normal. Occasionally, he would call and talk about an opportunity that had changed his life. After communicating normally for a few days, I finally asked him about the business and i asked him if there were any job opportunities related to this field, as I was looking for one.

A few days later, he called me and told me they were expanding, and he had recommended my name. That same day, I received a call for a telephonic interview, and I was selected for the next round—a two-hour interview scheduled for the following day.

In the first half of the interview, they explained some basic things about their business and some basic business terms. In the second half, I spoke to another person who explained their business model. According to him, they have tie-ups with many industries, and my role would be to help them grow. If I managed to generate profits for them, I would receive a share as my income.

Then came the part that raised red flags. He told me that to get started, I would have to go through a process that required an investment of **₹2 lakh** for the license from the **Ministry of Corporate Affairs,** stating that I am authorized to work with those lines of industry under my name. The amount I paid would only be applicable if I got selected after document verification **(Aadhar, PAN, Passport, and a photo)**. I had only six days to pay the amount, after which I would receive training, followed by a personal interview.

Everything seemed **suspicious,** so I asked for the company's name. He responded that the company would be in my name and that it was a **"global business,"** not a job. When I researched the people involved, I found no professional profiles on LinkedIn or any other business-related platforms. They only shared their social media accounts.

After the interview, I updated my "friend" and told him that I didn't want to join this so-called business opportunity. He kept insisting that I explain why I had made that decision. He repeatedly told me that I didn’t understand the business and even offered to help me with the fee. He also suggested conducting another interview so I could **"understand the business better."**

Despite his persistence, I rejected the offer.

I researched this kind of scheme but couldn't find much information about scams like these or similar business models. Has anyone come across something like this? Is this a known scam, or could it be legit?

Would love to hear your thoughts!
