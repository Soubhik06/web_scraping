I recently saw some posts about the tactics used by some cab drivers to scam people and thought of sharing my experience to alert you guys - 

So last year, my wife and I were coming back from the Delhi airport at around 2 am, and she booked an uber with the payment method set as "UPI". Once the driver arrived, he asked whether we will be paying via UPI and I said yes. He started the ride but did not ask for the OTP (**Scam alert 1!**). After a minute, I reminded him of entering the OTP and he did that. Eventually, we arrived at our house and he started to ask for the payment. **BUT** - the ride was still not completed on my wife's phone and his phone as well. It was showing that the vehicle is still outside of our sector (**Scam alert 2!**). And the driver kept asking us to pay him on his personal QR code rather than via Uber app.

Although I sensed something phishy, I gave him the benefit of doubt and asked him to refresh the app on his phone, end the ride from his side, and let me complete the payment via app. However, he was still insisting me to pay on his personal QR code since the app on his phone wasn't refreshing. He also said that he we should pay him and Uber will not charge us anything later. Finally, I showed him my wife's phone and the fucker snatched the phone from my hands and cancelled the ride. **Here's when I realized that he had switched off his mobile data to prevent uber from refreshing and that's the reason the ride was showing up as on-going.** 

Since we realized that he his trying to scam us, we paid directly on the uber app, and asked him to go since the payment is already done. Here's the scary part now - the fucker out of the car and held my hand while I was walking away to my house. I was furious at this time and wanted to just push the fucker away, but I tried to keep my cool since it was a late night in winter and my wife was with me. By this time, I asked my wife to call the police and once the driver saw that we are actually speaking to the police, he sat down in his car, turned it around and was talking to someone on his headphone saying "yeh log police bula rahe hain, kya karun, niklun?". 

Now that he realized that police is coming and we caught his scam, he asked me to pay at least half of the ride. Since I was a bit scared and just wanted to make him leave, I paid him 100-200 in cash and asked him to just leave or else he is going to be in trouble. 

After 20-30 minutes, a PCR did came and checked with us about this issue, but we let it go since we did not have any details about the driver (since the fucker cancelled the ride and there wasn't any immediate support from Uber). Uber called me back after 2 days and enquired for all the details, refunded the entire payment, and assured that they will blacklist that driver.

Honestly, it was a slightly traumatic incident for us (especially because it was late night). Till now, I keep overthinking what would have happened if my wife or any female was alone with such a driver. After this incident, whenever I or my wife are availing a cab, we always check with the cab drivers if they are fine by us paying via app or else we will just book another one. Also, we have switched towards Blusmart since it is prepaid and there's no scope of such a hassle with the drivers. 
 
Stay alert, stay safe guys!
