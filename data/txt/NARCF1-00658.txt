A scam happened to me, where I got a call saying that your interview is scheduled with HCL tomorrow.
I went there 
"Add-2ND floor,Mago chamber17-A/9w.e.a Karol bagh metro gate no.1 near by Bank of Baroda".
There continuously students coming in going out.
This stopped me to even start a conversation with another student.
I got interview by them the interview barely lasted for 4 minutes and they asked for 2000 rupees ( Saying it's refundable within 45 days ). I was very confused but at last I paid them rs 2000. 
And when I got home back I search there udyam certificate and got to know they have changed their name previously it was RBM training and now it's Elegant Management.
After searching on internet about RBM Training it was clear that I got scam.
Hope you'll get saved if you ever encounter these type of scams.
