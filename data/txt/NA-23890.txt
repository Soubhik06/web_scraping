Today I boarded a cab through namma yatri. At the end of the journey, the driver showed me a QR scan code from the gallery which I paid. Driver came back after 10 min and started arguing that he hasn't received the payment. He asked me to scan again and send Rs 1 to validate. When I paid, I realized that it's another account no with the bank showing as .ibl whereas the one i paid ended with .ybl. everything else looked the same. I Went back to his gallery and scanned all the codes and finally got the one I paid and it was showing up in the balance. I guess he wasn't expecting to be called out on his scam .. stay safe
