I received a strange call today which made me a bit nervous.

The call was from a number in Kansas. I have 2 layers of spam identification on my phone, one is google's and the other is from Verizon. The number was not marked as potentially spam. (after googling it also later, it doesn't seem to be in a blacklist either).

I answered and a lady in an (Indian?) accent asked me if I am (firstname, lastname). She knew my legal name so I answered yes.

She then told me that she is from CPB (Customs &amp; Border Protection) and if I am expecting a package from Mexico. I told her no.

She then asked me if (street,number,zipcode,state) is my address. That address was one of my residences 10 years ago. I answered no.

Then the line immediately disconnected.

I didn't give her any information other than the yes/no answers above. I usually hang up immediately to those people but this one somehow knew my legal name and one of my old addresses so I was reluctant to hang up immediately.

I leave a small chance aside that it was a legit call (although I don't think US agencies like CPB call individuals directly), but I am afraid that if this was eventually part of some scam. Since I only gave her yes/no answers and nothing else, I think that this might be some part of some identity theft scheme where you need to answer questions like these to prove your identity. I am subscribed to services like experian and creditkarma which notify me if they detect a new credit inquiry, but at this point I'm not sure if there's anything else I can do. (maybe report this call to some organization or agency so they investigate?)

Any thoughts/suggestions welcome.
