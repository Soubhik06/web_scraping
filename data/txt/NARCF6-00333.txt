Is there a hidden agenda, or is it a clash of ideologies in this Sandeep Maheshwari and Vivek Bindra controversy?

Recently Sandeep Maheshwari uploaded a video named ”Big Scam Exposed”. In this video, a student told about a scam that happened to him, how he was sold a course in the name of making him a great businessman but its value was zero, he was asked to sell a course, he also said that he was not alone. There are approximately 50000 people who have purchased the course. When this video was uploaded, there was an uproar in the YouTube community, and as the video was published public started commenting asking who was talking about this video.

The controversy between Sandeep Maheshwari and Vivek Bindra began when a student claimed that a course they purchased from a YouTuber was not valuable and refused a refund. Dive deep into the world of controversies and arguments surrounding Vivek Bindra and his empire, Bada Business. This led to a legal notice from Vivek Bindra’s team.

Sandeep Maheshwari has also spoken about a large scam involving Vivek Bindra in a recent video. Initially, they didn’t name it openly but now it’s open. After this Sandeep Maheshwari made a post on his community.
