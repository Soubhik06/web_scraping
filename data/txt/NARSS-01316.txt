Recently while watching Instagram reels, I saw an ad for seeking help for their family medical issue.

I remember that video which was trending on social media about 4 or 5 months ago. Then I saved that video about that doubt. but then today I got the same reel twice but from different accounts, and total of 3 reels.

I checked both reels and description. And then I realised that it is a well executed scam.

they have changed the QR code on the video and added new QR code of the scammers.

also the description has changed.

And the crazy thing is these videos are getting millions of views and likes.

I am pretty sure many people has lost their money on this.

After checking the IFSC codes, these banks are based on Mumbai.
