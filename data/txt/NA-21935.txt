I've encountered a situation where on August 14th, I received a payment on Binance P2P Exchange from person named Deepak. Strangely, the same amount got hold on August 27, and I initially thought it was a KYC issue. When I inquired with the bank manager via call, he mentioned I needed to submit my Aadhar card. Due to being in another city, I ignored it.

Later, on September 27, my account was frozen without notice. I visited my home branch, submitted all KYC details, and they assured me that the account would be reinstated after a day.

Considering subsequent days were holidays, I had my family member contact the branch today, October 4. 

Digging into the transaction, it occurred on Binance P2P, a crypto exchange. The buyer who purchased USDT from me turned out to have used fraudulent money, likely scammed from someone else. The victim of the scam filed a complaint with the cyber cell, leading to the freezing of my account.

I reached out to the grievance officer, who advised me to contact the police. I did just that, and it's been more than two weeks without a peep from anyone. My life savings are in this account, and I'm at a loss for what to do next.

This scam appears to be quite common in India. a quick Google search on "Binance P2P Bank Account Freeze Scam" revealed similar scams. I've tried the solutions suggested online, but it seems like no one's really listening.

If anyone who has encountered a similar situation or has a solution or can help me out, please message me.
