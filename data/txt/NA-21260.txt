“Dear Customer, your A/c **XXXXX has been put on hold. For any queries, kindly contact your branch.XXXXX Bank of India”

So I called at the customer care and they said my account is debit freezed.

And after receiving that message from bank I received an e-mail regarding my grievance from Cryber Crime Portal that says -

“SUBJECT: Cyber Crime Complaint put on Hold

Dear Sir/Madam,

Status of your Complaint Acknowledgement No. XXXXXXXXXX,dated 28/05/2024 00:39:AM, reported on Cyber Crime portal is amount of ₹ 2500 has been Put on Hold.Kindly contact your respective Police Station.

Regards,

National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.”

Is this how this system works? They freeze the victim's account itself? Totally frustrated by this system and the way of their dealing things. I'm unable to make necessary payments and now my account is debit freezed.

I saw few videos on YouTube regarding this matter, they were saying that they had to visit the branch and talk with the branch manager on this matter, the branch manager would give a copy of the account hold notice that they received by the Cyber Crime department. And in that notice I'd get the officer's phone no. and email ID. And I have to go myself to that department by myself or e-mail and call them and say that it's not my fault.
And as we all know this is India and sometimes even Branch Managers weren't ready to co-operate.
