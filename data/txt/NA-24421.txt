I'm sharing my horrific experience with Colive in Bangalore, hoping to warn others and prevent similar incidents.

Background:
I found a room online through Colive's website and registered my interest. Shortly after, I received multiple calls from different numbers, all claiming to be from Colive. One of the representatives asked me to provide my Aadhar and PAN card details, citing "verification purposes." I hesitantly shared the documents, thinking it was a standard procedure.

Payment and Rent Discrepancies:
The representative demanded an initial payment of ₹5000 as "advance" and ₹2000 extra as rent. I paid the amount, thinking it was part of the rent. However, when I checked the Colive app, it showed a different rent amount (₹12,000). When I questioned the representative, he brushed it off, saying "those are just numbers."

Harassment and Intimidation:
A few days later, a different person called, claiming to be the manager. He demanded ₹9,000 as rent, which I refused to pay, citing the initial agreement. He hung up on me.
The next day, a group of rowdies showed up at my room, broke in, and threatened me, demanding ₹10,000. They threw my bed away and started throwing other stuffs of mine. I recorded the incident, but they tried to snatch my phone. I got grip of my phone then they stopped doing it.

Warning Signs and Red Flags
In hindsight, I noticed several warning signs:
Multiple calls from different numbers
Demand for sensitive personal documents
Discrepancies in rent amounts
Harassment and intimidation
