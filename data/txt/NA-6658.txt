grant of regular bail in case FIR No.510 dated 28.07.2018 (Annexure P-1)

under Sections 419 , 420 , 380 , 201 , 120- B IPC and Sections 66C & 66 D of

IT Act (added later on), registered at Police Station Surajkund, District

Faridabad. Heard. Allegations against the petitioners in this case are that they used

to commit ATM cards fraud. As per the record, they used to make calls to

the customers for upgrading their sim cards from 3G to 4G network, get the

account details and thereafter, prepare fake ATM cards with swapping

machines and then transfer the money from the account of the customers to
