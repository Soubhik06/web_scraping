Ordered a few items from Swiggy Instamart and a few of the items were missing despite me having been charged for it. Usually, my wife orders these items, and she usually does not go over the bill but today I went over the bill and found that a few items were missing but they had charged for it in the bill they sent (I paid for it as well).

This was not a one-off, isolated incident. In the past 2 years, I have been personally encountering more and more scams like these. Since many people don't usually go over the bill, they tend to overlook these tiny infractions. Imagine if they can scam even 50Rs off of you and with thousands of Instamart orders a day and even if 5% of the customers tend to overlook this scam goes unnoticed/unpunished, how much money are they making purely through scamming daily customers? 

NOTE: ALWAYS CHECK THE BILL EVERYWHERE. Be it Online stores or Offline. Cross-check every single item with the bill and only THEN pay. No BILL, NO PAYMENT. 

What Delhi and NCR were 10 years ago, Bangalore is right now. Incidentally, I found Delhi to be better in recent times when it came to random people not trying to scam me at every corner.
