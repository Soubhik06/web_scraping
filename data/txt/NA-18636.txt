Here is Fluffy's comedy video called [Why Indians can't rob a bank](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3YUxbe9V5Og) where he portrays a stereotype of Indians as being calm and tolerant people.

Positive stereotypes are a good thing - I have personally experienced this when I lived in US and I enjoyed it too 😀. Here are 2 more stereotypes about us - 1) smart and 2) hardworking. This image has helped many Indians in US, including myself, earn the trust of foreign bosses and **bring plenty of software business to India** (instead of going to East Europe or Asia).

&gt;Its an open secret in the advertising business that positive stereotype is good for business.

Today India is a rising superpower, however, centuries of foreign rule has hurt our pride (and rightfully so) and is affecting certain visible behaviors **without us even being aware of it**.

Since social **media** has become a primary source to understand a **nation's culture and mindset,** I quickly analyzed some of our Indian threads on Reddit and YouTube and saw that a significant proportion follow a "**us vs them**" theme. Entire communities/nations are **negatively stereotyped** without even analyzing if the posted message is true. Every time I did a random fact check, found it to be fake news. There is a pretty good dose of *name calling, and an undemocratic, hostile aggressive tone towards diverse viewpoints*. **We love it when foreigners praise us, but loathe them when criticized.**

&gt;Since folks doing this are triggered, they do not realize it comes across as "*we are powerless and helpless*". There could be "*unconscious* bias" in play as well. Don't get me wrong, points being discussed are very valid and there should be open conversation, however with mutual respect and verified facts. 
&gt; 
&gt;I analyzed **non-Indian threads** too, and wherever I found a similar pattern as above, those countries ***don’t*** *have a great global reputation or economy*. I would encourage you to do a quick analysis as well.

What does it say about our image of a tolerant **ancient culture** and "**mother of all democracies**"? I think this has the potential to adversely impact our **+ve image abroad** and India's future business outlook. As **patriotic** Indians, we all should be worried.

I am not writing to sow a seed of despair. Absolutely not. But we need to stare at reality in the face, and not bury our heads in the sand. **We still have time,** and as we help India become a superpower, we have our ancient culture's **inner strength and tolerance** to guide us through this.

**Will we change?**
