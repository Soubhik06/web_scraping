Prosecutor at Bar. 3. Having perused the material available on record, this Court

finds that as per the complainant, during the course of his

business/work, he came in contact with one Surya Prakash @

Suraj. Soon they became friends. Surya Prakash @ Suraj induced

the complainant to share his SBI and PNB bank account details

that Abhimanyu used these bank accounts to route proceeds of

Suraj and others formed a cyber fraud gang and by criminal

conspiracy, cheated and deceived the common people. In other

words, the investigation conducted in the matter so far indicate

that petitioner was part of an organized cyber fraud network which

is involved in misusing bank accounts of innocent individuals for

illegal financial/fraudulent transactions. 5. This Court finds that though the FIR does not disclose name
