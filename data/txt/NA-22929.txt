**Lessons I Learned (The Hard Way)** 
 
**1. Justice is for sale.** 
**If your opponent ( maybe a Sr.counsel too) has money and influence, they can delay, manipulate, and even hide your case files to keep you from even getting your case listed at MHC.**

**2. Listing a case isn’t about priority, it’s about payment. If you don’t bribe court clerks, your case can stay buried for years. ( Specifically at MHC )**

**3. Even senior counsels aren’t always on your side. Just because they charge hefty fees doesn’t mean they’ll actually fight for you. Sometimes, they just show up and go through the motions &amp; even be silent at the crucial moments too ( u can't ask them or meet them or have a phone conversation with them because they r senior counsels of MHC, which happened in my case. )**

**4. Compromise isn’t always justice. The legal system in India is designed to break people financially and emotionally so they settle out of exhaustion, not fairness.**
