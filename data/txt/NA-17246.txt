OTP puchte time hi laga ki OTP se kab se yeh log credit card dene lage aur maine call wahi kaat diya aur check kiya iske baare mai aur kaafi post dikhe ki yeh sab scam hai.

par kal phir se RBL ke credit card ke liye phone kiya kisi aur ne maine kaha mai OTP nahi dunga aur kitna scam karogi to woh ladki kehti hai Sir mai to pehli baar call kar rahi hu mujhe thodi kuch pata. 😅😅😅
