*1/1/23 (Pashim Vihar, District Park)*

It was around 19:45 of the evening and the park closes around 19:00 due to the winters and the parks are not usually safe in dark winter season. My friend and I parked the scooty in some corner just outside the park road, which was like a service lane. We were just sitting there and talking as two police officers came there out of nowhere and asked me for my license and RC. I was a bit confused and scared as there were no traffic police and I didn't even break any law as we were just sitting and talking at that moment. I asked them that I don't have the physical copies of the documents, but I have them on my **Mparivahan** app. There were two of them and only one of them was talking to me, the other one was just standing alongside his bike. The Let's name the X and Y. The X officer told me that there is nothing "valid or legal" about that app, and it was just a fraud thing and "Koyi bhi isse kuch bhi dikha sakta hai" I was more confused and thinking that i always showed my documents on that app and nobody ever questioned me for this. He keeps on arguing with me that this app is not valid, and he wants the documents in **Digilocker or physical form.** I had no idea what to do at that moment and I didn't have the Digilocker app as I never needed it in daily life. I started downloading that app and even at that time my luck was that bad that I couldn't get the OTP requested due to poor networks and soon the OTP attempts were reached max. limit and I can't attempt for more. Furthermore, I was more confused and wasn't in the situation to think what to exactly do, and it's been like 15 mins after all of this so the X officer asked me that I handle my keys to him, or he will carry it away either way, so I immediately took my keys and kept in my pockets and kept on saying that I didn't break any rule or anything. After a while he asked me that he is taking my scooty to police station and I can show them my documents to them in the station, and they will give me my scooty. **These were the exact words.** I was out of solutions, so I trusted him and gave him the keys as I thought there is no problem and I have every documents that he needs, and I don't have to worry about it. I went home with my friend and took all the documents he might be needed and went to the station. There he said that now your vehicle is in their custody and only ACP will release it in the morning. I was much more angry now that he lied to me and I did what he said to me, and now it was like really late at night.

There is a friend of mine, Who's father is in special service of India. I called him and his father talked to that police officer X, and he told him a different story to him as I was never ready to show him any documents and wasted their time. I was so angry at that moment and I just called him a liar that very moment which was a very bad move, but I was just thinking that probably my friend father would think that I might be guilty in this case, and I was lying to him Later, His father asked him to connect him to ACP but the X officer knew he was guilty, so he just went here and there and after a while directly went home. I couldn't do anything now, it was late, and I had to leave for home. My friend's father talked to the SHO of that area and talked about the case and then he told me that Mr. X officer had my scooty in their custody under the 66 DP act and did a legal work on it.

You can read what is the act and how illegally he had false allegation on me.

**Section 66 in The Delhi Police Act, 1978**

66. Police to take charge of unclaimed property.-[(1)](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/922029/) It shall be the duty of every police officer to take temporary charge-[(a)](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/385458/) of all unclaimed property found by, or made over to, him; and[(b)](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/1276136/) of all property found lying in any public street, if the owner or person in charge of such property, on being directed to remove the same, refuses or fails to do so.[(2)](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/1692854/) The police officer taking charge of the property under sub- section (1) shall furnish an inventory thereof to the Commissioner of Police.

&amp;#x200B;

Nothing happened as mentioned in these guidelines, and it was just an "EGO" thing that he Mr. X officer did and filed a case because now I have to deal the process and the high officers. He was free from his work, and technically it will take around 6–7 days to get my vehicle back.

But I had my friend, so it was easy for me that his father talked to SHO and fixed my situation that next day I had to give them an application and I will have my scooty.

***Next day***,

I went to the station and there was a special cell for cases like that where another officer was sitting, let's call him Z. So X and Z were friend's. I found out that when they were both back bitching on me that how their superiors were forcing them to release my vehicle. The Officer Z told me that I can't release it even any superior officer says it as it was his responsibility to leave my vehicle, at that very moment i was just thinking what i have done wrong? Trust me, I am someone who wears a helmet all the time even when it's not needed and all of this shitty things were happening to me. My friend's father had more calls and later i had the SHO called that officer Z so the convo happened in front me that SHO told him to release my scooty asap. I went to their cell and told him that now he has talked to him and now he should do the work and release but the funniest thing happens, **he says "SHO sir ne bola hai ki aap dekhlo jo karna hai release krne ko nahi bola"** i was like what the fuck???? At that moment they were just messing with me on the purpose like i actually heard him saying to release it, but Z and X are friend's, so I know it was all intentional. After 5 hours of hassle i had my scooty out and even i was polite and thanked him, and he didn't even reply to me.

While i was there i saw people coming for 5-6 days straight to ask for their vehicle and all there were getting "kal anna". I just think if i didn't have my friend i would be in the same position and waiting for days even i did nothing wrong.

That was the story of my new year celebration.
