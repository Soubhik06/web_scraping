FedEx , Mumbai Cybercell Scam

FedEx Scam: I got a call from a 9 digit phone number which I picked up by mistake. It was automated message stating that “ your FedEx parcel is confiscated “ , since I didn’t send any parcel I was scared of identity theft so I pressed 1 as per the automated message customer support executive.

Then a politely speaking representative ( pretending) to be from FedEx said that a parcel is sent from Mumbai to Taiwan with some illegal items. When I told him I don’t live in Mumbai neither have I sent anything he asked me register a complain to Mumbai Cyber cell which sounded believable.

Then he redirected the call to another person who asked me give statements on a WhatsApp phone call . After narrating him the incident he told me provide the ID to him . I was anyway skeptical of that number pretending to be of cyber cell since customer care number are never individual phone numbers but I still followed the conversation just to check what they upto. But eventually declined to share my id and told him that I will make a complaint about this to cyber cell in my city and they immediately disconnected the call.

Be very careful if these creeps and scammers and please do not share any personal info.I wanted to report these number to cyber cell website but the website is not working properly.
