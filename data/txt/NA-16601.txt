Okay so I get an sms from this company about resume filling and I took up the job because there were no registration fees or security deposit upfront, plus no scam reviews of the company on google. So I thought it's a legit site. The salary is kinda too good to be true, you fill minimum 35 resumes a day for a week ( total 250) and you get 15k if there are no errors in majority of them. They have an email id and customer care numbers but when I call on the customer care numbers, the person is always in a hurry to explain and cut the call ( but they do explain). I also can't find a physical address of this company and even though the customer care executive said it is in Surat, Gujarat, I can't find it online.

The company is Jobs Ocean [https://www.thejobsocean.com/cp/login.php](https://www.thejobsocean.com/cp/login.php)
