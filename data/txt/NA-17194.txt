--- POST TEXT ---

I had a Freecharge account once upon a time. I was unhappy with their service (lots of privacy issues, recharge SMS being sent to other users etc) and requested a deletion of account, which after months of no response was finally done. 

I had in the meantime been a Snapdeal user. This was when Snapdeal hadn't bought Freecharge. 

Now, today after seeing something I wanted to buy on Snapdeal, I downloaded their app on the phone. Surprisingly I noticed that the only way to sign in was using a mobile number. I already had a Snapdeal account and felt that this was a little weird. Anyway, I entered my phone number and instead of being logged in I was told to enter a password for a new account. Nowhere was it mentioned that I was being asked to sign up for a Freecharge account. So when I entered my previous password without being asked to enter my email address, I was told that a new account had been created under my name which was clearly different from the account I already had and that I had been signed up for Freecharge too, **without my consent**. 

This is a complete betrayal of trust and a massive data collection scam. I can't believe a company like Snapdeal can get away with shoddy practices like these. 

I always thought among the three, Snapdeal was the worst because of poor pricing and service quality etc. But now I'm convinced that Snapdeal isn't just the worst e-commerce service, it's a massive data collection engine with absolutely zero concern for a user's privacy. 

Rant over.
