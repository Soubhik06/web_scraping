I've gotten so many of these calls over the last 3 months I think one of the booking sites I've used in the past was hacked because they have my name and email address that I used with the a few online travel companies in the past. I don't have any travel credits. I know that for a fact. 

It's a scripted call and always a different company:

- A1 excursions
- Travel 1
- TransatTours

etc. 

It's always an American person in a call centre vs. the typical Indian person and they're always saying "do you see yourself travelling sometime in the future" 

Then try to book a timeshare type of vacation with a presentation needed at some point but have to give them a credit card for $500 to $800 in "processing" or "admin" fees.

I tried not to use actual words and use grunts and "hmm mmhh" so I didn't get my voice recorded but thought I'd share this. One of the oldest phishing scams but the fact they're using real people in real call centres to do this really sucks.
