Was trying to book a room for two days in Mantralayam, Andhra Pradesh, at the "Vijayeendra Vasati Gruha". Found this website in google search: [https://vijayeendravasatigruha.in/](https://vijayeendravasatigruha.in/)

It is a fraud site.

It has a number for WhatsApp and Call. Same number. Called and sent details to this number (including Aadhar). Asked me to send Rs. 4000/- for the room charges for two days and Rs.10/- for booking charges. Sent Rs. 4010/-. The fraudster called me back on WhatsApp and said I needed to send 2 transactions of 4000 and 10. He asked me to send again and then he will refund.

I knew then it was fraud. I asked him to refund first and he refused. He disconnected and stopped picking up calls.

The first smell of suspicion (which I should have caught on to). The name of the person was north Indian and was speaking Hindi while this is a South Indian temple. I should have gone with my gut but didn't.

An expensive lesson learnt. Learn from my mistake.
