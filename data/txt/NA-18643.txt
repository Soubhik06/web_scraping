--- POST TEXT ---

I am working and living in the US. I travelled to India last month and have installed some apps like airtel for recharge and Goibibo for booking fights. I denied some permission like notifications and location access because I don’t need them. Every time I open these apps, I get a prompt asking to enable them.

I also installed the Indian Bank’s iOS app called IndOASIS and every time I open the app it asks me for location access. Without granting the access, it does not let me in. The App explanation for location access is “To locate Branch, ATM and IFSC locator feature and to perform financial transactions.” I don’t want those features, yet I have to enable it to use the app. I also have another indian banking app which I can use without enabling location. Now, everytime I open it, I give location permission and after closing it, I disable it again.

Recently, installed a “shareit karo” app in My uncle’s phone for sharing pictures. When I opened the app, it asked me for permission to access location. When I denied it did not let me in saying the location feature is needed to detect nearby devices. I just wanted to use the wifi for sharing and do not want to use the location.

Registered with Paytm for UPI, now I am getting calls every week asking if I want credit card.

Also, every place I visit, restaurants, shopping, pharmacy, every one is asking for phone number. At one shopping mall, when I refused to give the phone number, the cashier insisted that I give him number to get rewards. From then, I started giving out fake number.

It seems to me like India has become a centre for mass data collection.

Your thoughts?
