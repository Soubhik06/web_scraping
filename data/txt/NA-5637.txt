This is the first application filed by the applicant under Section 482 of
 the Bhartiya Nagrik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS) for grant of anticipatory
 bail relating to FIR/Crime No.388/2024 registered at Police Station Saman,
 District Rewa (M.P.) for the offence punishable under Sections 420 , 468 , 471 , 409 and 120-B of the Indian Penal Code. 2. As per the case of prosecution, the present applicant being an Insurance
 Agent received amount from the complainant for getting his vehicle insured

 and assured him to give insurance policy. Although, the said amount was not
 deposited by the applicant with the insurance company, therefore, the vehicle
 of the complainant could not be insured and unfortunately it met with an
 accident and when demand was made by the complainant for giving policy
 so that the claim could be raised about the damages caused to the vehicle in
 the accident, it is informed that the amount deposited by the present applicant

 the complainant alleging that the present applicant being an Insurance Agent
 committed fraud and as such, loss caused to the complainant. 3. Learned counsel for the applicant submits that on an assurance given by
 the complainant for making remaining payment of premium of insurance, he
 issued a cheque to the company which got dishonoured and as such, the
 applicant has not committed any fraud, but it is the fault of the complainant. 4. On the other hand, the counsel for the State has opposed the prayer of
 bail and submitted that the stand taken by the counsel for the applicant
 cannot be considered to be true because the payment made by the
 complainant through PhonePe app and ID number is also given in the FIR so

 as to confirm that the payment was made to the applicant but he did not
 deposit the same with the insurance company and as such, the applicant has
 committed fraud with the complainant. 5. However, considering the aforesaid, prima facie I do not find any force
 in the submissions made by the counsel for the applicant for the reason that
 whatever grounds have raised, they can be ascertained only by adducing the
 evidence and when the payment made through PhonePe app, it cannot be
 presumed that the amount was not paid to the applicant. Being an agent, it
 was the duty of the applicant to deposit the amount received from the
 complainant and if it is not done, it is a fraud. As such, I do not find any
 ground for granting the benefit of anticipatory bail to the applicant.
