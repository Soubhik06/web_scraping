We recently moved to Hyderabad and had a disturbing experience that has left us both shocked and worried.

My wife booked an Uber and during the ride, the driver suddenly stopped the cab on the Outer Ring Road. He started crying and emotionally telling her that his father had just passed away and that the ambulance was charging him ₹22,000. He began begging for money, saying he would drop her but needed help urgently. My wife politely asked him to end the ride and told him she would book another so he could go see his father, but he kept insisting he would still drop her if she just gave him some money.

She called me in panic, explaining the situation. I told her if he is still willing to drop her, just let him do so and say nothing more. After reaching the destination, the driver started demanding money again. She gave him ₹500 and tried to leave the cab, but he aggressively asked how much she paid and kept yelling that it wasn’t enough. She quickly got out and ran off.

After checking online, she found that this is a known scam that some drivers pull off to emotionally manipulate passengers.

The biggest concern now is safety. The driver has our home location as he picked her up from there. We’re new here and this shook our sense of security. We had a great impression of Hyderabad till now, friendly locals, respectful auto and cab drivers and this experience was unexpected.

She reported the issue to Uber, but we’re unsure if it’s enough. 

Has anyone else experienced something like this?
