**India:**

* The real estate sector has high hopes for the new Modi government, particularly regarding infrastructure development \[Hindustan Times\].
* There seems to be a mismatch between new home launches and sales, with launches lagging behind but prices remaining stable \[The Hindu BusinessLine\].
* Indian REITs (Real Estate Investment Trusts) are showing rapid expansion as demand for office space surges \[The Hindu BusinessLine\].

**Company News**

* Godrej Properties acquired multiple land parcels throughout the year, planning projects with a total value of ₹21,000 crore \[The Hindu BusinessLine\].
* DLF's rental arm, DCCDL, reported a 7% rise in office rental income for the last financial year \[The Hindu BusinessLine\].
* Brookfield REIT saw a significant jump in income and acquired a stake in assets from the Bharti group \[The Hindu BusinessLine\].

**Other News**

* An FIR (First Information Report) alleging fraud was filed against the MDs of IREO Group and Oberoi Realty \[ET Realty\].
* The UP-RERA implemented a model format for possession letters to better protect property allottees \[ET Realty\].
