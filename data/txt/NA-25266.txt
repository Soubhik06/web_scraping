I was looking for an apartment (1bhk) in noida. I found one for 11000 on housing.com. I put in my details to get contact information of the owner ( who was the one who posted it) and share mine with them. 

I was immediately contacted on WhatsApp with the photos and video of the apartment. Then a minute later i got a whatsapp call from the same number. He told me that he was the owner speaking and the rent was 12000. I said it says 11000 on the website to which he replied that it is a fully furnished apartment so the rent is 12000 and you can get a discount after 1 year. 

When I inquired about a visit he said you can visit anytime tomorrow. Then he asked me to pay a token amount to hold the apartment and if I didn’t like the apartment when I visit I will get the money back. This is where I thought this could be a scam so I looked at the name of the caller and the name on the listing which turned out to be different.

I said I will pay after I visit and like the place he immediately cut the call. Was this a scam or is this the norm out here?
