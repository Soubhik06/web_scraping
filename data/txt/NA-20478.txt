I had placed an order for WD My Passport for Mac 5 TB External Hard Disk Drive on 11 June (purchased using Axis bank credit card) at a cost of Rs. 8504 /-

The package that was delivered to me came in a thin crumpled paper bag and the paper's bag had cello-tape wrappers on the top! The manufacturing date was July 2021 (a year old), it was missing the accessory cable and the unit provided was WD My Passport Ultra 2013 . Since it was clearly evident that I was given an old and tampered unit, I placed a return request immediately (within 2-5 mins of receiving the order). The Flipkart customer personnel encouraged me to open the box and see if the device was functioning as expected.

The item was picked up on 16 June by the wishmaster. Even though I had explained everything to the wishmaster during pickup and he had taken it, the personnel cancelled the return after 2 hours of the pickup  stating the item picked up from me was different from what is shown on their system. The return &amp; refund were since rescheduled and cancelled multiple times, the latest being today (1 day after return window has been closed).

Despite conveying my request and complaint over 50+ times in the last 7 days, all my requests were falsely cancelled on the allegation of being done based on my request (much to my shock).

Even though, I was assured multiple times by Flipkart customer service executives that my complaint will be resolved by 21 June but I never saw the slightest progress on it. The local pickup center personnel are even worse. They never contact, abruptly drop and do not attend calls. To return the picked up product, the wishmaster had barged-in to my home during my absence and since then have cancelled the pickup multiple times by themselves (despite the pickup request being raised by flipkart customer service representatives and/or senior executives)

During the aforementioned period of 7 days , I have (tried to) file a complaint against seller via Flipkart, communicated the event of receiving a defective and different item over the personal emails of the seller, complained on twitter , email etc and have even filed a grievance on National Consumer Helpline on Saturday of last week.

None seem to be working in my favour and I feel hopeless thinking that my money went down the drain (now that return window is closed). They have falsely cancelled my refund request stating that I requested it to be done and even the pickup in the same manner.

Can anyone please suggest what to do to get my concern addressed &amp; resolved ?

P.S - The Seller is ECOMDGMSTEL on Flipkart and MPS TELECOM RETAIL PVT. LTD. (on the invoice). They are based out of Delhi.

Will complaining to the credit card department of the respective bank (and requesting a chargeback or transaction dispute help me ?
