I know this recession thing has frustrated many IT people and freshers. But, doobte ko tinke ka saahara(grasping at straws) is not always an option in such a case. Today I got a call from PagePro(said to be an IT resource recruitment vendor or something, not the development agency). The HR says we have shortlisted your profile for Cloud Computing(first of all Cloud Computing is not a designation it is a technology). She asked me to send the candidate's(me) details by mail(info@pagepro.co.in) which seems legit. She said the job description, study materials, and assessment links would be shared after paying 1450. Before that, she asked me to give a passport-size photo, Resume, and ID proof. I sent her then regret. She sent me a form which was from their website which looked legitimate (not hosted from WIX etc). After receiving a mail that contained a QR code it made go "They had us in first half, NGL". The mail had a QR code which I had to pay RS. 1450 which is to an individual. The website has the address of Gujrat. They were talking not politely and professionally. I said to her that I couldn't send money on QR which isn't legitimate it is not going to any organisation it is going to a person. She disconnected the call, and boom busted! I was about to fall for this but my senses kicked in late. Although, I have been looking for job but also experienced such kind of scams where they ask for sensitive documents for just an interview. These kinds of documents should be shared while onboarding.

I can share the details of the scammer if allowed. 

TLDR: Don't pay a single penny to get a job/interview and share only masked ID proof before an interview, nothing beyond the resume.
