Hi everyone,

I’m an 18-year-old from Bangalore, and I’ve fallen victim to an online scam. I was selling a sofa on OLX for ₹5,600, and a buyer contacted me via WhatsApp. He claimed to have a "merchant account" and sent QR codes labeled as “Receive.”

At first, he sent a QR code for ₹2 and asked me to scan it, saying I’d receive the amount. When I did, it asked me to send money instead. He convinced me that this was how merchant accounts worked and that I’d get my money back. I trusted him and sent ₹2.

Later, he sent QR codes for the ₹5,600 payment, but Paytm said the limit for QR transactions from the gallery was ₹2,000. He then convinced me to send smaller amounts—₹2,000 twice, ₹1,600, and finally ₹4,995—claiming I’d get refunded immediately. In total, I’ve lost ₹10,595.

When I refused to send another ₹16,000 he demanded, he deleted all WhatsApp messages and stopped responding.

What I’ve Tried So Far:

1. Called 1930 (Cybercrime Helpline) – The line is always congested.

2. Visited my local police station – They told me to call 1930 and couldn’t help further.

3. Tried filing a complaint on the Cybercrime Portal – The website keeps lagging and won’t load properly.

4. Contacted my bank – They said they can block my account but can’t reverse or retrieve the money.

Evidence I Have:

Screenshots of the transactions.

WhatsApp chat details (though most messages are deleted).

The scammer’s phone number.

My Question:

What else can I do to recover my money or report this scam effectively? Is there a specific cybercrime office or agency in Bangalore I can visit directly?

Any advice or suggestions would be greatly appreciated. Thank you in advance
