Namaskara Bengaluru!

I received a call from an unknown number claiming to be from FedEx, stating they had my courier. Coincidentally, I had just returned a product from Ajio, and the delivery partner was Shadowfax, so I initially thought the call might be about that.

The IVR voice mentioned pressing 1 to get more details about the parcel. When I pressed 1, I was connected to a person who said they needed to verify my name and phone number. He repeated both for confirmation, which seemed unusual because normally, they ask for only the last 4 digits for verification.

Sensing something was off, I stayed silent. The person then claimed they had a courier for me, which was supposedly being sent to Iraq from Mumbai. 
Realizing this was suspicious, I hung up the call.

I recall reading a similar post about this type of scam a few days ago. It looks like a new scam is circulating.
