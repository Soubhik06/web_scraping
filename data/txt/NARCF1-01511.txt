Friends, just posting this so that no one falls for this. 

I wanted to book a room at Golkonda Resort. Went to TripAdvisor to check some pictures of the place. The first picture had text in large font with mobile number to book a room. I called and he sounded genuine. He said room is available and he sent me an invoice also on WhatsApp. I had to scan a QR he sent and pay by UPI. But just before making the payment, I went to Golkonda’s official website. I called that number there and asked if they take bookings with UPI payments. They said no and also told me that no rooms were available for that day. I told them about the scam and they said they’ll look into it. I got saved. 

TL;DR - Book rooms at Hotels only by calling official numbers or on known websites.
