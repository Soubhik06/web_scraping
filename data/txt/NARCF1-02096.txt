I saw an Ad on OLX for PS5 for just Rs 28500 and it surprised me and that too with a little suspicion. Because getting a PS5 at retail price in India is seemingly impossible due to the lack of stock and if you go online , the prices are listed mostly above 50K. But still I decided to give a call and the person who picked up said there are based in Kolkata and their shop’s name is Current Technology Retail. When I enquired about how they managed to get their hands on a lot of PS5 , he said something about the illegal goods being transported via containers and the govt seizing them , then auctioning at prices and that’s how they got their said stock. He said that I don’t need to pay the full amount , just the 50% after confirming the order and the product will be delivered through DTDC courier and then the rest of the payment. 

I’m genuinely in a dilemma even though my mind says this surely is some sort of scam. And that too PS5 at a rate lower than retail price. I’m from Kerala , hence coming over there and verifying that myself is not practical. So , People of Kolkata !! Please do help me !!
