I would assume most of these accusations are from Pakistanis who have incentives to deflect their own support of Kashmiri terrorists. I'll try posting this in the Pakistani subreddit later on but they don't seem very active.

What I really want to know is the true extent of India's covert ops in other countries, minus the pakistani propaganda. Good documentation on the topic, not sketchy pakistani websites.

What made me start thinking about this was a Chuck Hagel video I stumbled upon:

[India finances trouble in Pakistan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PNeKnMbAm8c). It looks like this speech happened way back in 2011.

Here Chuck Hagel makes the claim that India has used Afghanistan as a second front that India used to finance problems for their neighbor.

On the topic of Balochistan - Pakistan likes to make the claim that RAW supports insurgents. In 2010 Pakistani media ran a propaganda campaign using a hoax wikileaks cable. [The Guardian did a good job reporting this charade](http://www.theguardian.com/world/2010/dec/09/pakistani-newspaper-fake-leaks-india)

But then Council of Foreign relations has a section on RAW which talks about Balochistan [here](http://www.cfr.org/india/raw-indias-external-intelligence-agency/p17707):

&gt;However, experts point out that India has supported insurgents in Pakistan's Balochistan, as well as anti-Pakistan forces in Afghanistan. But some experts say India no longer does this.

I'm not particular sure how trustworthy Brent Pierce is, but the website has a [fairly detailed article on RAW involvement in Pakistan](http://brentpierce.com/2014/03/22/raw-indias-intelligence-service-involvement-pakistans-unrest/). Maybe it's all junk or Pakistani propaganda, I couldn't really tell where this website came from.

I found a couple of articles talking about how Indian covert ops are either [rusty](http://blogs.reuters.com/pakistan/2008/12/18/india-pakistan-and-covert-operations-all-in-the-family/) or [incapable](http://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/home/sunday-times/deep-focus/Why-Indian-intelligence-doesnt-work-too-well-in-Pakistan/articleshow/12577068.cms), and that an unnamed spy unit involved in covert ops was disbanded ([2013 article](http://intelnews.org/2013/09/24/01-1346/)) but not much on the actual state of Indian covert ops.

I find it somewhat strange that an emerging economic superpower has an incompetent secret service. Or is this actually in line with India's foreign policy ? Maybe RAW is primarily used to fix domestic issues. For all intents and purposes unlike the CIA I can hardly find any information about them. Whereas for the ISI or Mossad there are volumes.

I found an [article involving RAW's recent activities in Burma on The Hindu](http://www.thehindu.com/opinion/lead/indias-new-language-of-killing/article5963505.ece)

The author mentions IK Gujral a former prime minister of India ending RAW's offensive operations in Pakistan. But what happened before this decision ? And did operations begin after the Kargil War and 26/11 terrorist attacks ? I can't seem to find much information at all.
