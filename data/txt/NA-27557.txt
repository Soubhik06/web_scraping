I’m not sure where they were going with this scam. I received a phone call asking for somebody. I didn’t know when I said they have the wrong number. I asked what is this concerning and they said and they said my name and knew the city I lived in. 

They told me someone was trying to open a bank account at Bank of America in my name in Boston. I said well it’s obviously not me. Please mark it as fraud and the guy said he’s already done that then he went to say that they were trying to send a Zelle Transfer and was possibly trying to attach it to one of my other accounts. And that I should watch my accounts I said that I would look on my account and if I notice anything suspicious, I’ll have it reported he wanted me to take a phone number to call Zelle directly, I said I didn’t need it but he gave me a one, I said I didn’t need it but he gave me a 1 855 number anyways. 

I knew it was a scam. You could hear the call center of Indian voices in the background, but what were they trying to get out of me after he gave me the phone number we hung up with each other.
