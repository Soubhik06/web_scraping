Hey everyone,
I wanted to share a concerning experience I had with Rapido recently. I booked a Rapido auto for a ride, and when the auto arrived, it had a different number than what was shown in the app. The driver claimed he was the one assigned for my ride and asked me to get in. I gave him the OTP, and we started the journey.

After sometime, the driver kept turning back and looking at me and I was wondering why he was doing that. After driving for about 3-4 kilometers, we approached a traffic signal, and he suddenly turned off the auto, saying it had some mechanical issues and asked me to cancel the ride. It was a cash mode booking, he didn't ask me for any money, which I thought was considerate at that moment. Believing his story, I agreed and stepped out at the signal to find another auto.

Rapido didn't allow me to cancel the ride, and before I could ask the driver to cancel, he had already taken a U-turn and was on his way. Fortunately, I managed to get an Uber soon after. To my shock, two signals ahead, I saw the same auto driver with a family in his auto. He noticed me and quickly turned his face away, driving off as soon as the signal turned green.

In a way, it was a silver lining since I got a free ride for a few kilometers, which saved me some money. However, I can't help but think how many others might have fallen victim to this kind of scam. It wasn't an urgent ride for me, but imagine being in a hurry to catch a bus or train and getting duped like this.

If it had been a card payment ride, it would have been difficult for me to book another ride since I would have ended up paying money to book a new ride or would have had to reach out to support and waste time getting the money back.

This was my experience, and I hope it serves as a caution to others using these services.
