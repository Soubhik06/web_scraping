--- POST TEXT ---

There's a new scam pretending to be associated with "BlackRock Media," tricking people through WhatsApp. They initially offer money (50 rupees per task) for simple actions like YouTube subscriptions, with tasks available every 30 minutes and a payout after every three tasks. Once they gain your trust with these small payments, the scam shifts gears. They introduce an opportunity to earn more by joining a so-called VIP group, but here's the catch: joining this group requires a payment.

As you dive deeper, the scheme gets murkier. They promise refunds and additional earnings once you open a merchant account, but this is where victims get trapped. The scammers create a facade of legitimacy by showing payment proofs from others in the group. However, after joining, instead of earning more, you're pushed into a cycle of endless tasks and additional payments, with no real returns on your investments.
