So there is this Instagram page called- Thrifted Cameras by Kimmy (@thriftedshopbykimmy) that has a website. I got an ad from their side on my Instagram so I decided to check it out. Turns out they had a beautiful range of old school digital 
cameras at a very cheap price so I decided to buy one because my phone’s camera isn’t that good and I love photography. 

Unfortunately, they ended up being a scam and I received a microphone stand instead in my package. Now I do not know how to get my money back. I’m a young student who does not have much money and spent ₹1600 on this. The parcel was cash on delivery but the blue dart guy didn’t let me have an open box delivery. It was only after he had taken off did I realise it was not the item I had ordered. Please please let me know what to do in order to get my money back. I’m scared to tell my parents since they were warning me against ordering stuff online but I always have had a good experience with these sellers. This was my first above ₹1000 order and I don’t know what to do. 

I have filed an online complaint with consumer helpline, cyber crime as well as reported their account on shopify.
