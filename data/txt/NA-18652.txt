I just received a phone call from an unknown number and the conversation went somewhat like this -

Caller - "Hello sir, good morning. We are calling from the RBI and we need some information from you." 

Me- "oh, okay. How may I help you? "

C - "Sir, we need to validate your bank account. Can you please tell us in which bank you have your account. And what type of card you have? Debit or credit? 

M - "Uhmm no. Kiska chutiya bana raha hai bhai? RBI ka yahi kaam haina faaltu mei logo ko call karke details poochna scammer kahinke?

Unfortunately, the guy disconnected the call before I could say anything else. Or maybe I should have just given him wrong information just to waste his time.

Now, what I wonder is, I have access to the internet and I know all these are fake. But there are lakhs of people who may believe that guy and may just give out their bank details. 

Is there anything we can do to help besides just telling people not to share their valuable information to scums like these?
