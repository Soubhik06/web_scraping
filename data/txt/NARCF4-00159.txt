If you’re coming to Dehradun by bus or train, be very careful about the auto and cab drivers lurking around the station. These guys straight-up refuse to go by Ola/Uber/Rapido fares and will do anything to get you into their offline cabs.

A friend of mine from college had a bad experience with one of them. She took an offline cab at night, clearly told the driver where she needed to go, and he agreed. But just 1 km before reaching, he suddenly started demanding extra money, claiming she only asked to be dropped at a different landmark. It was late at night, and she was alone, so she had no choice but to pay him an extra ₹100 just to avoid being left in some random spot far from home.

This isn’t a one-time thing—it’s a pattern. These cab drivers literally surround you, trying to stop you from booking an online cab. They’ll say things like “Ola/Uber will charge you extra later” just to mislead you. But trust me, it’s all a scam.

So if you’re traveling to Dehradun, always book through Ola/Uber/Rapido. They are way more reliable. The offline guys, especially near transport hubs, are just waiting to overcharge and scam unsuspecting people. Be cautious!
