Heard counsel for the parties. The applicant has been arrested on 5.3.2015 in
connection with Crime No.22/2014 registered with Police
Station STF Bhopal (M.P.) - commonly known as VYAPAM
Examination Scam cases - for the offences punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 and 120-B of IPC and Section 3 r/w 4 of M.P. Recognized Examination Act, 1937. The role ascribed to the applicant by the prosecution is
that of a beneficiary. The applicant was the original candidate
of Forest Guard Recruitment Examination, 2013 conducted by
VYAPAM, but, the applicant through his brother and two
middlemen, namely, Brajesh Indoriya and Rajkumar Indoriya
took the assistance of impersonator, who appeared in place of
applicant in the said examination. It is, fairly, stated by the learned counsel for the State, on
instructions, that custodial interrogation of the applicant is no
more required. Further, the investigation against the applicant
is complete in all respects as of now and, therefore, the 2 M.Cr.C. No.10546/2015 presence of applicant is no more required. In that sense, the
link between the accused persons is already complete. Further,
the applicant is not involved in any other crime of similar type.
Besides, the applicant through counsel undertakes to appear
before the Investigating Officer as and when called for further
investigation including against him as also to punctually
appear before the trial Court to ensure early disposal of the
criminal trial. In the interest of justice, taking overall view of the
matter, we deem it appropriate to grant bail to the applicant on
following strict conditions:- The bail application stands disposed of accordingly.
