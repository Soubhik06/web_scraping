--- POST TEXT ---

Last night I came to know (via [this site](https://haveibeenpwned.com)) that my account details were leaked in the [yatra data breach](https://gbhackers.com/yatra-com-data-breach/). I don't even remember creating that account, so I reset the password and logged in hoping to delete the account.

Surprisingly, the website doesn't give an option to delete the account. I googled "delete yatra account" to no avail. They do not have a customer service for these cases. (they do have toll free numbers for holidays and ticket bookings). Their email support form asks for name, email ID and contact number (which I am not willing to give, just after reading about the reach). The [FAQ ](https://kbs.yatra.com/eCash/Introduction/876394351/I-want-to-deactivate-my-Yatra-account-How-can-I-do-it.htm)section says *The account can not be deactivated as it contains your booking's and transaction's history. However, you can choose to stop receiving promotional or offer email from your account*. 

So I waited till the morning and called one of those irrelevant toll free numbers to see if I can get some contact info to someone who can help me. They just repeated what the FAQ says. 
Can anybody suggest me what can be done in this case?
