The is a big scam going on in the Indian internet service provider market. Basically , the local ISP's are charging extremely low , around 5 dollars , for a very fast 50 mbps (both download + upload) speed without any data cap. The bigger companies still charge about 15-20 dollar for the same speeds.

The way they are doing this is by something like connection ratio (quite unsure about this term) but what it basically means is that they split that 50 mbps speed in 10 parts , like a 1:10 ratio . So basically 10 devices can simulateneously use the WiFi at 5 mbps capped speed. So the maximum speed any device can get is 5 mbps.

I believe this is a scam as you are falsely advertising internet speeds.
