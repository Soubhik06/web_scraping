Hon'ble Ajai Tyagi,J. Heard learned counsel for petitioner and learned AGA for the State-respondents. The instant petition has been filed for quashing of the first information report dated 14.10.2020 registered as Case Crime No.0263 of 2020, under Section 419 IPC and Sections 66 & 66-D of Information Technology (Amendment) Act, 2008, Police Station-Kurara, District-Hamirpur and for a further direction to the respondents to take on record the account-statements sought to be submitted by the petitioner to demonstrate that he was not involved in the alleged crime. As per allegation in the impugned FIR, the first-informant has been cheated of a sum of Rs.25,000/- while making some transaction on PhonePe application. During investigation, the Cyber Crime Cell reported involvement of the petitioner in the said fraud. Learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that he is in no way connected with the transaction in question; the report of Cyber Crime Cell is incorrect; he has tried to submit his statement of accounts and other relevant documents before the investigating agency, but the same has been declined. It is urged that the investigating agency be directed to take these documents into consideration during investigation. Having regard to the allegations made in the FIR, no case is made out to quash the same. At the same time, we direct the Superintendent of Police, Hamirpur, to accept the documents supplied by the petitioner and forward the same to the investigating agency for due consideration during course of investigation. The petition stands disposed of, accordingly.
