The Kasaragod Cyber Crime Police charged BJP National Secretary Anil Antony with promoting hate for sharing a video of Muslim students protesting for a bus stop near their college with a fake narrative that they were forcing a Hindu woman to wear a burqa. He stretched the narrative to say that Kerala led by these politicians (from CPM, Congress, and IUML) is becoming a hotbed of fundamentalism and radicalisation.

Based on Sidharthan's complaint, Kasaragod Cyber Crime Police added Anil Antony and Anandi Nair's name to Amy Mek's case on Tuesday, October 31, said an officer.
