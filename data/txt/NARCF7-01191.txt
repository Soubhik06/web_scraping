Beware of this fraud company- highly unprofessional and misleading

I had shortlisted a plot in Surya City Phase 2 near Bidadi and paid a token amount of ₹10,000. Bit I cancelled the booking the very next day after realizing that there had been no development in the area for over 3 years. I informed them of the cancellation through both email and chat and I have all the records as proof.

They initially told me that the token amount was refundable if I wasn't satisfied. But after visiting their office which is located on the outskirts of Bengaluru and returning the original payment receipt as they requested, they assured me that the refund would be processed within 60 days. It's been over 6 months now,and I havent received a single rupee.

Despite numerous follow ups, I received no response. Eventually they completely ghosted me and even blocked me from all their contact numbers and emails.

This conpany is highly unprofessional, dishonest, and lacks any work ethics. They are very proactive until you pay them but once you ask for a refund or raise a concern they completely cut off communication.

Please be cautious and don’t be fooled by online reviews. They aggressively push you to book plots and make token payments, but fail to keep their promises. Most of the properties they show are undeveloped and have no signs of progress.
