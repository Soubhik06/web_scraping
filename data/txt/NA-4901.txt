 W.P.(C) 528/2024 3. Present public interest litigation has been filed seeking directions to
 the Respondents to run an awareness campaign to make the citizens aware
 about new age cyber crimes as well as to simplify the procedure for filing of
 cyber crime complaints and to frame guidelines for swifter investigation and
 better coordination to stop the transfer of the proceeds of crimes in time. 4. Learned counsel for the Petitioners draws the attention of this Court to
 another form of cyber scam which is recently in news called 'digital arrest'
 whereby certain actors forge and fabricate fake orders and arrest warrants
 purportedly issued by the Courts of law in order to extort 'settlement
 money' from innocent citizens. The aforesaid scam also involves
 impersonation of government authorities and police officers, which in turn
 undermines the authority of the criminal justice system. 5. Learned counsel for the Petitioners states that the current mechanism
 of reporting of cyber complaints with the cyber crime unit of Delhi Police is
 insufficient to deal with the rapidly mutating and evolving cyber scams. She
 further states that the current procedure for reporting and investigating into
 such scams is neither effective nor sufficient in deterring such crimes or
