Today, I booked an Ola cab from Sector 104 to Sector 18. The cab displayed in the app was a white Desire, and the driver, whom I’ll call “Ola Driver,” greeted me and asked my OTP. Surprisingly, he started the car without entering the OTP on his mobile.

While we had only covered 20 meters of the distance, a white WagonR pulled up and instructed Ola Driver to stop. The WagonR driver claimed that I was actually his passenger, so we referred to him as “Driver#2.” To my astonishment, Driver#2 had shown the Ola app on his mobile, displaying my name and the pickup location. I checked my app and verified that there was no duplicate booking. I was seated in the correct cab, as indicated in the app.

Ola Driver informed me that he had received a call from Ola customer care before the ride, informing him of a duplicate booking made by the customer. I showed him my mobile and app, and only one booking was visible. However, the Driver#2 began playing the victim, claiming that he had driven 3 kilometers and would not receive any payment. He started demanding money from me.

Ola Driver also attempted to convince me to give him some money, stating that he had been a victim of a glitch or my mistake for booking two cabs. I firmly insisted that there was only one booking, which was visible in my app. Ola Driver then threatened to cancel the booking and asked me to leave the car and demanded payment for the cancellation charge.

Refusing to accept the cancellation charge, I suggested Driver#2 paying ₹20 or calling the police, which he agreed and I paid ₹20. I then requested the Ola cab to start the journey. Ola Driver entered the OTP and resumed the trip.

After ending my trip, I checked the app history and confirmed that there was only one booking. Considering the circumstances (I had two large bags and a baby), I was unable to take pictures or screenshots.

My suspicion is that both drivers are familiar with each other and have logged in from the same Ola account. When I booked the cab, it appeared on both of their mobiles, and they attempted to scam me or extort money from me. But why someone does this drama for 20/- rupees.
