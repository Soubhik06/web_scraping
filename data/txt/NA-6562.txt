 BEFORE
 HON'BLE MR. JUSTICE MANASH RANJAN PATHAK

18/02/2025

 Heard Mr. A A R Karim, learned counsel for the petitioners and Mr. B Sarma, learned Additional
Public Prosecutor, Assam for the State respondent. 2. This application under Section 483 of BNSS, 2023 has been filed by the petitioners, namely,
(1) Sri Anurag Jha, son of Mahendra @ Mahanand Jha, resident of Weyas Colony, P.S. Air Force,
District-Jodhpur, Rajasthan and (2) Sri Ratul Barman, son of Ratneswar Barman, resident of Ujan
Bazar, P.S. Latasil, Guwahati, seeking bail in Badarpur P.S. Case No. 305/2024 registered under
Sections 61(2)/318(2)/319(2)/318(4)/316(2)/ 303(2) of BNS corresponding to G.R. No. 2216/2024,
wherein they were arrested on 19.12.2024 and are in custody since then. 3. Both the petitioners were arrested on the basis of their involvement in the organized crime in
large-scale online financial frauds by deceiving gullible individuals on flimsy grounds by luring them
into sharing with their bank details and OTPs and transferring money from the bank accounts of those
individuals to the bank accounts of third parties. 4. Mr. Karim, learned counsel for the petitioners submitted that since 19.12.2024, the petitioners
are in custody for more than 60 days and as such, they are entitled for default bail in said Badarpur
P.S. Case No. 305/2024. 5. On being enquired, Mr. Sarma, learned Additional Public Prosecutor, Assam submitted that the
relevant case diary that was called for by order dated 11.02.2025 is available with him and the
charge-sheet has not yet been filed in the case. 6. In view of the above, the petitioners are entitled for default bail in said Badarpur P.S. Case
No. 305/2024. 7. Accordingly, the petitioners, namely, (1) Sri Anurag Jha and (2) Sri Ratul Barman are
granted default bail in said Badarpur P.S. Case No. 305/2024 registered under Sections
61(2)/318(2)/319(2)/318(4)/316(2)/ 303(2) of BNS corresponding to G.R. No. 2216/2024, on
furnishing a bail bond of Rs.1,00,000/- each, with 2 (two) local sureties each of the like amount, one
of which shall be a Government Employee, either Central or State Government, to the satisfaction of

the learned Chief Judicial Magistrate, Sribhumi (Karimganj) on conditions that the petitioners -- (i)
