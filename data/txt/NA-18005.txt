I've been using Remit2India for the longest time to send money to my parents in India. When I logged in today to send money, I noticed that it asked me for additional verification information, which I input, along with a scanned copy of my Drivers License (All pages until this step were secure)

As soon as I hit "Continue", it said that the field - "Years of stay in remitting country" was blank but I could swear that this field was not in the original form. This is when I noticed that this page wasn't a secure page any more and it was even asking me for my SSN which it didn't ask me in the original form

Now, I don't know if this is really a phishing or a scam page because I got redirected from within a secure page in Remit2India website but this whole experience has convined me to move my business away from them

If you use this service, just a word of caution to ensure that you are not giving away private and personally identifiable information without checking the validity of of the security certificates on the website
