I went to sbi bank ( louiswadi branch) with my friend for an account statement and was told to fill out a KYC form along with an insurance form, claiming it was mandatory. Suspicious, I questioned the staff and the manager, who insisted on the insurance form. When I refused and demanded an explanation, the manager finally gave me the statement without it. 

Beware of such scams—always question unnecessary forms and don’t hesitate to refuse if the reasoning doesn’t add up.
