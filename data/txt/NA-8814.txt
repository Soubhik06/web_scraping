(03.04.2023) 1. The issue under consideration i.e. the reliefs sought by the complainant in the
complaint dated 17.07.2021 due to alleged non-supply of information vide RTI
application dated 08.05.2021are as under:- 2. Succinctly facts of the case are that the complainant filed an application dated
08.05.2021 under the Right to Information Act, 2005 (RTI Act ) before the Central Public
Information Officer (CPIO), Punjab National Bank, Pune, seeking following information:- The CPIO vide letter dated 03.06.2021 replied to the complainant. Aggrieved by the
CPIO's reply, the complainant filed complaint dated 17.07.2021 before the Commission
which is under consideration. 3. The complainant has filed the instant complaint dated 17.07.2021inter alia on the
grounds that reply given by the CPIO was not satisfactory. The complainant requested the
Commission to take necessary action as per Section 20 (1) of the RTI Act. 4. The CPIO replied vide letter dated 03.06.2021 and the same is reproduced as under: (ix) Query is vague in nature and does not come under the preview of information u/s
 2(f) of the RTI Act 2005. The FAA did not pass any order. 5. The complainant remained absent and on behalf of the respondent Shri Pravin
Chandra Thakur, Chief Manager, Punjab National Bank, Nashik, attended the hearing
through video conference. 5.1. The respondent while defending their case inter alia submitted that they had
provided point-wise reply to the complainant vide letter dated 03.06.2021. They further
submitted that information sought on point nos. (i) and (vii) of the RTI application
pertained to third party information, held by the branch/bank under fiduciary capacity and
disclosure of which had no relationship to any public activity or interest. Therefore, they
claimed exemption under section 8(1)(j) & 8(1) (e) of the RTI Act. 6. The Commission after adverting to the facts and circumstances of the case, hearing
the respondent and perusal of records, observed that point-reply was given by the
respondent vide letter dated 03.06.2021. It is not the case that the respondent had not given
any reply nor the reply given was delayed. The respondent while exercising their wisdom
provided some information and denied certain information and that being so no mala fide
could be attributed to the decision taken by them. Moreover, the complainant neither filed
any written objection nor presented himself before the Commission to controvert the
averments made by the respondent and further agitate the matter. Hence, the submissions
of the respondent were taken on record. In view of the above, there appears to be no merit
in the complaint. Accordingly, the complaint is rejected. Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties. Sd/- (Suresh Chandra) (सुसुरेश चं ा)
