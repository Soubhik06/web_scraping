Title: **My Scary Encounter with a Scamming Couple Pretending to Offer a Threesome**

Hey Pune Reddit community,

I wanted to share my recent experience, which I hope can serve as a warning to others about a rather unusual scam. A few days ago, I was approached by a couple who claimed to be interested in having a threesome. It all started on a well-known dating app, where I had my preferences set to explore new connections and experiences.

They seemed friendly, and after some initial conversations, they proposed the idea of a threesome. As someone who's open to new experiences, I was intrigued. We exchanged photos, had further conversations, and even agreed to meet at a local coffee shop to discuss boundaries and expectations.

Here's where things took a strange turn. When I arrived at the meeting spot, only one of them showed up, and they immediately started pressuring me into sending money as a "security deposit" or "gift" before we could proceed with the threesome. Alarm bells started ringing in my head, and I declined their request, stating that I wasn't comfortable with such a demand.

The single individual who had shown up started to become aggressive and insisted that I pay or they would publicly expose our conversations. Feeling cornered, I decided to leave the situation, and I quickly blocked them on the dating app.

After some research, I discovered that this is a common scam where individuals or couples pretend to be interested in unconventional experiences, only to exploit others for financial gain. It's essential to be cautious when interacting with strangers online and never send money to anyone you haven't met in person and trust.

I hope my experience can help others avoid falling victim to similar scams. Stay safe!
