One97 Communications Ltd. Vs. Ganesh Devaguru 2. Brief facts of the case as per plaint are that the plaintiff, a well-
established company engaged in providing telecom-based value-added
services, is also one of India's prominent online platforms for utility
bill payments, recharges, ticketing (including movie and travel
bookings), hotel reservations, and other financial services. The
company operates through its website, mobile application, IVR, and
WAP sites under the Paytm brand. The defendant, the owner of the
"Satyakrishna Theatre," approached the plaintiff for promotional,
marketing, listing, and booking of movie tickets through the Paytm
platform. Consequently, a Ticketing Agreement was executed on
10.4.2019, under which the defendant agreed to upload his movie
ticket inventory on the Paytm platform and provide related services.
The agreement also included provisions for ticket pricing and the
defendant's obligations to maintain up-to-date information about
movie tickets and services. The agreement had a term of three years,
ending on 09.04.2022, with an addendum agreement dated 10.03.2022
extending and reinforcing the contractual terms. The agreement
between the plaintiff and defendant included a significant financial
arrangement in which the plaintiff paid an interest-free refundable
security deposit of Rs. 11,00,000/- to the defendant at the time of
execution. This amount was later adjusted in the form of an advance
against the base ticket price, with the defendant agreeing that Rs. 50/-
per ticket would be deducted from the advance until the total deposit
was exhausted. 3. Additionally, the plaintiff had the right to charge a convenience
fee from customers booking tickets through the Paytm platform, with
