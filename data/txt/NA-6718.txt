Md. Anwar Ali, aged 39 years, son of Haider Ali, Resident of
 Darbhanga Medical Campus (DMC), Weta, Near M.K. College, P.O.-
 Darbhanga Medical College (DMC), P.S.-Laharia Sarai, District-
 Darbhanga, Bihar. 2. This Criminal Miscellaneous Petition has been filed invoking the

 jurisdiction of this Court under Section 482 of the Code of Criminal

 Procedure with a prayer to quash and set aside the entire criminal

 proceeding arising out of Jamshedpur Cyber P.S. Case No.30 of 2022

 registered for the offences punishable under Sections 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 120-B / 34 of the Indian Penal Code and Sections 66C & 66D of the

 Informant Technology Act including the charge sheet and cognizance

 order dated 28.06.2022 in connection with the said case against the

 petitioner. 3. The brief facts of the case is that huge amount of money of several

 lakhs was withdrawn by committing fraud by the petitioner and the co-

 accused persons by cloning the cheque of the informant, while the 1 Cr. M.P. No.3021 of 2022 ( 2025:JHHC:26820 )

original cheque is with the informant and the specific role played by the

petitioner in the said offence is of activating the sim card, having the same

sim number as that of the husband of the informant, which was registered

with the bank and the same was done after the death of the husband of

the informant; on the basis of forged Aadhar Card of the husband of the

informant. During the investigation of the case, the complicity of the

petitioner was found and police submitted charge sheet inter alia against

the petitioner for having committed the offence punishable under Sections

420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 120-B / 34 of the Indian Penal Code and the learned

Special Judge, Cyber Crime, Jamshedpur upon submission of the charge

sheet took cognizance of the said offences vide order dated 28.06.2022. 4. Learned senior counsel for the petitioner submits that the petitioner

is innocent and has not committed any offence. It is next submitted that

the petitioner has activated the sim card of the husband of the informant

on the documents relating to the same being produced by someone,

hence, no illegality has been committed by him nor there is any laches on

the part of the petitioner. It is next submitted that the defrauded amount

of the informant has been deposited back in the account of the husband of

the informant by the Punjab National Bank. It is lastly submitted that the

prayer, as prayed for in the instant Cr.M.P, be allowed. 5. Learned counsel appearing for the State on the other hand

vehemently opposes the prayer of the petitioner made in the instant

Cr.M.P and submits that there is direct and specific allegation against the

petitioner that the petitioner in criminal conspiracy with the co-accused

person has forged the Aadhar Card of the husband of the informant and 2 Cr. M.P. No.3021 of 2022 ( 2025:JHHC:26820 )

activated the sim bearing same sim number, as that of the husband of the

informant, which was registered with the joint account of the informant

with her husband with Punjab National Bank and the petitioner was also

instrumental in issuance of the forged duplicate cheque for committing

the crime. It is lastly submitted that this Cr.M.P., being without any merit,

be dismissed. 6. Having heard the rival submissions made at the Bar and after

carefully going through the materials available in the record, it is

pertinent to mention here that there is direct allegation against the

petitioner that, with the complicity of the petitioner, forging of the Aadhar

Card of the deceased husband of the informant and by using such forged

Aadhar Card as a genuine one, activating of the sim card bearing the

same number, which was registered with the bank account of the husband

of the informant, was done fraudulently. Further, cybercrime of swindling

away the huge amount of money from the account of the husband of the

informant has been done by cloning the cheque, with the active

involvement of the petitioner. So, this Court is of the considered view,

that the allegations against the petitioner is sufficient to constitute the

offences in respect of which cognizance has been taken by the learned

Special Judge (Cyber Crime), Jamshedpur against the petitioner also, for

having committed the said offences in criminal conspiracy with the co-

accused persons. Therefore, this Court is of the considered view that there

is no justifiable reason to accede to the prayer made in this Cr.M.P. to

quash and set aside the entire criminal proceeding arising out of

Jamshedpur Cyber P.S. Case No.30 of 2022 registered for the offences 3 Cr. M.P. No.3021 of 2022 ( 2025:JHHC:26820 )

 punishable under Sections 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 120-B / 34 of the Indian Penal

 Code and Sections 66C & 66D of the Informant Technology Act including

 the charge sheet and cognizance order dated 28.06.2022 in connection

 with the said case against the petitioner; in exercise of its power under Section 482 of Cr.P.C. 7. Accordingly, this Cr.M.P. being without any merit, is dismissed. (Anil Kumar Choudhary, J.)
