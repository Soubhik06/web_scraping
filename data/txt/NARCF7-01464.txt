I recently avoided a scam! I didn't know this was a common scam, but after sharing my experience with my friends, I learned that it is.

I was looking for flats online when I came across a listing in NoBroker for a 2BHK fully furnished flat for 25k per month (including maintenance) at My Home Abhra. Yes! Such a good price at a My Home society! The price seemed too good to be true, so I called the number listed. The conversation went well, and the person on the other end said that I could come and see the flat that day, as there were many other people interested.

After a while, the person called me back and asked me to pay 2000/- as an entry fee for the society. They said that this fee would be fully refundable if I didn't like the property. This sounded strange to me, as I had never heard of such a thing before. I said that I would pay the fee when I visited the society.

Then he said, "Sir, please pay now while we are on the phone and send me a screenshot. It will only take 1-2 minutes." He then sent me a QR code. At this point, I was sure that it was a scam, so I hung up the phone.

I then asked a few of my friends in Hyderabad if they had heard of such a scam, and they confirmed that it is a common one! Please beware of such fraudsters.
