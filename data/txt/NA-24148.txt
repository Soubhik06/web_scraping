Wake up. No power. No problem I have inverter. Start morning routine, no hot water, heater separate from inverter. Oh well, bathed yesterday. Tap water starts to thin out and disappears. Tank is dry. Oh shit, pump is dead, no power. Urgent need to take a dump, cistern is still full. Will it flush in one go? It does, thank god. Have brunch, leave for work. 4 hour commute, no seats while coming back. Back pain from all the standing. Come home, still no power. Inverter on its last legs, turn off all lights, fans, only router running and me shitposting in the dark. No dota today. Finally power comes back on, take a shower, have dinner, early to bed.

Early to rise, hmm, no wifi, very odd. Fan still on, bit slow. Check router, low voltage, router turned off, inverter didn't kick in. No water, pump can't run in low voltage. Never mind, have breakfast and scram. Open fridge, everything warm. Fridge still on. Died in the morning from low voltage, even behind a stabilizer. Lie down in bed. Shitpost in 4G. FML.
