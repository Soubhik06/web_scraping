Had a call this morning from a heavily accented gentleman (India/Pakistan or thereabouts) saying he was phoning as my BT account was showing that my broadband was being used by 3 or 4 different IP addresses none of which were registered to me and they were stealing my broadband! I knew right away it was a scam but played along. I sounded worried and asked what to do about it and was told to log on to my computer and open task manager and type eventvwr into it. I’d see warning after warning flag up. I knew that doing this would always show these warnings. He said this was my proof. Then I needed to open a browser and ......At this point I handed the phone to my software tester son and he took over the call. He played along too. At no point had we even switched on any computers but apparently he could see everything we were doing online live. But my son wasn’t surprised to hear what the guy wanted us to do next, something about .exe (I’m clueless) and then my son told him he’d been recording the whole conversation for our YouTube channel and we’d also be advising BT (we don’t have one and are not even BT customers). At this point he told my son to do one more thing...go fuck himself! Charming.
