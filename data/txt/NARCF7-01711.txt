Matched this girl on Bumble a long time ago — suddenly she messaged me recently with a long emotional text. She said her dad passed away, she hasn’t paid her cricket academy fees, and can’t ask her family for help. On call, she even started crying and said she’s not a graduate, doesn’t have a job, and was asking me to help with money. In return, she said she’s okay being there for me “physically” if I want.

She added, “I’m not that kind of girl,” but said things are hard. The whole thing felt like emotional manipulation with a transactional offer underneath.

Posting this here just in case someone else runs into something similar — don’t let guilt or sympathy cloud your judgment. This has all the signs of a scam or trap.
