Don't know if this a new scam by taxi drivers or not but posting here so that you guys are aware of it as well. My mother was flying out of Bangalore T2 and had booked her cab to the same (For those of you who might be unaware, Uber shows you the list of different airlines while flying out of Bangalore and books your ride to either T1 or T2 based on the airlines you choose). On the way to the airport, the driver told my mother that the drop location is T1 and not T2. My mother knew that it wasn't T1 because firstly, her boarding pass said T2 and secondly, she had chosen the same airlines in the app as well (plus, she's travelled through T2 before so knows that the airlines operates from T2). The driver kept insisting that the drop location was wrong and demanded an extra 75 rs to drop her off at T2. My mother, believing that it might be a genuine mistake, agreed to pay the difference and got dropped off at T2. Here's the catch, upon checking the app after entering the airport, Uber showed that she booked the ride to be dropped off at T2 and not T1 as the driver was insisting. I don't know if this is a one off case or if other drivers are doing the same thing so be mindful while travelling to the airport! 

PS: My mom raised a complaint on the Uber app and they refunded the difference.
