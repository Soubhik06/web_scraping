Why are animal rescue organizations that rescue various animals such as dogs, cats, cows, birds or other animals that were bred for meat or dairy but somehow left behind, dependent upon public charity and donations to do their work? Why is the onus upon NGOs and NPOs to carry on such works? 

If government can subsidize dairy and meat farmers, give tax exemptions to animal businesses, exempt agricultural income, why can't government use the taxes to give medical help, shelter, rescue animals on their own? Are animals not responsibility of government where they're born? This is not just limited to India but in other countries as well, animal rescue organizations are usually funded privately. 

Recently I was seeing a video conference of an NGO in my city where they were discussing their yearly goals and budgets. The host said something along the lines of "I'm often insulted and called a beggar because I ask for donations for this work. People want animal to be rescued but if we ask for donation they call us fraud". 

Why is it not state responsibility to rescue and look after its animals? Why does government only intervene when it is time to make profit from animals and leaves them aside when it is time to take their responsibility?
