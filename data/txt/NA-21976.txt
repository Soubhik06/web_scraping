I want to share my recent experience to warn others about a scam by Club Resorto Hospitality that seems to be targeting people who have recently made big purchases like cars.

How It Started:

A few days ago, I purchased a car, and shortly after, I received an invitation call from Club Resorto. They invited me and my wife to attend an event at the Grand Mercure Hotel, Mysore, on 10th December 2024, under the pretext of a “special function.” They said they were giving out free luxury holiday vouchers and couple movie tickets as part of their "company advertisement and publicity promotion."

What Happened at the Event:

At the event, they offered me a seemingly exclusive deal: ₹80,000 for 50 nights at 4/5-star hotels.

Their sales team used high-pressure tactics to convince us that this was a limited-time offer and we couldn’t get such a deal later.

Under the pressure, I paid ₹80,000 via credit card on the spot.

The Scam Unfolds:

After coming home and reviewing the membership offer, I started feeling uneasy and decided to research Club Resorto. That’s when I discovered numerous complaints online describing the exact same experience:

Invitations to 5-star hotels under the guise of free gifts.

High-pressure sales pitches at the event.

False promises and refusal to refund money after purchase.

I immediately requested a refund within their 10-day cooling-off period, as promised during the event.

1. Initially, they confirmed via email that my refund would be processed promptly without deductions.

2. Over the next few days, they started stalling with vague responses.

3. Finally, on 16th December, they denied my refund, contradicting their earlier written assurance.

Why This is a Scam:

They target people who recently made high-value purchases like cars, making them think the event is special and exclusive.

They lure people to 5-star hotels with the promise of “free luxury vouchers.”

They pressure attendees into making large payments without giving time to think or research.

Getting a refund, even if promised, becomes an uphill battle filled with stalling and contradictions.

My Advice to Others:

1. If you receive invitations promising “free gifts” for attending events, be extremely cautious.

2. Never make large payments on the spot—always research the company thoroughly.

3. If you’ve fallen victim to such scams, immediately file a complaint with the National Consumer Helpline or your local consumer protection body.

What I’m Doing Now:

I have filed a complaint with the National Consumer Helpline and am pursuing all official channels to get my refund. I won’t stop until this is resolved.

If you or someone you know has faced a similar experience, please share your story here. We need to raise awareness and stop others from being cheated by such fraudulent schemes.
