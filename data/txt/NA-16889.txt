NEET 2024 EXPOSED:PART I(?)

CONTEXT(SKIP IF YOU ALREADY KNOW) 

NEET 2024 occurred on 5 May. 
The paper went smoothly and the difficulty level was the same as NEET 2023 more or less. 

When the students came back home, a small controversy erupted which was quickly dealt with by the National Testing Agency or NTA.
 
This controversy was that the paper had begun to circulate at around 4 pm. Now, this happened because inn a centre in Rajasthan i.e. ‘Girls Higher Secondary Adarsh Vidya Mandir, Mantown, Sawai Madhopur’ Hindi medium students were given English medium papers.

Now, the NTA admitted to this mistake and released a statement which said that while the invigilator was rectifying his mistake some of the students walked out forcefully with the question paper before the end of the exam. 

That is why the paper started circulating but since it was shared at around 4 pm,the exam had already started and the students were not impacted.

Now of course because this did not have any serious repercussions and it was a human mistake, it was forgiven but the next revelation was not. 

Source- https://indianexpress.com/article/education/neet-ug-2024-paper-leaked-heres-what-nta-says-9309506/

The second mistake was the paper was actually leaked. The answers were provided to 20 candidates on May 4 in a rented house in Patna,Bihar. The man who provided the papers was arrested and the candidates and their parents were withheld after the exam. Now we know that this happens almost every year but it hardly goes reported except this time. But Patna police also confirmed that the paper was found in hostels and leaked in telegram channels 2 days before the exam. 

Source- https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/education/20-students-got-neet-papers-in-patna-day-before-exam-say-cops/articleshow/109993668.cms

The third mistake was the awarding of grace marks. When the result was released students had gotten scores like 718 and 719 which isn’t possible in NEET because students had lost time due to unspecified reasons and were threatening to sue. Now this led to addition of 60-150 grace marks in the mark sheets of some students and they had scores like 718 which weren’t divisible by 5.

Now,this is wrong on so many levels. What criteria was used for the increase? What was the process? Who got grace marks and how much? How were they calculated and by which formula? And most importantly, why was this not known earlier? Why is this the first year for grace marks to be added? 

Source- https://indianexpress.com/article/education/neet-ug-2024-result-live-updates-nta-announces-neet-result-link-at-exams-nta-ac-in-toppers-cut-off-9365098/

The fourth discrepancy was that SNo. 62 to 69,95,75 have all gotten 720/720 and were all from two same centre-2307010168 of thanjar district of Haryana. Now this centre isn’t known to be a topper region, so something fishy is askew.

Source- https://x.com/kalapian_/status/1798333320197206176 

The fifth discrepancy- this is the first time the AIR1 rank holders are not from coachings but from centres and districts where the paper got leaked.
