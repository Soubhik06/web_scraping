So on Sunday, me and my friend went to CP after College. After walking a little bit we decided to have Ice cream from the kwality walls ice cream seller in front of haldiram. 
He was an old guy and first he said that he does not accept UPI and so me and my friend started walking away but then he called us back and told us to pay on a guy's phone who was standing with him. 
 
**First Scam :** He gave us a chocolate cone and said its 80 per piece. Now i was checking the MRP which was torn off by him and before i could say something my friend had already paid him the money on that QR code provided by his friend.

**Second Scam :** Now my friend and i were leaving, but then his friend said that he has not recieved payment. We told him that we paid it in front of him but he started saying that his name is smth else and the person who is paid is someone else. We knew he is scamming as he must have shown a different QR first, but since we had no proof we couldnt say anything. Now again he asked for 160 rupees.

By now i got very angry and said that we will only give u 100 and then he said that here in CP the ice cream is sold for 10 rupees more. I started shouting, saying law terms and taking police name and gave him 120 only.

**SO PLEASE DONT GET SCAMMED LIKE I DID**
