--- POST TEXT ---

So there I was ....single, unemployed, broke, and standing on the hot streets of Hinjewadi , and enough despair to power an Ekta Kapoor serial.

I had tried for a job at some shady tech startup with a name like “ByteSutra” or “AlgoKarma” or something that sounded like a meditation app with funding issues. Of course, they rejected me after offering me *chai* but no *chair*. Very disrespectful.

Now, here’s where it gets spicy.

I see this well-dressed, clearly upper-middle-class dude waiting on the footpath like he was about to get picked up for a podcast shoot. So I approached him with my “Sir, I am from a small village, wife and kid are with me, no money, only hope and an iron deficiency” story.

I expected the usual “Sorry bhai, I have only PayTM” or the classic “God will help you.”

But this guy goes: 
**“Ok, I’ll buy you bananas.”**

BANANAS.

Like I’m the last minion in Pune.

I acted cool though. I said, “Okay sir,” but then immediately levelled up the quest.

“Actually, sir… if possible… 1 kg rice. My baby also will eat.”

And he AGREED. Took me to a nearby shop like some rice-summoning wizard and BOOM 1 kg of white gold in my hand.

But then the plot twist - he starts asking *questions* like a lawyer doing pro bono.

* “Where will you cook this?”
* “Do you have a house?”
* “How will you go home?”

Bro I don’t even have a *plan* for dinner and you’re asking me about *infrastructure.*

So I gave him the classic emotionally vague reply: 
**“We will manage somehow, sir.”**

And then walked away with the rice like a Bollywood freedom fighter who just liberated dinner.

Long story short — I went out with hope and came back with carbohydrates.

Moral of the story: if you ever need rice in Pune, dress semi-decent, carry emotional damage, and find a guy waiting outside a restaurant. Worked for me.

**TL;DR** 
Tried to scam a dude with the “help me feed my kid” line. Ended up getting 1 kg of rice and a mini-interrogation. 10/10 would emotionally manipulate again
