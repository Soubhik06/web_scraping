My cousin received a call from a scammer whose phone number appeared as **+91-8918500585**. The individual who called her purported to be from India Postal Bank. 
He told her that her UPI (Unified Payment Interface) was not working and made her install the AnyDesk application. Then he asked for a 10-digit code, which she gave him. After that, he asked her to make a payment of **5,000 Rs** . Then he said to make another payment of **997 Rs**. He then asked her to send **5,997** **Rs**, promising to return the same amount. However, he didn't return the money. He then transferred the call to one of his seniors who told her to send **5997 Rs** again. She sent the money again but still, he didn't return it. He then asked for one last payment of **5,997 Rs**, promising to return all her payments. She sent the payment of **5997 Rs** to him but he didn't return her money. By that time, her account balance had reached 6.50 Rs . He asked her to make the payment from someone else's phone. He asked for **6,019 Rs** one last time. Again, she gave him the money, thinking that he would return all her money, but he didn't. Total of **30,007 Rs** has been lost. 
The money she lost was intended to finance her education. I have complained to the police, but I don't think they would be able to catch him, as they are least bothered and corrupt. Is there any way that we can get the money back.
