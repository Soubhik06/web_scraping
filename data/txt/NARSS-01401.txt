Today afternoon I received a product from a delivery guy. The guy said its a cash on delivery item and the price was ₹699. I was skeptical because I usually don't order items via COD. I thought it was something my mom ordered. I took it and gave the guy ₹700 in cash. Later, my mom said she didn't order anything like this. Turns out, nobody in my family ordered anything. No tracking info, no order history, no SMS alerts—nothing. The address label on the product was my mom's. Thats why I took it. It was a pretty small package and inside it was a Ayurvedic Toothpaste container. It looked used. There was no cover or any silver foil or anything. leakage was also there. It was just put simply inside the cardboard. 

We just lost 700 just like that.

Has this happened to anybody else? did this happen because of an address label mismatch from the delivery warehouse or something..or is it a scam? im not quite sure!
