Getting lots of spoofed (local area code) calls (20+ per day) recently from "Senior Benefits" (or a similar name) who connect me to "a licensed agent in my state". I believe they are after valid Medicare card numbers for insurance fraud and any other info they can phish out of you. The India call center will hang up if asked for a company website or call back number where as once you get transferred to the "legit" company they will not.

The initial call is either a computer generated voice or a live person who sounds Indian asking for age, zip code, and if I have Medicare A and B. They then transfer me to a supervisor or a "licensed agent in my state". The supervisor is always Indian whereas the agent will often have an American or southern American accent. I think the transfers to the American sounding people are legit Medicare supplemental insurance companies.

At first I didn't understand why a legit company would use a telemarking service that spoofs phone #'s then it hit me: The scam call (spoofed) originating from India is staying on the line after the "transfer" to the legit company so they can "listen-in" and get your personal info that way. The India scam call center still has control over the call and will terminate the session if things don't go their way.

Just posting this because I haven't seen it talked about much and it needs some more light shed on it.
