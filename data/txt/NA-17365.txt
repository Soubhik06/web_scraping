What Happened?

A 19-year-old woman from Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, has alleged that she was gang-raped by 23 men over the course of seven days, between March 30 and April 4, 2025. According to the survivor, she was drugged, taken to different places, and repeatedly sexually assaulted during this time.

---

Key Details of the Case

1. The Disappearance

The girl left her home on March 29, saying she was going to meet a friend.

When she did not return, her family filed a missing persons report.

She was found by police on April 4, unconscious and in a distressed condition.

2. The Allegations

She alleges that 23 men took turns to sexually assault her.

She was reportedly taken to places like a café, a hookah bar, and private residences.

The perpetrators allegedly gave her drugs, making her semi-conscious during many of the assaults.

She has named 12 accused in her FIR, while 11 remain unidentified as of now.

3. Medical and Legal Action

The girl underwent medical examination, which confirmed evidence of assault.

An FIR was registered at Lalpur Pandeypur Police Station under several serious charges:

Gang rape

Drugging/intoxication

Wrongful confinement

Criminal intimidation

Police have arrested 9 accused so far and are tracking the rest.

---

Charges and Legal Process

The case has been registered under the new Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), the updated criminal code in India (which replaced IPC).

These include Section 63 (gang rape), Section 97 (administering intoxicating drugs), among others.

Forensic evidence, digital trails (CCTV, phone data), and testimonies are being used to strengthen the case.

---

Public and Government Reaction

The incident has triggered public outrage in Varanasi and beyond.

Protests and candle marches have been held demanding justice.

The Congress party announced they would gherao the PM’s constituency office in protest.

The girl's family is seeking justice from CM Yogi Adityanath.
