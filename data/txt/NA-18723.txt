Tab Cabs is a taxi service [owned by SMS Infrastructure](http://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/nagpur/SMS-Infrastructure-to-run-4000-taxis-in-Mumbai/articleshow/9949948.cms) that runs in Mumbai. 

SMS Infrastructure is the [family business of the Sanchetis](http://www.smsl.co.in/management.htm) and Ajay Sancheti is the vice chairman (non-executive) of Tab Cabs.

Ajay Sancheti is also a [Special Invitee member of the BJP National Executive](http://www.bjp.org/en/organisation/national-executive?u=special-invitees). It is well known that Sanchetti family are close pals with Gadkari. 

Ajay Sancheti is well know to have [benefited from Gadkari's presence](http://www.openthemagazine.com/article/nation/the-not-so-secret-life-of-ajay-sancheti) both at Maharashtra's state government and BJP central government 

&gt; once the Shiv Sena-BJP combine came to power in the state and **Gadkari took charge of the Public Works Department, several infrastructure projects came Sancheti’s wa**y. SMS Infrastructure Ltd has done work in spheres as diverse as roads, highways, mining, railways, power, irrigation and urban and rural development. Later, once the BJP took charge at the Centre in 1998, **Gadkari became chairman of the National Roads Committee, and SMS got to execute two major projects under the National Highway Authority of India**. These involved the construction of flyovers, bridges and tunnels, all of which went to Gadkari’s public credit (the enviable Mumbai-Pune Expressway being the brightest feather in the BJP chief’s cap).

Sanchetti's driver (what is it with Gadkari &amp; co and drivers?!) and nephew had a flat allocated in the scam tainted Adarsh apartment too.

&gt; Sancheti had also applied for a flat in the scam-tainted Adarsh apartment building in Mumbai, and though he himself did not make it to the list of allottees, his driver Sudhakar Laxman Madhke did; the man is believed to be Sancheti’s proxy, and his nephew too is an allottee.

There is no way to know the intentions of Gadkari and he does make sense about the ban on Uber. But all said and done, I don't think I'd be off the mark if I say that Gadkari does have a conflict of interest with the cab industry and also other industries too.

Oh and one more thing ... Tab Cabs is a [very unique venture](http://shashwatdc.com/2013/02/merucabmumbai/). 

&gt; The company behind Tab Cabs , SMS cabs is owned by the Sancheti Family, the BJP Bigwig and Nitin Gadkari’s pal. The company is also apparently backed by the Vasai-Virar strongman Hitendra Thakur, whose son is a director. And then there’s the NCP MLA Dilip Mohite, another director. And finally the MD is Jagdish Purohit, who was apparently an associate of Satej Patil, the state-level Congress Minister. Thus, **Tab Cabs is a very unique collaborative effort, between the BJP, Congress, and NCP**. Little wonder then, it did not face any hurdles or troubles that plagued Meru.

Tab Cabs also did everything to scuttle Meru and its fleet. Read the linked article for an interesting narrative.
