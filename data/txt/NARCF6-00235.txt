Anyone here is from oakwood estate, sector 25 or aware of a visiting PASS system if that even exists.

So basically, I looked up a flat on magicbricks, called the guy, he said I need to have to have a visiting pass/card to visit the society and that I’ve to pay 2050 rs as a security something online to his account for the card to the society . He further told he’ll adjust that in my rent if deal is finalised else would return the same to me on the same day I visit if I don’t like the property. Presented himself as the owner. Also asked me to share Aadhar card + passport pic for issuing the visiting card.

Probably seems like scam, due to above plus rent and features are too good to be true. If it is a scam, be safe guys.

Edit: As others also confirmed, it is definitely a SCAM. 

Be Safe guys, Never give money for security/visiting pass or anything before visiting the location and meeting the guy face to face and carefully verifying everything. Even saw in one of the Facebook post recently that someone paid token amount online and it was a Scam.
