Complaining to BSNL or the Mumbai cyber cell or at pgportal.gov.in does nothing. The only advise I've got was: put your LANDLINE (and mobile) on a do not call list http://www.nccptrai.gov.in/nccpregistry/. I've setup a Facebook page as well: https://www.facebook.com/BSNLphoneFraud/ Check out this Aussie guys efforts at tracking down scammers - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8HbvY1ZDhwg basically the calls do not originate in India (though in my case it was a Hindi speaking woman) and are run by organized gangs making millions - additionally they are protected by the police and the banks - in the case of the Aussie, it was HSBC - Hong Kong and they wouldn't divulge info because of 'data privacy' laws.

The quality of call was poor - and I have a good quality land line - this is deliberate and done to lure you into getting worried and agitated. They pick/impersonate LIC because many people have LIC accounts - cheating the aam aadmi so to speak. I have caller-ID and the number originates in Mumbai, though the caller claimed to be from LIC New Delhi. They mentioned 14K rupees was in involved - large enough to tempt the greedy and small enough to create a sense of anxiety.

I asked them to e-mail me the details of the problem because of the poor quality line - they did not get back to me. It would have helped me trace their computer. If you get such calls - ask for their e-mail or ask them to e-mail you and give them some junk e-mail. Remember that our idiot government publishes Electoral Rolls that are a scammers dream come true! These Electoral Rolls have our name, address, age, sex-status etc and they just need to read/harvest that data.

Please complain on pgportal.gov.in if you get such calls.
