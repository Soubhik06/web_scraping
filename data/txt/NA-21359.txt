I came across an Instagram account called @hall_of_shoes, which had a lot of followers and posts about various shoes. I liked a pair of Puma Lamelo sneakers they were selling. The person behind the account, who claimed to be Kali Charan from Gorakhpur, UP, asked me to order through WhatsApp. To be safe, I opted for Cash on Delivery.

However, he requested an upfront payment of 200 rupees via Paytm, with the remaining 1,500 rupees to be paid upon delivery. I agreed, but when the delivery arrived, I couldn't check the package before paying because the agent didn't allow me to. To my dismay, it contained a pair of cheap shoes worth only 200 rupees. The sender's name was "Youth fashion something," on the parcel and the address was just my address copy pasted.

I contacted the delivery partner, but they couldn't help. So, I spoke to their manager, who promised to look into it. Frustrated, I went to the Barakhamba Road police station in Delhi and spoke to Sub Inspector Neetu, who advised me to visit the Mandir Marg police station, where the cybercrime unit is located. There, I explained everything to two officers who recommended filing an online report. I also tried calling the fraudster, but he didn't answer my calls.

It's been a month since I reached out to the Delhivery hub regarding a failed delivery and a potential refund. The person I was initially in contact with has left, and the new manager, Aniket, was unhelpful and rude when I visited. Despite filing a complaint with the Cyber Crime Police, there's been no progress, and contacting the South-East district cyber cell has been futile.

The fraudster responsible for this has numerous Instagram accounts and even runs ads. It seems Delhivery might be complicit since they accepted a parcel with no sender details. I'm not concerned about the small amount of money but feel frustrated with the lack of power to take action. Any suggestions on how to proceed and hold both the fraudster and Delhivery accountable would be appreciated.
