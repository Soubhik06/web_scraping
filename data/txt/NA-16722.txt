Hi India,

Just wanted to share an update about a scam I personally fell victim to. I never thought I could be scammed, but honestly, greed got the better of me lol. Here’s what happened:

I came across an Instagram ad that said, “Free Amazon/Flipkart orders. No advance payment required. Pay only when you receive the package.” It was obviously enticing since I needed certain gadgets for work, though deep down I suspected it could be a scam. Out of curiosity, I clicked on the WhatsApp link.

The person on the other end responded instantly. I asked a lot of questions, and he seemed patient and convincing. To cross-check the validity, I even consulted a friend of mine who’s a grey hat hacker. He mentioned it was possible to exploit Amazon panels to get refunds, as refund processing is done in bulk and some cases go undetected. I also searched Reddit and the internet for similar scams but couldn’t find anything. This gave me some false confidence.

I decided to test it out, thinking of getting an iPad Air for my drawings. But then I thought, let’s not be greedy and start with something small. So, I selected headphones. He asked me to add ₹2,000 to my Mobikwik wallet (why ₹2,000? Check screenshots). I did as instructed. Then he asked me to join a Google Meet call and share my screen so he could explain the process.

During the call, he asked me to open my Mobikwik app to confirm the payment had been added to my wallet. I did this. He then instructed me to open Amazon and try feeding certain card numbers he provided. After trying several, he kept saying they weren’t working and acted surprised, saying things like, “Aise toh kabhi nahi hota” (this has never happened before).

Later, I realized that the ₹2,000 I had deposited was taken from my wallet. Upon investigation, it turned out he had linked my Mobikwik account to Blinkit using my number (which he already had from our WhatsApp conversation). While I was busy entering his card details, I received an OTP notification to link my wallet to Blinkit, but I didn’t notice it (still not sure how). That’s how he managed to steal the money.

I’m glad I didn’t try for a bigger purchase—this could have been a much greater loss.

I don’t understand why Mobikwik allows screen sharing of the app, especially when other popular payment apps don’t.
