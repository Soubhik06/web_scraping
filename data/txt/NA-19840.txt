Law.com has published a commentary on power structures in the big law firms of India, and how these structures are changing (or nor changing):

[Will India’s New Era Sever its Old Law Firm Power Structures?](https://www.law.com/international-edition/2022/06/21/will-indias-new-era-sever-its-old-power-structures/)

This article is closer to truth than what is published by India publications, which are dependent on these large law firms for ad revenue. Some of the managing partners / proprietors have spoken out in this story. One, namely Nishith Desai Associates, refused to provide comments. The article mentions that the firm has been seeing mass exodus after a major leak of client confidential data. The details of the same can be read in our blog:

[The Fall of Nishith Desai](https://nishithdesaiassociates.wordpress.com)
