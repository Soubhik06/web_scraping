Husband came to me asking "does this sound like a scam? I got a message on an email I don't use a lot from a woman originally from India" I cut him off here and said "scam!" But then he asked to hear him out.

It's a recruiter in the US for a company called Educative. They teach tech related courses online and he is an expert in several of the areas they are trying to grow in. The company is legit, and people put courses on there. But he feels really sketchy about how he was contacted and the email they used. They told him to put together and outline and some course descriptions, send them to Educative, and they will send back a course publishing contract if they determine it's needed. I cannot yet see where there is a scam, or how he could be snowed. But it is weird that they contacted him about his current line of work and referencing that but doing so on a really old defunct email addy. Still being leary...
