ok so, I made a search for flats for sale in Mumbai on 99 acres and magicbricks and left my phone number there. Soon I received a call from one of those loan agents offering me a home loan ... only difference being that he was offering a zero interest loan ... now no way I was taking that cos clearly there has got to be a catch .. no one can give a zero interest loan .. and to top it he was saying he is calling from Reliance .. I asked him which Reliance company .. he had no clue .. and said the loan division .. but I wanted to understand how the scam works so I persisted ... and kept questioning him on the fine print to understand the modus operandi ... He finally said something which nauseated me immediately and I hung up refusing his loan .. one of the conditions was that borrower will have to take out an insurance policy in favor of the lender ... so you can see how they make money right ? they must be taking out the policy for say 1.2x the amt borrowed in favor of the lender ... and they must be bumping off the borrower after some time ... wonder how long they have been doing this and how many people they have trapped .. Do you think I am overreading things here ? anyone else heard something like this ? Is there anything I can do to bring this to light ? I share the common Indian middle class fear of getting involved in a police case and hence will prefer not to go to police. Please give me some guidance .. have been very disturbed thinking what might be going on. .. and this is a throwaway acc. Thanks
