Got a call from a New Jersey number. Slow day at work so I decided to have a little fun. Little did I know I’d be walking into my first IRS call of the year! Here’s how it went down.

Me: Hello?

Scammer: *Typical BS. “You owe,” “Sent a letter,” “Warrant,” etc. South Asian accent, 500 people talking in the background.*

M: Oh Wow. This is the first I’m hearing of this. It’s especially strange that I owe anything since I always get a return, but if the IRS is calling me I must have made a mistake somewhere. What can I do? How much do I owe?

S: Ok let me pull it up. Can I get your DOB, last 4 Of SSN, Full name?

M: Provided all fakes.

S: Is your address [Gives my actual address] (It’s always unnerving when they know your address)

M: Yes

S: Ok sir, you owe $5,686.24. Now you must go and surrender yourself or wait for the police to arrive. You will be taken into custody and will go before the judge tomorrow.

M: On a Saturday?

S: Yes sir. This is a very serious matter. Why did you do this? Or was this an accident?

M: I definitely didn’t do it on purpose. I use TurboTax. It does the work for you.

S: Yes. So do you want to fight this inside court or outside?

M: I definitely don’t want to go to court. I’ll pay it right now. I have my credit card ready.

S: No, it’s too late for that. We cannot take payment at this time.

M: Ok, so why are you calling if I can’t even pay what I owe?

S: Sir, to warn you that the police department is on the way.

M: The whole department?

S: Yes sir.

M: Can I have your badge number please?

S: Yes sir, it’s [Provides number]

M: Ok. And are you local to me?

S: No sir, I am in New Jersey. 

M: Why am I not being contacted by my local IRS office?

S: Sir I am with the crime center.

M: Are you sure you aren’t calling from a smelly Indian basement?

S: Why are you wasting your time? 

M: Let’s not point fingers. 

S: Check your bank account. You’ve already been hacked mother fucker. God bless you.

Good times.
