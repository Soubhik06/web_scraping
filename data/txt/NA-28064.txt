Recently, **about 90 Muslim women’s photos were auctioned as ‘Deals of the Day’ on an online app called ‘Sulli Deals’.** ‘Sulli’ is a derogatory term used to address Muslim women by communally divisive groups. **Isn’t this objectification of women infuriating? Women are not instruments to aid people's hatred towards a specific community.** We want your help in building pressure on GitHub to take this seriously. Ask for accountability. 

https://preview.redd.it/m5g8qqzbcig71.jpg?width=1080&amp;format=pjpg&amp;auto=webp&amp;s=e50563846d8126fffb2169476570a00b9171be9f

We are demanding 

* Github, the open-source platform on which this app was hosted, **reveal the identity of these misogynistic and Islamophobic perpetrators to the police and cooperate with their investigation**
* Github compensate the targeted women with an apology and a **strict set of safety regulations** to prevent occurrences of similar harassment in the future
* Github make its **safety mechanisms publicly available**
* The National Commission for Women recognise this **not just as a cybercrime but also as a hate crime** due to its communal nature

Help us get more support for this campaign.
