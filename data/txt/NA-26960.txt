Scam Alert 🚨 

In the digital age, a new scam has emerged targeting unsuspecting business owners across the U.S. Scammers, hailing from places like Bangladesh and India, offer fake review services to companies. When refused, they retaliate with a barrage of negative reviews on the company’s Google page, followed by a sinister demand for money to remove them. I turned the tables by capturing evidence of this scam, leading to the scammer’s arrest. This tale of digital deception and swift justice serves as a cautionary reminder: always be alert to online threats, safeguard your digital presence, and never underestimate the power of evidence.
