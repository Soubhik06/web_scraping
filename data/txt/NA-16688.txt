Is Jabong. com the worst online store in India? I came here after rating their app in Google play store.If there was a negative rating system for an app, I would have rated it negative. Orders are not delivered on time. We have to pay to talk to customer care who don't pick up our call easily. Even if they pick up, they pass on the buck by saying that,"you'll get a call within 6 hours". Even after calling so many times, they can't assure the problem will be resolved. Their partner GOJAVAS are the worst courier service that ever was. And Jabong keeps on saying that we can't give assurances for our partners. They are even delaying refunds. All in all they are running a scam. All these going on and they run new ads...
