For awareness. Just got off a call with a scammer from "TRAI", as my number was used for illegal activities and all my numbers are set for deactivation in next 2 hrs. Bottom line is I have to reach Mumbai Port Police Station in 2 hrs and register a complaint and get a clearance letter from them. If not possible to reach, they will connect to the station directly. Slightly modified version of the age old courier scam. 

So don't even waste your time, if you hear the automated message that all your numbers will be deactivated in 2 hrs. Just disconnect unless you are feeling bored af!
