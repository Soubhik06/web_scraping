Did anyone ever pay a recruitment agency/consultants to get a job?
I was called for an interview at a recruitment agency and I was surprised as they have some "security deposit" that needs to be paid. This will be refunded after I join the company and get the first salary or if I don't get selected. Either way there's nothing to lose [as per them]

This was the first time Inwas ever asked to pay money and it seems like a scam to me but I wanted to check with you all if there is actually something like this or if you have faced a similar situation. 

More details:
You pay 5k registration fees they fix your interview with company HR/Ops Manager - if you fail this round they claim that they will refund the 5k

If you clear the round then you need to pay security fees (depends upon CTC - 10k is minimum I guess, I was asked to pay more due to the package being offered was more than 10 LPA). Once you get salary they get paid from Company and they refund you the amount. 

I was told this is a security measure as many job applicants don't join the organisation after getting the offer letter and the recruitment agency has to suffer the loss. 

This recruitment agency is located in Dwarka.
