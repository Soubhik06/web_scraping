I'm reaching out for some guidance regarding a challenging situation I faced. In April 2023, I fell victim to a fraudster who applied for a personal loan using my details. I took immediate action, filing an FIR, a cyber crime report, and raising the issue with the bank's PNO. I also filed a report in the consumer court against the bank. 
During the legal proceedings, my account was frozen. In the hope that the legal proceedings would resolve the matter, I chose not to pay the EMIs during this period of about 6 months. However, as time passed with no significant progress, I decided to unfreeze my account, and ultimately closed the entire loan account. This decision was driven by the substantial impact on my CIBIL score, which plummeted from around 790 to 680. After repaying the loan, I managed to bring it up to 731. 
As I plan to apply for a home loan soon, I'm seeking advice on how to restore my CIBIL score to its previous level. Any insights or personal experiences would be greatly appreciated.
