Heard on the bail application. This is third bail application filed under Section 439 of
Cr.P.C. The second bail application has been dismissed vide
order dated18.11.2013 passed in M.Cr.C.No.8431/2013. The
applicant has been arrested in Crime No.176/2013 registered at
Police Station, Civil Lines, Vidisha, District Vidisha, under Section
376 of IPC, under Section 3 / 4 of the Protection of Children from
Sexual Offences Act and under Section 67-A of Information
Technology Act. As per prosecution case, on 22.2.2012 applicant Akash
called the complainant to take her to Jeetu's house. When they
reached to Jeetu's house, he was not found there, then Akash
told her that he has gone to Bhopal. Thereafter Akash had taken
her to the house of Jeetu's elder brother and there he had given
cold drink to the complainant. After consuming the same,
complainant became unconscious and thereafter Akash put off
her clothes and prepared video. In the meantime, co-accused
Nitesh Ahirwar reached there and he has also seen that Akash is
preparing video. Thereafter Akash has committed sexual
intercourse with the complainant and threatened her that if she
discloses this, he would made the video public. It is submitted that after rejection of the second bail
application the trial yet to begin. The applicant is below 18 years
and is in custody since 29.3.2013. The trial likely to take time,
hence prayed for bail. The prayer is opposed by learned Public Prosecutor.
 From perusal of the arrest memo of the applicant it appears
that his date of birth is mentioned as 28.12.1994. Learned
counsel has filed copies of statements of the witnesses recorded
during enquiry regarding the juvenility of the applicant. Smt.Rabia
Khan, who is Principal of the Nehru Memorial Higher Secondary
School, Kurwai, has stated that in the admission register for the
year 1999-2000 the date of birth of the applicant has been
mentioned as 28.12.1994. She further stated that the admission
was given by her. As per the admit card of Govt.H.S.Excellence
School, Vidisha the date of birth of the applicant has been
mentioned as 28.12.1994. The alleged incident took place on
22.10.2012. Considering that the applicant is in custody since
29.3.2013 and the date of birth entered in the arrest memo
coupled with the fact that the trial yet to begin, but without
commenting on the merit of the case, the application is allowed. It
is directed that the applicant shall be released on bail on his
furnishing personal bond in the sum of Rs.50,000/- (Rupees Fifty
Thousand Only) with one solvent surety in the like amount to the
satisfaction of Trial Court. This order will remain operative subject to compliance of
 the following conditions by the applicant :- A copy of this order be sent to the Court concerned for
 compliance. C.c. as per rules. (D.K.Paliwal)
 Judge
