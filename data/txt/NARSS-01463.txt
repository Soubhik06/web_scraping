--- POST TEXT ---

I’m not entirely sure if it was a scam, but it feels like it might have been. This happened today.

I usually post fan art on DeviantArt, which I create using PicsArt. A few days ago, someone reached out to me asking if I was available for commissions. I told her I just do this for fun, but I’d be willing to give it a try if I could. She then asked for my WhatsApp number, which I didn’t share. Instead, she asked if I had Telegram or Discord, so I gave her my Telegram ID.

She sent me a photo of a dog and said it was for her son. She offered to pay me $200 and even apologized, saying it was a “low budget.” I was honestly shocked because I’ve never earned that much money before, and I got really excited. I asked her, “Do you really want to go through with the payment? You can pay me after seeing the commission.” I didn’t want her to feel scammed. But she reassured me, saying she trusted me and was ready to pay immediately because she’s a businesswoman.

She asked for my PayPal email, so I gave her the one linked to my account. After a while, she sent me a screenshot showing that she had sent $200. I even got an email from PayPal saying I’d received the money, but it was on hold because my PayPal account wasn’t a business account. The email said I needed to request another $200 from her to upgrade my account.

She then sent another $200, making it $400 in total (again, confirmed by the email). However, the email claimed I could only access the money after refunding $200 to her from my own account. The problem was, I barely had 500 rupees across all my bank accounts. She suggested I borrow money from my friends and refund her through GPay.

At this point, I realized she had a GPay account, and I started wondering why she didn’t just pay me through that in the first place. I told her I didn’t have any friends who could lend me that kind of money. After that, she deleted our Telegram chat and blocked me.

Her name was listed as Emily Kate in the PayPal email, so I assume it was a woman. I tried contacting PayPal’s customer support, but I could only talk to their AI bot. When I asked to speak with a representative over the phone, they said someone would get back to me in 2–3 business days.

I don’t know if this was a scam, but it really feels like one. I was so excited about the $200 and was ready to do my best on the commission, but now I’m left feeling confused and disappointed.
