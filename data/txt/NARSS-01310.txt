--- POST TEXT ---

So, we posted something for sale on OLX.

We immediately got a phone call saying that his son is there nearby and he will pick the item after he make payment via Google Pay.

So, I gave him my mobile number....He told me that its not working, give him the QR code.

I gave him that. Then he said its not scanning properly.

So, I kinda knew it was a scam so I initially just send him a cropped qr code without my name, upi address etc.

Then I send him the entire Google Pay GQ code, the screenshot. I told myself I will not click anything without reading and examining properly.

He send me a request for payment. My parents were like "Click on it" and I was like "Nope, its a scam".

He send it after spoofing the name as "Xiaomi and Mi Ltd something" request you for Rs10,999.

He was talking to my dad and asking them to click on it fast!

I took the phone and said "You are a fraud" and ended the call.

He was talking in Hindi and told us he was from Andra Pradesh.

I am not xenophobic but I feel like Kerala is looked upon as the best place to kill and scam and run away. 

Now, I am contemplating about reporting him to OLX and Xiaomi.
