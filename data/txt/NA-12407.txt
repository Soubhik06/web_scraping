Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and learned

 Additional Public Prosecutor for the State. 2. The petitioner seeks bail in connection with Patna

 Cyber P.S. Case No. 887/2024 registered for the offence

 punishable under Sections 419 / 420 / 468 / 120(B) of the Indian

 Penal Code and Section 66(C) , 66(D) of the I.T. Act. 3. The petitioner was running a call centre in which he

 had employed females and was committing fraud by making

 calls and misguiding the persons to download AnyDesk App and

 thereafter, money lying in their accounts were transferred. The

 petitioner has been found to be the kingpin of the entire crime. 4. In view of the above, this application is dismissed. 5. The trial Court is directed to expedite the trial. (Sandeep Kumar, J)
