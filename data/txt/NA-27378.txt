Just for those that might have received similar threats…

A few months ago I was shopping for an engine for my motor home. I found an ad on FB Marketplace that looked legit. Fair price, not too low, a few pics and a normal sounding description. I called the number and a very professional sounding guy with an Indian accent answered and acted like a guy at a parts counter asking me what vehicle it was for, etc. He said he would look it up in his computer and give me a call back in 5 minutes. Not 3 minutes later I get multiple texts from a Washington DC number saying they were the Jalisco cartel, I wasted an escorts time, I owe them X amount of money or they’re going to k!ll my whole family. Basically copy and pasted my info from a cheap background check, if even that, maybe just google. I had already determined this was a scam when the dude answered the phone, but when I got the texts I blocked the number. Got several phone calls from a different number, blocked that too. Nothing ever happened to me. 

Then just now I got a word for word copy of the same texts, blocked the number, then a few phone calls, blocked those. Also sent the same video twice, no sound, of two dudes in ski masks holding pistols and some kind of automatic w3apon in a car. I don’t scare easy, and can smell scams pretty well, but I can imagine this would scare the s@&amp;$ out of someone. 

I’m about to figure out how to scrub myself off of Google, if anyone has any suggestions of where to start, and I suggest anyone reading this do the same.
