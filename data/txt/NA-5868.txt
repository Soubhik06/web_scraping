1. The present petition has been filed by the petitioner under Section 439 Cr.P.C for grant of regular bail in case having FIR No.654

dated 24.10.2020 registered under Sections 406 , 419 , 420 , 465 , 467 , 468 , 471 IPC and ( Sections 409 , 120-B IPC added later on) at Police Station City

Sirsa District Sirsa. 2. In the present case, FIR was registered on the basis of

complaint lodged by Excise and Taxation Officer, Sirsa against Tara Chand,

Proprietor of M/s Bhim Raj Tara Chand, Sirsa alleging that the said private

firm through its proprietor got refund of Value Added Tax (in short VAT)

worth Rs.29,63,286/- approximately by producing forged and fabricated

documents, during assessment year 2011-2012. During investigation, the

officials of the department including the petitioner were also found involved

in the said fraud. At the relevant time, the petitioner was working as a

falsely implicated in the present case. That the petitioner did his official

registered against the petitioner, he is already enlarged on bail. The counsel

for the petitioner further submits that petitioner retired from service in 2014

whereas the FIR in question was registered in 2016. The counsel for the

petitioner further submits that the petitioner is in custody for the last more

than 8 months and it will take time for the trial to conclude. 4. The State counsel filed status report by way of an affidavit of

Subhash Chand, Deputy Superintendent of Police (City), Sirsa which is

taken on record along with copy of inquiry report (Annexure R-1). 5. The present petition is resisted by the State counsel who

submits that the aforesaid private firm got refund of Rs.29,63,286/- from the

concerned department by producing fake and fabricated documents and the

said refund was claimed by the firm in connivance with the officials of the

concerned department including the petitioner. It is further submitted that

the petitioner is also involved in number of other criminal cases of similar

instructions from ASI Abhay Ram has not disputed the fact that at the

relevant time, the petitioner was working as a DETC and he retired in 2016. 6. I have considered the submissions made by counsel for the

detaining the petitioner in custody for any longer period. 8. In light of the above, without expressing any opinion on the
