I needed to ship my bike from Surat to Hyderabad, so I reached out to several packers and movers for quotations. Anil Chaudhary from Alfa Car Bike contacted me and quoted a price of 5k for the entire service. Sensibly, I inquired about their policy regarding damages. Anil assured me that they would cover 90% of the cost for any damage. To verify their credibility, I checked his mobile number (+918882303321) on Truecaller, which showed it as belonging to ISO-certified movers and packers.

Seeking further clarity, I requested a written quotation from Anil, specifying all terms and conditions. Although the quotation stated the total cost as 5k, it did not mention coverage for damages. When I raised this concern, Anil assured me there would be no additional charges for it.

After verifying their GSTIN number, which was registered as Alfa Car Bike Movers, and receiving an office location for Alfa in Surat, I felt confident proceeding with them. However, upon visiting the given location, it turned out to be Balaji Packers and Movers. Upon calling the number provided, Ajit Chaudhary confirmed his affiliation with Alfa Car Bike.

Despite these initial discrepancies, I decided to book with Alfa Car Bike. We agreed on a payment of 4k upfront and 1k upon delivery. However, Anil insisted on an upfront payment of 1k for pickup. After two days, my bike was collected, and I was given a LR receipt for the 1k payment. However, when I requested a bill, Anil assured me he would provide it only after the bike was loaded onto the carrier.

After a day, he informed us that the bike was dispatched. So, I asked him for the bill, and he requested me to pay the remaining amount, promising to provide the bill and tracking details afterward. Consequently, I paid 3k and informed him accordingly. He assured me he would check and send the bill. However, after an hour, he sent me a bill of 16k. I was shocked, thinking, "WTF is this?" I immediately called him, but he was now very rude, saying, "Tax nhi bharega kya" (Don't you want to pay taxes?). It was then clear to me that he was scamming me. I promptly contacted my father, and together, we confronted him on a conference call. We reminded him that he had previously stated that the cost would only be 5k without any insurance charges. However, he vehemently denied ever making such claims and threatened to unload my bike anywhere if we didn't pay the 16k demanded.

The next day, my father gathered 10-15 people and went to his office. Ajit was surprised to see so many people and, upon reviewing the quotation and bill, acknowledged that Alfa had wronged us. He reassured us about the safety of my bike, as it was in his truck. He advised us to pay the remaining amount, 1k to Anil (Alfa) and 1k to him, upon receiving the bike. It was Friday, and Ajit informed us that the truck would halt in Mumbai over the weekend for unloading, and we could expect the bike by Monday or Tuesday.

On Monday, we inquired about the bike's whereabouts, and we were told it would reach Hyderabad by Wednesday. Ajit assured us not to worry, stating that we would receive the bike soon. However, when we called him on Wednesday, he informed us that the bike had arrived in Hyderabad and would be delivered by evening or the following afternoon. Since it was a holiday for me on 1st May, I asked my father to inquire about the office location, intending to pick up the bike myself. However, when my father called, he was met with rudeness, with the response, "yaha baithe baithe thodi bata dunga kaha hai bike" (I won't tell you the bike's location just sitting here), followed by hanging up.

Concerned, my father's friend, who was in the press and knew some police in the area, provided Ajit's number to the police. Subsequently, Ajit shared the truck driver's number with us. We learned that the truck had started from Mumbai that morning and was 200 km away from Hyderabad. The truck driver provided a location 30 km away from my place, where he intended to drop off my bike, requiring me to pick it up from there. With no alternative, fearing the worst, I went to the location around 8 pm, near the highway (ORR).

Upon arrival, the truck driver insisted to pay extra 2k before releasing my bike. After a heated argument with Ajit over the phone, we reluctantly paid him the 2k and unloaded the bike. Upon unpacking, we discovered scratches on the clutch lever, a broken right mirror, deep scratches on the bike's head, and all four indicators were bent ); ); . It was a new R15 v4 in Red, only 22 days old.

My father advised me to take the bike home, assuring that his friend would help resolve the issue the following day. However, when we contacted the police, they informed us Balaji is not mentioned in the bill, legal action would be challenging. Nevertheless, I strongly feel that this is a case of fraud, and it should not be overlooked.

The next day, I visited a service center and had everything repaired. It's my earnest request to everyone never to opt for Alfa Car Bike Movers and to report their mobile number on Truecaller and other platforms to prevent others from falling victim to their scams.
