When the matter is called on for hearing, it

 is pointed out on behalf of the respondent-

 authorities that the matter pertains to a 'Ponzi

 scheme and, as such, ought to be heard by the

 appropriate Bench taking up such matters. Learned counsel for the petitioner leaves it

 to the discretion of this Court whether to take up

 the matter, pointing out that, although the matter

 arises from a Ponzi scheme, the same may not

 be directly related thereto. However, keeping in

 view the fact that the questions involved in the

 writ petition are related to a Ponzi scheme matter,

 judicial propriety would demand that the matter is

 heard by the appropriate Bench taking up such

 matters. Accordingly, W. P. A. 563 of 2021 shall go

out of the list with liberty to the parties to mention

for enlistment before the appropriate Bench

taking up Ponzi scheme matters. (Sabyasachi Bhattacharyya, J.)
