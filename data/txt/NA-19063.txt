This (rant) is the bubbling frustration that has been bothering me for months now. 

Yesterday, I came across two posts. 

First: Flipkart's merger with Myntra. This fueled my interest in how these Mergers and Acquisitions, Venture Capital, etc., work. 

Second: Someone posted- "why would any honest man work for GoI considering the amount of corruption and harassment that comes with the job. They can declare anyone a moron with a court order." This was something I thought was profoundly hypocritical. 
This is not a personal vendetta against the guy or something; its just something bothering me.

So I began my search on M&amp;A and how these things work. I came across lots of things including Lehman Bros, Financial Crisis, etc.

Then I saw [Preet Bharara's investigation and prosecution](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raj_Rajaratnam/Galleon_Group,_Anil_Kumar,_and_Rajat_Gupta_insider_trading_cases) of two ([1](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajat_Gupta) and [2](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anil_Kumar)) alumni of oh-so-prestigious IIT (many others were also convicted). They were charged with conspiracy and insider trading, corruption, etc. [Also if it matters, they were the co-founders of *"the prestigious"* Indian School of Business in Hyderabad. Ramalinga Raju of Satyam was a board member of the B-school.](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_School_of_Business#Controversies) This disgusts me.

Considering the obsession this subreddit has with these institutes, I would like to discuss its significance. People (and redditors) like the one I mentioned above leave no chance to blame the government for anything bad that happens:- to them personally or to the society or to this nation as a whole. Is our government to be blamed for the wrong-doings and misdeeds of these two individuals? Or the colleges from which they passed out from? Is is necessary and sufficient to say "Why would any honest man work for the private sector companies (like McKinsey and Company or IBM or Intel or Goldman Sachs or P&amp;G)"? or "Why would anyone ever join IIT or HBS?"

Is it our birthright as an Indian to be corrupt. Is it something that we have accustomed ourselves to be a part of or is it something we strive and thrive to be better at- *Kaun zyada ghoos lega?* *Ye sab to normal hai*, my college mates used to say this *jab signal par pandu motorbike rok le*. I used to object and even retort at first. After the fourth time this happened, I started using public transport for better or worse. 
Does it make any difference whether you are a two-bit goon (like Dawood or those that roam in your localities) or you are an educated individual (like these two Harvard and Wharton alumni or like Chidambaram who incidentally also did his PG from HBS.) with a responsibility of countless lives on your shoulder? What exactly is the difference between Kumar or Gupta and Chidambaram?? 

I was discussing the rampant corruption a few months back in a flight with a man who has some *kapde ka* business in Mumbai, a son who is an Engineer and daughter who aspires to be a Chartered Accountant with the ICAI. He declared after a 5-6 hour discussion that: *Ab bas karo, ye (ghooskhori, hafta-wasooli, etc.) sab toh chalta hai. Ye sab ek purana system hai.* 

[The corruption that started right after the independence has been ever increasing](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_scandals_in_India). 2014 has just started and wikipedia already has cited some cases of scams.

[The corruption in India.](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corruption_in_India)

Discuss.
