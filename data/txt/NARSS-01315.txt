I was ordering a cake for my sister on Cake Hut's website. Under the payment tab, they collected details for debit card, including CVV. I entered them as I thought it will be directed to the payment gateway directly.

I was rerouted to their paytm payment link where I had to re-enter the details. That would mean the first time around, I entered the fucking details on their website. 

Are my card details saved on their website? It's a fertile ground for fraud.
