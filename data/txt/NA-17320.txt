--- POST TEXT ---

After witnessing a string of incidents involving Identity thefts where people actually lost a lot of money and also their mobile numbers and worse, police cases lodged against them, would like to share with the community some information on one of the most common frauds committed and what they can do to safeguard against themselves.

The Identity theft will be done through an unauthorized SIM SWAP of your personal mobile number.

I have witnessed the following criminal activities being conducted using an unauthorized SIM SWAP

* Bank account password reset and adding third party accounts to transfer funds

* Threatening calls for other crimes committed through your personal mobile numbers

* Used for making obscene/lewd calls and sexting

* Using for prostitution rackets

* Selling your number for a profit with other operators by doing a SIM SWAP and an immediate port-out [If your number is a fancy number, I've seen mobile numbers getting sold for 10-15 lakhs]

**Safeguards**

All the mobile operators have acknowledged the growing severity of this crime [Except BSNL] and have implemented enough safeguards to stop unauthorized SIM SWAP being done. However people who commit these frauds are usually well acquainted with the operators and do this with the connivance of someone associated with the operators at some capacity. So there is no 100% fool proof way of stopping this. But you can take the following precautions to minimize your risk

* Regular usage of your numbers if they are registered with any banking services

* Not blocking operator messages using any application

* Calling the help desk or visiting the nearest show room if you receive any call claiming that you are using my number

* Not using any connection which is not in your name. This is unavoidable for some, but do ensure that you do not use connections registered in someone else's name for any critical activities like personal banking
