I'm a regular social media user, used to be active on FB, ig and Reddit. Due to the insurgence of boomers, I left FB in 2020, but pretty active on ig and reddit (this is my new acc). I always thought that ig and reddit are comparatively safer spaces cause these two aren't that accessible to boomers. I've seen lgbtqia+ folks comfortably coming out, women sharing opinions fearlessly, people confronting and calling out sexual predators, and people openly discussing mental health and related topics. 
 
But recently, I feel like these two platforms have become another version of FB with young Kesavan mamas and mamies. There are a bunch of ig handles targeting women and gender minorities, bullying them, making jokes out of mental health issues, being highly misogynistic, queer/transphobic, and castiest. For instance, profiles like toxicmalluv4, hitechdeibamv4, mallumeninist etc. From what I've noticed, these are mostly 20 y/os and teenage boys influenced by people like Aswin Madappally, Dr Chromental etc., regularly creating problematic content, slut shaming women who finally gather some courage to speak about the abuse they've faced, using photos of queer folks for cyber lynching. I feel like the situation is getting out of hand and I won't be surprised if someone decides to end their life because of the bullying. It's scary to watch teenage boys turning into absolute incels.
