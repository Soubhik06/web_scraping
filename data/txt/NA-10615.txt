 It is submitted on behalf of the petitioner that he is in custody

 for about 36 days. It is further submitted money which was illegally

 withdrawn from a bank account has been refunded. Learned Additional Public Prosecutor opposes the prayer for

 bail and submits FIR was lodged by one of the victims. Investigation

 revealed the crime was committed by a gang and has interstate

 ramifications. Investigation is in progress to unravel the extent of

 conspiracy and the number of victims. We have considered the materials on record. Profile of the

 crime discloses role of an organised crime racket operating in the State

 of West Bengal and Jharkhand. They utilised data leaked from a BPO

 and defrauded the de-facto complainant. Possibility of cheating other

 individuals in similar manner cannot be ruled out. Investigation is in

 progress. Under such circumstances, we are not inclined to grant bail

 to the petitioner at this stage. The application for bail is thus rejected. 2 In view of the nature of crime having interstate ramifications

which requires specialised investigation, we direct the Additional

Director General, Criminal Investigation Department (CID) to take over

the investigation of the case. (Ajay Kumar Gupta, J.) (Joymalya Bagchi, J.)
