I Was Scammed in the Name of an IT Job in Pune — A Warning for Job Seekers from Small Cities.

Fellowminds previously called Global Technovance

Location:Fellow Minds,Ground Floor Bund Garden,Pune

Pune/Ranchi — Coming from a small city like Ranchi, where IT jobs and tech companies are nearly non-existent, I made the difficult decision to relocate to Pune in hopes of building a career in the software industry. With dreams and determination, I spent over a month applying for jobs — but with no success.

Then, one day, I received a call from a job portal. They informed me about an opening for a software tester role. Excited and hopeful, I rushed to the address they provided. However, the moment I arrived, I realized something wasn’t right.

The questions asked during the so-called interview had nothing to do with the testing role I was promised. I couldn’t answer much because the questions were irrelevant. Soon, they started pitching a “plan” — I was told that if I paid Rs. 4,000, they would guarantee interview calls from at least three companies.

There was no job description, no professionalism, and the entire setup felt shady. Still, desperate for an opportunity, I tried to give them the benefit of the doubt. They sent me to a walk-in interview, but once again, it had nothing to do with testing. They demanded an internship certificate, which I didn’t have — and I was never informed that it was mandatory.

When I called them from the interview location, they told me to return home and promised another interview within two days. That day never came.

I tried reaching out to them, but their number was switched off. I visited the same place again — only to find that the company name had changed. The employees were the same, but they denied any connection, claiming the previous company had shut down a year ago. However, the building’s security staff revealed the truth: this group frequently changes names and scams job seekers using the same methods again and again.

This experience left me disheartened, but I’m sharing my story to warn others. Many people like me, from smaller cities, move to metros with big dreams — only to be taken advantage of by fraudsters preying on our desperation.

I urge authorities to investigate such consultancies and take strict action. And to my fellow job seekers: be cautious. Always verify the legitimacy of companies and never pay for interviews or job promises without proper documentation.

If this has happened to you or someone you know, report it to the local cybercrime cell and help stop these scammers from exploiting others
