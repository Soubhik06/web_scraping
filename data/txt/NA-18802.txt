I have been using this food delivery app Foodpanda for last 10 days. They have the best of the best coupon codes, too good to be true discounts. It looked all good till the time I placed orders. Now once I placed the orders I found following issues.

1. They have few selected items where you can use coupon code like TREAT9 to get that particular item at 9 rupees. Once you order these items, restaurants cancel the order.

2. They have cash on delivery as their payment option. But on discounted items they don't accept COD. They already know it is going to be canceled.

3. It always takes more than half an hour to get any confirmation from restaurants. Also after the confirmation it is not guaranteed that your order will be delivered.

4. Their customer service sucks big time. On most of the occasion you will not get any reply. But if you are the chosen one then you might get a cookie cutter reply from them. Anyway it doesn't help at all.

5. In some cases they will accept the order and not deliver the order at all. It has been more than 12 hours and here I am waiting for my lunch. I am begging their support to cancel the order but no luck.

6. Sometimes, your order is marked delivered but you don't receive it at all.

Mind you, these are my experiences from using this app for 10 days. I guess any sane person would give up on this after that.

I am 100 % convinced that this business is a scam and their top management is all aware and involved in this. They are using these dirty tricks to just increase the traffic and fetch next round of investment on that.

So just wanted to ask. Is there anything in place right now where we can lodge a complaint against this kind of fraudulent activities?
