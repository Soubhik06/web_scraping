It's interesting that they do not address each other in any way that may incline the viewer to give the appearance that they are a mother-son duo. The lady even got a picture with none other than Fucking Barack Obama. Can't really comment on the authenticity of that photo though.

This lady was operating as a con woman in India I guess a decade ago. She had in fact been on the list of Interpol's most wanted fugitives. Source: [Hindu](http://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/Delhi/wanted-womans-photo-surfaces-with-obama/article6364335.ece)

Now, if this woman is what the article says she is, a fugitive on the run and the Ex ACP was part of this, why is it that the latest news about these people is 2 year old. Why was ACP given an easy walk off with retirement. Why was she given a bail from police. Because I'm 100% sure she was at one point under arrest of Delhi Police.

I'm not an investigator by my job. I knew this case very well because it was somewhat related to my family. I just wanted to get this out in front of an audience which might have an idea on this.
