While I was doing a UPI transaction the bank declined the payment, I thought it would be a server error or something but later realised that my bank account has been frozen. 
I called up my home branch ( Federal Bank ) to enquire about the issue, they told me it has been blocked because of some cyber fraud suspicion related to some IT sector. I’ve never been to the place they’ve mentioned neither have I done any transactions on what assumes to be IT related. I was asked to contact the cyber team in Tamil Nadu and the bank told me they cant help from their end.
Has anyone faced a similar issue? and how did you go about it?
