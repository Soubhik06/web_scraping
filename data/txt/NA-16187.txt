To my fellow Indians, good evening. I know esports isn't big or popular as traditional sports like cricket, hockey, kabaddi or football but today as an Indian esports fan I come forward to you.

The Indian Counter-Strike scene has always been looked down upon post the Forsaken cheating scandal in 2018 and we are always made the butt of the joke in the CS industry since then. Recently one tournament organiser - Skyesports - revealed their $300k tournament - the Skyesports Championship 2024, which is scheduled to take place in Mumbai with international top teams and players attending.

Today was supposed to be its first day but Skyesports, who are notorious for cancelling events last minute, postponing events, non-payments/ payments delay struck again. There were 10 games scheduled for today but only 1 took place. The event is being held at Taj Lands End and the matches were paused because on NO ELECTRICITY and INTERNET.

Skyesports has later on gone to cancel the games for today and postpone them to tomorow. Since that announcement the abuse targeting at Indian CS fans on all platforms (Twitter, HLTV) has been on a meteoric rise - with blatant racism. 

Us Indian CS fans are being taken apart because of Skyesports' incompetence.
