I was just waiting to enter in movie theater, and a guy in half pant + Tshirt + full black goggle (no offence to attire, but very unique). 
Approached me and asking for ... "Anna, 100 rupee Anna. My girlfriend is with me and I am short of money".

I sensed the scam but just wanted to be as polite as I can, 

Me: No cash money.

Him: online Anna??

Me: No money.

He roamed around 5-10 minutes, probably asked some more people and came back, showing his digital watch.

"Anna,.. this is smartwatch. How to know, how much battery lost.". Started rosting dial and trying to find out. Told him some bettery is there will work for one more day.

 He was probably thinking I will change the mind and give some money 🤑 .

Just for caution: Anything similar happens to you. Keep the distance and be alert for your wallet and valuables.
