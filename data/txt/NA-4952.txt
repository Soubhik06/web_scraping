Today the present matters have been listed under the

 heading "To be Mentioned" for grant of provisional bail to the

 petitioner, namely, Vikash Kumar @ Karu in Cr. Misc. No. 7143

 of 2025 on account of the death of his grand-father. 2. At the outset, learned Senior counsel appearing on

 behalf of the petitioner Vikash Kumar @ Karu in Cr. Misc. No.

 7143 of 2025 submits that the grand-father of the petitioner

 died on 10.03.2025 and therefore, the petitioner may be released

 on provisional bail. 3. However, in the interest of justice, this Court is of

 the opinion to consider the bail of the petitioners on merit. 4. At this stage, learned Senior Counsel appearing on

 behalf of the petitioner Vikash Kumar @ Karu in Cr. Misc. No.

 7143 of 2025 prays to withdraw the interlocutory application. 5. Permissions is accorded. Accordingly, the I.A. No.

 1 of 2025 in Cr. Misc. No. 7143 of 2025 is dismissed as

 withdrawn. 6. Heard learned counsel for the petitioners and

 learned APP for the State. 7. The petitioners seek bail in connection with Cyber

 P.S. Case No. 62 of 2024 instituted for the offences under

 Sections 303(2), 318(2), 318(4), 338, 319(2), 336(2), 336(3),
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.1406 of 2025(3) dt.21-03-2025 3/5 340(2), 111, 61(2) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 and Sections 66 , 66(B) , 66(C) , 66(D) of the I.T. Act. 8. Prosecution story, in short, is that, police busted a

 cyber fraud gang involved in online courier scams using UPI

 and QR codes and apprehended fifteen accused persons

 including the petitioners. It is further alleged that several mobile

 phones and data sheets were also seized from the accused

 persons. 9. Learned Senior Counsel appearing on behalf of the

 petitioner, namely, Avinash Kumar in Cr. Misc. No. 1406 of

 2023 and petitioner, namely, Vikash Kumar @ Karu in Cr. Misc.

 No. 7143 of 2025 submits that petitioners are innocent and have

 falsely been implicated in the present case merely on the basis

 of suspicion. Learned Senior Counsel further submits that

 general and omnibus allegation has been made against the

 petitioners. No specific overt act is alleged against the

 petitioners. Learned Senior counsel further submits that two

 mobiles phones have been recovered from each of the

 petitioners. Learned Senior counsel further contends that it is

 evident that no financial transaction was made in the account of

 the petitioners. He further submits that co-accused Gautam

 Kumar has already been granted bail by the learned counsel
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.1406 of 2025(3) dt.21-03-2025 4/5 below against whom there was evidence that financial

 transaction took place through his mobile phone. It is next

 submitted on behalf of the petitioners that the petitioners are in

 custody since 16.10.2024 and have no criminal antecedent. 10. Learned counsel for the petitioner, namely, Chhotu

 Sharma in Cr. Misc. No. 4798 of 2025 submits that petitioner is

 innocent and has falsely been implicated in the present case

 merely on the basis of suspicion. Learned counsel further

 submits that general and omnibus allegation has been made

 against the petitioner. No specific overt act is alleged against the

 petitioner. No incriminating article has been recovered from the

 conscious possession of the petitioner. Learned counsel further

 contends that it is evident that no financial transaction was made

 in the account of the petitioner. He further submits that co-

 accused Gautam Kumar has already been granted bail by the

 learned counsel below itself. It is next submitted on behalf of

 the petitioner that the petitioner is in custody since 16.10.2024

 and has no criminal antecedent. 11. Learned A.P.P. for the State has vehemently

 opposed the prayer for grant of bail to the petitioners. 12. Considering the aforesaid facts and circumstances

 of the case, there being no specific allegation against the
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.1406 of 2025(3) dt.21-03-2025 5/5 petitioners as also there being no financial transactions to

 support the allegation against them and the period of custody

 undergone by the petitioners, this Court is inclined to grant bail

 to the petitioners. 13. Let the petitioners be released on bail on

 furnishing bail bonds of Rs.10,000/- (Ten thousand) each with

 two sureties of the like amount each to the satisfaction of Court

 below/concerned Court in connection with Cyber P.S. Case No.

 62 of 2024. (Rudra Prakash Mishra, J)
