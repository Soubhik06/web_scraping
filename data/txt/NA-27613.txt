The other day I got a “phishing” call. A guy with a thick Indian accent said that he was calling from Bank of America about my credit card. He had an old BoA card number of mine, and some old fake information that I had given to some other scammer a while ago (a fake name, SSN, DoB, address, etcetera). He confirmed all of my fake information (presumably in an effort to convince me that he must really be from BoA because he knew all of my personal information), and then he informed me that he was calling me because they detected some possibly fraudulent charges on my credit card. 

He asked me if I charged $300.00 online at Walmart yesterday? 
I said yes, I bought some furniture online at Walmart for $300.00 yesterday.

He asked me if I charged $600.00 at china.com today? 
I said yes, I bought some computer supplies at china.com for $600.00 today.

He asked me if I charged $1,200 to Western Union today. 
I said yes, I sent someone $1,200 by Western Union today.

He started to ask me about a fourth charge, but I just couldn’t take it anymore. I burst out laughing. I said come on dude, you made up three fake charges, and I told you that I authorized all of them – haven’t you figured out that I am just fucking with you? 

But he persisted in asking me about the fourth charge…
