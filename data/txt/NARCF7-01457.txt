A few days ago my brother had placed an online order that turned out to be a scam when we received 2 old and dirty clothes in the delivery today. 

Maybe a week ago, my brother came across a Meta ad on instagram for Cargo pants. He selected a pair and placed an order worth 899 Rupees, making sure that it's a COD to ensure delivery (Lmao, we got scammed regardless).

Website in question: 
https://diclon.in/

 He received the package today and after opening we found 2 children's trouser sized abominations covered in dust and scum. 

We understood that it's a scam right away and went to see the returns page. It was obviously staged with autogenerated text in place of Address. 

My brother then recounted how he didn't receive any emails confirming the order except text messages. The website is not even well made. I checked the order ID on the Delhivery website and found that the package was from "EXYTE SURFACE". 

Just one Google search and the results revealed consumer court complaints that were from 2021 in relation with the company. 

So kids, here's the moral of the story:

• Always make sure that the website you're purchasing from is not OBVIOUSLY shady. 

• Always place a COD order (but this is not the end of safety checks) 

• Perform basic Google searches about the company, although the company we placed the order from and the company that sent it are not the same. In this case, cross check the details from the delivery portal.

• Consumer court website is fairly easy to use and one I used to frequently visit for my work, I can vouch for how useful it is. 

• Know your basic consumer rights and use the forum when you're wronged. (My brother is embarrassed and doesn't want to complain since there were complaints dating back to 2021)

• JUST BECAUSE YOU TAKE PRECAUTIONS DOESNT MEAN YOUR FAMILY WILL. So educate your family. 

• When in doubt, ask reddit.
