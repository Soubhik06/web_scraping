Heard counsel for the parties. The applicant has been named as accused in two
crimes bearing Crime No.360/2012 registered with Police
Station - Kohe Fiza and Crime No.539/13 registered with
Police Station - S.T.F. Bhopal - commonly known as
VYAPAM Examination Scam cases - for the offence
punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 120-B of
IPC; Section 65 and 66 of the I. T. Act and also under Section 3 (d) 1 , 2 / 4 of the M.P. Manyata Prapt Pariksha
Adhiniyam, 1937. These bail applications have been filed in both the
crimes. The role ascribed to the applicant by the prosecution in
Crime No.360/2012 is that of an "impersonator" and in
Crime No.539/2013 is that of a "scorer". Learned counsel for the State, in all fairness, submits that
the investigation against the applicant in Crime No.539/2013 is
complete in all respect and custodial interrogation of the ---2--- applicant may not be necessary in that crime. Even with regard
to Crime No.360/2012 it is fairly accepted that the investigation
is otherwise complete except the report of handwriting expert is
awaited. The co-accused linked to the applicant in both the
crimes have also been arrested. In other words, continued
custody of the applicant may not be necessary in connection
with these two crimes as of now. The applicant is not suspected
to have committed any other offence of similar type. Besides, the applicant through counsel undertakes to
appear before the Investigating Officer as and when called for
further investigation including against him as also to punctually
appear before the trial Court to ensure early disposal of the
criminal trial. In the interest of justice, taking overall view of the
matter, we deem it appropriate to grant bail to the applicant
on following strict conditions:- The bail applications stand disposed of accordingly.
