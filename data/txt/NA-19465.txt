Hi folks! We're launching a new, free digital security newsletter! **#CyberSecCharcha** will sum up key security updates in India and internationally and deliver them to your inbox once a month. 

In the first edition, IFF's Digital Security Manager Shivani Singh covers recent data breaches that have accessed the personal data of millions of Indian users, global trends surrounding stolen data, and how to regain control if your data has been leaked in a breach. The goal of this newsletter is to open a conversation about the digital security crisis in India and understand why this is an important issue to actively start engaging with. Whether you're a student, beginner, security enthusiast, or tech expert, **#CyberCharcha** is for you!

[**Read edition 1, Breach pe Breach, and sign up here.**](https://internetfreedom.in/launching-cybersec-charcha/)
