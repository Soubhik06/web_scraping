So we all know that taxis and rickshaws are involved in scams in India. What alarms me more is that essential services like real estate, doctor's offices, food delivery, supermarket water bottles, pharmacy medicine, and cartons of eggs are also scams. 

You can find counterfeit water bottles even in a "posh" grocery store. WATER. Some people go out of their way to fake a carton of eggs. EGGS. You might accidentally buy phony medicine at a pharmacy. Even if you go to a hospital in India, you must be vigilant about scams and sexual violence. Can't even trust a hospital, which is supposed to be a bearer of quality care, especially during tough times? 

Somebody, please tell me that these are generalizations and that you don't encounter these things often. Is it just that it seems terrible only because people don’t post about the times that they don’t experience scams?

If not, how can there be such a significant lack of regulation on every corner? How do you navigate it? Is it just second nature at this point?

Why does the capital city have such a bad safety reputation? You would think that the city that represents the government of India would meet high standards. I am sorry for the people who experience it, especially the worst of it. 

India still has my heart for other reasons, but I had to say something about the lack of regulation.
