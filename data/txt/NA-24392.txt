so today around 2, a delivery guy showed up to my place. He had a parcel, which had my adress and my moms name written on it so i assumed it was ours (obviously) i asked her if she had ordered anything and she said no. The delivery guy asked us if the details were right and we said yes, and he then suggested to open it. We opened it and saw a burkha inside (we're a hindu family) obviously we hadnt ordered it. We then asked the delivery guy which company he delivered from to which he said shadowfax (delivery agent, not app like amazon flipkart etc.) anyway we asked him to call his supervisor to which he said why should he. He then insisted we pay the amount (800ish) since we've opened it now.. like?? we started arguing and called our guards and escorted him out. But this is scary cuz 90% of the time my grandparents are alone at home and they definitely wouldve ended up paying him.. how the guards even let him in our apartment is something we're investigating since we didnt approve his entry but scams like these are seeming to become more rampant.. please be aware 

TLDR ; delivery boy delivers burkha to hindu household with correct user info, then asks to open the package and demands payment.
