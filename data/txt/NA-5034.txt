(Made by the Hon'ble Chief Justice)
 We had heard the matter yesterday (ie., on 01.08.2023). We had

 kept the matter today for learned State Government Pleader to point

 out the provisions of the Tamil Nadu Government Servants' Conduct

 Rules, 1973. 2. The petitioner seeks directions that the Government Servants,

 such as Tamil Nadu Government aided Public Servants, Quasi

 Government Servants, Public Sector Servants should submit reports of

 details of their assets each year to their respective Heads of

 Departments, who ought to publish the details through Internet /

 online. https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis W.P.No.14720 of 2017 3. Learned State Government Pleader pointed out Rule 7 of the

 Tamil Nadu Government Servants' Conduct Rules, 1973. Under the

 said Rule, a Government Servant has to seek prior sanction for

 purchase, sale or entering into transaction of an immovable property. Further, Rule 7(3) requires the Government Servant to submit return

 of his assets and liabilities and thereafter, every five years, he has to

 give return of his assets and his liabilities to his employer. The said

 return is required to be given of all immovable and movable properties,

 shares, debentures, cash, including bank deposits and also debts and

 other liabilities. 4. The Rule is in place. As far as furnishing the details online is

 concerned, it is for the State to consider the same. The State

 Government will have to take a policy decision in that regard. https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis W.P.No.14720 of 2017 5. In the light of the above, the writ petition stands disposed of. There will be no order as to costs. The Union of India, Ministry of Finance
