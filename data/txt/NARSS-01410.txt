Here are few scams that i had come across recently.

1. Club Resorto / Club Mahendra scam : These guys get your details from malls , car dealerships etc and calls you up and say that you have a free gift of complementary stay in a reputed hotel and some PVR tickets. When you go to collect the gift at the said location, they use pressure selling tactics to buy holiday packages worth lakhs of rupees. 

2. Customs scam : you get a call from customs saying that your parcel is stuck and in the call they threaten you that they have found something illegal in the parcel and you are liable to pay some customs duty on this parcel.

3. Money doubling scam : They claim to provide 6 ~ 8% return every month on the investment made. They use some big financial terms to lure customers who are attracted by ROI. 

4. Unicef : This might be a legit scam and some might not like it to be called a scam. But what they do is target people in malls and popular hotspots in city, they start by saying are you interested in an activity and then use clever and passionate narrating to try and get a monthly contribution to Unicef.
