People call you telling your parcel didn't get delivered, then they tell you police seized at airport containing illegal stuff. 

Meanwhile you, the person hearing the phone call, haven't booked any such parcel from some arbitrary famous place in India to some enemy country or something.

Do avoid them if you find yourself in such a case.
