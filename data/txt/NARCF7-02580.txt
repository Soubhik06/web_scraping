Living in Nagpur I've always felt that this is a safe city but the data says otherwise, is it the higher reporting rate or is the city really unsafe? Is it just a few localities perhaps? 

* Nagpur's 82.7 crime rate in theft cases is the highest in the state, surpassing Mumbai (33.9) and Pune (31.0). 
* In the crime rate of automobile thefts too, Nagpur (47.0) is the highest in the state, followed by Pune (19.3) and Mumbai (15.2). 
* Nagpur's rate of burglary (27.5) is also more than Mumbai (8.9) and Pune (6.8) 
* Crime rate: Nagpur 7th in the country, 1st in State 
* Nagpur, Patna have highest murder rate in country: NCRB data
