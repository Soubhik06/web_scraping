This Criminal Petition is filed by the petitioner - accused No.2

seeking to quash the proceedings in FIR No.1786 of 2025 on the file

of P.S. Cyber Crime Cyberabad, registered for the offences under

Sections 189, 308, 318(40, 319(2), 338, 336(3), 340(2), 356 read

with 3(5) of The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (for short "BNS,

2023") and Sections 66-C, 66-D of ITA-2000-2008. 2. Heard the submissions of Ms. A. Sneha Vardhani, learned

counsel for the petitioner and Sri Jithender Rao Veeramalla, learned

Additional Public Prosecutor for respondent No.1 - State. 3. The learned counsel for the petitioner has submitted that the

petitioner herein is innocent and is not involved in any such offence

as alleged by the prosecution. He further submitted that he returned

from U.K very recently and that his sister was pregnant and so he

was at his village, assisting his sister and that he was not all present

at the scene of offence. His contention is that accused No.2 has

given Villa to his friend for accommodation and that he is not aware

about the operation carried on by his friend at his given address. He 2 ETD,J

further submitted that he is not arrayed as an accused in FIR, but in

Remand Case Diary, he got implicated based on the confession of

co-accused, which is not just and proper. He therefore, prayed to

quash the proceedings against the petitioner. 4. The learned Additional Public Prosecutor has submitted that

the allegations are of serious nature involving cyber crime aimed at

deceiving innocent customers. He further submitted that petitioner

herein is accused No.2 and is a tenant of the premises in which the

company is run committing the cyber frauds. He therefore, prayed to

dismiss the petition. 5. Perused the record. 6. The case of the prosecution is that accused Nos.2 to 12 were

Daily Wage Earners and struggling to meet their personal and family

needs and they were in dire necessity of money. Under those

circumstances, they came into contact with accused No.1, resident

of Kolkatta, who was already engaged in Organized Cyber Fraud

Activities and offered accused Nos.2 to 12 lucrative roles in his fraud

operation and promised them a salary of Rs.15,000/- per month with

a 10% commission on the money extracted from victims. He further

promised to provide free accommodation and travel expenses. Thus 3 ETD,J

accused Nos.2 to 12 agreed to join the cyber crime operations.

Accordingly, accused No.1 has arranged their travel to Hyderabad

and housed them at Villa in Bachupally and trained them for about

20 days in executing cyber frauds, primarily targeting U.S citizens

and it was carried out in an organized manner as one team used to

send Phishing emails, Mimicking Paypal or Greek Squad about fake

transactions, while another team, including accused Nos.2 to 12

received inbound calls from victims. These calls were handled using

VOIP-based apps. Once the victims were convinced, the accused

used remote access tools like Anydesk and collected bank account

details, transaction history and final data. This information was

handed over to accused No.1, who then handled the final phase of

extortion. Thus, they are charged with the offences under Sections

189, 308, 318(40, 319(2), 338, 336(3), 340(2), 356 read with 3(5) of

the BNS. Thus, the record points out serious allegations against the

petitioners alleging commission of cyber crime luring the innocent

victims and extracting amounts from them. The investigation is still in

progress. Thus, the fact finding process is still going on, hence, the

petition lacks merits. 7. In the result, the Criminal Petition is dismissed. 4 ETD,J
