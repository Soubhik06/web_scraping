Police Station, District - Deputy Commissioner (crime),

Commissionerate Jodhpur for offences under Sections 420 , 406 & 120-B of the IPC and Sections 66C , 66D of the IT Act. Heard learned counsel for the petitioners and the learned

Public Prosecutor. Perused the material available on record as well

petitioners submitted that whole story of the prosecution is
