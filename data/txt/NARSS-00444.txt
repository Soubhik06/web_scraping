I Request you to read this.

I’m not here for sympathy or advice. I don’t even know why I’m typing this. Maybe I’m hoping someone real reads this. Maybe I just want to let it out before I completely shut down. I’ve carried this shit for too long, and I’m tired. This is raw, unfiltered, and yeah, long as hell. But it’s real. So if you’re here, thanks for reading.

I was always the innocent, dumb kid. Class clown but not the funny type—more like the clueless one. In 6th grade, I was shifted to Section C. Lost touch with the only real friend I had, a girl from my old section. Then came someone new—let’s call her Rashi. Started off as friends, became best friends, then my girlfriend. I loved her like she was the only person left in the world for me. And in some ways, she was.

But our “relationship” was chaos. She broke up with me 17 times. Yeah, I counted. Each time crushed me. Still, I stayed. Not because I was strong. Because I had nothing else.

In school, I once took the blame for our relationship to save her from getting her parents called. That got me a black mark on my profile. Seniors beat me. My parents didn’t believe in me. I was always the kid who got blamed. My brother and I don’t even live together—we see each other on occasions. We’re not close. I thought my grandparents cared, but even that turned out fake with time.

I’m from a lower caste. I’ve had people point that out and use it to mock me. In 6th grade, my good friend’s parents insulted me for it. In 10th, a teacher did the same. It didn’t stop there. In college, I still get called out for my caste. Still get excluded. Still get treated like an outsider.

And yeah, I’ve made money—$10K to $15K—from the age of 15 to 18 through hustles and online work. But it didn’t feel like success. It burned me out. It made me hate working. Now I chase dopamine—a game, a new business idea, a quick hit. Then it fades. Every. Damn. Time.

I’ve skipped college for months. I don’t even have a room of my own. I live in my mother’s house, where there are constant fights. One day, my mom’s mother told me to move out. I’ve seen people use me for money, emotionally drain me, and leave. Friends I helped financially, emotionally—they vanished. They owed me money, and I didn’t even have the balls to ask them for it.

I tried therapy. The psychologist told me to write my emotions, never followed up. Just took her fee and dipped. That made me feel like even the help I sought wasn’t real. Another scam.

I’ve done shit too. Once scammed someone online—not proud of it. I regret it. But I know I’m not a bad person. I feel pain when I see others in pain. I help strangers. I’ve taken bullets for people who never remembered me.

I once lost ₹30K of my dad’s money on games using his credit card. I was a kid, just 11 or 12. I tried to make that money back. Found a guy online who promised to help. Paid him. Got scammed. I cried that night. My own brother laughed. My relatives mocked me. That night, a part of me died.

I got attached to a Muslim girl once in school. My grandparents mocked me for it. She left too.

Now I sit here, 18, in college, with 13% attendance. Can’t take my exam unless I beg the head supervisor. I’m embarrassed. I’m angry. I’m tired. I can’t focus. Can’t sleep. I overthink. I feel dumb. I compare myself to everyone. I’ve thought about dying—but never did anything because I know it’s not a solution. But it still comes back like a whisper at night.

I wanted to write a book. Share everything. But I stopped. Because what if people say it’s fake? What if they mock me again?

If you think this post is fake or I’m looking for attention—fine. I get it. But for me, this took months to type out. I’m a man. We’re not supposed to cry. But I’ve cried. Alone. Quietly. Often. i want to die but I can't cause my mom is soft hearted and I know she will lose herself if I do something to myself I know this is not a solution but I don't find a way either I've stopped myself a lot of time.

My name’s not really Pranav. I’m not sharing my real name because I don’t want people from my past to find this and make fun of me. This is not a cry for help. This is me… just breathing. Just surviving.
Wish I had a sister to share cause my brother never understood.
If you think this is fake i am sorry but this is all real this is what I've been facing and feeling

And if you’ve read this far, thank you. You saw me. Even if just for a second.
