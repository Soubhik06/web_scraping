Petrol pump scam at Bharat Petroleum Karve Road (near Karishma): 

Staff will directly start filling power/speed petrol (whatever that shit is) without taking your permission first. On saying no to speed/power petrol, he will stop and switch to normal petrol and start the meter from where he left off earlier, and in between he will trick you with the amount filled (In reality didn't even start filling the speed petrol). Later if confronted about it, he will show you the receipt with correct numbers on that small device in his hand. 

My cousin who is new to Pune got scammed here today. 

I thought this petrol pump was good and genuine, all others in Kothrud are fraud anyways. Don't know which one to trust anymore.
