These guys collect your phone number etc. From data breaches like big basket or purchase your data from data brokers at 10 paisa/person. They even target people at petrol pumps.

Then they call you (couples only) at a reputed 4/5 star hotel, saying ek presentation hai fir free dinner / ya gift vouchers denge. and then they offer their holiday resort , time sharing vacation scheme.

They offer huge discounts only if you buy their plans at that time n place only. Basically creating fear and urgency that the offer will go away if you come back tomorrow. These offers are mostly useless or scam or MLM schemes. 

Even popular ones like club Mahindra is not useful, they have offered memberships to Lacs of people and have limited resorts, that the resort you wish to stay is not available on your dates.

Tldr - Do not go to hotels for dinner, gift vouchers etc. they are scams.
