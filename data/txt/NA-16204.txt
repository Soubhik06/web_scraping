So my mother ordered a saree from a site called Missfasion, it got delivered this morning - except it wasn't the saree she ordered. So we found a customer care number on the internet (which I later discovered is a scam number), 8388066116.

When she called this number, the person promised a refund and that someone will pick up the package the next day. At this point I went to the bathroom, and when I had come out the scam had already happened and 5000Rs were taken out from her PNB account. The guy jad apparently sent a message to her number and asked to forward some details to anothet number, 9004676782 (found out this was also a fraud, courtesy of the internet).

The way this scam worked was that they askee my mother to add the last 6 digits of her bank account number and UPI password number and send it to them, which she did.

Now I would like everyone to share this number and trick as much as they can; I recall reading about this trick to scam people before but it is apparently not as well known.
