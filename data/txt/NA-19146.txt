There is a new WhatsApp scam that is going on currently. My friend got scammed and lost Rs. 1.8 lakhs. 
Here's how the scammers work: 
1. They somehow get access to a person's WhatsApp contacts and some of their profile pictures. 
2. Then they create a WhatsApp account and use that person's profile picture as their dp. 
3. After this, they contact the person's friends on WhatsApp and impersonate him/her and ask for money that they need urgently. 

My question is, how do these scammers get access to another person's frequently contacted contacts. Getting a dp can be easy, I get it, but how is it that they get the contact list of another person. 

Please be vigilant. Always cross-check the UPI ID and name, before sending someone money. 

Thanks.
