Fiend tied up into a scam and we're trying to undo it for him. He was sent a phoney paypal email about sunauthorized transactions, he called, they told him oh yeah you've had a bunch of fraudulent transactions on your account from Williams sonoma and oh coinbase too. Indian tech support guy supposedly.

They then explain (from his memory) that he needs to make a coinbase account and transfer $35,000 to it to "prove" who he is. Then wait 10 days and it'll be returned. Oh and Paypal will pay a 6% "inconvenience fee" for his trouble. Of course, teamviewer was involved during the process but I'm not sure why other than to "guide him through the process" -- but that's one of the things that has me curious.

Anyhow, he changed his passwords. His bank doesn't see a coinbase transaction yet, but his coinbase account does have the $35k. No word from scammer since he accomplished the transfer.

My worry is, I don't know what the end game was to get the money. A million things are possible, so, anyone seen this particular version of scam and know how it plays out?
