I got a call from a woman who was claiming that my current phone number used to belong to her, and that it was linked to her instagram account. She was insisting that I help her in getting her account back, and that she would be sending a link/verification code that I would have to share with her. 

I actually found it quite suspicious and I looked up online and a scam like this existed, in which the receiver's account gets hacked. I ignored it and moved on. 

I again started getting a call from the same number after a month or so and they even shared some id proofs with me (probably fake), but since this is a common scam now, I checked with my service provider if my number, which I have had for over a year now, had any previous ownership since I had asked for a new number with no previous ownership, and turns out it didn't have any previous ownership. 

I told the scammer that I'll be calling the police if I got a call from them again, all the id proof and other stuff got deleted from their side that very second. 

Take care guys, I'm sharing this here since our social presence is so important to us nowadays. &lt;3
