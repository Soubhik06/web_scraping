This Writ Petition is filed for a Mandamus, to direct the 1st respondent

 conducting enquiry, to cancel the settlement deed in document No.18537 of

 initiate proceedings under Sections 81 and 82 of the Registration Act. In

 fact, it has been explained to this Court that impersonation and fraud that has

 been played to such an extent that the thumb impression that has been given

 in the AADHAAR Card (Unique Identification Authority of India) itself has
