This company reached out to me and after reading about them on the mail thread, I asked the person — “ Jennifer Aniston” — to jump on a Zoom call to answer a few questions.

First, her camera doesn’t work and she claims management doesn’t allow them to use their camera.

Second, and most inline with this thread, she’s from **India**. The company claims to be from California, but she couldn’t even tell me the correct time.

Third, I asked Jennifer to share her magazine’s stats. She proceeded to send me a bunch of doctored images from Google Analytics (website stats, not magazine stats).

And finally, when I said “Hey, this is a scam. There’s no way I would pay you $2,500 based on this conversation,” she threw out some expletives and quickly exited the call.

It’s a scam. Don’t waste your time or money. Also here is some reviews of this scammers: [https://issuu.com/stellamvarela/docs/scam\_issue.docx](https://issuu.com/stellamvarela/docs/scam_issue.docx)
