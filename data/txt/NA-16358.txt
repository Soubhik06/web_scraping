Seriously, I'm beyond frustrated with JioMart.

I've been giving them the benefit of the doubt for months, ordering groceries repeatedly, but it's just gotten worse. Their whole "no hidden fees, no delivery fees" thing? A complete and utter lie.
Here's the rundown of my JioMart hell:

• Rotten Produce and Expired Goods: It's clear they're just dumping near-expiry stock from Reliance SMART Points. Everything is wilted, bruised, or about to expire. I'm basically paying for garbage.

• Phantom Deliveries and Missing Items:
Half my orders are missing items, and they just mark them as "returned" without telling me. No refund, no explanation. Just gone.

• Non-existent Inventory: Forget anything remotely fancy. Even basic stuff like capsicum and brinjal are constantly out of stock. What's the point of an online grocery service if you can't even get the basics?

• The "No Fees" Lie: The hidden fee is the time and money wasted on dealing with their terrible service and inedible products.

• Customer Service is a Joke: Trying to get help from them is like pulling teeth. They just ignore emails and complaints.

I've finally given up and switched to Blinkit and Zepto. Yeah, they charge a small fee, but at least I get fresh groceries and reliable 
deliveries. The difference is night and day.

Seriously, avoid JioMart like the plague. You're better off going to the store yourself.

TL;DR: JioMart is a scam. They sell expired food, mess up orders, and their "no fees" promise is a joke. Just use Blinkit or Zepto.
