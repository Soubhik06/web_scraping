# The Ticker

Ticker is a collection of interesting security news and happenings in Indian companies and startups.

- Hacks continue at Unocoin (An Indian Bitcoin focused company) [[Unocoin](https://twitter.com/Unocoin/status/896377051502006272)]

*Ed Note:* Indian bitcoin companies continue to be under attack from hackers and continue to be unreliable for cryptocurrency enthusiasts. Earlier as well there were hacks at other BTC companies, including Unocoin itself a week ago. This time the hackers got into the [sendgrid account](https://twitter.com/shantanugoel/status/896396165494611969) and were able to send a fake email to all Unocoin customers. It seems as around unocoin customers were duped for atleast &gt; 4 BTC (~10-12 lakh INR) going by the transaction numbers seen on the fake ICO site. We tried to get info from Unocoin but they didn't do a good job of opening up the attack/issue to the public and [passed the buck onto sendgrid](https://twitter.com/Unocoin/status/896665736281706496). If you are interested in investing in cryptocurrencies, make sure you do NOT leave any of your coins in the online wallets of these services and move them to offline wallets soon after buying.

- Reliance Jio HACKED!!! All customer information (name, address, CDRs, etc) already sold!! (Original headline of article. Please see Ed Note below for details.) [[Tumblr](https://aanonindia.tumblr.com/post/164044377694/reliance-jio-hacked-all-customer-information)]

*Ed Note:* I came across this post (supposedly by Anon India) last week. It claims to have hacked Jio and unearthed a cache of APKs, each of which has the subsriber ID hardcoded. I warned folks against using it because it wasn't apparent whether this is malware or not. Since then, I did more analysis on the apks in the downloads and established that they do have a hardcoded subsriber ID but it is not apparent whether they are real or not. I do not have a JIO sim to analyze this further but if anyone is trying, please do it only if you know what you are doing. There could be adverse implications for you with this in any case whether it is right or wrong. Alleged Anon India spokesmen indicated that this can be used to reveal all data about a customer but I wasn't able to validate this and they didn't provide further proof as well.
