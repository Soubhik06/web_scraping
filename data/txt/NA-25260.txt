Went to this mall with my friend and we were just chatting together, and this random guy approached us and asked me to talk to his brother. This was the conversation:

---

Unknown man (hands phone):
"Yeh lo, mera bhai hai call pe, usko baat karni hai aap se."

Me:
"Aap kaun ho?"

My friend:
"Hum aapko nahi jaante."

Unknown man:
"Arey ek baar baat toh kar lo. Mera hi bhai hai."

Me:
"Nahi, main jaanti nahi hoon mujhe nahi karni baat usse."

Unknown man:
"Arey, mujhe pata hai aap jaante nahi ho par mera hi bhai hai."

*Me (thinking):
As if hum aapke bhai ya aapko jaante hain... wtf*

Me:
"Sorry, nahi." 

My friend: "nahi, please."

Unknown man:
"Mera hi bhai hai. Bas ek minute baat kar lo. Aap mujhe ya usko nahi jaante ho, mujhe pata hai. Par ek baar baat toh kar lo."

Me:
"Nahi. Main aise kisi se baat nahi kar sakti. Please jao."

---

He then stood at the gate of the cafe we were in and kept talking to his “bhai” for another 30 seconds, and then left. We were obviously very creeped out and scared, so we went outside a min later and recorded a video of him just in case. Here’s a screenshot from the video (him still talking to his “bhai”).

Is this some scam going around, some very stupid prank, or what do you think he could’ve wanted? Because it was just so creepy, especially with how adamant he was.
