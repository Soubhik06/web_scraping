A [recent news](https://www.indiatoday.in/cities/chennai/story/jamtaara-style-attack-dmk-mp-dayanidhi-maran-loses-rs-99999-cyber-fraud-2446972-2023-10-10) about spouse of a Member of Parliament who had been a minister of Information Technology was themselves defrauded by phoney scamsters pretending to be from bank.
