Growing Demand For Cyber Security Professionals in the USA
According to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, cybersecurity roles are projected to grow by 32% between 2022 and 2032, much faster than the average for all occupations.
The USA faces a shortage of qualified cybersecurity professionals.
The threat of cyberattack is on the increase as companies transition to cloud computing, involve AI, and place huge amounts of sensitive information on the internet. The data on the finance, healthcare, defense, and technology industries requires trained professionals to defend their systems.

Industry-Aligned Job Roles
Industry-Aligned Job Roles are jobs that are created to suit the reality of companies in the job market. The cyber security roles of US firms are organized and based on actual business requirements. Positions such as SOC Analyst, Penetration Tester, Cyber Security Analyst, or Cloud Security Engineer are associated with well-defined tasks and resources. A skill-based course in cyber security helps Indian students with these industry-aligned jobs, eliminating any skill gaps and enhancing job potential. Students will be able to start their careers in the right way by specifically learning what the industry requires. It reduces confusion at work and helps them to work efficiently.
