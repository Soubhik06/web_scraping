# PLEASE READ THE FULL POST

I am a daily metro commuter and never had experienced this until very recent. I was in goraguntepalya metro when a guy approached me and asked me for some money ( 50rs) saying he lost his wallet and needed money to go home. Me ( foolish and kind ) straightaway gave him 50rs and he went away. I paid him through online so I got his number from his upi id and called. 
Guess what - Incoming calls were barred for that number. That's when I realised I had been scammed.

 
So fast forward to today near indiranagar metro station, me and my friends were hanging around when a random guy stopped us and asked for some money. But here's the catch, he repeated the SAME EXACT WORDS WHICH WERE TOLD BY THE 1ST SCAMMER. So I immediately told my friend to say no and go away.

And today at 3pm when I was in Srirampura metro. ANOTHER guy came and REPEATED THE SAME WORDS. 
 
**Here's what these people ask:** 
**" sir kannada barutta? " ( do you know kannada?)** 
**" sir nand wallet kalkond idini swalpa dudd kodtira oorige hogbeku, nimge wapas kasltini"** 
**( I lost my wallet and need to go to my town can you send some money, ill return it back"**

THEY OFC WONT RETURN.

I straightaway started question the 3rd scammer I met, to which he ran away giving some reason.

I just wanted to spread awareness to all the people of this ongoing scam and please please share this with your friends and family. 
The thing which I hate is that - because of this lofers , the real needy ones won't get help from the public.
