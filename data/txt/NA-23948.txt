--- POST TEXT ---

I recently almost got scammed out of Rs 10k through a phone call. The caller was a lady who mentioned that my CC reward points had expired and they wanted to convert it to INR and add it to my CC account. However, during the phone call, she asked for my CC number, CVV and expiry date and said that there would be an SMS which would be triggered and I had to tell her the OTP. Fortunately, I was in my senses and could catch the scam. Sharing here so that others are aware of it.
