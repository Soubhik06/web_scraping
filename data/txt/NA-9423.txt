The petitioner is a retired employee of the Indian Air Force. After retirement his Bank

Account was frozen by the CBI allegedly on the ground that the petitioner after retirement joined

a Company as Director and the said Company was running Ponzi Schemes. The petitioner was

arrested and he is in jail custody till date. The petitioner has filed the writ petition praying for a direction upon the Central Bureau of

Investigation to de-freeze his Bank Account. The learned advocate appearing on behalf of the Central Bureau of Investigation, upon

instruction, submits that the CBI does not have any objection to the prayer of the petitioner for

de-freezing his Savings Bank Account No. 22209824780 with the Allahabad Bank, Nasra Branch,

Rranaghat, Nadia. In view of such submission made on behalf of the Central Bureau of Investigation, no

useful purpose will be served by keeping this writ petition pending. The same is accordingly

disposed of by directing the CBI to de-freeze the aforesaid Savings Bank Account of the petitioner

at once. W.P. No. 13793 (W) of 2019 is disposed of. The written instructions given by the Superintendent of Police, CBI through his learned

advocate is retained with the records. Urgent photostat certified copy of this order be supplied to the parties, if applied for, as

early as possible. (Amrita Sinha, J.)
