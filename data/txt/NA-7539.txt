Heard counsel for the parties. These applications can be disposed of by this common
order. MCRC No.18108/2014 is filed by Manjeet Singh alias
Multan Singh Gujar. The role ascribed to this applicant by
the prosecution is that he impersonated Malkhan Singh
Gujar-applicant in MCRC No.20714/2014. Both of them
have been booked in Crime No.16/2014 registered with
S.T.F. Police Station Bhopal for the offences punishable
under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 and 120-B of I.P.C.
and also under Section 3 (D) 1 and 2 / 4 of the Madhya
Pradesh Manyata Prapt Pariksha Adhiniyam, 1937 -
commonly known as VYAPAM examination scam cases. It is not in dispute that charge-sheet has been filed
against both the applicants. Further, Babu Singh with whom
Malkhan Singh Gujar had interacted and incidentally
happens to be his brother, has also been arrested on
17.12.2014. No doubt, further investigation against Babu
 |2

Singh may be in progress, however, the applicants whose
role is only that of impersonator and beneficiary
respectively; coupled with the fact that custodial
interrogation of these applicants may not be necessary any
more as of now as accepted by the counsel for the State and
the applicants through counsel have assured to extend full
cooperation to the Investigating Officer for further
investigation including against them. Similarly, they have
assured the Court that they will punctually appear before the
Trial Court for facilitating early disposal of the trial. Taking over all view of the matter, therefore, in the
interest of justice, we deem it appropriate to grant bail to the
applicants on following strict conditions to be observed by
them during the trial and till further order:- Both these bail applications stand disposed of
