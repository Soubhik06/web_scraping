I order out quite a lot because _*reasons*_ and idk how long this has been there for but these 'Packaging charges' are complete bullshit, they end up charging ₹50-100 for this.

Now in some cases I can understand to an extent. Truffles for example sends stuff in a nice paper bag, if you order a drink they'll put it in this stabiliser thingy and in general their packaging is nice. It's also never been too expensive, the most I've paid is ₹50-60 for a large order. Is it still more than what I feel it should be? Yes, but it doesn't necessarily feel like you're being swindled. 

Some places OTOH are bullshit, and the most egregious I find to be places like McDonald's and KFC - it feels like their minimum charge itself is ₹50. And for what? Their packaging is hardly reusable as it's so flimsy and doesn't even keep the heat in. And if you go to these places and get takeaway they use the same packaging for very cheap if not zero cost. 

Delivery platforms in general are becoming quite annoying to use - the reduction of free delivery radius, clubbing of multiple orders* (so your food comes cold and late), charging or random bullshit fees, promoting and propping up fake cloud kitchens serving multiple brands and not verifying their quality etc. 

*I would be fine with this if it was at least done for some eco-friendliness reason, but that's not the case and their algorithms are shit so they end up clubbing orders together that are supposed to be delivered to two widely distant places.
