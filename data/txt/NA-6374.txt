Therefore, considering the totality of circumstances, the serious
nature of the allegations against the applicant, the potential impact on
witnesses and the course of investigation if the applicant is released, and the
overarching public interest in ensuring that corruption in public service
delivery is addressed with due seriousness, this Court is not satisfied that a
case for grant of bail is made out at this stage. Accordingly, without expressing any final opinion on the merits
of the case, the bail application hereby stands rejected. (Ashish Naithani J.)
