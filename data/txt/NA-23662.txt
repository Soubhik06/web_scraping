My cousin was looking for jobs and recently found a job through one of those Whatsapp HRs. It was a data entry job where she would be paid for filling out forms. They sent her an offer letter and got her to fill out some 1000 forms over 10 days.

Once she submitted her forms, they said that the forms were failing QC and she had to pay for the losses.

They emailed her first and then they have been harassing her on Whatsapp. They initially asked for Rs.4000 and if she doesn't pay that up, she would have to pay Rs.25000 as a legal fee.

She was stressed and she called me. I went through all the communication and everything about that job looked like a scam. They don't have a website, all their emails are from Gmail. The offer letter and her job instruction emails, none of it looks professional.

So I told her not to worry and to block all communication with them. I will be reporting them to the cyber police.

Company name: Clexis Data Services
