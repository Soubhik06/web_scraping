Today while returning from office 4 women came next to my car at Birla Mandir Circle during Red Signal and started knocking my window.
1 of them was looking pregnent and the lady who knocked my window told me that she is in Labour pain and delivery can happen at any moment. That Pregnant Lady was visibly in Pain and all they were asking for some money so that they can arrange an auto for her till SMS Hospital.

I immediately felt bad for them as I was watching such scenario for the first time and I gave them 50rs cash (Which All I have in my purse at that time)

That was the moment of realisation for me that I was scammed

That lady was disappointed at my contribution in her Scam and said we need minimum 300rs.
I asked her to return my money as SMS is barely 1-1.5km from there and E rikshaw generally charges 40-50rs only.
But she took the money and left for another car.

After that I crossed the signal and parked my car and stand there for sometime to see their modus operandi.

So basically these ladies were extorting money through Guilt and emotional blackmail and keep knocking Car windows.

I felt bad that in future if anyone is in genuine need of money in such scenarios I will hesitate to help.
