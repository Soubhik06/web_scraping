Of late the calls pretending to be fake DHL/Fedex/Customs/NCB are on the rise. The scammers are trying to set up a vritual fake environment which almost resembles a government interrogation room setup including all forged papers and documents to startle the victims making them to succumb to their demands. 

Our Law Enforcement and Cybercrime team needs highest amount of cooperation and solidarity from citizens to be watchful of such pranks. Even if an iota of doubt regarding such calls arise immediately call 100/112 (whichever is the applicable and reachable emergency numbers in the area).

One success story for the criminals is a huge motivation to prey upon more citizens and a setback/speedbreaker for Law Enforcement (by adding more load and backlog to the existing crimes). Hence it is our collective responsibility to exercise ***heightened amount of vigil*** against scams and scammers.
