It happened with me today. I travel a lot and usually book hotels by calling them directly as I've found that sites like MakeMyTrip charge you extra, and making booking through hotels directly can save you money.

I use Google reviews and information on Google profile to get contact details of the hotel (you already know where it's going). I fell for a scam today where someone had changed Google Maps profile of one such hotel. They have updated the website to their fake site, and have change phone number to their own. 

I called the number, the guy told me that hotel would cost 1500 and would need advance to confirm the booking. I sent him 500. He then called and apologized, and said he'll need full amount. Since it was only a matter of 1000 more, I didn't think twice and it didn't ring alarm bells. 

He called one more time and said, sir we'll need the amount in one transaction otherwise system won't take it as one booking. This sounded fishy, so I asked him to refund the original amount first. 

This is when he started making excuses, and when I pressed him, he straight up started abusing. 

I've now called bank, they raised a dispute, will call cyber crime tomorrow to formally file a complaint, and have gotten him blocked on gpay. I know it's a small amount, but I don't want anyone else to be scammed.

Stay alert, don't trust phone numbers on Google Maps profile.

Tldr: trusted information on fake hotel site and Google maps profle, sent money to scammer for hotel booking, got duped.
