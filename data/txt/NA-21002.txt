--- POST TEXT ---

Disclaimer: I understand that harassment is bad to any and all women, but hear me out on this.

As I see the #MeToo movement now, it appears like it's limited to young urban woman that have the privilege of being able to use internet and understand English to use Twitter. That is good, more power to them, but I do not believe that they represent a major portion of women in India, which does not use internet anywhere close to understanding what Twitter is. 

Is anything being done to have more rural woman, who arguable face more misogyny than the privileged urban woman chime in on this. Is it really a movement if it ignores 95% of the woman in our country?

Right now I see a quasi-westernized movement that makes a LOT of sense in USA where most women have access to Twitter and understand the language, not so in a country like India. I would love to see this "movement" not ignore a major portion of the affected only because they were too poor to have internet. Remember that these women have their own stories to tell, but no one is listening to them, and I frankly do not know if they will ever end up getting a platform that isn't "Twitter", I hope they do and I hope urban women (and men) acknowledge their privileges and ask more women that have never been given a platform to come forward.

I would suggest local publications, politicians and leaders to come forward and spread this to appeal more to the ignored section of the society. We can start by not using #MeToo because it doesn't make a lot of sense to these people, use local languages and make this Twitter movement bridge the digital divide.
