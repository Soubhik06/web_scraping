This Tinder scam has been happening in Kolkata for many years. A friend of mine experienced it firsthand: a girl named Naazia invited him to a cafe called 'The Vibe' (gps: 22.53786° N, 88.35268° E ) near Quest Mall. This place was crazy expensive. She ordered a lot of food, and the bill ended up around 20,000 INR. When the bill arrived, she claimed she didn't have any money. He said he was gonna call police and could somehow leave the scene after some arguments. This scam is a collaboration between the girl and the cafe. Often, the girl might say she studies at Bhawanipur College. Be aware of the these girls and the such caffe.
