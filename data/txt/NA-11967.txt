1. Heard learned counsel for the petitioner, learned

 A.P.P. for the State and learned counsel appearing on behalf of

 the EOU. 2. The petitioner apprehends his arrest in a case

 registered for the offences punishable under Sections 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 and 120(b) of the Indian Penal Code. 3. The Court is not inclined to extend the privilege of

 anticipatory bail to the petitioner for the reason that the

 allegation is with respect to committing cyber fraud. The

 offence has become a menace for the society and the name of

 the petitioner transpired in the confessional statement of Rahul

 Kumar who was apprehended and disclosed that he along with

 his associates used to get fake sim cards and open false account
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.8662 of 2025(4) dt.02-07-2025 2/2 for committing cyber fraud. Further, for the said purpose,

 services of the Blue Dart Courier Company were being utilized.

 It was Blue Dart Courier Company that informed the police

 when they became suspicious with regard to the parcels which

 were being demanded by the accused to be delivered in a place

 outside their establishment when Rahul Kumar came to be

 arrested. The investigation is in its nascent stages and in the

 event if privilege of anticipatory bail is granted to the petitioner,

 the petitioner may abscond, as such, the anticipatory bail

 application is rejected in connection with Economic Offence

 P.S. Case No. 07 of 2024 pending in the Court of learned

 A.C.J.M.-I, Patna Sadar, Patna/Successor Court. (Satyavrat Verma, J)
