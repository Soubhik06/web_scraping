I needed money, so terminated one of my FD s. This happened offline. After I came home, within 30mins I got a call asking if I wanted to get the interest amount credited to my account. 

I mean this is mostly common sense , the interest should come in my account so asked the woman at the other end. Where else can I deposit the interest? 

It was her fumbling which got the cat out of the bag. I realized that this is a scam call and predictably she said I will get an OTP. I actually got an OTP within seconds. 

The OTP was for a fund transfer which nearly was 1/3 of my balance. Maybe this is a coincidence or someone knows my balance and would have emptied in 3 steps. 

The bigger question is how do they know? Since I talked to the teller a bunch of people may have overheard. Or do these scammers have people in the backend? The figures and the timing are very accurate.

I am closing the account with this bank ASAP.
