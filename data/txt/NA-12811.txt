 representation dated 18/5/2019, to the Chairperson, National Council

 for Teacher Education, New Delhi and the Registrar, Tamil Nadu

 Teachers Education University, Chennai, praying for action against the

 Colleges. Representation dated 18/5/2019, reads thus:- http://www.judis.nic.in 3/7 W.P.No.23976 of 2019 These are all the ignoring facts really happening most of the

 surprise visit to all the B.Ed college at any time with other state
 educationist, then you may find this is fact.” 4. Contending inter alia that the said representation has not been

 responded, instant writ petition has been filed as a public interest writ

 petition. 5. On the aspect as to how the public interest writ petition has to

 be filed, High Court, Madras, has issued a Notification, in SRO C-2/2010

 dated 26/7/2010, which is extracted hereunder. The above rules will not be applicable to the Public Interest
 Litigations taken on file by the High Court" 6. Averments made in the supporting affidavit are not substantiated by

 any document. Averments are bald. That apart, instant writ petition filed as

 a public interest writ petition does not satisfy the requirements of public

 interest writ petition. 7. In the light of the above discussion, we are not inclined to entertain

 the instant writ petition and accordingly, the same is dismissed. No costs. National Council for Teacher Education
