--- POST TEXT ---

1. Our healthcare system is in shambles – there’s a lack of hospitals, doctors, proper infrastructure, and poor-quality medicines.
2. The education system is broken – students still rely on private tuition to learn the same syllabus they study in school.
3. Roads and footpaths are in disrepair – in many cities, shops occupy footpaths, and we've forgotten their original purpose.
4. Corruption and scams are rampant, involving government corporations like ONGC, IOCL, IRCTC, and others.
5. Educated and general voters continue to trust corrupt politicians, who, in turn, deceive us and steal taxpayer money. If you look at most MLAs and politicians, many have a net worth in crores, including government officials.
6. Wage and income policies are still not standardized for everyone, from laborers to skilled workers.
7. We continue to believe that politicians will improve our economic conditions, air quality, equality, happiness index, hunger, and education.
8. Many of the most intelligent and talented individuals are working for private organizations, earning significantly more than others.
9. We still feel superior when comparing ourselves to countries like Pakistan, Sri Lanka, and Bangladesh.

I’m curious to hear what others think about this.
