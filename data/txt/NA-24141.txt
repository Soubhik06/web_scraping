Yesterday around 11am I received a message from a US number, usually I just report and block. But being a saturday with not much plans I replied and asked for more details. They replied with usual BS about being HR blah blah and offered me a job. I logged into my spare gmail account and subscribed to all youtube channel they asked. Every 2-3 Subscribe tasks paying me 50/- each there was a merchant task. Get about 1350 when I pay 1000. Being skeptical I ignored it. Then they started paying me 25/- every subscribe since I did not complete a task. Initial joining bonus was 150/- and later I earned about 200/- more and it all got paid in two different payments to be from two different accounts in my bank. When they rolled a second merchant task I decided to take a plunge and pay 1000/- in hopes of getting 1350/-. At this point I was okay to lose 650/- net. They didn't pay me that amount easily and asked to sign up to a crypto platform and get authorised. I had an old company ID card with me which I uploaded and got myself authorised and then again they made me do a simple task of buying some cryptocurrency which was free of cost and took about 45 seconds to complete. Finally got payment of 1350/-. Making me net profit of 700/-. I did not wanted to do 5000/- task which would have made m 6150/- . So I pressured them to pay me 500/- which I had already made subscribing to YouTube channels. But they didn't budge and I closed the chapter there. I also noticed YouTube channels were mostly Malayalam and pretty random. They did not look like legit / fake reviews payment scam. Pretty sure after a certain slab of payments it would have stopped. I knew I have high chances of getting paid on lower level slabs since overall profit for scammers would have been low. I saw plenty of people paying over 60K in hopes to get around 71k back. Not sure how it turned out for them.
