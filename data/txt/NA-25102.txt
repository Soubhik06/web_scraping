Here's the story!

**Part I:** On May 9th, we went to the New Delhi Train Station at 5:30am to catch the 6:00am train to Agra. We took a planned cab there and had our tickets in our hand. Our only excuse for what came next was that we were a little groggy - we certainly should have known better. 

__

We didn't go straight through the security because I was looking for some signage with what platform we were trying to get to. A man in a uniform who looked like he was a railway employee, with pretty good english, asked what train we were looking for and convinced us that it was canceled. He was extra convincing because he acted exasperated that we were taking his time, and tried to brush us off. Then he 'helped' us get a tuk tuk to take us to "The government office" where, if we hurried, we'd be able to get a different ticket for a train leaving from the old train station at 7:20. 

__

We were taken to an office that says "India Tourism" where they **explictly tell you when asked that they are the government office.** I point this out to say that this absolutely involves lying, and not simply carefully constructed omissions. They are a '24 hour office' - the guy there is a 'helpful government tourist officer' pretending to work for ITDC, and he said that 'if the train was canceled it would be automatically refunded to our credit card' and checked for tickets on other trains - which allegedly were full because our train was canceled. We were going to leave and go have breakfast and make some plans, but they said everything was closed (probably true) and we were convinced to hire an AC cab to Agra for 12,000rupees.

__

When we got to Agra, we checked in and were able to confirm many things. First, that our train wasn't canceled at all, and second that even if it was, the cost of an AC cab to Agra should have been only 4000-5500Rupee. Additionally, the form that says 'Incredible India' is a rip-off of the original, ie the address of the place is gone, no phone number, etc. 

__

**Part II:** At the time, I thought I'd been ripped off by an actual government employee, and tried to make a complaint on ITDC's actual website, but I couldn't get it to work on the phone, and later investigation revealed it wasn't actually a tourist office. When we got back to Delhi, we went looking for the office, around the 17th I think? We first went to the actual Tourist office on Connaught Place and they explained about the difference between our receipt and the fake receipt, etc. We used the internet to find out which ATM we had pulled money out of, and that led us back to the location.
