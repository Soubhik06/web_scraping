--- POST TEXT ---

One hour ago I was watching WWDC then I get a text from BX-ATMSBI that there's been a transaction of Rs.50 at GOOGLE *SERVICES.

Then I get 4 more texts back to the back of fraud transaction of 4000, 600, 4000, 790 at GOOGLE *ASHPSHKMR and GOOGLE * Gaurav Singh. 

As texts were bombarding I opened a new tab and logged in to net banking of SBI and looking to block my card asap but I am panicking and it took me 30 odd seconds to find the atm card section then I blocked my card.

There was no OTP sent on my phone for the transaction so I know that fraud transaction didn't happen due to my negligence. 

Then I filed an unauthorized fraud charge form to Google. Then I googled the " GOOGLE *ASHPSHKMR " and found out that other people have also faced fraudulent activity on their card at POS GOOGLE *ASHPSHKMR.

I called customer care but they were no help as transaction were not showing at their end as of now. I will go to the bank tomorrow and file a complaint there. I hope that I will get my money back.

If you guys have faced the similar situation and can help me out here that would be great.

**UPDATE 1**: SBI's Customer care is pure TRASH. I've called then 6 times so far but supposedly due to some technical issues, my transactions are not showing up so they told me file complaint online or visit my home branch and same story there. 

I have tried filing complaint 4-5 times on SBI CMS portal and when I hit submit I get this message "WE REGRET OUR INABILITY TO BLOCK YOUR ACCESS AND LODGE YOUR CONCERN."

So I visited my home branch and manager told me to write an application and said he will TRY and see if he can submit complaint from there.

I have sent email to report.phishing@sbi.co.in and customercare@sbi.co.in as well.

Finally, I forwarded the Unauthorised Transaction messages to 9223008333. Which gave me multiple complaint ticket number.

**UPDATE 2:** As I was writing **UPDATE 1** at 8:15 PM and I am on the last line. I got this text from SBI "Your AC XXXXXX6969XX Credited INR 600.00 on 05/06/18 -REVERSE POS PUR. Avl Bal INR 696969.69. Plz, download Buddy.

I happily open a new tab and log in to online banking and there it is my money is back. I guess SBI's support is **not** pure trash. They definitely need to work on these technical issues so that customer doesn't have to contact them so many times but as the saying goes All's well that ends well so I'd say am happy with the final support from SBI. I'll stay with them and will be more careful in the future.

Now I'd like to take a moment and thank everyone who commented here for their helpful advice.

If I could ask one more thing from you guys what precautions should I take from now on regarding banking in general? I will request new debit card today and definitely disable international transactions, POS etc. I am thinking of getting a Credit Card so that if this ever happens again I can dispute the charge as it is much more straightforward with the credit cards and will use debit cards just for ATM withdrawals.

**FINAL UPDATE:** As I mentioned in Update 2 that I got money back. On 4th June I also submitted Unauthorised Charges Form to Google and they investigated the charges and confirmed that they weren't made from my account.

 This is the email I got from google

&gt; I understand that you are concerned about the unauthorized charges made from your account. Please don't worry, I'd surely assist you. 
&gt; 
&gt; We've investigated the charges and confirmed that they weren't made from your username@gmail.com account. For security reasons, I can't provide information about who made the charges. However, we have taken action to prevent further charges to your payment method from this account.
&gt; 
&gt; Our refund policy requires that you report unauthorized purchases within 65 days of the unauthorized purchase, but as a courtesy, I've refunded all the charges that were done within the past 120 days that you claim were unauthorized to YOUR CARD-ISSUING BANK account. The time it takes your refund to appear depends on the payment method.

So as it turns out it was Google who reversed the transaction on my request. As far as I can tell SBI had nothing to do with. Kudos to Google and their awesome support.
