&gt;Those who are saying that Electoral Bonds are like Bitcoin are totally wrong. Read this for explanation:

&gt;If A is buying Bitcoin through any exchange after KYC and transfer it to wallet B, you can track transaction of Bitcoin from wallet A to Wallet B to Wallet C through a transaction hash.

&gt;In case of Electoral Bond, A can buy the bond and then handover it to B in exchange of cash and then B can give it to C and C can give it to a political party who can redeem it at the SBI branch.

&gt;A Shell company in loss can buy Electoral Bond through KYC and then it can sell the bond to a real Company which can give to the political party in exchange of some benefits or to save itself from ED/IT raids.

&gt;You can't ever figure out who gave donation to the ruling political party in the end as there is no traceability in the system and that is the reason the name of 'obvious' suspect is not the electoral bond buyers list.

&gt;No only this, in case of opposition getting the donations, govt can get the list of buyers from SBI and then using ED they can arm twist the buyers to get the name of the second party in the chain. This is the reason you see so many raids in opposition ruled states.

&gt;In short, electoral bonds are the biggest fraud on democracy by BJP in the name of bringing transparency to the political funding.
