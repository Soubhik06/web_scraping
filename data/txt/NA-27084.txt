Recently got contacted from a company called “Lancesoft” by an male with a thick Indian accent that told me the details of a job for a business in a major city that I live in but it kinda did feel fishy, that got my name, phone number and email address, I guess they got my name and phone number from Indeed as I obviously got both of those on my online resume. Luckily I didn’t give up my SSN number or bank routing information or anything too sensitive, found out it was a scam after looking at a few threads on Reddit after the call was over. I did feel weird enough to end the call early telling them if I am interested they would call back, they did send an email about the “job description” though which just looks plain off
