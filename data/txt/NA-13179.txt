X (formerly Twitter) has filed a case in the Karnataka High Court (HC) challenging the platform, stating that the Government is using the portal (and Section 79(3)(b)) to bypass the procedural safeguards under Section 69A of the IT Act , which is the provision actually meant for content blocking orders. As part of the judgment, the court emphasised the role of intermediaries (such as social media platforms) in checking the proliferation of child pornography. It had also emphasised the importance of Section 79 of the IT Act, which contains the due diligence requirements for an intermediary to be exempt from liability for the content that people post on their platform.Â

The Government had said that in light of the judgment in this case, it was working on Phase II of the Sahyog portal.
