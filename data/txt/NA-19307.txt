I changed my name from Anaya to Ananya 4 years ago. I was 20 and liked the name Ananya better. I had to make an affidavit and then publish a notification in gazzette. I also got it changed on my Adhar card and pan card.

I want to change my name to Anahita now, as per the recommendation of a numerologist and since I've been going through hell since my childhood, I'd like to try and see if this helps.

Now, the problem is, all my educational documents have the name Anaya on them. So, if I'd change my name from Ananya to Anahita, I'd always have to submit the proof that Anaya is Ananya and Ananya is Anahita. Hence, Anaya is Anahita. It makes me look stupid and I'd have to drag the name Ananya everywhere, even though it won't be on any of my documents.

If I'd change my name back to Anaya and then Anaya to Anahita, I won't be able to update it on my Adhar card twice since I've already changed it once and one is allowed to change his name only twice on Adhar card.

If I'd just publish a gazzette notification of changing the name from Ananya to Anaya and then, without updating it on Adhar card, publish yet another notification of changing the name from Anaya to Anahita and the second time around, submit my previous adhar card as a proof (for gazzette) I don't know if they'd allow me to do that, since my original adhar card would still have the name Ananya on it and I'd be submitting the previous adhar card.

If I'd get a notification published of changing the name from Anaya to Anahita, I'd probably be committing fraud and I don't think I'd be able to submit it as a supporting document to get my Adhar card updated.

If you've read so far. thank you! \^\^
