Heard through Video Conferencing. The applicant has filed this first bail application u/S.439 Cr.P.C for grant of bail. Applicant has been arrested on

15.09.2020, by Police Station- Hazeera, District Gwalior, in

connection with Crime No.223/2020, for the offence punishable

under Sections 376 , 323 , 294 , 506 of IPC. It is the submission of learned counsel for the applicant that

applicant is suffering confinement since 15.09.2020 on false

pretext and charge-sheet is likely to be filed soon. It is the case of

honey trap because prosecutrix tried to take the applicant into her

fold for abstraction of money which is evident from the fact that

after lodging the complaint at police station on 04.07.2020, she

settled the matter initially after taking Rs.20,000/- from applicant

which is evident from the affidavit deposed in this regard by

prosecutrix. Now she rescinded from her stand / commitment and

intends to abstract more money, therefore, pursuing the

complaint. Earlier also she levelled same set of allegations

against the another person in which trial conducted and acquittal

has been recorded in favour of that accused. Even otherwise,

contents of FIR nowhere suggests implicative nature of

allegations. Confinement amounts to pretrial detention. He

undertakes to cooperate in trial as well as investigation and would

be available as and when required. He would not move in the

vicinity of complainant. He would not be a source of

embarrassment or harassment to the complainant party. Under

these grounds, prayer for bail has been made. Learned Panel Lawyer for the State opposed the prayer and

on the merits of the case, the application is allowed. It is hereby

the satisfaction of trial Court. In view of COVID-19 pandemic, the jail authorities are
