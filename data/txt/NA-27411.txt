--- POST TEXT ---

Earlier today my mother got a call from "Apple Tech Support" saying that they needed to access her laptop to fix some things. While that was going on they also told my mother to go out and buy $5k worth of apple pay. While my mother was off doing that she let the people be on her laptop with the AnyDesk app. My little brother was being smart and told my mom not to listen to an Indian guy on the phone. Unfortunately my parents did not take the advise from my brother and proceeded to pay the $5k to them.

The one thing that I would like answered if anyone can help me is, **what should we do now to prevent further damage?**

The $5k is gone, nothing we can do about that. But they had a good 6 hours on the laptop to do whatever they wanted. I know they probably copied over all the files from her laptop. But what else could they have done? Malware? Took passwords?

So far we deleted the AnyDesk app and my mother is keeping her laptop on airplane mode and off the wifi for now.

At this point I'm a little agitated that my mother fell for a really old scam. I can't believe she followed the directions of some Indian guy.

&amp;#x200B;

Edit: Things are fine now, nothing was compromised on her laptop, and we looked through every single file and nothing of too much importance was on there (wasn't her main work computer). Any important account that she had, she changed their passwords. But loosing $5k still sucks, and hopefully they didn't retrieve any important information, hope they have fun going through half a terabyte of pictures, videos, and word documents.
