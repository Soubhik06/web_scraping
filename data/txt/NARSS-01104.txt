Hey everyone, 
I’ve been working in content moderation and recently came across a concerning pattern that I think the business community here should be aware of.

While monitoring suspicious platforms, I noticed that many scammers tend to misuse visuals from legitimate businesses based in Gujarat—especially those in the clothing, textile, and jewelry sectors. These scammers often take images or promotional materials from social media pages of real Gujarat-based businesses, then edit in their own fake contact details and use them to scam people.

What’s even more interesting is that although the visuals are from Gujarat, the contact numbers these scammers use often trace back to SIM cards from other states like Assam, Kerala, or regions near Bangladesh. In 99% of the cases I reviewed, this was the pattern.

So I just wanted to share a couple of tips for local businesses to protect themselves:

* **Claim and verify your Google Business Profile** – this helps establish authenticity and gives you more control over your business info online.
* **Use watermarks or branding** on images you post online, especially promotional content. This can deter scammers from reusing your materials.
* **Regularly monitor** your brand presence online. Reverse image searches can help identify misuse of your visuals.

Let’s help each other stay safe and protect the genuine businesses of Gujarat from being exploited by these scammers.

Stay alert and take care!
