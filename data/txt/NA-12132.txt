Heard. On behalf of the petitioner, this second petition is
preferred under Section 439 CrPC for grant of bail, as he is
in custody in connection with Crime No.138/2013 initially
registered at Police Station Jhansi Road, Gwalior for the
offences under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 120-B and 201 IPC and Section 3/4 of MP Recognized Examination Act.
Subsequently, in compliance of the order of the Apex Court,
further investigation of such crime was taken over by the
CBI. Taking into consideration the order of the Apex Court dated
14/12/2015 passed in a consolidated manner for various SLPs
in Special Leave to Appeal (Cri) No.9803/2015, by passing
the elaborate speaking order today in MCRC No.5668/2015
(Virendra Patidar Vs. State of MP & anr.) filed for grant
of bail u/s 439 of CrPC, the same has been disposed of with a
direction to the CBI to take an endevour to conclude further
investigation of the matter upto 31/3/2016, so also by
extending liberty to the petitioner to revive the prayer
thereafter for grant of bail, present petition being identical
and relating to the same scam and the same prayer is made
by the CBI counsel, thus on the same logic, reasons and
conclusions, with the same terms of the aforesaid order, this
bail petition is also disposed of. The order passed in MCRC 5668/15 today shall be applicable
to the present petition and the same shall be deemed to be
part and parcel of this order and accordingly, the CBI
authority shall take an endeavour to conclude further
investigation of the case upto 31/3/2016 and thereafter, the
petitioner shall be at liberty to revive the prayer for grant of
bail. It is also observed that prior to 31/3/2016 if any subsequent
order is passed by the Apex Court, which could give any
benefit to the petitioner to revive the prayer prior to such
date, then, in that circumstance, the petitioner shall be at
liberty to revive the prayer for grant of bail before 31 st
March, 2016. Let a copy of the order passed in MCRC No.5668/15 be kept
in the record of this bail petition also.
