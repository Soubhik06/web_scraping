I came across a troubling post on X(Twitter) that I wanted to share with the community here.

A X(Twitter) user ordered a Tissot PRX watch on 21st July from Amazon India, from a seller named "Mega Store LLP." The watch was delivered on 28th July, and everything seemed fine until the user decided to check its authenticity.

When they entered the serial number on Tissot's official website, they found out that the watch had already been purchased on 15th February 2023. This clearly indicates that the watch they received was either a return or potentially a previously used item.

It's disturbing to think that such a thing could happen when purchasing from a major platform like Amazon. Has anyone else experienced anything similar? What actions should this user consider? I'd love to hear your thoughts and advice on how they should proceed!
