 Heard both the sides. The petitioner- Sahdeo Mandal is in custody since 07.04.2023 in
 connection with Cyber P.S. Case No. 21 of 2023 for the offence registered
 under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 120B of the I.P.C., Sections 66(B) , 66(C) , 66(D) and 84(C) of the Information Technology Act pending in the
 Court of Additional Sessions Judge-II, Deoghar. It is submitted by the learned counsel on behalf of petitioner that he
 has been implicated in this case only on the basis of seizure of three mobile
 sets, apart from this, there is no other material to implicate him in the offence.
 There is no material to show that the mobile sets were used in any case of
 cyber fraud. Learned A.P.P. opposed the prayer for bail. It is submitted that there
 is no money trail with respect to any victim but there were suspicious e-
 wallets created from the mobile seized from him and series of call had been
 made. Under the circumstances, the above named petitioner is directed to be
 released on bail on furnishing bail bond of Rs.25,000/-(Twenty Five
 Thousand) with two sureties of the like amount each to the satisfaction of the
 Court below. (Gautam Kumar Choudhary, J.)
