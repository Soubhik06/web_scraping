I recently visited Kerala &amp; when returning I boarded flight from Kochi airport. Till this point I had never received fraud/scam calls. But last Friday as I entered Kochi airport, checked in &amp; had to wait for 2 hours to board the flight, I received 2 scam calls while my wife recieved 1 scam call.

The first call came to my wife. It was a typical DHL courier stuck with customs call. Here the number was from Nepal. She identified it forst few seconds &amp; disconnected.

Second call came on my number which I didn’t pick up was being called from Russian number. I let that call go into voicemail. I heard it later &amp; again it was courier stuck type of call.

Third call came from local number. I picked up and it was again I am speaking from ICI bank &amp; there is a problem with your credit card.

Two days later, I received the typical KBC lottery message on WhatsApp.

Today I again received call from Qatar number. Let it go into Voicemail. Heard it later it was from Indian Telecom department who wants to disconnect my number in 2 days because some illegal activity has been done.

I am now having suspicions that either the airport or some hotel that I stayed in has passed on our numbers to scammers. Though I am aware of most scams but still don’t want to be unlucky. What can I do?
