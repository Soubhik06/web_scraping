I suffered a scam on the 26th of June, 2024 around 9:40 am.

Here's my story: 
I was booking a cab from lonavala to panvel, and there was only one available, for 1934 rs, that was 11 minutes away. 

After I book the cab, the cab driver calls me and tells me that the OLA cab service is banned in the Lonavala area, and asks me to cancel the cab, and he will come regardless. He comes after 45 minutes, postponing despite him being only 11 minutes away in the app. He waits for us to load our luggage, and sit in the backseat( we were 5 people). 

**Then he tells us that the charge is Rs 3000 rupees and shows us an edited screenshot that shows that.** (This is a lie, the prices from lonavala to panvel are around 2000 rupees)

**He then talks about how he came from 31 kms away for this ride**( This is a lie as well, the ola app showed he was only 11 minutes away, so he wasn't that far away) Then, since we didn't have any other options as we had already checked out, we try to get him to reduce his price. 

He just agrees at Rs 2600 and starts driving the cab without our agreement. Then after we are in the middle of some road near the highway, after some of our protests, he asks us if we want to get off. We couldn't do anything then and he knew it. He then calls his friend and gossips about the scam he just did and how his customers i.e. "us" were trying to get off. This was a total scam. 

On top of that, I received a penalty fee of Rs 150 on my ola app. I mailed OLA, but got no response. Such people should not be allowed to use these apps for their twisted profits. I don't care about a refund, but I would genuinely be glad to see such people banned so that they don't scam other people
