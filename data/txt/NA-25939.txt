Recently me and my friend went to see a 1bhk property for rent which my friend saw on magicbricks . The rent was too low for a fully furnished flat (around 10k) so we didn't wanted to lose it. It was a we'll known gated society.
Around reaching the place we called the person who's number was in the app and who claimed to be the owner. He asked us to send 2k to get some sort of guest pass from the gate to enter the society which will be refunded if we didn't like the flat.
This seemed off for me and I asked the security if there's actually some kind of pass for which we have to pay the amount. The security guys denied the existence of any such pass and asked us the block and flat number. We called the guy again, this time he was frustrated and told us he'll send someone over to the gate after we paid the amount and refused to tell us the flat number. We argued for some time over call and then decided to not to send anything and to leave the place without seeing the flat. The security guys also said it has to be some fraud. 

Is this really a scam? Or is the pass thing legit? Have any of you faced anything similar?
