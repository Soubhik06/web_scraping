Our Hon'ble Railway Minister mentioned in his rail budget that 100 railway stations will have WiFi this year. I am not sure if it is a need for a normal person. Who will get benefits from this and what? 

As per me, it will bring disadvantages

- Another opportunity for corruption, ghotala.
- Will increase cyber crimes (hacking, stealing private data, images)
- More crowd on overly crowed platforms. Will become anti-social elements favourite haunt.
