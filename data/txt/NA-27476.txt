As others have posted in the past, I got a text from "31478" that read:

&gt;Please contact Radius Global Solutions LLC @ 833-xxx-xxxx

&gt;Reference Number: Fxxxxxxxxxx

&gt;To view your rights or make a payment: rad-gs.com

&gt;Text STOP to End texts.

I saw numerous posts on Reddit from the years saying this was a legit company etc. and I *thought* I might have some outstanding debt from years ago that got picked up. Visited the website and put in the last 4 of my social, but it gave me an error.

I've had my phone number for about 5 years now and some woman has been giving it out to places so I have had to deal with her debt collectors in the past - I figured this was exactly that again and wanted to nip it in the bud.

Called the number on the site, which is associated everywhere else with Radius Global Solutions, and the call was recorded throughout - I kept asking for them to tell me what the name on the debt was, how much it was for, or even where it came from. They kept transferring me to different "departments" and trying to get my full name and DOB.

Finally got some man with a heavy Indian accent, and I kept asking him what department I had been transferred to only for him to keep pausing then saying, "This is Radius Global Solutions." 

I hung up, but I'm pretty angry at myself for turning over the last 4 of my social to their site, giving my first name, and having my voice recorded which they can obviously use AI to fake now.

**I already have my credit frozen with all credit agencies - what else should I do?**
