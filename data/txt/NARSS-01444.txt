Want to know opinion about the recent trend people going to canada and places like that to study and work. They are taking mortgage by putting ancestoral property as collateral. Is it worth?. What will happen if the person who went did not do well. The bank will come after them any way after two years. Is this another " aadu maanchiam" scam by agencies or really worth it. I was not able to stand up without dry whiskey and cigerettes. Locals are cold towards people from other places. What you people think about this?
