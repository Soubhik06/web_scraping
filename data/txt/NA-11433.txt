Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and learned

 APP for the State. 2. The petitioner has prayed for bail in connection

 with Cyber P.S. Case No. 61 of 2025 registered for the offence

 punishable under Sections 75(3), 79, 31/8(4), 336(4), 351(2),

 356(2) of the BNS and 67, 67 (A) of the IT Act . 3. As per allegation in the FIR, the informant who is

 married women came in contact with the petitioner on social

 media and they started talking over phone. Thereafter, the

 petitioner recorded the nude and obscene videos of the

 informant and started blackmailing her for marriage as the

 informant was married she denied marriage with the petitioner

 and the petitioner made her nude videos viral and has also

 threatened the informant. It is also alleged that the obscene
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.89436 of 2025(3) dt.10-03-2026 2/3 video of the informant was sent to the mobile number of her

 husband and others which has created turmoil in the

 matrimonial life of the informant. 4. Learned counsel appearing on behalf of the

 petitioner has submitted that petitioner is innocent and has

 committed no offence. He has been falsely implicated in this

 case. Learned counsel for the petitioner has submitted that from

 perusal of the FIR, it is clear that the informant is already a

 married and how a boy of 19 years can force a lady who is

 already married in year 2012 to marry with him. It has further

 submitted that the photos which are alleged to have made viral

 by the petitioner on social media were only sent to the whatsapp

 of the informant as far as the allegation regarding sending the

 videos to the husband of the informant no such material has

 come during investigation. A statement has been made in para 3

 of the petition that petitioner has no criminal antecedent.

 Petitioner is languishing in judicial custody since 03.09.2025. 5. The application for bail is opposed by learned APP

 for the State. 6. Having heard learned counsel for the parties and

 considering the facts and circumstances of the case as well as

 period of custody, this Court is inclined to enlarge the petitioner
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.89436 of 2025(3) dt.10-03-2026 3/3 on bail. The above named petitioner is directed to be enlarged

 on bail on furnishing bail bond of Rs. 10,000/- (Rs. ten thousand

 only) with two sureties of the like amount each to the

 satisfaction of the learned J.M. 1st Class, Dharbhanga in

 connection with Cyber P.S. Case No. 61 of 2025. (Ashok Kumar Pandey, J)
