We all know what happened in Bangalore two days ago:

1. Video surfaces of two youth beating two cops on the road, everyone bays for their blood and call to hang them in public for beating cops on duty. Media runs amok saying such people should be made an example of, don't spare them, the usual media mileage.
2. Youth arrested immediately, Police Commissioner says they were performing wheelies on empty roads and attacked cops when stopped. Sent to jail under stringent sections. Rowdy sheets opened against them, which means their future is screwed.
3. Day later, video surfaces on Facebook showing what happened *before* the youth attacked the cops. They are seen been kicked around by the cops, feet stamped with police boots, pockets emptied, all the while they're pleading with the cops to let them go. This video is shot by someone from a balcony. Obviously something triggered them to fight back.
4. No media attention to this video, no explanation from cops. The youth in jail, obviously beaten to a pulp by the cops. Future gone.
5. Yesterday: this is the icing on the cake - youth taken to scene of crime for God knows what. Apparently they attacked a police woman with stones and had to be shot in the leg! We all know about the police style of revenge.
6. This doesn't add any value to the story, but the arrested are from the minority community and their friends are crying on FB saying no one's coming to their help.

And there you have it. Another entertaining day in the life of a policeman.
