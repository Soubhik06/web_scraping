The amendments reduce the compliance window for takedown on actual knowledge.

When an intermediary receives actual knowledge through a court order or a reasoned intimation from an authorised officer in the appropriate government or its agency, the intermediary must remove or disable access within three hours.

The FAQs also clarify authorisation requirements. The authorised officer must hold written authorisation. Police administration can designate one or more officers not below the rank of Deputy Inspector General of Police, with special authorisation by the appropriate government.

Under Rule 2(1)(wa), synthetically generated information (SGI) covers audio, visual, or audio-visual content that a computer resource artificially or algorithmically creates or alters in a way that appears real or authentic and depicts an individual or event in a manner likely to be perceived as indistinguishable from a natural person or real-world event.

In practice, the definition targets realistic deepfake-style harms rather than ordinary editing.

The FAQs exclude three categories where users act in good faith and do not misrepresent meaning or context.

This includes edits such as formatting, technical correction, colour adjustment, noise reduction, transcription, or compression that do not materially alter the underlying content.

This covers routine creation or formatting of documents, presentations, PDFs, educational material, and research outputs, including illustrative or hypothetical content.

However, the exclusion does not apply when someone uses AI tools to create fake certificates, forged IDs, fabricated official letters, or false electronic records.

The exclusions cover subtitles, captions, translation that does not manipulate meaning, summaries and search tags, audio description, and clarity improvements.

The SGI definition applies only to audio, visual, or audio-visual content. Text-only AI output does not qualify as synthetically generated information under Rule 2(1)(wa).

The broader due diligence duties therefore continue to apply to unlawful content in any form, including text.

The FAQs clarify that removal or disablement of access to information, including SGI, in compliance with the rules does not violate the safe harbour conditions under Section 79 of the Information Technology Act.

The ministry says that moderation actions taken through reasonable technical measures or automated tools will not by themselves undermine safe harbour eligibility.
