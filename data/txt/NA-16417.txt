My fellow students,

Today, I stand before you not just to speak, but to ignite a fire within each of you. A fire that refuses to be extinguished by complacency, a fire that will blaze a path to justice. For too long, we have let those in power sit comfortably, shielded by our silence. This ends now.

Think of our history. Think of Mahatma Gandhi, who wielded the might of millions with nothing but truth and unity. He brought an empire to its knees not through violence, but through the relentless, unyielding will of the people. It was not just his vision – it was our collective strength that won our freedom.

Today, we face a new tyranny. The NEET scam is a betrayal, a dagger aimed at the heart of our future. And what do they do? They delay, they deceive, they hope our outrage will fade into murmurs online. They believe we are weak, that our anger will dissipate into the void. But they are gravely mistaken.

Students, you are not weak. You are the tempest that will shatter their illusions. Imagine the impact if just 1% of us – a mere fraction – stood together, in person, at the NTA office. They wouldn’t be able to ignore us. They couldn’t. Our presence would be an earthquake, a storm, an unstoppable force demanding justice.

Think about the countless hours we've dedicated to our studies. The late nights spent pouring over textbooks, the weekends sacrificed, the social events we skipped, all for the pursuit of our dreams. Our hard work is a testament to our dedication. Our parents, too, have made immense sacrifices. They have stood by us, supported us financially and emotionally, believing in our potential and our future. Their dreams for us are intertwined with our own.

The future is ours. We are the ones who will be affected by this betrayal. It is our dreams, our careers, our lives that hang in the balance. Let the wrongdoers be punished. Don’t let them remain still. They must be held accountable for their actions, for every injustice they have inflicted upon us.

It’s time to rise. It’s time to act. Don’t wait for heroes. Be the heroes. Don’t wait for justice. Demand it. When we unite, we are invincible. Our voices, our bodies, our very presence – these are our weapons. Let your anger fuel you. Let your passion drive you. They have underestimated us for too long.

So go. Stand up. Show them who you are. You don’t need to move mountains. Just be there, united, fierce, and unyielding. Together, we are an indomitable force. Let your presence burn like a beacon of change. Believe in your power. Feel the fire within you. Let’s make our fury a catalyst for justice.

Together, we will roar like thunder. Together, we will bring them to their knees. Together, we will win.
