Long story short, I purchased a piece of jewelry from India on Etsy.

Two days after purchasing, Etsy sent me an email, stating that the shop had closed, and suggested I open a claim to get refunded.

I tried to go to the seller’s shop, but the shop was indeed closed. I immediately opened a claim and was refunded right away.

The next day, I received an email directly in my inbox, claiming that I robbed them and that Karma would get me. I don’t understand how they got my email from Etsy, which never happened before.

I then went back and forth on Etsy with the seller, explaining the situation. In the meantime, I received the jewelry even though I got refunded.

Etsy closed the shop themselves. The seller told me they lost their money and many people got refunded. He asked me if I could pay on PayPal. 

I feel terrible, I tried to contact Etsy but they gave me the run around with their automated messages.

My question is, is there any danger to sending this seller money on PayPal? Is this a scam? What is this?
