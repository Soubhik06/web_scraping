* A lot of people speculate on possible scams in mutual funds
* Front running has happened a few times - the AMC insider knows the decisions and profits from them personally
* i.e they run in front of the fund
* A case from the past was with ersthwhile Duetsche Mutual FUnd
* The fund manager is alleged to have used inside information to trade in parents names
* They have settled for &gt;5cr without admitting or denying guilt
* Almost 50% is settlement fees, 25% is disgorgement of profits and 25% is interest
* BTW, DHFL Pramerica - now PGIM - acquired Deuestche Mutual Fund in 2016
