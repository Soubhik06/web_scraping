**TLDR: What could’ve been a great concert, turned out to be a one of the worst (if not the worst), border-lining scam.**

# Preface

I've been listening to Enigma for a fairly long time (I’m a millennial, nearing 40) and must confess that I'm a big fan. When I heard about the Enigma concert in Bengaluru, it seemed like a god-sent opportunity. “Must not miss this at any cost” is what I thought to myself.

I wanted to have the best experience possible and ended up booking the VIP table for 4. This costed me around Rs.40,000, but since my wife and close friends decided to join me, it seemed reasonable. So, it was all set. 5th October, 2024 - Jayamahal Palace. I had big expectations.

# The day of the event

On the day of the event, I was driving down from Coorg after enjoying rather relaxing couple of days at a resort. That’s when I started reading about the Alan Walker concert fiasco on Reddit. The rain and mud situation seemed concerning. But what really caught my attention was how the organizers did nothing to help and the whole thing led to a stampede kind of situation. Not to mention there were accusations of theft as well.

Since the Enigma concert was being organized by someone else, I wondered what could possibly go wrong. Or even if it does, how bad could it possibly be.

# The event

So we booked a cab to avoid parking hassles at the venue and reached there around 7:30. I noticed that there were a good number of people already waiting in cue to get their wrist-bands. We hurried through the short VIP line and got our bands and headed towards the stage.

...we are interns, we've no idea sir

I immediately noticed the poorly setup VIP section and thought to myself that this possibly cannot be it. But alas! We moved into the cordoned off VIP section and found ourselves a table. To my surprise, a couple of interns, who were helping with the F&amp;B, but had no clue of what’s going on. There was no way to identify some of them who were part of the staff. To be fair, they really were trying to help but seemed like were getting exploited in the name of event management.

&gt;

Out of the blue someone came and asked what we would like to drink and mentioned that drinks are paid. I informed them that the VIP table comes with a full bottle of IMFL, so I’m not paying crap. That’s when they got us a bottle and said the glass and mixers would come shortly. As of writing this post, I’m still waiting for them. But in the hindsight, this was to be the least of our problems.

# What followed next

* **RAIN**: It was announced that Enigma would take the stage in 10 mins. And then, it just started pouring. People rushed to find cover into the food stalls. Some people were smart enough to get umbrellas (learnt from Alan Walker concert?). Others were not so fortunate and just got drenched.
* **SEATING**: Since we had the VIP seated tickets, we got the seats, but they were completely soaked and very nasty to sit on. There was slush everywhere.
* **FOOD**: We had topped our our card for 3K in anticipation or trying out everything on the menu. It was a very short menu but it was all we had. Turns out that this was worse than dog-food.
* **SOUND**: Well, this was something I was not willing to overlook. I’m an audio enthusiast and ex-member of an old classic rock-n-roll band from Bengaluru. So the sound thing really irked me. It was bad. At times I felt, Ganesh festival celebrations have better calibration. 
* **RISING TEMPERS:** As the rain continued for over 45 mins, people realised that this is not going to stop. So some people requested the management to provide cover. However, the event management team got makeshift tents, and that too only for the VIP section.
 * On one-hand we are being asked to take our seats, on the other hand, some people behind the section started arguing and creating ruckus about it. Most people understood their issue, that this was blocking their view and requested the tent to be moved to corner, just so that we can watch the concert, under the cover.
 * This fiasco kept us moving from one place to another in the rain rather than enjoying the concert. Meanwhile, some people kept shouting and asking to talk to management. Again, not the fault of the people in the general section but the management. Given what happened at Alan Walker, they could have planned better I felt.
* **MISMANAGEMENT**: I'm going to keep this section short. It was overwhelmingly evident at each and every step, how bad and inexperienced the folks were at organizing events. The sound, lighting, food, seating, staffing.. the list goes on. This was a classic example of borderline-scam.

At thing point, we’ve had enough, and we decided to call it a night. We made our way to the pick-up/drop-off point near the ticketing stations. While waiting for the cab, I enquired about who the organizer was, and no one wanted to reveal the same. Someone uttered out the name ‘Suhas’ or something and said this wasn’t an organization and it was some first-timer. Not sure if this information was correct. So at around 9:30 we left the venue for good and happily got stuck for the next 45 mins in the traffic jams before reaching home.

At the outset, this was a complete waste of time and money. I felt they rushed the band on stage and got the event started so that no one can even ask for a refund. Most importantly, this was not the Enigma I had been listening to. It felt different, it felt off.

Anyway, in the true spirit of Bengaluru, I’m moving on and waiting for the next good one.

Cheers folks!
