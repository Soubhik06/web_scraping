YouTube has a three-strikes-and-you’re-out-policy both with community guidelines and copyright violations. This means that if your account gets three strikes for community guidelines violations in a 90-day period, the company can permanently remove your account from YouTube.

Speaking to MediaNama’s editor Nikhil Pahwa about the three-strike policy in case of copyright violations, lawyer Ameet Datta of ADP Law Office said that this system seems contrary to the established jurisprudence in India, when Indian courts have “eschewed the blocking of entire portals, and has opted for individual URL blocking, excluding for circumstances when you can show that the entire portal is a pirate portal”. Notably, a popular creator’s YouTube channel may have non-infringing content that the company could end up wrongfully taking down under this three-strike system.
