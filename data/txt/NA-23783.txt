I read this reddit post praising IME and then went to the website : [https://indianmusicexperience.org/](https://indianmusicexperience.org/)

It said all good things, high internet , cafe, work from museum - I thought wow I could spend half day there and eat when hungry something

But to my dismay, it is just like any other Indian scam of promise high and deliver low, firstly Bag is not allowed inside ? I was like WTF why would you say work from museum then !

Went inside, it was good, I did not feel comfortable because they said dont take pictures with mobile, which I respect and am ok with it.

Inside there is no place to sit and relax, only some sitting chairs in front of big movie screens

The cafe was outside and open air as they said, the tables were not clean and dusty, so i did not feel like working there or sit and read a book there

I have no clue why would they try to make an impression that you could come and spend a day there when it is absolutely not like that !
