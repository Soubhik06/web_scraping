TITLE: Delhi Police Busts Immigration Racket, Arrests 11 for Forging Documents
DATE: 2024-12-26
SCORE: 1

--- POST TEXT ---

Delhi Police have uncovered an illegal immigration racket, arresting 11 individuals accused of forging documents to facilitate the entry of Bangladeshi nationals into India, according to ANI.

The group, which included document forgers, Aadhaar operators, and tech experts, allegedly created fake websites to produce counterfeit Aadhaar cards, voter IDs, and other identification documents.

Deputy Commissioner of Police (South), Ankit Chauhan, revealed that the immigrants used jungle routes and express trains to cross into India, aided by the forged documents generated by the accused.
