You know how a lot of companies have outsourced their customer service overseas?  (the stereotype is a call center in India, and a lot of the scam call centers are there too.)

I think THAT makes people more likely to believe the scam caller is real.  If cust svc was US based, getting a call from someone with a foreign accent instead of a generic middle American accent would be an instant red flag.

(Not that there couldn't be a scam call center based in the US, but sooner or later a US based scam company would be found out--people would either feel guilty or brag)

But this makes me wonder: Who's to say that the call center in India that handles customer service for our health insurance, Xomcast internet, etc. doesn't sell customer information (OUR information!) to a scam call center?  Maybe the call center owners are directly colluding to do this, or maybe some of the individual workers are recruited by the scammers to work for the legit call centers and collect info during their daily calls?

And...when did people START getting scam calls from overseas? Was it before or after American companies outsourced the call centers?  Is there a connection or not?
