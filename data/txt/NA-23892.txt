--- POST TEXT ---

I was coming out of the metro station when a girl approached me with a piece of paper. In it, it was written that they're from some village and they had to relocate due to "natural calamities". There was also an older lady with a kid (I'm assuming that's her mother and sister). She asked me to write my name and I did. Then she asked me to pay. She entered her number in my Gpay. I kept asking her if she has anything to prove her claims but she didn't understand what I was saying. She only knew Hindi and I don't know Hindi. Then thankfully the payment failed because Google pay thought it's "suspicious". She then showed me another QR code (this one belonged to an Auto driver) and asked me to pay there. I was thinking "this is definitely a scam" but I kept asking her "how do I trust you?". She wasn't understanding English and she kept saying "kardo kardo". In the end I was like "No no I'm not paying you" and left. 

I'm a bit annoyed that I wrote my name. They can't do anything with it right?
