**TLDR: Costoso Italiano: A Scam Business – Avoid at All Costs**

Recently, I ordered a pair of Black Leather Orion Chunky Derby Shoes (SS23) from Costoso Italiano after seeing a Google ad. The website's images seemed suspicious—either stock photos or taken from a third party. Despite this, I proceeded cautiously, especially after reading negative reviews.

In urgent need of the shoes, I used the WhatsApp chat feature on their site and was connected to a representative named Mr. Mahendra.

Mr. Mahendra quickly called, promising superior quality and claiming the shoes were custom-made for comfort. I stressed that I needed the shoes within five days and asked him to be honest about delivery, given the complaints in their Google reviews. He confidently assured me that if I paid the full amount upfront, he would personally oversee the order’s priority production at their factory in Agra. He also said that there is a conspiracy against them and their competitors are writing these paid negative reviews. I still expressed concerns, but considering the urgency I opted for a partial payment of INR 3,180/- instead of the full INR 9,180/-.

As expected, once payment was made, communication ceased. My attempts to follow up—due to the urgency of my niece’s first birthday—were ignored. Ultimately, I had to buy another pair of shoes elsewhere.

Determined not to let this go, I visited their store in Shahpur Jat, New Delhi. I was appalled by the poor quality of the shoes displayed—comparable to pairs priced at INR 500-1000/- in local markets like Palika Bazaar or Chandni Chowk. I even have photos for those interested.

While at the store, another dissatisfied customer arrived, sharing a worse experience. He had ordered shoes for his engagement, paying nearly INR 10,000, only to receive a poorly made product late. His story was heart-wrenching—his father is battling cancer, his mother is unwell, and he has been running from Ghaziabad to New Delhi for over a month, chasing a resolution from Costoso Italiano.

Regarding my order, after causing a scene at the store, they reluctantly claimed they had issued a refund via email. To this day, I have not received any money, and they remain unresponsive.

Fortunately, I have documented evidence, including emails and recorded phone calls, capturing their false promises. I am pursuing legal action and welcome others with similar experiences to join my complaint.

**Bottom Line:** Costoso Italiano is a fraudulent business. Avoid making any payments to them.
