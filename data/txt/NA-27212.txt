My friend recently got his identity and pretty much everything stolen from a scam job called Twin Health Inc. They approached him via LinkedIn, but it's a SCAM!! The worst part is we found out just after the first day and we couldn't find a single thing on the internet about it, but things started looking suspicious after they gave him a check to deposit on his account to purchase items needed for the job through them. Anyways, the so-called company is TWIN HEALTH INC. with the pink/orange color and they are supposedly located in California and India, unsurprisingly.
