Fake device insurance scam

So, I belong to a part of country where most people are tech illiterate. Here is a shop where many people from my town has come to buy their first laptops. My uncle also bought from here. Now,

1st. They sell all their laptops at mrp. It may be acceptable since they also offer physical device right there. But then

2nd. They force you to buy an insurance of around 15% of the price of laptop. My uncle bought two laptops from them each with insurance.

 I am not even lying when i say they force you to buy the insurance. I was right there with uncle watching all this. When i said i dont want an insurance from you they changed their begging into threatening that they already bought an insurance for us and they need to pay that amount to the insurance company so they wont let them have my laptop even if i had already paid. 

After so much headache thought atleast i have an insurance. But a simple google search revealed that this insurance company never settles up such claims and is fraud. (If you want details, please comment).

 I was really pissed off. I was scammed and so are the people of my town. It is the most famous laptop shop there.
