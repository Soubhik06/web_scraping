1. Rule. Learned APP waives service of notice of Rule on
behalf of respondent-State. 2. The present application is filed under Section 439 of the
Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973, for regular bail in
connection with the FIR being C.R. No.11213022230118 of
2023 registered with the Jetpur City Police Station, Rajkot for
the offence punishable under Sections 306 , 384 and 114 of the
Indian Penal Code. 3. Learned advocate Mr. P.S. Datta for the applicant has
submitted that the applicant is arrested on 07.02.2023 and
since then, she is in judicial custody and now the investigation
is over and the chargesheet is filed. It is submitted that the
allegations leveled against the applicant are that she aided
and instigated the deceased to commit suicide as also the Page 1 of 5 Downloaded on : Fri Dec 15 20:45:02 IST 2023 NEUTRAL CITATION

allegations of honey trap and extortion of money, however to
substantiate the said charge, during the course of
investigation, the concerned Investigating Officer has collected
mobile phones from the accused persons as well as the
deceased but along with the chargesheet papers, no
documents to substantiate the said evidence, have been
produced. It is also submitted that identically situated person
has already been enlarged on bail by this Court. It is, therefore,

the present application may not be entertained. 5. Learned advocates appearing on behalf of the respective

from the papers of the chargesheet that the role played by the

has not produced any documents. It is also found out from the

down by the Hon'ble Apex Court in the case of Sanjay

applicant in the FIR, without discussing the evidence in detail,
prima facie, this Court is of the opinion that this is a fit case to
exercise the discretion and enlarge the applicant on regular
bail. 9. Hence, the present application is allowed. The applicant
