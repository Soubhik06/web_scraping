I'm an 2nd year ECE student from a tier 3 college. It is the only college in my state with somewhat placements (except the IIT). Being an average student since the start I'm doing normal here. These days we are having some "Placement" training program where there are some trainers from corporate teaching use some "Technical and non technical stuff" for placement. They way the schedule is going is so frustrating and also waste of time for 8 hours every day. I know it is good for us students only but the way they present and also talk about the corporate stuff and jobs specifically for us students of this college it seems like they are training us to be a slave. The syllabus of the college is also not that good. The staff is usually the same students who passed out and joined back as a teacher. It scares me the dreams i saw to be. The college like i imagined is nothing like this. I hate going to the college but if I have less attendance I'll be detained. There's no college life in my state also most of the students with me are from a business background. Don't ask me why i joined this specific college. I had no intentions of it. In the end i want to be a pilot, engineering is not what i want to do. Coding yes i want to learn but it's being very hard to learn it due to difficult syllabus of ECE. 
One of our placement cell staff told us during the training that you will have to work 9-6 with very high pressure. Even the etiquette training they give us fells like how to become a servant or a waiter of the company.

I don't wanna be like this, engineering is not what i thought and saw on internet to be like. It's a scam.
