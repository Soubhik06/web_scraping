This Criminal Revision Case has been filed by the petitioner to set aside

 the order dated 07.02.2025 made in Crl.M.P.No.522 of 2025 in Crime No.222

 of 2024 on the file of learned XI Metropolitan Magistrate, Saidapet, Chennai

 and direct the respondent herein to return the Car (Candy White Colour MG

 HECTOR PLUS 7 SHARPO CVT) bearing Chassis

 No.MZ7DD3S1C7H139346 and Engine No.18R11520225. 2. The case of the petitioner is that based on the complaint given by one

 Bogeswara Rao alleging that the petitioner along with other accused cheated

 the him to the tune of Rs.10 lakhs through online scam, the case in Crime

 No.222 of 2024 was registered on 10.09.2024 for the offence under Sections

 316 & 318 of BNS and Section 66 of the Information Technology

 (Amendment) Act 2008 and subsequently, the petitioner was arrested. Further,

 the petitioner's car was also seized alleging that the said car was bought from

 the cheated money. Subsequently, the petitioner was granted bail by this Court 2/6 https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis ( Uploaded on: 07/03/2025 05:22:00 pm ) Crl.RC.No.368 of 2025 vide Crl.O.P.No.28373 of 2024 dated 19.11.2024. Thereafter, the petitioner

 filed a petition under Section 497 read with 503 BNSS in Crl.M.P.No.522 of

 2025 before the XI Metropolitan Magistrate, Saidapet, Chennai, seeking

 interim custody of the vehicle. The said petition was dismissed by order dated

 07.02.2025. Challenging the same, the present revision is filed. 3. The contention of the petitioner is that the learned Magistrate

 dismissed the petition on the ground that the petitioner purchased the said car

 using the cheated money, but failed to consider that the cheated money was

 already recovered by the respondent police and the confiscation proceedings

 was also initiated. His further contention is that the vehicle is kept in the open

 space exposing to sunlight and rain since the date of seizure and if the vehicle

Heard both sides and perused the materials available on record. 6. It is seen that the said vehicle is alleged to have been purchased from

 the cheated amount and the investigation has not been completed and the

 charge sheet has also not been filed. Pending investigation, it is not advisable
