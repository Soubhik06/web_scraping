So, this started with someone who has been a friend of mine for past 12 years, and given that I(23M), am about to graduate my bachelor's soon, I got scammed by him.

This friend of mine(23M), he started a new side business in August 2022, in collaboration with many mobile phone shops as to buy phones at cost price with the help of EMI at 0% or very negligible processing fee and sold them to people who could not afford paying all at once and don't have credit score enough for loans. He would charge them 20-50% extra for the service every month, such as if a phone cost him 20k, he would charge 26k as monthly EMI of 3-4k.

Now he asked me for help and at that time, I was just 21 year old and in second year of my college, I could not afford him financially, but then he asked me to log on to Zest Money which is linked to various ecommerce websites and back then it used to provide Amazon gift cards at 0% for 6 months and 3% fee for 9-12 months. Given the trust for him, I helped him through that and he paid his EMIs on regular basis to me. The process of in-out started then, which was like once an EMI was paid, the limit would be unblocked and he would again take out that limit as gift card.

Coming to November 2022, I got my first credit card from HDFC co-branded with Paytm with a limit of 30k, so he asked me if he can borrow 18k from that and he would pay me a margin of 5-6% on the borrowed amount as commission. Now again, being friends I helped him through this. But then he started suggesting more apps for the same, like OneCard Credit Card(20k limit), KreditBee(18k), IBL(10k), RupeeRedee(30k), Amazon Pay(2k), Flipkart Pay Later(35k), Paytm Postpaid(65k), and many more**(no Chinese Apps, only RBI registered NBFC)**, approximately to 5L till August 2023.

He paid his dues on time, and whenever I asked for some money, he would provide me with some. But since September 2023, he was lagging in his EMIs, such as would pay 5-6 days late, and in some cases, a month late. In December 2023, after asking for money many times and faking scenarios of loan agents coming to my home, he finally said that he doesn't have any money. At this time, the total due amount was about 3.25L, out of which 2.98L was due to Loan apps, as pending, and 27k as my personal money which I earned through internships and freelancing.

Post hearing that, I told the whole thing to my father who prompted in taking immediate action and recently I got my whole money back.

But because of this, my credit score fell down from 792 to 458. In January I have started an internship at Mohali based firm with 25k as stipend and in July, I would be joining a Gurgaon based MNC as SDE at CTC of 12LPA(7.5 inhand).

In future, I want to purchase a home for my parents and for myself according to my needs as my parents live in Rajasthan and I would be living in other cities given the nature of my job.

**Please suggest me ways to repent this whole thing and get back on track regarding my credit score.**

Right now I have the two credit cards, HDFC-Paytm and OneCard, but I don't use it much often until and unless it is absolutely necessary.

**TL:DR;**

Got into many loans through friend, couldn't pay them off on time, starting new job, credit score fell down 350 points, suggest me ways to get it back on track.
