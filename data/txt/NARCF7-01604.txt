I thought it was police as they have beacon lights and siren but not. Interestingly the last car had someone dressed in police uniform but he was sleeping with mouth wide open.

Every vehicle has a license plate which ends with 0393.

The last vehicle was a safari with number plate AP 9 G 0393 and when I look it online it is CBNs vehicle but a black scorpio instead of a beige safari what I have seen.

The benz cars had the plates

TS 11 FC 0393
TS 11 ED 0393

But looks like these are all fake plates.

This motorcade sped towards Begumpet airport. Trying to figure out who it was.
