Having read so many scam stories, the ones where people are contacted through whatsApp, are asked to do simple tasks and are paid i.e. initially for confidence building and later they don't return your money. 

Many of us, including me wished that these scamsters approach us so that we can just earn the initial (confidence building) money and later just block them. 

Finally, I have been contacted by one of them. So, yes.... as told already, I am trying to earn some money through them and block them later on. 

They said that for the first part, they will give me 3 tasks to do and pay ₹180 later. For the first task, they asked me to like a random google link. I did that. Now, they have asked me for my telegram and my bank account no. 

The issue is whether or not should I provide them with my bank account no.? If I do give them my bank account no., and later after earning some money I block them. Can they do anything against me/my bank account? 

Should I try to earn some money from them or just let it be?
