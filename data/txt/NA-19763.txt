I dont think so. 

I use an MIUI ROM which in which we can dynamically allow/deny permissions to running apps (just like many other ROMs also support). 
And till now , and till now the app has accessed only location and camera (I used this to test image search feature)

Yes , while installing it asked for permissions like reading SMS, contacts , call logs etc , but till now it has not used those permissions as can be verified from the below screen grab as per my usage of the app till now: 

http://i.imgur.com/lb1WmF2.png?1

And yes , one thing is worth mentioning here: 
There is a permission category called 'Others' for apps and the data that can be accessed using subpermissions under this category are quite scary , eg Read your social stream! 

[Source](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/6014972?p=app_permissions&amp;rd=1) 

Yes , FK has some permission requests under this category viz. 

1. receive data from Internet 
1. read Home settings and shortcuts 
1. write Home settings and shortcuts 
1. install shortcuts 
1. control vibration 
1. use accounts on the device 
1. control flashlight 
1. read sync settings 
1. create accounts and set passwords 
1. toggle sync on and off 
1. read Google service configuration 
1. prevent device from sleeping 
1. view network connections 
1. full network access 
1. uninstall shortcuts 

I don't think permissions under this category are privacy breaching (except may be 6,8,9,10,11 , I'm not sure how they can make use of these for data mining)

And while updating an app which make use of permissions under 'Others' category , even if a single item is added by the app in its update , then play store make you to review the permission (even if automatic update is turned on for the app , which is good) 

So we need to be careful in general while updating apps which use 'Others' category 

The subtext is FK is not going app only just for data mining and product suggestions based on it , there might be some other motives as well (like notifications as someone pointed in other thread).But we are blind to that now. 

PS : Im not in any way associated with FK , it is just my personal opinion!!
