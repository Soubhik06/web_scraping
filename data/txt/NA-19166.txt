--- POST TEXT ---

Free alternatives like Mastodon and Diaspora are too complicated for most people, and one same problem as Facebook: they don't actively educate and protect users against propaganda, misinformation or disinformation.

I've created an alternative as a personal project, but as you see in the title, most people are too mentally trapped by "free" to see the real problem. They say "it's all as bad as Facebook" or "pay for anything? must be a scam!" So they're brainwashed into staying with Facebook and the problem continues with no change at all.

Legislation only helps a bit -- giant companies like Facebook and Amazon just change to ignore the new rules. Laws often keep smaller competitors out by imposing financial burdens to comply with them, rather than help reform and fix the problem.

How do we finally help ourselves out of the Silicon Valley trap?
