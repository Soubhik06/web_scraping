respondent regarding the maintainability of the complaint by the
revisionist, who is stated to be the husband of the victim, it is the
legal proposition that any person can report the commission of
cognizable offence irrespective of his status as a victim as the
cognizable offence is deemed to be committed against the state in
which the investigating agency is duty bound to conduct an
investigation. Thus, the argument of non maintainability of the
complaint and locus of the complainant does not hold any merit. 14. Revisionist sought to bring the criminal law into
motion having filed the complaint under section 156(3) Cr.P.C
for commission of the offences under section 506 / 509 / 120B / 34 IPC read with 66/67 of IT Act . The complaint of the revisionist
encapsulated two sets of allegations, the first being the messages
sent to him at his Instagram ID i.e. rajnesh117 from Instagram ID
of rahu.lrathi480, which during inquiry and as per the status
report was found to be registered by respondent no.2 Ms.
Deepika with the registered mobile number 9289087921,
however, those Instagram messages prima facie do not contain
any offensive words so as to spur any criminal action. Ld. CMM

revisionist on her number as mentioned in the complaint from an
unknown sender and prima facie those messages asking for
sexual favors appear to be offensive inviting criminal action
under section 354A / 354D / 509 IPC. But the impugned order does
not reflect any application of mind qua these allegations. The
status report filed by the inquiry officer also does not reflect that
any inquiry was conducted as to the identity of the sender of
those messages. The impugned order only discusses about the
invocation of section 506 IPC which is not found to be made out,
and rightly so, however in the absence of any finding in the
impugned order as regards the aforesaid allegations as well as in
the absence of any inquiry as to the identity of the sender of those
messages to the wife of the complainant, the impugned order
deserves to be set aside. In so far as the probative value of the
screenshots of the chats is concerned, that is the subject matter of
investigation, and thus, on this basis, the screen shots of the
chats cannot be negated. 16. Ordered accordingly. The present revision petition
stands allowed to the aforesaid extent with direction to the Ld.
CJM to call for further status report on the afore-noted aspects, to
