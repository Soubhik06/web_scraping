A few months ago, someone posted about the rude behavior of auto drivers. Uttarakhand is still considered relatively safe, but without implementing Bhu-Kanoon and Mool Niwas, our culture might collapse, and women's harassment will continue to rise.

Why are Pahadis Becoming Misogynist or Intolerant?
