My wife also presented her PAN card, AADHAR card to the bank. The DD payee/beneficiary name is the same as my wife's. We kept pleading with the bank employees to just give us verbal information on whether the DD has been encashed or not. But they did not cooperating. Finally I lost my cool and gave them an earful and told them that they were complicit in the scam too.

Do the banks not have a responsibility to provide this information to a citizen? Is there any rule or law that can help in obtaining this information from the bank?
