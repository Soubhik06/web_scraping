So yesterday morning, went to the Surya Nagar IOCL pump which is my regular pump for refuelling. Told him to do a tankful. As soon as he put in the nozzle in the tank, the electricity went out and the meter switched off. I observed he didn’t remove the nozzle for 30seconds even after he knew the electricity is off. Then the electricity came back and he reset meter and filled for 2800 which is what I was expecting as per mileage. Then comes the surprise - he casually says “sir aapke hue 3300, 2800 abhi k aur 500 bijli jane se pehle” 
At this moment I got furious as I knew this was a known scam. I told him I’ll pay you 600 extra if you can prove that you have filled 500 worth of fuel. He knew he was fucked he tried acting hard to get the bill worth 500 from the machine but couldn’t. 

Went to complain to the manager but no one was present being Independence Day morning. Will complain to IOCL surely. 

P.S - Don’t remove your eyes from the meter when refuelling.
