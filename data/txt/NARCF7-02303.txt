Hello all, I just wanted you all to be aware about a girl ID from tinder.

She'll connect with you and tell you that she is new in town. Also she mentioned that she's afraid of talking to strangers. Next moment you'll be fixing a date and she'll become generous enough to pick you up in her "Car". 

Just before the date she'll text/call you that she have cash and she needs some money online. And then when you send her the money she'll not answer you. Just a normal scam, but informing you all here.

If you face any such encounters of girls asking for money then please be aware and do not send any.
