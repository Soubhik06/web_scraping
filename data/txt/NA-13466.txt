On March 11, Meta announced new anti-scam features for WhatsApp, Facebook, and Messenger. Previously, a Reuters report citing internal Meta documents said about 10% of the company’s 2024 revenue came from scam and illegal ads. Users were exposed to an estimated 15 billion scam ads each day.

What this means for Indian Users : These tools do not assist users who have already lost money to scams on Meta platforms, as there is currently no redressal mechanism. The new features aim to help prevent scams, and Facebook has not yet launched some of them, such as friend request alerts.
