 Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 by applicant Mukesh Chaudhary for grant of
 regular bail relating to FIR/Crime No.265/2025 dated 17.04.2025 registered
 at Police Station Budhar, district Shahdol, for the offence punishable under
 Sections 318(4), 319, 336(3), 340(2) and 111(2)(B) of BNS and Sections

 66(C) and 66(D) of Information Technology Act, 2000. The applicant is in
 judicial custody since 27.04.2025. This application has been argued on the grounds that applicant has

 requested that the application for regular bail be allowed. State is opposing the application on the grounds that applicant was a

 transfer money and also for withdrawal. In this entire conspiracy, applicant
