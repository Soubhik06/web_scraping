My older friend needed to get a refund from Best Buy for a bad repairman they sent to her house. She googled the number for Geek Squad online and called them herself (no one called her). She got a guy in India and this is where it all goes wrong. She said he said we can process your refund no problem, but he said they don't do refunds onto the card, they do a transfer back to your bank. She he asks her to log into her bank account, and then do the transfer a small amount at a time (a reg flag I know ... but in her mind she's like well I called them myself and it was their listed number!). She transfer 110$, but then it's a refund scam where the guy claims she transfered 11000 to herself and she needs to pay him back. In the background her husband says I'm calling the bank, and the scammer is yelling at them that they can't call their bank. He locked their computer via some kind of remote access and told them they needed to take out 11k at the atm and deposit it at a specific atm he was going to give them. At that point my friend refused, and the guy has locked her computer. 

She's a super nice older lady who doesn't deserve this kind of bullshit. She's contacted her bank who has frozen all her accounts, I told her she needs to change all her online passwords and wipe her computer and reinstall, is there anything else I shoudl tell her to do? I'm so mad this happened to her.

When she told me about this, I googled "geek squad" to see what i'd get. The first "result" was a google ad with title "Geek squad" but the url is something shady like [indianscammerz.com](https://indianscammerz.com) or whatever, but I'm thinking that's what happened to her.
