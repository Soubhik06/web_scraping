After repeatedly asking, she finally confided in me that she’s being stalked both in person and online by her ex-boyfriend. They were in a relationship (sort of) until she discovered that he was a drug addict and abusive.

He used to control her, restricting her phone usage, forbidding her from talking to other boys, and demanding daily reports of her app data usage. He had access to her WhatsApp, Instagram, and other accounts. Once, he even physically attacked her by kicking her legs because she couldn’t answer his calls while she was with her parents.

She endured all of this silently and didn’t tell anyone, including her parents. When they lived in the same area, he would stalk her in person. She frequently changed her phone number, but he always found ways to contact her through mutual friends. After she graduated, she cut all ties, blocking him everywhere, but it didn’t help.

When I met her, she was in a depressive state of mind. We live in different states, so most of our communication is online. One day, after being threatened again, she broke down and told me she couldn’t take it anymore. She moved to another city, staying in a PG (paying guest accommodation) and finding a remote job. However, he continued stalking her. Through one of her Instagram friends, he contacted her work colleagues, asking intrusive and embarrassing questions about her, such as if someone was her boyfriend.

Eventually, she broke down again, and we decided to inform her parents. Her parents are conservative and were already dealing with family issues, so this was overwhelming for them. After learning about the situation, they began accompanying her whenever she went out.

Even with her parents’ presence, the stalking didn’t stop. The man followed her, stared at her, and even spat in front of her parents to show disrespect. This became too much for her family, and they decided to relocate to another city. However, they didn’t file a police complaint, fearing it would damage their family’s reputation.

Despite relocating, the harassment continued. The stalker escalated his actions, calling and messaging both her and her parents relentlessly, including late-night calls (around 1:20 a.m.) and WhatsApp messages like “How are you?” Her parents initially answered his calls but eventually stopped.

Her father couldn’t cope with the stress and turned to drinking. They didn’t file a case because they feared backlash due to the man’s SC/ST status and his connections with local goons. Recently, her father approached someone from the SC/ST community to intervene. This person contacted the stalker and warned him, but the very next day, her mother received a WhatsApp message from him saying, “How are you?”

When they called the number, the person pretended not to know the local language and spoke in Hindi. However, my GF recognized his voice. This happened a week ago.

As evidence, my girlfriend has a few voice recordings, contact numbers, screenshots of his threats on Instagram, call logs showing harassment of her parents. Additionally, I have information on the accounts he currently uses, including his Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, and his mobile numbers.

We’re at a loss on how to handle this situation. Due to health issues in my own family, I can’t assist her in person. Her family is falling apart, and I can’t bear to see her or her parents suffer like this.

Could someone please help or guide us on what to do?
