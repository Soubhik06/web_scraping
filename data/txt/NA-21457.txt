Background: One of my friend was talking to his cousin's fiance on Facebook private message, during the conversation he shared his concern with her about cousin lying about his background to marry her and provided details when she asked for it. Later she went to the cousin and told him about this interaction.

The cousin has relatives in cyber crime branch(Bangalore, Karnataka). He got the cyber police to track the account(friend was using a diff account) based on IP, and filled a defamation FIR against my friend. The police have confiscated the mobile used for this interaction. During this communication between those two, there was no public posting of any kind, there were no vulgar or provocative content that were exchanged. she specifically asked for more information on the cousin which he provided. The information shared were true to the best of his knowledge and any opinion shared were just that, opinions(Thinking ***Kalandi Charan Lenka v. State of Odisha*** does not apply )

Is this even legal, what are his options.

1. Can communication between two consenting parties about a third person be used to file an defamation FIR?
2. Can police file FIR and track the account based on IP because third party was offended by communication and because cousin has connection with police?
3. what are his options(currently he has hired a local lawyer)? Are there any relevant cases on this matter?
