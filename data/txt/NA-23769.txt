--- POST TEXT ---

I had gone to watch Wild Karnataka with my SO yesterday at the PVR screen in Orion Mall (Sandal Soap Factory). I was looking at which seats were available on my phone as we walked to book the tickets at the counters. My SO casually paid whatever amount was quoted, but I realized that one ticket on Book My Show (for the seat we chose) was 138 rupees, while our total bill for two tickets came to 480 rupees. I asked my SO which seats did she book and she said the ones we had seen on the app. When we confronted the cashier as to why the ticket is almost so much higher than online, the cashier said internet charges (as in if we booked it online, the cost would be the same as she is billing us). So I went ahead and booked 2 tickets in front of the cashier and the total bill was less than 400 rupees (with the internet handling fees etc.)

This is when the cashier had to spill the beans. She said she had given us two food vouchers worth 80 rupees without our consent. On asking her how she can do that, she is telling it's part of the system. We repeatedly told her to give us 160 rupees back, to which she said she can't do anything and in fact, it's a good food offer. My SO was getting really hyper by this time and started telling her to give all our money back while the cashier kept being adamant about forcing these vouchers.

By then, some lady behind us began asking us what is happening and I explained to her. This is when the cashier just hurriedly gave back our 160 rupees, albeit very rudely.

See, the problem for us was not the voucher but in the manner, it was sneakily added to the bill without our consent. And on catching this, the cashier kept trying to deny it.

The moral of the incident is, always ask PVR people how much the individual ticket costs before paying them.

**TL;DR: Me and my SO were sneakily given a paid voucher at the PVR ticket counter which increased our bill by over 150 rupees. On catching this scam, the cashier refused to acknowledge it. Please make sure you ask them the actual price of the ticket and only pay that. Hope this helps.**
