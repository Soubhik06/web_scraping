My friend n I were travelling from Koramangala to Sarjapur Road(Street 1522) and we booked a Rapido Auto. The guy came on time and the fare was around 250. We specifically checked for the route after entering the auto and giving the otp and eta was around 57mins. Few minutes later, the auto driver stopped somewhere on the way after HSR and told us that this was the drop location. We were completely surprised because the actual drop location was atleast 7-8kms away from the spot he stopped. To our dismay, the fare price was 230. We told the auto guy that we specifically booked till Sarjapur Road and not the spot where he stopped. He said he had no clue and stopped where the maps showed. After arguments, we ended up paying 230 and checked the ride history. The ride showed Koramangala to the spot driver dropped itself and not to the place we originally booked.

We had checked atleast 2-3 times before booking and once after booking about the drop location. There is no way the driver stopped somewhere near HSR and told us this was the drop location. What’s surprising is for the whole ride(around 12kms) it showed 250 but only till HSR it ended up showing 230. I’ve never had this experience before with uber or ola or even namma yatri. Is rapido so unreliable? Their customer support also seems to be useless. Can this be some scam from the driver side (ik sounds improbable) but he was barely surprised when we told him about this. He was just bothered about collecting the fare
