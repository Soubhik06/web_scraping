--- POST TEXT ---

Got a call early in the morning. The caller told me there was a parcel from Singapore to Mumbai, claiming I was the sender. I told him I had no idea about it. He suggested it might be an identity theft and misuse case and recommended I report it to the police. He insisted on filing an online complaint and then transferred the call to a police officer.

The "officer" asked about the situation, my work, age, and other details to estimate the amount involved. Then he asked me to install the Skype application. Another person joined the Skype chat, asking for my Aadhar card or other government ID proof. He pretended to connect with a data-checking team and then claimed there was a murder or money laundering case in my name. He sent some fake documents as proof over the Skype chat.

He asked if there were any deductions in my bank accounts and requested the exact balance figures from all my bank accounts. After wasting two hours of their time, I sent them a picture of my middle finger. They got pissed off and cut the call.

**TL;DR:** Received a scam call about a suspicious parcel, involved fake police and Skype chat, asked for personal info and bank details. I wasted their time and then sent a rude picture, causing them to hang up.
