I was going through the [International Fact-Checking Network signatories](https://ifcncodeofprinciples.poynter.org/signatories) and was bit surprised to see names, some of them I had never heard of.

A few of these websites came to the limelight primarily after its partnership with Facebook and get funding from them as well.

&gt; Feb '19: [Facebook expands fact-checking network in India, adds 5 more partners to spot fake news](https://www.businesstoday.in/top-story/facebook-expands-fact-checking-network-in-india-adds-5-more-partners-to-spot-fake-news/story/318468.html)

&gt; "Starting today, India Today Group, [Vishvas.news](https://Vishvas.news), Factly, Newsmobile, and Fact Crescendo, all of whom are certified through a non-partisan International Fact-Checking Network, will review news stories on Facebook for facts, and rate their accuracy..." Facebook said in a statement.

&gt; It added that this will be done for content in languages including English, Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, Malayalam and Marathi.

&gt; It already counts BOOM and news agency, Agence France-Presse (AFP) as its partners.

&gt; Besides, Pages and domains that repeatedly share false news will also see their distribution reduced and their ability to monetise and advertise removed - which helps curb the spread of financially-motivated false news.

&gt; Facebook said once a story is rated as false, the platform was able to reduce its distribution by 80 per cent.
