Hey everyone, this is more of a PSA. There's this group of scammers who are targeting profiles of Indians on Instagram by luring them under the premise of a free sexchat or being a sugar daddy. So here's how the scam works. 

1. They try to get your attention by sending a DM and liking many posts of yours. The DM will say something on the lines of "Hey there sexy", "You're really attractive", etc. 
2. Then they'll try to make small talk and before you know it you'll get video call from them.
3. The moment you join the video call, they'll take a screenshot of it and hang up.
4. They'll photoshop a pornographic image to the screenshot to make it seem as if you were actually on a video call with a person who was naked.
5. They'll threaten to send this photoshopped pic to everyone on your follow list unless you pay them a certain amount. They also go to the extent of creating a fake account with a similar name and a DP of yours and send messages to the people on your follow list. 

Many people fearing the shame end up paying these scammers an upward of **₹** 50,000 and lose their hard earned money. 

How do I know all this? A lot of my friends have actually fallen for this scam and ended up posting stories on their IG profiles that read, "Account hacked, please ignore any message you've got" or "Someone pretending to be me is sending vulgar images, pls report XYZ account". 

Today I ended up getting a [text from a similar account.](https://imgur.com/a/c84PBNg) As predicted, I even got a video call which I didn't pick. I [dressed up for the call!](https://imgur.com/a/Nwyp03a) Then called the account back repeatedly. After about 7-8 missed calls, they finally picked up. [This](https://imgur.com/a/gtOIebH) is how the screen looked like. I could hear english being spoken with a heavy African accent in the background. When they realised that they couldn't see my face, they hung up and [blocked me.](https://imgur.com/a/xMINMrE) 

Beware y'all. Stay safe and browse safe.
