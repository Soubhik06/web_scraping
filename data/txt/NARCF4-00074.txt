When I heard first about cyber-bullying, I sort of downplayed it in my mind like who cares if people post unkind things on social media. But I think the difference between this and old-time bullying is (the victim) does not even know who the people are that do it. The perpetrator has put unknown people who are part of a group up to it and the reason those people participate is so they can have someone else that they know harassed later.

Also, the actual event does even not have to be online. For example, 

Online message: He or she (the victim) is walking down Commercial street passing Liberty now, who is available to mess with the victim?

I almost feel people don't want to talk about it for fear of giving more people the same idea or attracting unwanted attention to themselves. It isn't necessarily children doing it, and it may be a service that people are paying for. 

If this is a problem, then is the Salem PD doing anything to deal with this? I don't think the traditional "file a police report" scenario is going to do anything. Is there something on the Salem PD website that I missed?
