Hi all. Recently my dad became a victim of fraud where the scammer phished his credit card details(along with CVV) and later made 3 txns worth 70k from his card. 

Fortunately my dad was using his phone at the time so he blocked the card immediately and reported to ICICI customer care. They said they'll look into it and get back in two days. They even said that he'll get his money and asked my dad to lodge a complaint in the office. He did and he patiently waited for any response from them. They came back to him later saying that the transactions were made using 3D secure pin which is a 2nd level protection, so it's authorised by the user. They told him to take it to regulatory authority ombudsmen in case if we're unsatisfied with this outcome.

We're planning to go ahead with this but was wondering if anyone here has had to deal with RBI in such cases. We thought to report it to police and cybercrime as well immediately after the fraud happened but ICICI completely hid the transactions so we couldn't even see the transaction IDs.

Any help is appreciated.
