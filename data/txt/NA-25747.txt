For all those people who didn't know about the exorbitant parking fees at Secunderabad Railway Station Bhoiguda entrance ( platform no.10). 

The parking fees for exceeding 35 mins is Rs.500/-. I didn't know it so had to pay it, couldn't even argue with the guy coz it was written right there. Checked online for any solutions but couldn't find any. It feels a lot like a scam but it is what is. So better be safe than sorry.
