This petition under Article 226 of Constitution of India has been
filed against order dated 10/10/2023, by which petitioner has been
placed under suspension. 2. At the very beginning of arguments, it was pointed out by the
Court to the petitioner that since petitioner has not preferred a 2 W.P. No.27162/2023 departmental appeal, therefore this Court would not like to entertain the
petition against the order of suspension directly. 3. It was submitted by counsel for petitioner that as per M.P. Civil
Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1966, appeal would
go to Hon'ble The Governor. However, petitioner has been placed under
suspension for the misdeeds of District Manager, Madhya Pradesh Civil
Supplies Corporation Limited, Katni. It is submitted that petitioner has
been placed under suspension mainly on the ground that for the year
2022-23, M/s Bajrang Rice Mill was entitled for Rs.115/- per quintal for
milling of Paddy, whereas petitioner has made payment at the rate of
Rs.315/- per quintal. It is submitted that petitioner is merely required to
carry out calculation. Thereafter, an OTP is generated which goes to the
mobile of District Manager and only after OTP is disclosed by District
Manager, payment gets transferred. It is submitted that in fact District
Manager is responsible for incorrect payment and petitioner was merely
an instrument and therefore, petitioner has moved an application on
12/10/2023 for review / recall of his suspension order but even said
application has not been decided and it is still pending. 4. Heard learned counsel for the parties. 5. In letter dated 15/09/2023 written by District Manager, Katni, it
was mentioned that M/s Bajrang Rice Mill was entitled for milling
charges at the rate of Rs.115/- per quintal and accordingly, an amount of
Rs.24,89,750/- was to be paid. Whereas, petitioner has made payment to
M/s Bajrang Rice Mill at the rate of Rs.315/- per quintal and in all paid
Rs.68,19,533.50. As a result, an amount of Rs.43,29,783.50 has been
paid in excess. Even otherwise, loading-unloading charges have also
been paid in excess of what was fixed by the Corporation. 6. It is the case of petitioner that it was his duty to carry out the
calculation. Petitioner has been placed under suspension only on the
ground that instead of making payment at the rate of Rs.115/- per
quintal, payment at the rate of Rs.315/- per quintal has been made. It is a
pure case of calculation. Furthermore, merely because some more
persons may also be involved in the scam, is not sufficient to exonerate
petitioner. 7. Since petitioner was not interested to prefer an appeal and insisted
that this Court should decide the petition on merits, therefore merits of
the case have been considered. 8. Suspension is not an order of punishment. Even otherwise, from
show cause notice dated 15/09/2023, it is clear that a departmental
enquiry is under contemplation and under these circumstances, it cannot
be said that suspension of petitioner is not warranted where huge loss
was caused to the Corporation. 9. Accordingly, petition fails and is hereby dismissed. (G.S. AHLUWALIA)
 JUDGE
