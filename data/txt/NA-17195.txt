I bought a t-shirt from Under Armour for my gym, and as with anything, you have to pay processing+insurance+international shipping+customs duty on almost everything that you import. This extra amount is usually paid upfront while you check out at the merchant's website, which I did.

Anyway, Under Armour gave it to Borderfree, and Borderfree gave it to Aramex, and it reached my city in 4 days from New York. Now comes the scam.

Before they delivered, they told me that I had to pay a custom duty of Rs. 1,500 on this product, otherwise it'd be returned. I told them that I had already paid that, and showed them two different receipts (Under Armour + Borderfree).

*"Alright, we'll just send it back, then. We've received an e-mail from someone higher up stating that we absolutely have to get that money from you."*

*"Email from whom? Where's the receipt for the money Aramex paid on my behalf?"*

*"We can't disclose that information."*

I contacted Under Armour and Borderfree, and since they're fucking professional, they replied to me within a minute (on Twitter, and also on Facebook). UA offered to send me a new one (free of cost) via FedEx, and I asked them to wait a couple of hours.

I contacted Aramex's higher ups (got their email from Google), and got a call back saying that their system is showing that the money's already been paid by me, so they don't know why this delivery guy is asking for more. They called him, and I got the product within 10 minutes.

I searched more on this, and Google is full of such cases where they try to basically extort people by lying to them about paying customs duty. I just thought I'd share here in case it helps someone.
