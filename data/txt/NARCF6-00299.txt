So I am writing this to tell everyone about a scam that is going on. 
It is related to crypto and exact name is "exchange rate bug", in this there's a website named "binchange" where one bitcoin is 276 etherium (irl it's actually 27.6) so you need to make a transaction of minimum 0.0029 bitcoin (inr 30k) and they say your etherium will be credited. There is a youtube video and with same comments.

I got fooled of 30k, you all should be careful.
