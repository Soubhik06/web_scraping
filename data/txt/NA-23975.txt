The Bengaluru Police has arrested 10 more Pakistani nationals on charges of staying illegally in India on fake documents, bringing the total number of accused in the matter to 18, police said on Wednesday.

The development follows the arrest of Parvez, the alleged kingpin behind the network involved in creating fake documents for these individuals. Parvez, an Indian national, was reportedly aiding these illegal immigrants.

The accused were reportedly connected to the Mehdi Foundation and living in India using fake passports and forged Hindu identities.

According to the police sources, the investigation revealed that some of the detained Pakistani nationals had been hiding in various parts of the country. Four of them were arrested near Jigani, while three others were taken into custody in Bengaluru's Peenya area.

Parvez, a native of Uttar Pradesh, was arrested for the third time in connection with this case. He has been accused of facilitating fake documentation for Pakistani nationals. His second wife, a resident of Pakistan, is also under police investigation in a separate case.

Following Parvez's arrest, he allegedly told his associates to surrender to the police, resulting in ten Pakistani nationals appearing at the Jigani police station. The police have indicated that more arrests of Pakistani nationals are possible as the investigation unfolds.
