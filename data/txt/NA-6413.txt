 ......... 06/ 29.09.2023. Heard learned counsel for the parties. The petitioner named above has been made accused in connection with
 Cyber P.S. Case No. 20 of 2023 for the offence under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 120B of the Indian Penal Code and Sections 66(B) , 66(C) , 66(D) , 84(C) of I.T Act, pending in the court of Addl. Sessions Judge-II, Deoghar. As per the prosecution case, three mobile phone sets, four debit cards
 along with cash of Rs.23,500/- was seized from the house of this Petitioner. It is submitted by learned counsel for the petitioner that there is no
 material to show that mobile sets that were seized was used in cyber fraud.
 There is no criminal antecedent except in one case which was instituted after
 the instant case. Cash seized were not crime proceeds and were personal
 property. It is submitted that similarly situated co-accused has already been
 enlarged on bail by this Court. Learned counsel for the State has opposed the prayer for bail and
 submitted that the mobile sets seized from the petitioner in which 94 e-wallets
 were created. As per Paras 103 and 112 of the case diary, complaint(s) had
 been received regarding phishing calls having been made by the mobile
 phones seized from this Petitioner. Investigation has revealed his involvement
 in other cases also. Considering the nature of allegation and gravity of offence, I am not
 inclined to enlarge the petitioner on regular bail and accordingly, the same is
 rejected. (Gautam Kumar Choudhary, J.)
