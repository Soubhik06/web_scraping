1. Heard the parties. 2. The petitioner apprehends his arrest in connection with

 Cyber Police Station (Purnia) Case No. 38 of 2023 dated

 22.09.2023 registered under Sections 467 , 468 , 471 , 419 , 420 , 414 , 379 , 201 , 120(B) of the I.P.C. and Section 66(C) , 66(D) of

 the I.T. Act, 2000. 3. The prosecution case in brief is that on 22.09.2023 at

 about 13:10 hours the S.H.O. of Cyber Crime Police Station,

 Purnia received secret information that several suspected

 persons have assembled at the Western gate of Indira Gandhi

 Stadium and they are sitting inside the car and are planning to

 commit cyber crime. The Police at about 14:00 hours reached at
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.33290 of 2024(4) dt.25-09-2024 2/4 the place of occurrence and saw that a white colour car bearing

 Registration No. BR39V/4366 was parked and some persons

 were talking with each other and upon seeing the Police they

 tried to flee away from there but were apprehended. Altogether

 seven persons were arrested. The informant searched the

 apprehended persons and recovered mobiles, Aadhaar Cards,

 PAN Cards, Pass Books of different Banks, Laptop, Finger

 Scanner machine and one white colour I-20 car. One

 apprehended co-accused / Dipak Kumar @ Chhotu disclosed

 that they operate an organized gang of cyber crime by tempting

 innocent persons to open accounts in the Bank and link it with

 their accounts with their mobile for committing cyber crime. He

 further disclosed that screen shots of Aadhaar Cards, PAN

 Cards, Bank Pass Book, ATM Cards and money transactions are

 available in their mobiles. 4. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the

 petitioner is innocent and has been falsely implicated in this

 case on the basis of confessional statement of the arrested co-

 accused Dipak Kumar who has inimical terms with the

 petitioner. He further submits that the petitioner is a bona fide

 person and runs business of providing on-line services to the

 customers. He next submits that the petitioner has got cyber
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.33290 of 2024(4) dt.25-09-2024 3/4 security pledge certificate issued by the Ministry of Electronics

 and Information Technology, Government of India and on the

 basis of that certificate the petitioner started providing on-line

 service to the customers vide UDYAM registration certificate

 issued under the Ministry of Micro Small and Medium

 Enterprises and started running a shop in the name and style of

 Singhiya to provide on-line services to the customers and

 mobile repairing. The petitioner is an on-line service provider

 and has not committed any cyber crime or any other crime nor

 he has issued any Pass Book to any customer and got it linked

 with his mobile. 5. I have heard learned counsel for the parties and perused

 the materials on record. The Police apprehended seven persons

 with incriminating articles which appears to be used in cyber

 crime. The apprehended person has disclosed that they,

 including the petitioner, operate an organized gang for

 committing cyber crime. The Police recovered a large number of

 mobiles, Aadhaar Cards, PAN Cards, Pass Books, ATM Cards,

 Laptops, Finger Scanner Machine from accused persons and the

 fact that all accused persons appears to have formed a gang to

 defraud the innocent public and the phishing/cyber fraud is

 rampant in the area where the attackers try to trick
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.33290 of 2024(4) dt.25-09-2024 4/4 individuals into providing user names, password and other

 details and are indulged in e-mail phishing, voice phishing,

 SMS phishing etc. and the case is still under investigation, for

 which, custodial interrogation of the petitioner may be required,

 I am not inclined to grant the privilege of anticipatory bail to the

 petitioner. The same stands rejected.
