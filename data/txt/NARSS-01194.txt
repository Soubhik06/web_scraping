I've been tracking the crime scenario in Kollam District, Kerala, and it's quite alarming to see the range and frequency of crimes reported here. The Kerala Police websites for both Kollam Rural and Kollam City provide general categories of crimes reported in the area, such as crimes against children, crimes against women, SC ST Atrocities Act cases, cyber cases, missing cases, road accidents, COTPA violations, and POCSO cases.

This raises some serious questions:

What are the root causes leading to such a diverse range of criminal activities in Kollam?

Could socio-economic factors, cultural aspects, or administrative challenges be contributing to this situation?

What are the local authorities doing to address these issues?

Are there any community-based initiatives that have proven effective?
