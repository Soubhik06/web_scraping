I just wanted to share a potential scam I’ve come across while traveling by train over the past few months in Kerala. I’ve personally had 4 incidents where people approached me at different railway stations, asking for actual cash in exchange for transferring money via UPI to them I. 

At first, I didn’t think much of it, but something just feels off. In one instance, I told the person there was an ATM nearby and suggested they withdraw money from there instead, which they didn’t seem too keen on. While I’m not entirely sure what their endgame is, this behavior seems suspicious. 

Maybe I'm just being skeptical, but I wanted to put this out there in case anyone else has had similar experiences. Stay cautious when dealing with money, especially if you’re approached by strangers. If something feels odd, trust your gut! 

Has anyone else experienced this?
