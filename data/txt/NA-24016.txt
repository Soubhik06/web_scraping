Hey everyone,

I wanted to share an alarming experience I had recently to help raise awareness about a type of scam that’s been circulating. I received an email that claimed to have compromising information about me and threatened to send it to my contacts unless I paid a significant sum in Bitcoin.

Here’s a brief overview of the email content:

	•	The sender claimed to have installed malware on a porn website and accused me of accessing similar sites.
	•	They alleged that they had recorded my webcam and gathered personal data, including photos and contacts.
	•	They demanded $950 in Bitcoin to keep this supposed footage private and threatened to send it to my contacts if I didn’t comply.
	•	The email had a specific Bitcoin address for payment and a sense of urgency, warning me to act quickly.

This is a classic example of an extortion scam. The goal is to intimidate you into paying money by making false claims. Here’s what you should do if you receive such an email:

	1.	Do Not Pay: Engaging with the scammer or paying them will not guarantee they won’t come back for more.
	2.	Mark as Spam/Phishing: Use your email provider’s tools to report and block the email.
	3.	Secure Your Accounts: Update your passwords and enable two-factor authentication.
	4.	Report the Scam: Notify your email provider and local authorities about the scam.

Stay vigilant and be cautious of any unsolicited emails that try to create fear or pressure you into action. Always verify the legitimacy of such claims before taking any steps.

Stay safe!
