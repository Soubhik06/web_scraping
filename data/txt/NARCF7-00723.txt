So in morning while i was playing game.. Ek call aaya there was someone telling me - "Beta aapke papa ne number diya hai. Unhone bola aapke account mein 12k bhejne hai". So, papa normally apne dosto se mere account mein transactions karwate rehte hai, toh maine bol diya haa uncle aap kardo issi number pe..

So woh kehne lage beta phle main 10k bhej deta hu and then remaining 2k.. Though i forced him ki pure ek saath hi bhej do.. Tukdo mein bhejne ki kya need hai... He still forces ki 10 and 2 karke bhejenge and i was like theek hai uncle jldi bhejo mujhe kaam hai..

And now main thing begins phle unhone 10k bheje and then 2k ki jagah 20k. Bhej diye and rhen unka call aaya ki beta galti se 18k extra aa gye toh woh aap wapas karwa do... Toh na actually mere pe Bank se message aaya tha ki paise aa gye hai... Still Maine Papa ko ek baar call kiya ki papa aapne kisi ko paise daalne ke liye bola hai account mein.... And papa was like nhi toh kon hai woh.. Maine kaha ki mujhe nhi pata... Keh rhe the ki aapke dost hai... Maine bola thik main puch ke btaunga..

I called him and he was started like Like Acha aap Ashok ke bete nhi ho.. Ohoho i am sorry beta galti se aapko bhej diye and aap pure 30k wapas kardo.. And maine abhi tak Upi check nhi kiya tha khol ke.. I was just relying on messages i recieved... Toh mujhe laga shyd genuinely mistake ho gyi ho... 

Maine Phone pe on kara and check kiya toh like koi payment receive nhi hu.. Mujhe laga shyd koi glitch hoga and abhi update na hua ho. So maine Bank ke official app pe bhi check kiya.. But waha bhi koi payment show nhi kiya.. Toh maine bola ki mere pass koi payment nhi aayi.

Woh banda jid pe add gya ki aapke pass payment gayi hai 30k mere yaha se gaye hai paise ab aap mujhe do.. Maine usse apne balance ka screenshot diya ki nhi aaya kuch mere pe... And maine bola ki agar aapse payment gayi hai toh... Aap wrong number par transaction ki complaint kardo bank wale aapko paise de denge... But woh maana nhi as soon as i said ki thik main official complaint file karta hu that you are trying to scam me... 

Woh block karke chala gya
