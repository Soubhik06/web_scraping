There is an ongoing scam in Bhubaneswar involving fraudulent ‘babas’ who exploit people, especially educated individuals. Despite their education, many of these victims are ignorant and have little to no knowledge of spirituality or witchcraft. These so-called *pravachaks* and *babas* attract followers, most of whom are wealthy but unaware of the dangers of witchcraft.

These self-proclaimed spiritual gurus appear knowledgeable and convincing, but in reality, they are dangerous frauds. They use *vasikaran* (a form of black magic) and witchcraft to manipulate people, extort money, and even cause illnesses—some victims have even died as a result.

The biggest misconception is that witchcraft only means ghosts and horror, as seen in movies. These scammers take advantage of this ignorance. They often claim that witchcraft is fake or that it doesn’t exist, misleading their followers. Worse, those who are actually affected by it often don’t even realize they are victims.

When people question these frauds or demand their money back, they resort to witchcraft to silence them. They also conduct programs on TV, spreading fear about global destruction to manipulate and attract more followers. Once someone visits them, they find ways to exploit and loot them.

Stay alert and don’t fall for these scams. Ignorance can be dangerous.
