Not sure which subreddit I should be posting this on. Suggestions are welcome.

So basically, my flatmate (23/F) and I (22/F) moved into this Pune flat about a month ago and got the house through a broker. I was clearly looking for a house with no restrictions and had made this clear to the broker right in the beginning, and was also willing to pay a higher rent for the same. When he gave us the house, he said, "It's your house, you can do what you want; there are no restrictions." He even joked about how this house is perfect for parties. My flatmate's boyfriend was with us when we went to check out the house. In fact, he was the witness for our contract signing.

**TL;DR at bottom**

**BACKSTORY**

2-3 days into moving in, the watchman starts harassing mine and my flatmate's male friends saying they're not allowed to enter the society. He changes the rules according to his convenience, sometimes saying they're not allowed at all, other times that they're not allowed after 11 PM. We tell him to get us in contact with the chairman of the society so we can talk this out. He says yes, but keeps delaying the matter.

Meanwhile, I continue to get my friends home cause I knew I was legally right in doing so. There was nothing in my agreement about this. I haven't signed any document that says that I won't get male guests into the house. I was not even informed about this before signing the agreement even though the caretaker had met my flatmate's boyfriend.

A few days later, the watchman sees me and friend go up to my house. The time is around 10.30 PM. A strange woman comes to my doorstep (she is neither the chairman nor the secretary). When I open the door, she doesn't bother to address me, instead looking at my friend says to him in a rude voice "I'm sorry but you need to leave right now". I ask her to speak to me. This sets her off and she starts screaming at me and threatening me about how I'll be evicted if I don't follow the "rules". I try to tell her that her "rules" are restricting our freedom of movement. I explain that the broker didn't tell us anything. I tell her my agreement doesn't say anything. I tell her that this is not a hostel or a PG where she can impose such rules on me. She keeps reiterating to me that these are the rules and I must follow them, with no other explanation. We decide to speak to the owner.

We have never seen the owner; she lives in Mumbai. She wasn't at the signing of the agreement. She has never spoken to us on the phone. She has a guy who acts as a caretaker for all her properties. We ask him for the owner's number. He doesn't reply. We ask the broker, who says he will send it and then doesn't.

A few days later, my other broker (female) calls me and says what I'm doing is not allowed. This time I clearly state how I'm legally right in doing so and tell her that I will sue everyone. She tells me not to do anything rash. A day or so later, the caretaker comes home because there are repairs to be done. We talk to him about the incidents so far and he says the society members gave him a call. He says, in these exact words, that the same woman called him and he asked her if there was a party/loud noises/nuisance being caused. When she said no, he told her then there is no problem with friends coming over. So we continue getting friends over. Watchman continues to harass them. We ignore him.

**Now coming to what happened last night.**

Friend gets done with work at 10. We plan to have dinner and catch up on Game of Thrones at my place. My cat had run away, so we go looking for him together, find him, and enter the society. The watchman loses it when he sees my friend and says he's not allowed inside. Tired of this shit, I tell him I want to speak to the chairman and resolve this once and for all. He tells me to go to the chairman's house, which I refuse to do. I tell him to call the chairman downstairs so we can speak. My cat is struggling too much so I go home to drop him off, while my friend waits in the parking area. While I'm gone, the chairman shows up and tells my friend to leave as "it is late" according to society rules (it's 10:20). My friend asks why and he just walks away without a word.

When I go back down, the watchman says the chairman will be back in soon and makes us wait until it is almost 11 PM. My flatmate is waiting with us. At this point we're starving and tell the watchman we need to eat, so we're going up. We tell him to come up and call us when the chairman is ready to talk.

Barely 10 minutes later (now it is "officially" past the "deadline"), 3 people from the society show up with the watchman. None of them are the chairman. 2 of them are men we've never seen before; the 3rd is the woman from the previous incident. They start off the conversation (if it can be called that) in an extremely aggressive manner. They are saying it's past 11 PM so my friend should not be here. I explain to them that we were in fact waiting to talk to them, but they aren't having any of it. Unbeknownst to them, my flatmate is recording the conversation on her phone, as decided by us previously. One of them goes to the extent to say that he'll drag my friend out of the house. 2 minutes into this, the same guy threatens to call the cops. My flatmate tells him to go ahead, as we think he's bluffing. We watch him make a call as they all leave fuming.

After some time, someone is banging on our door. It's almost 12 by now. I open the door to two male cops. I inform them that they cannot enter my house without a female cop; they enter my house anyway. One of them is video recording us and not even being lowkey about it. The other cop tells my friend to come to the police station with him as there has been a complaint against him. He refuses to say what the complaint is, says "you'll find out when we get there". While we try to explain the situation to them, my friend takes out his phone and starts video-taping the situation for our own safety. (Note that this is inside my own house) The cops get pissed off and ask him to delete it, trying to snatch the phone from him.

I know that they can use excessive force against him so I snatch the phone from him myself. This doesn't deter the cop and he comes close to me in a threatening manner, almost grabbing my arm to take the phone away. I run into my room, and get the rent agreement and police verification with me, which I then show him. I tell him what the society is doing is harassment. He says I have no authority to say such things and threatens to arrest me. He questions my character and says it is wrong for a man who's not related to me to be in my house at this time. I tell him we asked my friend to stay because we were scared for our safety after the way the society members behaved.

The society people also come now and enter our house without permission. Everything goes to shit. The woman is making it about her kids, saying they understand "what you're doing". The cops are screaming at my friend. The crazy guy is incessantly screaming in Marathi that the cops have proof and hence must arrest my friend. Nobody lets us even complete our sentences and speaks over us loudly, as they have the majority. My friend and I don't understand Marathi, only my flatmate does. So we request them to please speak in Hindi/English. The woman says they are Maharashtrians hence will continue to speak in Marathi.

Everything is chaotic. Everyone is screaming at each other. The cop is trying to arrest my friend. The woman starts screaming at me when I mention freedom of movement, saying "there is no such thing as freedom of movement".

This situation is stressing me out too much so I tell the society people to please leave the house. The crazy man is still pressuring the cops to arrest my friend, even though they now seem reluctant to do so. They want us to apologize, tell my friend to leave, and end this drama. We agree and leave the house. Flatmate and I go downstairs with them to make sure my friend is okay.

Despite of this, the crazy guy hasn't had enough. He wants to escalate the situation further and calls up some higher up cop who's a friend of his. He joins us at the gate. They are now saying they can't let my friend go so easily and are telling the cops in Marathi to take him away and "do what is needed", which clearly means to beat him black and blue.

I have informed my dad (who lives in another state) about the situation and by this time, he freaks out and calls a family friend to handle this. Said friend comes over and cops start questioning him about his relation to me. When they find out we're not related by blood, they want to arrest him as well. He did not even step into the society.

At this point they are plain bullying me. Everytime I try to state laws, they laugh at me, saying that these laws don't exist. My friend tells me and my flatmate to go home and that he'll handle this. We watch the rest from my balcony. They harass my friends for another 30 minutes or so, after which a police van pulls up, right when my friends were about to leave. I see my friend get dragged out of his car and slapped by the cop. Note that all of this is happening outside the society now.

Eventually we see the cops leave - both the jeep and the original two cops, who are on a bike. Unsurprisingly, the ones on the bike take a u-turn and come right back at the gate and proceed to have some shady exchange with the society guys. They shake hands and leave.

Now I feel threatened for my safety. The law is not on my side as I learnt clearly yesterday. It was downright harassment and there is nothing I can do about it. The cherry on top was the broker calling me in the morning to yell at me, saying that "society rules" are not restrictions. He wants us to apologize for disturbing the "harmony" and then get on with life as though nothing happened. Owner is still missing from the picture.

**TL;DR**

Broker refuses to acknowledge that society rules are "restrictions". Society members don't care about the broker's fuckup or the fact that the agreement doesn't mention any such rules. They refuse to give these rules in writing, but are willing to call the cops on us and enter our house at odd hours, making us feel unsafe in our own home. Any guy that enters our house henceforth could be arrested and beaten up. Caretaker/owner refuse to get involved.
