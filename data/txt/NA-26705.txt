Last year an agent sold us a TATA AIA ULIP policy fraudulently. The fraud agent promised over 12% assured XIRR and when we got the policy which differed, he deceived us by calling in the name of Tata AIA and confirming that the assured returns are correct. We realized it a few months later and called Tata AIA but they said they can't do anything. Then I came across a post by a fellow redditor who got a policy cancelled and suggested to complain on bima bharosa portal. 

We did that and followed up for over 3 months. We had over 15 service requests with Tata AIA and they never responded to any of them properly. Even the bima bharosa complain was closed shoddily despite submitting call recording with the agent. The funny thing was when we raised the complaint with Tata AIA the agent was aware and kept calling us to reassure that his assured returns are correct.

But after 3 months of follow up, 20+ emails, 10 tweets, 2 bima bharosa complaints, finally they accepted the misselling and agreed to cancel the policy and refund the money. 

Really happ my to get out of this shit. Anyone else who's stuck in something like this, please go through bima bharosa portal, it really works.
