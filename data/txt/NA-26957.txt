--- POST TEXT ---

Yesterday, on July 21st (Sunday), I received a call from an unknown number: +916002494516. The number seemed suspicious. The caller greeted me by saying, "Hello Chandrakumar," which is my father's name. I responded, and he claimed my father asked him to send ₹2000 to my account. I suggested he use my UPI number.He said he would send ₹1 first to confirm and then asked me to verify an SMS notification. I received a message saying, "Your a/c no. XXXXXXXX1803 is credited for ₹1.00 on 21-07-24 12:25:48 (UPI Ref no 418340487309)." Below adding screenshot for the same. After this, he asked me to confirm a ₹2000 acknowledgment, but I received a message for ₹20000 instead. He then claimed he mistakenly sent ₹20000 instead of ₹2000 and requested I return ₹18000. He provided a PhonePe number, 7064505567, registered under 'Anjali Sahai', asking me to transfer the money there.I told him I needed to confirm with my father first and ended the call. My father denied asking anyone to send me money, confirming it was a scam. I checked my bank balance and recent transactions, and no money was received. The scammer called again, urging me to send back ₹18000, claiming he was in the hospital and needed it urgently. I told him I would report his number to the cyber cell, and he hung up.I immediately tried to contact the Maharashtra cyber cell, but my call wasn't answered. I'm sharing this to raise awareness about such scams. Stay vigilant and safe.
