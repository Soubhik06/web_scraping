#Nirmal Bharat Abhiyan 

- **Railways Bio-Toilets** 
Modi made a lot of noise about Bio-Toilets for Railways. But it was under UPA in March 2010 that Indian Railways &amp; DRDO started working on technology for developing Bio-Toilets. In Jan 2011, they ran the first train with bio-toilets - Gwalior Varanasi Bundelkhand Express. In 2011-12, 67 coaches were fitted with BioToilets, 546 coaches in 2012-13.

#FDI 

[FDI was flat during the Vajpayee's time](https://tradingeconomics.com/india/foreign-direct-investment). Manmohanji grew FDI continously. 

[India was the world's second most favoured investment destination in 2005, 2006, 2007, 2008 and 2010](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/economy/indicators/india-slips-to-4th-place-in-unctads-fdi-destination-ranking/articleshow/37136228.cms)
