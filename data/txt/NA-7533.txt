Heard counsel for the parties. This bail application has been filed by the applicant, who
has been arrested on 16.02.2015 and is in Jail in connection
with Crime No.2/2014 registered with Police Station S.T.F.,
Bhopal - commonly known as VYAPAM Examination Scam
cases - for the offence punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 474 and 120-B of IPC and also under Section
3(Gha) (1, 2/4 of the M.P. Manyata Prapt Pariksha
Adhiniyam, 1937. The role ascribed to the applicant by the prosecution is
one of beneficiary. The applicant with the help of middleman
and racketeer indulged in unfair means during the said
examination. After the arrest of applicant, the impersonator
Ajit Kashyap and middlemen Jai Karan and Arun Sikarwar
have been arrested. It is, fairly, stated by the learned counsel for the State, on
instructions, that custodial interrogation of the applicant is no
more required. Charge-sheet has also been filed against the
applicant. Further, the investigation against the applicant is
complete in all respects as of now and, therefore, the presence
 of applicant is no more required. In that sense, the link
between the accused persons is already complete. Further, the
applicant is not involved in any other crime of similar type.
Besides, the applicant through counsel undertakes to appear
before the Investigating Officer as and when called for further
investigation including against him as also to punctually
appear before the trial Court to ensure early disposal of the
criminal trial. In the interest of justice, taking overall view of the
matter, we deem it appropriate to grant bail to the applicant on
following strict conditions:- The bail application stands disposed of accordingly.
