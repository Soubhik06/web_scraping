From the past year or so scam calls have increased so much. I receive close to 3-4 such calls everyday, 99% of the time Truecaller warns about it, and I usually ignore an unknown number.

Same has been reported by my parents and my brother. It's strange that we receive more scam calls than just regular calls. 

I was about to slip up once where in I was expecting a parcel and I received an automated call saying that my parcel is stuck and to press 1 for more information. I did so and a person asked my name to check for parcel details (1st red flag) and then mentions I have a parcel from Taiwan which led me to immediately cut the call.

My dad theorizes that these scam calls increase during elections as a means to funnel money into political parties but this feels more of a WhatsApp University oped. 

I anyway don't have much friends, so I can get away with not responding to unknown numbers 99% of the time.

Has this experience been consistent with others too?
