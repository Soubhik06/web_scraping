I have encountered an app called HP where you basically invest some amount and after some period ( let's say 30, 33, 45, 60 days) your investment will be tripled or quadruple even. You will be getting a certain amount each day which you have to receive and then the amount will be transferred to your wallet and then you need to withdraw that money which will take 24-72 hours as per the app. Even though I knew it could be a scam the lust or greed of money got me. I have invested in this app and got money for some days and now I am not receiving any money since the 19th of April, they have introduced a thing called tax card where they said the withdrawal can be made only if you have purchased the tax card. Sure enough I have bought it and received no money. There are at least 10 whatsapp groups regarding this app whereas I'm in just 2 of them. So many people have invested so much money and lost it. A couple of people have killed themselves too. Please beware of these scams. I'll post some pics for reference.
