Hey Delhi folks, just wanted to share a really concerning experience I had with Lenskart.

I bought a pair of specs from a Lenskart store here in Delhi 3 days ago. Today, I got a feedback link via SMS from Lenskart to rate the store. I gave them an 8 out of 10—which I thought was decent.

But later in the evening, I got a WhatsApp message from the employee who handled my order, literally begging me to change my rating to a 9 or 10 because “8 is considered negative” by the company. He even sent me a new link (which didn’t work).

My question is: how did this employee get my personal number?

I never consented to being contacted. It’s clear Lenskart’s system either gives staff access to customer phone numbers tied to ratings, or they’re just freely sharing that info.

This is a serious privacy breach, and possibly a violation of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act. Feedback should be anonymous. I should not be getting pressured personally just for giving an honest 8.

If this is how they treat our data for something as small as feedback, imagine what else is happening behind the scenes.

Has anyone else in Delhi faced this with Lenskart or other retail chains?

Let’s talk about it. This kind of thing shouldn’t be normalized.
