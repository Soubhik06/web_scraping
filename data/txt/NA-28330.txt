New Delhi, Apr 1 (PTI) Taking cognisance of a video circulating online, the Delhi Police has identified and initiated legal action against a man for driving on the wrong side of the Dwarka Expressway, an official said on Wednesday.
“Police traced the vehicle and its driver and registered a case under Section 281 (rash and negligent driving) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS),” a senior police officer said.
The incident came to light after a dash-cam video surfaced on social media showing a vehicle, suspected to be a taxi, being driven on the wrong side of the Dwarka Expressway. The vehicle suddenly appears in front of another car that was moving in the correct lane, forcing it to halt.
