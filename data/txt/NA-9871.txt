Heard. The petitioners seek quashing of the order dated Patna High Court Cr.Misc. No.32771 of 2011 (10) dt.01-12-2014 2/4 29.08.2011 passed by the learned Chief Judicial Magistrate,

 Siwan in connection with Complaint Case No. 1094 of 2011,

 whereby cognizance has been taken under Sections 406 , 418 and 420 of the Indian Penal Code. It has been alleged by the opposite party no. 2 that he

 was known to the petitioners from before and only on their

 assurance that huge profits would be reaped by him, he became

 agreeable to invest some money in the shares. It has further been

 alleged that shares which were purchased by the accused persons

 were allegedly kept in the demat account of theirs and the

 assurance kept coming from the accused persons that later such

 shares would be transferred in the name of the investor i.e. the

 complainant. It has also been stated in detail in the complaint

 petition that the name of the investment company was changed

 every now and then and ultimately the issue of the complainant

 having been cheated came to the fore only when he visited the

 Angel Broker Center. It was learnt by the complainant that no

 money was deposited in the demat account of the complainant.

 This complaint led to institution of the Complaint Case No. 1094

 of 2011. Learned counsel for the petitioners submitted that an

 absolutely false case has been lodged by the opposite party no. 2. Patna High Court Cr.Misc. No.32771 of 2011 (10) dt.01-12-2014 3/4 Investment were made in the year, 2001 but the case came to be

 filed in the year, 2011 i.e. after a lapse of about 10 years. It has

 further been submitted by the learned counsel for the petitioners

 that some of the petitioners are complete outsiders to the

 transaction between the complainant and the person who had

 assured that good benefits would come to him if he invested

 money in shares. Further submissions on behalf of the petitioners is that

 without opening of the demat account, no fraud could be played

 on any investor and unless the formalities are complied with, the

 shares cannot be transferred or sold. Learned counsel appearing on behalf of the opposite

 party no. 2, however, states that till date there has not been any

 transfer of the shares in the account of the petitioners. With respect to delay in lodging the case, it has been

 stated by the learned counsel for the complainant that though the

 investments were made in the year 2001 it was only much later

 that the complainant came to learn about his having been cheated

 when he met some persons at Angel Broker Center. Learned counsel for the Complainant further states that

 because of the name of the firm being changed every now and

 then, the complainant could not ascertain as to when and how Patna High Court Cr.Misc. No.32771 of 2011 (10) dt.01-12-2014 4/4 should he approach the accused persons. After having considered the submissions made on

 behalf of the parties, this Court is of the opinion that such factual

 issues could be wrested out only at the appropriate stage of

 framing of the charge. The learned Court below has not at all

 faulted in taking cognizance as against the petitioners for the

 offence under Sections 406 , 418 and 420 of the Indian Penal Code. The application has no merits and the same is

 dismissed. (Ashutosh Kumar, J)
