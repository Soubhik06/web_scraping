Just wanted to share a scam attempt that almost happened to me on OLX. Someone contacted me about an item I was selling, we agreed on the price, and even had a call to discuss. After that, they sent me a QR code on WhatsApp (for ₹5) and asked me to scan it to "confirm" the deal.

Something felt off, so we told them they could just pay at the time of delivery instead. But they kept insisting on scanning the QR code, which made us even more suspicious. We quickly searched online and found several articles talking about this exact scam happening in Bangalore.

The WhatsApp number they used was +91 6002554494, and it was under the name "Star Furniture" on whatsapp — the same name mentioned in many scam reports.

Just wanted to put this out there so others don’t fall for it. Be careful, never scan any random QR codes, and always stick to safe payment methods during delivery.
