 1.0. This is an application under Section 439 Cr.P.C read with Section 482 Cr.P.C for grant of regular bail in case FIR no. 276/2020, under Sections
 376 / 120-B / 376-D Indian Penal Code, 1860 and under Sections 4 / 6 POCSO
 Act, PS Laxmi Nagar. (Charge sheet filed for the offences under Sections
 376 / 376-D / 506 / 120-B / 201 Indian Penal Code, 1860 and under Sections 4 / 6 Of POCSO Act). 2.0. Ld. Counsel for the petitioner submits that the FIR was registered
 after a delay of 25 months and the petitioner has been falsely implicated in
 the matter ; Petitioner is in judicial custody since 09.06.2020. Ld. Counsel
 also submits that as per the FSL report, the petitioner is not the biological
 father of the child delivered by the prosecutrix. 2.1 Ld. Counsel further submits that the prosecutrix, her parental aunt as
 well as father have already been examined and nothing substantive has come
 up against the petitioner. Further, as the material witnesses in the matter
 have already been examined, there is no possibility of the petitioner
 influencing these witnesses/tampering with evidence. The petitioner thus
 deserves to be released on bail. 3.0. Per contra, this application is strongly opposed by the Ld.
 Prosecutor. It is submitted that there are serious allegations of continuous
 sexual assault/exploitation of the prosecutrix by the petitioner along with co-
 accused persons by blackmailing her, by threatening to circulate her nude
 videos. Its only when the prosecutrix became pregnant, she revealed the
 same to her bua, who in turn informed the prosecutrix's father. Only
 thereafter, the legal action was taken, which explains the delay in
 registration of the FIR. 3.1. Ld. Prosecutor also submits that no data could be extracted/retrieved
 from the petitioner's seized mobile phone as it was PIN protected. Ld.
 Proseuctor also submits that the petitioner was duly identified by the
 prosecutrix as one of the culprits. 3.2 Ld. Prosecutor further submits that the prosecutrix as well as her
 father and bua, the key witnesses have supported the prosecution case. Ld.
 Prosecutor also submits that trial in the matter is taking place expeditiously
 and 8 out of 17 witnesses, have already been examined and the matter is
 now fixed for 13.11.2022 for further prosecution evidence. 3.3 Ld. Prosecutor submits that as far as the FSL report is concerned,
 DNA report qua three (out of four) of the accused persons has already been
 received; the FSL report qua accused Suraj is still awaited. 4.0. I have duly considered the submissions made by both the sides. 5.0. There are serious allegations against the petitioner. Not going into the
 detailed appreciation of the evidence, suffice it to be state that the
 prosecutrix and other material witnesses examined so far, have apparently
 supported the prosecution case. In view of the same and considering the
 above facts and circumstances in entirety, I am of the considered opinion
 that the petitioner does not deserve to be released on bail at at this stage. 6.0. The bail application is accordingly, dismissed. 7.0. The pending applications, if any, are closed. 8.0. A copy of this order be given dasti under the signatures of Court
 Master. POONAM A. BAMBA, J
