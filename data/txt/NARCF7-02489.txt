राम राम सा!🙏🏻

We all hoard a lot of stuff that we don't need, and someone could really use it. 
That's why we are starting this thread, where our users can list image of their things in comments, and if anyone really needs it, can request in the replies, decide to meet on some public place and collect the goods.

Remember:
• Don't exchange phone numbers, even in DMs.
• Don't giveaway residential addresses, meet at public places with lots of people around.
• Beware of financial scams.
• DO NOT REQUEST FOR MONEY, POST ONLY THINGS TO GIVEAWAY.
• Actively report users who are sus.

This post will be pinned, you can turn on notifications for this post. It is a version one initiative, let's see if it succeeds.
