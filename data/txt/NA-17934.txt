My wife forwarded a message to me today saying that our electricity bill was unpaid, electricity will be cut off today and to call/WhatsApp an officer at a given number.

I scrambled to pay the bill (noticed it wasn't due yet) and whatsapped the payment receipt to the 'officer'. He called me back after a while and said that your payment is not reflecting in the system yet, and to update it so electricity is not cut off. How to update? He tod me to download an app on my phone (anydesk). Ofcourse, that's when I realised it was a phishing kind of scam and kicked myself for believing it so far.

I'm a very tech savvy person, used to hack software in the 90s for fun, and I fell for it (partly because the message was forwarded, I probably would have noticed that it's from a private number and not a commercial sender code if I had received it directly). But goes to show social engineering can really pull anyone's handles. Watch out everyone &amp; warn your loved ones.
