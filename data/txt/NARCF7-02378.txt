Fake Apple products are being sold throughout the country alongside national highways and public places. Last week I made a fool of myself by buying a counterfeit master copy Apple AirPod from a guy at a petrol pump on the Pune Bangalore highway.

To my surprise, the AirPod was packaged well and the packaging and box looked genuine. When I checked the serial number on the Apple website the product was listed send all the information was showing.

&amp;#x200B;

[Looks and feel legit though](https://preview.redd.it/zwtn4y1yxzt91.png?width=3840&amp;format=png&amp;auto=webp&amp;s=dd25e50660297b60933d6e78ea10a154aff6c106)

The guy was saying that he took out the AirPods from the container and he even has an Apple Watch. I purchased these two units for Rs.10,000 rupees (I feel like a jackass 🐴right now 🫣). After two days of trying to change the tip, the AirPods popped out. I saw the internals of this device I immediately understood that this was a fake product.

&amp;#x200B;

[Even apple MacBook recognizes as original Airpod](https://preview.redd.it/69mrve3xxzt91.png?width=3840&amp;format=png&amp;auto=webp&amp;s=0720f6fe419949abd7f90caf038dd23d2c730dfe)

These people are selling these AirPods throughout the highways and public places in India. Not everyone is a fool like me, but if I can help a single person through this post and video, that will be a win for me. Please be safe from such people and as soon as you see them don't even interact with them. Not only AirPods Pro they also have a master copy Apple Watch.

Please be safe and do share this video with others so that this information can be spread throughout the country. Share the message with others🙏🏻
