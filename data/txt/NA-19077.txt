so, I received a message from WhatsApp about working from home. 

I decided to play along, She told me to ping on Telegram and so I did, The job was to give 5-star ratings on Google Review and I would receive 50 rupees per review. I thought there was no harm in doing so, I gave my account details which hardly had any money left there.

They gave me 5 tasks to give 5 stars to some shops and hotels in Gujarat. I did so. 

And bravely, I received money. How idiotic! so far so good, I know such things also happen for Amazon, Google Maps, etc. 

But the next task was to invest in BitCoin for a few seconds and I will receive around 30% more money back. 

I thought I could afford to lose 1K money just in case, but I would learn a new scam and decided to pay along. I was told to create a normal account on a Bitcoin website just with an email and password. so I did. 

I was told to buy BC worth 1K and wait for the timeout and I will see 1200 in my wallet. I did so too.

after 120 seconds timeout happened and I saw the amount in my wallet. 

I knew the scam would happen now, and I could not withdraw from this account to my bank account. 

I was told to send the screenshot and so I did, But somehow I received the amount. 

I asked her(maybe him/them) how it works, and this is what she replied. 

*"Prepaid orders help to stabilize Bitcoin. For example, if you hold a currency that is used by many people, you can ensure price stability. We are helping the merchant generate real data and the merchant will pay us a commission when it is completed."*

I could not understand much and I left the conversation. 

Please beware of such people. *Janhit me jaari.*
