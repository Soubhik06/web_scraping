This morning I was going from Chd to Kullu. So, I went to Sec 15 petrol pump opp PGGC-11 for fuelling up my car. As I was carrying a Kinnauri cap on my parcel tray the petrol pump worker greeted me and called me “aate saheb” (big brother in kinnauri language) to win my trust. I told him to fuel up my car and then a guy from another side came and started to distract me by cleaning my car’s window and suddenly I saw the fuel meter started from the amount Rs. 200. I confronted the guy but he denied, I stood firm on my stance and threatened to call the police on him. Then he apologised and requested me to not create a scene as this would lead to his termination. As I was getting late I did not complain and just paid him 200 less. I don’t know if the owner is involved in this scam.
