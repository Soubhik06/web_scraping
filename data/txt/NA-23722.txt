I was planning to buy a used 2 wheeler. 

Here's what i found out during my ordeal:

- Most ads online are scams
- People are expecting crazy prices, asking 70-80% of purchase price for 5-6 year old vehicles
- Organised sector - Credr and Beepkart are way too overpriced
- Vijayanagar 2 wheeler market is full of scams; lot of people said they sell stolen vehicles and they steal from you after few months. 

I will either save up to buy a new one or continue to struggle without last mile connectivity. I have given up on this search. 

Any suggestions to overcome this would be helpful.
