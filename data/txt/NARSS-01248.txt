This is a post about **Greenlight Immigration Services**

Just posting here, as this company is fooling people. 

Link to reviews : [https://jsdl.in/DT-25QIIMUYUY6](https://jsdl.in/DT-25QIIMUYUY6)

 
Last week my friend called me and said, one of their friend is trying to migrate to Sweden or Finland, through this company.

Initially the sounded genuine, its a company registered in Nordic, and they will provide work permit etc. There are 2 options either to work in Sweden or Finland.

I asked them to enquire about the company where they are going to work, just to make sure everything is genuine. But The were not ready to provide the names. 

Then I asked them if they are asking for any money, found that they have asked for 5 lakhs. As further reading on move to Sweden pages, the work permit would only cost around 2000 SEK and 1500 SEK for dependent.

Which is less than 30,000 Rupees. Even if they charge processing charge 1 lakh rupees. They can make a decent money.

But looting people with these huge amount of 5 lakhs and no guarantee which company you work.

I don't understand why do educated **fools** fall for this. The person mentioned in this story is working in TCS and has decent experience.

 
In this world of internet, just a quick google on the company and every one can see the reviews. Without making sure if they are frauds or not, people blindly pay money.

Everybody has dreams and that's great, at least people should do minimum research especially when moving to foreign places. How to obtain visa, what is work permit, who applies for a work permit. All these details are available on internet. Plus since many has migrated, its quite easy to find some one to talk to.

Just sharing here, so that some one might find this useful.
