--- POST TEXT ---

So this just happened to me last week. I booked a Ola to Bangalore Airport and to my surprise the driver didn't call me when he reached my location. I was having my breakfast and didn't look at the phone for any updates from the App. But I thought the driver is taking unusually long to reach, so I just called him and he replied saying he's been waiting at my location for more than 10mins. I apologized and said I'll be there.

And as soon I sat in the cab, the driver complained that the screen in the cab is not working. He tried for more than 15mins and later I complained that I need to reach airport soon. He then asked me to cancel the ride as he won't be put in the OTP without the screen starting. I told him try with your phone, but he said it works only on the screen. He said he will drop me to airport at the same fare as shown by Ola but I need to cancel the ride. 

I said I cannot cancel the ride as it's showing 75rs cancellation charges. But eventually after looking at the clock I decided I'll just get out and book a Uber but still not cancel the Ola and see what happens. And as soon as I stepped out of the cab, I get a notification saying driver cancelled ride after waiting for me. 

This is when I realised this was all a scam of not giving the commission to Ola for the ride and charging the entire amount to customer and making profit. People be aware of Ola drivers, they are actually getting creative with their scams!
