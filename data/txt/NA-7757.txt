the order rejecting anticipatory bail to the petitioner passed by the

Additional Sessions Judge, Palwal, on 18.06.2022 would show that in

paragraph 5 of the same it has been argued on behalf of the petitioner that

the I-card of the petitioner had been misused by some persons. The said

argument raised before the Additional Sessions Judge, Palwal, has neither

been raised nor substantiated before this Court. The petitioner is prima

facie involved in the alleged offence. With respect to the delay of 7 days in registration of the FIR, it

is the case of the complainant that after receiving the call on 11.01.2022, he

had made payments in installments at various points in time and it is

thereafter, that he had filed a complaint on 19.01.2022. At any rate, in view

of the seriousness of the offence, the said delay would not entitle the

petitioner to grant of anticipatory bail. Keeping in view the above said facts and circumstances, the

present petition for anticipatory bail is dismissed. Nothing stated above shall be construed as an expression of
