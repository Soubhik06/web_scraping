Accused- Sanjay Chouhan is in custody in a case registered under Sections 467 , 468 , 471 , 419 , 420 / 34 of the Indian Penal Code and Sections
 66(C) / 66(D) of Information Technology Act in connection with Baghmara P.S.
 Case No.04 of 2019. It is submitted on behalf of the petitioner that petitioner is in custody
 since 10.01.2019 and he has no criminal antecedent. It is further submitted that case
 relates to cloning of A.T.M. and withdrawal of cash and petitioner is named in the
 F.I.R. at serial no.3 on the basis of confessional statement of co-accused Rahul
 Kumar. Learned counsel further submitted that co-accused Manoj Kumar @ Mauji
 and Amit Kumar Singh have already been granted bail by Bench of this Court in
 B.A. No.3597 of 2019 and B.A. No.4338 of 2019 respectively and case of this
 petitioner stands on better footing with the co-accused who have already been
 granted bail. He further submitted that after submission of charge sheet
 cognizance has been taken in this case. Learned A.P.P. for the State opposed the prayer for bail and submitted
 that petitioner used to commit cyber crime and there is recovery of mobiles, SIMs.
 ATM cards, skimmer, Aadhar Cards etc. which would appear from Paragraphs 14,
 15, 19, 20, 39, 40 and 41 of case diary. Perused the case diary. Considering the fact that co-accused have been
 granted bail and taking the period of custody, accused- Sanjay Chouhan is directed
 to be released on bail on furnishing bail bond of Rs.20,000/-(Twenty Thousand)
 with two sureties of the like amount each to the satisfaction of court of Sub
 Divisional Judicial Magistrate, Dhanbad in connection with Baghmara P.S. Case
 No.04 of 2019. (B.B. Mangalmurti, J.)
