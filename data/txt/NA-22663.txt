One of my friend's father was working in a bank as a manager and after Covid many mortgage loans started going NPA because the loans were taken by many different persons on behalf of an agent who was using the loans for funding his business and used to pay loans on their behalf and provide them additional money over the loan account. For example, for a loan of 100 rs. You would get 3 to 4% of interest per month and the agent would pay the entire loan on their behalf. And these people saw easy money and willingly took the loans and mortgaged their property. The agent used to take the loan and transfer the money in different accounts using these people's checkbooks and original signs. The complainants were quite aware of the entire process. 

After the 2nd wave, the agent's cashflow was severely disturbed and he could not pay back many of the loans. Bank's management was changed, my friend's father retired from the bank and the new management started going after the NPA accounts and started collecting the underlying properties through Sarfaesi act. 

That's when many of these people who were willing to take the loans for easy money from the agent started crying foul and went after anyone and everyone in the bank. A clear pattern of losing the Sarfaesi hearing and going straight to the police station for claiming 409 and 420 has emerged. 

Even in FIR they have claimed that they willingly took the loans but received no money and filled FIR only in 2023 when the transactions took place before or around 2018. 

&amp;#x200B;

Sequence of events: 

\- Many people, wanted easy money, willingly took mortgage loans on their property on behalf of an agent with an underlying agreement that they will get some money every month and the loan agent will pay the loan. 

&amp;#x200B;

\- Things went haywire after covid, and accounts became NPA.

&amp;#x200B;

\- Bank's management was changed and Sarfaesi proceedings were started. 

&amp;#x200B;

\- Many people started claiming fraud and got the agent, and bank managers and chairman arrested on claims of fraud. 

&amp;#x200B;

\- Despite them going to the registrar and willingly signing all the documents and also admitting in the FIR that the loans were taken willingly, the lower courts didn't grant bail and we had to approach high courts or apply for bail post chargesheets. 

&amp;#x200B;

\- In one of the cases, the complainant killed himself and has left no suicide note or anything and yet the family claimed that it was due to the harassment by bank and specifically my friend's father when he had never contacted them beyond procedural calls. 

&amp;#x200B;

Would love to know what can be done with respect to my friend's father who has been embroiled in all of this.
