Alright, hear me out—OTV News has been lowkey (and sometimes not so lowkey) shilling for Baijayant Panda for ages now, and it’s getting ridiculous. I used to think it was just an Odisha-centric channel doing its thing, but the more you watch, the more it feels like a propaganda machine for Panda and his BJP buddies. 

For starters, the channel’s got some pretty obvious ties to Panda—like, it’s no secret his family’s influence looms large there. Every time Baijayant’s name pops up—whether it’s about his Delhi election wins, some random committee he’s heading, or even crop loss assurances in Kendrapara—it’s all glowing coverage. No criticism, no tough questions, just “Look at this amazing guy fixing everything!” Meanwhile, anything that paints him in a bad light—like those old land scam rumors or his BJD fallout—either gets buried or spun into some heroic redemption arc. 

And then there’s the BJD obsession. They’re out here peddling fake news about Naveen Patnaik and BJD like it’s their full-time job. Half the time, it feels like they’re just parroting WhatsApp forwards from some uncle’s rumor mill—no fact-checking, no sources, just pure “trust me, bro” vibes. Like, they’ll run a story about some supposed BJD corruption scandal or a wild conspiracy about Naveen, and it’s straight-up lifted from those sketchy chain messages with zero validation. It’s the kind of lazy, agenda-driven nonsense you’d expect from a troll account, not a news channel. Meanwhile, Panda’s BJP gets a free pass to look spotless. 

I’ve seen clips where they’ll spend half the segment hyping up his “strategic brilliance” for BJP’s latest moves, while opposition voices get maybe a 10-second soundbite if they’re lucky. And don’t get me started on how they frame Naveen or BJD—it’s always shady vibes or corruption jabs, like they’re settling some personal score from Panda’s exit. 

It’s not just the news either. The whole tone feels like they’re trying to build him up as Odisha’s golden boy, especially now with his national BJP clout. I get it, he’s a big deal—five-time MP, Delhi campaign mastermind, whatever—but the coverage is so one-sided it’s basically BJP fanfiction at this point. 

Anyone else notice this? Or am I just reading too much into it? Curious what you all think—especially if you’ve got examples of OTV pulling this crap lately, like those WhatsApp-fueled BJD hit pieces.
