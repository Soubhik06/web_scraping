So, this person named 'Deepak Kumar' called me &amp; I answered because I thought it is from my colleague. To my surprise, this person had an old age uncle voice who answered me by my First Name! He said this was about my father's Jeevan Beema amount of ₹12500 is to be refunded &amp; so my father has shared my number to him. I immediately realised it is a scam call as we as a family do not have any insurance. Then too I played along with the call saying yes, you can send the money. I received two messages - ₹10000 &amp; ₹25000. He immediately started his over acting about extra money he accidentally shared. I only recieved fale message from his number about money credited but not from my banks. He then gave me another number to which I need to return him ₹22500. I said yes &amp; checked my bank balance. I immediately disconnected call after sending same money credited message to him just after editing amount. He called me back &amp; started swearing which made me laugh uncontrollably. I then blocked &amp; reported his number from contacts, Truecaller, &amp; whatsapp.
