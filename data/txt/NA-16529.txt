I got a friend request from some doctor girl in Kolkata. After few messages she asked if i was on WhatsApp. We carried on our conversation on WhatsApp. After few messages she said asked for a video call to which i became a bit suspicious but said okay. We got on a video call where i could see her chest part only and no face. I had not put on my lights because i was a bit skeptical, hence my side of screen was black.

The call was cut from other side within 3-4 seconds and i received a bye message. I replied with ? which didn't get delivered for about 20 minutes post which i said i am going now which was left on read.

I have blocked from all places including WhatsApp and phone but i had been blocked first on Facebook so couldn't do anything there.

Regarding the Facebook profile and WhatsApp DP and Truecaller number, everything seems so genuine with 250 plus photos on FB spread across 5 plus years.

So moral of the story is genuine or non-genuine profile, whatever it is do not respond to unknown friend requests, connection requests etc. I shall live by this mantra forever now.

Just posting for my bros out there to be careful and exercise super caution in such situations - best is to completely ignore such things.
