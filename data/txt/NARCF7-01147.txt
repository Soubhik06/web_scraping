#National lock down for 21 days, till 14th April.

##Services available during the 21-day period

1. Shops including ration shops (under PDS) dealing with food, groceries, fruits, veg, dairy &amp; milk booth, meat &amp; fish, animal
2. Banks, ATMs and insurance offices
3. Delivery of all essential services including food, pharmaceuticals, medical equipment through e-commerce.
4. Petrol pumps, LPG, petroleum &amp; gas retail and storage outlets, power generation, transmission &amp; distribution services.
5. Hospitals and all related medical establishments. Telecom, internet, broadcasting and cable services.
