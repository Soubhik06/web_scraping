First of all thank you everyone here for bringing the net neutrality debate to public attention. 

Following are the refutations to arguments against Net Neutrality and OTT's I send to trai, (though I don't think anyone is going to read this, considering the massive email protest they are getting)

**1. Revenue loss for telecom providers, loss for traditional tv services.**

That is the very nature of a competitive market. New innovations disrupts existing products and services. Ultimately it is the choice of the consumers to use what. It will be disastrous in the long run to save a failing industry due to better alternatives, by savaging that alternative. It is simply taking away the choice of consumers and sabotaging innovation. What if telephone and mobiles where sabotaged to create more revenues for postal services. What if the automotive industry where sabotaged to save the horse and bullock cart trading, where the world would have been today? 

**2. OTT's are free riding on ISP's infrastructure, which requires investments.**

It could also be said that, it is because of these websites and applications that people are subscribing to the internet. Not for the sheer pleasure of being an internet owner. OTT's does require capital investments and unlike the isp's most of the websites and applications do not charge money directly from the users, they are supported by advertisements. Since the OTT's are creating greater demand for the internet and ISP's are directly benefiting from it, does the ISPs should also pay these websites a part of their revenue? If you see it as preposterous then the notion that OTTs' should also pay ISP's are also equally preposterous.

Second, the argument is that OTTs are free riding on their infrastructure, therefore the consumers should pay according to the websites they use. ISP's are in the business of providing internet and charges the users based on the speed and data they have consumed. Just like electricity providers are in the business of providing electricity and they charge per unit consumed. What if electricity providers suddenly decided to charge based on the brand of appliances you use instead of the units consumed. How ridiculous that would be?

Third, the argument is that OTT's cause revenue loss and ISP's require capital investments. As said earlier, unlike OTT's, ISP's are directly charging consumers for the services they are providing. People who wants higher speeds and higher data usages are paying accordingly. Now the notion is that consumers and OTT's should chip in even more for their bottom line. What if energy companies suddenly comes with a similar demand. The automobiles are literally riding on the fuel the energy companies provide, and these energy companies too require substantial capital investments. Consider a scenario where energy companies demand for revenue share from automobile companies and consumers are charged for the fuel they use based on the brand, type and color of the car they are riding instead of in liters. 

**3. TSP's has a lot of barrier to entry and OTT's not.**

How is low barrier to entry supposed to be a bad thing? Anyone anywhere in the world, with an access to internet can now create and promote his or her product and services, thereby solving a consumers problem and creating more employment, in what way a bad thing? This low barrier to entry and scalability has revolutionized the digital world and why you need to spoil it? Unless of course you are on the wrong side. 

**4. OTT's only benefits the countries they are located in.**

Leading websites and applications hire a lot of employees world wide not necessarily from the location it operates. They also outsource their jobs to different parts of the world benefiting everyone, not only the country they are located in.

Second, the internet also presents great opportunity for Indians to create their own products &amp; services and the emergence of many successful start-ups are the evidence.

Third, due to the cost cutting achieved by local businesses due to ott’s, they are also benefiting greatly. Now they can pass on this gains to consumers or re- invest their profits creating more employment.

Fourth, the increase in purchasing power of consumers. The availability of free websites and apps have increased the purchasing power of consumers which they can spend locally or save or invest which in turns provide more jobs.

**5. Blocking or throttling speed will benefit local app service providers.**

Indians are not inferior! Indian entrepreneurs and start-ups does have the passion, knowledge and resources to compete on a global stage. They don't need the government, regulators or isp's blocking and throttling the competitors products or services. They are capable of competing on an open free market.

**6. Revenue losses for TSP's will lower government revenues.**

Again this is a very short sighted view. One, the cost cutting advantages of existing businesses due to the ott's increases their profits thereby adding more revenue to the government.

Second the increase in purchasing power of consumers. For example, someone who used to spent Rs.100 on internet and Rs.200 a month on sms package, due to the emergence of free sms apps, now have Rs.200 to spent on other local products or services, which in turn leads to revenue for the government.

**7. Communication OTTs can entirely unintentionally cause disturbance and affect the social fabric. There is also the possibility of deliberate misuse of communication OTTs, to sow disharmony and discord.**

That argument can be used against any communication devices. Even for pen and paper, or against teaching people how to read and write. Of the fear of misuse by a small section of people, if we have stopped educating the youths in the past, think where the world would have been today.

**8.Most applications can trace the user’s location for underlying processes (such as GPS apps finding the nearest restaurants etc.). This information may be used to commit a crime, or the location itself may be the target of a crime.**

Such crimes were committed long before gps. Don't you think criminals have many other more effective alternatives to get to a location they want. Does we also need to ban maps? Now look at the immense uses the gps provide, like routing to the nearby hospitals in case of emergency and the role it can provide to other emergency services, on top of making our travel much easier and safer.

**9. Causes cyber bullying and other attacks.**

This is a serious issue, and fortunately there exists laws to punish such crimes. Personally if someone wants to bully or attack me, I'll any day choose the digital option than a real physical attack.

**10. According to some experts, if Net Neutrality is imposed, due to rise in data traffic, TSPs will be forced to increase the cost of access for consumers and consumers would be worse off.**

Reading through the horrendous plans that is to be implemented in the absence of net neutrality, It'll be better to live in a country where the internet costs are higher, than in a dictatorship. 

And finally to all the 20 questions asked. Internet, as of now, works! And If it ain't broke don't fix it.
