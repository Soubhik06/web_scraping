• *IAF submits dossier* - [The IAF has submitted dossier to the government containing high resolution satellite images proving that 80% of the bombs deployed during the airstrike hit their intended targets](https://m.businesstoday.in/story/iaf-takes-detractors-submits-proof-balakot-air-strike/1/325328.html)

• *NTRO's Report* - [At least 263 recruits had assembled at Balakot before the airstrike](https://m.timesofindia.com/india/263-terrorists-at-balakot-camp-ahead-of-air-strike/articleshow/68366633.cms)

NTRO's Report states that atleast 263 mobile phones were active in that area and all of them vanished without a trace after the airstrike. Now there can be terrorists who don't own a mobile while others might own 2. But this data has helped in estimates regarding the total number of casualties occured there.
