Hi Redditors!

My grandmother got a call yesterday from a person, who claimed to be our relative who lives in Canada. He said he met with an accident and his car was impounded. He was in dire need of money and any financial assistance would be helpful. On asking about his phone number, he said he was talking from the police station.

My grandmother is quite a soft hearted woman, and she fell for the trap. He was all crying on his phone, creating all drama to prove his case. As kind hearted she is, she considered his drama to be reality, and started asking me to do a transfer of INR 70,000 to him. I was quite skeptical, as he shared bank details of an Indian person. He said that person would send money to his lawyer who would help him with the case.

I never wanted to do the transfer. But upon innumerable cries of my grandma, I sent him 70K. That was all I had with me, was left with Rs. 500.

In the evening he asked for more money. She again asked me for a transfer. But I had nothing left, so she asked my cousin and he sent him INR 30,000. To check his legitimacy, my brother created a link which upon clicking would capture his current location. Just after he clicked, we got to know he was from Lahore, Pakistan.

When we called our relative(Canada), he said he was perfectly alright. We immediately filed a complaint with Cyber Crime Reporting portal.

But I don't feel it would be much fruitful, as he belongs to a different country altogether. Is there something else we can do? Is there any chance we would get our money back? Any help will be really appreciated my fellow citizens...Thanks in advance!
