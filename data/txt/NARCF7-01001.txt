On my way back from park, A labourer stopped me asking for help and told me he came here from shahadara to work but lost his tiffin and money when he was drinking water at the PP, and asked for 60 rs as he didn't have any to go back(nobody was helping him). I didn't have any at that moment, I told him to wait, went to a shop in my society, took 60 rs from there and went outside to give it to him. I came back home wondering if I had been scammed but kya hi farak padta, I waste more on snacks everyday. And if it was genuine, I'm so glad I helped someone reach home 😭 

This happens often at the metro station, but I end up ignoring thinking it must be a scam. One time I was stopped by a man, and he said he wanted to go somewhere but he had no clue how to go. I don't remember the details, but when I took my phone out to explain the route to him, he got irritated and left???
