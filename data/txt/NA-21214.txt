Hi

Yesterday, my parents received a call from someone masquerading as IGL gas. They resorted to their social engineering tactics and claimed the bill hadn't been paid and the connection would be cut with immediate effect unless they paid the bill. Parents are 75+ and not very tech savvy. These scammers suggested payment over the phone and got them to share all the debit card and adhaar card details. They even dissuaded them from checking anything with me because aparently "what will your child living abroad do, we can pay this bill right now and connection will be cut by the time your child wakes up". No surprise, within 24 hours multiple transfers totalling to Rs.5 lakh+ were done from their account to some unknown account.

Luckily, as soon as they got the SMS notifications, my parents called the bank customer care and registered the complaint. It's a PSU and they've told them it will take 40 days to resolve. Appatently bank also complained to cyber crime cell and suggested parents to do the same and register a police complaint tomorrow.

Unfortunately, I am abroad and not sure what I can do on the ground for them. My questions are:

1) Since they willingly shared debit card details, will the bank cover the liability and refund the full amount? Is there any law or grievance mechanism around this that can help?

2) Is there anything more my parents/I can do? I'm also concerned about potential identity theft or them knowing where parents live since they shared Aadhar card details.
