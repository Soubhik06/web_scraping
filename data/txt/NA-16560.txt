Hey folks of r/india.

I was facing a lot of pain in buying/selling second hand items online. Getting the right kind of buyers or sellers was very tricky on olx , dealing with scams was not worth the time. I am sure, a lot of you would have faced the same. 

To save time and with an intent to connect genuine buyers and sellers, I want to introduce you to r/delhi_marketplace. A community for connecting buyers and sellers, where we try to get the most out of our time. 

Needless to say, its you, awesome folks reading this who make a community go from good to great. So for whoever that's interested or just wants to join and keep it handy, head over to r/delhi_marketplace. Feedback is always welcome. 

Thank you. Please spread the word.
