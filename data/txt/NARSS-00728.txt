My friend asked me to order the same shoes from my Instagram again and we again prepaid Rs. 150 for the COD order. On delivery, we insisted on opening the parcel before accepting it. The delivery man asked where you ordered from. We said, "from Instagram". He recommended us to, not to accept the order and said it must be a fake seller. We requested him to open the parcel to show us and then reseal it with cello tape and cancel delivery. He agreed and opened the box and showed it. This time also it is the wrong cheap shoes.

Our Total loss : 1,699 + 150 = Rs. 1,849
