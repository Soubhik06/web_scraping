decision of the Hon'ble Supreme Court in the case of Munjal Sales Corporation

(supra), wherein the decision in the case of Abhishek Industries (supra) has been

discussed. However, the ld. CIT(A) while adjudicating the issue has also given

in the case of Elmer Havell Electrics (supra) is distinguishable and not

applicable to the facts of the present case. We do not find any infirmity in the

reasoned order of the ld. CIT(A). The grounds raised by the assessee and
