--- POST TEXT ---

After getting carried away by AK47's speeches last week, I decided to donate to AAP. I went to their website, clicked on their large Donate button, and filled in my info. Not wanting to be personally identified on the AAP site by my passport #, I decided to type in some fake number. Maybe the number of fake digits were wrong, but the AAP site detected my number to be fake, and didn't let me proceed to the AAP button. Frustrated, I decide give in, and submit my real passport number. I get to the next window. In the text box for the donation amount, I type in **Rs. 5000**. As I was to hit the submit button, I get a phone call from friend. Silly talks start, and we're rambling. Imagining myself to be some multi-tasking expert, I hit the submit button while still chatting. After the phone call, I check the donation's thank you mail, and this is where it hits me. *I had just submitted Rs 50,000 and not Rs 5,000 for the donation!!*

Holy crap, I can't afford that! What do I do? I live within my means, and this will shake my monthly budget!! I check my Credit Card company, and the transaction is already showing *Pending*. I called the CC company, and they tell me the only recourse for you is to talk to the merchant, otherwise we'd need to dispute the amount.

Frustrated, I call the AAP phone number that they send in the thank-you mail. A guy picks the phone. I was ready for a long negotiation conversation with the dude to explain on refunding me the amount. To my surprise, the guy patiently listens, and said that I should just reply to my thank-you mail, and the money would be refunded. &lt;Skeptical african child&gt;

I reply to the mail, and I get another automated reply back with a [ticket #](http://i.imgur.com/emxoyfS.png), and the link to my ticket. That's when I realize they have full site for logging feedbacks: http://feedback.aamaadmiparty.org/tickets.php. In few hours, I get response to my ticket - my money would be partially refunded so that only Rs 5,000 are deducted on my card.

I wait, and wait for the CC transaction to turn from Pending to Sale of Rs 5,000. After a couple of days, [it did](http://i.imgur.com/7mhTu6E.png)!

AAP refunded me the money that I incorrectly ended up paying. Now, this might have happened with any political party if I had donated to them. I've never donated to any, so I don't know. I would say though, that it did made me feel I donated to a good party. Don't get me wrong - I don't think AAP is as good as they like to make themselves to be. There's too much self-righteousness to the point that it gets annoying. AK47 has people like Ashutosh who make you cringe and laugh at the same time. But, it does feel to be the first honest party that I've dealt with in my life time (I'm 30 years old). Maybe JP movement was like this, may Pt. Nehru's Congress was like this, but I never saw them. Anyway, just thought I'd share my experience.

**TL;DR**: Donated 50,000 Rs to AAP instead of Rs 5,000. Called AAP for refund, and they immediately did. &lt;feels good&gt;
