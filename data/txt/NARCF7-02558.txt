--- POST TEXT ---

A few days ago, I received a call from an unknown person claiming that my dad’s LIC policy was settled and that they needed to pay me some money. They said I would receive a message confirming the deposit, and shortly after, I did receive such messages. Then, the caller claimed that they had accidentally transferred an extra ₹3,000 and requested me to refund the excess amount.

At that moment, I realized it was a fraud. I told the caller to wait as I intended to file a complaint against them. I reported the incident to the Cyber Crime Bureau. After some time, I received a call from the local police station. To my surprise, they informed me that they couldn’t file my complaint because I hadn’t lost any money.

I asked them, “Should I wait until I lose money? Why don’t you investigate it now?” Their response was dismissive, advising me not to respond to such calls and giving the usual lecture. It left me questioning the system’s approach to preventive action in such cases.
