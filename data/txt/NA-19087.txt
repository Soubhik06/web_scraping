Okay, so I'm 19 (M). My father is a businessman and the sole caretaker of our family. He's in his mid-50s. I have always struggled with anxiety and depression. I am the kind of person who excessively worries when something ill-fated happens to their close ones.

Recently, I have come across a plethora of news about how scammers are being creative and coming up with new scams, like the fake parcel delivery one, the links riddled with malware, etc. Also, some chain snatchers recently snatched a middle-aged lady's chain while on her morning walk near our locality (North Mumbai). I know there has always been news about bad things happening around us, but because I am only 19, I worry about my parents, and I just don't want them to suffer from these things.

My father's health is kinda deteriorating. He will only be able to work for 5–6 years more. I am the elder son, and I have a younger sister, 15F. I am constantly worried about my family. I have been off meds for the past 1 year and I don't want to depend on meds any more. They have so many side effects that I don't want.

How do you guys deal with such news that hits so close to your home? Also, how do I keep them aware of and safe from such scams?
