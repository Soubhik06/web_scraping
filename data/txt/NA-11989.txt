ex parte, however, still the plaintiff has to stand on his own legs
and has to prove his case with his evidence. As per rules of
evidence, onus to prove the substance to claim is on the person
who makes the claim. In the present matter, claim has been made
by plaintiff that defendant owes money which remains unpaid
and therefore, it was incumbent on plaintiff to prove substance of
this claim. The impact of defendant being ex parte is that the
whatever evidence is produced by plaintiff, it remains unrebutted

ISSUE NO. 1. Whether the suit is barred by limitation? 10. This issue was framed pursuant to preliminary
objection raised by defendant contending that since the
agreement to sell is dated 24.2.2013, the period of limitation shall
start from that day and suit filed on 26.9.2018 is time barred.
Plaintiff has examined three witnesses to prove its case. The
agreement to sell dated 24.2.2013 is proved as Ex. PW1/A. In the
affidavit of admission/denial filed on behalf of defendant Tilak
Raj, the agreement to sell is admitted except that the amount
claimed to have paid in cash is not admitted. Perusal of
agreement to sell Ex.PW1/A shows that in the terms and
conditions of said agreement to sell and purchase, condition it
incorporated that balance sum shall be paid by the second party
to the first party on or before 27.2.2018. The agreement to sell
and purchase is a contract/agreement between the parties wherein
they can reach at any terms which is mutually acceptable to both
the parties. Agreement to sell is admitted by defendant Tilak Raj.
Besides defendant could not shake the credit of this document. A
suggestion was given in cross examination of PW­1 that
agreement to sell is forged but besides this suggestion no other
material is produced to disprove the agreement to sell Ex.
PW1/A. In the written statement as well execution of this CS DJ 909/18 SHIV LAL Vs. TILAK RAJ (Chander Jit Singh)

 document is not denied or controverted. Therefore, terms
contained in the agreement to sell and purchase which is Ex.
PW1/A shall stand and apply. As noted above, the second party
i.e. plaintiff herein had time till 27.2.2018 to make the
remaining/final payment as contended. Further, material on
record shows that Plaintiff had learnt about the transfer of suit
property to third person in March 2018 and suit was filed in
September 2018. Legal notice dated 6.6.2018 was sent to
defendant calling upon defendant to repay the money along with
interest and penalty but no amount was paid by defendant. Thus,
this non payment after the date mutually agreed by both the
parties would be the time when the limitation would start
counting. Therefore, limitation shall start after 27.2.2018. As per
record, suit is filed in September 2018 and therefore, is within the
limitation. Hence, this issue is decided in favour of plaintiff and
against defendant. ISSUE NO. 2 AND 3:­ 11. Issue No.2 and 3 are decided together. Plaintiff has
