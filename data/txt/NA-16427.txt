This post is divided into pros &amp; cons of Modi govt from 2014 to date.

Pros:

1. Rapid growth &amp; improvement in transport infrastructure.
 2. Make in india initiative which enhanced the manufacturing capacity of india in some aspects &amp; increased both domestic &amp; foreign exports of various goods. The export sector has particularly improved.
3. Terrorist &amp; public mass bomb attacks on civilians have declined considerably. Excluding J&amp;K, the overall civilian safety from terrorist attacks is now far better.
4. The no: of international companies &amp; their investments increased to some extent.
5. Military has been modernised &amp; equipped with better quality weapons &amp; various other tools to some extent. 
6. Social welfare schemes such as LPG, toilet construction, pension &amp; loan facility were successful to a good extent.
 7. Various online govt services have improved. There has been an overhaul in the system to some extent. Digitalization of various services has gone ahead.
8. Govt medical infrastructure &amp; services have generally improved to some extent.
9. UPI is a huge success of this government. It has revolutionised the payment system in india along with its unparalleled effectiveness &amp; efficiency. Its user friendly system ensure that people from all walks of life can easily use it.
10. India's foreign relations have considerably improved especially with european union &amp; GCC countries.

Cons:

1. Various botched projects/policies such as demonetisation, GST, farmers' issues, manipur ethnic war issues, delhi/ haryana/uttar pradesh/gujarat/madhya pradesh communal disharmony, violence &amp; riots, aadhar card data leaks, wrestlers' issues, covid lockdown, oxygen cylinder &amp; vaccine policy &amp; so on. Demonetisation was a fiasco. It was a ludicrous policy by govt. GST was initially bungled by govt to a huge extent. But now, its successful to a good extent. 2. Disdain for human rights.
3. Fundamentalism/bigotry &amp; peddling of pseudoscience. Drastic increase of extremist violence &amp; public nuisance of organisations such as RSS, VHP &amp; Bajrang Dal.
4. Controlled mass media Bjp/nda supporters &amp; followers while defending godi media should understand that media was one of the paramount reasons for the defeat of congress/upa government. During the rule of congress/upa govts, media was far more independent &amp; powerful. A lot of medias were against govt. They often criticised &amp; trolled govt. They informed the general public about govt's various issues &amp; faults from time to time. Some of the national medias were usually on their toes to chase &amp; find out the govt's misdeeds. This was especially very evident during the UPA 2 govt. To put it in a nutshell, media was the 4th pillar of democracy during those days. But unfortunately, today that's no more the case.
5. Distraction of actual issues by diverting public attention to islamophobia/christianophobia/sikophobia &amp; pakistan/ china along with ever growing nationalism &amp; jingoism to also deviate people from other relevant issues (to be not confused with patriotism which is the positive side). 6. religion (hinduism) and various government policies/ actions are mixed with each other. Majority appeasement (hinduism).
7. Protection &amp; garlanding of rapists &amp; hate speech proponents.
8. Rampant crony capitalism &amp; all level govt corruption (gautam adani &amp; PM CARES fund are apt examples).
9. Complete lack of transparency in various govt accountability matters/incidents such as PM CARES fund. 
10. Suppression of citizen participation in democratic processes like RTI &amp; improper response given to their queries by RTI.
11. Inauguration of half-baked infrastructure projects. Many of these projects were &amp; are incomplete with lack of property quality, safety &amp; reliability at the time of their inauguration &amp; thereafter.
12. Make in india project's vision was not fully realised. There were several issues with it in respect to quality &amp; reliability of various products. The govt failed to extensively utilise its scope &amp; further potential.
13. Electoral scams.
14. Absolute lack of PM's &amp; govt's unscripted interviews &amp; press conferences.
