#Scenario 1: Your nude photos are being threatened to be uploaded online:

* This would fall under the umbrella of Non-Consensual Intimate Image (NCII) abuse. Sharing or threatening to share intimate images without consent is a form of cyberbullying and harassment, as I mentioned earlier. The advice I provided about documenting the threat, reporting it to the authorities, and seeking support also applies in this case.

#Scenario 2: Your friend consensually shared their nude photos with someone, and now that person is threatening to upload them:

* While this situation wouldn't technically be considered NCII in its strictest sense, it still involves a potential breach of trust and can have serious consequences for your friend. The advice about seeking support, changing passwords, and reporting the threat to social media platforms or the authorities still applies.
