Hi Redditors, 

My wife fell victim in a online trading scam originating from a Whatsapp group. However, she could manage to get a part of money back to her Kotak bank account with a promise of planning to invest more in future. (Small win over fraudsters. Phew!) Due to this, her account got captured as a outgoing account from the fraudsters' account. 
 
Another victim from Kolkata raised a complaint with cyber crime police department there. As my wife's account was one of the outgoing account, it got debit freeze on the ask of West Bengal police department. On suggestions from Kotak bank and few others, she raised a complaint (FIR) in Bengaluru (our residing area) cyber crime police department as she is a victim herself. Bengaluru police department has sent an email stating details of FIR and her being a victim to West Bengal police department. Upon following up with West Bengal police department, it doesn't seem to be progressing well. At time, they say you may need to come here. Kotak bank would unfreeze account only when the original requester (West Bengal police department) would ask so. She is caught between dealing with two states and acting as an intermediary between two state police department to get this resolved, which has been draining and futile. Any suggestions on what more could be done. Should we just wait ?
