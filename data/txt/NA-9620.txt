 Heard Mr. U.K. Nair, learned Senior Counsel assisted by Ms. S.P. Nair, learned counsel for
the petitioners; Mr. N. Goswami, learned Junior Government Advocate, Assam for the
respondent nos. 1, 2 & 6; Ms. P. Borua, learned counsel appearing on behalf of Mr. P. Nayak,
learned Standing Counsel, Gauhati Municipal Corporation [GMC] for the respondent nos. 3
and 4; and Ms. A. Gayan, learned counsel for the respondent no. 5. 2. An Order under Memo No. KMJ.62/2022/890 dated 29.06.2022 passed by the District
Magistrate, Kamrup Metropolitan District, Guwahati has been placed on record. It transpires
from the Order dated 29.06.2022 that the District Magistrate, Kamrup Metropolitan District,
Guwahati has constituted a Task Force to ensure that all meat dealers under the GMC area
would slaughter their animals only at the certified slaughter house. The Order further states
that the Task Force has been constituted so as to ensure that no meat dealer is allowed to
slaughter their animals outside the certified slaughter houses. The Order further reflects that
the Task Force will visit the sites and submit a weekly report in that connection. Page No.# 3/3 3. Ms. Gayan by placing a communication bearing no. ALPCO//T/01/2020(Prt-1)/37 dated
30.07.2022 of the Administrative Officer, Assam Livestock & Poultry Corporation Ltd., has
submitted that at present, the respondent no. 5 has 2 [two] nos. of slaughter houses [Poultry
slaughter house and Sheep Goat slaughter house] which are working commercially for
general public and meat vendors/shopkeepers since 2019. The communication further reflects
that the respondent no. 5 is in the process of upgradation of the Poultry Slaughter House and
Pig Slaughter House for commercial use for general public, butchers and vendors. 4. Having regard to the fact that a Task Force has been constituted and that Task Force is
going to submit weekly reports with regard to slaughter of animals in the GMC area, Mr.
Goswami, learned Junior Government Advocate, Assam for the respondent no. 6 shall submit
the weekly reports for the subsequent weeks from the this date, prepared by the Task Force
on the next date of listing for perusal of the Court. 5. List the case again on 02.09.2022. 6. The Order under memo bearing no. KMJ.62/2022/890 dated 29.06.2022 passed by the
District Magistrate, Kamrup Metropolitan District, Guwahati and the communication bearing
no. ALPCO//T/01/2020(Prt-1)/37 dated 30.07.2022 of the Administrative Officer, Assam
Livestock & Poultry Corporation Ltd. be kept on record. JUDGE
