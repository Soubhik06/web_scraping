Shri K.S. Wadhwa, Government Advocate for the State. Heard counsel for the parties. The applicant has been named as accused in two separate
Crimes in respect of which present bail applications have been
filed. . The present fourth bail applications pertain to Crime
No.12/2014 and Crime No.06/2014 registered with Police
Station S.T.F., Bhopal - commonly known as VYAPAM
Examination Scam cases - for the offences punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 & 120-B of IPC and
Section 3 (Gha) 1, 2/4 of M.P. Recognized Examination Act,
1937. The role ascribed to the applicant in both these crimes is
that he assisted the middleman - Bhoopendra Singh Rathore in
the commission of crime. The said Bhoopendra Singh Rathore
arranged for impersonators. The impersonators as well as
beneficiaries have since been released on bail. The said
Bhoopendra Singh Rathore is already in jail in connection with
the said crimes. The next link with the applicant on either side in both the
crimes or investigation in that behalf is already complete. The
charge sheet has been filed against the applicant in Crime
No.12/2014 on 29.11.2014 and in Crime No.6/2014 the charge
sheet has been filed on 23.11.2014. Considering the fact that investigation is complete in
both the crimes and next persons linked to the applicant have
already been arrested and later on released on bail (except
Bhoopendra Singh Rathore, who is in jail) and that the
applicant assures to extend full cooperation for further
investigation to be done by CBI, if any, coupled with the fact
that the applicant is in custody in connection with Crime
No.12/2014 since 5.9.2014 and in Crime No.6/2014 since
28.8.2014, which is for sufficiently long time, taking overall
view of the matter, in the interest of justice, we allow these
applications and order release of the applicant on bail in the
respective crimes on the following strict conditions : The bail applications stand disposed of accordingly.
