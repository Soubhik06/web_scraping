 The bail application has been filed under Section 438 CrPC.

in connection with FIR No. 231/2018 registered at Police Station

Choupasni Housing Board, District Jodhpur for the offences under Sections 420 , 406 , 467 , 468 , 471 & 120B of IPC whereupon the

investigation was commenced. Apprehending his arrest, the

petitioner moved the bail application before the court below, which

was dismissed. Hence, the bail application has been filed. Heard learned counsel for the petitioner as well as learned

Public Prosecutor and perused the entire material available on

record. The allegation against the petitioner is of having received

1,20,000/- from the complainant- Deepak Srivastava for investing

in bitcoin company later on the amount was not deposited. (Downloaded on 27/07/2021 at 08:41:52 PM) (2 of 2) [CRLMB-9517/2021]

 fraud committed, I am not inclined to grant benefit of anticipatory

 bail to the petitioner. The bail application is accordingly dismissed. (SANJEEV PRAKASH SHARMA),J
