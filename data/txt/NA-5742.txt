Aggarwal, petitioner and some other persons committed fraud

 The petitioner was arrested and as per custody certificate

furnished by the U.T. counsel the total custody period of the

petitioner comes to be 2 months and 11 days and he is not involved in

any other criminal case. It appears that now compromise has been

without commenting on the merits of the case, the present petition is

allowed and the petitioner is hereby ordered to be released on regular

bail subject to furnishing bail and surety bonds to the satisfaction of

the CJM/Duty Magistrate concerned during the pendency of the trial.
