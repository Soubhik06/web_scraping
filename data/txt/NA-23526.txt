--- POST TEXT ---

Today, my family narrowly avoided falling victim to a sophisticated scam, and I feel it's important to share our experience to help others stay vigilant. 

&amp;#x200B;

My dad, who lives abroad, received a call on his local number purportedly from FedEx, informing us that a parcel containing illegal substances had been sent from Mumbai to Thailand under his name and was intercepted. The caller claimed the parcel had drugs along with other items and had been detained. Sensing something amiss, my mum mentioned that my dad wasn't in the country and that there is no way my dad can be involved, to which the caller suggested it might be a case of identity theft. They then redirected us to someone claiming to be from the Mumbai Cyber Crime Cell.

Red flags started popping up: 

1. The initial call purportedly from FedEx came from a United Kingdom number, while the subsequent call from the "police officer" was made via WhatsApp.

2. The "police officer" insisted on absolute secrecy, instructing us not to inform anyone, including my dad, despite him being the supposed concerned person.

&amp;#x200B;

Thankfully, we decided to verify the legitimacy of the calls before taking any further action. After a quick Google search, we discovered that this was a scam that has targeted multiple people in Bangalore using the same modus operandi. The scammers prey on fear and confusion, urging victims to provide personal documents like Aadhar and PAN cards under the guise of assisting with the investigation.

&amp;#x200B;

This scam is not only financially harmful but also emotionally distressing. It's essential to spread awareness and caution others, especially in Bangalore, about this fraudulent activity. Please stay vigilant and never hesitate to verify the authenticity of such calls before providing any personal information.

&amp;#x200B;

Stay safe, everyone.
