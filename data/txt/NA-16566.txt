I'm pretty sure most of us know the brand Vans for it's premium canvas sneakers collection. For the ones who don't, Vans is an American brand that manufactures skateboarding apparel. They have a huge fan following in the US and they've started opening up showrooms in Indian Metropolitan cities as well. 

Having set the premise, let me move on to the scam itself. So it was my friend's bday and as good friends do, we decided to pool in money and get him a pair of Vans canvas shoes since he's been wanting to get a pair for a long time. So here's the deal... none of the big names like Myntra, Ajio, Flipkart, Amazon, etc had a sale going on at that time and hence we looked up Vans India on Google. The first thing that comes up (it was not an ad) was vansstoresindia.com . Seems legit right? Vans probably has their own website to sell sneakers? Just like Nike, Adidas, Asics and literally every other sneaker brand out there? Nope. Nope. And nope.

This website right here (www.vansstoresindia.com) is a scam. Who would've ever known that the first search result on Google right now when you look up "Vans India" or "Buy Vans shoes online India" is a scam. You can check for yourselves how legit the site looks. Makes me question myself for doing a degree in CSE and still falling for an online scam. 

[Vans themselves recognize that this is a scam on their Instagram profile](https://imgur.com/a/tp10xpJ). 

We placed the order and waited for a couple weeks thinking that since it's an international shipment, it might take time for the shipping status to get updated (had a similar experience with Nike's official website. Didn't get a status update for a week or two and it ended up getting delivered at the end of the second week). I genuinely got very worried today and went on to their Instagram page to ask them for my order information. That's when I found the above comment on their latest post. 

Being such a big corporation, it surprises me as to how Vans has not taken action against these scammers. It's literally the first search result when someone looks up their brand from India. People will continue to get scammed if this doesn't stop. 

This is a PSA post for anyone who's out there looking to buy Vans. Also, I'm hell bent on bringing these people down. If possible could I get some legal advice as to how we could maybe bring down the website?

TL:DR :- Buy Vans shoes only from reputed online stores like Ajio, Myntra, Vegnonveg, Superkicks, etc.
