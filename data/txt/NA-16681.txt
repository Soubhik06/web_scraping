Super frustrated with this scam of a service. I lost more than Rs. 900 and wasted lots of time so writing this review to forewarn others against using them.

Booked a bike with them and was asked to pay entire amount in advance [red flag!]. When I reached to collect my bike at the specified location, I couldn’t reach the phone number of their customer rep. I tried calling for more than 20 minutes and then came back thinking of taking it up with their online support. Sent of a mail, no response for 1 week. After one week their support staff called and started off talking like a goon, that is after I had undeniable proof that their number was indeed unreachable.

Their customer service won't take blame for any issues. Your pre-payment and deposit would be confiscated without any regard to your side of the story. God knows what's up with Indian sites and poor customer service.

In summary, stay away if you want to retain your sanity.
