21/05/2025 1. The instant bail application has been filed by petitioner under

Section 483 B.N.S.S. in connection with F.I.R. No.75/2025 dated

27.03.2025 registered at Police Station Dev Nagar, District

Jodhpur City (West) for the offences under Section 64(1) of

B.N.S., 2023. 2. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the petitioner

has falsely been implicated in this case. He further submits that

the petitioner is 39 years old and the complainant is 38 years old

mature lady, who is presently contesting divorce case against her

husband and is having two children (20 and 19 years of age) out

of their wedlock. He further submits that the complainant and the

petitioner were having love affair and the petitioner has been

implicated in this case while alleging that he made a false promise

of marriage to the complainant. Learned counsel for the petitioner

FIR has wrongly alleged that the petitioner established sexual

relationship with her on pretext of marriage and also captured

some nude photographs/videos and thereafter, blackmailed her

while giving threat that he would viral her nude photographs and

videos, on account of which she was forced to have sexual

relationship with the petitioner. He further submits that no

obscene video/photographs of the complainant have been

recovered from the petitioner and also no recovery is due against

the petitioner. He also submits that the trial of the case will take

long time, therefore, the petitioner may be enlarged on bail. 3. Per contra, learned Public Prosecutor opposes the bail

application. 4. Heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the

material available on record. 5. This Court, upon perusal of the case diary and the statement

of the complainant recorded under Section 183 B.N.S.S, finds that

prima facie it appears to be a case of consensual sexual

relationship. 6. After careful consideration of the submissions and having

regard to the entirety of facts and circumstances of the case as

available on record and looking to the fact that the trial will

consume time, thus, without expressing any opinion on the

merits/demerits of the case, this Court is of the opinion that the

petitioner deserves to be released on bail. 7. Accordingly, the bail application is allowed and it is ordered
