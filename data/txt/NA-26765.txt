I bought into an ICICI GSIP years ago with a 15 year lock in (I was young and naively bought into what the ICICI rep told me).

Apparently, in that time, that investment has been deemed non-compliant (by SEBI I think) and is no longer sold.

There is absolutely no way for me to withdraw the funds, or track the current NAV. All I see is a tracker on the ICICI Pru Life page which updates once a year with an accrued bonus. The total accrued bonus so far (12+ years in) is just 3x the amount I was putting in each year.

This equates to an annual rate of return of ... 3.9%. I would have been better off in a savings account.

Does anyone else have the same plan (and is / was in a similar situation?). Do you know if:

* The funds are actually invested, so I should be getting a 12% return *on top* of what the accrued bonus shows?
* Is there some secret hidden way to track the NAV?
* At the end of the period, has someone got the full funds back into their account? Any issues faced in getting this?

Given the extremely strict 15-year lock in (no way to withdraw even by paying a penalty), the investment should ideally generate excellent returns. Otherwise it is a complete scam.
