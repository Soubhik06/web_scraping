Perhaps it is just around me, do correct me if it's not common in your circle but the amount of retired professionals whom I know of falling into scams is saddening and I am not talking of some Jamtara inspired person calling and pretending to be from the Indian Army selling their BMW for 50K olx wala scam. 
 
A lot of retirees in my context typically government employees, teachers, etc post-retirement amass a decent amount with savings, they live off their entire lives modestly, and after retirement, they are left with a lot of cash, and all of a sudden, the person who spends 15 minutes fighting with the local dhania seller because the man is shortchanged of 2rs is now suddenly looking at his lifetime saving and going all, "Meh hu Harshad Mehta" and then proceeds to enter into the shadiest of deals with his shady neighbor because why not. 

 
Like I can't even be mad with people falling for crypto scams, fake guru preaching because 17-year-old Raju will come out wiser and broke after losing 10K, Family ka respect, khud ka self-respect, Tommy the kutta ka respect but nikal jiaga, (i know there are serious instances even among young people but this is just to set my point).

 
Meanwhile, uncle after retirement is suddenly an investment expert/contractor expert/law expert and proceeds to enter into shady deals with shady people, lose all his money, and then spend the next 10 years battling in court and getting side-eyed by his wife every time he breathes.

 
Hopefully, this is only a me thing and I am surrounded by dumbfucks but I have a feeling if you hear a story of an uncle getting scammed more than twice in a year, it probably is a problem at large.
