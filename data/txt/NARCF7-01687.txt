--- POST TEXT ---

AI is already replacing jobs in customer support, data analysis, and countless other industries. The scale of disruption there is massive compared to AI-generated art. Yet, no public outcry. No viral protests.

So, why now? This could be because, art isn’t just a job- it’s identity. It’s culture. For many, It’s deeply personal. 

On the flip side, when AI generates/automates corporate reports, it’s efficiency. When it mimics an artist’s years of hard work and unique style? It feels like theft (at least majority people online thinks so (including me)).

To be honest, AI automation in customer support is expected-companies have been optimizing this for years. But AI that replicates artistic genius without consent? That’s a direct hit to independent creators already struggling to survive. 

This is a classic example of how AI is perceived in different industries- efficiency in some, exploitation in others.

Lastly, to the users who are uploading all their personal photos to openai for generating Ghibli-style images just for fun, please disable 'improve the model for everyone ' toggle in data controls inside settings. You can thank me later!
