In the month of May was contacted by a person claiming to be owner of a startup helping people with job assistance. He even took a web seminar regarding the same. When I opted for his service he told me that role of APM is available in Google and asked Rs 18000 for referral and Rs 2500 for resume which he guaranteed will be refunded if I didn't get placed. The price was definitely high but I was in dire need of a job. So after reading some fake articles written by him and reviews on websites like Ambitionbox, I paid him the said amount. 
After a month he asked me for my pay slips which I provided. He denied them saying that these will be rejected directly by Google HRs, he can help me in this by showing fake experience from a good startup but will cost another Rs 15000 and the same will be refunded if I didn't get the job. I being dumb and desperate, paid him the said amount. After a week in July, 2023 he said that my resume is rejected and the refund will be initiated.

Till date I haven't received the amount (I know this is a scam and won't get it pleading to him.)

But in August, I raised an online complaint with Delhi Police submitting all the evidence along with this person's photograph and address (90% sure its legit), screenshots of payments, WhatsApp chats. But till date the Enquiry officer hadn't done a single thing regarding this case (It has been 2.5 months). 
What steps can I take to escalate this issue. 

The person is residing in Greater Noida and I am residing in Delhi.
