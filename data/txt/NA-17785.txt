So, recently someone I know got a call from a person pretending to be a police officer in Hyderabad. The scammer was on the video call wearing the uniform of a police officer and told my acquaintance that his son has been implicated in a rape case and is currently sitting in the police station. He was able to correctly place the name of both the father and the son, which isn't something a random scammer would be able to do. Not only that, while on the videocall the son who was 'sitting' with him and not in the frame reffered to his father as 'baba' , which is very curious because that's how the son addresses his father. 

How could have they known this detail? Generally, most sons refer their father is papa or dad. How do these scammers get so much detail about the people they are scamming? 

I'm pretty sure the police administration and bankers are involved in scams which have become so common these days. But is there more to it. Until, the whole 'baba' thing, my theory was that it has to bank officials leaking info about father-son relation from the nominee details we give them, when we open a bank account. But this baba thing has me really puzzled. What do you think?
