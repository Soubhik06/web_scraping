--- POST TEXT ---

Initially I was looking to get a credit card with Axis bank: https://np.reddit.com/r/india/comments/7zpytl/axis_bank_is_offering_guaranteed_approval_of/ but couldn't make up my mind because of the account charges of Axis bank, they are charging for debit card and imps/neft even on their 25k minimum balance prime account.

Few days later I went to a SBI branch near me to withdraw some money, it's a non home branch. On my way out I saw some girls sitting there, I asked them about Unnati credit card, she asked if I'm salaried and interested in getting other cards, but I said I want the unnati one because I'm still a student, she said fine and asked me to bring my aadhaar card and open a FD. She pointed out to another girl and told her to fill up my forms. I told them that I'll come few days later because I have to arrange the money and think about how much credit limit I want. 

**5th March** 

I went to the branch on 5th March, that day there was the later girl who was pointed out by the other one. She also asked me why I want Unnati? where I spend the most? Online or offline. Then she told me I can apply for any card against a FD, credit limit will be 75% of FD amount. Yeah on Unnati AMC is free but on SimplyClick I can get 5x rewards on online spends (Amazon, BookMyShow and more) + there's a ₹500 Amazon gift voucher and normal 1x rewards on offline spends. Now sure I don't care about rewards but nice to go with the one which provides better rewards on online spends since most of my shopping are done online and the Unnati card is plain ugly. So I went with SimplyClick.

She told me to submit the fd opening form while she fills up my form. Later she done a instant ekyc with my biometric authentication. Got a immediate approval that my application has been approved with a credit limit of 0. They'll Authorize the limit later after verifying my documents. 

I didn't have to give them any documents except my telephone bill, address and identity was verified by Aadhaar. 

The FD opened on the same day.

**7th March**

Got a call from SBI card for my address and details verification. She explained all the facilities and details of card. 

**13th March**

Got a text and mail that my card has been approved and they'll dispatch the card soon. Also checked my FD, they have put a lien on it. 

**15th March**

Got the card. :)

I have made a FD of 40000, got credit limit of ₹30000 and cash limit of ₹15000. AMC charges are ₹500, will be waived off on spending of 1 Lakh. 

The credit limit is not high because I don't wanna burden myself by purchasing unnecessary things, every month spend is around ₹5k and that's it.

Why I got the card is because of providing a safeguard to my savings accounts, now I don't have to worry about debit card fraud and money being stolen from my account. 

If you are new into credit cards and don't mind the ugliness of Unnati credit card then you can take it, it has no amc charges for 4years. also look at SimplySave if you purchase most of the things offline. 

If you have any questions then feel free to ask.
