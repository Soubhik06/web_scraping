Hi Reddit India, most of you might know me from [my AMA](https://www.reddit.com/r/india/comments/4l5jga/hello_rindia_im_the_metro_rail_guy_ask_me_anything/) here 3 years ago. For those who don't know me I run a [small blog](http://www.themetrorailguy.com/) tracking updates on India's metro systems since 2015. I wanted to seek your guidance on how to proceed with this issue I'm facing.

Back in 2017, a website called MetroRailNews (won't link to them) started copying my material - information pages, breaking news on contracts etc. This included text (word for word), images and maps. In 2018, they copied all of my custom-made Google Maps and hosted them on their website. Dealing with this spammy site (they flat-out refused infringement), Google and their website's host was not fun (I didn't get a positive outcome) and one of the reasons that contributed to why I stopped writing in mid-2018.

During my hiatus, a new spammy site called UrbanTransportNews (won't link to them either) appeared. The owners of this site used to work at Metro Rail News and I guess decided to part ways and create their own little thing. When I [returned to writing](https://themetrorailguy.com/2020/01/03/update-on-me-and-sites-future/) in January 2020, this site continued with the same scummy behavior of copy/pasting, but this time slightly reworded content. My new post on Bangalore Metro's 4 new TBMs (reporting a development from back in Nov 2019) got copied right away without attribution while [TOI](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/bengaluru/bengaluru-four-tbms-to-reach-in-february-to-bore-through-cbd/articleshow/73102209.cms) credited me. After that, pretty much every post of mine has been ripped off in one way or the other.

To combat this, I have -

* Recently started watermarking images and administrative notices exclusive to me - something which I'm not a fan of. It's time consuming, not visually appealing and doesn't feel right.

* Lodged DMCA violation take-down requests with UTN's website host (BlueHost) in March. Guess what they did? They filed counter-notices feigning ignorance and indicated the material actually belongs to them. In turn, they filed DMCA take-down requests with my host for 3 Chennai Metro images which they don't even own and were appropriately attributed by me to their rightful owner (CMRL). I filed a couple more DMCA take-downs and their host this time refused to acknowledge infringement.

Today, one of my followers brought to light that an impersonating website domain TheMetroRailGuy.in (with .in instead of .com) had been created with fake social media accounts to closely resemble mine. Another follower did some [sleuthing work - see thread](https://twitter.com/rsrikanth05/status/1256939180250165248) and found these were registered by none other than UTN. 

* Impersonating [Website Link](https://www.themetrorailguy.in/)
* Impersonating [Twitter Account](https://twitter.com/TheMetroRaiiGuy)
* Impersonating [LinkedIn Page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/the-metro-rail-guy/)
* LinkedIn Page's [Fake Owner] (https://www.linkedin.com/in/kshipra-datar/)

If you can take a minute, please report the above Twitter and Linkedin accounts. 

Dealing with this is not fun. It's draining and not a constructive use of my time. I'd rather be liasoning with industry folks and writing about stuff. 

What baffles me is that UTN and MRN are both listed on Google News as verified news publishers. 

* Each and every "article" of theirs is either a copy-paste or a slight rewrite of my post or the mainstream media's. 
* They don't have any in-house journalists or perform independent investigative reviews. The [profile picture](https://i.imgur.com/UiW4BRv.png) of one of UTN's writers named "Anushka Khare" is in fact of Samantha Ruth Prabhu - a Tamil movie industry actress. 
* The owner recently photo-shopped [herself](https://www.facebook.com/TheMamtaShah/photos/p.2776779279072226/2776779279072226/?type=1&amp;theater) onto a [Niti Aayog event image](https://twitter.com/NITIAayog/status/1236555967203958786) and claims to be on their advisory board "working for Women Entrepreneurship Platform".
* They've been changing their publishing times to appear before my blog's posts - to manipulate SEO and trick Google to believe they were the original/first to publish a news item. This bumps their post to the very top of Google search results.

How can this all-round scummy behavior of these two spammy sites be corrected? I'm at a loss.
