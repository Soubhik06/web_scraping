Hyderabad: A district consumer forum here has directed Karvy Stock Broking Ltd to refund about Rs 16 lakh to members of two families for pledging shares from their demat account without their knowledge and not returning their money as per Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) order.

&amp;#x200B;

Six complainants, who have all filed separate cases in the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission– I, Hyderabad, were also awarded Rs 60, 000 in total towards compensation and costs of the complaints.

&amp;#x200B;

&amp;#x200B;

Jagadeep N, one of the complainants, submitted that he has a demat account with Karvy Stock Broking in which he was holdings Rs 12.5 lakh. The firm, however, pledged securities belonging to him without any intimation. He alleged that the said firm had also violated SEBI circulars and committed serious breach/fraud with all their clients.

The complainant further said despite National Securities Depository Limited (NSDL) directing the firm to transfer securities from its account to respective clients, he had not received any money in his account. He complained that whenever he approached Karvy to enquire about his investments, the opposite party gave an evasive reply without disclosing the actual proceedings. 

&amp;#x200B;

&amp;#x200B;

&amp;#x200B;

Jagdeep said he even got a legal notice issued on May 7, 2021, but did not receive any reply. Alleging that the negligent attitude of the opposite parties caused severe agony, mental stress, and inconvenience, he filed this complaint. Similar complaints were also filed by five other complainants against the said firm.
