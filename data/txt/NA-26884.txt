Have you ever trusted a friend in your time of need—only to be betrayed? I’m sharing my personal experience to warn fellow NRIs about a sophisticated scam that exploited longstanding personal relationships and targeted vulnerable individuals.

**My Story:**

* **A Close Bond Turned Betrayal:** In Late 2021, I helped a close friend from my university days in India during a difficult time. I believed in mutual support among NRIs. Unfortunately, that same friend later orchestrated a complex -"Arrest &amp; Detention" scam that turned my trust into a weapon against me.
* **How the Scam Unfolded:**
 * **False Arrest Claims &amp; Urgent Demands:** In January 2024, a third party—posing as a police officer— contacted me on WhatsApp, warning that my friend was about to be arrested and that urgent payments were needed to avoid deportation and even physical harm. The friend verified this on video call. 
 * **Exploiting International Jurisdictions:** This scam wasn’t confined to one country. It involved impersonators and fraudulent agents operating from **Slovakia, Estonia, Lithuania, Latvia, and India**, using multiple phone numbers and fake identities to pressure me for money under severe emotional duress. 
 * **Direct Financial Impact:**
 * **7,416 EURO** extorted during the initial “arrest scam.”
 * An additional **9,678 EURO** forced into a bank account in Slovakia between January and April 2024.
 * Prior payments from May to December 2023 totaled **13,207.38 EURO** under false pretenses such as emergency deportation, eviction, and legal fees.
* **Legal &amp; Emotional Fallout:** Beyond the severe financial loss, I have faced significant legal complications and emotional distress. False complaint emails and fraudulent legal notices were sent to my workplace and local authorities in Singapore, further complicating my situation- all coercing me to continue sending money- But I refused &amp; blocked. 

**Current Status of the Case:**

* I have filed police cases in Singapore and India for fraud, cheating, and cyber harassment- All cases closed due to overseas jurisdiction restrictions
* Asset recovery agencies in Singapore have been contacted, though no effective response has been received so far.
* Lawyers in Europe contacted- Rejected due to mandate issues
* Now this, friend has coerced professionals to approach me with lucrative investment opportunities or business ventures that too good to be true. 

**Key Warnings for NRIs involving overseas requests &amp; transactions:-** 

* **Always Verify Urgent Demands:** If you receive sudden, pressured requests for money—especially those citing legal emergencies—always seek independent verification from trusted professionals.
* **Question Inconsistencies:** Be wary if a familiar contact unexpectedly resorts to coercion, impersonation, or conflicting narratives involving international jurisdictions.
* **Protect Your Finances &amp; Reputation:** Secure all communications and evidence. Consult legal or financial experts immediately if something seems off.
