1. Applicants have filed this bail application under Section 439 of

 the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 for grant of regular bail as

 they have been arrested in connection with Crime No.302/2021,

 registered at Police Station - Abhanpur, District - Raipur (C.G.)

 for the offence punishable under Section 420 , 120-B of the Indian

 Penal Code. 2. This is the third bail application of applicants. Their earlier bail

 applications were dismissed as withdrawn vide order dated

 07.12.2021 and 12.09.2022 respectively. 3. Case of prosecution in brief is that applicants alongwith other co- accused persons have fraudulently withdrawn an amount of

 Rs.63,33,439/- from account of complainant Ashok Kumar Sahu

 using mobile phone. Based on complaint, offence was registered,

 Police during course of investigation seized one Mobile Phone

 and SIM card from possession of applicants, based upon which

 applicants were arrested on 10.08.2021. 4. Learned counsel for the applicants would submit that other co- accused persons namely Kundan Das, Dinesh Mandal and

 Ashok Das have been enlarged on regular bail by order dated

 26.08.2022, 04.11.2022 & 10.11.2022 respectively. Case of

 applicants also falls on the same footing, therefore, the

 applicants may also be enlarged on regular bail. He further

 contended that applicants are in jail since 10.08.2021. Offence

 registered against applicants are triable by Judicial Magistrate

 First Class and the trial may take sometime for its conclusion.

 Hence, prayed that applicants may be enlarged on bail. 5. Learned counsel for the State opposes the submissions made by

 learned counsel for applicants and would submit that mobile

 phone and SIM have been seized from possession of both

 applicants. SIM, which is used in mobile is seized from applicant

 No.1, from which first call was made to complainant asking him to 3 provide OTP, which will be reflected in his mobile phone and

 thereafter, aforementioned fraud of Rs.63,33,439/- has been

 committed. He further contended that from applicant No.2 also

 mobile phone was seized from his possess. As per material

 available in the charge-sheet his SIM has not been used for

 making phone calls to complainant. Case of applicant No.1 falls

 on different footing as he is the main accused. Applicants are

 resident of Jharkhand and if they are released there are chances

 of their absconding. 6. I have heard the learned counsel for the parties. 7. Taking into consideration the submission of learned counsel for

 applicants, submission of learned State counsel that SIM

 8391870025, which was seized from applicant No.1 was SIM

 from which, complainant received first phone call and thereafter,

 he was subjected to fraud, I am of the view that case of applicant

 No.1 falls on different footing as he is main accused, therefore, I

 am not inclined to enlarged the applicant No.1 on regular bail. So

 far as applicant No.2 is concerned, from his possession one

 mobile phone has been seized and as submitted by learned

 State counsel that SIM, which was seized from possession of

 applicant No.2 was not used for commission of offence and

 further considering the pre-trial detention of applicant No.2, I am

 inclined to enlarged the applicant No.2 on bail. 8. Accordingly, the bail application filed under Section 439 of the

 Cr.P.C. in respect of the applicant No.1 is rejected. However, the 4 bail application in respect of applicant No.2 is allowed. It is

 directed that the applicant No.2 shall be released on regular bail

 upon his furnishing a bail bond in the sum of Rs.50,000/- with one

 local surety in the like sum to the satisfaction of the Court on the

 conditions that:- Certified copy as per rules. Sd/- (Parth Prateem Sahu)
