So I went to Bharat Petrol Pump and they had introduced a new online payment system connected to the fuel meter.

I scanned a QR Code on the wall, and I had to make a UPI payment first.

I paid Rs.150 and then received an SMS with a link to another confirmation screen that opened on my phone browser.

The attendant scanned the confirmation screen, and directly started filling the petrol without doing any entry.

The attendant filled the fuel to Rs.150 exact, and I also checked the petrol reading from 0 to 150 and the liters volume. Everything looked correct. All good.

But what I didn't understand is this, he was distracting me continuously all throughout since when I scanned the QR code.

First he said "Sir, your engine is super hot, there are fumes coming out"

There were literally no fumes.

Then he put his hand near the silencer and saying "look it is hot and I can see fumes"

Literally I saw no fumes, and my scooter is fine.

I denied anything, and then I showed him the QR code. While he is scanning, he said "sir please stand far, petrol will fall on you" He wasn't even holding the pump.

Then once he scanned the code, it literally showing on his POS screen "Rs.150 verified"

He is asking me "Sir, how much did you pay?" - I had also told him long before 150

I didn't reply anything, he proceeded to pickup the pump to fill to Rs.150

My question - what scam was he trying to cook in this process? Usually they distract you like this for cash scam by filling to wrong amount etc. But even for UPI payments?
