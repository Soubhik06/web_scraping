Private jobs are unstable, in almost every sector there are cut-throat competition, irrespective of your skillset or industry, you're always replaceable. 

In government jobs there's definitely a career and financial stability. But again the competition makes it almost like a impossible dream.

Risk-free investments gives low returns and high-return investments are often comes with the risk of losing everything(ar amar ja kopal, I'll probably lose everything, no matter how cautious i am), also there are just too many scams running in this case.

Business is impossible here, considering the lack of such entrepreneurial environment and also the lack of capital. There are some other factors too...
The only Business model I see being somewhat successful here is the fast food sector.
I actually was talking to some of the fast food vendors in our locality and according to them the business environment in this sector is not that good too. Especially after covid19 and the advent of big fast-food chains, people have become increasingly reluctant of consuming fast-food. Cherry on top, they also have pay 'certain amount' to certain groups of people to be able to continue doing business.
