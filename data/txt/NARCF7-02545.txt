Today I got a call from an unknown number.
He had my 2 relatives names correct including city of their residence (though i don't have very good relationship with them and I'm not in touch with them). Also he knew my name.

The fraud part: he asked me if he can transfer some money to my bank account via UPI id which i have to return after 24 hours.

I recognised the fraud as soon as he said "he is xyz and (his relationship with me)" as i recognise the real person's voice. Also his tone wasn't something that runs in my family

Be careful.
