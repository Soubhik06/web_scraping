At this point she got cautious and denied to go to office, told him a reason (bluff) for not coming to office as she got an important bank related work. Today, she replied to the termination email acknowledging the termination rather than resigning, and asking them to complete the exit formalities including the F&amp;F(Full and Final settlement), Salary slips and Relieving letter.
