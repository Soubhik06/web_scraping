Hey, 

So I was sadly scammed by a Swiggy delivery guy - mostly cause of my own stupidity but thought I’d share so that people don’t fall to the same thing. 

At around 930-10pm when delivering the delivery guy told me his bike had broken down and he was hurt and asked for 550 bucks because he needed to get home. He was limping (I guess this was fake) and begged for a bit saying he’ll give his ID and send it to me tomorrow. I was suspicious for a bit but and declined as well but I felt bad so I gpayed him the money &amp; took his number. Plus I was alone at home and eager for the guy to leave my door way. 

Basically he didn’t send it back - after speaking with swiggy and my society gate it seems this guy was using 3 different name and number. Different on swiggy, a different name/number to enter society &amp; different name/number on gpay. Unsure if it’s the 1 guy doing the scam or 3 people together but beware folks! 

Even if you help someone out of the good of your heart, just make sure there’s actually a way to track them so they pay you back. Clearly an idiot but a lesson learnt 🤷🏻‍♀️
