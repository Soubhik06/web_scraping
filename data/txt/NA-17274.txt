You all must have heard about WhatsApp and Telegram scams where people are approached by scammers for part-time jobs. Like, spend 10-15 minutes to earn Rs 5000 daily.

I was approached by tens of such scammers. A few days ago, I decided to play along out of boredom.

So, what they do is that they add you to a group where they ask you to do simple tasks like reviewing etc. They transfer Rs. 50 - Rs 100 to your bank account for each task to win your trust. I mean one has to have an IQ of less than 10 to fall for this scam. The group was full of bots repeating the same messages again and again.

And after that, they ask you to make a transaction with your own money and then they ask you to click on some links, share OTP, etc. I made a Rs 2000 transaction using my "credit card". 2000 was a small amount for me. Immediately, I made a complaint to my bank that I was scammed (that's why I used my credit card). The scammer got scared and returned my money back.

So, I got my Rs 2000 back + I made Rs 1300 profit (the scammer paid me for each task to win my trust). Plus, there is an ongoing investigation by the bank as I have still not closed my appeal. LOL

I earned Rs 1300 on my Rs 2000 investment. LOL. I was ready to let this pass and leave the guy but he abused me afterward which was completely unwanted.

**2000 + 2000 + 1300 = Rs 5,300**

**This is how you make money from scammers.**
