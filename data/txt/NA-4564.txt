This is the First bail application filed under Section 483 of the Bhartiya

 Nagrik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 for grant of regular bail to the

 applicant arrested in connection with Crime No. 129/2025 registered

 at Police Station - Cyber Thana Range, District - Raipur (C.G.) for the

 offence punishable under Sections 317(2), 317(4), 317(5), 111(3) and

 3(5) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS). 2. As per the prosecution story, Account No. 60486847875 with IFSC

 Code MAHB0002335 of Bank of Maharashtra, Raipur Branch is stated

 to be in the name of the applicant/accused. The mobile number

 9993347683 is registered with the said bank account. It has been

 reported that an amount of Rs. 58,41,876/- was deposited in the said

 account. An online report of cyber fraud has been filed by the 2 complainant Ramkali Jatav and another person, bearing

 Acknowledgement Number 32107240022785, regarding a financial

 transaction dated 25.04.2024. It is alleged that an amount of

 Rs. 1,833/- was transferred into the said bank account. It is further

 reported that there are 21 bank accounts in the above bank with IFSC

 Code MAHB0002335, which have been identified as mule accounts. In

 the case diary, the account statement of Account No. 60486847875,

 maintained by the present applicant, has been produced for the period

 06.04.2024 to 30.08.2024. Upon perusal of the statement, it appears

 that approximately 30 to 40 transactions were carried out daily in the

 account. The credit transactions include amounts such as Rs. 2,000/-,

 Rs. 5,000/-, Rs. 10,000/-, Rs. 500/-, Rs. 50,000/-, Rs. 3,000/- and

 other amounts on a daily basis, which the prosecution claims to be

 transactions of an extraordinary nature.. The applicant humbly submits

 that he has been falsely implicated in the present case and has not

 committed any offence. 3. Learned counsel appearing for the applicant submits that the applicant

 is innocent and has been falsely implicated in the present case. It is

 further submitted that the charge-sheet has been submitted in this

 case. It is further submitted that the applicant has been falsely

 implicated in the present case, and absolutely no seizure of any kind

 has been made from the present applicant. The instruments allegedly

 used for committing the said fraud have also not been seized from the

 applicant. Neither the mobile numbers nor the devices have been

 seized. He further submits that the co-accused Ishika Singh was

 granted bail by the Hon'ble Supreme Court in SLP (Criminal) No.

 13678/2025, and another co-accused Sanjay Makhija was also

 granted bail by the Hon'ble Supreme Court in SLP (Criminal) No. 3 14519/2025. It is alleged that the present applicant deceitfully received

 a total amount of Rs. 8,92,842/- through fraudulent means from the

 co-accused Sanjay Makhija. Furthermore, another co-accused

 Ku. Chandramukhi Sinha was granted bail by the Hon'ble High Court

 in MCRC No. 395/2026 on 13.01.2026. He also submits that the

 applicant has no previous criminal antecedents and the applicant is in

 jail since 26.03.2025, and the conclusion of the trial shall take quite

 long time. Therefore, he prays for grant of bail. 4. On the other hand, learned State counsel opposes the bail application

 and submits that the charge-sheet has been filed. He further submits

 that from the perusal of record, it transpires that according to the

 prosecution story, Account No. 60486847875 in Bank of Maharashtra,

 Raipur Branch, having IFSC Code MAHB0002335, is stated to be in

 the name of the applicant/accused. The mobile number 9993347683

 is registered with the said account, and it has been reported that an

 amount of Rs. 58,41,876/- was deposited in the said account. An

 online report of cyber fraud was lodged by the complainant Ramkali

 Jatav and another person, bearing Acknowledgement No.

 32107240022785, in relation to a financial transaction of Rs. 1,833/- in

 the bank account dated 25.04.2024. It has further been reported that

 in the above-mentioned bank having IFSC Code MAHB0002335, a

 total of 21 bank accounts have been identified as mule accounts. 5. In the case diary, the account statement of Account No. 60486847875,

 maintained by the accused, has been produced for the period

 06.04.2024 to 30.08.2024. Upon perusal of the same, it appears that

 approximately 30 to 40 transactions were carried out daily in the

 account. The credit transactions include amounts of Rs. 2,000/-, Rs.

 5,000/-, Rs. 10,000/-, Rs. 500/-, Rs. 50,000/-, Rs. 3,000/- and other 4 amounts on a daily basis, which indicates transactions of an

 extraordinary nature. Thus, it appears that the applicant/accused has

 carried out extraordinary financial transactions through the said

 account. Considering the nature of the offence and the circumstances

 in which it has been committed, it prima facie appears to be an

 organized crime carried out through cyber crime. Thus, it is an

 organized cyber crime committed by the applicant and the co-accused

 persons. As such, the bail application deserves to be rejected. 6. Learned State counsel further submits that the bail applications of

 co-accused, namely, Jai Baghel, Sheikh Shoheb and Jujesh Sona and

 many others have already been rejected by this Court vide orders

 dated 28.02.2026 passed in MCRC Nos. 797/2026, 966/2026 and

 901/2026. Therefore, the bail application of present applicant is also

 liable to be rejected on the same ground. 7. I have heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the case

 diary. 8. In compliance of the Court's order dated 03.02.2026, the Investigating

 Officer of the subject Crime Number, Police Station Range Cyber,

 Raipur, District - Raipur (C.G.), has filed his affidavit showing the

 incriminating evidence collected against the applicant during the

 course of investigation, which is quoted here as under: The said Bhavesh Udwani gave Rs. 2000/- as consideration
 for the account sale to accused Ajay Sahu. 9. Considering the facts and circumstances of the case, allegations

 levelled against the applicant in the FIR, and from the perusal of the

 records available, it transpires that the applicant was actively and

 consciously involved in a well-organized and systematic cyber crime

 syndicate, further, from perusal of the record, it transpires that during

 the course of investigation, it was found that, the arrested accused

 mule bank account holder Ajay Kumar Sahu, Resident of Gudhiyari,

 Raipur vide Bank of Maharashtra, Raipur Branch, Account Number

 60486847875 in which, the Mobile Number 9993347683 was found to

 be linked/registered in which, within a short span of time, financial

 transactions of Rs. 58,41,876/- has been found to be transacted

 illegally through cyber fraud since its opening date, further considering

 the fact that the bail applications of co-accused, namely, Jai Baghel,

 Sheikh Shoheb and Jujesh Sona and many others have already been

 rejected by this Court vide orders dated 28.02.2026 passed in MCRC

 Nos. 797/2026, 966/2026 and 901/2026, thus, this Court is of the

 opinion that it is not a fit case to enlarge the applicant on regular bail. 10. Accordingly, this first bail application of applicant - Ajay Sahu

 involved in Crime No. 129/2025 registered at Police Station - Cyber

 Thana Range, District - Raipur (C.G.) for the offence punishable under

 Sections 317(2), 317(4), 317(5), 111(3) and 3(5) of the Bharatiya

 Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS), is rejected at this stage. 7 11. Needless to say that the learned trial Court concerned is at liberty to

 proceed with the trial and conclude the same, expeditiously. 12. Office is directed to send a certified copy of this order to the trial Court

 concerned for necessary information and compliance forthwith. Sd/- (Ramesh Sinha)
