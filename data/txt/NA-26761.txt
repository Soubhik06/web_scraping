I, 26(M), am being pressured by my family and extended family into building a home for my parents and me to stay in. My parents are financially illiterate, and I am really scared of taking any kind of loan. My mom is an ultra-religious person who thinks investing in stocks, mutual funds, and even saving money via fixed deposits is a sin. She does put her money in chit funds as they are run by a religious institution (losing money in this case, but she thinks it's an investment). My father, on the other hand, loses money in stupid crypto scams and at one point had a ton of credit card debt.

Seeing all this, I have been very frugal and want to set up a nest egg for my retirement. I have been investing in mutual funds and stocks behind my family's back. Recently, they saw my appraisal letter and found out what I am earning. Since then, they have been pressuring me to take a home loan.

I am okay with taking a loan; it's just that I am really scared of my future, which is filled with uncertainty, along with a family with such an unhealthy relationship with money.
