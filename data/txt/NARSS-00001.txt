Beware of this fraudulent taxi service!

We had one of the worst experiences with a taxi service operated by Suraj Kumar Singh (UPI: arunachaltaxiservices@okicici). This company is a complete scam, and I strongly urge everyone to avoid them at all costs.

Here’s what happened in detail:

We had planned our trip meticulously, discussing the details with them over ten times—our destinations, timing, and itinerary. Everything was finalized. Suddenly, just before the trip, at around 10 PM, they informed us they wouldn’t be able to go. This happened in the middle of the night, only hours before our scheduled departure at 7 AM from our hotel. We were shocked and repeatedly questioned why they hadn’t mentioned this earlier, as we could have made alternative arrangements. Instead of providing a proper explanation, they became argumentative and unresponsive.

After a heated exchange, they stopped answering our calls altogether. We kept trying, both ourselves and through the driver they had assigned, but there was no response. Even when we tried to settle the matter, Suraj Singh refused to call back or pick up the phone.

The worst part came during the trip itself. The driver, who was unaware of the situation, told us he hadn’t been paid a single rupee by the agency. At this point, we realized we had been completely duped. Despite already paying the agency an advance, we were forced to pay the driver extra—essentially paying double the amount—just to release the vehicle.

We were left stranded in the middle of our journey, stressed and helpless. Thankfully, our hotel came to the rescue and arranged a different vehicle for us to continue our trip. While this saved us, it was an unnecessary expense and an overwhelming ordeal caused entirely by this fraudulent agency.

This was a nightmare scenario for us, and I wouldn’t wish it on anyone else.

If you’re considering booking with them, DON’T. This agency is nothing but a scam that preys on travelers. Spread the word and help ensure that no one else falls victim to this fraudulent operation. If anyone wants more details about our experience, feel free to reach out.
