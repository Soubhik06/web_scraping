I have come across a scam that I would like to bring to light so that no one else loses their hard earned money to these scamsters.

I am looking for a 1bhk flat in Bangalore and shartlisted a few properties in electronics city. 

The scamster Arun Kumar (+91 91 0 96956 46) and his associate Prem Rajan (
+91 95176 02685) have listed multiple 1bhk properties for rent on squareyards.

https://www.squareyards.com/rental-1-bhk-pooja-room-study-room-650-sq-ft-apartment-in-ajmera-infinity/6453027

When I contacted them for 1bhk rental, they sent me the flat photos on WhatsApp, and asked me to send 2000 rs on UPI for visitor card creation on 16th feb 2024. 

On 17th feb, i was planning to go for the flat visit, I get a call from Mr Prem Rajan, claiming to be from Visitor management department from Ajmera infinity apartments association and said that there was some issue with my visitor card creation and asked me to send another 9000 rs which will be refundable. I declined and told them that it all seems very fishy and I want my 2000 deposit money back, they told me they will refund my money after the call. 

After that they have been unresponsive on phone as well as WhatsApp.

Shoutout to squareyards for letting this scam run rampant on their platform.
 
I have already emailed cybercrime office Bangalore (cybercrimepsbcp@ksp.gov.in) with all these details. Hope they catch these scammers.
