--- POST TEXT ---

A lots of questions have emerged about providing evidences of F16 shot down by India. If by evidence, one means "debris" of the aircraft, then there is none. However, if one connects all the known facts then it becomes clear about the happenings of February 27th. Let me start with few known facts-

1. At 11:49 AM IST, [DG ISPR, Pakistan gives a statement](https://twitter.com/OfficialDGISPR/status/1100641491679150080)
&gt;In response to PAF strikes this morning as released by MoFA, IAF crossed LOC. PAF shot down two Indian aircrafts inside Pakistani airspace. One of the aircraft fell inside AJ&amp;K while other fell inside IOK. One Indian pilot arrested by troops on ground while two in the area.
	
	Pakistan believes that it had shot down two Indian aircrafts and has arrested one pilot while two other pilots remain in the area
	
2. Later in the day, roughly 1-2 hours later [DG ISPR, Pakistan holds an official press conference (In Urdu though)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EkGeXOiGhBU). Important are these two points claimed by him (i) At 4:45 in the video he claims that Pakistan has arrested *two pilots*. Then he claims that Pilot 1 is hospitalised in CMS (indicating that he is severely wounded) while the Pilot 2 is with them. (ii) At 5:52 he says that "India claims that they have downed PAF's F16 but first of all PAF has not even used F16. Secondly, they have no such information about any Pakistan plane being lost"
	
3. Again in the evening, [DG ISPR tweets](https://twitter.com/OfficialDGISPR/status/1100739613486915584) that they have only one person in their custody-
	&gt;There is only one pilot under Pakistan Army’s custody. Wing Comd Abhi Nandan is being treated as per norms of military ethics.
	
4. Meanwhile, Indian media [ANI (not official) tweets](https://twitter.com/ANI/status/1100644064452472832) at 11:59 AM that India has shot down Pakistan's F16-
	&gt;Pakistan Air Force's F-16 that violated Indian air space shot down in Indian retaliatory fire 3KM within Pakistan territory in Lam valley, Nowshera sector.
	 
5. India doesn't make any official comments on 27th February. However, [India holds a press conference of Indian Armed forces on 28th Feb (in English)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hypnYPsPh-w). Following points are important (i) At 3:35, he claims that Indian MIG-21 Bison has shot down Pakistan's F16 which has fallen in POK. (ii) At around 21:00 in the video, India shows a missile - AMRAAM AIM 120C. The only Pakistan jet which can fire this missile is F16.
	The contract number mentioned on the fired missile (FA8675-05-C-0070) matches with that given on [official US gov website](http://archive.defense.gov/Contracts/Contract.aspx?ContractID=3384)
	
6. Unofficial, unreliable yet very important source because the source is independent- [In this video](https://twitter.com/Natsecjeff/status/1100753311114907650) tweeted by a Pakistani and reportedly shot at the location of dogfight, the interviewee claims that he saw three parachutes opening after the fight. There are several other videos ([Video 1](https://twitter.com/5OActionExpress/status/1101172754211061761), [Video 2](https://twitter.com/ajayjandyal/status/1101196235787124736)) from different angles showing at least two parachutes opening. One may choose to ignore this point if one feels that none of the three sources are reliable, it won't change my point much.

7. It is also a well accepted fact by all the sources that an Indian MIG 21 was shot down. 
8. No debris of any other plane has been found out (here, I am ignoring several reports which claim that some of the pics of were of F16 because of dubiousness of the claims)

Now that we have laid down the known facts and have heard all the relevant stories of all the sides let's explore the possibilities. In total, there can be exactly three possibilities regarding the 'second plane'. (i) No other plane was shot down (ii) If other plane was shot down, it wasn't a Pakistani plane (iii) One Pakistani plane was shot down.

----
*"One should always look for a possible alternative, and provide against it. It is the first rule of criminal investigation." Sherlock Holmes, The adventure of Black Peter.*
 
----

Possibility 1: No other plane was shot down.

This possibility is certainly not true. India claims to have shot down F16 (Point 5(i)), Pakistan too claims to have shot down an extra plane (Point 1). Independent videos too have circulated suggesting two planes have fallen (Point 6). Also, the only known fact supporting this possibility is Point 8 which says that no debris has been found out. This is a very weak argument because if the debris will be in Pakistan and of F16, they won't make it public. Similarly, if the debris is in India and of Su-30, India will not make it public. Thus, this point can safely be discarded. Because it contradicts the known facts and all the known testimonies from all sides.

Possibility 2. If a plane was shot down then it wasn't a Pakistani plane 

In this case, the other plane must be an Indian plane.

Assuming this to be true, a question arises that where is the debris? The answer is simple- the debris is in India and India is hiding it. This seems like a plausible explanation until one looks at Point 1, Point 2 and Point 3. Pakistan initially claimed that they had two other people are in the area (apart from the captured one). Where are the other two then? They later revised their claim to say that they have two people in their custody (MIG-21 was single-seater btw). Where is the second pilot then? There are several independent testimonies and videos too showing that there was a second pilot. More important what is the nationality of the second pilot? If the second pilot was Indian then the Indian Air Force has to know that it has lost another pilot. Would India bluff saying that it has lost only 1 plane, knowing perfectly well that Pakistan can show the second pilot and call out India's bluff very easily?

Why did Pakistan change its statement from saying that Pak has two pilots in *its custody* to saying that it has only one? Why did Pakistan lied about not using F16 when they obviously did use F16? 

All of these questions remain unanswered. This possibility, if true, raises certain questions which cannot be answered. A possibility which cannot answer the known facts and even the Pakistani claims in this case is least likely to be true.

----
*"How often have I said to you that when you have eliminated the impossible, whatever remains, however improbable, must be the truth?", Sherlock Holmes, A sign of Four.*

----

Possibility 3: The downed jet was a Pakistani Jet.

Again the same question raises that where is the debris? Again the corresponding answer follows that the debris is in Pakistan and Pakistan is hiding it. Interestingly, this possibilities answers all the questions which were left answered by the previous possibility. Where are the other Pilot(s)? They are in Pakistan's custody. They are Pakistani Pilots, why should Pakistan show them.

Why did Pakistan change its statement from saying that Pak has two pilots in *its custody* to saying that it has only one? Because when the Pilot was caught, they had thought that the Pilot was Indian. Later after inquiry, when it was ascertained that the Pilot was Pakistani, they had to retract the previous statement.

Why did Pakistan lied about not using F16 when they obviously did use F16? This would have given more credence to Pakistan's statement. If they didn't fly it how can it be shot. In fact, if that damned missile was not found by India, there is absolutely no way that India could have shown that Pakistan even used F16 India had electronic signatures (IDK what it means) but India could have hardly shown it to public. 

----
*"We balance probabilities and choose the most likely. It is the scientific use of the imagination." Sherlock Holmes, The Hound of Baskervilles*

----

Hence, I rest my case that the only story on which all the known facts and narratives fit like a piece on a jigsaw puzzle is the story that the downed jet was a Pakistani jet. The only thing missing from this story is the debris of the plane, which would anyway be missing if the possibility 2 was true. All the other stories are filled with contradictions making them extremely unlikely to be true.

Also, if one wishes to contest this claim, one can start by giving examples which contradicts the Possibility 3 or alternatively, one can try to answer the questions which in my opinion are unanswered in Possibility 2.

Edit- However, this is entirely true that a completely distinct Indian jet was down in India which had nothing to do with the two extra pilots in the area. Note that even if another Indian plane was shot down, Possibility 2 will still be negated. It doesn't mean that a Pakistani plane wasn't shot down. It simply would mean that a total of three planes were shot down- A Pakistani and two Indians.
