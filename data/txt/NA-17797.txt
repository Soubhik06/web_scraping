6 years back my parents joined a spiritual meditation group called “Sahaj Marg”. Which is not called “Heartfulness”.

Initially I was skeptical about this group but didn’t stopped them because they are not asking for money and focus on meditations. I thought what could go wrong with meditation.

Later covid happened, and one day my parents watching a video of master called [“Daa ji” (Kamlesh D. Patel)](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamlesh_Patel_(Daaji)). He was speaking in English and telling his devotees to cure covid by [“Neti kriya”](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neti_(Hatha_Yoga))

I told my parents to stop listening to this man, this is BS. They ignored me.

Fortunately next news papar debunked this myth of Neti, and told people to stop believing it. Then they somewhat listened me.

In 2022-23 a girl on kbc comes and told on national television that she can see blindfolded which is thought by “Heartfulness” subgroup “Brighter Minds”. Everyone mesmerised by the demonstration on KBC. 

I called this BS again and told them it is already exposed on stage by ([Narendra Nayak)(President of the Federation of Indian Rationalist Associations)](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narendra_Nayak)Later that episode were withdrawn from most place even sony apologised for airing it.

Few months later my father told me that it is not a scam and he have seen it live. Now his whole devotee group is going to promote it in local school and make them join it.

I told him stop doing this you got scammed and now you are scamming people. But we had heated conversation he told me you wont understand this is a spiritual thing. Even knowing I am the most educated person in the family.

Recently one of my loving relative also put his son in this blindfolded reading program by “BrighterMinds”, my father now pushing my niece into this.
