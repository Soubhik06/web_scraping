--- POST TEXT ---

I recently posted about an older friend of mine who got scammed by calling a fake geek squad number she found on a google. The number didn't lead to geek squad but to a scam call center in India. The scammers were able to convince her to install some remote access software, and did the refund scam with her. They wanted her to take out 11k in cash to put in some bitcoin atm, and were yelling at her to not call her bank, and not tell anyone about what they were doing. She's a super nice person and thought she had received 11k by mistake and really wanted to not take the money. 

Older people are targeted in these types of scams as they have more money, are more trusting, and are not knowledgeable about technology. When I was a kid adults would have conversations about stranger danger eg. if an adult asks you to keep a secret, it's suss etc. In 2024 we need to be telling the older people in our lives that there is stranger danger when:

\- "Jim" with an Indian accent telling them to keep financial secrets

\- "Bob" with an Indian accent is telling them to install software when the reason for their call has nothing to do with a computer problem, eg "refund scam"

\- Major company is trying to give you a refund and it's not just a refund onto your credit card, they want to pay using some convoluted method. 

\- Anything gift card related, anything bitcoin related, anything with a "big company" needing your bank account info when you paid by credit card

Basically any time a stranger to you wants to get into your online banking, or your computer, it's a red flag. There's legitimate cases of course, but you should be careful when this comes up. if they're telling you to keep this a secret you should NEVER keep this a secret.
