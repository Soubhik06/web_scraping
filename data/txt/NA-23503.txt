--- POST TEXT ---

I want to bring to everyone's attention that on Saturday afternoon, a friend of mine received a call from a courier service claiming to be from FedEx. They informed him that a parcel was being sent in his name, containing laptops, mobile phones, clothing, and illegal drugs, using his name and Aadhaar number. 

This understandably frightened him, leaving him feeling very unstable about the situation. The FedEx representative then stated they would transfer the call to Mumbai Cyber Crime.

Following that, a group of individuals posed as cyber crime investigators and interrogated him aggressively. They scolded him and insisted he read documents they sent in the chat, which looked authentic and included his name. This led him to believe his identity was truly being misused, but he didn’t realize he was being scammed.

The call continued over Skype, and the ID they used looked very legitimate, leaving him with no doubts. He was terrified and completely confused about what was happening. His only thought was to find relief from the situation. Eventually, they connected him with someone posing as a DCP officer, claiming he would be the one to investigate him. This "officer" interrogated him harshly, insisting they needed to verify his identity in connection with a major criminal case.

In a state of heightened anxiety, he didn’t think clearly. They pressured him to share all his bank details and account balances, and unfortunately, he complied. Then, a person claiming to be from the RBI contacted him, instructing him to transfer money to a criminal’s account to check if it was linked to illegal activity. They kept him on the line for about 30 minutes after he transferred nearly 2 lakh rupees.

They sent all the documents via chat as proof of everything they claimed. After about 30 minutes, the inspector returned and assured him that he was safe, stating that he had no case against him and that he was now free.

After everything was over, he called me, and I went to his place. At first, everything he told me seemed legitimate. However, I gradually realized that a scam had occurred, so we quickly rushed to the police station.

Then we came to know not only We but so many people were there for the complaints. Completely blanked out.
