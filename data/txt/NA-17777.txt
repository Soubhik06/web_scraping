I don't know if this subreddit allows personal posts like this but I have no idea what else to do.

I'm 22 years old from Guwahati, Assam. And everything is f***ed up right now. I'm a high school dropout as I suffered from severe mental and physical health problems, I've been trying to pass my class 10 exams from NIOS and if I passed one more subject I would have passed class 10. Unfortunately because of Corona virus, exams got cancelled and I can do nothing about it. Also as you might know, in India a high school drop out is basically a parasite who deserves no respect or recognition as a human being and he is given no opportunity to redeem himself. Best option for a job for a person like me is a scam call centre where I worked for sometime before the lockdown. I didn't want to do it even a little bit because it was illegal, I absolutely hated scamming people and I didn't like being called "a piece of shit" and sorts of harsh words on a daily basis by Americans.

My older brother was my only backup in my life, he lives in Gurgaon and he had a decent job but he lost his job because his company suffered great losses as a result of the lockdown. Now he can't find any other job as no one is hiring.

My father is 74 years old and he was always a bit deranged but recently he has absolutely lost it. He treats his own family members like his enemies and tells us to go away and live separately. He has no pension, no source of income and is completely bankrupt. We don't have a house of our own and pretty soon will have to live on the street and eventually starve to death.

Because of all this, my mother is seriously considering suicide and I too think that is the best option for us right now. She has been desperately trying to get a job for herself, she is well educated, smart and has a lot of experience working but she is 62 years old and because of her age nobody wants to hire her.

I am completely lost and confused. Any advice?
