--- POST TEXT ---

I was just sitting, reading a newspaper when my phone rung. I reached for it and swiped the screen. It's an unknown number.

Caller: "Hello"

Me: "Yes?"

Caller: "Sir, your ATM has been blocked. If you want to keep using your card, you must pay..."

I hung up the call. I am aware enough to know that that's not how banks function. Which, obviously, makes it a fraud. But how many people are aware of this?

It's not like this is first time I've crossed paths with this practice either.

In the past week, one of my uncles has been ripped off of ₹25,000/- in a PayTM scam.

Another uncle, I suspect, is participating actively in a bank fraud as the manager of a prominent bank.

Which is why, I wasn't surprised when I quite surprised when I read about scams and frauds on r/India these days. These are not immensely popular threads and usually fly under the radar for most of you, I imagine. And these are not all.

[Indian banks have reported **73%** spike in frauds at Rs 71,543 cr in FY19](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/banking/banks-see-whopping-73-spike-in-frauds-at-rs-71543-cr-in-fy19/articleshow/70075131.cms?from=mdr).

[PayTM frauds like the one my uncle recently fell a victim to, are ubiquitous](https://www.gadgetsnow.com/tech-news/paytm-fraud-2-arrested-juvenile-apprehended-for-cheating-people/articleshow/66525465.cms).

Everytime I'm in Canada, I get [CRA and immigration scam calls from Indians](https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/national-cra-india-rcmp-scam-1.4883796) on a regular basis.

And banking-related scams like the one from my experience moments ago, have shot up in the recent times, based on my personal experience. I've lived in India most of my life and I have hardly gotten as many frauds calling me as I do nowadays.

Why is that? Why are we seeing such a sharp spike in these illicit activities just now? Well, I believe the answer to that is simple: 

When a nation's economy is brutally murdered and left to rot in the sewers, when millions of people lose their jobs in an instant yet they refuse to acknowledge the situation and cheer their leader on who took their jobs away and distracting them by merely appeasing to their nationalistic side, what you are left with is those same people turning to scamming and defrauding their own countrymen.

Because after their celebration is over and they return home, that's when reality hits- pseudo nationalism doesn't feed your family. So they do the one thing that they can do- resort to activities that lead to the results as discussed above. Like shipwrecked men turning to seawater from thirst, these desperate nationalists decide to be at each-other's throats and in that instant, unbeknownst to them, their hypocrisy of being a nationalist is exposed, for a nationalist, a real one, first and foremost, considers his/her countrymen equal and treats them with respect. But it seems the definition these days has changed to blindly following your leader down the cliff.

I won't be surprised if in the coming days, India is engulfed in flames with unemployed rioters and anti-social elements clash with the police in the streets. An economy dead in the water combined with a largely uneducated and superstitious citizenry can have further reaching consequences than one might imagine.
