I'm writing this to share a horrifying experience I had with a PG called Colive in Bangalore, and to warn others about a potential scam. I hope you can help me spread awareness about this issue so that others will be cautious and safe.

I moved into a Colive PG on November 28th. Before moving in, I was asked to send my Aadhar and PAN card details, and I paid ₹5,000 as an advance and ₹2,000 through Colive app. I was told my rent would be ₹3,000, plus about ₹3,000 for food and water (about ₹6,000 total per month). I made sure to make the initial payments before occupying the room as instructed by them.

Things immediately went wrong. The person I initially spoke to blocked my number. Then I was contacted by other people from different numbers. The rent amount on the Colive app on December 1st showed ₹12,000! When I questioned them, I was told not to worry about it. Later, another guy who introduced himself as the manager demanded ₹9,000.

The worst part happened yesterday, December 11th. Four men broke down my door and threatened me for ₹10,000 in rent! They threw my things and acted aggressively. I was terrified and started recording them with my phone. They also tried to snatch my phone and other things. Then I started grabbing a video, their attitude changed. When I told them I was vacating, they eventually said I didn't owe them anything. They became very incoherent when I started recording, so I decided to move out to a safer place immediately.

False Rent Promises: I was initially told ₹6,000, which then kept escalating.

Harassment &amp; Intimidation: I was threatened by "rowdies" who broke into my room. They broke the door. 

Lack of transparency: I was never given a proper agreement or a breakdown of the charges, and they have different contact numbers.

No proper information: They didn't provide a proper explanation of why different people were contacting me.

I've already left the PG. I have documented everything (screenshots, recordings, transaction records, their numbers etc). I'm planning on filing a police report and also reporting to cyber crime cell.

Please, be extremely cautious when dealing with Colive or any similar PGs. Do your due diligence, verify their claims, and never engage with such individuals without any proofs.

If anyone has experienced similar issues, please share your story. Any advice would be greatly appreciated.

TL;DR: Moved into a Colive PG in Bangalore, was promised one rent, later harassed and threatened by "rowdies" for higher amounts. I left the PG and am looking into legal action. Be warned of potential scam activity.
