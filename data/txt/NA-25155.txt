This is a relatively old scam but people should still be aware about it 

Yesterday i received a call from someone who said that they work with my father and they'd had a word with him and he gave him my UPI id and that he had sent me money he owed to him( my father) but he accidentally payed ne 500 more and just wanted me to check whether or not I'd received it as the payment was pending from their end.

Seemed pretty normal as my dad has used my UPI ID to receive payments sometimes but here's where i figured out it's a scam 

1. He referred to my dad as "my last name + ji" which is not accurate because my last name is different 

2. He said he was in "line duty" in Delhi and can wait for me to confirm the fact that my dad asked me to receive money from him. This was sus because my dad works in Mumbai 

I checked my bank statement and saw i hadn't received any money in my bank account. But by that point I'd figured out it was a scam so I told him, I need to confirm this with my father before I move further and he said he's' fine at first but started to pressurize me a little by saying he just needs the excess of 500 back and nothing more. 

So I told him to get the money back from my dad himself as he'll probably see him tomorrow at "line duty" ( my dad is an options trader lmao), this pissed him off and he said he'll have his son contact me because I'm being "badtameez" 

So I told him he can either put the transaction in dispute from his end or get 500 back from my dad tomorrow and hung up and blocked him.
