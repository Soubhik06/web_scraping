suit which is undisputed. Sec.12A of the Commercial
Courts Act 2015 provides for undergoing the process of
Pre Institution Mediation prior to filing of the suit. The
Hon'ble Apex Court in the decision reported in (2022) 10
Supreme Court Cases 1, Patil Automation Private
Limited and others vs. Rakheja Engineers Private
Limited , has observed that any suit where no urgent
reliefs are sought it has to mandatorily undergo the
process of Pre Institution Mediation as contemplated
under Sec.12A of Commercial Courts Act 2015. In the said
decision it has been further observed that any suits which
are filed subsequent to 20.08.2022 must necessarily
undergo the process of Pre Institution Mediation unless
urgent reliefs are claimed and the plaintiff is exempted
from undergoing said process. The above observation and
the position of law has been reiterated by the Apex Court
in its subsequent decisions reported in 2025 SCC Online
SC 1129 M/S Dhanbad Fuels Private Limited v.
Union of India & Anr. (2025). 11. In the present case suit of the plaintiff has been filed
on 14.12.2022 i.e., subsequent to 20.08.2022. In para 3 of

operating as a collecting agent of plaintiff with respect to

proceedings under Sec.12A of Commercial Courts Act
2015. In fact along with the suit I.A.Nos.1 and 2 under
Orders XXXIX Rules 1 and 2 seeking a relief of temporary
injunction and attachment before judgment under Order
XXXVIII Rule 5 have been filed upon which emergent
notice has been ordered. The proceedings of the order
sheet would also does not indicate any order being passed
dispensing undergoing the process of PIM as mandatorily
required under Sec.12A of Commercial Courts Act 2015. Therefore it is crystal clear that the present suit has been
instituted without undergoing the mandatory requirement
of the Sec.12A of the Commercial Courts Act,2015 and
also without seeking leave of the Court to institute the suit
by exempting the mandatory process of Pre Institution
Mediation. Though after contest this Court vide order
dated 12.12.2023 granted the relief of temporary
injunction nevertheless there is no order exempting PIM
proceedings as mandated under Sec.12A of the
Commercial Court Act 2015. Hence it can be held without 18 Com.OS.No.1845/2022

hesitation that the suit is barred under Sec.12A of the

Courts Act 2015 would require the plaint to be visited with
rejection. Hence it can be held without hesitation that the
