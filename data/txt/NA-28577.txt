Understanding UPI
UPI is a real-time payment system developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) that allows users to link multiple bank accounts to a single mobile app.

It facilitates seamless money transfers, bill payments, and merchant transactions through a simple interface. Since its inception, UPI has seen exponential growth, with billions of transactions conducted monthly.
Myth 1: UPI is not secure
One of the most prevalent myths is that UPI is not secure. This misconception often stems from a lack of understanding of the security measures in place.
Reality: UPI is highly secure
UPI transactions are safeguarded by robust security protocols. Each transaction requires two-factor authentication: a mobile PIN (MPIN) and a one-time password (OTP). Additionally, UPI transactions are encrypted end-to-end, ensuring that data is secure during transmission. The NPCI regularly updates its security standards to counter evolving threats, making UPI a reliable and secure platform for digital payments.
Myth 2: UPI transactions are prone to fraud
There is a common belief that a
UPI payment
is highly susceptible to fraud, with stories of unauthorised transactions often circulating.
Reality: UPI has multiple fraud prevention m,easures
While no system is entirely immune to fraud, UPI has several measures in place to protect users. These include:
Transaction limits
: UPI imposes transaction limits both per transaction and per day to minimise the impact of fraudulent activities.
Secure authentication
: The requirement of an MPIN and OTP for each transaction ensures that unauthorised users cannot easily access accounts.
Instant alerts
: Users receive instant notifications for every transaction, allowing them to quickly detect and report any suspicious activity.
UPI PIN
: Users must set a UPI PIN, which adds an extra layer of security. Without this PIN, transactions cannot be authorised.
Myth 3: UPI is only for tech-savvy individuals
Another myth is that UPI is complicated and only suited for those who are technologically adept.
Reality: UPI is user-friendly
Some of the popular UPI applications in India such as the Bajaj Pay are designed with user-friendliness in mind, making them accessible to a broad audience.
The interface is typically intuitive, with straightforward instructions for setting up and conducting transactions. Many banks and financial institutions offer customer support to assist users with any difficulties they may encounter.
Myth 4: UPI can lead to privacy breaches
Concerns about privacy breaches are common, with users worried that their personal and financial information could be compromised.
Reality: UPI Prioritises user privacy
UPI is built with stringent privacy measures to protect user data. Only essential information required for transactions is shared, and this data is encrypted.
Additionally, UPI adheres to the data privacy regulations set forth by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and other regulatory bodies, ensuring that users’ personal information is handled with the utmost care.
Myth 5: UPI transactions are not reversible
Some believe that once a UPI transaction is made, it cannot be reversed, leading to fears of losing money if a mistake occurs.
Reality: UPI supports transaction reversal
In cases of incorrect transactions or fraud, UPI supports mechanisms for reversing transactions. Users can raise disputes through their UPI app or bank, and if the claim is valid, the amount can be reversed. The NPCI and participating banks have a grievance redressal system in place to handle such issues efficiently.

Transaction monitoring

User education

Customer support

Best practices for safe UPI usage
While UPI is inherently secure, users should follow best practices to enhance their security:
Use trusted apps
: Only use UPI apps from reputable sources, such as your bank or well-known third-party providers like Bajaj Pay, Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm.
Keep your MPIN confidential
: Never share your MPIN with anyone, and avoid writing it down where it can be easily accessed.
Regularly monitor transactions
: Keep an eye on your transaction history and report any suspicious activity immediately.
Update your app
: Ensure that your UPI app is always up to date to benefit from the latest security enhancements.
Beware of phishing scams
: Be cautious of unsolicited messages or calls asking for your UPI details. Legitimate institutions will never ask for your confidential information.
