I just completed my final exams and I'm desperate for a job so I applied online through Monster, Indeed and Naukri. 

So far all I have got are scam message offers from Jaguar 'jaguarlandrovercvunit@gmail.com' (very convincing mail address) and some fraud job consulting angencies posing as industrial companies asking me to pay registration fee of ₹300 for jobs. 

They had a quite believeable website and pdf documents untill I noticed the registration fee. A quick search in Google reveals, this is their modus operandi, where they ask registration fee of ₹300 then ₹3000 for job postings.

If there's anyone who got job through such portals, and anyone recruiting through such portals please advise on the Do's and Don'ts. Thanks in advance.
