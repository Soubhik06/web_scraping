I knew my first instincts were right about the government not being capable enough to safely keep the data of a billion people away from hackers.

I begged and begged my family not to force me to get an aadahar but they didn't listen. I showed them documents saying that high court has specifically mentioned that aadahar card cannot be mandatory. But they were adamant, get it NOW!!

so i did.

what a stupid mistake.

People of that IRCTC leak thread were bashing aadahar card for all the right reasons and that made my stomach hurt. 

With that said , I have no idea what a profession identity thief can do with my aadahar card information. 

Lets say if the data does get leaked tomorrow, how bad would a general aadahar holder will be fucked?
