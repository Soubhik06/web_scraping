I used to telemarket for 3 years when I was in high school. We cold called people on a list and offered furnace and air duct cleaning. Sometimes they would buy, most times they’d hang up or get pissed, but if they EVER told us to put them on the Do Not Call list, we obliged and ended the phone call right there.

Since paying off a bunch of credit card debt, I have been getting non-stop calls from Indian call canters asking me about loans or some shit who knows. I normally don’t answer, but when I have asked to be put on the Do Not Call list in the past, they will actually argue about it with me and tell me to just hear them out. What the fuck is this? I literally screamed at a lady, and she told me “sir dis is nod a scam, dis is a program…” Repeater and told her to put me on the Do Not Call list, which I doubt was done.

I then called the number back, because they must use proxy phone numbers, and a little old lady answered and told me it wasn’t her who called. I informed her that her number was being used.

In any case, is this scamming, or are telemarketers these days just that bold? Better yet, what can I do about it besides watching “Scammer Loses His Mind When I DELETE His Hard Drive!” videos?

God damn.
