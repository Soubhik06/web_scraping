Hi all! 

Usually these days scammers are finding new ways to scam you. Some scams are common these days like the telegram ones in which they approach you for a "part time jobs" and give you starting tasks like hotel reviews, insta/youtube subscriptions, etc.

I have researched on this a lot and found that how they works. When they first time give you the money (like Rs. 150 - 300/-), they are not giving it from their accounts but from other victims account. So please don't accept any money from them, for those who thinks they are tricking them; understand this if some victim complaints against Cyber Crime and they can track the link to your account, your account gets freezes!

Just for your information purposes. 
Be Vigilant and Be Aware.
