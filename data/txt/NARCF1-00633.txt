Recently, I ordered food for myself and when the delivery guy came to deliver the food, he told me randomly that recently he was scammed by someone for a few thousand rupees. I asked if he was scammed online so that I could help him better. He said yes. I told him that if it was over UPI / Bank, you can try to ask the bank to the refund the money back to your account (I thought that banks might have an option to revert back the transaction in case or missed / fraudulent transactions).

He gave me the order and left. After 20-30 mins, he called me and said that he is sure that I am the person who scammed him and that my voice exactly matches the scammer’s voice. He also pointed out that the moment I heard the word “scam”, I asked “online hua hai kya?” and so he thinks I already knew how he was scammed. I believe that most scams in today’s day and age happen online so it was a natural question to ask.

Later, he said that he is filing a complaint against me in the police station and that he is warning me about it beforehand. And that he is damn sure that I am the scammer.

Context on me : I am a working professional who has recently started my job in Gurgaon. 

Is this a scam? Should I be concerned?
