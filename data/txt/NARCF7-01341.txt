This post is only intended to serve as an awareness for those intending to transport their goods and vehicles across the country. I hope the mistake I made doesn't happen to anyone else. And sorry about the long read, but I had to fill in all details.

&amp;#x200B;

This is Krishan Kumar Choudhry aka KK Choudhry (in the images). 

&amp;#x200B;

In December 2022, I had shifted apartments in Hyderabad and had engaged a local Packers &amp; Movers company (Ekta Packers &amp; Movers) to shift all my stuff. Since my new apartment only had a single parking lot allotted to me, I decided to send my older car to my hometown (Trivandrum, Kerala). Being an old car, I didn't want to engage the services of top P&amp;Ms in the interest of cost efficiency (a decision I dearly regret). I asked one of the workers from Ekta to put me in touch with someone they knew who transports vehicles. The next day, I get a call from Surendra Kumar, who told me he was MD of a car transport company and that he'll move the car for me, for Rs. 18,000. He asked for a payment of Rs. 5,000 as advance. On payment, the next day (December 22nd), KK Choudhry came to pick my car up. Reminder that at the time, due to not being used over long periods, my car's battery was dead and had to be towed away. The car was promised to be delivered in Trivandrum within 7-10 days. The remaining Rs. 13,000 was agreed to be paid on delivery of the car.

&amp;#x200B;

The next night, I get a call from Choudhry, and this is when all of the criminal activities began. He threatened me that if I don't pay up the remaining amount, he would abandon the car in the middle of nowhere. And this time, he wanted Rs. 28,000 and not Rs. 18,000. After an entire night of argument, he agreed to settle for Rs. 23,000, and that I had to pay up Rs. 19,000 immediately. And of course, the Rs. 5,000 that I had transferred to Surendra Kumar didn't count. It was only later that I realised that Surendra Kumar (a cousin of Choudhry's) was a scammer based in Rajasthan. I transferred the Rs. 19,000 to Choudhry in the hopes that I get the car back or that they don't end up using it for any criminal activities.

&amp;#x200B;

On January 7th, 2023, I went to my nearest Police Station to file a complaint about my missing car. Staying true to their never-ending endeavour to keep crime rates low in the city, I was told that they will not accept my complaint, and that I can investigate on my own if I wanted to. On January 11th, through the help of a close friend's father (a retired top official in Telangana Police) and an MLA acquaintance, I managed to get some facetime with a top official in Telangana Police. Over the next few months, a few calls were made to Choudhry asking about the status of the car delivery, by the police. Still, an official complaint was never accepted. 

&amp;#x200B;

On March 21st, via the police, Choudhry coerced me into buying a new battery for the car so he can drive it into the truck. I had to oblige. He sent me a video of the car being driven into the truck. And then there was silence. For a month. After several calls being made to him, KK Choudhry finally delivered my car on April 25th. The Police actively followed up several times with me to ensure that I had paid him the full amount (despite having told them about all the scams and threats that Choudhry had put me through). 

&amp;#x200B;

I had filed an online complaint with Hyderabad Cyber Cell against Surendra Kumar for the scam. After a month, I get an email saying that the case had been transferred to Kerala Police since that's where my address is. After yet another month, I get a call from Kerala Cyber Cell stating that they followed up with the scammer's bank and figured out that he's a serial scammer, but they don't have the budget (from the government) to pursue the case. That was it.

&amp;#x200B;

In total, this whole ordeal had cost me upwards of Rs. 30,000. But that doesn't stop there. The car, after reaching Trivandrum, had mysteriously stopped running, despite having the new battery. After taking it to my local workshop, the mechanic took a good look at the engine and informed that the engine oil had been drained, and had been filled with water instead.

&amp;#x200B;

KK Choudhry's plan was to pocket all the money and abandon the car somewhere, hoping that I would let go(?). For all the 'trouble' I put him through, he poured water into my engine, waited for a month to ensure that the car would become un-revivable and returned it back to me. Such is the mentality of people. Such criminals have the confidence that the police system in our country will never get to them perhaps? I have nowhere to reach out for help. All I could do is post this here so no one else would have to go through the amount of mental stress that I had to endure.

&amp;#x200B;

KK Choudhry's transportation company goes by the name of 'MRC Packers &amp; Movers', 'Car Transport Car Carriers' among other names. His known accomplices (who have also called me to discuss payments) include Kavita Choudhry and Sandeep Choudhry.

&amp;#x200B;

And yes, I acknowledge that some amount of due diligence on my part could have helped me avoid this whole situation, but then, some people only get to know that the pan is hot when they touch it and burn their hands.
