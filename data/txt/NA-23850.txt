I'm 17(M) studying in 12th, Was on my way back from college (it was around 7pm). Got down from the bus around 100 metres from home a old man around 60-65 with an old Nokia phone approached me, His english was surprisingly good

He told he was a retired army officer who came to meet his friend all the way from Yelahanka but underestimated how much it would cost to get here and was low on cash (I live nearby Pheonix marketcity) I asked him if he accepted UPI but then realized he had a button phone. Since I didn't have cash I asked him to wait while I go get my wallet from home 

2 Minutes later I'm at my phone picked my wallet up and was about to go back to him, my dad stops me asks where I was going. I explain him the situation and he understood almost instantly that it's a scam since he's more experienced with this, he then takes his bike key, asks me to sit and we go to the same man.

My dad smartly then asks "You want to go Yelahanka? come sir I'll drop you" in a pretty kind tone but the man gets very defensive and literally just starts running away

So these scams been always here? and how do these people speak this good English (his was probably better than mine)
