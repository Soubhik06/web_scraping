So I found this website [transtutors.com](http://www.transtutors.com/) which says I can get paid for solving as well as creating and uploading assignments for college undergrads throughout the world. There are various fields-maths, stats, physics etc for which you can apply. They say that a Bachelor's Degree is required in Science.

The real bummer where I start to get suspicious is the salary mentioned-a max limit of $5000/month. It just sounds too good to be true for someone just solving/uploading assignments online.

Some reviews on various websites say it's all fake and a scam, and that in fact the real address of the company (which is mentioned to be somewhere in NY, which another review said is an address that doesn't exist) belongs in India and the directors are both Indians.

Others say that it was a good experience using that website and their assignments were duly done and they were glad with the service (these are from people using the service on the website, I've yet to come across a review that comes from someone who was working for the website, ie, solving the problems for other students).
