And I recently got a call from Bandhan Bank saying I applied there and asking for my documents. I gave my masked adhar card and my last qualification certificate with a passport size photo and after 30 mins I received a call saying my documents are verified and I got the job but I need to pay ₹240 and fill out a form. Since this is online where I don't need to sit for any exam. According to him this ₹240 will be refunded when I join and my salary will be in hand ₹24K. So this is a scam??
