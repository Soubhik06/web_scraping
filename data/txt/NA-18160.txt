TL;DR: A WhatsApp chatbot that can be used to instantly validate rumors rampant on social media. Refer to the [YouTube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnfTOcMVYpA) or our [website](https://thedetektor.com/) for more info.

Today, fake news has plagued social media. Polarizing articles, hate speeches, and misinformation is rampant on platforms like WhatsApp. It has the power to influence everything, from vaccination drives to communal harmony and elections. A simple forwarded message, sent without checking the validity of its content, can instigate riots, spread mass hysteria and even cause someone to lose their life.

As students, we have developed Detektor to resolve this problem. If a person wants to check the validity of a claim, they can simply send it to Detektor on WhatsApp. It replies instantly with fact checks and information from verified sources. Realizing the proliferation of this issue throughout the country, we have designed it to support all languages.

We hope that you make use of Detektor and share it with all your friends and family, helping them become more informed digital citizens!
