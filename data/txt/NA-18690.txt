Hello everyone,

This might sound petty, but it adds up. 

I want to understand the pov of people around our country and how do they deal with daily occuring issues relating to some or the other small financial loss.

Its best explained with examples of daily life. 

* We need to check 0 in petrol pumps or u would easily be scammed by them by not resetting the meter. If you miss ones due to distraction they would scam.

* Getting any appliance service done.

 Learnt the hard way, shifting air conditioner does not need a total gas refill. There are many scams related to air con which most still don't know.

Recently got gas stove serviced, they did a half ass job n forced to pay up. Ofcourse after checking/ researching later figured I paid for a service I did not get.

* car repair shops. Well, if you dont have basic knowledge they will find a way for u to pay extra. They will make up lies, wont change the parts and charge for it. 

*BSNL - Had fiber connection&gt; closed it &gt; paid balance bill and they still have deposit of 2 months &gt; got a legal notice after 1 year saying I owe them money. (basically they did not do their job of closing the connection for months even tho I have sms proof)

* Hit n run on the road.(not a scam but financial loss) This would be more in metro cities. The cops dont help much either. Just ask us to claim insurance, but who will pay thr NCB that is lost? And the time wasted.

* Auto rikshaw scams, atleast now this is limited with the help of apps. 

Many dont know they are being scammed because they believe the person.

There are many more I have not mentioned but you get the idea. 

It really gets to me and spoils my entire day even if its a small amount. We work hard and earn with honesty then there are these people who scam and take away our money.

I want to understand how do people let this go because we cant avoid it always. Nor we can keep fighting all the time.
