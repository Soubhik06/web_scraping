So a few days ago I applied for a job at Guwahati airport that I found on Quikr. Here is the screenshot of it: http://imgur.com/a/RCE3Vhg

Today I received this message: http://imgur.com/a/SsG9hMn I didn't call the number since I didn't have the time.

At 1:15 PM I received a call from the number 8750874824. It was a woman who spoke in Hindi and said that my application has been shortlisted and asked for details like my age, my height and weight and what my parents do. I told her briefly but didn't provide too many details. My doubts started when she asked what my educational qualifications are because I had already mentioned that in my résumé. She then said that I had to pay 800 Rupees to confirm my interview seats and it will be refunded after I appear for the interview. She said that I will receive a message and I would have to pay via an UPI app like GPay and that I had to pay it within 1:30 PM. I really started to doubt her at this point since she didn't sound professional and kept calling me "dear" after every sentence and she pronounced "résumé" as "resume". She also said that the interview will be done first via email where I would have to answer some questions and that she will help me with it. Second via a telephonic conversation and third a face-to-face interview at the airport and they will provide pickup service so I could go there.

After she hung up, I immediately checked IndiGo's website and they explicitly warned that they do not take any fees for recruitment. I then received this message from her: http://imgur.com/a/BVzkHeE

After I received the message she called me back a few seconds later. I told her I had read IndiGo's warnings and that I didn't trust this whole thing, she kept trying to convince me but I wasn't convinced. She then asked if I would like to speak to her senior manager, I said yes. The senior manager (also a woman) also tried to convince me that IndiGo doesn't hire people themselves and because of the Covid situation they are using this method. I then read aloud the warnings on IndiGo's website to her. She said that 800 Rupees is too small an amount to scam someone. She then said if I wasn't convinced then I could do a face-to-face interview to which I agreed. I asked when should I appear for the interview but she had no answer for it.

After hanging up I checked as many websites as I could to see if IndiGo was asking money for the Covid situation but I always got the same answer: IndiGo never asks for any money/fees for interviews.

The first girl called me back after a few minutes and in a frustrated voice asked "aapko yeh job karna hai ki nahi karna hai?" To which I replied "IndiGo baar baar warn ker rahé hai ki woh koi paise nahi lete so main yeh trust nahi kar sakta." She said "Theek hai" and hung up.

I then downloaded Truecaller to check who these people really are. The girl told me her name was Mamata Sharma but on Truecaller her name came as Kirti Sharma: http://imgur.com/a/tCfEdnk

In the first message I received it said HR Amardeep but on Truecaller his name came as Rishabh Dixit Air: http://imgur.com/a/AqiENFY

And this was the name of guy I was supposed to send the money to (Mamata said he was the CEO or something): http://imgur.com/a/V6gWmn8

Unfortunately I didn't think about recording the calls. But it didn't seem legitimate to me. Was this a scam or is this how things are actually done?
