This happened years ago, before the plague transitioned my office to WFH.

I was at work when my phone rang, didn't recognize the number so I hung up. They left a message stating they were from the Social Security Administration and that I was going to be arrested for fraud if I didn't call them back. I want born yesterday so I deleted it.

Few hours later I'm on my break chatting with my boss when my phone rings again, same number. Smiling I pick up and put it on speaker.

Thick Indian accent comes out of the phone.

"Hello, yes, is this OP? I am calling from the Social Security Office. You are being investigated for fraud."

My boss smiles cause she's knows what's next.

"Really," I say holding back laughter. "Cause my wife works for Social Security and -"

Call ends. Didn't even get to finish the sentence. My boss and I burst out laughing.

My wife really does work for SSA but it's a good line to use on anyone trying this scam no matter who you're married to.
