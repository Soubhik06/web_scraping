I opted for a local mover and packer (company name goes by Maruti Express) - there are tons of them I guess, and I wanted to shifted my good from BLR to UAE. First, the overall freight charges was mentioned 320 Rs per KG. When the bill came, the CGST AND SGST charges were slapped even though the GSTIN number wasn't mentioned on the bill. The total bill came around 1lac !!!!! 
The guy mentioned that it will take a week to deliver all goods. I thought I will get my goods when I needed, so I asked for 1 month storage and then asked for my goods. I sent all the relevant documents and then the guy ignored me for 15-20 days saying its in process and he will send tracking ID. Until one day he mentions, the shipment will only be released when the insurance money is paid, so to avoid any issues and to get my items I paid 16k more. 

Again he did the same, ignored calls messages everything. Out of the blue I get his message that freight charges have changed so now its 500 per kg and the amount he asked was not even equating to the number of KGs and amount he asked. I refused to pay. I have filed the fraud case in consumer court - items are stucked either in mumbai or bangalore no idea. He did mention that it is in mumbai but keeps asking for money. I told him to send it back - he says there will lot of charges incurred etc. So basically he won't stop pestering until he gets money.

I am waiting to hear from consumer court and get items back maybe later? but happy to hear what other steps can be taken. Thanks
