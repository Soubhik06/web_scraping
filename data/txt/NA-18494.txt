So, i placed an order yesterday and today I received a call from (supposedly from the seller) and asked me if he is talking to (my name) and confirm this is the order we received.

I was shocked and cut the call. I hardly receive any spam calls ( and no one who knows me calls anyway) cuz I dont put my number anywhere.

Now amazon claims they do not give out PII to sellers especially if the order is fulfilled by Amazon which in this case was. I chatted with amazon and the agent first said its fraud call and we never give contact number to seller. Later agent asked for the number from which I received call and said this is the sellers number and she has no idea how seller got the number.

An hour later, received call from azon who asked more details to investigate further and told me that I won't receive this call anymore. I mean how? Seller already has it and you dont know that they wont call me again?

So yeah.. they may investigate but just keep in mind...
