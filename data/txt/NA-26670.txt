Hi,

I recently booked a car and, while I decided to buy insurance from an external provider, I made the mistake of taking a loan from the dealership itself to have a hassle-free experience. Unfortunately, it turned out to be a poor decision, as I later realized that the dealer’s bank agents are not transparent about the loan terms.

I initially chose a private bank for the loan since they offered slightly lower interest rate, and I already had a salary account with them. However, the private bank’s agent seemed unqualified, struggling with both spoken and written English, which made communication difficult. The agent began the loan application remotely, handling OTPs for Aadhaar authentication etc, and later visited my home for final signatures.

When he arrived, I reviewed the terms and discovered that the pre-payment charges were quite steep, along with other significant fees like processing and documentation fees. Pre-payment flexibility was crucial to me, as I had that option in my previous car loan. This made mt furious, so I called the dealer’s loan coordinator to switch to a nationalized bank instead. However, the dealer tried to pressure me, warning that any payment delay could result in my car being reallocated to another customer.

Since the bank agent was already at my home and much of the paperwork was completed, I reluctantly decided to proceed with the private bank. He collected the documents and left.

Shortly after, the dealer's loan coordinator informed me that they had a tie-up with a nationalized bank as well, allowing me to switch. Since pre-payment flexibility was a priority, I chose to go with the nationalized bank. I contacted the private bank representative to pause the disbursement and return the signed documents. He agreed but expressed frustration over his time being wasted. He asked me to collect the documents from him at the bank, but due to work commitments, I couldn't go on Friday. I now plan to pick them up on Monday.

The private bank agent did not provide a customer copy, nor have I received any loan terms via email. I’ve communicated the entire situation to all the involved dealership salespeople and sent an email to the private bank employee. However, I’m still worried about potential misuse of the signed loan agreement.

I visited the dealership to initiate the loan process with the nationalized bank and shared my concerns about mess that happened with the private bank and the sensitive documents being with bank. They reassured me, speaking to the private bank agent in front of me in their local language. They also informed me that the bank should cancel the documents with a pen and send me pictures as a precaution. I then sent the email to the private bank employee. I haven’t received an acknowledgment and these pictures from the bank employee yet, likely due to the weekend.

I’m having so many negative thoughts about possible fraud scenarios:

1. What if the dealer and private bank coordinate behind my back, leading to the disbursement of the loan?
2. Without a customer copy from the private bank, what if the loan is disbursed to an incorrect account?

Am I overthinking this?
