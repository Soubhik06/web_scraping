Hi, everyone I nearly got scammed by people pretending from the Army, and is post is more of awareness post/ rant post for it. So anyway I run a nursery and landscaping business in Siliguri, and last night a call from a guy saying that he was from the nearby army transit camp. He placed an order for 200 plants, worth Rs. 18500, so far so good. Anyway when I went there with the plants this morning with the plants, he gave his phone to his “Senior Officer” and that guy asked me how much money I had in my bank account. This was the first sign of trouble, I just gave him a random no. Rs. 3521 and then he sent an QR code which said I have to pay him Rs. 3000. This sent my alarm bells ringing, please note that he was continuously in call with me.

I said “Sir why are you asking me to pay?”

He said “It is an Army QR code, just to check if you have an account or not”

He quickly sent me another QR code and told him to send Rs 1 in it. Now please note that I am giving him the benefit of doubt, I have dealt with people who are not tech savvy and kinda don’t understand procedure. So I sent him Rs. 1.

Now the first guy comes in the call and tries to reassure me that this is the proper process and he was busy getting the gate pass issued for my entrance, and I needed to clear the payment before issuing the gate pass. Also the first guy sent Rs. 2 to my account.

I said “Sir, I am getting trouble in internet, you can just pay me in cash or cheque. Also can you please come out to check the plants? I am at the entrance of the army camp”

He said “No I cannot come out I am busy issuing the gate pass. Clear the payment through QR code, we don’t issue cash or cheque”

Note that by this time I already know it’s a scam. I have supplied to the Indian Army and SSB before, and have garden maintenance contracts with NHPC, NRL and couple of other private companies so I have an idea about their payment method. No company or organization issues payment through QR code. But I am pretending to see how far he will go.

I said “Then can you atleast send a Jawan to check the plants and give physical confirmation?”

He said “Are you the PM or what? That a jawan will go to receive you? Just do the payment and I will issue the gate pass”

And he sent me another QR code which said I must pay the guy Rs. 2000.

Anyway I continued it for a bit and when he started using abusive language, and I ended the call.

So well here is the experience about how I nearly got scammed, I am attaching the screenshot of their UPI id just if someone whats to know. Stay safe everyone.
