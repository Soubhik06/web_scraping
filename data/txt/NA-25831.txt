A few days ago my mom received a facial epilator. It was already paid, so we confirmed with everyone we know if they placed the order, but nobody said they did. My mom tried complaining to the customer care, but since it wasn’t in her order history, they couldn’t help her. She kept it.

Today though, she received another pack which was COD. It was a diesel watch in a fastrack box :D My mom paid for it, thinking it was my brother’s, he places orders on COD sometimes. It wasn’t his, he hadn’t placed it. We called the delivery person later, It was from flipkart apparently. I read on the amazon website that these are called brushing scams. “Brushing is a deceitful technique sometimes used in e-commerce to boost a seller's ratings by creating fake orders.” 

How Brushing scams work: 

-A seller creates a fake account using your name and address. (These addresses are publicly available) 

-Brushing scam vendors then use this account to purchase their own product and send it to your address.

-Once the package is delivered, the scammer uses the fake account to post a glowing five-star review.

We weren’t aware about these, and wont accept such packages in the future. Just wanted to inform everyone about such scams.
