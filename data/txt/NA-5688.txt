This is the first Criminal Appeal/anticipatory bail application filed by the
 appellants under Section 14(A) of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes
 (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 seeking anticipatory bail in connection with
 Crime No.430/2024 for offences punishable under Sections 296, 351(3) and
 308(2) of BNS and Sections 3(1)(da) , 3(1)(dha) and 3(2)(va) of SC/ST Act
 registered at Police Station-Kirnapur Thana, District-Balaghat (M.P.). 2. Learned counsel for the appellants has submitted that the victim has taken

 Rs.13,39,000/- from time to time on the promise that he will invest the money in
 share market and will return Rs.30 Lakh after three months, he has paid the
 amount on that promise time to time but victim has not paid the amount as the
 returned only Rs.6,91,000/-. The appellant filed a complaint before the S.P.
 Balaghat on 04.07.2024 and the victim has also taken the money from other
 persons on promise that he will double the money and in this aspect, news was
 published in the newspaper in which the complainant was named as the person Signature Not Verified Signed by: NITESH PANDEY Signing time: 17-01-2025 10:53:37 NEUTRAL CITATION NO. 2025:MPHC-JBP:1771

 who has taken the money on the basis of fraud with other persons also and the
 appellant's father himself has admitted that they have returned the money. Thus,
 this is a case where the bar of Section 18(A) of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled
 Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 is not applicable. On this point, the
 judgment of Apex Court Prithivi Raj Chauhan Vs. Union of India and others AIR

 return the money as demanded by the appellants. He has further submitted that
 only Rs.6,91,000/- were taken for purchasing the car and the money was returned

 the appellants are demanding Rs.12,00,000/- (Twelve Lakh) the money instead of
 Rs.3,54,000/- and make them to sign in the blank papers and stamps. Thus, no
