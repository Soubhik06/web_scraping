2014 saw massive protests against Congress due to the 2G spectrum scam. It was "estmiated" that this led to a loss of 1.7 Lakh crore. This estmate was later found to be extremely overestimated. But BJP won 2014 nevertheless riding on this lie. 

Fast forward 2023, Adani is said to have wiped out 8.80 Lakh crore rupees from Indian economy and stock markets. This is not an estimate because the loss was well recorded and document in the stock markets and investors. 

However, the difference is that today's opposition is not allowed to protest or even utter the name of Adani and Supreme leader in the same sentence. 

As a vishwaguru once said. "Hypocrisy ki bhi seema hoti hai".
