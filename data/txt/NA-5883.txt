petitioner had sent obscene messages to respondent No.3. On 22.02.2022, parties were directed to appear before the Trial

Court/Illaqa Magistrate and get their statements recorded with regard to the

compromise arrived at between them. The trial Court was directed to record the statements of all the

concerned and send its report regarding genuineness and voluntary nature of the

compromise. In compliance of the order dated 22.02.2022, learned Judicial
