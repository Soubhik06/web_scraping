but no cogent evidence was brought on record, so as to prove this plea of fraud.

The Will in question was not found surrounded by any suspicious circumstance.

below to decree the suit of the plaintiff. In fact, in the peculiar facts and

circumstances of the case noticed hereinabove, plaintiff had no case right from

day one either on facts or in law. In this view of the matter, no fault can be

found with the concurrent findings of facts recorded by both the learned Courts
