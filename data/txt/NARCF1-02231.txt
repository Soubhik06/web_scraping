About two months ago, I bought a Jio SIM and made it my new primary number. I updated everything with this number, including my banking and Aadhaar details. Initially, I received a few calls asking for someone else, presumably the previous owner of the number. I'd just explain that the number is now mine, and the callers would hang up. 

However, starting last week, I've been getting an increasing number of strange messages. The first one was from an organization called BFIL, saying that a woman had taken out a loan and included a link to some loan documents. I assumed it was a scam, so I ignored and blocked the sender. 

But then I received a PDF on WhatsApp indicating that this woman had purchased life insurance from Care Health Insurance. I'm attaching screenshots of the messages and the PDF. 

I'm really concerned about why I'm receiving these messages. So far, there hasn't been any money deducted from my account, nor have I received any emails from my bank about this. What should I do?
