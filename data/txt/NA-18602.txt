Just about an hour ago, I got a call, by someone pretending to be from bank regarding credit card, anyways I don't have credit card, doing right with my public sector bank provided debit card. Before I could treat him the way randians have been trained here to deal with fraud callers, he cut the call! I tried to call him to complete the treatment, but he is not receiving. On truecaller, the number 09211690618 shows to be from icici pru Mumbai. So be alert.
