I got on an auto from silkboard jn this morning, coming back from home. I wanted to go to Marathahalli, I'd usually take the bus but since I had a lot of luggage I decided to take the auto (which I usually don't do but this guy says only meter charge I have to pay). I got in the auto and all was going good.

I kept glancing at the meter and it stayed at 25 for sometime. Near Agara Lake, I checked again the meter had spiked to 112, still could be worse.

I saw the meter dashing a rupee every 5 second even though the auto had slowed at signal. Nearing RMZ ecospace I saw the price was at 245 and I said to the driver to stop. I gave him 500rs note since he said he didn't have any UPI ID.

He said I need to give 40 more. I asked why and he said I have to pay meter double. I told since it's only 245 it'll be less than 500, he showed me the meter saying it was 272 which is not even possible because we had barely covered 50 meters after I said to stop.

Couple of seconds of dispute and he gives me the 500 rs note saying it's torn (which I'm almost pretty sure wasn't torn). I replace him the 500 with another checking it carefully and walked into a bus nearby and he chases me and tells the 2nd 500rs note is also torn which just proves that he tore the first one. Luckily the bus door was open and he didn't come into the bus.

I knew auto drivers would do different kinds of scams but this meter scam and the note scam is on an another level. I'm just sitting here sad on trusting an Auto driver and paying the price for it. 

Lesson learned, Never again will I get into an auto unless it's that urgent.
