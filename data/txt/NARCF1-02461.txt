I received a call today from +8855499426. An automated voice claimed it was from the Mumbai High Court, saying a petition was filed against me and asking me to press 9 for details. I pressed 9 and was connected to a woman who said she was from the Notification Department of the High Court, claiming there was a revenue scam case against me. She mentioned that they had sent the petition to my address and email. When she asked for my Aadhaar number, I refused. She couldn't confirm my correct address but kept insisting on the petition number, which I hadn’t received. When I asked her for verification, she only gave her name, age, and department but couldn’t provide any other proof. She then threatened that my account would be frozen unless I came to the High Court. I refused and ended the call.
