Bit of an odd one so I'll give lots of detail to help explain. TL;DR: Company with too many odd coincidences and red flags shipped my parents 25 videos games and is asking us to return to sender.

On Thursday around 8PM, I received a call from a number only two digits different from my mother's cell phone number. Weird coincidence, so I answered. It was a lady with an indian accent asking for my father by name. She verified she had the correct address for my parents and told me she got my number from my Amazon account (I share it with my parents, but the account phone number and number on the order she referenced should have been my father's). She told me she had accidentally used the address from a previous order we had placed with them through Amazon to ship 25 used video games that were supposed to go to Best Buy for return, and she asked if I could hand it back as return to sender. They called a couple more times Friday and Saturday, and I told them each time I'd get with my parents when I could. My parents received the video games (25 of the same opened Switch game) Saturday. The company also emailed me from a hotmail account to confirm the tracking number and listed a company name that I can't find anywhere on Google. Too many red flags here for me to ignore--is this some sort of scam? Are we getting caught up in some sort of drug shipping or money laundering or something?

I am aware that we have no legal obligation (in the US) to pay for or return an item we did not order, but they have been nice and it may just be an honest mistake so we would like to return them if it isn't some scam.
