The Scam: 
Fell prey to a scam yesterday at St Alex Church, Curtorim when this dude approached me after mass and said he's a brother from the seminary and asked me about myself and mentioned he knew a few priests from where I stay. Then he asked me if I could transfer him ₹1500 which I did (ik I shouldn't have done that) and he'd transfer it back as soon as he reached back. He scooted away as soon as I transferred the money and I did not get a chance to get his phone number. 

The realisation:
Couple of hours after I sent him the money, I texted him on PhonePe asking him for his phone number and to repay the money. To which then he asked for another 1k( it was at this point that I realised it's a scam). I said I cannot transfer any more money and immediately he blocked me on PhonePe. 

What I know:
While he was leaving I noted down his bike's registration number and the owner's name matched the UPI name. With the help of a friend, I managed to find out his address in Aldona. 

What should be my course of action? 
I don't wanna go and file a complaint as that would take too much of time and effort for just ₹1500 but I also wanna make sure he scams no one else.
