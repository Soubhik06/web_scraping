In today’s digital world, online scams are becoming increasingly common. I recently fell victim to a fraudulent Instagram seller who scammed me out of my hard-earned money. I am sharing my experience so that others can learn from it and avoid making the same mistake.

How the Scam Happened

1. Fake Instagram Page Offering Unrealistic Deals

I came across an Instagram page called _cell_phone_h(Instagram Username) that was selling iPhones at very cheap rates. The deals looked too good to be true, but the page had decent engagement, and some fake customer reviews, which made it look real.

2. Contacting the Seller on WhatsApp

I messaged the seller via WhatsApp and inquired about an iPhone 15. He offered the phone for just ₹15,000 and even said I could get it on EMI by making a ₹7,000 down payment via Google Pay. The seller sounded convincing and shared images of the product to build trust.

3. Making the Payment

I transferred ₹7,000 on 1st March 2025 via Google Pay, expecting my order to be processed soon. The seller sent me a tracking ID: 7D111710723 and claimed that the order was being shipped through Shiprocket (DTDC Courier).

4. Fake Tracking ID &amp; No Response

Initially, the tracking ID showed "Ready to Ship" on 9th March 2025, but after that, no further updates appeared. When I tried contacting the seller for an update, he stopped responding to my messages and calls. That’s when I realized I had been scammed.

Actions I Took After the Scam

After realizing the fraud, I took the following steps to report the scam and try to recover my money:

 Filed a complaint on the Cyber Crime Portal .

Reported the seller on National Consumer Helpline .

Contacted Shiprocket &amp; DTDC to verify if the shipment was even created (it wasn’t!)

Initiated a dispute with Google Pay for the fraudulent transaction

How to Protect Yourself from Such Scams

 1. Never Trust Unverified Instagram Pages – If a page is offering luxury products at unrealistically low prices, it is most likely a scam. Always check for official websites and reviews outside Instagram.

 2. Avoid Making Advance Payments – Scammers ask for upfront payments and then disappear. Always prefer Cash on Delivery (COD) when dealing with unknown sellers.

 3. Check Reviews from Real Customers – Many scam pages use fake reviews. Always search for the business on Google, Reddit, and consumer complaint forums.

 4. Verify the Tracking ID on the Courier’s Official Website – Shiprocket is just a logistics aggregator, so always check the tracking directly on the courier’s website (DTDC, Blue Dart, etc.).

5. Report Fraud Immediately – If you ever fall victim to a scam, immediately file complaints with Cyber Crime, Consumer Forum, and your bank/UPI service.

Final Words

I am still fighting to recover my money, and I hope my case serves as a warning to others. Please share this article so that more people are aware of such Instagram scams and do not fall for them.

If you have ever experienced a similar fraud, don’t hesitate to report it and take legal action. Awareness and action are the only ways to stop such scammers!

🚀 Stay alert, stay safe!
