 country had just gone through a historic transformation. From
 being under colonial rule, India had become a free and sovereign
 republic. Along with this, the Partition had caused a massive

 decide who would be accepted as citizens of the new nation.
 Keeping both these realities in mind, the framers of the
 Constitution decided to make an immediate but temporary

 came into force. This arrangement was included in Part II of the
 Constitution ( Articles 5 to 11). 13. The rules in Part II decided the citizenship status of people as
 on 26 January 1950, but also gave Parliament the power, under Article 11 , to make new citizenship laws in the future. This
 ensured that there was no gap in the law at the start of the
 Republic, and at the same time allowed the elected Parliament to 5 ::: Uploaded on - 12/08/2025 ::: Downloaded on - 12/08/2025 21:41:48 ::: ba1510-2025 final.doc
