Hate the hype or love it, this movie is about to stay. Here is a compilation of reviews of the movie from a few websites:

[Firstpost:](http://www.firstpost.com/bollywood/bahubali-review-hunky-heroes-great-battle-scenes-ss-rajamoulis-fantasy-film-epic-2335958.html) "With hunky heroes and great battle scenes, SS Rajamouli's fantasy film is epic"

[Rediff](http://www.rediff.com/movies/review/review-bahubali-is-mega-indigenious-and-envelope-pushing/20150710.htm): "Almost three hours go like a breeze in the company of Bahubali's eclectic protagonists, where every single one makes an 'entry' designed for wolf-whistles"

[Reuters:](http://blogs.reuters.com/india/2015/07/10/movie-review-bahubali/) The film,..., is an adrenaline rush, a spectacle of the kind we haven’t seen from an Indian film-maker before and hopefully it is a sign of good things to come.

[Pinkvilla](http://www.pinkvilla.com/entertainmenttags/bahubali/pinkvilla-review-bahubali): "Till before interval, Bahubali will push you to walk out. Survive that and the film blooms. The backstory, thankfully, is peppered with solid drama backed by a sturdy story of two chiseled bodied hunks who are competing for the crown."

[Free Press Journal:](http://www.freepressjournal.in/movie-review-baahubali-fake-accomplices/) "My biggest peeve with this film is the manner in which the characters are drawn- too cartoonish and derived from mainstream Bollywood/Hollywood to curry favor with a discerning audience." 

[Filmfare:](http://www.filmfare.com/reviews/movie-review-baahubali-9786.html) "Bahubali is truly an epic experience. Had the story not been so jaded, this would've gone into the history books as an all-time classic. But that's not the case. It has its set of storytelling flaws, but even those are overshadowed by Rajamouli's ideas and execution. "

[Greatandhra:](http://www.greatandhra.com/movies/reviews/baahubali-review-visual-spectacle-with-weak-climax-67457.html) "Visual Spectacle With Weak Climax" 

[123telugu:](http://www.123telugu.com/telugu/reviews/bahubali-telugu-review.html) "పరాజయమే లేని ఎస్ఎస్ రాజమౌళి – యంగ్ రెబల్ స్టార్ ప్రభాస్ కాంబినేషన్ లో భారీ అంచనాల నడుమ విడుదలైన బాహుబలి సినిమా అందరినీ సంతృప్తి పరిచే సూపర్బ్ గ్రాఫికల్ విజువల్ వండర్ గా నిలిచిపోయింది. ఇండియన్ సినిమాలో ఇలాంటి విజువల్స్ మీరు మునుపెన్నడూ చూడలేదు."

[TheHindu:](http://www.thehindu.com/features/cinema/cinema-reviews/baahubali-review-a-little-more-a-little-less/article7407650.ece) "Visually spectacular, but we expected more from the storyteller."
