I want to share my terrible experience with Virat Bikes in VV Puram, hoping that it will save someone else from falling into the same trap. I bought an Activa 6G scooter from them on 27-01-2024 for ₹64,000. At the time of purchase, the dealer promised to issue the RC within 2-3 months. However, despite regular follow-ups, he failed to provide the RC, giving excuse after excuse.

He claimed there was some issue with the insurance and that he was supposedly paying extra to the agent from his own pocket to resolve it. It became clear that he was deliberately letting the insurance expire. Once the insurance expired, he had the audacity to tell me that I would need to renew it myself!

When I initially bought the scooter, the dealer pretended to be very helpful, saying that any issues occurring within the first 2000 km would be taken care of by him. However, when problems arose, like the indicator and fuel gauge not working, he refused to fix them. His promises were empty from the start. The owner and the dealer lack humanity and integrity—they're wolves in sheep’s clothing.

As time went on, I was in urgent need of money. Since they were refusing to issue the RC, I asked them to take back the scooter and return my money. Instead of understanding my situation, they tried to force me into renewing the insurance or leaving the scooter with them so they could find a buyer and give me a “good” amount of ₹60,000. I knew I couldn’t trust them and refused their offer.

Eventually, they started pressuring me to take ₹51,000, saying that was the maximum they would give me, or else I should apply for the new RC myself. Left with no choice, I had to accept their unfair offer, losing a significant amount of my hard-earned money in the process. Even when I found a buyer on my own and took him to the shop, the dealer said something to him in Kannada, after which the buyer backed out.

These people are complete scammers. They act like saints when selling the scooter but reveal their true colors when it’s time to honor their commitments. I don't want anyone else to go through what I did.

**Virat Bikes in VV Puram is nothing but a fraud.** Their sweet words and empty promises are all part of their scam. Save yourself the heartache and avoid this shop at all costs. If you value your peace of mind and hard-earned money, stay away from Virat Bikes. They have no integrity and are only interested in tricking people out of their money.

I have been a victim of their scam and would hate for anyone else to fall prey to the same deceit. Be vigilant, and beware of these fake saints.
