(Per the Hon'ble the Acting Chief Justice Raghvendra Singh Chauhan)

 The petitioner, Smt. Shashi Roshan, has filed the present

petition, ostensibly, on the ground that on 16.04.2019, the detenue,

Smt. T. Soundarya Sravanthi, W/o. T. Praveen Kumar Raj, was

picked up by the police, and her whereabouts are unknown ever

since then. Mr. A. Manoj Kumar, the learned Assistant Government

Pleader for Home, submits that on 18.03.2019, an F.I.R. was lodged

by the prosecutrix, wherein she alleged that the detenue's husband,

Mr. T. Praveen Kumar Raj, has repeatedly committed rape upon her;

and both the detenue and her husband have blackmailed her by

having her nude photographs and videos. It is in consequence of the

said F.I.R. that the detenue was picked up by the police and

produced before the concerned Magistrate. The learned Magistrate

has ordered that the detenue should be sent to judicial custody.

Thus, according to the learned Assistant Government Pleader, the

custody of the detenue is a legal one and not an illegal one. In order

to buttress this plea, the learned Assistant Government Pleader has

submitted documents clearly showing that the detenue is presently in

judicial custody. Since the detenue is in judicial custody, the

custody cannot be said to be an illegal one. Therefore, this Court does not find any merit in the present

Habeas Corpus Petition; it is hereby dismissed. There shall be no

order as to costs. The miscellaneous petitions pending, if any, shall stand closed. _______________________________
