Shri Christopher Kurbah ..... Petitioner - Versus - HON'BLE SHRI JUSTICE DINESH MAHESHWARI, CHIEF JUSTICE

Shri K Paul, for the petitioner
Shri S Sen Gupta, Public Prosecutor

BY THE COURT: (ORAL)

 The petitioner, who is one of the accused in Laitumkhrah PS

Case No. 134 (8) 2017 for offences under Sections

120B / 385 / 419 / 420 / 468 / 34 IPC and is in custody since 29.08.2017,

seeks release on bail under Section 439 of the Code of Criminal

Procedure. Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and learned Public

Prosecutor and perused the Case Diary. The First Information Report ['FIR'] in question came to be

registered on a communication sent by the Superintendent of Police

(City), East Khasi Hills, Shillong to the Officer-in-Charge, Laitumkhrah

Police Station on 28.08.2017, pointing out that after receiving tip-off

regarding a wide ranging scam, where certain persons were cheating

the American nationals and extracting money while personating as

Internal Revenue Service Agents through the facilities of a Call Centre,

steps were taken to understand the working of scamsters; and 2 BA No.27/2017 Shri Christopher Kurbah v. State of Meghalaya thereafter, the authorities concerned were able to unearth the

fraudulent dealings and to apprehend several persons related with the

alleged 'fraud Call Centre namely IFA'. The relevant contents of the

communication so sent by the Superintendent of Police, forming the

basis of FIR, read as under:- It appears from the material placed on record that during the

course of search and seizure in the premises of S.S. Net Com, the said

Superintendent of Police gathered the information about other rackets

of similar nature, where fraudulent calls were made to the foreign

nationals by personating and the victims were duped one way or the

other. In regard to such other alleged rackets, Laitumkhrah PS Case 3 BA No.27/2017 Shri Christopher Kurbah v. State of Meghalaya

No.135 (8) 2017 was registered on 28.08.2017 and Laitumkhrah PS

Case No.136 (8) 2017 was registered on 29.08.2017. All these cases

are under investigation. The petitioner, though not named in the FIR as such, has been

arrested in Laitumkhrah PS Case No. 134 (8) 2017 on the allegation

that he was working as a mobilizer in S.S. Net Com, Dhanketi,

Shillong; and he was recruiting other persons for the fraudulent

operations. Learned counsel for the petitioner has strenuously argued that

the petitioner was only an employee under the S.S. Net Com and was

dealing mainly with the supply of manpower for the maintenance and

up-keep of the premises and had otherwise no connection with the

offences said to have been committed by the other persons; and no

purpose would be served by his detention further. Learned counsel for

the petitioner has also pointed out that the other accused persons in

Laitumkhrah PS Case No. 134 (8) 2017, namely Shri Amit Bhowmik,

Shri Raj Sohphoh, Smti. Naina Gurung and Shri Pynshai Mawthoh

have been granted bail by the Subordinate Court on 12.10.2017. It is

further pointed out that two accused persons in the cognate matter

carrying similar nature allegations, i.e., Laitumkhrah PS Case No.135

(8) 2017, namely Shri Rijjo Joseph and Smti. Sweety Sutnga, were

also ordered to be released on bail by the Subordinate Court on

22.09.2017 and 25.09.2017. The learned counsel has placed the

copies of orders so passed on 12.10.2017 and 22.09.2017 before the

Court for perusal. Learned PP has duly opposed the bail application with the

submissions that the rackets unearthed in this matter are of wide 4 BA No.27/2017 Shri Christopher Kurbah v. State of Meghalaya import and implications; and as several of the accused persons are yet

to be apprehended, releasing of the present petitioner may hamper the

investigation. However, it is not in dispute that the other accused persons in

the present one and cognate matters have already been ordered to be

released on bail. The matter being at the stage of investigation, this Court would

not be making any comment on the merits but after having heard the

learned counsel for the petitioner and the learned PP and having

perused the Case Diary; and looking to the totality of circumstances, it

appears just and proper that the petitioner, who is in custody since

29.08.2017, be released on bail subject to the requisite terms and

conditions. Accordingly, this bail application is allowed and it is directed that

in relation to Laitumkhrah P.S. Case No. 134 (8) 2017, the accused

petitioner Shri Christopher Kurbah shall be released on bail on his

furnishing a personal bond in the sum of Rs. 20,000/- (Rupees twenty

thousand) with two sureties in the sum of Rs.10,000/- (Rupees ten

thousand) each to the satisfaction of the concerned Judicial Magistrate

First Class, East Khasi Hills District, Shillong; and further, with an

undertaking not to hamper or interfere with the investigation in any

manner and to remain present before the Investigating Officer and

before the Court concerned as and when called upon to do so. The bail application stands disposed of accordingly. CHIEF JUSTICE
