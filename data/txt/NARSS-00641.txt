Don't know why but somehow this sub looks right for this post.

So just got a call from a no. It was an IVR call. As soon as i picked up the call IVR told me that there was an attempt to change my Paytm a/c mobile no. If this was'nt done by you press 1 else press 2.
I pressed 1 and after that it asked me to enter an OTP sent to my mobile no. to verify it's me. 
I use Truecaller and it directly display OTP from message without any other text. I entered the first 5 digit of OTP and as I was about to enter last digit of OTP, God knows why but my mind told me to check the whole message. As I checked the message it was from Paytm saying that "OTP is XXXXXX FOR PAYTM LOGIN TO PAY CROMA."
I immediately disconnected the call and within a minute i got a call again from the same number and this time I entered the wrong OTP, but to my surprise IVR told me that OTP is incorrect.I hung up the call.
Later I called Paytm customer care and told them the whole thing and they told that it was a fraud call. I asked them to block my account.

Guys if you are using Truecaller or any other app which directly displays OTP on screen then don't enter OTP without reading full text.
