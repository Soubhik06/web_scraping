FACTUAL BACKGROUND 1. It is the case of the prosecution that on 13.01.2005 at about
9:15 PM at Bus Stand Nanaksar Wazirabad Road, Delhi within the
jurisdiction of PS Khajuri Chowk, the complainant who is a Delhi Police
official was on picket duty from 8:00 PM to 8:00 AM and at about 9:15
PM, when he was standing near Nanaksar Bus stand, he witnessed that
the offending vehicle i.e. Bus bearing no. DL-1PA-4677 (Having route
No.212) stopped/halted near Nanaksar Bus stand for passengers to
disembark, however before one of the passengers i.e. deceased Kamal
could alight from the bus, it began to move due to which deceased lost
his balance, fell and subsequently the bus run over him leaving him in an
injured and wounded state. The complainant alongwith the help of other
police officials stopped the bus. Thereafter, PCR call was made and
deceased was taken to hospital and during treatment he succumbed to his
injuries. The accused being the driver of the offending bus was
apprehended on the spot. FIR was registered u/s 379 / 304A IPC.
Investigation was taken up and after conclusion of investigation,
chargesheet was filed u/s 173 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973
(hereinafter, " CrPC "). Digitally
