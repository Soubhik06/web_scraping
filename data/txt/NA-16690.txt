I know this is going to ruffle some feathers, but bear with me.

I've made a living online one way or another for nearly a decade now. In these 8-10 years, if there's one rule I've encountered in virtually every industry, it's this: "beware of Indians".

The "Indian SEO spammer" trope is pretty well known, but even in service focused industries such as tech support, there is a well established pattern of Indians, or Indian owned companies indulging in very shady practices (see: Microsoft scam, a couple of well known "people directory listing" scammers operating out of USA, even sites like Shopclues).

There just seems to be a complete breakdown of ethical behavior online among Indian people. It's almost like they see their online lives as completely separate from their "real" lives - that what they do online has no actual repercussions.

Go on any online marketing forum, especially the blackhat ones and you'll see them swarming with Indian "marketers". It's the same on service provider marketplaces like Fiverr, or Freelance sites like UpWork.

This gives a bad rep to Indians. I've lost potential clients at times because many clients have been burned in the past by Indian providers.

Why is this? Is it just our sheer numbers online?
