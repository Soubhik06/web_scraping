[**Himachal Pradesh Integrated Drug Prevention Policy**](http://esomsa.hp.gov.in/sites/default/files/PDF/State%20Drug%20Policy%20Notification%2017.02.2022.pdf)

 To monitor/curb the problem of drug abuse in the State in all respects, rehabilitation of drug addicts, alternate livelihood generation for people indulged in illicit cultivation of Cannabis and Opium.

In Himachal Pradesh, you can **contact the authorities** regarding drug abuse problems through the following **numbers and helplines:**

 * **Nasha Nivaran Toll-Free Helpline: 1908**. This helpline was launched by the state government to encourage the public to share information about drug traffickers and to provide counseling to drug addicts and their parents regarding de-addiction. The identity of informers is kept secret.

 * **Police**: You can contact the local police by dialing 100 or the single emergency number **112**. They can take immediate action against drug-related crimes and provide assistance.

 * **State Police Control Room Shimla**: You can also reach the State Police Control Room in Shimla at the following numbers: **(0177) 2621711, 2621618, 2621723, 2621736, 2621714, 2621614, 2621746, 2621796**

 * **Complaints via SMS**: You can also register complaints related to crime, including drug abuse, via SMS to **9459100100**

 * **Cyber Crime Cell:** For reporting cyber-related drug offenses, you can contact the Cyber Crime Cell at **1930 or 0177-2620331**

 * **National Toll-Free De-addiction Helpline: 14446.** This is a national helpline that provides primary counseling and immediate referral services to individuals struggling with addiction. Himachal Pradesh is included in the states covered by this helpline.

 * **Medical Helpline**: For medical assistance related to drug abuse, you can dial **102 or 108.**

 * **District Public Relations Officers (DPROs)**: You can also contact the DPRO in your specific district for assistance or information. For **Shimla**, the DPRO can be reached at **0177-2657024 or 9418103637**. Contact details for DPROs in other districts are also available on the Himachal Pradesh Government's website.

It's important to reach out to the appropriate authorities as soon as you encounter a drug abuse problem, whether it involves yourself, someone you know, or if you have information about drug trafficking. **Your timely action can help individuals get the support they need and contribute to making Himachal Pradesh a drug-free state.**
