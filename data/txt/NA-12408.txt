CORAM : HON'BLE MR. JUSTICE SUBHASH CHAND ------ This bail application has been filed on behalf of the abovenamed
 applicant with prayer to release on bail in connection with Jhinkpani P.S. Case
 No.15 of 2019 registered under Section 420 of the Indian Penal Code and Sections 66(C) and 66(D) of the I.T. Act pending in the court of learned Chief
 Judicial Magistrate, Chaibasa. Learned counsel for the applicant has submitted that the F.I.R. of
 this case was lodged by the victim--informant against the unknown miscreants
 with these allegations that on 06.04.2019 from her bank account, Rs.24,897/-
 was withdrawn by playing fraud upon her. It is further alleged that she had
 ordered one piece Lehanga from Wedszon Company but she had to return the
 same. Further she contacted the customer care no.8777645491 and she installed
 AnyDesk remote PC/Mac Control app. Learned counsel for the applicant has submitted that the F.I.R. was
 lodged against the unknown persons. The said mobile number did not belong to
 the applicant. It is further submitted that except the confessional statement of
 co-accused, there is no evidence against the applicant. The similarly situated co-
 accused Mahadev Mandal has been granted anticipatory bail by a co-ordinate
 Bench of this Court vide order dated 02.02.2022 passed in A.B.A. No.338 of
 2022. Co-accused Prabhakar Mandal @ Prabhakar Kumar Mandal has also been
 granted bail by this Court vide order dated 09.05.2022 passed in B.A. No.2939 of
 2022. The applicant has been languishing in jail since 02.03.2022. Learned Spl. P.P. appearing on behalf of the State vehemently
 opposed the contentions made by the learned counsel for the applicant. In view of the submissions made and materials on record, the bail
 application of the applicant is hereby allowed. Let the applicant be released on
 bail on furnishing bail bond of Rs.25,000/-(Rupees Twenty Five Thousand) with
 two sureties of the like amount to the satisfaction of the court concerned in
 aforesaid case. (Subhash Chand, J.)
