There is an online scam going on(not sure how they grab your money or whatever because I didn't fall for it) using fack whatsapp accounts. 
I forgot to take a screenshot of the message before blocking the number but this is what they're doing.

A message from a number preventing to be a general manager of Lazada project will ask you to register yourself for some part-time job. 
Pleas don't click on any links. These aren't real whatsapp accounts rather fakes ones created by using phone numbers of those who don't have a whatsapp account. 
If you want to help the actual person who owns the phone number just call the number and ask them to install whatsap and delete the account in it
