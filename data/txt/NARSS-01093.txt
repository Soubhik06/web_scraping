I need to know if this is a scam or was it legit.

I recently hired a car with a driver in Goa for roughly 4 days. It was a high demand week, so I had to agree to some expensive terms.

The driver and I agreed upon a Garage to Garage system. Everyday he would drive back and forth to his home/garage. Any time outside of the 8-10 working hours, I'd have to pay him an extra amount per hour as well.

On one of the days, we were 50+ km from garage and it was a bit late (11 pm). Since we had to start the day early, I suggested that I could get him an accomodation close to the hotel I was staying. He obliged and stayed with us.

A disclaimer: He was not comfortable at the hostel because it was a mixed dorm. He cancelled the reservation late at night and slept in the car.

Later, at the end of our stay in Goa, he started demanding the fare for those extra 100+ kms (Back and forth). This did not make sense, because
1. That journey did not even happen
2. His calculations even included the cost of petrol, which did not used
3. I already got him accommodation. He cancelled the reservation at 11.45 pm.

Of course, I was pissed at this because I was already paying him a lot of money for 4 days worth of stay. I did not pay him this extra amount.

Other than that, his service was amazing, and I did tip him for his service.

Did I do the right thing? Was this a scam?
