--- POST TEXT ---

Recently, I visited Fig at Museo, a high-end restaurant in Gurgaon, for lunch with some visiting friends. It was our first time there. While my family proceeded inside, I went to park my car. As I approached the restaurant entrance, the security guard demanded my phone number to be entered into his register. I explained that my family had already provided my details when they entered, as noted in the register. However, he insisted that it was mandatory for all patrons to provide their mobile numbers. His demeanor suggested frustration, almost as if he believed not collecting my number would impact him financially or cost him his job. He even threatened to deny me entry if I didn't comply. Since I had guests, I provided and moved on. 

I'm concerned about the lack of consideration for data privacy and personal information, especially given the prevalence of scams. Why aren't there stricter laws in India similar to those in Europe to protect against data breaches? These scams not only risk financial loss but also disrupt peace of mind with endless unwanted calls from property dealers, banks offering loans, and credit card promotions.
