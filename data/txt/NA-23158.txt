I gave a samsung phone to my dad but as we dont get charger anymore in the box, he decided to buy a new one. As I live in a different and couldn't wait for online delivery, he went to local shop and bought a Samsung PD charger.

Now as per the shop owner, this is an original charger, gave an invoice as well. My dad used this for some 10-15 days and the phone suddenly stopped charging and bricked.

As checked with the service centre, the phone is dead and only motherboard replacement can solve the issue, which costly near to a phone nowadays. So I sent him a different phone to use meanwhile.

When I came back home during diwali, i saw the adapter and in the first glance i knew it is a fake one. I opened it and confirmed as i found low quality board and extra steel bar placed to increase weight to give a feeling to high standard product.

What can be dont with these shopowners who take advantage of not so technically sound people and sell them fake products which further costs us more. It could have even resulted in something far worse like blasting of phone's battery.
