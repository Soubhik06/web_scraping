--- POST TEXT ---

I Yesterday, I ordered a product worth rs 7990 and paid with my credit card. Today I received a call that the cycle is ready to be shipped within 1 hour but the company is unable to generate a bill for the transaction. According to him the said amount is refunded and will reflect soon in my account and I also over paid. So I should make another payment of rupees 6000 to the company's account. 

Something very fishy,right ?

I told him to send me an email with details of the issue and QR for payment. Meanwhile, I checked the Flipkart site and found that the order was not even shipped yet. 

Scam confirmed. 

This person that called knew the product I bought, it’s exact price and payment method. So it can only be a company employee who has access to all this information.

This is the major issue here.
Our data is not protected with these companies. Fraud was easy to detect and avoid. But it's the data that we must be concerned about and how it might be further misused in more deceptive ways. 

I'll write an email to Flipkart and another to cyber cell. Hope that'll do some good.
