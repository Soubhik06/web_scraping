I got a call from an unknown number who said that they were from Bharti AXA and that there is a life insurance policy under my name. This was weird because I don't remember ever taking one out. They said that the policy was taken through a broker and they provided the address on record. The broker was india infoline finance limited which is a company I have never had any contact with.

The address was correct down to my apartment number but they said that the policy was taken in 2015 but I hadn't started living at that address in 2015.

They finally stated that only one premium of 50k had been made (which ofcourse, I never paid) and wanted to know if I would like to continue or cancel the policy. I asked them to cancel it. They said something about the policy having matured and that I could pay something to get the maturity amount but it looked quite convoluted and I didn't really understand what they wanted.

I checked the number on Truecaller and a lot of people had written that it was a scam number who were fishing for bank account details. At first I treated it as a scam and just blocked the number but I continue to keep getting these calls.

Is it possible for someone to take out a life insurance policy under my name without me knowing about it?
