I would like to set up a community where, when you discover online scams, and they link back to some address that has NOTHING to do with where you are (do you live in Alabama and the scam email is coming from California?) that you could still “Tell on them“ as it were. I am well-aware that there are other entities whose mission is to combat this, but let’s face it, it’s not enough and it’s not working. SOOOO, I would to try SOMETHING DIFFERENT. Here it is: people who live in the area of the scam businesses PURPORTED LOCATIONS/business addresses actually GO TO THAT LOCATION. That’s it, that’s all. I know it seems simple, but if everyone everywhere were willing to spend an hour or less of their time and JUST WALK OVER to a business that says it exists (that DOESN’T EXIST, except on some phone call-in line with some poor sucker manning the calls 24 seven from India or the Philippines or whereever), we would be better off.

Whaddya think?
