I have a max life insurance policy purchased through Axis bank. The premium payment occurs once a year around 22nd December.

So two days ago, I received a call by a person claiming to represent Max Life. He mentioned all policy details correctly and informed me that it would be possible for me to pay directly to Max Life. 

I asked him to send him an email to give all the details. Within a few hours, an email came from account@maxlifeinsurance.com which seemed legitimate and considering how every other detail matched, I performed an IMPS transaction.

But within a few hours, I grew suspicious and called Axis bank to check who informed me that the account belonged to some Dinesh Chinni.

I realized my stupidity and started calling every other agency I could. I plan to lodge a police complaint on Monday regarding the fraud. 

Are there any other options that are available to me legally? Especially, it seems suspicious that someone had all policy details and also access to email registered at maxlifeinsurance.com. Would it be possible for me to complain against this? 

I never thought I would fall to a phone scam, this is an extremely low point in my life where everything seems to be going wrong in my life.
