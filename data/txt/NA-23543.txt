--- POST TEXT ---

Today one of my relative was trying to sell something on OLX platform. They reached an agreement with one guy who introduced himself as an Army office and asked them to scan one barcode code which resulted into debit of 35 K from their account.I have asked them to report their bank and to [cybercrime](https://cybercrime.gov.in/) and also report to the police station.

**What more can be done? Please advise.**

I know about such kind of scams as I keep seeing them often on Reddit but my relatives were not aware about such scams.

**Update**: They received a QR code, where they were told that they have transfer a small amount so that their account will be visible in the scammer's UPI apps. As soon as they scanned money was debited from the UPI linked account 3 times, totalling 35K. 
**As some people asked, no they didn't get any prompt to enter any kind of pin or any confirmation.**

They called Cyber crime (link given above) where they provided all the evidence including bank statement. They were told that they have to go to the police station and get an FIR copy and share it.
