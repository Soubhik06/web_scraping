--- POST TEXT ---

**TL;DR** \- Ordered an Asus Zephyrus G14 laptop from amazon. The screen was flickering and filed for a return asap. The return guy rejected the product as the laptop serial key wasn't matching. No help from amazon customer support at all, the deadlines just keep extending.

**Long Version**:

Hi all, I want to share with you all the extreme harassment and panic I have experienced from the last 10 days. Following are the series of events that occurred in order. And I want to share this to raise awareness regarding what level of scam is happening on online deliveries. Sorry if this becomes long, I am just writing events that took place sequentially with an open mind.

**June 14**:

I ordered the Asus ROG Zephyrus G14 laptop online from Amazon from this link: 
[https://www.amazon.in/gp/product/B08CV1CLTW](https://www.amazon.in/gp/product/B08CV1CLTW). I had heard a lot about this laptop for its performance and gaming and did my due diligence/research and concluded to buy it. Offline store price was relatively higher and having a tight budget I decided to order it from Amazon when it was available at 85000 INR. I waited a lot for the prices to reduce.

**June 20**:

I received the laptop at my home. The first thing I noticed - the package did not have any amazon branding. It was an Asus laptop box. Even the delivery guy could see it was a laptop that he was delivering with all the price tags visible. This was the first moment I realized something might be wrong. I instantly asked my dad to video log the unboxing. Opening up the case, I could see finger stains on top of the laptop lid. I was 100% sure now something is wrong.

When I booted it up I could clearly notice the screen flickering. I also booted it with a USB having Fedora (a Linux distro), to check if was a driver issue. Nope, the screen was still flickering.

I instantly called the Amazon helpline asking for a replacement for the defective product. As it was out of stock, they agreed to give me a refund and scheduled my return/refund for 23rd June.

I was relieved!

**June 23:**

The return guy was the same guy who showed up for the delivery. There was one more guy with him though. They both started inspecting the laptop if it was booting up or not - obviously, it was. I also videotaped this part. I showed them the issue and they agreed to take it back. They took the product and went outside my apartment.

I hadn't received any notification about the refund initiation or successful picking up of the item, yet. I could see them on the pavement - they were again opening up the Asus package and handling the laptop with just plain stupidity (things were scattered on the road). I thought they were trying to find something and approached them to check what was the issue.

Now, this part is what scared me. They were talking to their manager on speaker. They couldn't verify the serial no. of the laptop. They claimed it wasn't the same one from what was delivered.

A couple of things happened -

1. The manager was accusing me on the call blaming me for laundering laptops. Obviously, he was talking with the return guys but this legit scared me.
2. Both the return guys started questioning me about where I got this laptop from, with an investigative tone.
3. They told me to take back the laptop, which I obviously revolted. And they told me if I won't take it back, they don't have any issues keeping it - "Ham ghar le jaenge hahah hamko kya dikkat hai hahah".

I was literally harassed by this experience and panicking. All I was feeling was the burden of losing a huge sum of 85k INR. I had to keep the laptop with myself and again called amazon explaining everything that transpired. They re-arranged the pickup for June 26 and the support guy promised me that, he had created a "label" for the return and the return guys won't verify the pick up the next time. And of course, sorry for the inconvenience caused. I had no other option but to wait.

**June 26**:

No one came! The return guy canceled the pick-up from their side without even showing on my door. I felt really bad and cheated. I called Amazon again and asked to speak to their leadership team. I explained everything; literally every event again. They re-created the pick-up on June 27 and filed for a **NOC -** which is an investigation on the issue and if it was a problem on their end I would get an advanced refund. The NOC takes 3 working days to be concluded. I again had to wait.

**June 27**:

No one showed up for the return... No calls, nothing! The return guy canceled the pickup. I dialed back the return guy from Amazon SMS we get for pickup, he told me his manager has asked him to cancel the return.

I called Amazon again.. explaining everything. They rescheduled my return for June 29 and told me to wait for the NOC result, as it was an investigation and it would clear up everything. The leadership team also told me they again have put up a new label for the pickup to be done without verification and **escalated** the ticket again. June 29 then.. I had to wait.

**June 29**:

No one came, the same story.. pick-up canceled again. My nerves were now relaxed, I wasn't shocked this was happening. I called Amazon again and they requested me to wait for 1 more day for the NOC results to come out. They rescheduled the return for **June 30, the day NOC results would come.**

**June 30:**

Obviously, no one showed up. But this time the return guy didn't even bother to cancel my return request. He was probably bored while doing it again and again. I called Amazon today after 6 pm, the end of 3 working days since NOC was filed. They told me **you have not been approved for an advanced refund, and that's about it.**

I asked them what is the exact conclusion of the investigation - where is the issue? They told me they have no idea. NOC only mentions that no advance refund can be given to me and that's it. I was flabbergasted. I waited 3 days for the investigation results hoping that if the proper issue was found I could get my refund. Now I wasn't even sure if an investigation was done or not.

They rescheduled my return on July 3, telling me, and promising me like every other Amazon customer representative, that next time my pickup would be done and they have put a new label again.

I am too much overwhelmed by all of this. I don't have so much time. I am doing WFH weekdays for my corporate, and it's not possible to ride the emotional rollercoaster every day explaining everything, with the same repeated robotic replies. It's completely unfair and clear, that they want to wear me off until I give up on this.

This is the result of buying from Amazon with your hard-earned money. I don't know how to end this long extract, I just hope the warranty fixes the screen flicker issue. I will also extend the warranty to 3 years so that I can use it for that duration without any issues. I have learned it the hard way - to always buy electronics or anything expensive in general from an authorized offline store even if it's 10-15k extra. It is simply not worth it, to order from online stores just for getting a fair deal and losing nights of sleep. **Peace of mind is above everything.**
