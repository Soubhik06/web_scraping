I've spent more time today debunking fake messages sent in the family groups than I usually do. Usually I laugh at the creativity behind the nonsense because that's the only way to stay sane.

But right now it's just really really exhausting. From messages about fake delivery services to fear mongering about grocery hours and people getting diagnosed in between all the other fake nonsense being circulated about this pandemic and lockdown. Especially when it feels like it's falling on deaf ears and I'll have debunked one piece of nonsense only to have to go do it again minutes later.

Giving our parents, uncle and aunties whatsapp feels like the biggest mistake sometimes.

Seriously Mumbaikars, how are you guys dealing with it?
