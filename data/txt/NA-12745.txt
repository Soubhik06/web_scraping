Through this petition, the petitioner seeks anticipatory bail in

case bearing FIR No.179 dated 01.10.2021, registered under Sections

420 , 406 , 465 , 467 , 468 , 471 IPC, at Police Station Kotwali, District

Bathinda. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that from the

other arrested co-accused, namely, Nitin Aggarwal, Gurdarshan Lal,

Sarwan Lal, Deepak Kumar, Baljit Kumar and Sunny Kumar, nothing

was recovered; that the petitioner has falsely been involved in the present

case and that nothing is to be recovered from the petitioner. He further

submits that the petitioner has been indicted in the present case on the

basis of the disclosure statement of co-accused, namely, Nitin Aggarwal. On the other hand, learned State counsel, while opposing the

prayer for grant of regular bail to the petitioner submits that from the co-

accused recovery has already been effected. He further submits that some

 The petitioner is a member of a gang that had been involved

in getting sanctioned loan(s) from the banks on the basis of fake and

forged documents of various persons. Such acts of impersonation, fraud

and forgery, are serious offences and shake the very fabric of an orderly

civilized society. Keeping in view the seriousness and gravity of the offence,

no case is made out to grant anticipatory bail to the petitioner. Dismissed.
