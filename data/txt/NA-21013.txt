Hello everyone,

I’m posting this to raise awareness and seek support.

I have been a victim of a major online scam through the gambling websites Dafabet and Dafawin. Over time, I lost approximately ₹54.26 Lakhs (around 5.4 million INR) due to fraudulent activities on their platform.

A formal Cyber Crime FIR has been filed under FIR No. 2191/2024.

Official notices were sent from Cyber Crime Police to the fraudulent operators.

Despite multiple official emails and escalations, including final warnings, the companies are either ignoring the matter or sending meaningless automated responses.

They even made false promises of settlement via phone calls from Indian numbers while pretending to call from London offices.

I have shared full case documentation including FIR copy, transaction proofs, and communications with Cyber Crime Police.
