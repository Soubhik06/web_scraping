I am writing this as a thank you to this subreddit as I had my questions cleared here about the same topic from the various very helpful redditors, so this is my way of giving back to the community.

If anyone has any questions feel free to ask me below. 

Ordered a signed memorabilia (Music CD) on December 17th,2021 and received it on January 19, 2022.

I had ordered a signed memorabilia (signed music CD) worth 61$ (40$ for the item and 21 $ for the delivery through eBay international standard delivery). 

I payed for the item in rupees, the dollars were automatically converted at the time of payment. 

My seller shipped the item from Texas, USA the very next day and the estimated delivery date was 27th January 2022. 

The item was shipped through the eBay global shipping program. The first tracking number I received on eBay was on December 21st, 2021 and it was initially being handled by USPS international. From December 22nd, 2021 to December 30th, 2021, i was receiving regular updates every 1-2 days. The package during this time was being delivered from Texas to Ohio. 

The package handling was then transferred to DHL on December 28th (DHL handled the package from 28th till it reached india)

On December 30th, I received the status “Scanned into sack/container”, after which I received no updates for another 5 days. At this point I had contacted DHL and they told me the package was heading to Germany. I was very confused and thought they sent the package to the wrong country after which I had asked on DHL Reddit and a Redditor replied stating that the Germany Hub is the main hub and that DHL doesn’t usually sent packages of very long distances in one flight (usually uses transit flights, so if this happens to you it’s normal).

Then on Jan 5th I received the status that the package arrived in Germany and on 5th it started its journey to India.

On Jan 13th, the package arrived at Delhi customs (Indela), and at this point I could track the package through three services that is through eBay tracking, through DHL tracking and through indian postal service tracking (DHL transferred the package to indian postal service who then delivered it to my house).

The DHL tracking within two hours or so showed that the package cleared customs while the eBay tracking showed that it was still at customs (I used the DHL tracker mainly cause I think eBay was updating later as compared to DHL)

About Customs:

PLEASE NOTE THAT CUSTOMS WILL NEVER CALL YOU ABOUT YOUR CUSTOMS BILL, AND I DID NOT RECEIVE ANY EMAIL OR ANY COMMUNICATION WHICH IS NORMAL. THERE IS A HUGE SCAM GOING WHERE ONCE YOUR PACKAGE IS IN CUSTOMS, A PERSON CALLS YOU AND TELLS YOU TO PAY INSANE AMOUNTS THROUGH BANK TRANSFER. PLEASE DO NOT GO FORTH WITH THIS. THE CUSTOMS AMOUNT HAS TO BE PAID TO THE POST MAN OR DIRECTLY TO THE POSTAL OFFICE AND A BILL WILL BE GENERATED. THE ONLY UPDATE I RECEIVED WAS THAT THROUGH DHL AND INDIAN POSTAL SERVICE TRACKING THAT STATED CUSTOMS WERE CLEARED. 

The package was then transferred to the indian postal service where they were transferring the package from Delhi to Bangalore which took about 5-6 days. 

From Bangalore the package was immediately dispatched to mangalore in the same day and I received the package the next day. 

I did not have to pay any customs amount probably because it was just a music cd although worth 60$, they usually look at the market value of the item which in this case would have been a couple hundred rupees. 

FAQ (questions I would have asked lol)

1) Did I pay any amount equivalent of customs at the time of eBay payment?

No I didn’t, I only payed 21$ for delivery

2) did customs open my package?

They did not open my package and I can guarantee that because of the way my parcel was packed by the actual seller

3) Did I declare any amount?

No I didn’t have to declare any amount. The seller declared the amount as it was. There was a sticker on the package with the price, item description and weight of the package. 

4) was it mentioned as a gift to reduce customs?

Nope it wasn’t, however I have seen a poster outside the Bangalore postal service that shows it is 0 amount for gifts but I don’t know how that works

5) how long did customs take?

It took a couple hours for me but i have seen it varies from person to person . Some got it cleared in a couple hours while others it took days or even weeks

6) did I pay any extra amount to DHL or indian postal service ?

I did not have to pay any extra amount to either. I guess eBay global program handles the whole thing

7) what tracking was accurate?

The DHL tracker was the most accurate up until it reached india after which it was the indian postal service tracking that was accurate. The eBay tracker was accurate up until usps passed the package to DHL after which I think the eBay tracker depended on the DHL tracking.

8) how did I receive a tracking number for Indian postal service?

DHL provided a local tracking number which started working on the indian postal service website the day it reached india.
