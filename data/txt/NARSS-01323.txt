I was intrigued by how the various TV channels would proceed after the LDF's comprehensive victory in the recent elections. And sure enough, for the last couple of days I haven't heard a word about the Gold/LIFE mission scams (do correct me if I'm wrong). Most "debates" have now turned to talking about minorities, and the role of Muslim organisations in the UDF. This seems to be in anticipation of Assembly elections and the possibility of UDF fielding a CM candidate from the IUML. Would like to hear what the others here make of it.

As a side note, this seems like a good time to read about and discuss with others the differences between organisations like the Muslim League and Jamaat-e-Islamia, which I often see clubbed together (bizarrely) as 'terrorist sympathiser' groups.
