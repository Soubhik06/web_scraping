Others have reported on this scam, but this is the first time that I have experienced it personally.

I just got an e-mail notice from PayPal about a payment request. The e-mail came from a real PayPal account. I checked my PayPal account, and sure enough there is a real payment request there. The request gave the name of the PayPal account owner (which can be ANYTHING that the PayPal account owner wants it to be), and the e-mail address associated with the PayPal account. The request asked for $378.99 and it gave a telephone number to call about the request. I called the telephone number and some shithead with an Indian accent said that he was with PayPal support.

Just a notice here to reinforce that scammers can send you REAL payment requests to your PayPal account (in addition to the more common fake e-mail notices that do not really come from PayPal).
