As someone working at senior levels of Compliance Department of an international Bank overseeing a number of countries, including India and UAE, I just wanted to share this article with fellow Malayalees.

Do not EVER loan your Bank account (or Wallet, or Card, or any financial products that store value) to anyone to conduct transactions that are not yours or without your full knowledge as to its provenance. The article shines a bright light on the dark world of fraud, social engineering scams, impersonation, preying on people's fears, laundering of proceeds of crime internationally through multiple rails and in this case, crypto, and as a consequence the minions get caught. 

Please know that well organised, highly resourceful transnational criminal organisations are often behind these elaborate scams structures spanning continents and gullible (I won't say young or old, as anyone can be affected) people trying to make a quick buck (commissions) edns up being caught. 

Please spend some time educating yourself of these financial crime risks and be extra careful when dealing with crypto. If you need to trade, ensure you do so 100% legally, through a properly licensed VASP and / or Exchange, and only deal with counterparties who have been adequately vetted through their KYC CDD programs. Also, know the tax implications of India and UAE.
