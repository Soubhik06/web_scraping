So apparently my neighbour told my mother that she got her specs removed by going to physical eye sight improvement classes in Saket. She said that my neighbour had gotten her specs removed after going to their centre and exercising there for 3 months. Now she is all for it because I have had my glasses for over 5 years and I am just going to college. 

&amp;#x200B;

I have done my fair share of home eye exercises during lockdown and I did them consistently for months but did not see any improvement, instead, my eye sight actually degraded (-0.75 to -2.75) over the course of 3 years. (Out of which i spent 8 months exercising) 

However I am a little skeptical because the fees is Rs. 9000 per month for the offline center with 1 hour sessions per day and 1500 for online pre-recorded videos. Obviously this is the same thing we have been doing all our lives but just overpriced as fk. They also have another type of course which they call 'marathon' which is a one time 3-4 hour session for Rs700 per session. 

I wanted to know if this is legit or just a scam and if they are only going to repeat the same exercises we see on facebook every now and then. The link for the website is- [https://prekshaeyeyoga.com](https://prekshaeyeyoga.com/programs/)
