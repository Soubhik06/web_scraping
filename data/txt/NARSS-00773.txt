Just an awareness post to warn you about a scam.

If you ever want to order cakes, flowers, or any other gifts and you come across a website called Bloomsvilla. Run. I repeat run.

Ordered a mother's day cake today. It was so pretty and my mom loves rosettes. Paid 1500. Set delivery time and everything. Then nothing. 
I got a tracking link that hasn't changed it's status till now. Called customer care number. Guess what? Number doesn't exist.
And the website looks so legit. Just like F&amp;P and igp etc
Then I searched on Google 'bloomsvilla' and got multiple pages about people warning that it was a fake company and the scam they are running. I was so sad yaar. I wanted to suprise my mother with a pretty cake. Eventually ordered a cake at 10 pm off swiggy. Mom was surprised yes but still the scam thing just killed my day.

Funnily enough last night I had a dream that was I scammed by verstappen.com. And I laughed it off in the morning :(
