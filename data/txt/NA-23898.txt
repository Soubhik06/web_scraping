So, a friend of mine is searching for a 1RK/1BHK flat here in Bangalore. She found a beautiful flat on OLX and contacted the dealer. However, the dealer spoke mainly in Hindi, and since my friend couldn't understand Hindi, she asked me to help her get the details. So, I called the guy, and I couldn't believe what I heard next.

\- 1200 Sq ft fancy looking flat, Wilson Garden location, 15k rent, and above all what made me confirm it is a scam- a meager 20K deposit!! In my mind, I think it's just too good to be true and is likely a scam. 

And to top it off, if I wanted to visit the flat, I needed to pay 1K for him to make me a "visitors card", which he claims to be refundable and instantly sends me a QR code! 

Oh, by the way, my conversation ended with him hurling B\*dk at me when I mentioned that I was just inquiring and not planning on visiting.

What do you guys think?
