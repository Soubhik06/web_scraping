So, I have received multiple calls from numbers with the same area code upwards of 2 to 3 times every single day for the past month. Normally if I pick up, it's complete silence on their end, then it hangs up. But the other day I picked up, not thinking, and ended up connecting to someone on the line. They had my name, address and phone number, and were claiming to be a collections service. They knew the info regarding how much money I owed for an unpaid phone and all that, and were offering weekly payments to help settle the debt. But the suspicious part is the previous calls being silent, they had the exact same accent as normal scammers (the stereotypical Indian accent), I spoke to two people and they both sounded very similar, and when looking up the number, instead of the company they were claiming to be popping up, it took me to websites where you can see who the number belonged to, and the website I checked said the number was "Faked Number." Normally just that fake number bit makes me know that it's a scam, but I worry about the fact they know all my info. What should I do and how can I tell if it's a scam or not?
