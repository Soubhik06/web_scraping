Hey everyone, in my last post on r/IndianStreetBets I highlighted a huge problem people are facing in the cypto industry due to lack of good governance by Cyber Crime Unit and the RBI. Due to this problem I had to file a Writ Petition in my local High Court and force all the respondents to give a response as to why my bank accounts were getting frozen and also why I was getting liens put on my money. You can read my previous post on [here](https://www.reddit.com/r/IndianStreetBets/comments/18quvob/lien_and_unwarranted_freezing_of_bank_accounts/) to get all the info. 
 
This post is basically an attempt to find more victims who are in the same situation as me. My Writ Petition is moving very slowly in the High Court and there is a good chance that before my next hearing I might get most of my Liens removed as the police stations have started sending their notices to the banks. There is also a good chance my case could get withdrawn if all my liens are removed but that still leaves the problem unresolved. People who got liens put on their money are still waiting to get their money back. Not everyone can fight this in the court which is why there has to be a better solution. 
 
My advocate has said this could be brought in front of the Supreme Court as an SLP (Special Leave Petition). He mentioned it would cost around 12-13 Lakhs in total to file this petition and if there are more people then surely everyone could pitch in and get the case filed in supreme court. Obviously the more the merrier but there is a strict category of victims who can join this petition. 
 
Following are the people who can qualify 
 
You were trading on foreign exchange prior to the 14th January 2024 ban 
 
You were using your own account for trading and not a third party 
 
Your source of money is clean and genuine 
 
Your account was verified on these foreign exchanges 
 
I am hoping this would force the Supreme Court to take action against Cyber Crime and RBI to issue guidlines and proper procedure that does not target innocent people. The onus of due diligence should only be on the first person who accepts funds in their account from a fraud victim. Layer 1 and above should not be liable as you have no protection even if you do everything by the book. I just hope that there are plenty of people who are suffering from this issue and I request you to share this to those who are facing these issues. We certainly need to form a coalition to go against the current system given it is not doing anything to help innocent people who are getting caught up in all this mess. Thank you in advance and I look forward to hearing from you all
