--- POST TEXT ---

So, I was driving back from work and suddenly a man signals me to stop my car on the side on the bridge near Tin factory. I stopped my car on the side and two men came rushing towards me shouting that I've hit their friend and he's in the hospital, they claimed i was rash driving and come out and see that I've scratched the car because I've hit him, and they've been chasing me from there and finally caught hold of me. Two more guys came and claimed that they've seen me doing it.
I really freaked out and started explaining it to them that I've not hit anyone. They said that I've to come with them to the hospital where their friend is now. 
I called a friend, he said just drive to the nearest police station. So i told those guys that lets go to the police station, they said okay let's go.
I start driving again and as soon i get off the bridge I see two traffic police guys standing on the service lane, I drive to them and explain everything to them. They asked me where are those men, and to my surprise those guys are nowhere to be seen now. The cops said next time note down the vehicle number.
I drive back home and check my car and there were no scratches as claimed by them. I think I've saved myself from a scam.
