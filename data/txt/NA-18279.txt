--- POST TEXT ---

There is a common headline that The Onion (premier satirical news outlet) uses whenever there is a [mass](https://www.theonion.com/no-way-to-prevent-this-says-only-nation-where-this-r-1820163660?utm_content=Main&amp;utm_campaign=SF&amp;utm_source=Twitter&amp;utm_medium=SocialMarketing) [shooting](https://www.theonion.com/no-way-to-prevent-this-says-only-nation-where-this-r-1819580358) in the [USA](https://www.theonion.com/no-way-to-prevent-this-says-only-nation-where-this-r-1819578287). It is very unfortunate that that we get to read this headline frequently. Most of the article is also redundant where only the place and dates are changed. This is not trivializing the grave occurrence that it is talking about but on the contrary, it is working on the principle of castigat ridendo mores which means ‘laughing corrects morals’. Simply put, satire does cause social change.

It is evident that India too is due, for some time now, to have a standard headline with formulaic subject matter, regarding the barbaric rapes that we are beleaguered with. Let’s face it, we are desensitized to rape. The tolerance level has reached its saturation. Our empathy is immune to human suffering. What if there is a helpless child at the end of the crime? Our feelings are in such short supply that it is limited to sharing cat videos over Facebook. So, why not have a rudimentary recycled piece masquerading as news? That’s all that we are worth for anyway.

More importantly, our outrage is very selective and short-lived. We must understand that the catastrophe must fit our rhetoric, our blind beliefs, our unshakable faith. Prime example is the vitriol we see in our social media, where fake news is fed to us like fodder. Enlightened educated souls are so gullible that they lap it up like a bone thrown to a ravenous dog. But what is apparent in the chain of messages and propaganda filled videos is the Dunning-Kruger effect, where people with less knowledge think that they know a lot about everything under the sun. This happens more with [politics](http://www.psypost.org/2018/04/study-people-less-political-knowledge-think-know-lot-politics-51062) than with any other topic. We see a sudden surge of in-depth analysis of the ethnic background of the victim and how that is relevant to the crime, relatives turned sleuths finding out the conspiracy behind all of it that certain sections of the society, be it media or the opposition that doesn’t want us to know, God-men on TV making a mockery of the issue by saying rapes happen daily and has become inherent to the society. This is the time that we have to remember that barba non facit philosophum, a beard does not make a philosopher. 

We have always been good at one thing, where we have nurtured the juvenile habit of shifting the blame by attacking others – the dreaded [whataboutery](https://www.thenewsminute.com/article/why-not-outrage-about-other-rapes-whataboutery-kathua-and-unnao-must-stop-now-79668). Rape happened in Kathua? So, what about the [rapes](https://www.boomlive.in/kathua-rape-fallout-assam-bihar-police-refute-claims-of-viral-social-media-posts/) in Assam and Bihar? We will make videos of those and circulate them everywhere. How can we forget the incidents that has not happened at all? The rape happened in a temple? What about the rape that happened in a mosque? We will spread the article as if it has happened recently even if it had happened [years](https://www.altnews.in/old-news-of-14-year-old-raped-inside-mosque-in-moradabad-circulates-as-recent-incident/) ago. We have stooped so low where we are spreading fake propaganda filled videos to shift the heat away from the incident. There are some who shifted the blame on refugees as they are the soft targets with no one to represent them. But there is still some [hope](https://www.nationalheraldindia.com/india/senior-lawyer-prashant-bhushan-filed-criminal-complaint-against-madhu-kishwar-tweets-kathua-rape-case-tweet) with respect to that. We have to understand that whataboutism is the last recourse of the guilty. We fail to understand that it is in itself a fallacy, a faulty reasoning. If we resort to whataboutery, then we must understand that the logic is flawed. The pot calling the kettle black approach is no doubt tantalizing and will appeal to the hypocrisy. The ad hominem Tu quoque hyperbole tactic is for the coward, to the bigot and the vice. 

Even if all the other rapes which allegedly happened, had occurred now, the Kathua case takes center-stage because of the planned brutality of it, because the police were also the perpetrators, because a [religious](https://www.telegraphindia.com/india/the-reach-of-hindu-manch-223767) group with tricolor marched and tried to prohibit the filing of the charge sheet, because two [members](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/kathua-rape-attended-rally-on-partys-order-says-bjp-leader/articleshow/63765714.cms) of the ruling party joined the protest which was shielding the accused, because the bar association was threatening the [lawyer](https://thelogicalindian.com/news/kathua-rape-case-lawyer-deepika-rajawat-threatened-by-bar-association/) fighting the case. 

There were some people in power asking not to [politicize](http://indianexpress.com/article/india/bjp-minister-meenakshi-lekhi-accuses-opposition-of-politicising-kathua-rape-murder-case-and-unnao-rape-case-5135860/) the issue. When you communalize the issue, be sure that we will politicize it and as citizens of the largest democracy, we should too. When due process is obstructed by asking what the religion of the victim is, then we too will ask who the ruling party is when the incident happened. When you try to divide us on false sense of nationality and make a mockery of the ordeal on communal lines, then obviously we will have to question the authority and rightly so. An efficient government is only as good as the opposition. Besides, the unity of a nation shouldn’t be up for debate in cases of rape. Chauvinistic patriotism shouldn’t be used as a tool to split us when the dignity of our daughters is on the line. Jingoism must take a back seat when humanity is questioned. 

It is of paramount importance on how we react to incidents like this. If we resort to trivializing the issues by bickering because it does not confine to our pomposity then it is a recipe for hatred, and hatred breeds hatred. We have failed as a nation when you see the name of the victim trending in [porn](https://www.scoopwhoop.com/asifa-searches-on-porn-website/?ref=latest&amp;utm_source=home_latest&amp;utm_medium=desktop#.223jw8wu1) sites. This type of sexual deviancy has no place in a civilized world. We also have to remember our social responsibility towards our impressionable, impatient, naive youth. यद् भावं तद् भवति, you are what you believe and they trust what they see in social media without due diligence. The grief of the parents, be it of the victim or of the perpetrator is what we are toying with. This incessant, insensitive retort will achieve nothing and in essence, we will all be party in raping the victim again, the poor innocent and impoverished soul. Rest in peace? No such thing.
