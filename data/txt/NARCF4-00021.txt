Hey everyone,

I recently got scammed by a so-called "broker" who promised to get me a rental home in Rasulgarh, Bhubaneswar. Sharing this here to warn others and also to see if any genuine house owners have leads.

How the Scam Happened:-

A broker claimed he could get me a rental house.

He asked me to send one month’s rent + his commission, which I transferred via PhonePe.

Later, he asked for half of the security deposit, which I said I'd send only when I see the home.

After taking the money, he told me to come see the house but ignored my calls.

Then he called me angrily, claiming I missed his calls and demanded the remaining deposit.

When I asked for the house location, he refused to share it.

Now, he says he will return only one month’s rent minus ₹500, but I haven’t received anything yet.

I am still actively calling him to ask my money back but he isnt responding.(my peer suggested me)

This guy is most likely a scammer targeting people looking for rental homes. Even the person who he claimed to be the owner sounded like a child on call. If you're house-hunting in Bhubaneswar, be cautious and never send money in advance without verifying everything in person.

I have already filed a complaint at the Cyber Crime Office, Bhubaneswar UPD. I’ll update here if there’s any progress, but in the meantime, please stay alert and don’t fall for such scams.

I am still actively searching for a 1RK independent room within ₹5,000 max in Rasulgarh or nearby areas. If any genuine house owners or people with leads can help, please comment or DM me.

Let’s spread awareness and help each other avoid such frauds. Stay safe!
