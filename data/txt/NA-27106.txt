Ok, so i got a bunch of missed calls from theses guys who claim to work for " Education Network". I looked them up and ended up on this sketchy looking website.([http://www.theeducationnetwork.us.com/](http://www.theeducationnetwork.us.com/)) 

I have never heard of them b4 and they knew my full name, email address, zip code, and obviously my phone number. I asked where they got my info and they said an 'online form' that i filled out to apply for college. I wasn't told the name of the form or the website. They said they were gonna help me search for college opportunities and asked for my date of birth, and if i have a green card. Mind u, the person on the line had the typical Indian scammer accent so i was already skeptical from the get go. However, after giving them the information they asked for although incorrect, i was put on a call with someone from an actual college (Miller-Motte College). The person from Education Network said to give them all the necessary info and to ask questions if i had any, and that they were there 'to help me'. This person was an African American, and he asked me if i had a job, when i graduated, and if i have my transcript at hand. I told him i didn't have it and he said he would call me later in a few days when i get it.

I am confused, and i don't know if i blew an opportunity or dodged a bullet. Iv'e already applied to my community college and i'm waiting to get my Texas ID so i can be admitted. If anyone else has an experience with these "Education Network" guys and knows if they are a scam or not plz let me know. Thx.
