Told the bunk guy to fill in diesel for 2000. He stopped at 300 and asked card or cash. Then again I told him diesel for 2000 and told payment via google pay. By the time he resumed from 300 he went to get the QR code for scanning and told me something to see in the fuel meter. Caught only ‘1700’ from the sentence he said and didn’t catch the remaining sentences. Gave me the QR code and asked me to pay 2000. With that tag in hand I was waiting for the fuel to get filled without paying. He stopped at 1700 and said done sir. Again I loudly said 2000. Might have realised that I am aware of the tactic and then he filled it upto 2000 without saying anything and I paid the amount using gpay and left. 

What bewildered me is that these people are still taking public for fools and thinking they can scam using these methods.
