I have been getting quite a few of these recently, the last one coming moments ago.

&amp;#x200B;

For those who are unfamiliar, you'll get a call from an 800 number with a name ID of "Apple" or "Apple Support" attached to it. When you pick up you will be greeted by a female voiced automated operator who alerts you to an urgent problem with your Apple account and told to press one and stay on the line for assistance. If you do, you will be redirected to a "technician" (all of whom had Indian sounding accents in my experience) who will inform you that your Apple iCloud account has been hacked and that someone has stolen your identity. I've gotten nearly a dozen of these calls and hung up on most of them, or cursed them out and had them hang up on me. But once or twice out of curiosity I asked the "tech" what the nature of the breach was and they listed random locations from where they had apparently "viewed suspicious activity" coming from my account. So far I've tallied Nebraska, Texas, and Mexico. Evidently these phantom identity thieves enjoy spicy food.

&amp;#x200B;

If you do stay on the line the "tech" will throw some official sounding info at you, ostensibly establishing their legitimacy. They'll list the name on your account along with some other easily accessible public info, and tell you to go to your computer and bring up your Apple account. They'll then go into the familiar protocol of attempting to gain remote access to your computer where they will then hold it hostage much in the same way of the old tech support scam. Obviously I didn't let it get this far, but I can confirm that they're after itunes gift cards. The way I know this is on one of these calls I told the "tech" that this had happened to me in the past and asked her, "How many itunes gift cards will I need to purchase to make this go away?" She admitted that it was two.

&amp;#x200B;

Fuck these people. I'm not gonna lie, I'll admit that I've said some vile stuff to them, but again, \*fuck these people.\* About three weeks ago they must have called my mother's house no less than eight or nine times in the span of a single afternoon. And as we all know, they wouldn't be doing this stuff if it wasn't working.

&amp;#x200B;

So I propose that the mods add it to the stickied master list.
