We are a couple of developers, who have developed a prototype of our app called [Consumercracy](https://consumercracy.com) (Inspired from the word "Democracy") 
This app will be an ecosystem for all types of consumers to spread awareness, help each other and prevent and report the bad practices and frauds held in any marketplace, through collective knowledge. This would be totally consumer driven. Consumers can share their experiences regarding any product/service they have used. They can review or recommend any product/service that they used. They can alert other consumers about any frauds. They can ask any question related to any product/service or answer the queries of other consumers. 
The main motivation to build this app was that we saw an increasing number of scams happening in online marketplaces and nowhere to verify such services. So we decided to build an ecosystem for the consumers where they can help each other and spread the awareness. Also, we didn't find any single platform which provides all these features under one roof. 
Link [Consumercracy](https://consumercracy.com)

We want your feedback on how we can improve our app so as to make it more useful to the consumers.

TIA, any feedback is appreciated .
