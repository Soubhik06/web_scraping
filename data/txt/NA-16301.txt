An hour ago, I received a call from a unknown number, I picked up the call as there is no true caller red flag. On the other end is an automated voice said “My FedEx parcel was flagged down at customs and please press 1 to talk to customer care”. I was surprised as I didn’t send any parcel at all. I pressed 1 and immediately someone picked up the call and started speaking in hindi (Now, I’m almost sure it’s a scam because, any big company customer care would definitely give an option to language), the person on the other asked me to confirm my name and phone number and told me the parcel I sent from Mumbai to Taiwan has been flagged down at Mumbai customs due to illegal contents in the parcel. I told him I didn’t send any such parcel, he mentioned my Aadhar card has been attached as the proof and advised me to file a complaint with Mumbai cyber cell. He then said, they handled similar case last month and worked with mumbai police to resolve the issue. O told him I’ll file a complaint with mumbai cyber cell, he then said he can forward the call to mumbai police right away and get it resolved, I told him I’ll file the complaint myself and asked him share the tracking Id, booking slip etc via email, he said he’ll share it ( he has my email id too). Then I cut the call.

Immediately I looked up Fedex customer care number, called them up and explained the situation, they assured me there is no such package connected to my phone number. 

Oof! These scammers are getting smart! Creating a guise of a scam to scam people!

Be careful folks!
