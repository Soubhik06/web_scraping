This happened with me and one of my friends/colleague from office some weeks back.

We were heading for lunch outside our SEZ (practically with heavy prices for food inside we can't really afford it on everyday basis and taste also sucks) to a eatery where we get the traditional North Karnataka food with Holige, Jowar Rotis and all. 

While we were ordering our lunch we saw that there was this weak, sad old lady who was having a piece of paper from a hospital with prescriptions and was showing it to people so that she could get money for treatment of her daughter. My colleague being very sympathetic gave her some money and we left after having lunch.

Cut to evening, we see the same old lady while going out to home still asking for money since she wasn't able to arrange as much necessary. My colleague again became very sad and wanted to help her and I suggested since the woman was in need we xould get her all the prescribed medicines from a nearby medical store and give it to her at once. 

My colleague liked the idea and took the prescription and showed it to me and I just saw the date on it being more than couple of years old. That actually broke my colleagues heart and also faith in helping people since we could never judge who actually is in need or not.

The lady had gone for couple of weeks and now she seems to be back again with same prescription.

Has someone also faced similar incidents near their workplaces or home? Is it a big scam that's going on?
Any idea on who is regulating it? How could we work on reducing the same?

Hoping to see some inputs in.
