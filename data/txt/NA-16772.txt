--- POST TEXT ---

Internet/Call based scams have grown significantly in recent years.I've seen countless Facebook posts in groups.I realized the problem with posting them on Facebook is that only group members would know about the scams and rest of the world would probably never know of it. To tackle this problem of scams in India. I came up with an idea, why not build a platform where people can share their story of being scammed and help fellow netizens by telling how it could have been avoided? After few hours, I came up with a basic site, a community-driven platform [WatchScams.com](https://WatchScams.com) where people can share their story of being scammed which would help others.

&amp;#x200B;

It's still in BETA, I've posted about one recently growing scam myself and would really appreciate if members can share their story of being scammed and help others. Please note that its limited to internet/call based scams (or anything related to tech in general) 

&amp;#x200B;

I don't intend to make money with this, it's completely free and open for everyone. As a responsible netizen, that's the least I could do to fight scams and make web a better place for my country. If you like my work and would love to make web a better place. Consider sharing about it. :)
