NEVER GO WITH TURNKEY CONSTRUCTION

Turnkey construction means that material and labour both provided by the contractor at a fixed per square feet rate
and you have to pay in installnents as per the position of work done

Word Contractor includes engineers too. Whether contractor is engineer or not doesn't matter. problems you face are same

This is where most frauds happen with big amounts 

You have to visit site every single day else they will do big blunders which cannot be repaired without demolition

They delay the project very long. They talk very sweet at the time of taking installment

Don't follow written contract and will not listen to your problems except at the day of taking next instalment and won't fix previous problems at your site

They halt previous work which you already paid for and ask for next instalment to continue else they run away 

Since a single installment is worth lakhs the loss is huge
The contractor will not visit the site the day you give the first instalment(except at the start of the work) 

Rather will switch off his phone and only call for next instalment

Ultra poor workmanship, your whole work will be left on the responsibility of labourers (mistri). They don't care even your foundation is poor, room measurements not as per 2D floor plan
Poor brick work, weak columns, poor lapping at beams
Poor quality mortar mixing

No security of site if there is no material brought by contractor at your site and they intentionally bring less quantity so that their loss is minimum and even if they run away, they run with huge profit because they didn't bring materials so they almost kept all your money in their pockets which is very difficult to take refund 

You call the contractor and he will start making excuses and say that construction is good quality and if you become too strict, he will run away without refunding you any excess amount left with him after taking instalment

Then you have to go to courts in that case

In labour only contract, even if contractor runs away after poor work you will only be losing a portion of labour charges and suffer a theft of materials on site but loss is far less than turnkey(With Material) construction

Construction is very hectic job and labour only contract is the least risky but still a headache
