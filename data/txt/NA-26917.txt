My 60 something year old roommate was determined to get one of these and without me knowing, found a seller online that turned out to be an Indian scammer.

She claims she paid thru Paypal and could get her money back, but I caught her looking at Wal-Mart's Gift Card Fraud page indicating she bought gift cards for this purchase.

She didn't realize it was a scam until they called saying her puppy was delayed in Ohio and they wanted $300 more for insurance.

I once walked in on her installing software so "Apple" could access her PC. She has an Iphone and fell for that BS. Thank God I stopped her because she has both her and her brother's financial information right on the desktop.
