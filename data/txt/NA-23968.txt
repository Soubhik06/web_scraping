So I recently applied for a fiber connection from Airtel by filling this form [https://www.airtel.in/broadband/feasibility/](https://www.airtel.in/broadband/feasibility/) . Shortly after I was contacted by an Airtel employee via phone, who asked me to send her KYC documents via Whatsapp. Next, she said that I'll receive an otp, which I have to text her and then I'll get the link for payment. 

This whole thing smells fishy to me. You never give your otp to anyone, that's security 101. So this looks like a scam. When I asked why she needs the otp, she says that it's for verification while she fills up a form on my behalf. When I insisted that I want to do the form-filling myself, she says that it's an internal application! 

On the other hand, I can't find any other form from Airtel website, where i can directly register. It seems I have to go through an employee over phone. But I have no way to verify if the person calling me is actually an employee or not. I tried calling customer care number, but it's useless because I am not an existing customer yet. 

So I want to ask if anyone here has gone through the process of getting an Airtel fiber connection? Is this a standard practice for them to ask for an otp over call/text?
