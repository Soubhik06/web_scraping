So a couple days back I posted this on the Sub. A lot of smart people started asking me “where is the scam”? Here is an update:

In last two days several (now in double digits) flats in my society have delivery people coming in with same / similar requests for OTP/Payment for something they have not ordered. Just a while back, they caught a man that tried to sneak into the flat behind a woman after asking for water. He went to several flats in one building with the same parcel asking for an OTP for delivery. This is a relatively secure society with several guards on duty at a time. Despite that, these people are brave enough. Most of them look normal but kept fiddling with their phones/avoid eye contact. Ask your parents to be careful. I know you are very smart. They may not be!
