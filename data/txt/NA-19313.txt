So I applied for the change in pan details and paid the fees of rupees 500. I receive a mail from them stating to send a physical copy of dob proof etc. This is when I got suspicious as to why they need it. They can just ask for a scan copy or something like that. 

I realised that this is not official website for making changes to your pan. The official website is the nsdl website. 

Now I am scared that I have provided my personal details such as pan number, mobile number, aadhar number and email id to this website which is not the official govt. website.

Can anybody give me advice on what next steps to take. Anybody had similar experience of using online-pancard.com? What are the ways in which my information can be misused? How can I prevent it?
