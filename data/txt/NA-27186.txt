I came home from work to find my dad on his computer while also on a call. I saw his bank account open and heard that the operator on the other side had an Indian accent, and as messed up as this is gonna sound it didn’t take that long to piece things together. They claimed to have been “required to be giving out refunds because they violated privacy regulations” or some BS like that. By the time I told my dad what was really happening it was already too late. They took 13k from him which is almost everything he had. He called his bank and he assured the scam dept. that he certainly didn’t approve of the transactions of thousand of dollars being sent to the random bank number. They didn’t make any promises only saying that they were going to launch an investigation that will take up to 10 days max. They certainly shut down the accounts so they can’t take what little we now have left. I know they told my dad to install “AnyDesk” which is how they got into his personal info. Luckily I came home when I did I just can’t believe this all happened to us. I know this is common sense to everyone else likely reading this but my dad has Alzheimer’s. Anyways he’s unplugged the computer from the WiFi and uninstalled the system that they used to gain access to his computer. I’m telling him he should also get it taken into a computer store to get it cleaned before connecting it to internet again. If anybody has any tips or reassurance that things are gonna be ok I’d really appreciate it we are going through a tough time rn.
