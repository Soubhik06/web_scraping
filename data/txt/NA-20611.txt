--- POST TEXT ---

I ordered one dress from Meesho, but it came with wrong colour, so I initiated Return.
Next day Delihivery delivery partner picked up my order and gave me return receipt. 
Next day I observed in Meesho app that return is in cancelled state, so I called Meesho customer care. They told that I will get email and I need to send them photo of return receipt, which I did.
After 4-5 days still I didn't get any refund, so I called customer care again.
They told that return order is cancelled and they didn't receive package, they created complaint number but they are not ready to email the complaint status. They make us wait on customer care call for 30 min. and disconnect call after that. The number is not toll free.
Thier refund policy is not straight forward and we don't get any status of complaint. We are expected to call them every time and get disconnected from call while paying for the call ourselves.
Beware.
