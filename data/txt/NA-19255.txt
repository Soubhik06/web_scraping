--- POST TEXT ---

I was naive back then and I sent my Aadhaar to my CA when they asked me. It seems they used it during the filing of my returns. I also linked it to my PAN myself before July 31 2017. I didn't know we could use all 0s to fill up Aadhaar number in IT return.

Now with all these security and privacy concerns and solid arguments in SC against Aadhaar I feel scared. Can I unlink my Aadhaar from my PAN? I have luckily resisted from linking my bank accounts and phone numbers to Aadhaar.

How can a PAN card linked to Aadhaar be misused?

Also I feel scared because I've sent scanned copies of my Aadhaar for KYC to many companies. How can someone's Aadhaar card alone be misused?

I strongly believe SC will strike down Aadhaar however cynical you are. The arguments I read are solid and some top lawyers are petitioning against Aadhaar.
