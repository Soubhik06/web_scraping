So as the title says I’ve probably been and should have absolutely known better.

First day in Delhi and ask a rickshaw driver to take us to Delhi gate instead offers to take us to tourist agency. Ended up sorting out our dream itinerary which include flights, driver for 10 days and over night trains as well as Hostels/hotels. There was written messages from previous “customers” which added to the believability of the proposition and through excitement at the prospect we ended up paying half of the money each and were given a driver for the rest of the day for a tour of Delhi. 

At the end of the trip he showed us “vouchers” but said he would give them a printed copy of our flight e ticket as well as our written itinerary and the driver then dropped us home promising to pick us up the following morning for a driven tour of old Delhi.

When it’s proven it is inevitably a scam would there anyway to get our money back or to involve police or the tourism ministry to get out money back if they refuse.
