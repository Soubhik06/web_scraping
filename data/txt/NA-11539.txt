(Delivered on 29-03-2023) 1. By way of present criminal revision petition, petitioner
impugns the order dated 02.12.2022 passed by ld. MM-02, North
West dismissing the application under Section 156(3) Cr.P.C. in

SC No. 96/2023 Arun Kumar Pandey Page no. 1
 Vs.
 Bank Manager IDFC First Bank Ltd. Ct. Case no. 12173/2020, titled as Arun Kumar Pandey Vs. IDFC
Bank Ltd. 2. As per petitioner, the case is that on 30.04.2018, he went to
buy an AC at E.P. Electronics and took a consumer loan from
IDFC First Bank. Petitioner met Sandeep Upadhyay, bank
employee of IDFC Bank who told the complainant to submit a
copy of Aadhar Card, a cheque of Rs. 3850/-, two photographs
and a copy of PAN card. On submitting these documents, a loan of
Rs. 3850/- was sanctioned to the petitioner against loan account
no. 15114979 and an EMI of Rs. 3850/- was scheduled. Petitioner
stated that he had paid his entire loan EMIs by 02.04.2019 and
cleared the loan amount. Petitioner further submitted that on
20.02.2020, he received a whatsapp message from one Ravi Joshi,
bank employee of IDFC First Bank whereby it was conveyed to
the petitioner that he is eligible for a loan of Rs. 50,000/- on the
basis of his previous track record. Petitioner further submitted that
on 24.02.2020 he received a call from from said Ravi Joshi asking
for a copy of Aadhar Card and PAN card of petitioner. Petitioner
was told that his loan request has been processed and approved
and for the disbursement of the same he was asked to provide the
OTP number received on the mobile number of petitioner.
Petitioner provided the same to the said bank employee after
which petitioner was told that a bank employee will come to him
for verification but no one came to the petitioner from the bank.
On 25.02.2020 petitioner called the said bank employee but his
mobile number was switched off. On 29.03.2020 petitioner
received two messages on his mobile number 9911986614 that

 Bank Manager IDFC First Bank Ltd. petitioner had paid an EMI of Rs. 3546/- against loan account no.
30845072 and another EMI of Rs. 2459/- against loan account no.
30883005. Petitioner made representations to the accused Bank,
SHO and the DCP concerned but no action was taken. 3. Grounds taken in the revision petition are that: SC No. 96/2023 Arun Kumar Pandey Page no. 3

investigate the facts that from where accused purchased the
product online and where the same was delivered. And IO also
failed to investigate the IP address used for online shopping. 4. While dismissing the application under Section 156(3) Cr.P.C. ld. Trial Courts records that: 5. Record perused. Submissions heard. 6. Prima facie complaint of petitioner discloses commission of
cognizable offence which requires investigation. In the impugned
order, ld. Trial Court erred while holding that payments of EMI
had been made by petitioner till 02.09.2020 i.e. even after five
months of sanctioning of loan, from his account. However, the
payments paid till 02.09.2020, as recorded in impugned order, are
the payments which are due and not paid by the petitioner. SC No. 96/2023 Arun Kumar Pandey Page no. 4
 Vs.
 Bank Manager IDFC First Bank Ltd. 7. From the perusal of complaint of petitioner and nature of
allegations, complainant is not having access to all the documents
which will be required and necessary to prove its case. There is
need of recovery or discovery of documents and the persons who
played fraud upon the petitioner, for which investigating agency is
required to be set in motion. 8. Therefore, the impugned order dated 02.12.2022 is set aside
with direction to SHO PS concerned to formally register an FIR
on the complaint of petitioner and investigate the matter. SHO PS
concerned to file compliance report within 10 days from the
receipt of this order, before concerned Court. 9. With these observations, revision petition is disposed off.
