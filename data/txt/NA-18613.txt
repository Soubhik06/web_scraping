Today morning I received a call from a guy, speaking in Hindi. Here is how the conversation went (I am translating the whole thing to English).

Fraud : Sir, can I speak to neoindian. 
Me : Yes, speaking. 
Fraud : What is the problem with your LIC? 
Me : I have no problem. 
Fraud : You could have doubled the profit in the last few weeks, why didn't you? 
Me : What? 
Fraud : We had sent you an intimation to your address, regarding changes to be made to your policy. If you had done that, by now your money would have doubled. We sent it to (Fucker knows my address, though he is not able to pronounce it properly). 
Me : No, I did not receive any intimation. 
Fraud : Can you give me your LIC number so that we can cross check? 
Me : OK, its 394B9S83 (not exactly, I told him whatever alpha-num came to mind then). 
Fraud : Sir, this is not an LIC number. 
Me : I know. 
Fraud : Sir, please give me your LIC number. 
Me : I don't know. 
Fraud : Can you ask someone at home? 
Me : OK. 
Fraud : I will call you back in 2 mins. 

exactly after 2 Mins.... 

Fraud : Sir, can I have the number now. 
Me : No, I don't know. 
Fraud : But you asked someone at home right? 
Me : Yes, they don't know either. 
Fraud : Can I call you in the evening? 
Me : OK. 
Fraud : Will you have your LIC number by then? 
Me : No. 
Fraud : Sir please, Why? 
Me : I don't have anything invested in LIC. 
Fraud : ..... 

Any of you experienced anything like this?
