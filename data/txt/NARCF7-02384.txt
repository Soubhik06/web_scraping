In August 2024, I visited RTO Pune (Sangamwadi) to make my learner's license permanent. While I was there, I met an agent named "Sonu," whose name sounded suspicious even back then. He assured me he could help with the process and gave me his number. However, since my learner's license was still new, I had to wait another 15 days, and I didn’t follow up during that time.

Yesterday, I called him about the same issue, and he seemed fine with proceeding. He asked for ₹1500, but I offered to pay ₹1000 upfront and ₹500 later in person. I made the ₹1000 payment to someone named Adi (as instructed by Sonu). However, when I tried calling Sonu later at 4 PM, he didn’t pick up. I called him multiple times, but he never responded.

This morning at 11 AM, I went to the RTO to find him, but he was nowhere to be seen. Some people there knew about him, while others didn’t. One person mentioned he stopped coming to the RTO around four months ago. Another said he has charges against him at the police station. Someone even claimed that many fake agents use made-up names to scam people.

Later, Sonu called me, claiming he had met with a minor accident and would get my license done on the 24th (Tuesday) after recovering. I confronted him with everything I had heard at the RTO and mentioned that I was considering going to the police to verify his claims. He said there are two groups of agents at the RTO, and competition between them often leads to such rumors.

Now I’m feeling regretful for paying ₹1000 upfront and unsure whether he’ll show up on Tuesday or not. Do you think I’m being scammed, or is there a chance this could still work out? He seemed to be a good person on phone and offline also , and he even said "Will 1000Rs make me a fortune of wealth or what if i scam you?"

Is there a fraud detection cell, RTO helpline, or police station I can approach to report this or verify his claims (After tuesday if he doesnt show up) ? Any suggestions would be appreciated.
