MediaNama is gearing up for its virtual discussion on “Impact of SIM Binding on Social Media”. This event looks at the recent Department of Telecommunications (DoT) directive, which requires platforms like WhatsApp and Telegram to enforce continuous SIM–device binding , making the physical presence of a SIM card a mandatory condition for service continuity.

While the stated rationale is to prevent cyber fraud using smuggled SIMs, the directive overlooks the dominant fraud patterns in India — phishing, caller ID spoofing, OTP theft, SIM swaps, and VoIP-based impersonation — none of which SIM binding meaningfully addresses. Instead, the rule introduces friction for legitimate users, impedes remote work, disrupts CRM systems, and breaks continuity for NRIs and travellers using carrier-locked or single-SIM devices. The discussion looks to unpack the technical feasibility , policy rationale , and operational impact of SIM binding, and to surface alternative frameworks that address abuse without degrading user experience or business functionality.
