This Writ Petition is filed praying this Court to direct

respondent Nos.1 and 2 to regulate and control working of

Online Digital Lenders doing business through mobile

App or any other platform, stop charging exorbitant

interest on loan from petitioner, stop harassment to the

borrowers from recovery agents, fix maximum rate of

interest chargeable by online digital lenders and for other

appropriate reliefs. 2. It is stated that the petitioner has availed the loans

from various financial institutions through online Apps

and there are outstanding dues and he is not in a position

to pay the said loan amount as he sustained heavy loss in

his business. It is further stated that the financial

institutions, who have sanctioned loans, are not following

the guidelines issued by the Reserve Bank of India while 2 CVBR, J

collecting/recovery of the loan amount payable by the

petitioner. Hence, the writ petition. 3. A perusal of the material on record would show that

the petitioner has sought relief to regulate and control

working of Online Digital Lenders doing business through

mobile App and also to stop charging exorbitant interest

on loan from the petitioner and not to harass the

borrowers. While making specific allegations that recovery

agents of financial institutions are harassing the

petitioner and they are charging the exorbitant interest on

the loans, the petitioner has not chosen to make them as

party respondents to the writ petition. Without

impleading the financial institutions, the writ petitioner is

not entitled to any relief claimed by him in the writ

petition. Since the financial institutions against whom

the petitioner has sought relief, are necessary parties for

disposal of the writ petition are not made as parties to

this writ petition, this Writ Petition is liable to be

dismissed solely on the ground of none joinder of

necessary parties. CVBR, J
