Zuckerberg is such a bullshit artist it's not funny.

He claims that Internet,org is only meant for people who aren't online due to financial reasons! 

So they can afford Rs.5000 for a smartphone, but not Rs.100 for an internet pack? Tell me another one.

If they really wanted to promote Internet, why not offer access to the ENTIRE internet instead of just their own (and their crony's) apps and services.

This is a huge fraud being perpetrated on the unsuspecting Indian public and if the govt is buying this BS we must let them know it is BS.

Internet,org is as big a threat to NetNeutrality in India as Airtel Zero and other Zero plan apps - they prevent new users from discovering new apps that might be competitors to these services.

And that is terrible for the Indian Startup / Internet Ecosystem. You already have to compete with deep pockets to get eyeballs, now you'll have to pay extortion money to even be in the address book!
