 Order

17/02/2021 1. The appellant has preferred this appeal aggrieved by order

dated 16.01.2021 passed by Special Judge, SC/ST (Prevention of

Atrocities) Cases Kota whereby bail application filed by the

appellant was rejected. 2. F.I.R. No.208/2020 was registered at Police Station Sultanpur

Kota for offence under Sections 376-D , 354 , 367 , 368 I.P.C. and Section 3(2)(v) SC/ST Act. 3. It is contended by the counsel for the appellant that

prosecutrix is in a habit of lodging cases by involving the persons

in honey trap. Some cases have been produced before the Court (Downloaded on 23/02/2021 at 09:29:25 PM) (2 of 2) [CRLAS-140/2021]

 which goes to show that the prosecutrix is involved in lodging of

 honey trap cases. 4. Learned Public Prosecutor and counsel for the complainant

 have opposed the appeal. 5. I have considered the contentions. 6. Considering the contentions put-forth by the counsel for the

 appellant, I deem it proper to allow the appeal. 7. The order dated 16.01.2021 is set aside and the appeal is

 allowed and the appellant is directed to be released on bail

 provided he furnishes a personal bond of Rs. 1,00,000/- (Rupees

 One Lakh only) and two sureties in the sum of Rs.50,000/-

 (Rupees Fifty thousand only) each to the satisfaction of the

 learned trial court with the stipulation to appear before that Court

 and any court to which the matter is transferred, on all dates of

 hearing and as and when called upon to do so. 8. The fact that prosecutrix is invloved in honey trap cases,

 should be brought to the notice of the Police Authorities to take

 appropriate action. 9. However, if the documents produced by counsel for the

 appellant are found to be fake or are not pertaining to the

 prosecutrix, complainant-respondent would be free to move

 applicaton for cancellation of the bail. (PANKAJ BHANDARI),J
