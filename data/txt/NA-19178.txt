Here's what I feel about the matter:

1) Privacy concerns - Honestly, I think that we are already being tracked. If govt really went after us, they can today trace all my life events by bank transactions / tickets bought on Passport etc &amp; my phone's GPS &amp; sim signals can be used to track my location history.

2) Giving a switch to turn me off completely: Once again, even today if govt declares you anti-national, you are screwed. 

They will put a phony charge on you &amp; you are in jail. And then what am I going to do with saving my passport, bank accounts etc. which I cannot operate in any case.

3) If I was a leader &amp; I need to track whether all the right people have got the aids, then I think having an ID for them which has their Bank accounts linked, from which I can check whether the amount was credited or not is probably the way I would implement it, which is essentially aadhaar.

4) Identity theft - I have all my life submitted photocopies of my passport / driving license for Sim card, bank accounts etc. I am certain all my info has already been collected and stored in some DB already.

A positive use case for Aadhar:

A mismatch in the income declared in ITR &amp; number of phones, airlines trips, cars etc linked to your Aadhar, can help in tracking those who are evading taxes.

I know that the system is not perfect, but I think that this does seem like to way to move forward, to bring visibility to leaks &amp; defaulters.
