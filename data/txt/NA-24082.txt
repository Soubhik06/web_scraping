Here's my [previous post](https://np.reddit.com/r/bangalore/comments/zo1wez/fraud_by_packers_and_movers_company/).

I can give you a TL;DR for it if you haven't read it. I moved from Indore to Blr. Hired a local Packers and Movers who supposedly was from Indore. My belongings never reached me. The owner of the company kept on ignoring my texts.

So this is what happened subsequently: My cousin who is in Indore tried to reach the office address that was on the bill. To his surprise, there was no such address in Indore. (Reminds me of the scene from Phir Hera Pheri 😭). At that moment I knew I messed up!

I got scared. I tried to reach out to [https://www.thepackersmovers.com/](https://www.thepackersmovers.com/) support. This site is where I found the company. They didn't help me. I saw it being mentioned on the site that they verify each and every company via address proof and Pan Card. So, I asked if they could just help me in reaching out to the scammer. They instead replied that they don't verify each of the companies. Also, scolded me for no reason and never took any action. Till today that company is listed on their website.

I told my father and he started to contact MP Police. Meanwhile, I started my own research. I realized that this company was registered in the name of one person who operates from Rajasthan. I got to know that he sent my goods to the godown of a transport company named VExpress in Indore only.

I thought maybe my stuff was sent via VExpress. I needed the tracking number to track where it is right now. I tried various combinations of tracking numbers on the VExpress website's tracking dashboard in case I can get information. It didn't work. Wanted to have a Hacker moment :(

The Police didn't help much. They kept delaying their investigation.

More than a month passed since I moved to Bangalore...

One day I got a text from the P&amp;M guy: "Hey, your belongings are still in Indore in Godown of VExpress. Pay them Rs 5400 so that they can proceed. Meanwhile, I will contact the head office of my company and get your money refunded". 
Awesome 👏. So professional for a company that is run by just one person.

I immediately contacted my cousin and my father. My cousin told me to not take any action myself. He decided to take control of the situation. A lot of things happened after this.

In the end, my cousin impersonated the police(On a phone call), threatened the guy, and got him to pay for the transport.

After almost 1.5 weeks after that. I got back everything. Yeah!!!

The table I got back was damaged though. Nothing can be done now(Even though I had also paid for the insurance🥲). At least I got back the rest of the things in good condition.

Well, Thanks for reading through all this. Just do your due diligence in hiring anyone. Don't fall for cheaper prices randomly. Do your homework. ✌️
