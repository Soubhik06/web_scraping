--- POST TEXT ---

I know airlines are notoriously anti-consumer, but this is bizarre. A friend of mine booked a flight from Bangalore to Delhi with SpiceJet, which got cancelled. She received a call from a representative informing her of the cancellation and asking if she wanted an alternative ticket. The representative shared a link for available alternative tickets, and she booked one.

This morning, when she reached the airport, she was told she couldn’t board the plane because she had supposedly talked to a representative and got her ticket cancelled, and the amount was to be refunded. She received no email or text confirmation, no phone call confirming the cancellation. The representative she talked to at the airport refused to accommodate her, saying that she gave verbal confirmation and they could only help if the refund had not been initiated, which is ridiculous. When asked if they could confirm the call recording of the cancellation, she was told they don’t keep call recordings.

No one listened to her, no matter how much she cried, shouted, or tried to talk sense. Now she’s left with wasted time from having to go to the airport and more wasted money as ticket prices have now soared to 15k.

My question is, how can they do this? Are they saying I'm so dumb that I asked to cancel the flight on a call, received a link for alternate flights, and then went ahead and booked one? The whole thing feels like a scam. Has anyone else gone through this? What could she have done?
