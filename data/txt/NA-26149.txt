Hey guys, as i was selling my extra tickets here.. i feel like its my responsibility to inform everyone to be aware of scams, specially now.

The process of getting the concert tickets was -
1. You purchase a ticket from BookMyShow
2. You get a QR code for getting the ticket.
3. You had to go to a Croma Store in Chennai (5 different Croma Store options were available) from 1st March - 4th March. There you could show the QR code and get the handbands for entry into concert.

Now the croma counters for this are CLOSED. You cant do anything with the QR Codes. 

If you see anyone trying to sell tickets ONLINE, its fake! So beware.

Only way of getting ticket now is to meet the seller PHYSICALLY and get the handband.

Thanks alot and whoever is going to concert, have a nice time!

Edit - apparently u can still go and stand in a line at the concert entry and get it. But its still suggested to be careful. Specially because the concert is in Mahabalipuram which is 60kms away from Center.
