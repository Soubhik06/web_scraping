Hey, Reddit community,

I wanted to share a distressing incident that happened to my friend recently involving an app called Solar Shop or Canadian Solar. It's essential to spread the word and raise awareness so that others don't fall victim to this scam.

My friend was lured into investing his hard-earned money in this app with the promise of daily returns on his investments. Excited about the opportunity, he invested 22 thousand rupees into what he believed to be a legitimate investment opportunity.

Initially, things seemed promising as he received returns for the first 7-8 days. However, to his dismay, the app suddenly stopped providing any returns altogether. It turns out that the company he invested in was nothing more than a fake scam organization, deceitfully using the name of a well-known and trusted company called Canadian Solar.

The scam took an even more deceptive turn with the presence of a group where only the admin could message. The admin of the group used edited photos to further enhance the trust of unsuspecting investors. These manipulated images portrayed a successful and trustworthy company, leading people to believe in the legitimacy of Solar Shop or Canadian Solar.

What's most disheartening is that this scam seems to have targeted numerous Indian individuals, tricking them into investing their money. It's clear that the scammers exploited the reputation and trust associated with Canadian Solar to gain people's confidence before running off with their funds.
