Airtel has been running this irritating scam for some time. It used to happen about once a year. The SIM will mysteriously lose its signal and will get deactivated for no apparent reason. Initially, I gave them the benefit of doubt, but then I started noticing the pattern. 

The events were regular within 1-2 month of every successive year, and I had to pay for the new sim. KYC was on record so no additional hassle. They would then try to coax me to get the postpaid plan every time I visited the stores for this purpose. 

It is about to be two years now, that this has not happened during this pandemic. Do my fellow Indians have a similar experience with Airtel/other networks?
