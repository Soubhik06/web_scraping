I am writing to seek advice regarding a potential legal threat. I am an 18-year-old male high school student residing in Whitefield. During a conversation with a friend involved with NGOs addressing child abuse and other societal issues in Delhi, the topic of online "home esc*rt" services arose. These services are widely known to be scams, often employing heavily photoshopped pictures of women to attract customers with unrealistically low rates. Having learned about these through online platforms like Reddit and Quora (without ever engaging with them myself), I decided to investigate one such service out of curiosity.

I contacted the service through a website that redirected me to a WhatsApp profile. Mimicking a typical customer, I inquired about their esc*rts. As expected, they sent me pictures of women who appeared to be high-fashion models, further solidifying my suspicions about the scam. After asking a few additional questions, I blocked all communication, believing the interaction to be over.

However, 24 hours later, I received a call in Hindi from a man claiming to be associated with the service. He threatened legal action against me, alleging that my inquiries violated the privacy of the women in the photos he had sent. Despite immediately blocking his calls and reporting his messages on WhatsApp, he continued to harass me, even managing to learn my name, possibly through Truecaller.

While I am fairly certain this is a bluff, given the tone of the claims and the overall nature of the interaction, I am still seeking reassurance due to my age and the potential for any dangerous consequences. I want to make sure that I had no malicious intent and the offered prices were far beyond my means. As a result of my actions, I am now concerned about potential legal or non-legal repercussions.

I am open to any suggestions you may have in light of this situation.
