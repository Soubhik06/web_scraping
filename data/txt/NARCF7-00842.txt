I’ve been a victim of this scam.

Here is how it works the man behind the counter will give you a false chip token which is expired (3.5 hours is the deadline for any token) and when you try to pass through the gate it will not detect in the machine and upon confronting him he will allow you to use the emergency gate which is right next to the counter, the problem comes when you will reach to your destination and the chip won’t work and then again you have ask the man behind the counter to let you pass through but he will either deny you or ask you to pay the same amount because the token is not functioning. 

I realised about this scam when I reached my destination and the man was not allowing me to pass and on above he was scolding me for getting the token without slip which makes me wonder whether the slip was mandatory or not because nobody gives slip while with token then I realise he was trying to just pressurise me into giving him money to let me pass. I refused and went on to complain in the control room and then from far he called me back and let me pass whiteout any hustle. 

So guys never loose your dignity and never doubt your innocence if something similar happens to you in future just go to the control room and rip the metro staff from their job. Write a complain letter and make a video and share here. 

Have fun!
