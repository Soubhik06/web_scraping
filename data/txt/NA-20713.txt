I have been a fan of Speedpost for long time. Even after it's sarkari service, it delivers stuff to me in less time than Blue Dart takes. It's much much much cheaper too.

But, recently I am noticing that Speedpost is being shaddy with their tracking records. A few months ago I was sent a letter by a govt department. I had the tracking number and it showed it was delivered. But nobody came to deliver it. I was there the whole time. The post man actually attempted delivery 3 days later. 

I ignored it, I considered it as a case of a small post office trying to fake its performance record.

But just the previous week I had a similar experience. And this time it was a post office in metropolitan city. I was being told by their tracking system that my item has already been delivered. But it was not at all true. if someone had come to deliver my stuff, the security guards would know. It was something important so I was worried. But it was only three days later when they actually delivered my speedpost article.

Another weird thing they have been doing with their tracking record is that they immediately flush the tracking number a next day or by the second day of delivery. As far as I remember, earlier tracking numbers used to be flushed after 15 days or 30 days.

I don't know if it's some large scale shaddy shit or just two different events in isolation.

anyone experienced the same recently ?
