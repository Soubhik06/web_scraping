What did I want to buy: Macbook Air M1 Silver 

What happened : (**TL:DR at the end**)

I am one of those "techies" whose work laptop has crazy restrictions because "fintech". Cannot visit youtube, cannot install discord, basically cannot do shit. 

My 3 year old Asus personal laptop had r/agedlikemilk to the extent that I felt like wanting to stick my finger in the processor to make it run faster.

After many months of fighting my miserly instincts(thanks poverty-filled upbringing), I decided that I was fucking up my career progress and personal life by not having a decent piece of hardware to use.

I woke up feeling like "Today is the day". In the afternoon I decided to go ahead with the purchase. (Macbook Air M1 Silver, incase you forgot). Is it costly as fuck? Yes! but I wanted something that would last me for years.

I went to the product page, selected right color, clicked on "Order It", made payment and congratulated myself for the purchase. Couple of seconds later, I am seeing the orders page and am horrified. 

Horrified because I had selected "silver color" and the orders page was showing "Space grey". And this text is in very small size and barely noticeable. Very easy to miss. Had I missed seeing this, Flipkart would have delivered the product tomorrow and I would have been stuck with a 90k worth laptop with a color I do not even like. They wouldn't have replaced the product because their orders page would have matched the delivered product, even when I technically did not opt for.

Since this whole thing was strange, I thought I had made a mistake while placing the order. I thought I selected the wrong color. Naive me. I contacted CC and they said they cannot help me and I was forced to cancel the order. Now I did not have a laptop on the way, nor the 90k in my bank. 

By evening I got desperate and thought why wait for the refund while I still have money in account to place a fresh order. BIG MISTAKE.

This time I made sure I selected the right color before placing the order. And baam! Flipkart fucks up again. Again the orders page says "Space grey". I cursed my luck for a few minutes before contacting the CC again. 

The executive says she cannot help. She said "It is impossible for a website to select the wrong product". I asked to be connected to her supervisor to seek more clarification. I was kept on hold for 5 minutes and then disconnected. 

I call this an *unintentional* scam because had I not gone through the order again, I would have missed seeing the color. The product would have been delivered to me by 11am tomorrow and I would not be eligible for Replacement as the delivered product would match the details on orders page. I repeat again, I am not saying this was done on purpose. There is no proof of this. But CC not acknowledging it and not putting me in touch with a superior and ensuring that this does not cause huge losses to other customer, in my opinion, comes very close to shady tactics and unethical behavior.

Whatever has happened to Flipkart? It used to be such a great place to shop. Even after Amazon came, I stuck to Flipkart for most of my purchases. Their service is shitty. Their UI is shittier. 

Never going back to Flipkart again, not even for a brick to bang my head on. 

I have sent an email to the CEO. Let me see what comes of it. Will post an update if anything worth mentioning, is done. I obviously am not expecting the kind of response that one gets when a grievance is sent to Jeff Bezos.

**TL:DR**

**Ordered a Silver coloured Macbook. Flipkart generated order for SpaceGrey Macbook due to technical glitch. Blamed me for it, twice nonetheless. Had I not noticed the color written in small fontsize, I would have been stuck with 90k piece of hardware in the color that I don't like(no replacement because product received and order page would match). Both orders are now cancelled and Rs 1.8L refund is in processing state. FML**
