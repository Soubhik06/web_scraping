Stages of a pandemic -
1. Cases come into the country from most affected countries (STAGE 1)
2. Local transmission from positive cases (STAGE 2)
3. Disease spreads in community, large groups of people and areas will be affected (STAGE 3)
4. Disease takes shape of an epidemic with no clear end point. Only vaccines can save the situation. (STAGE 4)
***
Telangana is at [stage 3](https://twitter.com/XpressHyderabad/status/1286273084710772743?s=19) where contact tracing is impossible, so you will not know how or when to get tested. Next 1 month will be crucial as he said, so there will be rapid growth in cases as well as number deaths(don't just believe the official govt numbers).
***
• Don't go out. Only if it's an emergency follow all the sanitisation process and wear a mask that closes your nose and mouth.
• Since online deliveries are there opt for them as much as you can.
• Don't follow fake news from the WhatsApp forwards.
