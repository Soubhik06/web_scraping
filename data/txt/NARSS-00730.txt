Hello guys!
Today i avoided an olx scam. I uploaded a furniture ad today and a guy 'too good to be true' called. 
He asked me to send my location and told me he will send someone in the evening. He said he was from 'Jindal Furniture house'. 
He told me he will send the money over paytm right away. I told him to come and see the product and later pay on the spot. However, he refused. Looking at his eagerness, I knew he was a scammer. He even asked me to mark the ad as sold and not sell it to anyone. 
I gave him my brother's paytm number (who has just 1k in his account) and hung up the phone stating "mujhe pata h tum kya kr rhe ho". 
Later, he called my brother and said ki aapke bhai se baat hui thi kya. My brother acted all innocent that he didnt know anything. Later he sent him a 'Split Pay' to my brother for Rs. 30,000. And very 'cleverly' wrote in comments - "30,000 credit" 
We read it and laughed amongst ourselves and tried to test him more. 
We wasted his 1 hour and delayed the process acting like the old people from scam youtube videos. 
Later, he lost his cool and started abusing. Me being a valorant player hurled 1000s and 1000s of abuses at him in one breath xD 
Now we have blocked that guy and reminiscing the encounter.
