My roommate (23, F) just became a victim of a credit card scam. Today at around 4'o clock, she received a call from a scammer, Truecaller ID showed it was a call from ICICI Credit Cards. The guy posed as ICICI customer support person and told that her credit card had 2600 monthly charges and she needed to open imobile app and follow certain steps.
My friend hadn't activated her credit card at the time of receiving this call and told him this.

The scammer somehow convinced her to download an apk on her mobile which she did after disconnecting the call. However, she did not enter any details as she was not aware about her card number, CVV etc. She didn't even share any otp or other personal details with the scammer.

At around 8:40pm, she received two OTPs for 79k and 80k approximately and later a mail confirming that transactions had been done from her card.
She had reported about this fraud transactions to the bank within 20 minutes and got her card blocked. FIR has been filed. 

Is there anything she can do to get the money back? Can the bank be held responsible for this?
