I recently booked a room in Thoraipakkam using the OYO app. Paid the exact amount online, but when I reached the room, the receptionist dropped a bombshell – the AC wasn't working. Despite booking an AC room, I was told to move to another place in the same street, with an extra charge of 300 bucks! Two teen couples, probably college-goers, also faced the same issue and got diverted to another room. They ended up paying an extra 500! 🙃 They shared their frustration with me on the same day.

Asked for a refund, got a vague response. Tried OYO customer care, and guess what? They were as unhelpful as it gets. This happened in June, and chatting with a friend later, I found out he faced a similar OYO headache.

Has anyone else gone through this scam? 😤
