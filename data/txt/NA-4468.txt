CORAM : HON'BLE MR. JUSTICE SUBHASH CHAND -------- This bail application has been filed on behalf of the applicant -Umesh Kumar
 Verma @ Umesh Verma with a prayer to release on bail in connection with Cyber Case
 No. 73 of 2021, arising out of Katras P.S. Case No. 315 of 2021, for the offences
 registered under Sections, 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 120-B of the Indian Penal Code read
 with Sections 66 (C) / 66 (D) of Information Technology Act, pending in the court of the
 Additional Sessions Judge-II-cum-Special Judge (Cybercrime Cases & Electricity Cases),
 Dhanbad. Learned Counsel for the applicant has submitted that the F.I.R. of this case
 was lodged by the informant against 3 named accused persons including the applicant
 with these allegations that the accused were involved in committing cybercrime and from
 possession of the applicant one Redmi 9A mobile with SIM cards, one A.T.M. Bank of
 India and one pass-book are alleged to be recovered. There is nothing on record to show
 that the applicant had committed any cybercrime against any person by using this
 recovered articles. No victim has been alleged in the whole F.I.R. against whom the
 cybercrime was committed by the applicant. The applicant has been languishing in jail
 since 22.10.2021. Learned A.P.P. appearing on behalf of the State vehemently opposed the
 contentions made by the learned Counsel for the applicant and contended that the
 applicant along with other co-accused had committed cybercrime. In view of the submissions made and materials on record, the bail
 application of the applicant is hereby allowed. Let the applicant be released on bail on
 furnishing bail bond of Rs. 20,000/-(Rupees Twenty Thousand) with two sureties of the
 like amount to the satisfaction of the court concerned in aforesaid case. (Subhash Chand, J.)
 P.K.S.
