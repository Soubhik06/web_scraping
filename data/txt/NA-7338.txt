Heard counsel for the parties. This application has been filed by the applicant who has
 been named as accused in connection with Crime No. 2/2014
 registered with S.T.F. Police Station, Bhopal commonly known
 as VYAPAM scam cases. The role ascribed to the applicant is that of a beneficiary
 as the applicant took assistance of impersonator during the
 Police Constable Examination, 2013 conducted by VYAPAM in
 the year 2013. The applicant was assisted by the impersonator
 Roshan Singh who was arranged by racketeer Hemraj Yadav
 on payment of Rupees two lacs. It is not in dispute that charge-sheet has been filed
 against the applicant in connection with the said crime. The
 applicant is not named as accused in any other crime. Further,
 the racketeer, as well as, the impersonator have been
 arrested. In other words, custody of the applicant is no more
 required in connection with the said crime, as of now. Accordingly, the applicant is entitled for parity along with
 other beneficiaries who have been granted bail by this Court in
 similar type of cases. As a result, in the interest of justice, we are inclined to
 grant bail to the applicant as the applicant assures through
 counsel that he will extend full co-operation to the Investigating
 Officer, if and when required in future in connection with the
 crime in question, as well as, make appearance before the trial
 Court punctually. The applicant is ordered to be released on
 bail in connection with Crime No.2/2014 on following
 conditions: The bail application stands disposed of accordingly.
