Dear Reddit Community,

I hope this post finds you well. I am writing to share a distressing experience I've had with Nexgen Systems, and I believe it's crucial to alert fellow Redditors about the potential risks involved.

Back on June 9th, 2023, I ordered a system from Nexgen Systems, Poojappura- Trivandrum and paid in advance. After encountering issues, they promised to refund the money within 3 months. After too much effort on my part, they sent a promissory note. Following additional persistence, a cheque from IndusInd Bank was supposedly submitted on November 20th, assuring the resolution of the matter.

However, my hopes were shattered when the cheque they provided bounced. This turn of events not only raises serious questions about Nexgen Systems' credibility but also leaves me concerned about the legitimacy of their operations.

I felt it was my responsibility to share this experience, not only to vent my frustration but to serve as a warning to others. If anyone else has faced similar issues with Nexgen Systems or has advice on how to proceed, please share your insights.

Let's support each other and stay vigilant against potential scams.
