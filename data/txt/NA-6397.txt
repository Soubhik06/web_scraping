 Heard both the sides. The petitioner- Mukesh Kumar Mandal is in custody since
 31.08.2022 in connection with Cyber Crime P.S. Case No. 52 of 2022 for the
 offence registered under Sections 414 , 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 120B of the
 I.P.C., Sections 66(B) , 66(C) , 66(D) of Information Technology Act pending
 in the Court of learned Additional Sessions Judge 1st, Jamtara. It is submitted by the learned counsel on behalf of petitioner that the
 offence alleged will not be made out as only allegation against him is
 regarding seizure of three mobile sets but there is no material whatsoever to
 connect him to any cybercrime. Learned A.P.P. opposed the prayer for bail. It is submitted that the
 raid was conducted in the present case on the basis of complaint received from
 Ramchandar Singh regarding cyber fraud of Rs.64,996/- On raid, this
 petitioner was apprehended with mobile phones which had suspicious
 e-wallets. Under the circumstances, the above named petitioner is directed to be
 released on bail on furnishing bail bond of Rs.25,000/-(Twenty Five
 Thousand) with two sureties of the like amount each to the satisfaction of the
 Court below. (Gautam Kumar Choudhary, J.)
