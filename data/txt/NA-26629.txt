I was shocked to discover a website called ginicapitals.com that claims to offer trading services with unbelievable returns. The website is full of red flags - no registration numbers, fake testimonials, and a Gmail ID in the Contact Us section. But the worst part is that when I clicked on "Trade with us", the site redirected me to smcmoneywise - an apparently reputed online brokerage platform

How can these firms allow such a scam to use their name and reputation? This is a clear case of misleading and cheating the retail investors who trust these brokerage houses for their trading needs. I was utterly shocked to discover this unethical practice.

Do you guys know any more such fraudulent means of advertisement?
