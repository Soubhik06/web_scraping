My friend got scammed by roughly 30k on their HDFC credit card. I know it sounds really naive but They were asked to screenshare while the scammer was assisting through the site and payments. The scammer initiated a payment and got hold of the OTP through the popup. My friend didn't even get a chance to react and the money was deducted.

Please help what should be done in this scenario. Honestly we are quite worried and unsure about what to do.

.

Update: Filed a complaint with cyber cell and the refund request with the bank

Also please be aware everyone, there is another kind of potential scam going on. This dude, after reading this post, dmed me from a throwaway account, he assured me that my money is safe and to contact nfxcyber insta account to get help. I was about to contact it when I searched about it on reddit itself. And found out that it's a scam on top of scam. So well hopefully this post helps anyone stay away from screen share scams and from online scams like nfxcyber too.
