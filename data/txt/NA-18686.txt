Hi all, 

I'm raising awareness about [**https://www.93mobiles.com/**](https://www.93mobiles.com/) **scam**.

 
I recently purchased a hard disk since it was on discount on this site, worth 4500/-. I did not receive any updates over SMS / email / WhatsApp. When I tried contacting their custom care number through WhatsApp and call, but there was no reply, and even the number is invalid (as per operators).

The second grievance number displayed on their [Grievance Officer - 93Mobiles](https://www.93mobiles.com/grievance-officer/) page, is of Myntra and when I looked at Myntra's grievance page, I realized that it's definitely a scam, since they copied Myntra's grievance page as it is.
