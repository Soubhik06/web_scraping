I was intrigued by [this](https://www.reddit.com/r/india/comments/81x86t/is_this_fake/) post just now. It links to a guy selling PS4+games for 15k. 

____________________ 

What you should do in this case is right click on the image of the product the guy is selling and *Copy Image Address*.

Then you go to google image search(images.googe), and search via an image. Here you can paste the link that you copied and it'll show you where on the internet the same image is being used. 

If you're getting multiple results on different websites, then it is probably a stolen image and you **are** being scammed. If not, it's probably hand-clicked and lesser chance of it being a scam.

Hope this helps!
