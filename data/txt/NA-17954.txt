A long-term family friend got scammed yesterday night at around 9 pm for 5lakh rupees.

Lo and behold soon the scammers transferred 5lakh from his account.

He has already:
1. Talked to a local SBI branch officer leaving near his home 
2. Filed an FIR to local police
3. Is filing a complaint to the cybersecurity branch
