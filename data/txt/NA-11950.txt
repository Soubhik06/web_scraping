 services rendered by the plaintiff as well as the interest thereon. 3. Defendant was served by publication in 'The Statesman' dated

 plaintiff, the suit ultimately resulted in judgment and decree dated 
 03.10.12, decreeing the suit of the plaintiff. 4. The application has been filed through Mr. Kapil Verma, one of the

 MB Road, New Delhi for execution of the decree. The application has 
 been moved on the ground that in December 2009, defendant M/s. 
 Sanka India Ltd. was not operating from F­322A, Lado Sarai, New Delhi

 F­320, Lado Sarai, Old MB Road, New Delhi. Further, it is stated that
