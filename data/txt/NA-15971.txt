Hey everyone.

I received messages from Citi-Bank about some transactions which i have NOT done. These are the following transactions whose messages i received-

1) INR 50 By "GOOGLE*Services".

2) INR 26,000 By "GOOGLE*Octro Inc".

3) INR 26,000 By "GOOGLE*Octro Inc".

I have no idea what Octro means. Upon research i found it to be [this](http://www.octro.com/). I have never played their games or ever even opened their sites. I immediately called my bank and blocked my card. The bank customer care lady just told me they will look into the transactions and created some dispute resolution complaint. 

Upon searching about this fraud i found out the exact same thing happen to literally HUNDREDS of people in India and its always the same type of transaction. See this-

https://www.complaintboard.in/complaints-reviews/google-octro-inc-l633943.html

https://www.complaintboard.in/complaints-reviews/google-octro-inc-l647255.html (This person also has lost the same amount at the same day as mine.)

https://complaintwire.org/complaint/XZ7JebY36BA/google-octro-inc

I have not even added my credit card number to the Google Play Store. I have seen shit ton of videos about scams, Atm skimmers and other things and I am very very careful when ever I use my card. The bank lady also informed me that it was an International Transaction. All this happened at around 1 AM IST when i was fast asleep.

Also tried to contact google about this. Got this link to report unauthorized charges. After submitting the form, it said some google representative will contact me in 1-2 days.

https://support.google.com/googleplay/contact/play_unrecognized_charge

Does anyone have any idea what this is all about? What more should i do now? And how do i proceed to recover this amount? Please help. Thank you.

Edit 1: Thanks everyone for the valuable inputs. So far the citi-bank has acknowledged that the money debited has not been transferred to the merchant and it will be refunded after an investigation. I asked around a bit in the Cyber Police department and was shocked to know that such fraudulent transactions are widespread and happen on a daily basis. Anyway, be careful guys. This shit can happen to anyone. Adopt the best security practices always. Prevention is better than cure.
