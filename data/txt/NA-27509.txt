--- POST TEXT ---

I get my daily dose of scam/phishing emails like everyone else and my usual go to move is 1: Mark as read. 2: Delete. 3: Completely forget about it. However, it could be I had too much time on my hands, another creeping episode of mania or just good old procrastination, but today I decided to take a bit of a closer look, and it was strange...

An email notification popped up from "Just Eat" with "Your Broaster Hut Order wil..." and I get the strange urge to open it (before anyone says anything, I NEVER open any links etc, I merely look at the email and hover over things). Here it is:

&amp;#x200B;

https://preview.redd.it/699j53wxy8a71.png?width=1012&amp;format=png&amp;auto=webp&amp;s=526b2cdad7f2f22922ce832058093abf7a1cacf5

In case there was a chance I might think it was legit, I see straight away that it's not from a Just Eat email address. I looked up the delivery address too and it's a clothing store that imports clothes from India to resell (not sure if there's a possible connection or not). I find it hard to think that this person has anything to do with the scam and I suspect that they are a victim themselves who's had an old email address stolen for this purpose:

&amp;#x200B;

[I'm not sure why they made no attempt whatsoever with the email address here...](https://preview.redd.it/j178myjxv8a71.png?width=632&amp;format=png&amp;auto=webp&amp;s=abb602458bc6c1f3658f02b2ef09ad8ca6844d67)

The main email body isn't the worst I've seen and the spelling and grammar was much better than most of these. This is usually attributed to English being a second language and/or terrible translations. I checked out the restaurant "Broaster Hut", and not only is this a real place but they even listed a real item from the menu with the price right lol! The attention to detail is above average for a scam email which is why I'm so surprised by the big mistakes they made...

What's with this URL?:

&amp;#x200B;

[www.dirtyclean.in eh??](https://preview.redd.it/5zbkkh3vw8a71.png?width=798&amp;format=png&amp;auto=webp&amp;s=87555a3375a923774960a53f7bfb9d9ab81e0a09)

Now, I'm clueless when it comes to anything beyond using Google, sending emails and checking social media so the website name in the URL looked like a possible dodgy site to me. However, after checking that out I found this:

&amp;#x200B;

[A bloody car detailing website for a company in Mumbai...](https://preview.redd.it/fqr621zcx8a71.png?width=1366&amp;format=png&amp;auto=webp&amp;s=a32fa321bcb4484a02f86f639e35b1a2cadddf71)

I can't find any connection between the name in the senders email address, Just Eat or the detailing company. Would this link send you to this website? Is this company just a victim of these scammers too with no clue that their website is being used for this? It all seems very bizarre and because I don't follow links they give, I tried replying to their email asking if I could just give my card details over the phone for a refund because the link doesn't work. And even though there's more chance of hell freezing over than there is that I'll get some sort of reply, if I do I will post an update because I've got questions dammit!

* Who are they?
* Where are they based?
* Does this actually work on people?
* Who orders **15** Fillet Burger Meals???
* Where did they get my email address from?

On a serious note, I know that there are many people out there that don't know what to look for when spotting fakes like this and I guess that Just Eat is as good a target as any for them to hit with a lot of people saving their debit/credit card details on their accounts. I sincerely hope that nobody has fallen for this and I'm curious to know if anyone else has had this email sent to them. Is there any way to get back at these scumbags because I hate what they do and they take money from people like myself that just don't have money to lose and it's just not right! Thanks for reading and any tips or further info would be awesome!
