This is an appeal by the revenue against the order dated 16/11/2009 of CIT(A) 38, Mumbai relating to assessment year 2005-06. The grounds of appeal raised by the revenue read as follows: 2.	During the scrutiny assessment proceedings of the Assessee for A.Y.05-06, the AO, noticed that the assessee had received an amount of loan of Rs.9,36,038/- from M/s. Nakshatra Software Pvt. Ltd., an amount of loan of Rs.37,496/- from M/s. K.N.Parekh HUF, an amount of loan of Rs. 21,000/- from Kartik K. Parekh and an amount of loan of Rs. 24,000/- from Shri Jayant N Parekh otherwise than by Account Payee Cheque or Draft in violation of section 269SS . Accordingly, penalty of Rs.10,18,534/- was levied. The Assessees explanation was that aforesaid transaction of loan were not made in cash but through journal entries and therefore, this did not amount to contravention of provision of Section 269SS . This was not accepted by the AO, who referred to the Joint Parliamentary Committee's report on the Stock Market Scam and held that all these transactions were made with an object to evade tax in various hands. Since the aforesaid transactions of loan were not made through account payee cheque or draft, the AO held that penalty u/s.271D was leviable. 3. Parekh: 6. As the facts existing in the present case were identical, following the aforesaid ITAT order, the penalty levied u/s. 271D was cancelled by CIT(A). Aggrieved by the order of the CIT(A), the revenue has preferred the present appeal before the Tribunal. 7.	Before us the ld. D.R relied on the order of the Assessing Officer. We are of the view that in view of the decision of the ITAT referred to by the CIT(A), the penalty was rightly deleted by the CIT(A). Admittedly the transaction in question were made through journal entries and in such circumstances the penalty provisions are not attracted as laid down by the Tribunal in several decisions. A copy of the order of the Tribunal "E" Bench Mumbai in ITA No.6079&6080/M/09 for A.Y 2005-06 in the case of M/s. Saimangal Investrade Ltd. vs. ACIT, order dated 13/8/2010 was also filed. Respectfully following the decisions of the co-ordinate bench on identical facts, we uphold the order of the CIT(A) and dismiss the appeal of the revenue. 8.	In the result, the appeal of the revenue is dismissed.
