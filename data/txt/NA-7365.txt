Heard counsel for the parties. These applications can be disposed of by this common
order. These bail applications have been filed by the applicants,
who have been arrested and are in Jail in connection with
Crime No.17/2014 registered with STF Police Station, Bhopal
for the offences punishable under Sections 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 and 120-B of I.P.C. read with Sections 65 & 66 of the
Information Technology Act and also under Section 3 (D) 1 , 2 / 4 of the Madhya Pradesh Manyata Prapt Pariksha
Adhiniyam, 1937 - commonly known as VYAPAM
examination scam cases. The prosecution case is that Pradeep Rawat had
appeared in examination conducted by VYAPAM in 2012 for
the post of Jail Prahari (Jail Guard). He had contacted one

Munesh Rawat to provide him impersonator to appear in the
examination. The said Munesh Rawat interacted with Nitin
Upadhyay, who agreed to impersonate Pradeep Rawat during
examination. As a result, these applicants were liable to be
proceeded for having conspired to commit unfair means
during the examination conducted by VYAPAM. The applicants have been arrested quite some time
back. Admittedly, charge-sheet has been filed against the
applicants on 9.12.2014. It is fairly accepted by the counsel
for the State, on instructions, that the presence of the
applicants may not be required for further investigation of
the case and more particularly by way of custodial
interrogation. Since the charge-sheet has already been filed and for
the stand taken by the respondent-State, as recorded above, it
presupposes that the investigation against the applicants is
complete in all respects. Further, the applicants are not
involved in any other offence of similar type. Besides, the
applicants through their counsel undertake to extend full
cooperation for further investigation of the case to the
Investigating Officer if and when required including against
them. In addition, the applicants will punctually appear
before the Trial Court to ensure early disposal of the criminal
case and that they would abide by all the conditions strictly
as may be imposed by this Court. MCRC No. 20565/2014 MCRC No. 20884/2014 & MCRC No. 422/2015
