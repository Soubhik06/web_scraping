I'm receiving new comments and DMs every day from people responding to my [last post](https://new.reddit.com/r/india/comments/1h4nt68/someone_keeps_ordering_under_my_name_and_address/) about this issue from 10 days ago. Many have reported the scam, yet it’s still going strong. The sad reality is, once your money is gone, there’s nothing I can do to help. 

There’s a new scam spreading fast through Delhivery deliveries. Random COD parcels are being sent to unsuspecting people, often under fake brand names like "Sanghvi Beauty and Technologie" or "AMATEURS SURFACE." The items are often around ₹700 – like creams or small goods – which makes it easy for people to mistake them as legitimate orders.

What’s happening is that scammers are using stolen personal data – names, addresses, phone numbers – to place fake orders. These parcels get delivered without any prior notice or tracking updates, only an OTP at the time of delivery. Many people, especially those who are working, don’t check or track these orders. When the delivery arrives, family members (brothers, sisters, parents) unknowingly accept the parcels, assuming they’re from someone in the house.

The real danger is that once these COD orders are paid for, there’s no way to reverse the transaction. The sellers disappear, and there’s no refund option. Delhivery could easily stop this by suspending these fraudulent sellers and brands, but the fact that they continue to deliver these parcels shows they are either turning a blind eye or profiting from the scam.

If you receive random deliveries, especially ones with no prior notice, **do not accept them**. Always double-check with your family before paying for any unrecognized orders. This scam is growing, and Delhivery isn't taking action. 

If anyone has a Twitter or Instagram account, or knows any influencers who can help spread the word, please share this issue and the link here. The more people we can warn, the better chance we have of stopping others from falling victim to this scam. Let’s work together to put an end to it!
