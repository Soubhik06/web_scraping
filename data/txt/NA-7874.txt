CORAM: HON'BLE MR. JUSTICE GAUTAM KUMAR CHOUDHARY

 For the Petitioner : Mr. Rajesh Kumar Singh, Advocate
 For the State : Mr. Bishambhar Shastri, A.P.P. ------ Order No.03 Dated : 25.01.2024
 Heard both the sides. The petitioner- Shahid Ansari is in custody since 26.07.2023 in
 connection with Pathalgadda P.S. Case No. 22 of 2023 for the offence
 registered under Sections 366 , 511 , 354 , 506 of the Indian Penal Code pending
 in the Court of learned Judicial Magistrate, 1st Class, Chatra. As per the F.I.R., the petitioner had contacted the informant by
 repeated mobile calls and thereafter, they were sometimes on talking terms. It
 is alleged that during course of video call, her indecent photographs were
 recorded and she was being pressurized and blackmailed on the basis of those
 video clips and threatened to kidnap her. It is submitted by the learned counsel on behalf of petitioner that the
 prosecutrix is a major girl and no offence under IT Act has been made in
 support of allegation of indecent photographs been captured during video call. Learned A.P.P. opposed the prayer for bail.
 Under the circumstances, the above named petitioner is directed to be
 released on bail on furnishing bail bond of Rs.25,000/-(Twenty Five
 Thousand) with two sureties of the like amount each to the satisfaction of the
 Court below. (Gautam Kumar Choudhary, J.)
