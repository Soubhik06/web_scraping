--- POST TEXT ---

I ordered some food this week worth 4k, and paid it via card. 10 minutes after the delivery, the agent returned claiming that there was some issue with his app, that he could not see any bill details of the order, and requested a photo of the order with the amount. Stupid me handed over my phone with the order details. Then he stated that due to an app error I might get a refund, and if I did, I would have to pay again.

A few hours later I received the refund amount notification on Swiggy for partial amount (because it was marked as bad food by the agent) and right on the dot received a call from an unknown number asking for payment. I said I would check and hung up the call. Since then I have received at least 10 missed calls and a few text messages from the same number.

There's more to the story here. Apparently this guy had taken a ride from a Rapido agent to deliver my food, and when the agent asked for money, he said to collect it from me. I only got to know this after the Rapido agent turned up at my doorstep the next day asking for payment.
Not only did the Swiggy agent cheat the restaurant of its money, he has cheated the Rapido agent of his fee as well.

Lesson learnt 🙄
