Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and

 learned APP for the State. 2. The petitioner seeks bail in Cyber P.S. case No.

 49 of 2023 instituted for the offences under Sections 419 , 420 , 379 , 34 of the Indian Penal Code and Sections 66(c) , 66(D) of the I.T. Act. 3. Prosecution allegation, in short, is that the

 petitioner along with co-accused persons were indulged in

 cyber fraud pertaining to opening of bank account. Several

 Aadhaar Cards, mobile phones and other documents were

 recovered from their possession. 4. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.16454 of 2024(2) dt.18-03-2024 2/3 the petitioner is innocent and has falsely been implicated in

 the present case merely on the basis of suspicion. No

 incriminating material has been recovered from the

 conscious possession of the petitioner. So far the recovery

 of ATM cards and PAN cards are concerned, the same

 belong to the petitioner. There is no allegation of

 withdrawing any money with said ATM cards. It is lastly

 submitted that there is no compliance of Section 100 of

 Cr.P.C. Co-accused has been granted bail by a Coordinate

 Bench of this Court vide order dated 13.03.2024 passed in

 Cr. Misc. No. 16906 of 2024. 5. Learned A.P.P. for the State has vehemently

 opposed the prayer for grant of bail to the petitioner. 6. Considering the aforesaid facts and

 circumstances of the case, clean antecedent of the petitioner,

 the period of custody undergone by the petitioner and the

 claim based on parity, this Court is inclined to grant bail to

 the petitioner. 7. Let the petitioner be released on bail on

 furnishing bail bonds of Rs.10,000/- (Ten thousand) with

 two sureties of the like amount each to the satisfaction of
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.16454 of 2024(2) dt.18-03-2024 3/3 Court below/concerned Court in connection with Cyber

 P.S. case No. 49 of 2023. (Rudra Prakash Mishra, J)
