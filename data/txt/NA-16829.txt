Hi everyone,

So I used to be a typical seedha-saadha bhola padhaku ladka (innocent &amp; studious kid) in my childhood and I was always the topper in my class throughout school. My sole focus was on studying, and I didn't pay much attention to worldly affairs. Unlike my classmates, I never talked about or knew much about things like cars, investing, business, or other career options so I kept feeling like I know nothing about this world but I could manage it later. Being an introvert and socially anxious person I didn't have many friends either, I was never good at sports and was obese and physically weak so that didn't help. (At that time I didn't worry about these things)

A string of events led me to this current situation. I scored 95% in my 10th standard and even got a girlfriend at that time. After the 10th standard, I started preparing for the NEET exam, so I didn't attend school and only did study at home (Online Coaching). This led to me to feeling lonely and getting even more anxiety issues.

Two months before my 12th board exam (Feb 2024) , my girlfriend emotionally cheated on me and then broke up with me. I kept begging her to stay (I found out about the cheating much later), but she blocked me everywhere, causing me to spiral into clinical depression. After seeing a psychiatrist and getting treatment and therapy I eventually overcame my depression.

During this period, I gave my 12th board exam and barely passed (60%). I only scored 370 in NEET, which is considered a pretty bad score especially this year. If I hadn't been depressed, I'm positive I would've scored at least 550+.

Now I'm at a point in life where I don't know what to do. I just watch anime and play games all day . As I mentioned, I wasn't involved in any worldly affairs, so I feel like I don't know anything about the world. I barely have any friends because everyone that even talked to me felt so fake like they are talking to me only for some benefits . My parents have also been quite cold to me as I disappointed them with my academic performance.

I still wanna appear for NEET next year, but I feel like I have no passion for the medical field especially because the pay isn't enough for work you have to do.

So what do you think I should do?
