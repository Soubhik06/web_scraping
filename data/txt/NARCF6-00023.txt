The practice of taking 'kharcha pani' when police does verification for passport is very prevelent all around India, the "kharcha pani" ranges from 200 to 5k. 

They have audacity to ask for it directly and even in main station. The process exist solely as a harassment now, seeing the progress in digital India, this process is very unnecessary. 

For those who have been asked for "kharcha pani". Refuse clearly or act like you didn't understand. 

They cannot reject your application for silly reasons and have to update passport office in max of 21 days. 

You can also file an RTI as well asking for valid reason. 

You can also write email to commissioner. 

Nothing to worry about. DO NOT PROMOTE THIS PRACTICE.

And always record all calls and conversations
