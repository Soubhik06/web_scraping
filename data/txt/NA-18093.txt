I want to warn fellow fragrance lovers about a website that claims to sell 100% genuine perfumes at super cheap prices. Unfortunately, my experience with this website has proven that their claims are completely false.

I've ordered three perfumes from them so far, and every single one of them turned out to be fake. The decants were of poor quality, and the full bottles were even worse. Here are my reviews of the products:

1. Jo Malone - Wood Sage and Sea Salt: This was my first purchase from the website, and I had never smelled the original before. While the scent was pleasant, it stung so badly upon application that I couldn't continue using it. I later found out that the scent and bottle were both fake when I tried the real thing at a physical store.
2. Dior - Homme Intense: This scent also stung upon application and smelled nothing like the original. Again, I later found out that the bottle and scent were both fake.
3. Chanel Chance (gift): This was a gift for a friend, but the bottle arrived damaged, and the scent barely lasted for 20 minutes. It was clear that it was not an original Chanel perfume.

Overall, this website is a complete sham, and I urge everyone to stay away from it. As the saying goes, if something seems too good to be true, it probably is. Genuine perfumes are sold at a premium price, and any seller offering them at significantly lower prices should be approached with caution.

I hope my experience serves as a warning to others and prevents them from falling into the same trap. Always do your due diligence before making a purchase, and if you're unsure, stick to reputable sellers or buy from duty-free stores. Don't make the same mistake I did and waste your hard-earned money on fake products.
