My company was contacted today by an awards company called 'Global Business Leaders Mag' asking for $2,500 to provide an award for up and coming businesses. I asked to jump on Zoom to clarify a few questions:

1. They claim they are in Atlanta, but they're in India
2. They show fake information about website stats and magazine readership
3. They have no individual presence on LinkedIn or Facebook (anyone tied to the company)
4. The gentleman who got on Zoom said he couldn't work his camera

If you're a small business, don't fall for this. Their website is [https://globalbusinessleadersmag.com/](https://globalbusinessleadersmag.com/).

\-- Edit --

They also claim they have been in business for a number of years. From the domain report in this thread, the domain was registered less than 2 years ago.
