Someone just added me to a Telegram group where people are being paid to post fake reviews on Google Maps. I knew it was a scam, but I was curious about when they would ask me for money. Surprisingly, they didn’t. So, I played along—and guess what? I actually received an initial "salary" of ₹200 for my "work."

The hard work they required? Insane. They even asked me to wake up at 8:30 AM tomorrow to continue the "job". Obviously, I won’t. But here’s the crazy part—there are 700 people in the group!

For those thinking about making quick cash through these schemes:

Use an alternate mobile number

Fake your identity

Never share bank details (UPI is safer)

Never pay a "caution deposit"

Oh, and one more thing—you can always delete the fake review later.
