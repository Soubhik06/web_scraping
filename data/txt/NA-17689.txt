Mainstream media never tires of
telling us that bygones should be
bygones. That 2002 cannot be
repeated again. That Mr.
Narendra Modi has turned a new
leaf. That Indian democracy
perforce transforms all
claimants to power into
secularists.
The Modi campaign too hasn’t
tired of showcasing its
inclusiveness. Who could have
missed the symbolism of a
judge, boatman, weaver and
singer proposing Modi's
nomination in Benaras – if only
Ustad Bismillah Khan’s family
hadn’t played spoilsport. But no
one is fooled. Beneath the thin
veneer of ‘development’, the
Hindutva agenda is alive and
thriving. And, no, it’s not the
loony fringe, excitable and un-
tame-able, that is indulging in
politics of hate. Modi is leading
from the front, assisted ably by
his lieutenant Amit Shah.
Here is a select sample of
‘inclusiveness’ of the Modi
Brigade. Is this a sign of times to
come? Kya acche din aane
wale hain ? Ya kuch aur?
Modi has not travelled that far
from the days of his ‘Mian
Musharraf’ barbs or declaring
refugee camps as baby

Hindutva. Here are some
