 Earlier on 9th of September, 2023, Mr. Trivedi, Ld.

 DSG, pointed out that the subject-matter of ECIR/KLZO-

 1/29/2021/1362 relates to provisions of 'Ponzi Scheme' as

 the company which has been involved is a chit fund

 company. The petitioner is detained in custody and it is the

 specific submission of the ld. DSG that the matters of such

 nature relating to application for bail should be taken up by

 a Division Bench in seisin of the Ponzi Scheme matters. 2 Mr. Dastoor, Ld. Senior Advocate, appearing for the

petitioner draws the attention of this Court to an order

passed by a Hon'ble Division Bench of this Court in WPA

6184 of 2023 (Sanjay Basu -vs- Union of India & Anr.) and

also to the order of the Hon'ble Supreme Court being SLP

(CRL.) No. 3863 of 2023 (Directorate of Enforcement & Anr. -vs- Sanjay Basu). Accordingly, I am of the opinion that the records of

the case be placed before the Hon'ble the Chief Justice, in

course of this day, for taking into account as to whether this

Court is empowered to take up the application for bail or the

bail application should be heard by a Bench in seisin of the

Ponzi Scheme matters. Parties to act on a server copy of this order duly

collected from the official website of the Hon'ble High Court,

Calcutta. (Tirthankar Ghosh, J.)
