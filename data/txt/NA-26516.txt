--- POST TEXT ---

One guy on a bike will tell you that air in the tyre is very low, coincidentally where the puncture shop is just ahead of you. And then they will show in the pressure checking gauge that pressure is low and tyre is punctured, after which the guy somehow tries to prove that there are multiple punctures and price of each is 120rs. One of these scams is while on dange chowk road towards bhumkar chowk, just before the conversion of roads. Other is while going to shivaji chowk after McD. Don't go to these guys, even if you have an actual puncture. Just go some distance ahead and fill you tyre, and go to your trusted puncture wala
