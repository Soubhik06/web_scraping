Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 and what is

the effect of Clause 46 in Arbitration Agreement between the

parties. We find that additional remedy has been conferred on the

Consumer Forum by Section 3 of Consumer Protection Act 1986. It

is the choice of litigant either to approach the Consumer Forum or

to approach the other equivalent authority. The complainant has

not approached the other alternate authority, but approached the

Consumer Forum straightway and Section 3 conferred additional

remedy on Consumer Forum to try such cases. The contention of Consumer Complaint No 445 of 2017 11 OPs is misconceived that this complaint is barred by Section 31 of

Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 and Section

46 of Arbitration Agreement containing arbitration clause. The

contention of Ops is not accepted on this point. Similar, proposition

apply even if there is arbitration clause in the agreement. 13. The next submission of counsel for OPs is that complainant

ceased to be consumer of OPs after delivering the possession. We

find that complainant came to know regarding alleged lesser area

delivered to them on receipt of approved copy of site plan by MC

Kharar only. The limitation to file the complaint would commence

from the date of knowledge on receipt of certified copy of the

approved site plan and hence complaint cannot be said to be

barred by time. Even otherwise, complainant filed complaint before

District Forum Mohali without losing much time and it was returned

and thereafter presented before this Commission, hence it cannot

be said that it is barred by time. The point as to whether

complainant ceased to be consumers of OPs after delivering of

possession also does not seem to be acceptable. If there is

allegation of unfair trade practice for delivering of lesser area than

agreed upon and charging overcharged price for the area, then it is

consumer dispute cognizable by the Consumer Forum. The

relationship between the parties does not come to an end merely

by delivery of possession, moreso; when there are serious

allegations of delivery of lesser area by complainant on behalf of

OPs despite charging the amount of more area. 14. Admittedly, the complainant booked a flat bearing No.G-15

Also, the sale deed has not been executed by the opposite parties. From the above, there are two issues to be decided: 15. With regard to the 1st issue of super area of the flat No.B-

51, after the case was remanded back from the Hon'ble National Consumer Complaint No 445 of 2017 13 Commission, during hearing on 16.01.2019 both the parties

through their counsels agreed to appoint District Town Planner,

Mohali as Local Commissioner to resolve the dispute. Accordingly,

the District Town Planner, Mohali was appointed as Local

Commissioner who visited the flat No.B-51, at 3rd Floor in the

project of the opposite parties in the presence of the owner i.e.

complainant and representative of the company i.e. opposite

parties on 18.02.2019. After taking detailed measurement at site

and calculations a detailed report dated 27.02.2019 was submitted

in this Commission on 28.02.2019 which was taken on record. As

per report of the Local Commissioner, Total super area of the flat

was calculated on 1936.21 sq.ft. Further, as per Typical Floor Plan

attached with the report, it is evident that the Flat is comprising 3

Bed Rooms, Living Room, Dining Room, 3 Toilets, Kitchen etc.

However, the complainant has alleged it to a 2 Bed Room Flat in

his complaint. 16. The complainant submitted his objections on the Local

Commissioner's report wherein stated that the Local Commissioner

has considered 159.39 sq.ft. balcony area which cannot be

considered in the super area of the flat as balcony is at level of 7ft.

and has not covered any land. Likewise, the Local Commissioner

has added proportionate area of mumty, Machine room, Water

Tank area which was never agreed to be included in the super area

by the complainant. The objections raised by the complainant are

without any evidence. There is clearly mentioned in the Buyer's Consumer Complaint No 445 of 2017 14 agreement the super area of the flat as 1900 sq.ft. There is

nowhere mentioned the definition of super area in the agreement

executed between the parties. However, as per definition of Super

Area, I am of the opinion that 'super area' includes the covered

area, verandah and balcony (including utility balconies), inclusive of

the area under periphery walls, area under columns and walls, area

utilized for services, viz. staircases, decks, cupboards, lofts,

circulation area with corridor, passage and staircase, lifts, shafts

(electric, fire, plumbing), service ledges on all Apartments, common

corridors and passages, stairhouses and machine rooms, lobbies

and refuge areas, shared area of walls common with other

premises/ Apartments, which shall form integral part of the said

Apartment and common area shall mean all such parts/ areas

which the Allottee(s) shall use by sharing with other occupants. The calculation of Super Area shall not include the following: 17. In view of the above definition, Local Commissioner has

correctly calculated the super area of the flat except for including

area of the water tank of 29.36 sq.ft. After deduction of 29.36 sq.ft.

area from the super area calculated by the Local Commissioner is

still more than 1900 sq.ft. as mentioned in the agreement. 18. The next issue is with regard to the sale deed of the flat, in

question, the opposite parties have already written to the

the sale deed. There is no evidence that the complainant has

replied to this letter of the opposite parties or made any effort to get

the sale deed executed. 19. In view of the above, the complainant has failed to prove the

allegations as alleged by him in his complaint. Therefore, the

complaint is dismissed. However, both the parties are directed to
