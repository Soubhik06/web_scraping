I am a Kenyan, and I recently travelled to India. I am amazed at how well UPI payments have been executed, and how simple the system is to the end user. In Kenya we have had m-pesa for nearly 12 years, and it works really well, except that you need to be on a specific mobile network to use it, and it has been the exact same thing for the past 12 years. UPI has literally disrupted the entire payments industry, and has made it such that you do not need to be on a specific platform, or attached to a specific organisation in order to avail it. 

&amp;#x200B;

My question here is, how good is the adoption rate? - I can see from the statistics produced by RBI that adoption is growing quite quickly. Are businesses able to accept payment directly to their bank accounts by use of a VPA? Is there potential for fraud, and are there any notable cases of fraud within the system?
