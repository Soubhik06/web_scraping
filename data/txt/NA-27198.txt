Jukin Media preys on people who are experiencing viral success, without understanding what they do and why you shouldn't trust them...

Jukin owns content brands like FailArmy, People Are Awesome, The Pet Collective, Poke My Heart, and a myriad of other brands...but their primary business is actually digital rights management.

They will tell you by signing exclusively with them, you will make money on your viral success, but the truth is 9 times out of 10, you won't make any money at all...however, Jukin will make bank on your footage.

**When do creators actually get paid:** The only time the creator gets any payment is if your content is licensed by other companies...say, for an advertisement, use in a movie, or for use on a morning show or news program. Brands can access the public Jukin library, shop through what videos they want via search, and go through a checkout service to license the content they want...and that's not usually much money at all (depending on usage), but the creator gets a split on that that seems very appealing, despite this not being as common as they claim it to be...

**How Jukin gets paid:** The primary income for Jukin is using your footage in dozens, if not hundreds of compilations they puke out at ungodly speeds, because they run a content mill in India that makes dozens of these edits every week. So they'll spam junk edits "Craziest Fails Of the Week" with your content in there, but since it's an owned channel - it's not part of your deal with Jukin as it says on the contract that they're allowed to use your content on "owned channels", and thus the creator makes $0 on this video. Also, since they are a legacy internet company, they have long relationships with platforms and get better ad-rev deals directly from YouTube and Facebook so they're making a few thousand on each of these videos vs what most creators get...

On top of it, they load your videos into their YouTube CMS and start claiming every instance of this video across YouTube to make more money on other peoples uploads and usage of your content, even claiming the video on YOUR CHANNEL...they do own the license, after all...and again, the creator gets $0 for this.

With that, if you email them about how you've seen them use your content and how you got no money, they'll offer to tag you on an IG post as a way to appease you...but being tagged by them accomplishes little, to nothing...no new followers, no interest, barely any CTR, because 90% of people who follow any of their social accounts don't care, they just want to see fails, pets, etc...

**Removing your Content from Jukin** - This process took me 6 months...that's right, 6 months to get them to remove my content from their platform. They claim you remove your content with ease and terminate your contract...but they make it physically impossible to do so. First, there's no way to do it in their CMS so you need to email them...next, they're insanely slow to respond to emails...**I wrote them in December,** they said "we're sorry to see you go" and proceed to ghost you...after multiple follow ups, **they finally got back in April claiming to remove the content...but they didn't...**I wrote them again in May, to which they said **"It appears the termination agreement was not signed to officially release. I will resend the agreement"...but, they never sent it to begin with.**

**tl;dr** avoid Jukin, or other licensing companies of this sort. It's a scam, they don't actually pay you but they make BANK on your content...and in my opinion, their abuse of the content licensing has kind of ruined the internet. They're basically the content version of oil barons...they'll drink your milkshake...
