March 6, 2025, marks **Slam the Scam Day**, it's a reminder to stay vigilant against fraud—and what a day for this news!

A fraudulent call center in Telangana was busted by the Cyber Security Bureau (TGCSB), where 63 people, and it is reported that mostly from the Northeast, were caught scamming NRIs and US citizens.

Scammers tricked victims into calling a fake "fraud prevention team" mentioned in emails. Once on the call, they were pressured into sharing banking details, believing they were securing a refund.

Do not share personal information over the phone and question suspicious calls.
