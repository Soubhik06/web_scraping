Hi, I’m 26M working in Hyderabad. I flew from Delhi —&gt; Hyderabad on the night of Sunday, 18th of August 2024 on the busy schedule of Rakshabandhan with extreme rush at both the airports. Now usually I pre-book a cab using uber/ola so that it’s convenient for me to collect my luggage and just wait for my cab which, most of the times, is already waiting. 

This time though, I decided to sit inside a shared cab with ‘apparently’ all 4 parties (3 others who were also travelling alone) going to a similar location (as shared cabs should work, ideally). The guy asked for 800 rs per head which I thought would be a fair deal given the late night hours and festive season, will probably double the travel time but I was prepared to take that risk. 

I’ve been working for about 2.5 years in corporate culture, most of which was spent in Pune and I never encountered any such incident where you are scammed to pay 800-900 rupees for a shared cab when all 4 destinations can be XYZ, so you could be in a situation where you’re the 4th one to go and you’ll have to wait until all 3 before you have been dropped (wherever and however long that takes)

I felt like I’m travelling all across Hyderabad at 11 pm with a whole lot of luggage! The scam is they take 800 rs per passenger which adds up to 3200 per trip. To give you a brief idea, I exited the airport at 9 pm and reached home (Kondapur) at 11:40pm, usually which takes about 45 mins.

Guys, seriously, don’t go for these scammers in the interest of money. Pre-book cabs or book from Ola/uber official app/booth only. Posting this here so no one gets scammed again
