Hello, CJI. Remember what you said last week? I see that you've been busy being shady last week (what with imposing fines and all). You might've forgotten. We haven't. Or has your Delhi vs Center case not finished yet? How long do we have to wait for the coercion to end?

Also. Question to anyone familiar with Aadhaar case. The case in SC is about whether or not Aadhaar violates privacy. Say SC rules that it does. Will that stop forced linking it everywhere? That is, e.g., what will linking Aadhaar to PAN be considered as: a reasonable exception or violation of privacy? Can any of the following be used in the case based on Right To Privacy?

* Aadhaar has been used as a tool for exclusion (by denying rightful services to those whose biometrics don't match).
* Data leaks have been rampant.
* Fake biometrics.
* Coercion. You cannot file ITR without quoting PAN in spite of SC ruling in June 2017 that those who don't have Aadhaar cannot be forced to get one just for this. Yet, IT website has made it impossible to file ITR without the damn UID.

While points listed are, IMO, evils of Aadhaar, they may not be necessarily viewed as violation of Privacy and hence may not be usable in this particular lawsuit. Hence the question. And anyway, if SC rules that Aadhaar is constitutional, we're all fucked anyway.
