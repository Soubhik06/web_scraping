Apparently Thursday Boots does not have a separate website for India but, there are two scam sites operating with their name. 

[https://www.thursdayboots-india.com/](https://www.thursdayboots-india.com/) 
[https://www.thursdaybootsindia.com/](https://www.thursdaybootsindia.com/) 

I came across a comment here in r/india suggesting one of these links, but checked around in r/ThursdayBoot and they confirmed that [http://www.thursdayboots.com/](http://www.thursdayboots.com/) is the only site they have and the other ones are fake. Posting for awareness. 

BTW, the original Thursday Boots are awesome.
