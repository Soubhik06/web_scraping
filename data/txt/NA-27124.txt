So I applied to a job in Linked In. It’s by a company called GEP Worldwide based in Edison, New Jersey. The guy who picked up the phone had an Indian accent with his family making noise in the background. All employees on LinkedIn seem to be from India too. I have nothing against India it’s just that is where most phone scammers tend to be from. The position is supposedly in-person. I applied for a software position and had to do a test . He told me I didn’t have to know the specific technology that they use to develop their products. But all the questions on the test had to do with that specific technology. So intuitively, I think it’s a scam. I was wondering if anyone else heard about it ?

Edit: So when I was speaking to him on the phone he told me that they were a world wide supply chain consulting company based in Edison, New Jersey. He said the job is $19-21 an hour. The title is “software intern” but when he spoke he pretty much said that it didn’t have much to do with software. I’d be doing a lot of document analysis and a whole lot of meetings. He said he’d give me an online test. But when I took the online test it was completely technical. It was one of those that lock the screen so you can’t exit. There was a lot of typos in the questions too. I did good but a lot of it had to do with a software framework that I told him that I didn’t know. He actually called me a second time the day I did It, but before , and I still heard a baby and a lady in the background.

Check out this job at GEP Worldwide: Software Intern https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/2796512539
