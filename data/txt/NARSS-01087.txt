Be very careful if you're renting a scooter in goa,. i rented a scooter from this hostel in Anjuna (name : xoxo Hostel) for 6 days, the scooter was already damaged and old, so i took a video beforehand and told their guy to check the condition so they don't blame me later. my phone had broken in a small incident on new years, and a minor crack had come on the pairing (cost of fixing it would be 1500 maximum). When i came back to return the scooter, they started accusing me of damaging everything on the scooter and demanded 15k ,(since my phone got broken so no video proof :( ). I told them i'll pay them 2k for the crack, but they threatened to call goons and even brought 2 goons with them. in the end, i had to pay them 6k just like that. Remember the name : XOXO hostel. never rent a scooter from here, or even come here, it's a shit hostel and a scam entirely.
