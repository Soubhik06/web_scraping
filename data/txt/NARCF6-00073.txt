First let's see what is included in hdi according to UN, it has 3 indicators - Health, education and standard of living. 

1. Lets start with standard of living, its measured in terms of GNI( per capita income) 
Here is the data according to mp government 
Indore - 151101
Bhopal- 140729
Jabalpur - 132043
Gwalior - 125351
Ujjain - 131900
And so on. 

Source - https://des.mp.gov.in/publication/view/WHJ4Qk1BQjY4bXp6S3U2ZnNZOXFQQT09

As you can see, jabalpur is 3rd in the state and above Ujjain and gwalior 

2. Talking about education, its measured in terms of average years of schooling and dropout rate, since i could not find this specific data anywhere ( curious about from where those guys found it to come up with these questionable hdi stats), so i would be taking average literacy rate as a proxy measure

Jabalpur - 81%
Indore &amp; Bhopal- 80%
Gwalior - 76%
Ujjain - 72%
and so on.. 

Source- https://www.census2011.co.in/census/state/districtlist/madhya+pradesh.html

As you can see , jabalpur has literally the highest literacy rate in the state.
