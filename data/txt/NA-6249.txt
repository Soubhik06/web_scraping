Since the companies of consortiums apply in development of mega projects and if this vacuum is not filled, even solvent companies, which are members of the consortium might be pulled into insolvency because of insolvency of one of the consortium members. As the insolvent memember would not be able to complete its part of the project and non-completion of the project will definitely impact the other companies, who are the Members of the Consortium.
