Para-12:- Now turning back to the facts of the present case,
assuming that the presumption under section 139 of the said Act

attract section 138 , the debt or liability has to be a "legally

under section 139 of the said Act that the debt is a legally
recoverable debt. In the case of Goa Plast (P) Ltd.Vs. Chico

alleged debt cannot be said to be a legally recoverable debt.
Para-15:- The Apex Court has held that the laws relating to the
