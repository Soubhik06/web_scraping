I have been seeing lots of posts regarding people getting scammed into sharing sensitive personal information / banking information etc. 

You can now start reporting these online to TRAI for action.

https://sancharsaathi.gov.in/sfc/Home/sfc-complaint.jsp

Additionally

1. Start using the services of Truecaller or enable automatic blocking of spam numbers on your Android phone 
2. Banks / Financial institutions / Government departments / online services will never send you SMS from a 10 digit number. 
3. Similarly Banks / Financial institutions / Government departments / online services will never call from a 10 digit number starting with 9/8/7 etc for marketing / verification purpose. Authorised call centre service have numbers starting with zero.
4. In spite of all this, if you get calls/SMS - never acknowledge any information, instead encourage them to talk / share more, and close the call by saying you are not interested or ask them to share info on email / post - if they are who they say they are - they should already have all this info in their system and you should not be sharing anything or confirming anything.
