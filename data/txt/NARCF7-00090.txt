Wanted to book appointment in Jupiter hospital Thane. Since were in hurry, my wife googled Jupiter hospital apointment and clicked on the first result showed up in Google. It's a sponsored result. Operator picked call (without any typical waiting music) and straight away asked for Dr. and patient details. We asked if appointment for a particular X doctor available so he said yes it is available but can't book on call, can only be done online. So he said I have shared booking link on your WhatsApp please book using the form. I was listening to the conversation, wife was speaking with the guy. He offered to help in filling the form on call itself but wife insisted we will do on our own. I opened the link, form hosting url looked suspicious but I ignored and inputted the details. Then came select payment option and bank and amount. How come amount is blank. I called on that WhatsApp no. and asked how much is the fees? He said you put Rs. 10 as of now, rest will be taken at hospital billing. I said fine. He again offered to help in form filling. After that came page Enter UPI PIN. At the very first glance of it I said to that operator, this doesn't look NPCI user interface. He said sir it is like this only, hospital uses its own gateway. I said this is strictly against RBI policy and probably illegal. He said ok sir you can go to hospital and book there. At this point I was 100% sure that it was a fake webpage to scam people.

It was an elaborate scam. Below I have shared link or you can try by clicking the first result on Google. It is coming for any query in Google related to Hospital booking. Just thought will make this public here. I reported that ad on Google as a scam. 

https://appointmentbookings2.wixsite.com/online2
