1. The applicant has filed this eighth bail application u/S.439 Cr.P.C for
 grant of bail. Applicant has been arrested on 17-01-2022 by Police
 Station Dinara District Shivpuri in connection with Crime No.21/2022
 registered for offence under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 470 and 471 2 of IPC and Sections 25 / 27 Arms Act. His earlier bail applications were
 dismissed as withdrawn. 2. It is the submission of learned counsel for the applicant that he has
 been falsely implicated in the case. It is further submitted that some of
 the witnesses have been examined before the trial Court and they did
 not support the story of prosecution and declared hostile. Therefore,
 case of applicant be considered on the basis of period of custody and
 nature of allegations levelled against him. Thus, prayed for grant of
 bail. 3. Learned counsel for the respondent/State opposed the prayer and
 submitted that it is the case where hundreds of forged Adhar Cards and
 Sim Cards have been recovered. Those Sims cards have been sold to
 the different persons in Haryana, Delhi and in other States.
 Investigation is still going on and incidentally one co-accused Pramod
 destroyed the Adhar Cards and Sim Cards just to make the case
 difficult for investigating officer to complete the chain. Two accused
 persons are absconding and Adhar Cards have been sent for forensic
 examination recently, therefore, the forensic report is awaited in the
 case. Thus, prayed for dismissal of this application. 4. Heard learned counsel for the parties at length and perused the case
 diary. 5. This is a case where allegations against the applicant are serious in
 nature. Some of the co-accused used to prepare forged Adhar Cards
 and those Adhar Cards were sold to different persons and on the basis
 of those Adhar Cards, Sim Cards were taken by the accused persons
 from service provider and those Sim Cards were sold to different
 persons of different States. Particulars of Adhar Cards/Sim Cards 3 seized from possession of different accused persons are as under: 6. Independent role of the accused may vary but collectively their role
 appears to be challenging and having wider ramifications in respect of
 national security and cyber crime. These Adhar Cards and Sim Cards
 can be used in NDPS offences, Terrorism related Activities, Cyber
 Frauds for Kidnapping and Ransom purposes and for offence of
 grievous denominations. 7. Considering the facts and circumstances of the case and the fact that
 two accused persons are still absconding in the case and also the fact
 that allegations are in respect of preparation of forged Adhar Cards and
 other essential documents which has wider ramifications, therefore, in
 the considered opinion of this Court, no case for grant of bail is made
 out in favour of applicant. Therefore, the bail application preferred by
 the applicant is hereby dismissed. (Anand Pathak)
