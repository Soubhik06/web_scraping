So I got the typical “your social security number has been used for fraud” call today and decided to talk to the guy that answered. 

He said his name was Henry and I played with him a little before he realized I was messing with him. I told him how he didn’t need to be a scammer and he could be doing better things with his life. 

He proceeded to tell me all about his life. How he’s from Myanmar and on a visa in El Paso, Texas. He told me that his building is controlled by the “underground” as he called it, which makes it really hard for him to leave and find another job. He also told be about how there are great places to vacation in north east India, right across the border from Myanmar. 

It was cool and a little odd talking to this guy (who kept insisting his name was Henry even though I wanted his real name). Hopefully what I said maybe meant something, but at least it wasted some of his time. 

I want to believe that what he said was true, but of course, it could have all just been bullshit. 

Not much reason behind posting this other than that I just wanted to share my experience.
