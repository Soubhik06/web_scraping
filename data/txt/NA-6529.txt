 In Re: - An application for anticipatory bail under Section 482 of the
 Bharatiya Suraksha Nagarik Sanhita in connection with Bidhannagar
 (East) PS Case No.19 of 2025 dated 21.02.2025 under Sections 318
 (4), 319 (2) of the BNS, 2023. And
 In the matter of: Akshat
 ....Applicant/Petitioner. Mr. Abhijit Kumar Adhya

 ...for the petitioner

 Ms. Amita Gaur, Sr. Adv. Ms. Sana Naaz
 ..for the State

 Learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioner submits as

 follows. The petitioner is a student of law and also in the business of

 selling mobile phones. The principal accused was arrested and was

 granted bail. Learned counsel appearing on behalf of the State opposes the

 prayer for anticipatory bail. She submits that two phone numbers

 were used to perpetrate fraud upon the victims. One belonged to the

 present petitioner. OTPs were given from these numbers and money

 was fraudulently taken out. Considering the serious allegations and the incriminating

 materials available in the case diary against the petitioner, , I do not

 consider this is to be a fit case for granting anticipatory bail.

 Accordingly, the application for anticipatory bail of the petitioner

 is rejected. (Jay Sengupta, J.)
