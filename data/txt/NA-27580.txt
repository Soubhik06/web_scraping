Several days ago I received a call from a guy named John with a very thick east Indian accent (1st red flag)
He claims he was calling from Wells Fargo and that he needed to confirm a purchase pending of over $1200.00 on my CC was coming from me.
He wanted some information to confirm that he is in fact talking to the correct CC holder. He claims he has all my information and that he needs me to verify it. I then started to ask him why he would need me to verify all this info if he already has all the pertinent information? His voice then started getting firm.
 "MA'AM, I ALREADY HAVE ALL YOUR INFO! Your address, your bday, ssn, CC #, mother's maiden name etc.."
Me: Which card do you have? Can you give me the numbers on it?
: sounds off with a bunch of #'s
Me: I don't have that, are you sure that's me? 
He cuts me off, and he's getting irritated. 
:"Ma'am, listen, I'm sending an authorization code to your phone, and I just need you to give me the code okay? This will confirm that it's you!
Me: wait, what's your number and extension? I need to call u back..
: 18006424720, there's no extension. This is my direct line. But I just sent the authorization, can you give it to me before you call me back!?
Me:? Gives him a fake code. Ok I'll call u back. I hung up and call the REAL WELLS FARGO to report this scam.

This twit was so angry that his purchase didn't go through, he kept calling me and hanging up from different phone #'s.

Edit:
I immediately contacted WF cx service, and the fraud dept. Closed the account and issued new cards. 

Not a day later, these same twits called using the same line, " My name is Charlie calling from Wells Fargo, wanting to authorize a purchase of $900 on your WF CC..
"Right, how about an I Don't think so.." click.
