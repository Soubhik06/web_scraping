Recently Jim Browning, Mark Rober and The Triology busted down a scam in Kolkata . This honestly isn't the first one , and theres a lot more youtubers always uncovering something like this from the metros, mostly from kolkata though. 

Yesterday they released the videos on their respective channels , and honestly watching Jim Browning's CCTV footage , it seems like these scammers have no shame about what they are doing , so much as to going ahead and laughing as they scam people out of their life savings . As an Indian as well as Bengali I honestly feel disgusted as well as at awe how very few people actually know or talk about it.
