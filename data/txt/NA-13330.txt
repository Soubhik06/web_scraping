According to a recent report by CUTS International, UPI has become the primary channel for processing deposits to illegal gambling apps, which also function as hubs for online scams such as fake loans and e-commerce fraud. Using malicious mobile applications like XHelper, criminal syndicates recruit numerous UPI mules to launder illicit funds, through pyramid-scheme-like practices that also involve forging official documents and taking advantage of lax regulatory scrutiny. The volume of such illicit transactions has continued to rise, despite government efforts.
