Just learnt from TelecomTalk about a new initiative by Department of Telecommunications named as Chakshu which is available in Sanchar Saathi portal to report Cybercrime like KYC Fraud, Phish etc. Currently it supports Voice, Text, Whatsapp medium.

Just reported two text messages that were in my Inbox as received on 4th March 2024 and 28th February 2024 both impersonating State Bank of India.

Chakshu is available at this [URL](https://sancharsaathi.gov.in/sfc/Home/sfc-complaint.jsp). We just need to upload the screenshot of the message and the text, sending number is automatically read from the screenshot itself. All the effort required from us are the following:

1. Take a screenshot of the message
2. Select the type of medium through which the communication happened.
3. Upload the image. (Text and Sender automatically gets populated)
4. Enter your name and mobile number (on which the correspondence got received).
5. Confirm your report through a One Time Password (aka) OTP
6. Check the declaration.
7. Click Preview button to verify and then tap Submit.
