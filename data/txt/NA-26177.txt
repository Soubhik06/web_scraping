I work for a logistics company and in the last two months we have been slammed by plenty of fake shipments.

Yeah, the ads on instagram offers cheap price for a kurti, you order it and will end up with only dirty jattis from vadakkans. 

One you paid for it and the delivery agent closed the order, there is no way to get your money back. Threatening the delivery person with legal action or violence is useless.

I'm not saying that all the products that are being advertised are fake. There are some genuine small businesses promoting their products on instagram and there are two easiest ways to identify the fake shipments.

First, the sellers only offer Cash on Delivery way of payment and no genuine sellers will ask you privately via Chat to send money via Gpay or phonepe. 

Second, is to see the seller address. If there are no details of the seller on the waybill, it's 99% fake. Sometimes there will be only website name. Like clickbuys.in or hairmyre.in. 

And yes , the delivery agents will need a OTP to cancel the order. Calling them fraud and scammers for asking the OTP is not wise. They are doing only their jobs. You can read the entire message before sharing the OTP.
