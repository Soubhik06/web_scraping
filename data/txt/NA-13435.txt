Digital gold functions much like an electronic receipt representing ownership of physical gold. There are two main players in the ecosystemâthe importer and the distributor. The importer sources gold from the bullion market, which then goes into a vault. The distributor, typically a fintech platform, provides the customer-facing technical interface through which investors can buy or sell gold seamlessly.

For example, when a user purchases Rs 100 worth of digital gold, they are essentially acquiring gold of equivalent value (after accounting for markups, taxes, and related charges) that is physically stored and allocated in their name.

However, digital gold differs from other gold-related instruments, such as gold ETFs, which are low-cost, regulated and require a demat account.

To explain, while digital gold offers the convenience of 24/7 access, it lacks the statutory protections of ETFs or sovereign gold bonds and still attracts GST at purchase, just like physical gold.

From an investor’s point of view, some of the key things to consider while investing in digital gold are pricing, liquidity and the cost structure. Digital gold prices are tied to live bullion rates, with most platforms selling 24-carat, 999-purity metal.

Some examples include Tata Group-owned CaratLane, MMTC-PAMP, Augmont and SafeGold. Apart from these players, several fintech platforms offer digital gold, including Jar, Paytm, Google Pay, PhonePe, and InCred Money.

Further, digital gold purchases currently attract a 3% GST in India. Add to that the distribution fee markup shared between the distributor and the gold custodian, which is typically around 2-3%. Notably, these markup charges are already baked into the gold rate, and the price of digital gold varies from platform to platform: being higher than the prevailing rates in the gold spot market.

While one can redeem digital gold into cash instantly, conversion into physical gold usually takes a few working days and incurs a delivery fee, along with a minimum weight threshold, commonly 0.5 grams to 1 gram.
