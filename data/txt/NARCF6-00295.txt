Hi guys!

My dad received a call (on whatsapp) by a guy with a policeman's pfp. Here's what the conversation went like:

**Scammer:** Hello sir! I'm talking from Sadar Police Station, Gurgaon. Are you XXXX's father? 
**Dad:** Yes, I am. 
**Scammer:** Sir your son has been caught in a rape case. His name was identified in a call trace. We have him with us right now. Please speak to him. 
*now someone bleats like a 16 year old, swallowing half the words they said and only words like "Papa", "Help", "Police" being recognizable, rest everything was gibberish* 
**And the call was disconnected.**

Now the funny part is that I was feeling a bit low earlier so I came home yesterday.

The scammer calls back, and it's me answering as my dad now 
**Scammer:** Sir your son is crying a lot and we know that he is innocent. His friends are the culprits and he is being framed. We have detained him for now, but we have to take him to the station. I thought I should inform you first. 
**Me (as dad):** My son has committed a crime, it should be filed and he should be booked. Please do the needful. 
**Scammer:** Sir! Don't you understand!? Your *(he remembered his mom here)* son will be imprisoned for minimum 10 years, I swear on my name! 
**Me (as dad):** You don't have to worry about that. I'm a well connected guy, I'll get him out. 
**And the call was disconnected again.** (I think he realized that I caught his scam)

Later when I checked my dad's phone, the number started with **+92**, and was already registered as "**fraud policeman**" on truecaller. Now my parents are relatively aware about such scams, and they're pretty chilled out too. But with this kind of a scam, even they were petrified, especially my mom (In her eyes, I'm still a super innocent and gullible chap).

I just think, what would have happened if I wasn't at home today. Like I know my dad isn't so gullible that he'll send them the money right away without even checking it twice. But the mental trauma of being accused for rape would be devastating. 
Stay safe guys! And do talk to your parents often. Let them know that you're doing fine, and if anything comes up, they should check with you first.

tl;dr: Someone tried to impersonate a police officer and extort money from my dad using me as a bait. Fortunately, I was sitting next to him, safe and sound.
