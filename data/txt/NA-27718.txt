--- POST TEXT ---

In July, I received a call from a private number. It was a nice, professional, and eloquent female (albeit with a slight indian accent) on the other end, suggesting that she is from Service Canada and they need to clarify details regarding my employment. She requested to clarify some personal details before disclosing the nature of details required - I said no obviously and she then gave me the official number for Service Canada (I double checked that it was correct) and I never looked back. This morning, I received a follow-up call, suggesting that I still needed to follow-up with Service Canada. This time, it was a different girl who sounded younger, also eloquent, and with a pretty normal North American accent. She cited that the private call was due to them working remotely from home, and again she provided me with the Service Canada number as I didn't feel comoftable to disclose personal details. 

My gut tells me that it's a sophisticated scam, and they provide the real number so that when they follow-up, I'm more inclined to trust them. Has anyone has a similar experience?
