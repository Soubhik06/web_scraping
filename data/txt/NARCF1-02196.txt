I work in a data analysis startup and we have a town hall meeting once a month, sometimes we talk to new joinee and the fresh guy turned out to be from Pune, so I told him about my being from Kolkata. One of the US guys exclaimed, "oh I know that place, It's like the capital of scam call offices" 

Although everyone was just kidding and he wasn't trying to hurt me. After the call, talked to some friends and searched google about this.

I felt really bad
