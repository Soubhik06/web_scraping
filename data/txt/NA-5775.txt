been invoked 3rd time for grant of anticipatory bail to the petitioner in case

Station Dharamkot, Moga 2. Learned counsel for the petitioner contends that it is on

account of alleged fraud the petitioner has been roped in, in spite of the fact

that no amount was ever credited to his account and he is not the

beneficiary. 3. This is the third petition seeking pre-arrest bail within almost

4½ months whereas, earlier petition i.e. CRM-M-14597-2023 was
