Hi all, today my father got a call from a lady (name and number removed as per R/India rules).

This bitch was claiming to be calling on behalf of IGMS and advising of some refund of insurance policy that you would get after depositing some amount of cheque. 

She kept speaking some nonsense for about an hour and all I could get from it (after speaking to my father) was that, we had to send a cheque of amount rs.24780 in the name of "star health" and after a period of time 195500 rs would be deposited back to our bank account.

Clearly, it sounded like a total scamster. So, I called her myself to ask about their genuinity. She gave me their website details as igms.co in and igms.gov.in, none of which actually exist! After I told her that, she still kept insisting they those urls are genuine.

Then I asked her office location in mumbai, she said she would provide it and then she hungup. I kept calling but she never answered.

I googled about this scam and found an article about it on jaagoinvestor.com
https://www.jagoinvestor.com/2012/05/beware-of-fraud-calls-in-name-of-irda.html/amp

Luckily my father contacted me about this and I could warn him but cant imagine how many other innocent citizens these scamsters must be calling everyday and cheat them. It seems already a lot of people have been victim of this.

Hope this post help you in dealing with such people and I highly encourage you to make others aware of this.
