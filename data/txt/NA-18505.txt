My mom was having trouble accessing her 3 PNC bank accounts and upon talking to the local branch they advised her to call the 800 number. She called the 800 number and they opened a ticket. Immediately upon hanging up someone called saying they were from PNC bank and were going to help her access her account. She of course didn't realize it could be a fraud call since it happened so quickly. She gave all the info anyone would need to take the money out and within minutes all 3 accounts now has 0 balance. It is hard to believe that someone from PNC is not in on it. Does she have any hope at all? I'm not in India so can't do much from here.
