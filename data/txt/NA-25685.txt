Hello everyone!

I'm doing some research on the concept of money pools, also known as rotating savings and credit associations (ROSCAs), where a group of people contribute to a common fund and take turns to receive the lump sum. It's a practice that has been around for generations in various forms across different cultures.

I'm curious to learn more about modern experiences with money pools:

1. If you have participated in a money pool, could you share where and how you did it? Was it through a local community, friends, family, or via an online platform?
2. What measures did you take to ensure the safety of your money? How did you mitigate the risk of fraud?
3. Have you encountered any digital platforms that facilitate money pools? If so, what has your experience been like?
4. What are the features or factors that you would consider most important for safely participating in a money pool?

Please feel free to share your experiences and thoughts. Your insights will be incredibly valuable to anyone looking to participate in money pools safely and securely.
