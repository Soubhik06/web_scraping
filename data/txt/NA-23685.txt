I recently fell for an Instagram scam on eyewearlab.online. They were selling a “5-in-1 clip-in sunglass” for Rs. 900, promising cash on delivery and a 10-day return. When it arrived, it was a cheap plastic pair worth maybe Rs. 50, with no real return info. Turns out, many others have been scammed, but fake positive comments hide the real complaints.

Here’s the plan:

Go to 

https://eyewearlab.online/products/5-in-1-magnetic-polerized-sunglasses

 place an order with a fake address (try locations like Kashmir, Kerala, or Arunachal). Just use a legit phone number; they only send an automated WhatsApp message for address confirmation—no calls or further interaction. Once you confirm, they’ll ship it.

Since they’re based in Delhi, deliveries to distant locations cost them more. If enough of us do this, they’ll face significant losses from returned deliveries.

You won’t have to pay anything—just confirm the address through the bot’s message and let it ship. This small action could help stop them from scamming others.
