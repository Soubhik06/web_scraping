--- POST TEXT ---

I received a mail from Myntra today morning [informing me about a purchase](https://imgur.com/a/AEdVK1P) (Boult Black Audio Loupe Wired Headphones With Mic and Mi Unisex Black Band 3) using my Myntra Credits. I was pretty surprised as I didn’t make the purchase and immediately logged in and cancelled the items.

The items were marked to be delivered to to a chawl in Worli in Mumbai.

The refund was initiated and I received the money back into Myntra credits immediately. I called up Myntra from Dubai on (0091)80615611999 and the call was picked up by Ms. P . She said nothing much can be done and suggested to change the password and complain to the cops. When I requested to refund the credit back to my bank account, she point blank refused. She said the only way is to shop for the amount and after that to delete the account if required.

Assuming it was a one-off thing, I didn’t speak much after this and changed the password using LastPass. I then purchased shirts for my dad using the Rs 2703 credit in the account and some Rs 27 or so through netbanking.

I thought this was it. But I was wrong. I got an email informing that the shirts I ordered were canceled! I panicked and went to the account to see that my address was again changed to the Mumbai address. And the Mi band was back in the bag.

I again called up Myntra, escalated the call to Ms. A , a supervisor who was utterly unhelpful ! She went on to lie to me first saying that the ordered was cancelled my Myntra staff sensing fraud and then after a couple of minutes, she said it was canceled by the user. But I am the user and I didn’t cancel !

She then agreed that this could be a case of hacking and I need to go to the cop. She had zero empathy to the fact I am in Dubai and it wasn’t possible for me to do so. Despite repeated requests to refund the credit back to my bank account as cash, she declined saying it was not possible. The lady was not ready to escalate this and connect me to a manager nor was she ready to give any email on which I could send an email. I then asked her if there is any way to log out from all current sessions of my account to which she said its not possible, which was extremely surprising !

The [international call went on for around 40 minutes](https://imgur.com/a/NjMldw5) till I hung the call after getting frustrated. She repeatedly asked me “is there anything she can do for me” in spite of not getting a resolution on my issue.

After the call, I was wondering how the person was able to log into my account after I changed the password. On a hunch, I opened myntra on another browser (with my current session on my default browser) and changed my password. And voila! My old session on the default browser is still active !

With this, I understood Myntra has zero seriousness about cybersecurity and thinks such incidents are just a joke. They don’t even have an option to log out from all the active sessions which could have prevented the person from accessing my account again.

The funny thing is, it’s not a one-off thing. A quick google search led me to multiple instances of Myntra credit being misused in the past.

So Myntra, thanks for letting me know that your security features are extremely childish and your CS resolutions are useless. I am now checking my myntra account every 30 minutes to see if the person has brought something again. Because no matter how many times the password is changed at my end, he can still use the session he is on!

Kudos, Myntra.
