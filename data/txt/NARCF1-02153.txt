A new day, a new scam.

Received the call few minutes ago, where an AI voice claiming to be TRAI (Telephone Regulatory Authority of India) threatened to cancel my number for 'abnormal usage'.

Upon choosing furthur option, I quickly got connected with a person where he asked my concerns, and my name. If they really got info on me, why need it? Moreover, the SIM I use isn't even on my name (was bought by father back when I was a minor) lmao.

I told him to put my number in the system to pull up the information. The guy claimed he can't access system info without 'permission' and my name (I have previous experience in CS, and this was horseshit).

He then had the audacity to threaten me by saying I can either co-operate, or disconnect the call.

My final response: "Okay" *hangs up*

🔪

[P.S.- If someone receives similar calls, please be careful. Educate the elders of your home about it as well. Keep in mind, that TRAI doesn't have the authority to cancel a number under a private service provider company. I hope this post helps in alerting everyone. And don't forget to report the number I shared in the screenshot here!]
