Let's delve into some noteworthy insights from Harsh Goenka:

Renowned chairman of RPG Group, Harsh Goenka, has raised concerns about potential market malpractices reminiscent of the infamous Harshad Mehta scam from the 1990s.

**Key Points:**

1. **Cautionary Tale:** Goenka has sounded an alarm for retail investors, highlighting the risk of stock prices being artificially inflated by certain brokers, echoing the tactics seen during the Harshad Mehta era.
2. **Historical Precedent:** The Harshad Mehta scam, a dark chapter in India's financial history, involved illegal banking activities and manipulation of share prices, resulting in a significant market crash and losses for investors.
3. **Identifying Culprits:** Goenka has singled out "Gujarati-Marwari brokers," alleging their involvement in driving stock prices to unsustainable levels, potentially setting the stage for another market downturn and causing harm to small investors.
4. **Call for Action:** Urging regulatory bodies like the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) and the Finance Ministry to intervene, Goenka emphasizes the importance of investigating these practices and safeguarding investor interests.

**In Summary:** While Goenka's warning is based on market observations rather than concrete evidence, it underscores the necessity for regulatory oversight to prevent a recurrence of past financial scandals and protect investors from potential market upheavals.
