Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and learned

 APP for the State. The petitioner is languishing in custody since

 02.03.2019 in a case registered for the offences punishable

 under Sections 341 , 323 , 324 , 379 , 354A , 354C , 354D , 386 , 504 , 506 , 509 of the Indian Penal Code and Sections 66 and 55C of the I.T. Act. The informant, being the teacher in a primary school

 alleged that she was acquainted with the petitioner from before

 and in this background, the petitioner took a loan of

 Rs.1,00,000/- and on demand being made, the petitioner

 started blackmailing the informant by posting her some nude

 photographs on social media. Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.34647 of 2019(2) dt.28-05-2019 2/3 It is submitted by learned Sr. counsel for the

 petitioner that in the background of dispute with regard to

 refund of loan amount taken by the petitioner, the accusation

 has been levelled. A statement has been made in paragraph

 no.3 of the petition that the petitioner is not having any

 criminal antecedent. Learned counsel for the informant submits that it is a

 clear case of blackmailing a girl by posting her obscene

 photographs on social media. Considering the nature of accusation, being

 levelled in the background of contractual relationship between

 the petitioner and the informant from before, coupled with

 statement made in paragraph no.3 of the petition that the

 petitioner is not having any criminal antecedent, let the above

 named petitioner be released on bail on 3.06.2019, on

 furnishing bail bond of Rs.10,000/- (ten thousand) with two

 sureties of the like amount each to the satisfaction of learned

 ACJM-I, Muzaffarpur, in connection with Muzaffarpur Mahila

 P.S. Case No. 8 of 2019. However, learned Court below will be at liberty to

 cancel the bail bonds of the petitioner, if he defaults for two

 consecutive occasions or if the informant produces some
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.34647 of 2019(2) dt.28-05-2019 3/3 substantial proof suggesting that the petitioner is still

 blackmailing or harassing the informant. (Dinesh Kumar Singh, J)
