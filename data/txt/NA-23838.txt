--- POST TEXT ---

We run a car wash and have been recently getting calls to our numbers. 
They start off by asking if a specific car had come to the car wash and when we say yes, they would say that they are yet to make a payment and will send a text message which says money has been credited to the UPI. 
They would then call back saying they have mistakenly sent a higher amount and send back the remainder. 
In case you are too busy to not check correctly and since they will mention a car which had actually come to the car wash, you might send the amount. 

If you call back and say that it was a message they will block you immediately. 

Just an fyi to the community. In general, always be aware and prudent. Never take decisions in haste and never give into pressure from people on calls or messages or from strangers. Looks like there is a large employed youth of India into the scam sector.
