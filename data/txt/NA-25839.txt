--- POST TEXT ---

I booked an auto from Suchitra to Medchal about 7.30 in the morning. I was coming from Bangalore, and my drop point from the intercity bus was at Suchitra. I tried booking via the Ola app, and instantly some auto guy accepted. Like normal, he came and asked me to sit. When I gave him the OTP, he said he has to go somewhere and he will take me to some point 2 kms away from where his brother will pick me up. Instant red flag, I asked him to drop me as I will not take the auto. He still kept rolling the auto trying to convince, until I had one foot out of the auto asking him to stop and speaking quite loudly (though there weren't many people in the streets to gain attention). He eventually stopped and I got out of the auto. 

With the recent mugging and scam incidents coming to light, I can't help but wonder if I was about to be scammed / mugged. I got down from the auto and booked another one via Rapido, and instantly again the same auto got booked. I cancelled it again and decided to wait it out for 5 mins, despite the fact that it was raining and there was little to no shed.
