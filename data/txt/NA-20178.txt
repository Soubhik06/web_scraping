I am referring to medical information, banking details, Aadhaar card stuff, or basically any kind of personal information that you wouldn't want to be made public.

There was an incredibly alarming report last week about [a pathology lab in Mumbai accidentally releasing over 43,000 medical records of patients, including those with HIV](https://www.buzzfeed.com/pranavdixit/the-medical-reports-of-43000-people-including-hiv-patients-w?utm_term=.ysR3Gg8JY#.yc62QMY1q). Hundreds of test reports were on the path lab's website - patient's name, medical history, doctor's name, everything! A case like this in the U.S. would have almost immediately resulted in class action lawsuits and the path lab would have come under big time scrutiny from all kinds of regulatory agencies, mainly the FDA. And yet, in India, it seems like the regulators may not even be aware this happened. This was a MAJOR data leak that we should all be concerned about! 

If anyone's got any insight, I'd really like to know that as an Indian citizen today, what are some of the ways my personal information can be (or is being) compromised? What is really happening out there, and how are companies thinking about this. I remember reading Reliance saying that "data is the new oil" for them. All these big companies seem to be really interested in data lately, so must be a lucrative business opportunity. What are companies doing with our data? Is there really a black market out there where patient information is being traded between insurance companies?

Let's have a discussion and share what we know.
