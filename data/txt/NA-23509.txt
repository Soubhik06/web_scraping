**Awareness Alert**

There's a scam circulating under the name of Sumit Chaudhary, who is supposedly followed by Mr. World 2016, Rohit Khandelwal.

Here's how it works: Scammers target you through Instagram or Facebook ads, claiming to offer brand collaborations with names like Myntra, Flipkart, and Manvayar. Once you share your profile, they send you a PDF with fake phone numbers but legitimate-looking Instagram profiles and website links under the name "Leaf Netwrks."

The scam is in the PDF—they ask for a security deposit of Rs. 2599, promising it will be refunded within an hour. However, as soon as you pay, they block you on all social media platforms, and the phone numbers become unreachable.

A case has been registered with cyber crime authorities.

Details:

- **Instagram ID in the PDF:** sumit.chaudhuary
- **Website Link:** [Leaf Netwrks](https://www.leafnetwrks.com)

Read more about it [here](https://consumercomplaintscourt.com/online-insta-fraud-of-leaf-networks/). 

Stay vigilant!
