Hey everyone 21M here. I'm new to Bangalore (6 months) and I got scammed for the first time in my life. This was near Indiranagar metro station. I was very tired after work and not in the right state of mind, and suddenly a random guy walks up to me with his "mum" on call and he demanded Rs. 375 to send to his mom for the cab to the hospital, since his Paytm bank server was apparently down.
I should've caught the scam right there, but I don't know what was wrong with me at that time, but I went along with it. I paid the amount to his "mom", she requested the amount on Gpay and I paid it. The guy spoke in very good English and seemed to come from a well off family, so that put me off guard. I feel really stupid now and going to the bank or Cybercrime division for Rs 375 seems stupid, but I wanted to let you guys know of this scam so that you can avoid it. 
This was certainly a life lesson.
