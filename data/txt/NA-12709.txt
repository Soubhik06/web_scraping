1. The petitioner is seeking the concession of anticipatory

bail under Section 438 of the Cr.P.C. in case FIR No.131 dated

16.04.2023 under Sections 406 , 420 , 465 , 467 , 468 and 471 of the IPC

and Section 24 of the Immigration Act registered at Police Station City

Kharar, District SAS Nagar. 2. Learned counsel appearing for the petitioner inter alia

contends that the petitioner, who is working in IndusInd Bank, Branch

Daun, Tehsil Kharar, has been falsely implicated in the instant case for

allegedly conniving with co-accused Harjit Singh @ Gurjit Singh by

opening two bank accounts in the names of both the complainants,

without their knowledge. It has been asserted that the very fact that the

complainants had been depositing their cheques and doing various

transactions from those accounts falsified the prosecution version. It
