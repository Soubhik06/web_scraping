My sister recently got an OPC registered and yesterday she [received a VPP](http://imgur.com/dneGnbY) in the name of the OPC. It was sent by Divya Book Co. in Delhi. She was too naive to accept the parcel thinking they would be some documents. Turns out it is a [long running fraud](http://www.consumercomplaints.in/other/divya-book-book-co-c808808) where such book shops get a list of newly registered companies and send VPPs to them sending irrelevant stuff.

The VPP page on [India Post Website](http://www.indiapost.gov.in/VPPost.aspx) says that for sending a VPP the sender needs to give a declaration that the goods are being sent against a bonafide order received by them. Considering that India Post would have received complaints against this, is it possible that the Post officials are complicit in this? What is the recourse in this case and how can this be stopped?

Also similar modus operandi is followed by [Legend Book Co.](http://www.consumercomplaints.in/complaints/legend-book-co-mumbai-city-maharashtra-c463514.html) 

Be careful not to fall for such scams.
