1. By this Miscellaneous Application, the applicant is
 seeking compliance with the order dated 04.05.2022 passed
 in O.A. No.195 of 2021(SZ), wherein the issue of
 encroachment, dumping of C & D Waste, debris, jack well
 pump house work, etc. were considered in respect of Yele
 Mallappa Shetty Lake/Tank and its buffer zone. 2. The Tribunal, on 04.05.2022, while disposing of the
 Original Application, had issued as many as ten directions to
 different authorities. 3. In this Miscellaneous Application, the 5th
 Respondent/Karnataka State Pollution Control Board has
 filed the memo/report dated 30.07.2024, pointing out the
 following status of the lake:- 4. The above disclosure made by the Karnataka
SPCB reveals that directions issued by the Tribunal were not
complied with till the filing of the report. 5. The 1st Respondent has subsequently filed the
report dated 07.09.2024 and it has been pointed out by the
learned counsel for the 1st Respondent that the directions
summarised in sub para (i), (ii), (iii), (viii) & (x) of paragraph
(2) of the report, concerning the 1st Respondent have been
complied with. 6. The applicant alleges that the debris are still lying
on the spot and have not been cleared till now. 7. The learned counsel appearing for the Bruhat
Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP) has stated that the
compliance report dated 04.01.2024 has been filed, but it is
lying in defect. He is permitted to cure the defect so that the
report comes on record. It is stated that to prevent the
dumping of the garbage and debris etc., marshalls have been
employed by BBMP. He is directed to place on record the
latest status of the lake before the next date of hearing. 8. The applicant is also permitted to file the relevant
 material disclosing the latest status of the lake, as also the
 status of clearing of debris. 9. List the matter on 01.04.2025. Sd/- Justice Prakash Shrivastava, CP
