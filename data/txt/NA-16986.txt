So I got a call today from a person whose name on Truecaller showed Amazon.in . They gave an offer where if I shopped for over 5000 they would include a gift. The gift can be chosen from many of the available items like laptop, refrigerator, phone, camera, TV etc. Initially I thought something was fishy but I decided to play along. After I added the items to my cart and chose the gift, he asked me to pay the amount via netbanking, upi, or Paytm. He asked me not to click on proceed to buy but instead pay via these specific methods so as to generate some transaction id which they can verify. 

By this time I knew it was a scam. So I told him I'll pay by Google pay. He gave his number and surprisingly enough it showed up as Amazon.in .
I cut the call after giving him a piece of my mind.

I know the general reddit populace is smart enough to be aware of such tricks but I also wanted to know if I can do something about the number to which I was supposed to transfer the amount. Can I submit the number as scam somewhere so that the account associated with it can also be documented as fraud?
