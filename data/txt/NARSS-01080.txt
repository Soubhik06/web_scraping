Hi folks,
I got scammed by a tiffin service and I assume I’m not alone in this situation.
The name of this business was “Masaledaar” and they operated from somewhere near the Nagoa circle (North Goa’s Nagoa). I had been their customer since Feb 17, and so far everything was fine, so I had no reason to believe that they will scam me.
I recently renewed my subscription for the next month and things went fine until last night when they stopped delivering. I didn’t mind because I had my dinner elsewhere. But today when the food didn’t arrive, I called my delivery guy and he said the owners seem to have absconded after closing their kitchen in the above-mentioned location. They had apparently outsourced the delivery to some people, who now don’t know where they are and can’t contact them either.
I tried calling the owners’ numbers, all were switched off. They are not online on WhatsApp either, where I used to have daily conversations with them regarding delivery location (office or home) etc. Their Instagram page has been deleted as well.
I lost 6000rs. and so did at least 14 others as per the delivery guy - he told me he made around 15 deliveries everyday. They had at least two others delivering food for them as well.
This is the first time something like this has happened to me, and I’m absolutely confused. Is there anything that I can do? Is there anyone else who has gone through the same situation?
