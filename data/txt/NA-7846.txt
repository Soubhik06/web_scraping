01. 1. This is an application under Section 439 of Cr.P.C. has been

 filed by the Petitioner for grant of bail in connection with Plantsite

 P.S. Case No.245 of 2023 corresponding to G.R. Case No.1213 of

 2023 pending in the file of learned S.D.J.M., Panposh, Rourkela

 under Sections 498-A , 376(2)(n) , 292(2) , 324 , 34 of IPC read with

 66(A), 67 (A) of IT Act, 2000 . 2. Heard Mr. G. Madani, learned counsel for the petitioner and

 Mr. A.P. Das, learned Addl. Standing Counsel for the State. 3. The allegation against the Petitioner in brief is that he has kept

 forcible physical relations with the victim-informant with the aid of

 her husband, who is also an accused in the case. They have taken Page 1 of 2 photographs of the informant in compromising position and were

 blackmailing her to make the video calls and photographs viral. I

 have perused the FIR annexed to this BLAPL and the statement of

 the victim annexed to BLAPL No. 10530 of 2023 filed by the co-

 accused. 4. Mr. Madani, learned counsel appearing for the Petitioner

 submits that the Petitioner was in a relationship with the informant

 and he has been made a scapegoat on account of the dispute

 between the Petitioner and her husband. 5. Considering the submissions and the nature of the allegations

 against the Petitioner, I am not satisfied that this is a fit case for

 release of the Petitioner on bail and, accordingly, the prayer is

 rejected. 6. Consequently, the BLAPL is dismissed. 7. An urgent certified copy of this order be issued as per rules. (Savitri Ratho)
 Judge
