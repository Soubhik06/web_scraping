Hey folks,

This is a new scam/technique that I have come across being used by Ola/Uber autos &amp; cabs.

They would start the ride as usual and after a certain distance stop the vehicle on the pretext of running out of fuel or the car/auto having trouble. They now end the ride and you look for another ride where you are probably charged again.

This scam/technique is employed by these drivers if they do not wish to go to your destination or do not want a non-cash trip. This was verified by one driver who told me that he just used this technique I described when he got a trip to Attibele (almost the outskirts of Bangalore).

**What to do if this happens to you -**

Do not panic. You cannot call out the driver nor check if the cab/auto is functioning. Your best bet is to book another ride, but before you do that report that the cab/auto has broken down on the app - which you can find in the "Support" or "Help" section.

Once you do that, you might be asked to speak with a customer care rep where the rep would call the driver and verify the same. At this point in time, you might get a full or partial refund of the trip by Uber/Ola. Until you speak to the customer care rep, DO NOT have the driver end the trip.

If you end the trip, and find another ride and seek to get a refund from Uber/Ola later they might deny it and even the driver could deny their cab/auto breaking down to avoid potential penalties.

Having the drivers admit to the customer care rep that their car/auto has broken down right there would work more effectively than a retroactive refund.
