Hey everyone,
I’m posting this because I recently came across something really concerning on the Visakhapatnam Discord server that I think the wider community needs to be aware of.

One of the moderators there, who goes by the name Raijin, has allegedly been running a long-term scam targeting server members. Over the past year, several people trusted him with money—over ₹1,00,000 that we know of—after he offered to get them high-end tech products like laptops, phones, and Elgato Stream Decks at “discounted” prices. He backed these offers by claiming he worked with companies like S8UL and India Today Gaming.

But here’s the issue: none of the promised products ever arrived. Not a single one. And this has been going on for over a year.

Two people, Jeevan and Lawman, were refunded ₹6k and ₹11k respectively, but only after months of them constantly calling and chasing Raijin. Others haven’t seen a single rupee returned. The worst part? When some folks reached out to actual India Today Gaming officials—Musab Zaman(head of India today gaming)and Akshat Mathur(HR)—they confirmed Raijin never worked there. Apparently, he interned there for just one month. That’s it. No real position. No full time employment

What started as a trusted moderator offering “helpful deals” has turned into what looks like a serious case of online fraud. Based on everything I’ve read and the discussions in the server, this could fall under:

Cheating and misrepresentation (Section 417 IPC / Section 316 BNS)

Fraudulent collection of money (Section 420 IPC / Section 317 BNS)

Criminal breach of trust (Section 406 IPC / Section 308 BNS)

Digital impersonation and cyber fraud (Section 66D of the IT Act)

If you’re part of that server and have sent Raijin money—or know someone who has—please preserve every screenshot, payment proof, and conversation. This is serious. Some folks are considering taking legal action, and honestly, I don’t blame them.

We have contacted the subreddit moderators and have provided them with evidence against this fraud.

Stay safe out there, and don’t trust random discounts online—even if they come from a “trusted” mod.
