There is a scam going on in BLR where a person poses to be from Dhani (Indiabulls) and is scamming poor people of 18-30k by telling them the initial payment is for insurance. My househelp fell for this trap, the cyber police and local police were of no use, they instead blamed them for falling for the scam. The person who was contacting them has now blocked them, and is unreachable.
