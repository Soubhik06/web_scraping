My husband is trying to sell his car and isn’t having much luck. He got a call from a guy with a thick Indian accent who said his name was a ridiculous American name similar to “Chad Jones”. He said his son, “Mike Jones” is going to school in our area and they want to take a look. I said “oh yeah, that’s a huge scam. Chad will all of a sudden have an emergency and send “Mike” to test drive it. Chad will ask if he can give a check or pay digitally and then it will bounce and you’ll be out a car. Or it will accidentally be for too much and he will ask for a refund. Oldest scam on Reddit.” Husband got all pissy and contrarian and said “if that’s the case why does he want to see it, huh??” Duh, because the scam is in the refund and the “courier”. That’s the whole point. 
He thinks I’m cynical and negative. I didn’t say this will definitely happen, I just pointed out the red flags and said to be alert. 

Insight?? Am I crazy??? Sometimes men only listen to men, is there a way to communicate what you all have taught without coming across as a cynical harpy shrew?
