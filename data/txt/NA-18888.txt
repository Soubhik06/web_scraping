--- POST TEXT ---

Ola/Uber lured the drivers by saying they will earn around **Rs 1 Lakh/mo**, [proof](http://imgur.com/wwvkXsq). This happened initially from few years, drivers believed in it and were super excited. This inflated income was coming from investor money of course. Seeing this many other drivers wanted to join and were told by Ola/uber that if they buy a new car of even around Rs 10 lakh they can easily pay off the loan @Rs30k/mo for 3 years tenure. Loan financing(with car as collateral) was done by Ola in partnership with banks, obviously taking a cut in it. Still after paying EMI drivers can take home **Rs 70k/mo** which is more than decent. Great till now. 
Real fuck up starts now. The incentives are stopped. The income per month presently has come down to **Rs 35-40k/mo**, that's after working for 10-12 hours per day. If you subtract EMI, petrol/diesel costs from their monthly income it will be a **negative** number. Thus they have to pay Ola every month and will continue to do so for **next 3 years.** Imagine working so hard with zero or minuscule income. Also most of these drivers are married with 2-3 dependents. Wow. 
Ola automatically deducts EMI and it's share from drivers account daily income thus drivers are at the mercy of the company. They don't even have option of defaulting. 
Recently there were [numerous protests](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yvjUJJIJDwM&amp;app=desktop) across different metro cities . 
Isn't this a big business blunder or even akin to ponzi scheme? Ola/Uber founders can run with investors' money but how will drivers pay off their debt ? What is the way out for them ? 

**Edit:** All those who are saying drivers took a business decision and should have thought before joining, aren't Ola/Uber responsible for how they treat their business partners? It's not a only a moral argument but an economic too. They should take care of them and listen to their grievances rather than just taking their own cut and be happy. Otherwise the drivers will protest and these companies will close down. They have caught the drivers in a web, its their responsibility to help them get out of it.
