I placed an order on Flipkart with cash on delivery. A delivery boy called and said my order had arrived, but due to a system update, cash on delivery wasn't possible. He asked me to make an online payment, assuring me that my request would be accepted and my order would be delivered within 5 minutes. I paid him 939 rupees.

After the payment, he said that if I made the online payment, I would get a 3-rupee discount, so I needed to pay exactly 936 rupees for my request to be accepted. He also promised that I would get a 949-rupee refund within 1 minute, along with my parcel. I made the payment of 936 rupees, but neither the parcel arrived nor did I receive the refund.

Then he asked for an additional 1025 rupees, claiming that I would receive a 2000-rupee refund. I refused to pay and asked him to cancel the parcel and return my money. He said I would get my money back within 24 hours. I told him I would file a complaint with the cyber department and demanded my money back quickly. He replied that I had to wait 24 hours. I waited 24 hours, contacted them, but received no response. It's been 3 days with no reply.

What should I do to get my money back? I can't file a complaint for such a small amount as they might not take it seriously. It seems like dealing with the police would be complicated and a waste of time, and I would also lose the money.
