I recently bought a Galaxy Watch from Flipkart (as it had better offers on Flipkart, else I don't prefer to buy from Flipkart). It was an open box delivery where delivery boy opened the box in front of me and everything looked OK. Just checked the product as delivery boys are usually in hurry. 
I received the package after sharing the OTP. I was just randomly checking the box when I came back and noticed that there was a smaller seal (already torn) beneath the larger seal (which delivery boy opened) and the inner seal was not visible directly unless you pull the outer seal completely. 

So it looks like the package was already opened and a larger cover up seal was put on the box. I raised this instantly with Customer Support. Initially they were not considering the request at all but I pushed them since typically we don't expect having a cover up seal right? 
They raised the replacement and since then the due date for resolution is getting pushed again and again. 
I raised it on other platforms as well and Flipkart Support has been giving same copy pasted responses. 
As of now, I have not used the product and just wanted to replace it but seems like Flipkart support and seller are deliberately pushing the resolution date.

I asked them about the policy in case of this particular scenario and they all are clue less. It looks like a scam and fraud where already opened products are being dumped on the customers in the name of new ones.

I have logged a complaint on Consumer Portal and not sure if they are taken seriously by Ecommerce platforms. Do you know if Consumer Portal complaints are acted on by Ecommerce platforms? If yes, then how long (in general) it takes. If it doesn't what other options are available.
