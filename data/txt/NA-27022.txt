She's stupid and has fallen for many other scams before this. All I can say is that she's luckily been smart enough not to send any money to them but it doesn't make it any less annoying.

This time she thinks she's talking to 'Keanu Reeves' he contacted her through Facebook and got her to download and join Telegram. She's also been talking to his 'agent'. 

The only reason I found out was because she called me to talk about my recent car accident and started asking me about anime that heard of but never watched. When she gave me the list to see if I knew any of them I googled each show and they were almost all anime from the early 2000's and two of them were cartoons from India. When I asked why she was asking she told me that if she told me I couldn't tell anyone else. I told her okay and that's when she revealed she was "talking to Keanu Reeves".

I immediately told her it was a scam and that no celebrity would ever contact her through Facebook and that if they asked for money not to give them anything. She said she wasn't going to and that she was just going to play with them.

Later in the call she told me his 'agent' had told her some of the movies he was going to be in soon as if it was some super secret info. I immediately googled all the movies she listed off and told her the list she gave me is public info you can just look up online. She didn't seem concerned at all and was just like "Oh, I didn't know that."

I'm worried what all she has told the scammers. Will they just give up if she doesn't give them money?
