Hi, I am in a senior management position in a consulting company. Presently, there are a number of fake emails being sent to people in India, with job offers. Everything in these emails screams scam to me (poor wages, working conditions that don't align with our country's laws etc) and also the fact that...why would you get a job offer from a company you have never interviewed with or applied to. 

The offer letters are quite sophisticated and do include all our branding, often my name, and signature (fake signature), and for real positions we are advertising on our website.

We have now been contacted tens of times with people thanking us for the offer but being a little confused themselves. We have even had people physically coming into our office looking for people with Indian names, claiming they work for us, but we have never heard of them. 

Anyway.....we don't seem to be the victim, but these random people in India.....but we are still finding this inconvenient and slightly worrying that we are being used for some type of identity fraud. 

We have contacted police and our internet safety advisory group all of which say there is little to do as we are not the target, but the individuals receiving the letters. 

Any thoughts?
