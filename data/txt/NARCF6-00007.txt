So my parents are visiting and we were climbing the steps at Kashi Vishwanath corridor when man stopped us asking us our ethnicity. I don't know if he heard us talking to each other or something, seems unlikely because we weren't talking that muchh it that loudly or if it was how we were dressed, he correctly guessed our ethnicity. He then asked us for some help in our native language. Said their luggage was stolen in the train. Classic scam. BUT then he went on to say stuff like - you can buy us a return ticket (which a scammer would never say) and that they would return the money later, they were uncomfortable asking for help as is etc. He even showed us his aadhar card. We helped him out, not completely but gave him ₹1000 for their meal. That was all the cash we had on us at that moment. They didn't seem satisfied with it which made me doubt them further. Now that I'm thinking about it, his 2+2 isn't adding up. Online money transfer is so common nowadays, he could've easily called a relative for help. Why stop an absolute stranger? How did all of their money vanish? If it was their luggage, they must have their wallets, right? They were two men, a woman, a kid and an elderly man. Although I am trying really hard to write it off as a donation, I can't help but come up with alternate scenarios. So, did we get scammed?
