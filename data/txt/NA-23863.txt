My company ships customized anniversary jackets on completion of 2year, 5 years etc. These jackets are shipped from US/Europe.

This morning, the delivery guy came to deliver the shipment and asked me to pay the customs duty for the same.

It seemed suspicious to me because I didn't see payment status for the duty in the manifest that he showed me. Furthermore, when I inquired about how to make the payment, he told me to do it via either cash or upi to his number.

I refused to accept the shipment and he told me to collect it from local post office. 

I have received my anniversary jacket before and didn't have to pay anything. I discussed this with my colleague and they also confirmed the same.

Does anyone know if this is a known scam or more details of how customs duty payment works. I'm awaiting a clarification from our HR team but thought to ask about this here as well.
