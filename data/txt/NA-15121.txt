The petition states that confidential information is exempt from disclosure under Section 8(1)(a),(e) & (g) of the Right to Information Act, 2005 .

The plea added that this information was sold to third party entities by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways under the Bulk Data Sharing Policy (BDS Policy), which was subsequently scrapped, after concerns over privacy breaches.
