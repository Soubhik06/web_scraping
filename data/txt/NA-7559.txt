Heard counsel for the parties. This is forth bail application filed by the applicant,
who has been arrested on 29.10.2014 and is in Jail in
connection with Crime No.266/2013 registered with Police
Station - Civil Lines, District - Rewa - commonly known
as VYAPAM Examination Scam cases - for the offence
punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 109 and 120-B of IPC; and Section 3(Gha) 1 , 2 / 4 of the M.P.
Manyata Prapt Pariksha Adhiniyam, 1937. The role ascribed to the applicant by the prosecution
is that of a middleman. The applicant was responsible for
facilitating the commission of unfair means during the
examination conducted by VYAPAM by way of
impersonation. The applicant had employed the services of
Umesh Kumar Mourya taking the help of co- accused/middlemen - Pushpendra Yadav and Sunil Tomar. All these persons (co-accused) have been arrested. It is, fairly, stated by the learned counsel for the State, 2 on instructions, that custodial interrogation of the applicant
is no more required. Charge-sheet has also been filed
against the applicant. Further, the investigation against the
applicant is complete in all respects as of now and,
therefore, the presence of applicant is no more required. In
that sense, the link between the accused persons is already
complete. Further, the applicant is not involved in any
other crime of similar type. Besides, the applicant through
counsel undertakes to appear before the Investigating
Officer as and when called for further investigation
including against him as also to punctually appear before
the trial Court to ensure early disposal of the criminal trial.
Further, he will abide all the conditions as may be imposed
by this Court. In the interest of justice, taking overall view of the
matter, we deem it appropriate to grant bail to the applicant
on following strict conditions:- The bail application stands disposed of accordingly.
