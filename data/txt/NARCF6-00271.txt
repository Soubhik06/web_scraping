So this happened today to my friend who was in a client meeting at 10 in morning. She got a mail that her Ajio order is going to be delivered today and after 30 mins a call on her phone ( on a different number than registered number on Ajio) that delivery agent is not able to find address please send on WhatsApp . She was really busy so did as asked and sees all her messages and calls are forwarded to that number 

But luckily she sees it and calls me and within 5 mins we press ##002# and it stops the call forwarding and otp’s. Also she saw no money was transferred to anyone.

I checked on truecaller and to my surprise this has been ongoing from some time and idk where to report. Called on 1930 they said can not do anything until a scam has happened. That’s india for you guys!
