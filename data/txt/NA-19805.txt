Here is a take of his govt from a common person's eyes.

Pros

1. Rapid growth &amp; improvement in transport infrastructure. 2. make in india initiative which enhanced the manufacturing capacity of india in some aspects &amp; increased both domestic &amp; foreign exports of various goods. The export sector has particularly improved.
3. Terrorist &amp; public mass bomb attacks on civilians have declined considerably. Excluding J&amp;K, the overall civilian safety from terrorist attacks is now far better.
4. The no: of international companies &amp; their investments increased.
5. Military has been modernised &amp; equipped with better quality weapons &amp; various other tools to some extent. 
6. Social welfare schemes such as LPG, toilet construction, pension &amp; loan facility were successful to a good extent. 
7. Various online govt services have improved. There has been an overhaul in the system to some extent. Digitalization of various services has gone ahead.
8. Govt medical infrastructure &amp; services have generally improved to some extent.
9. UPI is a huge success of this government. It has revolutionised the payment system in india along with its unparalleled effectiveness &amp; efficiency. Its user friendly system ensure that people from all walks of life can easily use it.
10. India's foreign relations have considerably improved especially with european union &amp; GCC countries.

Demonetisation was a fiasco. It was a ludicrous policy by govt. GST was bungled by govt to a huge extent.

They informed the general public about govt's various issues &amp; faults from time to time. Some of the national medias were usually on their toes to chase &amp; find out the govt's misdeeds. This was especially very evident during the UPA 2 govt. To put it in a nutshell, media was the 4th pillar of democracy during those days. But unfortunately, today that's no more the case.
