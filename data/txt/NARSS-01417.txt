It is because the Communists have made Kerala into a “model state” that the rest of India is so leery of communism.

If a state with that many resources, that much foreign remittance, that much literacy, that much water and this good a history can be reduced to a state with a mediocre economy — the state ranks 11th in income [Comparing Indian States and Countries by GDP per capita] — something is wrong

If God wanted to make a perfect state for prosperity it would be Kerala. He gave it the rains and a long coastline with a peaceful sea. Beautiful ghats run parallel to the ocean and between them both lie fertile plains that form the state’s cities. Food is abundant and tourism potential is immense. The trade winds made it perfect for global traders to come to its shores. Roads and railways connect every city in the state on a straight line. And the state had good political leadership under the Travancore kingdom. The citizens are enterprising and can create great companies everywhere [other than at home].

I’m from Tamil Nadu and I’m envious of Kerala. Look at our beaches - they look pathetic. We are dry and at the mercy of various states for water. Our politicians partook at massive scams. We had to deal with refugees from Sri Lanka and fractious relationships with the neighbour country. And we are substantially underperforming. We don’t even have a real Chief Minister now. But, even we beat Kerala in economic indicators, educational institutions and investments.

The state was a leader in India’s trade and foreign traders would eagerly come to its shores as their first stop in India. Now, the state doesn’t many financial centers or great trade happening. Foreign investors would rather stop at nearby Bengaluru than Kochi.

There used to be a time when educated workers from TN and other places would migrate to Kerala, settling in places like Palakkad. While the state now attracts uneducated workers from Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and those from the north, its own educated people leave its shores to either Bengaluru, Mumbai or to Dubai. Unemployment in a state of bountiful resources is sky high - Not a rank the state wants: Kerala high on unemployment, we need more entrepreneurs govt says.

The state in the past produced scholars such as Adi Sankaracharya who would defeat world’s best. The state enjoys near 100% literacy. Despite all that, the state has not produced a Nobel prize winner and doesn’t have very many education institutions of repute.

The state produced super administrators of the type of VP Menon [the guy who united India], TN Seshan [the guy who terrorized powerful political parties], Kurien [India’s milk man] and Sreedharan [the metro man]. They all got their fame outside their state - at home and the administration is bereft of great projects.

The political scene is one of killing fields as both CPIM and RSS engage in killing each other.

The state could easily be the #1 in India in prosperity. But, undersells itself. Everyone would love to move to a state with relaxing beaches of Kollam and the boats of Alapuzha if the climate is right for investment. The state could have 10 Bengalurus or even 10 Singapores.

The state could be the Virat Kohli [or should I say Sunil Chhetri], but wants to play like a substitute.

Rather than getting defensive the state citizens should actually think why cannot they be the model state who can inspire the rest of us.
