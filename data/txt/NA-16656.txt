TL;DR - Wanted to help create awareness about the issues of online privacy, started a little blog, but unsure **how and what** to write such that the **average non-tech background Indian internet users will read and understand it**. 

**Background:**

Couple of months ago, I started a blog on various topics relating to online privacy to educate people (and learn myself) about the issues. The motivation came from seeing my mom and dad spending time on Facebook going through the buzzfeed like 'news' articles, forwarding random things on Whatsapp, clicking on (obvious) scam links, Aadhaar etc.

I can stop some of that one way or the other, but I want to make them understand. And if I'm able to do that, I should be able to explain it most people, especially those who're new to the entire Internet. I don't wish to convert anyone. I just want them to have sufficient exposure regarding the problems that exist so that they can choose for themselves.

**My motives behind this:**

* Learning in depth about the way people see the issue of privacy, especially the non-tech people like my parents.
* Educating people on how their data is (mis)used and if they're okay with it. If, after learning about this, they think the merits outweigh the drawbacks, who am I to tell otherwise.

**Why do I feel I'm eligible to talk about this:**

* I'm not. But I want to learn.
* I have some background in information security, and having understood the technical side of this issue to some extent, I want to better understand the people and law side of it.

**Problem:**

* Most of my target audience won't be reading 1500 word blog posts that I'm used to writing. How should the topics break down be such that reading an article here is as easy as reading a joke or a whatsapp forward.
* What topics to cover? 
Currently the blog has the following topics
 1. What is internet and online privacy?
 2. How to: Safe browsing on the internet
 3. The Right to privacy in India.
Is this the right way to go? Any better ways to accomplish the above mentioned goals, any suggestions are most welcome. 

To not make this seem like self promotion, I'll not post any links publicily, although if need be, can be shared on pm. 

Thanks guys!
