Today afternoon I got a call from someone claiming to be bluedart executive and said the delivery guy is unable to find my address and I need to call him asap. He shared the number on sms which was *402*number. Naive stupid me dialled that number and a computer voice said that call forwarding has been activated. Before I could figure out what happened I saw that I got logged out of WhatsApp on my phone and I couldn’t log back in. Immediately I understood I was scammed and frantically searched online and disabled the call forwarding. All this happened over 5 minutes. I tried logging back into my WhatsApp but now I got the message “this account is not allowed to use WhatsApp due to spam”.

WTF kind of scam is this? All they wanted was my WhatsApp account?? No money was stolen, no other social accounts were tampered with. How do I recover my WhatsApp account now?
