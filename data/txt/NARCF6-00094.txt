Dear r/madurai, 

recently, there has been an alarming trend of men posting personally identifiable information ("**PII**") of women they see in real life, with hopes of identifying them or meeting them. We cannot allow [r/madurai](https://www.reddit.com/r/madurai) to become unsafe for this reason. 

When people post PII of random women they encounter in real life, it has the potential to land the woman in trouble/harassment/embarassment, even if the woman may not even know such a thing called reddit exists. On the other hand, all users posting here have the privelege of being absolutely anonymous. Posts like this are also in violation of rules 2 and 3 of this subreddit.

Humble request to everybody to not make such posts. As redditors, the biggest weapons you have when you see such posts is to downvote them and report them. The mod team is doing the best we can, but sometimes, it can take time before we respond.

Let's be responsible as an online community in keeping this a safe space for all people.
