Yesterday, my mother got a call from an Indian number on WhatsApp. The guy claimed to be a "CBI" officer and said that your son had been caught in a murder with 8 other boys. My mother, not being technically educated, believed it was a call from the real CBI and got scared. She randomly said that my son doesn't live in India (I was actually in the office in India at that time). Then he said, "No, not your son, your husband (real name of my father) has been caught." This terrified her, and she cut the call and immediately called me and my father. I informed her not to pick up the call again, as it's a scam.

When I came home and called the number, there was a screen sharing request coming from his side (I don't know if it's possible in WhatsApp to get remote access). I declined the request and cut the call; he blocked me immediately.

Now, here are the things that I'm scared of:

1. They knew my father's actual name. (They called my mother "Sharmeela jii," which is not her name, though)
2. They know our city's name.
3. What would happen if he had requested remote access when my mother was alone? She might have clicked "SHARE" because she doesn't know what it is.

Is there any way to report this to the police??

Please educate your parents regarding this scam. The thumb rule is not to pick up the call on WhatsApp if the number is not saved.
