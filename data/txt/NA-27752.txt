I got a call from a nearby town and it was someone claiming to be taking a survey for “Holiday Inn”. They knew my name and when I checked out of an IHG hotel which was NOT a Holiday Inn. His accent was certainly Indian and there was static muffled noise in the background. I looked up the number right away and it was not affiliated with IHG. I asked for confirming info such as where I last stayed and they said they couldn’t say for security reasons. 

Hung up and called the IHG 24/7 support and reported the incident. They said the situation was escalated and would follow up with the hotel I was last at (which they named correctly and the location without me giving anymore info so that felt legit). They also said they checked for suspicious activity on my IHG account which appears to be fine. 

Ofc I’m keeping an eye on my credit, but my question is how did they have my name, number, AND checkout date? The only possibility I can think of is the hotel itself was compromised, either an insider selling info or their data was breached. I haven’t found anything similar on the internet, but wanted to ask here if anyone has heard of something related. TYIA!
