**This raises serious
concerns about how secure CRED really is.**

We recently encountered a scam that everyone needs to be aware of. It starts with an automated call from a suspicious number stating they're from your bank, claiming a large transaction is being made on your credit card. The call then urges you to block the transaction by entering a 6-digit OTP you receive shortly after (see the screenshots).

Here’s the twist: if you enter that OTP, you're not blocking the transaction—you’re actually authorizing it.

In our case, we also received an SMS claiming our CRED account was logged into from a device near Chennai, which we never did.

These calls sound very convincing, as if they’re from your bank.
Under no circumstances should you share the OTP they ask for, or your account could be drained.

Please be vigilant and protect your information. Share this with others to prevent them from falling victim to this scam.
