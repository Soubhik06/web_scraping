# Background

If you don't know about [Dissent Doe](https://twitter.com/PogoWasRight) you ought to follow her right away. She's 
been [voicing](https://www.databreaches.net/) security, and privacy concerns for a while now. 

Sometime last year, a security researcher contacted her about **Schoolcountry**'s website leaking 
thousands of students' personally identifiable information such as names, email addresses, right down to grades.

She wrote to SchoolCountry on August 5, then again on August 10, without any tangible response. 
Or, action. She tried again on December 30, only to get back an autoresponse, and reported the whole
ordeal.[¹][a] 

We had been in touch with her around December, working on another breach[²][b], when she
asked us to help out. So, we did. This is how it went.
