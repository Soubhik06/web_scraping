Buffet Restaurant Scam [Barbeque Nation edition]. Based on experience last weekend at around 8pm.

I will avoid telling it like a long story and tell how the scam works.

1. You visit restaurant reception without booking a table.

2. You get assigned a table manually (not via their system).

3. After your dining is done, you ask the waiter to bring the bill.

4. He/she comes back 10 minutes later, saying there is issue with the bill printing. However he will show you the bill calculation on his mobile phone. 

5. Then he will show you a QR code to make payment.

6. This is when you realise you are being scammed and refuse to pay until a proper GST bill is produced. If you give in to the waiter, and make payment on the QR code it shows, the waiter/their manager pocket the full amount themselves.

7. I sternly refused to pay without bill, he came back promptly with a printed bill 10 minutes later!
