I am an NRI currently living overseas . I was in India during the COVID pandemic and recently relocated back for work. While I was in India, I was a victim of online cybercrime. 

I called the number listed on a website to find out if their liquor shop was open. 

They informed that they provide online delivery services for liquor and I provided my debit card details and OTP required to pay.

After this, my card was deducted 40k. I immediately contacted my bank to block the debit card and also made a report with the Telangana Cyber Crime portal.

With the Cyber Crime report acknowledgement number, I went to my nearest Police Station to file an FIR. 

After the report was filed, I also found out that my bank account had been blocked and I am unable to access it. My bank informed me that they would need approval from the police department to unfreeze the account. 

This is my only bank account in India, I use it to send money to my family for savings, pay EMIs and loans.

I have asked my brother to visit the police station a few times in this matter and they asked him to come back later as the officer is not there. The number bank has provided me is some call center for cybercrimes and they are not answering my calls from overseas. 

What can I do now to get my bank account accessible again ?
