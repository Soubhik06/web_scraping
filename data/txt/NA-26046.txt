I don't know if its a honest mistake OR the pharmacy guy is using this ploy to micro-scam people. So we visited Apollo Clinic &amp; the doc prescribed 2 tablets. The Clinic has attached **Apollo Pharmacy** &amp; we got the tablets there; got back &amp; all-is-well. Now the thing is he did not give me a bill nor did I ask for one since based on phone number, any purchase ever done at anything related to Apollo is synced to their Apollo app history.

Next day I happen to see the purchase history on their app &amp; I saw that 3 tablets are billed. the 3rd tablet which is not part of the prescription is some **DOXT-SL** &amp; amount is 26. I went to the pharmacy later &amp; confronted them but the guy is not in the shift; however they refunded the amount. Initially I thought to file a complaint with the clinic folks but gave him the benefit of doubt when the pharmacy ppl said it could've happened by mistake!
