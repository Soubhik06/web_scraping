J. S. KLAR, PRESIDING JUDICIAL MEMBER :- 2. Complainant Paramjit Singh filed the complaint U/s 12 of

the Consumer Protection Act, 1986 (in short, "the Act") against the

OP on the allegations that he has been an account holder bearing

number 912010066549900 with OP bank since 20.12.2012. His

salary being employee of Government Primary Chak Bhaike as Seva

Incharge has been crediting in his above account with OP. The

amount of Rs.600/- was debited from the above account of

complainant on 22.03.2014. He talked to employee of OP bank on

cell number 9888615172 from his cell phone number 814666105 for

giving this information. He also moved an application to the Manager

of OP bank to close his ATM on 24.03.2014 by visiting the bank

personally. The salary of four months amount of complainant of

Rs.1,10,000/- was credited in his above account on 23.04.2014. The

complainant visited OP bank on 24.04.2014 for withdrawal of the

amount of Rs.1 lakh to meet the domestic needs. OP bank intimated

complainant that it was not possessing such big amount and asked First Appeal No.322 of 2015 3 him to visit afternoon for this purpose. The amount of Rs.40,000/-

each was debited twice from the account of complainant on

24.04.2014. When enquired from Manager of OP bank, he could not

give any satisfactory reply thereto. The complainant told Manager of

OP bank that he got his ATM card blocked on 24.03.2014 by moving

an application to OP, how the amount of Rs.80,000/- was then

debited from his account on 24.04.2014 and it all took place due to

utter negligence of Manager of OP bank, but no fruitful result came

out of it in favour of complainant. He, thus, instituted the complaint

against OP praying that OP bank be directed to credit the amount of

Rs.80,000/- which was wrongly debited from his account, besides to

pay Rs.40,000/- as compensation for mental harassment. 3. Upon notice, OP bank appeared through its Branch

Manager and filed written reply and contested the complaint of the

complainant vehemently. It was averred in preliminary objections

that previous complaint filed by the complainant was dismissed as

withdrawn due to technical defect therein. No such technical defect

has been disclosed by the complainant in the previous complaint.

The complainant has wrongly averred that amount of Rs.600/- was

withdrawn from his account through ATM. It is evident from entry of

Rs.600/- which pertained to the bill deposited by the complainant, in

his statement of account. The complainant misstated the facts in

filing the complaint. The complainant took contradictory pleas in this

case,0 even mobile number given by him is not of correct figures i.e.

814666105. OP bank denied the allegations as false that

complainant visited OP for withdrawing of Rs.1 lakh for accretion of First Appeal No.322 of 2015 4 four months salary. OP bank denied the averments of complainant

as bundle of lies. It was pleaded that the complainant availed the

debit card facility from OP, which he withdrew on 24.03.2014 by

moving an application to the effect that some unknown person took

the details of debit card through fraud from him and he requested OP

bank to block the debit card. The complainant himself is negligent in

sharing the particulars of debit card details with some unknown

person. Vide letter dated 24.04.2014, complainant intimated the OP

bank that amount of Rs.80,000/- has been debited from his account

by unknown person online and hence requested to freeze the

account, whereas vide letter dated 24.03.2014, the complainant only

requested to block his debit card and not the saving bank account.

Thereafter, the complainant himself withdrew the amount of

Rs.30,000/- on 24.04.2015 and the credit balance remained

Rs.921.21 paise in his bank account. Vide letter dated 09.05.2014,

OP bank informed the complainant that amount was withdrawn

through immediate payment service (IMPS) mobile banking and the

transactions carried out were the valid transactions for Rs.40,005.62

paise each and details were only known to the complainant. He was

also informed that he might have divulged his account credentials

including the debit card details and one time password received in

his registered mobile number, which was known only to him. The

complainant was duly informed that person namely Basant Kumar

Yadav account no.3333863435 of Central Bank of India Branch

Jatahi City Deoghar, State Jharkhand was the beneficiary in whose

account the funds were transferred. He was advised to file complaint First Appeal No.322 of 2015 5 against the said beneficiary. Any deficiency in service was denied by

OP bank on its part. On merits, it was reiterated by OP bank that

complainant never asked it for freezing of bank account and only

asked for blocking the debit card. OP prayed for dismissal of the

complaint. 4. The complainant tendered in evidence affidavit and

documents Ex.C-1 to C-7 and closed the evidence. As against it, OP

tendered in evidence affidavit and documents Ex.OP-1 to Ex.OP-16

and closed the evidence. On conclusion of evidence and arguments,

the complaint was accepted by the majority view taken by the

Members of the District Forum Mansa, which is operative order

under law in this case. Aggrieved by this order of District Forum, OP

now appellant has directed this appeal. 5. We have heard the learned counsel for the parties and

have also examined the record of the case carefully. Pleadings of

both the parties have also been appraised by us on the record.

Evidence adduced on record has also been evaluated by us with the

able assistance of counsel for the parties. Complainant Paramjit

Singh tendered his affidavit Ex.C-1 on the record. He testified that he

is account holder with OP bank and his four months salary was

deposited in his account with OP bank. He stated that amount of

Rs.600/- was debited from his account on 22.03.2014 and he gave

due intimation to employee of OP bank from his cell phone. He

visited the OP bank and asked to block his debit card service on

24.03.2014 by moving an application to the Manager of OP bank.

This witness stated that on 24.04.2014, he visited OP bank and First Appeal No.322 of 2015 6 found that amount of Rs.80,000/- has been debited from his account,

each of Rs.40,000/- twice. Manager of OP bank disowned any

deficiency in service. He further testified that he moved an

application to block his ATM on 24.03.2014, but despite that, amount

of Rs.80,000/- was debited from his account. He sought the crediting

of amount of Rs.80,000/- in his bank account with compensation of

Rs.40,000/- for mental harassment. Ex.C-2 is the salary account

detail of complainant and his customer ID number is also recorded in

it. Ex.C-3 the copy of application dated 24.03.2014 moved by

complainant. It is stated in this application by complainant that some

unknown person has taken the particulars of his debit card in a

deceitful manner. He prayed that his ATM card be blocked and new

ATM card be issued to him. He also sought the detail of debiting

amount of Rs.600/- from his account. Ex.C-4 is the application filed

by the complainant on 24.04.2014 regarding debiting the amount of

Rs.80,000/- from his account number 912010066549900 in his

name. This application is addressed to OP bank. Ex.C-5 is the

application filed by the complainant to Manager of OP bank on

28.04.2014 to the effect that he has been sending this application

through registered post. He sought the help of OP bank for refund of

his wrongful withdrawn amount. The statement of account of

complainant is Ex.C-6 on the record. Ex.C-7 is the account ledger

inquiry. It has proved that the amount of Rs.40,005.62 paise each

was debited from the account of complainant on 24.04.2014 on two

occasions. After debiting the amount of Rs.40,005.62 paise twice First Appeal No.322 of 2015 7 and the amount of Rs.30,000/- by complainant himself, the balance

amount has been shown as Rs.921.21 paise in his account. 6. To counter this evidence, OP filed affidavit of Navdeep

Singh Arora, Branch Manager of OP bank on the record. This

witness has testified that complainant availed the debit card facility

from OP and he withdrew Rs.600/-. On 24.03.2014, he submitted a

letter to OP bank that some unknown person took the detail of his

debit card through fraud and requested OP bank to block his debit

card issued to him. This witness stated that complainant himself

shared the particulars of debit card with some unknown person and

was himself negligent in this regard. The complainant intimated OP

been debited from his account online and requested to freeze the

the debit card and not the saving bank account. Vide letter dated

28.04.2014, the complainant disclosed that some of the personal

and security of the account was shared by him with unknown person

and that unknown person withdrew Rs.80,000/- from his bank

account by fraud. Ex.OP-2 is the previous complaint of complainant.

Ex.OP-3 is the order passed in previous complaint of complainant.

Ex.OP-4 and OP-5 are the copies of statement. Ex.OP-6 is the copy

of statement of cash balance. Ex.OP-7 is the copy of statement of

25.04.2014. Ex.OP-9 is the application dated 24.04.2014 from

complainant to OP bank. Ex.OP-10 is the customer request form.

Ex.OP-11 is the account ledger inquiry. Ex.OP-12 is the copy of First Appeal No.322 of 2015 8 application dated 24.03.2014 by complainant to OP bank. Ex.OP-13

is copy of application dated 24.04.2014 from complainant to OP

bank for freezing the saving account of complainant. Application was

moved by the complainant to OP bank for the withdrawal of the

balance amount. Ex.OP-15 is the reply from OP bank to

complainant. 7. On conclusion of evidence and arguments of counsel for

the parties, we find that the order given by the majority view of the

District Forum by accepting the complaint of the complainant and

directing OP to credit the amount of Rs.80,000/- in the account of

complainant within 30 days failing which to pay interest @9% per

annum till realization is sustainable in the eye of law. The dispute in

this case boils down to the point as to whether the amount of

Rs.80,000/- has been debited due to negligence of the OP bank or

not. Vide application Ex.C-3 dated 24.03.2014, the complainant

requested OP bank to de-activate ATM card and to issue new one

because some unknown person extracted some information from

him by means of deception practiced upon him. The amount of

Rs.600/- debited from the account of complainant is reflected in the

statement of account of complainant on the record. It is not disputed

fact that complainant asked OP to deactivate ATM card on

24.03.2014 by moving a written request. The submission of OP bank

is that complainant took contradictory pleas in the present complaint,

viz. a viz. in the earlier complaint. The complainant filed previous

complaint no.75 of 2014, which was withdrawn by him on

04.07.2014, vide Ex.OP-16 due to some technical defect with First Appeal No.322 of 2015 9 permission to file fresh one on the same cause of action. The

complainant has to apply for deactivation of his ATM card due to

disclosure of some information by deception on him to some other

person. The submission of OP is that complainant mentioned nine

digit mobile number in his affidavit Ex.C-1 and it is without any

substance. 8. We hold that the transfer of money has been done

through internet banking system in this case. There is categorical

version of the complainant that he has not taken any such facility of

net banking from OP. The money was transferred form

complainant's saving bank account to some unidentified person.

The stand of the OP bank is that this was possible only through two

password which was delivered by bank to complainant, as per his

own instructions. We find force in the submission of counsel for the

complainant that complainant has not moved any request duly

signed by him for getting any password for internet banking from OP

bank. The amount of Rs.40,000/- was withdrawn each twice through

IMPS, vide Ex.C-7, the statement of account. The customer can

transfer and receive fund via IMPS by means of their registered

mobile and mobile money identifier (MMID). The complainant has

not been enjoying the facility of internet banking and this fact is

proved on the record. Thus, there is no question of transfer of funds

through IMPS by the complainant. It is possible through an MMID,

which is seven digit number issued by the bank upon due

registration. The person who wants to send money and person, who

wants to receive the money through IMPS using mobile number and First Appeal No.322 of 2015 10 MMID should have them for indulging in internet banking funds

transfer. We find force in the submission of counsel for the

complainant that complainant has not requested for availing the

facility of internet banking in this case from OP. No MMID was

provided to complainant and hence question of transfer of money

through IMPS is out of consideration. As per statement of account

Ex.C-7, the transaction has been done through IMPS and not

through ATM card. There is no document on the file proved by OP

bank to substantiate this fact that they issued the password of seven

digit to complainant. The complainant already applied for

deactivation of ATM card on 24.03.2014 through application Ex.C-3

to OP. The complainant wanted to protect his money from undue

phishing. There is nothing on the record when the OP bank actually

deactivated the ATM card. The document to this effect is missing by

the OP, when it deactivated the ATM card of the complainant to

protect his money. We find OP bank negligent in not taking the

proper precaution to save the amount of the complainant. We hold

that law relied upon by OP bank in "Punjab National Bank Versus

Lt. Col. Jagdeep Gahlot" decided on 16.05.2014 in RP no.2139

of 2013 decided by the National Commission would not be

applicable in this factual controversy of the cited case and is not pari

materia with the facts of the case in hand. In the circumstances of

the case, we uphold the order passed by the majority view of the

Members of the District Forum in accepting the complaint of the

complainant by directing the appellant to credit Rs.80,000/- in the

account of complainant within 30 days, failing which this amount will First Appeal No.322 of 2015 11 carry interest @9% p.a. till final realization and to pay the cost of

litigation of Rs.2500/-. The minority view of the President of District

Forum is not a correct one. 9. As a result of our above discussion, the order of District

Forum passed by majority of the Members holding OP bank deficient

in service is upheld in this appeal. The appeal of the appellant is

without merit and the same is hereby dismissed. 10. The appellant had deposited the amount of Rs.25,000/-
