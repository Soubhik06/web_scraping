I'm interested in finance/ banking sector but opening a firm is so ridiculously difficult it's like they inly want a handful of (VERY rich and influential) people to even try.

I fully understand the risks involved with this sector and I appreciate the laws but some requirements are frankly ridiculous. Every firm gets investors etc anyway.

1) You have to have a background in finance for it. Why? You don't need a medical degree to open a hospital. As long as you hire people with proper degrees and follow guidelines isn't it okay??

2) You have to have a very high net worth. Why? So only rich people should start such firms? What happened to encouraging startups? I personally have a net worth in crores and I qualify but this is bs.

3) Opening a bank requires a capital of atleast 500 crores this is laughable. A regional/ zonal bank can easily start with 10 crores or even less. The bank size increases with deposits and investments.

These people say they behave like this because everyone is trying to scam but in reality they just want to keep the rich people richer. You literally can't justify this amount of red tape. Even hospitals don't require this bullshit even though a lot of scams happen there.
