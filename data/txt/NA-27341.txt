I recently got a call from GIVA offering assured gifts without asking for any OTPs initially, which seemed genuine. However, they later requested a payment of 5000 rupees to their business account via GPay, making me realize it was a scam. After reaching out to GIVA for confirmation, I received an email, leaving me surprised by the advancements in scam techniques these days. Please BE AWARE GUYS...
