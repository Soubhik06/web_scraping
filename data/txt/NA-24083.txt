So I’ve been actively looking for a job for a few weeks now and today someone called me saying they’re from Naukri.com. He said I’ve been shortlisted for job in Tata motors and the interview will be conducted today afternoon but only if I pay 2,500. So, the moment he said that I had a few doubts and when I expressed it he said he’ll reduce the amount to 500, to which I agreed but since the payment didn’t go through he started abusing me and using very bad derogatory words, which forced me to hang up. How common are frauds like these, because when I searched the number on true caller it seemed fishy and the account details also didn’t seem very legitimate, I’m glad I didn’t post anything. Be careful guys, don’t fall into the traps.
