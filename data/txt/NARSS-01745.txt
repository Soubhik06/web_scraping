Hi Guys,

I'm here to share one of the worst mistakes of my life.

Someone recommended me a college for degree in Yoga. Excited about continuing my job and doing my masters.

I visited the college with my friend and inquired about the course. The principal mentioned they would conduct one month of online classes before the exams. On the same day, I paid the full semester fees of ₹15,000 (a mistake I regret; always pay only the registration fee initially).

They held only two online classes: one for introductions and one related to the course. After that, nothing.

On exam day, I was shocked when the teacher distributed the answer sheets along with the question papers. I refused to take the answers, but others did. I thought, "Let them cheat; I will focus on my studies." I think rest of the people were aware about this!

During practical exams, I met a guy who told me he was writing papers for a girl from Bangalore, charging her ₹5,000 per exam. Despite the rampant cheating, I passed the exams.

For the next semester, the college asked for fees again. Doubtful about continuing, I consulted a friend who said this situation is common in Uttarakhand and advised me to complete the degree. So, I paid ₹10,000 this time.

The second semester began, and again, the college asked for fees. I paid ₹10,000 but this time, I didn't study anything because I paid the fees just one day before the examination and they didn't provide any study material. I started writing with the answers they provided, feeling extremely embarrassed and guilty. I kept asking my fellow students, "How can you write like this? Don't you feel ashamed?" After the exam, I couldn't sleep for two days because of what had happened.

During the exams, I met new students who were directly writing second-sem exams despite not being there in the first semester. They revealed that the college staff or somebody else wrote exams on their behalf for extra charges.

On my next day, I decided to record the exam center, capturing staff distributing answers and marking absent students as present.

After the exams, I confronted the principal about the fraudulent practices and demanded a refund. He refused, I threatened legal action, and he asked me to return the next day with all the pay slips. Btw he returned me second sem fees.

Guys, please avoid Uttarakhand colleges if it is college like Patanjali then is completely fine. Second you can enroll for this course it is legit https://youtu.be/V8z5KDEPHoY. And you have to study for it.
