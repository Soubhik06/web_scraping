One of the top posts on /r/India today is about how [a retailer almost got scammed because of an SMS with grok.io hyperlink](https://np.reddit.com/r/india/comments/ajxrq8/a_small_retailer_nearby_got_robbed_off_of_rs/).

And now I think why do more and more scams take place only in India? There are as many intelligent retards who are always looking for IT systems that can be exploited (like grok.io tunneling), as there are innocent and gullible people who fall for it.

Now, we can't do anything about the intelligent retards, but at least we can do something to educate the gullible? Isn't it a great idea if DoT makes it mandatory to have a basic crash course or training before buying a SIM card in which all these things will be explained in detail?

If only that retailer had undergone such training while getting his phone connection, he would have known to not open that link today. Imagine how many innocent people will be saved from having their wealth robbed if they were educated and knew about these things beforehand?
