Two years ago, my father invested everything into a retail company, helping set up 10 stores and mentoring my cousin—who is two years older than me—on how to run the business. Since I was a student abroad, I had no direct involvement, leaving my cousin in full control.

Initially, he reassured my father that a ₹1.6 crore deposit from the brand partner was pending. Over time, this number mysteriously shrank—first to ₹80 lakh, then to ₹60 lakh. After months of avoiding calls and giving vague responses, he now claims there was never any deposit at all. Instead, we received a legal notice from the brand’s lawyer demanding ₹1.2 crore from our company.

While my father built steady rental income in his town to secure the company’s future, my cousin exploited it under the pretense of financial distress, draining even fixed profits and exhausting security deposits. Meanwhile, he was diverting store rental income into his personal account, all while falsely claiming the business was struggling.

To make matters worse, he secretly transferred the company’s registered office lease to his personal business—along with interiors, furniture, and air-conditioning—without compensating the company. We only discovered this when we visited the office for a financial review. When confronted, he provided fake documents, then fled to another city when pressed further.All the company MOU and net banking passwords or check power was held by him solely never signed a check only signed the lease agreements for rental income as these properties were in my town. 

Now, tenants are demanding refunds, landlords have withheld deposits due to unpaid dues, and our company is being dragged into legal disputes—all while my cousin continues to cover his tracks. I need legal advice on how to expose his fraud and protect our interests before it’s too late. How should I proceed?
