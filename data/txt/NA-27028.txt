The caller claimed to be from my bank, needing to verify my credit card details for security reasons. Although she knew some information about me, I was suspicious. Real banks never ask for private details over the phone, and her urgency felt like a red flag.

To stall, I pretended to fetch my card details, giving me time to think. This was clearly a scam, and I needed a way to protect myself. That's when I discovered VCCGenerator, a tool that creates fake credit card numbers. Perfect for situations like this!

**Discovering VCCGenerator**

After the call, I searched online for a solution and found VCCGenerator on Product Hunt. This tool generates fake credit card numbers that look real but aren’t linked to any bank account. It also has a validator to check if a card number is valid without it being real. This means I could give the scammer a fake number without risking my actual details.

**Turning the Tables**

Armed with VCCGenerator, I decided to turn the tables. Here’s how I did it:

1. I opened [VCCGenerator.org](http://VCCGenerator.org) and selected a Visa card, as that’s what the scammer asked for.
2. I chose India as the country and picked a random bank.
3. Within seconds, I had a fake card number, expiry date, and CVV code.

I went back to the phone call and gave the scammer these fake details. She tried to verify them, but of course, they didn’t work. I could sense her frustration.

**The Confrontation**

Eventually, the scammer realised the details were fake. Surprisingly, she started to explain her situation. She was a mother struggling to make ends meet, working for a boss who paid her very little. This job, though unethical, was her only option.

This revelation was a mix of relief and sadness. I protected myself from a scam but also understood the human side of the story. It reminded me that people in desperate situations might resort to wrong actions, though it doesn't justify their behavior.

I advised her to seek a better job and hoped she would find a way to support her child without deceiving others.
