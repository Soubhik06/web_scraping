Rupeezy, a leading digital investment platform, has introduced Specialised Investment Funds (SIF), a new investment category designed to offer High-Net-Worth Individuals (HNIs) access to sophisticated investment strategies previously available only through high-ticket Portfolio Management Services. The ISO 27001-certified platform, registered with SEBI and AMFI, aims to democratise institutional-grade investment solutions for India’s growing affluent investor segment.
Decoding Specialized Investment Funds
Introduced by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) on April 1, 2025, SIFs represent a new regulated framework that allows Asset Management Companies to launch advanced, strategy-based investment schemes. These funds provide retail and HNI investors access to institutional-quality strategies, including long-short positioning and dynamic asset allocation, with a minimum investment requirement of Rs.10 lakh per AMC.

Strategic Investment Approach
Portfolio managers employ sophisticated investment techniques such as hedging, sector rotation, and tactical asset allocation to adapt to changing market dynamics across different cycles.

Derivative Trading Capabilities
Unlike conventional mutual funds, SIFs possess the flexibility to take unhedged short positions up to 25% of the portfolio, enabling fund managers to protect against market downturns.
Pooled Investment Structure
SIFs operate as pooled investment vehicles, either open-ended or interval funds, offering professional fund management with greater flexibility in security-level allocations.
Unified Portfolio Tracking
The Rupeezy platform enables investors to monitor SIF performance alongside their existing stock and mutual fund portfolios in real-time through a single integrated dashboard.

Investment Rationale for HNI Investors
Specialized Investment Funds present several advantages for high-net-worth investors seeking sophisticated investment solutions:
Reduced Entry Barriers
SIFs provide access to PMS-caliber investment strategies at a ₹10 lakh entry point, significantly lower than the typical ₹50 lakh minimum required for traditional Portfolio Management Services.
Portfolio Protection Mechanisms
Short-selling capabilities built into SIF structures provide downside protection during market corrections, helping preserve capital in volatile conditions.
Enhanced Asset Diversification
Investors gain exposure to alternative investment classes, including Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs), Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs), and various derivative instruments.
Tax Optimization
SIFs maintain the same taxation structure as conventional mutual funds, offering better tax efficiency compared to several alternative investment products.
