Then i logged out of all the accounts with the link provided in sms.

Paytm login detected from a new device at 11:46 PM, 03 Oct. Not you? To logout from all devices, click: https://ap.p-y.tm/PzfH or report fraud @1800120130

As I have logged out of all the paytm logins and transferred my salary to a non-linked bank account, I continued the conversation.

I wonder how fraudster able to read OTP while the call in progress. Any thoughts..
