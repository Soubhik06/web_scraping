I was watching a YouTube channel where the interviewer randomly asks people on the street what they do, how much they earn, and how someone can build a career in that field.

There is a girl who says she works at a European Investment Banking firm in business development, where she doesn't earn much but has the potential to earn a lot if she plays her cards right.

The comments section is full of troll comments like 'lol, tell people it's just a call center job,' '4 lakhs? A govt peon earns more than that,' 'lol, you basically scam people to sell insurance,' etc., and many more.

Like WTF? Why is working at a government job, which earns a similar amount or more, always looked at as a superior assignment? Why do we need to demean a call center job just to look cool?

I work at a boutique investment bank in the strategy and business development team in the investment banking division. Yes, we talk to a lot of potential clients throughout the day, we try to build relationships in order to get the business, and we do a lot more than just calling people. That's a typical day for a front-office banker. And if you are not working at a big bank, then you'll probably start with a salary of 4-5 lakhs.

The irony is, most of the people who are trolling are most likely jobless.
