22.02.2025 had lodged an FIR before the Officer-in-Charge of Kharupetia Police
Station, inter-alia, alleging that he has been subjected to fraud in respect of
Sovereign Gold Bonds purchased by the informant ICICI Demat account. The
name of the petitioner is also shown as an accused as a local agent of the ICICI
Direct Demat account. 4. It is stated in the FIR that the informant has deposited an amount of
Rs. 3,00,000/- through the local agents including the present petitioner. 5. However, he received the profits only for four months and thereafter,
no profit was paid to him. Neither he was allowed to withdraw the invested
money by the ICICI Securities. 6. The learned counsel for the petitioner has submitted that the petitioner
is in no way involved in the offence alleged in the FIR as the FIR would reveal
that the allegation is against the company ICICI Securities. 7. The petitioner is only one of the local agents of Vintage Global
Marketing. 8. The learned counsel for the petitioner has submitted that the petitioner
is ready to cooperate in the investigation. 9. On the other hand, the learned Additional Public Prosecutor has
opposed the grant of any interim protection at this stage and has called for the
case diary. 10. Considering the nature of acquisition levelled in the FIR, the perusal of
the case diary would be necessary, hence call for the same, fixing 25.06.2025
