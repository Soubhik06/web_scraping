There are a large number of sponsored taxi aggregators that are involved in BLR-one way drops to neighbouring cities.Typically appear first result on google search.
The MO is to ask for an advance and once you realise that it wasn’t the intended operator, they refund 50% of the value and pocket the rest.www.dropstaxi.in is one among them.
No one way taxi aggregator asks for an advance- checked every registered ones today. As always check for secured(padlock) websites and if it’s a not secure one, then it’s not.
