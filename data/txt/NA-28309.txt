Best Technical Skills that Employers Looks
The work of cybersecurity requires practical tech expertise. Here are the must-haves:
Networking Basics: Learn about the flow of information over networks. Understand protocols such as TCP/IP, traffic sniffing, such as Wireshark. This is a test of UK firms during interviews.
Programming Knowledge: Do Python or Bash scripting. It is critical in automating threat detection or malware analysis. Simple projects are a common subject in a cybersecurity course.
Cloud Security: With widespread use of AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, securing cloud environments is a must. Skills in identity and access management (IAM), encryption, and cloud configuration security help prevent large-scale breaches.
Ethical Hacking: Practice penetration testing with Metasploit or Burp Suite. There are other certifications, such as CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker), which will demonstrate that you can locate and rectify vulnerabilities.
Incident Response: Respond to breaches habitually. Monitor the logs and react to the alerts with the help of SIEM tools such as Splunk.
