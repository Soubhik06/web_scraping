Heard counsel for the parties. This bail application has been filed by the applicant, who
has been arrested on 23.3.2015 and is in Jail in connection with
Crime No.390/2011 registered with Police Station Gopalganj,
District Sagar - commonly known as VYAPAM Examination
Scam cases - for the offence punishable under Sections 417 , 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 474 and 120-B of IPC and also under Section 3(Gha) (2) / 4 of the M.P. Manyata Prapt Pariksha
Adhiniyam, 1937. The role ascribed to the applicant by the prosecution is
that of a middleman. The applicant facilitated the beneficiary -
Sanjeev Kustwar to commit unfair means during the PMT
examination 2009 conducted by VYAPAM. The applicant is
alleged to have arranged for impersonator - Lavlesh Singh,
who appeared in place of original candidate - Sanjeev Kustwar
(beneficiary) in the said examination. It is not disputed that beneficiary and the impersonator
have already been arrested. Learned counsel for the State on instructions submits that
investigation against the applicant is complete in all respects.
 No further investigation is pending against the applicant nor
custodial interrogation of the applicant is necessary in
connection with the subject crime. It is stated that investigation
is complete and presence of the applicant is no more necessary,
coupled with the fact that the applicant through counsel
undertakes to appear before the Investigating Officer as and
when called upon to do so for further investigation including
against him as also to punctually appear before the trial Court
to ensure early disposal of the criminal trial. Since the applicant is also involved in four other crimes
of similar type, being Crime No.898/2013, 138/2013, 539/2013
and 12/2013, in the interest of justice, taking overall view of
the matter, we are inclined to grant bail to the applicant by
imposing heavy bail bonds on following strict conditions:- The bail application stands disposed of accordingly.
