--- POST TEXT ---

Whatsapp is truly a double-edged sharp sword with infinite abilities. People often abuse/misuse it in their zeal to overuse it. A while ago there were grapevines and rumor mongering due to reckless unverified forwards. Meta did a bit of thing to restrict forwarding limit to 1 (for many times forward) and 5 (as a normal limit for Asia Pacific)/10 (elsewhere).

Yesterday I found one post in our Ward Councillor's welfare community group. One person had inadvertently shared a settlement agreement in full. It seems he was intending to forward to some one else but left it to reach the group. Left him a private message yesterday for which he reverted he will delete it. But as of today morning it is still there. Our councillor is the sole admin but he used to proactive in responding. Unfortunately he was travelling over the weekend. And by this time the group has scrolled through a lot of messages and he might miss seeing the old one or if he just clears his history (or might have) it means the ability to delete for everyone is gone for ever.

I tried to use Report to Whatsapp. How else to protect the victim from a potential identity theft or fraud for having leaked one of their confidential document? Should a copy of the document be sent to registation office along with a screenshot of the message (with timestamp) indicating potential leakage so that any wilful fraud can be stopped? I admit I could have closed my eyes and scrolled down but on a good citizenship/netizenship gesture wanted to stop a potential fraud from letting get completed.

People complain of land grabbing and other embezzlement frauds besides lack of proactiveness by Law Enforcement towards such complaints. How are we to deal with such people who have left their door open in the night to sleep as a welcome to burglars!
