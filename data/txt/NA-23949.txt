Today I booked an auto on Namma Yatri App from HSR to BTM 2nd stage.

The auto driver came to pick up and straight away asked for an extra 30 over the fare in app. 
I denied him, saying I have already added 20rs tip on the app. Over that another +30 would be too much. 

He asked me for the OTP and started the ride.

Barely 500mtr (10% of my overall distance) from where he had picked me, he stopped the auto, stating that there is no CNG. He ended the ride and the app showed around 65% of the original fare as my final fare for this 500mtr joyride.

While I was pissed off about paying this fee for almost nothing, I didn't want to spoil my weekend and just paid the amount, almost feeling bad when he had to push his auto.

NOW COMES THE SCAM PART!!!

Guess what the driver did next..
He pushed his Auto for a few meters till the nearest signal. Took a turn, started his auto (CNG got automatically filled), and just drove off. 

How do I know this?
I walked a little to try if I could get a running auto till the same signal.

How do you even stay alert for such scams. 😅
