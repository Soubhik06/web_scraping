omeone called me claiming they were from CDSL raising awareness on companies miss selling insurance linked investments. I heard them out. Then I complained I have a similar policy and want to close it, but the company has asked me to visit their office. She said she is registering a complaint and that someone will call me from the insurance company. She then asked me my date of birth which I declined. She said she understands and that she is registering a complaint. Fast forward to today, a guy called me and informed me he is from TATA AIA. And received a complaint on miss selling. I told him the situation, and the returns I was promised. He said it’s a case of cheating and they will register a complaint against the bank representative that sold me the policy. He then said he needs to take a look at the policy, and asked for my date of birth. This time I gave the DOB. Then I received a login OTP, he asked for the OTP, and I declined. He insisted, I declined again. He then changed his tone completely, and said, you are only creating this drama because you can’t afford the policy and don’t want to pay the premium. I was taken aback and was clear on Thai is probably a scam, so I disconnected the call. I confirmed this with TATA AIA customer care, and they informed me there is no way we operate this way, or will ever ask for this information. 

Usually I am really cautious of revealing sensitive info, but got taken for a sucker this time( well second time, first was when I decided to buy this policy). These people are getting extremely creative with the scams, so beware!
