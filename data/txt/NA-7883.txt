Applicant Ajeet is in judicial custody in

FIR/Case Crime No.174 of 2022, dated 28.07.2023,

under Sections 420 , 120-B IPC, Police Station Kotwali

Pithoragarh, District Pithoragarh. He has sought his

release on bail. 2. Heard learned counsel for the parties and

perused the record. 3. According to the FIR, the informant was

telephonically threatened, some obscene videos were

sent to him and thereafter, he was blackmailed and

induced to deliver `4,34,3000/-. 4. Learned counsel for the applicant would

submit that the applicant is innocent; he has passed

intermediate examination; his maternal uncle's son

Pawan was close to their family; they wanted to

purchase some property, therefore, the identification 2 details, Aadhar Card of the family members are with the

Pawan; Pawan had got `3 Lacs deposited in the account

of the applicant and subsequently, took money also; the

applicant wants to assist the police so that real culprit

may be apprehended. 5. Learned State counsel would submit that the

informant was threatened on obscene video call and he

was cheated to the tune of `4,34,3000/-. 6. It is argued that `3 Lacs was deposited in the

applicant's account, which he withdrew and deposited

in various other accounts. The account details have

also been filed. 7. It is a very serious case. A retired person was

shown obscene videos calls and he was thereafter,

blackmailed and threatened to deliver money, which he

did. Undoubtedly if released, the applicant would again

repeat such offences. Therefore, there is no reason to

release him on bail. Accordingly, the bail application of

the applicant deserves to be rejected. 8. The bail application is rejected. (Ravindra Maithani, J.)
