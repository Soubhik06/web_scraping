--- POST TEXT ---

I was ordering a laptop from Flipkart. Wanted to exchange a 7y old laptop of mine. Entered the specifications into the exchange prompt on flipkart and the algorithm told me I'd get 10k for it. There was a disclaimer saying that the person inspecting the device may reduce the value on inspection in which case we would have to pay the remainder back. Skeptical knowing how old it was, expecting around 3k reduction. Delivery came (early, in fact). On inspection, the delivery guy quoted 4k as the value of the laptop. Shocked at the price discrepancy between the algorithm and the real value. Feel like it's a scam, they are quoting high price for the exchange item so we buy from them but then reduce the value of the item on inspection. We requested a refund, hope Flipkart does better service
