There is a barren HUDA landif front of my lane of my house (gaddha). All the residents of sector used to dump their old tv, chair ,tables and liquor bottles which made our living in that lane a hell. We used to complain it to RWA for a lot many times I guess for about 3 years but no actions were taken so we decided to convert that gaddha into a small personal park on our own personal expenses without any help of RWA. 4-5 houses of our lane invested more than 1 lakh to convert that land into a very great park of our own. But now some people of Sector has started to envy us as we created a personal park so they played some little politics and now all of sudden president of RWA has decided to put a pump operating system in that very place of the whole sector. It was decided that all the members of our lane and RWA will be having a meeting on Saturday but today in morning they dug up the whole park without even consulting with any of us
Is there any lawyer or member of MCF that can help us out with this ?
