TLDR; Got a call from FedEx employee saying I'm sending illegal parcel to Taiwan; connects me to Mumbai cyber crime that he has on speed dial for some reason; Tried to scam his way but did not really work. 

I received a call from someone claiming to be a FedEx employee named Ishant Sharma ( Snega, indha per la dhan naa vizhundhen) from mumbai telling me that a parcel which I sent from Mumbai to Taiwan is seized by customs because it contained MDMA. 

He told me the parcel contains 5 passports, 6 ICICI Bank Credit cards, 2 Kg clothes, 1 laptop &amp; 150 Gms of MDMA but when I said that I've never been to mumbai &amp; I didn't send this parcel, he said I need to raise a compliant against this at mumbai andheri police station &amp; gave me additional details about parcel receiver, parcel tracking number &amp; his employee ID. Man had the Mumbai police on his speed dial, impressive tactic. 

Anyway, this police woman spoke to me and said she's transferring the call to skype to record my oral statement because I'm in chennai and i won't be able to go to Mumbai. So, I get on skype, speak to this cop who goes by the name 'Ajay Kumar Bansal', he asks me questions about my recent travel, if i have enemies who could possibly do this blah blah, proceeds to ask me what i do for a living and by this time I've established that I'm a lawyer and not one thing he's telling me is scaring me. 

So, man just asks me to file a complaint with the Mumbai police online and ends the call. Haven't received a call back from him, as yet. Post all this, I read up online about Aadhar leak and if there are other instances like mine, only to realize that this whole thing is STAGED! The FedEx call, the cop, the Skype call, EVERYTHING. And this has been happening since [jan](https://twitter.com/geetiga/status/1612763859168923648?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw%7Ctwcamp%5Etweetembed%7Ctwterm%5E1612763859168923648%7Ctwgr%5E956afe4e5f7e67490cd6c4b263f571df997af6ef%7Ctwcon%5Es1_&amp;ref_url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.news18.com%2Fnews%2Findia%2Ffedex-mumbai-police-a-parcel-that-never-was-how-you-can-stay-safe-from-latest-jamtara-model-6808477.html)?!!?

I've now filed a complaint with both mumbai police and the central cybercrime portal. 

Beware, lads! This could literally happen to anyone which is 10x scary.
