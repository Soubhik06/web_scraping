Heard Shri Suresh Chandra Dwivedi, learned counsel for the applicants and learned A.G.A for the State. Perused the record. The present application under Section 482 Cr.P.C. has been filed for quashing the summoning order dated 18.11.2019 passed by the Special Judge, S.C./S.T. Act/Fast Track Court, Sambhal at District-Chandausi as well as entire criminal proceedings in Complaint Case No.355 of 2019, under Sections 354 , 504 , 506 I.P.C. and Section 3(1)(R)(S) of SC/ST Act, Police Station-Rajpura (Smt. Shashibala vs. Girish and others). Submission made by learned counsel for the applicants is that on account of certain mischief and fraud committed by the opposite party no.2 and others with regard to certain land, the applicant no.1 Panjabi had lodged an F.I.R. against opposite party no.2 and certain other persons u/s 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 120-B I.P.C. on 3.4.2017 in Case Crime No.264 of 2017 at Police Station-Gunnaur, District-Bhim Nagar, in which after investigation the Police submitted charge sheet and the trial is going on against opposite party no.2. It has been contended by learned counsel for the applicants that opposite party no.2 got infuriated by this and she in retaliation to it has lodged the present case concocting imaginary story in order to falsely implicate the applicants. The police after completing investigation has submitted final report and the same was protested by the opposite party no.2, which was treated as complaint and the learned Magistrate proceeded with the case. After recording statements u/s 200 and 202 Cr.P.C., the learned Magistrate by impugned order dated 18.11.2019 summoned the applicants to face the prosecution u/s 354 , 504 , 506 I.P.C. and Section 3(1) Da, Dha of SC/ST Act. Submission of counsel for the applicants is that the present prosecution is nothing but an arm twisting to kneel down the applicants and dilute the earlier criminal prosecution faced by the opposite party no.2. Keeping in view the facts and circumstances of the case, the matter requires consideration. Notice on behalf of opposite party no.1 has been accepted by learned A.G.A. Issue notice to opposite party no.2 through C.J.M., Sambhal at an early date. Let a counter affidavit be filed within a period of four weeks by learned A.G.A. as well as opposite party no.2 and rejoinder affidavit, if any, may be filed within two weeks thereafter. List after expiry of the aforesaid period. Till the next date of listing, no coercive action shall be taken against the applicants pursuant to impugned order dated 18.11.2019 passed by the Special Judge, S.C./S.T. Act/Fast Track Court, Sambhal at District-Chandausi in Complaint Case No.355 of 2019, under Sections 354 , 504 , 506 I.P.C. and Section 3(1)(R)(S) of SC/ST Act, Police Station-Rajpura (Smt. Shashibala vs. Girish and others), with the condition laid down by Hon'ble Apex Court in Asian Resurfacing of Road Agency Pvt. Ltd. and other Vs. Central Bureau of Investigation decided on 28.03.2018, which reads as under :- It is made clear that in the event, if the pleadings are not exchanged or any non-co-operation by contesting parties in filing their counter version, it would be open for the Court to pass any suitable order at the end of abovementioned upper limit of six months. Order Date :- 2.1.2020
