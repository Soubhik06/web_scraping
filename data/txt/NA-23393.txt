There's a huge scam going on in which equity buyers get SMS from some companies suggesting to buy stocks. 

I, on 18th January received text from 3 different companies -&gt; angelstock.in , sirfpaisa.biz, stock-hifi.com all suggesting me to buy shares of Abhishek Infraventures . The stock was 58 Rs, then, they all were suggesting targets of 100+ in a week and the very next day stock dipped to Rs. 46 and next day to that it went down to Rs. 37.15 , so people who were tricked probably lost 35% of their investment in just 2 days.

Stay the fuck away from these texts, companies and calls all together. Bad mouth these companies wherever you can :)

** I did not invest in it, but I'm sure someone might have done **
