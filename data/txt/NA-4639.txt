rights. While it serves as a crucial tool to prevent the misuse of the power of arrest

and protects innocent individuals from harassment, it also presents challenges in

maintaining a delicate balance between individual rights and the interests of

justice. The tight rope we must walk lies in striking a balance between

safeguarding individual rights and protecting public interest. While the right to

liberty and presumption of innocence are vital, the court must also consider the

gravity of the offence, the impact on society, and the need for a fair and free

investigation. The court's discretion in weighing these interests in the facts and

circumstances of each individual case becomes crucial to ensure a just outcome. 14. This Court vide order dated 18.07.2024 passed in CRM-M-32750-
