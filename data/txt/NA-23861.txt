The Premise: I was at a mall yesterday and was waiting for my auto at around 12 am. The auto driver agreed to drop me for the same price as what’s shown in Namma Yatri. Because it was drizzling and the driver was far away, I agreed to go with this fella and cancelled the ride on the app.

The Scam: After we reached my destination, I asked him for UPI QR code and made the payment ( Rs 335/-). Right after i made the payment he said he hasn’t received the money. After arguing back n forth, he asked me to scan again the QR to check, I was hesitant but did it. This time the QR code was different (The name of the person was different) I realised he has swapped the QR code. We argued for over 20 mins and at the end I didn’t want to create a scene in front of my apartment as its a quiet neighbourhood. So i sent him 335 again. I was all alone and he was a rowdy, couldn’t pick beef. I have raised an HDFC UPI complaint saying the money was sent to a wrong person. Although it’s a small amount, it sucks.

TL;DR: Auto driver swapped UPI QR codes while doing payment, lied about not receiving money and had to pay twice.
