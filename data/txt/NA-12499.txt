 Trial Court is excessive? Re: Question No.(i): 11. It is the contention of the defendants that the works

having been entrusted to the plaintiff in the year 2006-07 and

2007-08 and the suit having been filed on 26.06.2014, the suit

further relevant to note that various correspondences have

been exchanged between the plaintiff and defendants with

regard to the bills payable by the defendants. 13. It is forthcoming from the material on record that the

defendants have conducted various proceedings including

inspection and audit with regard to various alleged irregularities

in the carrying out of work by various contractors. However, it

is forthcoming that there is no denial of work of the plaintiff by

the defendants. Even in the testimony of DW.1 as well as the

exhibits produced as Exs.D1 to D11, the defendants have not

placed on record any material where they have specifically

disputed the work done by the plaintiff. It is further

Court. The defence of the defendants is that various

inspections and audits have been conducted since allegations -8- NC: 2024:KHC:40089-DB RFA No. 1392 of 2015 have been made that the various contractors including the

plaintiff had not carried out the contract. However, there is no

material on record to specifically demonstrate that the plaintiff

has not carried out the work entrusted to him vide the work

order in question. 14. It is clear from the communication placed on record in the

suit that there was no denial of the work carried out by the

plaintiff by the defendants and that the defendants was in the

process of considering the bills submitted by the plaintiff.

Having regard to the same, it is clear that the defendants have

not specifically denied that the plaintiff had carried out the

work as claimed by him and has not denied payment of the

amounts claimed by the plaintiff. 15. It is relevant to note that the defendants have not taken

any contention that the suit is barred by time in the written

statement filed before the trial Court and hence, there was no

issue framed with regard to the limitation before the Trial

Court. Further, it is relevant to note that in RFA No.1162/2013

filed by the defendants wherein a challenge was made to a

ordinate Bench of this Court vide its judgment dated

30.09.2022 in RFA No.1353/2016 in the case of Bangalore

Electricity Supply Company Limited Vs M/S. S.B.

Constructions3 considering the said contention has noticed

that the demand made by the plaintiff in the said case was

pursuant to the work orders dated 29.01.2007 and 09.02.2007.

Since the bills of the plaintiff remained unpaid, the plaintiff filed

there has also been joint meetings, wherein the defendants 3 RFA No.1353/2016 - 10 - NC: 2024:KHC:40089-DB RFA No. 1392 of 2015 had undertaken to pay the bills of the plaintiff. Since the same

ordinate Bench of this Court in a similar fact situation in the

case of defendants, the suit cannot be said to be barred by

Re Question No.(ii): 17. The Trial Court has noticed the contentions put forth by

both the parties as also the evidence adduced on behalf of the

defendants and has noticed that the defendants have taken

various actions based on an anonymous letter that some of the

personnel of the defendants and the electrical contractors were

involved in a scam. The Trial Court has further noticed from

Ex.P7 that two teams were constituted to look into the

irregularities and the said enquiry is general in nature. The

Trial Court has also noticed that in the entire evidence of DW.1,

there is no material to demonstrate that the reports of the

investigating team were accepted or as to the correct position

of the defendants. Further, the Trial Court has noticed that no

action has been initiated against any particular electrical

contractor in respect of the work assigned to them. - 11 - NC: 2024:KHC:40089-DB RFA No. 1392 of 2015 18. It is also relevant to note that apart from testimony of

DW1, the defendants have not adduced evidence of any other

absence of the same, it cannot be said the plaintiffs have

committed irregularities. In view of the discussions made

above, the appellant has failed in demonstrating that judgment

and decree passed by the Trial Court is erroneous and liable to

be interfered with. Re: Question No.(iii): 19. The appellants have contended that the interest awarded

considering the appeal filed by the defendants challenging the

judgment and decree by the Trial Court in a similar suit filed by

p.a. It is also relevant to note that Section 34 of the CPC vests - 12 - NC: 2024:KHC:40089-DB RFA No. 1392 of 2015 a discretion to the Court to grant appropriate rate of interest as

it may deem fit. 20. Keeping in mind the factual matrix as well as the

proper that the interest awarded in the present case also to be

reduced from 12% to 10%. Hence, question No.(iii) framed for

consideration is answered in the Affirmative. 21. In view of the discussion made above, we pass the
