"The onus of proving 'good faith' and 'absence of

that in deciding whether a collecting banker has or has

not been negligent it becomes necessary to take into

that, if the cheque is of a large amount then the bank has

to be more carefully unless the customer was a customer

of long standing, good repute and with great person

credit and was one who regularly deposited and withdrew

cheques of large amounts". 28. Even though the defendant No.1 has taken up a
