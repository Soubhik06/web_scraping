 there is a suspicion that the petitioner has received Page 35 of 45 HC-NIC Page 35 of 45 Created On Wed May 03 23:57:15 IST 2017 R/CR.RA/264/2017 CAV JUDGMENT commission towards the hawala scam, and the investigation is

 Respondent. Infact, from this stand taken by the Respondent

 at this belated stage that FSL reports would be obtained to

 is any allegation or material to show that the petitioner was

 alleged modus operandi, nor is there any iota of allegation or

 material to show that he was receiving cash from any person

 whatsoever to fraudulently send the same abroad i.e. for

 tangible material, there is no strong and reasonable basis for

 such degree of suspicion, that the petitioner may have

 received commission towards the hawala scam, which can be Page 36 of 45 HC-NIC Page 36 of 45 Created On Wed May 03 23:57:15 IST 2017 R/CR.RA/264/2017 CAV JUDGMENT considered sufficient for proceeding against the petitioner and

 subjecting him to rigors of trial. I am satisfied that there is no

 circumstantial, to point out any connection of the Petitioner

 with the alleged offences of forgery, cheating, conspiracy, etc.

 Similarly, there is nothing to suggest any abetment by the

 Petitioner in any of the alleged offences much less in creation

 and submission of forged bills of entry, or of instructing directly

 or indirectly the concerned bankers to make remittances out of

 India on the strength of such forged bills of entry. It is a matter

 of record that neither any of the cheque discounters or the

 420 , 465 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 477-A and 120-B of IPC, sufficient

 enough to even subject the Petitioner to any trial on basis

 sufficient to proceed against him. I record my appreciation for

 the able assistance given by the Learned Counsels appearing Page 40 of 45 HC-NIC Page 40 of 45 Created On Wed May 03 23:57:15 IST 2017 R/CR.RA/264/2017 CAV JUDGMENT for both the sides. 34. The Learned Counsel for the petitioner has rightly

 case. The cognizance was taken without even prima facie
