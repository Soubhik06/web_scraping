--- POST TEXT ---

I was parked somewhere and sitting in my car and waiting for my friend. I random guy eyed me and I saw him do that and walked towards me. He narrated a story of how he came here because an agency promised him a job and took some money from him and he has been waiting for three days. 

He said they will give the job for certain but he has ran out of money for food and started asking me to give him money. I genuinely did not have any money with me then. I do not carry a wallet with me. I told him I will not be able to assist him but he kept insisting that I give him money. When my friend came. We asked him to show his WhatsApp messages with his agency and he made an excuse that his phone is not working while his phone was clearly working. We saw him use his phone. 

Then we told him that the police station is very close and he should go there and make a complaint and that we will take him there. He was visibly really reluctant to even call the police and he gave up after that because he realised he is not going to get any money from us. We used that opportunity to drive off. 

I got to learn that there are countless such scams going on in the city I currently reside in including taking money in the pretext of buying petrol to reach family because their money is not available because of some random believable excuse. 

I cannot even sit in my car peacefully without scammers harassing you for money. The phone calls and SMS are not enough. Everyone in India just want to extract money from you and will utilise any means to achieve that.
