I'm sorry for not providing the credentials beforehand, this is our president: Divinaa Raju (talking) and this initiative was started by my friend and I, as we both have suffered with mental health issues. Coming from a South Asian Background, my family often regarded my anxiety and depression as something that I was doing for attention, and there is so much stigma surrounding Mental Health around the World, mainly in Asia. I started this organization back in June, to have podcasts, blog articles along with daily posts to encourage teenagers and teach them more about Mental Health. Once we are upright and starting our vision is to spread our podcasts and blogs in different languages through translators.

(We are NOT registered yet) For our 501 c 3 status, we are currently in the process of filing it in Minnesota, we are raising money to file it currently. However, the organization is also in the process of introducing it into my school district. I have already contacted my principal about the organization and have plans about slowly disturbing the news across Minnesota.

For our statement of privacy/safety: We do not sell any information of our volunteers to other organizations, and all their private information including their email and phone number will not be shared to any other organizations without their consent, and will only be shared for partnership programs. All our volunteer hours will be credited through a spreadsheet.
