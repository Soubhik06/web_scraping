HEARD THROUGH VIDEO CONFERENCING 1. This writ petition under Article 226 of the Constitution of India read
with Section 482 Cr.P.C. is for quashing FIR No.426/2021 dated
18.06.2021, registered at Police Station Dabri for offences under Sections
420 / 468 / 471 IPC, on the ground that the parties have amicably settled the
disputes. 2. The learned Additional Standing Counsel appearing for the State
submits that the allegation against the petitioner is that he has misused the
stamp of the complainant for preparing forged Aadhaar Cards by taking
Rs.500/- per Aadhaar Card. It is further submitted by the learned Additional
Standing Counsel that it is also alleged that the petitioner used to change the
Date of birth and the address of the persons in the Aadhaar Cards. 3. The learned counsel for the petitioner states that from the last three
months there has been no other complaint against the petitioner and that the W.P.(CRL) 1447/2021 Page 1 of 2 allegations levelled against the petitioner are false. 4. The learned Additional Standing Counsel for the State submits that
the present FIR cannot be quashed because investigation is going on. 5. At this stage, the learned counsel for the petitioner seeks permission
to withdraw the present petition with liberty to file a fresh petition under Section 482 Cr.P.C after the charge-sheet is filed. 6. Liberty, as prayed for, is granted. 7. The petition is dismissed as withdrawn. SUBRAMONIUM PRASAD, J. AUGUST 05, 2021
