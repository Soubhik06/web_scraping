I saw a post recently about a good swiggy guy, so I thought I'd spread some more positivity.

The other day I was going home from a party after work at around 11:30PM, and was slightly drunk. I booked a Rapido all the way to my home.
It being a work day, I usually ride my cycle to the metro so it was still parked there. But I didn't book the Rapido to the metro station parking, as I figured I'm in no condition to cycle and I'd collect it the next day.

However, along the way we crossed the same metro station and I suddenly asked him to drop me off there itself. He did, and I was charged 150 rs, almost the full amount which was fine by me. I added a 10rs tip and tried to pay him via gpay, but his bank server was busy. Luckily I had some cash so I paid him 150 as he had no change, and I went on my way.

The next day, I saw that the initial 160rs also got deducted in gpay. I decided to call him later, but had mentally accepted the loss. 

To my surprise, he called me and told me about it and offered to pay me back if I share my UPI ID. I was pleasantly surprised and I offered him to keep it. But he was adamant, and did pay me back the full 160rs.

Alltogether I felt kind of bad that honest people work hard and make an honest living while the big fish are out there to scam or tax us to death.

Tldr; Rapido guy returned gpay money the next day.
