--- POST TEXT ---

Recently, Aakash Chopra, the cricketer-turned-content-creator, posted a review video of the Guj vs. Del IPL match.

At the start of the video, he issued a WARNING. A little while back, he had promoted the Walk Club App - a fitness application that incentivizes users for their movement. So did some other creators, like RJ Raunac.

But soon, they found out the app developers were running a scam by collecting data from users’ phones and installing malware on their devices.

Aakash owned up to it and apologized to his subscribers, telling them to uninstall the app immediately. That's what I call taking responsibility.

Now, I know nobody's perfect, and we don't expect finfluencers to be perfect either. But we do expect them to fess up when things go wrong, instead of trying to dodge the blame or acting all high and mighty.

So if you're a finfluencer, take note. Tell us what happened, apologize if needed, and make sure you do your homework next time. That's how you build trust with your audience.
