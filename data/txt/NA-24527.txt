My wife makes and sells chocolates and gift baskets with brownies and cupcakes from home. Someone told her to put her products on FB Marketplace. And only people contacting her there are scammers trying to scam via UPI platforms( gpay, PhonePe etc.) it's like daily 2-3 calls.

They all have same speil. "Hi I have a large order to place, We can not pick up directly as I am working for the Army/Airport security."

Whenever they contact me I start with, Hi, if you are army or airport security then we don't accept any payment online. CASH OR DEMAND DRAFT ONLY.

As a savvy tech literate guy, I am privy to all these scams and already have told everyone in my family how the scams work. So nobody in my family has fallen to these scams yet. But any less tech savvy person would fall..

Such scumbags.
