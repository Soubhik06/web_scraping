My friend got a call from unknown number as relative, and he ask about that he is in hospital but can't able to send amount to them. So he tells him that he will pay you, you pay them, and gives a fake message to him that amount credited to you of 15,000 with bank name, just like a real one. He didn't checked the amount, but lucky for him, he was not having money in the account, else he would have got scammed. That guy was saying not to cut call also, as it is emergency. After that my friend call his friend to send some amount as his relative is in hospital, then he checked the bank balance, and got to know it. He abused and reported that number. Good things is money saved, lesson learned.
