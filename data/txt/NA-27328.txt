I've been looking for jobs on Indeed and there is this one company I swear that they're posing as a temp agency. They just seemed so thirsty for my information. As soon as I applied for an assembler position at a company they responded right away. Right away meaning like 30 minutes to an hour after. That's unusual for a company to do that. It's usually the next day. I think there's a bunch of Indian scammers trying to run operations and get people's information on Indeed. They linked themselves to a site that looked legitimate. It wasn't like one of those cheap stock photo looking ones. I'm wondering if they were piggybacking off of an actual company. I got a call from some Indian guy and he said some other person was going to call. It was another Indian guy and he kept wanting me to write "I consent" in the e-mail or something. He wanted it worded in a very specific way. It seemed fishy and I hung up on him. I ended up blocking the numbers too. I've been through the hiring process on Indeed before and there were just a bunch of red flags. They had some kind of automated interview where they wanted you to answer questions over the phone. It seemed very odd that I had two Indian men call me one after the other from different states. The tenacity with which they were trying to get my information and the fact that they called me after I literally just applied seemed off. If they were scammers they got whatever information was on my resume I think. Maybe I reacted too harsh and I should have just gone through with the hiring process or is this really a scam? Have you encountered something like this before on Indeed?
