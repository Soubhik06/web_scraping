notice period salary from 14.08.2020 till 13.11.2020 totalling
Rs.9,79,509/-. In this regard, letter of appointment Ex.DW1/2
which is admitted document needs to be looked into. Ordinarily
clause 9 (c) of the letter of appointment would be come into
operation. Clause 9 (c) is reproduced as under :- CS DJ 489/21 Anurag Chauhan Vs. M/s Tiger Logistics India Ltd. & Anr. 11/17 23. However, this clause has to be harmoniously read with
clause 11 which provides for duties and responsibilities. As per
clause 9 (c) if the termination of service is initiated by the
employee, the company may, in its discretion, can leave the plaintiff
before the expiry of the notice period without incurring any liability
to pay compensation for the unexpired period of the notice period.
It implies that except for the unexpired period of notice, the
defendant company undertook to pay the notice period salary of the
employee i.e. plaintiff till the time he is relieved in case the
termination is initiated by the employee. 24. However, at the same time, the duties and
responsibilities in clause 11, inter alia, provided that the employee
was required not to engage himself in any other gainful or
commercial employment, business, part time or full time, honorary
or remunerative, directly or indirectly, simultaneously as long as he
is employed with the company. It also provided that in that
eventuality, the company will terminate the services without any
notice notwithstanding any other condition in the appointment
letter. It is an admitted fact that the plaintiff worked with M/s AJS
Logistics Pvt. Ltd. & received monthly remuneration of Rs.1 lac
starting from 07.07.20 meaning thereby that after stopping to work
for the defendant organization, the plaintiff had undertaken work on
commission basis as admitted by the plaintiff during his cross
examination. Even though the plaintiff was relieved on 14.08.2020,
the plaintiff is not entitled to any notice period salary since after
tendering his resignation on 06.05.2020 effective from the very next

CS DJ 489/21 Anurag Chauhan Vs. M/s Tiger Logistics India Ltd. & Anr. 12/17
 date i.e. 7.05.2020, the plaintiff is not entitled to any notice period
salary in the given facts and circumstances as the plaintiff was
gainfully employed elsewhere. 25. However, since the resignation was accepted by the
defendant company on 27.05.2020 but the said resignation
acceptance letter specifically refers to the same being effective from
closing of working day of 06.05.2020. The plaintiff's own mail
dated 06.05.2020 tendering resignation states that it is effective
from 07.05.2020. Therefore, the defendant company is not liable to
pay any amount to the plaintiff after the 06.05.2020. Merely
because it took some time to defendant company to hand over
resignation acceptance letter does not entitle plaintiff to any
emoluments for the extended period as plaintiff himself did not
offer his services to the defendant company till the time resignation
was accepted by the defendant company. Therefore, in the given
facts and circumstances, in my considered opinion, it is held that
plaintiff is not entitled to payment of any salary beyond 06.05.2020
till 27.05.2020. Therefore, plaintiff is entitled for the total sum of
Rs.45,701/- + Rs.3,26,503 +Rs.63,194/- = Rs. 4,35,398/- under this
issue. This issue is accordingly disposed off. 26. ISSUE No.3 : 27. Ld. Counsel for defendant had argued that defendant

suggest that any loss was caused to the defendant company on

plaintiff is entitled to ex-gratia bonus of Rs.3,15,850/-. Therefore,
this issue is decided in favour of the plaintiff and against the
