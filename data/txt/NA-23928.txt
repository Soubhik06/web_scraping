--- POST TEXT ---

A week back i went to order a dish at "the filter coffee", kalyan nagar. it was a take away order and costed around 128Rs. the billing counter person asked me if i want a cover, i said yes since its a takeaway and usually all hotels pack it single cover and give it like all the darshini hotels irrespective of number of orders. he said that it would cost you 7rs extra, to which my response was "arent you charging for the parcel". his response was that this doesn't include in that and i need to play extra.

while i didn't get it at the moment and moved to parcel counter. the parcel person gave me 3 separate items i.e a tin foil, 2 tiny plastic boxes without a cover or bag. the same was carried by me to home with a little difficulty.

this to me is a scam because, they are already charging for parcel but have not included the cover charges for bag. when ever there are multiple items in the parcel, they should have a overall packet in which they give it so it can be carried which can be a cover or a bag. unfortunately they want to not give that and make business off the paper bag.

the other restaurants in the area include the paper bag, carry box all together in the same parcel charges. but the filter coffee people are deceiving in charging the same separately hence scamming people opting for takeaway by charging two times for the same order.

for some one who goes regularly, will carry a cover from home. but for someone who goes there randomly to order the parcel, this is indeed a scam and the filter coffee management may need to fix this. a failure to fix this, will only mean that they are an accomplice in this paper bag scam. 

**Dear Filter Coffee,**

**If you want to protect environment and reduce plastic waste, then please have a sign board that you don't promote plastic and expect customers to bring their own bags for carrying parcels. Also don't have hidden charges for parcel separately and be transparent.**

**Regards,**

**A random customer**
