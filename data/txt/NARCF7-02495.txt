&gt;In a significant development, two doctors from Fortis Hospital in Jaipur have been arrested for their alleged involvement in issuing fake no objection certificates (NOCs) related to organ transplants. Nephrologist Dr. Jitendra Goswami and urologist Dr. Sandeep Gupta were apprehended by the police under various sections of the Indian Penal Code (IPC).

&gt;The investigation revealed that the accused doctors were part of a network that facilitated illegal organ transplants by providing fraudulent NOCs. These certificates were used to bypass legal requirements and enable organ transplants without proper authorization. The arrests come after an Anti-corruption Bureau team conducted a thorough probe into the matter.
