Unfortunately, I have become a victim of an online job fraud. The fraudsters sent me an email that I need to pay 1500/- for applying to the position of Python Developer at WIPRO and said that if I don't qualify then they'll refund. I had a gut feeling that I shouldn't pay them but ultimately I paid them since I'm desperate for a job :( 

They then sent me a link for an online test (which was just a simple G.K. test on indiabix). After the test, I was very sure that they are fraudsters but unfortunately the payment had already been done. Today they called again for technical interview (no technical questions were asked though, just typical HR questions). Afterwards they said that I have cleared the interview and need to submit another 7000/- as security fees. I told them I'm not interested and they said that I'll not get refund now as I backed out. 

But what's more unfortunate is that my relatives are telling me to not file a complaint at Cyber Cell because "they will then come to my home for enquiry regarding the complaint and then what will the neighbors think". And they are also stopping me from going there. So I just want to know that can the Cyber Cell do everything online (complaint and enquiry)?

TLDR: Fraudsters tricked me to pay them 1500/-. Need to file a complaint at Cyber Cell and want to know if everything can be done virtually because don't want them to come here for enquiry after the complaint due to personal reasons.
