My friend recently got scammed in a convincing phishing attack, and I’m looking for advice on how to help her. She received a call from someone claiming to be from DHL, saying there was an illegal package in her name. The call escalated when they claimed to be from CBI Mumbai, sharing personal details like her full name, Aadhaar number, and bank info, making it seem legitimate. They accused her of being involved in illegal financial transfers and pressured her into transferring ₹30,000 to clear the charges.

They later called her directly on Skype and convinced her to write a complaint to a fake CBI officer about a person supposedly making transfers in her name. After realizing it was a scam, she reported it to the cyber police, who said it’s unlikely she’ll get her money back. She’s also concerned that they may have taken screenshots or videos during the Skype call. The police suggested she change her phone number, as her phone might have been hacked.

Looking for advice on how to deal with the breach of personal information and prevent further risks. Any recommendations would be helpful. Thanks!
