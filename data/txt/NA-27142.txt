So a close friend of mine who lives in Canada was involved in an online romance scam. This was one of the typical online romance scam schemes where the scammer uses a fake profile and pretend to be working on a ship and needed money until he docks. This went on for a while and the scammer convinces and blackmails her into sending large sums of cash. She sent in excess of $30k. This happened during February/March of this year and we all accepted it as a scam and we did our best to be there for her. The matter was reported to local police where she lives and nothing came out of it. 
Most of the money was wired transferred to India. At first, she was reluctant to proceed with this and the scammer provided an account that is from the US with the person name, account number, routing number, and address. No money was sent to this account though but we have this information now as evidence. I was able to do some research and know that the person exists and lives at the same address. 
So my question is what legal action can we take here in the US base on the bank account information we have at hand?
