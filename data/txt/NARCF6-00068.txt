Hey everyone,

I wanted to share my experience as a first-time seller on OLX to warn others.

I was selling an item originally priced at ₹650, but I offered it for ₹600. A person messaged me on OLX saying he was interested and asked for my number. After chatting on OLX, I shared my number with him. He then called me, and we talked about the price and location.

Here’s how things unfolded:

1. He asked me to send a "Hi" on WhatsApp to his number.

2. Then, he sent me an OLX card of ₹1 to test the payment, and I received ₹1.

3. After that, he sent a business QR code (which had a different name than the OLX card name) and asked me to scan it.

4. When I scanned it, it showed that I had to pay him, not receive money. i said to him why I should pay .

5. He then told me to "value the card to ₹600," which immediately felt suspicious.

6. I refused and cut the call. Later, he sent a fake payment screenshot claiming he had transferred ₹600 to me.

7. He started pressuring me to send a screenshot from my side to "prove" that I had received the money (which I hadn't).

8. When I questioned him, he threatened me, saying he had filed a cyber crime complaint against me.

9. His WhatsApp profile picture looked AI-generated, which made him seem even sketchier.

10. I reported him to OLX — and OLX later notified me that his account was suspicious and they removed it.

11. I also checked his number on Truecaller — Truecaller showed that he had changed his name multiple times, a clear red flag.

In the end, I simply told him, “Don't scam anyone, brother,” and blocked him everywhere.

Thankfully, I didn’t lose any money.
Important tip: Never scan QR codes sent by buyers, never trust payment screenshots without checking your own UPI/bank app, and report suspicious activity immediately.

Just wanted to warn others, especially first-timers like me!
