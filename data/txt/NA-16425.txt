--- POST TEXT ---

Recently, my brother, who recently moved to Bangalore for work, encountered a disturbing scam that I believe everyone in the city should be aware of. One evening, as he was leaving his office, he was approached by individuals claiming to collect donations for various causes, such as improving Bangalore's infrastructure or reducing pollution through initiatives like introducing more electric buses.

Being new to the city and encouraged by a colleague, my brother engaged with these individuals. They managed to convince him and his friend to make a donation, resulting in a payment of 800 rupees each. What's shocking is that the payment was made through their net banking, and it turned out to be an e-mandate lasting for the next 30 years!

This experience exposed a disturbing scam targeting unsuspecting individuals who want to contribute positively to Bangalore's development. When I contacted customer care to rectify this, they directed me to visit the HDFC Bank branch, where officials informed me that canceling the e-mandate would take about 7 days. What's more frustrating is that the e-mandate isn't even visible in the SiHub of HDFC Bank, where one typically sees listed e-mandates for services like Hotstar or Netflix.

This incident serves as a reminder to always be vigilant and cautious when approached for donations or charitable contributions, especially when dealing with online transactions and e-mandates. Let's spread awareness to prevent others from falling victim to such scams in our community.

Stay safe, Bangalore!
