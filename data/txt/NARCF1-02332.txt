PSA

Relatively new to Pune, ~1 year. So, the driver was very weird from the beginning. He tried to drive in the wrong direction multiple times and I had to correct him. After reaching location, I paid via his UPI and he instantly drove of fast and never marked the trip as done.

I called him up, didnt pickup. He kept on spamming "I've arrived" every few mins. Since the trip was not marked done, I couldn't book a new cab or even cancel. I checked his profile and it was very new, only 40 trips, all 5 stars. It was very obvious to me then that it was a scam driver with a throwaway scam account.

Luckily, I escalated the situation and Uber solved it in an hour.

Wanted to share this experience worh you all. Please be careful so you don't end up in a similar situation to mine.
