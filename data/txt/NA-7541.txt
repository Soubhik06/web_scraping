Heard counsel for the parties. This application has been filed by the applicant, who
has been arrested on 28.1.2015 and is in Jail in connection
with Crime No.16/2014 registered with Police Station -
S.T.F., Bhopal - commonly known as VYAPAM
Examination Scam cases - for the offence punishable
under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 and 120-B of IPC;
and Section 3(Gha) 1 , 2 / 4 of the M.P. Manyata Prapt
Pariksha Adhiniyam, 1937. The role ascribed to the applicant by the prosecution
is that of a middleman. The applicant through racketeer -
Dr. Shailendra Pandey arranged for impersonator who, in
turn, appeared in place of original candidate-Vivek
Sharma in the Police Constable recruitment examination
conducted by VYAPAM. It is not in dispute that said racketeer - Dr. Shailendra
Pandey and the original candidate has been arrested. It is, fairly, stated by the learned counsel for the State, 2 on instructions, that custodial interrogation of the applicant
is no more required. Charge-sheet has also been filed
against the applicant. Further, the investigation against the
applicant is complete in all respects as of now and,
therefore, the presence of applicant is no more required. In
that sense, the link between the accused persons is already
complete. Further, the applicant is not involved in any
other crime of similar type. Besides, the applicant through
counsel undertakes to appear before the Investigating
Officer as and when called for further investigation
including against him as also to punctually appear before
the trial Court to ensure early disposal of the criminal trial.
Further, he will abide all the conditions as may be imposed
by this Court. In the interest of justice, taking overall view of the
matter, we deem it appropriate to grant bail to the applicant
on following strict conditions:- The bail application stands disposed of accordingly.
