With Diwali around the corner my mom booked a house cleaning service from NoBroker.

The person arrived, inspected the bathrooms, bedrooms etc and claimed the tiles are beyond cleaning and permanently gone bad. Hence there’s no point of cleaning it so just cancel the service. (Bullshit)

Now the clever part of the scam begins, he keeps insisting my mom to talk to the customer care saying SHE is the one who wants to reschedule and cancel the service. 
Keeps saying there will be NO CANCELLATION CHARGES if you simply reschedule indefinitely. (Bullshit again)

He even records the call and walks away, all with 5-10 mins of him coming to the house.

Now I talk to my mom and finally understand what’s happening. I quickly call the NoBroker customer care and explain the whole ordeal.
The customer care guy puts me and the serviceman on a conference call to discuss the issue and here is when the serviceman DENIES EVERYTHING. 
He kept repeating how my mom insisted on cancelling and he could prove it with the call recording.

But as a rare customer care win, maybe he started to see the bs and upon pressing a little serviceman admitted to denying service.
Got the full refund too.
