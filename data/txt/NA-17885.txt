TITLE: Why so much Aggression Towards Toll Booth Workers?

--- POST TEXT ---

I frequently read news articles and watch videos about physical violence against toll booth workers in India. I'm unsure why there is such aggression directed towards these individuals. It seems to me that this type of aggression is a misplaced reaction against the system, which unfortunately targets these frontline workers.

At times, I feel that Indians, in general, display a certain level of disrespect towards the law. They often act as if they are above the law, which creates a sense of power. This mentality often victimises people who are simply doing their jobs. It's not surprising to me that those who are victims in one situation could potentially be perpetrators in another.

This observation is based on my travels to various places in Europe, where most toll booths are now automated ( I know India also have automated toll). Even when they are not, people generally do not display disrespect towards the workers. I've noticed that this aggression isn't limited to so-called "BAHUBALI" - even common people exhibit this behaviour.

Toll booth workers are just doing their jobs. They should be left alone. Pay the toll and move on. I understand that tolls can sometimes be perceived as a form of extortion, which might explain the aggression. However, this is just my perspective on the matter.
