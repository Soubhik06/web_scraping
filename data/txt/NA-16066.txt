I just need to make quick payments at the shop, which I'll probably repay on the same day but in the comfort of my home when I'm not in a hurry.

Now I did search for it and I found some apps that do this but they are asking for my Pan details and asking if they can get my credit score and stuff. For people who use credit cards, this might be very regular, but it's a bit daunting for me and I want to be vary of any potential scams or frauds.

So, any suggestions please? I'd rather prefer services that you have yourself used and can vouch for (both pros and cons) instead of relying on just online results. 

Please let me know which service(if any) would be the best for my use case.
