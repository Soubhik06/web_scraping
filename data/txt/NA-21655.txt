I fell for a Credit Card Scam on a phone call and lost Rs. 1 Lakh to PhonePe Rentals from my HDFC Credit Card. The scammer already had my Card details but in a lapse of judgement I shared OTP with him. He tried to do multiple transactions but they failed only one was unfortunately successful. 

When I got the message that 1L has been debited from my CC I immediately cut the call reported the fraud transaction within 5 minutes to my bank and they blocked my card. Next day went to police station and filed FIR. Also reported to Cybercrime and shared all these documents with the dispute department of HDFC. I have always maintained I didn't share card details or OTP with anyone. Today I got this in mail from HDFC 

“
.. upon investigation, we observe from our records that the said charge is secure transaction incurred on secure merchant web site and was duly authenticated by dynamic authentication process (OTP) which is confidential and known only to the cardholder. In view of the above, we wish to inform you that the said transaction was incurred by an authorized person and hence the transaction remains payable on the card account.

We also wish to inform you that your case has been reviewed by our Bank's Internal Ombudsman appointed as per instructions from the Reserve Bank Of India.
"

What are the next steps? Bank has provided details of their Grievance Redressal Officer in the above mail. Should I contact him? I know as per RBI the Burden of Proof lies with the bank. So is it their responsibility to prove I shared OTP? Is there a way I can recover my money?
