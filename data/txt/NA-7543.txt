Heard counsel for the parties. The applicant has been arrested on 8.9.2014 in
connection with Crime No.266/2012 registered with Police
Station Gopalganj, District Sagar (M.P.) - commonly known as
VYAPAM Examination Scam cases - for the offences
punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 472 , 474 of IPC and Section 3 (Gha) (2) read with Section 4 of M.P.
Recognized Examination Act, 1937. The role ascribed to the applicant by the prosecution
is that the applicant has acted as a middleman to facilitate
Ravi Gotil (beneficiary) to commit unfair means during the
PMT examination 2010 conducted by Vyapam. The
applicant with the help of another middleman - Pavan
Rajput arranged for an impersonator - Sanjay Patel Kurmi,
who appeared in place of original candidate - Ravi Gotil in
the said examination. It is, fairly, stated by the learned counsel for the State,
on instructions, that custodial interrogation of the applicant
is no more required. Charge-sheet has also been filed
 against the applicant. Further, the investigation against the
applicant is complete in all respects as of now and,
therefore, the presence of applicant is no more required. In
that sense, the link between the accused persons is already
complete. Further, the applicant is not involved in any other
crime of similar type. Besides, the applicant through counsel
undertakes to appear before the Investigating Officer as
and when called for further investigation including against
him as also to punctually appear before the trial Court to
ensure early disposal of the criminal trial. In the interest of justice, taking overall view of the
matter, we deem it appropriate to grant bail to the applicant
on following strict conditions:- The bail application stands disposed of accordingly.
