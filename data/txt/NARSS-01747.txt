Last month, I visited Mussoorie and stopped by a coffee shop called 'The Brew Marca' at Jolly Grant Airport. I ordered some items and made a payment of approximately ₹800 using UPI. However, the transaction didn't appear on the shop's machine, even though the money was deducted from my account.
After receiving our order, we began having it on a nearby bench. However, the coffee shop employee later approached us, claiming that the payment hadn't gone through. The shop owner then arrived and suggested that I pay him directly, promising to refund the money if the original payment was received. He assured me that he wouldn't scam me.
A month has passed, and the shop owner continues to assure me that he will check with the bank and provide the transaction history on the following Monday. However, he has never followed through on his promise and never replied me back with any kind of details.
I've filed two disputes with my bank, and they've confirmed that the payment was successfully transferred to the vendor's account. Despite this, the shop owner remains unresponsive to my messages or simply says he'll look into it the next Monday.
In the past two weeks, the shop owner has claimed that his father is ill and he'll check the transaction details by the following Monday. However, even after that deadline, he has not responded. When I contacted him again on Wednesday, he said he was in Delhi with personal problems and could only provide the transaction information on Monday and not even checking my messages.
I'm frustrated and feel deceived by his behavior. I want to file a complaint but cannot find any contact information for the shop or a relevant authority.
I stay at Chennai so cannot visit the airport just for the money.please help me how I can get my money or whom I can complain to.
