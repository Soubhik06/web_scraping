Heard counsel for the parties. This second bail application has been filed by the
applicant, who has been arrested and is in Jail in connection
with Crime No.17/2014 registered with STF Police Station,
Bhopal for the offences punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 and 120-B of I.P.C. and also under Section 3
(D) 1 , 2 / 4 of the Madhya Pradesh Manyata Prapt Pariksha
Adhiniyam, 1937 - commonly known as VYAPAM
examination scam cases. The role ascribed to the applicant by the prosecution is
that of a beneficiary. The applicant had appeared in Jail
Guard (Jail Prahari) Recruitment Examination 2012
conducted by VYAPAM. He contacted one Pradeep Tyagi,
who in turn arranged for the impersonator Sanjay Tyagi to
appear for the applicant in the said examination. Thus, the
offence was committed by this applicant. The applicant was arrested on 15.9.2014. Charge-sheet
has been filed against the applicant on 9.12.2014. Besides,
said Pradeep Tyagi as well as impersonator has already been MCRC No. 80/2015 |2

arrested and are in Jail. Since the investigation against the applicant is
complete in all respects and presence of the applicant is not
required for custodial interrogation and that the link person
between the applicant and as also the impersonator having
been arrested and that the applicant is not involved in any
other offence of similar type, the applicant deserves to be
released on bail by imposing strict conditions - as assurance
has been given by the applicant through counsel to extend
full cooperation to the Investigating Officer for further
investigation if and when required including against him
and/or will punctually appear before the Trial Court to ensure
early disposal of the trial. Taking over all view of the matter, therefore, the
applicant is ordered to be released on bail on following strict
conditions to be observed by him during the trial and till
further order:- This bail application is disposed of accordingly.
