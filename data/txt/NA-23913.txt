Booked a cab in the morning for my uncle and aunt as I couldn't go with them for check-up. I did see the price and rapido being the cheapest at the time booked it and same as normal one routine of hailing a cab.

He drops them at their location, collects the amount and goes on. After about 10 mins, he calls me thrice and I thought did my uncle left anything on cab. Then the real sh*t starts, and asks me that we paid around ₹100 less and requested to pay for the same. He sent me his SS and I thought how there was so much Fare difference. I told him to give 5 mins and suddenly when I saw his drop location, it was 4.5 freaking kms away from our original one!!
