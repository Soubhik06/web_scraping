Order No. 09.06.2021

 03 1. This matter is taken up by video conferencing
 mode. 2. Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and
 learned counsel for the State. 3. The petitioner is an accused in G.R. Case No.48 of
 2020 arising out of Kashinagar P.S. Case No.49 of 2020
 pending in the Court of learned J.M.F.C., Kashinagar,
 registered for the offences under Sections 420 of the
 Indian Penal Code read with Sections 66(C) / 66(D) of I.T.
 Act. 4. The brief fact of the prosecution case is that
 without the knowledge of the informant a sum of
 Rs.1,97, 736/- has been debited from the SBI account
 in four occasions without receipt of any message or
 verification code from the bank. So on the information of
 such fraud transaction to the concerned Bank, the
 investigation was conducted and during investigation it
 was found that a sum of Rs.99,000/- has been credited
 in one Axis bank account in two installments at
 Mumbai Branch and on 24.04.2020 a sum of
 Rs.97,736/- has been transferred through paytm and
 // 2 //

the other one throughICICI bank Account at West
Bengal . Both the accounts stands in the name of the
present petitioner. Thereafter, the accounts were seized
and he was arrested on the strength of N.B.W. on
26.02.2021. 5. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that
the petitioner is a business man by profession and
doing many transactions in a day in his bank account
by remitting and receiving lakhs of money from different
businessmen. It is also submitted that the petitioner
had informed the Manager of the concerned Bank about
such transfer of money to his account. Apart from this,
he has already returned back Rs.93,000/- to the
informant with due acknowledgement. An affidavit has
also been filed on 22.03.2021 to that effect showing
acknowledgement of receipt dated 05.03.2021. Further,
the petitioner is not the principal accused as someone
has sent the amount to his account through paytm. The
investigation has been substantially progressed and
most of the key witnesses have already been examined
by the Police. Since there is no fault found on the part of
the petitioner, he deserves to be released on bail. 6. Learned counsel for the State though opposed the
bail application but concedes the fact of returning
amount and submission about willingness of the
petitioner regarding returning of amount, if any,
mistakenly credited to the account of the petitioner. 7. Considering the submissions made and the facts
and circumstances of the case as well as period of
detention in custody, it is directed that the petitioner be
released on bail in the aforesaid case on such terms
and conditions as deemed just and proper by the Page 2 of 3 // 3 //

 conditions that:- 8. The BLAPL is, accordingly, disposed of. 9. As the restrictions due to the COVID-19 situation

 Court's website or print out thereof at par with certified
