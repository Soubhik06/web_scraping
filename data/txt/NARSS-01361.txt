As some of you may know , scam calls are increasing day by day. TRAI and RBI keeps calling me LOL. 
They say they are concerned about me and my credit card details are out in the dark web. Never engage in these calls, if you feel like something is fishy just end the call immediately. Don't ever share any personal detail even if they accurately say some of yours.

And please educate the elders about this as they may fall victim easily . 
Another problem is the spam calls. Credit card veno? Loan veno? Flat veno? Bunch of craps like these. 
They even call from Phone numbers which look like personal phone numbers. They aren't supposed to do that right ? 
Airtel does a good job in marking some calls spam so that I can end a call before attending. When attending I don't feel like being rude to the person, so I have to listen for like atleast 30 secs to end the call. 
If only I could assign a ringtone to all the spam calls.
