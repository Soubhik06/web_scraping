Amazon India is running a scam which most of the people can't identify. These are steps of the scam
 
 1. You place the order on Amazon 
 2. Amazon shows an ETA of the delivery 15 days down the line 
 3. Amazon cancel's the order the next day of the delivery ETA 
 4. Refund the amount to customer.

What is the scam, you ask? In that 15 days, Amazon has your money in their account, and they can use it however they want. Ex: you are buying an item worth ₹15000, and they refund it after 15 days they do this for 1000 orders. They have access to ₹1,50,00,000 to use.
 So just by investing in a short term fund they can get a return of \~2% == 3,00,000 (1 or 2 engineer salary). And imagine the scale Amazon operates and the amount of money they can earn. There should be a policy to handle this kind of situation. If the order is cancelled, By the seller, then Amazon should refund the total amount + interest in holding that money. Because, as a customer you are waiting for the product to delivered for 15 days and seller cancels order at last minute then as a customer you should get some comp for the distress. 

What do you all think about this scam? Happened to me on an order worth ~18k, placed an order and the seller cancelled the order on the day after delivery ETA shown in Amazon order page.
