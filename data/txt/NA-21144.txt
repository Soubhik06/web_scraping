Hey everyone,

I’m reaching out for advice regarding a close friend of mine who fell victim to a scam and is now facing a serious financial burden. Here’s the full story:

On June 1st, after finishing a night shift, my friend (she) received a call around 10 AM from someone claiming to be a FedEx delivery agent. As we now know, this was part of a scam. The caller then shifted his role, pretending to be a CBI officer, and told her that her Aadhaar card was being used for illegal activities from outside India. (Yes, it’s the usual script we’ve heard about.)

For context, she is really fearful of anything police-related. Even when she spots a police officer in public, she instinctively wants to leave the area, feeling anxious. So, you can imagine the panic and fear she felt when she believed someone from the CBI was contacting her.

The Scam:

The scammer convinced her to connect via Skype, where he asked her to share a photo of her Aadhaar card. He then told her she needed to make payments for “verification purposes.” She had around 3.25 lakh INR in savings, which she transferred to him via IMPS. After that, he pressured her to apply for a loan, claiming it was necessary for further “verification.” Because of her good credit score, she was approved for a 3 lakh INR loan. He ensured she transferred the loan amount to him as well, again through IMPS. Once the transactions were completed, he ended the call.

What We Did Next:

As soon as she told me, I took her to the SP office in Indore, and we filed a report. We also reported the incident to ICICI Bank. In response, ICICI Bank froze her account and blocked her debit card. They deposited a temporary balance of around 6.25 lakh INR during the investigation, which they said would take up to 120 days.

Three weeks later, we got a call from the Cyber Crime team, advising us to obtain a court order. We contacted a lawyer, submitted the necessary documents, but unfortunately, another claim had already been made before ours. By the time the investigation proceeded, there were no funds left in the scammer’s account. ICICI’s investigation also returned a negative outcome, stating that the payments were made using her OTP and grid verification, so they couldn’t offer any reimbursement.

We have all relevant details, including the recipient’s bank details, contact number, the court order, and the cyber complaint number.

The Real Issue:

I understand she could have been more vigilant, but she’s now facing a massive financial challenge. She recently lost her job due to a layoff and is the only earning member of her family. This scam has left her with a 7k INR EMI for the next 60 months, which is a heavy burden given her current situation.

What I Need Help With:

Is there any way we can seek relief for her? Specifically:

Can we request a waiver of the interest rate, or possibly even some portion of the loan, given that she’s a documented victim of fraud?

Is there a way to request a temporary stay on EMI payments, at least until she finds a job?

Any advice, resources, or suggestions would be deeply appreciated. This is a tough time for her, and she really needs all the support she can get.

Thank you so much for taking the time to read this, and I appreciate any help you can provide.
