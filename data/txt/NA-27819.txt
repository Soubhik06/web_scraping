Here's the story:

A friend of mine recently encountered a job scam while searching for work opportunities. He received an email offering a data entry project, promising a payment of ₹10,000–₹12,000 upon completion, depending on the amount of work done. The task involved manually transcribing information from 500 resumes into documents.

After successfully completing the project, he received an email stating that he had done well. However, before processing his payment, they requested a "security deposit" of ₹1,000 via an Amazon gift card to verify his legitimacy. Believing it to be a small amount and assuming it was standard procedure, he complied. Shortly after, they demanded an additional ₹2,000, which raised suspicion. Realizing it was a scam, he requested a refund, but as expected, the scammers never responded.

This experience serves as a cautionary tale. Be extremely cautious of data entry jobs or any offers that claim you can earn ₹1,000–₹10,000 in a day. Legitimate employers never ask for upfront payments, security deposits, or gift cards as verification. Fortunately, my friend lost only ₹1,000, but even that amount is significant. Always verify job opportunities before committing, and never send money to secure a position. Stay vigilant and protect yourself from fraudulent schemes.
