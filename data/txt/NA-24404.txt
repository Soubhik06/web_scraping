Hey folks,

I recently had a concerning experience with my BESCOM bill that I wanted to share with you all, as it might help others avoid falling victim to a potential scam. Here's what happened.

Last month, I received a bill stating that I consumed only 37 units, which seemed unusually low. However, this month my consumption was 197 units and my bill shot up to 570. According to Gruha Jyoti scheme, I was entitled to 134 units.

I decided to file a complaint with BESCOM via email yesterday and I received a call from them today. They instructed me to take photos of my electricity meter and press the "black button" multiple times to display the previous month's readings. These photos were sent to BESCOM through WhatsApp.

After reviewing the previous values, I discovered that the previous month's reading had been entered incorrectly in the bill as 14,220 instead of 14,283, which conveniently put me over the entitled units for this month.

They corrected the error and sent me an updated bill via WhatsApp, showing my actual usage and bringing the bill down to zero.

I wanted to share this experience to caution everyone to double-check their bills and meter readings. If you notice anything suspicious, don't hesitate to contact BESCOM and request a review.

&amp;#x200B;

**TLDR:** Received Rs. 0 bill for 37 units last month, but this month's bill skyrocketed to Rs. 570 for 197 units. Contacted BESCOM, who asked for meter photos. Discovered previous month's reading was falsely entered lower to exceed entitled units this month. BESCOM corrected the error, sending an updated bill of zero.
