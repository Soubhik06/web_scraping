I had a call from HMRC here in England United Kingdom HMRC is Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs you could say it’s the equivalent of IRS… as soon as I heard the caller I knew it was a scam call because it was an Indian* who didn’t give his name and this is how the call went.

HMRC
Good morning, this is HMRC calling about your unpaid taxes. You need to make a payment so not to avoid being arrested by HMRC.

Me
Unpaid taxes I doubt that very much because my taxes are deducted on my wage slip so I doubt that very much.

HMRC
What is you name please sir…

Me
Well you called me you tell me what my name is…

HMRC
Pardon…

Me
You called me you would have all the details on your screen.

HMRC
What is you name please.

Me
Look let’s cut to the chase here you and I both know that is is a scam call… and here is how I know.

HMRC would never call you demanding money that is overdue. HMRC would send you a letter advising that you have unpaid taxes and they would give you a reference number on the letter.

The biggest mistake that you made was security clearance you didn’t ask any security questions and the one big mistake you made was asking my name when you would have know who I was as you called me.

HMRC
Hung up the call.

I had unpaid taxes from my employer HMRC sent me a letter over a decade ago asking for £197 to be paid and I sent them a very stern letter and I got a reply back saying it was an oversight and not to worry about it.

* A vast majority of these scam calls for Barclays Bank, HSBC, HMRC, Lloyds Bank, Royal Mail and various other organisations originate from India. I know that people may perceive in blaming India as being racist and I can assure you that isn’t the case at all. Research has proven that a lot of these calls are originating from India. I know the system they’re are using however, I cannot mention this on Reddit for security reasons… but they use a system to mask their number and replace it with a fake number which appears on your phone and they can change that number at will. Which is why when you block a scam text by the it will always appear as a mobile number which you block and then it appears again a couple of days later and the last 4 digits have changed.
