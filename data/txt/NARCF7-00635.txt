These issues occurred due to Amazon's policy of not permitting package inspection upon delivery. Their delivery agents outright refuse to open the package for inspection before sharing the OTP. This practice appears to be a scam, making it exceedingly risky to order expensive items on Amazon. In contrast, competitors like Flipkart allow package inspection during delivery for some of the products, providing more security and peace of mind.
