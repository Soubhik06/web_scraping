This thing is legit scary considering that bank employees mainly those who verify your details know a lot about you starting from your salary to everything and I have reasons to believe they are already leaking and from multiple banks.

1. I had applied for an SBI card and had also gotten calls from sales agents before applying itself but choose not to get it. I get scam calls that are also marked on True Caller as fraudulent. They tell you that your card is going to be replaced and you will get like X% discount on online shopping. How do they know I have applied for SBI card?

2. I had applied for Flipkart Axis bank cc and got it last month. Only people who know are Flipkart and Axis bank and their employees who did verification and stuff. Today I get a call again marked fraudulent from someone saying they are from Axis CC verification and they would verify my info and I would get a replacement card that gives 20% discount on online shopping. Clearly scam again.

This is very concerning and especially with banks heavily pushing the CC industry and sales people are going to any lengths. Different thing, I got a call from SBI sales agent and here is how it goes. 

- Hello, can you come to the bank today with aadhar card, and...

(no regard to explain the offer. directly asks to come to bank as if it is compulsory. very predatory) 

- Why? 

- SBI has shortlisted accounts with good transaction report and you will get a new card.

I side railed the topic a bit but we have some legit concerns in the industry.
