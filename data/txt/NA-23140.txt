Hello everyone, I have been a Flipkart shopper for over 3 years. However, in the last 4-5 months, I have noticed numerous cases of people falling victim to scams on Flipkart, receiving broken, used, or different products, sometimes even copies. I had a personal experience where I purchased a trolley bag on April 4th, but the packaging was inadequate. Despite alerting the delivery person about the packaging issue, I accepted the delivery. Upon opening the bag, I discovered it was damaged and wouldn't open. I filed a complaint and initiated a return request, but the delivery person (who knew the packaging was damaged) refused to pick up the bag, offering excuses. I then requested a refund, but they repeated the same actions. In the end, I did receive a refund, but it took Flipkart a month to resolve my issue.
So, my question is, how can we bring these issues to Flipkart's attention? In other countries, people often resort to legal action against companies for such problems.
