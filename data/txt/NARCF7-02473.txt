&amp;#x200B;

The Mahadev betting app controversy has once again made headlines after the government imposed blocking orders against 22 illicit betting apps and websites, including Mahadev Book Online and Reddyannaprestopro. These restrictions coincide with the ED's persistent investigation into the Mahadev betting app case.

&amp;#x200B;

Recently, the ED filed the initial chargesheet in the Mahadev app betting case, pinpointing app promoters Shubham Soni and Prem Soni among the accused. The central agency alleges that the app operated by franchising 'panel/branches' to known associates with a profit-sharing ratio of 70-30. The chargesheet contends that the firm utilized the online betting app to recruit new users, generate IDs, and funnel money through a network of benami accounts.

&amp;#x200B;

The proprietors of the Mahadev Book app, apprehended under Section 19 of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), are presently in custody, facing charges for the offense of money laundering under section 3, punishable under section 4 of PMLA, 2002.

&amp;#x200B;

Despite the current confinement of the Mahadev betting app owners, Shubham Soni and his partner Prem Soni had a conventional rags-to-riches journey before their legal entanglements. Here's an insight into Shubham Soni, his partner in crime Prem Soni, their ties to the D-Gang, and the Mahadev betting app deceit.

&amp;#x200B;

About Shubham Soni and his ‘Man Friday’ Prem Soni

Shubham Soni, originating from an unremarkable family, operated Mahadev Juice Centre in Chhattisgarh's Bhilai until 2018. During that period, he engaged in betting using online apps, incurring losses of around Rs 10-15 lakh. His close friend and accomplice, Prem Soni, also from a modest background, held odd jobs and similarly lost around Rs 10 lakh through gambling.

&amp;#x200B;

In Dubai, the duo took on odd jobs, pooling funds to launch the Mahadev Book App, named after Chandrakar's Mahadev Juice Centre in Chhattisgarh's Bhilai. Developed by a collaboration of Indian and European coders, the app was unveiled during the pandemic lockdown in 2020. The business expanded significantly, with Soni boasting around 2,000 centers.

&amp;#x200B;

How was the Fraud Conducted?

The Mahadev app's software had its roots in Hyderabad initially and underwent further development by international developers. Soni and Prem journeyed from Hyderabad to Bhilaspur, where IDs and software created in Hyderabad were distributed to panel operators for betting activities.

&amp;#x200B;

Following this, the betting activity assumed a video game format, depicting winnings as points rather than money to obscure the gambling aspect, as per the Enforcement Directorate's chargesheet in the case. Users were subsequently furnished with a link to claim their purported winnings.
