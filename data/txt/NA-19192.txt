In the wake of demonetization and all the push towards going cashless which seems inevitable, what are your thoughts on the fool proof ways of enabling this? Keeping in mind that most of our people aren't well educated and will be having a hard time using cards. Also the scope of fraud will be relatively high like rigged swiping machines and identity theft. I just thought that a system based on fingerprint (think of touch ID based banking) would be a best bet.
