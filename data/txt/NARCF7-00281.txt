So an explanation of how I got scammed even tho I thought I wouldn't ever fall for a scam.
There was this Instagram page that I've been following from a long time called @lavanyajoseph4 which looked like a 19 year old girl from south Delhi who liked anime (lmao that was a trusting point for me) she puts out a story saying that she has been earning from a typing job and anyone interested can dm her, so I dmed her (having an idea that it might be a scam) She said it was a typing job for 30,000 a month (ik unrealistic) she said it's a company called hubpage (which is a legitimate earn by typing platform I looked it up but she was just using their name) she sent me the details and asked for 500rs as registration fees (which I gambled since it seemed believable), I've reported the bank account. So Beware her insta was @lavanyajoseph4 before but now it's @samanthaanderson5648 it had around 3000 followers and was quite active, it had her pictures also.
