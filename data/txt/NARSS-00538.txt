--- POST TEXT ---

A scammer will call you and tell you that you've won a lucky draw because of your purchase in Croma, and that you can choose one out of five prizes. All prizes are very expensive such as iPhone 13 Pro, etc. 

They'll build trust by telling you your address and your last Croma purchase details such as the purchase amount. I suspect they have gotten access to some of Croma's customer data.

They'll then proceed to tell you that you just need to make an online purchase of 5k or more to get the prize.

From there the scam starts.

This is also where I caught on and cut the call.

But if anyone of you gets a call like this, hang up immediately and don't get greedy.
