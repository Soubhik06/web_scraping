Tl;Dr my girlfriend signed a rent lease agreement which turned out to be fake. She lost the "rent" for the first and last month, the security deposit and "insurance" money, which she paid upfront. Totals to around $7000.

 * My gf, a Neonatal ICU nurse living in Florida got a job in a hospital in Manhattan. She looked up housing through a website called nakedapartments (well reputed, they apparently pre screen landlords and brokers for security) and found one she liked. So she called the broker on the number listed on that site and double checked the broker's real estate license number. The name and the number matched on the NY Gov database.

* He emailed her a lease agreement and account number for wire transfer. She sent the money and the signed lease three days ago, and upon showing up on that address today was told that the apartment management doesn't know of any such lease, nor do they use a broker. The money stolen was almost all of her savings.

* She called her bank, which filed a claim. She met the cops, who told her that she was stupid to decide on a place online, although they did file a case. But I am told that hers is an established method many people, particularly those who live a long way away, use. The cops told her she should have had a friend in NY do this for her, but she didn't have any. They even thought they she gave away her bank account number to a shady guy in a Craigslist type site, and allowed him to take all the money in her bank, which is not how she did it. They offered her no sympathy and wondered if she will learn a lesson from this "mistake".

* She is alone, young, in the big city, nearly broke with no permanent place to live in, and terrified. She might or might not get her money back. She will have to find another apartment and show up at work on Monday. Meanwhile, a guy who either is, or claims to be a Government verified broker, has all her money. 
 
* If you are wondering how it is related to r/india, I am an Indian. Gf is American.
