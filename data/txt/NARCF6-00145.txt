This post is regarding a travel agency that is duping people and turning their happy moments into horrible and troublesome situations.

I booked a tour of jibhi via indiaramblertour for 29th Jan for me and my gf and paid 4k. I repeatedly asked for the payment receipt but the guy kept on eluding. 

On the day of travel I was added to a WhatsApp group of another travel agency named voyage beat. And I received instructions on that WhatsApp group. I was supposed to be picked up from chd at around 12am but the traveller arrived at 4am and I had to wait in open with temperatures around 3 degrees. 

Once the traveller arrived i received a text from the guy from which I had originally booked to pay the remaining amount which I did instantly. 

We reached the hotel at 11 am and in the evening I received a call from voyage beat. They told me that they have not received my payment and that they will pause my trip and I will have to checkout of the hotel. I was baffled and I told them that you should have told me earlier that I have to pay you and not the indiaramblertour guy.

I tried calling indiaramblertour but couldn't reach out to him. I had the most harassing experience as the voyage beat guys kept on calling me again and again when I was supposed to enjoy my trip. 

I had to pay the amount myself to voyage beat as i did not want to drop off from the tour.

Guys pls don't book any tour from indiaramblertour or voyage beat as these guys transfer the travellers and then harass you.

If possible report both these accounts on WhatsApp, Instagram, Google etc. as these are fraud people and will get you into unexpected situations and will dupe you. Don't want this to happen to anyone else.
