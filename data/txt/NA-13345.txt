A Prepaid Payment Instrument (PPI) License is a regulatory approval issued by the RBI that allows companies to issue prepaid payment instruments. PPIs are designed to facilitate cashless transactions. These instruments can be physical cards, digital wallets, or mobile apps that store money for future transactions. Users can load money and use them for various payments, including online purchases, bill payments, and in-store transactions.

Companies issuing PPIs must comply with RBI guidelines. These include customer verification processes, transaction limits, and maintaining a certain level of liquidity. The RBI mandates certain consumer protection measures, including ensuring the security of transactions and offering grievance redressal mechanisms.

An online aggregator license from the RBI enables a company to operate as a payment aggregator, facilitating online payment services for merchants and customers. A payment aggregator is an intermediary that enables merchants to accept various payment methods (credit/debit cards, net banking, UPI, etc.) without having the need for merchants to create a separate payment integration system of their own.Â Â

Payment aggregators provide a platform where businesses offer services such as fraud detection, charges management and compliance with regulatory requirements. The RBI regulates payment aggregators under the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007. This ensures that they adhere to specific operational standards and consumer protection measures.
