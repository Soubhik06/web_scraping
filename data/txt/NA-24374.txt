Namaskara Bengaluru!

From the past few weeks- I've seen a surge in spam calls from marketeers across financial services (Bajaj Finserv, HDFC, Axis, Standard Chartered, IndusInd) selling me credit cards, personal loans, overdraft, etc. and from real estate companies selling me fake dreams.

I don't remember registering at any of these firms and the problem is that I just have one phone number. It is impossible to change my primary number since it is linked to tens of accounts and services that require OTP. Truecaller doesn't help either since most of the numbers are personal mobiles. Once, I blocked a number listed as spam and it was bluedart guy calling me for my parcel delivery.

I get calls from prospects and sales, blocking unknown numbers is not an option. I've tried TRAI DND, and asking the caller to remove my number from their calling list, but politeness isn't something they're looking for. What's the best way to curb this menace?
