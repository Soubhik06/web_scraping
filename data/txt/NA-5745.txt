(09.05.2019) 1. The issues under consideration arising out of the second appeal dated
03.10.2017 include non-receipt of the following information raised by the
appellant through his RTI application dated 26.05.2017 and first appeal dated
15.08.2017:- 2. Succinctly facts of the case are that the appellant filed an application dated
 26.05.2017 under the Right to Information Act, 2005 (RTI Act ) before the Central
 Public Information Officer (CPIO), State bank of India, Vadodara Administrative
 office, Vadodara seeking aforesaid information. The CPIO replied on 17.06.2017
 and 02.08.2017. Dissatisfied with the response of the CPIO, the appellant has filed
 first appeal dated 15.08.2017. The First Appellate Authority disposed of the first
 appeal vide order dated 30.08.2017. Aggrieved by this, the appellant has filed a
 second appeal dated 03.10.2017 before this Commission which is under
 consideration. 3. The appellant has filed the instant appeal dated 03.10.2017 inter alia on the
 grounds that the reply given by the CPIO is unsatisfactory. The appellant requested
 the Commission to direct the CPIO to provide the complete information
 immediately and impose penalty on the CPIO under section 20 of the RTI Act for
 providing misleading information. 4. The CPIO vide letter dated 17.06.2017 replied to the appellant. Aggrieved
 with this, the appellant filed first appeal. Thereafter the CPIO vide letter dated
 02.08.2017 gave revised point wise reply. The FAA vide his order dated
 30.08.2017 has agreed with the views taken by the CPIO. 5. The appellant remained absent and the respondent Mr. Piyush M. Shah,
CPIO, State Bank of India, Vadodara, Gujarat attended the hearing through video
conferencing. 4.1. The respondent submitted that they have given point wise information to the
appellant's RTI application vide letter dated 02.08.2017. Besides that the
respondent brought on record that the appellant has filed three RTI applications on
the same subject matter. They also submitted that complete information has
already been provided to him. 6. The Commission after adverting to the facts and circumstances of the case,
hearing the respondent and perusal of records, feels that due reply has already been
given by the respondent and there is no public interest in prolonging the matter.
Accordingly, the appeal is disposed of. Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties. Sd/- (Suresh Chandra) (सुरेश चं ा)
