For example

The PnB branch at the Brady House is historically relevant since all the loans of Nirav Modi were sanctioned from this one branch, and later the scam was exposed at this branch only.

Bust of Tukaram Omble at Chowpatty where he sacrificed his life in pursuit of capturing Ajmal Kasab alive.

Dagdi chawl at byculla infamous for being the fortified home of Arun Gawli (mafia turned politician).

i.e. the places which holds some kind of national/inter-national importance, but they are not exactly a tourist destination.
