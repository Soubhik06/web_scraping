I wanted to share my recent experience with Zepto to raise awareness and hopefully prevent others from facing a similar issue.

# What Happened:

I placed a prepaid order on Zepto and received a delivery confirmation notification. However, **I never received the order**. Two hours later, I called the delivery rider, and to my shock, he admitted that the package was **stolen** and advised me to request a refund.

Afterwards I immediately contacted Zepto’s customer support. The supervisors I spoke to assured me they were reviewing camera footage and promised to cancel the order and initiate a refund. But guess what? **No action was taken**, and my money is still stuck.

# Why This is Alarming:

1. **Scam Risk:** This feels like an organized scam involving both riders and perhaps the staff.
2. **No Accountability:** Despite my repeated follow-ups, Zepto hasn’t resolved the issue.
3. **Paid Orders at Risk:** Prepaid orders make you vulnerable, as there’s no leverage if the company fails to deliver.

# My Advice:

* **Avoid Prepaid Orders:** Stick to **Cash on Delivery (COD)** to protect your money.
* **Try Alternatives:** Platforms like **Blinkit** or **Swiggy Instamart** are more reliable and transparent in my experience.
