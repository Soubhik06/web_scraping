We've all played the blame game many times before but in the end we all suffer no matter who does their part or doesn't in making Mumbai a better place.This isn't about that.

BMC has created a branch of twitter accounts with main ones being @mybmc (for general complaints) and @mybmcSWM (for solid waste) recently for complaints and it has been really active in getting job done and reporting about it constantly (check their tweets and replies section,there's atleast a tweet every hour).

You can make complaints about things ranging from trash laying over to potholes to drainage system being damaged to waterlogged areas to illegal hawkers etc and they're getting most of them corrected under 48 hours.

You can find out which ward you belong to here: [http://dm.mcgm.gov.in/ward-maps](http://dm.mcgm.gov.in/ward-maps)

 
We all used to complain about BMC's shoddy job either with friends,family or internet hubs like this here but now there's a place to direct your complaint to now.

Whether you had no idea where to direct your complaint before or thought it was too much of an hassle to do or didn't think complaining was worth it, now there's a change with a more active and easier to access place that is their twitter account, maybe things might go back to the way they were before or work will be done half assed but for now they have given their hand and now we citizens should lend our hand and atleast try to see through this first.

This won't make Mumbai a better place just like that but help us take steps moving towards a better place with fixing one "small" problem at a time.
