TITLE: Why are courts prohibited from taking cognizance of Aadhaar related offences except on complaint from UIDAI?
DATE: 2017-01-08
SCORE: 27
URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/india/comments/5mlxno/why_are_courts_prohibited_from_taking_cognizance/

--- POST TEXT ---

Section 47(1) of [Aadhaar Act] states,

&gt; No court shall take cognizance of any offence punishable under this Act, save on a complaint made by the Authority or any officer or person authorised by it.

Is there a good reason for this, other than covering UIDAI's ass in case of breach of Aadhaar data? If no, why the hell is there not much noise about this draconian clause?
