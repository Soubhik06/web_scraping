So, I get a lot of calls because of medical stuff, so when I was woken up at around 10 am from a phone call, I instantly answered it because I have been woken up by phone calls from my doctors before.

The voice on the other end was an Indian woman. I was still half asleep and there are many peoppe with accents in my medical care team so I didn't think anything of it.

She asked me questions about "checking your account with us" which I assumed they meant my online medical portal account because, well, I still thought this was coming from the hospital (and I was not fully awake).

She asked me for my email, so I gave it (just the name obviously). She then said that wasn't the correct email, so I gave another, because the first one I gave was a newer one that may have not been used for my "account".

Again, she said that was not the correct email. At this point I'm waking up more and getting confused by this situation, so I actually look at my phone and see the caller is not coming from my hospital, but just from "India". It hits me so I make up some bullshit about "going to check my account real quick" and hang up. About 30 minutes later they tried to call me again, I instantly declined and I have not been bothered since.

I've not gotten any weird emails or anything yet, and this all happened about 6 hours ago. I'm usually very good at avoiding these things, so I do feel a little dumb, but I'm mostly confused. Does anybody know what the purpose of this scam was? They only have the name of 2 of my emails, is this dangerous?

For the record, I have like a million emails that I use for different stuff. Neither of the 2 I gave were emails I use for any sensitive information like banking.
