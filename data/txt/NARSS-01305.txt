The plus one exam which is supposed to be conducted on Monday (06/09/2021) is postponed due to the stay issued by the Supreme Court siteing the current covid situation in Kerala.
Now Kerala Government is trying their best to revoke the stay and conduct the exams.These are the questions I want to ask:

 Is this struggle all worth it?
 Is putting the lives of the students at risk worth it? 

The primary argument I heard is that, exams are important for the future of the students. There would be no future if these students cease to exist. Whatever the precautions that are taken won't help at this point of time. Most of you people knew what happened when KTU decided to conduct offline exams.
I'm don't mean to cancel the exams. Exams are necessary. There are certain alternate ways to conduct it and all know that too. 
If most of the renowned and prestigious universities of India can conduct their exams online, why can't they do the same for the higher secondary students? 
And there are some cool innovative ways to avoid cheating during the exams and at the present we are well equipped to do it and handling it.

This is what I think. Correct me if I'm wrong. I'm all eyes to read all your opinions.📖👓
