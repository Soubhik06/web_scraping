--- POST TEXT ---

A few weeks ago, a girl reached out to me, explaining that she got my number from a list of attendees at a board games meetup I often attend on weekends. She mentioned she had difficulty communicating with strangers and that her therapist advised her to talk to strangers over the phone as part of her therapy. This seemed suspicious to me, but I didn’t want to dismiss her outright, so I continued the conversation, which lasted about 5-6 minutes as I was busy at the time.

The following week, she called again, and we discussed work and general topics for about 30-40 minutes. This raised another red flag, as it seemed unusual for someone who claimed to be shy to talk for so long with a stranger. A week later, we had a similar call, during which she shared her resume and asked if I could help her find a job or refer her to open positions at my company. I started suspecting that her true motive might be to leverage my connections since I work at a reputable company, which I had mentioned during our second conversation.

I wasn’t interested in dating, and I never initiated contact with her. I only answered her calls and engaged in brief conversations if I was available. Today, I realized this might be leading towards a multi-level marketing (MLM) or investment scam. During our call, she asked if I was available on the weekends, and I made up an excuse to avoid meeting in person. She insisted on needing just two hours of my time and then suggested an online call. When I asked for details about the opportunity, she vaguely mentioned it involved business with multinational companies and that it could make me a millionaire. She claimed she trusted this opportunity.

What raised my suspicion further was when she asked if I remembered why she initially contacted me. Although I knew, I pretended to forget, and she claimed it was always about this opportunity, which contradicted her earlier reason. I didn’t confront her and decided to go ahead with the call with her supervisor, intending to record it and expose any potential scam.

Is this a known scam, and how should I proceed?
