I found a car transport firm with the original quote of $1100 from CA to MT. They deducted $500 off the balance.
Then the day of pickup they said the driver is sick and non available. 
They next want $1700 for the transport. 
It was too much so I wanted to cancel. Then they said to refund, I need to cashapp them $500 to the money back. 
It didn’t make sense.

I told them to go ahead with the cancellation. Then he said he’ll get the manager. A few minutes later, a different car transport firm called. It sounded like the same dude. They all had Indian accent. The second guy claimed he doesn’t know the first company. This whole time he sounded he was trying to be my bro.

Then the first firm started to cut price heavily. From 1700 to 1600. Now it’s 1400. Then he said “ what do you want.”

WTF this gotta be a scam. At least I used credit card for the payment when I first signed up. Gonna dispute this.
