I got a call this morning from FedEx. The person on the line informed me of a shipment that was booked by me was detected to have items that are illegal to send by courier. The contents that were told to me were 3 passports, 4 atm cards and 4 Sim cards. The shipment was booked on 20th July, 2023. The sender details on record was my name and phone number, but an address unknown to me. These items were being sent to Australia. I was told that this shipment is being sent to the police station of the area where the shipment was booked.

Initially, I thought that this would be a Jamtara-style call, where I would be pressured into some extortion saying a case would be pursued against me, unless I paid money, etc. But as the call went on, the caller sounded genuinely from FedEx and gave me all the details of the shipment including the recipient's details including name, phone number, address and the senders details, which had my name and number but an address unknown to me. The caller urged me to reach to the police station of the area the shipment the shipment was sent from and report that my identity was falsely used to book the shipment. I was also given a TCN number which I was able to find on the FedEx website. Also, no money was asked of me. This coupled with the true caller identification of FedEx makes me believe that the call was genuine.

How serious is this? Should I register an FIR with the police? If so which police station should I go to, the local one or the one near the sender's address? Are there legal or police actions that could be taken against me? 

Any advice would be much appreciated!

Edit: Thank you for responding. I called FedEx customer care to confirm this. The FedEx customer care guy did not skip a beat in telling me 1. FedEx does not call from random phone numbers. In fact, FedEx will not call you even if a package is stuck anywhere. They only email and/or message. Only in extreme emergency cases will a call be placed from their 1800 number. 2. A TCN number is generated for a pickup only and not for any active shipment. An AWB (Airway Bill Number) is generated immediately after pick-up, and the TCN number is discarded. No AWB, no shipment. This was my confirmation that this was most definitely a scam. 

I am just surprised that I was not put through to an IVR like some of you have mentioned. Thank you all for your responses. Stay safe.
