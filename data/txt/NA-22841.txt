\- My Elder sister, Pooja, 38 years old, tragically passed away on 14/01/2024. It was a regular Sunday, completed daily tasks, cooked. Pooja, Sandeep (Husband), and Rohan (Son) lived separately in a 2BHK flat. Their in-laws resided not far from their place. 
\- Pooja and Rohan took a nap at 5pm, Rohan woke up at 7pm, went to his father Sandeep, and sought permission to play downstairs with friends. 
\- Upon returning an hour later, Rohan discovered his mother hanging from the ceiling fan with a noose made from her dupatta. He informed his father, who was in the kitchen. They attempted to revive Pooja, but she was already deceased. Sandeep and neighbors took Pooja’s body to a nearby hospital for examination. 
\- A medical professional at Rainbow Hospital declared her deceased, and her body was taken to a nearby Hospital for Post Mortem. Police filed an ADR on 14/01/2024 and conducted a Spot Panchnama report at 2 o'clock in the morning on 15/01/2024. Rohan accompanied us home that night. 
\- Funeral rites were performed on 15/01/2024 at our residence. On 16/01/2024, we were summoned by the police to provide our statement. We deferred filing an FIR that day, opting instead to write a letter to the respective police station requesting time until the Full Post Mortem report was available to ascertain whether it was Murder or Suicide, considering our shock and grief. 
\- The Post-Mortem report arrived on 02/02/2024, attributing the cause of death to hanging. On 04/02/2024, we convened a Panchayat to seek justice and secure Rohan’s future. However, our chosen representatives succumbed to corruption, leading to an unsuccessful resolution. 
\- Note: After a month, it became evident that Sandeep was acting in bad faith following his wife's demise. My parents, being senior citizens and lacking financial and emotional support, initially refrained from filing an FIR. My name is Siddharth ( Brother of Deceased), aged 28, the sole earner in our family, currently employed at an IT company. We reside in a rented house 6 kms away from my sister’s residence. 
\- On 13/02/2024, my mother and I attempted to file an FIR, but the police dissuaded her, citing concerns for my future.Police officers are corrupt and they went out of their way to invoke fear in my mother. They requested a written statement indicating that we required time to decide whether to file an FIR or not. My mother appealed to the police officials to reach a settlement agreement with Sandeep. 
\- Note: Pooja and Sandeep owned three properties, all mortgaged without Home Insurance. Sandeep was expecting insurance benefits, gratuity, six months’ salary, and PF. To secure Rohan’s future, we demanded that all assets be transferred to him because Sandeep turned out to be a fraud which I later learned after going through my sister’s email. 
\- Note: Pooja worked as a Manager at Axis Bank Headquarters, Mumbai Central, earning a substantial income. Conversely, Sandeep misrepresented himself as a Siemens employee but was a desktop support at a third-party company with only a 12th-grade education in MCVC stream. 
\- After several weeks, an agreement was drafted through legal assistance. However, on 02/03/2024, we discovered that Sandeep and his sister had absconded with the personal effects and household items. They cleared the residence on 17/02/2024 from 12 am to 5 am. CCTV footage corroborates this incident. As Sandeep breached the agreement pre-signing, I decided to file an FIR. 
\- On 04/03/2024, my mother and I attempted to file an FIR again but were stalled and pressured into a settlement agreement. Mr. Broker ( Republican Party Politician) intervened to dissuade us from proceeding with the FIR, leveraging his influence over police officials. Despite my objections, we succumbed to the pressure. 
\- On 05/03/2024, an agreement was drafted on a non-judicial stamp paper, witnessed by Mr. Broker. However, Sandeep failed to honor the agreement, prompting our decision to file an FIR on 18/03/2024, facilitated by legal counsel. 
\- As of 05/04/2024, the police have yet to record our statement or commence an investigation. Meanwhile, Sandeep remains at large, with his lawyer seeking Anticipatory Bail. The next hearing is scheduled for 10/04/2024. 
TL;DR: 
My elder sister Pooja tragically passed away, and suspicion surrounds her husband Sandeep. Despite evidence suggesting foul play, Sandeep, who is well-connected, evades accountability. The police are corrupt and influenced by Sandeep and his associates. For now I just want Sandeep's anticipatory bail to get rejected.
