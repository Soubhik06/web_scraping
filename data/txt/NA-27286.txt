Some tips for anyone seeing people on FB offering tickets to a concert...

This weekend I went to a local rock show, which was being coordinated by the band via a Facebook Event. I noticed a lot of people posting something along the line of "I can't make it. I'm selling my x tickets."

I did a little digging and found that every one of them was a scammer. You can easily verify this by running this search in Google:

**\[unique phrase from their post\] \[profile's full name\] site:facebook.com**

When you get a ton of hits, it's a scam, as they are posting to that events all over the place and they just copy-and-paste. I picked one to dig more deeply into. She used the phrase "Unable to attend this event due to the loss of my mom" as part of a longer sad-story post (note how "this event" is there to make the copy-and-paste work, just like the FB Marketplace scammers who want to buy "your item"). I found her posting the exact same paragraph in well over 100 different events across the US, including the same spelling error and '**kindly**' in each one!

I messaged her the night of the show just to get more scam info and she replied the next day (presumably because it was the middle of the night in India when I reached out) to sell me the tickets - she obviously had no clue the show was over. She wanted me to pay by Cash App or Zelle.

Some takeaways:

\- it looks like these are FB accounts that were once genuine, but now controlled by scammers (they used to have real posts, but nothing real in the last 6-24 months)

\- it looks like they strip out or hide the location info, to make them plausibly look 'local' for whatever event they are posting in

I reported the accounts to Facebook for scamming. I haven't bothered with that in the past; we'll see if they actually act on it. BTW, the path to do that (it took me awhile to find it) is: Report Post &gt; Something Else &gt; Fraud or Scam
