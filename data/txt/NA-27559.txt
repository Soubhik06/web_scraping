I got a call last night from someone claiming to be from revolut (a prepaid debit card company that I use) saying my card was trying to be used to purchase something for like £500 on amazon. 

At that point I kind of fell for it because one of my cards was getting tiny random transactions applied to it, and about 20 mins before that call another one was attempted, but I had frozen the card. 

Then he says he needs to know my balance in all my bank accounts (it sounded much more legitimate than that) saying it was for making a fraud report to ensure what my balances where so If any money gets taken then there's a record of my "net worth" as he said. 

He said he needed to know a recent transaction to make sure he was really speaking to me, so I gave him a bogus charge to test the water, because at that point I was really not sure if it was a scam call, he wasn't Indian and spoke very well and did sound pretty legit.

Then he asks me to confirm my bank balance so I tell him I have £100 in my revoult, and only £1 in my only other bank, and he hung up instantly.

How is this supposed to work? Do they try find people with lots of wealth to come back and scam later? Either way I frozen all my cards imidiatly after that as a precaution and canceled the card that was getting those random charges (two of 15p each)
