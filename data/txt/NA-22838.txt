There is a barren HUDA land in front of the lane of my house (gaddha). All the residents of the sector used to dump their old tv, chair, tables, liquor bottles, and all sorts of garbage which made our living in that lane hell. We used to complain to RWA a lot many times I guess for about 3 years but no actions were taken so we decided to convert that gaddha into a small personal park at our own personal expense without any help from RWA. 4-5 houses in our lane invested more than 1 lakh to convert that land into a very great park of our own. But now some people in Sector have started to envy us as we created a personal park so they played some little politics and now all of a sudden president of RWA has decided to put a pump operating system in that very place of the whole sector. It was decided that all the members of our lane and RWA will be having a meeting on Saturday but today in this morning they dug up the whole park without even consulting with any of us Is there any lawyer or member of MCF that can help us out with this?
