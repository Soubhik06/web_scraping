If you're scrolling through housing.com or nobroker and see a deal that's too good to be true, then it probably is.

I've been looking for a 1BHK for about a week now keeping my budget in mind. And then I see this posting on housing.com
- 1BHK flat in Baner
- fully furnished
- 7th floor
- comes with a HUGE balcony
- comes with washing machine and a fridge
- rent 12k (sus)
- deposit 12k (sussier)

At 1:30AM in the morning I was losing my mind over this deal.

I called up the owner and he said he is charging 2.5k for a "visiting pass" to check this flat out. He also asked me for my name, number and a picture because "too many people visit this flat and can't pay the rent". Brother this house was posted on housing.com like 2 days ago and he's talking about having too many visitors.

Now I needed to confirm if this was indeed a scam, so I called him out. This man ERUPTED on me talking about it is an expensive and rich society and the doors open up with a RFID card (if your front door uses an RFID card as a key you are asking for trouble).
DING DING DING SCAM CONFIRMED.

I reported his posting as a fraud.

Again, if a deal is too good to be true, then it probably is.

And the house hunt continues...
