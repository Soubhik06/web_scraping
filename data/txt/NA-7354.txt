Heard counsel for the parties. The applicant has been arrested on 06th August, 2014 in
connection with Crime No.16/2014 registered with Police
Station S.T.F., Bhopal - commonly known as VYAPAM
Examination Scam cases - for the offences punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 120-B of IPC and Section
3D (1)(2) read with Section 4 of M.P. Recognized
Examination Act, 1937. The role ascribed to the applicant by the prosecution is
that of a beneficiary. The charge-sheet has also been filed
against the applicant on 1st November, 2014. The prosecution asserts that the investigation is not
complete as the person, who impersonated the applicant, is yet
to be arrested. For that, we have already given sufficient time
to the Investigating Agency by adjourning this matter from 21 st
November, 2014. However, the Investigating Agency has not
been able to trace the said impersonator. Considering the role of the applicant being a beneficiary
and not a facilitator or middleman or for that matter a
 racketeer, the prayer for bail of the applicant can be considered
on account of the unconditional undertaking given by the
applicant that he shall make himself available for further
investigation as and when required and extend full cooperation
to the Investigating Agency as also punctually appear before
the Trial Court for early disposal of the criminal case and
having shown willingness to abide by any strict condition to be
imposed. On parity with the case of another beneficiary-Rajesh
Solanki named as accused in offence of the same type vide
order dated 18th September, 2014 passed by this Court in
M.Cr.C. No.14337/2014, in the interest of justice, and taking
all the facts and circumstances into account, the applicant is
ordered to be released on bail in connection with Crime
No.16/2014 on following strict conditions : The bail application stands disposed of accordingly.
