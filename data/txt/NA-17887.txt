Just when I thought my biggest problems as a student were assignments and instant noodles, I get a call that sounds straight out of a crime thriller. “Hello sir, this is Mumbai Customs,” the voice says, super serious. They go on to inform me they’ve intercepted my parcel on its way to Malaysia. The contents? Oh, just drugs and a fake passport—totally what every broke student has lying around, right?

The “customs officer” then says, “Sir, there’s a case registered in your name.” I’m just sitting there in sweats, wondering when I went from surviving on a student budget to becoming an international smuggler. Like, really? Do I look like I’m running a crime syndicate between study sessions?

But it gets better—they even offered to transfer me to the Mumbai Police for “interrogation.” Because obviously, my life of textbooks, budget groceries, and trying to get student discounts has all been a cover for my global smuggling empire. I’m sitting there thinking, what’s next? Am I about to get a “wanted” poster?

It’s clear they wanted my bank details to “clear up the case.” But joke’s on them—my bank account is so low it could use a rescue operation of its own!

So, yeah, new scam alert: apparently, students like us are now international criminals without even leaving our dorm rooms
