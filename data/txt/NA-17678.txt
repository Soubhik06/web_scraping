--- POST TEXT ---

So I've recently witnessed a conversation of some people around my house-a man was arrested (news) for illegal admissions of children in primary schools. The comment that he made on television was 'Indian education is a scam'; and something caught my attention in particular-in between the conversation someone said '*name* ka phone aaya tha mere paas, kehta ki ye ho hi nahi sakta! Wo bahut saaf game khelta tha!' (*name*'s phone came, he said this isn't possible! His game ran pretty smooth); and then i asked myself-after witnessing my college admissions and comparing them to the primary school admissions these days.

I had a clean admission, thanks to a good rank in AIEEE; but many of my friends were talking about how they got through thanks to management quota happening in colleges. Talks were there trying to reduce the prize so their kids could study in good colleges in Delhi. The amounts paid were in lakhs, and the admissions guaranteed.

Apart from the top tier colleges (I'm not even sure about them anymore to be honest), is education a big scam in India? Parents force their kids to study hard for IITs and select colleges as if B.Tech. is the only degree out there; and these days if you want quick and easy money flowing then open an engineering college and market it properly; and many colleges have ads on TVs too these days (eg. the one where the whole world's at); and the quality of research happening in these colleges shows itself. We are breeding a generation of coolies with B. Tech. tags on their jackets! And still people go for fake degrees/fake certificates/MQ admissions. Why?
