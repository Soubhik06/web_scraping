--- POST TEXT ---

Got a call on mom's number. The caller asks her your ATM card hasn't been verified. We need to verify it. We have this number from the bank. Mom got suspicious and handed it over to dad. The caller repeats the same thing to dad. 

Now mom hasn't got an ATM card of her own, and its not her number that is there with dad's bank.

Dad probed further asking what sort of verification do they need to do? The caller says that your card is about to expire and they need to verify before sending a new one. Dad told him that the card is supposed to be active for another 5 years and there is enough time before it expires. To this he says this is so because your card has not been "verified", that it will expire and if you don't let us verify it, we will disable it. Then he proceeds to ask for the card number. Dad told him that if you are indeed calling from the bank to verify the card, you *must* know the number. He says, no, we need you to speak it out loud. To this, dad basically told him to f off and do whatever he wants.

Anybody else received such calls?
