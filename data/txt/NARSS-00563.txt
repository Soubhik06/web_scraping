It's a straightforward process for most countries. Agents / consultancies are largely fraudulent. They just mediate because students fear government processes. They're also decried by most governments (for some it's illegal for anyone to represent you, unless authorized by their courts - and agents mess up applications more than aiding them). Consultancies like Y-Axis, etc. charge upward of 50k rupees and give 0 actual value. These are some of the biggest scams in India right now. Don't fall for them. Do it on our own, get free help or consider paid technology. If you're smart enough to use the Internet and read with common sense, you're better off doing everything yourself or buying cheap digital assets like [stamped.wiki](https://stamped.wiki) for the whole Canada process / [checky9.com](https://checky9.com) for practicing the IELTS / magoosh for GRE / GMAT prep.

My friend and I got scammed. Don't be like us. We wasted our parents' hard earned money on a consultancy.

We need awareness in this regard.
