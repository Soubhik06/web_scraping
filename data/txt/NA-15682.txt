The National Commission for Protection of Child Rights (NCPCR) held a meeting with social media platforms like Meta, X, Snapchat, Reddit, Sharechat and Bumble to discuss protecting children from coming across adult content and preventing the distribution of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), IANS reported . The Commission made a set of recommendations to the platforms including requiring parental consent for using social media and holding parents liable for children having access to adult content.

The NCPCR is a statutory body constituted under Section 3 of the Commission for Protection of Child Rights Act, 2005. The commissions’ functions include monitoring the proper and effective implementation of the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 and the Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act, 2015.
