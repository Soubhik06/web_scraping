I have a work phone and I get a lot of calls from customers with random numbers so I usually answer every call I get from unknown numbers.

This means I answer a lot of spam calls and I get a lot of them since my number is in their systems that it's a "live number"

A couple months ago, I would get the usual calls from some Indian person with a very "American" name claiming to be from Medicare, the IRS, Amazon, etc. I'd know immediately these are scammers, mess with them a bit to waste their time, and they'd hang up.

Now, all these Indian guys seem to have been replaced by an AI voice. These voices are very obvious they are AI with their awkward tonality and latency issues. I'll mess with the AI a bit too. I've found these AI voices are quite poorly programmed and obviously programmed to do a specific script. If you ask them a random question, they glitch out or try and revert back to the script.

My worry with these AI calls is that they are going to get more sophisticated over time to the point where many people can't recognize them and I fear they'll become convincing enough to fool people.

Please tell your elderly family members that the majority of scam calls are now AI and that the Indian scammers are getting replaced by AI
