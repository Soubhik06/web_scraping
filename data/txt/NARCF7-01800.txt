Wall of Text ahead: 

My parents sold their apartment near Mambalam and purchased one in the suburbs. To help with the moving process, they used a moving service, "Jeyam Packers &amp; Movers", who were supposed to pack things from the older apartment, store them for a few months (While they were visiting my sister and me in the US), and move them to the new place after they went back. The company had got a payment from them, and had packed up things in the apartment in July, and were supposed to deliver the packed things on January 31, but has not done it yet, and is not returning calls.

 My dad went to their office in Padi, and other people in building told them that the company had moved out to a different location 2 months ago (But they didn't know where). My dad has been in regular contact with him over the phone while in the US, and he had never mentioned that they had moved their office, and had maintained that they would be delivering as promised earlier. 

A few days ago, he did get a text message saying that they will be delivering to the new place, an that they have been having some issues on their end with finding transport etc, but the number was unreachable when he tried calling them (This was the case even when he tried calling from different phones). He has filed a complaint with the police, and they have also instructed him to file a complaint with the Cyber-crimes division, and will be doing that as soon as their office is open. 

The company has also been listed on a few forums (Justdial, Sulekha etc - Which is where my dad got their contact information from, in the first place). They do have a review dating to December 2014, that describes a similar incident, but it's not shown if that's resolved:

http://tinyurl.com/nqw3ha7
https://www.facebook.com/jeyampackers (Last active in 2013)

Their main website seems to be down, and I have asked s few friends in Chennai to call them up, but they couldn't reach them either.

Have you guys heard of similar incidents? What are the options for possibly resolving this? 

P.S: Also note that the company that I'm talking about is 'Jeyam Packers and Movers'. There is a different moving company called "Jayam Packers and Movers" ,which seems to be legitimate, and from what I gather, has no relation with the former.
