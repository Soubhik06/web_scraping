Hey siblings, I hope all of you are having a good day so far. I live in Canada and got myself diagnosed here after struggling for 30 years. But my folks who live in India were still struggling with the diagnosis. I have been so worried lately for my mum's mental health. One of the doctors she visited, told her that ADD "Disappears" magically after childhood. Two of my nephews and a niece had issues getting diagnosed until now. 

Someone in the reddit community mentioned about this psychiatrist and how he has been helpful. So I took an online appointment with him and talked to him first before booking consultations for my nephews. The doctor was so patient and he asked me a lot of questions. He assured me that irrespective of what some of the doctors have been saying, Adult ADD is real.
