Just wanted to share this modus operandi of scammers of which I fell a victim so others don't. 

I booked a hotel through oyo (I know my mistake) and prepaid the amount (mistake number 2). On the day of my check in I got a call from the supposedly hotel manager, saying OYO had not paid them their amount and they are not honouring any of their bookings.

He said I can pay him directly, he is overbooked and wants a confirmation asap else he will deny me check in. He said he will cancel the booking from OYO, will get the refund from them and provide me cash once I check in. He had the exact booking confirmation number, hotel address details, everything that OYO had provide me in their booking confirmation (which he shared on whatsapp) which led me to believe it was a genuine call.

Now I had not checked in, I was still on my way to the destination and I panicked since I had no place to go. Naive me paid him the entire amount again through UPI. He then calls me again saying that the amount is not credited (although I had gotten a confirmation already), it is stuck in payment gateway and I need to do the transfer again.

At this point, I realized I had been scammed and disconnected. He kept messaging me to pay else he would cancel my booking, later I abused him on messages and blocked him. I checked into the hotel normally later and the real hotel guy had no idea people were scamming him in the name of his hotel. Later I filed a complaint with my bank, but the bank guys said there is nothing much they can do.

Just wanted others to be aware of this scam. These OYO guys have zero web and app security and you should know that any scammer can get access to your personal details very easily.

Don't book through OYO guys.
