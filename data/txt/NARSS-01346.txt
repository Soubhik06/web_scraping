Does anyone else notice the pattern in breaking sensational news that connects with a real-world incident prior to or during the release date of a movie (especially, in Malayalam)?

As we have all seen the [recent incident about Sreenath Bhasi](https://www.onmanorama.com/entertainment/entertainment-news/2022/09/23/sreenath-bhasi-interview-row-chattambi-movie.html), who exploded toward a journalist while giving an interview for a film called [Chattambi](https://in.bookmyshow.com/kochi/movies/chattambi/ET00340502). No matter what, the current incident is also kinda matching with the film's name.

Even in the news channels and other media also, the movie name also gets fair coverage along with this case.

In short, Sreenath's case begins when he [shouts at an anchor during an interview](https://youtube.com/shorts/6gx__AVm8Mk?feature=share), [abused a radio jockey](https://youtu.be/ud_XwLLCZjg?t=1084) and being a [paavam mode in an apology interview](https://youtu.be/8MF9xRaeghU?t=1), [his arrest news came around 5:30 pm](https://www.manoramaonline.com/news/latest-news/2022/09/26/sreenath-bhasi-sought-time-to-appear-police-will-check-the-cctv-footage.html) today and at 9:30 pm [another news came regarding his bail](https://www.mathrubhumi.com/news/kerala/sreenath-bhasi-released-on-station-bail-1.7907923).

Unlike this incident, during the release date of one other movie called "Nna Thaan Case Kodu" also created some sensational news about potholes which was related to the story of the film. Similar to Sreenath Basi's Chattambitharam, the other movie "Nna than case kodu" was also kinda linked to a real-world scenario and got shoutouts from multiple coverages.

If it was a normal film launch, they wouldn't be getting this much coverage for the film. However, by following Bobby Chemmanur's publicity style, movies are getting free shoutouts from multiple news channels, youtube channels and even getting better searches on Google for a short period of time.

Even though the publicity is a little bit negative but, they're getting better reach than money spent for a normal promotion. From a marketing perspective, the film caught many eyes of the netizens with less effort.

PS: I didn't mean all these are fake or these are a part of the planned agenda. There might be people who are working hard to solve these cases to not affect their film, however as we all have the freedom of speech, I just shared my thought here.

What do you think?
