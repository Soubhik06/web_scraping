I wanted to post this rant since a long time. Saw this in the news and wanted to post it today.

What is actually wrong with Chennai Airport Customs? Why are they so greedy? I frequently travel from UK to Chennai either alone or with family to visit my parents. Why are the customs officials in Chennai so problematic? 

1. As soon as we cross immigration after all the stupid questions like asking why are you back to India to an Indian with Indian passport, the customs team immediately ask us to scan our HAND BAGGAGE. I have never faced this hand baggage check in Bangalore or Mumbai or Delhi airport TBH. I am OK that they are preventing some hi-fi scam and want to be careful with everything, but in that case, they should have atleast 2 or 3 scanners like other countries (usually airports do a random check and still have multiple scanners for ease). They literally RUSH everyone even if we are with babies to put everything into the scanner including watch, passport bag and so on. They don't have a table for this. We have to stand, hold our baby with one hand and remove or put back the watch and arrange everything else on our own.
2. Right after this, within 5 or 6 steps, there is a officer in mufti asking most of the solo passengers some random questions and trying to scrutinise some way or the other. 
3. After this hand baggage and initial set of harassments, when we wait for the checked baggage at the baggage counter almost all baggage are marked with "C" for customs check. Now while going out, we have to scan every large baggage again. This is OK if a 10% or 15% travellers are scrutinised but why do they trouble almost 60 to 75% of passengers? 

These problems are not prominent in Bangalore or Delhi airport afaik. Most of my Chennai flights are via Dubai or Doha. Are they only troubling these flights which travel via Arab countries due to the duty free or low tax? We also faced this once when our family was returning from Thailand. What is actually going on with Chennai Airport? Why do they trouble passengers so much?
