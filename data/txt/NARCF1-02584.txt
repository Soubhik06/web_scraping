Hi there! I met a girl on a dating app and she asked me to go to a café or club with her. Yesterday we went to a place called Berlin near Mount Road Sadar. Before we went there, I checked the ratings and told her that the café wasn't good. However, she insisted that we try it. The entry fee was 500 rupees and the girl ended up drinking 12 Jagermeister shots. I don't want to disclose how much I ended up paying, but I want to warn others about these kinds of scams. Please be careful and aware that not everyone you meet can be trusted. Don't go to the Berlin café.
