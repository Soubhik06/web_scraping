Hello there everyone, I've been seeing a lot of posts on here regarding your bank account getting frozen, so here's a step by step process to get it active. I've helped over a hundred people get their account unfrozen so this is based on my experience. 

1) First step is to contact your Bank Branch immediately, either by visiting them or sending an email to find out the reason behind the freeze. Make sure it's not due to your KYC being out of date. Most common reason is due to a cyber crime notice due to recieving funds from a suspicious source. 

2) If your Bank does not give you the reason behind the freeze, file a complaint on PG portal and watch how you get a response within a few days. 

3) Once you know the reason and get to know which Cyber Cell has frozen your account (most of the time it's gujarat cyber cell), your next step is to file for an NOC with that particular department, attaching screenhots of transactions and other necessary proofs. For this step, it's advised to get a cyber crime lawyer as they have connection with the cyber cell and can get you the NOC quickly. 

4) If you do not get the NOC, you should file a report on the RBI ombudsman portal, and if still you do not get a response, majority of the state chief ministers have their website, you can file a grievance there

5) Once you recieve the NOC, send it to your bank with the attached KYC documents and continue following up every day. Within a week your account should get active

I've tried to keep this post short and only state important points. If you have any other queries, feel free to drop a dm.
