Is this a new trend or am I just one of the few unfortunate ones to be targeted this way?

Every second ad is a gimmicky claim by some "digital expert" who promises to teach how to make 6 figures a month with an online business. Or some noob with hardly 5 to 6 yrs of experience claiming they will teach Content Marketing or WhatsApp Tricks or Podcast Tricks or &lt;insert whatever arm of online media you can think of&gt;. It's almost like a kingdom they've agreed to divide and share parts of.

This is just the most recent ad I just came across:

 https://imgur.com/a/dtu1Rfg 

I mean, don't all the testimonials seem shady? 11.5 lakhs in 30 days?

These kind of charlatans were always around, but it seems like their numbers have tripled during the pandemic times. And the TA is usually the starry-eyed just-out-of-college folks or overtly optimistic, gullible people in smaller towns, who also seem to be falling for this. 

I understand this isn't something the platforms can pull down and that I can modify my settings, etc. Just concerns me that no one in media is writing about this disturbing trend of sudden rise of coaching scam. And the massive phenomenon it has become! I mean, I will be damned if I go online and don't see Vaibhav Sisinty smiling a mile wide, promoting LinkedIn Growth Hacks or some Whitehat Jr. ad. Jeez!
