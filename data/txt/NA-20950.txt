For those of you who don't know, Mercer Mettl is an online proctoring company. It uses AI to detect suspicious movements. Along with this it also employs manual proctors who sit throughout the test and manually review alerts by the AI.

Now coming to the problem. In January NMIMS used the same company for proctoring its exams. During this there were multiple events of the manual proctors harassing female students by asking them to change camera angles. Along with this there were instances of stalking on Instagram and Whatsapp.

This is not all. There is a data privacy and security issue as well. However, what was done here is that personal information about us students like : Names, Address, Phone Nos., Adhaar details, etc. are given to the company without the students' consent.
