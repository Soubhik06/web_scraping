This happened 3 weeks ago when I was at Mantri mall with my fiancée. We were shopping at this supermarket on the ground floor (cannot remember the name). We were approached by a girl holding a writing pad asking if we had some time to spare. I moved on with my cart saying, "No, sorry" but my fiancée stayed back to hear the girl out because she did not want to be rude. 

The girl then explained to fiancée about some lucky draw that they were conducting. She asked if we were married and then sought phone numbers. Fiancée gave her phone number to the girl. Since my fiancée is new to the city, I told her not to give phone number away like that and explained how there is no way to know if these lucky draws ever happen; and the most she would get from these "lucky draws" were promotional calls or sms.

A week later I got a call from my fiancée excitedly telling me that she'd won the lucky draw and the prize was 3 days and 2 nights free stay at any of Country Club resorts/hotels across India and South Asia. She then goes on to tell me how we are invited, as a couple, to coffee shop near MSRIT to claim the prize coupons within 3 days. I was pretty sure this was a scam at this point. I asked her to tell them to send the coupons via email. But she said they were insisting on us coming to the coffee shop and especially as husband &amp; wife only because the offer was only for married couples. If we cannot come as a couple, we'd not be getting any vouchers. When fiancée told them she's not married yet, they told her to pretend to be married and lie to their executives.

All this was very fishy. I rummaged through the internet and found a couple of consumer complaints on mouthshut.com about exactly the same scam. These country club guys apparently call you to their office or a cafe and try to sell you their annual membership packages. If you refuse they will say that they will send your lucky draw prize vouchers via email which never really happens.

So beware of giving away your phone numbers to just anyone.
