Could this all be a coincidence? I need you're help reddit.

I'm a freelance web developer. The other week I was contacted via my online portfolio and offered a web maintenance gig for a business that runs two almost identical websites that sell generic "no-name-brand" Viagra and Cialis online. As far as I'm aware, the business seems legit in the sense that people actually get what they pay for. The medication is not counterfeit. It is manufactured legally in India and follows Indian government drug regulations. It's just simply a cheaper source, so they undercut pharmacies and mail to Australia. It's openly declared on customs (as far as I've been told). They require a prescription from their customers to place an order (as far as I'm told) and obey the government-set limit for how many of these pills you are allowed to be in possession of (I've been told).

I was also told the drug regulatory authorizes "have sniffed around" them before, but have left them alone for years. They are the only company doing this in Australia they said. The legality of the business is a gray area at best. However, the hourly wage they offered to pay me far exceeds anything I'm currently making. So I decided to meet at a cafe just to hear more about the project.

After chatting with the guy these are some things that concern me:

* they process visa and mastercard transactions overseas
* have a fake address on the 'contact us' section so people feel it's a local business. It actually has no central office
* keep their money in a bank overseas
* prefer to meet me every month and pay me in cash
* gave me an envelope with $500 cash and a bunch of free viagra and cialis on our first meeting - just as a "token of thanks for agreeing to helping out"
* won't provide me with the previous web developer's details
* don't operate with a business license or pay tax to my knowledge

Anyway, I'm not one to judge people's choices so I agreed to take a look at their sites. They told me they only required some minor maintenance. I juggle work for many clients so I told them I couldn't do anything more than a few hours a week on it. They were totally fine with that. Sweet deal I thought.

But... and here's the real kicker. Today, they call me and say the business is in jeopardy. Visa and Mastercard have struck a deal with big pharma and have haulted processing transactions for companies that sell generic viagra. Therefore the revenue stream for their business has suddenly came to a hault. Then 2 hours later they email saying everything is back to normal. wtf? Are they just crazy or paranoid or what?

What am I in for here? Do I just work for crazy people who make way too much money? Or are they setting me up for something? If this is a scam I'm unfamiliar with this one.
