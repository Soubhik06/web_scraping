My sister got a call from someone impersonating a FedEx agent and they mentioned they found a package with my sisters name being sent from Thane Mumbai to Iran which had illegal items. She got scared and asked what can be done. She was transferred to a so called Custom Investigation officer and he asked her to come on Skype call dressed as officer. He asked her to not let anyone interrupt her and asked her to close the door. As soon as she was about to close the door I asked what’s the matter and she said someone sent parcel from FedEx with my name and all the Reddit posts came flashing in My mind. 
I’m happy that I caught her at the right time or else I’m pretty sure she would’ve sent them all the money she had. I just realised how important it is to bring awareness to the people around you regarding these scams as it could happen to anyone.
