TL;DR: I have 2+ YOE, currently working at a startup. Was on PIP few months back, lowest point of my career so far, was approached by the Sales Rep of HeyCoach. I was at a very low mental place at that time and submitted the enrolment fee of ₹4,000. Now, I realised my mistake, but they keep bringing up their no-refund policy (which they didn’t mention when I was being enrolled).

I am a Frontend Developer with currently 2 years 7 months worth of software development experience. I was put into Performance Improvement Program (PIP) by my org for 2 weeks because I wasn’t able to cope with the project at that time. Got out of it with good reviews from my seniors. This was a very low time in my entire career. I was approached by an EdTech called HeyCoach and the sales rep convinced me to get enrolled into their Super30 program. I reluctantly agreed and submitted my ₹4,000 registration fees. It was some DSA and System Design course worth ₹1,59,000. The registration included their banking partner Kuhoo, I signed up for 3 years EMI option, ~6,500 per month. 

But before the first class ever commenced, I informed the POC, that I want to unenroll from the program as I might lose my job. He asked me to write a mail to the support and it will be done, which I did instantly. It’s been more than a month and they just keep reiterating that they don’t have a refund policy. I am not attending their classes and have written almost 17 mails telling them that I won’t be able to pay the EMIs and all. This no refund policy thing wasn’t even mentioned in their offer or advertisement, neither did the sales rep told me about this.

I read the reviews on Reddit later that it is a scam and fraudulent program and they only teach basic DSA stuff and free codes from Leetcode. The EMIs are linked to my bank account, NACH e-mandate, which I can’t cancel from the bank, I’ve asked them. One thing is I’ll get that account frozen and open a new account. For my credit score drop, I’ll report it as a fraud.

What should I do? I feel stuck
