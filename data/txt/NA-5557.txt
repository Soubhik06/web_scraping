1. The petitioner is seeking concession of regular bail under Section 439 Cr.P.C. in case FIR No. 248 dated 26.12.2022 under Sections

419 , 420 , 120-B of IPC and Section 66(D) of the Information Technology

Act, registered at Police Station Sarabha Nagar, Ludhiana. 2. Learned counsel for the petitioner inter alia contends that there

is no grain of truth in the allegations levelled in the FIR in question that the

petitioner had misrepresented himself to be a senior police official and

thereafter tried to forge a relationship online with the complainant, who had

been introduced to him through Jeewansathi.com. It has been contended that

investigation in the present case is complete, charges also stands framed,

however, till date none of the 13 prosecution witnesses has been examined,

hence, there is no possibility of the trial concluding in the near future. A

prayer has, therefore, been made to extend the concession of bail to the
