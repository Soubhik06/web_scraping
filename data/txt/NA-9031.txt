 .... The matter was taken up through Video Conferencing. Learned counsel for the parties had
 no objection with it and submitted that the audio and video qualities are good. 06/23.07.2021 The present application has been filed under Section 14(A) of the
 Schedule Castes/Schedule Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act. Heard learned counsel for the appellant and learned counsel for the State
 as well as learned counsel for the respondent No.2-victim. This criminal appeal has been filed against the order dated 28.11.2020
 passed by the learned Additional Sessions Judge-I cum Special Judge, Gumla in
 B.P. No.982 of 2020 in connection with SC/ST Gumla P.S. Case No.18 of 2020
 registered for the offence under Sections 354(B) / 385 / 376D / 504 / 506 / 34 of the
 Indian Penal Code and Sections 3(1) (S) (W) / 3(2) (Va) of the SC/ST (Prevention
 of Atrocities) Act by which prayer for bail of the appellant has been rejected. It has been submitted by the learned counsel for the appellant that the
 appellant is in custody since 08.10.2020. Investigation has already been completed
 and charge-sheet has already been submitted on 04.12.2020 under Sections 354(B) / 385 / 376 / 504 / 506 of the IPC and Sections 3(1)(r)(s)(w) / 3(2)(V) of the SC/ST
 (POA) Act. It has been further submitted that the appellant aged about 19, student
 of Graduation, has taken friendly loan from the victim and thereafter he was being
 blackmailed and as such he lodged a complaint on 23.08.2020 and thereafter he has
 been falsely implicated in the present case. The appellant further undertakes to
 participate in the trial. On above facts, prayer for bail has been made. On the other hand, learned APP and learned counsel for the victim has
 opposed the prayer and submitted that a lady belonging to the poor sections of the
 society has been sexually exploited by this appellant and as such he does not
 deserve the privilege of bail. Having heard the parties and on perusal of records, it appears that the
 appellant is 19 years old student and he is economically weak and as such he took
 loan from the victim, who is a married working lady. Considering the fact that the investigation has already been completed
 and the charge-sheet has already been submitted on 04.12.2020, the appellant,
 above named, is directed to be released on bail, on furnishing bail bond of
 Rs.10,000/-(Ten Thousand), with two sureties of the like amount each, to the
 satisfaction of learned Additional Sessions Judge-I cum Special Judge, Gumla in
 B.P. No.982 of 2020 in connection with SC/ST Gumla P.S. Case No.18 of 2020
 subject to condition that the appellant will submit self-attested photocopy of his
 Aadhar Card and also submit his mobile number before the learned court below
 which he will always keep active and will not change it during pendency of this
 case without prior permission of the court. Accordingly, instant criminal appeal is allowed and the impugned order
 dated 28.11.2020 passed by the learned Additional Sessions Judge-I cum Special
 Judge, Gumla in B.P. No.982 of 2020 in connection with SC/ST Gumla P.S. Case
 No.18 of 2020 is, hereby, set aside. (Rajesh Kumar, J.)
