I have been a member of this community for some time and and have read the posts about scams that happened to people. Today when I was the one getting scammed I realized that I had read about something like this here and was able to not get scammed. So I decided to write about my experience here so that it might help someone else. 

5/5/24 - I book a luminous inverter &amp; battery from amazon with the vendor being Liteon Solutions, Akshaya Nagar, Bangalore-16. The delivered is scheduled to happen at 7th.

7/5/24 - I get an otp sms from amazon but delivery never happens. The status on amazon shows delivery was attempted but I was not available. I speak with customer care and have the delivery rescheduled to 9th.

9/5/24 - Delivery does not happen. Speak with customer care again and have it rescheduled to 10th.

10/5/24 - Someone calls me from their personal number and says they are delivering the inverter and asks me to send the location to that number in whatshapp. I send it, the guy comes gives me inverter and hands me my bill and asks me to write my name, phone no and sign on his bill. I don't think too much and do it. He did not ask for any otp and just takes the bill and leaves. 

11/5/24 - I get a call from another personal number and an automated voice says that my fedex shipment could not be delivered, press 1 for more info, press 2 to talk to support. The status on amazon for the inverter was still showing that the inverter was not delivered, so i thought i was related to that and pressed 1 to check if it is. Instantly some guy connects and confirms my name and phone no. After i confirm, he says that on 5/5/24, I sent an international shipment from mumbai and that could not be delivered because illegal substance was found it it. I said i did not send any package and the he says the package has my name, phone no and sign. I realized that it is a scam and immediately cut the call and reported the number. 

TLDR: dont sign any papers shown to you by any delivery person without checking them properly.
