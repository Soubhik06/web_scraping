Today, I realized it was a scam when I visited Sector 31 Main Market (Huda Market). This market is full of food options, and my wife and I were exploring it. While walking around, I happened to make eye contact with a man carrying a baby, accompanied by his wife. They were dressed like villagers but well-presented.

The man stopped us and said they didn’t have money for food and needed to get to Maharashtra, asking for help. We told him we didn’t have cash on us but were willing to offer food instead. He quickly responded that they had UPI and we could transfer money directly. This felt off, so we decided to walk away, though I felt bad about doing so.

Not long after, another couple approached us with the exact same story, confirming my suspicion that it was a scam.

Please stay cautious and avoid giving money to anyone in such situations, especially in Delhi NCR. These scams prey on our empathy but are often well-rehearsed cons. If you want to help, consider offering food instead, which often exposes their real intent.
