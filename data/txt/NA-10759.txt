 I.A. 18499/2025 (seeking recall of the order dated 23.04.25) 1. This application has been filed on behalf of the plaintiffs seeking
 recall of the order passed by the Predecessor Bench on 23rd April, 2025,
 whereby the defendants no.2, 3 and 4 have been deleted from the array of
 parties. The relevant extracts from the said order are set out below: 2. It is the case of the plaintiffs that the defendants no.2, 3 and 4 were
 not impleaded merely as Directors of the defendant no.1 company, but
 substantial averments of misappropriation and syphoning of funds have been
 made against the said persons. 3. It is further stated that the plaintiffs have also filed a complaint before
 the Economic Offences Wing alleging data theft, misappropriation of funds
 and fraud. Therefore, the plaintiffs seek restoration of the defendants no.2, 3
 and 4 in the suit. 4. I have heard the counsel for the plaintiffs and perused the averments
 made in the plaint. 5. In light of the averments made in the plaint, in my considered view,
 the defendants no.2, 3 and 4 cannot be deleted at this stage of the suit as they
 are necessary to enable this Court to effectually and completely adjudicate
 the present suit. 6. Since the defendants are yet to appear in the suit, the present
 application is being decided without issuance of notice to the defendants. 7. Accordingly, the application is allowed and the order dated 23rd April,
 2024, to the extent it deletes the defendants no.2, 3 and 4 is recalled.
