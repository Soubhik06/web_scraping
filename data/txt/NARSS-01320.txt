&gt;Fraudsters pose as customer care representatives of mobile phone service providers, advise victims to replace physical SIM with an e-SIM by entering a 32-digit e-ID through app or website. Once the victim sends a QR code, the fraudster take control of the phone and the original SIM becomes inactive.
