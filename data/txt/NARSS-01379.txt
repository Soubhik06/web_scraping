Is there any political leader who is capable enough to take the decision and make plans to go ahead with it?

It's not an easy task, they'll have to make a deal with TN, get the centre involved, work out a joint venture or something and a strategy that can help both government n then proceed with it. That too will take a lot of time. Still, a solution needs to be found. 

I see many people making change.org petition, but I feel like that's all just cowardly protest. Sitting at home and making petition expecting the government to take notice of a website? How practical is that? Why isn't there any strong movement? 
Don't we have the right to protest? Or are we supposed to sit quite and fear action from government for spreading fake news?

Party People &amp; public were protesting for Farmers bill which barely affects us. There was a strike and hartal for the same. Everyone out in the streets, banners everywhere even behind private buses. 
Both congress and communist parties were making rallies and roadside prasangams.
Athoke evide poyi ividethe karyam vannapo?

Is there any one who is capable to stand up n lead this?
Or is it just like always where it's sensational for few weeks n everyone forgets about it n moves on.
