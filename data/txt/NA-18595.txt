--- POST TEXT ---

While booking a ticket for someone I know serving in the Army, I found that the Air India fares for Armed Forces 'concession' are actually more than the regular non-concession fares.

Screenshots of fares : http://imgur.com/a/ikaPb

This is the reason they gave : The Armed Forces concessionary fare is applicable on published full fare. You may opt for non-concessionary fares which are lower, since these are market driven. 

However, My point isn't that the Armed Forces personnel don't have a choice. The fact that the concession is not on the regular fares which are market driven is the problem. All other airlines that have this concession have it on the base fare of a regular ticket, not some hiked up fraud called 'published full fare'. 

(here is the DM from them : http://imgur.com/vFpIK0R)

It is very very disappointing that MLAs/MPs,etc get free rides, and our Armed Forces don't even get a decent concession.

Yes, as an individual, I wouldnt book on these prices unless the govt is going to reimburse my expense (which happens only if the tickets are booked from AI), but isn't it atleast their responsibility to give a proper concession? And if you can't afford it, don't give a concession, lots of airlines don't. Atleast don't mock it. 

For reference, Indigo fares for Armed Forces personnel on same date. 50% off on Base Fares of regular 'market driven' fares. Even though their market driven fares are way cheaper since it is a low cost airline. http://imgur.com/fBObZ1m
