So I was on Glassdoor applying for jobs in my field - I was applying for anything that sounded remotely interesting and sending out my resume, so at first I didn't realize this company was fishy. Well within half an hour of sending my resume out, a recruiter is calling me (from a US number registered to the company, but it's a non-fixed VOIP number). I didn't pick up as I thought it was a bit odd to be calling so soon, but the recruiter left a voicemail asking to speak on the phone about the job. He had a very thick Indian accent. Then I immediately get an email from him saying he's left me a voicemail. Then minutes later I get a text saying the same thing as in the voicemail! This seemed really weird to me, so I start digging into the company.

They have literally HUNDREDS of job openings on their website (mostly for engineers, all openings are in the US in various states - the salaries listed are well BELOW market rate). Also, for an IT company... their website is hilariously bad (see screenshot). So I figured it's a scam and ignored the emails etc. Weird thing is that they are on legitimate job posting sites, and they even have company pages on sites like Crunchbase. Anyway, the recruiter kept on calling and emailing asking to speak with me. So I told him that I didn't think I was a good fit for the role and left it at that. Within minutes he's emailed asking to speak with me about another role. I gave him a time that I can speak on the phone (I am curious about the scam more than anything), and he then sends me a weird "Right to Represent" email posed as some sort of legally binding contract (pretty sure an email can't be legally binding....plus the wording of the email just sounds scammy).

I am curious what sort of scam they're playing - this all seems so fishy to me. I am also hesitant to engage further with this recruiter because these people have my resume (which has my name, phone number, city that I live in, email and current workplace on it) - who knows what they're capable of. I did email the recruiter this morning saying I'm no longer interested because this sounds scammy. Haven't heard back since. 

Anyone else come across this company/a company like this? Maybe their plan is to offer me a job, ask for my SSN and bank details, and then steal my money and identity? But if that is the case, surely Glassdoor etc would have booted them off their website by now, and there would be more bad stuff online about them?
