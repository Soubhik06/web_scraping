--- POST TEXT ---

Last month my dad came to me with a pamphlet for this e-commerce site called [OnEMI.com](http://www.onemi.in/). It was the first time I'd heard about the site. My dad wanted me to buy him a pair of shoes that was displayed on the pamphlet. Against my better judgement, I placed the order with Credit Card as payment option instead of COD. 

It's been almost three weeks and there is no sign of delivery. Every day I get a mail saying that the shoes are out of stock and I should wait. Finally I lost my patience and shot them a mail couple of days back, to which I haven't received a response yet. Today I called the number that was on the mail they sent me regarding the order. The phone kept ringing, but nobody picked up. 

So yeah, does this sound like a scam? 

EDIT: Forgot to mention. Their tracking doesn't work either. No matter how many times I enter the order ID and phone number, it keeps returning with no answer. That rules out cancellation too.
