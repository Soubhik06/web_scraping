Hello everyone, I believe I've gotten involved in some kind of loan scam, and I'm unsure what to do. A few days ago, I received a call from an unknown number, but I didn’t answer it at the time. Later, I decided to call the number back, and the person answered. He asked for my name, which made me think it might be related to an institution or coaching class. To be cautious, I didn’t provide any information and hung up.

Yesterday, I received another call from the same number. This time, the caller mentioned a random name and combined it with my surname, although he mispronounced my surname. For example, he said "XYZ + my surname." I have never heard of this name before, and they are not my relative, friend, or acquaintance. The caller then mentioned my full name and claimed that "XYZ" had taken a loan, wasn’t responding to calls, and had listed me as a reference. Specifically, he said, "XYZ ne haram ka paisa liya hai aur pata nahi kahan gaya hai paisa leke, par usne tumhara naam reference mein daala hai."

I explained that I didn’t know this person, but the caller was extremely rude and said, "I don’t care about your name. He has put your name as a reference, so why would he do that if he doesn’t know you?" He kept speaking aggressively, so I hung up.

I searched the number on Truecaller, and the caller’s profile appeared with the name "Fraud." Someone else had commented on the profile, confirming it was a scam. I left a similar comment explaining my experience and blocked the number.

After reading posts on this subreddit from people in similar situations, I decided to record calls from unknown numbers. Today, I received another call from an unknown number. When I answered, a woman spoke, but she couldn’t hear me, and she hung up. After the call, a Truecaller popup showed the same name the loan shark had mentioned, but it only displayed the first name, not my surname.

I decided to call the number back. This time, a man answered and asked, "Who’s this?" I told him I had just received a call from this number, and he rudely replied, "I didn’t call you." So, I hung up.

I don’t know what to do next. If anyone has advice or has dealt with something similar, please reply as soon as possible. Thank you.
