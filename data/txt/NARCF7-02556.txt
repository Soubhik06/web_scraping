I recently started receiving Amazon orders wrapped simply in transparent plastic instead of the usual box packaging. Curious about this, I asked the delivery guy why, and he told me that in Kakadeo, most Amazon customers are students, and a lot of them return products claiming that the item inside the box was missing or different.

To counter this, Amazon started delivering in clear plastic so that customers can’t later say they got the wrong product. Now, this could mean either some students genuinely faced this issue before, or maybe some were scamming Amazon with fake return claims.

I just want to know—are others also receiving packages like this, or are you still getting standard Amazon box packaging? Let me know your experience!
