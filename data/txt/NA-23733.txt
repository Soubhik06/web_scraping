Listed a property for rent. This person posting as an army officer from Hyderabad can't visit the property, but ready to pay advance and rent. At this point I knew it was a scam wanted to have some time pass. Asked for aadhar card surprisingly he sent aadhar, driver license, pan card.
Ofc the photos on the documents doesn't resemble one another.
The aadhar is a valid one as per uidai website. But one serious flaw in aadhar verification is that it confirms a aadhar number exists but doesn't give the registered full name and registered phone number 😶
But when I searched the name on Google I got all the previous scams they have done in the same person's name https://www.google.com/amp/s/m.timesofindia.com/city/chandigarh/fake-army-capt-cheats-pkl-resident-of-10l/amp_articleshow/94339572.cms

Finally i blocked his contact due to mental pressure and realization that i may be targeted for further such scams. Within 2 mins another one nsg commando(arent they supposed to be a secret org), called me to ask if the place is available for rent, when I said it's already rented out he's asking if it's for an army personnel or local. These guys have better customer obsession that amazon 😅

Please don't fall such scams, it's your hard earned money🙏
