 RFA(COMM) 216/2024 & CM APPL. 32665/2024 [Application filed on
 behalf of the appellant seeking interim relief] 4. This appeal is directed against the judgment and decree dated
 21.03.2024 passed by Smt.Kaveri Baweja, learned District Judge
 (Commercial Court) - 07, Central, Tis Hazari Courts, New Delhi. 5. Via the impugned judgment, the trial Court decreed the suit filed by
 the respondents/plaintiffs for Rs.42,78,500/- along with simple interest at the
 rate of 8% per annum from the date of filing of the suit till realization. 6. The record discloses that the respondents/plaintiffs had filed a suit for
 recovery of Rs.65,63,201/- against the appellant/defendant due to the
 subsistence of a "banker indemnity policy" which provided insurance
 against "employee dishonesty". 7. It appears that personnel of the respondents/plaintiffs had, without
 authorization, withdrawn monies out of a bank account maintained by the
 respondents/plaintiffs. 8. According to counsel for the appellant/defendant, the impugned
 judgment is flawed as it failed to take into account the 'Exceptions' clause in
 the Bankers Indemnity Policy [in short, "policy"], more specifically clause (g). 9. It is the submission of the counsel for the appellant/defendant that
 since the employee of the respondents/plaintiffs whose conduct triggered the
 insurance claim was involved in fraud on an earlier occasion, the
 appellant/defendant could not be called upon to pay under the subject policy
 as this fact brought the matter within the ambit of the Exception Clause. This is a digitally signed order. The authenticity of the order can be re-verified from Delhi High Court Order Portal by scanning the QR code shown above.
