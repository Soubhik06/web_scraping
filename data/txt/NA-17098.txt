Your college career advisor may tell you to follow your passion, but for a lot of us, there’s one vital question – where’s the money?

Since the world is changing and the industries are rapidly growing, it might be a little confusing to choose which area you should actually be in to earn respect and financial stability. But do not worry as there are options available far and wide with this list of high-paid jobs in the next 5 years.

**Content Writer**

Content writing is one of the most thriving jobs at the moment and has a wide pay scale right from INR 1000 to over INR 25,000 per month. Anybody can become a content writer from school students to fully grown adults and can pursue it part-time or full-time. Experience of few years would let you earn 2 to 8 lakhs INR yearly.

**Software Developer**

Software developers create and compose the software which operates on computers and smartphones. Some developers build software for particular tasks, whereas others focus on the operating systems that computers and networks depend on. 

The average income for a system software developer is INR 589,581, with an entry-level software developer earning around INR 460,000 per year. 

**Medical and Healthcare Professionals**

Healthcare is one of the highest-paying industries in India.

The median wage of healthcare practitioners in India is about 10 LPA, with the average wage of a General Physician in India is INR 6,89,397 LPA, whereas that of a General Surgeon is INR 10,78,167 LPA. Nursing is also emerging as one of the most sought-after professions in India that offers a handsome salary.

**Chartered Accountant**

Because of their experience and position, chartered accountants are often in high demand. As a new employee, you can expect to earn up to 15 lakh INR per year, with new CAs earning between 6 to 8 lakh annually. Hence, it is one of the best paying jobs even for fresher.

**Digital Marketing**

Digital marketing is among India's extremely fast-growing industries. In India, it is rising at a 25-30% annual pace. By 2018, India had 500 million internet users and as more people purchase smartphones, this figure will continue to rise. With a fresher starting out at an average of 3,00,000 INR to an experienced Digital Marketer taking home an average of 8,00,000 INR per annum it is unquestionably one of the several great career options for the future. 

**Management**

There is still always a need for managers as organizations grow and expand. If you've ever wanted to be in a position of leadership, management is the career for you. An entry-level wage of INR 3,00,000 to a mid-level experienced wage of INR 8,00,000 per year makes it is a great career option. After earning an MBA, you can work in a variety of industries, including accounting, finance, investment, tech, and healthcare. 

**Cyber Security**

Cybercriminals use the virtual world as a laboratory to create new methods of collecting confidential material, like fake maps, corporate ransomware attacks, spear-phishing emails linked, etc.

Although COVID-19 has increased lockdowns, the Work From Home specialists have grown exponentially from 1 million in 2014 to 3.5 million in 2021 and the demand is only increasing day by day. With an average salary of INR 732,476 LPA, it is an excellent career option.

**Social Media Marketing and Management**

Social media marketing works on increasing brand recognition and user interaction on various social media platforms to drive business and consumer loyalty. It is not only one of the highest-paid non-IT jobs in India, but it is also one of the most rapidly increasing ones.

In India, the median social media marketer and manager salary is INR 344,137 per annum.

**Business Development**

Business developers serve as a company's primary communication medium for both potential and prospective customers. Identifying sales leads, promoting product and service offers to prospective customers, keeping strong working client relationships makes business developers an indispensable part of any organization. With a starting salary of INR 24,000 per month for freshers to INR 1,000,000 per month for experienced executives, it is a rewarding career option.

**Advertising and Media**

The advertisement and media industries are both dynamic and lucrative. The initial annual salary for professionals in this sector is around INR 420,000, and it rises in line with skills, expertise, and experience. The average salary in this sector is INR 8,35,865 per year.

**Graphic Designer**

With an average salary of INR 299,417 per year, graphic designing is an innovative career path that is available to people from non-tech fields as well. It employs a combination of text, photographs, and visual concepts to convey ideas and connect to the intended audience.

**Technical Writer**

Technical writing is among the top non-IT jobs in India, involving the compilation, recording, and distribution of content to consumers, designers, and producers. Individuals with a backdrop in engineering, computer science, or information technology may be interested in this non-tech profession. In India, the median technical writer payment is INR 486,985.

**Content Manager**

Content managers are in charge of handling online digital content campaigns in today's offices.

A content manager's duties involve overseeing content departments, incorporating input from individual copywriters, developing and engaging a brand's online audience, monitoring community engagement, and executing content strategies.

In India, the average wage for a content manager is INR 509,938.

Hope you find this helpful. 

Happy job hunting!
