alleged stolen property, the factum of snatching or theft, the identity and presence of
the accused at the time of the alleged offence, and the recovery of the mobile phone
from the accused have not been proved in accordance with law. The case rests solely
on uncorroborated testimonies of police witnesses, which, in the absence of
independent support and in light of the noted deficiencies, do not inspire confidence.
