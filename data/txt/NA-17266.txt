I was in the process of applying for a student visa for my Master's program in Italy through VFS Global. Unfortunately, I encountered a technical issue on their website while attempting to book an appointment, which prevented me from receiving the OTP for the booking process.

Feeling frustrated by the appointment delay, I made the stupidest decision to contact an unauthorized service provider I found on Facebook. My intention was simply to inquire about appointment availability and gather information. They then asked for my passport and my stupid ass sent them a pic of it and without my consent, they proceeded to book an appointment on October 4th, 2023, at a cost of 536 Rupees. To make matters worse, they processed this payment themselves and then demanded an additional 35,000 Rupees as a "service charge" to cancel the appointment. They literally book it after 3-4 texts and without confirming the date and price with me.

After an hour or two of arguing, they are now asking for 3k. I'm at a loss about how to proceed. I'm reluctant to fall victim to a scam, but I'm also worried about the time I've lost in the visa application process. What should I do now?
