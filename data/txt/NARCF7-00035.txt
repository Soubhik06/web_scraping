I dont know if posts like these are allowed. i just want to make my fellow Indian users aware of the bullshit the Zety is pulling.

Its a long story. Hope you guys have the patience to read through it.

So on 23rd April I tried to buy a subscription from www.zety.com because I wanted to download the CV I made. There was a "Pay via UPI" option on their website so I used that option just for the sake of convenience. When I entered my UPI id in the field I received a payment request from JUMAR SOLUTION PRIVATE LIMITED for 395 rupees, the same amount that the subscription was for. To some this might seem an insignificant sum of money but to a student like me its literally my pocket money for the week.

There have been multiple occasions where the payment request for a service has been on some other name, so I didn't mind it much and finished the payment. After paying the website refreshed on its own and took my back to the payment page. 

I went to my profile dashboard to see if I have the subscription but there was not subscription listed there. I thought the transaction must've failed so I checked my Bank app but the money was debited from my account.

So I emailed them about this. The first time I emailed, I send them the screenshot of the transaction as proof of the payment. They said "We don't do UPI" and closed the thread without informing me further.

So I called their customer service (which was a pain in the ass cause of timezones. Their office opens 6:30PM IST) and told them about his situation. They again said that "There is no credit card information on my account for the subscription" so I explained to them what happened in detail and they told me to email them once again with the proof.

I emailed them again. Sent them the screenshot of the payment as well as the screenshot of their website showing the UPI payment option as proof. to which they said that I need to send them my bank statement. So I sent them my bank statement which clearly shows the money being debited fro my account.

To which they had the audacity to repeat that "They dont do UPi. and it was a virus or extension on my end that was auto populating that option for me". I was very furious at this point. I opened the website in incognito mode where extensions dont work, the UPI option was still there. I downloaded firefox just to check if maybe it was just a browser thing, UPI option still there. I even entertained the fact that my computer had a virus and opened the website on my phone. GUESS WHAT? The UPI option was still there. They had the fucking audacity to tell me that I need to see an expert so that I don't get defrauded again. Fucking assholes.

I called their customer support, very angrily explained what the issue was and they kept circling back to the same thing "We don't see any credit card information on your account". To which after 30 mins of talking to their so called "superior" came to no conclusion and am now here to rant about the situation I am in.

I filed a cybercrime report against Zety and Jumar Solutions, I dont know how much those help.

TLDR: DO NOT PURCHASE A SUBSCRIPTION FROM ZETY USING YOUR UPI ID. DONT PURCHASE A SUBSCRIPTION AT ALL. USE [Overleaf](https://www.overleaf.com/gallery/tagged/cv) INSTEAD.
