As a CS Student I never felt I could be fooled ..

I was contacted by HR of reputed company namely pfizerpharma , telling me I was shortlisted for an interview as a position for Junior Data Scientist. I applied on phizer through Linkedin , So I felt it was Genuine

The interview Lasted 1 hour with HR Interview Included... The employers Looked Genuine enough

However (Red Flag 1 - Interview was very easy)(Red Flag 2 - All rounds were telephonic which is not expected from a big company)

I was offered a double digit lpa salary and also got an official offer letter except I was needed to produce an IBM SPSS Modeller Certification and tell its Certfication ID

As I was excited, my lack of better judgment took place and I cracked the exam in 13 days , the certification costed me few Days and 15k money , however I didnt sit for many good companies in those 13 days which this phising attack also costed me

After Getting Certification Phone went unanswered , so does email everything , its like two employers never existed

The email looked genuine enough with all the credentials so it is necessary to double check or triple check the email id

PS In the end it didnt costed me a lot as The certificate i think would help somewhere even slighly and am already doing masters in ai in new iit , but I really wonder how bad it could have gone for others
