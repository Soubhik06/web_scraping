**Final Thoughts:** I’m perplexed at the ignorance of reddit. Seriously, can’t believe that people still think us Biharis are milking and grazing our cows only. Many are asking how am I using internet. While I don’t blame you guys for ignorance, certainly onus goes to the mainstream media. Whenever it comes to Bihar, they either show Lalu Yadav or roadside beggars

I would like to clear some confusion:-
1)	Yes. Bihar still lacks in many socio-economic indicators but it aint as bad as it used to be. Bihar’s State average GDP growth rate in last 10 years has been over 10%. There is good road connectivity of villages with urban areas. My hometown is 13 KM away from the nearest town but state highway is only one KM away from my home. 
http://www.thehindu.com/news/national/other-states/bihar-tops-in-growth-says-central-statistics-office/article6395138.ece

2)	Only few people in my village have cows/buffaloes and mostly for household use purposes. It’s better to have a cow and drink fresh milk than buying adulterated milk. 

3)	Statistically speaking, Bihar fares better than majority of so-called developed states like Maharashtra in terms of crime. Take this with a truckload of salt. 
http://www.hindustantimes.com/india-news/newdelhi/statistics-prove-raj-wrong-bihar-ranks-low-in-crime-against-women/article1-986384.aspx

4)	As I said already, education system is fucked up in Bihar but such is the case with India as a whole. Yet You will find so many Biharis cracking the IAS and IITs/AIEEE simply because they are hardworking. Bihar has as many teachers with forged degrees as rest of India. No big deal here. And some students cheat in the exam but that’s the case with whole world.

5)	Since you guys have been asking how I’m using internet. I would like to show you some govt statistics

http://articles.economictimes.indiatimes.com/2013-02-24/news/37270140_1_bihar-deputy-cm-mobile-phone-sushil-kumar-modi

http://data.gov.in/catalog/state-wise-number-telephones-india#web_catalog_tabs_block_10

 

You can go to their profiles. All of these folks are from different villages. Most of them of course use internet on their smartphones. Cyber cafes to have sprung up at various nukkads.

6)	Some of my relatives have bought flats of crores of Rupees yet they are BPL on paper. One of the persons from my village is project manager of Bin-Ladin group in Saudi Arabia. He oversees one of the biggest properties of Bin-ladin group in Mecca managing over 1500 workers; mostly from Bihar. Guess what, on paper, he is just regular aam junta of Bihar. There are lakhs of such cases in Bihar. My cousin alone spends 12k in Delhi and on paper my uncle’s income is just Rs. 60k per annum.

7) last but not the least, we Biharis really suck at English. You can embarrass most of us just by speaking english. Anyway, I had fun answering people here. ENjoy your weekend. :)
