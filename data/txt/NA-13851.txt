India’s Minister of Finance, Nirmala Sitharaman, held a meeting with startup and fintech firms on February 26, during which the following action points were discussed, according to a press release from the Finance Ministry:

Representatives from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), the Department of Financial Services (DFS), the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Fintech Association for Consumer Empowerment (FACE), Digital Lenders’ Association of India (DLAI), Payment Council of India (PCI), Fintech Convergence Council (FCC), along with various startups and fintech companies, were present at the meeting.
