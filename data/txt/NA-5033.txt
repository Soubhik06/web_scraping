Date : 08.11.2023

 Heard Mr. D.P. Borah, learned counsel for the petitioners. Also heard Mr. M.
Phukan, learned Public Prosecutor for the State. 2. This is an application made under Section 439 Cr.P.C., seeking bail
by the accused-petitioners, namely, (i) Rupjyoti Talukdar (ii) Taufiq Alam,
in connection with Cyber P.S. FIR No. 08/2023, registered under Section
120B / 419 / 420 / 385 / 467 / 471 / 468 / 507 IPC r/w Section
65 / 66 / 66B / 66C / 66D of IT Act 2000. 3. It has been submitted by the learned counsel for the accused-
petitioners that the petitioners had not committed any offence as alleged in the
FIR. It is also submitted that the petitioners were arrested only on suspicion
without having any materials against them. It is further submitted that the
accused-petitioners have been detained in custody since 17.10.2023, i.e. 23
days. Nothing was seized by police in connection with alleged case. Learned
counsel has pointed out that no any victim has made any whisper that they
have been cheated by the alleged accused/petitioners. FIR was lodged by one
Inspector, Crime Branch, Guwahati. 4. It is also the submission of learned counsel for the petitioners that
except the offence under Section 467 IPC, the mandatory period of other
alleged offences are 60 days. Section 467 IPC is not attracted here in this case
as because there is no allegation against the petitioners that they forged any
document which purports to be a valuable security/will etc. In fact, police has
failed to seize any article/document during investigation. 5. It is also submitted that the petitioner No. 1 has been working in
 Page No.# 3/4

Shree Northeast Connect Services Private Ltd. since 01.04.2012 and the
petitioner No. 2 has been working in Internet Service Provider Company vic.
Gignet IT Service as a Support Engineer since 2018. They only provided internet
connection to the call centre for which they received commission only. They had
no knowledge about the alleged scam, if any. Considering the nature of offence
as well as length of detention, petitioners may be enlarged on bail. 6. In response, Mr. Phukan, learned Public Prosecutor for the State has
opposed in grating bail to the accused/petitioners. It is submitted that the crime
has been committed in an organized manner and the investigation is still going
on, so that the prayer of the accused/petitioners may not be considered at this
stage. 7. I have considered the submissions made by the learned counsel for
the parties. I have also perused the case diary along with other relevant
documents available thereon. 8. Considering the materials found in the case diary as well as the fact
that substantial part of investigation has been completed and no any documents
have been recovered from the possession of the accused/petitioners, this Court
is of the view that further custodial interrogation of the accused-petitioners is
not necessary in the interest of investigation of the case. 9. Accordingly, the accused-petitioners, named above, shall be released
on bail in connection with Cyber P.S. FIR No. 08/2023, registered under Section 120B / 419 / 420 / 385 / 467 / 471 / 468 / 507 IPC r/w Section
65 / 66 / 66B / 66C / 66D of IT Act 2000, on furnishing bail bond of
Rs.50,000/- each with two suitable sureties each of the like amount out
of which one of the sureties should be a Government employee, to the
satisfaction of learned CJM, Kamrup(M), Guwahati. Page No.# 4/4 10. The direction for bail is further subject to the conditions that the
