Hi folks, need help! 
My cousin was scammed today for ₹70,000.

A fraudster called her, claiming she had credit card dues, and directed her to contact a "bank representative" (another fraudster).

The fake bank representative then told her that her phone number was being used for money laundering, with multiple cases registered in her name, and informed her that it's a sensitive matter.

As part of the scam, they conducted an ID verification via a video call, where **they showed a fake police station setup and someone dressed in a police uniform spoke to her.** They scared her so much by showing an arrest warrant and making her read it.

 
They further asked how much money she had in her account and managed to get her to transfer the entire amount—₹70,000—to two UPI IDs.

 
**What we did about it so far**: 
1. She went to the police station, they said it's a cybersecurity matter so register a compliant online. 
2. She filed a complaint with the cyber cell 
3. She did a complaint with both her banks which she used to send the money. 
4. She's calling 1930 to register compliant with RBI

 
It's a big sum of money for her and she's devastated. Is there anything we can do to help her out of this?Any suggestions are welcome.
