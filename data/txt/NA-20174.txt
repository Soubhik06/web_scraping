**Indian Black Money**

Black money is, 

1. Money stored in Foreign Bank Accounts (Swiss Banks)

2. Money in circulation which is untaxed (Shadow Economy)

**Shadow Economy**

Again, there are no reliable data. [This](http://ftp.iza.org/dp2315.pdf) old paper has done it thoroughly. It was **25.6 % of GDP in 2002**. But it was rapidly growing. [Recent reports](http://www.thehindu.com/business/Economy/India’s-black-economy-shrinks-says-Ambit/article14386313.ece) say it is **20% of GDP**. I have assumed it is **25% of GDP**.

(The paper although old is very clear that it includes assets the Hindu report is not very clear about the context)

* Push Cashless so that Gov can tax more. And the system is cleaner.
* Increase liquidity of Banks so interest rates go low. Businesses increase if that happens.
* Effect UP election so BJP gets more seats in upper house. So passing legislation is easier for Modi.
* Disrupting Terror Funding / Illegal businesses.
