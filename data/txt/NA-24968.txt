I've been getting this scam call wherein the recorded voice says that my FedEx parcel is stopped due to some error, talk to anyone executive. 

I always disconnected it because I know it's a scam but 2 days ago curiosity got the best of me and I ended up talking to the executive. 

He said that he's speaking from FedEx Mumbai Andheri East branch and apprantky there's been a parcel in my name with multiple passports, laptop, some 4-5 kg clothes, and 50g MDMA. I was shocked and kept thinking of how they're trying to trap the innocent people. He then asked me if I know what MDMA is to which I denied then he explained. I played along and asked for the solution. He said I should be registering an online cyber FIR and he wants to redirect the call to the cyber security department to which I will obliged. 

Redirected the call to cyber department, the person said he was some head of the department. Behaved as if he doesn't know anything and asked me to explain how I ended up in this call. Explained everything to him and upon hearing the term 'mdma' he tried to make me understand how fatal this can be for me and how someone is using my ID and how it can disrupt my life, blah blah. 

While he was explaining, the call got disconnected twice. On 2nd disconnection, when he called me back 3rd time, he was furious and thought I'm doing this on purpose, which I was not. He then came down to abusive language and I tried to ask him what is this behaviour Pooja but I think he cut the call. 

While our generation is smart enough to understand this is all scam, please let your parents know about this. They might fall prey to this. I told this to my mother and she was so scared and got angry on me only. :(0
