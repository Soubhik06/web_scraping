--- POST TEXT ---

Today I had received a call from a person claiming to be an employee from Fedex international Andheri East. They have specified that my Aadhaar identity is compromised and that someone has tried to export something illegal in my name, they have even further conveniently connected me to the cyber crime department of Mumbai by providing some details about the package that was sent, while I was waiting a soothing jingle that said "Welcome to Cyber crime department" was playing. The lady who claimed to be the DCP Tejaswi Satpute of Mumbai has asked me to download Skype and connect with her for a vocal statement, and that Cyber crime department of India uses Skype officially.

&amp;#x200B;

Instead of connecting on Skype, I had called Fedex India directly to ask if this is genuine, to which they have verified that this is definitely a scam, and I could report it to Fedex India through an email, which I did. 

I have tried calling the cyber crime department of India on their toll free number to report the phone numbers but I didn't get through. I will most probably try again, I was skeptical about posting the numbers here on reddit publicly, so I haven't, "yet".

Then I have read these articles: 
[https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/delhi/doctor-duped-of-rs-4-47-crore-by-fraudsters-posing-as-fedex-employees-mumbai-cops-8620500/](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/delhi/doctor-duped-of-rs-4-47-crore-by-fraudsters-posing-as-fedex-employees-mumbai-cops-8620500/) 
[https://www.indiatoday.in/technology/news/story/indians-losing-lakhs-to-the-new-courier-scam-what-is-happening-how-to-stay-safe-2399043-2023-06-28](https://www.indiatoday.in/technology/news/story/indians-losing-lakhs-to-the-new-courier-scam-what-is-happening-how-to-stay-safe-2399043-2023-06-28)

&amp;#x200B;

What's saddening about this whole experience was when I imagined someone who would be a little older than me and isn't as tech savvy go through the same experience, someone like my mother herself going through the same, the sheer amount of anxiety they would have gone through with this. So I thought I would make a reddit post and if this would at least help one person in the future, I am happy.
