*This might be often pointed out or obvious but just to refresh everyone's mind again so that no one accidentally falls for this and to nip them in the buds.*

 
Got several calls telling me I had an outgoing parcel via FedEx from Mumbai to some other country and its blocked. Press "0" to talk to an agent. 

The "agent" asked me if I am living in Mumbai, I said no, and then a few BS after that.

I am unaware what goes after that, since I cut the call after a few seconds. but possibly asking you to pay money to gt the parcel unstuck and such. I got the same call 3 times afterwards from different numbers. Not FedEx but random numbers that I reported via TRAI DND.

 
Again, this is a widely used scam, just posting it so that no one entertains it or falls for this unkowingly in their busy day to day lives.

 
Cheers :)
