Fair warning: My English is going to be pretty rough.

This happened in V-block. This is around 9:30 AM today. A woman showed up at the door and kept on ringing the door bell. I am a bit under the weather, so was asleep. I rushed thinking it might be something urgent only to discover her with a piece of paper with a grin on her face.

I looked at the paper and told her to wait for a second, so I could grab my glasses. Read the paper and it had names of several people and she was asking for donations for orphans. First I told her I don't have cash and she said to UPI. At this point of time, she was trying to enter the home and I was blocking the door by standing in the way. She said to just donate 200 with still the same grin - I refused. She said 100 - I refused again. She said God will guide you, I said I'm good. She said don't you care about orphans?! I replied with I don't. 

This continued for about 5 min, even though I kept on denying. A woman so I couldn't have been too harsh as it might have lead to more trouble (I'm a guy). I told her that I'll call the cops and only then she left. But before leaving, she called me chashmish and with now no such grin visible. I called her a beggar as she was leaving. 

Is this normal in Gurgaon? I moved here this year only to comment. It was very likely a scam tho as she wasn't able to give any proof of where the donations are going. I just saw a lot of people had donated in hundreds to some in thousands.
