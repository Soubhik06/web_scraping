Background: In 2016 my father was approached by a real estate agent (who was working informally for a real estate firm on commission basis). Showed us a plot in a gated community layout located in a village. They assured future development of City and also showed plans for avenue plantation, club house, swimming pool and other amenities. Though it was a remote location, my father invested his life savings just before retirement in a plot in 2016 @ Rs. 8500 per square yard. Invested about 23 lakhs in total. When we bought there were basic facilities for selling like BT road and avenue plantation (no club, swimming pool, commercial complex or other amenities). 

Fast forward to Present 2024: Last week we visited that site. We were shocked at the state of entire gated community. The entire community is covered in thick bushes. We were unable to pinpoint our plot as there were no markings. There was not a single construction in the entire community. It was pitch dark in the night as there was no electricity. We called the agent but not response. Went directly to Real estate company found that the manager who gave commission to agent for our plot also left the company. We got the shock of our life when we got to know that the present market value of our plot is Rs. 9000 per square yard after 8 years time. A new manager of that firm informed that the value of that land in 2016 was just Rs. 3500. He confessed that both agent and previous manager might have duped us in buying it at Rs 8500. 
Because of this fraud we are getting breakeven now after 8 years. My Father is very upset because his life savings were invested in it. 
Is there anything we can do to the fraudsters legally? Is there anyway to claim compensation?
