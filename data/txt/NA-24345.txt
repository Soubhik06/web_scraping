--- POST TEXT ---

My husband and I have been looking for a rental apartment for the past few weeks. Apart from the fact that it's very difficult to find a good apartment in a decent location which doesn't cost an arm and a leg and allows us to live there, we came across a pattern of scams being followed in abundance. 

I looked through Housing.com for apartments, and tried to look for ones listed by the owner. The apartment would be listed for a very cheap price (most commonly 12k for a 2bhk, 22k for a 3bhk, with a very low deposit of around 30-40k) in a good location. On contacting, they even share pictures and location of the apartment. Everything seems fine till i asked if we could visit the place. Then they said that I've to pay a "token amount" of 2k or 1k to just see the place! I'd never heard of that before, and I've been in Bangalore for 8 years now. My husband is born/brought up here, and he also said he'd never heard of such a thing. One of the people said it's because he is coming from Mangalore to show the place and last time he came the interested people didn't visit so he lost time and money. Another person said that it's what the building manager has requested. All of them said that if we don't like the place, they will refund the amount immediately. Lol. As soon as i said "Scam" to one, he immediately blocked me. 

I found at least 10 listings like this, maybe more, and this is just in the area we were searching (Jayanagar and BTM). We did not fall for it, but someone who is new to Bangalore and doesn't know how things work here, or who isn't as street smart, might fall for it. 8 years ago, i would have fallen for it myself. To a lot of people this isn't a small amount either. 

Bangalore had become a hub for scams. Be careful out there, folks!
