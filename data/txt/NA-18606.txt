Hey /r/India,

I just want to warn you about the latest scam trend.
A random caller will contact you telling that he's from XYZ bank and that he's contacting you regarding your Debit card which has been "disabled" according to him.

The guy will proceed by telling you some bullshit procedure and will say "In order to confirm, I will need the 16 digit debit card number". Do not provide with ANY details that are on your debit card.
Remember that if the bank calls you, chances are that they already know the details required to do most of the tasks. The only thing they will ever ask are trivial details just to confirm that you are the intended user.

How to check if the call is really from the bank:

* Ask the caller who does he wants to speak. 90% of the time the scammer won't know your name or will take time in replying (while he searches your number in TrueCaller)
*Ask the person which bank are they calling you from. If they name a bank you dont have an account with, its a scam call.
* Ask them if they know which branch your account is associated with.
* Try to confuse them by saying you have a credit card and not debit or vice versa. If they easily change their words, its probably a scammer.

Please be very caution about such calls and emails. They can easily empty your accounts and there wont be much you can do about it afterwards. Report any fraud calls and email addresses.
