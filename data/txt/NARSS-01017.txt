Date: 18-11-2023

Beware guys! 

I landed around 10:30PM and booked a cab from the airport. The fare was ₹691 to Gurgaon. I checked the driver details and it was an uncle with a rating of 3. I was little suspicious about him looking at the rating. Also, he has less than 50 trips on Uber, I thought he is new maybe he is learning. I ignored everything and boarded the cab. 

He asked me about from where I am coming? What I do here? I did answer them and he continued driving. He was driving rashly but he has the control on it.

After I reached I usually look into the driver’s phone for the bill and this time it came out as ₹1098. It did surprise me a bit but I paid him as the airport fares get usually high. I know it was my mistake that I was not aware at the moment. 

I came home and checked notification on my phone and it was ₹691 only. That moment I knew I was scammed. 

What I realised:
1. The rating of the driver was less because of the scam.
2. He showed me a screenshot of my name and exact replica of uber screenshot.
3. He asked about me just to make sure I am not a local.

I demanded refund from Uber and I got the money back. Please beware of these kind of scams and make sure to double check the amount before payment.
