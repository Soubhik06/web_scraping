So I encountered a new scammer in town. He is a guy wearing white shorts and blue tshirt and rides a splendor.

He will come to you and ask for money to fuel up his bike because one of his friends met with an accident near Ramnagara. 

He will say he is in college and don't have money, but he definitely doesn't look like a college guy. He says it's his birthday today, his parents will come in evening and then he will pay you back. He will ask to transfer in bank account and no UPI.

How I know for sure that this is a scam. First time I encountered him near Rameshwaram cafe in Indiranagar, next time I saw him in HSR near Chaitini, telling same story to someone else, in same dress and and same bike.
