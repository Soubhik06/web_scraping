I’m Shivam, currently based in Pune. I wanted to share something I’ve been building and would love to get your feedback on it. 🙏

The Problem:

Over the last couple of years, I noticed a major problem — whether it's selling a ticket, buying a sneaker, or even doing small freelance deals — there’s always a fear of getting scammed.

* Buyers fear paying first and not receiving the product. 
* Sellers fear shipping something and not getting paid.

The Idea:

I kept wondering — why isn’t there a simple, trusted way for two strangers to complete a deal safely?

So I built SecureSwap — a platform that acts like a middleman.

Here's how it works in simple words:

* The buyer’s money is securely held until they confirm receiving the goods/services.
* Only after both sides are happy, the payment gets released.
* No need to blindly trust a stranger anymore.

The Stage:

The website is live (still in early testing phase)
