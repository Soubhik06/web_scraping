(05.06.2023) 1. The issue under consideration arising out of the second appeal dated Nil include
non-receipt of the following information sought by the appellant through the RTI
application dated 11.10.2021 and first appeal dated 10.11.2021:- 2. Succinctly facts of the case are that the appellant filed an application dated
11.10.2021 under the Right to Information Act, 2005 (RTI Act ) before the Central Public
Information Officer (CPIO), Punjab National Bank, Jind. The CPIO vide letter dated
09.11.2021 replied to the appellant. Aggrieved by the same, the appellant filed first appeal
dated 10.11.2021. The First Appellate Authority (FAA) vide order dated 08.12.2021
disposed of the first appeal. Aggrieved by that, the appellant filed second appeal dated
27.01.2021 before the Commission which is under consideration. 3. The appellant has filed the instant appeal dated 27.01.2021 inter alia on the grounds
that reply given by the CPIO was not satisfactory. The appellant requested the Commission
to direct the CPIO to provide the complete information and take necessary action as per Section 20 (1) of the RTI Act. 4. The CPIO replied vide letter dated 09.11.2021 and the same is reproduced as under:- The FAA vide order dated 08.12.2021 provided revised point-wise reply to the appellant
and the same is reproduced as under. Without sharing account No /Debit Card No / ATM PIN /OTP, mobile
 banking cannot be registered / activated. Without sharing
 login/MPIN/transaction password /registered mobile No., e-commerce
 transactions/ fund transfer cannot be done using IMPS. PNB verify App
 cannot be registered without sharing OTP which is used to authenticate
 Internet Banking transaction in place of OTP. (vii) Treat reply at point No 06." 5. The appellant and on behalf of the respondent Shri H.C.Ahlawat, Chief Manager,
Punjab National Bank, Jind attended the hearing through video conference. 5.1. The appellant inter alia submitted that he was not satisfied with the reply given by
the respondents. 5.2. The respondent while defending their case inter alia submitted that they had
provided point-wise reply to the appellant vide letters dated 09.11.2021 and 08.12.2021. 6. The Commission after adverting to the facts and circumstances of the case, hearing
both the parties and perusal of records, observed that due reply was given by the respondent
vide letters dated 09.11.2021 and 08.12.2021. Perusal of the records further revealed that
most of the queries raised by the appellant was in the form of queries which did not fall
within the definition of "information" as defined under section 2 (f) of the RTI Act.
Moreover, the appellant had grievance with regard to transaction held in his account which
may be raised before an appropriate forum. In view of the above, there appears to be no
public interest in further prolonging the matter. Accordingly, the appeal is dismissed. Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties. Sd/- (Suresh Chandra) (सुसुरेशचं ा)
