Yesterday, I placed an order on Swiggy, and the delivery partner upon reaching my place, called and said 'bhaiya chot lag gyi hai' when I asked him to get to the 1st Floor. I went down, and he started asking for money, as he wanted to get an X-ray done. He said he got in an accident. He had torn trousers and was limping.

I felt sorry that hurt himself while delivering my order, so I sent 500₹ to help him. He said ki X-ray 1300-1400 ka hoga and this would not help, he said he'll return the money by 5 pm, next day (today). So, I transferred 500 more and said that's the max I can do. Then he said he doesn't have any money with him, and said ki 2000 krdo, he'll return by next day surely. He said you can keep my mobile as a collateral. I sent another 1000, then he added ki doctor's fee would also be there, toh total 3000 krdo, he swore on him mom, dad, family and started begging by touching my feet. I gave in and transferred the money to him, and took his mobile number.

Now, after some hours, he again called ki bhaiya fracture ho gya hai, 6000 lagenge for plaster, and started crying a lot. But by this time, I had realised that I was being scammed, and refused. He sent me random photos of hospital. I replied by asking him for a report, but that went ignored. This is when I alwas completely sure I was scammed. I don't know why I didn't realise this, when he kept increasing the amount pehle.

Posting just in case he tries this with someone else.

TLDR: Swiggy guy faked an accident, and ran away with 3k :*(
