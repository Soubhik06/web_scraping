I’m sure lawyers are privy to a plethora cases and can share insight on what I’d like ask as it’s really bothering me on a visceral level. 

Why has scamming become so common, like second nature?

I’m taken aback by the explosion of this epidemic. Scams are taking place online and/or the moment we step out of our house. I’m a helpless victim too and everyday I witness, whether on tv, internet, word of mouth, or from friends and relatives, that they’ve fallen victim to a scam whether it’s related to bribe, educational institutes, property matters, travel agencies, health sector, car dealerships, marriage scams, kyc/otp, shopping portals, auto/cab drivers and sadly some folks are being scammed by their own friends and relatives. 

Scams are taking place freely, spreading like wildfire without fear of law or repercussions. Is there no regulatory body to investigate the fraudulent activity happening day in and day out? Or is the investigation only reserved and expedited for people with power and a fat bank balance? We regular citizens are useless sacks of skin and bones right? Because look, cases in court take ages, people have no faith anymore. 

A person posted about his legal battle, he got justice after 20 years for his father’s death due to negligence. Sadly the hospital still hasn’t paid compensation even though he spent triple the amount fighting the case. Who has authority to handle civil cases other than courts?

Should we stop associating with everyone and cry in a corner?
