There's a lot of confusion among young unmarried couples (well, at least in the friends circle that I belong to) to choose a safe and welcoming hotel in their city (or wherever they stay) so that they can some fun, among other reasons. Apps and websites do make the task easier but still it's a huge ass problem to decipher the ratings and reviews along with options like 'couple friendly hotels' and 'relationship mode' etc., a combination of these factors along with the budget make it difficult to choose a proper hotel as there will be a compromise in one factor or the other. Not everyone can afford a luxurious 5-star hotel, and therefore this list is aimed primarily towards affordability as an aspect. 

Legally, no law prohibits adult couples from staying in any hotel but since these businesses hold the right to admit, they can deny entry for such people for whatever reason they might claim irrespective of the booking status. 

This is the Google form link-- [https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1H1uDy-m\_GUvBdWujtvp5Rr82TP4bXEBPSi7rGv1lOCU/edit](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1H1uDy-m_GUvBdWujtvp5Rr82TP4bXEBPSi7rGv1lOCU/edit) To those of you all who have been there, please fill it up. Do share it with others as well who can contribute as well. 

A series of questions have been placed, based on the responses a list will be released with both-- hotels to choose and hotels to avoid (if any). Any changes or addition in the questionnaire will be duly informed, there are some obvious pitfalls like combatting fake responses or verification, among others, but still I am hopeful to find a way to counter them. For some, all this might seem like a jobless work, but I really want to do something out of it through my efforts. There are numerous other target areas to make a crowdsourced list like free-food/low cost food distribution centers etc, but that will be seen based on the responses I get over here, fingers crossed. 

TL;DR- Basically the title, go fill up the above form if you ever happened to visit a hotel with your partner.
