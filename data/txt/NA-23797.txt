--- POST TEXT ---

So a desperate distant relative reached out 3 weeks ago to find him some offline courses from one of those ORR software training institutes. He has stopped getting call-backs from recruiters long ago and is now seriously considering up/reskilling. He made me call a few institutes and inquire specifically about the market value of the offered courses and if the institute does any placements.

While a lot of institutes were reluctant to admit the market situation, some frankly talked about it and said, for e.g. jobs based on a couple of cloud technologies, BI developer tools such as Power BI, Informatica, etc. have stopped to a large extent even though they were in demand a year ago. 

They admitted that there is no guarantee of placements, however, they said they would "create a good resume" for you, and upload it on job portals for you. They would also share all the frequently asked interview questions and prepare you for all offline interviews. When I said that nowadays interviews are often online, they said, in such cases they would teach how to write "prompts" and use speech prompts during the interviews. I acted as if I did not know what they meant and probed further. Some of them sounded convinced that using OpenAI prompts in interviews is not cheating and belive that everyone is using it to clear interviews so nobody will get caught.

I have asked my relative to just stick to YouTube and MOOCs for now.
