India’s parliamentary committee openly accepted that India’s medical system is going to collapse soon. According to a report by zee news, 44% surgeries done are undesired, done with the sole purpose of looting patients.

Distinguishably, 55% heart, 48% uterus, 47% cancer, 48% knee replacement, 45% C-section surgeries being done are unnecessary.

Corporate structure of hospitals: salary of a newbie doctor- 1 lakh/month, salary of an experienced doctor- 1 crore/month, difference is because of their targets, senior doctors are given definite monthly targets to deceive outgoing patients to get into high end treatments, hospitalization and surgeries.

To renew the doctors’ annual contracts with the hospitals and revise their payscale, their history of targets are assessed which coerces them for illegal scams.

Scams of looting dead patients- charging for hospitalizing (like ventilating) a dead body, doing an emergency surgery on dead body and claiming that patient died during surgery.

How to avoid health insurance policy scams, so that insurance companies can’t reject providing aid at the time of need:

\-check type of insurance, if it is personal or family plan

\[I think for family plan it is important to check for how many people will the company afford to aid simultaneously at the time of crisis, for an instance, there are chances that 5 family members get injured in an accident simultaneously, but the insurance policy is such that at a time the company will provide aid for a maximum of 3 members\]

\-pre and post hospitalization expenses covered or not

\-day care treatments should be covered

\-room rent limit, eg: company may provide only Rs 5000 per day, but the hospital’s room rent is Rs 10000 per day.

\-don’t hide premedical conditions in greed of more premium, at the time of buying insurance because it may cause problems later, while claiming insurance

\-don’t rely on your job’s corporate health insurance because they provide low covers and leaving the company cancels the insurance

\-cashless network hospital- amount paid by company directly to the hospital, insured person isn’t needed to pay to the hospital and claim the amount later

\-ideally, the insurance company’s network of hospitals for the free service of their customers should be as high as possible

A case in 2019 caught an organ trafficking nexus in Fortis hospital, New Delhi when the patient Sangeeta, who was taken to the hospital by a friend (involved in the scam) for getting a health certificate required for a job, heard the doctors calling her ‘donor’, and ran away from the hospital.

\-fraudulent diagnosis: doctors are commissioned by labs to recommend the patients for unwanted tests from the labs, many labs generate fake reports, approximately, only 1 thousand out of 1 lakh labs in India are accredited/government registered.
