1. The petitioner apprehends his arrest in connection with

 Complaint Case No. 233C / 2020 dt. 17.08.2020 registered for

 the offence under section 420 , 467 , 468 , 470 , 472 , 473 , 120B / 34 of the I.P.C. 2. As per the complaint petition the complainant received a

 call on his mobile from mobile no. 7632874872 caller Swaroop

 Kumar and one Abhishek Kumar Tiwari told him that he has

 won an amount of Rs. 6,60,272/- and one Hero motorcycle

 under a lucky draw scheme and the complainant was told to

 deposit Rs. 1,01,600/- in the account bearing no.
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.21780 of 2025(2) dt.30-04-2025 2/4 33121545705, IFSC Code - 0005758 of one Prakash Kumar

 Pathak i.e. petitioner for receiving the said reward and was

 assured that the amount of the reward / prize along with

 motorcycle will be provided within three days. Thereafter the

 complainant deposited the amount of Rs. 1,01,600/- in the

 aforesaid account of the petitioner on different dates in five

 installments but after receiving the above amount the accused

 did not pay the reward / prize money to the complainant. It is

 further alleged that the accused persons further demanded Rs.

 8000/- for delivery charge upon which the complainant got

 suspicion. The accused persons after receiving the amount have

 cheated the informant. 3. No one appears for the petitioner. 4. Learned counsel for the state submits that the offence

 falls under the category of cyber crime which is a serious

 offence as such the petitioner may not be given benefit of

 anticipatory bail. 5. The petitioner in the bail application has submitted that

 he is innocent and has falsely been implicated in this case due to

 ulterior motive. In paragraph no. 8 of the application it has been

 stated that Rs. 1,01,600/- has been deposited in the account of

 petitioner by the complainant on the instruction of the
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.21780 of 2025(2) dt.30-04-2025 3/4 co-accused Abhishek Kumar Tiwari and the petitioner has no

 concern with the said person. It is further stated in paragraph no.

 9 that no conversation took place between the petitioner and the

 complainant in the entire episode. In paragraph no. 12 of the

 application it is stated that there is no direct allegation against

 the petitioner. The petitioner has no criminal antecedent as per

 statement made in paragraph no. 3 of the petition. 6. From perusal of the complaint petition and the impugned

 order it is evident that the petitioner in connivance with his

 accomplice / co-accused cheated the complainant of his hard

 earned money Rs. 1,01,600/-. In the order of learned Additional

 Sessions Judge-II, Supaul it has come that the petitioner has

 conceded that aforesaid amount has been transferred in the

 account of the petitioner from the account of the complainant

 and the same has been withdrawn by the petitioner. The

 petitioner operates an organized gang for committing cyber

 crime. All accused persons appear to have formed a gang to

 defraud the innocent public and phishing / cyber fraud rampant

 in area where the attackers try to trick individuals into providing

 user names, password and other details and are indulged in

 e-mail phishing, voice phishing, SMS phishing etc. The case is

 still under investigation for which custodial interrogation of the
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.21780 of 2025(2) dt.30-04-2025 4/4 petitioner may be required. Accordingly I am not inclined to

 grant the privilege of anticipatory bail to the petitioner. The

 same stands rejected. (Anil Kumar Sinha, J)
