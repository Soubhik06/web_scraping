Just wanna make sure I didn’t fool myself here

I have a small debt to the Canadian revenue agency that I have been in talks with them to work a payment plan. This week I was getting bombarded with calls spoofing a real CRA number. I wasn’t answering them because the CRA typically doesn’t communicate in phone calls and there was no voicemail being left. But then I got a letter from the CRA at the same time these calls were coming in, so I answered the next time they called

First I hear a loud “beeep” then a very muffled Indian man started asking for “Barbara” my name isn’t Barbara, but the previous owner of my phone # was named Barbara. And every so often I still get people calling or texting looking for her. So I explained I get lots of calls looking for this lady and I don’t know her. Then the man asked for My information so he can upda- (phone call suddenly ends)

I think im good, it was clearly a scammer but where he adruptly ended the call I wasn’t sure if he just needed to keep me on the phone to download my information or something? I never said “yes” but I did say my first name, only my first name. Should I do anything to cover my tracks? I needed to call the CRA later today so I better bring up the fact that a scammer called me. But I’m worried that they may attempt to hit me with identity theft now.
