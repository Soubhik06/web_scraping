Heard counsel for the parties. The applicant has been arrested in connection with
 Crime No.09/2014 registered with Police Station STF,
 Bhopal - commonly known as VYAPAM Examination
 scam cases - for the offences punishable under Sections
 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 120-B of IPC and under Section
 3 D (1-2) 4 of the M.P. Recognized Examination Act,
 1937. The role ascribed to the applicant by the prosecution
 is that he acted as middleman and arranged for Vikas
 Pandey to appear in the examination as impersonator to
 the original candidate. The applicant has been arrested on 28.08.2014 and
 the charge-sheet has already been filed against him on
 19.09.2014. It has now been noticed that Vikas Pandey,
 who is the next link between the applicant and the original
 candidate, has already expired. The Investigating Officer
 has verified the fact that the said Vikas Pandey had
 appeared in the examination as impersonator to the
 original candidate. Since the custodial interrogation of the applicant is
no more necessary and the investigation qua applicant is
complete as of now, coupled with the undertaking given
by the applicant through counsel that he will extend full
cooperation in further investigation including against the
applicant, if any, and that the applicant shall punctually
appear before the trial Court for early disposal of the trial
and further would abide by all the conditions that may be
imposed by this Court, in the interest of justice, the
application is allowed on following conditions to be
strictly observed by the applicant:- The bail application stands disposed of accordingly.
