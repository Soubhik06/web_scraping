So, I ordered a [bluetooth mouse](https://www.amazon.in/gp/product/B088TTV7S4/) It got delivered today, I could clearly feel mouse is inside so did not record a video. Opened mouse, seems like original packing, works really well, except some lag in scrolling. So, I checked serial number on back of mouse and on box by entering it in HP website and tadaaa, it cannot find any product with that serial no. 

There are similar other [complaints on HP forum](https://h30434.www3.hp.com/t5/Desktop-Hardware-and-Upgrade-Questions/HP-Wireless-Mouse-Z3700-Serial-Number-Invalid-Issue-or-HP/td-p/8157971) and all of them have bought from amazon. 
Amazon has agreed to refund upon return. But fake product from a trusted seller like this is totally unacceptable. The product works and looks really good so easily many people will get fooled. I have asked them to investigate this and provide me the updates on this. 

Please be aware and always check serial numbers on official sites for electronic items.
