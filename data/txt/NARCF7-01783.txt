Hello Chennai friends I'm an out of state student here doing internship and I'm usually aware of frauds but today while exiting ashok nagar metro station one man approached me and said he was in dire need, asked 1000 in upi and he'll exchange in cash.. I had to reach so I took his phone number, I should've taken photo too. Not trying to defend myself but consider this my eye opening moment as this hadn't occurred to me before.. anyways I've sent him payment requests on both gpay and paytm, he's not picking calls also. Reported him on gpay, and according to their instructions, filed cyber crime report and emailed my bank (they have not responded yet). Anything else I should be doing? Idk if the money will ever come back but I had saved it for some clothes and I'm super bummed now. It pays to be naive and I feel super disappointed in myself.
