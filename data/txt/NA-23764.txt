Yesterday I was stopped by Police at Kundalahalli &amp; Old Airport Road Junction. Asked me for my Driving License, Insurance of my Activa, Pollution Certificate. Everything was arranged and I showed the documents.

Then the Police told me that I have due penalties which need to be paid. I was in doubt as I checked online 2 months back, there is no due on my Activa and since then I have not been on road. So I asked him to re-check, and he was confirming me yes there is due. I told him ok, I am not gonna pay you directly, let me check on my mobile, and then I will pay the due online(if any). The Police start wondering and after few seconds told me I can go.

I am quite surprised how easily the Police can lie to scam us, where they are supposed to protect us.
