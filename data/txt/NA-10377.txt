Heard Mr. Ashok Kumar Jha, learned counsel

 appearing on behalf of the petitioner, Mr. Vijay Shankar

 Shrivastava, learned counsel appearing on behalf of Opposite

 Party No.2 and Mr. Ajit Kumar, learned APP for the State in Cr.

 Misc. No.58710 of 2023 and heard Mr. N. K. Agarwal, learned

 Sr. counsel along with Mr. Pushpendra Kumar Singh, learned

 counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioners, Mr. Vijay

 Shankar Shrivastava, learned counsel appearing on behalf of
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.58710 of 2023(3) dt.06-12-2023 2/9 Opposite Party No.2 and Mr. Md. Aslam Ansari, learned APP

 for the State in Cr. Misc. No.61007 of 2023. 2. As both the cases arise of same P.S. Case number

 being Sitamarhi (Mehsaul) P.S. Case No.52 of 2023 dated

 21.01.2023 registered for the offences punishable under Sections 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 / 34 of the Indian Penal Code, they

 are being heard together. 3. As per the allegation made in the FIR, the

 informant had sold 4 decimal land belonging to new Khesra

 No.138, Khata No.750 situated in village Panchhor to Kamlesh

 Mishra (petitioner of Cr.Misc No. 58710 of 2023) by a

 registered sale deed. Thereafter Kamlesh Mishra used the copy

 of informant's AADHAAR Card, PAN Card and mobile

 number and by impersonating other person in place of the

 informant, fraudulently sold the land belonging to the informant,

 appertaining to Khesra No. 136 and 137, by executing five sale

 deeds in favour of other co accused persons, out of them the

 petitioners of Cr.Misc No. 61007 of 2023, on 31.12.2022 have

 prayed for pre-arrest bail. 4. Mr. Ashok Kumar Jha, learned counsel appearing in

 Cr. Misc. No.58710 of 2023 on behalf of the petitioner

 submitted that petitioner has not committed any forgery nor has
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.58710 of 2023(3) dt.06-12-2023 3/9 he misused AADHAAR Card, PAN Card or mobile number of

 the informant. The description of the land in sale deed and

 mentioned in the FIR reveals that after purchasing of 4 decimals

 of land by the petitioner, the informant has sold the raiyati

 land to different persons belonging to the informant on different

 dates, by executing five sale deeds. The sale deeds have not

 been made part of the FIR. Learned counsel submitted that in

 these backgrounds in the absence of any evidence in support of

 allegation made in the FIR that petitioner has committed forgery

 by using the AADHAAR Card, PAN Card and Mobile number

 of the informant cannot be sustained as well as, the fact that the

 petitioner is the genuine purchaser and the informant has not

 questioned the sale deeds with respect to 4 decimals of lands

 purchased by the petitioner. The petitioner deserves to be

 released on bail. 5. Mr. N. K. Agarwal, learned Sr. counsel along

 with Mr. Pushpendra Kumar Singh, learned counsel appearing

 on behalf of the petitioners in Cr. Misc No.61007 of 2023,

 submitted that the land was sold by the co-accused Kamlesh

 Mishra (one of the accused) to the petitioners and the petitioners

 had paid money to him through bank transaction as would

 appear from the bank details (Paragraph no. 8 of the petition).
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.58710 of 2023(3) dt.06-12-2023 4/9 Learned Sr. counsel further submitted that petitioners are

 innocent and they have been cheated by the co-accused

 Kamlesh Mishra. On these backgrounds, petitioners seek to be

 released on bail, having not been involved in any manner in

 commission of forgery. 6. Mr. Vijay Shankar Shrivastava, learned counsel has

 tendered his appearance on behalf of informant and submitted

 that he has filed counter-affidavit bringing on record all the five

 sale deeds, which have been executed by accused Kamlesh

 Mishra. He has submitted that such a large scale forgery

 committed by the accused Kamlesh Mishra after having

 purchased 4 decimals of the land registered fully described in

 the FIR and got it registered in his name and thereafter by

 misusing the AADHAAR Card, PAN Card and mobile number

 of the informant executed five sale deeds in connivance with

 the other co-accused and the staffs of the Registry Office. In

 support of his submission he has referred to paragraph nos.3 to

 7 of the counter-affidavit, which are reproduced hereinafter: 7. Mr. Ajit Kumar, learned APP for the State

 vehemently opposed the prayer for grant of pre-arrest bail in Cr.

 Misc. No.58710 of 2023 and submitted that prima facie the case
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.58710 of 2023(3) dt.06-12-2023 6/9 made out against the petitioner is that after getting the sale deed

 registered at the registry office in league with the contractor,

 who is responsible for computerized sale deed, it appears that

 they may have indulged in committing forgery by getting the

 land registered in name of different persons by misusing the

 AADHAAR Card, PAN Card and Mobile number of the

 informant. He further submitted that such large scale forgery is

 not unknown in State of Bihar and action is required against all

 the accused persons including staffs of the registry office. 8. Heard the parties. 9. The specific allegation against the petitioners of

 above two criminal miscellaneous applications, namely

 Kamlesh Mishra (Cr. Misc. No.58710 of 2023) and Parimal

 Ranjan and Dilip Kumar (Cr. Misc No.61007 of 2023) which

 arises out of FIR of P.S. Case No.52 of 2023 is that the

 informant has paternal land at Mauja-Mehsoul, Thana No.316,

 Anchal-Dumar, PS & Distt-Sitamarhi bearing plot no.136 and 137. The informant had sold 4 Dec. land of new Khesra No.138

 to Kamlesh Mishra (Petitioner of Cr. Misc. No.58710 of 2023)

 on 11.03.2022 during course of sale Kamlesh Mishra took the

 copy of AADHAAR Card, PAN Card and mobile number of the

 informant and allegedly using the said documents and by
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.58710 of 2023(3) dt.06-12-2023 7/9 impersonating other person in place of the informant

 fraudulently sold the land to the petitioners of Cr. Misc

 No.61007 of 2023 and others in collusion with other accused

 persons by executing five sale-deeds on 31.12.2022. 10. In view of the facts and circumstances of the

 cases, I am of the opinion that such a large scale forgery can not

 be committed by the petitioners alone. The petitioners in Cr.

 Misc. No.58710 of 2023 and Cr. Misc. No.61007 of 2023 are

 directed to be released provisionally in connection with

 Sitamarhi (Mehsaul) P.S. Case No.52 of 2023 pending in the

 court of learned Chief Judicial Magistrate, Sitamarhi on such

 terms and conditions as contained in Section 438(2) of Cr.P.C

 after accepting an undertaking along with two responsible

 persons of their village that they will not commit any criminal

 offence in the future. 11. At this stage, learned counsel appearing on

 behalf of the informant has raised his apprehension that

 petitioner of Cr. Misc. No.58710 of 2023, namely, Kamlesh

 Mishra has been released on bail and he is of notorious

 character, as it would appear in the allegation made in the FIR,

 considering the threat perception, proper protection should be

 given to the informant. Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.58710 of 2023(3) dt.06-12-2023 8/9 12. In case, apprehension has been shown by the

 informant that the petitioners have threatened him of dire

 consequences, and reported to the Superintendent of Police,

 Sitamarhi or S.H.O. then in that order granting provisional bail

 to petitioners shall automatically lose its force 13. The I.G., Registration, Bihar, the District

 Magistrate, Sitamarhi and the Superintendent of Police,

 Sitamarhi must verify the revenue records and details of the

 lands mentioned in the five registered sale deeds and its

 genuinity and if it is found that the complicity of government

 servant and staffs of the registry office in commission of forgery

 along with the computer operators is prima facie found which

 exemplifies the misuse of fingerprint on the AADHAR CARD,

 PAN and telephone number. The Superintendent of Police,

 Sitamarhi may constitute a team of experts to investigate into

 the alleged crime committed at the Registry office located at

 District Headquarter. 14. The District Magistrate and the Superintendent

 of Police may hold a meeting to assess the threat perception to

 the informant and his family, and if they find it so, then in that

 case proper security should be provided to the informant and his

 family members. Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.58710 of 2023(3) dt.06-12-2023 9/9 15. The Superintendent of Police, Sitamarhi is

 directed to submit his report within a period of one month. 16. Renotify the present bail applications on

 08.01.2024. 17. Let a copy of this order be communicated to the

 Additional Chief Secretary, Excise Prohibition and Registration

 Department and Revenue and Land Reforms Department for

 taking necessary action. (Purnendu Singh, J.)
