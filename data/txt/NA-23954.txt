This was weird. I was waiting for a bus at Majestic terminal 2A when a woman sat down next to me and told me to book a cab for her.

Just “book an Uber for me to Windsor Manor”, no please, no reason. She said she needs help and does not have much time. I booked one for her and gave her the cab number and OTP, to which she insisted I accompany her until the cab because “they” would see her

I did accompany her until there were people in sight, and the cab people are really confused coming to the right spot in majestic. After a couple of cancelled rides, she asked me to “quietly” check if there was a train to Kollur as people around this place will “know” her and asked me to take her till there.

She seemed in no hurry, wanted me to buy a Mosambi juice in the middle of me rushing her to the cab. In the end, I was late for my bus and decided to apologize and walk away. I have no idea what to do about this, extremely weird experience, do I report this or is this some kind of a scam?
