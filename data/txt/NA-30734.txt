New Delhi, Jul 30 (PTI) AAP’s Punjab MLA Jaswant Singh Gajjan Majra on Tuesday withdrew from the Supreme Court his plea challenging his arrest and remand by the Enforcement Directorate in a money laundering probe linked to a bank fraud case.
A bench of Justices Bela M Trivedi and Satish Chandra Sharma allowed the request by Majra’s counsel to withdraw the plea with liberty to move the Punjab and Haryana High Court for bail.
The top court had earlier refused to grant interim bail to the MLA from Amargarh constituency to campaign for the Lok Sabha polls. The Punjab and Haryana High Court had also dismissed a plea by the legislator challenging his arrest, observing that there was no illegality.
