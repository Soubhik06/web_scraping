All, I’ve been receiving a lot of scam calls lately, with the most common one being from someone pretending to call from TRAI. They claim that a phone number is registered with my Aadhar and is being used for illegal activities. The caller then asks me to note down some information and offers to transfer the call to a police station to file a complaint.

After the transfer, someone posing as a police officer insists that the complaint must be filed via video call. At this point, I usually give them an earful and hang up after wasting their time.

However, I’m curious—how exactly do they manage to scam money through this? Is it possible they are trying to make you say certain things and later blackmail you to extort money? How does this scam really work?
