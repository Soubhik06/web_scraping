The Scam in Our Backyard: 

The Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) has cracked down on Coaching Centers (including Belagavi)—especially those prepping students for KCET, JEE, NEET and CET. These so called “Institutes” are accused of: 

✔ False advertising – “100% selection!” or “Guaranteed KCET rank!” (TRUTH: No such guarantee exists.) 

✔ Fake success stories – Using toppers’ names without proof or permission. 

✔ Cheating students – Taking fees, then denying refunds or providing substandard teaching. 

The Reality Check: 

CCPA has issued warnings under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, demanding: 

✅ No more fake claims – Ads must be honest and verifiable. 

✅ Clear refund policies – No more vanishing acts when students drop out. 

✅ Transparent results – Real data on past ranks, not cherry-picked toppers. 

Why Belagavi Should Care: 

Thousands of students here pour lakhs of rupees into coaching centers, hoping for a shot at top colleges. But when institutes lie about results, dreams turn into debt traps. 

👹 But…..Is It Really the Coaching Centers’ Fault? 

Let’s flip the debate—are coaching centers the problem or just a symptom of a bigger issue? 

\- Parent Pressure: Families demand “Rank 1 or bust,” pushing centers to make unrealistic claims. 

\- Survival Game: Small Belagavi institutes compete with big chains (Allen, BASE, Resonance) by overpromising. 

\- Student Blindspots: Many ignore warnings, desperate for any shortcut to success. 

 Maybe the real issue isn’t just greedy coaching centers—it’s the brutal education system that forces students into this rat race.
