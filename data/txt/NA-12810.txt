(Order of the Court was delivered by Dr.A.D. Maria Clete, J.)

 This Writ is filed in the nature of Mandamus to the respondents to

 take appropriate steps for mandatory linking of Aadhar cards with Voter

 IDs and implementation of Biometric verification in EVMs. The

 respondents are the Union Government and the Election Commission. 2. The petitioner has contended that linking Aadhaar with Voter

 ID will eliminate fake votes, detect multiple registrations, prevent repeat

 voting, and simplify voter identification, creating a reliable electoral

 database. The petitioner claims this will deter fraud, maintain accurate

 rolls, and incentivize turnout by prioritizing voters for government

 benefits. It was also stated that biometric verification through fingerprint

 and iris scans is proposed to enhance security, streamline voting, and

 ensure transparency via real-time discrepancy resolution, thereby

 modernizing and safeguarding elections. The petitioner states that the

 Electoral Rolls are going to be published by the respondent authority

 without linking the Aadhaar of every elector. Hence, the writ petitioner 3/7 https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis W.P.(MD)No.940 of 2025 prays for direction to the authorities to take appropriate steps for

 mandatory linkage of an Aadhaar Card with Voter ID by consideration of

 his representation, dated 27.09.2024. 3. No deliberation is required in the matter as this writ petition is

 liable to be dismissed at the threshold because the matter has already

 been dealt with by the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India in W.P (Civil)

 No.177 of 2023 ( G.Niranjan vs Election Commissioner of India &

 Anr ) order dated 18.09.2023. [2023 LiveLaw (SC) 805]. The order of the

 Hon'ble Apex Court is extracted below: https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis W.P.(MD)No.940 of 2025 2. In view of the above statement, the Writ Petition is
 disposed of." 4. Before concluding, we would emphasize that privacy must not

 be compromised lightly for the sake of administrative efficiency as the

 Supreme Court has consistently underscored that any Aadhaar linkage

 must withstand the rigorous tests of necessity and proportionality. These

 principles resonate with the ethos of the social value theory of privacy,

 which posits that privacy is not a negotiable personal preference but a

 non-negotiable societal principle. Sacrificing privacy for convenience or

 isolated benefits risks undermining the collective trust and integrity that

 form the bedrock of a democratic society. 5. In fine, this Writ Petition is dismissed. No Costs. (M.S.R.J) (A.D.M.C.J)
