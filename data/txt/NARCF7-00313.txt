Have seen many restaurants with such fake positive reviews. Its easy to identify they are fake because of the number of followers they have and the choice of words they use. I report such restaurants. This has made it difficult to judge a restaurant by its star rating because they use these fake reviews to get their ratings over 4 but quality of food turns out to be poor. Its highly misleading and big gamble to order food from restaurant unless its a well known place you know about.
