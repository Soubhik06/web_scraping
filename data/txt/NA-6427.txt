 For the State : Mr. Rajnesh Vardhan, AddI P.P.
 ......... 02/ 26.02.2026: This bail application has been filed u/s 483 & 484 of BNSS, 2023
 wherein, prayer has been made for grant of bail as he is in custody for
 allegedly committing offence punishable under Sections 111(2)(b), 111(3),
 111(4), 319(2), 318(4), 338, 336(3), 340(2), 61(2) of the Bharatiya Nyaya
 Sanhita and Sections 66B , 66C , 66D and 84(C) of I.T. Act. 2. Heard, learned counsel for the petitioner learned counsel for the State
 and have also gone through the impugned order. 3. Learned AddI. P.P. opposes the prayer for bail. 4. There is an allegation that this petitioner along with others are
 committing cyber fraud by using mobile phones. The mobile phones and
 SIM Cards were recovered from the possession of the petitioner. He could
 not produce any document relating to the recovered articles. Further, from
 the CDR, it transpires that in the mobile phones several e-wallets were
 created, forged SIM Cards were used and many serial calls were made. 5. Considering the fact, I am not inclined to grant privilege of bail to the
 petitioner. Accordingly, the prayer for bail of the petitioner in connection
 with Cyber P.S. Case No.167 of 2025, pending in the Court of learned
 Additional Sessions Judge-II-cum-Cyber Crime, Special Court, Deoghar,
 stands rejected. 6. Accordingly, this bail application is dismissed. 7. The petitioner is at liberty to renew his prayer for grant of bail, after
 framing of the charge. (ANANDA SEN, J.)
