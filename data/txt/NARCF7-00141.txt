These are usually women with or without children who request funds for the days. I have heard rumors that these are coordinated scams similar to begging. Some person brings these poor people into the city and employs them to go around collecting funds. I have no issues parting with 100-200 Rs for a genuine case but I don't like to feel cheated. It's very difficult to identify genuine cases as the pattern is usually the same.

I have had three different encounters in the last week where one lady was wearing golden jewelry (could be fake) yet still asking for funds. One lady was waiting outside an office complex and targeted whoever walked out.
