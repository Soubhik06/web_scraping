There is no doubt why Vodafone is famous in posh areas of Delhi, I have noticed most of my friend who live there going for Vodafone Postpaid for their personal use. They use Airtel Postpaid mainly for their businesses etc.

I asked some why vodafone postpaid? why not airtel or jio? Most replies were they had good coverage of Vodafone and they do want to have some good privacy.

Back in times i had good speed of vodafone (30 mbps indoors, 45 mbps outdoors). This number was fairly new and prepaid. &amp; was not shared to anyone i used it just for data. 48 recharge something but it worked out pretty good.

I switched to Airtel and felt that after the porting the same number started getting spam from LensKart, Puma, loans and a lot of telemarketers. I Got my answer why these people are buying Vodafone and staying with vodafone for their personal use.

Airtel is just selling number to spammers, jio is just mining data and Vodafone do not have good coverage. Go to wikipedia pages of both airtel + jio you will find a Controversies of some data leak.

Also Vodafone do not blocks porn (at least majority of em) or restrict any webpage (like jio do).

EDIT : I'm in no way promoting Vodafone, I'm just discussing some personal factors. No network is better in India. Some people will complain about Vodafone Looting them, Some will do this for Chortel. Also i do get if you have poor Vodafone speed in your location as it depends on Number of users + Revenue per user + Location of tower + peak hours etc. Some will get better speed in airtel due to this factor. Some will get better with Jio.
