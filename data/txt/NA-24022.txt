I recently ordered Optimum Nutrition protein powder from Blinkit, expecting the usual quality and authenticity. However, what I received was shocking – a fake product!

The original packaging includes an authenticity URL, but this one came with a QR code that redirected to a fake website for verification. On top of that, Blinkit doesn’t provide proper support to address this issue.

If you’re considering buying supplements or other products from Blinkit, think twice. They seem to sell fake products without verifying their origin. Check out my Twitter post for more details:

https://x.com/Krilleanb/status/1882844017047785935
