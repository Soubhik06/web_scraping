Order No. (Through hybrid mode) 02. 1. Heard Mr. P.S. Nayak, learned counsel for the petitioner and Mr.
 D.K. Mishra, learned Addl. Government Advocate for the State. 2. This is an application under Section 439 of Cr.P.C. for grant of bail
 to the petitioner in connection with Plantsite P.S. Case No. 245 of 2023
 corresponding to G.R. Case No.1213 of 2023 pending in the Court of the
 learned S.D.J.M.(P), Rourkela under Sections 498-A , 376(2)(n) , 292(2) , 324 , 34 of IPC read with Sections 66-A , 67-A of I.T. Act. 3. The prayer for bail of the petitioner has been rejected vide order
 dated 24.08.2023 by 2nd Additional Sessions Judge, Rourkela on the
 ground that there is chance of tampering of the prosecution evidence and
 considering the nature and gravity of the offence and manner of
 commission of the offences. 4. The prosecution allegation in brief is that the husband of the
 informant had taken loan from one Afroz Akhtar @ Papu but could not
 repay the debt and forced the informant to keep physical relation with said
 Afroz Akhtar @ Papu used to come to the house of the informant and
 force himself on the informant. This continued for 7 to 8 months and then Page 1 of 3 the informant changed house and moved to Panposh. However, the said
Papu and the husband of the informant still used to harass the informant.
So, the informant left that place and stayed with her father at Bisra Check
gate. On 20.07.2023, the husband and brother-in-law of the informant
(petitioner) forcibly took the informant and her children in a car. Papu
tried to force himself on the informant in the car and the husband of the
informant assaulted the informant on his head. The informant was able to
escape with her children. Papu and the husband of the informant used to
make the informant video call and circulate the same with the others and
blackmail the informant. The accused persons threatened the informant,
they would do away with the children of the informant and make her
videos viral. 5. Mr. P.S. Nayak, learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the
petitioner is in custody since 22.07.2023. He further submits that the main
allegations in the case are against co-accused Md. Afroz Akhtar @ Pappu
and Mojammil Hussain, husband of the victim who is the brother of the
petitioner. Although the FIR has been registered under Sections 498-A , 376(2)(n) , 292(2) , 324 , 34 of IPC read with Sections 66-A , 67-A of I.T.
Act, charge sheet has been filed in this case where the petitioner is
accused of committing the offence under Sections 498-A , 354 , 109 of IPC
read with Section 67-A of I.T. Act even though there is no allegation
against him for commission of offence under Section 67-A of I.T. Act. He
further submits that the chargesheet has been filed against the other co-
accused persons under Sections 376(2)(n) , 292(2) , 324 , 506 of IPC read
with Sections 66-A , 67-A of I.T. Act. 6. Mr. D.K. Mishra, learned Addl. Government Advocate for the
State opposes the prayer for bail stating that the petitioner is the brother-
in-law of the victim and has done nothing to protect her. On the other
hand he had gone with the main accused to bring her from the father's Page 2 of 3 house and she has been assaulted on the way by the co-accused. 7. The prayer for bail of the two co-accused has been rejected by this
 Court. BLAPL No.10530 of 2023 filed by Mojammil Hussain has been
 dismissed on 04.10.2023 and BLAPL No.10587 of 2023 filed by the co-
 accused, Md. Afroz Akhtar @ Pappu has been dismissed on 04.10.2023. 8. Considering the nature of allegations against the petitioner who is
 the brother in law of the victim, the period of his detention in custody, I
 am inclined to allow the prayer for bail. 9. Let the petitioner- Danish Hussain be released on bail on such
 terms and conditions as may be fixed by the learned Court below in seisin
 over the matter, including the following conditions: 10. Violation of any condition will entail in cancellation of bail. 11. The BLAPL is accordingly disposed of. 12. Urgent certified copy of this order be granted on proper
 application. 13. A copy of this order be supplied to Mr. D.K. Mishra, learned Addl.
 Govt. Advocate for onward transmission to the I.I.C., Plantsite Police
 Sukanta
 Station. (Savitri Ratho)
