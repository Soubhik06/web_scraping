A lot of times Amazon fulfil their delivery promises but the delivery guys are trying to scam the system.
I ordered something from Amazon couple of days ago with promised next day delivery. 
I get a call from Amazon delivery the next day, which gets cut instantly before I pick up( Had the phone in my hand so I am sure that I did not miss the call).
The delivery guy then proceeds to mark that delivery was attempted but customer was not reachable and hence it will be attempted again the next day.
I buy a lot from Amazon and never get any calls before delivery, only when the delivery boy wants to delay the parcel.
I raised this issue through Amazon chat support and got the parcel the next day, even got a call from the delivery supervisor that action will be taken against riders using such practices. So if you ever face this, report such instances to Amazon support.
