So basically a huge scam has been going on where people steal your Axis bank debit card details from somewhere and transact using it online. Now the thing is, these cards also require an OTP for the transaction to proceed which these scammers get access to somehow. My guess is they hack through Truecaller and gain access through it. (When my relatives got scammed, they faced an odd activity on Truecaller. Their messages were being deleted). How they get access to your card details is also a mystery to me.

This scam has been going since 2018 from what I can see and Axis Bank doesn't provide any resolution except saying "Sir OTP was used so therefore its not our problem, it can't be called a fraud and we can't help" 

This scam is EXCLUSIVE to just AXIS BANK so im guessing a rogue bank employee being involved is highly likely but I can be wrong. The sources I have provided below, all had Axis Bank accounts

So if you have an Axis Bank account and use Truecaller, pls be advised and take the necessary steps. Even if you uninstall Truecaller, your card details are still out soo it's upto you to take the wise decision. And pls inform this with others to make them vigilant

This is also a sign to delete Truecaller
