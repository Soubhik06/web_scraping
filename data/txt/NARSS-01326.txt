I wanted to share a scam experience I had today that almost caught me off guard. I placed an order on Flipkart recently, but I accidentally used my spouse' old mobile number (now used my me as a second sim) in the billing address. Today, I received a call to my spouse' number from someone claiming to be from Flipkart, saying there was an issue with my payment and that I needed to provide an OTP.

Initially, I thought it was legitimate because I saw the name of the caller as "Dayashankar Flipkart" and i also received an OTP from Flipkart. However, the message was for logging into my Flipkart account, not for a payment issue. When the caller asked for the OTP, I realized this was a scam. The caller became insistent, but I refused to give any details and told them I would handle it on my own.

I’m still puzzled as to how they got my spouse' number, which was only used in the billing address, so I’m worried there might have been a data leak or breach. I’ll be contacting Flipkart support about this. Please be cautious and never share OTPs with anyone over the phone, even if it seems like a legitimate call.
