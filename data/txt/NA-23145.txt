My dad recently got an offer to work as a supervisor at a Metro Station in one of the metro cities in India. They (A contractor) did an interview and right after that, they gave him something called a "Posting Card" which mentions the name, the station in which he would get posted, and few other personal details. 

Now, immediately, they asked for ₹3500 for the uniform, which they said they would return back in parts in the initial salaries. And then they said that he would have to appear at their office the following week.

The reason why he wasn't suspicious because he confirmed from two of his friends who work in the metro, who also had to pay that much money initially. Which is why my dad wasn't reluctant paying them for the uniform charges. 

Yesterday, he received a call from someone in that office that he has to come to their office 2 days later with ₹21,000, which they said, would be refunded within 7 days.

When my dad told me about this, I got suspicious. I got sure that it's a scam and I really feel bad about it. 

Now, I'm thinking to file a case in the Police Station to bust that gang so that other people don't get duped. 

But, my father is worrying that complaining to the Police may involve court hearings later.

Is his worry justified? Or he shouldn't have to worry about what happens next? I would really appreciate if anyone can guide me.
