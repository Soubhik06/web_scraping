My Experience with the Be1scouting India Tour Model Hunt Scam. In January 2023, I eagerly registered for the India Tour Model Hunt organized by Be1scouting, a supposedly reputable international agency. 

Little did I know, this decision would lead to a frustrating and costly ordeal.After making a payment of 3000 with the transaction ID BE1000000213, I patiently waited for the Pune/Mumbai audition, which was repeatedly postponed. 

Eventually, in 2024, they outright canceled the event, citing various reasons.Assurances of a refund were offered, but sadly, those assurances proved to be nothing more than empty promises. Despite countless follow-ups, with Sabrina and Ramu Korwa Be1scouting has failed to honor their commitment to refund the registration fee.

Feeling deceived and cheated, I am now determined to take action. I intend to report this scam to the cyber authorities. However, I also feel compelled to share my story here to raise awareness among aspiring models.Be1scouting may be a big name in the international modeling industry, but my experience serves as a cautionary tale. 

Don't let the glamour and prestige blind you to the risks of falling victim to scams. Stay vigilant and always research thoroughly before engaging with any agency, no matter how reputable they may seem.Let my experience serve as a warning to others and a call to action for greater accountability within the modeling industry. If you've encountered similar issues with Be1scouting or any other agency, I encourage you to speak up and share your story. Together, we can protect aspiring models from falling prey to exploitation and fraud.
