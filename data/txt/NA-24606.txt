"Scam Alert: Beware of High-Pressure Tactics When Shopping for Laptops/PC in Lamington Road, Mumbai"

Incident where I was scammed while shopping for a laptop at Lamington Road in Mumbai. I was in the market for a new laptop, and after three days of extensive research and negotiation with various shops, I found a shop named "Dynamic Technologies" on Lamington Road in Mumbai that had an incredible deal on the specific laptop model I wanted. The price was around 70k-80k, so I knew I had to negotiate and make an informed decision.

When I approached the shopkeeper, he initially seemed friendly and knowledgeable, and after some negotiation, he offered me a price much lower than what I had seen elsewhere. However, he asked me to pay a Registration amount of Rs. 2,000 just to continue the negotiation. I've blurred my info for my privacy.

[Registration Receipt](https://preview.redd.it/706t9ewccsta1.png?width=720&amp;format=png&amp;auto=webp&amp;s=98a6d96098bdf936b422bac7f3f573d5a3985cc7)

Initially, I agreed to pay the Registration amount as I was assured by seller that the price was the best in the market. The shopkeeper talked nicely and even showed me the laptop, and I was impressed with the deal he was offering.

However, when I decided to do some more research and asked for some time to think, the shopkeeper started to use high-pressure tactics. He told me that no other store would sell me the laptop at a lower price, and that the offer was only available for a limited time and the Registration money is non refundable as it is our policy.

Feeling intimidated and afraid, I told the shopkeeper that I needed more time to think and left the store. However, things turned for the worse when the shopkeeper (everyone : around 5 people) started to threaten me, harass me and started abusing me for no reason. He became abusive and made it clear that I had no choice but to buy from him.

Feeling vulnerable and helpless, I decided to call a few friends for help. They advised me to take a stand and threatened to call the police. After 1-2 hour of hard core negotiation and threats, the shopkeeper finally relented and gave me back my Registration amount because of fear of Police.

To prove my side of the story, I shared the picture of the Registration I paid as evidence of the incident. It's important to do proper research and negotiation before making such a high-value purchase, which is why I visited various shops and even shared the photo of all the bills that I had visited (some of them are lost).
