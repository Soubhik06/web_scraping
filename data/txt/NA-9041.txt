Petitioner seeks bail in connection with Mahila P.S.

 Case No. 33 of 2019, registered for the offences punishable

 under Sections 376 , 384 and 506 of the Indian Penal Code. As per prosecution case, informant has applied for

 loan in the Industrial Department and she was called and

 thereafter she was raped and her photographs were taken for

 blackmailing her and petitioner also used to call her and

 demanded Rs.5 lac. Submission of learned counsel for the petitioner is

 that she is a married lady and as per FIR no photograph was

 produced during investigation and whole allegation is false and

 he is in custody since 30.7.2019. Heard learned APP and learned counsel for the

 informant, who has opposed the prayer for bail on the ground
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.61790 of 2019(3) dt.06-02-2020 2/2 that those photographs were shown before learned Sessions

 Judge-cum-Special Judge which will appear from the impugned

 order and he has photographs with him. Considering the above, I am not inclined to grant bail

 to the petitioner. Accordingly, this application is dismissed.

 However, let the trial be expedited. (Vinod Kumar Sinha, J)
