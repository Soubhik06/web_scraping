I start my post by apologizing to my non-bengali people in this subreddit and just know that how I phrased my question isn't the most polite way of phrasing it. Kolkata surely is for everyone; bengalis, non-bengalis and everyone else! 

That being said, I don't know if you guys are into scam related videos on YouTube and you've heard of amazing creators like Scammer Payback, Kitboga, amidst others. If you've followed Scammer Payback or seen any of his videos, you'll see that 9/10 times the scam call centers he hacks into are based out of Kolkata. In almost all of the cases, he comes up with the details of the scammers and it ends up showing that the scammers are mostly non-bengalis. Just like a video he [uploaded just yesterday](https://youtu.be/ychJ5Np-VvU?si=PCmMrdbe_m7Q5pxU), the conversation the scammers are having within themselves are in Hindi and this is just one of the thousand other examples. 

So my question is, or rather I would like to discuss the reason why these scam call centers are mostly based in Kolkata, and why are the people working at these call centres mostly non-bengali? Because intuitively, it would be more easy to find Bengalis in Kolkata rather than non-bengalis and thus employ them for these scams. Also other than these content creators on YouTube, how can we as young people help to get rid of these filthy scammers from Kolkata?
