I work a job where I get calls from potential customers on my personal phone, so when I get a call from “wireless caller”, I generally pick up.

Today I got one of those calls, and unsurprisingly I heard a sting Indian accent but they asked for me by my first name. 

I responded with “who’s calling”, thinking maybe it was something legit if they had my name, and they continued saying they were calling from the Amazon fraud department. 

At this point I knew it was a scam but was slightly concerned that they had my name so I asked “wait, who did you say you were calling for?” and they responded with my full name - first and last. 

This kind of freaked me out a little so
I tried playing dumb saying “no, sorry, you have the wrong number, this is Roger” (fake name, obviously)

The guy got angry and said “don’t try to pretend with me. I know this is [full name] who lives at [address].”

This spooked me and I hung up. 

Wtf.
