--- POST TEXT ---

So, a few months ago I had posted here enquiring about how I can surrender my Airtel prepaid SIM which was apparently a recycled mobile number as I had been inundated with calls and SMS asking for the previous owner who had allegedly defaulted on some loans and forgot to unregister the number from the scooty service centre and in worst case me being on the receiving end due to language barrier as the SIM was originally purchased in Chennai. Link to post here: https://www.reddit.com/r/kolkata/s/3PDXCOG9yj

Now, coming to the solution: I started discussing the same with my family, while there was an intial resistance regarding my decision, but I had to finally bite the bullet and get a new-old SIM as this was becoming unbearable. Confronting Airtel and asking for a solution fell on deaf ears as I realised this is a much larger problem. So, I started to intimate my college friends to delete my previous number. Now, you may ask: What's the guarantee that your new number won't be a recycled one? Which is a valid one. Here's the thing, my dad had a sim of Tata Docomo and he never used it much which was long deactivated due to non-usage and the number wasn't available in pool. As Airtel had acquired Tata Docomo in 2019 so I went to my nearest Airtel store and faked it a little bit as I started that I was out of the country for a few years and during that time my SIM Card was damaged and there was no means for me to obtain a replacement SIM and have it sent to me from India as Airtel needs you to be physically present for activation of the SIM. They recreated the number and I have bought it as a postpaid SIM for now, but I'll convert it back to pre-paid after 90 Days elapse. Life has never been so peaceful with the absence of unwanted calls! 😌

Now, considering our country's population it's impossible to generate fresh numbers, so TRAI allows telcos to deactivate and resell the number after 90 days of use. But, the problem is our mobile number is registered in several places like Ola, uber, Banks and government. My suggestion is if these apps/services don't detect a usage of the same for 90 days or more they should deactivate and delete the data of the previous user for the sake of privacy after sending reminders over SMS/email!
