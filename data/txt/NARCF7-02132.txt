I was given the form and was made to go around in circles when I was stopped by probably the manager. 
He talked me into not closing the account but raising a complaint. The reason was if I closed the account they won't investigate. So on Monday I will provide a written complaint along with screenshots etc. 🤞 something may happen. No form was given since I was not scammed. I will raise a complaint at the website. The manager has asked me to change debit card and e-banking password. The whole process will cost some money.

I won't disclose the bank name, just it's a large PSU one. While I was sitting in the managers cabin , I noticed a bunch of important papers just lying around. KYC , Life Certificates , Adhaar copies. Sure it had a CCTV.
