*(Using a new account to post this as I do not want to get doxxed from my regular account)*

I bought a Moto C Plus from Flipkart as a Diwali gift for a relative and it just got delivered yesterday (22/10/2017). The packaging looked normal and there was absolutely no reason to believe this was not a new phone, just as expected.

Everything was okay until I booted the phone. Weirdly, it did not show me any setup screen, language selection, or anything that you expect when you boot a phone for the first time. Instead, it shows me two unread messages from Vodafone Care congratulating me on my new 4G phone. Do keep in mind that I have NOT inserted a SIM card into the phone until now. And I do not use Vodafone. And the language on the phone is set to German. Since when has Flipkart entered the business of buying used phones from Germany, repacking them and dumping them in India? What if someone has installed some malware or spyware on the phone sold to me?

What this effectively means is that Flipkart has cheated me and has sold a phone that was previously USED by someone else. It is definitely not a "new" phone.

[Images of the phone's screen showing the text messages and language](https://imgur.com/a/Dj2pW).

I have already raised a complaint on their Facebook page and it's been more than 24 hours and I am yet to hear from them on a resolution. All I have asked for is a refund and they are reluctant to even provide that after being caught red handed in their fraud schemes.
