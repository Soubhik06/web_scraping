Heard on I.A.No.17097/2022, an application for permission to withdraw

the Vakalatnama filed on behalf of Shri Ravi Ballabh Tripathi. For the reasons mentioned in the application, the same is allowed and the
Vakalatnama of Shri Ravi Ballabh Tripathi is permitted to be withdrawn. Heard on merits. Case diary is available. This fourth repeat application under Section 439 of Cr.P.C has been filed
for grant of bail. Second bail application was dismissed as withdrawn by order
dated 16/08/2022 passed in MCRC No.38958/2022. The applicant has been arrested on 08/04/2022 in connection with Crime
No.239/2020 registered at Police Station Cyber Cell, District Gwalior for
offence under Sections 420 , 204 of IPC and 66 D of IT Act. It is submitted by the counsel for the applicant that the Supreme Court by
order dated 23/09/2022 passed in SLP (Cri.) No.6972/2022 has granted bail to
co-accused Devansu Chauhan, who is main accused and the case of the
applicant is identical to that of co-accused Devansu Chauhan. Per contra, it is submitted by the counsel for the State that the applicant
as well as co-accused had committed fraud on Amazon online platform and
goods worth of Rs.16,00,000/- and more were misappropriated by adopting

fraudulent manner. So far as the parity is concerned, it is submitted that the applicant could
be arrested only on 08/04/2022 whereas co-accused Devansu Chauhan was
granted bail after remaining in jail for a period of one year and seven months as
he was arrested on 27/02/2021. In reply, it is submitted by the counsel for the applicant that in fact the
bank account of the applicant was misused by co-accused Devansu Chauhan. Heard the learned counsel for the parties. According to the prosecution case, multiple transactions which runs in
lacs had taken place in the bank account of the applicant. Whether the applicant
was actively involved in fraud or he had bonafidely allowed co-accused
Devansu Chauhan to use his bank account is a question, which is yet to be
decided by the Trial Court. Looking to the fact that the multiple bank transactions in lacs had taken
place in the account of the applicant, coupled with fact that co-accused
Devansu Chauhan has been granted bail after undergoing pre-trial detention of 3 one year and seven months and the applicant has not undergone the same
period of detention, no case is made out for taking a contrary view in the
matter. Accordingly, the application fails and is hereby dismissed. (G.S. AHLUWALIA)
