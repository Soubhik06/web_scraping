Ranchi, Mar 19 (PTI) Jharkhand Chief Minister Hemant Soren on Thursday extended wishes to people on the occasion of several festivals, including Chaitra Navratri, Ugadi, and Nav Samvatsar.
These festivals symbolise new beginnings and inspire people to move forward in life with faith, restraint and renewed energy, he said.
“Heartfelt congratulations, best wishes, and greetings to all on Chaitra Navratri, Gudi Padwa, Ugadi, and Nav Samvatsar. These sacred festivals inspire us to move forward with faith, restraint, and renewed energy. May Maa bestow excellent health, happiness, peace, and prosperity upon everyone. May Maa’s grace remain upon all – this is my heartfelt wish,” Soren posted on X.
