So guys I just received this text that exactly look like OTP from Amazon but on close looking into it you will realise that it isn’t any normal otp text 

1. It’s coming from a number +91 which isn’t company number it’s an international code 
Usually code for companies starts with AX/VA/VK etc.

2. It is showing me deny (you can deny to take delivery even when deliver person is on the doorstep)

3. Most important link it seems like normal Amazon link but it isn’t 

Just be careful everyone and make sure your family/friends who order from these platforms won’t fall under these scams
