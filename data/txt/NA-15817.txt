--- POST TEXT ---

In the morning I got a call from someone claiming to be my branch manager at my bank, he didn't state the bank so I pressed him to name his bank. He said it's the bank where I have my ATM card, at this point it was ridiculously evident that it's a scammer so I told him I've got several bank accounts so please tell me the name of the bank. This guy wouldn't give up and asked me to name the banks where I have accounts, so I decided to play along and wasted around 30 minutes toying around with him. He basically wanted me to confirm the debit card number, CVV and then an OTP to ensure that the card would not get deactivated. Finally, got tired of screwing around with him and submitted a formal complaint to the cybercrime division of the police.

I fail to understand how people fall for such hilariously inept scammers who can't even make up details about a bank branch to buttress their false claims. Either a substantial number of people are gullible enough to fall for such a scam or this was a novice scammer.
