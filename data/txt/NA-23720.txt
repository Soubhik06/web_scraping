--- POST TEXT ---

About 2-3 weeks ago, something unusual occurred. It was a Sunday morning, around 11:30 am, when my doorbell rang. I answered the door to find a man who appeared to be in his 30s or 40s. He introduced himself, saying, "I am XYZ. Despite being married for years, we haven't been able to have a child. So, we're planning to visit a temple next week, and supposedly, according to tradition (as he claimed), we need to collect donations from households before we can go."

I began to sense that something was amiss. He seemed like he might be making this up, and he was dressed quite formally. Like anyone else, I declined to give any money. This is when he became aggressive and impolite. Our conversation unfolded as follows (conducted in Kannada, translated into English for clarity):

**Me: I'm sorry, I don't have any change, and my UPI is not working. Him: Please check again.** 

**Me: Sorry, maybe next time. Him: If everyone says the same thing, what can we do? I'm not asking for your property; can't you at least spare Rs500? Me: I'm sorry, but I can't give you anything. Please leave.**

**Him: I was part of the Vande Bharat team. Will you believe me now? You can verify it online.** 

**Me: I'm sorry, but I won't give you anything.**

**At this point, he began to curse at people and society, saying, "This is the problem with society; this is why things like murder and robbery happen because people refuse to help others and have no compassion."**

In the end, I managed to close the door and get rid of him. 

He never mentioned his name, and I had no interest in knowing it. 

Has anyone else encountered such a rude person/situation, and do you think it was a scam?
