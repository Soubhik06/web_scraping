So yesterday at around 2pm I was returning home from my local grocery store which is 100m from my apartment. Just before entering the elevator, this man calls for me desperately. He was middle aged, wore a mask, in track pants and a tshirt and sandals. He said that he was visiting his friend and someone swiped his phone and keys and his wallet while travelling to BTM (where I live). He said he worked in a company in Whitefield and could show me his payslip etc if I had the laptop. Under no circumstances I was gonna let him know which apartment I lived in so I declined. I lied to him saying I was a student and I don't have enough cash. He said he lived near Harohalli police station which was 30kms away from where we were standing. The fun part is, if it was a scam it was very well thought because an outstation cab has variable bill amount than predicated so paying the cab driver before hand would have been impossible so he asked for liquid cash. He knew the route so I checked on Google how much it would cost approximately. He asked for 300 rupees which I had handy and I wanted to get rid of this situation since it were my working hours. He promised me to return the money in 24 hours and gave me his phone number and email ID. Sent him a reminder to pay it back a while ago but maybe I'll wait it out until night time. 
Stay safe out there and if you sense a scam, it probably is.
