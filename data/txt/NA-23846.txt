I booked for jio fiber by paying 1000 installation and 6000 advance for 6 months for 100mbps connection. I requested a connection using website jio.com

After the order was placed, I received a code which was only to be provided after the installation is completed. 

Various people called me using different numbers and tried to trick me in giving this code. One guy send me a fake cancellation message and informed me to give the code to stop cancellation. 

Ofcourse there is no jio fiber in my area which I was not aware about. I was scammed for 7000, and there is no support for jiofiber where I can get refund.

Even when I call jio they cannot transfer the call to jio fiber.

I called the jio guy who did the transaction and threatened him that I will make a compaint about him, the person on the phone laughed at me and told me to do whatever I can and make complaint against him as much as I want.

The person is now trying to do UPI scam on my number. I am worried now as they have all my details including aadhar card, photo etc.
