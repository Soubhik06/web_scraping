So I was doing some work and out of sudden a call came from a unknown number which was 8981703437. I picked it up and it was a lady on the other end and she said : "Good evening sir ami Reliance Jio theke kotha bolchi". 

Ami bollam: Ha bolun.

She: Sir apnader kono bari aache jekhane bhara te thaka jawa jabe office er kaaj korar jonno?

Ami: Na.

She: ok sir, dhonyobad.

Dhonyobad bole call kete dilo. 2 things struck in my mind. 

1. Why would reliance who is a multinational conglomerate would let it's employees rent out random flats/houses for their office work?

2. Ami to just akta college student, barite khete pacchi etai boro bepar, nijer kono income nei ar e madam amake jigesh kore amr kache kono bari bhara te aache naki. 🥲 jaa tah.

Well I also typed the same number in true caller and it showed someone by the name Kiran Mousi💀.

Anyways jokes apart, a company wether be any national or multinational wouldn't allow it's employees to rent out random flats for office work. Just be safe from this scam calls.
