I am the owner of a small bakery in Hyderabad. I opened it in May this year. For most challenges I face I can Google stuff to understand what procedures to follow, however I was simply lost when I encountered hijras demanding money from me when I first opened the store. Nothing prepared me for that as I started simply by learning things on my own along the way. Is there anything that can be done to stop this harassment?

I would like to point out that I am a foreigner and I might receive a backlash for my opinion. I understand the status of hijras in Indian society, but I cannot spin my head around on how they influence local businesses to cough up money for no good reason at all.

A quick update:
Thank you very much, everyone, for your valuable advice! My plan of action is to visit the local police station and introduce myself overall to check what can be done officially from their end. In case any ruckus happens, we do have CCTV installed and now we have one of their phone numbers, too (previously they had an auto driver to drive them around and the auto driver was handling digital payments...). I will be shortly installing another camera in the front of my store to capture the audio, as advised in one of the comments, to have the audio proof of the happenings in the shop.
Thank you once again for the advice and support!
