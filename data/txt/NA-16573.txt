--- POST TEXT ---

First of all, let me start by saying that Euphonycart is a company that **scams you**, **sells faulty products** to you and **then do not answer your calls, messages, mails etc.**

I had bought a guitar (worth around **Rs 25,000**) from their online store, [**www.euphonycart.com**](http://www.euphonycart.com/) on 4th august. I received the guitar on 14th August 2021. I was deeply unsatisfied with the product as I had noticed some scratch issues and paint issues with the guitar. I had informed this to the store personnel through WhatsApp, along with which I had sent pictures showing the issues I had with the guitar. Fortunately, they agreed to send a new replacement piece.

I received the replacement piece on 14th September 2021 and noticed that this piece was worse than the first and it was significantly damaged. It had a bent neck and it could not be played properly. The action at the 12th fret was about 1 cm (when normally it is almost 0.3mm) and the neck's bend could be seen easily. I took the guitar to some well reputed musical instruments service centers and sellers in my locality (Calicut) to see if they could adjust it using the truss rod. However, they told me that the damage was way too severe to be adjusted and it had been caused due to a severe bend in the guitar neck which cannot be adjusted. (They also said that the bend may have been caused due to poor storage of the product in the company’s storehouse.)

I messaged them (on WhatsApp) on the same day that I had received the replacement piece (14th September) telling them that they had given me a faulty product (AGAIN!). They replied the following day and told me that they would call me and help me out with the issue, and I said fine. However, after that message, **I have neither gotten a call from them, nor any reply to my WhatsApp messages from them nor did they reply to my mails. In fact, whenever I'd call them, it would say that their line is switched off.**

It has been 1 month since I have received this faulty guitar and have been trying to get any response from them. I waited one month expecting any kind of reply from them, however **they have been completely avoiding me (mails, calls, WhatsApp).** I am left with no choice now but to take legal action against these **scammers.** Hopefully the consumer court will understand my situation and help me get my money back.

**---------------CONCLUSION:------------------**

\-&gt;DO NOT TRUST THIS STORE (trust me I've learned this the hard way).

\-&gt;They sell you faulty products and then do not reply to your messages, mails and calls. They are nothing less than scammers.

\-&gt;If anyone you know is thinking of buying from them, please let them know about the risk of getting scammed by this store.

\-&gt;I hope this review will help you take a wise decision as to whether you should buy from this store or not.
