I recently fell victim to a scam when trying to purchase CWC tickets through a seller on Twitter. The scammer convinced me to continue our conversation on WhatsApp, even sent screenshots of the tickets, and asked for payment through UPI. As soon as I made the payment, they blocked me. 

I tried contacting them with different numbers, but they blocked each one. Eventually, I reported the incident to my local Cyber Police but i lost the hope for getting my money back.

However, something interesting has come up recently. I discovered that the scammer is a journalist at Republic Bharat with a significant social media following, and their banking name matches their social media name.

This strongly suggests that the scammer and the journalist are the same person.

I'm considering taking this evidence to the cyber police for further action. Any advice on my next steps would be appreciated.
