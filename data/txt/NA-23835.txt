Hey everyone,

I wanted to share my recent experience at the Mantri Mall metro station to warn others about a scam that caught me off guard.

So, I was just minding my own business when a guy approached me, claiming he needed help because his sister was pregnant and in need of cash. He said he couldn't withdraw money due to some limit on his account and asked if I could do a quick UPI transfer for him.

The scammer initially requested ₹2000, but when I told him I didn't have that much cash on me, he pressed me for how much I could send. I mentioned I only had ₹300 available. He then insisted that I send him ₹200, assuring me he would send the cash back from his sister's phone right after because of some transaction limit.

Feeling rushed and wanting to help, I went ahead and transferred the ₹200. As soon as I did, I felt a sinking feeling in my stomach. I realized I had just fallen for a classic scam.

Now, I know I'm not getting that ₹200 back, and I have his number and UPI details, but I didn't report the incident anywhere. I guess I was just too embarrassed and frustrated with myself for falling for it.

I'm also worried that by sharing my phone number and UPI ID with the scammer, I might be at risk for further fraudulent activities or identity theft. I'm not sure if he can use this information to access my accounts or cause any other harm.

Lessons Learned:
Be cautious when approached by strangers asking for money, especially in public places.
Verify any claims before making a transaction, no matter how urgent it seems..

I just wanted to share this experience to help others avoid making the same mistake. Stay safe out there!
