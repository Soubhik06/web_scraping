27 jul ku sab se bada scam hua mere saath jab mai, Zomato gold aur Swiggy one purchase krra.
Zomato &amp; Swiggy got the best way to scam people by coming up with this gold &amp; one where delivery is free under 5km and some small amount of offers too. 

I noticed that raat mein ek toh bhi msg aata zomato ya Swiggy se so that we open the app and order something. 

People who gets night craving frequently get in this trap by ordering food showing "FREE DELIVERY" label.

Also due to only takeaway in many restaurants at night we are forced to order online.

Some Cloud kitchen &amp; Restaurant only support Zomato &amp; Swiggy after 12:00 pm.

I being a common man without any source of income; surviving on pocket money that too very less amount, have spend more than ₹2,300+ on online food delivery.

Which I regret now.
