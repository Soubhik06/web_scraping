[Does Internet.org Leave Latin Americans Without A Real Internet?](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2015/04/does-internetorg-deprive-latin-americans-real-internet) 
 
The article, linked above, by the Electronics Frontier Foundation talks only about Internet.org in Latin America. But in the end it raises an important question on the issue of **User Privacy**: 
 
&gt; Will the main impact of Internet.org be to make information more accessible in Latin America, or 
&gt; 
&gt; to make the personal information of Latin Americans be made more accessible to Facebook, and 
&gt; 
&gt; in consequence also to the United States government? 
 
 
The same question must be asked by India to Internet.org while the Net Neutrality debate is going on.
