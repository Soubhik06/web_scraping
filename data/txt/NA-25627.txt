--- POST TEXT ---

I was in the middle of a trip when, out of nowhere, I couldn’t use UPI anymore. I checked my bank balance—it showed exactly ₹0. I was in shock because the last time I had checked, there was around ₹10,000 in my account. Since I was traveling, I decided to let it go for the moment.

Once I returned, I reached out to a family friend who works at SBI. He informed me that my account had been placed on hold by the Cyber Crime Cell for ₹12,000. Confused and stressed, I went to my home branch, but they said they couldn’t help, as the hold was put by the Cyber Cell.

I called 1930, the Cyber Cell helpline, and they provided me with the contact details and email of the police station that flagged my account. To my surprise, it was Navi Mumbai PS 🥲.

I’ve sent them emails and tried calling every single day, but so far, no response. I’m at a complete loss.

What should I do? Should I just give up on the money? Or is there another way out? Has anyone else faced something similar?

Additional Information:

I was informed by the bank that my account has been flagged due to a transaction. This transaction was part of a peer-to-peer (P2P) transfer process.

Context:

As a freelancer, I receive payments from clients through Airtm. To withdraw funds from my Airtm wallet, I need to submit a withdrawal request. Upon doing so, a random peer is assigned to transfer the amount. This peer will send the funds to my account, and in return, they receive a commission on the amount they send.
