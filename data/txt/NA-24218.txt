--- POST TEXT ---

So recently I visited an apartment complex to meet an acquaintance. As usual the security stopped us but instead of a visitor register, noted my phone number and name using his phone... I'm assuming in an app. Now I'm not sure what sort of privacy the app has to begin with but I didn't say anything as that much info we have to give almost at every apartment complex in Bangalore. But then he said I've to take a photo and before I could react he clicked my pic! That really infuriated me as he didn't even ask for permission. Of course I got into an argument but all he said was that he was asked to do that and that's the rule in the apartment. I mentioned it to my friend and he too said yeah that's the policy! I think that's a bit crazy and feel it's an invasion of privacy to take our pic in this manner. I understand having security cameras recording visitors entry and exit but this is too much personal data going into an app that probably has questionable security against data leaks and that too it's being forced on people just because we are visiting. I've seen this sometimes in government offices but it seems a bit much for apartments. What do you think?
