My friend is being harassed by a money lending app people
(App name- cash advance).They got access to his phone contact and started sending abusive messages to all his contacts.
So we decided to register a complaint at the cyber crime station(CID Annexe Building, Carlton House, # 1, Palace Road)
and to our surprise he was not allowed to go in and register a complaint because he was wearing shorts!!!!
 The reason given by the police man was"this is a government office and there is discipline and dress code in here".
I mean that should be for the officials,I don't understand how he expects a victim to be well dressed when he is coming to seek help!

I just felt so helpless as a common man at that moment .
If this is how our police treat the victims ,then what's the point of having this whole law and order system!!
