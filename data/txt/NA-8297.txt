 HON'BLE MR. JUSTICE ASHOK KUMAR GAUR

 Judgment

24/04/2019

 The issue in this batch of the writ petitions is with

regard to allotment of division on the basis of merit-cum- preference on the post of Senior Teacher/Teacher Grade - II in

pursuance of the recruitment process initiated vide advertisement

dated 13th July, 2016. Learned counsel for the petitioners as well as learned

Additional Advocate General Mr.Ganesh Meena, appearing on

behalf of the respondents, submitted that the same issue has

been decided by the Coordinate Bench of this Court in the case of Sunita Kumari Meena Vs. The State of Rajasthan & Ors. in

SB Civil Writ Petition No.23680/2018 along with other

connected writ petitions, decided by a common order dated 2 nd

April, 2019. This Court finds that the Coordinate Bench of this

Court, in the case of Sunita Kumar Meena (supra), has laid down

the law that Meritorious Reserved Category (MRC) candidates

cannot be placed in disadvantageous position by not permitting

them to be treated as reserved category candidate when the (Downloaded on 28/06/2019 at 01:51:58 AM) (30 of 33) [CW-6924/2019]

occasion of arises for considering them allotment of

ranges/divisions. The Coordinate Bench has directed that in

determining the preference of MRC candidate for allocation of

ranges/divisions as Senior Teachers, they should be considered

against the reserved category post in each of the division/range,

for which, they have sought allocation on the basis of their inter se

merit vis-a-vis other reserved category candidates. Relevant

portion of the order passed by the Coordinate Bench is quoted

hereunder for ready refernce : Learned counsel for the petitioners submitted that the

State Government is required to comply with the said order and

similar directions need to be given in the present cases as well. Learned Additional Advocate General Mr.Ganesh Meena,

appearing on behalf of the State Government submitted that the

order passed by the Coordinate Bench of this Court in the case of Sunita Kumari Meena (supra) is being examined by the State

Government and since two months' time is granted to comply with

the order, necessary exercise can be taken only within the time

prescribed and further implication of the order is also to be

considered by the State Government. This Court finds that the law on the issue of allocation

of ranges/divisions to the Senior Teachers pursuant to their

selection in view of the advertisement dated 13 th July, 2016 has

been decided, the State Government is required to comply with

the order and all those persons, who have participated in the

selection process are required to be given the same right in

respect of their allocation for ranges/divisions as per merit-cum- preference. This Court finds that since the issue is settled by the

judgment passed by the Coordinate Bench of this Court, the State

Government is expected to follow the judgment passed in the case

of Sunita Kumari Meena (supra) as judgment in rem and every (Downloaded on 28/06/2019 at 01:51:58 AM) (33 of 33) [CW-6924/2019]

 candidate, who has to seek the same relief, should not be forced

 to come to the Court for redressal of his/her grievances. The

 principle of law, decided by this Court, needs to be applied to all

 the candidates, if they are similarly situated and if they have

 participated and being selected on the post of Senior

 Teacher/Teacher Grade - II. The present batch of the writ petitions is decided in

 terms of the order passed by the Coordinate Bench of this Court in

 the case of Sunita Kumar Meena (supra) and the directions, which

 have been given shall also apply mutatis mutandis in all the cases

 of present petitioners and the respondent State would apply the

 judgment passed by the Coordinate Bench of this Court as

 judgment in rem and necessary exercise of allotting particular

 range/division may be done by the State Government, as time has

 already been fixed by this Court giving outer limit of two months. Accordingly, the present writ petitions stand allowed. A copy of this order be placed separately in each

 connected file. (ASHOK KUMAR GAUR),J
