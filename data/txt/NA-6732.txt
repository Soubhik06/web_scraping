1. Heard learned counsel for the accused-applicant as well as learned Additional Government Advocate and gone through the entire record. Counter affidavit filed on behalf of the State is taken on record. 2. By means of this application under Section 439 CrPC, the accused-applicant seeks bail in FIR No.0308 of 2020, under Sections 420 and 406 IPC lodged at Police Station Kotwali, District Unnao. 3. The accused-applicant was arrested in respect of an FIR, which came to be registered by the complainant against an unknown person, who in furtherance of criminal conspiracy, got the complainant's credit card and, thereafter withdrew Rs.55,000/- from his bank account; besides, a purchase of Rs.15,000/- was also made from the credit card; from possession of the accused-applicant and co-accused, 36 ATM Cards with respect to different cases registered on same allegations, 21 SIM Cards, two four wheelers and 8 mobiles etc were recovered; even in the CCTV Footage, the accused-applicant and co-accused were captured; 11 ATM Cards were recovered only from the accused-applicant. It appears that the accused-applicant and co-accused are members of a gang, which operates defrauding people of their money by cloning the ATM Cards and, withdrawing the hard-earned money from accounts of innocent persons. 4. Considering the seriousness of the allegations and, the large-scale recovery of ATM Cards from the accused-applicant and co-accused, including machine etc. for cloning the A.T.M. Cards, this Court does not find any ground to enlarge the accused-applicant on bail at this stage. 5. Thus, the bail prayer is refused and, the application is rejected. [D.K. Singh, J.]
