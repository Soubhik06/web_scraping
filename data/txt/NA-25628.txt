I know that a ton of scams are happening everywhere. My dad himself was also a cyber crime victim. But… people are too stubborn to do any kind of help and that’s so sad!

1. I had some package to post and they wanted only cash (100₹) at the post office and I asked at 10-12 stores and 5-6 ppl on road including pan shops and restaurants and none of them helped me.
I mean… free ga adagaledu. I said I’ll send money through PhonePe! Felt very strange with the reasons I got!

2. Then evening I had a scrum call to take and my wifi wasn’t working since morning. My work laptop doesn’t connect to mobile hotspot for some security reasons.
Asked 2 of my neighbours to lend me the wifi for 30 mins and all of them denied.

I mean… I even told them “I’m not asking for your router. I just want to connect to it for 30 mins” but they denied blatantly!

I think… only ppl who are nice to us are valet drivers and restaurant staff coz they get a tip! So no money = no honey 🙂

Ok… feels nice to have people around us like this 🙂
