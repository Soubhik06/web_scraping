A lot of redditors post about their experience and try to educate others in the sub and today I am one of the beneficiaries of those 

So this is how it goes 

Morning around 9.00/10.00 AM I posted a property for rent on housing.com they verified my property and immediately I got reponse on whatsapp from a guy who calls himself Aniket Vijay had a fake CRPF id and told he works as SI in CRPF. He then asked me to share the property photos I did then in the afternoon he called me I handed over the call to my mom as she is the one who takes the final call. He accepted to all our conditions like literally all the conditions I had qouted ₹60000 as advance on the website but he agreed to pay 10 months rent as an advance also, then he asked few questions about property and all those then he was like send us your Google pay number I will send you advance of ₹50k now only as token advance and was very persistent about it

As this happend my dad was also speaking to him asking about his family and all those suddenly I remembered a post from not so long ago about people posing as Army officers and scamming others so I immediately rushed to my mom told he is sus. We then asked him to come visit us and give cheque payment for advance he then cut the call and never replied. Later when we did a google search of his name came to know he is a habitual offender 

I should now report this to cyber crime Idk how to will figure out, If you guys have any idea let me know 

Once again thank you guys :)
