Got Scammed by Stock Market Broker - Walton Street India (WSIA)

I was part of WhatsApp group that provided stock buy/sell calls, through which I made decent money, earning around INR 1000 to INR 1500 per day from well-known brokers like Upstox and Groww.

The group later introduced a new program that provided calls for upper circuit stocks, promising assured profits of 200%-500%. However, to participate, we were required to invest through their broker app, Walton Street India (WSIA).

They provided a SEBI registration number (INA000008996), which I verified on the SEBI website (screenshot attached). Trusting their legitimacy, I initially deposited a total of INR 17,000 into account number 42975383219 (Shri Radha Krishna Mandir Trust) from my HDFC bank account and began trading and again deposit amount 10k,35k,15k then . I successfully withdrew INR 20,000, which further built my confidence.

Driven by greed, I continued investing more. Over time, I added a total of INR 8 lakhs, including INR 4 lakhs borrowed from friends and family. My profits eventually soared to INR 17 lakhs, and I decided to withdraw the funds. However, they demanded INR 3 lakhs in taxes before processing the withdrawal.

I requested them to deduct the tax amount from my profits and release the remaining INR 14 lakhs, but they rejected my request. A few days later, both the WSIA website and their trading app disappeared, including from the Play Store. All contact channels were cut off.

I have since filed an FIR under Sections 406 (criminal breach of trust), 420 (cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property), and 120B (criminal conspiracy) of the IPC, but I feel helpless. Since they were SEBI-registered, isn't there any action SEBI can take against such fraudulent brokers?

I need advice on how to proceed further. Aside from filing a police FIR and a complaint with cybercrime, is there anything else I can do to recover my funds?
