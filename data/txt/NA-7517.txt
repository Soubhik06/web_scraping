Shri Shishir Purohit, learned counsel for
applicant Dr. Saurabh Chandra Gupta s/o Dr. G.C.
Gupta. Shri Dharmendra Chelawat, learned counsel for
the non-applicant / CBI. Heard. This first application under Section 439 of the
Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 for grant of bail has
been filed by applicant, who is implicated in Crime
No.1137/2011 [RC No.217015S0043/CBI/ACU-
VI/NEW DELHI registered by the Central Bureau of
Investigation, Bhopal] (Originally registered at Police
Station, Sanyogitaganj, Indore) [VYAPAM SCAM
CASE] for the offence punishable Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 and 120-B of the Indian Penal Code,
1860 and also under Section 3 (D) (1) and (2) read with
Section 4 of the Madhya Pradesh Recognized
Examination Act, 1937. The applicant is in custody since 12.06.2017.
 As per the prosecution story, allegation against
the present applicant is that he impersonated his
identify and appeared on behalf of co-accused
Purshottam s/o Lalliram Khoiya in the PMT
Examination, 2009 conducted by the Madhya Pradesh
Professional Examination Board, Bhopal, scheduled to 2 be held on 05.07.2009, and therefore, he is implicated
in the alleged offence. Learned counsel for the applicant has submitted
that at present the applicant is working as a Junior
Resident Doctor at Safdarganj Hospital, New Delhi.
He further submitted that one more criminal case was
registered against him and in the aforesaid case, his
application has been allowed by order dated
04.10.2016 passed in Miscellaneous Criminal Case
No.7561/2016. In the present case, no charge sheet
has been filed. He also submitted that bail application
of all similarly situated accused persons has been
allowed from time to time, and therefore, it is prayed
that this application for grant of bail be allowed and he
be released on bail. On the other hand, learned counsel for the non-
applicant / CBI opposed the prayer and prays for its
rejection. On due consideration of the facts and
circumstances of the case, material evidence available
in the case diary and the nature of allegation made
against the present applicant, we allow this bail
application and it is directed that the applicant Dr.
Saurabh Chandra Gupta s/o Dr. G.C. Gupta shall be
released on bail, subject to his furnishing a personal
bond in the sum of Rs.2,00,000/- (Rupees Two 3 Lakh) with two local sureties in the like amount to
 the satisfaction of the trial Court for his regular
 appearance before the Special Judge on all dates of
 hearing, as may be fixed in this behalf by the Court
 concerned during the trial in connection with Crime
 No.1137/2011 in Police Station Sanyogitaganj, Indore
 (MP). It is also directed that the applicant shall abide
 by all the conditions enumerated under Section 437 (3) of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973. Additionally,
 the applicant will report to the nearest Police Station
 where he would reside [at Police Station
 Safdarganj, New Delhi] once in a week on every
 Sunday of the month between 10.00 AM to 12.00
 Noon unless required to attend the Investigating
 Officer on that day. The applicant will deposit his
 passport, if available, with the Investigating Agency
 else file an affidavit in the Court, declaring that he does
 not have any passport of any country. C. c. as per rules.
