A couple of years ago, I fell victim to a scam in Viman Nagar. A fellow biker alerted me about low air in my bike tire, and trusting the advice, I stopped at the next temporary tire shop. Little did I know, the so-called helper there ended up tearing the tube and scamming me. It was a one-time incident that left me cautious.

Fast forward to last week, I found myself in the same area, and shockingly, I encountered two bikers pulling the same trick. Remembering my past experience, I confronted the second biker, and upon shouting, he hastily made his escape.

Has anyone else faced a similar scam in a different part of the city? Let's share our experiences to raise awareness and help others stay vigilant
