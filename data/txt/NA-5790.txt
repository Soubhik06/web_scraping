BY Mr. K.B.SANGANNANAVAR : JUDICIAL MEMBER 1. This is an Appeal filed U/s 15 of Consumer Protection Act,

 1986 by the OP.1 & 2/Appellants herein, aggrieved by the

 impugned order dated 15.05.2019 passed by Bengaluru 1st

 Addl., District Consumer Disputes Redressal Forum in

 CC/1849/2018 (for short District Forum/Commission and the

 parties as arrayed in the consumer complaint). 2. The brief facts of the case of the Complainant are: the

 Complainant is a SB account holder with OP.2 and has been

 transacting with said account for the last 16 years. He is also

 provided with debit card attached to his SB account. It came to

 his notice that from 21.03.2017 to 05.12.2017 some fraud has

 taken place and a sum of Rs.90,000/- in all, has been debited

 from his account. This was brought to the notice of OP on

 08.12.2017 and also lodged complaint. However OP kept quite

 without taking any action by giving evasive reply. He has

 suffered loss of Rs.90,000/- due to no fault of him. In this

 regard, raised consumer complaint, wherein OPs upon service of

 notice through RPAD remained absent, are placed exparte. The

 Forum below held an enquiry by receiving affidavit evidence of

 Complainant along with Ex-P1 to P5, thereby allowed the 3 Appeal No.1745/2019 complaint in part and direct OP.1 & 2 jointly and severally to

credit a sum of Rs.90,000/- to the account of the Complainant

along with prorata interest as is being given to the SB account

holder and do pay Rs.25,000/- towards damages and

Rs.5,000/- towards cost of litigation expenses within 30 days

from the date of receipt of the order. It is this order, impugned

in this appeal, by the OPs on the ground that the alleged

grievance of the Complainant is with regard to the PAYTM

transaction. The Paytm is the proper and necessary party to the

complaint to know the details of exact transaction such as the

place of transaction and the beneficiary establishment/person

of the respective transaction. The Complainant's account

discloses that during the period 21.03.2017 to 05.12.2017 for

about 9 months 50 transactions taken place at different dates

and even prior to 21.03.2017 on 07.03.2017 a Paytm

transaction has taken place was not considered by the Forum

below and the Forum below erred in passing the impugned

order. Further due to non-service of notice, the OPs were not

appeared before the Forum and not filed the version and

material evidence against the claim made by the Complainant.

The Forum below has gravely erred in observing that as per the

RBI guidelines and regulations, it is the OPs responsibilities to 4 Appeal No.1745/2019 prove that Complainant has shared the details of debit card

 such as card number, pin number and CVC number to absolve

 its liability or to minimize its liability and making the

 Complainant for the loss caused to him due to disclosure of the

 above details. 3. The Commission heard learned counsels on record. We

 examined the impugned order and grounds of appeal along with

 account statement for the period 01.03.2017 to 30.12.2017 and

 now we have to examine, whether the impugned order passed

 by Forum below calls for any interference for the grounds of

 appeal ? 4. At the very outset, it would be appropriate to make mention the

 guidelines issued by RBI dtd.06.07.2017 in respect of 'customer

 protection - limiting liability of customers in unauthorised

 electronic banking transactions' in order to strengthening of

 systems and procedures under RBI letter no.RBI/2017-18/15

 DBR.No.Leg.BC.78/09.07.005/2017-18. Let us examine as to

 'Limited liability of a customer (a) Zero liability of a customer in

 para 6 which would play a vital importance to decide this

 appeal: A customer's entitlement to zero liability shall arise

 where the unauthorised transaction occurs in the following

 events: 5. Let us skip as to the Reversal timeline for zero liability/ limited

 liability of a customer. But in para 12 the 'burden of proving

 customer liability in case of unauthorized electronic banking

 transactions shall lie on the bank.' Further in para 13

 'Reporting and monitoring requirements' are provided. Thus

 keeping in mind the above guidelines which in fact is ignored by

 the Forum below only on the ground that OP.1 & 2 upon service

 of notice through RPAD remained absent and are placed

 exparte, proceeded to appreciate the materials placed by the

 Complainant without noticing the fact that even prior to

 21.03.2017 on 07.03.2017 Rs.1000/- and Rs.1500/- debited at

 Paytm mobile solutions, Noida at 8.24 and 8.40 respectively

 which would be said contrary to the contentions of the

 Complainant that OP.1 & 2 deficient in providing banking

 services to him. It is also to be noted herein that more than 50 6 Appeal No.1745/2019 transactions taken place commencing from 21.03.2017 to

05.12.2017 at Paytm Mobile Solutions, Noida by debiting the

account of the Complainant either with Rs.1000/- or Rs.2000/-

or Rs.3000/- as the case may be. In such circumstances, in our

view, the Forum below has failed to evaluate the entire account

statement before concluding to direct OPs to credit a sum of

Rs.90,000/- to the account of the Complainant along with

prorate interest as is being given to the SB account holder

followed by the guidelines of RBI dtd.06.07.2017, since the

Complainant herein in his complaint itself had stated that on

08.12.2017 he lodged the complaint with OP for the

transactions taken place through his account commencing from

21.03.2017 to 05.12.2017 to which application of entitlement to

zero liability could not be applied for the simple reason that

Paytm is not made a party to his complaint. It is not that one or

two or three transactions taken place. If it is third party breach

which has to be brought to the notice of OPs within three

working days of receiving the communication from the bank

regarding unauthorised transaction. However in his case, he

waited till 08.12.2017 for the transactions taken place between

21.03.2017 to 05.12.2017, they are numbering more than 50

transactions besides two transactions taken place prior to 7 Appeal No.1745/2019 21.03.2017, on 07.03.2017 debited Rs.1000/- and Rs.1500/- at

 Paytm mobile solutions, Noida. All these debit entries made by

 OPs pursuant to Paytm mobile solutions, Noida. It is therefore,

 learned counsel for the Appellant/OPs is right in contending

 that Paytm is proper and necessary party to the Complainant.

 In such view of the matter, impugned order passed by Forum

 below is liable to be set aside and the matter requires to be

 remanded back to the Forum below to give a direction to the

 Complainant to implead Paytm, Noida as party to the complaint

 and to ask the Paytm to furnish details of transactions to

 examine, whether the Complainant has parted with the details

 of Paytm such as pin/otp and cvc number and to appreciate all

 the materials on record followed by the guidelines of RBI stated

 supra. 6. In so far as contention of the Appellants that no notice served

 which also has to be examined by the Forum below and if the

 Forum below found some force in such submission has to

 consider in accordance with law and if RPAD were served on

 OP.1 & 2, even OP.1 & 2 have lost their right to file version,

 have right to engage counsel to assist the Forum below and to

 place required documents to appreciate the materials placed on

 record by the Complainant as well as Paytm, Noida. In such 8 Appeal No.1745/2019 conclusion, proceed to allow the appeal, consequently set aside

 the impugned order dtd.15.05.2019 passed in CC/1849/2018

 on the file of Bengaluru 1st Addl., DCDRF and remand back to

 decide the case as observed as early as possible. 7. The amount in deposit is directed to be transferred to District

 Commission for needful. Notify copy of this Order to the District Commission and parties

 to this appeal.
