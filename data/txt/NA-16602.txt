--- POST TEXT ---

The tech support call centres are everywhere, especially in my city of Guwahati. They lure gullible young men to scam Americans. Yesterday I came across yet another one doing the exact same "windows tech support" nonsense where you call up people to tell them their computer has a virus so they will need to pay a huge amount to get it fixed.

If you're young, can speak English and are looking for a job then these people will hire you immediately. If you have no experience with these scams you will be used like an idiot. Look for different jobs.

If you ever appear for an interview for a call centre, make sure the company has a good reputation, you can easily find all about a good company online. And a legitimate call centre will have a decent office and it won't need to be in a small apartment in some dark corner.

I absolutely hate these scam centres. I became part of a scam centre unknowingly. That one ran a grant scam. and I don't want any one else to get involved with these shady activities either.

I'm sure there are legitimate call centres in India but like I said, they will have a nice offices and their information will be online. Never work for a call centre you've never heard of.

If you're not sure if it's legitimate or not, join them and look at their script. If it's all outbound calls where you have to say that person on the other end has either won something or has a virus, just walk away from there.
