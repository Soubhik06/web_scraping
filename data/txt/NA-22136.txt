I was hospitalized for 25 days due to an anxiety disorder. My Dell laptop was left powered on but unplugged during that time.

When I returned, the laptop was drained and wouldn’t power on.

I found a service center in Santacruz, Mumbai, listed as a Dell service center, but later discovered they were a third-party service.

Initially, the service center claimed the battery needed replacement, quoting ₹5000 for an official battery and ₹3000 for an unofficial one. I opted for the official battery.

They logged into the laptop (with my password) and confirmed my data was intact, but later claimed the SSD wasn’t working.

They then said the motherboard was faulty and kept escalating the repair costs from ₹3000 to over ₹20,000.

They also claimed there was fresh water damage inside the laptop, which is impossible as my room was closed while I was in the hospital.

I complained to Dell, who advised me to take the laptop from this service center and bring it to another one. I expressed concerns about potential deliberate damage.

Dell suggested getting an SDR report from the service center, but the report was handwritten, missing crucial details (e.g., the fact that the SSD and motherboard were functioning since they logged in using my key).

Dell initially assured me over the phone that repairs would be done free of cost, but I didn’t receive this confirmation in writing.

After retrieving the laptop, Dell sent me a quote for ₹21,000, contradicting their earlier verbal assurance.

I suspect the service center deliberately damaged my laptop, and Dell is now refusing to honor the initial promise, asking me to pay for repairs instead.

I have proof that I was not in my room for a month (hospitalization bills), so any damage could not have been caused by me.

Multiple calls to Dell resulted in disconnections after I explained the situation, and I haven’t been able to get the address to send a legal notice.

Dell customer service also informed me that no authorized service center should offer unofficial batteries, further raising suspicion.

The laptop is out of warranty, but I believe I’ve been misled and my device has been tampered with.

Looking for advice:

How do I proceed with filing a legal notice against Dell and the service center?

Should I involve consumer court, or is there a better legal route?

Any suggestions for holding these parties accountable for potential fraud and damage?

Would appreciate any help or legal insight!
