**Context:** 

* Who is doing it? - BJP led alliance 
* What is it? - Rs. 1500 directly in bank account for eligible women of age 18 to 60. Annual Family income less than 2.5Lacs per annum (No Taxpayer or Government Employee) 

**My reply to opposition:** 

* Pandering due to election: So any election promise or progress in education, infrastructure, healthcare is also pandering then? 
* Why just women? - I agree, expand it further to include wider population (i.e. men) but that doesn’t mean current scheme should be discontinued 
* Why now during election time? - I agree this is political strategy, but you cannot blame someone using their heads to plan a good strategy to appeal to voters 
* Unwanted Burden on taxpayers? - As a taxpayer I would prefer a scheme which is directly benefiting poor section, instead of my tax money stolen by few political and bureaucratic cronies without actual results (e.g. clean india cess)
* Financial viability?- This I feel is a valid point, but if this is not viable then the government will stop it. No political party is going to fund it from their pockets. But to discontinue or not to do it despite having funds is dumb. 

**My suggestions to make it more effective:** 

* Job opportunity: financial assistance and empowerment is good, but don’t just limit yourself to giving freebies. Focus on creating job opportunities for these women, which will pay higher than Rs. 1500 per month 
* Strict auditing and implementation: Keep strict execution control on the scheme, so that there is no leakage or fraud
* Financial viability: don’t put additional burden on middle class taxpayers to finance this scheme. Instead find new revenue drivers. Maybe the Corporate/ Ultra-rich should pay for this.
