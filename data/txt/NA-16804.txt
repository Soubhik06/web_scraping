Hello r/india ! 
I live in France, I've never been to and I don't know anyone from India. A few days ago I received a [weird email](http://i.imgur.com/xrZyZJv.jpg) from "Reliance Customer Care" (aka Reliance Telecom Limited or Reliance Communications) sending me an "ebill". I couldn't even figure out what the currency was (Rs, had never seen that before), so I dismissed it as a very badly targeted scam and moved on. Today I received a second mail : a [receipt for the payment](http://i.imgur.com/DXfrVKA.jpg). After I checked my bank account (there was nothing suspicious), I thought it was the weirdest scam I've ever seen so I went back to the first mail and realized there was a 9 pages pdf doc attached to it : an actual phone bill. I was even more confused because it really looks like a proper phone bill, with all the information one would need in a phone bill, you know. 
So I started writing an email to the company to tell them there was a mistake but I wasn't 100% sure it wasn't a scam. I thought that if it were, it would be the most elaborate scam I've ever seen and so I suddenly wasn't sure I wanted to bring their attention to me. I also thought that people from India would be more able than me to tell if it's legit or not, that's what brings me here \o/ ! Will you help me figure it out ? Perhaps you can already tell, but if not, here's my take. 
**What makes this look legit :** 

* As I said, it really looks like a real phone bill and I've never seen so much dedication put into a scam. Here's the [easiest page to censor](http://i.imgur.com/lcJMfac.jpg). There are also 3 pages with very detailed phone calls logs. 
* Again, I got a receipt for the payment, so no one is currently asking me for money, I don't feel pressured. And even if I didn't get the receipt, Rs 400 is about 5 euros (or 6 dollars), so not that much ! I'm more used to Nigerian kings asking for 5000 dollars to get 5 millions or something... 
* My google searches on Reliance and scam direct me to angry customers which only reaffirms that the company is legit, like their [website](http://www.rcom.co.in) tends to show. All my searches based on the customer's personal information gave nothing. 

**What makes me suspicious :** (warning: blatant ignorance about indian culture probably incoming) 

* My email address contains some letters of my first name and my last name and it looks nothing like the customer's name. The more I think about it, the more clueless I am about how I ended up receiving these mails, like where the mistake would be. 
* I'm super confused about the locations names in the address. It says "Orissa" state but Wikipedia tells me its name was changed to "Odisha" in 2011. For all I know, they could have been recycling a fake bill since then, only changing every dates to make it look legit but forgetting the state ? There's also Brahmapur / Berhampur that confuses me. And under the street name there's something like "near Nimapali" (changed the name here) and Google tells me it's "Nimapalli". I guess the answers to all this could be that it's a matter of India or indian languages and that I'm too used to rely on Google but I really can't tell =/. There are just so many discrepancies here ! 
* The exact address of the customer doesn't exist on Google Maps or anywhere (the street's name gives me nothing). Don't know what it means. 
* There's nothing but english language in the mails and the bill ? I know that a lot of indian people speak english perfectly, but as an ignorant European idiot, I would expect companies in India to communicate to their customers in at least two different languages, to be more user-friendly ? Like, what about indian people who don't speak english, don't they use phones too ? Idk, I feel so stupid right now, yay ! 
* As you may have noticed, the receipt mail provided an eBay discount code. It seems like it would work for anything on eBay, I don't see any restrictions (it links to ebay.com). I just can't wrap my head around that. Is this a common practice from indian telecom companies ? 
 
. 
 
Now, if all this is indeed legit and it's a real phone bill that was wrongly sent to me, it infuriates me. I've got **way too much information** about that customer : name, address, phone number, calls history logs, and probably more than that. With the customer ID and my email address I can log in some parts of reliance's website and I can probably retrieve the password and do all the sh\*t I want ! I mean, the email address wasn't even checked before I got all these data, it's ridiculous. The company's called "reliance", ffs. I really wish I could reach to the customer to let them know about all this but I have very little hope I would be able to find them on the internet and I'm not sure using their phone number would be a good idea (even if I knew how)... 
If it's a scam, I'm speechless. That's dedication. 
 
So, what do you think ? If you think it's real, I shall send my response asap. 
 
Thank you and please excuse my broken english.
