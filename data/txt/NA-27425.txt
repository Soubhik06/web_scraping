I recently applied for a job on Indeed, and the employer asked me to complete a test. After I submitted the test, they expressed interest in hiring me. I had a brief phone conversation with a woman from their office who called me. I didn’t pick up at first because the call came from New York, and I don’t normally answer random calls, especially since the company is based in Texas. The owner mentioned that I hadn’t answered, so I apologized and called her back. During our conversation, she asked if I was still interested in the job, which I found odd since I had already been emailing with the owner. I confirmed that I was interested but asked for more details about pay and hours. She didn’t have that information and said she would call me back, but I never heard from her again.

Subsequently, the owner requested my official school records, which I sent, but I edited out my SSN for safety. Then, he told me they were ready to hire me right away (I'll attach images). However, he asked for my SSN and bank details, which raised a red flag for me. I did some research and confirmed the company is legitimate and operates both in the U.S. and India, though the LLC is relatively new in the U.S. The company also has a physical office.

To be cautious, I emailed the company's official email address to inquire if this was a phishing attempt. The owner responded, but from a different email address, not the one he had been using previously or the official company address. He seemed upset that I was verifying the details and told me I needed to learn how to do better research. This whole exchange felt strange and unprofessional.
