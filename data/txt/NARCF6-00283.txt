Okay, so one of my friends joined in a Startup last year. The culture is extremely toxic. Work hours 10 hours per day for six days a week and it’s all WFO. Hire-Fire culture. People are asked to join within 2 days and if the founders are not happy with their performance, they are fired within 5 days. All this is done without any prior intimation. Like you leave the office all good, work done, but you reach home and boom you get a mail that you are terminated. FnF is not credited even after two months of the LWD. Moreover, it is looking bad on her resume since she is searching for a new opportunity now. A lot of people approach her on LinkedIn enquiring about the company culture since the company is always recruiting and firing the employees. The founders ask the employees to post fake reviews on websites like Ambition Box. Also, she told me that there was no deduction in her salary not even for PF. What does this mean? And are all startups the same? Has anyone had different experiences?
