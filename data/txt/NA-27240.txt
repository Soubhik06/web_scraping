--- POST TEXT ---

Got called from a random number at noon on a Saturday claiming they were calling from Dell (number said Las Vegas). A Indian voice (not sure if relevant or not) was asking me how my computer was doing and saying that this was a courtesy call because they found that a lot of people were using my internet connection. He was referring to a computer that I hadn't used in months, saying that it was in the last few weeks. 

Anyway, he had me put "netstat" in the cmd application of my PC and said that if I saw more than one IP address listed, that it was because many people were using my net address. 

Not sure if it was a scam, but it sounded really fishy. He was getting defensive when I asked how they could go through that computer if it hadn't been used in months when he said it was happening in the last two weeks. 

What is really weird though is that he knew that I had called Dell a few months ago asking about a replacement charger for my laptop.
