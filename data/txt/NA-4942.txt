crime No 47/2020 registered at Police Station State Cyber Police Department,
Indore Zone Distt. Indore for the offence punishable under sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 406 , 409 and 201 of IPC and Sec 66-C and 66-D of I.T. Act. Ms Kher, learned Dy. Advocate General on instructions from
Investigating Officer, Shri Ambrish Mishra present in the Court states that it

is an organized crime of misappropriation of public funds in large scale in the
branches of Union Bank of India at Scheme No. 54, 78 and Borasali. Under
the Prime Minister Mudra Loan Yojna, provision has been made for extending
subsidized (small) loan facilities to poor and down trodden people as a
measure of self empowerment. A typical modus operandi has been adopted
in the instant case. On a false promise of providing loan, various poor
persons have been allured to hand over their aadhar card, ration card,
residence proof for opening bank accounts in Union Bank of India at its
branches mentioned above. Such accounts are opened with the help of kiosk
operators and bank officials sanctioning the loan on purported verification of
documents. Amount of such loan credited in such accounts are transferred to
self-styled service providers on the strength of fake documentations justifying
transfer and thereafter, the amount is siphoned to different persons involved
 2 MCRC-26328-2020
in this chain of organized crime. It is only when the individuals are informed
about the EMI dues on their mobile phones, they came to know about
sanctioning and releasing of the amount of loan out of which they received no
amount at all. On a complaint in that behalf, the State Cyber cell Indore has

investigated extensively such bank accounts in the branches of Union Bank of
India at Scheme No. 54, 78 and Borasali. This Court on 28.08.2020 while dealing with bail application registered
as M.Cr.C. No. 17674/2020 of the co-accused Mahesh Dubey had ordered
for more intense and comprehensive investigation. Thereafter, the Cyber Cell
has further deeply investigated the case. Initially, the scam was found to be
that of Rs. 63 lakhs which was credited in the account of one Vishal Dangi
(service provider) and one of the accomplices. On further investigation, it has
been found that the present applicant holds an account in the Indore Premium
Cooperative Bank Ltd. vide account no.156000820155 and the first
transaction had taken place on 09.12.2016. On 21.12.2019 the account
balance was of Rs. 3,37,41,691/- and the amount was shown to be nil as the
entire amount was withdrawn on the same date. Amount has been credited
during the period from 09.12.2016 to 06.08.2020. That apart, recently, Union
Bank of India has supplied the details of issuance of eight bank drafts of Rs.
7,00,000/- each in the year 2019 prepared and credited in the said account of
applicant. That apart, from one Avtar Spares Centre; another service provider
and one of the accomplices has credited an amount of Rs. 48,000 and Rs.
3,41,0000/- in the same bank account. Now further investigation is in
progress to crack down names of more such accounts wherein the public
fund though supposed to be distributed to the needy persons as subsidy
loans under the Prime Minister Mudra Loan Yojna have been defalcated and
misappropriated. Hence, it is apparent that its' a case of fraud, cheating and
misappropriation. Shri Sirpurkar,learned Sr. counsel for the applicant submits that

sole reason, learned counsel for the applicant avoids to explain huge deposit

Union Bank of India of Scheme No. 54, 78 and Borsala; nationalized bank.

the Prime Minister Mudra Loan Yojna in the name of subsidy loans has been
clandestinely routed through the fraudulently opened accounts for the benefit
of accused persons in the name of service providers on the strength of forged
and fabricated documents. In the opinion of this Court, no indulgence is

 warranted at this stage in the matter of grant of bail. The application is
 accordingly rejected. Not only in Union Bank of India, but also such other nationalized
 banks where loan facilities under the Prime Minister Mudra Loan Yojna are
 available and loan disbursement have been made in this region, the possibility
 of such loan scam of large scale may not be ruled out. It is regretted that such
 pious and laudable loan disbursement schemes for empowerment of poor
 persons are being subverted by handful of miscreants for unjust enrichment
 through unscrupulous acts, misrepresentation and cheating playing with
 innocence and ignorance of poor persons. The investigation must come to a logical end. Before parting with the case, it is hereby directed that investigating wing
 of the Cyber Cell shall continue to investigate the matter to rule out the
 possibility of any such clandestine flow of public fund under the garb of
 fictitious loan transactions. Let report of extensive investigation as indicated above be submitted to
 the Office to Additional Advocate General, Indore which may be
