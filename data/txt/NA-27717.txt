We have a small business fundraiser on Facebook to get my husband's photography studio off the ground. It works like a Kickstarter where certain donations get you certain tangible rewards from the studio like framed wall art, etc. Most of the donations have been from family and friends who are happy to help out and happy to receive my husband's beautiful photos for their homes.

A few days ago a complete stranger donated around $1000, more than anyone (even me!) has given so far.

"Her" profile was created in November, has zero posts, a single photo of a white woman with a bland American name, all her employers and schools listed are in Louisiana except for one high school in Cameroon. Most of her 99 friends have Indian or African names.

There's no question that's a fake profile. What I'm trying to figure out is, what is the scam?

Husband has not touched the money. He has also changed his Facebook password and added two factor authentication just in case.

Could this be a version of Digital Wallet Scam? He hasn't received any communication from her, not even to claim her donation reward. Facebook says that if a donor requests their money back they need to contact Facebook, not the fundraiser owner.
