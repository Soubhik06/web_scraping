In this post, you'll learn about how I got scammed by a food digger for 11,000 rupees. I’d heard about food diggers before, but I totally forgot about the pricey hookah part of their scam.
Here's what went down:
I matched with a girl on a dating app. She had her face hidden but seemed alright. After our match, she sent me a disappearing picture and was hesitant to share her Instagram ID. She kept pushing to meet up. This was the first time I tried to be cautious, knowing about food diggers. I suggested meeting in a restaurant in Hauz Khas, where I lived.
But she said it was too far and wanted to meet in Rajouri Garden. I went there, thinking I’d check the menu before ordering. When we met, she seemed convincing but gave off little chappri vibe.
We started looking for a restaurant, and she kinda pressured me into one. Trust me, it’s easier said than done to refuse in that situation. I was planning to check the menu, which turned out to be overpriced, but I was fine with it. Everything was like 750–1000 rupees. She ordered a hookah, vodka, and veg hakka noodles for herself, while I went for pizza and a coke. She complained about the taste of the vodka and got another coke to mix with it. I was expecting a bill of 3–4k, including the hookah. But when the waiter gave me the bill, it was a whopping 11,000 rupees! The hookah alone cost 6,000, and the food and drinks were 5,000. I had to pay and get out of there. It might seem easy to say why I didn’t argue, but in that shady place, with a girl I wasn't sure was scamming me, and maybe some dodgy guys around, paying was the quickest exit. That night, I felt terrible. I felt more terrible not because I lost the money but someone used me for being desperate, and read old Reddit posts about the food digger scam in Rajouri Garden. I was still in denial and texted her about the bill along with even some reddit post links I found. She told me she didn’t know how much the bill was and agreed to send me 5,000 rupees, but only after getting her salary.
We had plans to meet at my place on next Tuesday before all the bill incident while we were in restaurant. And the next morning, I faced reality, read more Reddit stories, and spoke to friends who'd avoided similar scams.
She’s still texting me and might want to meet up again. We were supposed to meet at my apartment, but I fear it might be a bigger scam. I'm still chatting with her in a smooth way for two reasons.

Firstly, to reclaim that 5,000 rupees she offered. I hope she might send the money to boost my trust, setting the stage for a bigger scam when we meet up, although most probably she won't send it.
Secondly, to make her travel a long way, possibly with any scammers accompanying her, and then cut off contact after they waste half a day traveling. 
What should I do? Do you have any ideas for payback or guiding her to understand that her actions are wrong? I’m all set for Tuesday. I'll keep updating as she texts me here. Please guide me through this.
