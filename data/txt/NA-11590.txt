 7.1 In the show cause notice dated 29.04.2004, the respondents

 alleged that the applicant was allowed to join at Lucknow Division on

 the basis of transfer order and associated documents which were found

 to be fake on enquiry as part of a larger scam and asked the applicant Page 8 of 11 CAT,Lucknow Bench OA No. 332/00224 of 2014 Bikrama Ditya Sahu Vs. U.O.I. &Ors.

 to produce inter alia his appointment order. In our view, the main

 controversy in this case is whether the applicant was validly appointed

 to the post he was holding, and to settle this question production of the

 appointment order becomes essential. In the reply dated 23.05.2004,

 the applicant did not produce the appointment order and instead asked

 the respondents to supply copies of the documents relied upon by

 them. Interestingly, in this reply, the applicant stated that he was

 engaged by the respondents on the basis of an application made by him

 and asked the respondents to supply a copy of that application as well.

 7.2 Now, if any appointment order was issued in favour of the

 applicant, in the normal circumstances it should be available with the

 respondents as well as the applicant, and thus either side should be

 able to produce it. However, when the factum of appointment itself is

 disputed by the employer alleging that the appointment has been made

 through fraudulent means based on fake transfer order, it is obvious

 that the employer's claim is that no appointment order was issued in

 favour of the employee. In such circumstances, the employee is

 required to produce the appointment order through which he was

 appointed to the service to rebut the employer's claim. In other words,

 the burden of proving the factum of appointment shifts to the employee.

 7.3 Viewed in this context, when the applicant made another

 representation dated 29.08.2013 to the respondents, he asked for the

 same documents he had requested earlier on 23.05.2004 and did not

 disclose the appointment order through which he was taken into

 service. This can only lead us to the conclusion that the applicant is not

 in possession of his appointment order, assuming that such an order

 exists. As we have observed, it is factum of appointment of the

 applicant which is at the heart of the controversy. When the factum of

 appointment itself is challenged, the only way to settle the issue is to Page 9 of 11 CAT,Lucknow Bench OA No. 332/00224 of 2014 Bikrama Ditya Sahu Vs. U.O.I. &Ors.

 produce the appointment order. Relevance of other documents

 becomes material if the applicant is able to produce his appointment

 order. In other words, all other documents and records are ancillary to

 the central issue of the validity, or otherwise, of the applicant's

 appointment to be proved through the production of the appointment

 order which the applicant has failed to do. Consequently, we do not find

 any infirmity in the decision dated 18.11.2013 of the respondents.
