the law and facts and is liable to be set aside. 2. Brief facts of the case are that Baljeet Singh complainant filed

written application alleging that he was using credit card of SBI bearing

No.4611-1993-5632-0716. He had requested through net banking to SBI

for enhancement of Credit Card Limit. On 10.07.2020 at about 2.46 pm,

he received a call from SBI Credit Card Customer Care that his request for

enhancement of credit card limit was being processed. He was told that to
