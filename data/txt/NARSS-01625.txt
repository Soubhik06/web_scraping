I'm giggling as I'm writing this. Yesterday, a scammer (intro as Vijay Kumar) called me and said that my passbook and credit card was expired and that I've to renew it immediately. I was already lmao when I heard his accent in the first place. I played along with him asking for my acnt no., cif no. Bla bla bla. But later he slightly got a sense that I'm making fun out of him and his dumb idea and cut the call off. I tried calling him again (for fun). He picked up at the third time.
This time a new guy was talking to me and intro himself as "Seena pandi" (me and my mom were already laughing) and said the guy Vijay also called him, connected our lines and cut the call. I said that's not how a conference call works and quickly I heard the scammer (Vijay's) voice. He was a little bit frustrated that his silly scam didn't work on me and when I asked him about the bank details he said a "curse word- bank" as a name. I made him repeat about 10 to 12 times and he got more frustrated. BHJAJAJAHAHA, me and my mom couldn't atp.
He later asked me if I'm married and when I said it's none of his business he said "naan ungal kalyaan panikre" (yup exactly the accent).
I warned him that Im going to raise a cyber security complaint and he said he had balls. And then I cut the call. 
Haven't laughed in days like this!
