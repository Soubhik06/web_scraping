Expect a large amount of people you encounter on roads to be drunk, or in a celebratory rash driving mood tonight... As they say, "the best driving is defensive driving", while we may not know if others are as careful as usual, it is best to be very focused and defensive during your rides to the venues and back home or hotels tonight

Stay safe, drink consciously and keep it under control, don't take unnecessary risks and don't go to secluded or isolated areas no matter what

Keep this in mind, but don't forget to have fun! May this NYE be the best party of your life!
