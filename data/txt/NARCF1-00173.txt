The incident:

- female comedian makes a joke on whatsapp university folks spreading misinformation about an upcoming statue of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj in Mumbai. She has taken the video down from Youtube, but here's a reupload on twitter - [video of the joke](https://twitter.com/NityanandDeven2/status/1281229818508398593?s=20)

- refers to Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj as Shivaji

- the video resurfaces on social media - comedian [receives rape threats](https://www.indiatimes.com/trending/social-relevance/agrima-joshua-rape-threat-youtuber-shubham-mishra-517783.html) and other cyber abuse and harrassment

- [SS MLA Pratap Sarnaik calls for her arrest](https://mumbaimirror.indiatimes.com/mumbai/other/stand-up-comedian-faces-wrath-shiv-sena-mla-pratap-sarnaik-demands-arrest-for-cracking-jokes-on-shivaji-maharaj/articleshow/76905190.cms)

- [MNS goons vandalise the location where she performed the stand up](https://www.mid-day.com/articles/mumbai-mns-vandalises-khar-s-the-habitat-over-2019-stand-up-act/22887298) - Wouldn't leave until she apologised

- [She apologises](https://twitter.com/Agrimonious/status/1281948379795214336?s=20)

- National Commission for Women files an FIR against the Youtuber, Shubham Mishra, who made rape threats against her. [He is booked by Gujurat police in Vadodara](https://ahmedabadmirror.indiatimes.com/ahmedabad/others/vadodara-shubham-mishra-booked-for-abusing-stand-up-comedian-agrima-joshua/articleshow/76927880.cms)

- Another youtuber (mumbai based) [arrested](https://www.hindustantimes.com/india-news/mumbai-police-arrests-nalasopara-resident-for-giving-rape-threat-to-comedian-joshua/story-JL17orwJqKhNf3WTFlu0wK.html) for [rape threats](https://twitter.com/bhaoraoamtibhat/status/1282392758192238592)

My personal opinion is that this is much ado about nothing. She was not insulting Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj and even if referring to Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj as just Shivaji is offensive to some folks, rape threats and vandalism is not the answer to this. No one is legally obligated to respect any historical figure.
