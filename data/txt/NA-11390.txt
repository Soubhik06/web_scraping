 APP for the State. 2. The petitioner seeks pre-arrest bail in connection

 with Chapra (Town) P.S. Case No. 237 of 2021 registered for

 the offence punishable under Sections 376 , 467 , 468 and 471 / 34 of the Indian Penal Code. 3. Prosecution story, in brief, is that the petitioner has

 taken certain objectionable photographs and video of the

 informant and thereafter he started blackmailing her and

 pressurized her to show her nude body on video call.
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.34663 of 2022(5) dt.23-03-2023 2/3 4. Learned counsel appearing on behalf of the

 petitioner submitted that the informant and the petitioner were

 in love relationship and due to pressure of the society and the

 petitioner. 5. It appears that after the amendment in the Code of

 Criminal Procedure , the informant is required to be noticed,

 this bail application has been listed today defect free. 6. This Court finds it proper that notice should be

 issued to the informant. Learned counsel appearing on behalf of

 the petitioner is directed to implead the informant as opposite

 party no.2 in course of the day. 7. Issue notice to the opposite party no.2 through both

 the processes, ordinary process as well as under registered cover

 with A/D for which requisites etc. must be filed within a period

 of one week. Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.34663 of 2022(5) dt.23-03-2023 3/3 8. No coercive step shall be taken against the

 petitioner till the next date of hearing. 9. List this case forthwith after service of notice. (Purnendu Singh, J)
