The crypto world is exciting, but it’s also a playground for fraudsters. Recently, scams have surfaced across India targeting unsuspecting individuals. Here’s how they work:

1️⃣ The Lure: Fraudulent websites/apps claim to turn a ₹5,000 deposit into millions.
2️⃣ Fake Profits: Victims are shown fabricated account balances to build trust.
3️⃣ The Catch: When trying to withdraw funds, additional "processing fees" are demanded.

💡 Red Flags to Watch Out For:

Guaranteed high returns (crypto is volatile, no one can promise fixed profits).

Upfront payments or "unlock fees."

Suspicious platforms lacking SEBI or RBI approval.

✔️ How to Stay Safe:

Stick to verified platforms like WazirX, CoinSwitch, or ZebPay.

Research before investing—if it sounds too good to be true, it probably is.

Report suspicious activity to the National Cybercrime Portal.
