When the police questioned him about his education and training details, Manjhi admitted that he had been faking as a trainee IPS officer. It has been learnt that Manjhi has studied till Class 10. 

A ‘fee’ of Rs 2 lakh to a job racketeer, a pair of police uniforms, and a toy pistol were all that it took an 18-year-old Bihar youth to become a ‘trainee IPS officer’. But his joy was short-lived, as the Sikandra police in Jamui arrested him Friday.

The accused, Mithilesh Manjhi, 18, a resident of Govardhan Bigha village of Halsi, Lakhisarai, was nabbed by the police when he was roaming around in Sikandra market in uniform posing as a trainee IPS officer.
