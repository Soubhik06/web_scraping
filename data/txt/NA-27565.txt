My company wrote a check to a subcontractor for a large sum of money (high 6 figures). It was stolen in the mail and deposited into a JP Morgan Chase account. The subcontractor doesn't bank with Chase and never received our check.

Beginning last Friday (3 days after our check was cashed), we got these suspicious calls from JP Morgan Chase 888-373-1969 number. They spoke to our receptionist several times and our AP Dept. and just seemed very off. My team never gave any information to them. We also had several calls from a cell phone claiming to be the owner of this company, which was a fraudulent claim as well because I personally know the owner of the company we wrote the check to. We gave this gentlemen no information either. We filed an affidavit of fraud with our bank to recover the funds and also a police report. 

 I finally spoke to "Chase" today, and I believe it not to be Chase because the lady speaks in VERY heavy Indian accent and was very rude when I refused to give her any information over the phone. I told her I would only answer yes or no questions. She asked my name, which I refused, then our zip code which I also refused. Then hung up.

What is the MO here? I assume Chase is holding the balance and they need more information for them to release the funds? But what information could they need and why wouldn't Chase reach out directly to us or our bank? 

 And also, if they didn't start calling like that last week, we might not even be aware of this still to be 100% honest. So they must really need something for this check to go through. And also, my bank said they would reach out to Chase directly once they receive the signed affidavit of fraud in the mail and told me not to do that myself yet or I would.
