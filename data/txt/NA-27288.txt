Hi everyone, I just joined reddit to spread awareness and hopefully stop Taak!Zone, a "company" that offers fake remote internships against payment of a 120$ fee, so to avoid people falling for this.

In synthesis, they claim to be a project-management/consultancy company established in 2019 in Miami and now operative also in Europe (Netherlands). They also claim to work for several big brands such as Samsung and Unilever, but there is no evidence whatsoever about this: no projects, no reports, no conferences, no nothing. Recently, ads for their "internships" have popped up in great number on almost any major job-search website, especially LinkedIn and Glassdoor ([Here's a standard example](https://www.glassdoor.com/job-listing/remote-project-management-internship-junior-project-manager-taak-zone-JV_IC2203308_KO0,59_KE60,69.htm?jl=4041019977)). At first sight, it seems pretty legit: after applying, you receive a confirmation email and after some days you are contacted by HR saying you passed the selection and, to access the next phase, you have to answer a couple of questions about project management. After doing this, some more days pass, and you are scheduled for an interview via Zoom. In the same mail there is a brochure about the incredible opportunities of the "internship" and, at the end, they mention for the first time that you have to pay to join them. No need to tell, you will pass the interview and start "working" after having paid. Obviously, the "project" you'll be assigned is the same stupid stuff for every "intern", while the "lectures" are probably prerecorded bullshit to make them seem legit.

I know all this because I submitted several application to them and have always been selected, with the same pattern as above. Every single time. Even if I didn't answer the questions and sent a blank pdf or had a ridiculous CV.

Furthermore, all their social pages and websites have been created in December 2020 at the earliest, while they claim to be active since 2019. All the people linked to this company are either impossible to contact and have obviously fake LinkedIn profiles ([here's the "CEO &amp; Founder"](https://nl.linkedin.com/in/daniel-hakvoort-2459531b4)), or are poor "interns" that have been duped. Also, all their addresses are fake, and the one in Amsterdam belongs to a co-working company that I have already notified and confirmed they have never heard of Taak!Zone.

It is despicable that someone is exploiting a situation in which many young people are in need of work to scam them out of their money and I felt like it was obligatory for me to do something about it.

Here are the links to report them:

FTC: [https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/#/assistant](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/#/assistant)

Dutch Fraud Helpdesk (it's in Dutch but Google can translate it automatically): [https://www.fraudehelpdesk.nl/fraude-melden/](https://www.fraudehelpdesk.nl/fraude-melden/)

Feel free to report them anywhere else you want and how many times you want, the strength is in numbers in these cases.

**UPDATE**: after I rescheduled for the umpteenth time the interview they told me they reported me to Google and LinkedIn for harassment, and swiftly followed up telling me that I am "not anonymous, even in India :)". So yeah, they are scammers confirmed beyond reasonable doubt.
