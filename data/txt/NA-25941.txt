--- POST TEXT ---

Contacted a company called Service Repair accidentally to get the gas stove fixed. It was an accident because when I clicked on the link it mentioned the original stove company name. That was my mistake. The men visited at lunchtime on a working day (maybe strategically?) They changed the filters + knobs on the stove etc and gave us a hefty bill. It was much later (on the same day) that we got to know we contacted the wrong people but what's worse is they were frauds. They took our original stove parts, replaced them with who knows what quality products, and charged about 3x what the original company does for servicing. The stove worked for a few minutes and created problems again. Later they stopped picking up our call. I've never come across such things in Hyd till now but this was the first time. Beware of such companies because they have faulty links and reviews.
