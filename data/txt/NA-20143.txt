Today I got one of those spam calls that made me think that someone has ripped off a large chunk of data from Amazon.in recently.

So this spammer chap called me and said that he is giving me some free gifts considering my recent purchases at various online shopping portals. And in return, I have to give some positive feedback to his new website and take part in a contest. Says there are lots of people who are nominated for this contest and I'm just one of them.

I said no thanks and I'm already registered at payback.in.

Then the guy said that this is a different scheme and eerily recounted some of my last few purchases I'd done at Amazon.in.

I just said I'm not interested and disconnected. When I checked on Truecaller app, it was a Delhi number and about 2500 people had marked that guy's number as spam.

After the call, I started thinking how could he possibly know what I had purchased from Amazon.in. Another interesting thing is that he told that the said contest is being organized at a certain small village in South India and this is the exact village where I had ordered one of the purchases he mentioned. It means he not only has the purchase history of many Amazon clients, but also knows where the products were ordered.

The only logical explanation of this whole thing is that there could have been a big data leak at Amazon.in. Maybe this particular chap is a disgruntled employee who is now trying to use the stolen data to help his own new site?
