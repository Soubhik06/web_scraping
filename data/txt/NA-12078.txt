1.The present anticipatory bail application under Section 438 Cr.P.C. has been filed seeking anticipatory bail in case crime No.0091 of 2023 under sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 I.P.C., P.S. Kotwali Nagar, district Gonda. 2.Heard learned counsel for the applicant, Mr. V.K. Shahi, learned Addl. Advocate General, assisted by Mr. Alok Tiwari, learned Additional Government Advocate for the State and perused the record. 3.A perusal of the record makes it prima facie evident that this matter is part and parcel of a big scam, having far reaching consequences, as the involvement of employees of Sub Registrar's office, Gonda may also not be ruled out. The first information report has been lodged by an employee of Sub Registrar office, Sadar, Gonda. S.I.T. is conducting investigation. 4.As per instructions provided by learned counsel for the State, the applicant is not cooperating in the investigation. Categorical averment to this effect has been made in para 21 of the counter affidavit. There are approximately 60 first information reports lodged in identical matters by the State administration, Forgery in the records of the Registrar office has been made by several accused persons in active connivance with employees of Sub Registrar office, for which thorough investigation is going on and is being conducted by S.I.T. It is submitted that there cannot be a false implication.. 5.Keeping the above facts and circumstances of the case, as also the seriousness of the offence, coupled with the fact that the applicant is not cooperating in the investigation, I am not inclined to enlarge the applicant on anticipatory bail. The bail application is accordingly rejected. Liberty is given to the applicant to move regular bail application. Order Date :- 30.7.2024
