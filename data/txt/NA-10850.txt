Order No. 02/Dated: 12 June, 2024

 Heard learned counsel for the parties. The petitioner has been made an accused in connection with
Ahilyapur P.S. Case No. 47 of 2023 corresponding to Cyber Case No. 03
of 2024 registered for the offence under Sections 411 , 414 , 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 120B / 34 of the I.P.C. and Sections 66(B) , 66(C) , 66(D) , of the
Information Technology Act., pending in the Court of learned Special
Judge Cyber Crime-Cum-Additional Sessions Judge-II, Giridih. As per FIR, allegation is that on 18.12.2023, informant, Md.
Musha, A.S.I. received an information that in house of the Nageshwar
Mandal at Ghasco village of Ahilyapur Police Station area, some
miscreants had gathered to commit Cyber fraud by making serial call to
Bank/ATM customers without any fear and using social engineering
in the name of blocking and verifying ATM cards. Learned counsel for the petitioner has submitted that petitioner is
innocent and has committed no offence at all as alleged in the FIR.
Petitioner undertakes to cooperate in the trial of the case by remaining
physically present as and when required and shall not indulge in any
manner in tampering with the prosecution evidences or influencing the
witnesses of prosecution. Hence, the petitioner may be extended the
privilege of bail. Petitioner is in the Judicial Custody since 05.03.2024.
Co-accused with similar allegations have already been extended privilege
of bail by Co-ordinate Bench of this Court vide order dated 27.02.2024
passed in B.A. No. 10463 of 2023 and passed in B.A. No. 10356 of 2023.
Hence, the petitioner may be extended the privilege of bail. Page 1|2
 On the other hand, learned Addl.P.P appearing for the State has
opposed the prayer for bail of the petitioner. Hence, petitioner does not
deserve bail. Regard being had to the facts and circumstances of the case, nature
of allegation coupled with the materials available against the petitioner, I
am inclined to release the petitioner on regular bail. Accordingly, the
petitioner named above is directed to be released on bail on furnishing of
bail bond of Rs.25,000/- (Twenty five thousand) with two sureties of the
like amount each to the satisfaction of learned Special Judge Cyber
Crime-Cum-Additional Sessions Judge-II, Giridih in connection with
Ahilyapur P.S. Case No. 47 of 2023 subject to the conditions:- In case of violation of the aforesaid conditions, the bail of the
 petitioner shall be cancelled and shall be taken into custody by
 the learned Trial Court itself. (Pradeep Kumar Srivastava, J.)
