So the other morning our internet was no longer working and it appeared to be an issue with our netgear router saying it needed some kind of update and the link the router provided wasn’t working.

My wife googled netgear customer support which has a sponsored link for routerworld.com. Well the number went to some fraudulent call center in India. At the time I was out running an errand they played through the scenario as customer support then sent a (probably bogus) pay pal link wanting 250.00 for new firmware.

My wife said they even had her type in a link which led to her providing a security question and a password they provided this appears to be some kind of phishing attempt. 

They went as far as connecting a three way call with our local internet provider stating they did their job the internet provider terminated the call. 

To say the least I am very frustrated with this we disputed the charge and have to get a new account. We also have a new router and modem and I feel like now we’re in damage control mode. I reported the link as fraud to google but it still comes up.
