1. Heard learned Counsel for the petitioner and learned

 Additional Public Prosecutor for the State. 2. This application, for grant of anticipatory bail, arises out

 of Cyber Police Station Case No. 22 of 2025, dated

 14.02.2025, disclosing offences under Sections

 303(2)/318(2)/318(4)/319(2)/336(2)/338/340(2)/61(2) of

 the Bhartiya Nyaya Sanhita. 3. The prosecution case, as per the First Information Report,

 is that the police got information through Cyber Portal

 Pratibimb App that cyber fraud has been committed from

 the customer of mobile no. 8904439023 by the user of

 mobile no. 8293808217 in the name of providing loan in

 cheaper interest rate. The police registered the compliant

 under the acknowledgment no. 31602250018716 and
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.40132 of 2025(2) dt.09-07-2025 2/3 traced the location of the mobile number used for

 committing cyber fraud in village Kurma. Thereafter, the

 police party reached near the tower location of the mobile

 no. 8293808217 and saw that a person, after seeing the

 police party started fleeing away, but was apprehended,

 who disclosed his name as Aakash Kumar. The police

 recovered two mobile phones from his possession. It has

 further been alleged that during search another person,

 was apprehended who disclosed his name as Sonu Kumar

 from whose possession three mobile phones were

 recovered. Details of seized mobile viz company name

 and number of SIM and IEMI are mentioned in the

 seizure list. 4. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the

 petitioner is not named in the First Information Report

 and he is not having any criminal antecedent. He next

 submits that name of the petitioner has transpired on the

 basis of confessional statement by the arrested accused

 persons, namely, Aakash Kumar and Sonu Kumar. The

 petitioner is co-villager of accused Aakash Kumar and

 Sonu Kumar and due to previous enmity his name has

 been disclosed. He further submits that at the time of
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.40132 of 2025(2) dt.09-07-2025 3/3 arrest and recovery of mobile phones, name of the

 petitioner was not disclosed by the arrested accused

 persons. 5. Regards being had to the submissions made on behalf of

 the parties and taking into consideration the fact that

 arrested accused persons have disclosed the name of the

 petitioner, stating therein, that the petitioner supported in

 the commission of cyber fraud. It appears that all accused

 persons have formed a gang to defraud the innocent

 public, phishing/cyber fraud is rampant in the area, where

 the attackers try to trick individuals into providing user

 names, password and other details and are indulged in e-

 mail phishing, voice phishing, SMS phishing etc. The

 case is still under investigation and on the basis of

 disclosure of name of the petitioner, further investigation,

 including custodial interrogation, of the petitioner may be

 required. Hence, I am not inclined to grant the privilege

 of anticipatory bail to the petitioner. 6. This application is, accordingly, rejected. (Anil Kumar Sinha, J)
