Recently I received whatsapp message from scammer for part time job where they pay Rs 150 initially and then scam you later. I did mistake as I pretended to be jobless and less educated person and as a result I could not scam the scammer and i learnt few lessons. Following tips should be kept in mind while dealing with scammer so that you can scam them :-

1) Act self sufficient : Never show them you really need their sh*tty job. Always say you have fultime job as they need those people who can pay them back.
2) To the point answers : While talking to scammers always answer to the point so that you are a mystery for them
3) Never disclose your identity : For obvious reasons never disclose your real identity to the scammers.
4) Means of receiving payment: Scammer might ask you for your Google Pay number or bank account number, to be safe and risk free just share the scanner barcode of UPI and nothing else.
I hope many of you have already scammed the scammers, you can share your strategy or story to enlighten everyone.
