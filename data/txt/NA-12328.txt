Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and the

 learned A.P.P. for the State. Petitioner is languishing in judicial custody since

 28.09.2015 in connection with Pirbahore P.S. Case No. 80 of 2015

 registered for the offence punishable under Sections 461 and 379 of the Indian Penal Code and Section 66(C) (D) of the Indian

 Technology Act. The prosecution case, as lodged by the General

 Manager, Prizm Payment Services, Pvt. Limited, is that they were

 engaged in installation, maintenance and management of ATMs of

 many banks but it has been detected that by injecting a malware in

 the ATM, Rs.21,04,000/- has been taken away by some

 unidentified persons. It has been submitted by the learned counsel for the

 petitioner that he is innocent, not named in the FIR and charge- Patna High Court Cr.Misc. No.52833 of 2016 (2) dt.20-12-2016 2/3 sheet has already been submitted, hence, there is no chance of

 tampering with the prosecution evidence. It has further been

 submitted that his name surfaced on the confessional statement of

 co-accused Ritesh @ Ramit @ Amit @ Vikash Chaturvedi and co-

 accused Shambhu @ Karan Singh @ Shambhu Jha and his own

 confessional statement. It has further been submitted that on

 similar allegation other co-accused persons have since been

 granted the privilege of bail by a Coordinate Bench of this Court

 in Cr.Misc.No.13282 of 2016 on 07.04.2016 Cr. Misc. No. 14486

 of 2016 and Cr. Misc. No. 18068 of 2016 on 13.05.2016, Cr.

 Misc. No. 29093 of 2016 on 20.07.2016 and also by this Court in

 Cr.Misc.No.50985 of 2016 on 28.11.2016. However, learned A.P.P. for the State opposes the

 prayer for bail. Be that as it may, since charge-sheet has

 already been submitted and other accused on similar allegations

 have been granted bail, let the petitioner, above named, be

 enlarged on bail on furnishing bail bond of Rs. 10,000/- (Rs. ten

 thousand only) with two sureties of the like amount each to the

 satisfaction of the learned Chief Judicial Magistrate, Patna in

 connection with Pirbahore P.S.Case No.80 of 2015, subject to the

 condition that one of the bailors would be close relative and other

 bailor would be a person, who has sufficient immovable properties Patna High Court Cr.Misc. No.52833 of 2016 (2) dt.20-12-2016 3/3 within the jurisdiction of the concerned police station/Court and

 petitioner shall present himself on each and every date during trial

 and in case of default of two consecutive dates without assigning

 any reason will entail cancellation of his bail bonds. With these observations and directions, this

 application stands allowed. (Nilu Agrawal, J.)
