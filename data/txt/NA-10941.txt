This appeal filed by the Revenue is against the order of the

(hereinafter referred to as 'the Act') for AY 2012-13 dated 04.03.2025. 1.1 The Registry has informed that the appeal is barred by limitation
by 30 days. The Revenue has filed a petition for condonation of delay
explaining the reasons that the due date for filing the second appeal
was 02.06.2025; however, till 12.06.2025 the case records were not
readily available with the Department. On 25.06.2025, the certificate of
filing the appeal was received from the office of the Pr. CIT-2, Kolkata.
It was requested to condone the delay of 37 days in filing the appeal
before the Hon'ble ITAT. After perusing the same, we are satisfied that
the Revenue had a reasonable and sufficient cause and was prevented
