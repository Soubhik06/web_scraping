**Fake Marital Rape Cases:** Marital rape is not recognized as a crime in India, but it can be recognized as an act of cruelty and a valid ground for divorce. However, false allegations can still be made, leading to legal complications. 
 
**Fake Dowry Cases:** Even if no dowry is taken and an affidavit is made annually stating the same, a dowry case can still be filed anytime after the marriage. The man will definitely be arrested, requiring him to prove his innocence.

The man will definitely be arrested and it would require him to prove his innocence.
