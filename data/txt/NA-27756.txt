Friend just got a “promotional call” from Direct TV. They spoofed Direct TV’s number. They offered him a GREAT DEAL and upgrade. He is VERY savvy and thought this was a real promotional call up until they asked for payment for the first five months upfront in, yes, you guessed it... GIFT CARDS. The thing is... they already had his Direct TV account number and knew the amount he paid monthly. 

So apparently, this (supposedly, from the accents he heard) India based sweatshop has obtained data that they then used to call him and pose as Direct TV. The email address they had for him was one he rarely uses and Direct TV was one of the few times he did use it. This points to a Direct TV data breach. He has informed Direct TV of this and is awaiting response from appropriate people on Monday. 

And, by the way, at the end of the call when my friend realized it was a scam he called the agent a scamming goat-f**ker and he hung up. The agent CALLED HIM BACK and left a message saying he was going to have sex in the corner with his American wife. LOLOL
