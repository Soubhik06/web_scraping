Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and learned

 APP for the State. The petitioner has prayed for bail in a case registered

 for the offence punishable under sections 376 , 420 , 120(B) of

 the Indian Penal Code and section 67 of the I.T. Act. As per allegation in the FIR, petitioner has provided

 intoxicate laced breakfast to the informant and soon after she

 became unconscious and petitioner raped her made her video

 viral. It is further alleged that petitioner was in habit of

 blackmailing the informant and threatened her to make viral her

 video on social medial if she not pay him Rs. 30,000/-. It is submitted by learned counsel for the petitioner

 that petitioner has been falsely implicated in this case due to

 dirty village politics. He is innocent. Medical examination of the
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.66142 of 2022(3) dt.20-03-2023 2/2 informant was not done which raise suspicion of alleged

 occurrence. The real fact is that the informant took loan of Rs.

 30,000/- from the petitioner and when he asked her to return it,

 she implicated the petitioner in this false case only with a view

 to grab his loan amount. Petitioner is languishing in judicial

 custody since 14.07.2022. The application for bail is opposed by learned APP for

 the State. Having heard learned counsel for the parties and

 considering the facts and circumstances of the case, this court is

 inclined to enlarge the petitioner on bail. The above named

 petitioner is directed to be enlarged on bail on furnishing bail

 bond of Rs. 10,000/- (Rs. ten thousand only) with two sureties

 of the like amount each to the satisfaction of the learned Judicial

 Magistrate 1st Class, West Muzaffarpur in connection with Karja

 P.S. Case No. 81 of 2022. (Sunil Kumar Panwar, J)
