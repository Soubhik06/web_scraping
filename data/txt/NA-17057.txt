I am planning to move out and put a sale as on OLX.
 
A guy calls me, saying he owns a renting appliances shop and would buy almost everything (chaddi bhi? Idk) and offered me a price close to what I was asking.
 
All good and he asked me my gpay number, I asked why? That too before he has seen the products. Big No from me.
 
He pushed and said he wanted to transfer token amount, I wasn't interested still that guy sent me a QR scanning code, I sensed the scam and gold the guy to knock some other door.
 
Now the guy started abusing me and disconnected the call.
 
Guys beware, this is becoming normal now, people using cheap tricks to fool you.
