This was actually my first time buying from SD, I had read the 2-3 threads about Snapdeal here but I got carried away trying to save a few hundred. I was like "fuck it i'm sure they must've learned by now".

So I ordered a [Western Digital 1 TB Desktop SATA Hard Drive \(WD10EZEX\)](http://www.snapdeal.com/product/western-digital-1-tb-desktop/2079050609) last week from Snapdeal, as you can see the product ID, it's "WD10EZEX", which means it's the "Blue" version of the Western Digital drives, which is the 7200 rpm model.

Reference: 

* [WD's official site](http://www.wdc.com/global/products/specs/?driveID=1092&amp;language=1)
* 
* [Newegg.com](http://www.newegg.com/Product/Product.aspx?Item=N82E16822236339) 

I got the drive in 3 days, BUT instead of the WD10EZEX Blue version, I got the [WD Green version "WD10EZRX"](http://www.newegg.com/Product/Product.aspx?Item=N82E16822236070) which is the slower and cheaper one, with various other features which are not suitable for my needs. I don't know if this was an honest mistake or a blatant scam, but I'm guessing the latter because no where on the site they mention that the advertised drive (WD10EZEX) is the Blue variant. 

[* Proof](removed)

Called their Customer Care immediately, asked for an exchange, their reverse pickup guy comes home 2 days later, shows a big list of items that other customers have made a complaint about to refund/exchange (40+ requests) due to wrong product delivery. Continued talking to him he said "this is how snapdeal makes money, you pay, get the wrong product, then your money is utilized during the refund process, as a refund they offer you snapdeal money so you have to buy from their site no matter what", I said "well I'm done buying from snapdeal, flipkart never gave me any issues" to which he even personally recommended Flipkart (LOL), overall experience with SD = ABSOLUTELY TERRIBLE, 0/10 WOULD NOT RECOMMEND. 

* **Update: Got an email that they'll ship my replacement within 48 hours, if this one's also WD10EZRX I might kill myself.**
