First of all, I would apologise if my grammar is not right. I am still working on it.

I was scrolling through Instagram reels today and saw a lady who was in a pretty much village attire spent money to get a makeover and it was captioned "Scam ultra pro max". Why? I found this to be so disrespectful. This is 2022. We all know with a little makeover anyone can look good. Where's the scam? Even the kids (because mature adults wouldn't behave that way) in such reels were like "this is why I have trust issues" or "Antariksh ka ticket kaato" as if the lady ripped them off to get an appointment to salon.

It goes both ways, men getting a makeover isn't a scam either but I am tired of seeing such reels and getting second hand embarrassment from the comments on anyone's looks especially on women's looks these days. It really feels like someone who doesn't adhere to modern beauty standards doesn't deserve to even try getting a makeover. When are we going to get out of such mentality?

Men of India, a seemingly "ugly" woman getting a makeover is not a scam to anyone. Please stop. 

This is just a rant, please try to not be rude as I have tried to be as polite as possible.
