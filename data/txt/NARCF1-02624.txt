My uncle was tricked by a scam. His son studies at Medicaps University, which is 150 km away from our hometown. The scammer called my uncle, pretended to be a police officer, and said he had arrested his son. The scammer played a recording of someone crying and claimed it was his son. He demanded ₹80,000 from my uncle in exchange for his son's "release", saying his son was in trouble and needed the money to avoid legal consequences.

The scammer had obtained my uncle's phone number and his son's personal information, including his identity and phone number, likely through a data breach or illegal access to the college's records.

This raises serious concerns about the security of personal information and how scammers can exploit vulnerabilities to target innocent individuals.

My uncle was worried and believed the scammer, thinking his son was really in trouble. He didn't know what to do and was very stressed. The scammer's call was very convincing, and my uncle was terrified. He didn't have any other option, so he traveled 150 km to the university to check on his son, only to find out it was a scam and his son was safe all along.
