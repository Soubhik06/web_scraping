--- POST TEXT ---

I got my iPhone snatched by two people few days back in Delhi. Nobody helped afterwards not even the police. They just filed an FIR. Everybody said it would be sold into parts. I too tried to move on and forget about it. But day before yesterday, suddenly I got an SMS about it's location being tracked. Later I came to know it was a phishing link to remove my device from Lost Mode. 
I was way too quick and entered my credentials and even phone's passcode. I entered my passcode because my another Apple device asked it once when I got locked out of that device but both having linked to the same apple id. So to authenticate, it did ask to type your another device's passcode.
So that moment I didn't realise anything and thought maybe it is all genuine and asking for it similarly as it had asked earlier once.
I was in a panic and desperation to know the location, my mind kinda fucked up the logic thing. 

Now I know they would be able to access my data stored internally in the iPhone. I did change all passwords and all banking apps are safe as of now. Tbh, I am more concerned about my personal data and my family photos.
But do they really care about all those photos and other stuff on the phone or would they just reset it asap and resell?

PS- I have been feeling so paranoid since then and I am really regretting everything. 

How do I deal with all this. I was already having a tough time with life and this one just made things even worse for me. Can't stop crying these days. FML.
