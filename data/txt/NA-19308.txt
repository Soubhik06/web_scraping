I was dumb enough to trust this business with the inter-city transfer of my bike. 

They agree to transfer your bike for ₹2500-3500. 
Then they give the order to any local transporter and they give you the consignment bill. (but there wont be any cost written on it). They will pickup the bike/car.
After the goods go in transit they will share the actual bill, which will be 3-4 times the agreed amount.

The actual moving and packing is done by a different person and they're just a middle man. From what I have learnt, its run by two guys, Vipul and Vikas.

I lodged a complaint in consumer court, but dont think it will yield any results. I dont want other gullible people to fall for such scam.

Here are other people who have faced same situation

https://www.sitejabber.com/reviews/logisticguru.in

Edit: Upon further investigation we found its a complete fraud company. Its GST number is blocked, its PAN is also bogus.
