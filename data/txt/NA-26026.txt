I want to share my story/harsh reality of being scammed.

&amp;#x200B;

Around two months ago, I found myself in a situation that left me feeling like the world's biggest sucker. A guy approached me, claiming he'd lost his wallet and needed 1,000 rupees urgently to get to the airport. He assured me that he'd return the money through Google Pay as soon as he reached his destination. He was on a scooty, and his plea seemed genuine.

&amp;#x200B;

Without much thought, transferred him 1,000 rupees, genuinely believing I was helping someone in need. I wish I could say this story had a happy ending, but it doesn't.

&amp;#x200B;

Once he sped away, my attempts to reach him fell on deaf ears. He spun a web of excuses, and the promised money never arrived in my account. It took me three agonizing days to accept the bitter truth: I had been scammed. To add insult to injury, he had given me his phone number and PhonePe details, making it easier to trust him initially.

&amp;#x200B;

This experience has torn my heart in ways I can't fully express. It's profoundly disheartening to think that some people out there would exploit the kindness of others without a second thought.

&amp;#x200B;

So, I've made a difficult decision - I've decided not to extend my hand to help anyone again, no matter how desperate their situation may appear. This incident has left me feeling somewhat cold-hearted, but it's a defense against those who would abuse my trust.

&amp;#x200B;

What prompted me to share this story now, after two long months? Well, it's seeing more and more scam posts in this group that stirred me to action. I realized that my experience isn't an isolated incident, and by sharing it, I hope to raise awareness and caution everyone in our community.

&amp;#x200B;

In closing, I'm sharing this story not to seek sympathy but to urge everyone to be cautious and maintain a healthy dose of skepticism, even when you're inclined to help others. Trust is precious, and it's painful when it's betrayed.

&amp;#x200B;

Thanks for taking the time to read my heartfelt message. Let's look out for each other, and together, we can make our community a safer place.
