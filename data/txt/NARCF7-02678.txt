Sometimes, an incident shakes you up for a few days.

A few days ago, I ordered something from Zomato. Surprisingly, the app showed the order as "delivered" even though it was supposed to arrive in five minutes. I tried calling the delivery person twice, but he didn’t answer.

Frustrated, I contacted Zomato support and started chatting with them, ready to report it as fraud. Just then, I noticed a boy holding a parcel, talking to my neighbors. From a distance, I asked him, “aap Zomato se ho ?”

He looked at me but didn’t respond. I signaled for him to come closer. When he approached, he gestured towards his ear and showed me his phone, typing, "Is this your order?"

I was shocked—he couldn’t speak or hear. I felt so bad at that moment.

A few minutes earlier, I was about to report him for fraud. Imagine how unfair that would have been—I would have felt so guilty.

After the delivery, I gave him a tip, but the incident left me thinking: sometimes, reality is so different from what we assume.
