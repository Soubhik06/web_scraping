I have been looking for remote creative writing jobs &amp; I got a call from this one organization which claims to be associated/ affiliated with the government.They said they have various openings for creative writing post in the city..They mentioned they have full time as well part time openings. The lady on call proceeded to say I would have to give an online test by Saturday &amp; then mentioned i would have to pay Rs 1800 for the online test. She said they would provide me with study material for the same.Incase I don't clear the round they would offer me the full refund but this refund is only for women as it comes under the kaushalya scheme.I asked them to send me their website link &amp; they sent me one but I still have doubts. It just seems suspicious . I asked them to provide me with the job description &amp; they mentioned they would do so after I share my documents. The documents they needed are CV , adhaar card &amp; my highest qualification certificate. They said they got my number from timesjobs. The whole thing made me sceptical &amp; I can't believe we have reached a point where people are using job calls to scam people.Is there a chance this could be genuine ?
