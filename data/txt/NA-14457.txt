Nikhil Pahwa: Let’s talk about AI and cybersecurity . So cybersecurity is changing because of AI in two uncomfortable ways, right?

On the defensive side, it can surface vulnerabilities a lot more easily. Somewhere, someone was able to find 500 vulnerabilities in a product that hadn’t been found previously in a matter of minutes . But the same capability also makes offense cheaper and more scalable . Douglas Kramer: Yeah, it’s a fairly sobering thing. DDoS attacks, which is one of the core things we deal with this sort of attempt to assemble a lot of data from hijacked sources and throw it at web properties to shut them down. The number of those attacks has gone up 53% over the last year alone.

What we call monster attacks, which is one terabyte per second or more of data, the frequency of those has gone up 20 times over the last year .

And the one that I think we can all associate with, because we’re in that time of year of the Olympics and world records and all that, the largest DDoS attack on record that was mitigated was 31.4 terabytes per second of data . And at that scale, you’re not just taking down a website, you can take down entire networks, Internet networks . That we saw last October, that was seven times the largest that had ever been seen just 13 months before.

So the attacks are just frightening. The number of malware and phishing attacks we’re seeing are now about 94%, if you have hijacked credentials , about 94% of the attacks we’re seeing through those are coming through bots now, not human beings fashioning them.

And like you said, the deepfakes are getting better, the phishing is getting better, they can get past voice recognition now, they can do very personalised phishing campaigns. So all of that is terrifying.

The other parts of this that you sort of got to is one we call a little bit of the shadow AI problem, that even the most well-meaning employees, we have found that 91% of employees who say that they’re using AI to help them do work better , because their workplaces are telling them, be more productive, be better, use AI, 91% of them are using AI products that haven’t been screened or approved by their employers . And the last thing on your point is, unsophisticated people can now move faster as cyber attackers and hackers than groups of the most sophisticated people could do a year ago because they’re using and leveraging AI.

If you look at something like the Salesforce breach, I’m not sure if you’re familiar with that, from earlier this year, a plug-in to Salesforce was breached and a massive amount of data was taken. And just protections for the websites that use our services that help sort of register that the bot traffic that is coming in is validated and something you’re anticipating and expecting, making sure that you have insight into what is happening with the bot traffic and the AI assets that access your site.
