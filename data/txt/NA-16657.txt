The MHROD announced 6 universities as "Institutions of Eminence" that would get access to public funds for development.

3 of these are public, 3 private.

The public ones? IIT-B, IIT-D, and IISc

The private ones? Manipal, BITS...and Jio University

As you can see, one of these is not like the others.

Jio University doesn't exist...yet. Has no track record, no enrolled students, nothing. Just an idea on a piece of paper.

But it gets the same status as IITs.

How blatant is this corruption?

Even worse, look at the media coverage. The only outlets that have mentioned Jio University as one of these institutes are either online publications or Hindu.

The rest of the mainstream press - NDTV, ToI, Business Line - have run with the "IIT, IISc awarded institutions of excellence" angle and buried the Jio University mention deep in the copy.
