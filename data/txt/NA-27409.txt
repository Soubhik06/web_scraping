But as for what happened in text form...

KEEP IN MIND, I haven't slept yet and I was super tilted off of playing League 
So I'm sleep deprived, my mind isn't at its most attentive, and I am pretty vulnerable... 
I suddenly get a message from a friend telling me that they accidentally "reported me for duping items" 
And that I should contact their Steam Support friendo that this was a mistake. 
Spoiler alert, this isn't my friend, it was a scammer impersonating one of my friends.

So I added the "Steam Support Admin" they referred to and we chatted for a bit about this false report 
And they told me they sent me a verification code. HOWEVER... 
What they did was go on my account and asked to change the password, and what I gave them instead was the access code to my account. So they had full control over it.

I honestly didn't notice all these red flags until it was too late, and my steam account got hijacked because of it. 
So please, stay vigilant and don't give out PERSONAL INFORMATION.

So after all that has happened, the person asked for my discord, and if I was available for Voice Chat. 
I agree'd to this, and right at this moment, the Hamster Wheel in my Head started to roll. 
And I instantly knew this was a scam.

The other person on the other side of the call was from India, the discord Region was in Hong Kong... 
And they told me to turn on Screen Share. I flash some pornography for some lols. 
Then they sent me a link to [Tradeit.gg](https://Tradeit.gg) and told me to open it. Right then and there, I got off the call 
and called my Bank to change cards, and looked over my Paypal to see if they might make any transactions there.

I have sent a Steam Support ticket about this, and only found out later on that they have deleted my account. 
So while I have avoided getting scammed out of my wallet, I have lost my Steam account in the process.

I do hope that It'll be reinstated soon, but I feel that I should let people know that there are scammers out there on Steam willing to hijack your accounts through impersonating people on your friends list, and their jobs I'm assuming is to redirect you to another scammer that'll do this whole charade of spouting bullcrap and getting your Steam information. And afterwards, while I'm only assuming since I got out and doged before this point, hold your steam account hostage and have you purchase CSGO skins and crap for them.

Please don't fall for these traps as I have, please stay vigilant\~
