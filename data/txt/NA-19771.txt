[Great analysis done by The Hindu journalist](http://www.thehindu.com/data/the-many-shades-of-rape-cases-in-delhi/article6261042.ece) on all rape cases in Delhi disctrict courts in 2013. Article says that this level of research has never been done in india before. 

* Of the 460 cases that were fully argued before the courts, the largest category (189 cases) dealt with cases involving or allegedly involving consenting couples

* Another 109 of the 460 cases fully argued before the courts deal with “breach of promise to marry”

* The 162 remaining cases of the nearly 600 heard in 2013 dealt with rape as it is most commonly understood - with only 12 that involved strangers. Men preying on young children in slums was the most common type of offence
