**Background**

Met a girl on a matrimony app. We hit it off in chat, calls and video calls. She was in India for a few days before going back to her job abroad. We connected so well that we decided that we would meet in her hometown in India as she was flying out; so it didn't make sense for her to come to my hometown. I flew into her hometown to meet her. The date was perfect from the minute we met. We spent 10 hrs together that day and then I flew back home. She is now back abroad to her home/job there and we have continued to speak on chat/calls for the past 1 month. We both adore each other and are also physically/emotionally attracted to each other. So much that we couldn't stop our selves from holding hands throughout the time we were together in India. We also spoke about some important topics during our date and decided to continue speaking and opening up about our lives before committing to be in a serious relationship that leads to marriage. She adores me and I adore her. We both were actually wondering how we are still single despite being so understanding etc. 

**The issue :** 

We have decided to start exploring many of the marriage related important topics over video calls in the coming weeks. During our date, I told her that I don't see eye to eye with my parents and if we are to get married, we would have to stay separately. So if she is looking for a setup where we will stay with parents, that may not be feasible in my case. She now wants to understand more about my broken relationship with my parents. 

The issue here is that there's a part of my life I cannot share with anyone. My dad is toxic, selfish, manipulative, abusive and a psycho of sorts. He's beaten our mom 100s of times when we were growing up. He has a foul mouth even today. He looses his temper very quickly and starts hurling abuses on my sister and I; sometimes even throwing things or breaking things. In the past few years, we have stopped taking these abuses and abused him back/hit him back if he tries to do something like that with us. He loves blackmailing people to get what he wants (such as threatening to suicide if things don't go his way). Despite our father being like this, we have continued to stay with him for the sake of our mom; however, mom started becoming more and more like him over the years. She has a foul mouth too now. 

The other issue is that our parents treat us like their slaves. They create issues out of the tiniest things and call us bad kids. For example, they won't let us be out later than 12 am and will latch the door so that we can't enter home if we are late. If the cook accidently makes more rice, they get angry on us for not watching over the cook. They won't fix anything that breaks in the house. They won't ever keep the dustbin out and text us to put the dustbin out or create a big scene out of it if we don't. They won't ever get any supplies or groceries for the house and will get angry on us if something is out of stock. Now all this sounds very petty and it may not make sense to many why this is such a big issue. But being grown up adults, our parents are treating us like slaves and still think that they have a right to control our lives. 

**How do I tell her all this?** 

Before I say anything else, I want to point out that I am nothing like my father. I would never be like him. I am not my father..

With that in mind, I am wondering where to even begin from and how to even tell her about this. I am pretty sure no woman would want to be with a man who has such toxic parents. She comes from a loving family where her father and mother have treated her with much respect and vice-versa. 

But there's something really really amazing between us. For the first time, I've found someone who adores me as much as I adore them. And I know that hiding these details will only create issues later in life. 

Do I have to tell her everything in detail or can I just tell her that my father is a toxic, selfish, manipulative, short tempered man who I don't see eye to eye? Do I need to tell her the tiny details of why I think so with examples? 

I've gone on 100s of dates and only met a few women I've adored. And this is probably the first time a women has adored me equally where I would need to start opening up to her about my life.

How do I do this? I don't want to loose out on a life partner because of no fault of mine! I can move out of the house as the first step to make sure there are no more fights with my parents. But still, what has happened has happened. How do I tell her and what do I tell her?
