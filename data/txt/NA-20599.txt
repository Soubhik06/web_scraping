Just sharing for the interest of everyone...Varafi was one of the options as a travel operator under a Skyscanner result. Tried them, but they called 2 days before my departure to say the ticket is aborted. A refund would be initiated but it's been a month and each update only states that the refund has been initiated and would take 48 hours.
On more research, it seems several folks are facing the same issue so it's definitely a scam. Avoid and beaware!
