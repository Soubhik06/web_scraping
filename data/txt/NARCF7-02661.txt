A new type of scam is prevalent in our beloved city 
Bhopal. 
One of my NRI friend who recently came to Bhopal shared an unpleasant experience.
While in Bhopal, he browsed on an online friendship app and got a match soon.
The girl immediately started sending messages for a meet up to hang out. 
My friend after receiving repeated requests in the afternoon finally agreed to meet her at M.P Nagar Zone 2 in front of Rajhans restaurant at 6:45 p.m on Sunday 14th July 2024
My friend was taken back as she immediately asked him to go to one club nearby without wasting a minute.
She took him to a place called Drink and Dance (D&amp;D) Bar &amp; Kitchen (the pitchers club).
Without any inhibition or hesitation she pressed order for Jägermeister shots along with red bull energy drink.
She finished 5 shots in straight 20 minutes. At this point my friend got sceptical of the setup. 
Without even asking my friend, she pressed order for shisha and food. 
My friend while having a beer,sensed the conspiracy and freaked out as she told the staff to bring sparks emitting pyro torch for no good reason as the place was completly empty.
After spending around 35 minutes the girl stood up and told him that she has to leave as her mother is feeling sick at home and just left. 
My friend asked for the check out invoice and when he received the bill, it certified that he just got scammed.... 
The price for every single item was exorbitant and the place with no air-conditioning, full of mosquitoes and cockroaches were a total mismatch.
My friend sat there for a while laughing on himself and left with take away food packet.
Just avoid getting scammed at this place called Drink &amp; Dance at M.P.Nagar Zone 2 .
