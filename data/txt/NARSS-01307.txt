I purchased an item from Peter England showroom Kannur . 
The MRP given is Rs. 699. 

I paid to the machine qr, payment went to Madura Garments. The store keeper said the payment should be to his personal UPI qr and not the machine one saying he didn't started initiated and told I paid to wrong /default company qr which is managed by headquarters team. 
He told it will get refunded and asked me to pay to his personal account qr. I said ok and paid him the amount. 
But then when I asked him about the refund he was unsure. 
Upon multiple request he repaid the amount paid to personal account qr to mine.
And said now that you already done mistake let's do official transaction and ask headquarters for refund for first payment. 
Then he start a bill of Rs. 649 for the item i purchased and I paid and got the bill for same. 

I suspect fraud from the store keeper regarding this whole situation. For the first payment to the said default/wrong transaction and the next to his personal account he charged me 699. Upon realising the issue can be escalated from my side he did the original billing of 649. So the item actually cost Rs. 51 less than the initial asked amount which he would've expected to his personal account. 

So I understood this is a fraud that might've going on in the store for long. Just because I made a wrong transaction things unravelled.

TLDR; Made upi payment of 699 to company qr and told it was wrong qr and paid to store staff's personal UPI qr. When store staff realised things might get escalated he refunded the money which paid to personal one and asked to pay the bill which came to be 50 RS less than what was originally asked.
