Hey fellow Delhiites,

So, I think I got scammed today at the Durgabai Deshmukh South Campus Metro Station, and I wanted to share my experience to warn others.

A guy approached me, saying he lost his metro card and the staff was charging him ₹140 as a fine. Initially, I tried to refuse, but he seemed genuine, explaining he had his bike parked in Rajouri and was in great trouble. He even promised to return the money within 20 minutes and took my number (on paper, because apparently, he didn’t have a smartphone).

I felt bad for him and decided to help. Since I didn’t have exact change, I gave him ₹200 &amp; told him to return ₹60 but he said that he will return me the whole amount on upi but i refused. After a while, he went to the customer service desk to ask for change but couldn’t get any. Eventually, I took back my ₹200 and gave him ₹100 instead.

Fast forward an hour, I tried calling the number he gave me, but all I heard was, “Iss number par incoming call seva band hai.”

At this point, I’m pretty sure I got scammed. It’s disheartening because people like this ruin trust for those who genuinely need help.

Has anyone else had a similar experience? Is this a known scam tactic at metro stations?
