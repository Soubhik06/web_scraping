Someone approached me at Balewadi high street near basketball court asking me for my phone since he wanted to make a call saying his phone battery is down. Looking at him I didn't feel like giving out my phone since he and his friend were just sitting there watching me continuously while I was into my morning exercises. I have heard about scams where random people ask for phone, dial a number and all calls are forwarded to different numbers especially SMS. Or they call someone and give OTP etc. There have been numerous posts about different scams especially near the high street. With number of scams and different ways of scams increasing, a person with real need of help gets ignored because the same pattern of scam happens. I have heard of stories from people that someone asked for phone to make a call and there entire bank account got empty by EOD.
