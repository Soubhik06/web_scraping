 proceeding against the accused. The materials

 personal e-mail ID of either Jeyapragash (A1) or
 Dr.Balaji (A2). 28.The police investigation shows that the said
 e-mail ID was created with the Mobile
 No:7299008833 of Jeyapragash (A1). Jeyapragash
 (A1) has no animosity towards either Dr.Gunaseelan

 mail ID and sending the impugned e-mail to all the

 e-mail ID. The impugned e-mail ID [email protected] , proprio vigore, would make
 one think that it is an official e-mail ID. This is one
 form of “spoofing”. This is far more dangerous than
 printing and circulating scandalizing materials. By
 using the medium of e-mail, thousands of persons can
 be reached in one stroke all over the world and the 14/18 http://www.judis.nic.in Crl.RC.No.1368 of 2018 reputation of a person can be marred. This Court
