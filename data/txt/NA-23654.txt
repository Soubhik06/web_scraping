I am no stranger to pitches for e-Biz and QNet. So I know the whole, "Do you want to do something big in your life?" and the hush-hush about company details before the face-to-face "interview" where you are forced to make a decision without being able to talk to anyone or doing basic research about the company.

Well, I came across a similar call yesterday. Of course, it started with an old acquaintance getting back in touch and acting all pally for no apparent reason. All the calls were about how I am smart enough to do something of my own and "motivation" for the same. 

On Saturday, the "pitch" happened - we're expanding our business in Bangalore and are looking for like-minded professionals. My "senior business partner" will get in touch with you tomorrow and will have a phone interview and what not. When I asked him the name of the company - he didn't tell me. When I asked them what they do:

"We use the internet as a platform and try and find new markets for companies. We then leverage these new markets and drive sales for brands and that's how we make our money. Samsung, Panasonic, Apple, etc. are our clients."

Sounds like a truckload of BS to me.

So why am I here?

If any of you know about this scheme (scam, rather), please leave a comment with the details. I tried finding out the company name using their elevator pitch but was unsuccessful. If someone can tell me the name of the company, I can do my own searching and expose this fraud to more people. Because I am sure there are a lot of gullible people; the likes of which fell for the quick money scams like QNet, e-Biz and other Ponzi\\MLM\\Pyramid Schemes.
