2. Soodhini Srujan Reddy(brother-in-law)

Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation (AMRUT) is a development mission launched by Prime Minister of India Narendra Modi in June 2015 which establishes infrastructure for water supply and sewerage in urban areas.

The department of Municipal Administration amd Urban Development in Telangana has not been alloted to anyone and is still under Chief Minister Revanth Reddy. 

In February 2014, tenders were called for various water supply works under the AMRUT scheme. Revanth Reddy's brother-in-law Soodhini Srujan Reddy owns a company called Shodha Infrastructure limited. This company has no prior experience or knowledge with such projects and also did not have enough funds to participate in the bidding process. 
Since the company is ineligible to participate in the bidding process, a public listed company called Indian Hume Pipe limited was made to participate in the bidding and was awarded the contract. Indian Hume Pipe Ltd., was forced into a joint venture with Shodha Infrastructure for this project, with Indian Hume Pipe assigned only 20% of the project work, while Shodha Infrastructure was handed 80%.
