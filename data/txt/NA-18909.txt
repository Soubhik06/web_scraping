So, our PM recently launched a very ambitious jan dhan yojana
and he is promoting swacch bharat abhiyaan. I wont comment on his actions, but his agenda is clear - for a better India. So I have some suggestions. What are your views on it?

the PMJDY. Its a great initiative to educate masses and curb saved money from going into unsafe ponzi schemes or conversion to bullion. However, most people do not know how to properly operate a bank account and the folks at the bank are quite busy. You will be shocked to know that most "literate" people in the cities are also not aware, and I personally learnt it from my dad. but not many people have dads or family who know about operating bank accounts. So I thought to myself, why not have a chapter on "**LIFE SKILLS**" . Modi Ji has himself been emphasizing about skill development, am I right? life skills are very , *very* important.

In most CBSE books you have value education and I think paper also carries a question on moral ethics and the like.

So, what I would do, if i were the HRD minister:
A whole unit on 'Life Skills'
sub parts :

* Gender Sensitvity(to include racial sensitivity as well)

* Adult responsibilities(to vote, to help nation etc)

* General Procedures(how voting is done, How bank account can be opened, how to get name in electoral rolls, correct/edit it, about aadhar)

* Economic practices - this is a big part of what should contain - proper manual on operating bank accounts, types of accounts which exist across banks, public and private sector banks differences, **how to calculate and pay taxes**, Job ethics, etc.

* Sanitation practices - since clean india is such a big issue, if people are educated about proper sanitation practices then clean india would benefit(this section *ideally* should cover sex education too)

these steps would go a long way for a better india. I will try to x-post this mygov.in, but I want your views on if you think im correct or not. please dont bring in politics here. my one point agenda is for a better india and nothing else , fellow randians.
you educate one child, an entire family gets educated. I still dont know what tax and pan card is. I'll have to ask my dad. If a chapter would be there....atleast someone would read it! :)
