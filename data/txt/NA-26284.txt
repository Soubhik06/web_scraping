Saw a post yesterday on this subreddit regarding bankers begging for us to take policies. Just when I thought it couldn't get any worse, got this in my LinkedIn the same afternoon.

Am so frustrated with this am gonna help u guys get rid of em as well. India has a National Do Not Call Registry. Enroll your number and after certain amount of days if you still get marketing calls, you can legally take action against them.

This also helps identifying spam calls cause legitimate vendors are not allowed to call you and anyone else who does is most likely a scam.

Another tip, don't trust the words of agents for credit cards or loans saying zero service charge etc.. They will just charge you and refuse they ever said anything. Get anything and everything as a mail from the official mail ID and save it so you can threaten them with legal action.

Wish government also has a National registry which we can use to opt out of our data collected from supermarkets and pharmacies being sold to anyone.
