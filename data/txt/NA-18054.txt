Hey everyone,

I've got a heads-up for everyone – watch out for a bogus website posing as BookMyShow.

I was looking for IPL tickets online, and I found a website that looked just like the real BookMyShow. But here's the twist – the URL and domain was slightly off. Instead of ".com," it was ".live." and instead of "[bookmyshow.com](https://bookmyshow.com)." it was "[bookmyshows.live](https://bookmyshows.live)." **(with an extra S)**

At first, I didn't think much of it and started booking. Later I noticed a bunch of spelling errors, like **"summery"** instead of **"summary."**

What really tipped me off, though, was when I saw that while the legit BookMyShow site showed all IPL tickets as sold out, this fake one had plenty up for sale. 

To add to my suspicion, when I attempted to navigate away from the IPL section to explore other events like movies, none of the links were clickable. The entire page was static, with only the IPL section being dynamic and responsive. 

Luckily, I caught on before it was too late and backed out of the transaction ASAP.

I'm attaching screenshots of both the real and fake websites for reference. Stay sharp, stay safe!
