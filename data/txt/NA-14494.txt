Sensitive data of 1.2 million cardholders leaked on a Russian-speaking dark web forum called BidenCash, reported CloudSEK. The breach was discovered by CloudSEK’s “contextual AI digital risk platform XVigil ” on October 6, 2022. “Approximately 508000 debit cards were breached with 414000 records of Visa payment network. This was followed by Mastercard”, said CloudSEK’s website. It added that the top three affected banks were American Express US Consumer, Finserv Solutions LLC and State Bank of India.

CloudSEK shared a screenshot of the advertisement for sale put up by the hacker.

Not the first data breach on BidenCash

On June 15, 2022, CloudSEK identified a database of 7.9 million cards and associated personally identifiable details being advertised on BidenCash for free. Adding to this the recent leak of 1.2 million cardholders takes the total number of users affected by BidenCash to 9.1 million.

“Marketplaces like BidenCash emerge frequently where the threat actors trade-sensitive card data for carding and cloning services. While the modern-day security mechanisms are able to minimise the impact, threat actors regularly check to deploy new methods to bypass them,” said Rishika Desai, Cyber Threat Researcher- CloudSEK according to Business Insider.

Impact of the leak

The leaked personally identifiable information could enable “threat actors to orchestrate social engineering schemes, phishing attacks, and even identify theft”, CloudSEK’s website said. It added that “Card details can be used for carrying out attacks such as card trafficking, card cloning, and unauthenticated transactions to facilitate illegal purchases”.
