In any business, especially those involved in multi-level marketing like QNET, a robust and transparent Know Your Customer (KYC) process is essential. A proper KYC process ensures transparency, security, and trust between the company and its customers. Here’s a comprehensive look at what an ideal end-to-end KYC process should involve:

**End-to-End KYC Process:**

**Start**
 │ 
 ▼ 
**Customer Onboarding Initiation**
 - Customer submits application 
 - Provides identity and address documents
 ▼ 
**Document Collection &amp; Verification**
 - Identity proof (Passport, Aadhaar, PAN) 
 - Address proof (Utility Bill, Aadhaar) 
 - Business documents (for corporate accounts) 
 ▼ 
**Identity Verification**
 - Automated checks (OCR, AI-based ID verification) 
 - Manual review (if required) 
 - Face match (if applicable) 
 ▼ 
**Screening &amp; Due Diligence**
 - Sanctions list screening (OFAC, UN, EU) 
 - PEP (Politically Exposed Persons) check 
 - Adverse media screening 
 - Risk assessment (Low, Medium, High) 
 ▼ 
**Risk-Based Decisioning**
 - Low-risk → Auto-approval 
 - Medium-risk → Enhanced review 
 - High-risk → Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) 
 ▼ 
**Customer Approval &amp; Account Activation**
 - Approved → Customer onboarded 
 - Rejected → Customer informed with reasons 
 ▼ 
**Ongoing Monitoring &amp; Periodic Review**
 - Transaction monitoring 
 - Periodic KYC updates 
 - Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) if needed
 ▼ 
**End**

---

**Why is KYC important?**
A proper KYC process helps prevent fraud, ensures compliance with regulatory requirements, and builds trust with customers. It also helps companies identify and mitigate potential risks, ensuring a secure and transparent business environment.
