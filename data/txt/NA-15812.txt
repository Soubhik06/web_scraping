My colleague's bro got scammed in 3 days.

Modus of Operandi:

Links and posts in Facebook job groups where applicants frequent.

These posts lure gullible job seekers and get in contact with WhatsApp or FB chat.
All payments via Gpay, phonepe, 

Hook:
Saying that they partner with Amazon and other e-commerce companies they make the job seekers install app via APKs. 
Trust building by making the person talk to "Manager"

Line: 
Once installed they offer some tasks to do after making the person recharge wallet, wallet would show the recharged value. 
This can be used to do some BS tasks in app.
Once done, end of day they show some profit and allow withdrawal. (1000Rs-3000Rs)
Next day, another withdrawal allowed for tiny amount. 

Sink:
Once trust is gained, they start to coerce and persuade to add huge amounts, back to back, (in this case bro added 3.4 Lakh in one day). End of day, showing 1lkh profit in app, withdrawal is blocked and asked to add 80k more for fund release. 

Poor guy, is now jobless and broke too. 
Now they are trying to take help of cybercrime by registering a complaint

Similar one with replica operation has some complaint and comments filled with people who lost money.
