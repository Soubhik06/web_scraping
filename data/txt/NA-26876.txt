I run a small business where I own and manage a few rental properties. One of my tenants started receiving random packages of clothing items in November and December. They did not know what to make of it until a neighbor notified them that the same thing was happening to him. He had looked up the name on the package and found that his address and my rental property were both listed as the business and warehouse address of multiple fraudulent websites. The websites sell cheap sweaters and based on the reviews- usually just take people's money and never send the merchandise or if they do send it, it's not what was ordered or it's in terrible condition. Consumers are now trying to "return" these cheap, lousy items to my property. Most packages are coming by mail but some people are coming there in-person. My tenants are wonderful people and excellent tenants. However, they are now afraid of scammed customers showing up at their door (this is a single family home). It's likely that they will not renew their lease because of these scam websites committing address fraud (which is illegal) and I don't blame them. I've reported the websites to local police, FBI cybercrimes, Cloudflare (their web host), Namecheap- they have hidden their identity, the FTC, US Postal Service, the Attorney General of my state, Paypal, ApplePay, GPay and Stripe. I've left messages on a voicemail number listed on the sites- telling them to remove my address from their webpages but nothing has happened. Most of the places I've reported them to don't seem to care at all and appear to be doing nothing about it. 

Is there any other way to get their websites taken down? Is it worth hiring a lawyer? Would a company like Brandshield or Redpoints help? 

Thanks!
