Just a month ago one foreigner couple (Mr &amp; Mrs A) arrived at Delhi International Airport (first visit to India). (Mr &amp; Mrs A) landed at midnight as many international flights arrive at night time. Mr A decided to take airport prepaid taxi. After 20 mins drive some body called the driver and after the call taxi driver told Mr A that there is communal riots in Delhi between hindu and muslim and you can't go to Paharganj area (where Mr A has already booking). Now the problem is normally many people don't activate Internet / Roaming plan while traveling to other country. Mr A was not able to check news on internet or can call someone and reconfirm. Driver said that i will call hotel from my phone and you can talk to them. Driver asked hotel name and number. He dialed number himself and after some talk gave it to Mr A. The other guy on phone pretended that he is from hotel (Mr A made mistake in tension and forgot to check the number which driver dialed). When asked about riots the other guy said yes there is riots in delhi and hotel is not safe. You can go to some other safe area. After the when Mr A asked about safe hotels driver said that he will take them to Govt Tourist Office. Mr A was confused because he thought Govt office will be not open at midnight however being first time in india he thought may be its normal in india. The driver took him to shop where on the board was written "Tourist Information Center". Now the guy in that office told Mr A that driver and hotel guy is right there is riots and your hotel is not safe. He said i will try to find hotel for you. He dialed some number and after 3 to 4 calls he said only one hotel he knows which is available and due to riots some hotels are full and other are not giving room because they are afraid of strange people. He said the hotel which is available it is asking 100 USD per night. Now being first time in india Mr A don't know what to do. He gave 100 USD and asked what he can do now as he came to visit. The tourist information center guy said that where you want to visit. Mr A said i am going to agra after delhi. They told him that agra is safe and they can send him to agra next day morning by car safely. It will cost 250 USD more and they will safely drop him to agra. In short Mr A paid 350 apart from prepaid taxi charges in which they took them to cheap hotel near by (max 10 USD per night type) and next day morning drop the couple at Agra in small car (max 80 USD). However he made some mistakes but many of them had no idea what to do in different country at the middle of night when your phone is also not working. 

This is not the first case there are hundreds of cased with the same story. Normally many foreigner avoid making complain once they realized that they are cheated to avoid wasting time which inspires this kind people to cheat more. Even if they want to make complain normally they don't have taxi number and also they forget direction to that so called tourist information centre because they visited that in night. Next time when you travel near railway station or karol baug pahar ganj where most hotels are you will find many shop with no name and just tourist information center and even sometime tourism ministry logo. May be in some cases if the complain is made this cheaters will pay money to police and avoid legal actions and start again. 

If your foreigners friends are coming make sure that they don't get cheated. 

If you have any ideas how to stop this you can share.
