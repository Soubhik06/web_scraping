Some of you must be already aware of this scam or even effected by it over recent few years,, but if anyone is still not aware then this post is for you.

When you try filling air in your vehicle tires in a fuel station, and you were told one of the tires has a puncture, then it's most likely a scam. Especially if you have a car and the guy filling air is doing his duty when you are sitting inside the car, and you cannot see what he is doing clearly. These guys across many fuel stations have been poking the tires with sharp objects and go on telling that the tire has a puncture and needs to be fixed. They'll guide you to a puncture shop immediately next to the air pump(both these guys are together in this scam). They'll bring out the puncture kit and use puncture plugs to fix the hole they themselves made. They will charge anywhere between 200-300 minimum. If you are found gullible they'll cook some better story with a bigger problem for the tire and they'll rob you off more money, and even your time.

Better to be cautious with Air filling pumps that have a puncture guy next to it. Or when the guy who fills air himself keeps a puncture kit with him, it's better to be avoid these guys. Or if you have to get the job done with them due to some reason, it is better to come out of the vehicle and keep checking what he is doing with the tires while filling air. Generally they poke the wheel with a sharp object hidden under a Muslin cloth
etc. Sometimes without even poking your tire, they might say that the tire has a puncture and the tube needs to be opened and checked for, run away from them. You can get the same checked in a shop you trust near your home etc.
