Hey everyone, I’m sharing this because I don’t want anyone else to go through the mental and emotional stress I faced. Please take this seriously if you’re planning to transport your car or belongings.

I trusted a local transport company ( [**Ramakrishna Logistics**](https://www.ramakrishnalogistics.com/)**.** ) to move my car from Andhra. They initially quoted say ₹14,000 and gave a bill assuring me this was the “final charge.” But once the car was picked up, the nightmare began. They suddenly demanded ₹28,000 and outright refused to deliver unless I paid.

I was already under a lot of personal stress, and this added a level of frustration I wouldn’t wish on anyone. I spent two days constantly arguing, calling, and feeling helpless. They finally agreed to ₹14,000 after someone else complained to the police, which froze their account. I thought it was over—but they delayed delivery by 5 days and again demanded extra charges at the last minute. By then, I was so drained, I just paid to get it over with.

When the car finally arrived, it was a disaster. The side was damaged, there was a crack, and small accessories were stolen. The money isn’t the main issue—it’s the sheer helplessness of being extorted while they had something valuable of mine. And imagine this happening to someone relocating to Bangalore with their entire household goods, worth ₹1 lakh or more!

I just didn’t have the bandwidth to take the headache of involving the police or a lawyer at that point. It felt like these frauds prey on people’s stress and desperation.

These frauds operate under multiple company names and I know for a fact they have done it to other [people here](https://consumercomplaintscourt.com/tag/ramakrishna-logistics-company/) , based on a consumer forum report. They are based out of **Nelamangla** **(13.06441° N, 77.46823° E)**: 
If you’re planning to transport a vehicle or goods, take precautions:

* Always demand a *clear, written final bill* (not WhatsApp) before handing over your vehicle or belongings. Beware of fine prints and never give them benefit of doubt.
* Hiring a driver even for the long drive or choosing a reputed transporter will save you so much stress and frustration.

This was one of the most mentally draining experiences of my life. Please stay cautious, and don’t let scammers ruin your peace.
