AAP has gone a long way from their activist days. And not in a good way.

Every day brings new revelations about how 'normal' their 'clean' politics really is. Dictatorship, corruption, fraud, alliances with corrupt parties, vandalism, extortion, rape etc. etc.

And yet AAP's 'clean' image sticks, at least as far as Delhi and their online fanbase is concerned.

**But what exactly has Kejriwal done to deserve this kind of blind devotion?**

He got elected with the greatest mandate in history. He faces zero opposition in the Delhi assembly. He made big promises of cleaning up and transforming everything and even gave deadlines in __ days or months.

Now 6 months later there is absolute zero progress on

**-Jan Lokpal Bill**

**-Swaraj Bill**

**-Investigating past scams and putting Sheila Dikshit, Kalmadi etc. in jail**

**-Installing 15 lakh CCTVs and improving women's safety**

**-Fixing Delhi's water and power issues**

**-Creating 5 lakh jobs**

**-Regularizing all unauthorized colonies**

And so on.

In fact 90% of AAP's election promises, have been forgotten completely by them, their supporters, media and opposition and their narrative these days is mainly about attacking BJP and Modi.

Why is nobody asking about these rather loud, conspicuous promises? **As far as the above points are concerned Kejriwal and his spokespeople can't even use the *'Modi govt is interfering!'* excuse. They've got a 97% majority! Heck, if Kejriwal wanted he could get the Jan Lokpal Bill tabled and passed tomorrow itself!**
