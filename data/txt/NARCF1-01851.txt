One of the 'die-hard' Dhoni fans here. I've never been to a cricket stadium before (affordability reasons and other thing being not so obsessed in going to stadiums) and this year, I wanted to watch a CSK match live at Chepauk since it is going to be the last season for MS as a player. *'Thala' velayadradha oru vatti achu nerla paakanum nu oru chinna aasai*. Was so excited for this and I planned to book tickets. There were two options - go to the counter/ book online. First option was not possible since it is a working day today, so I decided to book online - from PayTM insider/ CSK website. I even decided the stands to book lol (any one of I,J,K with ticket price 2000+taxes)

The tickets were to be sold from 9.30 AM and I was ready refreshing tabs from 9 AM. 9.30 arrived and still no signs of opening of booking. I thought it would get resolved in few minutes due to server load. Since 16000 tickets were to be sold, I was pretty optimistic in getting one since I have a good record in booking tatkal tickets lol. News channels started showing that counter ticket sales had just begun. I was still refreshing tabs and Twitter feed to get updates on online booking opening. Around 9.45, link to book showed up on PayTM insider and to my surprise, only I, D, E stands were shown. Opening I stand, I was shown the real horror thing. 99% of the seats were 'shown' as booked and there were single seats here and there that were not booked. Tried booking those single seats and it showed that those were also booked?! These free seats were changing positions randomly and I'm damn sure that 16000 seats didn't get booked in that small span of time. Tried in D,E stands (3000+taxes) and faced the same issue. Thought it was not my day and closed the browser.

Now comes the frustrating and annoying part. CSK/Sports influencers/trackers started posting that tickets got sold out online within minutes because of the power of fandom. this/that/blabla but that was not the case. Sad to see people standing in queues from midnight to get counter tickets - how many of those who get these tickets REALLY go to the match? I'm sure lots of these people sell these tickets in black. CSK has somehow managed to create this fake hype by restricting the supply - first by reducing no. of tickets sold to fans (only 16,000 out of some 38000 seats are reserved to fans, others are for corporates it seems. Such a shame. I know that there are some reserved tickets for sponsors/corporates but more than 50%? Pathetic!). And secondly, by not allowing to book online. This ticket mafia is so big - Just search 'CSK ticket' in Twitter - you'll get tons of results of 'Got X tickets for CSK-LSG. DM for price'. Selling for several times the original price. Just imagine if they had opened tickets for all the 7 home games. Many would have got tickets for a game at least and this counter ticket queue would also have drastically reduced. Also, ticket prices in Chepauk are one of the highest in the nation (750/1000 vs 1500 here). Don't know who is to be blamed - the franchise or TNCA. 

Overall, I'm so disappointed with this behavior of CSK towards fans. Also heard that this is not the first time it is happening and it happened in the previous seasons of IPL as well. News channels are not reporting this scam either. *Just 16000/38000 lam romba aniyayam da. I like Dhoni so much but black la ticket vangi pora alvuku worth ah nu therila tbh*. Hope CSK takes cognizance of this and online ticket sales happens in a well-organized manner for future matches and I manage to book one soon.
