Hey everyone, just a heads-up about a fake Amazon warehouse job scam happening in Bangalore. A company called Trico Solution is claiming to offer jobs at Amazon but is asking for ₹3,900 for a uniform. They are so focused on getting atleast half of money.

Amazon never ever charges money for uniforms, applications, or any hiring process. These scammers are just trying to steal your money. If you're looking for a real job at Amazon, apply only through their official website or trusted job portals.

If anyone else has been contacted by Trico Solution or similar scams, please share your experience to warn others. Stay safe and don't fall for these fraudsters!
