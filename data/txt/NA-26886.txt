Posting on behalf of a friend.

It started when he got a call from someone claiming to be from FedEx. The caller told him that customs had seized a package headed to Bangkok from Mumbai, and that it contained **six passports** and a few thousand dollars. My friend was totally confused because, obviously, he hadn’t sent any package anywhere. But the scammer kept pushing, saying that his **Aadhaar number** had been used for this illegal shipment. They even threatened him with legal consequences if he didn’t cooperate.

The scammer then told him they were going to transfer the call to the **cyber police** to “verify” the situation. At this point, my friend was probably a little freaked out and in no condition to think clearly. The person on the other end of the call, pretending to be the cyber police, told him his **bank accounts had been hacked** and that there was a risk of more fraud happening if he didn’t act fast. The “officer” said he needed to transfer money to an “alternate” account, which they claimed belonged to the **Reserve Bank of India** to protect his funds.

Naturally, my friend was super anxious at this point, and in his panic, he made the mistake of trusting these scammers. He transferred **₹2 lakh** initially, thinking that was the right thing to do. Then, under more pressure from the scammer, he transferred another **₹3 lakh**. The scammer then said to my friend he has been a good obedient boy and can relax now. Post that, everything went silent. The scammer stopped responding. Once he realised he has been scammed, he contacted the “real” cyber police and registered a complaint. My friend is desperate and willing to pay a fee to get his funds back if possible,

I want to know what are his chances of getting his money back? Do banks insure customers against scams like this one? What legal options does my friend have to get his money back?
