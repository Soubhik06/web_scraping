This is regarding an online scam while applying for a personal loan

Was helping a friend of mine last week apply for a personal loan and we got to a page online called “ kreditSathi”. 

He entered all his details and within minutes got a call from one of their customer care representatives. That person confirmed all his details and told him that a loan of 50k is sanctioned but he has to pay a processing fee of Rs1950 to a bank account provided on his WhatsApp.

Once that was done, he got a call within 10 mins and he was told to pay another amount of Rs 3950 as processing fee. Here, I got suspicious and told him not to pay further. We did a simple Google check on said company and found out that multiple people have already filed a cyber complaint against them for taking money and not giving any loan. 

Further, I found out on the official RBI website that this fraud company wasn’t affiliated with them. Thankfully my friend only lost 1950. We went on YouTube and saw more videos regarding these fraudsters and how people have been duped big time. Some have even lost lakhs of rupees believing these scamsters. 

The only saving grace here is that my friend lost a minimum amount. The kreditsathi page is still present if one googles it. Not sure why the cyber crime have not closed this operation down yet.
