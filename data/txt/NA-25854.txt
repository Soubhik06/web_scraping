Remember the video someone had shared here earlier saying that 'it's a video of voting malpractice being done by an MIM worker in Bahadurpura'? 

Turns out it was a fake video of West Bengal being attributed to Hyderabad. Also, no surprises to see that a BJP corporator was arrested for circulating this fake video. 

I hope this is a reminder even for people on this sub to not indulge in fake news propagation and to verify and validate news before you share it across on social media.
