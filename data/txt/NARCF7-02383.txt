How They Operate:

Before the Meeting:
1) They will insist on meeting at a specific place. 
2) They will arrive late to create a sense of urgency. 
3) They will avoid talking on the phone. 
4) They won’t share their Instagram or social media handles. 
5) They will only have a few photos, all from Tinder. 

During the Meeting:

1) They will frequently go to the washroom. 
2) They will insist on drinking and smoking. 
3) They won’t check the menu but will order directly. 
4) When the bill arrives, they will claim they don’t have money and promise to send it later. 
5) They will try calling friends to ask for money. 

 Club Staff Involvement:
1) They will say, “If you can’t afford such places, why do you come here?” 
2) They will insist that you have to pay the bill, no matter what. 
3) Bouncers will constantly hover around you. 
4) Once you pay, they will ask you to leave. 
5) They will protect the girl by escorting her out of club.

Before the Meeting:

1) Suggest meeting at a neutral, well-known location.
2) Engage in phone conversations to verify authenticity.
3) Research the chosen venue beforehand.

During the Meeting:

1) Be cautious of individuals avoiding direct communication.
2) Monitor the items being ordered and their prices.
3)If uncomfortable, consider leaving the venue promptly.

It's disheartening to encounter such deceitful practices. I hope my experience serves as a cautionary tale for others. Always remain vigilant and trust your instincts.

Stay safe, everyone.
