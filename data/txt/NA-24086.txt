Some time back in 2018, there used to a be a cooperative society called Kanva Souhardha. Apparently, they issued a bond **"without any terms and conditions"**, accepted the money and provided a receipt, with a promise of 12% interest per annum for a bond period of 1 year, which optionally can be renewed. Kanva is a well-known group with resorts, hospitals schools, textile businesses etc accross Bangalore. So basically, people assumed this cooperative society is well secured and cannot go default.

While I was not in Bangalore during this period, my mom went ahead and paid a part of her fortune to these frauds and collected the receipt of payment. She started receiving the interest money every month and was also attending the meetings, where in Kanva members presented a rosy fake picture of how beautifully they are investing the money and generating good returns and company was generating good interest despite paying 12% interest to its depositors.

At the end of 1 year my mom decided not to renew her Bond and asked for bond premium. Kanva group acknowledged the same and provided an acknowledgement that her bond premium will be transferred back to her account.

**This is where Shit begins.**

The great Karnataka Government then blocked all the Kanva group's bank accounts on account of financial irregularities. The CEO of the Kanva group Nanjundappa was also put behind bars for fraud.

The shit didn't stop here. Since all depositors were protesting for their monies, Kanva group decided to provide land worth equal to that bond premium and settle the issue. In that process they presented my mom with a site and provided the originals of that site. We verified the papers, and everything was fine. So decided to go ahead.

But here is second problem. The land worth was slightly higher than settlement amount and my mom decided to pay the balance in cheque. Cheque amount was debited from her account, and while this process was settling smoothly, the great Karnataka Govt decided to freeze this operation of land transfer as well.

So, in summary my mom paid, X amount for bond premium then y amount for land value and ultimately ended up with neither the bond premium nor interest on (X + Y) since 2019. Both X &amp; Y were big amounts.

Unfortunately, my mom passed away in 2022.

Now we are simply clueless on how to take this case forward and whom to approach. Any suggestions on this would be welcome and looking forward to responses from this vibrant community.
