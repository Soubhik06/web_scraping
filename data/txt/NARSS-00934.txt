Just got scammed near Delhi Airport Metro station (IGI T3) by a guy pretending to be in distress. He claimed his luggage and money were stolen and he needed help to get home. To gain trust, he showed me his Aadhaar card, PAN card, and Driving License — all looked completely legit. He even gave me his phone number, said he’d return the money within a day, and emotionally convinced me.

I ended up giving him ₹8000(I know how stupid and emotional fool I was as the guy was speaking marathi), thinking I was helping someone genuinely stuck.
But now — he’s not responding. The number sometimes rings, sometimes shows “not reachable” — but he clearly isn’t picking up. I’ve realized it was all a scam

Scammer Profile (from what I remember):
	•	Seen near Delhi Airport Metro Station / IGI T3
	•	Height around 5’6, decent clothes, spoke calmly and emotionally
	•	Said his wallet and phone were stolen
	•	Showed multiple IDs to gain trust
	•	Gave a number which is still sometimes active
	•	Claimed he was heading home after something got stolen at the airport

I also clicked his aadhar card picture.

Why I’m posting:
	•	To warn others — this guy is definitely scamming people using fake sob stories and showing real-looking IDs
	•	If anyone spots him or confronts him, and helps recover my ₹8000 (even partially), I will personally reward ₹2,000
	•	I know it’s a long shot, but this post might save someone else from losing money
