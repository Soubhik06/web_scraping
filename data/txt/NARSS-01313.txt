Some days ago there was a post here about Netflix premium sharing for RS.200 / month. I was kind of really needed it as I couldn't subscribe it myself because I haven't activated international transactions on my card yet.

So , this guy gave me the username password upfront via telegram and shared his mobile number too. I tried logging in and it worked. This somehow made me trust him and I payed money via UPI. Things were going good and I binge watched many shows for 5 days. But yesterday , a notification popped up on Netflix regarding failing automatic payment transfer for next month. I texted this guy and he said he lost his wallet and thus blocked his credit card.

This was when it struck me I got scammed. Seems it was a 17 year old kid who was spamming in all Indian subbredits regarding Netflix sharing and he was trying to make some money out of the one month free trial.

I know I was being dumb but if anyone comes across random offers from strangers who seems so nice and trustworthy giving login credentials and all upfront , don't get scammed like me.

TL;DR - A redditor scammed me by offering Netflix premium sharing for Rs.200 which was actually his one month free trial and I ended up paying 200 via UPI. Beware of this scams.
