Petitioner is an accused for allegedly committing offence
 punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 120-B / 34 of the Indian Penal Code, Section 66(D) of the Information Technology Act, in connection with Cyber
 Crime P.S. Case No.04 of 2017 corresponding to CNR JHR
 No.10042612017, pending in the Court of learned Additional Judicial
 Commissioner II, Ranchi. There is allegation that the petitioner committed fraud and
 have cheated the informant by obtaining the OTP, CVV and PIN of Bank
 Cards and transferred huge amount illegally. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that it is one
 Deepak Verma, who is the main accused, who had obtained the Card
 details. He lastly submits that Khublal Mandal has been granted bail by the
 Court below and produced copy of the order granting bail passed by the
 Court below. Learned A.P.P. appearing for the State opposes the prayer for
 bail of the petitioner and takes this Court to paragraph 106 of the Case
 Diary. Paragraph 106 of the Case Diary suggests that at the instance of
 this petitioner, the amount was transferred. Mobile phones, which were
 used for the offence, belongs to this petitioner and Khublal mandal. Money
 was transferred in the account of Khublal Mandal and his son. Thus, there
 is allegation against the petitioner that he has participated actively in this
 offence. Since there is a prima facie allegation of transferring money
 against the petitioner, I am not inclined to grant bail to the petitioner at this
 stage, even if the co-accused Khublal Mandal has been granted bail by the -: 2 :- Court below. Accordingly, prayer for bail of the petitioner, abovenamed, in
 connection with Cyber Crime P.S. Case No.04 of 2017 corresponding to
 CNR JHR No. 10042612017, pending in the Court of learned Additional
 Judicial Commissioner II, Ranchi, is hereby rejected. This application stands dismissed. ( Ananda Sen, J.)
