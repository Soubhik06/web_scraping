**TLDR:**

I dialed my bank's phone number using the Google Voice app on wifi. Somehow my outgoing call got intercepted and rerouted to a scam call center. This happened twice, and the third time I pressed "call again" I finally reached the bank.

**Edit:** Googled the scam I got signed up for and found other accounts of this exact thing happening: https://www.scampulse.com/globaltek-md-reviews

I have no idea how the phone line was intercepted, but I am furious. I had to cancel the credit card and dispute the charge.

---

**Facts:**

* I have a Google Voice number
* I used the Google Voice app on my iPhone to call my bank
* I had wifi on, so it wasn't using data

---
**What happened:**

I called my bank by manually dialing the number on their website.

The robot voice said "Press 1."

I pressed 1.

A man of Indian descent answered and asked how they could help me and I said I'm calling to verify something.

He said he was going to transfer me to the proper department but before doing that, they were doing a special promotion and I had won a $100 gift card. All I had to do was pay $2.95 in shipping.

I agreed (planned to pay the $2.95 with an almost depleted Visa gift card for low risk).

Every time I asked him to identify who he worked for, he would misdirect and say he was part of the "rewards department." That and the language barrier or accent made me look the other way. I just wanted to talk to my bank and it was the correct phone number, so I just kept going.

He said I would have to listen to an advertisement but it had nothing to do with the promo and I just had to say "Yes" at the end and answer the questions as prompted.

What happened next seemed to be that he was having me listen to a third party phone line, having me answer vocally while he used phone keys to go through the process of me signing up for something.

He played an advertisement that said I was signing up for some trial and could cancel at anytime. I told him I didn't want to do that and he assured me that I wasn't signing up for anything, and to just say "Yes."

I did that, and he pressed a phone key.

I was further prompted by the phone line to give my phone number, email address, mailing address, credit card info, and credit/debit. Each time I gave a piece of info, he pressed a key *beep*.

After the whole thing was done, he hung up.

-----

**Afterwards:**

I called the same number again by pressing the "call again" button in the app. Same thing, got the scam number again: "Press 1."

I hung up and called a third time using the "call again" button. This time I got correctly connected to my bank's automated system.
