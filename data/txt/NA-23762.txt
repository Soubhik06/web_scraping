For customers arriving from Mumbai, a sort of harassment has started at the airport arrival.

Folks from a private lab "Auriga Research Private Limited" are given the task to screen passengers arriving from Mumbai for an RT-PCR report. For someone performing such risky task, not one of their personnel is wearing a PPE kit, a face shield or even a fully covering face mask. Not even the nice lady checking everyone's documents is aptly protected.

They are programmed to
1. Check RT-PCR report.
2. No need to verify authenticity of said report.
3. Convey that a Vaccine certificate is useless.
4. Get a test done right then and there if one doesn't have a test report.
5. Show passengers a shady carbon copied govt order asking for a negative test result.
6. Inform passengers that govt recommended broadcast channels such as ones on telegram aren't updated ontime.

They just let go of people who:
1. Say they've been tested but awaiting test result, they just let go without tracking them.
2. Say Bengaluru isn't their final destination.
3. Say they departed Bengaluru 2 days ago.
4. Ask where the restroom is and fuck off from there 😉

All these exceptions while their online registration portal has not listed any domestic flight operators. If they sense an urgency in the passenger to get out, they just fill in anything random, collect test fee and sample. They advise them to lookout for an email with test results.

Everything is so random, that it reeks of a scam. Question is, who is running this? State govt? Central govt? DGCA? AAI?
