--- POST TEXT ---

There was this scan going around through WhatsApp in India towards the end of 2018 -- a fake Amazon big season sale or something. Dumb as I was, I clicked on the link given, and bought some stuff for 1 rupee, thinking it was a steal (very dumb, I know). I gave my home address and a fake email address I used. I don't remember if I typed out the password in the link, but if I did, the password was the same as all my other email accounts and main apps. I did not give any bank account info, as I had made the payment option "cash on delivery". All I had typed out on that website was my name, my home address, my email address, and maybe/maybe not my password. As soon as I "bought" the items, I sent the link to my sister wherein she told me it was a scam, and they could send a malware to my phone through the link I clicked. I quickly backed up my messages and photos and all the info on my phone and within two hours, factory reset my phone. Unfortunately, I did not change my email address password. Two years later, I realize my stupidity and am genuinely scared about criminal identity theft and whatnot. I have changed all my email passwords now, although I fear it's too late. I don't remember my email being hacked though, or the password being changed by anyone other than me, and I was not aware of anyone accessing my email account using another device. 

Since the email address is a fake one I created for myself for dumb reasons, I do not care much about losing it. But I'm concerned about the home address I gave, and the possibility of them hacking into my phone through a malware that got into my phone upon clicking on that link. Can I do anything about it now? How soon do victims lose their privacy/money after giving a scammer their details?
