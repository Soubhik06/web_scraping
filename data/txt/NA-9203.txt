This petition has been filed to direct the respondent police to register a case based upon the petitioner's representation dated 20.10.2015. 2. The petitioner was living with her father-in-law, mother-in-law and her two daughters. The petitioner's husband is working abroad and the petitioner has been working as a Nurse in the Government Hospital in Adukkamparai. At this juncture, one Mr.Senthil kumar, who was working as electrician in the same hospital was known to her and her family members. He impressed the petitioner's husband and her in-laws and obtained loan from the petitioner and her husband and also returned the same. Subsequently, the said Mr.Senthilkumar met with an accident in 2014 and he asked the petitioner to help him and the petitioner arranged loan for Rs.6 lakhs for his treatment and for his family expenses. Again, he asked the petitioner to arrange for Rs.5 lakhs stating that he is in a very critical situation. Hence, the petitioner gave her 23 sovereign jewels in front of one Michal, who is the member of the petitioner's church. The said Mr.Senthil Kumar pledged the petitioner's jewels and got money. One month back, when the petitioner asked about Rs.6 lakhs and 23 sovereign jewels from Mr.Senthil Kumar, he acted in a different manner along with his friend one Sudhakar. He even threatened the petitioner that they would morph her photos and videos in the internet and created mental agony to the petitioner and her family members. Hence, the petitioner lodged a complaint before the respondent police on 20.10.2015. She also forwarded the complaint to the Superintendent of Police on 17.11.2015. Since no action was taken so far, the present petition has been filed. 3. Heard the learned counsel for the petitioner and the learned Additional Public Prosecutor appearing for the respondent. 4. Learned Additional Public Prosecutor submitted that on receipt of the complaint C.S.R.No.164 of 2015 was assigned and the petition enquiry is pending. 5. Considering the submissions made on either side, this Court directs the respondent police to conduct enquiry on the complaint of the petitioner dated 20.10.2015 in C.S.R.No.164/2015 and upon enquiry, if the materials are gathered indicating any cognizable offence, he is directed to proceed further in accordance with law as early as possible. 6. The criminal original petition is disposed of accordingly. 07.12.2015
