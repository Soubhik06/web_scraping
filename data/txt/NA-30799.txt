Jammu, Mar 24 (PTI) A senior Army officer on Tuesday visited Romeo Force headquarters to review the prevailing security environment and reinforce the counter- terrorism grid in the twin border districts of Rajouri and Poonch in Jammu and Kashmir, officials said.
General Officer Commanding of Jammu-based White Knight Corps Lt Gen P K Mishra’s visit to Romeo Force headquarters in Rajouri comes amid heightened vigil in the wake of recent infiltration bids by terrorists from across the border.
On March 10, Army troops gunned down a Pakistani terrorist of proscribed Lashkar-e-Taiba (LeT) outfit during an abortive attempt to infiltrate into this side in Nowshera sector of Rajouri.
