I got an email, I went for interview they asked for me to pay 2k for job security.
That time the recruiter told me about job and asked for more extra 700 for training.
When I was returning to home I thought it's a scam, I went again to that place and asked for refund.
They told me it can't happen right now, I need to come to Saturday so I can feel cancellation form.
When I went to Saturday, they told me I can't say person reason for cancellation.
Each time their rules are changing.
Now this time they told me to submit my documents on their email, and directly I'll get HR round after one day of submitting it.
I said them I didn't paid for the training, so will I still get a job? They said me yes, just send your documents. 
When I was asking them questions, they were talking like they were making answers (fake).

I am seeking for job and I was depressed that day so much, therefore I didn't realised quickly that I shouldn't pay them money.

Here's are few things I am mentioning so it can't be happen with anyone:
0) multiple companies hiring in one office. 
1) ask them they are internal/external recruiter.
2) if they are asking very very easy question in interview, more chances that it's a scam
3) they will ask for payment for job security and they will explain its flow even after cancellation (that doesn't happen)
4) they will ask you to submit lots of documents so they can steal your data.
5) they will say : "you can ask for hike in 3-6 months in company, free can service, and other enough perks, so it will look a genuine job.

It was my first scam experience and big mistake I made. I am regretting it and feeling bad for other people who paid more than me and feeling bad that it shouldn't happen with anyone.
Thay office was in Kothrud, but it can happen anyplace.
IF THEY ASO FOR MONEY, DON'T BE CURIOUS AND ASK THEM WHY SHOULD YOU GAVE THEM MONEY. THEY WILL ALWAYS LIE. JUST SAY NO, ANS YOU ARE NOT INTERESTED IN THIS TYPE OF RECRUITMENT WHERE YOU NEED TO PAY MONEY FOR GETTING JOB. THEY WILL USE VERY PROFESSIONAL WORDS LIKE "JOB SECURITY AND ETC."

Upvote this post and share it, so it will not happen with other people.
