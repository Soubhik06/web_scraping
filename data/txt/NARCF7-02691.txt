Recently ma mother ki mobile konadaniki vellanu. All the stores i visited are in dabagardens. Here is how i got scammed:

I went to BNEW mobiles, dabagardens. I liked a phone and thought to buy it. They said the color which i like was not available at the time and they will get it from somewhere else in an hour. I agreed and paid an advance of ₹2000 (the biggest mistake). I went to store after 2 hours only to hear them say to come after 3 days. I said give my advance back, then they became cautious and said they would deliver the phone next day morning itself.

Again i visited the store next day morning, they said the color which i want was not available and they tried to convince me to take the available one. I agreed as i liked the phone and its urgent requirement. I made the payment by card. They said i would get 10% instant cashback for this ( not received it till now, called the bank customer care and they said the offer was stopped 2 weeks before i bought the phone). 

I had to pay full amount again as they claimed cashback wouldnt be available if i cut the advance amount. I took the phone and was said by the manager that they didnt have cash today and they will give me the advance amount once any cash transaction happens.

This continued till 1 week. Yes 1 freaking week. I was busy with other work and waited that they will eventually return, but no response from them. Whenever i call they used to ignore my number. 

Finally i have used my local influence and gave a warning to the store. They gave me my advance and also the cashback.

So guys beware of these scams. Lesson learnt, always buy electronics online. Luckily i got my money back but it may not be the case for all!
