So today morning I got down my bus in mgbs and going to metro station. One man comes to me and asks for cash. I didn't have so he requested me to come to a shop to get cash. I paid the shop person 30 rupees extra for that cash. After wards he asked me that money, claimed to be an engineer who lives in financial district and told that he came to mgbs all of a sudden to drop his parents. He didn't have his phone charged and he looked decent enough to be trusted. I gave him 500Rs because I was in a same situation 2 days ago and wanted to help him whole heartedly. He gave me a number to call and also told me that after 1.5 to 2 hrs he will send me the money back. I called the number given by him and it says number doesn't exist. If that person is reading this then this is for him. "have some self respect and dignity before asking help from others, get some job and get some shame"(if he tried to scam me) or else send me my money. 

Also people don't fall in these traps people actually innocent infront of you for money and they just disappear. Came to home after many days of college and he spoiled my mood and also my money. Please people never trust anyone people are too much bad out there I feel. A bad start of my holiday 😞🙏
