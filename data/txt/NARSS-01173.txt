LONG POST ALERT: 

My friend, who lives abroad, needed a Medium of Instruction (MOI) certificate for a sponsorship application. He contacted his university, which referred him to an education services provider listed on their official website, indicating they are a recognized partner. My friend needed the certificate urgently, so he reached out to the provider. 

From the moment he showed interest, the company constantly called and messaged him to fill out forms and make the payment, which he eventually did. They promised to deliver the certificate in 15 days, but it has now been over 25 days. The company has stopped responding to his emails and calls. They only reached out when he left a negative Google review, threatening him to remove it. He refused, asking for the document instead. The company then claimed delays due to university holidays and promised delivery in 2-3 days, but this deadline also passed without any update.

Upon checking reviews, my friend found numerous complaints about the company scamming people, especially those abroad who can't visit the office in person. Some customers reported receiving blank envelopes to resolve PayPal disputes. The company appears to ask insiders or fake accounts to post positive reviews after negative ones to maintain their reputation. 

The company is called Uttarika, and their website is https://uttarika.com/ 

My question is, since my friend cannot travel to Karnataka to take legal action, is there any grievance department or authority that monitors such companies where we can file an online complaint? This blatant cheating needs to stop. Perhaps other users have experience with this company and can provide guidance.
