An Automated Message will say that you are getting a call from Customs and there’s a problem with your parcel. Press 9 to resolve. I didn’t know about the scam so I pressed 9. Then a person sounding like a middle aged officer tells you how a parcel is being sent in my name. I was half asleep, and he cut the call in disappointment. So, my sleepiness saved me.
