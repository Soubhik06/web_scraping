My relative scored 330 in NEET. His parents are desperate. They want the son to do MBBS. I have TWO major gripes.

1) There are some really shady agents out there. They look like somebody who'll simply run away with your money. I figured out because they're quoting random figures and saying random stuff. E.g. one agent was asking for 22Lakhs for admission. He was saying 15% of seats in pvt colleges are reserved for govt seats. He has tie-up with MCI and all. He'll get my relative added in that quota. What the fuck!

2) I find the whole direct MBBS and direct PG admission to be highly unethical. It's not the exact same thing as engg course. A fraud and incompetent engineer can do limited amount of damage to the society in comparison to a fraud/incompetent doc would do. I don't understand how could this even be a thing! I have heard that many shady things happen in MBBS courses of pvt colleges. Students dutch money and pay college clerks(and maybe profs) to figure out questions in the lab/main exam. Plus there are so many other shady stuff.

Update: The agent was saying after 1st and 2nd counseling there are some dropouts. Colleges can admit anyone against these dropouts after taking some money. So that's his modus operandi.
