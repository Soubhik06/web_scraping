Sify Infinit Spaces Limited has filed its Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) to raise up to Rs 3,700 crore through an initial public offering (IPO). The issue comprises a fresh issue of equity shares worth Rs 2,500 crore and an offer for sale (OFS) of shares aggregating up to Rs 1,200 crore, according to the draft filed on October 16, 2025.

Under the OFS, Kotak Data Center Fund plans to sell shares worth Rs 643 crore, while Kotak Special Situations Fund will offload up to Rs 557 crore. Furthermore, the company has appointed JM Financial, CLSA India, J.P. Morgan India, Kotak Mahindra Capital, and Morgan Stanley India as the book-running lead managers (BRLMs) to the issue. KFin Technologies will act as the registrar.

Incorporated in 2017 and headquartered in Chennai, Sify Infinit Spaces operates a network of data centres and provides colocation, managed hosting, and interconnection services. A subsidiary of Sify Technologies Limited, the company caters to enterprise, cloud, and government clients seeking scalable and secure digital infrastructure.

Sify Infinit Spaces Limited plans to deploy the Rs 2,500-crore net proceeds from its IPO towards a mix of infrastructure expansion and debt reduction, according to its DRHP. The company intends to use the funds for three primary purposes.

Firstly, it will partly fund the completion of Tower B at its Siruseri data centre in Chennai and the construction of Towers 11 and 12 at its Rabale data centre in Navi Mumbai. Together, these projects will receive the largest share of the proceeds â Rs 860 crore of the net funds will go towards the Rabale expansion alone, and Rs 465 crore will go to the Chennai data centre.

Secondly, Sify Infinit Spaces will use a portion of the proceeds for the repayment or prepayment of certain borrowings, reducing its leverage and strengthening its balance sheet. Thirdly, the remaining funds will support general corporate purposes, including operational expansion, working capital needs, and potential future investments in technology and infrastructure upgrades.
