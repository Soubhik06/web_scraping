I just got a call from a restricted number. Female caller with a South Asian accent (Filipino, maybe Indian), with a very busy call center in the background. Had my first/last name (easy obtainable), said she was from NASDAQ, and asked me if I was still an active trader, and if I was with a traditional broker or online. I knew it was a scam and played along, saying "online", and she asked me which one and gave me examples of Fidelity, TD Ameritrade, or Robinhood. I said "all of the above". She thanked me and said they would send me some literature on how to make money, and the call ended.

It's probably no coincidence this is happening with all the WSB movement as hundreds of new people try to cash in on these damn things. Given a lot of my personal details (address, DOB, phone number, full name, email) have been compromised in various breaches throughout the decade, I guarantee they will try to sim swap, password reset, or social engineer their way into my accounts and drain them. I do have accounts, but they're zero'd out already, have MFA enabled, and no links to my bank accounts.
