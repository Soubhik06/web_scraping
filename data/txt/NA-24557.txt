--- POST TEXT ---

So I wanted to courier some stuff to another city and I enquired SRS travel agency about it, the number listed on Google connected me to some guy pretending to be working for SRS.

He told me he will collect the luggage from my place. And I will have to pay a nominal fee to get the booking done. He asked me if I had upi to pay for it. 

First he asked me to forward him a confirmation message of booking. I got some random gibberish which he asked me to forward to a number that was for Indian Bank. That was the first strange thing that alert me. Stupid me did it anyway.

Then he told me, my booking is successful. And now I will have to pay nominal fee to confirm it via upi. I was like sure, upi is safe, if it's fraud it's 10 rupees at the most. He asks me if Google pay works for me, I said sure. He then forwards me a Google Form, that has a text box for amount and upi pin.

So what I was afraid of had now become a reality. This was clearly fraud happening. I told the guy I wouldn't enter my pin, he said then Google pay won't work. I told him, I'll pay directly via Google Pay, he said that won't work. To keep him engaged, I said then I need another mode of payment. He waited for 10 minutes and sent me a link of surveymonkey and said this is different upi, but the text boxes still asked amount, where he asked me to enter the nominal fee and upi pin.

I didn't know how to proceed, shared SS with Mumbai police on twitter. But with UPI being so new, I'm quite sure they will have fooled a lot of people in this manner.
