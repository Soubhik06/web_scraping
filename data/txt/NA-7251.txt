 UNLAWFUL ASSEMBLY & RIOTS 13. Testimonies of PW3, PW4, PW6, PW7 and PW11 mention about
 the mob, which was indulging into vandalism and arson in the
 area D block, Gokal Puri on 25.02.2020 during morning and
 noon time. But except for PW3, PW6 and PW7, none of these
 witnesses claimed to have seen the incidents at the properties of
 both complainants herein. PW3 deposed that he was present at
 his residence (on the 1st floor of the property), when the mob
 attacked his property including his shop on the ground floor. He
 also deposed about vandalism and arson in his shop as well as
 injury caused to him due to pelting of stones. In his complaint
 Ex.PW-3/A, he had mentioned time of such incidents. Such facts
 have not been challenged by the defence. Thus, there remains no
 doubt over the fact that a riotous mob indulged into vandalizing,
 pelting stones and arson in the property no. D-141/142, Gokal
 Puri, on 25.02.2020 at morning hours and in the afternoon. 14. As far as vandalism at property of PW4 at D-3/4, Gokal Puri is Page 11 of 14 (Pulastya Pramachala) ASJ-03, North-East District,

 PW6 deposed that on 25.02.2020 the mob which had assembled
