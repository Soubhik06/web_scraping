--- POST TEXT ---

Today, I was tricked into believing that my Aadhaar card was linked to money laundering cases and that I had a national security case against me. The scammers, dressed in police uniforms, contacted me via video call and successfully deceived me. I admit that I was easily fooled and believed the scam because I thought the telecom department would block my number. It wasn't until a friend mentioned similar scams that I realized I was talking to scammers.
This experience serves as a reminder that anyone can be a victim of such scams, especially if they are easily scared or who are not aware.
