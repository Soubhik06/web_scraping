**Nobroker is a Scam - Please Stay Away**

We subscribed to Nobroker’s Super Relax Plan on June 26th, which promised “Virtual Home Tours with a Field Relationship Manager (FRM).” However, in two weeks, we only received one home tour. The properties I had shortlisted on the app were sent back to us as recommendations. When we tried to contact the numbers shared on the app, none of them worked. The have lot of fake listings.

Despite clearly explaining my requirements to the relationship manager, she kept recommending houses that didn’t match our criteria. It felt like an automated system was shortlisting and recommending the houses. Every time a new relationship manager called, they had no knowledge of our previous inquiries. One day, the support staff even said they couldn’t schedule a home tour, either from their end or through the app. Nothing that was promised to us was delivered.

When we raised a complaint and asked for a refund, the relationship manager stopped working altogether. 
We were still shortlisting houses and relationship manager couldn't care less, looks like this happens a lot and she might have moved on to the next scam.

After waiting a week, someone finally called and informed us that the plan was non-refundable. They said they will change the relationship manager and help, but they didn't do anything.

Ultimately, we found a house through MagicBricks with the help of a broker.
