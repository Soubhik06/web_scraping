On Friday 13th October 2023 (3 days ago as of posting here), my mother received a message saying 20k was debited from her account without any OTP or confirmation, and shortly after she received a call from a person claiming to be from axis Bank, and he asked if the transaction was not made by her, and if so, to click a link he sent.

 However, according to my mom he was quite shady, so she cut the call. However immediately she got a call back from the same guy who sounded very angry because of the fact that she cut the call, upon which she informed that she would complain to the police. 

Following this, she repeatedly getting transaction messages immediately for upto 1 lakh. She immediately contacted axis Bank and reported this fraudulent transaction, and filed a complaint with the local police station, cyber crime and then lodged a complaint with the bank within 3 days of the fraudulent transaction. Now axis Bank has informed her that they would go conduct an investigation and within 5 days decide whether the transaction was due to customer negligence or fault from the bank's side. 

I do however feel it would be very unfair if axis Bank were the sole conductors of this investigation and not some third party, since they have a great motivation to pin the blame on us the customer.

Does anyone who has faced a similar issue have any suggestions regarding what next steps I can take if the bank refuses to provide us our refund?

Also, anyone with knowledge on banking laws and regulations, it would be of great help if you could provide any suggestions on next steps.
