To Aaj kya hua, mai (saalo se gharwalo ke paise uda ke thaka hua ladka) gaya ek part time job ke interview ke liye.
Ab vo jagah ka crowd dekh ke muje idea lag gya tha ki ye vohi MLM scene hai but fir b me baitha raha. Karib 45mins baad meko interview ke liye bulaya aur, literally interviewer ki shakl dekh ke pata lg rha hai scam hai. She asked a few questions related to academics, meri percentage puchi, kyu job karni hai vgera vgera. 
Fir didi ne mange 2000 rupe. Meko pata tha ye hone wala hai. Iske baad convo went like:

Me: ye paise kis liye?
DD: 1500 aapki training and 500 registration fees
Me: training kis baat ki? Aur logo ko scam karne ki?
DD: ye kya bol rhe ho aap? Ye paise aapki salary ke sath aapko wapas mil jayenge. Aap abhi ye pay Karo aur 2 din me aapki training start ho jayegi
Me: 2 din me to aap gayab ho jaoge.
DD: aap final batao apko job chahiye ya nahi. Accept ya reject. Kya karu mai?
Me: band. Band krdo please mat kro ye sab, kuch kaam krlo.

Fir didi ne bahar se 2 bhaiyaz ko bulaya jinhone aate hi mujhe kaha chup chap lift se neeche chala ja. Mai hasne laga. Aur isse pehle ki vo meri pitai karne lagte mai bhaag aya~
