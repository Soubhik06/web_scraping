I've unfortunately been hit by this and it's fucking up my life.

Received a call around 6 or 7 PM through the number claiming to be The Royal Bank of Scotland (I later found out that this is an ingoing ONLY number) with an Indian man claiming to be from the fraud department, who then sent a code through to my phone which I did not realise at the time was GetCash code, and then to delete my mobile banking app from my mobile. Because of this, the bank has suggested I delete my online account and send me a new card and then to make a new account when the card comes, but I think the very same people have also managed to cancel my mobile contract through PAC code.

I just wanted to warn everyone in Scotland &amp; England incase you receive calls like these because it's slowly ruining my life.
