I received a fraud call today from FedEx with the same familiar tune, informing me that my order is being returned. I pressed one for inquiries since I've placed recent orders, and it's Secret Santa week. My call was transferred to customer care, where I was informed that my order wasn't sent due to an issue. They asked about my correct number and if my Aadhar was linked. The representative mentioned illegal items in the parcel, like fake passports. I ended the call immediately after I realised this is a fraud call. And I did not gave any information except location. Worried about the information that this guy has like aadhar card and number. Now, I'm concerned about my number, Aadhar card, and location details. I also pressed one when the FedEx tune played so maybe something can happen. Maybe i am thinking too much
