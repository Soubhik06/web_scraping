Best thing you can do to help yourself, your family, and the healthcare system- Don't get infected. These new strains are scary, we're starting to see children with severe disease now.

#Protecting yourself from the virus: Masks, hygiene, distance and vaccines.

**Save the entryway:** You may not protect your body from the virus at all times, maybe your family is infected, or you have to get medicines from a crowded place. As long as you can protect your nose and mouth you'll be safe. Masks have a metal strip or wire which should be moulded according to the shape of your nose. Blow into the masks after wearing to determine where is a leak and make sure the seal is proper, that you're breathing through the mask material and not from around it. N95s with a cloth covered hole on them are equivalent to wearing a mask of that cloth.

Already CoVID positive? Caring for an infected person and have accepted your fate? WEAR THE MASK. Viral dosage is a determinant of severity of disease. If you inhale less virus, less chances they are deposited in your lungs and that you develop severe disease. Careless people are more commonly developing severe disease as they inhale more viruses. Careful people are receiving low viral dose and developing nasopharyngeal symptoms, they are less commonly developing severe disease. This is my personal observation and advice (There are many determinants of severity of disease and doesnt mean if someone's taking all precautions he cant get severe disease).

If you've come in contact of an infected person, symptoms may come in 5-14 days, but you'll start infecting ~[2 days](https://youtu.be/uCIC8MwBebU) before symptoms onset. Be careful! Isolate yourself for 2 weeks or at least take all precautions so you don't infect others. A doctor can write you a prescription advising isolation that you can show at your place of work if you've been in contact.

[**Mask effectiveness**](https://youtu.be/Y47t9qLc9I4): Viruses are much smaller than the pore sizes of even N95, but individual viruses rarely cause disease. We need to stop the droplets from infected people, for which it isn't necessary to go for all N95s. Triple layer surgical masks are good enough. This video gives a good explanation of why individual masks don't have to be 100% effective. Save N95s for when you're going to meet someone you know is definitely infected.

**Masks rotation**: Don't waste N95s if you are using it, they're always in limited supply. When working in Covid centres or hospitals, we get only 3 N95 masks for 2 weeks of duty. Viruses die in most surfaces by the 5th day, sun exposure accelerates their death. So we number these masks 1, 2 and 3 so theres a 3 day gap between each use and keep them in a sun exposed area for the rest of the time. You can follow this when caring for an infected family member.

**Hygiene**: Have been to a place where CoVID exposure is high? Caring for family? Take a bath when you return home ASAP. And remember to remove masks last after removing every other clothing. Removing clothing can make aerosols out of deposited viruses on them which you may then inhale.

Wash/Sanitize hands before eating and touching anything at home, and after touching anything when outside. Don't go to a crowded place unless you have to.

**Vaccines (Astra Zeneca/Covishield and Bharat Biotech/Covaxin):** Get vaccinated! Efficacy of preventing CoVID maybe low, but they have 100% efficacy of preventing hospitalization and deaths. Also the efficacies of different vaccines [can't be compared](https://youtu.be/K3odScka55A) due to different testing conditions. These vaccines are said to be effective 2 weeks after 2nd dose. 

Covishield is live adenovirus (yes, not a coronavirus) of chimpanzees (yes, not human). SARS CoV-2 proteins are slapped in top of it. So it cant cause coronavirus in you. Stop the misinformation. It may cause some symptoms for a couple of days, but that's it. 

For Covaxin we have little data, but it is inactivated coronaviruses. They can't do any harm. Hearsay results are good from health staff that have received it.

Get the vaccine to all the comorbid and elderly people that may get severe infection.

Don't go for vaccination if you're pregnant, lactating or have symptoms of coronavirus (fever/cough)

**Fomites:**

Don't have to rub everything with sanitizer. Can clean keys, glasses etc with soap for 20 sec. 

Also if there's an article you got and you're unsure if it's safe to use, just leave them alone for 5 days (Banknotes, shopping bags etc).

Get yourself hypochlorite solution to kill deposited viruses in your home. You don't have to use it clean your house to a shine, just make sure every inch of the floor and surfaces have been rubbed with it. Use it on vehicles, for handles, shoe soles. You can keep a wet hypochlorite floor mat on the entrance of the house or isolation room.
