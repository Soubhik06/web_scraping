Got an SMS from a random number like “077520 18533” claiming your bank account is blocked? It’s likely a phishing scam! 

🕵️‍♂️ TRAI rules say banks must use registered IDs like “VA-BDNSMS” for legit messages—not mobile numbers.

Here’s a quick tip:

✅ Legit SMS = Alphanumeric ID (e.g., “VA-BDNSMS”)

❌ Scam SMS = Random mobile numbers, urgent threats, or weird instructions

Protect yourself:

🔒 Don’t click links or share info

📞 Verify directly with your bank

🚨 Report to TRAI (1909) or cybercrime.gov.in
Stay safe, and share to spread awareness! 💡
