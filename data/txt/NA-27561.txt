Since about 3 months back, I've been getting a crazy amount of calls from various (mostly Indian) scammers. About 3-4 per week. They claim to be calling from organizations/companies such as **International Fraud Group, Blockchain,** and **Microsoft**, etc and they usually call from a Swedish number (I'm in Sweden so it's not very surprising they'll be using some VoIP solution). 

I have no idea how they got my name and number in the first place. Records of phone numbers aren't very hard to come by in Sweden, but the fact that they can connect the number to my name, and the fact that I get so many calls leads me to believe that there's been a breach in one or more sites that I've signed up for, and that the records have been leaked/sold to these call centers.

If I have the energy, I try to make it a game to make **them** hang up on **me** and I might spend a couple of minutes leading them on/playing dumb until it gets too ridiculous for them. But – I've noticed that they will pretty much always open the call by asking my name; "Hello, is this &lt;name&gt;?", and if I say "nope", they'll hang up immediately. If I act suspicious and ask counter with "who's asking", they'll keep going up until I've either explicitly confirmed or denied that I in fact am the person they're looking to speak with.

Why is it so important for them to get my name right? Any ideas?
