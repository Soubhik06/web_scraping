If this source based report by the senior journalist, Arati Jerath, is to be believed, then the Central Govt has plans to impose President’s Rule in Delhi citing "constitutional breakdown"

http://thestatesman.com/opinion/did-life-get-too-hot-for-jung-1482885700.html

Here is why I think this information can't be dismissed entirely calling it as "Political Gossip"

* Central Govt has already dismissed/destabilized two State Govts in the past two years - Arunachal and Uttarakhand. I don't think this Govt has much respect for Democracy and Federalism 
* SC may overturn parts of the HC Judgement which assigned all the administrative powers in Delhi to LG. If the SC judgement goes in favour of the AAP Govt, then the strategy of using LG to attack AAP may no longer be effective (SC has already said that the Delhi Govt should have some powers. The judgement is expected in the second week of January) 
* If the SC judgement gives back the control of ACB to the AAP Govt, then Arvind Kejriwal can pose a very big political threat to the Modi Govt by going against the corruption of the BJP leaders 
* Arvind Kejriwal has been the most outspoken critic of the Modi Govt. He has become a headache for the Modi Govt by occupying Delhi. He has been the most effective opponent of all the political opposition against Demonetisation. 
* There were no indications whatsoever of Jung resigning before his term. Almost all the reports and sources have claimed this was a sudden decision. My guess is that he was asked to do something against the AAP Govt which even his "compromised soul" felt was too extreme. It must have been something big, not the usual arrest of the AAP MLAs on trumped up charges.
* Close to 15 AAP MLAs have been arrested so far, IT has been sending notices to all the high value donors of AAP, party's CM candidate in Goa has been summoned by the ACB in another fake case immediately after his announcement as the CM candidate, and so on - given such brazen misuse of power, it doesn't seem too far fetched or giant leap for the BJP to dismiss the Delhi Govt.

What do you think?
