Fintech company MobiKwik suffered a fraud of Rs 1.26 crore due to an ex-employee’s misappropriation of funds from August 2023 to September 2024. In an exchange filing on March 27, 2025, the company disclosed that the Gurugram police are investigating the matter, and that the accused, Gaurav Sharma, is in custody.

MobiKwik made the disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

This development comes at a time when MobiKwik’s shares have plunged 41% from its market debut on December 18, 2024. For context , the company’s shares opened at Rs 442 on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) and Rs 440 on the National Stock Exchange (NSE), up from the issue price of Rs 279. Notably, its Initial Public Offering (IPO) was oversubscribed 119.38 times over the shares on offer, resulting in bids worth Rs 40,000 crore from December 11 to December 13, 2024.

Coming to its earnings , the fintech firm reported an income of Rs 274.5 crore in the recent quarter (Q3FY25). This growth was driven by its payments business. The payment revenue saw a 166% year-on-year (YoY) increase, standing at Rs 196.5 crore in the quarter. Meanwhile, MobiKwik incurred a loss of Rs 51.32 crore, a 21.77% YoY decrease from the same quarter last year.

Recently, the company has expanded into the stock broking business, launching its wholly-owned subsidiary, MobiKwik Securities Broking. Additionally, it has become one of the first fintech firms to offer a Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) wallet.
