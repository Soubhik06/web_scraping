Decision Qua Accused : Acquitted. 1. The Complainant has inter alia alleged in his Complaint filed
under Sec. 138 , Negotiable Instruments Act that he is in the business
of petrol pump at Faridabad in the name and style of "INDER
FILLING STATION" and the Accused was working as a Salesman
with the complainant. It is further alleged that in 2019, when the
Complainant inquired about outstanding amount due with customers,
maintained, looked after by the Accused after verification and cross-
checking with customers, it transpired that the Accused had taken
payment on behalf of the Complainant and misappropriated for his
own benefits. The payment as received by the customers were never
deposited to the Complainant. It is further alleged that the
Complainant was about to take legal action but at the request of the
family of the Accused and staff of the petrol pump, criminal action
was not initiated. Resultantly, the Complainant suffered huge loss and
to discharge his legal monetary liability/debt, the Accused had
executed a letter of undertaking along with advance cheque and due to
Covid 19, the Complainant has taken a lenient approach and had given
enough time to make payment of the legal debt. 2. It is further alleged that on 12.09.2021, the accused had
instructed the complainant to present the cheque in question and the
same was presented by the complainant on 16.09.2021 and the same
was dishonored on 16.09.2021 for the reason "Funds Insufficient".
The Complainant through his advocate issued Legal Notice dated
29.09.2021, through speed post and the same was delivered to the
accused on 01.10.2021, however, no reply was received and hence, the
present Complaint. 3. Summons were issued against the Accused vide order dated
04.12.2021, notice under Sec. 251 Cr.P.C. was framed against the
Accused on 01.11.2022 and the Accused pleaded not guilty and
claimed trial. The Accused in his plea of defence inter alia stated that
the cheque in question was stolen, he had filed a complaint for the
theft of the cheque in question, the same did not bear his signature and
he did not fill the cheque in his handwriting. He further stated that he
was an employee of the Complainant, the complainant was not giving
his salary and he filed a complaint against him in the Labour Court.
The Accused further denied signing Ex CW 1/1 and stated that he had
not written the statement in Ex. CW 1/1. Lastly, the Accused denied
receiving the legal demand notice. On the same date of hearing i.e.,
01.11.2022, upon the oral request of the Accused, he was granted an
opportunity to cross-examine the Complainant under Sec. 145 (2) , NI
Act and the court directed the case to be tried as a summons case. 4. The Complainant on 18.04.2023 adopted his evidence by way

presumption that the cheque was drawn for consideration and given by
