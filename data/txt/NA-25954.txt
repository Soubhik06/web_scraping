Like the title suggests, Iam sure you must have come across numerous channels on telegram promising ridiculous returns for investments made. 
They even go on to attach "chat screenshot(s)" with other supposed investors to prove their legitimacy.
Sadly,a friend of mine recently fell victim to one such scam and it's hard to see how badly it has affected him.
Now keep in mind that he'll probably be back on his feet since he's quite sorted financially but the feeling of instant gratification took over him and made him do something he regrets now.
His experience made me think of those less fortunate that might have done the same thing in hopes of winning big but obviously couldn't. I can only imagine what they might have done after, because some live to never repeat it and some don't( I've heard stories of victims taking their lives, which is tragic) 
Therefore, I advice everyone who's ever thought of doing something like this to kindly stray away from such scams. I know it might sound tempting but it is not worth it. Keep your money and find a way to grow it organically.
Stay mindful.
