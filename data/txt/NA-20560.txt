I’m doing a course from the Indian Institute of Governance and Leadership based in Delhi. They have a youth org called Confederation of Young Leaders (CYL) which apparently organises youth dialogue between India &amp; other countries. 

I was sent an application to fill for a high level youth delegation visit to China this October &amp; got selected. I’ve to pay 1,65,000₹ for this visit which includes accommodation, visa fee, flights, meals, engagements, events, logistics. So I feel it’s actually cheap. 

My problem is they haven’t shared any testimonials of past participants, only photos which is making me sus. Upon asking the same, they’ve sent me email addresses of 2 past participants. Also, in every photo, I can see only the Chairperson of CYL with other delegates. In another photo, there is a photo of a girl with her name tag but I couldn’t find her on LinkedIn. Regarding the chairperson, he seems wealthy enough to make sure there is nothing negative about him on the web. All this is too good to be true. 

They have however said if the delegation doesn’t go ahead with it, they shall refund me my money. They’ve asked for 15k as down payment to block the seat.

And I found out that there’s Konfuzius-Institute in Düsseldorf which is recruiting 3 people from there to join this camp for the exact dates mentioned to me. 

Does anyone have any idea about this? I don’t want to lose my hard-earned money over this if it’s a scam but I also don’t want to let go of this opportunity if this is legit.
