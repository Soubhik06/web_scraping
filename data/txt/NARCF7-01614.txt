So is this some new kind of scam that is going on? 

Yesterday I got an offer from a buyer for my AirPods Pro that I listed on OLX. 

The guy was very polite and we had a good conversation. We agreed on price and meeting place. 

He insisted on meeting at any apple service center to check if airpods are genuine before purchasing. I thought it was fair enough and agreed. 

Upon reaching the service center, the guy inspected my airpods and kept saying they were fake. 
This is after me showing him the official invoice, warranty info on my phone, verified the serial number on apple’s website, inspecting the box, etc. 

The funny thing is, he probably talked to the service center guy before my arrival and even that guy kept on insisting that I had fake airpods and they’re not original without explaining or inspecting anything. 

And later he said, as these are fake, he would only offer me 5000 for it and not the initially agreed price of 15000. 
He also started guilt tripping me and said I should sell it off as they’re fake and no one would buy them. 

I immediately left left without looking back. 

Be careful when you deal with these scammers.
