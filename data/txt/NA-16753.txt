Scam site 1: [https://perfectx.in](https://perfectx.in)

Scame Site 2: [https://widgetshunt.com](https://widgetshunt.com) 

Both the sites are related to #Indian consumers, I assume. 

Scam site 1 is selling access to digital products and also offering unlimited Google Drive space for a low amount, which is most likely a sign of fake offer. In my opinion, once they achieve their target goal, the site will be erased from the Internet and all the money that consumers spent will be lost forever. Most importantly, if anyone signs up for the unlimited Google Drive space offer, the data will be lost as well.

Scam site 2 is selling electronic products in the form of combo offers for a really low price by offering huge discounts on these branded products. Overall, the website itself looks very suspicious and fake. 

DO NOT SHOP OR SPEND MONEY ON THESE SITES, YOU WILL BE CHEATED!
