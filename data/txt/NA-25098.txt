Yesterday evening near uttam nagar east metro ,, a lady asked one of my friend to dial a phone number as she said she lost her phone.

He gave the phone to her to dial it by herself, she dialled some number and said that it got disconnected 
Suddenly my friend received a call and he got busy on the call for a minute (possibly a wrong number

Few minutes after that he opened his WhatsApp just to check new messages and he realised he got logged out of whatsapp

He tried to login but now his WhatsApp asked for a 2 factor authentication password which he had never setup

He realised that his WhatsApp got hacked and no longer in his control

Then he searched internet and found that , it is a typical scam 

The number the lady dialled is a "call forwarding activation number" 
And the hacker possibly in some other place requested the otp for login via call instead of message 

And hacker called my friend to make his number busy so that otp on calll gets forwarded to the hacker

Thats how easy it is to hack one's whatsapp

Solution - Enable 2 factor authentication in ur whatsapp settings
