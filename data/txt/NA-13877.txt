In order to monetise business communications on its platform, WhatsApp has introduced a new pricing structure to for charging WhatsApp Business Accounts. According to an update by Meta, businesses using the WhatsApp Business Platform will be charged per 24-hour conversation, which includes all the messages delivered in a 24-hour session.

Currently, businesses are charged Rs 0.48 per conversation. The rates for these conversations will differ based on different categories. The four new conversation categories and the pricing for them will be effective from June 1, 2023.

The current business-initiated conversations–wherein a business sends a message to the user outside the 24-hour customer service window—will now be split into three different categories. These will require the customer to “opt-in” for the service.

A fourth category of conversation includes user-initiated conversations, which will be called as service conversations. These are mainly for customers to get their queries addressed and will be charged Rs 0.29 per conversation, which is same as the current price.

Why it matters:

The pricing changes to WhatsApp comes as a move to other monetising opportunities , specifically business messaging, in the backdrop of Meta’s losses with respect to its ad-revenue and its Metaverse projects . With over 400 million users in India, WhatsApp has been a driving force for many businesses in India. The new changes are also reported to help getting rid of spam messages given that users have the option to not opt-in for these conversations and also due to the increased cost of promoting businesses.
