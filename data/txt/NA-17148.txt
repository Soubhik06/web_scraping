--- POST TEXT ---

We are living in uncertain times. This pandemic has put a full stop to all our plans. And one of the most affected people due to this pandemic is 2020 graduates from all the streams. I was unable to find a valid source but approximately around 50L Indians graduate every year. I understand the difficulty for these graduates in finding a new job. So if you are one of the 2020 graduates, please read this. 

* 1. No legit company will ever ask you to share your debit card details. 
* 2. No legit company will ask you to pay some money upfront so that the offer letter can be released. 
* 3. No legit company will ask you to register for a course in return for an offer letter. 

In the past 3 months, I was able to see how desperately my cousins and their friends wanted a job. They had to encounter some ridiculous scams. I have highlighted the gist of the scams in the above paragraph. Some of my cousins and their friends were so desperate for a job that they were unable to see through the scams. 

The scammers have upped their game. They sound very professional, I was really surprised at the way they presented the job. Their e-mail game is also top-notch, but there are some legit gaps in their approach which you can catch hold of. 

* 1. HRs will never sound ambiguous in their e-mail. They will always be on point on the role and compensation that you are being offered. 
* 2. Check the HR background. Most of the scams used legit companies names like Tata, Sun Pharma, Infosys, etc. Check if these HR profiles are present in Linkedin. The scammers won't have a LinkedIn profile but everything in their e-mail will look legit with regards to the company they are trying to impersonate. 
* 3. They will ask you to accept the offer immediately. They will convince that you need to accept the offer in the next 5 minutes. Usually, the HR will give you at least 1 day and in many cases, the offer letter will be valid for even 10 days. 

These are some of the points that I thought could help you with your job search. Others can pitch in and share their ideas. 

Stay safe guys!
