Today morning I had a call from a service agent where I was offered 'Bajaj Finserv Card' at free of cost.  They offered me the card because my credit score was good.

Initially I was interested in buying the card but later I have changed my mind to not buy at this time, because a friend of mine who owned the credit card said he has faced lot of issues. Like they will call you around 50 times in a day is you to failed to pay on due time.

One of the field executive, located at Coimbatore, Race Course branch contacted me for updating KYC to activated the card. I have genuinely said that I'm not interested to buy the card at this time. He became rude and questioned me that it was me who have agreed to activate the card. I politely replied him that I do not need the card right now. 

Later, he insisted to meet me in person for "**Deactivating my account**" which I have **never created**. I agreed to meet him and he demanded the details of my social documents such as Photo, PAN card, Aadhar card and later OTP. This made me feel suspicious and later realised that the actual reason for asking these details was to create an account without my **consent**. I was promised to have a free card in the call but I was asked to pay 500rs to create the account. I refused to cooperate with him any further and left the place.

This is purely a scam, and I did not like the way he responds to the customer. This act cannot be justified because he wants meet his daily target.

Please be aware of these kind of people, they will lie to you to any extent to get customers and god knows how they will treat you if you failed to pay the loan on due time. It was an awful experience for me where I had to argue with an stranger for no reason today.
