The Parliamentary Standing Committee on Home Affairs tabled its 254th Report on Cyber Crime in August 2025. The report discusses the growing risks of cybercrime in India, including financial fraud, deepfakes, and cyber trafficking. It calls for stronger laws, enhanced enforcement mechanisms, and new regulatory measures to address these evolving threats. The report also highlights the challenges in tackling cybercrime, particularly in the context of emerging technologies like AI and cryptocurrency.
