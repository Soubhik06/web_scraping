Last year, I had deleted my Facebook/Instagram/WhatsApp accounts since I was having major anxiety watching stuff that only belonged in my echo chamber and it was severely affecting my mental health. Moreover, I had discovered that a lot of my personal data was in some data breaches. 

Since then I became more conscious of my online activities. So now I read privacy policies and only join websites which do not allow third-party sharing of data, at least not any personal ones. 

And then I read the [privacy policy](https://www.kooapp.com/privacy) of Koo. It is by far one of the worst that I have read. Jio is another such company which actively shares user info but at least Jio went through the trouble of making a more elaborate policy. 

In short:

THEY ARE WILLING SHARE PERSONAL INFO WITH PARTNERS 
THEY WILL NOT BE PARTY TO ANY LIABILITY

[The content policy](https://www.kooapp.com/terms) is basically "do whatevs, no porn" 
I mean, I would love more tech startups coming out of India, but we cannot have desi innovations to be worse than the existing ones. 

Adding images but would recommend you to read the whole thing.
