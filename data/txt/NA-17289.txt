I am putting my experience out here so that it will help people be vary about many unscrupulous but ingenious ways in which they can get scammed. Throwaway account because of personally identifiable info present with Cashify.

**TL: DR** : Bottom of the post.

Background : 

I am the kind of person who does not sell their phones easily. Yes, the same kind that likes taking the phones out from storage, giving them a good wipe, charging them to 50~60%, thinking about all the experiences that was shared, take one last nostalgic look and put them back in storage. Also, I am afraid of data recovery tools more than I care to admit. Hence no selling of phones or any other kind of storage based devices.

The responsibility was taking a toll on me. I started feeling like an overburdened parent. But I realized that unlike kids, I can actually sell the phones and lighten my load.

Needed fast and hassle (haggle) free way to sell them. Saw good reviews for cashify on here. I opened an account and requested a pickup.

[Phone pick-up confirmation was received](https://i.imgur.com/3p5QPD1.jpg)

Pickup was scheduled on 19th April and the technician arrived 2 days late. I did not mind it because covid times are tough on everyone. 

Technician checks the phone and quotes 1000 rupees lesser than what the website showed. Thing to note : Cashify promises haggle free experience but haggling is exactly what I got. I will not go into technical details of his frivolous arguments. I decided against the deal and ask him to hand over my phone. At this point in time, he has fingerprint locked my phone. He did that while "testing" the sensor. 

He starts clicking pics of my phone and typing in the IMEI, etc. I thought to myself "why do this if you are not going to purchase the phone?"

After he was done entering the details, he asked me to wait for the Cashify OTP for the "cancellation" of the sale. Only after the OTP he will be able to handover the phone he said. 

Such a red flag this was. I asked him to remove the fingerprint lock and handover the phone, and only then he will get OTP from me. After telling him couple of times, he budged. 

I check my phone for damage, and then take out my active phone to look for the Cashify OTP. I received an email and an sms, contents of both said the same thing. 

[This is what I received](https://i.imgur.com/7q4Yq1g.jpg) . For the lazies, I have copy-pasted the email below - 

&gt;We have initiated payment to your Account/Wallet for Rs. 100.0. Please share the confirmation code [retracted by OP] with our agent after ensuring that your personal data has been wiped and sim/memory card retrieved.

**Okay, if the scam is not clear** also **TL DR** : 

Cashify agent had fingerprint locked my phone, and initiated a payment of 100 rupees for it. If I had given him the OTP, then he would legally own my phone. All for 100 rupees. 

I thanked my extra paranoid self when I saw the content of the OTP message. I already had my old phone in my hand, with no fingerprint lock on it, and I had not given the OTP to the agent. I was in the green by all means. 

I asked him to explain why cancellation OTP says what it says. He said it was a "zero sum" transaction to confirm that I had cancelled. I smiled away while he tried his best to pretend like he did not try to pull a fast one on me. 

I started walking back into my house while he was pleading for couple of more photos of the phone. I felt like a bollywood diva being cajoled by paparazzi.
