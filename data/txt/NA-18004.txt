So here's the lowdown. I got a call from a phone number which was unknown to me. Picked it up only to hear a guy speak in heavily-accented Hindi asking me which bank's ATM cards I use. 

Upon inquiring about why he wants to know the same, he told me that most major bank ATM cards have been blocked and can only be set straight if i give him my card details. Acting like a naive person, i give him the number 123456-chutia samjhe ho kya :)

The guy goes on defensive, says its not what I think it is. He can prove his credentials only if I give him the opportunity. To this, I just laughed. Dude went silent for a moment and then started off on an MC BC tirade which just cracked me up. Since its been a long time I have spoken to my friends from Delhi, I got excited and practiced some of my chosen shlokas too :) Guy then decided to finally cut the call.

After 2 minutes, without much hope, I just called back on the same number. I got an automated message in Bengali saying the number I am trying to call is busy. That sort of surprised me, and I feel this number is easily traceable. So regarding that, what should my next step be? There are bound to be people who fall in these scammers trap easily.

I already sent the phone number to the phishing reporting email IDs for SBI, HDFC, and ICICI.
