Few hours ago I got a phone call from a person who claimed to be from a NGO called "Relief India Trust". He said there is a little girl who is suffering from blood cancer and the family is incapable of paying hospital charges. It is **urgent** and important. He said I can go to their website ( indiarelieftrust dot org ) and check for medical documents of the little girl. He requested me to help the girl and donate fund online through their site as soon as possible.

Now there are several questions:
-Is the NGO legit ? By legit I do not mean legal but will money I donate to them would reach to the needy people ?

-How did they get my contact number ? I am privacy conscious person and I do not even give my email easily, and except for few major sites (Facebook, Google etc) I have never submitted my mobile number elsewhere.

-I made a Google search and found that the NGO has been scamming people

-The person who called me, informed the girl's name was "Dazy Parveen" and it was emergency and needed immediate medical attention as she was suffering from **blood cancer** . But I found that they have been calling people and mentioning same name since as early as **March 2015**(that was oldest comment I could find on a website)

The NGO seems a fraud to me. Did anyone get call from them as well ?
