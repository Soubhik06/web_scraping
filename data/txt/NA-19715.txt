# The Ticker

Ticker is a collection of interesting security news and happenings in Indian companies and startups.

- Jio's customer data leak [[Factordaily](https://factordaily.com/news/reliance-jio-user-data-breach-magicapk/)]

- Byju's internal development details, customer information, credentials leak [[Twitter](https://twitter.com/shantanugoel/status/885078091294539776)]

*Ed Note:* Indian companies continue to take customers' (and in most cases, their own) data. In response of the above two reports, several users shared similar issues with many other companies ranging from other cellular operators leaking customer data or companies keeping their trello boards (and other tools) open to public unknowingly.

Also to be noted is the fact that though Jio news got some coverage, the byju leak got through completely unreported in the media. Even for Jio leak, all publications missed the point that came out in the open later on that every Jio retailer has access to the Jio customer database and can perform searches on any of the customer. It is not long before there's an underground market to sell this information (maybe it already exists).

- Indian firm explores secure quantum communications from Russia [[ET](http://economictimes.indiatimes.com/articleshow/59581966.cms)]
