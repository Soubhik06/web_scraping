sui generis that mandates its categorical exclusion from the judicial indulgence

for granting benefit of regular bail. Digital economy is the unassailable locus of

modern commerce, sustained entirely by the bedrock of public trust. Cyber

Fraud acts as a corrosive insurgency, causing not merely an isolated pecuniary

loss, but an aggravated systemic damage upon the public financial exchequer,

thereby inflicting profound in rem detriment. Given its borderless architecture,
