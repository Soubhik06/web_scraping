Heard Sri Sanjiv Sharan, learned counsel for the

 Sri Samrendra Kumar Jha, learned counsel for the informant. 2. This application for grant of regular bail

 the I.T. Act, 2000. 3. The petitioner is a member of a gang

 involved in sextortion. It is alleged that the petitioner demanded

 Rs.2,00,00,000/- from the informant, who is a Member of

 Parliament. The informant, on the instructions of the Police, has

 deposited an amount of Rs.1,00,000/- in the bank account of the

 petitioner and that is how the petitioner and co-accused Anish
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.68495 of 2023(2) dt.19-10-2023 2/2 Kumar were traced and caught by the Police. 4. This Court has no sympathy for such

 criminals who are involved in sextortion. This application is

 accordingly rejected. 5. The trial Court is directed to expedite the

 trial of the petitioner and other accused persons of this case and

 receipt/production of a copy of this order. 6. Let a copy of this order be communicated to

 the District Judge, Patna forthwith through FAX or e-mail for its

 compliance. (Sandeep Kumar, J)
