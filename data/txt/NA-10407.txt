Act , P.S.-Khair, District-Aligarh. 2.	Both the bail cancellation application No. 838 of 2023, which has been filed for cancelling the bail granted to Suresh Kumar @ Suresh Sagar and Somveer, and bail cancellation application No. 840 of 2023 filed for cancelling bail of Gaurav Kumar were heard together. The case was taken up in revised call but no one appeared on behalf of the accused Suresh Kumar @ Suresh Sagar, Somveer and Gaurav Kumar despite the fact that vakalatnama on their behalf has been filed by two counsels namely Ms. Reena Pal, Advocate (A/R-0140/2021) and Sri Adarsh Srivastava, Advocate (A/A- 0020/2016). 3.	The prosecution story as unfolded from the First Information Report is that applicant who is informant had lodged the First Information Report against accused Suresh Kumar @ Suresh Sagar, Somveer and Gaurav Kumar under Section 384 , 504 , 506 of I.P.C., alleging that these three persons for last three months were harassing and blackmailing her daughter. They were threatening to upload her nude photographs on the social media in case Two Lacs Rupees was not given. When the said fact came into the knowledge of the informant he went to the house of the accused, who threatened the informant and demanded money. The three accused were sending vulgar messages on the Mobile No. i.e. 9837969193 of the informant. The said messages were sent from Mobile No. 8218903798. On 23.09.2022 and thereafter on 24.09.2022 again nude photographs of the daughter of the informant were sent not only on the mobile of the informant but also on the mobile of his brother-in-law, Bhupendra Singh. 4.	The statement of the informant under Section 161 was recorded by I.O. Thereafter, on 16.09.2022, statement under Section 161 of the victim was also recorded. Subsequently, Section 354-B and Section 67-A of the I.T. Act were added. 5.	Accused, Suresh Kumar @ Suresh Sagar and Somveer Singh moved an anticipatory bail application under Section 438 Cr.P.C. being Application No. 2666 of 2023 before District and Sessions Judge, Aligarh enclosing their Aadhaar Card wherein the number mentioned on the Aadhaar Card of Suresh Sagar was shown as 1669 1126 9817, while the Aadhaar Card of Somveer Singh revealed the number as 2739 6730 8120. The anticipatory bail application of Suresh Sagar and Somveer Singh was rejected by Sessions Judge, Aligarh on 22.05.2023. The I.O. submitted the charge-sheet on 17.07.2023. On the said charge-sheet, Judicial Magistrate, Khair, Aligarh on 04.08.2023 passed cognizance order and directed that Suresh Chandra @ Suresh Sagar and Somveer @ Sonveer who were released on interim bail till 10.08.2023 will surrender on that date. While summons were issued for appearance to Gaurav, the third accused. 6.	Earlier Gaurav Kumar, one of the accused had moved a bail application which was rejected on 06.10.2022 by Judicial Magistrate, Khair, Aligarh. While the other two accused Somveer Singh and Suresh Sagar were extended interim bail by Judicial Magistrate, Khair, District-Aligarh on 03.08.2022. 7.	On 02.08.2023, the applicant lodged another F.I.R. against Somveer and Suresh Sagar under Section 468 , 471 of I.P.C. alleging that the Aadhaar Number of Somveer disclosed in the anticipatory bail application was 2739 6730 8120, while that of Suresh Sagar being 1669 1126 9817. While Suresh Sagar has another Aadhaar Card in the name of Suresh Chandra son of Mahipal, Aadhaar No. 2641 1669 3004 which was used to purchase a sim card. In the said ration card, Aadhaar No. of Suresh and Somveer has been shown as 3327 2502 4217 and 8120 2739 0730. On 31.07.2023, a bail application was moved jointly by Somveer Singh and Suresh Sagar before Judicial Magistrate, Khair, Aligarh wherein an affidavit has been filed by Somveer Singh disclosing his Aadhaar Card No. 2739 6730 8120. The said affidavit is dated 10.08.2023. The Judicial Magistrate, Khair, Aligarh on 10.08.2023 granted bail to both Somveer Singh and Suresh Sagar. On the same day i.e. 10.08.2023, the applicant moved an application before the Judicial Magistrate alleging that the Aadhaar No. disclosed by both the accused Somveer Singh and Suresh Sagar are forged and fabricated. 8.	Learned counsel appearing for the applicant submitted that it is not only a case of forgery of documents by accused but also the offence under Section 354-B I.P.C. and 67- A of I.T. Act has been made out. According to him, Judicial Magistrate while extending the bail to the accused had not recorded any reasoning for doing so. The bail order is cryptic and does not assign the reason as to why the bail has been granted to the accused. Reliance has been placed upon decision of Apex Court rendered in case of Ms. Y vs. State of Rajasthan and another , Criminal Appeal No. 649 of 2022, decided on 19.04.2022. 9.	Learned A.G.A. has also supported the argument raised by the applicant. 10.	I have heard applicant counsel as well as learned A.G.A. and perused the material on record. 11.	The allegations in the F.I.R. are serious in nature. The accused had sent vulgar messages and nude photographs of the victim/daughter of the informant, not only on the mobile phone of the informant but also on the mobile phone of his brother-in-law. The charge-sheet was submitted by I.O. on 17.07.2023. 12.	The court below without recording any reason, or noting the facts of the case proceeded to enlarge the accused on bail. Initially, two of the co-accused, Somveer Singh and Suresh Sagar @ Suresh Chandra were granted interim bail who were subsequently granted regular bail on 10.08.2023 after the submission of charge-sheet and the cognizance order by the trial court. 13. Prima facie, the Aadhaar Card which has been used while moving anticipatory bail application, for obtaining ration card, and sim card is different with the same name but different Aadhaar numbers. In view of the decision of Apex Court and also looking to the gravity of the offence and material on record, the bail order dated 10.08.2023 passed by Judicial Magistrate, Khair, Aligarh granting bail to Suresh Sagar and Somveer Singh is hereby cancelled, and the bail order dated 21.10.2022 granted by Additional District Judge, Court No. 16, Aligarh to Gaurav Kumar also stands cancelled. 17.
