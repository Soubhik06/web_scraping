 Hence, to say that the offence under the GujCTOC Act
 would not be attracted in view of the offences registered
 against the applicant under the Prohibition Act is a
 misnomer. The trail of money from the illicit liquor trade
 can only be unearthed during the custodial interrogation
 of the applicant. Hence, this Court is not inclined to
 exercise discretion in favour of the applicant. Thus, the
