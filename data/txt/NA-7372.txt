Heard counsel for the parties. The applicant has been named as accused in Crime
No.09/2014 registered with S.T.F., Police Station, Bhopal -
commonly known as VYAPAM scam cases. The role ascribed to the applicant is that he paid
Rs.50,000/- to one Ramavtar - his own relative, who
promised him that he would arrange for some impersonator
through some middleman to appear in the examination for
appointment on the post of Forest Guard. It is noticed that
the charge-sheet has already been filed concerning the
applicant. That presupposes that the investigation against the
applicant is complete at that stage. It is also noticed that
Ramavtar, who is relative of the applicant has been arrested
and is in Jail. The middleman Deepak Jain, who arranged for
the impersonator, cannot be arrested because he has
reportedly died in some accident. The name of the
impersonator is not disclosed, which was known to Deepak
Jain and at best, Ramavtar, who facilitated the applicant. Since Ramavtar is already in custody and as no further
investigation is pending against the applicant coupled with
the fact that there is nothing to indicate that the applicant
 directly interacted with the middleman or racketeer, in the
 interest of justice, the applicant deserves to be granted bail
 on following conditions to be strictly observed by the
 applicant:- Murena (M.P.) once a week on every
 Sunday between 10:00 a.m. to 12:00 noon
 unless required to attend the office of the
 Investigating Officer at Bhopal on that day.
 The bail application stands disposed of accordingly.
