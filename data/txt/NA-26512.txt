Got a call from a guy pretending to be from Icici retention team and informed me that my company invested 50k few years ago for a pension scheme as a initial payment but was inactive as I didn’t make any contribution after the initial payment. I did some scrutiny and he gave me his manager number and id. To gain my trust he even confirmed my address, company address, Emp Id and other details. Which was absolutely correct and I was flabbergasted. Made me wonder how secured our data is.
I knew this was a scam and didn’t want to involve in any argument as they have all my details. I told him I’ll reach out to my local branch and deal with them. He then mentioned the branch won’t give you the option we are providing as a retention team which is either pay thé cancellation fee and claim all the amount with interest which is around 1L or pay reactivation fee and continue your policy. I am sure they might be luring others by the same scam so please be careful.
