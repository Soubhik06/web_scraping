So i have seen a lot of scam posts on this sub in last few days. A small task scam, Bumble scam and what not. 

I have had my share of scams and missellings. With time I have realised this - "Nothing is free". There are no free lunches. So now if a stranger or a person less acquainted with me tries to give me a favour/product/advice that will benefit me a lot, my first thought is - what will this person gain from this. Because he cant surely care about me for nothing.

So If you get a message to earn quick money
Or a girl/boy asks you out within the first few chats
Or if you get a mail from UK saying you won 1million
Or you get a message from esteemed brothers who dont want your electricity to be disconnected
Or an empathic banker calls to furnish your KYC over call
Heck, even if your own bank offers you an "incredible plan/insurance that you can't refuse"

You simply question what the other person stands to gain from this.
