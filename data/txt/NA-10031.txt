1. Heard Mr. Prabhu Prasanna Behera, learned Counsel (in Virtual

 Mode) for the Applicant. 2. The allegation in the Original Application is that Respondent Nos.12,

 13 & 14 are operating Chromites Mines in the South Kaliapani

 Chromites Mines and Sukurangi Chromites Mines, under Jajpur-

 Tahasil, District- Jajpur, Odisha and are discharging untreated

 effluent into the Damshala Nala. 3. It is also alleged that the site was inspected by the Odisha State

 Pollution Control Board on 28.12.2017 and 29.12.2017 to verify the

 status of operation of upgraded ETP. It was stated that the mine had

 completed and commissioned the upgraded ETP of capacity 1200

 KLD and 600 KLD to treat the mine drainage water of "D" quarry &

 "F" quarry since 30.10.2017. 4. It is alleged that from the effluent analysis report it was revealed that

 Hexavalent Chromium Concentration at the outlet of the ETP of "F" 1 quarry was 0.029 mg/l and at the outlet of the ETP of "D" quarry was

 0.027 mg/l. 5. It is also alleged that the inspection report was submitted on

 07.08.2019 with regard to South Kaliapani Chromite Mines wherein it

 was stated that the mine has constructed 4135 m creator boulder

 wall, 13,800 m earthen garland drain and 19 settling pits. The mine

 has also constructed about 4200 mts garland drain around the

 dump. The surface run off which collectes in the settling pit is

 pumped out to the existing ETP for treatment. However, it is alleged

 that the runoff collection and treatment facility is not provided for ore

 stack yard and south side of OB dump-1. Part of runoff generated

 from ore stack yard, OB dump-1 and new OB dump (Dump-2) near

 security gate of Sukurangi Chromite mine was directly discharged to

 the outside which ultimately flows into the Damsala Nalla. 6. The Odisha State Pollution Control Board also took water samples of

 the runoff water discharged from OB dump-1 and new OB dump-2

 which was sent for analysis and Hexavalent Chromium concentration

 (Cr+6) was found to be higher than the requisite 0.05 mg/l. In this

 view of the matter, the Respondent No.5 recommended that direction

 may be issued to the Unit to rectify the lapses in surface runoff

 management system. 7. It is stated that a show-cause notice was also issued to Respondent

 Nos. 13 and 14 i.e., South Kaliapani Chromites Mines and

 Sukurangi Chromites Mines respectively show-cause as to why the

 Consent to Operate granted to the mine up to 01.03.2020, not to be

 revoked. 8. It is stated that the Respondent No.13 filed its reply stating that the

 entire constructions shall be completed by May, 2020, before 2 monsoon and that they have maintained the slope and designed the

 terraces in such a manner that no water is allowed to go beyond the

 lease area and they shall ensure to maintain the same till permanent

 mitigative measures are in place. 9. In our opinion, matter requires consideration. 10. Issue notice to the Respondents. 11. Mr. Prasenjeet Mohapatra, Additional Standing Counsel who is

 appearing in Virtual Mode accepts notice on behalf of State

 Respondents i.e., Respondent Nos.1,2,6,7,9,10 12. Ms. Papiya Banerjee Bihani, learned Counsel who is present in court

 accepts notice on behalf of Respondent Nos.4,5 & 8. 13. Mr. Ashok Prasad, learned Counsel who is present in court accepts

 notice on behalf of Respondent No.11 i.e., CPC B. 14. Issue notice to Respondent Nos.12, 13 and 14, returnable within

 three weeks. 15. All the Respondents shall file their counter-affidavits within three

 weeks. 16. Considering the allegations made we deem it appropriate to constitute

 a Committee comprising of the following Members:- 17. The Committee shall visit the site in question of both the Mines of

 Respondent Nos.13 and 14 and inspect the same with reference to

 the allegations made in the Original Application as well as other

 violations, and violations of conditions of Consent to Operate if any, 3 and submit its report with its observations and recommendations and

 in case, violations are found the Committee shall also compute the

 Environmental Compensation and fasten liability against the

 violators. The Committee shall submit its report within one month. 18. Mr. Prabhu Prasanna Behera, learned Counsel for the Applicant shall

 provide e-copy/soft copy of the Original Application along with all its

 annexures to Mr. Prasenjeet Mohapatra, Ms. Papiya Banerjee Bihani

 and Mr. Ashok Prasad in the course of the day. 19. List on 07.11.2022. ..................................... B. Amit Sthalekar, JM
