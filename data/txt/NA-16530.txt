Hi all, 

This post isn't to take jabs at anyone in particular. Just an observation I have noted chatting with people I know, and from what I read online as well. 

Unfortunately, due to the amount of major scams that have come out from India over the past few years- Americans have started to grow a mistrust in Indians, and it actually sucks for Indians who are doing legit work. For example, there are many Indian recruiters with legitimate job offers looking for candidates, but on the other end- due to the "PTSD" that came from these major scams, they are having a tough time getting a hold of major candidates in America. They get hung up on, don't get messages back, blocked, or even reported. This coming from a few Indian friends of mine who work in IT as managers and recruiters. 

Americans in the tech sector are starting to grow an animosity against Indians due to the influx of Indian workers in the tech industry. Thankfully most understand it is exploitations by the company, not the fault of the workers. 

I have a Indian friend who works for a major bank call center and she told me that many people hear her voice and start questioning her, ask to speak with someone else, or just hang up. These are customers calling the bank customer support! Not the other way around. 

What I am trying to say is, if you are Indian and have any sort of South-Asian accent in general, it's getting tough. 

I am Indian-American, first generation born and raised in the U.S (family came from India in the 1980s). So I understand both sides of the issue. 

Anyway, has anyone else witnessed this issue? Just curious.
