Like an idiot, I did not make my FB page locked and private. Please do this asap!

Someone went through my FB page, downloaded a picture of mine, and used it to create a Whatsapp account with my name. This person then identified a bunch of my relatives from FB, and (I am assuming here) he tried to find their phone number on google. Two of my uncle's number was available on Google (they have their business). The person then whatsapped them asking for INR 2 lacs (using my identity), mentioning I urgently needed money! 

While no one paid this person, and we figured out the identity theft and scam relatively early, this has been super disturbing for me personally. 

Guys, I reiterate, please lock your FB profile.
