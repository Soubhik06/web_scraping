Hello r/bangalore I am newly shifting to Bangalore with my mother. What are all things one should check and know while searching for flats/houses to rent in bengaluru ? 

Below are few ones I have come across:

\- Physically visit the place. Beware of token amount scam.

\- How are the roads to the home. How will it fare during rainy season.

\- Talk to existing tenant. (not sure if owners will allow this)

\- Avoid brokers, if not Keep brokerage to 15 days rent.

\- Keep deposit under 4 months rent.

\- Check all the equipments in the home. (geyser, fans, lights, taps, flush)

\- Any Religious establishments nearby.

\- Check about water. borewell or tanker.

\- Clarify Maintenance. Water and Electricity bills. How it is calculated. Garbage disposal.

\- Know about the Lock in period and Notice period. Painting charges while leaving.

\- Don't pay token amount for visiting the place.
