Recently, I encountered an Amazon delivery scam in Ahmedabad that raises serious concerns about the platform's delivery reliability. Here's a detailed account of the incident:

I ordered an item through Amazon Prime delivery, and the initial tracking showed the package was out for delivery at 8 AM. By 12 PM, Amazon unexpectedly messaged that the delivery would be delayed, instructing me to contact customer service. Later that evening at 9 PM, I received a delivery confirmation message, but no actual parcel arrived.

After raising a query with Amazon's customer service, the agent claimed they would contact the delivery person and update me. Subsequently, Amazon stated they couldn't reach the delivery person and asked me to wait 48 hours for resolution. After the waiting period, they simply offered a refund and suggested reordering.
Alarmingly, the exact same pattern repeated with the replacement order:

8 AM: Out for delivery
12 PM: Advised to contact Amazon
9 PM: Received a message about a delivery attempt
The delivery agent made a brief 5-second call and immediately disconnected when I tried to call back

These recurring incidents suggest a potential systematic issue with Amazon's delivery process in my area. The consistent pattern of delayed and undelivered packages, coupled with unhelpful customer service responses, raises red flags about potential fraudulent activities.
