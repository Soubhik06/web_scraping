This is first bail application filed by the applicant under Section 482 of the

 Bhartiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 before this Court for grant of

 anticipatory bail. The applicant is apprehending his arrest in connection

 with Crime No.188/2024 registered at Police Station Ganj, Raipur (C.G.)

 for offences punishable under Section 420 and 120 B of the IPC, Section

 7 of C.G. Gambling Prohibition Act, 2022, Section 66C of Information

 Technology Act, 2000 and Section 25 of the Indian Telegraph Act, 1885. 2. As per the prosecution case, on 22-04-2024 the police of Police Station

 Ganj, District Raipur received secret information that online gambling is

 going on in a house situated at Satkar Hotel Lane, Raipur. When police

 party conducted raid in the said house, they found accused Gurmeet

 Singh engaged in online gambling and in his memorandum he admitted

 that he engaged in online gambling through his mobile number

 9893615586 and 6266227503 and from his possession one mobile phone Digitally signed by MOHAMMAD AADIL KHAN 2 having SIM number of 9340883726 was seized from him. The offence has

been registered against Gurmeet Singh @ Rinku under Section 7 of the

C.G. Gambling Prohibition Act, 2022 and the police started investigation.

In his memorandum the accused Gurmeet Singh @ Rinku disclosed that

the persons who were having the said two mobile numbers 9893615586

and 6266227503 are at Kolkata, West Bengal from where they were

playing gambling online in IPL cricket. He also disclosed three other

mobile numbers, i.e., 8643053789, 8357862555 and 9827166626 who

are also in West Bengal, Kolkata and engaged in online gambling. When

police party after tracking location of the said mobile numbers conducted

raid in the premises of other accused persons, they have been arrested

and huge numbers of mobile phones, laptops, ATM cards have been

seized from them. From the co-accused Raj Chouhan, Ranjan Chouhan,

Babalu Chouhan laptops, mobile phones have also been seized which

was used in accounting of the business of Mahadev panel 45 and Lotus 365. Considering the nature of offence and the material seized during the

investigation, offence of Section 420 , 120B of the IPC, Section 7 of the

C.G. Gambling Prohibition Act, Section 66C of Information Technology

Act, 2000 and Section 25 of the Indian Telegraph Act, 1885 have been

added. From the laptops, mobile phones, pass-books, cheque book, ATM

cards seized from the accused persons, transactions of huge amount of

more than crores of rupees have been found and with the

correspondence made with the bank the said accounts were seized and

amount have also been seized. From the memorandum statement of

Gurmeet Singh @ Rinku, the mobile number of the present applicant

disclosed, i.e., 9827166626 and as per his memorandum the person who

is having the said mobile number is at Kolkata and connected in online

gambling. From the mobile phone of said Gurmeet Singh, the contact 3 photo and poster of the present applicant were extracted in which the

 mobile number 9827166626 is reflected and name of Mohit is also shown

 in the said mobile number. 3. Learned counsel for the applicant would submit that there is no evidence

 against the present applicant to implicate him in the alleged offence. He is

 nowhere involved in the offence and has no nexus with the said Gurmeet

 Singh. The offence is registered on 22-04-2024 only against Gurmeet

 Singh and from the FIR also there is no allegation against the present

 applicant. The present applicant was admitted in Seven Hills Healthcare

 Hospital, Vishakhapattanam from 25-04-2024 to 27-04-2024 for treatment

 of his left ankle and underwent surgery there. He would also submit that

 the other co-accused persons have been granted regular bail by the

 learned trial Court and therefore, the applicant may be enlarged on

 anticipatory bail. 4. Per contra, learned counsel for the State opposes and has submitted that

 it is huge scam of online gambling having connection with Mahadev Book

 App and Lotus 365 and also the Reddy Anna website. The accused

 persons have made syndicate and organized the crime of online gambling

 and running their panel in online platform for gambling and abstracted

 money from it. The accused persons are connected through mobile phone

 and they are working in online mode. From the other accused persons

 who were arrested in the offence, huge number of mobile phones, laptops,

 bank pass-books, cheque books, ATM cards etc. have been seized in

 which the details of account of Mahadev Book, Lotus 365 and Reddy

 Anna Book was there. From the memorandum statement of Gurmeet

 Singh, the mobile number of present applicant is disclosed which also

 finds place in the abstract of contact photo and poster abstracted from the

 mobile phone of Gurmeet Singh which reflects the same mobile number 4 9827166626 in the name of present applicant. Therefore, he is not entitled

 for anticipatory bail. 5. I have heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the case diary. 6. Considering the submissions made by learned counsel for the parties,

 considering the nature of allegation against the present applicant and also

 the evidence collected during investigation, seizure of huge number of

 mobile phones, laptops, pass-books, ATM cards etc. from the other co-

 accused persons which connects the offence with Mahadev Book App,

 Lotus 365 and Reddy Anna panel, further from the mobile phone of

 Gurmeet Singh the contact photo and poster of the present applicant has

 been extracted, it cannot be said that there is no evidence against the

 present applicant or he is not involved in the offence. Further, in view of

 the seriousness of the offence, the nature of organized crime of online

 gambling, I am not inclined to grant benefit of anticipatory bail to the

 applicant. Consequently, the application filed by the present applicant for

 grant of anticipatory bail is rejected. Sd/- (Ravindra Kumar Agrawal)
