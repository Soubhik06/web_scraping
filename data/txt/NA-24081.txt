I don't know if this is new but I came across this today while searching for a house to rent. We found an attractive listing in Whitefield (DSR Green, hope farm) and when we contacted the owner (Dude could only speak Hindi, first red flag) he asked to ping him on WhatsApp and he'll share more pics of the place. After sharing we got a call from another number (another red flag) and we discussed a few more things. When I asked him to share the location he asked me to pay a token amount (1.5k) and he'll generate a gate pass to visit and if we're not interested he would refund it. That was it for me, this was clearly a scam as there's no such system like this and moreover he was posing as an owner, but dealing like an agent. I mean noone asks for a token amount just to visit. He followed up a couple of times, I blocked that mf on whatsapp and reported fraud on Magicbricks listing, but beware folks, I'm sure there are many such listings, don't ignore the red flags and do not pay anything unless you're going ahead with the deal and you've verified that person.
