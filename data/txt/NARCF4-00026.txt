Bhai aau tankara bhauni mane,

It’s getting ridiculously difficult to book an auto through Ola, Uber, or any online platform in Bhubaneswar, especially from major hotspots like Master Canteen Railway Station, the Airport, and Kharvela Nagar area.

No matter what the app shows, they almost always demand ₹20-₹50 extra. If you refuse, they either cancel the ride, stand there and abuse you, or even instruct other autos not to take you. Even at 10-11 in the morning, they still want extra!

We all ditched bargaining with local autos and switched to online platforms, hoping for fair fares and hassle-free rides. What a joke that turned out to be! Now, we’ve simply traded one mafia for another. Seriously, what’s the point of online booking when you still have to negotiate like it’s 2010? 

If this keeps up, the only way to escape the scam is to start walking everywhere, but who knows, maybe the "Walking Mafia" will start charging a “convenience fee” for using the footpath next!
