--- POST TEXT ---

What India needs right now (more so, what the whole world needs right now) is an unbiased, *pure* source of news. An agency, be it print, television, or online - that *actually speaks about people's issues, brings scams to light, exposes wrongdoers, and generally does what journalists should ideally be doing* as the fourth pillar of democracy, is what is MUCH NEEDED right now.

However, this news agency should work entirely on crowdfunding, which will ensure that corporate and political interests are given the middle finger from the word go. The moment advertising and PR are weeded out from journalism, journalistic integrity can be brought in to save the day.

Presently, all that we have in our so-called "news" are celebrity gossip and what X politician said about Y, petty blame games and generally anything that is basically useless to the citizens of our country. What we need is a fully transparent, crowdfunded organisation with every penny accounted for -- that brings back real, fearless journalism -- in spite of the risks that come with it.

Rest assured that people would fund such an initiative far more than you'd ever imagined; if only such an idea was to ever be turned into reality! I sure would, I can guarantee that at least.

**TLDR:** Journalism is fucked thanks to advertising, PR, political and business interests - which just happen to be their only sources of generating revenue. Can someone with entrepreneurial abilities please create a crowdfunded, transparent news organisation that can bring real journalism back?
