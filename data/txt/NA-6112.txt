 CM(M) 2049/2023 & CM APPL.64216/2023 3. These petitions filed under Article 227 of the Constitution of India,
 impugns the common order dated 30.11.2023 passed by ASCJ, Saket Courts,
 New Delhi ('Trial Court') in Misc CS SCJ No. 36/2023 and Misc. CS SCJ No.
 35/2023. 3.1. The Trial Court vide the impugned order dismissed two (2) separate
 applications moved on behalf of counter claimant namely Renu Dawra and
 Sanjay Dawra under Order XXIII Rule 3 of CPC seeking setting aside of the
 decree dated 09.12.2021. 4.1 After some arguments, learned counsel for the Petitioners states on
 instructions that the Petitioners will peacefully vacate and handover the
 possession of the property bearing no. B-55, Freedom Fighter Colony, Neb Sarai,
 New Delhi-110068 ('suit property') to the Respondent on or before 31.01.2024.
 4.2 He states for verifying compliance of the said undertaking, the matter be
 listed before the Executing Court on 01.02.2024 and states that the keys will be
 handed over before Executing Court. 4.3 He further states that a petition for quashing of FIR no.38/2021 has been
 filed with the registry and the same is likely to be listed in due course before the
 Coordinate Bench after removal of objections. 5. In reply, learned counsel for Respondent states on instructions that in view
 of the statement made by the Petitioners today that they will handover vacant and
 peaceful possession on 31.01.2024, the Respondent and her husband will remain
 present before the Coordinate Bench for making statement in the quashing
 petition. 6. The statements of the parties are taken on record and they are bound down
 to the statement. 7. It is made clear that if the Petitioners resile from the statement made before
 this Court, it will tantamount to fraud on this Court and Petitioners will become
