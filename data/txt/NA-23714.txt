I asked her how much it would cost me and the price she quoted seemed suspicious I just said fine I’ll do it later, then she said “no sir there’s an online option I’ll send you the link do it now”. Then I said “1st party isn’t mandatory as per govt rules so I’ll do it later I have my third party for 5 years”. Then she starts arguing saying ”no sir who told you that it’s not mandatory, it is. Why do you think I have been calling you from morning then?” For a second I started doubted myself and then googled it and my friends doing law too told me that it’s not mandatory, and I tell her this. She says “sir so what if your friend does law even my cousin is doing law it is mandatory” I didn’t have the patience to deal with this lady I said fine relax I’ll pay it later, she says “no sir no relaxing how can you argue with me telling it is not mandatory when I’m saying it is” I cut the call cuz I didn’t want to yell and ruin my mood. 

Either this lady was trying to scam me or she was misinformed or something. Anyways pay your premiums only on websites and be safe lads. Cheers. :)
