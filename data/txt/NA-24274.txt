Hi all,

A scam happened with me at the hospital, A guy approaches me and says he needs 50rs to pay for some tests. Since 50 wasnt a great amount i gave him the money. Later he comes back and asks for 1000 rupees as his card was declining. He told me to pay at the counter for him but since the counter was very far away from the place where he approached us we decided to give him the money. He gave us his phone number, address and an emergency contact also incase his number wasnt available. Just as i was abt to hand him the money another person asks me what is going on and i explained the incident the other guy. He says all this scam and dont trust the guy asking for money. They both started arguing and just few moments later a police officer comes in sight. (Since it was a govt. hospital police officers were seen) Seeing the police officer the guy runs away. I thanked the person who helped me out and went for my work.
