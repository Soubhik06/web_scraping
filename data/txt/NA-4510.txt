 Heard learned counsel for the parties. 2. Counter affidavit has been filed in B.A. No. 5277 of 2024 in Court
 itself. 3. The petitioners have been made an accused in connection with
 Deoghar (Cyber) P.S. Case No. 45 of 2024 registered for the offences
 under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 and 120B of the Indian Penal Code
 and Sections 66(B) , 66(C) , 66(D) and 84(C) of the Information
 Technology Act, pending in the Court of learned Additional Sessions
 Judge-II-cum-Special Judge, Cybercrime, Deoghar. 4. As per FIR, it is alleged that on 01.04.2024, on the basis of secret
information, a raid was conducted by the informant along with armed
force in different places, resulting in the arrest of multiple persons,
including the petitioners. Various mobile phones and SIM cards were
seized from the suspects. The suspects, upon interrogation, failed to
produce any valid document. From preliminary investigation of the
mobiles seized from the apprehended persons, prima-facie, it appears
that they are involved in cybercrime. 5. Learned counsel for the petitioners has submitted that petitioners
are innocent and have committed no offence at all rather they have been
falsely implicated in this case. It is further submitted that as per F.I.R.,
there is recovery of several mobile phones and SIM cards from the
possession of petitioners. No money transaction has been done. The
petitioners are languishing in Judicial Custody since 02.04.2024 without
any rhymes and reasons. Petitioners have no criminal antecedents
except petitioner Sunil Mahra @ Sunil Kumar Mahra, who has one
criminal antecedent in which he is already on bail. Charge-sheet has
been submitted. Petitioners undertake to cooperate in the trial of the
case by remaining physically present as and when required and shall
not indulge in any manner in tampering with the prosecution evidences
or influencing the witnesses of prosecution. Hence, the petitioners may
be extended the privilege of bail. 6. On the other hand, learned Addl.P.P appearing for the State has
opposed the prayer for bail of the petitioners. 7. Considering the facts and circumstances of the case, nature of
allegation coupled with the materials available against the petitioners
and period of custody of the petitioners, I am inclined to release the
petitioners on bail. Accordingly, the petitioners named above are
directed to be released on bail on furnishing of bail bond of Rs.25,000/-
(Twenty five thousand) with two sureties of the like amount each to the
satisfaction of learned Additional Sessions Judge-II-cum-Special Judge,
Cybercrime, Deoghar in connection with Deoghar (Cyber) P.S. Case No.
45 of 2024 subject to the conditions:- (Pradeep Kumar Srivastava, J.)
