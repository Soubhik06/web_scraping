This Criminal Petition is filed by the petitioner - Accused

under Sections 480 and 483 of Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha

Sanhitha (for short "BNSS") to grant regular bail in Crime No.1025

of 2024 on the file of Medipally Police Station, Medchal Malkajgiri

District registered for the offences punishable under Sections 376 (2) (n), 384, 506 of Indian Penal Code , Section 5(1) read with 6 of Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012 , Section 66-C , 67 , 67-A of Information Technology Act, 2008 2. The case of the prosecution in brief was that on

26.08.2024 at 19:00 hours, the victim girl came to the police station

and lodged a report stating that she was a student of B-Pharmacy 1st

year and while she was studying intermediate, the accused person

followed her and stated that he was in love with her. She also believed

his words. After some days, the accused person took her to Boduppal

OYO room and met her physically in the guise of love and marriage.

After that he collected her phone number and many times met her 2 Dr.GRR,J Crl.P. No.12863 of 2024 physically. He also took some nude videos and photos of her through

his phone without her knowledge and by showing the said nude videos

and photos, he forcibly extracted amount from her. The accused

threatened her and took 10 grams of gold and net cash of Rs.30,000/-.

On coming to know about his behavior when she started avoiding

talking to him, he started blackmailing her and created a fake

instagram account on her name and uploaded her nude photos and

videos. Basing on the said complaint, the above case was registered as

Crime No.1025 of 2024 for the above said offences. 3. Heard the learned counsel for the petitioner-accused and

the learned Additional Public Prosecutor for the respondent-State. 4. Learned counsel for the petitioner-accused submitted

that it was a love matter and when the matter was leaked to her

parents, her parents arranged another marriage proposal to her. After

registering the case also, the victim went away with accused. Only

on threatening by her parents, she filed a false case against the

accused. The victim was now a major aged about 21 years. The

petitioner was in custody since 24.09.2024 and prayed to enlarge the

petitioner-accused on bail. Dr.GRR,J Crl.P. No.12863 of 2024 5. Learned Additional Public Prosecutor opposed grant of

bail to the petitioner contending that the allegations against the

petitioner were grave in nature. 6. Perused the record. Considering the allegations against

the petitioner that he not only threatened the victim girl and

blackmailed her by taking her nude photos and videos but also

uploaded the same in Social Media, it is considered not a fit case to

enlarge the petitioner-accused on bail. 7. In the result, the Criminal Petition is dismissed.
