I have premium banking option with SCB. The relationship manager suggested me a endowment plan. I thought I will post it here and get perspectives of it. Below details he gave me as an example. 
Details: 
Yearly premium: 5L
Duration: 7 years
Cooling period: 8 years
Dividend dispersal start on: 16th year
Dividend: 3.5L
Dividend duration: 14 years. 
On 30th year: 110% return on premium paid. i.e I get 38.5L back. 

Seem to be a tie up scheme with ICICI prudential. 

He said annual ROI around 8.6%

As per my personal perception I think it's better to go for index funds instead of these schemes. 
Insurance schemes like endowment are a scam and only term insurance actually makes sense for salaried people.
