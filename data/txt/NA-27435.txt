Earlier today I downloaded “Tunnel Bear” a free VPN service. After closing out of my chrome tab I noticed on my MacBooks desktop a very similar message to the photo posted above. The map showed a city in India, so I clicked “Don’t Allow” (I don’t live in India). I found this strange since I used the VPN for North American servers only. After some research, it seems this may be from the origin of the VPN service confusing my Apple ID and prompted this message. I also saw a post in r/apple from a few days ago explaining a recent Apple ID scam. So needless to say im a bit worried. Im aware if you are the target of a scam freaking out makes it easier for phishing attempts. So I took a breath, and on a different device I started to change all my passwords, iCloud, Gmail, Paypal, etc… I also deleted all “Tunnel Bear” applications on my Mac and any other files I didn’t recognize. At this point I feel relatively safe which brings me to reddit. Am I actually safe and Is there something im missing?
