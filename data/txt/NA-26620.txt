So ill try to keep it short and precise. 
I have made enough stupid financial mistakes in my life ranging from mindless trading, investing in ULIPs getting scammed during covid (online purchase). 

Present day, when I had made peace with my past mistakes and started saving judiciously Life has other plans. 

This cousin brother calls me suddenly out of nowhere and asks me for 95k urgently and said he’d pay it back in the next morning. He’s a smart guy and their family not so well to do financially and he’s always hustling, so I thought it could be for his new business that has been trying to setup. 

Today he calls me in the evening explaining me what has happened and turns out he’s fallen for the classic TELEGRAM scam where they keep taking money promising this will be the last payment before they repay with interested. He was at wits end so called me and told me they were asking to pay 4.83L and they’d repay everything with interest. 

I am not sure how to get my 95k back now. I’m toiling in a different city away from home grinding my ass saving money by not going in flights and choosing trains. And life showed me this.

He’s lost 2.25Lakhs. Not sure how I’m going to ask my money back.

Thanks for reading through. Money, luck and I aren’t compatible I guess.
