A friend of mine, living in rural Kentucky, in her 70's and not very savvy, received a call on Thursday from someone with an Indian accent, and he convinced her that she could upgrade the quality on her Spectrum cable TV connection (she doesn't have Spectrum internet). I don't know the particulars of their conversation but I know he got her Spectrum account number and passcode, and got her to agree to send a check for $299.79 to a post office box in Albany, New York within three days. Fortunately she checked with me first and didn't send them a check. I did a Google search and found one sheriff's office somewhere saying that "Skyline Techno" was a scam. 

I called Spectrum and they said there had been no funny business with her account and they were going to send her a new access code.

If anyone knows how to get this information to the right authorities, please do so. I submitted information to a post office form online, but it seemed inadequate. I also gave the information to Spectrum. 

I attempted to give the Skyline Techno address and phone information, but a bot deleted the earlier message, but the p0st 0ffice was the New Scotland branch in Albany New York.
