As another assassination plot against Modi *kaka* is "revealed", lets rewind a bit on how many times such plots have been exposed whenever elections draw near:-

- Oct 2009: ToI Ahmedabad - [Phone calls threaten Modi assassination, two detained](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/ahmedabad/Phone-calls-threaten-Modi-assassination-two-detained/articleshow/5119618.cms).

- Dec 2010: Zee News - [LeT planned to kill Modi, set up bases in Kerala, TN](https://mobile.twitter.com/zeenews/status/11617375690432512).

- Apr 2013: TV9 Gujarati - [Journalist Madhu Kishwar claims Narendra Modi is a target for assassination according to some "Central govt" official](https://mobile.twitter.com/tv9gujarati/status/324844678610563072).

- Oct 2013: ANI - [*Mera aarop hai,kahi Nitish Kumar ne Mujahideen ke saath milkar Narendra Modi ki hatya ki sajish toh nahi ki,eska jawab chahiye:Giriraj Singh* (Translation - I demand an answer to my accusation - has Nitish Kumar planned to assassinate Narendra Modi with the *Mujahideens*)](https://mobile.twitter.com/ANI/status/395780766983933952).

- Nov 2013: TV9 Gujarati - [Human bombs meant to kill Narendra Modi, says arrested IM operative Imtiyaz](http://www.dailymotion.com/video/x1760nm).

- May 2018: Zee News - [ISIS operatives planned to assassinate PM Narendra Modi, claims Gujarat ATS chargesheet](http://zeenews.india.com/india/isis-operatives-planned-to-assassinate-pm-narendra-modi-claims-gujarat-ats-chargesheet-2106902.html).
