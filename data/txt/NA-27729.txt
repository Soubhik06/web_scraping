Guys, beware of these telegram scam. Im posting this to avoid further damage in the community. 

These indian voices with unknown numbers will call you and they will tell you that: you are selected to join the company etc and will tell that it is regulated by google etc. 

Then they will give you simple tasks to do, like to rate 5 star those location in google maps, and each rate will reward you with corresponding small amount like ₱50-100. 

After you complete the tasks, they send you the payment. 

And for you to continue, there are specific task in between that they will ask you to shed some of your money. They call it “pre-payment” to proceed to the next task with big commission. 

First they will give you your commission and once you’re excited and happy to move forward to the next task, they will ask you again for pre-payment with a much larger amount. 

Then they will give you a tricky situation, then once you messed up. They will tell you it’s your mistake. 

For you to get your money and fix your mistake, they will ask you again a specific amount much bigger from the first one like ₱30,000 to continue. 

Once you settle the ₱30000. They will tell you again you cannot withdraw your money since you have a small “credit score” in their trading website. 

The website is 599bit.com

They will ask you to shed ₱50000 to increase your credit score. 

And that time you will realise all you are doing is shedding money to them. 

Carefull with these people. Most of them are in telegram and whats app. With indian accent when they will call you in your number.
