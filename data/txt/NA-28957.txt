New Delhi, Mar 16 (PTI) Bharat Sharma led the Delhi Sports Journalists Association (DSJA) to two runner-up finishes in the table tennis competitions at the Sports Journalists Federation of India (SJFI) National Convention here on Monday.
DSJA-1, comprising Bharat Sharma, Kushan Sarkar and Norris Pritam, lost to Sports Journalists Association of Mumbai-1 (SJAM-1) represented by Amol Karhadkar and Ashwin Ferro in the team championship final of the AC Bali Table Tennis Tournament at the Roshanara Club.
In the men’s doubles event, Bharat and Kushan again finished runners-up after losing the final to the Mumbai pair of Amol and Ashwin.

Amol also enjoyed a successful outing in the individual events, winning the men’s singles title ahead of Keerthi of the Tamil Nadu Sports Journalists Association.
North Zone win JK Bose Inter-Zonal T20 Trophy ================================== North Zone won the JK Bose Inter-Zonal T20 Cricket Trophy after registering a dominant victory over East Zone at the Roshanara Cricket Ground.
Batting first, East Zone posted 135 for 6 in their 20 overs, with Pranab Shil scoring 47 not out off 23 balls and captain Abhishek remaining unbeaten on 39.
North Zone chased down the target emphatically, reaching 136 without loss in just 9.5 overs. Rishabh Singh smashed an unbeaten 87 off 40 balls while captain Akash Rawal remained not out on 31.
Rishabh finished as the tournament’s top scorer with 289 runs.
In another match, West Zone defeated South Zone by 10 wickets at the Delhi Police Ground. South Zone were bowled out for 97 in 18.5 overs, with V.L. Sashank scoring 34. Deep Sudhakar (4/13) and Taus Rizvi (3/13) led the bowling effort.
West Zone chased the target comfortably, reaching 98 without loss in 13 overs, with Taus Rizvi scoring an unbeaten 53 and Devendra Pandey contributing 36 not out.
In other events, Bengaluru emerged champions in the basketball competition with Indore finishing runner-up, while in the futsal (freekick) event Sports Journalists Association of Mumbai-2 (SJAM-2) were crowned champions and Sports Journalists Association of Mumbai-1 (SJAM-1) finished runners-up.
The sporting competitions were held as part of the Golden Jubilee celebrations of the SJFI National Convention in New Delhi, hosted by the Delhi Sports Journalists Association.
