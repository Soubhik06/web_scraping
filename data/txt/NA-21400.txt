As the title says, I got scammed for Rs. 50,000/-.

I applied for a credit card while opening my salary bank account. My credit card was not being approved for some reason and I was receiving calls from different numbers saying that my Card will be approved and soon it will be delivered. I was receiving calls multiple calls a day to confirm my personal details which the caller already had with them.

On 21st June, a lady called me and said your card is approved and now just confirm your delivery address. Later in the evening, I received a call from another lady, who had all my card details (including CVV) and asked me to share the OTP. I thought her to be genuine and foolishly shared the OTP without paying attention that it was for a transaction which I came to notice later on.

Later, on 28th July, I received a message from the bank to pay the dues and I was shocked. I called my relationship manager and he said someone has took the money to NOBROKER BENGALURU IN. I never had any transaction nor I know the party.

I have reported the same to the cyber crime and have submitted the dispute form of the transaction. Till date I have not received my physical credit card and all my info has been already leaked.

Request all folks here to guide me further.
