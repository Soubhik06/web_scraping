I went to Lamington Road after many years and things have changed as far as I can tell. Its not a reputable place to buy pc hardware anymore except for couple of shops. Majority of them are outright shady and try to scam you.

I found this one shop who gave me such a tempting quote for my whole build that I was literally surprised at the price difference quoted by other shop and online websites. I was about to make a decision to buy from the shop but **he told me to pay Rs.2000 in advance in order to do some registration or something.** Without seeing any component in front of me, he wanted me to pay him advance for the order. I confronted him but he again went on to the same rant of making proper bill and all that warranty bullshit. I knew he was trying to scam me cause no way I would pay him any advance. I had so much to say to that guy but I decided to leave the place as I was not in the mood of arguing but the level of cheat and dishonesty is shocking. I mean what the fuck is wrong with this people?
