I saw ad in Instagram for online simple job.

After that he gave me telegram id and asked me to message them , when I did ,she asked my basic details and my bank account details for money transfer. 

And she transferred ₹150 to my bank. 

Then she added me to a group which has 20 tasks which is to like all and send screenshot. 
I did that and at end of day she sent me ₹200 to my bank. ( ₹10 per video like ) 

Next day task was to join in VIP group which pays ₹50 per video like and ₹4500 if we like on exact given time. 

After giving 2 task they messaged in group that we need to pay them money for covering ads cost and they'll return the money with 25% extra within 20 mins. 
I know this is scam from the beginning but for fun i went with it untill they ask for money. 

The suprising part was the group has more than 2000 people and many many people sent they the money in sum of ₹1000,₹5000,12000.

They build trust by giving the money at beginning and has taken and scammed way high from many people.
