Just a heads up folks, was hit by a new type of fraud call today. Thankfully had the good sense to not get entrapped because I have worked in fintech and am super aware of fishy signs. A number calls you, you pick up and the automated voice says 'This is an emergency call from Customs. You have an issue with your parcel. Press 9 for further details '. If you press 9 it connects you to some gavar dumbass who goes HALOOOO. His accent was sign enough for me to know this isn't a custom agent. So I kept the call. Never spoke. Then I searched and there is a tweet about a similar incident, only the person didn't cut the call and was threatened with false report of transporting drugs and ATMs.
