--- POST TEXT ---

I was at the Indian Oil Station near Domlur , and I asked the guy to fill fuel for ₹3000 in Kannada. The scammer made his assistant fill it only for ₹500 and when I asked him about it he said that he misheard me . (500 and 3000 sound nothing alike in Kannada btw)

So he told me that they'll fill the remaining ₹2500 and in the meantime he tried to distract me by giving the QR code and asking me to pay the ₹3000 upfront so I can save time . I told him that i wasn't in a hurry and that I'll pay when it's done , to which he replied by suggesting I pay to his personal number as it's easier . I declined and said I'll scan and pay when it's filled . While all this was happening, his assistant finished filling for ₹2500 , but the guy didn't reset the meter to 0 , which means he continued filling from ₹500 and that only ₹2500 worth of petrol was filled to my car . Then the scammers asked me to pay them ₹3000 coz they claimed to have filled from 0 to ₹500 , reset the meter and filled again from 0 to ₹2500 . 

Luckily I was aware of this scam thanks to a Reddit post I read about this a few months back , so I asked him why he didn't reset the meter to 0 for the second time . He knew I had caught him so he tried to coverup by blaming his idiot assistant and asked me to pay only ₹2500. 

TLDR : I asked an Indian Oil Station guy to fill petrol for ₹3000 , but he only filled for ₹500 , then said that he'll fill the remaining ₹2500 again . But he didn't reset the meter to 0 the second time and continued filling from the ₹500 mark , so essentially he filled only ₹2500 worth of fuel but asked for ₹3000 . 

It would be helpful if you guys post a review about it [here](https://maps.app.goo.gl/MhaQa3NHYBFhM3yd8) so that more people are aware of this .

(The place is saved as an Emission Test Centre in Google maps, but it still fills fuel under the name of Indian Oil)
