I had applied to several jobs on platforms like indeed and Linkdin and some directly at the hospitals websites. I got a message with a link from Apollo hospital sending my profile has been selected and it’s an interview offer letter. When I opened it, it stated that for offline interview I have to pay 2990/- as a security deposit which will be refunded once I’m done with my interview no matter if I get the job or not. And 1990/-for online interview. The first step of the interview was to cross verify my resume with the HR through WhatsApp which I did. They asked me to give some basic details and then sent me an upi scan to send the money. Is this a new sort of scam?
