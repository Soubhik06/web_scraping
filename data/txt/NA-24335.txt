I saw a post today on the Swiggy delivery scam (Though this wasn't really a Swiggy scam) so I thought of sharing the one that I came across.

A few days prior, I placed an order with Swiggy. After some time, I noticed the delivery guy's location was unchanged and he was still near the restaurant. 
On calling him, he said he is at the location and couldn't find me. He gave me his WhatsApp number and asked me to share my current location.

After a while, he called and told me that my location is not matching the Swiggy app destination. Initially, he blamed me for entering the location incorrectly, then transferred the blame to the app after some argument.

He said my shared location is 4+ km away from his current location. He can only deliver the order if I give him more money, as Swiggy won't reimburse him for the additional kilometres.

I said I will call back in and called customer service. After 5-6 mins debtate and taking into account the fact that I often placed orders from the same address, the customer executive realised what was going on and agreed to what he had initially refused.

After speaking with the delivery guy, customer executive called me back, cancelled my order, and offered a copoun as an apology along with the refund. He said he will report the delivery person further.

Don't know how many have faced these kind of scam and how often it happens but I faced it only once.

**TLDR:** Swiggy delivery guy asked more money for order delivery citing order delivery location mismatch.
