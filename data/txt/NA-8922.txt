1. Heard learned counsel for appellant, learned A.G.A. for the State and perused the record. 2. The present appeal has been filed for quashing the order dated 18.08.2022 passed by learned Special Judge (SC/ST Act), Raebareli arising out of Case Crime No. 111 of 2022 U/s 323, 354, 376, 308, 504, 506 I.P.C ., Sections 3(1)(da) & (dha), Section 3(2)(v) of SC/ST Act, Police Station- Shivratanganj, District- Amethi; for releasing appellant on bail in aforesaid case crime. 3. Learned counsel for the appellant submits that appellant was working as a tailor in his own shop. He further submits that complainant was working at appellant's shop and sum of Rs. 50,000/- was given as loan to victim but the same was not refunded and when appellant tried to insist to refund the same, the F.I.R. of the case in question was lodged. He further submits that undisputedly, victim is a major lady and F.I.R. of the case in question was lodged on 18.06.2022 with general allegation that victim was being harassed by a person, namely, Chand Babu since last two years by making call from X-call app in the way of getting abused and getting life threat from him. As per the contents of F.I.R., it is alleged that on 18.06.2022 in morning, victim was beaten with bamboo stick by Chand Babu who also tried to outrage her modesty. He further submits that investigation was started in lieu of the said F.I.R. on pointing out of victim and the site plan was prepared on 18.06.2022; in the site plan, no sign of tracking was found. As per the statement of victim recorded U/s 161 Cr.P.C ., it is alleged that on 17.06.2022 at about 5:00 A.M., she went for defecation and after defecation, when she was coming back then appellant was present in the way having 'danda' in his hand. It is further alleged in the said statement that accused person abused and assaulted victim; thereafter, she fell down and her clothes were removed by him, as a result, she cried so he caught her throat, given her life threat, committed wrong with her and beat her with 'danda'. He further submits that statement of victim was recorded U/s 164 Cr.P.C . also in which she again stated that incident was taken place on 17.06.2022 in morning at about 5:00 A.M., but in the F.I.R. which was lodged on 18.06.2022, it is alleged that incident was taken place in morning on the same date of lodging the F.I.R., hence, there is contradiction in the prosecution case. It is further submitted that medico legal report is also contradictory in the way that victim was medically examined on 20.06.2022, on which date, she stated to doctor that the incident was taken place on 17.06.2022 at about 5:00 A.M., but duration of the bruises on the body of victim has not been mentioned by doctor meaning thereby the medico legal report is also superficial and contradictory which was prepared by doctor, namely, Kajal Sahu. It is further submitted that charge-sheet was submitted by Investigating Officer in the most mechanical manner. It is also submitted that appellant is in jail since 28.07.2022 but till today not even a single witness is examined and there is no possibility of conclusion of trial in near future, hence, appellant may be enlarged on bail. 4. Learned A.G.A. vehemently opposes the prayer of appellant and submits that after detail investigation, charge-sheet was submitted by Investigating Officer. She does not dispute the fact that in written complaint which was given by victim of the present case on 18.06.2022, it is stated that incident was taken place in morning of the same day, thereafter, she developed a case in her statements that incident was taken place on 17.06.2022 in morning. She also does not dispute the fact that in the F.I.R., no allegation of rape was alleged, but the said allegation is found in the statements of victim recorded U/s 161 & 164 Cr.P.C . 5. Considering the submissions of learned counsel for the appellant, learned A.G.A. and going through the contents of appeal, F.I.R., impugned order, written complaint of victim, statement of victim recorded U/s 161 & 164 Cr.P.C ., medico legal examination of victim as well as other relevant documents; it is evident that in the written complaint of victim, she stated that appellant tried to outrage her modesty and beaten her with 'danda' on 18.06.2022 in morning but thereafter, she developed a case that incident of rape was taken place on 17.06.2022. Therefore, this Court is of the view that the impugned order is liable to be set aside and appellant is entitled to be released on bail. 6. Resultantly, in view of the above facts and circumstances of the case and the fact that the trial of the case is not likely to be concluded in near future, hence, the appeal has substance and it is accordingly allowed andthe order dated 18.08.2022 passed by learned Special Judge (SC/ST Act), Raebareli is hereby set aside. 7.
