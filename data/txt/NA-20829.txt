I am surprised. Really. 

Email accounts can be easily phished and the vast majority of Android devices run outdated versions that are vulnerable to malware attacks. 

My concern is for the web based Internet banking; they have the most insane security practises. While I had been abroad, I had a fancy digital device wherein I keyed a randomly changing number. That device was small enough to be carried in my wallet. I also used Yubico Key for access to my emails from work, if needed or upload files to Dropbox. 

This has kept me away from trouble but Indian banks are atrocious. I have had to rely on my password generators to ensure I get a unique password and set it up accordingly. 

Does anyone know about any Indian bank which is ahead of the curve?
