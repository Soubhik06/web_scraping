So today i booked tickets for a movie at cinepolis mantra mall.... At noon time for a show... After i selected my seats... The cashier asked mode of payment. As soon as i said cash payment... He did some settings on his side of screen and asked my num to send SMS of ticket. I paid the full amount by cash and received an sms. When i opened the sms it was just BLANK page....no QR code, no seat numbers NOTHING! clearly he put that money in his pocket... And scammed me. Although i got entry into the movie... But the money i spent went into cashier's pocket. Guys be aware of such scams. EVERY MOVIE TICKET U PURCHASE MUST HAVE A QR CODE IN IT. Otherwise ur just getting openly scammed. Pathetic experience. I didn't confront him because i didn't realise it until now.
