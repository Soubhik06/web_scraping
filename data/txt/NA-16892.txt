I am talking predominantly about Hindi-speaking areas like Delhi, Rajasthan, UP, Haryana, Bihar. They will act friendly to you to earn your trust, then get what they need from you (e.g. money, information, contacts etc) then dismiss you once they get it. If they sense a vulnerability in you (e.g. you are a foreigner, or not familiar with something), they will take advantage and extract as much out of you as possible (e.g. inflating prices), and if you don't give them what they want, they will harass you or turn aggressive. They think it is OK to lie, cheat, commit fraud, misuse resources, forge documents, fake references, engage in corruption etc. If they are caught doing something wrong, they will offer a bribe to make it right and continue with their attitude. 

They use such dirty tactics to progress quickly in life, and have zero regard for using and abusing others. If you look at the surnames of the people doing these things, you will quickly identify that they are usually from upper castes, who have a history of exploiting others. If they can take advantage of others and get away with it, they will do it. This is unfortunately, destroying the reputation of other Indians all around the world. When will they develop a civic sense to contribute positively to society and build trust?? Nobody loves visiting an area or dealing with people that they have to have their guard up all the time.
