I had received an exorbitantly high electricity bill on my village home(approx 10,000 units) for may 2023 compared to my regular usage pattern. A complaint was immediately registered through a letter to the concerned officials as well as the SBPDCL online channel. 

My meter upon investigation by the local authorities was deemed faulty which was promptly replaced by a new one and i was assured of a bill correction. Now its been more than a year the issue remains as it is and the bill has piled up along with the late payment charges, and they are deducting those past arrears through their new prepaid meter. I don't live there, only my parents do and the local JEE is never in the office. 

I think this is a huge scam as everyone in that area is complaining about the same. All of them used to get average electricity bill every month, and all of a sudden they were surprised with a big bill.
