Hi everyone,

I'm at my wit's end and desperately seeking advice. I've been working in the US for the past few years, earning a decent living. I send home Rs 40,000 every month to my parents in Chennai, without fail. On top of that, I regularly send extra money whenever they claim to need it - sometimes around Rs 1 lakh at a time. We own our home and receive Rs 10,000 in rent from another property. My mom also works and claims to earn at least Rs 20,000 per month. You'd think with all this, they'd be financially stable, but that couldn't be further from the truth.

Growing up, things were incredibly stressful. My mom lost a significant amount of money (around 50 lakhs) in a scam, some of which belonged to relatives. We were constantly hounded by debt collectors. I have vivid memories of people coming to our house demanding money, and even being physically confronted by them. It was a traumatic experience.

Now, as an adult, I'm facing a new set of challenges with their financial decisions:

* **Shady Investment Scheme:** My mom recently started working for a company that promises unbelievably high returns (50% per year!). It feels like another scam waiting to happen, especially since the company and its owner are completely unknown. I've tried to warn her, but she's even trying to get relatives involved, which terrifies me.
* **Car Loan Deception:** I helped them with a car loan last year, sending Rs 7 lakhs to close it. I just found out that she didn't pay off the loan and instead invested the money elsewhere. To make matters worse, the loan is in my uncle's name because my parents have terrible credit. This adds another layer of stress and guilt.
* **Hidden Debts:** My parents are vague about other debts they owe, particularly to my mom's brother. They constantly complain about having no money, but refuse to tell me exactly how much they owe so I can help. My mom denies that they owe him anything and claims the money is tied up in a chit fund that's experiencing delays.

Honestly, I feel like I'm constantly walking on eggshells. My mom lies a lot, and her financial decisions are incredibly irresponsible. For example, she recently bought an AC unit for the living room, which seems completely unnecessary given their financial situation. I've sent them close to Rs 30 lakhs over the past year, but the debts and problems persist. I'm losing sleep and my mental health is taking a toll.

I'm at a loss. Has anyone else dealt with a similar situation? How can I approach my mom about these issues without things escalating? Should I involve other family members? Any advice on setting boundaries and protecting myself, while still trying to help them?

Thanks in advance for any guidance you can offer.
