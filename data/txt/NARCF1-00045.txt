I am a young guy with average knowledge about internet.
I thought I was smart enough to never fall for low effort scams.
but today I almost got scammed 
I somehow ended up on a Flipkart page with a ad for latest redmi phone 
worth Rs 19000 selling for 498 
the website looked so authentic
it said limited time offer with a 10 min timer 
I was like thats a great deal 
I filled all info and there was no option for cash on delivery
but the timer was running low 
so I decided to pay with UPI
while my upi app was loading 
something in my mind struck I reloaded the page and looked at the URL it was obviously wasnt Flipkart so I was saved 
but if they had a convincing URL and that timer and that very authentic looking website i probably would have been scammed.
