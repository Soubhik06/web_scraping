**Nirmal Bharat Abhiyan**

In 2013, the Manmohan Government launched the Nirmal Bharat Abhiyan to improve sanitation and build toilets. The program aimed to make India open-defecation-free by 2022. [https://jalshakti-ddws.gov.in/sites/default/files/swajal\_nirmal\_bharat\_enewsletter\_0.pdf](https://jalshakti-ddws.gov.in/sites/default/files/swajal_nirmal_bharat_enewsletter_0.pdf) and [https://pmposhan.education.gov.in/Files/WASH/Final%20Guidelines%20(English).pdf](https://pmposhan.education.gov.in/Files/WASH/Final%20Guidelines%20(English).pdf) 

**Swacch Bharat Mission**

The Modi Regime rebranded the program as Swacch Bharat Mission, as it did to countless other programs of the Congress government.

There is no doubt that some good work has happened in the past 10 years. Credit is due to the countless soldiers of the public works departments both centre and state. 

**Odd Data**

Data from the Modi Regime has proven to be highly unreliable (e.g., the opposite directions of CPI and WPI). Even if we are to take the Regime's data at face value, two oddities emerge:

1. The usage of toilets increased between NFHS 2015-16 and NARSS 2017-18 before dipping in NSS 2018. Then again usage increased in NARSS 2018-19 and declined in NARSS 2019-20, before finally dipping in NFHS 2019-21 (refer to image below).

Data from previous governments does not show such oddities. Therefore, the Modi Regime data may indicate that there have been malpractices in reporting to the Nation.

**Further Evidence**

The Rehabilitation Research Initiative (RRI India) and South Asian Labour Network (SASLN) conducted a study on the Swaccha Bharat Mission in 27 states, between 2017 and 2021. The following are the findings of the study:

■ 1.2 million or 30% of toilets were never built (the government's website claims these were built between 2014 and 2020) - this goes on to prove that Modi is a liar or feku and his goverment cannot be trusted;

■ of the toilets that were built another 30% were not in use;

■ 6,723 insanitary latrines were built despite the PMSR (Prohibition of Manual Scavengers and their Rehabilitation Act, 2013);

■ Rs 1,440 crore unaccounted and has not been received by the supposed beneficiaries (Toilet scam); and

■ 3.2 lakh beneficiaries are not aware of the toilets constructed in their names through SBM-G.
