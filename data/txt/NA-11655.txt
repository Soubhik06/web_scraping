 Heard the parties. 2. This anticipatory bail application under Section 482 of
 the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023, has been
 preferred by the petitioner apprehending his arrest in connection
 with Palamu Cyber Crime P.S. Case No.12 of 2023, for offences
 under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 IPC and Section 66(C) and 66(D) of the Information Technology Act. The case is presently
 pending before the Court of learned Additional Sessions Judge-II,
 Palamau at Palamau. 3. Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and learned
 counsel for the opposite party - State. 4. As per the FIR, on 13.10.2023, the informant sought
 assistance regarding his parcel from Flipkart Customer Care
 number on Google and contacted a WhatsApp number
 (1169278499). He was advised to speak with a senior, after which
 he received a call from another number (7864891254). The caller
 informed him that his payment had not been received and
 instructed him to make a payment. Before doing so, he was asked
 to download an app named 'Complain Register' and subsequently,
 'Anydesk'. After downloading 'Anydesk' and receiving an OTP, the
 informant lost access to his bank account. On 14.10.2023, upon
 checking his account, the informant discovered a shortage of
 ₹23,90,000. Realizing that he had been defrauded. 5. Learned counsel representing the petitioner submits
 that the petitioner was also the victim of cyber fraud. He further 1 2025:JHHC:13595

 submits that the petitioner was approached by one Ranjit Maity,
 who is now identified as the prime accused. He further submits
 that the main accused (Ranjit Maity) approached this petitioner and
 convinced him to open Bank accounts to avail loan facility and the
 same Bank accounts had been used for fraudulent transactions.
 Thus, on these grounds, he prays for grant of anticipatory bail to
 the petitioner. 6. Opportunity was granted to the State to oppose the
 anticipatory bail, which the State availed and opposed. 7. From the impugned order, I find that though the
 petitioner has not been named in the FIR, but during the course of
 investigation it has come that the informant as well as few
 witnesses have supported the case of prosecution. The impugned
 order further suggests that Rs.23,90,000/- was withdrawn from
 the account of the informant and this petitioner has role to play in
 the offence. Thus this case needs custodial investigation. 8. Considering the nature of allegations and also the fact
 that there was siphoning of huge amount from the account of the
 informant, I am not inclined to grant the privilege of anticipatory
 bail to the petitioner. 9. Accordingly, this Anticipatory Bail Application is
 dismissed. (ANANDA SEN, J.)
