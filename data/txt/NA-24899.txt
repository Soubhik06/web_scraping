I recently got scammed by people posing as representatives of the Minimalist brand. They contacted me claiming I was among the top 5 winners eligible for a free gift, but only if I made a small initial purchase through their app. Thinking it was legitimate, I went ahead with the purchase.

Unfortunately, one payment led to another with excuses about GST errors, processing fees, and “verification steps.” Each time, they promised refunds and insisted this was the final step. Eventually, they even pressured me to raise my bank balance to enable the refund process, which is when I realized I was caught in a scam.I nearly lost 30,000Rs.

I've already filed a complaint with the cybercrime portal, but I’m unsure of what else I should do.Is there anything I can do to increase the chances of getting my money back?
