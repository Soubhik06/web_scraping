Three months ago, a scammer defrauded my father of around 10 lakh rupees by posing as a branch manager. Initially, he contacted my father from a number saved as 'Branch Manager.' Later, he started using a different phone number, which I discovered was stolen.

At that time, I filed a complaint with the cyber cell and also lodged an FIR. I reported all the numbers involved in the fraud.

Today, I tried calling the 'Branch Manager' number again and found that it was still active. The police had not blocked any of the numbers, and the scammer was still using the original number he had used to scam my father. Interestingly, this number only supports WhatsApp calls, and when I attempt to make a regular phone call, it says the number is invalid.

What steps can I take to get this fraudulent number blocked? I want to ensure no one else falls victim to the same scam my father did.
