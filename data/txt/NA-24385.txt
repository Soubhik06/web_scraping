The TLDR; version of the saga – Rate of land in urban is rising across Karnataka; hence bureaucrats and politicians out to leverage their ‘power’ to make a quick buck

* At the heart of the scam is a 3.2-acre parcel of land that was gifted to the Chief Minister's wife, Parvathi, by her brother Mallikarjunaswamy in 2010. 
* The land was subsequently acquired by MUDA for layout formation. 
* Parvathi sought compensation and was subsequently allocated 14 plots. These plots are said to be substantially more valuable than the original piece of land.
* The controversy revolves around the alleged irregularities in the allotment of compensatory sites
* The opposition parties claim that the total value of the scam could potentially fall within the range of Rs 3,000 crore to Rs 4,000 crore.

----

This story is more than academic for me: My family has been running after local revenue department officials for over 25-years.

* My ex-serviceman father had purchased a 20 guntas parcel of land in 1995. 
* [After getting the sale registered, Tahisildar and DC officials refused to update revenue records (katha).](https://chng.it/GWpFX4CDMy)
* I took the issue to Karnataka High Court four ***(yes, four) times.*** Each time the court ordered officials to do the needful. Only after a contempt of court did the DC of Bengaluru Urban passed an order asking the Tahisildar to update Katha records. [(my story in Hindu)](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/bangalore/bengaluru-urban-dc-accused-of-violating-hc-order-over-issuance-of-khata/article66985535.ece)
* The Yelehenka Tahisildar has been dragging his feet despite those orders.
* As a last resort, I have filed a complaint with the Lokayukta, but I am not holding my breath. 

Although I am an educated taxpayer, I am not politically connected to BJP, JD(S) or Congress ……hence Tahisildar and his minions wont act without a HUGE payback..
