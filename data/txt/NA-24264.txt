Unfortunately, my in-laws trust them blindly, hoping for returns of 30-40-50 crores, which were promised a year ago but never arrived. Instead, the scammers keep giving excuses and taking more money.

They talk about a 10,000 crores deal and assure my in-laws of a luxurious life with cars and bungalows. Sadly, my elderly in-laws have fallen for it, losing a lot of money, about 2 lakhs per month for the past 2 years. They've lost their retirement savings and still hope for 30-40 crores and a better life, which the scammers keep delaying with false reasons every week.

My in-laws, aged 65 and 55, refuse to listen to us and continue giving money despite countless excuses made by the scammers. Every week, they promise luxury but deliver nothing.

It's concerning that they've been promising money 'next week' for the past 2 years. They even meet one of them in Mysore regularly. The excuses for not delivering the money are endless, from bank issues to political events.

We've tried explaining that it's a scam, but it leads to tension at home. We're helpless and hope these scammers get exposed someday. Any advice on handling this would be greatly appreciated.
