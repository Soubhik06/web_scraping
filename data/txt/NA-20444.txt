--- POST TEXT ---

I got a call the other day on my **BSNL landline**, from someone called **Nehal** claiming to be from **Integrated Grievance Management System** New Delhi, stating that an amount of **Rs.3,50,500** is due to me. This is from a fund maintained by the government for all private employees who have invested in LIC and other government bodies, that got accumulated for my dad who passed away while on duty. He passed away 20 years back.

Though I was skeptical, I was hopeful that it was true (who doesn't want money). I listened patiently, and she sounded very professional. She asked me to take a pen and paper to note down details of the file related to this fund, like PNR no, File no, Office Address, her name, etc, and asked me to repeat all I have written.

I asked her why after 20 years. She responded saying the file was languishing in the branch office, and in 2014 it got pushed to main office in New Delhi.

She then transferred the call to her alleged manager, who then asked me to repeat the PNR number and File no. Then, she asked I needed to send her **my aadhar card(front and back), pan card (front), date of birth, nominee name,** etc documents on WhatsApp. I asked her why WhatsApp, she said the system is facing issues.

I was almost convinced and was willing to share the documents, till she asked me that **I would need to open an ICICI health insurance to avail of this fund**. That's when I smelt a rat. If not for that product push, I think I was convinced.

People beware of scams like this. It was so utterly convincing.

**I am still unsure of how to spot fakes. Maybe any advice will help others as well.**
