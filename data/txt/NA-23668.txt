I've advised my girlfriend and her brother to file a cyber complaint immediately. Given that her mom doesn't know about me, my involvement has to be discreet.I'm at a loss and deeply concerned about the financial and emotional repercussions for her family. Any advice on how to proceed, especially after the ill-advised blocking of the scammer's account, would be much appreciated.
