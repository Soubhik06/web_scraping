So as many have posted here, I was using bsnl for my second sim to keep it active for incoming and sms, using their 360 days validity 797 plan for the same. 

But from last week, I’m getting a message saying this plan has been withdrawn and to recharge again. Checking their website, my number has no active plan and if I try to recharge with 797, 997, 1199 plans it tells me “number isn’t eligible for these plans”. The only annual plans I can choose are 1999(with daily data and voice) and higher which is pointless as my main sim is already postpaid with unlimited data and voice. 

I checked airtel and jio but both of them have annual plans at 1500-ish, which is unaffordable for a secondary sim which is rarely used. Vi has a 15day validity plan at 99, but that need to be purchased 24 times a year which isn’t feasible. 

Thanks to the scam that numbers can expire and be resold if they don’t have a valid pack now(and not just having balance), operators have found an easy way to mint money by not giving an option for many to keep secondary sims active with such validity plans. 

And we get nothing in return too, 4g max speed is 80mhps if you’re lucky, when in reality it’s capable of 300-400mbps easily.
