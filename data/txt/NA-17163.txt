I was conversing with a relative whom I'm currently visiting in Bangalore when I came to know of this fact. Every other Indian airport I've been until now allows e-ticket/m-ticket display in your smart-phones, but not Bangalore. Relative says its because of security reasons, but its obvious this is a scam. The only option to print a ticket at airport usually costs 100-150 bucks which is a loot. No doubt, they want to keep this cash mill running even at the customer's inconvenience?

**Edit**

Based on the responses, it seems that my relative is highly mistaken about this. Maybe, he was asked a paper ticket many years ago and still believes that is the norm, I'll ask him once I catch hold of him! Anyways, I'm glad that its no longer true today.
