Recently there have been a new fraud going around where people are made to invest in online platforms and they get money by trading. One of such fraud is under name of "godrej" where they tell you to install an app and invest some 3000 rupees initially and all they have to do is guess the price will go up or down and they will get a profit if the guess is right. 
This has happened with my friend and he told me about this. He was introduced to this by his girlfriend and his girlfriend got introduced to this by her colleague. Well two people in this case lost somewhere around 10,000rs when their accounts suddenly got suspended and the company people were nowhere to be found. 
One of such fraud is been running wildly in rural areas under the name of forex trading. Its basically like chain marketing where you join the system and they give you an app that will make you profit on its own, all you have to do is put in the mumbers provided to you daily. Basically just like trading using telegram signals,But here the app doesn't do shit the whole backbone of the thing is network marketing. 
I'd like to tell people that before investing in such companies just make sure to ask them their gst numbers and lookup for the registration details online. The only way you can recognise such companies is by knowing their core product coz each one of these companies need a product to run. Most of the time these products are bogus like trading courses, online courses on any subject. Make sure to know whats their product and what are they capitalising on. 
Also if you know about any such frauds please share it in comments, so that everyone be aware. 

Note: Godrej company name is only used to loot people. The real company itself is not doing any fraud.
