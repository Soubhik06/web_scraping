This one’s sneaky. Almost got me. Almost.

Here’s how it works:

1️⃣ You get a call about a **COD parcel** from BlueDart/Delhivery/E-Kart.

2️⃣ They send **photos** of the parcel. It’ll have some random product name like *Bee Venom Bone Pain Relief Cream*.

3️⃣ They insist **you ordered it COD** and must pay in cash.

4️⃣ The amount? **Small enough** to not raise suspicion.

5️⃣ **DO NOT PAY. DO NOT ACCEPT.**

6️⃣ If you refuse, they’ll say you need to **share an OTP** to cancel the order. You’ll even receive an actual OTP from the delivery company. (Fun fact: BlueDart *does* send OTPs for rejecting deliveries—a *very* unsafe system, if you ask me).

7️⃣ **DO NOT SHARE THE OTP.**

They tried it on me. I was suspicious. I didn’t pay. I didn’t share the OTP. I Googled. Scam confirmed.

Of all the scams people have tried on me (none successfully, *touch wood*), this was **the most sophisticated**. Almost had me. Almost.

Warn your family. Warn your friends. And yes, **‘bee’ careful!** 🐝😛
