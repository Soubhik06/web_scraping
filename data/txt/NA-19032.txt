#1. Corruption Everywhere#

Bribery and scams are part of everyday life, from getting a government job to dealing with the police. Politicians and officials steal public money, and very few ever get punished. It’s the people who suffer while the rich and powerful walk free.

#2. The Rich Get Richer, The Poor Get Poorer#

India has some of the richest people in the world, yet millions struggle to afford food and housing. Government programs meant to help the poor often fail because of corruption and mismanagement. The gap between the rich and poor keeps growing.

#3. A Healthcare System That Doesn’t Care#

Government hospitals are overcrowded, doctors are overworked, and medicines are often unavailable. Many villages don’t even have proper healthcare centers. If you don’t have money, getting treated for a serious illness can be nearly impossible.

#4. A Broken Education System#

Many government schools lack proper teachers, books, and even clean drinking water. Instead of learning useful skills, students memorize outdated lessons. Even college graduates struggle to find jobs because the education system isn’t preparing them for real life.

#5. No Jobs for Young People#

Millions of young people graduate every year, but good jobs are hard to find. Many are forced to work for low wages or leave the country in search of better opportunities. The government promises new jobs, but nothing ever really changes.

#6. Poor Roads, Dirty Cities, and No Electricity#

Basic infrastructure is still a mess. Many roads are full of potholes, public transport is unreliable, and electricity cuts are still common in many areas. While cities see some improvement, rural areas are mostly ignored.

#7. Destroying Nature for Money#

Air pollution is getting worse, rivers are filled with garbage, and forests are being cut down for big projects. The government makes promises to protect the environment but keeps approving industries that pollute the land and water.

#8. A Slow and Unfair Justice System#

If you’re rich and powerful, you can escape the law. If you’re poor, you can wait years—sometimes decades—for justice. There are millions of cases stuck in courts, and corruption often decides who wins, not the truth.

#9. Dividing People for Votes#

Politicians keep using religion and caste to divide people. Instead of solving real problems, they make people fight over temples, reservations, and religious differences just to win elections. This keeps the country stuck in endless arguments and hatred.

#10. Leaders Who Don’t Take Responsibility#

No matter how badly they run things, politicians rarely face consequences. They make big promises during elections and then disappear when it’s time to act. When something goes wrong, they blame someone else instead of fixing the problem.

India has so much potential, but bad leadership is holding it back, I truly hope that the government changes its ideals or gets changed by the people into one that fulfills its needs either by pressure or by elections.
