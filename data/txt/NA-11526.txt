This is second application filed under Section 439 of Cr.P.C.
by the applicant, who has been arrested by the Police on 20.01.2021
in connection with Crime No.267/2020 registered at Police Station
Cyber Cell, Indore for the offence punishable under Sections 420 , 419 of IPC and section 66-D of the IT Act. 2. Mr. Yashwant Yadav, has lodged the FIR in the Police Station
Cyber Cell, Indore on 29.12.2020 at 19.08 hours disclosing that he is
working as Manager at Punjabi Jewelry shop. On 3.12.2020, near
about 1.23 the owner received a phone call from Varun Mehta,
Shilpi Jewelers,Delhi that he needs Rs.4.00 Lakhs at Delhi and by
the evening, he would return the said amount. He disclosed the name
of one Ashok Bafna, at present in Pithampur and by evening, he
would return the amount in the shop. Varun Mehta gave the mobile
number of Vikram Bafna to him. According to him, he arranged the
payment of 4 lakhs at Delhi, but till night, no one came to return his
money back. He gave a call to Shilpi Jewelers on another mobile
number, but they denied the call in the morning and receipt of 4
lakhs, therefore, he felt cheated by online fraud. The Police put
Mobile No. 8006078418, 9978052087, 9731205560 and
8306588870 on surveillance and found that the employer of the
complainant i.e. Sumit Anand has been cheated by the holders of
aforesaid four mobile numbers. Accordingly, the police have
registered the case u/s. 420 of the IPC and u/s. 66D of the 2 Information Technology Act against the holders of Mobile No.
8006078418, 9978052087, 9731205560 and 8306588870.
According to the complainant, mobile Nos. 8306588870 belongs to
Ashok Bafna; 9731205560 and 80007814 belong
to Vikram Bafna. The Police has recorded the statement of Sumit
Anand an owner of Panjabi Jewellers on 5.12.2020 and according to
him, Vipul Mehta requested for help of Rs.4.00 Lakhs, on phone
then he gave his mobile number to his Manager Yashwant Yadav and
directed him to arrange the payment of Rs. 4 lakhs at Delhi.
Thereafter, he found that Vipul Mehta has switched off his mobile
phone, then he obtained his alternative number from Bunti Mehta as
9899104104 and when Vipul Mehta was contacted on the said
number, he said that he never demanded any money.
In the course of the investigation, the Police has arrested
Ramkrsihna Purohit; Pradeep @ Shaitan; Vijay Singh; Narendra
Singh @ Dashrath Singh; Morsingh Rajpurohit; Jitendra Kumar @
Gautam Rajpurohit. Theyall admitted in memorandum statements
about cheating with Sumit Anand Punjabi Jewelers and distributed
the amount amongst them. From their disclosure, the police has
recovered the amount of Rs.4.00 Lakhs from
them (from Ramkrishna - Rs.25,000/; from Vijay Singh -
Rs.50,000/-; from Narendra Singh - Rs.1,00,000/-; from Morsingh -
Rs.1,50,000/-; from Jitendra Singh - Rs.50,000/-; and from Pradeep
@ Shaitan Singh - Rs.25,000/-). 3. Learned counsel appearing for the applicant submit that the
applicant has been falsely implicated in this case. None of the
mobile numbers as disclosed by the complainant belong to him.
Except memorandum u/s. 27 of the Evidence Act, nothing
incriminating material has been collected from him. Though the
various cases under the IPC are already registered against him, no
criminal case under the Information Technology Act has ever been
committed against him. No further custodial interrogation is
required and no further recovery is to be made from him. As of
today, the entire amount has been recovered. He, therefore, prays
that the applicant be released on bail. 4. On the other hand, the learned Panel Lawyer opposes the
prayer for grant of bail by submitting that the applicant is a habitual
offender as the number of cases are registered against him. The
investigation is still pending, hence applicant and co-accused
persons are not to be released on bail. 5. I have perused the case diary and heard the arguments
advanced by the learned counsel appearing for the parties at length. 6. As per the Police - Prativedan sent to the Office of Advocate
General, the entire amount of Rs. 4 lakhs has been recovered from
the present applicant and other co-accused persons. According to
them, on the basis of forged ID, they got sim and after committing
the offence, they have destroyed the mobile sets, therefore, offence u/s. 201 of the IPC has been added. The Dy. Superintendent of
Police has not mentioned that any further custody of the present
applicant is required. According to the complainant, he made the
payment of Rs.4.00 Lakhs at Delhi. Neither the complainant nor
owner of the Jewellery shop Sumit Anand has disclosed as to how
and which mode they made the payment of Rs.4.00 Lakhs at Delhi.
They have also not disclosed as to whom they directed to make the
payment to Vipul Mehta. It is also under secret that the complainant
has transferred the amount to the unknown person who made
payment to Vipul Mehta Delhi on the complainant's call. According
to the complainant, Vipul Mehta has denied calling the complainant
and receiving any payment. The police are also required to
investigate the link as to how the complainant arranged for payment
of Rs.4.00 Lakhs. Unless the link of payment of Rs.4.00 Lakhs is
established by the complainant or Sumit Anand , it cannot be said
that the applicant and other co-accused have cheated the
complainant. I.O. present in person submitted that he called the
complainant but he did not appear. This reply cannot be accepted.
The investigating officer has not made any effort to investigate the
matter despite the direction given by this Court. Therefore,
DIG/Superintendent of Police, Indore is directed to appoint another
investigating Officer in this case and further direct DSP to supervise
the investigation. 7. The police have not collected any material to establish that the
 applicants have obtained the sim by forging identity. In view of the
 aforesaid facts and circumstances of the case, without commenting
 on the merits of the case, the application is allowed and applicant is
 directed to be released on bail upon his furnishing personal bond in
 the sum of Rs.1,00,000/- (Rupees One lakh only) with two surety in
 the like amount out of which one local surety to the satisfaction of
 the Trial Court for his appearance before that Court during the
 pendency of trial and shall also abide by the conditions enumerated
 under section 437(3) Cr.P.C. Before releasing the applicant from custody the jail authorities
 are directed to medically examine in order to rule out the possibility
 of COVID -19 infections and shall comply with the direction given
 by the Hon'ble Apex Court in Writ Petition No. 1/2020. Let a copy of this Order be sent to DIG/Superintendent of
 Police,Indore. (Vivek Rusia)
