--- POST TEXT ---

So my cousin who used to have Tata Docomo 3G Dongle wi-fi recently got it removed because the prices have gone up too high. If he had to continue using it as per his old pattern, he would've had to pay like 5k/month.

I know a bunch of younger cousins who've also cut down on their internet use slightly because of 3G price increase.

Now telecom companies say that they've had to increase 3G prices because their costs have gone up after the auction. Fair enough.

Kapil Sibal's entire 'zero loss' argument was that the lower 2G prices helped grow telephone use at a faster pace than it would have.

Which is to say, that the economy had a net gain because more consumption + communication added tons of value indirectly to the economy.

Seeing how consumption has dropped (but only slightly), would it be right to say that the 2G scam had some indirect benefits to India?

Any thoughts?
