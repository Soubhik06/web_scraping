 Petitioners wants to operate above said account(s) and share

 certificates being one of surviving legal heir of deceased, who is

 entitled for succession certificate. There is no impediment in

 issuing the succession certificate. According to the knowledge

 of the petitioner, deceased has not made any WILL and no

 application has been filed before any court for probate of any

 WILL or for letter of administration of the estate of the deceased

 and prayed accordingly. PC No.34/2015 Page No.3 of 10 5. It is averred in the application under consideration that after

 death of grand mother, petitioners approached the respondents

 for their claim in movable and immovable assets of the

 deceased. But, respondent no.3 flatly refused to give their

 shares and told that he is nominee in the bank accounts, demat

 accounts, locker etc. of the deceased, so, he is wholly entitled

 for the same and this is surprised for the petitioners, as earlier

 respondent no.2 was nominee of bank accounts etc. of deceased.

 On believing claim of respondent no.3, petitioners are having

 reasonable apprehension in their mind that they can be duped

 from their lawful entitlements. There is no plausible reason as

 to why deceased transferred her all rights in respect of accounts,

 shares etc. in favour of her one son excluding others. The assets

 were purchased from the ancestral funds, so, deceased was not

 having any authority to transfer the same to any single heir and

 said act caused of respondent no.3, could be of fraud or undue

 influence over the deceased. Without informing petitioners,

 respondent no.3 transmitted the request with respect to Demat

 account to Integrated Enterprises India Ltd and Stock Holding

 Corporation of India Ltd and Central Depository Services India

 Ltd. It was revealed that respondent no.3 has withdrawn some

 fixed deposits from HDFC account of deceased from March­

 June, 2015, which clearly shows his intention. Petitioner no.1 PC No.34/2015 Page No.4 of 10 vide e­mails dated 22.06.2015 & 25/26.06.2015 sought freezing

 of aforesaid demat accounts. Vide e­mail dated 25.06.2015,

 Integrated Enterprise India Ltd has refused to suspend the said

 account and informed that transmission request has been

 received from nominee, which cannot be refused until valid

 court order. Vide e­mail dated 26.06.2015, it has been intimated

 to petitioner no.1 that respondent no.3 has partially succeeded in

 his malafide endeavours and his transmission request was

 processed by DB (International) Stock Brokers Ltd, which was

 not intimated by him to the petitioners. In these circumstances,

 if respondent no.3 is not injuncted, then petitioners will suffer

 irreparable loss and injury. Nominee is merely a custodian of

 the amount and is not entitled to realize the same in his name to

 exclude the legal heirs. The money or other assets of deceased

 shall be distributed according to law of succession. Petitioners

 are having strong prima facie case in their favour and are having

 reasonable apprehension and prayed for granting exparte order

 thereby restraining the respondents from operating any bank

 account(s), lockers of the deceased or

 withdrawing/realizing/appropriating the shares held by the

 deceased on on date of her death and for grating exparte order

 thereby restraining the respondent no.3 from proceeding with

 transmission request made to Integrated Enterprises India Ltd. PC No.34/2015 Page No.5 of 10 having its office at Preet Vihar Branch and to Stock Holding

 Corporation of India Ltd having its office at Bahadur Shah Zafar

 Marg­ITO and to Central Depository Services India Ltd with

 respect to demat accounts qua alleged nominee of deceased. 6. Reply to the said application has been filed only on behalf of

 respondent no.3. It is averred therein that application has been

 application. 7. I have heard the ld. counsels for the parties and perused the

 The list of movable properties left behind by the deceased has

 no.3 has pointed out that saving bank account appearing at 6A,

 respondent no.3, articles mentioned at 6C & 6E are not in

 same, so, the respondent no.3 is hereby restrained from

 proceeding with transmission request made to Integrated

 Enterprises India Ltd. having its office at Preet Vihar Branch,

 and to Central Depository Services India Ltd with respect to

 demat accounts qua alleged nominee of deceased, both

 mentioned at 6C & 6E in petition. 16. Deceased Padma Ajmani expired on 20.02.2015. The

 movable property i.e. bank account of HDFC, as mentioned at

 6F, is in joint names and can be operated either or survivor. The

 statement of account placed before the court on record, is having

 the entries upto 24.01.2015 only, having a balance of Rs.

 22409.52. The same can be operated by respondent no.3 also,

 there is no ground to restrain the respondent no.3 to operate the

 respect of movable property appearing at 6F in petition. 17. Neither any evidence has been given nor it is not known as

 articles, as mentioned at 6G in petition and what is the quantity,

 so, there is no restraining order regarding movable property as

 mentioned at 6G in the petition. Application stands disposed of. 18. Nothing expressed herein above shall amounts to an
