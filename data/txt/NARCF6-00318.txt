If you see a property first check it physically. Check if all the electrical and plumbing is working and nothing is broken. Secondly ask about the owner and do ask the neighbours about the owner. These time crooks come from different cities and take these 1rk on rent and try to scam people. Beware of them and please check and sign the rent agreement before giving the advance. You can’t trust anyone here.
