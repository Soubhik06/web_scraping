Hey folks,

I am a techie and I have been using a CA for filing my IT returns from early on. I got in touch with the CA after a recommendation by a family member. I have had no major issues with income tax filing through him and my returns are quite accurate. There have been no notices etc so far. He charges a ton of money though.

However I feel a bit uncomfortable about how we operate with my personal details. This is how we operate:

- Every year around May / June, I send the CA in an email my form 16, other TDS forms, capital gain statements, bank account statements, etc via email. Some of these are password protected by my PAN which my CA knows.
- The CA responds back with a detailed spreadsheet containing the proposed filings. This spreadsheet contains EVERYTHING about me: my email, phone, address, PAN, Aadhaar number, employer, bank accounts, salary, EVERYTHING. It gets sent via email without a password. If the CA sent it to someone else by mistake, someone else would get to access this.
- When the spreadsheet looks good to me, I send the CA my IT account password (after changing it). He logs in and files my return. I change my password immediately.
- Another thing that worries me is that the CA office has employees who keep changing. So each year some employee gets to go through my returns.

A few things happened which made me have mini panic attacks:

- Once the CA sent an email to all his clients in CC (not in BCC) about some reminder to pay advance tax. So everyone could see who all his clients were.
- One other time, the CA said he sent me my proposed filing. I said I didn't get it. So he said he had sent it but he would check again. Then he sent it to me. I wonder if he sent it to someone else?

Is living with this anxiety the norm? Is this something we have to risk and live with?

I know several ways in which I could teach my CA to be more tech savvy, encrypt stuff, use various software, etc but there is no way I could teach someone not tech savvy to become tech savvy.

**EDIT**: My anxiety has sky rocketed since making this post. What are some ways your data could be misused by someone if it falls in the wrong hands?
