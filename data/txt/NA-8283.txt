thereafter Kalpesh went to the house of Pushpa Baliram Mharse

her house on 3rd March, 2019 at 10:30 pm., but he left the house

alongwith the applicant sometime after 10:30 pm. 9. I have perused the supplementary statement and FIR. The FIR

suggests that complainant was feeling relatives of girl were

instrumental in eliminating his son. However, in the supplementary

statement, he would allege that the applicant was harbouring grudge

against his son and would further say that since the applicant had

illegally fell tress, to the dissatisfaction of the deceased. As such in

supplementary statement, it appears, complainant was suspecting

that his son was murdered by the applicant. 10. I have perused the final report. 3/7 6.Cri.BA 3013-2019.doc 11. It may be stated that on 9 th March, 2019, complainant

reported to the police that his son had left the house at around 8:30

pm. on 4th March, 2019 after dinner and since then his whereabout

were not known; whereas in the FIR, he would say that his son was

missing since 3rd March, 2019. This material contradiction in missing

complaint and FIR has not been explained by the prosecution at all.

This contradiction, infact goes to the root of the prosecution case.

Be that as it may, the learned Prosecutor has argued that deceased

was last seen in the company of the applicant on 3 rd March, 2019. As

against this, the father in missing complaint, reported that deceased

left the house on 4th March, 2019 after taking dinner. 12. Prosecution has recorded statements of many witnesses.

However, their versions are inconsistent and prima facie do not

corroborate each other. I have perused the statement of Sandesh

Baliram Mharse, Pushpa Baliram Mharse and Mahesh Hindurao.

Statement of Pushpa Mharse recorded on 22nd March, 2019 (a day

after the registration of FIR) indicates Nandu @ Bala Mharse was not

approving love affair of Kalpesh (deceased) with his sister-in-law.

Surprisingly in her statement recorded on 3 rd March, 2019, she would

state that quarrel had ensued between deceased and the applicant on 4/7 6.Cri.BA 3013-2019.doc

account of felling of trees by the applicant whereafter applicant

chased the deceased in the direction of the spot, where the dead

body was found. . A statement of Shraddha Suresh Wangar recorded on 16 th

March, 2019, shows that on 4th March, 2019 at 04:30 pm., she saw

Kalpesh on Motor Cycle, proceeding towards Sheduli. Besides a

statement of Balu Hari Mukne suggests that his relative Sagar

Mukne informed him on the phone that he had seen deceased at

Ghodegaon, Pune on 11th March, 2019. 13. Thus, in view of the statements of witnesses, it is uncertain as

to from which date, the deceased was missing. Furthermore if

Sharaddha Wangar, had seen the deceased on 4 th March, 2019 riding

a Motor Cycle, then the complainant's statement that his son was

missing from 3rd March, 2019 becomes a suspicious. Even otherwise

in missing complaint, he reported deceased was missing from evening

of 4th March. 14. Thus, after going through the statements of witnesses the

prosecutions' case that the deceased was seen last in the company of

the applicant is quite uncertain. The statement of witnesses referred 5/7 6.Cri.BA 3013-2019.doc

above, prima facie do not support the prosecution case that he was

last seen in the company of the applicant on 3rd March, 2019. 15. Be that as it may, the investigation in the case is wholly

defective and has not been carried out in just and fair manner. That

since the prosecutions' case is based on circumstantial evidence, final

report has still more loose ends. It is apparent that investigation has

not been carried out in proper direction, for the reason, best known

to the Investigating Officer. 16. Let a copy of this order be forwarded to the Superintendent of

Police having Supervisory Jurisdiction over the Murbad Police

Station, who shall look into the final report and shall take

appropriate steps to ensure just and fair investigation in the interest

of justice. 17. Resultantly, the applicant is directed to be released on bail on

executing P.R. Bond in the sum of Rs.30,000/- with two sureties in

the like amount; (i) The applicant shall furnish his residential address as well as

permanent address and contact details to the investigating officer 6/7 6.Cri.BA 3013-2019.doc

forthwith; (ii) The applicant shall cooperate in the investigation and report

to influence or contact the complainant, witnesses or any person

concerned with the case; 18. Application is allowed and disposed of accordingly. 19. Learned APP as well as Registry of this Court shall forward a
