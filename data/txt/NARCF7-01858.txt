So I had visited Chennai for work and I had this happen to me, so just sharing so more people are aware how these guys play with you.

I booked a ride from the beach to my hotel and the guy accepted ride and came to the location and picked me up, as he asked for otp I gave it and went to sit on the bike, he immediately says the cost will be double, showing 40rs he asked for 80, I asked for what, and he says the location is too far for him and he won’t get rides back at this time, it was 8:50 pm, and I was not at all ready to even negotiate, got too annoyed and straight up told him to cancel ride I will book another, he then tells me to cancel it myself.

Alright simple, I cancel the ride and move on, then Uber asks me 50rs for cancellation, that’s when I realised his game, why he waited to take otp first, as you can cancel before giving otp and that charge won’t come up, he sits there with a smug face telling me again, cancel the ride, I realised the game being played with me and I told him my internet is wonky but I have cancelled it might take a while and started walking away, he stood there and watched me for 10 mins smoke a cig and book a ride on Rapido and leave, I closed the app and didn’t look again, an hour I get a notification that ride was completed and I have to pay Uber 67 rs, this guy never cancelled the ride and I don’t know what he did.

Fast forward to Uber support not addressing the issue after multiple complaints that I never took the ride because rider played game of chicken on who will cancel ride so I never cancelled the ride and left on a Rapido, still have the charge on my Uber. 

It’s really not about the money, it’s the principle, I know what people say about language barrier in Chennai and you are treated how you speak, but I had no issues until this guy in Chennai played this with me because I spoke to him in English as I only know Marathi and English, maybe it’s not the reason and he just thought he can pull a quick cancel ride scam on me, but idk, Uber moto is new to me and I’m used to only using Rapido so stay safe out there folks
