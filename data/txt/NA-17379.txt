In the recent few months, people have been getting scammed in the name of Digital Arrests. Here are some tips for you guys to prevent you from getting caught. Also, share them with the elders in your family as they are the soft targets.

**Tips to not get "Digital Arrested"**

1. First of all, **there is no term like Digital Arrest**. If the police authorities or government security agencies want to arrest you they will come to your doorstep with an arrest warrant and not video call you.
2. Second, **never ever receive calls** other than the +91 code number. If you get calls from ISD codes like +44 or +65, +880, or something like that, don't pick and straightaway block that number.
3. **Only receive video calls** from your friends and family and the contact numbers that you have saved and not unknown numbers.
4. **Never disclose your OTP or bank details** to anyone. Not even the bank officials over the call. Always remember authentic bank officials will never ask for your OTP. So please keep this in your mind.
5. **If you have like lakhs or crores of money in your bank account**, you don't need to be scared of it. It is the money that you have earned through hard work and labor. Don't let anyone put evil eyes on it. In most cases, these scammers attack recent retirees as they know that these people have received retirement funds in lakhs and crores.

Also, it is not important to answer every damn call. You can keep the unknown numbers ringing, if they are your known ones, they will either message you personally or call other family members in the family.

 Pls be cautious, be awar,e and be vocal!!
