I see many listings for buying a part of commercial property in 32nd Ave Gurgaon and many other locations like malls, shopping complexes etc. You buy part ownership for 50-70 lakh and they in return give you 5-10% ownership and pay you a fixed income 20-80k per month. You get laind, even if property is vacant.

The catch? You can’t lease your portion of property to anyone, the decide who get the lease. You can sell anytime though. 

Is this a good deal? Is it worth it or there is a clear scam brewing here? Has anyone done such investment?
