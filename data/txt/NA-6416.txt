CORAM: HON'BLE MR. JUSTICE GAUTAM KUMAR CHOUDHARY

 For the Petitioner : Mr. Kaushal Kishor Mishra, Advocate
 For the State : Mr. S.K. Tiwari, Special P.P.

 Order No.05 / Dated :19.08.2023
 Heard both the sides. The petitioner- Alimuddin Ansari is in custody since 15.04.2023 in
 connection with Jamtara Cyber Crime P.S. Case No. 21 of 2023 for the
 offence registered under Sections 414 , 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 120B of the
 Indian Penal Code and Section 66(B) , (C), (D) of Information Technology Act pending in the Court of learned Additional Sessions Judge 1st, Jamtara. As per the prosecution case, the petitioner was running CSP Centre
 and it is alleged that he in connivance with others, facilitated cyber frauds and
 transfer of the proceeds of crime obtained by cyber fraudsters. On getting this
 information, the police conducted raid in which two mobile, four SIM cards,
 six ATM cards, six passbook and checkbook, one POS machine, one laptop
 and Rs.50,000/- cash were seized from the CSP shop. It is submitted by the learned counsel on behalf of petitioner that
 there is no material to connect this petitioner in cybercrime committed against
 any person. The seizure does not connect the petitioner to the cyber offence. Learned Special P.P. opposed the prayer for bail. It is submitted that
 suspicious e-wallets were found to be created by the SIM of the petitioner.
 However, it is fairly conceded that during investigation, by use of mobile of
 this petitioner cyber frauds were committed. Under the circumstances, the above named petitioner is directed to be
 released on bail on furnishing bail bond of Rs.25,000/-(Twenty Five
 Thousand) with two sureties of the like amount each to the satisfaction of the
 Court below. (Gautam Kumar Choudhary, J.)
