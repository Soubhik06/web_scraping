Got a call from an unknown number today (was from India, starting +91). Picked it up, and the guy from the other side called out my name and confirmed my identity. Then he said he was a police officer, and started abusing me on the grounds of "aapki pichle 3 mahino ki internet history he humare paas, aap ashleel cheeze kyun dekhte ho, illegal he ye". I didn't say anything, and was quietly listening to this guy mumble, and then I guess he got pissed and started abusing me, trying to get me to shit pants.

I didn't know what was really happening so I acted as if their audio wasn't really audible, and the guy started acting even more pissed. I heard some police sirens in the background (as if he was in a police station or in a patrol car), and then he said "gaadi side me lagao, awaz nahi aarahi he sahi se", guy said "hello, hello" a few times and then went with "mukadma karo inpar", after which I was like ???

I cut the call, and he tried calling me again. Later I confirmed my suspicion and looked it up on truecaller, and it was a scam call. I bet if I had replied to any of his BS, he would have 100% asked for money.

Beware guys, earlier it was stupid rape scam calls, and now this...
