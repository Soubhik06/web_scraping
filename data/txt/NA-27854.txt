Had a billing dispute with adwords, and sent a very nice email (you waste your time suing Google) to their email address used for following up on legal service, just figuring an attorney would understand my argument. Google-legal-support@google.com (proof that's correct in 1st paragraph : https://support.google.com/faqs/answer/6151275?hl=en)

So I'm waiting for a call back over the past week, and finally one comes in from Dos Palos, Ca. Thick Indian accent, but **knows the whole story**. Really professional, asks my account number (not my password) and says that Google is giving me a refund on the disputed charges in that they found an error in my account. Says he needs access to my computer and I can watch what he does...I'm like "whaaaaat???" and quickly googling on my phone "Remote access scams Google" with mixed results. Keep in mind this guy is promising me lots of money back on a dispute so I'm trying not to hang up on him. Finally I say "look, would you mind if I check that you are with Google...could you tell me the click count on that campaign?" He says "sure, hold on" and a minute later line goes dead.

This isn't a typical scam in that not many people are writing to Google's Legal Dept, but it is frightening that my email to them (I triple checked where I sent it just now) got hijacked like this.

Could someone low level in Google's legal dept be in on a scam?
