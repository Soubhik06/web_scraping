--- POST TEXT ---

I just got a call from **+91 8981612235.** She started telling me that since I've placed an order or parcel of Rs. 383 on some website and the delivery courier is DTDC, the person knows my name. Then she went on about me winning some DTDC contest with a coupon or something. I couldn't hear well and sensed it was a scam, as I hadn't bought anything online. I hung up, blocked the number, and reported it on Truecaller.

I considered calling back to maybe record them or extract some info. A different woman answered. She mumbled something like "Thank you for calling us" and mentioned something about promotional stuff. It might have been the same woman, but her voice seemed a bit different. I explained that the previous call was cut short and asked about the contest they mentioned when they called me. The woman brushed off my question, saying, "Okay, okay, you'll receive a call back from us," and then hung up. That's the whole situation.

Has anyone else got any such calls? Should I be worried?
