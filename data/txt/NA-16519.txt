I'm a sophomore at a gov Law College , so to gain some pocket money and some experience I decided to go for internships , part time jobs aren't practical when you have regular classes 

There are pet shops, stationery shops, xerox stores ,Akshaya centres where I can work for part time but my parents doesn't want me to do those kind of jobs because " we aren't that poor yet , why would we educate u this much just to end up working in some pet shops " anyways so I gave up that idea

Made a profile on naukri.com , got some data entry jobs and yes they were scams , they began sending spam mails and stuffs and I wasn't getting any relevant jobs so I deleted it , made a profile on internshala and began applying for internships , I applied for mostly online teaching internships ,and some sme ( subject matter expert ) internships , an ngo that I applied to ,shortlisted me and they asked me to take a demo math class on that day itself , and it went well and I got hired 

Now about the internship that I'm doing 
It's for 6 months and it's unpaid 
Have to take 3 science classes in a week ( Tues, Thurs and Saturday)
For 1 hour nd currently I have to tutor only one kid, the number will gradually go up I guess 
Make and submit weekly reports every week 
Attend regular meetings on Sunday 

On the second day of my demo class ( first demo class was with an employee, second one was with a student ) they did call me beforehand and gave me a brief idea about their organisation ,that they do collect money from these students , part of that money goes to paid interns working with them and the rest goes to charity 

I do understand that paid internship offer requires the candidates to have some kind of knowledge and experience about what they are doing , and no employee is just going to hire and pay for a inexperienced person

I do love teaching very much so yes i can tolerate and i'm willing to teach for 6 months but meantime have you guys got any idea by which I can gain some pocket money ? 

Data entry jobs here on interhshala are scams mostly , I did look for content writing jobs especially in law but they want candidates with atleast a bachelor degree in law , mostly all law related jobs or internships posted on internshala requires the candidates to have atleast a bachelor's degree
