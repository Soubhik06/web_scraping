An year back , my mother and father bought the membership of country club at the cost of ₹80000. 

The country club salespersons sold the membership by saying that it was a silver jubilee offer from country club . They presented it as an investment opportunity in which we’ll have to pay ₹80000 and we would get 5 years of membership where we could avail the hotel services of their country club houses’ and their gym , once every year , with the payment of **an additional maintenance fee of ₹6000/year** . The investment offer being that if we chose not to avail the facility of country club houses in a certain year , we can rent our facilities out to someone else and we would receive **a certain amount of revenue** from it . 

Now here’s the catch . In November,we decided to rent out our country club room . My mother repeatedly called the saleswoman who sold the membership to her and told her to rent out our room . However the saleswoman said that she’ll look into it and in the end , she didn’t rent it out at all . She tries to delay us , deflected our request and ignored us by not picking up our calls . 

So when this happened , my mother decided to cancel our membership but the saleswoman referred her manager to us . He said that we must avail their facilities at least once so as to cancel our membership and claim refund . 

So just to get it over with , we asked them to book a room for us in one of their properties at Goa . When were requested them to book a room for us , the sales woman directed us to contact another woman who worked with country club . When we asked her , the woman said that there are no rooms available for us in Goa so she booked a separate hotel for us , which was a really shitty one. We paid ₹6000 for the maintenance , just to get it over with and claim a refund and cancel our membership . 

Now when we asked them to cancel our membership after the trip was done , they delayed us by saying that our country club office was closed and that we should mail them our complaint and they also gave my mother the number of a person who we should call to register our complaint and cancel our membership. 

A few days later I went to check And the office was finally closed . So as per what they told us to do , I mailed them our complaints . However the person whose number was given to us is not picking up our calls at call. 

Yesterday , my mother called the country club office at Hyderabad and they said that we cannot claim refund . This triggered my mother a lot and she blasted herself at the customer support person who said that she just saw our email and said that our complaint will be looked at . 

**Now , a few months back , after I came back from Goa , I read the agreement and found out that it had two clauses : country club would neither refund our money nor rent our room to someone else in case we do not avail our facility . 

I’m literally shocked at how my mother and father could make this mistake and not even read the agreement . 

Why I think this is a fraud : 

1) before November , my mother called tr customer office and when she asked about the silver jubilee offer , the customer support said that he didn’t even know about the offer 

2) constant ignorance and deflection by the country club employees 

3) Saying something in an oral offer (renting of rooms) which is strictly prohibited in the agreement . 

However the employees are very much conspicuous with their phone numbers and WhatsApp numbers and are answering our phone calls . 

So what is my legal recourse ? Will my complaint in the district forum be considered ? Is there any way I can claim a cancellation of agreement/contract ?
