This happened yesterday while I was driving on the Mumbai-Pune expressway. I stopped to refuel my car and then went to get my Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificate. A man, who looked like a laborer, knocked on my car window and started talking. I ignored him at first, but he was persistent, so I opened the window. He said he had some AirPods from customs and asked if I was interested. 

I've recently switched to Apple products. I got an iPhone last year and also have an Apple Watch, and I had been considering buying AirPods. He showed me a sealed box of AirPods Pro 2nd generation. I checked the serial number online, and it looked genuine. He asked for half the online price, which was in the 22,000-26,000 INR range. I negotiated and got them for 2,000 INR. I opened the box in front of him; it had all the accessories, and the AirPods connected to my phone and worked fine. I have using it since yesterday and that’s the best audio quality I have ever experienced. Now I know why people are crazy for AirPods. 

 I'm wondering if this was some sort of scam or if I got lucky?
