Enviroglobe is a device that reduces background radiation from phones, laptops, mobile towers, wifi routers, etc, thereby helping reduce stress and improving sleep, among other things.

 
My mom recently bought this device, and I told her it was a scam. But when I looked further into this, I found the product to be backed up by AIIMS delhi and Apollo Hospitals. WTF?

This kind of radiation is non-ionizing and negligible and should not impact the human body; moreover, how can a small round object reduce this radiation? Can someone with more knowledge help? Is AIIMS, whom we trust so much, a sellout?
