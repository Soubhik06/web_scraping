So this happened yesterday. I received a text message from EcomExpress saying that they have dispatched a Cash on Delivery parcel on my address.

https://preview.redd.it/nvyoc4ixz5nd1.png?width=1168&amp;format=png&amp;auto=webp&amp;s=c87f77203e6f17dd9e3bea69fb96947e4f3dc122

I was a little confused, as I never use cash of delivery. I decided to dug a little deeper and found that this is a common scam ran by a company called Sociofy Enterprises. 

https://preview.redd.it/k4pmjs2zz5nd1.png?width=1170&amp;format=png&amp;auto=webp&amp;s=767569050291a9e1626a7293af207883d446bec6

https://preview.redd.it/x4y6577006nd1.png?width=913&amp;format=png&amp;auto=webp&amp;s=0f0bebf4ad6be6278a5b6e13d91c830c32e1687d

They randomly send things to people who give cash to the delivery guy thinking that someone else from their family ordered something. 

Now the question arises, how did they get my number, and how did they know that I use online shopping sites frequently. 

The only way these scammer could have gotten my details like my phone number, name and address is from Flipkart.

The phone number on which I received these messages is brand new, and I made a new user ID on Flipkart on 1st September to order something. 

Please be aware, and check where your parcels are coming from, especially if you use COD.
