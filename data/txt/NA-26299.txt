--- POST TEXT ---

Hello Chennaites, Yesterday, I booked an Uber auto and the fare displayed was 215 rupees. The driver asked me to get down on the opposite side of my drop-off location, but I told him it was difficult to cross due to a divider and heavy traffic. He then took a U-turn and dropped me at the correct location. When I got down, he showed me his phone with a fare of 249 rupees. Thinking the U-turn increased the fare, I paid him 249 rupees without checking my Uber app since I was in a hurry. After he left, I noticed the ride was still ongoing in the app. My question is, if he didn't marked the ride as completed, then it wouldn't have displayed any amount right. Did he show me a screenshot or something? (If yes, well planned scam). 

Five minutes later, he marked it as completed, and the fare showed 215 rupees in my Uber app. Feeling cheated, I contacted Uber with a screenshot and details, but they declined the refund, possibly because I had a similar refund issue two weeks ago. I wouldn't mind paying the extra 30 rupees if he asked me genuinely, but this felt dishonest.

At night, I booked a cab from Uber and got home. Even though the pay was a bit higher than auto, no more bargaining or hassle.
