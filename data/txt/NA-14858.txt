Twitter was the first in the race to release the latest transparency reports. As such, it gets the best review with apparently declining grievances in December. For WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and Google, the complaints seem to have risen in varying numbers between October and November.

Since no one likes mulling over reports, here’s MediaNama’s summary of all the company reports so far.

Declining trend of grievances for Twitter

Grievances in December fell drastically since November. As per Twitter’s latest transparency report, here are the data points.

Worrying activity on Instagram

Like other platforms, Instagram too reported a hike in grievances in November. However, worryingly, content relating to self-harm had hit a million by this month. In October too, this category had the highest amount of content actioned.
