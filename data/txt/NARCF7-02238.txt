--- POST TEXT ---

Lately in Kolkata, the bike taxi and cab drivers in Uber, Ola, Rapido, inDrive, Yatri Sathi have become way too greedy, and they keep asking for more money than the final fare. It has become a chain, and now almost everyone I come across has this weird tantrum. They create a fuss over turning on AC, the mode of payment, the destination and paying extra.

I just refuse to give in to their annoying tantrums now. You all shouldn't budge into their silly demands as well and stand up for what's right. Also, try and keep the mode of payment online, as that leaves no room for further harassment as the end of the ride.

I don't witness such behaviour in other cities, but when I come to Kolkata, their leeway is way too prevalent. If people become a bit firm to their stand, then slowly this annoying behaviour will stop as well.
