Well Well Well......this is going to be a long story.

So I've known this guy since class 7 when we shared the same English tuition. Tokhon o maal ta ke niye onek kichu kotha batra chorato.(But bro was a playboy) Ar ami jokhon e oke ei shob niye proshno kortam he used to reply, "Amar baap maa borolok toh tai shobar jhat jole jay, tai ei shob rumours spread kore - karon lekha poray toh kono khut khuje pabe na." - which is partly true because exams e motamoti bhaloi peto.

Fast forward to class 12 - jokhon COVID er por finally offline classes and, since we went our separate ways (Ami Engineering ar o NEET) - tai kotha batra ar hoto na - until he suddenly took admission into my Maths class. (oh ar o amader school e 11-12 poreni because kharap conduct er jonno). Diye addek din o class e asto na. Ekdin dekhi class theke phirte phirte je o metro te onno kono meye r sathe ghure beracche. This went on for some time - at times he returned to classes after 2 months, And sometimes left classes an hour late. I didn't usually care much about it - karon amar jibon e emni onek jhor boichilo ar oi maal tar khabar is my least concerns.

BTW oi class e ar ekta guni nami byakti chilo [(ei je ei article](https://www.anandabazar.com/west-bengal/justice-kaushik-chanda-of-calcutta-high-court-found-out-a-joint-entrance-students-fraud-for-changing-his-wbjee-rank-by-scanning-qr-code-dgtl/cid/1441013)) , eke niye ar beshi kotha bolchi na.

Now fast forward to June 2023 jokhon NEET ar Advance howar por sobai counselling er jonno apply korte laglo) when amar maa bollo je oi maal ta Diamond Harbour Medical College e chance peyechilo. I was a little surprised je without kono effort ekta maal MBBS seat pelo ki bhabe. I dismissed my thoughts - saying je maybe tukka lagiye kono bhabe ekta thik thak rank peye giye hoyto boshe geche.

BUT BUT, amar maa er friend circle er ekjon okhan kar professor and sekhan theke rumours berote shuru holo - je onek political, backdoor, Jaal kore o dhukeche. (or rank was apparently 5 lakhs something). Sekhan thekei puro jinish ta leak hoyeche.

Honestly - I think it's karma. He was a shitty person anyways - and now that the whole of Bengal has got to learn about it, maybe it will put some shame into his mind.

Ei SC/ST problem is really getting out of hand - this brings us to the question, Why do we need reservations anyway. It was intended to bring up the socially backward sections of the society. Ajker din e jodi kono Innova ta chora lok nijeke SC/ST bole ekta govt college er seat niye naye - is it justified, or just a glitch in the already fucked up judiciary system.
