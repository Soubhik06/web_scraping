CORAM : HON'BLE MR. JUSTICE GAUTAM KUMAR CHOUDHARY
 ......... The petitioner who is in custody in connection with Cyber P.S. Case No. 21 of
 2023 for the offence registered under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 120B of the I.P.C., Sections 66(B) , 66(C) , 66(D) and 84(C) of the Information Technology Act pending in the
 Court of Additional Sessions Judge-II, Deoghar. It is submitted by the learned counsel on behalf of petitioner that he has been
 implicated in this case only on the basis of seizure of mobile sets, apart from this, there is
 no other material to implicate him in the offence. There is no material to show that the
 mobile sets were used in any case of cyber fraud. Learned S.P.P. opposed the prayer for bail. It is submitted that the mobile
 sets which were seized from the petitioner having Sim Cards in the name of some
 other persons. It has come in Para-71 of the Case diary that the IMEI number of the
 mobile sets which were seized from the petitioner was used by different sim cards.
 Suspicious e-wallets were created from the mobile seized from him and series of
 call had been made and there is specific reference with respect to two victims in
 Paras-99 & 101 of the case diary. Considering the nature of allegation and materials on record, I am not
 inclined to enlarge the petitioner on regular bail and accordingly, the same is
 rejected. (Gautam Kumar Choudhary, J.)
