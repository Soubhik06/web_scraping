I switched from Jio to Vodafone and its been close to 4 months now, mostly because Jio had really bad reception from where I live. 

Vodafone was fine for the most part , until I received my bill today, I usually don't look at my bill as I pay it without looking much into the details of it, but today something caught my eye. 

I was charged 100 plus for VAS, which I never use, I usually get my bills around 450 to 510 even if I make ISD calls, but this time around it was a bit higher than usual, even though most of my conversations were through Messenger apps, which I used through my home WiFi connection.

I did try out only their ZEE5 Premium plan, but it was as per their policy completely free of any cost, so if they charge me for it that would be straight up fraud. 

I am happy to pay for their VAS services, but this is done without any explicit consent from my end.

Their argument for me asking them was that "Certain Pop-Ups can cause me to download files which are VAS and will add to my Vodafone bill", though Mozilla both on PC and Phone won't allow Pop-Ups and actively blocks any incoming connection of that sort. I download everything on my fibre at home, but this is retarded.Who is stupid enough to download anything through a fucking popup.

Plus, I don't own a phone with any adware. I don't have any RAM Cleaners or any of those Chinese clone apps. They also said that I would have to receive OTP to confirm payment, I have been receiving fake uninvited OTPs for a long time. Even then, the OTP has to be confirmed by the server on the other end to complete payment.

This just generally looks and feels shady as fuck. I know 600 is not a huge sum of money, but the general idea of scamming people into paying for stuff they don't use is just fuckery.
