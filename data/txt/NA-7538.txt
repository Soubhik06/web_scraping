Heard counsel for the parties. This bail application has been filed by the applicant, who
has been arrested on 31.10.2014 and is in Jail in connection
with Crime No.390/2011 registered with Police Station
Gopalganj, District Sagar - commonly known as VYAPAM
Examination Scam cases - for the offence punishable under Sections 417 , 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 472 , 474 and 120-B of
IPC and also under Section 3(Gha) (2) / 4 of the M.P. Manyata
Prapt Pariksha Adhiniyam, 1937. The role ascribed to the applicant by the prosecution is
that of a middleman. The applicant facilitated the beneficiary -
Sanjeev Kustwar by arranging impersonator to commit unfair
means by the original beneficiary during the PMT examination
2009 conducted by VYAPAM. It is not disputed that
beneficiary and the impersonator have already been arrested. Learned counsel for the State on instructions submits that
investigation against the applicant is complete in all respects.
No further investigation is pending against the applicant nor
custodial interrogation of the applicant is necessary in
connection with the subject crime. It is stated that investigation
 is complete and presence of the applicant is no more necessary,
coupled with the fact applicant through counsel undertakes to
appear before the Investigating Officer as and when called for
further investigation including against him as also to
punctually appear before the trial Court to ensure early
disposal of the criminal trial. In the interest of justice, taking overall view of the
matter, we deem it appropriate to grant bail to the applicant on
following strict conditions:- The bail application stands disposed of accordingly.
