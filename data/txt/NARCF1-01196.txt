**TL;DR:** 
No easy way exists to get help with everyday offline tasks — phone repairs, small moves, home setups, tech help for seniors, etc. We’re building a platform to fix that for busy folks in Blr.

👉[ Join the waitlist for early access!](https://pity-task-pal-06.lovable.app/) 
 
Hey everyone, 

We’re pre-final year students at BITS Pilani and are currently validating a product we’re working on something we believe solves a very real, very under-addressed problem.We tried operations in an area and got amazing response. 

After speaking to working professionals and students who’ve moved cities, one thing stood out: there’s no simple way to get help with everyday offline tasks that fall outside of the “Swiggy/Zomato/Uber” universe.

Things like: 
 
⁠ &lt;**Broke Your Phone But No Time to Fix It?** 
We’ve got you. A trusted tasker picks up your device, gets it repaired within your budget, and drops it back — no long queues, no shady shops, no surprises. Just easy, hassle-free fixing. 

**&lt;Moving Homes But It's Just You and a Bean Bag?** 
Whether it’s shifting a few things, assembling furniture, or setting up your new place — get a helping hand when you need it. Quick, reliable support for small moves and home setup. No trucks, no fuss, just help. 

**&lt;Messy room or overflowing closet?** You don’t need a specialist — just a capable hand that knows the job. Get help sorting, folding, and bringing order back to your space. Simple, stress-free support.

**&lt;Hosting a small party or gathering?** 
Sometimes all you need is an extra hand,not a professional. Get quick, reliable help from someone who's done it before. Simple. Affordable.Stress-free.

**&lt;Grandpa Can’t Join the Zoom Call Again?** 
We get it. Tech can be tricky for the elderly but help is just a tap away. Our friendly taskers assist with phone setups, apps, video calls, online banking &amp; more. Safe, patient, and scam-free support for seniors, right at home. 

All of these are real, recent examples and they don’t require any professional skills. Just time, reliability, and willingness to help.

1. **Right now, there's no easy platform where you can just say, “Hey, I need help with X,” and get matched with someone nearby. We think that's a gap, especially for busy 22–30-year-olds living away from home in fast-paced cities like Blr.**

If this resonates with you, we’d love your feedback: 
What are some small but annoying tasks you’ve wished someone could just take care of for you?
