#SCAM_ALERT
I just got Scammed in Delhi Metro at Mandi House metro station. I was waiting for the blue line metro towards Dwarka when a man probably in his mid-fifties, wearing a grey coloured kurta approached me asking if there was a UCO bank ATM anywhere in the metro station.
He spoke fluent English, I replied to him there's instead a PNB ATM. Then he asked me if I could lend him 30-40 rupees, and he'll transfer it back to me via a UPI payment. He boarded the train with me, I took out 40 rupees from my wallet and handed it over to him.
The next, he asked for my UPI QR, and I showed it. He clicked the picture and said there's no network and he'll transfer as the network comes back again. By this time, I'd known that I have been scammed. He moved around and left the train at the next station, while I foolishly saw him walk away.

The only smart thing, I "probably" did was that I showed him QR code of my PayTM UPI, which is linked to one of the bank accounts with quite low funds.
