This Writ petition has been filed by the petitioner seeking direction to

 the respondents to constitute a Special Wing of the Vigilance and Anti

 Corruption Department to conduct enquiry for the scam made by the

 officials of the “Arulmigu Mariamman Temple” which is situated at

 Avadhanapatti Village, Agraharam Post, Krishnagiri Taluk & District and

 initiate action within the stipulated period fixed by this Hon'ble Court. 2. The case of the petitioner is that the petitioner's temple i.e.,

 “Arulmigu Mariamman Temple” which is situated at Avadhanapatti Village,

 Agraharam Post, Krishnagiri Taluk & District and it is a village temple and

 the petitioner and the other parties are hereditary Trustees-cum-Poojaris of

 the said temple and earlier their ancestors who were Trustees of the said 2/11 https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis WP.No.25507 of 2018 temple as per order in O.A.No.83 of 1976 dated 06.07.1977. That the temple

 is under the control of the HR & CE Department and the administration is

 hereditary one. 2(i) The annual income of the temple is Rs.50,00,000/-. The said

 amount is misused by the Inspector Mr.Shankar, Thakkar Raja. For the past

 five years (2012-2018), the Joint Commissioner Mrs.Mangaikarasi did not

 take any steps and indirectly indulged in misusing the income of the temple

 and also the present Joint Commissioner Mr.Varadarajan is involved in it.

 The above said officials are involved in the following scams: 3. The learned Additional Government Pleader filed a counter

 affidavit on behalf of the respondents by denying all the allegations and

 averments made in the petition. The petitioner has filed this petition with

 malafide intention to usurp the temple properties and thereby causing a huge

 loss to the temple as well as its devotees. The said temple is situated at 6/11 https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis WP.No.25507 of 2018 Avadhanapatti Village, Krishnagiri District and the same is listed temple

 under Section 46 of the TN HR and CE Act, 1959. The temple owned

 several movable and immovable properties. The administration of the

 temple were carried out by hereditary trustees appointed through various

 suits and judicial proceedings which subsequently resulted in two rival

 factions. Further, it resulted in maladministration and swindling of temple

 funds. Therefore, for better administration and to protect the interest of the

 temple as well as the devotees, the second respondent herein, pursuant to

 the order of this Court vide order dated 11.12.2016, appointed a fit person

 through Government Order which is extracted here under: 4. Heard both sides and perused the materials available on record

 including counter filed by the 2nd respondent. 5. Considering the facts and circumstances of the case, the

 submissions made by the learned counsel and on going through the records

 and taking note of the fact that the aforesaid allegations are pertaining to the 8/11 https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis WP.No.25507 of 2018 year 2012-2018 and the enquiry was conducted in the year 2013 is not

 sufficient, this Court directs the Chief Audit Officer, Office of the

 Commissioner(Audit Wing) HR & CE Department, Chennai to appoint an

 enquiry officer to conduct an enquiry. The Audit Officer hailing from Office

 of the Commissioner(Audit Wing) HR & CE Department Chennai is

 appointed as an Enquiry Officer to deal with the said allegations made and it

 is also seen that the some of the properties were sold, but, the same has not

 reflected in the counter. Hence, enquiry to be conducted by the authorities

 concerned without fail after affording an opportunity to all the parties

 concerned within a period of six months from the date of appointment of an

 new enquiry officer and proceed further in accordance with law. 6. With the above direction, this writ petition is disposed of. No order
