--- POST TEXT ---

I don't have any marketable technical skills at the moment. Naturally as an unemployed guy, I wanted to try my hand at freelance content writing.

I enjoy doing research online, hoarding resources and sometimes I write in my leisure.

I can weave together a narrative, but often it's not cohesive and concise. I'm impatient.

Still, I want to get better somehow. I'm quick with my Googlefu and I know where to get my resources from. I lurk through reddit threads to find the most interesting bits of information. And I bookmark them to read later.

You see, I suffer from a lack of guidance and self-confidence. And this has left me vulnerable and in a loop of procrastination.

...

So I wanted to find a content writing course that will hold me accountable with daily tasks and check my writing, help me with feedback and offer professional career advice.

I started my search with "best content writing courses in india".

*IIM Skills* topped the first page of Google with outstanding positive reviews from a variety of sources. Without thinking too much, I trusted my gut instinct when I quickly skimmed through various reviews and within moments, I was ready to pay the sum of 15,800 Rupees.

After the paid status flashed on my screen, I started seeing a few redflags. (Yes, this is how people who get scammed feel like.)

The SEO tool was showing a low domain authority and relatively low monthly visits for a site that was claimed to be best of the best. There was no login information that allowed me to view the course right after I had paid, just a receipt in my email.

At this point, I knew what was happening. I knew my money was gone. I started feeling guilty for my stupidity but somehow managed to think that if I immediately let them know that I am not satisfied with the course, maybe I would still have a chance of a refund.

I was greeted in text support by Mr. Shiva Shrivastava, the customer support guy. He told me he's going to call on my phone, after which he started telling me about IIM Skills' faculty and alumni, and how they will help me with my content writing career. The 4 month course consisted of 1 month training (3.5 hours of classes for 4 Sundays) and the rest for helping with internships, which would be assisted training for another 3 months, writing ebooks and articles for clients like Times of India. The idea was to grow an online presence and visibility. He tried to give the impression of a professional course but the guy really sounded like he was trying to manipulate me with empty words. And he didn't want to hide it.

I eventually told him that I want a refund and expressed concerns on red-flags. He went on with assuring me by calling me "dear" and telling me that he "will explain each and everything so I don't need to worry".

Although there was no proper explanation, he told me to escalate the issue further with his superiors as he was not in the position to give me a refund.

So I sent an email to them explaining briefly that I am not happy with the course and I would like to get a refund. They reply: "Our refund policy does not allow refunds in such cases. We would also like to know what changed your mind."

I didn't reply back after that.

Fast forward two weeks: on Friday I get a text on my WhatsApp about a Zoom meeting from Mr. Vaibhav Kakkar.

I decided to join it and see what it's about.

As the meeting started on Zoom, someone turned the camera on for a couple of seconds who looked like a foreign woman. It's difficult to say whether it was a virtual cam, or they actually paid a foreigner to attend a class OR someone from outside India searched for IIM Skills and wanted to learn content writing from this institution where the lead instructor spells Country as "County" during demonstration.

Mr. Vaibhav emphasized the importance of having a niche and told the students to install Ubersuggest. Then he told us to make a Google a sheet with "different terms related to writing and the niche that we selected and find their search volume". And that was it. That was the task. The instructions were poor.

Although there were over 80 students in this "batch" (who are paid actors) to give a premium feeling to the course but really failed at it.

I left the zoom call within 10 minutes and had a discussion with Mr. Shiva again who told me that Mr. Vaibhav will talk to me regarding this issue in the afternoon. Vaibhav never called me so I gave him a call in late afternoon and immediately he told me that he would get back to me on Monday.

I tried to make it clear and said that I know what they are doing here and that the entire course is an absolute shitshow. To which he said that he knew my "intention" from the beginning. And that just trying a course for 5 minutes for the sake of trying doesn't qualify as a genuine response from my end. When I said that I had seen their course content from previous recordings and pointed out the low quality he said "we don't do things like that over here in IIM Skills" and "we don't want to associate with people who are not on the same level". Implying that I was the one who isn't suited for their class.

After that the call ended and I am yet to receive another call from him but I don't think he's going to offer a refund.

\*Sigh\*

Upon further searching through trustpilot, I found the review of a former student going by the name of Simran who has expressed their concerns about the lack of professionalism, and the entire chaos that is IIM Skills. If you try to argue with them with logic, they will play dumb and it's difficult to win against them when you look at the arsenal of people that are likely associated with forged reviews.

Anyone joining this course will know that *IIM SKills* is a **scam**.

And there are more like them out there. It's easy to fall prey to these businesses when someone feels lack of confidence and seeks guidance, but doesn't understand what they really need. All product based scams operate under this very principle: they bait the impatient, impulsive people into getting quick relief through false sense of security and value.

The real lesson is that **nothing comes without struggle and perseverance**. Self-learning will make you a fortune if you're disciplined enough.

This particular scam was very primitive and I feel bad that I fell for it so quickly. I hope no-one else has to waste their time, money and energy on businesses like this.

**tl;dr** *IIM Skills* promised to teach me the skills and techniques needed to become a successful content writer. However, as I progressed through the course, it became clear that the material being presented was quite irrelevant. The course had grammar and spelling mistakes, and the lecture was nonsense for the most part. They have a massive army of fake reviewers and paid students that give the impression of legitimacy.

Overall, I would strongly advise against wasting your time and money on this scam of a content writing course. It is a complete waste of resources and will not provide you with any valuable skills or knowledge
