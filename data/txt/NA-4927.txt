Heard Mr. FKR Ahmed, learned counsel for the accused and also heard Mr. D. Das, learned Addl. P.P.
for the State respondent. This application, under Section 439 Cr.P.C. is preferred by the accused namely, 1. Md. Abdul Jalil, 2. Md. Rofiq Islam and 3. Md. Mamun Oli Ullah who has been languishing in jail hajot since 23.8.2023

in connection with Jagirod P.S. Case No.230/2023, under Section 420 / 468 / 471 / 34 IPC (G.R.Case No.
1367/2023) since 2.8.20123 for grant of bail. The above noted case has been registered on the basis of an FIR lodged by one WSI(P) Kanchan
Basumatary on 1.8.2023. The essence of allegation made in the aforesaid FIR is that acting on a tip off regarding running of
illegal cyber crime scam in his house by one Abdul Jalil, she alongwith other staff conducted search in
the house of Abdul Zalil and recovered 504 nos of SIM card, 5 nos of smart phones, 4 nos of keypad
mobile handsets, Cash Rs. 1,40,000/-, two nos of bank pass book, ATM card and one cheque book from
the possession of Md. Mamun Oli Ullah, Md Abdul Kalam and Md. Baharul Islam and similar
operation was conducted in the house of Rafique Islam and during search recovered and seized 739
nos of VI and Jio SIM card alongwith the 4 nos. of mobile handsets from the possession of the
accused Md. Rafique Islam, Md. Abdul Jalil and Md Mufeed Ali. Learned counsel for the accused submits that all the three accused persons were arrested on
2.8.2023, and since then they are languishing in jail hajot for last 55 days and no charge sheet has been
submitted till date and there remains five days only to elapse the statutory period of detention and
therefore, considering the period of detention, it is submitted that the accused may be granted bail. On the other hand, Mr. Das learned Addl. P.P. has submitted that he has not received the case
diary, but, he has not disputed that the accused are behind the bar for last 55 days. Having heard the submissions of learned Advocates for both sides, I have carefully gone
through the petition and the documents placed on record and though the case diary has not been
produced before this,Court yet considering the period of detention of the accused person and balancing
the same with the nature and gravity of the offence and the punishment prescribed for the same, this
Court is inclined to allow this petition, it is provided that on furnishing a bond of Rs.50,000/- each,
with one surety of like amount to the satisfaction of learned CJM, Morigaon the accused persons be
enlarged on bail. In terms of above, the bail application is disposed of. JUDGE
