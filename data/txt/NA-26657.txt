Since UPI transactions have become like second nature for many it’s important to take a breath and note the implications of indiscriminate usage.

As most of us know UPI is the preferred mode of choice for many of the rampant scamming activities across the country right now.

If and when the scammers are reported either for this or some other bigger scam conducted by someone in their ring and they come under the scrutiny of law enforcement a lot of bank accounts that have engaged in any transaction with any of the numbers owned by the scammers can get frozen until investigations are on. 

If your account is frozen you may even at some point have to show up for questioning as it’s a SOP for the scammers to route the money to their primary account for cash withdrawn through a chain of around five bank accounts and during investigations they have no way of knowing if an account belongs to an accomplice or a victim until they finish tracing the money trail.

How to protect yourself? 

1. Keep your UPI contact circle as clean as possible. Most UPI scams work because victims are very blasé about these transactions.

2. Use a separate bank account with basic balance for all random transactions or one time payments to cabs, shops, street vendors etc. So that even if it gets frozen you can chill and wait for it to be cleared in its own good time. While your primary account with all major scheduled credits and debits stays clear.
