[https://www.newslaundry.com/2025/01/11/explained-are-votes-really-being-illegally-deleted-from-the-electoral-rolls](https://www.newslaundry.com/2025/01/11/explained-are-votes-really-being-illegally-deleted-from-the-electoral-rolls)

It's all but confirmed that the Central government has been deliberately deleting names of voters in electoral areas where they previously lost. There are many cases where BJP has won by a small margin after deleting nearly thousands of votes.

In Delhi, in the 2024 elections, people from Muslim-majority areas and areas like Chandni Chowk complained of having their names deleted from the voter list without their KNOWLEDGE. No notification arrived from the election commission or anything, it was just deleted anonymously. 

Not only that but there are reports of fake voter ID's flooding the voter list. So it is important to check the voter list of your area and see if you find any unknown or suspicious people on that list and report them. 

Regardless of your political leanings, you cannot deny this blatant violation of our fundamental rights. 

The final voter list is out, but you can still apply to voteif your name isn't on the list. It's a simple process and you will easily receive your voter ID before the elections. 

Here are the steps:-

* Confirm the presence of your name on the voter list by going to this [website](https://electoralsearch.eci.gov.in/). Type in your voter ID and select your State. If your name is there then good. If it isn't, then move to the next step. 
* If your name isn't on the list then you need to go to this [website](https://voters.eci.gov.in/). Then go and fill Form 6 for "new voters for general electors."
* You can also go to this [website](https://www.ceodelhi.gov.in/) to see the entire voter list of your area. Click on "Final Electoral Roll" under electoral services, then put in your area and you should be able to view the entire voter list of your constituency. Check the voter list to see if your neighbours are on it or if there are some fake voters on it. 

I hope this is helpful. Do not sit this out and do not underestimate the importance of your vote.
