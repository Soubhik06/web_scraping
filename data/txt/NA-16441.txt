Hi,

**I wanted to share this to prevent others from incurring additional charges at BLS centres.**

This is my first time applying for a Schengen Visa. I reside in Bangalore, but applied through BLS Chennai as all slots were showing full for BLS Bangalore, like a month early. The below is based on my experience in Chennai centre. There was only one person handling everything inside a room. They had no print/photocopy options within that place.

I work in IT, so the below guide may be useful for salaried professionals. It may differ if you are self-employed/student.

**Disclaimer:** It may be possible you are asked for other documents as well. Use it at your own discretion.

**Mandatory and Non-negotiable documents:**

1. Filled application form - Take it from [here](https://www.schengenvisainfo.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/spanish_schengen_visa_application_form_english.pdf). Don't expect to fill there.
2. Latest passport size photos (2).
3. Original passports, ***including old ones***. BLS website says *can* give old passports. But it is mandatory to give. Non-negotiable.
4. Photocopies of *all non-empty pages* of ***all passports.*** Don't take pages only containing other country visas. They are asking for all pages with entry/exit seals as well.
5. Roundtrip flight tickets - I had real ones.
6. Accommodations - I had booked airbnbs for all my stays. Airbnb has an option to *print for visa.* I used that.
7. Travel Insurance from a recognised insurance partner. check the approved ones [here](https://india.blsspainvisa.com/pdf/ANNEX.pdf).
8. Salary account bank statements - Last 6 months *- Sealed and signed by Bank*. Non-negotiable.
9. Payslips - last 3 months
10. ITR - V Acknowledgement slips - Last 2 years
11. PAN Card copy.
12. Cover Letter.
13. Leave approved letter in Company letterhead.
14. Filled BLS Authorization form/Sworn Declaration Form/Courier Form(if needed) - Download all these from [here](https://india.blsspainvisa.com/short_term_visa.php) under *Download Form* tab. 
15. Travel Itinerary (This was not asked, but I provided them myself).

**Apart from the above documents, I had the following ones (was not asked):**

1. Proof of residence (Rental Agreement/Company letter mentioning address) - This was mentioned in their list of documents to provide. They were not okay if the address was in Passport/Aadhaar when I had contacted BLS support through mail.
2. Form 16 - last 2 years. As it was not clear if they would accept *ITR-V Acknowledgement* slips, as in the list of documents to submit, it only lists ITR-V.
3. Internal travel reservations - Flights/train bookings within Spain. 
4. Company offer letter.
5. Savings account bank statements - sealed and signed.

**Pitfalls/Traps by BLS Center:**

1. Bank Statements should be until the day before appointment date. They should be sealed and signed in the bank. You have to do this in person by visiting the branch. Online download copies won't be accepted.
2. All passport photocopies of all non-empty pages are needed. Don't take only your current passport photocopies. Also, you have to submit all your original passports.
3. BLS will not send these 3 forms - BLS Authorization form/Sworn Declaration Form/Courier Form when you book appointment/ask them what documents are needed to be submitted. You will not find these 3 mentioned in the [list provided](https://india.blsspainvisa.com/pdf/Tourist-Visa.pdf) in the official website. But these are mandatory except maybe courier form if you want to collect it yourself.
4. Only get Travel Insurance from approved insurers.

**BLS Scam:**

If you miss any of the above documents or even if it only requires you to get a print out/photocopy, BLS centre person will refuse to give you any time and will tell you that if you want to submit today you have to come only in the afternoon. And afternoon batches are premium slots and ***it will cost extra Rs.3000*** or if you want to do normal get normal appointment for another day. If you check their website, it will be blocked for an entire month. This is a scam that they do with every applicant. 

Both me and a guy who came next to me, didn't have our old passport photocopies. Even though it took only 5-10 mins to get these , he refused to entertain in the same appointment. This is also the reason why they only allow 5-10 mins before your appointment. 

They make sure they find some reason to send you out to get a print/photocopy so that they can force you to pay Rs.3000 for premium processing. Apart from these, they also add Value Added Services (VAS) without asking for permission. 

Some VAS added to my bill are:

1. SMS service - Rs. 200
2. STS service (phone call service for every status change in application) - Rs. 350
3. Courier Assurance - Rs. 145

BLS is benefitting by unchecked control over their customers' visa application processes, exploiting loopholes and creating unnecessary hurdles. The lack of a standardized and transparent list of required documents from visa-issuing countries leaves applicants vulnerable to arbitrary demands and additional charges.

I wanted to confront the centre person about the VAS, but realised there is no accountability for any of his actions. I mean, what if he just removes a document from my application after I leave? They don't give any acknowledgement that I have provided these set of documents.

Reading about other centre experiences, I feel this is common or worse in other centres in India. 

P.S: I did get my Visa approved.
