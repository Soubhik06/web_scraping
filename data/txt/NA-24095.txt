Recently Nithin Kamath shared in LinkedIn how one of his friends got scammed of several lakhs by some fraudsters where they claimed they were from Mumbai Crime Branch and involved in drug and money laundering. 

In the exact same manner one of my friends got scammed where they called him and told that their FedEx parcel had drugs and currently with them. They called him over Skype and asked for all bank account details, balances, PAN, Aadhar. They also shared their fake id cards with their photo in Police Uniform (which looks very genuine). To close the case they asked to transfer the money or he will be put in jail. Some out of the court settlements. He agreed and paid 98000 over Google pay. After this he casually Google searched Mumbai Crime branch FedEx which led him to the fact that he was scammed. 
These people are scamming the hell out of honest earning citizens. There are many friends who have fallen into the traps of these fraudsters. I try to teach people around me but still it's happening very frequently. Please be careful. We will file case but don't think the money is coming back or the culprits will be apprehended.
