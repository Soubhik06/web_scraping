It's not a scam but more like manipulation, they are official coolies for the metro and they will identify you when you feel kinda lost, this happened to me at the New Delhi metro station and was going to the airport through the airport express line. The coolie identified my cluelessness and asked about where I wanted to go, took my luggage, kept it on the trolley, and asked me for 140rs (60 for me, 80 for luggage, he gave receipt of both) in rush by saying jaldi jaldi metro nikal jayegi, I thought he will leave me till metro station, I was like okay, its good if he will leave me at the airport, but he just took me from elevator to a floor below, and left me and the luggage there, on the platform, and asked for tips, and as a gullible teen I gave him the note with least denomination in my wallet(20rs), and he asked for more. Bruh I just paid 100rs to him for walking with my luggage for 10metres. Be aware guys, not to get into such traps.
