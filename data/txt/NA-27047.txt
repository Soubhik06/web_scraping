Last Friday, I was on my laptop browsing dog houses (for my dog....LOL!) and all of a sudden I got an alert pop up window with a shrilling alarm sound and my laptop was locked. In the pop up message there was a 1-877 number to call. I thought this was legit so I called the number. I spoke to what I thought was a MS support agent, (East Indian Accent) and we went through the steps to recover my laptop from what I thought was a virus. He guided me to open the command prompt window and guided me with prompts, did a scan and at the end of it, there was a message: C:\\illegal activies found on www.pornhub.com//china child pornography downloaded//russian weapons ordered//phone lines not secured//online banking attempted//cards 3xxx 4xxx 5xxx 6xxx used illegally// girls, drugs..\*\* was at the bottom of the Command Prompt message. This concerned me thinking my laptop was/is being used, however, what tipped me off, the "support agent" was asking me what network I was on, I didn't know and didn't offer the information up, then he asked what country I live in, I said Canada, and he asked "Is Canada in the United States?" ah...yeah, no. I felt that was a tip this was a scam, so I hung up. I alreted my bank, changed ALL my passwords on laptop for banking...etc...did a virus scan using McAffee...cleaned up my laptop for suspicious files and what not. It's been 48 hours since this happened, and so far nothing suspicious is going on, so I take it, this was a scam?
