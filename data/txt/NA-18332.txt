As many of you might have already heard/read, the very famous and popular KAT got taken down and mercilessly so. 

https://torrentfreak.com/feds-seize-kickasstorrents-domains-charge-owner-160720/ 

https://torrentfreak.com/can-kickasstorrents-make-a-comeback-160721/ 

This is huge. 

I couldn't find a post on the sub about this news, there should be given that how much usage KAT got from places like India. 

What about alternatives? 

The 2 biggest things i would be missing is the HEVC source all in 1 place and finding new content that i would otherwise have had no idea even existed. 
Like TV shows or independent movies from some small European Country or some other East Asian content. 

Now its all a mess. We now will have to scour manually over more than 2-3 sites to get all this, if one is lucky. 

And before anyone says, Piracy and what not. Even if you don't like it is still a reality, esp in India. 

Reddit already has r/trackers and /r/kickasstorrents etc as its other subs. Nothing wrong in having a talk on this, as long as there is no files being shared, its just news and sources which are relevant.
