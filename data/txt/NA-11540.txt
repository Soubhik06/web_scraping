 prove which is on the Defendant. 21.It is the case of the Defendant in his WS that he has repaid
 the entire loan with interest. However, during the course of
 the trial, it has been explained by him that the same was
 wrongly written in the WS and that till now only
 Rs.58,000/- has been repaid. 22.This is clear from para 8 of his Evidence Affidavit Ex.DW-

 25.07.2023 as well as Rs.25,000/- in cash. 23.The UPI repayment of Rs.33,000/- is admitted by the
 Plaintiff, as noted above, but the cash repayment of
 Rs.25,000/- is denied. 24.Thus, even as per the Defendant's own case, at best, he has
