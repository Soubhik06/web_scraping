Seriously, just look up "expedia live support" and look how much bullshit is pushed to the top of google associating a fake number with expedia- none of which is expedia's website. Several of such articles are very recent. It is not expedia. 

I found it from googling expedia's live support &amp; gave it a call; it's an Indian call centre that will ask for your cc to book reservations manually. Huge red flag. If you find and google the number, dozens of articles will come up pairing it with the word "expedia." I wanted to put it in the title of this post so the post would come up when someone searched for expedia support or for the number, but it's not allowed.

Expedia does not send emails from trips at reservationsdesk.net. Expedia is already shady; now there is a concerted effort by scammers to take advantage of booking errors to deceive unsuspecting users.

This is a scam and any number that does not appear on their actual website is spoofed.
