--- POST TEXT ---

Received a WhatsApp call today from this number from an officer claiming to be from CBI. Understood it was a scam the moment he started speaking English. Told him to speak in Hindi instead.

Informed me that my sister was arrested by CBI and was being taken to Delhi. I'm a practicing advocate and asked about the particulars of the offence and arrest details. Instead of a proper explanation, warnings started to come in with threats of imprisonment for 10 to 15 years. I asked the offence details again and didn't receive any answer.

Told him to proceed as he wished, following which he cut the call. Contacted the Kerala Cyber Cell and reported the matter but I don't think any action will follow.

If anyone else receives such calls, please do not panic. Such unexpected calls informing that your loved ones might be in danger will be terrifying. But stay calm and try to ask as much details as possible without giving out any personal info. If there are any monetary demands, do not pay under any circumstance. Law enforcement agencies cannot call you and demand money to settle the matter.

Stay vigilant and safe dear people. ❤️
