This is my first post on r/india so pardon me if this is the wrong forum to post this. Also very long, so again pardon the length. Read the last 3\-4 paragraphs if you have no time.

I had to bring this up someplace, where it could get some traction. It could also serve to forewarn other prospective customers about Quikr's shoddy practices. I'll detail my ordeal in a timeline manner in order to make it more readable. Apologies for the length.

**Sequence of events**

**April 23** \- I placed an ad to sell my Samsung 318L refridgerator on Quikr with AD ID 307242735.

**May 30** \- Received an offer of 20,000 INR. The buyer called me to confirm the offer, I accepted and buyer promptly makes payment to Quikr. The arrangement is Quikr Doorstep will hold the money, pick the product up from my doorstep, deliver it to the buyer's doorstep and then once buyer signs off, transfer the money to me.

**May 31** \- I schedule a pickup in the 4\-6pm slot. Quikr driver calls me at 12 and asks to pick it up. I'm at work so ask him to stick to the slot. *Red flag 1:* He calls again at 3 and says he's already at my address and if I don't enable pick up then, he will drive away. I rush home and make it happen. The condition of the fridge at pick up is [here](https://imgur.com/a/W2u1fxM), [here](https://imgur.com/a/hXGccRH) &amp; [here](https://imgur.com/a/qiOoYgg).

**June 1** \- Buyer has scheduled drop off in the 12\-2pm slot. Quikr calls to tell him his area is not serviceable as per their doorstep delivery model, so they cannot drop it there. *Red flag 2:* They gave no such hint to the buyer when he was making payment. Buyer then agrees to come to their furthest serviceable point close to his residence (7km away) with his own truck to pick it up. Delivery rescheduled for June 5 in the 10am\-12pm slot.

**June 5** \- Buyer reaches spot with truck, combines this pick up with a few other purchases. Truck has cost him 1200 INR. *Red flag 3:* Quikr vehicle fails to arrive in the slot. Buyer waits until 2pm and customer care says the vehicle had broken down. Asks him to wait until 5. Buyer is frustrated and finally cancels purchase around 4pm. I contact buyer and hear about this, call customer care and they say they can do nothing now but return the product since buyer has already refused to accept it on the app.

**June 6** \- Return is initiated for 10am\-12pm. I take work from home to facilitate this. *Red flag 4:* Product doesn't arrive even until 1pm so I call customer care and they quote "operational issues" and that product will be delivered by EOD. Don't receive any update until 7pm so call the same cusomer service rep and he promises it will be there in the next 1 hour. Call again at 9 and he says "operational issues" are still ongoing so it can only be returned tomorrow.

**June 7** \- Return scheduled again for 10am\-12pm. This time I took sick leave, as I can't WFH more than once a week. *Red flag 5:* Product arrives at 10:45am with dents on the freezer side as seen [here](https://imgur.com/a/Tmo4Pzy). Wife and I are absolutely devastated, hold the truck, refuse to accept the return, call the logistics guy who meekly apologizes and says he can maybe pay 1000 for the damage. After an hour on the phone with him, he agrees reluctantly to 5000 as damage, but asks his truck driver to get back to the warehouse as we refuse to let it enter the house without the money in hand. We started ranting all over their social media pages, finally one person from the SM team calls me to say Quikr's policy allows only a compensation up to 5\-10&amp;#37; of the buyer's agreed purchase price. We tell her that the logistics guy agreed for 5000, she speaks to him and he denies ever agreeing this. After 3 hours of fighting over phone, they finally agree to 4000. We write a detailed mail with all damages listed and inform that we agree to this compensation only under protest, and that we don't admit that this is the right amount for damage caused. Return is scheduled for June 11, and the SM person agrees with us that we will once again check for other damages and then a final compensation over and above 4000 will be agreed upon delivery, if more damages are found. Return is scheduled for June 11, 7\-10am.

**June 11** \- Another day we had to skip work as Quikr failed on its return by 10am, changed the slot to 12\-2pm without consulting us, and did not even inform us. We had to call customer care to learn this. We wait until 3pm and call the SM person, who says there are "operational issues" again (quelle surprise) and she will get it delivered by EOD. Fridge doesn't arrive by EOD (quelle surprise, again).

**June 12**\- Start the fire fight early and the entire day was spent on calls &amp; emails with the SM person who again quotes "operational issues". Finally at 5pm, she says the fridge will arrive in 1 hour and the logistics team will call us. Got a call from the logistics person, who said the fridge will arrive before 1130pm. Which person in their right mind will let in strangers to their house at 1130pm? We refuse to take the return if it is done after 9pm. *Red flag 6:* It arrives at 8:45pm and to our utter horror, note further dents as seen on the [door](https://imgur.com/a/8OTgEtv), [side](https://imgur.com/a/N119Dkl) and the [front door](https://imgur.com/a/pTu0F46). We decide to accept it since the SM person had already promised the 4000 for damages noted on June 7, and agreed to compensate for further damages.

**June 13** \- We write a detailed email at 9am with all images of dents/damages to SM person and seek further compensation. *Red flag** 7*: No response. I call customer care at 3:45 pm and ask for her, they say she will call me in 2 hours. No call until 6:15pm so i call again and this time demand to speak. Her colleague comes on line and says we will definitely get the 4000 but she does not commit to a time line or anything more. I ask her to make a confirmation of this payment at least by EOD, as well as ask the SM person to call me before EOD.

**June 14 (today)** \- No response, as expected, from the SM person or any other Quikr rep. I have posted ads on other websites/Facebook and contacted local people to buy the fridge now in its present condition, and I am not getting offers for anything more than 8\-9k.

**Next step** \- We are considering pursuing legal action. My wife is a lawyer and we don't want to let go of this.

**Request** \- I would really appreciate it if you guys could tweet this at Quikr/other social media influencers and increase visibility for me. Here is my Tweet linking to this post which you can retweet/share - https://twitter.com/arvindhram924/status/1007114521712910336?s=19

**Warning** \- If you are considering Quikr doorstep, reconsider. If you are considering selling a product on an online marketplace, reconsider. Never let your product leave your door without cash in hand or money in the bank. It's not your responsibility what happens to it after it leaves your door, but in this case, as we learnt it the hard way, the burden is all back on us.

**Final word** \- Quikr run their scam at the mercy of unsuspecting customer, and it is no wonder that their CEO Pranay Chulet is inactive on Twitter since 2012. They've made it extremely hard to find email IDs of their people that matter so all our communication has been with a lowly social media executive. They are a shady, shady business that I hope soon goes bust. Eat shit, you guys.

**TLDR:** Quikr took our fridge for delivery on 31 May, did not deliver on time so buyer refused, damaged it in the process, agreed a partial compensation, delayed the return to me, damaged it further, finally returned, now they are dragging their feet on the even the agreed partial compensation. Our fridge has gone through incredible torment in Quikr's hands for 14 days, and we have gone through no less on an emotional/financial basis. Quikr's business is reliant on deceit and lies, shoddy delivery practices, and complete miscommunication between their own teams. Dishonesty in Indian e\-commerce is not too uncommon, but this is a level that takes the fucking cake.

Update - Quikr offered to pay the full price of the product as agreed by the buyer and asked me for the fridge in return. That's just insulting. After all this hassle and torture, we get only the price of the fridge back? We're going to court, folks. Legal notice going out tonight. Wish us luck!
