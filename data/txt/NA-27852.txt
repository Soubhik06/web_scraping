I called a number I found on the Amazon website to request a refund for a package that was marked 'delivered' but never arrived. Someone with an Indian accent answered (nothing against this, although it could be possible that this customer support person was not located in my country), and he told me that my account was on hold due to suspicious activity. I didn't believe him, because I had recently logged into my account to check the status of my package. He told me that there were 3 attempts to log into my account simultaneously in 3 different locations, and there was an order placed from my account costing some $400. He said this was why my package was delayed, and that it would arrive soon. He then asked me to download some app called Zoho Assist Customer so an agent could help me enable 2SV, which spooked me so I hung up and blocked the number. I didn't reveal any information, except the last digits of the order, for which I have successfully initiated a refund, so I'm not too concerned. Was this an attempt at a scam?
