 Learned Senior Advocate Dr. Vikas Balia, who would be

 leading the arguments in this bunch of writ petitions, is reportedly

 unwell. Shri Sunil Beniwal, learned AAG, puts in appearance on

 behalf of the Revenue Department in all these writ petitions.

 Hence, his name shall henceforth be displayed in the cause list. On going through the pleadings, we find that the

 dispute, which is involved in these writ petitions, revolves around

 the lease amount chargeable to the land allotted to the petitioners

 and hence, it is not 'stricto senso' a land dispute. Thus, the

 Registry is directed to check and list the matters in the

 appropriate category after two weeks.

 (KULDEEP MATHUR),J (SANDEEP MEHTA),J
