This post is based on my personal experience recently.

&amp;#x200B;

**What happened?**

\- Got a call from a courier delivery person (he was well spoken, like call center type)

\- He called to inform me that i have a delivery, but the order is mentioned as COD

\- He asked me to pay the amount for delivery to happen

\- I asked for more details to cross-check - He had my right **current** address and on further questioning he claimed the order is from netmeds.

&amp;#x200B;

**Why it got me thinking?**

**-** The obvious question : Did I order anything from netmeds? : NO

\- I had recently moved to my hometown for an extended WFH.. and that scammer had my hometown address.

\- How did he got my latest address.. when i haven't shared with anyone .. except r/amazon and r/flipkart

&amp;#x200B;

**Most probable Conclusion**

\- It seems some one went through my discarded mail packaging... noted down my name, address and mobile no.

\- Called me in an attempt to scam.. pretended the package to be from Netmed..coz frankly in my hometown ...where lots of retired people stay.. Netmed is pretty good guess.
