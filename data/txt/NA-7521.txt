Heard counsel for the parties. These three bail applications can be disposed of together
as the applicants pray for grant of bail in connection with same
Crime No.266/2012. The applications have been arrested on
4.4.2015, 25.3.2015 and 21.04.2015 respectively and are in Jail
in connection with Crime No.266/2012 registered with Police
Station Gopalganj, District Sagar - commonly known as
VYAPAM Examination Scam cases - for the offence
punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 474 , 120-B , 417 of IPC and under Section 3(Gha) 1 , 2 / 4 of the M.P.
Manyata Prapt Pariksha Adhiniyam, 1937 respectively. The role ascribed to the applicant in the first application
(M.Cr.C. no.8770/2015) is that of an Impersonator and the
applicant in the second application (M.Cr.C.no.8846/2015) of
Racketeer and of the applicant in third application
(M.Cr.C.no.9020/2015) as middleman having arranged the
impersonator. It is not in dispute that charge-sheet has been filed in
connection with Crime No.266/2012. It is fairly accepted by 2 MCrCs No.8770/15, 8846/15 & 9020/15

the counsel for the State that custodial interrogation of the
applicants is no more necessary as investigation is complete in
all respects. Further, since the applicants are not involved in
any other crime and have undertaken through counsel to
extend full cooperation to the trial Court for early disposal of
the trial and for the reasons already recorded hitherto, in the
interest of justice, taking overall view of the matter, we deem it
appropriate to grant bail to the applicants on following strict
conditions:- The bail applications stand disposed of accordingly.
