--- POST TEXT ---

This morning, I woke up to a call from a personal number. It started with an IVR (Interactive Voice Response) message saying it was about a package with customs and asked me to press 1 to connect to a person. I pressed 1, and a guy claimed he was from Indira Gandhi Airport (Delhi) Customs and started digging for my personal information. Feeling skeptical and still half-awake, I asked if this was a scam call. The call was immediately cut off.

**This call got me worried about how believable this could be to most people, especially since it started with an IVR.** Even out of curiosity, people might go to the next stage of the call and get duped into sharing personal or financial information or even become targets of some kind of fraud or blackmail. This is particularly concerning for elderly people.

Please take some time to inform people around you, especially parents and grandparents, and advise them not to respond to such calls.

The call came from this number: 9810592024. I wrote an email to ap@trai.gov.in and daca@trai.gov.in sharing these details.
