I'm not sure if it was a scam, but here's what happened today, Sunday: A young man, probably from UP or Bihar, came rushing to our door and started banging on it. I went outside to see who it was, and he claimed he was there to complete the KYC (Know Your Customer) process for our HP gas connection. He warned that if we didn't comply, our gas connection would be suspended. His behaviour was far more authoritative than any gas employee I've ever encountered, and the way he spoke seemed intended to create panic and force a quick decision. 

He asked for the identity details, like the Aadhaar card, of the person in whose name the gas connection is registered. It was around 8 AM, and since it was a Sunday, I wasn't fully awake. As soon as I went inside to inform my parents about the gas KYC, I started to suspect that it might be a scam. The man wasn't wearing a uniform, didn't look professional, and it’s clearly stated on the HP Gas website to be wary of anyone asking for sensitive details like that. Plus, why would he come on a Sunday morning? I'm not even sure if customer grievance services operate on Sundays. He arrived on foot, without any vehicle, which also seemed suspicious. 

I told him that the one whose name it is registered is not here to which he looked visibly frustrated, as I couldn't understand why he was so concerned about the KYC on a Sunday morning. After I said that, he just left. Now, I'm not sure if he was legitimate or not, but I'll verify this with the gas agency on Monday. What do you think about it?
