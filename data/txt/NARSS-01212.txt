Today I received a call from a guy who introduced himself to be part of election . Then he proceeded to say my name and my father’s name and then asked where I voted last time(also said some booth no. / constituency no.). I answered him. Then he told that my name has been removed from that booth and some other errors(call was not clear). I said ok and cut the call. Immediately received another call from a lady who stated the same reasons and then told me an election id no. and asked me to verify if it was mine. I told her that I did not have my card rn and will check for it.I checked and it was the wrong no(initial 3 letters were correct but no. was wrong). I did not think twice and gave her the no. She told that there was some error and also asked me to call a no.(the previous guy). I cut the call and then thought about the possibility of a scam as usually during election time party people come to each home and ensure whether the voters name is on list and whatnot. So I’m asking u guys if this has occurred to anyone or did I get scammed?

Tl;dr: Someone called me on pretext of election; got my electoral id through phone. Is this normal or scam? Should I complain to cybercrime? If so how?
