Okay, so I am a subscriber of Tathagata Satpathy's Odia newspaper. Today he wrote an editorial on godmen. I have translated it for you.

The title is a joke on Sarathi Baba's claim that he always wore dhoti and the controversy is because he wore pants, "What is wrong with wearing such attire? After all, even [Swami Vivekananda](http://odishasuntimes.com/2015/08/05/wear-jeans-to-avoid-attention-says-odisha-godman/) too wore pants, coat and tie when he went outside, didn’t he?"

And that there is no complaint against him, "There has been [no formal complaint](http://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/bhubaneswar/People-protest-near-Sarathi-babas-ashram/articleshow/48372428.cms) against the baba from anyone. The SP, however, has been asked to probe the authenticity of the photographs in circulation in the face of widespread public outcry", said collector (Kendrapada) Debaraj Senapati." 

Here is the editorial in [Odia](http://www.dharitri.com/Sambalpur/100815/p1.htm). And here is the English translation by me: 

**Wear pants, go to jail.**

**-Editorial: Tathagata Satpathy**

To wear pants is illegal! Odisha's Sarathi Baba has not exactly broken any laws, but he is in Choudwar Jail today because he wore pants. Only when the storm calms, we can understand the reality of the situation. Be it Sarathi Baba or Radha Ma, they are being blamed for breaking our slumber. They come from poor or middle-class and turn into gurus, these people promise that they can free us from our sins and profit from it. A serious problem has arisen. This problem has been completely created by us. We create injustice and oppression in large scale, and we find ourselves trapped in anxiety under them. We search for peace to escape that. But, the path of sprituality is time-consuming. The crowds in temples are thining out. The sermon-giving babas and matas seem much closer to reach. We reach for their thighs. If then they want to grab our arse, we call them great. In economics, it is called "demand". On the opposite of it stands "supply".

If we look at human history, charmastic men and women have always used mental insecurities as a vehicle to swindle fools. They have been termed "charlatans" in English. There is no discussion being held if Sarathi or Radhe have done anything illegal or not. If he has not paid income tax, then his assets will be seized. They cannot be punished for bigger crimes under any law, as they have not committed rape or murder. To outside observers of the Kendrapada incident, it seems the local administration has become extremely weak. No complaints were filed with the district police before this or even after this, as far as we are informed. Generally, the corrupted nature of the cops has crippled their perspective. The reason behind the agitation of the locals is not hard to understand. A little bit of politics and a lot of jealousy encouraged them to rampage. It can be seen that party politics and some select media outlets were the reason behind this encouragement. Generally no one dares to speak up about the jealousy of the common man, because that may have resulted in denunciation. If one overcomes the fear, one understands that for many years this baba and the activities in his ashram, were barely tolerated by locals, especially the young people. Today, there are several reasons for them falling prey to this extreme jealousy. When then protests started, the weak administration had crumbled in fear. Facing heat from the protestors, the actions taken by the police and judiciary can be called injustice and nothing else. This is what happened in Kendrapada. When these kind of incidents occur, a handful of goons and anti-social elements, with the failure of the administration, can take everyone under their control. For example, suppose 50 people come and protest at your doorstep, and manhandle all your family members, then without any fault of yours, the administrative will force you to go into custody. Not only babas and matas, a common man can also find himself in this hole.

In a democracy, the public opinion should be topmost. But in India, nowadays the *janata janardan* is most of the times mute. A handful of people show their distruptive nature and the administrative is swayed by it, in such cases social instability will surely occur. In middle east, the Islamic State (ISIS) has created a similar situation and completely taken the society into its control. In locales surrounding the ashrams of Asaram Bapu, his son Narayan Sai, Swami Nityanand, Baba Rampal and many other babas and matas, similar sentiments of jealousy and intolerance can be seen. The reason for it is the economic progess of these frauds happens much faster that the economic progress for the locals. Only only locals, but the gifts of ill-natured people who come rushing from afar also enriches these babas greatly. From this we must understand, we are desperate for babas and matas. Our own injustices and psychological weaknesses surround us and turns our lives into nightmares. Due the desire to escape from this, we fall prey to these tricksters. The fault is not that of these matas and babas. The real fault is our own. We don't want a gradual relief, we want instant gratification. Earlier, making tea used to take some time. Now, we can dip a tea bag in a cup of hot water and we have same desire to get everything in our life instantly too. The person who fulfills this desire, may be man or woman, becomes god for us.
