I said, I have not ordered a macbook pro. And he repeated the question again. I'm not sure if they needed me to answer a certain way so that my answer could be recorded or what. I repeated again. I did not order a macbook pro. It was quiet. He said we will start looking into this.... and seemed like he was going to say more So I said thanks for looking in that and then he just hung up. 

The first call asked if I was (wrong name). I said no. She seemed confused and then hung up on me. 

Just informing everyone that this is a common scam, however, I don't know how it plays out.
