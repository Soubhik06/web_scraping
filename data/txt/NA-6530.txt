complainant, a senior citizen, is an account holder (Account No.

issued by the opposite party No. 1. All along the ATM card was in the
possession of the complainant under safe custody under lock and key
beyond the reach of anyone. Also the information regarding the said ATM
card was neither shared with anyone telephonically, verbally nor through
e-mails nor made any purchases through ATM card in last many years. The
complainant was amazed to learn on getting his pass-book updated on dated
12.05.2015 that there has been unauthorized and fraudulent withdrawal
from his account between 28.04.2015 and 07.05.2015 through the ATMs
till the balance of Rs. 463.35 was left. The pass-book entry reveals that the
withdrawals on six days, i.e. 29.04.2015, 30.04.2015, 01.05.2015,
04.05.2015, 05.05.2015 & 06.05.2015 were to the tune of Rs. 25,000/- on
each day made through as many as three to four transactions and on dated
28.04.2015 and 04.05.2015 Rs. 50,120/- were withdrawn through six
transactions on each day and finally Rs. 9,020/- was withdrawn on dated
07.05.2015. Thus, a total amount of Rs. 2,59,600/- was fraudulently
withdrawn from his account. This fact was immediately brought to the
notice of the opposite party No. 1, who in turn advised the complainant to
get the said ATM card blocked and get FIR registered with the police and
