TITLE: Scammers impersonated airline customer service &amp; stole mom's flight credits
DATE: 2024-07-25
SCORE: 1
URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/Scams/comments/1ebu9ek/scammers_impersonated_airline_customer_service/

--- POST TEXT ---

A year ago, my mom canceled a flight over the phone. She gave the customer service rep her flight confirmation number. He said that they will give her flight credit that can be used anytime in the the next two years. He gave her his name, phone number &amp; extension, and a confirmation number for the transaction.

I'll be flying on that airline soon, so my mom gave me the info to see if I could use her credit. I called the number and my spidey sense started tingling when a guy answered immediately - no automated menu first. He had an Indian accent, which alone isn't sus, but when I asked for the extension that my mom gave me, he said that that person was on vacation but that he could assist me. I asked him if this was customer service for Breeze airlines, and he mumbled something vague and just reiterated that he'd be happy to help me. I ended the call while I was in the middle of a sentence (like the call dropped) and immediately got a call back from a number in Anaheim, CA, which was nothing like the number I called initially. I didn't answer.

I went to Breeze's website and this is what it said under tact Support: 
*"We don't provide support over the phone, but you can message us by clicking the* ***Contact Us Now*** *button at the bottom of this page."*

In their FAQ, they also say: *While we have a phone number that Guests can call, this number goes only to a voicemail that instructs Guests how to reach our Guest Empowerment Team through Facebook Messenger, text, and email. If you called and spoke with someone on the phone, it was not Breeze. Since it was someone impersonating us, it might be a scam. Please take any needed precautions if you've given them sensitive information.*

So I went to **Contact Us** &amp; it gives you a choice of being contacted via text or email. I selected text &amp; entered my phone number. I got a text that presented a menu of issues. I selected one &amp; got a follow-up text with more specific options. I selected "Other" and was asked to send a description of my issue.

I gave them my mom's confirmation number &amp; asked if it was possible for me to use her credit. Their response:

*"So unfortunately the flight credits that were issued when she canceled were used to rebook for another reservation 2 days later. While we can't say for sure, based on the information you provided it sounds like she contacted a scammer or an illegitimate third party travel agency and was taken advantage of."*

My mom doesn't remember where she got the phone number from (it's been a year), but I know that I myself almost got caught in a scam when I googled the support number for the Cash app. Called the number that showed up on what looked like the Cash app website. Again, the Indian accent didn't raise suspicions, but then he wanted me to download an app that would let him take control of my phone... "so they can troubleshoot the problem". Oh, hell no! Later, I found out that there was a spoof Cash app site with a bogus customer service number, and some people actually did install the app and gave them control, then watched in horror as the scammers drained their bank accounts.

Be careful &amp; on high alert when doing anything, online or by phone!
