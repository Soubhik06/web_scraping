 a settled law that cancellation of an instrument is covered under Section 31 of Specific Relief Act, 1963 whereas declaration of a document is covered

 availed even if the document has been executed by a stranger to title of the

 plaintiff. ( Deccan Paper Mills Co. Ltd. v. Regency Mahavir Properties 2020 SCC
