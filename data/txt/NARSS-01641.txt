With advent of internet and social media apps, scams are getting more nuanced these days. Even well educated folks are falling prey and losing tons of money. 

I was almost about to lose one lakh when someone impersonated my friend in WhatsApp as his account got hacked. He said he wanted help urgently and has to send it to a friend. When I was just about to send money through Gpay the amount looked suspicious as it was the max limit for Gpay. Luckily I held off and didn’t send anything and my friend was reaching out through other media telling not to send any money. 

What kind of scams were you able to luckily avoid and how ?
