I recently had a fake HMRC call (UK here)

I'm a avid reader of this reddit and can spot a scam call pretty quickly.

I got the call with it's momentary pause as the robo dialler connected me to the individual with a thick Indian accent introducing himself as tom from HMRC. 

Without asking who I was he launched into explaining he was calling about a outstanding tax bill and that it needed to settled today to prevent me being arrested.

He paused and the line when quiet, he then said sir do you understand

My response, I understand you just tried a fake hmrc call on a police officer. Can I ask where you are based.

Sadly they hung up 

A few points
I know answering the call may make things worse as they now know the number works. I was bored 

I am not a police officer, I just wanted to scare them
