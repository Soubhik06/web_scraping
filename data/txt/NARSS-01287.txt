I have an opensource project which is intended to help internet users easily identify online scams impersonating brands, banks, etc.

I ran a private beta with few of my contacts, and did receive some good responses. Now before onboarding brands and companies, I would like to know few suggestions and about the ease of use from here.

1. You could visit the website authifyURL ([https://url.authifyweb.com](https://url.authifyweb.com/)). Direct link will be in the comment.
2. Choose your bank of suggestion and submit enter.
3. Paste an URL from bank.(Play with the link, like modify one or more character, submit wrong link, typo links etc.) and it will show result. Don't use short links.
4. Results &lt; 30 seconds. Nearly real time.

Think from any of the following perspective

* someone who is not so tech savvy, old parents etc.
* Someone who self doubts often about the authenticity of a link, when you receive it through email or message.
* Someone who makes hasty decisions when they see an unbelievable offer.
* Someone who does transaction and online payment on behalf of parents.

Looking for opinion on the

1. Ease of use. What can I improve.
2. UI on the whole. What can be improved.
3. If your could make your parents test it directly and their suggestion(Like font-size color etc.)
4. Speed and accuracy of the result. What can I improve

Apart from owned websites, currently it supports 30+ 3rd party services too, including social-media sites like IG and Twitter, also play-store, apple store, browser extension etc. and will keep on adding them.

Also the code is opensource. Currently all the data is sourced from public. So they are prone to few errors.
