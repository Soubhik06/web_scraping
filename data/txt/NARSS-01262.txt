Introduces themself as FedeX consumer relation employee and tells that a package containing some illegal drugs with a few other things have been shipped by someone using my name,aadhar card and phone number to someone in Iran.

They tells you your aadhar card number and name to confirm with us.

They then proceed to tell us that Mumbai customs have seized the package and that i need to get some certificate declaring that I'm not responsible for sending that package.

They don't ask for money in the first call (brilliant play by them)

I think they'll call back from some other phone numbers as customs officers/Mumbai police and ask for money for clearing our name. ( I have no idea as I have not attended to those calls after the 1st one).

I think the scam is fairly common and found an article given by a cyber police officer based in Bangalore.

Have you had any similar experience?
Do we need to inform cyber police?
 And how easy is it for scammers to get your name phone number and aadhar card number, TF!!!!
