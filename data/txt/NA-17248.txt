Today, on Diwali, I experienced an unfortunate scam while trying to book a car (MG Hector Plus). When I first saw that the number plate was blurred, I felt a bit suspicious, but I decided to proceed regardless. Shortly after booking, I received multiple calls from the host, each time requesting an OTP—first for the driver to drop off the car, then another OTP to unlock the car.

After 20-30 minutes, the host called again, this time asking for a 10k security deposit. When I questioned why this wasn't mentioned earlier, he explained he was a third-party host and claimed the car owner was asking for the security. I was open to the idea of a security deposit, but insisted on seeing the car in person first. Strangely, he became pushy, dropping the amount to 5k, then to 3.5k. I also asked for the car owner's contact information, but he refused, citing that the owner was busy with puja.

The moment I shifted my tone and began pressing for clarity, he abruptly ended the call. Now, I’ve lost both my Zoom account access and the money.

I admit my urgency in securing the car led me to act too quickly, and this carelessness ultimately cost me and now don't want anyone else to get trapped in it. So, should I file an official complaint, or perhaps draft an email with all the relevant details, including phone numbers and screenshots? Any advice on how to proceed would be greatly appreciated.
