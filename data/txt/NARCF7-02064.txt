On 08.06.2023, I and my father received a shocking message stating an unauthorized AEPS cash withdrawal of the amount of Rs. 10,000/- from my SBI account. This incident raises concerns about financial security and the safety of others.

AEPS eliminates the need for OTPs and bank account details, relying instead on the bank name, Aadhaar number, and captured fingerprint during Aadhaar enrollment.

Scammers have been using leaked biometric details to bypass the need for One Time Passwords (OTPs) and drain funds from unsuspecting victims.

Preventive measures to safeguard against AEPS fraud: 
1️⃣ Lock your Aadhaar via UIDAI's website or app, preventing unauthorized use of biometric information. 
2️⃣ Be cautious of unknown sources and avoid scanning suspicious QR codes or clicking on suspicious links. 
3️⃣ Verify the displayed amount and request receipts for transactions before providing your fingerprint on a PoS machine. 
4️⃣ Regularly monitor account activity, check balances, and report any suspicious transactions promptly to the bank and the police. 
Remember, RBI guidelines offer zero liability for promptly reported unauthorized transactions within three working days.
