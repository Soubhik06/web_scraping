As of late I have been getting calls that have been screened as Midland Crdt Mg on both my cell phones. These have been everyday for about two weeks from Phoenix AZ, Southfield MI, and Winchester, VA. I did some looking around and found a mix of both fraud and an actual credit management company also called Midland Credit Management. These guys allegedly buy credit from some credit organizations and then Mafia call/hustle people into paying these off. They say to check your credit to see if any of your debt has been recategorized under Midland. Here is the thing, My credit is fantastic, I never miss a payment, and I check my credit often for fraud. I've got nothing even connected to Midland! So why is this organization calling me if it even is this company and not someone masking as a scam. I've answered twice with both long pauses before answering followed my a man the first call and a woman the second call, who both sounded either Indian or Pakistani, and both very unprofessionally claimed that this call was recorded and if this is a good number to call me back on and who were they speaking with. I immediately hung up and blocked the call. I'm sensing a scam here as I am pretty sure I don't owe anyone anything anywhere else.
