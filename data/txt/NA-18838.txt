Today I was contacted by an old friend who wanted to present a company named biosash selling seabuckthorn juice which is an "immunobooster" according to them. 
Covid-19 has lead to birth of several such fraudulent companies selling fake products using Multi level marketing tactics. They do this under the mask of "school of leadership" programs and career mentorship. 
These companies are very well aware of unemployment and Indian youth's desperation for quick money. We have seen rise in money chain &amp; ponzi schemes right after lockdown. I've also noticed growth in new DMAT accounts. 
Please share your experiences and knowledge on this. This is something that we should talk about and stop our youth from being misguided by fraud companies and scams.
