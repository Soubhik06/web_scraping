--- POST TEXT ---

I am from Mumbai. Today I got a call from someone pretending to be from Delhi Customs Dept.

They called and said that they tried contacting me multiple times and that apparently a parcel of mine is stuck in customs.

I said I did not send any parcel. The details they shared claimed that a parcel with several passports, bank cards, ID cards and clothes was stopped as an illegal package which was connected to my Aadhar card (similar to a SSN)

I said okay I will file a complaint, he redirected my call to a 'police station'. I cooperated for a while because I was scared that someone was doing illegal activity under my name.

When the caller asked me to give a statement on video call on WhatsApp, I got worried and my husband asked me to cut the call. And thank God for that. After that, I called another number for the police station he claimed to be from (the actual number), and they said there's no police officer from that name and please don't share any details with them.

So... I am glad my husband was around and I did not trust someone just because they sounded serious.

They were trying to scam me by saying someone was scamming me!
