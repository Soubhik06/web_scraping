Few minutes ago I received a call from an unknown number which I answered. It first went to an IVR that said my parcel has been returned, then a guy started speaking in English saying he was calling from FEDEX and that there was a parcel you sent from Mumbai to Iran that contained illegal items and it has been confiscated. The parcel he said contained a soft copy of my aadhar card. I quickly realised that it was a scam call , disconnected and blocked. I wonder if I should report this to Fedex India.
