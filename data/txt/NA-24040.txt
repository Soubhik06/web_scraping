My brother recently shifted from Bangalore to Germany. He parceled few things- some clothes, books, utensils and a bike (worth 2 lakhs) back to hometown.

Googled DTDC office nearby and one of the sites dtdcsomething (I don't remember the exact name and it kept changing later on) popped up. Looked like the actual one. There was an option where they'll come to pick up things at your doorstep. 

He applied for that and the same afternoon some people came and packed things. When my brother asked for a tracking ID, they said DTDC will call him and let him know in an hour and went with all his things. So he just checked their GST number online and it was a legit business so thought it's actually DTDC.

Never got a call for tracking ID and when he called either they didn't pick up or kept making excuses. He came back home the next day. Kept calling them several times a day but after some days eventually realised it's a fake business and not DTDC. 

On call, they kept postponing the delivery date. My brother asked his friends in Bangalore to visit the office address on their website- that too was fake. 1 month later, we still didn't get the parcel so my brother filed a police complaint (filed it in Bangalore through mail) 

They asked us to give 50k to get the delivery but after telling them about the complaint, they finally gave us an address in our hometown where we could go take the delivery. Got it one week before bhaiya left for Germany.

We escaped getting scammed by a hair's breadth. But beware of these fake websites
