I recently uncovered a major online education scam in India involving Careerwill, a popular learning platform. They have been streaming pre-recorded classes as "live sessions" while charging students for real-time interaction.

How I Caught Them

On March 13, 2025, during a "live" class by Jaideep Singh, he accidentally mentioned the date as November 8 instead of March 13.

The system date was intentionally hidden during multiple sessions in March.

Students’ comments were completely ignored for over 10 minutes, proving no real interaction was happening.

Careerwill’s Response (Or Lack of It)

I emailed them on March 19, followed up multiple times, but they never replied.

Their customer support denied everything and kept making me send proof to different WhatsApp numbers, only to ignore me again.

A supposed "senior" staff member, Sapna Chettri, finally called me. She was rude, made excuses, and promised an email response (which never came).

After my call, I noticed that the March 13 class was edited to remove the part where the teacher mentioned November 8!

Why This Is a Serious Scam

Students pay extra for live classes, believing they can interact and ask questions.

Careerwill is deceiving students by running pre-recorded videos and calling them live.

They intentionally edit videos when caught to remove evidence.

What You Can Do

If you or someone you know uses Careerwill in India, check your classes and see if they are doing this in other batches.
