So I am looking to purchase some land outside of my town. My friend received a letter in the mail about someone in the area he lives selling a property and wanted to offer it up to neighbors so they could either purchase or have family purchase. The letter looks suspicious but also somewhat legit at the same time. So I inquired about it thinking this could be a potential deal. The guy I spoke with on the phone sounded slightly indian (not racist) but he spoke clear and good English (assuming he was even foreign at all) and he had a phone number from a few states over. The way it would be handled is through owner financing, I put a certain amount down and then have a payment every month until it's paid off. The guy said it's not wire transfer and is rather an electronic payment for the down payment, then the monthly payments would be ACH. I am trying to setup an in person meeting to see if I can actually meet-up face to face with the owner. The guy is addressing all of my concerns. But it seems they want to move fast on the financial end and something has me thinking this could be trickery. I looked on the county GIS and the listed owner of the property apparently lives in California and the name doesn't match the letter signed on what my friend received in the mail. I'm 85% sure this is a scam but I need to be sure because it's setup very well. The company is called "vacantlandbargains.com" and they even have a youtube channel displaying various properties they have dealt with in the past. Videos go back like 3 years as well so I really am not sure here. I have to get a face to face meet-up and more due diligence. What are your guy's thoughts???
