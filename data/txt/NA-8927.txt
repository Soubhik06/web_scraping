1. Rule. Learned APP and Mr. Kazi, waive service of Rule on behalf
 of respondent State. 2. The applicant, by way of this application filed under Section 439 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, seeks regular bail in connection
 with the FIR being C.R. No. 11196030211368 of 2021 registered
 with Sayajigunj Police Station, Dist. Vadodara city, for the
 offences punishable under Sections 406 , 420 , 498(A) and 114 of
 IPC. 3. Case of the prosecution in brief is that, due to matrimonial dispute,
 the wife complainant lodged an FIR for commission of offence of
 cheating, criminal breach of trust and cruelty under different
 sections of IPC as referred above. It is alleged in the FIR that being
 a husband, taking the complainant in confidence, used her credit
 and debit cards and also withdrawn huge amount from the bank Page 1 of 4 Downloaded on : Fri Jun 24 20:40:53 IST 2022 R/CR.MA/11052/2022 ORDER DATED: 22/06/2022

 accounts and also made default in repaying the car loan installment
 which is in the name of the complainant, whereby, he has raised
 the debt liability of the complainant upto Rs.29,56,700/-. 4. In the aforesaid facts, it is alleged that the applicant has committed
 the offence of cheating, criminal breach of trust etc. 5. Learned counsel Mr. Prateek Bhatia, for the applicant, submitted
 that the applicant in fact is actually a victim to the false accusations
 made by the first informant, who without any reasoning and
 justification, has sought to be impleaded the present applicant by
 concocting facts only with a view to harass the applicant. The
 allegations of cheating and criminal breach of trust as well as
 cruelty and harassment are absolutely false and same would not
 attract Sections 406 , 420 and 498A of the IPC. It was submitted
 that there is a huge and inordinate delay in lodging the FIR. It was
 further submitted that due to matrimonial dispute, the present FIR
 has been lodged to pressurize the applicant with a view to get
 financial benefits. 6. In the aforesaid contentions, it was submitted that the applicant is
 in jail since 4.5.2022 and having a responsibility of the family and
 considering the nature of offence and punishment thereof,
 discretion may kindly be exercised and applicant may be enlarged
 on bail. 7. Learned counsel KI Kazi and APP Mrs. Krina Calla for the
 respondent State opposing the application, contended that the
 applicant herein with criminal intent had misused the debit and Page 2 of 4 Downloaded on : Fri Jun 24 20:40:53 IST 2022 R/CR.MA/11052/2022 ORDER DATED: 22/06/2022

 credit card of the complainant and spent huge money for his
 personal gain and also obtained huge amount of loan in the name
 of the complainant and failed to repay the same. They further
 submitted that the investigation is at the primary stage and
 considering the past antecedents of the applicant, he is in habit of
 indulging such kind of offences and therefore, no case is made out
 for granting bail to the applicant herein. 8. Having carefully scrutinized the contents of the FIR and material
 placed on record, it appears that the first informant being a wife of
 the present applicant, made allegations for the alleged offence of
 cheating and criminal breach of trust. At the time of lodging the
 FIR, due to matrimonial dispute, they are living separately. It is to
 be noted that when their matrimonial life was cordial, the husband
 applicant with the all understanding used the credit cards and
 obtained the loan etc. In such circumstances, considering the nature
 of accusation and punishment thereof, without expressing anything
 on the merits of the case, this court deem it fit to grant bail to the
 applicant herein. Hence, present application is allowed. 9. Hence, the applicant is ordered to be released on regular bail in
 connection with the FIR being C.R. No. 11196030211368 of 2021
 registered with Sayajigunj Police Station, Dist. Vadodara city,
 on executing a personal bond of Rs.10,000/- (Rupees Ten
 thousands only), with one surety of the like amount to the
 satisfaction of the learned Trial Court and subject to the conditions
 that she shall: 10. The authorities shall release the applicant if he is not required in
 connection with the any other offence. If breach of any above
 condition is committed, the Sessions Judge concerned shall take
 appropriate action or issue warrant against the applicant. The bail
 bond to be executed before the learned trial Court having
 jurisdiction to try the case. It will be open for the sessions judge
 concerned to delete, modify and/or relax any of the above
 conditions, in accordance with law. Nothing stated hereinabove,
 shall tantamount to the expression of any opinion on the merits of
 this case. Rule is made absolute to the aforesaid extent. Direct
 service permitted. (ILESH J. VORA,J)
