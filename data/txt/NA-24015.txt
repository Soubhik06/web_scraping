Part 1.
So I recently started as a rapido driver and day before yesterday around 2300 I got a trip from a Apollo hospital near Sarjapur to somewhere nearby, I accepted it and went to the location.
I called the guy and he said that his brother is in the hospital and will be coming out any minute. Then he called after a minute and said his brother's acc is not working and that he will send me 5300 to my number and I should keep 300 for myself for the ride( it wasn't that far )and pay 5000 to his brother.
I clearly said him to not transfer any money and that I will not help regarding this. He said that he is cancelling the ride and I said it's fine by me.

Part 2.
I met my friend yesterday who introduced me to this gig work and he said that today someone tried to scam him and after asking what it was..... it's the same story.
The guy even sent him a normal sms saying that some 3400 is credited to your account (from some random phone no.) and he asked him to pay the 3000 back cause his brother's acc started working miraculously and he need his money back.
My friend knew immediately what he was dealing with and cussed this guy badly and the guy just hangs the call.

Part 3.
The main moral is that we knew that scams like this happen, but for the uneducated mass who ride rapido for a living they might just fall right into it and lose a huge part of their earnings.
This guy may book back to back rides with rapido( or for that case any other platform) and trying to scam rapido captains.
There is a good probability that someone has already been scammed and it being outside a hospital ( for emotional reasons) and this is very frustrating for me.

Stay safe guys..
