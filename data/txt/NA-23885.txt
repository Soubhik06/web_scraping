Some background about how I got scammed, followed by how you can help,

We recently moved to Bangalore and engaged services of broker Mahaveer Enterprises, Murugeshpalya to find a flat.
While taking the flat, Mahaveer Enterprises prop. V. Harsha claimed that he manages property on behalf of the owner. 
He got us to transfer one month rent 34,000/- and a deposit of 1,50,000/-. 
He gave us receipts and assured we will get a formal agreement within that day. 
He also made other false promises that the flat will have parking for 4 wheeler even though there is none allocated.
Since moving into flat, V. Harsha has been constantly telling lies about agreement and parking since 2 weeks. 
He will say agreement and parking is ready and then spin some story about why I cannot have it now. 
Also, the real flat owner is now threatening us to pay the rent and deposit again. 
We also find out through Google reviews and talking to people in the area that he has been doing the same to other people and the prop. is a local gunda. 
He keeps deposit and about it month after month. People have not got their deposit back for 10 months after moving out. 

The reason he is able to do this is because there is a steady stream of outsiders like me waiting to be scammed and he does not have to rely on repeat business. People like us rely on google reviews to find trustworthy business. He knows that and has put up a lot of fake 5 star reviews. The language of the 5 star is so glaringly positive and with a consistent pattern of errors that it cannot be anything but their own men. 

**I would like prevent other people from falling in the same trap**, hence I request you guys to rate them 1 star preferably with some description. You can find their true reviews by sorting by low, some points you could use:
