For Respondent(s) : The petitioner has filed the present contempt petition, inter

 alia, alleging that notwithstanding the order of this Court dated

 12.9.2018, the respondents nos.8 and 9 are stalking, harassing

 the petitioner and intruding her privacy. The petitioner present in person asserts that the respondents

 nos.8 and 9 have been continuously tracking her phone and giving

 impression to the persons known to her that orders passed by the

 Court, particularly, the order dated 12.9.2018 cannot come in

 their way. Be that as it may, the submissions made before the Court

 and the assertions made in the petition are all disputed questions

 of fact, which cannot be gone into in the present jurisdiction under

 the Contempt of Courts Act , which has its own contours and

 fetters. Without entering the arena of inquiry into the factual

 disputes and commenting upon the merits of the allegation, this

 Court does not deem it to be fit case for exercise of jurisdiction

 under the Contempt of Courts Act, 1971 . The petitioner shall however be at liberty to file appropriate

 complaint and take recourse to law. With these observations, the contempt petition is dismissed. (DINESH MEHTA),J
