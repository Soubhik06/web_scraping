So I recently came across this post which was a scam by an investment company of around 2000 crores.
It wild thought, why does it take a scam to get to atleast a few hundred or thousand crores before we hear about it?
Is the media to be blamed who doesn't publish relatively smaller stories? Is the government to be blamed, who doesn't act too soon? Are the people to be blamed? Who don't care until a lot of money is involved?
All thoughts welcome!
