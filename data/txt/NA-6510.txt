Heard learned counsel for the parties. Learned A.P.P. opposes the
 prayer for bail. The petitioner is an accused for allegedly committing offence
 punishable under Sections 414 , 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 / 120B of the IPC and Section 66 (b) ( c) (d) of Information Technology Act . It is alleged that petitioner was engaged in cyber crime and duped
 several persons obtaining their OTP number and other details by fraud. From
 the possession of the petitioner two mobiles were recovered. In view of the aforesaid facts, I am inclined to allow this
 application. Accordingly, the petitioner, namely, Mantu Mandal is directed to
 be released on bail on furnishing bail bond of Rs. 10,000/- (Rupees Ten
 Thousand) with two sureties of the like amount each to the satisfaction of
 learned Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, Jamtara in connection with
 Karmatanr P.S. No. 245 of 2017, corresponding to G.R. No. 1057 of 2017,
 subject to the condition that petitioner should appear and mark his
 attendance in Karmatanr police station in the district of Jamtara once in a
 month, failing which his bail bond shall stand cancelled and the officer-in-
 charge Karmatanr, police station will take appropriate steps for his arrest. (ANANDA SEN , J)
