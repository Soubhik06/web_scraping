Apple has updated its App Store Review Guidelines to allow the removal of apps featuring random or anonymous chat services without notice. The company introduced this change as part of its broader effort to address safety concerns, particularly around user-generated content that has fuelled bullying, harassment, and inappropriate material, especially among younger users.

In the newly updated guidelines, Apple has specifically targeted apps that facilitate “random or anonymous chat”, along with those used primarily for pornographic content, bullying, and physical threats. Apps in these categories “do not belong on the App Store and may be removed without notice.”

This shift marks a significant change in Apple’s approach to moderating user content.
