Facts: 2. The appellant filed second appeal on 08.01.2018 with the Commission on
the ground that information should be provided to him. Hearing: 3. The appellant participated in the hearing through VC. The respondent,
Sh. N.S. Deepu, Assistant Director General and CPIO participated in the
hearing through VC. 4. The appellant had sent his written submission dated 02.09.2018 and
03.09.2018, which is taken on record. The respondent had sent their written
submission dated 19.09.2018, which is taken on record. 5. The appellant referred to his RTI application dated 20.10.2017 and stated
that complete information has not been provided to him by the respondent. 6. The appellant stated that one aadhaar card has been issued to his
brother by UIDAI on the basis of fraud and forged documents. The appellant
alleged that his brother is using the said aadhaar card for getting illegal
benefits. The appellant stated that the respondent has wrongly denied the
information under Section 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act. 7. During the hearing, the appellant referred various judgments of Hon'ble
Supreme Court of India i.e. S.P. Gupta Vs. Union of India (AIR 1982 SC 149), Babu Ram Verma Vs. State of Uttar Pradesh (1971) 2 Serv. L.R.659,
Mr. X Vs Hospital Z(Special (Civil) 4641 of 1998) dated 21.09.1998, etc. and
this Commission's orders i.e. Sh. Surender Singh Vs. Block Development
Office, Govt. of NCT of Delhi dated 03.06.2010, Animesh Srivastava Vs. Food
Supplies & Consumer Affairs, Govt. of NCT of Delhi , etc. in support of his
arguments. 8. The appellant stated that compete information should be provided to him
and also to direct the respondent to properly investigate the case, as the
investigation on the veracity of the aadhar card of his brother was not done
properly. 9. The respondent stated that vide their letter dated 30.11.2017, point-wise
reply/information has already been provided to the appellant. The reply was
read out. 10. The respondent stated that the appellant had sought information about
third party, and the same cannot be disclosed under Section 8(1)(j) of the 2 RTI Act and Section 28(5) and Section 29(3)(b) of the Aadhaar (Targeted
Delivery of Financial and other Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act, 2016. 11. The respondent further stated that the appellant in a related case had
filed second appeal before CIC under case no. CIC/MOCIT/A/2016/277025
dated 27.07.2017 titled as R Pullam Raju Vs. CPIO, UIDAI which came up
for hearing on 11.08.2017 requesting certified copies of the documents
submitted by Sh. Rudraraju Seetharama Raju S/o. Late Sh. Ramaraju while
obtaining aadhaar number. The respondent stated that this Commission in
its order had observed that, "no larger public interest was involved in the
matter." The Commission further advised the respondent to investigate the
matter and take action as per their policy. If necessary, they should
suspend/cancel the aadhaar and send an advice to the State Govt. about
this. 12. The respondent stated that an investigation was carried out by their
office regarding the documents submitted by Sh. Rudraraju Seetharama for
aadhaar number ending with 6908 and it was found that the documents
submitted were in accordance with UIDAI guidelines. The respondent stated
that same was informed to the appellant on 17.10.2017. The respondent
stated that action taken on the complaint of the appellant has been
informed to him. Discussion/ observation: 13. The action/steps taken by the respondent in dealing with the RTI
application is satisfactory, as the outcome of the complaint has been
informed to the appellant. The Commission observed that other queries of the
appellant is personal information of third party which is exempted from
disclosure under Section 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act.
Decision: 14. No further intervention of the Commission is required in the matter.
The appeal is disposed of. Copy of the decision be given free of cost to the
