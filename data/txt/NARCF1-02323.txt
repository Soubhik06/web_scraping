Today afternoon I got a call from Fedex. 
From this Number: +919846108781

They told me that a parcel was sent on 2nd July 2024 that contained some illegal items. The guy asked me to write the following details down

Department Fedex Mumbai Andheri East Branch
Parcel sent date 2nd July 2024
From Mumbai to Iran
Parcel consignment ID 228932129177
Parcel containing 7kgs. 5 expired passports, 3 debit cards, 1 laptop, anitbiotic medicines. 
Some capsules have 125mg of MDMA
FIR number: NCBMUM10850224
Parcel payment of 75450 INR
Fedex Employee: Nitish Agarwal
Employee ID: FD555977

Then they told me that they will guide me to lodge a complaint that my identity (Aadhar ID) is being misused. And I need to get a PCC Police Clearance Certificate from the narcotics department to avoid any problems.

Then they redirected me to another call saying that they are from Narcotics Cell Mumbai and they will have to take my statement from the official Skype account of Mumbai police.

Skype call Name and ID 8171 MUMBAI NARCOTICS CELL live:.cid.f74db80e61736e6b

They asked me to turn on my video and say out loud that I don't know anything about this parcel.

Then they told me there are fake bank accounts opened using my aadhar number and fetch and sent me photos of cash, drugs and suspects and asked me to identify it.

They asked me to send screenshots of my active bank accounts and I was stupid enough to send it.

Then they asked me for the login and password of my internet banking and that is where my eyes finally opened and I realized that I am being scammed.

They deleted most of the images they sent on skype. I also deleted the bank account screenshots that I had sent them.

I wasted 45 mins talking to all these guys. I am an IT guy and still got to this point and I feel stupid about it. Thankfully I didn't give them any more details.

But this is really scary and I hope this post raises awareness against such scams.
