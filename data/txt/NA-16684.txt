**tl;dr:** After heavy cultural shock we seek guidance to spend our 2 weeks in India safe and joyfully. We want to remember India as a great place, which it undoubtedly is. Please, share your places, good practices and hints for Agra, New Delhi and Goa or add anything you think would be helpful.

**Edit:** Thank you very much for your valuable insights. We had a great train journey from Jaipur to Agra and we were in Taj Mahal at 6:30 AM, at 9:00 we were done with Agra Fort, too. Now we are on our way to Delhi. We will stay in Paharganj for 3 days. 

**Edit2:** Today we spotted a new way of scamming tourists. There are a lot of tourist information centres in Delhi. Some don't hide that they are private companies and work on margins, which is totally ok, but there are also many that claim that they're governmental agencies and work with no commission.
We stayed alert and didn't let them screw us, but still we do not know if we'll get train tickets at the station.

**Edit3:** Once again, thank you all for your help. We came back safely and in good moods, despite the fact of hospitalisation in Varanasi. I've made a short video summarising our journey - https://vimeo.com/179005184

Hello, 
My name is Piotr and I come from Poland. Me and my girlfriend have chosen India as the journey destination.

We planned our 2 week trip with help of our friends and we wanted to see Mumbai, Jaipur, Agra, New Delhi, Goa and Mumbai.

Our first impression was really harsh. We were prepared to see completely different world, but the cultural shock was huge. We were also aware that it is not high touristic season in India (rain season).

Mumbai will be remembered as an expensive, rainy and very busy city. The hotel we've chosen online was disastrous and very expensive (4200 INR for night). We left Mumbai ASAP and headed to Jaipur, which occurred more friendly to us. We had a really terrifying journey from the airport, because the uber driver didn't know the route and claimed his GPS was not working. It ended up well, but we were scared as hell. We have found a great place to stay on airbnb and met Dutch couple that has been really helpful and insightful. It is our second day in Jaipur and tomorrow we will travel to Agra.

Because of the fact that we are white, locals think that we are rich and all the time want to scam us. We are starting to freak out, because we are suspicious even when people want to be friendly and helpful.

I do not want to sound negatively. We love the food, we met many friendly and helpful locals, the nature, architecture and monuments are great. Thanks to our friends and online resources we do not suffer from diarrhea and other popular diseases (for the time being :) ).

Please, share your places, good practices and hints for Agra, New Delhi and Goa or add anything you think would be helpful.

We are looking forward to hearing from you, Redditors of India and India-lovers. 
Piotr and Patrycja
