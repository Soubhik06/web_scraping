[6.0] Going through the complaint, it prima facie appears that
during the period from 2012 to 2013, accused persons named in
the FIR hatched the conspiracy and they have indulged in illegal
activity. In competitive examination, the accused persons forged
the documents and manipulated the photographs of various
students and candidates and then appeared through dummy
candidates in the examination. Considering the large scale
conspiracy and allegations leveled against the present applicant
and as large number of job seekers and job aspirants are
appearing putting their hard efforts in the examination and due
to such illegal activities and manipulations of forged documents,
such innocent candidates have to suffer a lot. Nonetheless, due
to such activity and illegal means, if any candidate secure the job
in fraudulent manner, it adversely affects the general
administration of government which is against the general
interest of the public and to unearth such large scale
consideration, the custodial interrogation is required. [7.0] Considering the aforesaid all facts as also the law laid down
by Hon'ble Supreme Court in the case of Jayprakash vs. State of
Bihar reported in (2012)4 SCC 379, parameters of grant of
anticipatory bail in serious offences are required to be satisfied
and further while granting such relief, the Court must record the
reasons thereof. Anticipatory bail can be granted only in
exceptional circumstances where the Court is prima-facie of the
view that the applicant is falsely enroped in the crime and would
not misuse his liberty. Herein, no case is made out as the Page 3 of 8 Downloaded on : Sun Sep 17 02:51:52 IST 2023 NEUTRAL CITATION

committed very smartly and very planned method which is not
just an offence against any individual rather the largest social
interest and in such circumstances, the delicate balance is
required to be maintained between two rights one against the
personal liberty and second is societal interest. Arrest is part of
the process of investigation and intended to secure several
purposes. In which the accused may provide information, during
