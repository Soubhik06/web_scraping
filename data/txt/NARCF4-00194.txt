I want to share a terrible experience I had with some so-called "engineers" in Latur so that no one else falls into the same trap.

Last year, we decided to build a house on our empty plot and started looking for engineers. That’s when we came across **Dyneshwar Bharati (Bharati construction) and Pawan Mangalkar** ([GreenSmile](https://www.greensmile.in/) ) **Let this be a warning—DO NOT trust these people if you are planning to construct a house!**

* Ignored **civil construction standards**
* Had **no understanding of architecture**

Because of them, we suffered **financial losses and extreme mental stress.** Please, do yourself a favor—**stay away from these fraudsters!**
