Petitioners are accused for allegedly committing the offence
 punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 468 , 471 , 120B of the Indian Penal
 Code, Sections 66(B) , 66(C) , 66(D) , 84(D) of the Information Technology
 Act, in connection with Mohanpur Police Station Case No.327 of 2017
 corresponding to G.R. No.1914 of 2017, pending in the Court of learned
 Sub Divisional Judicial Magistrate, Deoghar. Learned counsel for the petitioners submits that the petitioners
 have been falsely implicated in this case. He submits that there is no
 evidence that money was transferred in the account of these petitioners.
 He submits that there are no criminal antecedents of the petitioners. Learned A.P.P. appearing for the State opposes the prayer for
 bail of the petitioners, and after going through the Case Diary, submits that
 on a single day, petitioner has made 78 phishing calls to different persons,
 but, admits that some of them have stated that they have not divulged their
 bank details. He submits that there is nothing in the case diary to suggest
 any electronic transfer of money. After considering the aforesaid facts and also the fact that
 there is no criminal antecedents of the petitioners, I am inclined to enlarge
 the petitioners on bail. Accordingly, petitioner in B.A. No.1711 of 2018,
 namely, Manager Mohali and petitioner in B.A. No.409 of 2018, namely,
 Anshu Kumar Mandal @ Anshu Kumar, are directed to be released on bail
 on furnishing bail bonds of Rs.10,000/- (Rupees Ten Thousand) each with -: 2 :- two sureties of the like amount each to the satisfaction of the learned Sub
 Divisional Judicial Magistrate, Deoghar in connection with Mohanpur
 Police Station Case No.327 of 2017 corresponding to G.R. No.1914 of
 2017. (Ananda Sen, J.)
