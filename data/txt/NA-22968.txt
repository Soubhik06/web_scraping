In the legal notice the client has made statements regarding ME being unprofessional and harassing him by not shipping back his HDD. He mentions that I have harmed his reputation by leaving a negative review of my experience with him on Upwork and he’s now suffering monetary losses because of the same. This btw, is a common practice on upwork, encouraged by the website to guide fellow freelancers. Isn’t this my freedom of speech?

I had submitted work on upwork and had sent him messages on upwork and WhatsApp which he ignored.
