Con sumer community platform LocalCircles survey revealed that eight in 10 users have encountered dark patterns like bait and switch and forced action on ride-hailing platforms.

Bait and switch refers to the practice of advertising a particular outcome to the user (bait) which may ultimately differ from the actual outcome available (switch).

The survey comprised 33,000 responses from over 269 districts and asked users about the prevalence of dark patterns on app-based taxi platforms like Ola, Uber, Blusmart, Indrive, and Rapido.

The differential pricing or price manipulation issue resurfaced after a report by The Times of India, but it has been a point of concern for users for some time now. Responding to this, Consumer Affairs Minister Pralhad Joshi termed it an “unfair trade practice” and directed the Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) to conduct inquiries into the same and across other sectors like food delivery and online ticketing.

Besides cab services, LocalCircles has also published reports tracking such dark patterns across online travel , banking , insurance , food delivery , and event ticketing platforms.

On National Consumer Day 2024, the central government launched the Jago Grahak Jago app, the Jagriti app, and the Jagriti dashboard to equip the DoCA with identification tools for dark patterns on e-commerce websites. Besides the guidelines on dark patterns, the government had also organised a hackathon enabling participants to create and test software-based solutions to identify dark patterns deployed on e-commerce platforms previously.
