--- POST TEXT ---

When we (my father, sister and I) were out today my mother got a call from a scammer who sent her a msg with a link and was saying that he transferred her 10k by mistake so he want it back and the proof of transaction is the msg she received. He told in that msg there is a link which has to click and authorise the bank to revert the money which will be followed by a msg where she will get OTP to confirm it from her side. 

As we have told her so many similar stories of scam, my mother being a mother started telling him "ki beta kyu karte ho ye sab, achha lagta hai, kitni bad-duayen lagengengi..." and this that. During this conversation he tried to convince her that he not doing fraud and insisted a lot to click that link but my mother did not budge. That conversation went for more yhan 15 mins and he finally lost by saying ki "aap bahut samajhdar ho aise hi hona chahiye, sorry aunty". 

This was kind of amazing as mother is kind of vulnerable get scared easily. She couldn't study post 8th std as there were no highschool nearby, she can read and write but doesn't know much about tech and banking. It may seem simple to you guys but she really did a great job in handling him.
