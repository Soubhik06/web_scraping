03/07/2025 1. Heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the

material available on record. 2. The present bail application has been filed under Section 483

of Bhartiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (B.N.S.S.), 2023 on behalf

of the petitioner who is in custody in connection with F.I.R.

No.08/2025 dated 02.01.2025, registered at Police Station Savina,

District Udaipur, for the offences under Sections 308(2), 64(2) of

BNS. 3. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the petitioner

has falsely been implicated in this case while alleging that the

petitioner used to blackmail the complainant while threatening her

that he would viral her obscene/nude pictures and would also tell

her husband about it. He further submits that it has been also

alleged that the petitioner while blackmailing the complainant (Downloaded on 03/07/2025 at 09:42:36 PM) [2025:RJ-JD:28626] (2 of 3) [CRLMB-5968/2025]

committed rape upon her on several occasions and has also

coerced her for transferring a total amount of Rs.15-16 lacs till

date. He submits that the complainant has alleged that as she was

coerced to accede to the demands of the petitioner only for the

reason that he had blackmailed her on threat that he would viral

nude photographs and videos of the complainant and would also

tell her husband about everything. He further submits that in the

charge-sheet and inspection memo dated 20.03.2025 of the

mobile phone recovered from the petitioner, there is no such

mention of recovery of any obscene videos/pictures of the

complainant in the mobile phone of the petitioner and thus there

was no reason that the petitioner could have

threatened/blackmailed the complainant, therefore, the petitioner

may be enlarged on bail. 4. Per contra, learned Public Prosecutor opposes the bail

application, however, is not in a position to refute that as per the

charge-sheet and inspection memo dated 20.03.2025 of the

mobile phone recovered from the petitioner, no recovery has been

affected in respect to any obscene videos/pictures of the

complainant. 5. In view of above and, having regard to the entirety of facts

and circumstances of the case as available on record and looking

to the fact that the trial will consume time and thus, without

expressing any opinion on the merits/demerits of the case, this

Court is of the opinion that the petitioner deserves to be released

on bail. 6. Accordingly, the bail applications under Section 483 of

Bhartiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (B.N.S.S.), 2023 are allowed (Downloaded on 03/07/2025 at 09:42:36 PM) [2025:RJ-JD:28626] (3 of 3) [CRLMB-5968/2025]

 and it is ordered that the accused-petitioner Devi Singh S/o

 Balveer Singh, arrested in relation to F.I.R. No.08/2025 dated

 02.01.2025, registered at Police Station Savina, District Udaipur,

 shall be enlarged on bail provided he furnishes a personal bond in
