I visited Indusind Bank for some errands, and during my visit, an agent explained a peculiar and complex investment policy to me. They were quite insistent that I should invest a small amount in it.Here's how the policy works: I'm expected to deposit 1 Lakh every year for 3 years, specifically in the 1st, 13th, and 25th month. After making each payment, I'll receive 40K back into my savings account after waiting for a month (please note that this amount may slightly vary depending on your age), effectively reducing my net payment to 60K. The best part is that this 40K is entirely tax-free. So, after 3 years, I will have invested a total of 3L and received 1.20 Lakh back. Additionally, on those 1 lakh investments made each year, I'll earn interest at a rate similar to that of a regular savings account. This interest earned will remain tax-free as well.Furthermore, the 1 Lakh invested is eligible for exemptions under both 80C and 80D.At first glance, this policy seems to promise unrealistically high returns. However, I'm not entirely sure if there's something crucial they might have missed in explaining or if I've misunderstood any part of it. If anyone here is familiar with such a policy from Indusind or any other bank, I'd greatly appreciate your insights. Given that this interaction took place in a bank branch, it seems less likely that it could be a scam. They also mentioned that I'll receive a physical proof of investment, something akin to a bond.
