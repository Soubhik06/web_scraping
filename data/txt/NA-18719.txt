Hi,
Just made this account to post this. Beware of using AIRTEL MONEY. THEIR SYSTEM IS FLAWED.

Story:

I used Airtel Money for first time to buy tickets on bookmyshow and avail cashback offer. I credited my wallet with 800rs and paid through it. I was using "My Airtel" app. At the payment gateway Airtel asked me whether i would like to register a account or not. I didn't have much time so i didn't register. The payment went through successfully. This was on 3rd of December.

Now on 6th December my wallet on My Airtel was credited with cashback which was 150rs. I decided to transfer this money back to my bank account as i didn't had any use for Airtel Money (Using Jio). Well as you can't transfer without "Airtel Money" app. I downloaded it and it promoted me to register a account and i did. I thought Airtel would know about my previous left credit in wallet as both are on same mobile number. Little did i know that doing so will reset my account completely. So now i have no credit in wallet as well as no transactions history in account. I checked on both My Airtel and My Airtel Money app. Both shows 0.

Called them. They told me to email at wecare@airtelmoney.com. I did with screenshots of all transactions sms. But got a automatic email system response that my email was REJECTED as they didn't have enough space in inbox.

Now. This is a fucking scam. And I'm sure this is not the first time this happened to someone.

Tldr: Had 171rs credit on My Airtel app but wasn't registered. Installed Airtel Money app today and registered to find my wallet balance reset to 0 with no transaction history whatsoever.

Edit: Sent them email again. Not rejected this time. Also to make it clear i had 150rs as cashback + 20rs of my own. So its not like you only loose cashback money.
