This bail application is filed under section 482 of the Bharatiya

Technology Act, 2000. 3. According to the prosecution, the accused had cheated the

account transactions and after receiving the amount, failed to provide the job

and thereby committed the offences alleged. 4. Heard the learned counsel for the petitioner as well as the

learned Public Prosecutor. 5. The learned counsel for the petitioner contended that the entire
