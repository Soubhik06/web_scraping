I have heard a lot of Craigslist scams and actually someone tried to scam me when I was trying to sell an item over Craigslist but never thought that someone actually would try to scam someone in the photography field. Well this is what actually happened couple weeks ago.
A guy called mark johnson contacted me over text, and wanted pictures for family reunion taken, but refused to meet and wanted me to charge a creditcard and forward some money to an event manager who does not accept credit cards? WTF! Once I figured it out that this is not going anywhere I played with him a little bit.

[Here is the full text story message](http://www.zacharovphotography.com/Mark-Johnson/)

Be careful out there if someone tries to make you send an item to Nigeria, calls from IRS with a heavy Indian accent (IRS never calls) or does not want to meet and want you to charge a credit card (almost certain that the one got provided to me got stolen) and the forward money to someone else, think twice and look up names online. I looked up the number and it was coming from India.
