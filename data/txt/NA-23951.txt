I remember the conversation in bits and pieces.... This may not be the exact convo.

\*Received a call from a 10-digit Indian Number\*

**IVR**: Your phone number will be blocked after 2 hours. Press 9 for more information.

\*Pressed 9\*

\*Was redirected to a person with a matured voice of around 30+ age\*

**Caller**: Hello. I am from the Telecom Department. How can I help you?

**Me**: I got an IVR stating that my number will be blocked after two hours.

**Caller**: Yeah. Is your name \*tells my first name\*?

**Me**: Yeah.

**Caller**: There has been some suspicious activity on a number 9898052922 which is registered on your name. Are you using this number?

**Me**: No. That's not my number.

**Caller**: But this number is registered on your name in Mumbai on 3rd Dec 2023.

**Me**: I have never been to Mumbai. I was in Bangalore at that time.

**Caller**: An FIR has been lodged against this number due to illegal activities conducted using this number. So you're sure that you didn't register this number and were at Bangalore at that time?

**Me**: Yeah. But what's the full name over there?

**Caller**: \*Tells me the full name (Spells out my surname)\*

**Me**:Yeah. That's my name.(\*Okay. They got my name\*) **Me**:I don't remember receiving any OTP on my phone for Aadhaar linking.(\*Damn. Is my phone compromised?\*). 
**Me**:But how do I know if this call is legit?

**Caller**: My name is Rajiv Sinha. My ID is 1121467. I am from TRAI Notification Unit.

**Me**: Okay. What should I be doing now?

**Caller**: We'll record your response over a call that you didn't register on the mobile number and redirect the call to a police officer in Mumbai where the FIR was lodged. You need to file a complaint saying my personal information has been compromised and someone has registered a mobile number on my name and has been using it for illegal activities.

**Me**: Okay. So... Should I send a mail or post or what exactly are you asking me to do?

**Caller**: \*Sighs a little\* Take a pen and paper. I'll give you the details of the FIR. 

\*He gave me the FIR number, his details, the subject, the mobile number, and address of the Airtel store where the number was registered.\*

\*He was very patient. He let me write down all the details properly and repeated them in case it wasn't clear to me and asked me to tell them out loud once I was done to double check \*

\*Also told me what exactly I need to convey to the police officer with a legit concern for me in his voice\*

**Caller**: Once we get a clearance from the officer, your number will not be deactivated. I am redirecting the call now. Please hold on.

\*Call redirected to Mumbai Police Special Branch\*

**Police**: Mumbai Police Special Branch(or something)...(\*A typical laid back non-chalant voice of a police officer as seen in movies and what I have seen in general)

**Me**: Hello sir. I received a call from Mr. Rajiv Sinha, Notification unit of TRAI, stating that my number will be blocked.

**Police**: What's the FIR Number?

**Me**: \*Tells the FIR Number\*

**Police**: \*Types in the FIR Number on keyboard. Talks back after a while\*

**Police**: So you have been using the number 9898052922 for illegal activities, sending sexually explicit content and pornography to teenage girls and harassing them. \*Tells a few sections related to this crime\*

**Me**: Someone has used my personal information to register this number at the address \*Tells the address\*. I need clarification on this activity and prevention of abuse of my personal details.

**Police**: So... You have not been doing all this?

**Me**: No. And I have never been to Mumbai.

**Police**: Where are you from?

**Me**: \*Tells him the place I am from\*

**Police**: Education?

**Me**: \*Tells him my uni and degree\*

**Police**: Age? Where do you work? Where are you located right now? Are you sure you are not the person behind these activities?

**Me**: \*Provides him all the answers\*

**Police**: \*Takes a moment after every answer\* (\*He sounded like he was writing them down\*)

**Police**: What's your skype ID?

**Me**: Uh. I don't have Skype. (\*Uhh. Who uses Skype these days\*)

**Police**: Android/iOS? If Android, download the app from Play Store

**Me**: Okay.

**Police**: Did you give your aadhaar xerox to anyone recently? And did you lose your aadhaar anytime?

**Me**: Yeah. A few places for ID Proof. And no. I always had my aadhaar with me.

**Police**: Did you write anything across, or at the back while submitting?

**Me**: No.

**Police**. Uh. That's the mistake. You should have written the purpose on the xerox or should have put a watermark.(\*A tone of disappointment\*)

\*Sounded like he was writing it down\*

**Me**: So my skype ID is \*Tells the ID\*

**Police**: \*Interrupts\* Search for CCMD. \*Expands the abbreviation\*

**Me**: Yeah. Got it.

**Police**: Send a message to me

**Me**: Yeah. \*Sends a message\*

**Police**: I am hanging up this call and will connect with you over there.

\*Video Calls me\*

\*There's no video on his side\*

**Police**: I'll be recording your statement. I'll be asking you a few questions. Take your time to think and answer them carefully. Message me your address. 

**Me**: Okay.

**Police**: We have a case here who has been \*describes the situation and tells the FIR number\*.

**Police**: Please state your full name, aadhaar number.

**Me**: Does that.

**Police**: You are not involved in any of the illegal activities?

**Me**: No

**Police**: Did you give a copy of your Aadhaar to anyone?

**Me**: Yeah. For ID Proof.

\*Repeats a few more things we talked about before\*

\*Smoothly slips into bank related stuff\*

**Police**: How many bank accounts do you have?

**Me**: \*Tells him the number\*

**Police**: Which banks?

**Me**: \*Tells him the bank names\*

**Police**: Why do you have so many accounts?

**Me**: \*Tells him the reasons\*

**Police**: Do you have ATM cards for these?

**Me**: \*This is sus... Why do you care? Isn't this about my phone number\* Yeah. For a few.

**Police**: And Netbanking?

**Me**: All of them

**Police**: What is the largest transaction amount you had in the last six months?

**Me**: \*Tells him the ball figure. Which is pretty low BTW. I am not earning currently\*

**Police**: What's the closing balance?

**Me**: \*Checks it. Tells him the numbers.\* \*Honestly... Since I am not earning and it's month end, I literally had nothing and zero balance in a few accounts\*

**Police**: \*Writes them down non-chalantly\*. That's pretty low.

**Me**: I am not earning right now.

**Police**: Okay. I have recorded the call and I'll submit this.

**Me**: Okay.

**Police**: Do you know Hindi?

**Me**: Yeah. Aathi hai(I know)

**Police**: Abhey Saale. Pehle thoda kaam karle aur bank me paise jama kar. Phir dhekenge kaun loot raha hai/Phir lootenge(not sure what exactly he mumbled). Chal phone rakh...

Translation - Get a job, earn some money and then we'll see who's looting/then we'll loot(not sure what exactly he mumbled). Hang up.

**Me**: \*Interrupts\* I did tell you that I am working on my business.

**Police**: Ha. Haa. Work on your business. Earn money. Chal rakh...

**Me**: \*Interrupts\* But sir... The case. Someone's using my personal info.

**Police**: Yeah. I have submitted those details. Okay then. Jai Hind. (Still non-chalant)

**Me**: Okay. Yeah. Jai Hind.

End of convo...

 
Okay. Before you let your biases kick in and judge the conversation and me... Let me tell you a few things:

1. I read psychology articles avidly and I can smell BS in most situations.
2. The caller was articulate. He was very patient. No where in the conversation did I find logic gaps or change of tone. One thing which was off was the time when he was redirecting the call to the police. I mean. Since when did two different departments of the government have such great connectivity.
3. I am not much involved/aware of new happenings in legal stuff. So the video call seemed like a new upgrade.
4. The police had the typical voice you find in movies minus the drama. Non-chalant, interrogative, rough.
5. It was really hard to find logic gaps here because... The Police always asks unrelated questions and they sometimes abuse power if we question them back. But the asking me balances thing seemed pretty off though.
6. And... Yep. I have been living under the rock for a few months now, working on my business, avoiding information overload. And hence didn't know about this scam.

Did I compromise anything sensitive? Nope. A google search of my name would provide most of those details. I don't feel great about giving my bank name details though. It was a 70-30 situation, where I felt the call to be 70% legit. He didn't ask me for my account details.

 
The part which still confuses me is the Jai Hind thing at the end of the conversation. I mean he knew that I had no money and might also be thinking that I might be making up all the details regarding my banks(I was ROFLing imagining this and the moment he got to know that I have zero balance in a few accounts🤣), but he still stayed in character.

Damn... These guys are dedicated scammers. They spent 30 mins on this call and got nothing.
