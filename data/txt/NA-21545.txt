A few months ago, a family member of mine unfortunately fell victim to a cybercrime, where a scammer almost managed to swindle them out of around ₹9 lakh. At that time, I promptly filed a cybercrime complaint and an FIR, though the SHO was initially reluctant to register it.

A few days after filing, I checked the cybercrime portal and found that around ₹44,000 had been traced. However, there hasn’t been any progress since then.

Today, I received a message from the cybercrime cell stating:

&gt; Status of your grievance reported on cybercrime.gov.in with Acknowledgement No. XXXX has changed to FIR Registered. Login to cybercrime.gov.in to view the details.

I logged in to check, but the status remains unchanged from a couple of months ago. This has left me confused because I had already registered the FIR back then.
