Hey everyone, I wanted to take a moment to warn you all about a scammer who is actively cheating people under the guise of selling flight tickets and electronic items. This individual hails from Kerala but is currently operating out of Bangalore. Here's what you need to know:

1. False Promises: The scammer offers great deals on flight tickets and electronics that seem too good to be true - because they are. Once you make the payment, you either receive no tickets or nothing at all.

2. Smooth Talker: This person is very convincing and professional in their communication. They might even provide some initial proof to gain your trust, but it's all part of the scam.

3. Fake Credentials: He might present fake IDs or business credentials to appear legitimate.
Doublecheck any documents or IDs they provide.
Several people have already fallen victim to this scam, losing lakhs amounts of money. Please be vigilant and share this information with your friends and family to prevent them from becoming the next victims.

 If you suspect you've been scammed, report it to the local authorities and relevant consumer protection agencies immediately. Stay safe and cautious!
