Hey guys. This is the first bank scam i have received ever. Just now i received a call claiming to be from bank. The scammer told me that my atm was going to be cancelled because i had failed to reupdate my KYC. He then told me that he will guide me how to do that with the help of phone. He directed me to download an app called ANYDESK from appstore. I suspected that this was most likely a scam but i told him to call me in 5 min as net was not working during call. I checked the app and bingo it was a remote control app. Then i checked his number on truecaller and it was registered as Rahul - banks scam. His call came after 5 minutes and i told him that he is a scammer. He kept begging me till the last that how did i know he was a scammer. I seriously wish i could report his number somehwere so that he may not scam someone less informed.
