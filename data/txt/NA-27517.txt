I applied to this company and found out they are very shady. They operate under a lot of different names and I question their ethics. It's a paper-writing mill and homework answer factory that doesn't always deliver quality work (if they deliver at all). 

They operate as: 
[transtutors.com](http://transtutors.com/) 
Tutorhelp.club 
[https://www.goassignmenthelp.com.au](https://www.goassignmenthelp.com.au/) 
[http://www.transwebtutors.com/](http://www.transwebtutors.com/) 

If you search for their phone number, it's linked on some other pay-for-paper sites like: 

[https://waywrite.com](https://waywrite.com/) 
[https://homeworkfor.me](https://homeworkfor.me/) 
[wridemy.com](http://wridemy.com/) 
[https://varessays.co](https://varessays.co/) 
[www.saskbowl.com](http://www.saskbowl.com/) › do-assignments 

If you read the reviews from [www.trustpilot.com](http://www.trustpilot.com/), [www.complaintsboard.com](http://www.complaintsboard.com/), or [www.sitejabber.com](http://www.sitejabber.com/), you will see a lot of unhappy users and some fake, positive reviews. This company is based in India and even though they have a NY phone number on their website, you are going to get people not familiar with the American or Australian school system. 

When I applied, I saw an opening for TutorExperts {Current opening valid till March,2013 :-)}, and was confused by the date of 2013 on their site. Thinking that the site was defunct, I sent an email to [careers@transtutors.com](mailto:careers@transtutors.com) and was surprised when I received a response. Deepinder Kaur replied and wanted a copy of my CV (resume) and a list of topics I could tutor. Then he asked me to write a 2-3 page paper in APA format, showing that I knew how to write about Artwork and I needed to include a descriptive poem. WHAT?! It seemed fishy to me so that's when I found out about all the other sites and poor reviews. Do not apply here. His final message was "We solve assignments for Students". This is not a tutoring service. The "direct assignment experts" just solve the problem and get paid for a small amount compared to what this "company" charges. If you are a student, I would read the reviews on the aforementioned sites before paying them. As a tutor, I would not work for them because I don't know how much they would pay you and it violates the ethics of tutoring.
