 Heard Mr. P.A. Ahmed, learned counsel for the petitioner Shri Dilip Kr. Roy who is
apprehending arrest in connection with Golakganj P.S. Case No. 27/2022 under Sections
420 / 406 / 468 / 471 of the IPC. It is submitted that the petitioner is not involved with the alleged offence. The instant
case in brief is that an FIR was lodged by the Branch Manager of AGV Bank, Golakganj

Branch that on a field verification a scam of cheating and forgery was unearthed through
which loans were procured by Majibar Rahman. The learned Addl. P.P. has raised serious objection stating that there are cogent
materials against the petitioner. The objection of the Investigating Officer was brought to the
notice that the petitioner was deeply involved in procurement of loans in a clandestine
manner amounting to swindling of public money. Large funds were diverted by loans which
were procured through fake identity cards and two persons who have adopted unfair means
have been arrested. This case is under Sections 420 / 406 / 468 / 471 of the IPC. I have considered the submissions at the Bar. I have considered the concern of the
petitioner as well as the concern of the prosecution. I am not inclined to grant anticipatory
bail to the petitioner at this stage. The prayer for anticipatory bail is rejected at this stage. Send back the Case Diary. JUDGE
