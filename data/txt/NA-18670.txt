Hey guys,

So, today I found my parents as the target of a well thought out insurance scam. They get a call from a person who says is from the insurance company and says that they took out an insurance plan around 15 years back. It has accumulated to around 1 lakh and they need to pay around 30 thousand to cash all of it.

My father says that he doesn't recall taking out any policy. The fake agent replies with my father's name, address and other details and says that he might have forgotten about it but the money was being cut from his bank account all along.

The agent came up with some interesting rebuttals and creative stories to try to lure him. Where do I forward this guys phone number to?

And have any of you guys had similar experiences?

Edit:

So, reading around, it seems like a common enough scam.These guys are getting good in their background research.My heart goes out to the people who fall for this as I can see older people not being able to understand these.I'll report this.Please spread awareness.
