AADHAR is something BJP was vehemently against before they got elected, but it's obvious they now love it considering how rapidly they were able to implement it across the country.

Considering all the data breaches that UIDAI and other organizations holding AADHAR data have been affected by, I (and many others) obviously think it'd be a good idea to do away with it.

How do we get rid of it though? Moreover, how do we deal with all the leaked information just lying around for misuse?
