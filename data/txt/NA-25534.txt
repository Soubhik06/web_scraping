I used delhi metro one day prior to the scam day . When I tried to enter metro today , the machine said the there was a problem with my card . I went to the help desk . 
He said that the last trip fare was not cut .
I said okay cut it from the card . He said that he can’t, only cash is accepted (I got suspicious). After giving him cash , I put my card in the machine that tells about the previous trips and fare .

There it was clear as day . The fare for previous trip was cut . That too way less than he asked .
I showed him .
He started talking that it’s not possible this that .then he started doing something about refund on his computer .
Still he tried one last time . He told me that we are not allowed to give refund this that , just 1 min earlier he had done the complete procedure of refund .

But in the last he had to give me my money back .
