dishonest concealment of facts is a deception within the meaning of
this section. 141. It is germane to observe that the definition of offence of
cheating also embraces certain situations in which no transfer of
property has been occasioned by the said deception. In the case of Iridium India Telecom Ltd. Vs. Motorola Incorporated (AIR 2011 SCC 20), it was held that misleading statements which have the effect of
withholding vital facts as a result of which a person has been
intentionally induced to do or to omit from doing something will
amount to deception. It was also held in this case that non-disclosure
of relevant information will be treated as mis-representation of facts
which would also amount to deception. In other words, if somebody by
his actions causes the other person to believe something which is false
or misleading and is erroneous, it will amount to deception. Suffice to
say that Mens rea on the part of the person deceiving has to be
established by clear evidence in all cases. 142. In view of testimony of PW-29 examined by CBI, it
