Artificial Intelligence (AI) language-based models like Open AI’s ChatGPT, Google’s Bard, and Microsoft’s Bing can be used by threat actors to carry out various malicious activities and target individuals and organizations, the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) warned in an advisory issued on May 9. The agency also outlined measures users and organizations can take to safeguard themselves.

The concerns highlighted by the Indian government’s cybersecurity agency are not new and have been in discourse ever since the rapid rise of AI. However, this is the agency’s first advisory on generative AI, signaling growing concern among the government.
