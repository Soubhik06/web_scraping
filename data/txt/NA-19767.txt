Hi Reddit, We have been talking about Net Neutrality. Its now time to spread the word in our network. I with the help of /u/Blasticity have put together a message that you can share with your family/friends and spread the word about Net Neutrality.

&amp;gt; You remember those viral messages which said if you don't share this with 10 people, WhatsApp starts charging you? It's actually happening.

&amp;gt; Airtel and a few other companies have started offering separate data packs for services like WhatsApp and Facebook. So what's wrong you might ask?

&amp;gt; Think about it this way, if the government charged you separately for using AC, fridge fan etc. On the top of &amp;gt; your regular electricity bill, you would not be too happy about it, will you?

&amp;gt; What Airtel and the like are doing is called violating NetNeutrality. Right now you pay for some data. What you do with that data is up to you, they want to change that. This way they can curb innovation, take unfair advantage of their user base and promote services in their interest and even worse curb upcoming services that threaten their model.

&amp;gt; Please raise your voice against this and keep the internet free and open.

&amp;gt; So what's next?
