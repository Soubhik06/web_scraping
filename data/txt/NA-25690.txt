TLDR: asked for 500rs on WhatsApp for "visitor card" when I reached the location 

I went to visit a flat today that I liked on Housing.com

Called and confirmed multiple times on call &amp; WhatsApp before going.

Lady said to reach the place and call. I reached, it was a society gate.

She said a guy will come and receive me from there.

Waited 10mins, called again. She said to send "Aadhar and photo" for "visitor card".

I sent, waited again for 15 minutes.

I called again, she said "send 500 rs for visitor card, when the guy will come to pick you up, he will refund it".

I immediately deleted my details from the chat, blocked them, and booked uber to go back to my flat.

Wasted money and time. I hate this city already.
