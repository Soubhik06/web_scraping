Emax electronics based out of UAE is a scam!! They have electronics listed at a very low price on many popular websites like olx.in.
I pinged them on WhatsApp to check out their rates assuming they were genuine. They were selling an iPad Pro 2021 for 66k INR which is a good price
I told them I would make only 25% of the payment upfront since I didn’t know them. The sales person agreed and gave me payment details. I made the payment.
Next day I see a message from him saying the package is stuck with customs and I’ll need to make the rest 75% of the payment to get it out. I disagreed again and told him i can only pay him another 25% to keep the liability on my side low.

This time I repeatedly asked him if he would again call me back and ask for more money. “Never, I will be honest with you” was the answer. I must’ve asked him 10 more times if he was sure I would get the item and that he wouldn’t ask more money. And all 10 times he said this would be the final payment before I saw the item in my hands. I relented and made the 25% payment. By this time I had paid him 50% of the amount. All this while he had shared a tracking website like 

Yesterday, again like clockwork, another person calls me on WhatsApp again and says he’s the delivery guy and that he has my package but the CEO of Emax electronics has decided to keep my package on hold because of not making complete payment.

I wisened up at this point and asked the delivery guy to share his live location on why said. He ended up sending me a Google maps location link to the nearest airport instead of the live location. My suspicion is that this “delivery guy” wasn’t even in India and was running the scam online.

The salesperson calls me up and profusely apologises about not being able to keep up his commitments but wants me to make one more payment with the rest of the amount.

While he agrees that the fault is on his side, his “boss” is the supposedly problem. I don’t see this being anything else other than a scam at this point since any half decent company would at least pay out the rest of the payment amount after holding back a penalty for shipping , but not this one.[here’s his admission of guilt](https://i.imgur.com/4yiPznc.jpg)
