My parents, who recently retired and moved to our hometown, were unfortunately targeted by a scam yesterday. They received a call around 10 am from someone claiming to be from Gail Gas, regarding their recent application for a gas pipeline connection. The scammer had specific details about their application, which made him seem legitimate. The scammer convinced my father to pay a small fee of Rs. 5/- to resume the process. He then tricked my father into installing a malicious app sent via WhatsApp, pretending it was the official Gail Gas app. Once installed, they got my father to enter his card details to make a payment. They also managed to enable call forwarding on my father's phone by asking him to dial some numbers on dialer, allowing them to intercept all OTPs. Luckily, we were able to block the cards and UPI accounts quickly through family contacts. They attempted further transactions of 50k and 29k, but thankfully they were blocked. We have already filed a cybercrime complaint.

I'm posting here to seek legal advice on the following, based on the community experience: 
 
\- What are our legal options for recovering the lost amount (29k INR)? What are the chances of recovery given the complex nature of the scam? 
\- Is there any specific course of action we should follow with the police investigation? Any advice on things to emphasize or follow up on? 
\- Are there any legal remedies against Gail Gas for their system/platforms being exploited this way? Even if they were not directly involved, should we notify or claim anything against them, considering scammer was able to provide correct details about recent application for a gas pipeline. 
\- What kind of documents and proof will we need to ensure the chances of recovery? What else should we collect?

 
Any advice or guidance would be greatly appreciated. Thanks in advance!
