--- POST TEXT ---

I got a call this morning from "Spectrum" promising 1/2 off on my bill. I called the number. The greeting sounded like Spectrum. Got to "Noah Anderson" with an Indian accent. He says we'll offer you 1/2 off on your bill IF you pay $400+ upfront, no bill for six months, and you won't have to pay the $120 odd on this month's bill, but you have to hurry up. I said I have to check my accounts and hung up. Called the real Spectrum at the number on the website and yes, indeed, it was a scam. Additionally, the billed amount Noah quoted was not the exact amount. 

This, a day AFTER I found a false $90 charge on my credit card, to 4Sisters, a rice company in Lousiana. Another blocked credit card, in time for Christmas. The bank rep said IF the charge went through (it did), they would investigate it in the next 90 days (i.e., long enough for me to forget about it -- I won't). 

I am just FURIOUS that it seems every day I have to be on guard against scammers, and I had no real protection against the rice scam. I emailed the rice company for information about the order and to have it aborted. No response. Maybe they'll be more responsive when the damn bank calls.
