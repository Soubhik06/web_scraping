(22.07.2019) 1. The issues under consideration arising out of the second appeal dated
20.08.2017 include non-receipt of the following information raised by the
appellant through his RTI application dated 02.07.2017 and first appeal dated
18.07.2017:- 2. Succinctly facts of the case are that the appellant filed an application dated
02.07.2017 under the Right to Information Act, 2005 (RTI Act ) before the Central
Public Information Officer (CPIO), Reserve Bank of India seeking aforesaid
information. The CPIO replied on 17.07.2017. Dissatisfied with the response of the Page 2 of 4 CPIO, the appellant filed first appeal dated 18.07.2017. The First Appellate
Authority disposed of the first appeal vide order dated 04.08.2017. Aggrieved by
this, the appellant has filed a second appeal dated 20.08.2017 before this
Commission which is under consideration. 3. The appellant has filed the instant appeal dated 20.08.2017 inter alia on the
grounds that the CPIO did not provide any information. The appellant requested
the Commission to direct the CPIO to provide the requested information, penalize
the defaulting CPIO for wrongful denial of information and compensate the
appellant for delay and inconvenience caused. 4. The CPIO vide letter dated 17.07.2017 denied the information stating that
the requisite information was not available with the Reserve Bank of India. The
FAA vide his order dated 04.08.2017 agreed with the views taken by the CPIO. 5. The appellant remained absent and on behalf of the respondent S/Shri Ameet
Tanksali, Assistant Legal Adviser, Sudhanshu Prasad, General Manager and Tapan
Kumar, Legal Officer, Reserve Bank of India attended the hearing through video
conference. 5.1. The respondent submitted that they had given detailed reply vide their letter
dated 17.7.2017 as they were not custodian of the information sought by the
appellant, therefore, they could not provide the information. During the course of
hearing it was also mentioned that the appellant had raised a grievance and
redressal of the grievance lied in a different forum and not before this Commission.
Accordingly they have requested that reasonable reply had been given to the
appellant and the appeal deserves to be dismissed. 6. The Commission after adverting to the facts and circumstances of the case,
hearing both the respondent and perusal of records, feels that the appellant being
absent and not having filed any objection, the submissions made by the respondent
are taken on record. The Commission further feels that there is no public interest
in further prolonging the matter. According the appeal is dismissed. Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties. Sd/- (Suresh Chandra) (सुरेश चं ा)
