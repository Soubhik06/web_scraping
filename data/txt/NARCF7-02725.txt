This is a SCAM alert for those who would be buying New Year concert tickets (Ludhiana venue) from resellers. 
Unlike all previous concerts, this time the QR code would be shared by Zomato digitally only (generated on the app only, tickets would not be shared physically by courier). So, there are pretty much the chances that the reseller would share the same screenshot to multiple people. 
Requested to buy tickets only from the known people to avoid any embarrassment.
