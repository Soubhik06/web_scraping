I wanted to let everyone know a new way of scamming gullible people by convincing them to invest in Crypto exchange named ZIOPTO and causing people to lose large sums of money. One of my friends lost around 18 lakhs INR, and many others across different cities in India have also been scammed. 
**How It Happens:**

 
**WhatsApp Group:** 
Victims join a group titled "Pioneer Stock Investment Group". 
The group includes many other people, making it look legitimate. 
Two main scammers: Dean Akshay Varma and Assistant Aleena Bista.

 
**Initial Trust Building:** 
The scammers provide stock market tips, leading to some initial profits. 
They then introduce the victims to the Ziopto trading platform, a fake crypto exchange.

 
**Crypto Trading:** 
Victims receive a small bonus (e.g., 120 USD) to start trading. 
After making small withdrawals, victims feel the platform is trustworthy. 
Scammers encourage trading crypto twice a day and investing more money, offering rewards for larger deposits.

 
**Advanced Scheme:** 
Victims are urged to invest in new coins with promises of high returns. 
Initially, small profits are shown to build confidence. 
Victims are then convinced to invest large sums. 
Daily calls with tips on unknown coins lead to consistent losses.

 
**Loan and Further Deception:**

 
Victims are offered a loan (e.g., 50000 USDT) to invest in an IPO coin. 
Scammers claim the loan needs only principal repayment. 
Victims are told they won significant amounts (e.g., 700 coins worth 30 crores INR). 
To access the "winnings," victims are asked to pay more (e.g., 1750 USDT). 
When doubts arise, and victims refuse to pay, their accounts are frozen, and they lose their investments. 

**Conclusion:**

 
Victims end up losing large sums of money, often driven by emotional manipulation and false promises. As per the information provided by her, hundreds of people have fallen prey to this scam. 
Stay safe and informed. Share this warning with others and also suggest us how to report this to cyber crime team in Bangalore. She is really in shock right now and scared of reporting this.
