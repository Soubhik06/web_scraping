Haryana , AIR 1977 SC 2185 has observed that the interlocutory order
means orders of purely interim or temporary nature which do not decide or
touch the important right or the liabilities of the parties and any order
which substantially affects the right of the accused, or decides certain rights

observed that orders summoning witnesses, adjourning cases, passing
orders for bail, calling for a report and such other steps in aid of pending
proceedings, may no doubt, amount to interlocutory orders, against which
no revision would lie but orders which affect or adjudicate the rights of the
accused or a particular aspect of the trial, cannot said to be interlocutory
order. By applying the said yardstick, it is clear that the impugned order
