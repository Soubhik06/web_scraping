Heard counsel for the parties. The applicant has been arrested on 20.2.2015 in
connection with Crime No.266/2013 registered with Police
Station Civil Lines, Rewa - commonly known as VYAPAM
Examination Scam cases - for the offences punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 109 , 120-B of IPC and
Section 3 (Gha) 3/4 of M.P. Recognized Examination Act. The role ascribed to the applicant by the prosecution is
that of impersonator. The applicant facilitated the original
candidate - Paras Agrawal by appearing in his place in the
MBBS Examination 2010 by resorting to unfair means. It is, fairly, stated by the learned counsel for the State, on
instructions, that custodial interrogation of the applicant is no
more required. The charge sheet has been filed against the
applicant. Further, the investigation against the applicant is
complete in all respects as of now and, therefore, the presence
of applicant is no more required. In that sense, the link
between the accused persons is already complete. Further, the 2 M.Cr.C. No.8717/2015 applicant is not involved in any other crime of similar type.
Besides, the applicant through counsel undertakes to appear
before the Investigating Officer as and when called for further
investigation including against him as also to punctually
appear before the trial Court to ensure early disposal of the
criminal trial. In the interest of justice, taking overall view of the
matter, we deem it appropriate to grant bail to the applicant on
following strict conditions:- The bail application stands disposed of accordingly.
