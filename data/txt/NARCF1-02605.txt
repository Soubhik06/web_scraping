Today, my brother booked a cab from airport to home. The guy showed up at the airport, asked for OTP but didn't start the ride. My brother was continuously tracking it on the app and was asking him that ride is not showing up on app, he gave an excuse that the there might be some server issue due to which it is not showing up in the app. 

When he arrived at the destination, he started asking some random amount which was ₹ 150 more than the usual amount being charged for the ride to my home. After a lot of argument, We had no other option but to pay him the asked amount. You can't keep arguing with these people, they are always ready to fight on small things.

My brother was later charged cancellation fee for the cab assigned as this guy didn't accepted the ride and it was re assigned to some other cab.

These guys are coming up with one or other scam every now and then.

We will be complaining it to Uber, we have his UPI ID, name and mobile number.

Always make sure that driver has started the ride.
