I wad expecting an order of rs 757 from FLIPKART of jeans. I got a call from DELHIVERY official number which is verified on Truecaller so I picked the call. He told me that my parcel had arrived so i paid for the parcel to the official scanner. When I opened the parcel I was shocked to see a old low quality saree?! I checked my Flipkart to file a complaint but I had realised I fell for the scam because of difference in price and Flipkart parcel was still in transit. Then from who the hell have I received the product? (Someone by the name of FK_SELLER) How did my address and details that I'll be receiving a product of approx rs 750 get leaked? . Poor security and safety concern from both FLIPKART and DELHIVERY. Bangalore peeps out there do check your app before receiving the order and confirm the same.
