I want to share my frustrating experience with Eureka Forbes AquaGuard. Four months ago, my househelp purchased an AquaGuard RO system from them. It completely stopped working after just 4 months, and the nightmare of dealing with their so-called "customer service" began.

Since my househelp wasn’t sure how to raise the issue, I took it upon myself to call and email on their behalf. Here’s what I’ve faced:

No response: I booked a service through their app, but it has been 6 days with no action.

Failed follow-ups: I’ve called their customer support multiple times (at my own expense) and sent over 15 emails. All I got was false assurances and zero resolution.

Irresponsible staff: Whenever I asked to escalate the issue to a higher authority or manager, they placed me on hold for 10–15 minutes, only to disconnect the call.

Paid calls: Their helpline isn’t toll-free, so I had to keep recharging my phone to follow up with them.

It’s frustrating to think about how many honest, straightforward people like my househelp might be getting scammed by this company. It’s a complete fraud, preying on people’s patience and trust.

At this point, I’m seriously considering filing a consumer forum case and starting a Twitter campaign to expose this company.

If you’re thinking about buying an RO system, stay away from Eureka Forbes! Their service is non-existent, and their treatment of customers is beyond unacceptable.
