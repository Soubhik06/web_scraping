In Russia there is a well funded army of trolls who propagate pro-Kremlin narrative in the Russian as well as the Western Internet. This is a very interesting read from [NYtimes](http://www.nytimes.com/2015/06/07/magazine/the-agency.html?_r=3
). 

&gt; “The point is to spoil it, to create the atmosphere of hate, to make it so stinky that normal people won’t want to touch it,” Volkov said, when we met in the office of Navalny’s Anti-Corruption Foundation. “You have to remember the Internet population of Russia is just over 50 percent. The rest are yet to join, and when they join it’s very important what is their first impression.” The Internet still remains the one medium where the opposition can reliably get its message out. But their message is now surrounded by so much garbage from trolls that readers can become resistant before the message even gets to them. During the protests, a favorite tactic of the opposition was making anti-Putin hashtags trend on Twitter. Today, waves of trolls and bots regularly promote pro-Putin hashtags. What once was an exhilarating act of popular defiance now feels empty. “It kind of discredited the idea of political hashtags,” says Ilya Klishin, the web editor for the independent television station TV Rain who, in 2011, created the Facebook page for the antigovernment protests.

http://www.nytimes.com/2015/06/07/magazine/the-agency.html?_r=3

I would recommend everyone to read it how modern infowar campaigns are run. These are not only run to propagate one's political ideology but also make irrelevant topics trending and change public perception regarding something or to hide a truth coming out in the public. 

In fact this July, [Modi had met 150 online users](http://www.newindianexpress.com/nation/Left-out-from-PMs-Social-Media-Meet-Modi-Fans-Cry-Foul/2015/07/03/article2899241.ece), one of them being Tajinder Bagga. These users are widely known as a part of "SanghArmy" who spread Hindutva propaganda on Twitter and other social sites.

Watch out this hastag [#HindusAreHumansToo](https://twitter.com/hashtag/HindusAreHumansToo) on Twitter and how this is trending within few hours today.

The [Caravan Magazine](http://www.caravanmagazine.in/vantage/power-social-media-emboldened-right-wing-trolls) also mentions this

&gt; The Indian Twitter troll, however, is an oddly specific creature. This troll belongs to a motley digital mob comprised of Hindutva converts, misogynists, minorities, Congress baiters and “sickular”—a pejorative portmanteau coined for those percieved as having a secular point of view—haters, all united by their atavistic chest-thumping bhakti—devotion—for Prime Minister Narendra Modi.

&gt; Trolling has become such a problem that many find the daily abuse impossible to stomach and opt to remain offline. For instance, NDTV India’s Ravish Kumar, who anchors Prime Time—one of the most successful news shows across Hindi news networks that is broadcast at 9 pm—has recently ceased using both his Facebook and Twitter accounts.

http://www.caravanmagazine.in/vantage/power-social-media-emboldened-right-wing-trolls#sthash.thdLcIT9.dpuf

And this is not limited to one country or region or ideology. Those who have money and power but not the voice of public with them can certainly buy it with the help of social media campaigns. In fact every day almost half of the hashtags on Twitter and Facebook have been hijacked by such campaigns be it political or sponsored by corporates.

Few months back there was an [OffMyChest post](https://np.reddit.com/r/offmychest/comments/3gk56y/i_get_paid_to_chat_on_reddit/) which stated that she was infact employed by one of the PR companies to work online as a paid troll to propagate stuff. That thread was (mysteriously) removed due to some reasons after it hit the frontpage on Reddit. Here is an archive of that post - https://archive.is/JkqQ6

So in the end do we have an actual space for Free Speech online? Are our opinions being artificially manipulated in a scheme of narratives? Are we really aware of the truth? Are you aware [who owns the media](http://www.newslaundry.com/2014/02/05/who-owns-your-media-4/)?

What would be the scenario in the LS election of 2019 when Facebook and Twitter would be used as walled gardens to promote a certain ideology or hide certain posts?
