 Hon'ble Shri Justice Sachin Singh Rajput

 Order On Board

09/11/2022

 The applicant has been arrested in connection with Crime No.41/2022
registered at Police Station - Duldula, District - Jashpur (C.G.) for alleged
commission of offences under Section 294, 506, 354 (d), 354 (x) , 376 (2) (N),
509 ([k) of IPC and under Section 4 , 6 , 12 of POCSO Act, 2012 and Section
67 (B) of IT Act, 2000. 2. Case of the prosecution is that the applicant started talking to the
prosecutrix and developed friendship. Thereafter, he made obscene videos of
the prosecutrix during video calls and started blackmailing the prosecutrix that
the video would be made viral and in this manner, he sent one Dilip Yadav to
the prosecutrix, who committed sexual intercourse many times with her, video
of which was made viral. 3. Learned counsel for the applicant submits that no offence has been
committed by the applicant and he has been falsely implicated. The allegations
of sexual intercourse are against the other accused - Dileep Yadav. The
applicant is in jail since 15/04/2022 and trial is likely to take some time. Therefore, the applicant may be granted bail. 4. Learned State counsel opposes bail application and submits that the
 allegations against the applicant are serious in nature. The prosecutrix in her
 examination-in-chief has stated that the applicant made obscene videos of the
 prosecutrix and threatened her of making it viral. 5. I have heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the case diary. 6. Looking to the nature of allegations and seriousness of offence, I am
 not inclined to grant bail to the applicant. The application is, accordingly,
 rejected. Sd/- ( Sachin Singh Rajput )
