List of major scam types from the article: 

&gt; * Phishing scams trick individuals into revealing sensitive information like OTPs and bank details, leading to emptied accounts. 
 &gt; * Fake job and scholarship scams target job seekers and students, demanding hefty processing fees for opportunities that don’t exist. 
 &gt; * Digital arrest scams involve fraudsters posing as law enforcement officers, threatening false criminal charges to extort money. 
 &gt; * Romance scams emotionally manipulate victims, fabricating emergencies to extract money. 
 &gt; * Social media and online shopping scams deceive buyers with fake profiles and websites selling non-existent products. 
 &gt; * Loan and financial aid scams promise easy credit but vanish after collecting processing fees. 
 &gt; * Fake customer support scams con victims into granting remote access to their devices or making payments for bogus assistance. 
 
The article also mentions fake stock market profit scams
