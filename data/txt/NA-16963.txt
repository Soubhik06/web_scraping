Seeing so many people writing about different scams happening with them, i felt i should also share my experience. 

We friends were in search of economical stay in dehradun. There is jain dharamshala and agrawal dharamshala in dehradun who give rooms at very low price. We searched for their number on Google but the nobody picked the call. We went into photos section of Google just to have a look and found images of phone numbers. I thought maybe they are not able to update their phone number on Google so they uploaded this image.

We called them and a guy picked up the call. I shared the details that we need 2-3 rooms etc and he said the total will be 2k rs. He shared a QR code on WhatsApp and I paid the amount. After that while waiting for the bill or receipt i felt something was fishy as the wait was neverending. I called him again and he said the recipt will come on gpay app itself. He gave me another QR code and asked me to type some code '1956' in amount lol. And then he asked me to write the UPI pin to get the receipt. This time I was sure it was fraud so I gave him a few curses and he hung up. 

Lesson learnt:- never make payment on any QR code shared on WhatsApp for some booking or buying anything. Always think twice before making any such payment.

Getting a cheap deal blinded us and we got scammed.
