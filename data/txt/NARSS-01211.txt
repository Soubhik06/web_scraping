Hi Guys....I hold a crypto account in an Indian crypto app, and it has been there since 2018. Almost 2-3 months back I bought a considerable amount of crypto via this app and held it there. I know it should have been transferred to some wallets but alas I didn't do it due to high transfer fees. A week back, I noticed that my account is blocked and contacted the support team. Apparently it's been now blocked by Jharkhand Cyber Cell and there is a complaint from someone that led to this blockage with some acknowledgement number in the National Cybercrime Report Portal. I tried contacting the Jharkhand Cybercell to know the real situation and their response was frustrating. After multiple tries, by luck they would pick up the phone and then they would ask to share the details(which I did multiple times), and they say they'll look into it. This repeated for 3-4 times. Earlier I tried reaching NCRP and registered a complaint and that's also under process for last 4-5 days. 

I don't even know the reason why my account got blocked. We can resolve an issue only if you know the reason, right? I'm not even contacted by authority so far on this.Don't we even have the right to know what's happening to our money and investments? Is there a better way to deal with this situation?
