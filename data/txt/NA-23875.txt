I almost fell for an elaborate scam by someone impersonating a Bescom official. It started like this - A WhatsApp message from someone to my brother saying there is a due electricity bill and power will be cut tonight. The message gives a contact number too. He forwarded it to me, we already had paid and had the receipts. I contacted the given number and they called me back after a day. Weird coz its a Sunday. Anyways, the scammer asked me to install team viewer. He connected with my screen and asked me pay 1₹ through a link he sent to update my customer id. He insisted on using a debit card and not UPI. It was fishy and I came up with an excuse and escaped it. A quick search reveals this is a well established scam. Just posting here coz if it was my parents or grandparents they'd have fell for this scam.
