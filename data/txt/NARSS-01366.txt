I wanted to share a rather intriguing experience that recently happened in our family. A few days back, my father-in-law visited a so called medical practitioner in Wayanad to treat his kidney stone issue. The entire procedure cost only 500 Rs, significantly cheaper than conventional hospital treatments so he insisted to do this prior to a recommended operation/medication. The practitioner's method was unconventional – she simply rubbed the area with the kidney stone, and after a few minutes, the stone was reportedly under her palm. It sounded bizarre, and many of us initially considered it a potential scam. 

Despite the scepticism, our father-in-law insisted that this method worked, citing family history where they've done it in the past and verified by hospital scans. To put this to the test, he underwent a scan, revealing that the 2cm thick kidney stone had reduced to just a few millimetres.

The doctor was equally astonished and inquired if he had followed the prescribed medication. We remain genuinely shocked by the outcome. However, suspicions linger, particularly since the practitioner showed us the stone but didn't allow us to keep it, instructing us to dispose of it in her presence. Adding to the intrigue, there was a queue of people with similar issues eagerly waiting to consult her. But the fact that the scan showed the stone was almost gone puzzles me.

 I do not recommend anyone to do this since it isn't backed by science; I am just sharing here out of curiosity. While we're open to the possibility that the massage technique might have a direct impact on the stone, questions remain. Is it genuinely possible for a practitioner to remove a kidney stone just by rubbing the abdomen? I'd love to hear your thoughts and experiences on unconventional medical practices.
