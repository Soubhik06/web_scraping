Do you get inundated by requests from young people speaking English and asking for donations for some Child care program by Unicef at public places in Delhi? I've come across many of them at popular hang out areas, specially at CP. If you say you have already donated or don't want to, they ask for the number of one of your friends. Doesn't look like a scam exactly but more like a ponzi scheme. Why would UNICEF ask young, educated people to ask for donations at public places though?
