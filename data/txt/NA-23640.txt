I don't this is something new but thought will share here. It almost happened with me. 

 Overall the process is quite simple:
1. You look for wine and beer shops one google
2. You spot the one around your area
3. You check for their number. Usually google gives their with the call button icon. But these scamsters drop their number under the review section of the liquor shop. 
4. You call them, then they'll call you back on Whatsapp. Then they'll ask your location and list of items, followed by the QR code for payment. No COD because apparently they have to "bill" it before it's out for delivery. 
5. Interesting part-They ask you to pay via phonepe or paytm, no gpay. Why? Because when I scanned the QR they shared via gpay, the app warned me saying, "potential fraud".
6. The price they quote is unreal..Like it's super cheap. I think they just enter the brand you say and check price online and reply.
7. If someone falls for it, and pays via phonepe or paytm, they'll call you ask you to open the app, go to pay section and type 'bill payment for liquor' in the message bar under the section where you enter the amount.
8. Also, if you cut the call while they are scamming, they get really pissed.
9. I have the audio but I don't know how to upload here.

Stay safe friends.
