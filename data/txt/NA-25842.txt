PSA - Noticed a new type of scam with Ola/Uber rides, particularly with the rides to the airport. Considering that most of the airport drops from the city cost 700+, and hence are big ticket rides, the driver initially accepts the OTP without any fuss. Once the ride starts, the driver might marks the ride as done within 1-2Km from your pickup. Which means that Ola will bill you some very low amount compared to the initial estimate that you had seen, like bill will be some 180 bucks while your estimate would have been some 800 bucks. Then once you reach the airport, since you’ll be busy in getting down, the rider will show a random screenshot of a very inflated fare (1400 bucks). Since you’re busy in getting down and taking your luggage, you’d be surprised with the sudden spike in the final bill amount and won’t realise that it’s a fake screenshot. And you might let it pass thinking that it’s some Ola/uber surge pricing and end up paying 1400 bucks instead of paying 800 bucks. If in case you get vigilant at this point and check your app for a bill breakup, you’ll see that the bill on the app is a very small amount and the driver will argue that it’s probably due to some glitch in the app and that how can airport drop be 200 bucks. So be careful when you’re taking airport drops using Ola/Uber. Check if the ride is marked as done at anytime within the ride and flag it to the driver then itself. 

TLDR- Ola/Uber cabs are showing fake screenshots of inflated bill amounts for airport drops. Always start your ride with OTP verification, Check if the ride gets marked done/cancelled anytime during your ride and finally check the bill amount on your app before paying at the airport.
