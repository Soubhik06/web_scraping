the Respondents and further agitate the matter. The Commission is not inclined to accept the prayer of the complainant to impose
penalty as well as for recommendation of disciplinary proceedings against the
concerned CPIO in the absence of any mala fide on part of the respondent. The
conduct of the Complainant shows his intention to pressurize the CPIO with the
fear of penal action than actual delivery of information to him. This intent
militates against the spirit of the RTI Act which primarily focusses on providing
information to the citizen. Be that as it may, the Commission further observes from perusal of records that
