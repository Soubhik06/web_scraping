I was standing near the door of the metro and another dude was also standing there in front of me on the other side of the door. Just before JLN Stadium station, he asked me to pay him online and he will give me cash in return, for ₹300. I remembered I have read about this and said kyo? he said “mera practical hai aap id dekhlo”, i said nhi hai, he said 200 kardo, i said nhi, then he shouted “SHIITTT” and asked another guy the same, but they were in group and took him off at the JLN stadium station (holding his hands and throat), they might have beaten him.
anyways, thanks r/delhi for saving my 300💀
