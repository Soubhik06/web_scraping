I recently committed a blunder by downloading a shady app on Google play and clicking on the subscribe confirmation dialog.

My credit card is saved in Google play because I have a play music subscription.

One week later, I got charged ₹2600. I didn't pay attention to the sms and email. Today, it has been about 5 days and Google refuses to refund it.

The app was very deceptive. It didn't mention the charge anywhere. It said free trial all throughout. The only place I could have caught it was a small dialog that said 2600/week.

I know that it is my mistake, but this is obviously a bogus scam. The app has no meaningful service to offer for that much money. The info leading up to the confirmation never mentioned the charge either.

My queries to anybody who might know are:

1. How is it that Google is able to charge my card without an OTP? Isn't this a rule that any online transaction needs to be OTP authenticated?

2. What are my options here? Should I try to file a complaint to a consumer forum? Will it help? How to go about it?

Thanks for reading. If nothing else, please let this be a warning to everyone to be careful with shady apps on Google play.
