I've noticed a rise in the number of scammers on these websites, so I thought you guys would find this info useful.

***The Armyman / Merchant navy worker scam***

When you're trying to sell something, these people will tell you they are stationed in some other city or state, and want to buy your product for someone in their family. They will never ask you any details about the product and not even bargain much. 

They will assure you they will take care of shipping through FedEx. Once you give them your email id, they will say they have PayPal'ed you the money. If you open your email, you will actually see a legit looking PayPal email saying you've received the money + shipping cost. 

The catch is, the email will be from Pay.Pal and not [Paypal.com](https://Paypal.com). Then the person will tell you to NEFT / IMPS the shipping charges to some account to release the funds. Happened with me, but I luckily checked the sender of the email and it became clear.

***The courier charges scam***

These people flood OLX specially. They will tell you to transfer courier charges for the item you want to buy (Usually phones) and assure you you can pay COD for the phone itself when it arrives. The amounts involved here are not so big (200-500) but still, beware.

***The Advance scam***

These people will tell you they have a lot of offers for their product, so if you want it for sure, send them an advance. Once you send them an advance (Usually 1000-2000 on average) they will vanish and delete their accounts. Next day, they will be back with a new account.

**Here are my general tips on identifying a scam:**

* If they are not asking you much about the product and not bargaining, beware.
* Check when their account was created. if it was created a day ago with no name, chances are they're scammers
* If they're asking you for an advance, do so only after asking for a product video over video call. If he's a scammer, he will never agree to this
* If they ask you for courier charges in advance and the product as COD, high chances it's a scam. 
* Beware of people posting expensive items for dirt cheap (Like iPhone XS Max listings for 15k specially from locations like Samudrapur, Maharashtra). If they're selling a lot of similar products in the middle of nowhere, it's definitely a tightly controlled fraud operation.

Hope this helps :)
