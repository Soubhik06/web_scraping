This is the fourth bail application under section 439 Cr.P.C seeking bail
 in connection with Crime No.22/2018 registered at police station- Thana STF,
 District Bhopal for the offence punishable under section 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 and 120-B of Indian Penal Code and 66 of the I.T. Act . Applicant is in jail since
 13.12.2018. The earlier three bail applications of the applicant were dismissed
 on merit vide orders dated 29.03.2019, 03.09.2020 and 24.03.2022 passed in
 M.Cr.C. No.11007/2019, M.Cr.C. No.29831/2020 and M.Cr.C.No.9676/2022
 respectively. According to the prosecution case, S.P., STF has directed Dy.S.P.
 posted at police STF, Bhopal to register a case under Section 420 , 120-B of
 IPC against, Pushpendra Singh @ Chhotu (the present applicant), Manish
 Bhalse, Karan Singh. Thereafter, the police investigated the matter and found
 that the present applicant alongwith his companion running a International
 Forged Lottery Racket at Indore whereby they collected huge money from
 different parts of the Country. After due verification, on 19/11/2018 the
 investigation was started secretly and found that Pushpendra Singh @ Chhotu,
 Manish Bhalse, Vaibhav Singh are the culprits. Thereafter, recovered digital
 information from seizing their Mobile Handsets and found that they are in

 connection with Pakistani Agents namely; Maamu, Chhota Maamu, Afsar, Irfan,
 Asgar etc. and in connivance with them, they are running Forged Lottery; like
 Sim Card, Kaun Banega Karodpati etc. by alluring the people of the Country
 committed fraud through E-Wallet, ATM Card, Phone Pay applications and
 thereby misappropriated huge money by supplying information regarding Bank
 Accounts to Pakistani Agents. After due investigation, the police registered the
 offence against the present applicant as well as against other co-accused
 persons and arrested them in the matter. The present repeat application is filed mainly on the ground of delay in
 the trial. Learned counsel for the applicant has submits that applicant has falsely
 been implicated in the case and he is in custody since 13.12.2018 and apart
 from this all other co-accused persons have been granted bail by this Court.
 Trial has not been concluded so far. He further submits that the applicant has
 already undergone 50% of the jail sentence. He is a permanent resident of Satna Signature Not Verified Signed by: VARSHA SINGH Signing time: 3/14/2023 10:53:41 AM 3 and therefore, no question of fleeing from the trial. Charge sheet has been
