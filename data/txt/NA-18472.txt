I got a call this morning from ‘SBI credit card department’ saying that my points are expiring and I haven’t redeemed them. Her voice was too fast and sounded North Indian and so I misheard a few details and had to come out of office. In low coverage, the call got cut. She immediately called back.

*Strike #1*: Why is an SBI (gov) employee so enthusiastic to help me redeem my points? Ideally they would just move on to the next customer.

Then she said, multiple times, that she needs to verify my details which are in *public record* in my own voice if I need the points to be refunded in cash. She said my first number of the card begins from 4 and asked me to give her the remaining 15 digits. I took my time to read it out to her knowing full well I shouldn’t be doing this (because half of my mind still thought this is a genuine call but I wanted to see how far this goes).

They she said, do you know if the card is low, mid or high value? I said, I don’t know (true).

She said, please turn the card, on the back side you will see a black bar with a white box. The three numbers in that white area are related to the value of your card. What are those numbers?

*Strike #main:* I asked her, **that is my CVV number. Why do you need that?**

She kept repeating that this is public record and she needs to know it in the owner’s voice to make sure the verification happens. I said, If CVV is public record, why isn’t it stored in merchant sites? I wasted a few more seconds with her telling her that I am going to report their number and then hung up.

I tried to contact SBI card through their app, but it’s useless as they don’t have a section for fraud call reporting. So I just did a DND complaint at 1909.

I have seen many spam call centre videos on YouTube and always thought that it’s the senior citizens that can be fooled by spammers. As a tech savvy young man, today I respect how evil and manipulative these fraudsters are.
