--- POST TEXT ---

There are few scammers asking for money in the name of Langar near Dmart Carmelaram. They are having some fake identities to gain trust of the people.

I am from Punjab and I know for a fact that Sikhs never ask for money for donation like this.

I confronted them and told them that it is wrong.
Then I clicked their photos.

They got to know about the photos and they came back to me with more men to threaten and corner me.

I didn't back back down despite the threat. 

Since I was at a public place and I refused to back down and I threatened them back to record the video and call the police.
They finally went back.

They were not looking like a proper Sikh and all of them were wearing a black patka (head cloth).

If you come across anyone asking for donation or money for langar or Gurudwara , it's a 100% scam. Sikhs never ask for donations. People give money out of their own will.
