--- POST TEXT ---

The pandemic has placed India at risk of falling into the hybrid regime category, sliding down from a [flawed democracy](https://m.economictimes.com/news/politics-and-nation/india-falls-to-51st-position-in-eius-democracy-index/articleshow/73519661.cms), due to further erosion of civil liberties.

The government has used the crisis to act more authoritarian by silencing [doctors from voicing their concerns](https://www.vice.com/en_in/article/7kzyyq/indian-coronavirus-frontline-doctor-speaks-up-amid-censorship), [journalists from covering the government response](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/india/crackdown-during-lockdown-55-journalists-targeted-for-their-reportage-during-covid-19-outbreak-report-5419091.html) (mirroring China's actions) and enforcing mandatory curfew (nationwide lockdown). It has also consolidated power and funds for the crisis at the center away from states. We aren't free to get tested without government interference [limiting our access to getting tested](https://www.hindustantimes.com/india-news/delhi-limits-access-to-covid-19-testing/story-13HHfRIrUpX24PT7n1ineL.html). Our PM is the only head of state amongst democracies that hasn't faced the press. All this while the [health ministry press briefings have been stopped a month ago](https://www.indiatoday.in/amp/news-analysis/story/health-ministry-skips-media-briefings-as-coronavirus-cases-soar-why-so-silent-1679920-2020-05-20).

We were borderline before with policies like demonetization (where the government denied citizens access to their own money in banks). Actions like silencing of journalists, students, activists and dissenters using sedition and libel laws. Misuse of the amended Unlawful Activities Prevention Act, passage of CAA/NRC and suspension of liberties in J&amp;K are further examples of authoritarian policies. Building detention camps for those put in a citizenship grey zone by the state is clearly authoritarian.

Don't forget mandatory biometrics collection (Aadhar) and surveillance of all communications including the internet ([Central Monitoring System](
https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Monitoring_System)). Speaking of the internet, we had [increasing censorship](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_censorship_in_India) and [costly internet shutdowns](https://www.businesstoday.in/current/economy-politics/internet-shutdown-india-suffers-13-billion-economic-loss-3rd-most-affected-country/story/393584.html). Increasing cases of citizens being arrested for social media posts. The government even wants to [link your social media accounts to your Aadhar number](https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/should-aadhaar-link-to-social-media-accounts-the-questions-before-sc-6082794/).

The judiciary and large sections of the media have been complicit in this slide towards authoritarianism by not doing their job - checking the government's infringement on civil liberties. We've seen judgements ranging from mandatory standing for national anthem to citizens having no right to privacy (to cover for declaring Aadhar constitutionally valid and the Personal Data Protection Bill). Cobrapost sting revealed the extent of the corruption of media.

Civil liberties in India are dying and the pandemic is proving to be the final nail in the coffin.
