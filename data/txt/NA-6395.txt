 Heard both the sides. The petitioner- Pradip Mandal is in custody since 07.04.2023 in
 connection with Cyber P.S. Case No. 21 of 2023 for the offence registered
 under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 120B of the I.P.C., Sections 66(B) , 66(C) , 66(D) and 84(C) of the Information Technology Act pending in the
 Court of Additional Sessions Judge-II, Deoghar. It is submitted by the learned counsel on behalf of petitioner that
 although this petitioner has criminal antecedent, but he has been acquitted in
 one of the case. Learned A.P.P. opposed the prayer for bail. It is submitted that he
 was the king pin of the cyber fraud being committed from Deoghar. The SIM
 cards seized from him were running in third party name. Screen shots of
 payment was also found in the mobile. Further, during investigation the
 victims of cyber frauds committed by these mobile sets were also found.
 Suspicious e-wallets were found to be created in his mobile. Considering the facts and circumstances of the case, the prayer for
 bail of the petitioner, is hereby, rejected. (Gautam Kumar Choudhary, J.)
