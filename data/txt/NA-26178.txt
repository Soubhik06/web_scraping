Hello people of r/Chennai, I wanted you guys to know about a scam going on in the name of blood donation. 

Recently, a family member of mine was admitted to a private hospital and we needed 5 units of blood. As everyone in need of blood does, I sent a message describing the need for blood and had my number mentioned in it and asked friends and family members to forward it to their groups. 

Here’s where the problem started. I got multiple messages and whatsapp calls from people(most of them sounded like North Indians or from Andhra) claiming they've arrived at the hospital or they're on the way to the hospital and I just need to pay their travel expenses. They sent me QR codes asking me to pay for their rides. When I called them, it said the number is busy or switched off and when I told them that I'll pay them once they've donated the blood, they started talking rudely. That's when I realized the scam. One of them even said "If you don't pay, I'll leave the hospital. Find another donor". 

Since I realized that it's a scam, I was able to avoid these people. However, if it is an urgent need, people might fall for this easily. The scammers are taking advantage of our desperation and do not mind stooping to such low levels. So I request y'all to be very careful and warn people to not fall for the same. Hope this helps :)

TLDR: Lot of people call asking money in the name of "travel expenses" and try to scam if you've put blood donation requests.
