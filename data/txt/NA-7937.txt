 The application for bail (S.B. Criminal Miscellaneous Bail

Application No. 1863/2023) has been filed by petitioner- Pukhraj

under Section 438 Cr.P.C. seeking anticipatory bail in connection

with FIR No.59/2022 registered at Police Station Mahila, District

Nagaur, for offences under Sections 384 and 376-D of IPC. Learned counsel for the petitioners vehemently submitted

that on 09.07.2022, the prosecutrix lodged an F.I.R. stating inter

alia that she was subjected to sexual assault and rape by the

present petitioners from the month of June, 2021 under the threat

of making certain bathing videos which were illegally filmed and so

also screen recorded Whatsapp video calls viral. The videos were

transmitted by petitioner- Pukhraj to co-accused petitioner-

Bajrang. Learned counsel submitted that as per the F.I.R., the

petitioners by threatening the prosecutrix to viral the aforesaid

videos, extorted about ₹1,00,000 from her. Learned counsel

submitted that in the FIR, it was also alleged that on 17.06.2022,

petitioner- Pukraj sent a porn video on prosecutrix's mobile which

was seen by her husband and thereupon, the prosecutrix disclosed

all events to her husband. Ultimately, family members of the

prosecutrix, in particular her sister in law (jethani) encouraged as

well as advised the prosecutrix to initiate criminal proceedings

against the present petitioners. Learned counsel for the petitioners vehemently submitted

that the petitioners have been falsely implicated in the present

case. Learned counsel further submitted that from the perusal of

the FIR, it is evident that no satisfactory explanation has been

furnished for lodging FIR after a delay of more than an year, (Downloaded on 01/05/2023 at 11:42:27 PM) [2023/RJJD/012561] (3 of 5) [CRLMB-588/2023]

particularly when the prosecutrix alleged to have been subjected

to sexual assault and rape on various occasions by the petitioners

not only in Rajasthan but also in Hyderabad. Lastly, learned counsel submitted that as a matter of fact,

the prosecutrix is a major married woman who was in consensual

relations which has been given colour of rape only with a view to

extort 15 lakh rupees from the present petitioners. Learned

counsel submitted that the story narrated by the prosecutrix is

highly improbable, especially when she herself sent her videos to

the present petitioners and was in regular contact with them

through mobile calls/Whatsapp calls/Whastapp chats. Learned

counsel for the petitioner-Bajrang submitted that challan has

already been filed. On these grounds, learned counsel implored the court to

accept the applications for bail and enlarge the petitioners. Learned Public Prosecutor and learned counsel for the

complainant vehemently opposed the bail applications. Learned

counsel for the complainant vehemently submitted that the

prosecutrix was subjected to rape and subsequently blackmailed

by the petitioners. Learned counsel submitted that the delay in

lodging of FIR in such cases is not fatal. It was thus prayed that

the petitioners may not be enlarged on bail by this Court. Heard learned counsel for the petitioner, learned Public

Prosecutor and learned counsel for the complainant. From the perusal of the record of investigation conducted in

the matter by the police authorities presented before the Court by

the learned Public Prosecutor, it is evident that the prosecutrix was (Downloaded on 01/05/2023 at 11:42:27 PM) [2023/RJJD/012561] (4 of 5) [CRLMB-588/2023]

in constant touch with the petitioner- Pukhraj, the last

communication/conversation between them took place on

15.06.2022 on three different occasions. The exchange of videos

and also video calls between the parties is evident. The transcript

of telephonic conversation between one Raja Ram (cousin brother

of one of the petitioners) and husband of the prosecutrix on

Whatsapp call regarding deletion of various videos and payment of

₹15 lakhs, for settlement of the dispute between the parties is
