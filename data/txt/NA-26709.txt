We are in need of home loan and my father today got a call from Bajaj Finance and they told him to take loan against an insurance policy. The plan is if we take the loan of 5 lacs then first we'll have to pay them ₹50,000 which they will proceed into the policy and allot us the loan of said amount. Now to return the loan we'll have to pay them 50,000 annually for 10 years and when the policy matures the money will belong to the company no matter how big the amount is. Also if in the meantime something should happen to the candidate, the company will not bother the family for loan and will settle the amount from within the policy.

This scheme is feasible but could there be any chance that this could be a scam? How fraud could this be?

Any advice shall be helpful.
