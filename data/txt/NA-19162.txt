So a little backstory here. I bought this Jio SIM in mid-2018, had no problems, have been regularly recharging it. But on 17th January 2020, I had to go abroad, and I took the SIM along with me in my phone. The active pack naturally expired in due time, and being outside India I had no way of recharging it, besides the fact that I had no roaming pack active, so there was no service as soon as I left India.

So I bought a SIM abroad and temporarily swapped my WhatsApp number to my new number. Days passed by, and I was supposed to return to India in 2 months anyways. But two days ago, I noticed from my friend's phone that my WhatsApp profile with my Jio SIM's number has someone else's photo on it!

I have no idea how the heck he used my number to set up a WhatsApp account - because he wouldn't be able to get the OTP when the SIM is with me. So I decided to give the number a call, and holy shit, it rings! Like how the heck can they give my number to someone else like that? Especially when it was only inactive for barely a month!

So I decided to contact Jio customer service. And sure enough, Jio tells me that the number is active only in my name and that they tried calling the number and it was switched off. I don't believe them, because I called the number again, and it rang - while the SIM was in my hand! I checked my Jio app, and it shows my device was the only one that the number was active in. Then who the heck is that other guy?

I'm not sure what or how the heck this even happened...I have all of my bank and online accounts connected to that number, and he has already taken over my WhatsApp account that was in that number.

I am still abroad, but will get back within a week. Any ideas what I can do or whom I can approach?
