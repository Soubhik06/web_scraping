At CP today, a random guy came up to me with 2 packs of cigarettes in his hands and a tablet.

Guy : "Sir, we are doing a survey, and we are from ITC"

Me : "What sort of a survey ?"

Guy : "Tell us your mobile number and you will receive an OTP, post which we will ask certain questions and give you a cigarette for free (maybe a pack, I didn't hear it clearly)"

After this, I said a straight 'No' to his face. He kept on approaching people nearby and asked them for OTP(s).

Beware guys, this is a probable scam running near Central Delhi. 

TLDR : Scam near Central Delhi. They ask for your mobile number and then ask you to give them the OTP. They may offer a free cigarette or a pack of one.
