This writ petition has been filed for issue of writ of mandamus directing

 the 1st respondent to appoint an interim Administrator in order to handle the

 administration of Green Park International School based on the

 representation made by the petitioners on 29.01.2021. 2.The case of the petitioners is that they are having a share in the

 International School and they entered into a Memorandum of Understanding

 with the 8th respondent which clearly sets out the terms and conditions in

 relation to the shares belonging to the petitioners. 3.The petitioners have given a complaint to the respondents on the

 ground that the 8th respondent who is the Chairman-cum-Managing Trustee

 is misusing the funds of the school and he had defrauded many innocent 3 / 10 https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis/ W.P.No. 9554 of 2021 people in the name of the school and the very existence of the school is now

 in jeopardy. Hence, the petitioners made a representation on 29.01.2021 to

 the respondents seeking for the appointment of an interim Administrator to

 handle the administration of the School. Since the same was not considered,

 the present writ petition has been filed before this Court seeking for

 appropriate direction. 4.The 5th respondent has filed a counter affidavit. The stand taken by

 the 5th respondent is extracted hereunder: . . 9. It is submitted that the respondents herein
 unnecessarily impleaded as first respondent, second respondent,
 fifth respondent, sixth respondent herein as a parties to the
 above writ petition, which has resulted in much inconvenience to
 the administration. It is submitted that in case, the respondents
 1 and 2, 5, 6 herein succeeds in the above writ petition, the
 respondents 1 and 2, 5, 6 herein will not be in a position to give
 any relief sought for by the petitioners herein, since no orders
 issued by the Government of Tamilnadu has been challenged. https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis/ W.P.No. 9554 of 2021 10. For the foregoing reasons, it is humbly submitted that
 the first respondent herein, namely, Director of School
 Education, DPI Campus, Chennai 600 006 second respondent
 herein, namely, The Joint Director of School Education, DPI
 Campus, Chennai - 600 006. fifth respondent herein, namely,
 Chief Educational officer,Dharmapuri-5 sixth respondent herein,
 namely, District Educational officer,Dharmapuri-5 are misjointer
 in this case and they are not authorities to grant the relief as
 sought for, hence the issue now raised to the school matter can
 be settled only by the appropriate forum and the above
 mentioned respondents are not empowered to settle the issue. 5.The 7th and 8th respondents have also filed a counter affidavit. A

 careful reading of the counter affidavit shows that very serious allegations

 have been made against the petitioners as if they have misused the blank

 cheques, blank signed documents and already complaints under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act were filed against the 8th respondent

 which have become a subject matter of challenge before this Court. It is also

 alleged that the 1st petitioner has filed a suit in O.S.No.46 of 2020 on the file

 of the District Munsif Court, Pappireddypatti seeking for a declaration that he

 is the Vice-Chairman of the Green Park International School. According to

 the 7th and 8th respondents, the petitioners are relying upon forged and

 fabricated partnership deed and the petitioners are initiating one proceeding 7 / 10 https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis/ W.P.No. 9554 of 2021 after another only to ensure that the 7th and 8th respondents are put to

 hardship. Therefore, the 7th and 8th respondents have sought for the

 dismissal of the writ petition with cost. 6.Heard the learned counsel for the petitioner and the learned counsel

 appearing on behalf of the respondents. 7.On a careful perusal of the entire materials placed before this Court,

 it is clear that there is an on going dispute between the petitioners and the

 8th respondent. Various allegations and counter allegations have been made

 between the parties. Therefore, the official respondent has taken the right

 stand to the effect they cannot go into this issue and if at all there is any

 grievance, the same should only be resolved before the competent court. That apart there is also pending suit in O.S.No.46 of 2020 on the file of the

 District Munsif Court, Pappireddypatti. A reading of the plaint filed in the said

 suit also makes it clear that almost the same allegations have been made by

 the petitioner. This suit is defended by the 8th respondent. Therefore, it

 would be more appropriate to leave the dispute to be agitated before the

 proper forum and this Court exercising its jurisdiction under Article 226 of

 the Constitution of India cannot get into issues of this nature. That apart,

 even the official respondents cannot go into these issues which is more in 8 / 10 https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis/ W.P.No. 9554 of 2021 the nature of a personal dispute between the petitioners and the 8 th

 respondent. 8.In view of the above, the stand taken by the official respondents to

 the effect that no interim Administrator can be appointed for the school is

 taken on record and it is left open to the parties to agitate their grievance in

 the pending suit and seek for redressal. 9.This writ petition is disposed of accordingly. No Costs. Consequently,

 connected miscellaneous petition is closed. 30.09.2021
