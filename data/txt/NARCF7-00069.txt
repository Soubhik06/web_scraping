So basically these all startups will promise 20-30% discount on online medicine ordering, however this is a blatant fraud. These companies mark up the MRP of most medicine and then offer a discount, so effectively we get only 3-6% discount on the medicine after delivery charges and stuff.
If you don't believe me, buy a medicine from your local chemist and check the price on any of the online medicine sites and you will clearly see the MRP online is a lot more and they apply a discount on that. What I don't understand is that even on the medicine ordered online the printed MRP is also higher, how has the government not cracked down on this!!? Two same products having different MRP's online and offline, and please note this is not because they have two different manufacturing dates. Both have same manufacturing dates too.

Note: This is not the case for all medicines, but I found it true for most medicines.
