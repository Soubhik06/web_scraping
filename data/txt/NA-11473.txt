The parties may terminate the Agreement in accordance with terminiation
Clause 11 of this Agreement, the first five years being lock-in period
subject to fulfillment of the terms of the Agreement. Therefore, the cause
of action in the present matter would arise not from the date of last
adjustment as argued by learned Counsel for Defendant, but from the date
of termination of the Ticketing Agreement. The termination-cum-
recovery notice given by the Plaintiff to the Defendant is of 15.02.2023
and the pre-institution mediation was pending from 18.03.2023 to
19.04.2023 and the present suit has been filed on 22.02.2024. and is
therefore within limitation. 14. The suit of the Plaintiff is based upon the documents placed on
