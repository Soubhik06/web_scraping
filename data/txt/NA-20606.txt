Hey Indians, so recently i was scammed by some seller on flipkart, and the scam goes some thing like this.

Sellers are mostly creating accounts as users, using the address database that they have are creating orders of (Unsold or Unwanted)products using COD as the mode of payment. and start sending to multiple addresses. This scam works if any of our family members pay for the order thinking it was placed by us.

now comes the bad part, you check your flipkart account and find out that this order was not placed by you, and try contacting the flipkart customer care reps, and request for a refund, they have no process flows for refunding the money to me, they only have an option to send the refund money to the scammers bank account, and are not able to send the money by the mode of payment that was done by my family.

have anyone faced this kind of issue?
