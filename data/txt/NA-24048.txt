Hello Reddit,

This is Siraj Mohammed, I'm the Asst. Manager (Adm.) at Garuda Mall, Magrath Rd, Bangalore, also in-charge of Garuda-INOX joint operations in the complex. I'm here between you all because it has come to our attention that there are threads on this website claiming that we 'assaulted' one of our customers during his/her visit to our theaters for not standing up for the Indian national anthem.

We would like to clarify that no such incident ever took place, not on May 7th 2019, not ever in our mall's history. Had there been any such incident, I would have been the first point of contact for the person in question. I also verified this with my colleagues and staff members and nobody had witnessed any such incident to have taken place at our theaters - all our shows have been successful and without any disruptions. We would also like to make it clear that while we honor the hon'ble Supreme Court's order to play the National Anthem at our theaters, we believe that it is an individual's decision to stand up for the anthem or to abstain from it. We would never force any of our customers to stand up for the anthem.

We request you all not to fall for such fake internet stories. This agenda is being run to malign the image of our mall and the cinema premises. If needed, the authorities will not hesitate from taking legal actions against the people who are peddling these defamatory stories based on us.

Best regards.
