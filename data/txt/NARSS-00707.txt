TITLE: Kejriwal Alleges Plot to Arrest Delhi CM Atishi in 'Fake Case'

--- POST TEXT ---

AAP chief Arvind Kejriwal has alleged that Delhi Chief Minister Atishi is being targeted by the BJP and may soon be arrested in a "fake transport case." 

Speaking at a press conference alongside Atishi, Kejriwal claimed the BJP is unnerved by AAP's welfare initiatives like the Mahila Samman Yojana and Sanjeevani Yojana. 

He further alleged that central agencies, including the ED, CBI, and Income Tax Department, have been instructed to arrest Atishi and raid senior AAP leaders.
