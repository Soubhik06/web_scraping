Hey everyone, I just want to put out a warning about this guy Purab Darda, who calls himself “Sigma Binary Trader.” He’s all over social media showing off a flashy lifestyle and making it seem like he's some kind of trading genius. But here’s the truth: he’s just scamming people!

What He Does:

First, he used to scam people with “forex trading” (foreign exchange trading), which is actually illegal in India. When people started catching on to his forex scam, he switched to “options trading.” He now claims he can make huge profits for you – but here’s the catch: he asks for 50% of the profit before you even make any money!

How It Works:

Say you want to invest 5,000 rupees with him. He’ll tell you he can turn that into 20,000 rupees. But, to get started, he wants you to pay him the 5,000 plus 10,000 rupees upfront (50% of the “promised profit”).

So you’re paying him 15,000 total (5k investment + 10k upfront fee). But here’s the trick: if he “loses” your money (which he probably will), you lose your 5,000 and the 10,000 you gave him upfront. He wins, you lose.

Why It’s a Scam:

All his “proof” screenshots are likely fake.

He’s tricked a ton of people out of their money, using the same tactic over and over.

There’s even an article about him scamming someone in real estate.

What to Do If You've Lost Money:

If you’ve ever been scammed by this guy or know someone who has, report him by calling 1930 (India’s cybercrime helpline). Let’s stop him from taking advantage of more people.

Please stay away from this guy! Don’t fall for the fake lifestyle and flashy cars. He didn’t buy those with trading profits – he bought them with other people’s hard-earned money.
