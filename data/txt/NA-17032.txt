After /u/naren198 made [this](https://np.reddit.com/r/paytm/comments/5hjum5/paytm_fraud/) post recently, I am realizing that I'm not the only one. Here is what happened:

1. I applied for a Paytm wallet upgrade.
2. Received a call from their Pune KYC center after about 10-15 days.
3. They sent a person who took photocopies of my PAN and bank statement, and gave me a Paytm KYC receipt.
4. Today, after 10 days, I receive a mail from Paytm customer care, saying this:

&gt; Dear Sir,
&gt; 
&gt; Greetings from Paytm!
&gt; 
&gt; We understand that you want to complete your Paytm KYC process and had already submitted the documents for the same. We regret to inform that the documents submitted by you did not meet the minimum requirements
Would request you to kindly re-apply to complete your Paytm KYC process

I still don't know whether that person passed my documents to Paytm or not (or even if he works for Paytm or not). I'm just afraid that they may be misusing my photocopies in any manner. When I ask them why were the documents rejected, there is no reply. None of the Paytm call centers is taking any calls (tried Bangalore, Noida and Mumbai). They are not responding to any of my tweets too. All in all, this seems very fishy.

I did find out about that Paytm person's details through Truecaller and he is a Pune localite, but he is also not taking any calls. I'm considering what to do next.
