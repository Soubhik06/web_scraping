--- POST TEXT ---

Mumbai just gave me a taste of it. I am a recent graduate from MP and I have to shift to Mumbai for my first job. I looked for PGs and 1RK on nobroker.com and this PG owner contacted me on WhatsApp. He sent me the photos of rooms available in his PGs and mentioned attractive prices. 

Then he asked me to call him. On the call he mentioned me all the amenities of his PG and talked about how it is close to my office and everything. He then asks me to pay him 2 thousands rupees for my visiting card and said that It needs to be made beforehand. He told me not to worry about the deposit as it would be refunded once I visit the PG.

Eventually as needy as I was, I paid 2k to him online and then his manager called me to transfer 8099RS immediately. And then I argued about how could they expect me to transfer such amount to them without even looking at the actual property. Then it suddenly hit me, that it is a fraud. I demanded my money back but no response.

I told my father about this, he talked to him in a strong tone and asked for refund else we would be complaining an FIR against him. To which he said, " ab aap FIR hi kar liyije."

I searched online and found out an exact same fraud case happened with other people. I felt so stupid not to search this before making the payment.
