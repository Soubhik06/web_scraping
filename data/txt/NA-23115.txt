Last month, it was the last date of application of residential plot draw by Yamuna Expressway Industrial Development Authority (YEIDA)'s RPS08/2024.

Different sizes plots were to be drawn (120 metre, 162 metre, 200 metre, 300 metre, 500 metre, 1000 metre, 4000 metre)

Authority received good amount of application that they had to extend the application date as well.

They are giving plots at around 26K INR/sqmetre. They took 10% of the total amount as registration.

The draw date is on 10th October 2024.

But here the story begins, they received around 187700 application (according to calculation I did based on the data they provided).

Assuming, minimum 4L INR per application * 187700 applicants which is 75000 Crore INR, they received for the applications.

They said that they will refund the money after 1-3 months (don't remember the exact months) after the draw if the applicant's name didn't came in the draw.

At 8% interest for three months, this is 150 CR INR, they will receive as interest for free. 

But this is not the issue, since they have received around 2 lakhs applications, and the draw is on 10th October, they the ultimate cheaters are now saying that **they will only consider 1% of the applications for the final draw and they selected some [1877 applicants](https://www.yamunaexpresswayauthority.com/web/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/RPS08_RANDOM_DRAW-1WEBSITE.pdf) out of these 187700 applicants.**

Here are few questions:
1. Why did they took more applications when they only had to make draw using 1% applicants which is less than 2K people?
2. Why did they even extend the deadline if they have no caliber to handle the drawing of more applicants?
3. On what basis did they select these 1877 people?
4. Where is the transparency when they selected 1877 people for draw on their own? Who know what percentage of people belong to authorities' connections only?

Can a normal people do nothing and these cheaters can do anything that satisfies their need?

Why is no one talking about this this including those 2 Lakhs applicants?

Can anything be done legally here?

This is SCAM 2024.
