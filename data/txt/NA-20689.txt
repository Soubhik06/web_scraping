I ordered a pair of black JBL bluetooth headphones from Amazon last week worth ~4.5k. When I opened the package, the box had a photo of the original black headphones, but from the transparent part of the box I could see they were white. I filed a return request (without opening the box at all) citing incorrect product. 

The delivery executive came the next day, opened the box to verify and refused to take it back since it was white in color. I contacted support again explaining the black-white fiasco, they rescheduled another pickup the next day.

The next day another delivery executive came and took the box. After 2 hours I get a call from his manager and him saying the product I have returned is fake and the guy has to bear the losses for it now since he took it back. I called support again, they said they have emailed the delivery station asking them to take it back. The delivery agent called me again saying they still weren't taking it. Called support again, they told me since the delivery agent took it without verifying he has to bear the losses and asked me to block his number. 

I asked customer support if there was anything I could do to help the guy (when he called me he said he makes 8k a month and this parcel was itself 4k). Support made it very clear that it was his fault since he took a parcel.

Seller was Appario retail btw.
