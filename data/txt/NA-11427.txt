This is a petition filed under Section 528 of Bharatiya Nagarik

Suraksha Sanhita, 2023, by the accused Nos.1 to 3 in Crime No.501/2024

of Melparamba Police Station, Kasaragod, which is pending as CC.

No.148/2025 on the file of the Judicial First Class Magistrate Court-III,

Hosdurg. The offences alleged against the petitioners are punishable

under Sections 126(2), 115(5), 118(1), 351, 3(5), 74, 76, 77, 127(2), 333

of Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 and Section 120(o) of Kerala Police Act,

2011. 2. The prosecution case is that on 12.09.2024 at about 9.30 p.m, the

accused persons wrongfully restrained the defacto complainant,

voluntarily caused hurt to her, disrobed her, took her nude photos and

videos and blackmailed her using those photos and videos and thereby

they alleged to have committed the aforesaid offences. 3. According to the petitioners, the dispute has been settled with the

respondents 2 and 3 and they agreed to drop all further proceedings

relating to the above dispute. Therefore, the petitioners prayed for

quashing all further proceedings against them. 2025:KER:85181 Crl.M.C No.2005 of 2025 4 4. The respondents 2 and 3 4 filed affidavit endorsing the averments in

the Criminal MC. According to them, the case has been amicably settled

and that they does not intend to proceed with the case and also that further

proceedings in the case can be quashed. They have no further grievance

against the petitioners. 5. The learned Public Prosecutor, after getting instruction from the

investigating officer also submitted that the dispute has been settled

between the parties and that the respondents 2 and 3 are not at all

interested in continuing the prosecution against the petitioners. 6. Considering the fact that the offences involved in this case

are not heinous and very serious, but purely of private in nature and now

the dispute has been settled between the parties, this is a fit case in which

further proceedings can be quashed by invoking the inherent power of this

court. Such a course is also required to maintain the harmonious

relationship between the parties and also to prevent abuse of process of

the court. 2025:KER:85181 Crl.M.C No.2005 of 2025 5 In the result, this Crl. M.C is allowed. All further proceedings against

the petitioners/accused Nos.1 to 3 in CC. No.148/2025 on the file of the

Judicial First Class Magistrate Court-II, Hosdurg, arising from crime

No.501/2024 of Melparamba Police Station, Kasaragod, stands quashed

Section 528 of B.N.S.S.
