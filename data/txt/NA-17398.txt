What if India could have the most powerful passport but for only those who are not trying to immigrate. This passport will be backed by an asset which you lose if you violate the terms.

This passport will have a golden jacket.

 **Eligibility:**

1. Rupees one crore minimum asset required. The asset can be in the form of stock, cash, gold or fixed deposit. It could either be one crore in nifty fifty stocks and nifty 50 because market is volatile or five crore and above. If you have promoter holding in any other stock or one crore and above in FD or gold or gold bonds or it could be a property like a home which is also one crore and above and is not part of any legal dispute. 
2. One asset can have maximum of four people. One couple + 2 children (passport becomes ineligible as soon as they turn 18 or separate asset required.)
3. Rs One lakh fee per passport per person.
4. Income tax filed every year. Income source will be verified to prevent black money being funneled in this program.
5. Passport seva employees will come to your house to provide enhanced service and also for all verification.

**Features:** 

1. The passport could be world's first truly luxurious with features as listed.
2. Choice of second language for front page of you want. 
3. Biometric and digital passport. The countries that allow digital documents you won't even have to carry your passport. 
4. Priority security lines and immigration lines at international airports in India and foreign airports. Where more than half million Indian people pass through Yearly. Examples could be Dubai, London, New York. 
5. Priority security lines at domestic airports with golden beautiful entry.
6. Visa Free Access to most countries for 90 -180 days for tourist, business, sports or medical purposes. Less limits than regular visa travelers. (Like minimum gap between visits or number of entry). This passport will do away with a lot of restrictions.
7. 24x7 separate helpline with a unique number for each person so that each person can easily identified in the event of an emergency. Any kind of immediate medical, legal or rescue help will be given. Indian embassy usually does not pick up the phone abroad. Very bad service. This helpline service needs to be good.
8. Best legal firms will be on retainer in each country to provide initial legal help in case you get arrested. Users will pay if case drags on. (Most minor offences are solved with 1-2 visits.) 
9. Children will have their own helpline number. Children's number will be easier to remember. 
10. Dual citizenship is not allowed as a security issue. But could be allowed for golden passport holders. It will lead to more investments in India. Foreigners could also be allowed to apply for this passport for Rs 5 Crores locked investment. (Only certain counties). With luxurious treatment we take away golden investment away from Europe/USA 
11. Second passport availability in case you are a frequent traveler. One passport is stuck in documentation or an unfriendly country embassy. You could order temporary second passport.
12. You can order an international driver license valid for that country in their language. That is only valid for the duration of the trip. And you don't have to carry a passport everywhere. This also helps as many countries don't accept driver licenses of other countries and could be used to verify age for adult activities like drinking. 
13. Separate lounges with Indian food in international lounges. Current lounges are crowded. (Only few airports as too costly).

**How this works:**

1. Violating the travel guidelines/visa free days unless force Majure (health, weather etc.) will lead to loss of asset. This will also result in making you ineligible for golden passport again for next 5 years. 
2. Asset will be sold off to compensate the country in question. (Deportation charges, fines etc.) You may receive remaining funds. 
3. In case you seek asylum abroad; asset will be sold off in its entirety with no refunds and make you ineligible for any Indian Passport.
4. Indian government will pay for separate lines at international airports with high daily traffic of Indians like Dubai/London. These lines will be golden and superfast. In case the airport needs to use this line due to whatever reason; they will have to pay back some fee.
5. Indian government will negotiate to make this most powerful and prestigious passport.
