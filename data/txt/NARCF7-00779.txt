Today in newspaper, I read about a new version of Otp on call scam using Call merging

Here is the link - https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/tech-news/call-merging-scam-what-is-the-new-fraud-that-npci-has-warned-about/amp_articleshow/118444907.cms

Key paragraphs from the article for the lazy people --

"Scammers call unsuspecting victims about an event or an opportunity, claiming they got their number from a friend. They then request to merge the call with this "friend," who is actually another fraudster.

Once merged, the victim unknowingly connects to an OTP verification call from their bank. The scammer manipulates the user into revealing the OTP, enabling unauthorized transactions. “The moment OTP is shared, scammers complete the transaction, and your money is gone!”.

Solution- Don't merge unknown calls as our so called strong banking system is ineffective to create a more robust verification system
