In the wake of paradise papers leak, this post is again driven by curiosity about wealth of India's politicians. Following list is based on winning candidates, so there could be much richer or poorer politicians. Also, wealth listed includes both movable and immovable assets.

&amp;nbsp;

Be careful to not read too much from data. A rich politician doesn't mean a corrupt one and a poor politician doesn't imply a good one. However, data can still give some insight about corruption if one compares growth over years and background of individual, something which is again easy to gather, however, I wont be posting it. Obviously, majority of lawmakers have undervalued themselves but this is what is on affidavit. More interesting cases were those who are not even graduate and yet are multi-millionaires. Similarly, lawmakers with more than 200cr or less than 20 lac were interesting to compare.
