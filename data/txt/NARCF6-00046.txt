Today morning I got a call from Mumbai DTDC courier person saying that he want to verify my shipment that doesn't made by me from Mumbai to Taiwan. I told him that I didn't make that. Then he told that the shipment contains laptos, credit cards and some illegal drugs and someone is misusing my Aadhar Adhar card and sending the shipment from my name. 

After that he told me to register a complaint in Cyber crime and tell the details... He provided the FIR number and AWB number.. After these details I got convinced that he is trying to help me... He transferred the call... Another person started talking and was telling me the consequences of the drug smuggling.. Even though I didn't do nothing wrong, I got very scared.. He told me to come to a quiet place to give a statement... I was in my office and I asked my boss for a permission... I went to a conference hall and he asked my my mobile model and told me to download a skybe app... Suddenly my boss came with my other senior colleague and they told that it was completely fake and they are trying to scam you.

The person who called me is not really a courier person and other person is also not from cyber crime. 

 Immediately we went to local police for confirmation and they told not to worry . 

If my boss didn't came, I would have been in a huge mess .

I was very fool to get trapped in their scam. I had no time to think.. these scammers have become very smart and started to think how we can take control of other people by creating fear in them... Today was a great escape 😵.

So please don't fall into these kind of traps☠️.
