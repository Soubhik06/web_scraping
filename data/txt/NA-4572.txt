 constitutes the foundation of a fair and just legal system. The maxim
 audi alteram partem - hear the other side - is one of the two
 fundamental pillars of natural justice, the other being nemo judex in
 causa sua. These principles have been recognized and enforced by

 technicalities which can be dispensed with at the whim of the Court. They

 sanctity from the Constitution of India itself. Any order passed in violation
 of the principles of natural justice is a nullity in the eye of law.
