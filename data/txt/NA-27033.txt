Hello everyone,

For years, I've been toying with the idea of buying a VR headset. However, financial constraints and the state of VR technology always made it a difficult investment for me.

After numerous comparisons, reading up on reviews, and doing my research, it finally happened. The Meta Quest 3, which was my favorite in terms of price-performance ratio, was on sale at an unbeatable price.

No forums reported it, there were no active price comparisons to be found, but the offer had just gone online, and with 3,500 positive reviews on Amazon, it seemed legitimate to me.

So, I decided to take the plunge. A 50% discount was enough for me to buy a VR headset for everyone in the family to avoid any disputes. I ordered four units and was thrilled. Right after making the purchase, which took me four hours of consideration, the offer was taken offline. Assuming it was due to limited quantities, I wasn't too concerned.

However, my concerns grew the next day when I was at the neurologist's office for an infusion. I've been suffering from MS for years, and my current flare-up makes walking and exercising very difficult. One of the main reasons I wanted to buy the VR headset was to get some exercise and have fun within my own four walls, which seemed like a good idea for my health and recovery. But things turned out differently.

Suddenly, a new seller was offering the headset for an even lower price. I did some research and wanted to contact my seller, but couldn't find any detailed information. On the seller's homepage, there was a picture explaining that he had been hacked and was not selling VR headsets on Amazon. The new seller of the unbeatable offer turned out, after some research, to be an Indian gourmet food store, which, let's be honest, probably doesn't sell VR headsets.

This pattern continued every few hours: new sellers offering the headset at unbeatable prices, only to be replaced by another seller a few hours later. I tried contacting Amazon to alert them to this suspicious activity, but they didn't seem interested in investigating, despite the enormous losses they might incur through their A-Z guarantee.

All I can do now is hope to get my money back, but my dream of the VR world has likely been shattered for now. We would have loved to explore the META world, but it was too good to be true. I can only hope that my story reaches a few people who might avoid falling for this scam.

Stay safe and healthy.
