--- POST TEXT ---

Yesterday, on my way back from the office, someone approached me asking for nineteen rupees via Paytm, promising to reimburse me with cash. Due to a misunderstanding, I thought he said ninety and ended up giving him 90 rupees instead. After clarifying the amount, he handed me back 20 rupees as that was all he had in cash, and then requested an additional 10 rupees, assuring me he would repay 80 rupees immediately. However, when I attempted to transfer the money via Paytm, he admitted he only had a Paytm wallet, not UPI, and I couldn't fulfill the transaction as I only use UPI. He assured me he would refund the money once he got home and provided his contact number. 

Now, he's avoiding my calls and messages, leaving me frustrated not because of the financial loss, but because of the deceitful act. This experience has shaken my trust and makes me hesitant to assist others in similar situations. These scams are disheartening and pervasive. While he hasn't blocked me, he refuses to respond to my attempts to contact him. I'm struggling to remain calm, as it's not about the money but the feeling of being taken advantage of that's causing me distress.

 If I were to recover the funds, I would gladly pass them on to someone genuinely in need. However, being scammed has left me feeling anxious and wary.
