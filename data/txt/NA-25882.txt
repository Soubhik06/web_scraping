I got a fraud call impersonating Mumbai police claiming there is an FIR registered against me. They asked for aadhaar copy before proceeding. They video called me, asked me to show them if I'm alone in the room. They then proceeded to ask my exact address (in my aadhar the address is still old, and I live in a state away from my parents) and bank details. I got uncomfortable to share and they started getting abusive and I cut the call. Later I called local police (I live in Hyderabad) and they told me it's a scam. I have currently frozen all my accounts although I didn't share any bank details. I'm still scared that I've shared my aadhar card details... What's the worst that can happen here...
