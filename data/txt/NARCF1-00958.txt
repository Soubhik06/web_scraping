I received a WhatsApp message from a number that convinced me to join a scheme where they promised to pay me for completing tasks in exchange for 5-star ratings. They asked for my bank details to transfer my payments. 

On the first day (18/11/2024), I completed tasks and received 150 rupees. They then pressured me to pay 1000 rupees to keep my task payments adequate, which they later returned along with a small increase. On the second day (19/11/2024), I paid 3000 rupees, and they convinced me to pay an additional 500, claiming I would get 5770 rupees back.

On the third day (20/11/2024), they told me to pay 5000 rupees because a task was filled. Eventually, they pushed me to pay 23,500 rupees, promising I could earn much more. After increasing my investment to 42,000 rupees, they told me I needed to deposit 1,10,000 rupees for a third stage, but I couldn't afford that.

When I asked to withdraw my money, they said I couldn't until I completed the tasks, and tried to pressure me into paying more. Eventually, I realized it was a scam and now I'm left without any money. I need help to recover my lost funds.
