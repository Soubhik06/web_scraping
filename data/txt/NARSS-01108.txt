Hello, everyone! 

I am currently touring Gujarat. 
What a place! 

I was searching for cabs/ self driven cars in Bhuj. Stumbled on a website, tried to book, it took my contact and said someone will get back to me. 

Later, someone called me, claiming to be from Mahadev car rentals (this was not the name of the website I had visited). 
He gave a too good to be True offer (2000 / day for a Thar). I got a bit suspicious. He sent his website and asked to book and pay Rs. 150 as advance (second alarm bell - too low). The website asked for credit card payment. Suspicious as I was, I gave some random card number and tried to book a cab for yesterday (lol). 
Surprise surprise - it said OTP has been sent. 
Got confirmation that this is a scam. 

He later called and insisted my father and me to download his app as an apk, not on play store. My father blocked his number. 
He called me and started abusing me, saying he will get me blacklisted from all cab services. I said something, hung up and blocked him too. 

Phew. 

Please be careful. Just one click to download the apk, and you will loose all your money. Please be careful.

Anyway I can report this? 
Has anyone experienced something similar?
