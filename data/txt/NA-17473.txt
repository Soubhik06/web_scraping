I am an Indian living in the US. I usually disconnect scam calls when someone poses as law enforcement or tax authority (IRS) and tell me there is a warrant against my name or I owe some amount and if I don't pay, I will be arrested. Today I got a call from someone posing as US customs and Border protection officer and they told me there is an international package on my name (they asked my name in the beginning of the call and I told them an imaginary name with fake accent, as I knew it's a spam call, thanks to my cell phone service provider's spam protection service which alerts us in the caller ID). She said the package has drugs in it and I need to pay them fine to destroy it or else they will arrest me. I said, I am in jail currently (I know I should have played more and have had fun) but somehow my fake accent was slipping. She noticed my accent and said You are a fucking Indian and before I could cuss back at her, she disconnected the call. 

Even the scammers hate to have us answering their calls and I find it funny as well as something to be proud of at the same time.
