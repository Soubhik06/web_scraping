Lately, I've been seeing a lot of cooperative society layouts popping up on the outskirts of Bangalore. They ask you to invest in phases over 4-5 years, and after that, they promise to hand over the land. Their reasoning for the delayed handover is that they're buying agricultural land and need time to convert it for residential use before allotting plots. They also mention that if you want to back out, they'll deduct ₹1000 and return the rest of your investment.

Is this a legit new way of selling land, or does it sound like a scam? Would love to hear thoughts from anyone who’s been through something similar.
