Heard on this first application for bail under section
439 of the Code of Criminal Procedure filed on behalf of
applicant Gajendra Singh in Crime No.124/2019 registered by
Cyber Zone, District Jabalpur under Sections 419 , 420 , 468 of IPC and 66- D of the Information Technology Act . The case of the prosecution in short is that applicant
with the connivance of another co-accused Brijmohan
fraudulently floated an advertisement of selling a four
wheeler No.MP-28-CA-8398 and posed himself that this car
belonged to him and frauded the name of other registered
owner. Dr. Sanjay Mehrotra was interested to purchase the car
but applicant received Rs.1,59,500/- in the Google pay and
Paytm account but did not supply the car because that car did
not belong to the applicant. The applicant committed a fraud
and by adopting fraudulent procedure received the amount
from Sanjay Mehrotra. Counsel appeared for the applicant submits that the
applicant has been falsely implicated. He is innocent. One
another person committed a fraud by using mobile numbers in
Paytm of the applicant. Fraud has also been committed
against the applicant. Therefore, it has been prayed that the
applicant be released on bail. HIGH COURT OF MADHYA PRADESH:JABALPUR M.Cr.C No.35130/2019 Gajendra Singh vs. State of Madhya Pradesh

 Learned Panel Lawyer for the respondent/State has
 opposed the bail application. After hearing the counsel appearing for the applicant at
 length and Panel Lawyer for the respondent/State submits that
 more than one offence of Cyber cases registered against the
 applicant and considering all facts and circumstances of the
 case, this Court is not inclined to release the applicant on
 bail. Consequently, this first application for bail under Section 439 of the Code of Criminal Procedure filed on behalf
 of petitioner Gajendra Singh, is dismissed. Digitally
signed by BIJU
 (Vishnu Pratap Singh Chauhan)
