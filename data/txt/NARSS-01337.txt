Our media houses have become an absolute disgrace.

According to multiple so-called "news channels" like Reporter TV, 24 News, and Asianet News, Shehbaz Sharif has fled to bunkers or been exiled to Bahrain, General Munir has been arrested, government overthrow on process, the Karachi port has been attacked and destroyed, INS Vikrant is bombarding Pakistan, all major Pakistani cities are under siege, Turkey has supplied arms to Pakistan, and the U.S. is threatening Pakistan. All this—aired with blaring sirens, flashy VFX, and zero journalistic integrity.

At a time of such heightened tensions and uncertainty, instead of acting responsibly, these channels are shamelessly broadcasting unverified, sensationalist garbage. They recycle fake videos from Twitter without the slightest effort at fact-checking, and present them as breaking news.

If we still have to wait for an official statement from the Indian Army to know what’s actually happening, then what purpose do these media houses serve? If your “sources” are unreliable and your headlines are fiction, just shut down. You are not informing the public—you are misleading them, adding chaos to crisis, and insulting the very concept of journalism.
