I have been seeing so many posts daily about different scams with same modus operandi. So thought of summarising &amp; posting it.

Starts with following type of call :
- Call from TRAI
- Call from FedEx or any other courier company 
- Call from CBI
- Call from insurance company about old insurance 
- Call from Amazon call centre(fake) about return/cancelled/undelivered orders 
- Call regarding Undelivered passport with FedEx(if you have really applied for Visa at any embassy)
- Call regarding your dear ones getting into legal trouble living abroad or in a different city
- Could be any other pleas which I haven’t read about 

Be it any of the above reason, they will tell something illegal has been done using our Aadhar or PAN, some illegal mobile numbers or bank accounts are linked to our name, or parcel contains illegal content like drug and they want us to get a clear chit by registering a cyber complaint and for that they pretend to automatically transfer the call to cyber cell.. Most of the cases it connects to Andheri cyber cell branch, Mumbai (again fake) and then that person along with few of his supposedly seniors from IPS level brain wash you with different stories how can you be in trouble or get into legal cases if you don’t follow their instructions..

Final aim is that they will ask you to transfer money for supposedly RBI verification or so that they can help you get away with the situation.. 

Modus operandi is only one to extract money from you by mental and emotional torture.

All this is fake and this scam is wide spread.

Disconnect the call then and there. If somehow you scum to these morons please register a complaint by dialling 1930(cyber cell helpline) immediately. The earlier you complain the better are your chances of getting money back.

People are loosing a lot of money to tune of crores. Spread this message, stay vigilant and protect yourself.
