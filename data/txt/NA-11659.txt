CS(COMM) 67/2021
 Let the plaint be registered as suit. Issue summons to the defendants. Learned counsel for defendants
No.1, 4 and 5 accepts notice, respectively. Notice be issued to the other
defendants through speed post, e-mail and courier, returnable for
04.05.2021. 1. This application is filed under Order 39 Rules 1 and 2 CPC seeking an
ex-parte ad-interim injunction to restrain defendants No.1 to 5, their
 directors, partners, proprietors etc. or any other persons acting for and on
their behalf from allowing defendants No.6 to 47 to sell the products of the
plaintiff without due diligence. Other connected reliefs are also sought. 2. It is the case of the plaintiff that the plaintiff specialises in
microphones for Smartphones, DSLR Cameras, Camcorders, Audio
recorders etc. since 2007. The plaintiff has been selling products in the name
and style of 'BOYA', which allegedly became synonymous with high-
quality acoustic products. The aforesaid trademark 'BOYA' is registered
with the Trade Mark Registry. The Trademark 'BOYA' was registered in
2017. 3. It is stated that during pandemic, there was an increase in the business
of online purchase. It came to the notice of the plaintiff that some sellers
registered with the aforementioned online platforms are selling fake
products of the plaintiff including defendants No.6 to 46 having the mark
'BOYA' written on them. As there was a complaint regarding the quality of
the products, to verify the complaint number of test orders were placed on
defendants No.6 to 46 who are registered as sellers on the online
aforementioned platforms i.e. defendants No.1 to 5. On the arrival of the
products, it came to the knowledge of the plaintiff that defendants No.6 to
46 are selling fake products by playing a fraud. A legal notice was sent by
the plaintiff to defendants No.1 to 46. 4. The plaintiff has made out a prima facie case. Balance of convenience
is also in favour of the plaintiff. Accordingly, an ad-interim injunction is
passed in favour of the plaintiff and against the defendants restraining the
defendants their directors, partners, proprietors etc. or any other persons
acting for and on their behalf to sell the products of the plaintiff using the
 mark 'BOYA' or any other mark which is deceptively or phonetically
similar to the plaintiff's mark or in any manner representing to be that of the
plaintiff. 5. In case, on communication being sent by the plaintiff to defendants
No.1 to 5 asserting that the said defendants No.6 to 47 are indulging in
advertising, selling, or in any manner dealing with the fake products of the
plaintiff, defendants No.1 to 5 shall take steps to remove/delete the contents
of such listing from the aforementioned online platform. 6. The communication by the plaintiff to defendants No.1 to 5 may also
be sent through the learned counsel appearing for the said defendants. 7. Issue notice to the defendants. Learned counsel for defendants No.1, 4
and 5 accepts notice, respectively. Notice be issued to the other defendants
through speed post, e-mail and courier, returnable for 04.05.2021. 8. A complete set of papaerbook be supplied to the learned counsel for
defendants No.5. 9. The plaintiff to comply with Order 39 Rule 3 CPC within five days. 10. This application is filed for permitting the plaintiff to serve notice to
defendants No.6 to 46 through defendants No.1 to 5. 11. It is the claim of the plaintiff that they are not aware of the address
and contact number of defendants No.6 to 46. 12. This fact is denied by the learned counsel appearing for defendants
No.1, 4 and 5. It is pointed out that in case there is a listing by the said
defendants No.6 to 46, the same contains the appropriate details. Similarly,
the invoices that were generated on the orders also contain the appropriate
information. 13. Let plaintiff serve defendants No.2, 3 and 6 to 46 through e-mail. The
plaintiff is also permitted to serve defendants No.6 to 46 through publication
in the Statesmen Delhi edition, returnable for 04.05.2021. JAYANT NATH, J. FEBRUARY 10, 2021/v
