I recently searched Google for "refurbished phones price" on my phone, and there was this site called [Salebest.com](https://Salebest.com) that had all the best links on Google preferred shopping ads at the top of the page that scrolls sidewards. The prices were unbelievable. Just Rs. 4,000/- for a Xiaomi Mi 11 5G phone. This is an otherwise expensive phone to buy brand new. Even by refurbished standards, this price seemed way too low. However I figured that even if it needed repairs within a year, I could probably repair it for relatively cheap from local shops (compared to the price anyways). I also presumed Google would not let a scammer be on their front page, and in their preferred ads section. Boy how wrong I was. On tapping on the ad on Google, I was taken to a website called [Salebast.com](https://Salebast.com). After I paid via PayTM UPI option on their website, they showed my payment as received, but showed my order as processing. (It's probably still showing as processing after all these days btw). I tried searching my Order number on the My Orders page, and it just shows blank - likely because the order is still showing as processing. I tried calling the number on the website, and it was instantly disconnected and further attempts from other phones showed it could not be connected from existing phone networks. I then tried the listed email address, which returned back with Gmail saying it was unreachable after repeated tries on their part (I think it came back 2 days later). I tried contacting [support.google.com](https://support.google.com) to notify them they were promoting ads for a scammer website, but I don't seem to have any reply back showing on my email as yet. Worse yet, they continue to place a whole slew of ads which are almost all [Salebest.com](https://Salebest.com) on their preferred shopping listed ads on their front page when searching for "refurbished phones price". I found a link to report to some international consumer complaints site, but they didn't reply back either. There was a phone number for Consumer Complaints for India, but that number is always busy. It tells me I'm in a queue, but then disconnects me, leaving me to call them back and get back to the end of the queue each time. I gave up trying to reach them. I'm wondering if I should file an FIR with Cyber Crime, or what my next step should be? Btw someone else I know also tried buying a phone from them, and it resulted in exactly the same set of circumstances. Also related question, but why is Google harbouring and promoting this company that is clearly a scammer company? Shouldn't they be trying to protect us from such scam websites? Anyways, do help reddit. I'm cross-posting on r/legaladviceindia, r/India and r/Mumbai so others are also made aware. It is now been a week and I need to know what to do next? please upvote for more traction
