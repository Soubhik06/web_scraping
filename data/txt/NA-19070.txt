It's amazing that something like Coursera exists. The idea of being able to learn anything from the comfort of your home is incredible, and on top of that, there's a Financial Aid program! Sounds too good to be true, right?

Since I had some free time, I thought I’d dive into Digital Marketing—after all, knowledge never goes to waste. When I found out about their Financial Aid program, I figured, why not apply and see how it works?

And through this process, here’s what I discovered:

The full cost of a course bundle (7 sub-courses) is around ₹2300. 

After Coursera's oh-so-generous 90% discount, I pay ₹350 per sub-course. Let me tell you, the Application process for Financial Aid is quite tedious, long and boring, but of course you get 90% waived off, so worth it.

Also, all the 7 sub courses needs to be applied separately with same long and tedious process, and they don't take request for one bundled course, though they sell it as one bundled course, hmm!!

But here’s where it gets ridiculous—if I apply for financial aid, which is supposed to help, I still end up paying the same or even more! Coursera's "financial aid" makes me pay around ₹350 per sub course, meaning I end up coughing up ₹2450 for all seven sub courses

 That's more than the original price!

 
Honestly, I would have just paid the original fee and taken the course if I hadn't come across their Financial Aid Program. Now, it feels like a scam, and it’s left me with a bad impression of Coursera. At this point, I don't even feel like joining the course anymore.
