The Unified Payments Interface (UPI) ecosystem has recorded a total of 16.73 billion transactions in December 2024, marking an 8% increase from November’s 15.48 billion transactions, as per data from the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI). This surge reflects not only in transaction volume but also in value, with December’s transactions amounting to Rs 23.25 lakh crore compared to Rs 21.55 lakh crore in November.

Volume : The total number of transactions hit 16.73 billion, up from 15.48 billion in November and 16.58 billion in October.

Value : The total transaction was valued at Rs 23.25 lakh crore in December, a rise from Rs 21.55 lakh crore in November but slightly less than October’s Rs 23.50 lakh crore.

Daily average : The number of average daily transactions rose to 540 million from 516 million in November, showing a consistent daily use increase.

Transaction count : October saw 16.58 billion transactions, which dipped to 15.48 billion in November before rising again to 16.73 billion in December.

Transaction value : The value of transactions in October was INR 23.50 lakh crore, decreased to INR 21.55 lakh crore in November, and then increased to INR 23.25 lakh crore in December.

Year-on-Year growth : December 2025 witnessed a 39% increase in transaction volume compared to December of the previous year, with a 28% rise in transaction value. November’s growth was 38% in volume and 24% in value year-on-year.

Steady growth : UPI recorded a 39% year-on-year (YoY) growth in transaction count and a 28% increase in transaction amount in December 2024.

Smaller transactions driving growth : The average ticket size continues to decline, indicating greater adoption for low-value transactions.

P2M transactions leading the surge : Person-to-Merchant (P2M) payments volume contributed significantly to UPI’s expansion.

NPCI recently extended the deadline for third-party UPI app providers (TPAPs) to comply with the UPI volume cap by two years, until December 2026. This move benefits major players like PhonePe and Google Pay, which currently dominate the market, enabling them to maintain their stronghold without immediate restrictions.
