--- POST TEXT ---

I ordered an item from Flipkart but it wasn’t delivered on the expected date. Instead I received a message telling me how the delivery address was not correct and the item will be delivered next day. 
In the meantime, I received another text from an unknown number asking me to update the delivery address on a site that looked like the website of Indian Post. Out of curiosity I checked the link which opened to a note asking me to pay 25 Rupees as a re delivery fees. 
I entered the details since so thought it’s just a few pennies but the OTP I received mentioned a purchase of 492$ for some item on aliexpress. It was at this moment I was legit freaked out and realised it’s a scam.

Thankfully I didn’t enter the OTP else I would have been scammed.

Thoughts? Who is leaking our details to these scammers? I genuinely suspect the delivery channel which is letting the scammers know about the details of our orders.
