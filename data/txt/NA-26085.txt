Hello r/chennai, hope you are all doing well at this time. 

I'd recieved a call from 'TRAI' this morning, the moment I picked it up, it led me to a very convincing IVR that said my number was being blocked for some suspicious activity. They'd given me some details of the person who had 'impersonated' me along with an address and an FIR number. He even gave me his employee ID. The customer service rep connected me to 'Mumbai Police', who picked up almost immediately. That was when I got a bit sus and blocked the numbers. They'd made a call again but I blocked that number too. 

This reminded me of a message from TRAI that said that they never make calls regarding disconnection of numbers. If such calls are made, it is to be reported to the cyber crime portal. The lesson that I learnt: always always read TRAI's messages.

I wanted to make this post as a PSA so that nobody else gets scammed. Will definitely give a complaint on the cyber crime portal regarding these scammers.
