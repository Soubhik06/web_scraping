1. The PSI of Halasuru police station has filed this charge sheet against

the accused persons for the offences punishable u/S. 341, 504, 506 R/w 34 of IPC and Sec. 3 and 4 of the Karnataka Prohibition of Charging Exorbitant Interest Act

2004. 2. It is alleged by the prosecution that the accused No.1 lent Rs.2 Lakhs

to CW-1 at 5 percent interest and obtained two cheques drawn on Vijaya Bank and

promissory note as guarantee. CW-1 was regularly paying interest of Rs.9,000/- to
 CC.No.53637/2018

A.1, but failed to give interest for two months. On 12/11/2017 at 10.00 AM when

CW.1 along with CWs.4 and 5 proceeding near Subramanya Temple, Halasur, A.1

and A.2 wrongfully restrained CW.1 and threatened her with life if she does not

give interest and abused her in filthy language. Hence, it is alleged that accused

persons have committed the alleged offences. 3. On the basis of the FIS lodged by the informant, case was registered against

the accused persons in Halasuru P.S., Cr.No.414/2017 and FIR was submitted to

the court. On completion of investigation charge sheet has been filed against the

accused persons for the alleged offences. 4. Cognizance of offence was taken and summons was issued to the

accused persons. Accused persons appeared before the court through their counsel

and they are on bail. Copies of charge sheet were furnished to accused persons

u/ S.207 of Cr.P.C. After hearing, charge was framed against the accused persons

for the alleged offences. The accused persons pleaded not guilty and claimed to be

tried. 5. The prosecution in support of its case has examined 1 witness as

PWs.1 and got marked 2 documents as Exs.P1 and 2. As there was no

incriminating evidence against the accused persons, statement of accused persons

u/ S.313 of Cr.P.C. was dispensed with. CC.No.53637/2018 6. Heard the arguments of Sr.APP appearing for the state and the counsel

for accused persons and perused the records. 7. The following points arise for determination: 8. The above points are answered in the following manner; No.1 charged exorbitant interest for the loan advanced to CW-1. When CW-1

failed to pay the interest the accused No.1 and 2 wrongfully restrained CW-1 and

gave life threat to CW-1. Accused abused CW-1 in filthy language. The

prosecution in support of its case has examined the informant as PW.1. During her

evidence she has not stated anything against the accused. She has stated that she

had no transaction with accused. The accused have not restrained her, they have

not abused her in filthy language and did not give life threat. She was turned

hostile by the prosecution. Inspite of cross examination by the learned Sr. APP

nothing was elicited from her mouth in support of prosecution case. She has also

stated the she has compromised the matter with the accused persons. The other

witnesses were also not secured. PW-1 to whom the accused No.1 alleged to have

charged exorbitant interest has not stated anything regarding lending of loan by the

accused and payment of interest by her as demanded by the accused. 10. The prosecution has to prove the guilt of the accused beyond

reasonable doubt. After considering the materials on record, if two views are

possible in such circumstance, the one which is favourable to the accused be

considered. If any doubt arises, the benefit of the same should go to the accused. CC.No.53637/2018

The facts and circumstances revealing from the evidence by itself are sufficient to

extend benefit of doubt to the accused. By considering the facts and circumstances,

the prosecution is not able to prove the charges leveled against the accused beyond

reasonable doubt. Accordingly, Point No.1 to 4 are answered in the Negative. 11. POINT NO.5: For the afore said reasons, the following order is passed; ORDER

 Acting Sec.248(1) of Cr.P.C. the accused No.1 and 2
 acquitted of the offences punishable u/S. 341, 504, 506 R/w
 34 of IPC and Sec. 3 and 4 of the Karnataka Prohibition of
 Charging Exorbitant Interest Act 2004. Bail bonds of accused persons stand cancelled. (Dictated to the Stenographer directly on computer, typed by her, same was corrected by me on computer
