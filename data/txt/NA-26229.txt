1. We have Life and Non-Life (Health, Term, Property, Home Appliances, Home) insurances to cover each and every aspect.
2. We have Travel insurance to cover for any exigencies that occur during the travels.

Considering the increase in UCC (Call Bombing and Text Bombing), I guess, there is a dire need for an insurance against UCC Surge. 

Typical benefits should be:

1. Cost of airtime minutes expended particularly in International Roaming reimbursed
2. Cost of productive hours wasted as a percentage of Per Day wage as per Payslip could be reimbursed
3. Risk of Accident and Accidental Insurance included in UCC Insurance because automatic answering during Driving etc has these risks.
4. Covering of Recharge/Bills partly or fully dependant upon number of UCC calls received per day in a typical calendar month.

And as per current landscape, I think, this type of insurance will have very less NCB (No Claim Bonus) because of the surge in UCC faced by everyone.

What else do you feel justifies this type of insurance? What could be done to avoid claim from being denied maliciously on flimsy grounds (precisely fraud protection against wilfully mischievous agents in the insurance cos and telemarketers)
