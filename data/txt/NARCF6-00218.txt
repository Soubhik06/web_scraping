Due to RTO, I was looking at places to stay at in Gurgaon. I came across a very nice fully furnished flat in a rental site. It was a fully furnished 1 bhk for 11k (red flag #1) It seemed too good to be true, especially because it was at a prime location (around cyber hub)
I was like “chalo ghar mil Gaya” and called up the owner. I asked why it was this cheap and he was like “I am a social worker so I want the best for everyone”. Ngl I was quite shocked at how selfless he was (my bad, I get it) I’m also not from Delhi so I thought it was a genuine contact.
Now, I asked if I could se the flat and he said I have to give a token amount of 3k to a random number, and after that he’ll give a slip which I have to show to the guard at the property (red flag #2) only when I pay the amount can I go see the place. He also said if I don’t like it, it’s “fully refundable”. It seemed too sus and I didn’t send him the money. He called about 7-8 times in the span of the next few hours asking for the money. 
Talked to some people and they confirmed it to be a scam + a very similar incident happened in r/Bangalore. 
So yeah still don’t have a place and almost got scammed. Stay safe
