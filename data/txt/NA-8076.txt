1. Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and

 learned APP for the State. 2. The petitioners apprehend their arrest in connection

 with Giriyak (Katrisarai) Police Station Case No. 11 of 2025

 dated 25.06.2024 registered under Section 3(5) of B.N.S.S. Act

 and Sections 66(C) , 66(D) of the I.T. Act, 2000. 3. The prosecution case in brief is that on 12.01.2025

 at about 16: hours, the informant along with police party were

 on patrolling and when they reached near Hitachi A.T.M., they

 saw one person withdrawing money from the A.T.M. Police

 party suspected him and caught hold of him and on search, they

 recovered two A.T.M. cards of Punjab & Sindh Bank and cash

 amount of Rs. 1,000/- from him who is the petitioner herein. On

 interrogation, the apprehended person told that the said A.T.M.
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.30079 of 2025(2) dt.16-05-2025 2/3 cards are forged one and he along his brother Mohit Kumar and

 Sujit Kumar are doing the work of cyber crime. 4. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the

 petitioners are innocent and have falsely been implicated in this

 case on the basis of suspicion. He further submits that no

 incriminating article has been recovered from the possession of

 the petitioner. 5. I have heard learned counsel for the parties and

 perused the materials on record. The Police apprehended

 petitioner along with other accused persons with incriminating

 articles which appears to be used in cyber crime. The

 apprehended person has disclosed that they, including the

 petitioners, operate an organized gang for committing cyber

 crime. From perusal of seizure list, it appears that various ATM

 Cards, sim cards, mobile and Rs. 85,500/- have been recovered

 from accused persons. It further appears that petitioner along

 with other accused persons have formed a gang to defraud the

 innocent public and the phishing/cyber fraud is rampant in the

 area where the attackers try to trick individuals into providing

 user names, password and other details and are indulged in

 e-mail phishing, voice phishing, SMS phishing etc. The case is

 still under investigation, for which, custodial interrogation of the
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.30079 of 2025(2) dt.16-05-2025 3/3 petitioners may be required, hence I am not inclined to grant the

 privilege of anticipatory bail to the petitioner. The same stands

 rejected. (Anil Kumar Sinha, J)
