I have recently been contacted by a recruiting company in India. They said they can get me a direct entry to a job without any interview. Now, the problem here is that, they asked me if I am comfortable paying around 1.7k INR for "reference fee deposit". They said that this amount will be refunded back only on two conditions: 1. if I get rejected or, 2. if i get selected. I asked them what is the point of this deposit, if I am getting it refunded either way? to simply, they replied you can search for our company and you can see that we are reputed and have successfully placed a lot of people with jobs. I said I would get back to them regarding this (which I have not done till date). This all seems too good to be true, considering they are offering placements in reputable companies such as Tata Motors, Hyundai, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank and so on.. But, being India, you can literally get things done if you pay for it, I do not want to badmouth my country, but this is the reality. So, I am not sure if this is legit or not.

Any advice that you can offer me, my fellow redditors. Has anyone gone through this or someone you know have gone through the same, and if so, was it legit or a scam ?
