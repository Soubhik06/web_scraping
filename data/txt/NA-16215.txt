**Vodafone India auto-subscribes to unsolicited random value added services and deducts balance**

Their modus operandi of this scam is they send you a message at random times during the day, stating that they have deducted customer balance ([instance 1](http://i.imgur.com/O9hCw8J.png) and [instance 2](http://i.imgur.com/hJu6QoR.png)). There is an option to stop the service by sending a text (which is chargeable), they unsubscribe you but do not return your money. The only way to actually get a refund for your money and unsubscribe is by calling their regional helpline ([which is not toll free and charges you for call at INR 1.15 per minute](http://i.imgur.com/hdrgCi4.png)). This scam operates on the basis that less than 50% of customers (a very generous estimate) who have been deducted without consent take the time, effort and money to call them back and get a refund. Given that Vodafone India has [168.27 million customers \(as of May 2014\)](http://www.news18.com/news/india/733-million-gsm-subscribers-in-india-airtel-leads-with-28-41-market-share-696040.html), you can imagine the scale of this scam.

&amp;nbsp;

Each time you ask their service rep on the call the reason for this unsolicited balance deduction, they have a prepared answer "*this is because you have clicked on an advertisement and if you want to avoid this in the future, you need to install an adblocker*". The reason why I am sure that they have been trained to shift the blame on the customer is because I have spoken to six different service reps over the past five months and they all use the same terminology and words.

&amp;nbsp;

Research into this scam on the internet provides many insights how this is a industry wide illegal and unethical issue ([Mobile VAS Fraud is back, 2014](http://www.medianama.com/2014/11/223-mobile-vas-fraud-is-back/)) and how Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) is aware of this issue ([TRAI Strict On Activation of VAS Without Subscriber Consent, 2016](https://telecomtalk.info/trai-strict-on-activation-of-vas-without-subscriber-consent/56745/)). Similarly, [the National Consumer Complaints Forum is filled with complaints](http://www.complaintboard.in/complaints-reviews/vodafone-l302.html) of the same Value Added Services scam that Vodafone India operates.

&amp;nbsp;

I am on the verge of switching providers but I have a feeling this is the same for all operators.
