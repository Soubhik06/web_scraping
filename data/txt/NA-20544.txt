A lot of us might have come across such calls, and I wanted to bring to light how this scam works and my recent experience with one such company - Caribbean Holidays International Pvt. Ltd.

So, these folks called me in the evening with a diner invite for my family along with my kids to listen to their pitch for 45 minutes and then collect these 3 vouchers, have diner and be back.

However, their 45 minutes pitch turned into a 2 hour monologue where they started offering 5\* and 7\* properties at 2000/- per night. Which was unbelievable. I was almost pulled into the web, but when they started showing me Aadhar card &amp; PAN card of folks who have taken their package, my brain started giving me the red alert.

How can anyone or any company have ID proofs of 1000s of individuals? That was when I started reading their google reviews. They have a ton of fake reviews which brings their rating to 4.6+. But after sorting them and looking for 1\* reviews, I came across real reviews where people who took their package are stuck with 2\* or 3\* hotels and impossible refunds.

Thank God, I walked out without taking their offer. But which also meant dealing with hungry kids as their diner offer was a false thing.

Time wasted 2 hours and some lesson learned.

I got the vouchers and tried to use it. At that time, I realized that the vouchers are not free. There is a clause where I am supposed to pay around 2000/- for booking 2\*-3\* hotels. And movie ticket only up to 400 is possible.

I have been following up with their customer support team to "activate" the vouchers for over a month now. They keep on asking irrelevant questions and giving excuses to delay the booking.

I am very sure that the vouchers are useless.
