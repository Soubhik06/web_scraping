Initial Trauma

• It was happened during May-June-July-August 2024, but I am still traumatized by their kind of action.

Contest Details and Winning Circumstances

• It was a contest by Britannia called 'Hungry for gold' which offers 100 winners free trip to paris for attending olympics.

• So initially I faced many technical challenges during March and contest ended by April.

• I was not in the leaderboard but I was named as winner due to the technical issue I found from the game and scores of mine were not added ( contest was to play a game online by scanning the QR code in Britannia packets and top 10 participants weekly will be eligible for the prize ).

Post-Win Dispute

• Initially Bigcity Promotions started to call me from mid April to end of May and tried to convince me that I am not a winner as they have asked for the proof of all the codes I have played for and they said I did not submit all wrap of biscuits ( I still have the 400 wraps with me and most of the biscuits from those were dumped due to expiry ).

Initial Communication and Excitement

• I have asked them to share those codes and they failed to convince me.

• Secondly visa procedures were done by DREAM SET GO (from DREAM SPORTS).

• I received email by mid June and asked to be ready for the flight.

• They gave all the details of journey and I was so excited.

Scheduling the Visa Appointment

• I'm from Kerala and there is VFS in kochi for doing all procedures.

• But they asked me to come to Bangalore VFS.

• Date of journey was August 3 to August 7. 

• By July 4 they tried to get me a date for appointment after numerous follow-ups (I have most of the calls recorded and WhatsApp email communication documented).

The Visa Appointment and Documentation

• At VFS, a guy from MIRAS VISA came from nowhere and gave all the supporting documents for Britannia.

• Though the appointment was premium booking without any cost from my end, due to time constraints I could not check all of it.

• They all count atleast 40 pages altogether since it was included with dummy air tickets, name of 100 people and accomodation details with hotel name at Paris.

Visa Application and Rejection

• By July 17, visa processing email arrived and due to my work I couldn't get to VFS on same day as needed to travel to Bangalore hence went on 26th.

• It WAS REJECTED BY CONSULATE and that's the reason they did not deliver the passport to home address which I opted during application.

Post-Rejection Communication

• Now, my only concern is POC at DREAM SET GO keep on followed me up everyday after July 26th till when I got rejection.

• After the rejection, there is no more communication.

Aftermath and Personal Impact

• As a final turn, on July 30th i tried to connect the office of DREAM SET GO to enquire about POC, the person who took the phone said they already flew to PARIS.

• I was heartbroken though it was sure I'll be dumped.

• I said everyone in my office (for the long leave and required permission from manager, HR to leave country while working) and most of my friends and family knew about it.

• I didn't cancel the leave went somewhere as a ghost without contacting with anyone.

The DSG Olympics Trip and Alleged Scam

• Lastly,I saw the post of CEO of DSG saying, he always tried to get maximum people to PARIS for cheering the Indian athletes.

• I saw many celebrities being attending the Olympics.

• They all were like chota mota influencers of Instagram who follow DSG and the crew.

• I understood I was a part of big scam(and found many others from different part of India) and Britannia denied that it was done on purpose. But it was.

Visa Rejection and Lack of Supporting Documents

• I asked for supporting documents for appealing against the visa as consulate mentioned the same on rejection document.

• They never provided the soft copy or hard copy of supporting documents like letter head from Britannia, air ticket bookings, accomodation details, purpose of visit.

• I need this to bring into media or do something against them.

• I had wasted almost 10k on this but the mental pressure and depression cost way beyond it.
