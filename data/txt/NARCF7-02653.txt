Hi everyone,

I want to warn others about a scam I experienced with Ola Cabs today.

I booked an Ola from Mumbai airport to Thane at around 3 AM. The app initially showed an estimated fare of ₹1200, which seemed fine. When the ride ended at 3:56 AM, I saw that the final fare was ₹1900. This felt off, but I glanced at the invoice page, and it also showed ₹1900. I thought maybe I’d misremembered the initial fare, so I paid the driver in cash.

Even after paying, something didn’t sit right. Once I went inside my house, I checked the app again and found that the drop-off time was manipulated to show 7:48 AM, nearly 4 hours after I’d actually been dropped off! Clearly, this was a deliberate move to inflate the fare.

I’m frustrated because this kind of scam makes it hard to trust ride-hailing services. I don’t know how we can avoid such incidents, especially during odd hours, but I’d strongly suggest sticking to Uber or other alternatives.

If you’re using Ola, double-check your bills and drop-off times before paying. Let’s stay alert and spread the word to hold these companies accountable.
