International Model United Nations (IMUN) brings Youth together from around the world to learn and share ideas from a diverse set of experiences and backgrounds where the Executive board, International Press and International Delegates consolidate to learn about diplomacy, international relations, and the United Nations.

As a fresher/student you may have these questions-

1.**What is a MUN?**

**Ans.** MUN (Model United Nations) is a debate/delegation or simulation of the United Nations where students are allotted countries to represent on the basis of their Committee. Most of the time it is a 2 day event. 

2. **What is IMUN?**

**Ans.** International Model United Nations (IMUN) is an organization that brings youth together from around the world to learn and share ideas from a diverse set of experiences and backgrounds where the Executive board, International Press and International Delegates consolidate to learn about diplomacy, international relations, and the United Nations.

3. **How does each committee work?**

Ans. Just like every country has its own Constitution , each MUN also has its own Rules Of Procedure through which the committee works and everything goes smoothly.
