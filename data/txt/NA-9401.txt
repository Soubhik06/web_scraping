Mr. O. Sarif
 ...For the Respondent No. 4. The petitioner assails an action taken by the Enforcement

 Directorate in respect of a company, which is a ponzi company. Learned Advocate appearing on behalf of the petitioner

 submits that, the petitioner could not have approached the

 designated Division Bench as the petitioner is not a ponzi scheme

 company and the matter does not relate to a ponzi scheme. Enforcement Directorate and the Company are

 represented. To my understanding, the determination is such that, the

 designated Division Bench is to deal with the matters relating to 2 ponzi schemes and company. Here the Enforcement Directorate

is proceeding against the petitioner on the basis of a transaction

that, the petitioner had with the company which was involved in

the ponzi schemes. Therefore, in my view, I have no

determination over such subject matter. W.P. 21496 (W) of 2018 is released from the list to be

placed before the appropriate Division Bench in seisin of such

subject matter. Urgent photostat certified copies of this order, if applied

for, be made available to the parties upon compliance of the

formalities. (Debangsu Basak, J.) 3 4 5 6 7
