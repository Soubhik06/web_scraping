Look for Red Flags in Token Price Movements and Token’s Trading Volume
When considering a cryptocurrency, always watch its price movements and trading volume. Large, sudden price fluctuations could signal market manipulation or a pump-and-dump scheme. A token with low trading volume might indicate a lack of liquidity or interest, making it harder to buy or sell.
On the other hand, steady growth and consistent or high trading volume often suggest stability and legitimacy. By paying attention to these indicators, you can make more informed decisions and avoid potential scams or bad investments.

Check Liquidity Levels, Lock Periods, and Fake Holders
So, while we are talking about how to detect scam coins on Dexscreener one of the most important things to do is pay attention to liquidity levels. Why? Well, they directly impact how easily you can buy or sell. Low liquidity may cause problems like price slippage or difficulty exiting your position.
Also, take note of lock periods; long lock periods may indicate a project with serious, long-term intentions. Additionally, watch out for fake holders – if a coin shows many holders with little activity, this could suggest artificially inflated interest.
