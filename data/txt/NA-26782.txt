If you have invested in Growpital, your money is stuck indefinitely. Feeling bad for people who have invested large amount in Growpital, influenced mainly by big Youtubers who have promoted and made videos on Growpital. Some of them had already deleted their Growpital videos. 

I had warned some people on telegram and saved them from this ponzi scheme. So, next time please be very careful with your investment, don't fall for huge returns, tax free returns and do due diligence before investing.
