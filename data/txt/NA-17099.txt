--- POST TEXT ---

So, on the 2nd of April, my father came across an ad on Facebook of [https://galaxysaree.myshopify.com/](https://galaxysaree.myshopify.com/). The site was offering mangoes and dry fruits at a very low rate. Seeing such low prices, he immediately placed order for a few of them. The total amount was a little more than Rs. 3000 which he paid using UPI through Cashfree Payment Gateway.

It was only today that he told me about this and my first reaction was that we have been cheated. I come from a small town in north India and sometimes even Flipkart refuses to deliver items to my place. A company delivering fruits was an immediate trigger for me. I asked him if he had any tracking details and he showed me the messages that he received regarding order confirmation. All the links lead to an unavailable store: [https://galaxysaree.myshopify.com/](https://galaxysaree.myshopify.com/). 

I have raised a complaint with Cashfree regarding a suspicious/fraudulent transaction though I am not sure if they can reverse this. Do you people have any idea if such a transaction can be reversed? Please help!

Anyways, the main aim of this post is to ask you people to let your family and friends know about such fraudulent practices. I am much aware of people asking card details and OTP over phone but this was on a whole new level.

TLDR: Someone created a fake shopify store, placed an ad of it on Facebook, my father bought items from it, paid online and now the store doesn't exist.
