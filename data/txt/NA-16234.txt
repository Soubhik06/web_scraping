Hi businessmen/startup founders of r/india! I'm setting up a software business which is generic in nature and will be sold internationally (think Africa, the EU, US, SE Asia, Australia, Latin America). 

As someone doing this for the first time, I had some questions around which payment processing services you've used (could be fintechs/banks/payment gateways etc), and what are the pros and cons of each. Some services I've heard of are Razorpay and Stripe.

 
Some of the metrics I thought I should consider are the following:

* **Ease of payments** \- how easy is it to get money to my Indian bank account
* **Fee and FX charges** \- since my average transaction size is smaller, the fees for international payments + the FX conversion charges should be reasonable
* **Reliability** \- don't want potential customers abroad to face payment denied errors because of incorrect fraud detection
* **Time to settlement** \- how fast the money gets credited to my account
* **Legal, regulatory concerns** \- will the payment processing method handle legal and regulations of FEMA/other relevant laws concerning export of goods/services
* **Taxation** \- what are the relevant taxation laws I should keep an eye on wrt filing taxes in India. Do I need some proof of export/proof of forex money being credited to my bank account for filing taxes? Also do I need to file taxes in the country where I'm selling to (I've heard the EU has a rule wherein if we sell digital services to their citizens, we also need to then collect VAT and deposit it to the respective EU countries, something called a "place of supply rule"???? Are there other such regions?) 

Would be great to hear from people on here who export either goods or services abroad, and also would be amazing to hear a mix of responses which cover the different geographies such as Africa, South America, Europe, the US/Canada/Mexico, Asian countries, Australia etc. People providing a view of all these regions would help me (and other aspiring founders) understand the complexities of selling abroad and scaling. 

Please do not hesitate to reply even if you export goods (and not services). I suppose your experience will still be relevant.
