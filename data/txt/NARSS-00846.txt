PSA - a scam that i found about

Recently received an SMS from someone claiming to be from BSES Rajdhani saying my electricity will be disconnected at 9:30 pm since dues were not paid. But i had paid my bill at the beginning of the month. The SMS also mentioned a phone number of a contact person.

 

I contacted them and they said Rs 21 was still outstanding . They said download Anydesk app from Playstore to make payment.

 

Thats when i got suspicious. I quizzed them about credentials and they were very hesitant to say where they were from, or give their name or designation.

 

Also they were being unnaturally helpful. I remembered Karl Rock's scammer videos and realized that they wanted me to download Anydesk so they could gain access to my phone.

 

Here is the interesting part - they send these messages post 5pm. BSES Rajdhani's billing helpline closes for the day at 5pm. After 5pm BSES only takes complaints of connection issues or no power problems. So BSES customer care cant tell you if there are actually any dues in your account.

Now the scammer's message comes after 5pm and they say that power will be disconnected at 9pm. So that has the potential on putting you on the spot and making you feel panicky enough to pay up.

Believe me, i was almost rready to pay since i had a friend coming over at night and it would have been embarrassing and bad vibe if i didnt have electricity. Its just that luckily i wasnt thinking with my ass and used used my brain at that time.

 

So watch out. Don't fall for this scam.

If its an old scam then, just be aware people are still doing it.
