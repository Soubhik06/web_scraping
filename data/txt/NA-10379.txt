1. Petitioner who is the accused in Crime No.135/2023

registered by Channapatna Police Station, Ramanagara District,

for the offences punishable under Sections 406 , 419 , 420 & 471 IPC, is before this Court seeking anticipatory bail. 2. Heard the learned Counsel for the parties. 3. On the basis of the complaint that was lodged on

24.04.2023 by the complainant - Kumaraswamy.M.C., S/o late

Chennegowda @ Nathappa, Channapatna Police have

registered FIR in Crime No.135/2023 against the petitioner for

the aforesaid offences. In the complaint, it is averred that in

the month of April 2020, the officials of ICICI Bank had come to

the house of the complainant and informed him that he had

availed a loan of Rs.10 lakhs and had not repaid the same.

When the petitioner enquired with them, he found that the

accused person had forged the Aadhaar card and PAN Card of

the complainant and he accordingly brought the same to the

notice of the bank officials. The bank officials allegedly

informed him that they will look into the matter and went

away. In the complaint, it is further alleged that the accused by

misusing the Aadhaar card, PAN card and Ration card of the -3- NC: 2023:KHC:27413 CRL.P No. 6069 of 2023 complainant, had availed loan to the tune of Rs.34 lakhs.

Complainant's mother after coming to know about the same,

got depressed and she died in the month of March 2022. Since

the bank officials thereafter came for recovery of the dues from

the complainant, he had belatedly filed the complaint on

24.04.2023, based on the same, FIR was registered against the

petitioner. Apprehending his arrest in the said case, petitioner

had filed bail application before the III Addl. District & Sessions

Judge, Ramanagara, in Crl.Misc.No.426/2023 and the same

was dismissed on 15.06.2023. It is under these circumstances,

the petitioner is before this Court. 4. Learned Counsel for the petitioner submits that there is a

delay of more than three years in filing the complaint. He

submits that by misusing the Aadhaar card and PAN card, a

sum of Rs.34 lakhs cannot be borrowed. He submits that the

entire allegations in the complaint are false. 5. Per contra, learned HCGP submits that the investigation

in the case is under progress, and therefore, at this stage,

petitioner is not entitled for grant of bail. -4- NC: 2023:KHC:27413 CRL.P No. 6069 of 2023 6. From the perusal of the complaint averments which is

lodged on 24.04.2023, it is seen that the complainant came to

know about the fraud played by the petitioner in the month of

April 2020 itself when the officials of ICICI Bank had come to

his house for recovery of Rs.10 lakhs which was allegedly

borrowed by him. He has stated in the complaint that he had

verified the documents available with the bank officials and had

found that the petitioner had misused his Aadhaar card and

PAN card taking advantage of the fact that they had similar

names. Though the complainant had come to know about the

alleged offence committed by the petitioner in the month of

April 2020 itself, he has not taken any action against him for a

period of three years. The complaint is lodged after a delay of

three years, and therefore, I am of the view that the petitioner

has made out a prima facie case for grant of anticipatory bail.

Accordingly, the following order: 7. The petition is allowed. The respondent - Police or any

other police in the State of Karnataka are directed to release

the petitioner in the event of his arrest in Crime No.135/2023
