Heard learned counsel for the petitioners and learned

 Additional Public Prosecutor for the State. 2. The petitioners seek bail in connection with Gaya

 Cyber PS Case No. 126 of 2024, dated 25-09-2024 instituted for

 the offence punishable under Sections 318(4) and 319(2) of the

 B.N.S. and Sections 66(c) and 66(D) of the Information

 Technology Act, 2000. 3. The prosecution case in short is that on 24-09-2024

 at about 11.00 PM the informant got secret information that

 cyber fraud is being committed by the Cyber Gang in an under

 construction house of Chhotu @ Ejaj Alam. On the very

 information, he along with other police personnel reached at
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.4116 of 2025(2) dt.12-02-2025 2/4 house of Chhotu @ Ejaj Alam. On seeing the police party, 4 to 5

 persons tried to flee away, but three of them were caught by the

 police, who disclosed their name as petitioners and other co-

 accused. They admitted that they used to commit cyber fraud

 through App as Facebook and Amazon by way of offering huge

 discount to people at large. On search, mobiles, gold and several

 other incriminating materials which is used for commission of

 cyber crime, were recovered from their possession. The same

 were seized. 4. Learned counsel for the petitioners submits that

 petitioners are innocent and they have been falsely implicated in

 this case. It is submitted that from perusal of the Seizure list, it

 would appear that two mobile phones is alleged to have been

 recovered from the possession of petitioner no.1, Md. Arman

 Anwar. So far petitioner no.2, Md. Meraj Alam @ Pappu is

 concerned, two mobile phones, one box containing heater of

 luminous company having the logo of Eagle Network Supply

 Pvt. Ltd, one dull coloured box containing 4 pieces of clear

 LED flood light having the logo of Amazon Company and one

 sound system of JBL company's is alleged to have been

 recovered from his possession. Further submission is that no

 complaint has been made by any one against the petitioners. It
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.4116 of 2025(2) dt.12-02-2025 3/4 is next submitted that there is no cogent material available on

 the record to show the involvement of the petitioners in the

 alleged cyber crime. Charge sheet has been submitted in the

 case. Lastly, it has been submitted the petitioners are in custody

 since 26-09-2024 having no criminal antecedents.. 5. Learned APP has opposed the prayer for bail. 6. Having considered the facts and circumstances of

 the case and submissions of learned counsel for the parties, let

 the petitioners be released on bail upon furnishing bail bonds of

 Rs. 10,000/- (ten thousand) each with two sureties of the like

 amount each to the satisfaction of the learned A.C.J.M.-V, Gaya,

 in Gaya Cyber PS Case No. 126 of 2024, subject to the

 following conditions (i) that the petitioners shall cooperate in

 the trial and shall be properly represented on each and every

 date fixed by the Court and on their absence on two consecutive

 dates without sufficient reason, their bail bond shall be

 cancelled by the Court below, (ii) that one of the bailors will be

 their own blood relation, preferably father, mother, brother,

 sister and or his wife, (iii) that the bailors shall also state on

 affidavit that they will inform the court concerned if the

 petitioners are made accused in any other case of similar nature

 after their release in the present case and thereafter the court
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.4116 of 2025(2) dt.12-02-2025 4/4 below will be at liberty to initiate the proceeding for

 cancellation of bail on ground of misuse, (iv) that if the

 petitioners tamper with the evidence or the witnesses, in that

 case, the prosecution will be at liberty to move for cancellation

 of bail. 7. The application stands allowed. (Khatim Reza, J)
