Phone call scam, Indian accent. "Mr Johnson" is an entity using my (landline) phone to do his internet banking, and can also see my personal details and listen to my calls. I asked how any of this was possible and the mask slipped a bit, with a bit of putting me in my place because they were "technician" and I wasn't. I didn't get with the program and it became quite abusive, fully telling me off until they hung up.

I suppose I should be worried about "Mr Johnson", we never resolved that, although it could be possible "technician" had a Johnson for a head.
