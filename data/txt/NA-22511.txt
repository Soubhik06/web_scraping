How many of you would recommend visiting the government website to verify an Aadhaar card by entering the number? However, I have concerns about this method. If someone were to obtain another person’s Aadhaar card number and create a fake card, entering that number would still show that it exists.

My question is: how can we be sure that the Aadhaar number corresponds to the correct individual? What measures are in place to ensure that the verification process confirms the identity of the cardholder?
