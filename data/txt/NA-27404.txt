Anatoly Yunitskiy applied and received a patent on launching payloads into geostationary orbit (space research). This concept was used for transporting human payloads (people) in capsules using string transport. The research began through a registered company called Euroasian Rail Skyway System Holding Ltd. loosely under the brand name Skyways Group. 

They launched multiple prototypes in Russia that were shut down due to safety concerns and so SkyWay moved on to do the same in UAE and India, to have it shut down again. Yunitskiy was rumored to have floated numerous funds raising initiatives to fund Skyways project development as none of it had materialized. These initiatives were dubbed as ponzi schemes as zero Skyways products were/are in the market to this day. 

Skyways somehow managed to collect USD $3-$4 Billion under the pretext of providing shares and returns against investments even though none of his companies were permitted to collect public investments. Post further investigations it was found that Mr. Anatoly Eduardovich Yunitskiy and his son Dzianis Yunitskiy were promoting dubious ponzi schemes to collect money as low as $100 from gullible investors in India in the name of Skyways showcasing the work of Skyways Greentech FZE (which does top-notch work supported by the Govt. of Dubai and Mr. Yunitskiy has no role in it).

Skyway is a BIG FAT SCAM for sure. On their website they advertise saying "Global Transport Investments owns the intellectual property of Skyway, which is valued at $400,867,433,000 on the basis of which the shares of the company (1 share - $ 1) are issued." Now the value of a single patent is shown to be above $400 billion which makes it larger that Microsoft. Umm that doesn't sound right, does it? Oh and Skyway also mentions on their website that "An investor receives share in ERSSH III through SWI Inc. and becomes co-owner of the SkyWay technology." ERSSH III and SWI Inc are both not registered in India nor are they recognized by SEBI etc. and are still soliciting their shares for sale in India. By the way, these were claims were recently updated on their website and show the mode of operation adopted for scamming common people from developing countries.

After, my own unfortunate luck, investing 100 shares in Skyway and radio silence from their end, I did some digging and found out that they launder the money they get from small investors. Skyway is actually a fraud MLM company that takes money from investors in India by showing fake videos, audio messages and wrong advertisements in various languages like Hindi, Punjabi, Tamil and Bangla. As per their website, they have collected USD $250 Million from small investors in India. Skyway website ([www.skyway.capital/forinvestors](https://www.skyway.capital/forinvestors)) also shows misleading ads like "You can become a co-owner of the largest transport corporation of the XXI century" but they have no product in any market or any revenue from sales till now. I also found out that Skyway products are in research and testing phase since 2011.

Skyway is violating various laws and collecting millions from poor Indian investors. They receive investments in Bitcoins which is impossible to get back for investors from outside India. Many governments have warned against any investment in multi-level marketing schemes by Skyway but its still unclear why SEBI or other governmental agencies have not yet taken any action against Skyway from defrauding investors in India.

Skyway Investment Group (SWIG) is positively a scam. On their website, they mention that "An investor receives share in ERSSH III through SWI Inc. and becomes co-owner of the SkyWay technology." ERSSH III and SWI Inc are both not registered in India nor are they recognized by SEBI etc. and are still soliciting their shares for sale in India. They claim to have collect $250 million from Indian investors. 

I found some youtube videos (linked below) to prove that Skyway and its shell corporation groups has damaged Section 3 of The Prize Chits and Money Circulation Schemes (Banning) Act, 1978 which says: no person shall promote or conduct any prize chit or money circulation scheme or enroll as a member to any such chit or scheme or participate in it otherwise, or receive or remit any money in pursuance of such chit or scheme. All their ads for money in India also violate Clause 2 on conditions for the setting up of direct selling business vide letter no. F No. 21/18/2014-IT (Vol II) reading " It shall 1. be a registered legal entity under the laws of India." Sky Way Group, SWIG, ERSSH II etc are all not registered in India or recognized so it cannot be legit at all.
