The tamasha unleashed by Modi and Shah is just starting. It is a long road ahead. 

1. Economy will decline more severely in 2020 and 2021 than ever before. Banks have been recasting NPAs in other terms but that ends on March 31st (view Akash Banerjee's video for the links). 

More SMEs will be driven out of business due to slowing domestic economy. 

Government will suffer greatly on account of its fake fiscal deficit numbers, because now the world knows that India is cooking the books. 

2. Socially, the number of riots will be increased to distract people from their economic failure. More will die from all religions. 

Hindi will be enforced gradually and crores will be wasted in popularizing sanskrit. Meanwhile, crores of kids will come out of schools in UP without any english knowledge, unable to find jobs and thus work for the IT Cell or depress wages in other states. 

All education institutes will be privatized, research centers will be shut. Government will rather buy tech from abroad than make in India. After all, buying military tech from US gets you a family US trip and lots of cash for expenses. Read A feast of vultures by Josy Joseph. 

Only sweatshops and screw driver factories will remain in India later, just like what happened in Bangladesh. The auto industry will gradually die out except 2-3 companies. Lack of job certainty and horrific traffic and parking conditions will discourage buyers. 

Public safety will be a huge casualty. Surveillance state will enable any tom dick and harry to get you arrested for having a different opinion. 

3. Environment and farming. Under pressure to improve finances, the Government will give in to demands for destroying the forests for sweatshops like they do in Africa. 

Contract farming for monoculture and GMO crops will be brought in to temporarily raise farmer incomes.

Climate change will badly affect river flows and cause water crises. The government is likely to push its river linking agenda to give a temporary relief. But that will destroy the aquatic ecosystem. 

4. International relations- India will become a nothing. India will have destroyed the relationships with neighbors like Bangladesh and friendly countries like Indonesia. The only allies will be the ones India purchases weapons from, like Israel. US anyway does not give a shit about India except as a market with 1.4 billion poor consumers. 

China will be the defacto regional super power, and India will have 0 leverage over the country that supplies a majority of its digital infrastructure in case a conflict happens.
