Through apna. Com i got a data entry job invitation from a company called EDatamine Services. As i was free i took the job and registered with my documents. And started doing the job. The condition of the job was to fill 300 forms within 3 days and out of 300, 270 must be filled correctly other there will be a fine of 6100 or there will be a court case. Also for filling 300 forms the salary was 12k that is rs 40 for each form. I completed the job within 3 days and submitted it. And on the fifth day i got a mail from their advocate that out of 300, 299 forms filled by me was incorrect and he told me to pay 6100 or they will file a case legally. As i was confident enough i told him to move legally and i will also from my side. And after an hr he sent me a legal notice which was about a filed case against me. I showed it to my cousin brother who is an advocate and he told me that this is all fake. All the documents they showed me was fake. Also when i called cyber cell to register a complaint against them they also told me this data entry jobs are mostly fake. So everyone be aware of this scam. I was just trying to make some pocket money through this, but i didn't fall for there scam
