So I was in the amc garden in front of orchid white field makarba- prahalladnagar and I was doing pushups when a guy roughly 20-25 approached me. He made convo like what do I do etc and then told me that there's an exhibition tomorrow where they sell goods from premium companies at low prices. You shop for around 7000 and then you're a member now , you can bring people to spend 7000 and you get a cut from it that way you can make money once you spend 7k , basically a pyramid/ponzi scheme. I told him that that's a scam and he said "mai dhokha nahi de Raha Mai logon ke sapne pure karne aaya hun" I was like wtf lmao. Anyways I said not interested and left. 
New experiences everyday in this city what can I say ;) gg stay safe.
