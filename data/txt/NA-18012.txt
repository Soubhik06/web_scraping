# World

This section takes a look at the top security news around the world which impact most of us.

**[Hacking the Samsung Galaxy S8 Iris Scanner](https://media.ccc.de/v/biometrie-s8-iris-en)**

Hackers were able to bypass Galaxy S8's Iris Scanner by using a picture of the owner’s eye with a contact lens placed on top of it, to mimic the curvature of a physical eyeball. Unfortunately eye/face detection technology has been marred with loopholes and controversies often. They were trivially bypassed with simple photos earlier. Industry has added "liveness" detection to thwart it but it seems that those can be bypassed as well given sufficient interest in the hacker community. 

**[Linux Sambacry](https://arstechnica.com/security/2017/05/a-wormable-code-execution-bug-has-lurked-in-samba-for-7-years-patch-now/)**

After a torrid couple of weeks of the world reeling with the impact of WannaCry worm, it's turn of Linux now. Culprit this time is Samba (an implementation of Windows SMB protocol). As in Windows' case, this was an old flaw (7 year old to be precise) and patches exist as well. But as is usual, many folks didn't apply them. Hardest hit though are some NAS vendors who hadn't even released a patch for this yet. Synology released a patch couple of days ago now. If you have a linux system or NAS, make sure to install the patch NOW.
