Just wanted to give a heads up to folks in Delhi( and outsiders). A while ago I was this close to being scammed for 10k but saved my ass. Not going into much details, there are groups of scamsters who request for cash in exchange for bank transfer of money. They will give some stupid reason their UPI is not accepted in Hospital and only cash is and they will transfer us some money via UPI and they need cash in return. 

If such a thing happens, pay double attention to any message you receive which says money transfer is done. Such messages should only come from verified sources and not some random number. These mfs send fake messages to our phones and ask for cash in return.
