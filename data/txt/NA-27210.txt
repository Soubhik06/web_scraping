Hello Reddit,

I met this shady guy that offered me a few business oportunities and I was really wondering how do these scammers operate. Maybe the internet is wiser than I.

Here's the story:

I currently live in Ireland and am a freelancer (IT, but with a legal background). A friend of mine got contacted on Linkedin by an Indian guy. The guy visited his home city with a business proposal: he is to provide workforce from India and Pakistan, and my friend will draft the documents for immigration. 

He put me in contact with the guy. The guy came to my home town and took me out to dinner. He presented himself as the owner of a workforce agency in Pakistan.

Now, I can believe some smaller business owners reach out through social media. I gave him a shot to see if he is willing to pay in advance for my services and, thus, mean business.

The guy started telling me he owns multiple business, and that we will do business together. He told me he'll send me coal from his mine, for me to sell here, that he knows the prime-minister of India and all kinds of stories.

Of course, I didn't find the stories believeable. But it got me wondering. How do these guys make money? What's the catch? How do these scams work? Any experiences like the one I described?
