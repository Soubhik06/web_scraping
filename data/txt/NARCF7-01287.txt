The website of Army College of Nursing was hacked by a Pakistani group just days after the Pahalgam terror attack.

The hackers themselves openly wrote that this is about religion. Clear, shameless hatred.

But here we are, still debating whether it’s “really” religious hatred or just “politics” or “poverty”. Some people will bend over backwards to deny what is right in front of their eyes, because admitting the truth would shatter their fake ideals.

India is not poor. India is not weak. But the real danger is from within. From the mentality that refuses to unite even when our own soldiers, our own institutions, and our own people are targeted.

Other countries, far worse off than us, have a spine when it comes to defending their people and their faith. Here, we are stuck in endless debates about being “secular” and “neutral” while enemies are laughing at us.

If this isn’t a wake-up call, then nothing will ever be.
