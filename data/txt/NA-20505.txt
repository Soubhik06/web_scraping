TL;DR: Ordered gold, price dropped in budget 2024, returned it, seller claims they received empty box (only after enquiring about the refund 2 weeks later), Amazon's being useless, and I'm out thousands with no resolution in sight.

I've been buying gold on Amazon for some time now (yeah, I know dumb). I ordered some earrings on July 20th, but prices tanked 10% right after on the 23rd because the government decided to reduce customs on gold. I decided to wait and see if the prices jumped back, and of course they didn't, so I decided to return the product on August 5th. 

Fast forward to August 22nd; no refund and no update on it. Contacted Amazon, and the seller messages the next day that the that they got an empty box. Not sure why the seller thought it wasn't worth mentioning that they received an empty package the same day they received the return instead of waiting for 2 weeks and only mentioning this when questioned. It's obvious that someone in the logistics/courier/seller itself stole the earrings, but now I'm stuck in A-to-Z claim refund limbo.

Amazon's giving me the runaround, telling me to check back every few days as they are still investigating, first on 26th and now on 7th September. At this point, I am considering jumping straight to FIR instead of consumer court forums, as they are downright useless. 

Lesson learnt: Don't buy gold or really anything expensive on Amazon. Stick to platforms with open-box delivery like Flipkart or Ajio. Record handing them your returns if it's something expensive and not just your opening the delivery.
