Couple of times, I have come across a group of guys asking for a bag of rice, oil, daal sort of basic grocery stuff as donation. 
The group claims to be working for orphanage/old age home and asks you to buy things from nearby grocery store and donate, so that they can plan a meal for the organisation they work for. 
From the way they operate and explain things, I kind of get a negative feeling and end up buying nothing for them. 
I have a strong feeling that this could be another scam going on and just wanted to check if you guys also have experienced something similar.
