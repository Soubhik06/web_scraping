So I have been a victim of another Bangalore scam..

I found a healthy meal service via Instagram called 'Clean &amp; Green'.

I connected with them offline and found that the service appeared to be decent and seemed to offer exactly what I needed, healthy home cooked meals according to my nutritional requirements. 

I guess I was stupid and naive, and agreed to sign up for the trial. They said they had some emergency and will start the service a week later. A week passed. They kept making excuses and eventually stopped replying to me. I asked for a refund. Next thing I know, they have blocked me from every platform. I did not recieve a single meal.

I asked my friend to call them up too. They were very prompt in engaging with him, and shared the same marketing material again. But as soon I hopped on the call and asked about what happened to my subscription and why they are not replying to me, they hung up and blocked him too.

I guess I'm not trusting another Instagram business again...
