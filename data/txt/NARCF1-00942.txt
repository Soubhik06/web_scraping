A lot of you must be aware of this by now. But it still is a pretty new scam going on around. So I have been getting repeated calls from xyz bank with which I have a credit card. 

I picked a first call up. It was a landline number from Mumbai. The guy said I can claim upgrade to my credit card. But my card is already premium. The new card he told me was actually a lower card but I didn’t know that while on call. I asked him to call me back after 7pm.

Then I got another call from some mobile number saying he is from ICICI bank and I have accidentally allowed some charges and now I will have to pay 3600 extra rupees unless I fill the form sent by him. I told him I didn’t transact only. He said I was told this when I received the card and now since the deadline has expired… blah blah. Then I asked him to send me the from. But he sent me a .apk file. I asked him that send me a pdf file, why do you send me .apk. He said this is what the file is and you have to quickly open it or otherwise you will be charged 3600 extra as annual charges by evening.

I asked for his bank ID card. He sent a card which is probably fake. I disconnected the call and blocked and reported the number on whatsapp.

I noticed the .apk file. If something like this comes up with y’all, please see the file type and don’t engage with these callers.
