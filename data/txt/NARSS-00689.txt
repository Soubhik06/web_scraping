Ordered some products on the last sale. one was a dress that had marks on it when i opened it, so i tried to apply for replacement. They have disabled the buttons on their exchange process website on purpose, the process does not work at all. I tried many times for days but didn’t work. No support on their website either. Then I contacted their support on social media, they called me the next day, asked details and said exchange is not possible. So i said ok i will return it then. they took my bank details for refund via email. Now this was Aug 7, no contact since. They didn't even come to pick up the product and today i got a call from their Customer Support who asked for my bank details AGAIN. Same employee. I said i have already given it. She said "yes, but you will now receive an otp from your bank which you have to share." I knew this is a scam but i still asked why. she said, "we have to add your bank to our account" 🤡🤡🤡

I immediately cut the call and now I will never order anything from flipkart again.
