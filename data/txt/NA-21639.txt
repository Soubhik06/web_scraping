I have due diligently reporting each and every UCC aka Unsolicited Commercial Communication to the provider via the following interfaces.

1. TRAI DND 3.0 APP
2. SMS to 1909
3. MyJio App

Since some of the TSPs and/or their employees covertly cover up the UCC complaints as 'Not a UCC', recently seemingly suspicious calls I have also ensured shared the same with a hashtag UnsolicitedCommercialCommunication so that unscrupulous employees in TSPs can not bluntly close the UCC with flimsy reasons. 

I also take the following as a good netizenship/citizenship gesture that in case of Scams, the reporting the same is done to the recently rolled out Sanchar Saati Chakshu and Cybercrime. 

As a retaliatory gesture I have been on a receiving end a couple of weeks ago with an unscrupulous employee in Reliance Jio sending a threatening message that a telemarketing complaint was received on my numbers and then after 4 hours an apology note indicating it is inadvertent error in communication.

I have written back to Jio Nodal via email and/or also NCH portal with a copy to Cybercrime regarding this incident. I do not think we would look to end this with a flimsy apology from Tier 1 Customer Care to close the complaint but the particular employee must be taken to task Hope that will be a deterrent for similar erring employees in the TSP.
