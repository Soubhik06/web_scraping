(03.04.2023) 1. The issue under consideration arising out of the second appeal dated 15.11.2019
include non-receipt of the following information sought by the appellant through the RTI
application dated 13.06.2019 and first appeal dated 18.07.2019:- (iv) Certified copies of complaints submitted by the customers of SGSITS branch of
 OBC, for transaction which has been taken place due to fraud/false/system error
 transaction on 17.03.2018 by way of by passing OTP/compromising banking
 system etc. (v) Certified copy of complain resolution letter provided to customers/any other
 agency by the bank authority for complains as mentioned at point 4 above. (vi) Certified copies of complains withdrawal request submitted by the customers with
 respect to fraud/false transactions, if any, taken place on 17.03.2018. (vii) Information with regard to amount deducted from the accounts of customers of
 SGSITS branch of OBC due to fraud/false/system error transactions for the date
 17.03.2018 have been reversed /refunded back by the bank, provide details. 2. Succinctly facts of the case are that the appellant filed an application dated
13.06.2019 under the Right to Information Act, 2005 (RTI Act ) before the Central Public
Information Officer (CPIO),Punjab National Bank, Indore. The CPIO vide letter dated
29.07.2019replied to the appellant. Aggrievedby the same, the appellant filed first appeal
dated 18.07.2019. The First Appellate Authority(FAA) vide order dated 19.08.2019
disposed of the first appeal. Aggrieved by that, the appellant filed second appeal dated
15.11.2019 before the Commission which is under consideration. 3. The appellant has filed the instant appeal dated15.11.2019inter alia on the
grounds that reply given by the CPIO was not satisfactory. The appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide the complete information and take necessary
action as per Section 20 (1) of the RTI Act. 4. The CPIO replied vide letter dated 29.07.2019 and the same is reproduced as
under:- The FAA vide orderdated 19.08.2019 agreed with the views taken by the CPIO. 5. The appellant attended the hearing in person and on behalf of the respondent Shri
Ranjit Singh, Assistant General Manager and CPIO, Punjab National Bank, Indore,
attended the hearing through video conference. 5.1. The appellant inter alia submitted that the respondent authority did not apply their
mind and denied the information under section 8 (1) (e) of the RTI Act. However, they
failed to explain as to how said section would apply in the present case. She further
submitted that she had sought information which was very generic in nature and of
general public interest, and had nothing to do with fiduciary relationship with customers.
The appellant contended that information sought neither pertained to third party nor
affected fiduciary relationship of the customers of the bank. 5.2. The respondent while defending their case inter alia submitted that they had
provided point-wise reply to the appellant vide letter dated 29.07.2019. They stated that
information sought on point nos. (i) and (ii) of the RTI application was available on their
web portal i.e.obconline.co.in. As regard to the information sought on point nos. (iii) to (vii) of the RTI application, they informed that information sought pertained to third
party customer and the same was held by the bank in fiduciary capacity therefore, it was Page 3 of 4 exempted under section 8 (1) (e) of the RTI Act. Besides, they also informed that
information sought was not maintained in the format it was sought by the appellant. 6. The Commission after adverting to the facts and circumstances of the case, hearing
both the parties and perusal of records, observed that due reply was given by the
respondent vide letter dated 27.09.2019. The appellant failed to establish larger public
interest warranting the disclosure of information sought by him. In view of the above and
in the absence of any larger public interest, there appear to be no public interest in further
prolonging the matter. Accordingly, the appeal is dismissed. Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties. Sd/- (Suresh Chandra) (सुसुरेशचं ा)
