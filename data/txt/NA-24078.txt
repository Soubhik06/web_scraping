--- POST TEXT ---

So I was placing a food order through the drive-in at a Mcdonald on Kasturba Road this last Sunday. I was with my family in my car. A well dressed man approached my car and asked if I could buy him some food and that he was hungry. I politely refused, told him I'm sorry I can't help. But he refused to leave us alone and kept pressing me to buy him some food from Mcdonalds. 

My cynical mind suspected this was some sort of a fraud. After 1 minute of this back and forth while we were waiting for our food, I got really frustrated. I yelled at this man to leave us alone, called on the Mcdonald staff to get him out of the drive way. He gave me a disgusted look and just stood at the end of the driveway giving me a stare. 

The moment I drove off, I felt bad about how I handled that situation. Is this a scam of some sort? What would you have done in this situation? 

I have encountered people asking for money or food in the past. Usually if I'm on foot and have the time, then I'll try and buy them food from a darshini. Food from darshini is good value for money. Unfortunately in this situation I let the pressure get to me and let my cynicism lead me.
