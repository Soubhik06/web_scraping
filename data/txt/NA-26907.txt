If you care to know how I handed my identity to some jackass on a silver platter, check my post history. TL;DR I fell for the bank phone number spoof “fraudulent activity” scam. Some guy in India now has my full name, (old) address, DOB, mother’s maiden name, and the last four digits of my social. (Obviously, I did not use my mother’s maiden name for any of my security questions with the credit bureaus.)

So. Now that I’ve ruined my entire life, what are my next steps? I closed the bank account they got into. I froze my credit with Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion, and placed a fraud alert on each account. However, I read online that even with a credit freeze, scammers can still use this information to file for your tax returns and/or apply for medical benefits. Is there a way I can stop this from happening? Is there anything else I need to look out for?

It’s not like my name or SSN are going to change, and I doubt they’d just throw the info away. I’m worried that they’ll be able to use what they have to do something else with my identity, or that they’ll sell it to someone who will. What else should I look out for? What if I open a new bank account—will they be able to access it? And is this gonna be forever…? Like, am I gonna have to check my credit report weekly for the rest of my life? I will if I have to, obviously, but this is not doing wonders for my mental health lol. 

Yes, I am an idiot, I know. I’m kicking myself for being so stupid. I should’ve listened to my gut, but I had no idea that spoofing numbers like that was possible.
