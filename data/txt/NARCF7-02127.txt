More important for the ones living in the Salt Lake area.
My mother has a boutique which she runs with another business partner.
Aj parar kichujon mile just kotha bolchilo boshe, oi bondhu bandhob, typical Sunday morning/afternoon.
A man tried to get into the boutique asking for money to get medicine, well-dressed in a red fotua, of course this is one of the typical scams. 
now the problem is that after a minute, he was no longer asking for money, he started demanding for money while trying to force his way in. 
My father is rather short-headed but 
still tried to dismiss the situation calmly, the man did not listen. thankfully there were more people in the boutique and in the area. 
he stood outside for a fair amount of time.

Later on, we got to know that he got inside our neighbor's house because their main house gate was open (they live in a flat)

so yes, onekjon er e obviously cctv ba guard nei, thakleo, please dorja ba gate ta bhalo kore bondho korben.
