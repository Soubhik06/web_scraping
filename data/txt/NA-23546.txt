My friend just wanted to sell her books.
She uploaded an ad in olx, She got a offer from someone who was ready to pay more for the books. Deal was closed. And while receiving the money. The scammer claims he is from army and sent id proofs that he was from army. (My naive friend thought he was a real army guy). Scammer told her that if he sends the money it will be credited double in her account as per army rules😂. My naive friend fell for it. And sent him the money. The scammer used to send her QR codes to make payments. As soon as I got to know about this incident. We filed a complaint to cyber crime unit.

And the best part is, we've stalled the scammer that we'll pay more and he's agreed to it. He's number is still on and it's been 3 days from now.
