We found traces of NSFW content in the Facebook page of Thirunallar temple threw days ago. I brought this to the attention of the temple team, Karaikkal collectorate through email and phone. Post that a Cybercrime complaint was lodged too. Today I talked with the temple administration during my visit and it seems they have filed a Cybercrime complaint too. 
Two days later the cybercriminals are still active tarnishing the Facebook page with obscene content. The temple team has tried to clean up the unacceptable content till yesterday but today morning another round of defacement is being found. 
The law enforcement and cybercrime enforcement hadn't still been able to narrow down on the culprits. A news item regarding this has also appeared in Puthiya Thalaimurai television. 
Should we need a legal order from a competent court to get the law enforcement to prioritize on these complaints on the incident? Scammers and Cybercriminals are ways ahead, more sophisticated and quite advanced but it is unfortunate that Law Enforcement is refusing to catch up which is why I feel we may need judiciary to get them woken up.
