My opinion still stands. Avoid Tata Docomo. Nothing has changed since my previous observations, and the quality probably got worse over time.

It's been a month and my connection was cancelled today. I'll get into the details about what transpired in a bit. We had purchased two connections - one for myself and one for my brother (/u/CharAznablekaBaap).

Now about the situation:

* Timed out every night and every afternoon, sometimes for an hour at a stretch. Have received cooldowns in CSGO Classic Competitive Matchmaking because of that shit.
 * Sometimes it would just fail to connect and sometimes it would fail to authenticate. Technician would say "yes Kolkata side was down" every single time.
* Speed fluctuates between fucking 1Mbit and 95Mbit, usually stays at 10-14Mbit. Customer service excuse: "we offered *upto* 100Mbps"
 * Such scam much wow
 * Concedes I should get at least 90Mbit but I'm still waiting for that to work consistently on a daily basis.
 * I also get between 1% and 15% packet loss at times...
* Sometimes it allowed me to use the net without logging in. My ping to kolkata would be good, at 30ms (good for India lel)
 * As soon as I was able to log in, my location would change to Indore, Hyderabad or Bangalore and my ping would spike to 130ms within Kolkata. How they managed this black magic is beyond me (there's a limit to how crappy routing can get). Why the cock would my location change anyway?
* Finally I got frustrated and told the CSR to just cancel my connection.

He tells me that he will get a senior technician at the West Bengal nodal department to take a look at my connection. Also says to keep the connection only if the problem is solved to my satisfaction.

Customer service refuses to believe that and says "Aap apna *modem* check keejiye na." As if their motherfucking FTTH line comes with a bloody modem.

 * Fuckers don't have clean phone signals, how the fuck will they provide good internet? The responses from the guy on the other side was a garbled mess, given the poor quality of Docomo's transmissions.
* I get a call from their retention service in the evening. More raging ensues. I demand a refund for the days my brother's connection didn't function and he promises to talk to (you guessed it - the west bengal nodal office).

No communication with me, problems still occurred and they had the audacity to claim to have solved the problem?! Even more raging follows after which he says he will send them another email.

Common sense states that the cancellation request would be suspended until the problem was fixed or I chose to go ahead with the cancellation anyway.
