3. Although I knew , its a trust gaining tactic. But I wanted to know how will they make me a fool later on. So I proceeded. 
 
4. She then asked to complete other tasks i.,e to follow some random Instagram accounts. I did and I was paid Rs360 more in my bank account. 

5. Then SHe asked me to complete a Betting task which involved me to give Rs1000 to them first and I will get rs1300 back. So i thought here comes their scammy turn and I knew for sure , they wont return my rs1000 back. 

6. I calculated, even If i gave rs1000, my loss was still Rs440 only. It did not matter to me much. As I wanted to make sure in my head that this is the step where they scam people, so I gave 1000rs and asked for return. 

7. **TO MY SURPRISE,** I received rs1300 in my bank account. Till now I made rs860 and Now I was a little baffled how come they are able to give me rs860 . If I had stopped doing their tasks by then, I would have walked away with rs860. 

8. In the next task , they asked me Rs 3000 for a return of Rs3900 i,e 900 more earning 

9. Now , I will honestly admit, my logical mind stopped working for few minutes and I gave them rs3000 out of greed telling my mind that this is my last bet and i will not give more. 

10. But now they asked for more money i.e rs1500 saying market condition changed and need more money to get the return promised 

11. I asked pls cancel &amp; refund and bt they said they cannot cancel it now and cannot refund now as money was already put in the so called market. I knew no market existed , they just created fake website to make me believe of such betting .

So i lost Rs 2140. Then I intospected and it revealed that trust and greed overpowered my logical mind for few minutes and thats why I lost
