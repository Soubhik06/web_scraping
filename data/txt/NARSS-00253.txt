TITLE: Beware of Fake FedEx and Mumbai Police Scam
AUTHOR: Conscious-Finding-81
DATE: 2024-10-25

--- POST TEXT ---

Today, I almost became a victim of a cyber scam but, thankfully, didn't lose anything. Around 2 PM, I got a call from someone claiming to be from FedEx. They said a parcel I supposedly sent from Mumbai to Iran had been returned. They gave me a FedEx tracking number and said the parcel contained expired passports, credit cards, a laptop, clothes, and drugs (MDMA). They claimed someone was using my name for illegal activities.

They then connected me to someone pretending to be from the Mumbai Crime Branch, who asked for a video call to verify my identity with my Aadhaar and PAN cards. I almost fell for it but, luckily, I didn't share any details and reported it to the cybercrime department.

The fake officer tried calling again, but I blocked the number. No money was lost. Stay cautious!
