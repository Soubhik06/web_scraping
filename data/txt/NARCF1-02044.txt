Today around 6 PM, my father became a victim of a scam. He went to an SBI ATM to withdraw cash, but since the ATM was out of service, he decided to go to the BOI ATM nearby. He attempted to withdraw money at the BOI ATM and heard the sound of cash counting, but no cash was dispensed. At this moment, a scammer approached, pretending to help my father. My father, who is over 60 years old, was tricked by this individual. The scammer swapped my father’s debit card with another one and asked him to try the withdrawal again. The scammer observed my father's PIN as he entered it. Before my father realised what had happened, the scammer had fled.

Shortly after, when my father reached the bank, he discovered that the scammer had already withdrawn ₹46,000 from his account. We have since blocked the card and complained with cybercrime. What should we do next? Please advise.
