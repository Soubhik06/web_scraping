So my cousin's father yesterday received a whatsapp video call.

First part of scam is a 'Standard scam' used nowadays. Cousin's father saw a nude women on the other side of call(usually a nude video is played on other side pretending to be the actual person), then they took the recording of cousin's father and that nude women doing video call and cut the call.

Now here begins the Deepfake part. They received a video call again but now the other side was a person claiming to be SHO of Punjabi Bagh Vinay Malik. But they noticed the **voice and lip movement seem to not be in perfect sync.**But they were in panic situation so that non sync part got kinda ignored. The so called SHO demanded Rs 5L and said would spread the video on social media if money is not given. and would use his power to lodge a false complaint using that recorded clip.

Cousin;s family searched internet abt the sho and found his photo and he was same person with whom they had a video call. 

On complaining in cyber cell and visiting police station they came to know that its Deep Fake video of SHO which was made using some AI tool .

Now further investigation is in process

But this Deep Fake technology is scary af.
