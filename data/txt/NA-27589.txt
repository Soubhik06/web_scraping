They don't care. All they can do is update the case and say they are working on it.The "Supervisors" are equally as powerless. In the end, they will tell you to go into a Suntrust Branch Office for service.But you see, the agents in the Branch office - their job is to sell banking and insurance - not provide customer support.All they can do is call back into customer service. It's a recipe for extreme frustration...

SunTrust may advertise Fraud Protection and Customer service - but they FAIL to deliver.
