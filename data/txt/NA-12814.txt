Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and learned

 Additional Public Prosecutor for the State. 2. The petitioner seeks regular bail in connection with

 Gardanibagh P.S. Case No. 9/2025 registered for the offences

 under Sections 318(4), 338, 336(3), 340(2), 112(2) of the

 B.N.S.S. and Sections 10 and 11 of the Bihar Public

 Examination (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024. 3. The prosecution case in nutshell is that during the

 Central Selection Board Examination being conducted for the

 appointment to the post of Constable, the petitioner was found

 to be committing fraud and he had used another person in his

 place in order to secure job and during the biometric

 examination because of the differences found in the photo and

 thumb impression, the petitioner was apprehended.
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.21037 of 2025(2) dt.09-05-2025 2/3 4. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that

 petitioner is innocent and falsely been implicated in this case as

 he is a bona-fide student of the examination which was being

 conducted for appointment to the post of Constable. It is lastly

 submitted that the petitioner has clean antecedent and is

 languishing in custody since 05.01.2025. 5. Learned counsel for the State has opposed the

 prayer for bail of the petitioner and submits that the petitioner

 by such conduct is trying to defame the State as well as it affects

 the other candidates adversely and hence, he should not be

 released on bail. 6. Considering the aforesaid facts and circumstances

 of the case and taking into account that the petitioner is a

 student who is appearing in the examination conducted for the

 appointment to the post of Constable and taking into account

 that the petitioner has clean antecedent and he is in judicial

 custody since 05.01.2025, let the petitioner be enlarged on bail

 on furnishing bail bond of Rs. 10,000/- (Rupees Ten Thousand)

 with two sureties of the like amount each to the satisfaction of

 the learned A.C.J.M.-XIII, Patna in connection with

 Gardanibagh P.S. Case No. 9/2025 subject to the conditions:-
