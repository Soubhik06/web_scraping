I have been buying shoes from tofy since last year and has bought more than 10 shoes from them through visiting the stores or shipped to delhi. This october, I bought 3 shoes where 2 was shipped to manipur and 1 was shipped to delhi. Out of the 2 shoes shipped to manipur, I received 1 on 27 Nov and another one on 7 Dec but the 2nd shoe which was delivered has curtains inside instead of the shoe. The box was not opened nor the packaging torn. Being an old time customer, I prepaid the money and I didn't even took the opening video. I contacted the seller regarding the issue and he said he couldn't do anything because the courier was a third party and he can't raise complain without my opening video. I contacted the courier and they said that I should contact the seller and they can't do anything. The seller was not responding for 3 days straight after I first contacted him. So, I message him if he was the one doing the fraud and at last he call me back after 3 days. Meanwhile, I email the courier and once again asked the seller to contact the courier from his side. To my horror, he asked me to come to his store and contact the courier from his phone myself. I was so speechless that I didn't know what to say anymore. Now i am left with no shoes and money lost and nowhere to raise issues. Please be cautious when you buy from tofy and always do cod if you can't collect from the stores. Please let me know if anyone has experienced this issues.
