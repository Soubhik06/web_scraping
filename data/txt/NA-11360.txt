CORAM: - HON'BLE MR. JUSTICE AMBUJ NATH ----- For the Petitioner : Mr. Ashok Kumar Singh, Advocate
 For the State : Mr. P. D. Agrawal, Spl.P.P. --- 03/08.05.2024
 Heard the parties. Petitioner has been made accused in connection with Koderma P.S.
 Case No. 228 of 2023, for the offences registered under Sections 406 , 420 , 384 , 506 of the Indian Penal Code and Under Sections 66(D) , 67(A) of the
 I. T. Act, pending in the court of learned C. J. M., Koderma. On 28.12.2023 at about 12:00 midnight, Koderma police received
 confidential information that the petitioner and co-accused persons were
 involved in sextortion. They were apprehended and from their possession
 mobile phones were recovered. Obscene photographs and videos were
 loaded in their mobiles. It was submitted that no victim has come forward to claim that they
 were subjected to sextortion by the petitioner and co-accused persons. It
 was further submitted that co-accused Sachin Kumar has been granted
 bail by Co-ordinate Bench of this Court vide B.A. No.1494 of 2024
 Considering the aforesaid facts, I am inclined to release the
 petitioner, on bail. Accordingly, the petitioner, named above, is directed to
 be released on bail on furnishing bail bond of Rs.20,000/-(Rupees Twenty
 Thousand) with two sureties of the like amount each, to the satisfaction of
 learned C. J. M., Koderma in connection with Koderma P.S. Case No. 228
 of 2023. (Ambuj Nath, J.)
