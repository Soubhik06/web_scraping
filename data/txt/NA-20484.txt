I have been watching AADHAR framework for a while. It has the option of being commercially used to identify a person.
Ecommerce companies face a lot of consumer fraud. Customers claim that they didn't get the items asked for , empty boxes or even stones.
It's so difficult to figure out if delivery boy did it or the customer is lying. Some customers keep opening bank accounts , ordering iphones and get refunds saying it didn't get delivered.
In order to curb this if somebody suggests to Flipkart and they do indeed make aadhar mandatory for high value purchases, are they within their rights ?
As far as I know there is a commercial aspect to aadhar authentication platform.
