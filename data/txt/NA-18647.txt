--- POST TEXT ---

First off, I am in now way related to Rhea or anybody for that matter , nor want to take any sides. I like everybody else do want the truth and justice to prevail.

&amp;#x200B;

It is good that for months the fans have rallied, made trends soar to seek justice for Sushant. Keep posting about it to concerned authorities. While I agree all this has culminated in FIR's now being filed against some people and there is significant traction, investigation and media limelight to the issue.

&amp;#x200B;

I know the revolt and anger is because we know our justice system is corrupt and the powerful can buy its way to them. That's why we guys want to follow it to the very end, lest we become despondent about raising our voices, this issue might again be pushed under the carpet. Having said all this, my point is we have to considerate before jumping into any hate bandwagon against any particular person especially on social media and national news media.

&amp;#x200B;

I opened the news, subreddit's today and everything is pointing to now Rhea as the suspect. I missed a day's news cycle I guess. Now there may be truth in it or it may be false, we don't know the real story 100% yet. How about we respectfully consider that she might now be a suspect and let the proceedings go about. If you have observations, theories supporting it, that also can be done respectfully. 

&amp;#x200B;

Instead of that I see people immediately jumping on the hate bandwagon and cursing, making vile personal attacks on people on their social media profiles via DMing and posting.

&amp;#x200B;

Just imagine if it happens so, if all the investigation Rhea comes out as clean. I know with the bank statements out (who knows if they are real ?) , there is a good motive that can be tagged to her. But if it does come it out while she wanted to exploit him for money, she had no other ulterior motives, you know to gaslight him or make him take prescription pills as such. Then is it fair for her to be held guilty already, humiliated and ostracized by all of us?

&amp;#x200B;

I know this may still sound like I am backing up Rhea or something. But trust me, I am not. All I want is people to be a little considerate when they voice their opinions against or for anybody. The language used if crude can have devastating mental effect on the receiver. 

&amp;#x200B;

In the times where mental health trended on the death of SSR, would we be doing the same thing if we don't practice a decorum online and especially when you decide to personally DM the person in question. 

&amp;#x200B;

I want to present two cases as to why I want to take any proceeding that happens online with a pinch of salt and not take sides.

&amp;#x200B;

1. Jasleen Kaur case. 

&amp;#x200B;

You guys might remember this case, if not you can read the details in this

 

After 15 months since the incident made headlines across India, Sarvjeet Singh, the man accused of verbally abusing and molesting Jasleen Kaur, opens up about his life post the accusation and what he is fighting for now. 

People were quick to jump on to jasleen side on social media, even sonakshi sinha, arvind kejriwal and other celebs joined and overnight Sarvjeet singh became a poster boy for Eve teasing/creep/pervert and what not.

&amp;#x200B;

&amp;#x200B;

&amp;#x200B;

2. Mahima Kukreja and Utsav Chakroborty case.

&amp;#x200B;

Remember India's #Metoo movement spearheaded by none other than Mahima Kukreja. Turns out it was fake and the guy utsav who was a part of AIB, his life turned to hell after the incident.

&amp;#x200B;

There is a video which is basically a recording of the phone call between utsav , Mahima with her lawyer sister basically threating him not to publish any counter evidence to the claims made by Mahima. 

&amp;#x200B;

The dude really cries his guts out and tells the girls that his life is screwed, none of his friends talk to him and he gets no work. 

&amp;#x200B;

Now we can say this can be fake too. But that is the point, to treat anything that in insinuated online with a pinch of salt and recognize there can be an another side to it before we are quick to find an accused one to exhibit our outrage on.

&amp;#x200B;

I hope I am making a point that could be considered worthwhile here. Again have no intentions to take sides nor entertain illogical/accusatory comments in this post. Again might entertain if done respectfully ( see the point! ). And I am not telling all this setting on a high horse, I have done the same mistakes before, In fact I remember retweeting the hell out of Mahima's #Metoo posts as I was outraged then and did not have this learning. So real life lessons learnt here. When I heard the tapes and got the know the other side, was terribly sorry.
