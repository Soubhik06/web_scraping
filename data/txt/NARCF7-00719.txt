Hi, everyone. 

I went through an eerie incident recently, and thought I’d share it here to warn everyone. 

I was coming back from the airport through the Airport Express, and was walking towards the walkway for the Pink Line. I noticed a man following me. I was wondering if I was being paranoid, so I slowed down and walked fast in phases, and he matched my pace every time. So I decided to come to a complete halt and stand at the side. Generally, creeps have no option but to continue walking and pass you by when you do this. But this guy loitered around. Then he walked up to me and asked for cash. I said no. He then said that he had lost his wallet and asked me to “help” him (I hate people who try and scam/rob people using their kindness and empathy for others as a tool). I still said no. Then he said he’ll send me the money via UPI and asked for my number. I sternly and repeatedly told him no, and then walked off. I was careful to keep track of whether he continued to follow me. I also held onto my luggage for dear life. 

During this entire interaction, he only cornered me (a girl) and not the men who were standing around. I had just come back from a trip and within 15 minutes of being back in Delhi, this happens. 

I love Delhi, but I HATE people like this who make this city unsafe.
