I woke up to the news that [Kickstarter’s **security** was breached](http://www.b3naam.com/index.php/2014/02/16/beware-post-hack-possible-kickstarter-email-scam/) yesterday.

_(A warning mail from kickstarter staff)_I felt relieved a bit when I learned that only “_User data_” was _possibly_taken during the security breach(**No** credit card data). But then I went on to check another mail from Kickstarter(_which apparently wasn’t from Kickstarter!_). I recently backed Powerup 3.0 and was not expecting any reward at all. So when this mail came, I was surprised. But that was not what startled me.

It was the email which looked **authentic kickstarter _email,_** but probably wasn’t.

WARNING!! Do not click on the “Click here for instant access to your reward”!

When you click on this “REWARD” (as show in the image) you get redirected to another third party site, where in a systematic attempt to access your credit details is launched apart from other standard browser based attacks.

It looks like the plot of attackers was to get “Users basic data and the list of their backed projects” such that they can send personalized emails related to your recently backed projects, increasing the success probability of conning you, while arousing limited suspicion.

**Once again, If you have backed multiple projects (like me), use only Kickstarter website services for communication &amp; surveys . It ‘probably’ is much safer right now, than emails in your inbox**.
