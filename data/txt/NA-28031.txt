--- POST TEXT ---

I have been scammed. I booked for an international flight ticket agent on sulekha and received a call from someone claiming to be a flight booking agent. He was trying to be very sweet and asked us to only pay only when he gets us the flight booking/PNR confirmed . 

He told us in the call in the beginning that the booking confirmation will have his mail id and phone number and will change it later, we thought he was saying this so that we don't get away with the ticket without paying. We checked the booking/PNR from AIR INDIA website and we were happy to get the flight tickets at cheap price and then he gave us his bank account details and we made payment to his account. He told us that he will send the bill and change the email id/contact on the ticket the next day. 

Next day he did change the mail id and we received the mail from AIR INDIA that the booking was cancelled as payment was not made for the same. We called him and he started with his excuses story. 

Now we went to Bank as well as cyber cell in Delhi. The Bank could not help up as we made the payment and it was an authorized transaction. The cyber cell took their own sweet time and contacted us weeks later. Although in the cyber cell station , the police was able to track the culprit's phone in jaipur, Rajasthan border but said indirectly that SHO wont even allow them to go to that place due to budget constraints for a case hence cannot help much. We have the bank account's details of the fraud person and since each bank account gets KYC then why can't the bank/police do something about it.
