Applicant Shiva alias Sagar is in judicial custody in FIR
No. 68 of 2021, under Section 420 of I.P.C., Police Station Kaladunghi,
District Nainital. He has sought his release on bail. 2. Heard learned counsel for the parties. 3. The informant received a telephone call on his mobile
number and the caller asked for the bank details of the informant. The
informant revealed the bank details. A little while thereafter, the
informant received a message that Rs. 24,000/- was deducted from his
account. A report was lodged that he has been made a victim of online
fraud. 4. Learned counsel for the applicant would argue that the
mobile number from which the informant was given telephone call does
not belong to the applicant. The other telephone number, on which the
PayTm Wallet was made also does not belong to the applicant. The
applicant has no criminal history and it is a case fit for bail. 5. On the other hand, learned State Counsel would submit that
the amount from the informant's account was transferred in the account
maintained by the applicant and it is the applicant, who withdrew this 2 amount through ATM, and all these documents and transactions have
been collected by the Investigating Officer. In fact, the account number
of the applicant was connected with the mobile number, which the
applicant was operating and from this lead only the applicant was
arrested. 6. The offence is serious. It is an offence of cheating the
innocent people. There are chances that even if the applicant is released
now, he might commit the similar crime. Even otherwise, considering
the nature of the offence, this Court is of a view that it is not a case fit
for bail and the bail application deserves to be rejected. 7. Bail application is rejected. (Ravindra Maithani, J.)
