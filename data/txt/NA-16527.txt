I'm living in Haryana. 
 I ordered a galaxy watch from Flipkart last week, the delivery boy called me to say that he had come to deliver the product and then he asked me what is the product. 
 I told him that it is a smartwatch. Suddenly he was like sorry sir I forgot your order at the pickup place, you have to wait until tomorrow. and I told him (Then why you called me to receive the order, if you don't have my product) he even told me that I am free to cancel the order. 
I discussed the issue with one of my delivery agent friends, and what he told me was shocking. He told me that what the scammer was going to do is to take the original item out of the package and return it with some fake product to the seller. and the reason he may give is that the customer didn't pick up the call. 
This is the level of scams with both customers and sellers.
