Evidence Act is that court is competent to form its own opinion on the

call for the report of a hand writing expert. Therefore, the relevant 
fact before this court is the matching or non matching of the hand 
writing of an accused with the questioned hand writing. If the hand 
writings match, there cannot be any hitch in convicting the accused
