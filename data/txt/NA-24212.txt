To anyone coming to Bangalore for work and looking for PGs to stay. Please do yourself a favour and stay away from Stanza. It's a nightmare living there. Here are the reasons why: 

The rooms are incredibly small with rents soaring to 16K per month not including water, electricity and lunch. Rent may be more depending on the block you take or if you have a balcony facing a building or natural sun. 

Food quality is a joke. Often delayed. Cutlery barely available. We once had water with yellow food coloring suspended in it for "dal".

Electricity goes out every other day, generators are used sparingly. Hot nights with no fan. Hours during day time having trouble with charging laptop. 

There used to be washing machines but they have taken that out. Replaced it with a paid service. Have to fill out Google forms each time to do laundry. Who knows what happens with your clothes so ladies please think thrice before you give your clothes

Water is an everyday problem. Every morning you wake up to no water, they arrange 2 bathrooms for over a 100 people to use.... That's worse than public bathrooms. 

Wifi. Never works. Have to always rely on mobile data. 105GB limit with speeds slower than your grandma trying to walk up stairs. Will ask you to disable random Mac. I'm not a cybersecurity expert but I think there's a reason we don't share personal device's mac address. 

Previous point was about relying on mobile data, that is if you are fortunate enough to get a room where you have network. Jio users forget even getting calls. You are doomed 

You can forget everything getting your deposit back, my roommate had to threaten with an FIR and only then he got his deposit back. 

Any amazon,myntra etc parcels are not kept responsibly. The guards simply do not give a fuck. Every week there's someone whose parcel goes missing.

The fridges are not maintained. Big fault on residents on this, there are items months beyond expiry date in the fridge. 

The garage of Q block has a leakage problem which floods the floor with sewage water. This happened 6months ago and still hasn't been fixed despite multiple complaints. The RCs have been changed thrice but no action has been taken regarding the problem itself 

The staff aren't paid on time either. Doesn't affect you but it shows the reality of the situation. 1 block has 6 floors with around 10 rooms in each floor and 2 people sharing a block. That's 120 people each paying around 17K a month.... I wonder where all that money goes

I wish I could post screenshots to prove I'm not just another disgruntled resident who is just angry about something and making shit up.... Please let me know where I can post my proof as well

TL;DR: Avoid Stanza Living PGs in Bangalore. Overpriced tiny rooms (~₹16K+), terrible food, unreliable electricity and water, broken or paid laundry, poor WiFi and mobile network, mishandled parcels, hygiene issues, unresolved maintenance problems (like sewage leaks), and zero accountability on refunds. Even the staff aren't paid properly. It's a chaotic mess, not worth your money or peace of mind.
