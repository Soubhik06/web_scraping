In today's scam-filled world, protecting your assets is just as important as earning returns. Here are some practical rules to help you stay safe and secure:

1. **Never buy from someone who is "selling."** Truly valuable products don’t need pushy sales tactics. People who prioritize their commission over your needs are not worth trusting. 🛍️
2. **Avoid financial products sold by Relationship Managers (RMs).** Don’t fall for flashy guarantees, endowment schemes, or "plus/minus" products. Even if they offer perks like lifetime-free credit cards, stay cautious. 💳
3. **Never invest based on tips.** If you’re not financially savvy but want to grow your money safely, stick to SIPs in index funds for the long term. 📈
4. **Don’t trust anyone over the phone.** Scammers use emotional manipulation, from fake kidnappings to impersonating officials like CBI or police. Even if it’s your loved one’s number, always verify in person or through multiple trusted sources. ☎️
5. **If a "police officer" threatens arrest, stay calm.** Politely ask them to meet you at your home. Spoiler: they won’t show up. 🚓
6. **Never lend money to strangers.** Emotional stories are often scams. While you may miss helping one genuine person, you’ll save yourself from countless fraudsters. 💵

By following these simple rules, you can protect yourself from scams and live with peace of mind. Safeguard your hard-earned money and stay one step ahead of scammers. 🌟
