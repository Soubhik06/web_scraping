Final voting list(electoral roll) has been published on 6th Jan 2025.

If your name is not on the list , you can still add ur name in the electoral roll. You are allowed to add your name to the list even after few weeks of the publishing of final voters list.

* Go to this website of Election Commission's website to check whether you r there on the final voter list or not. - [https://electoralsearch.eci.gov.in/](https://electoralsearch.eci.gov.in/)
* If you want to see the complete voter list , you may visit - [https://www.ceodelhi.gov.in/](https://www.ceodelhi.gov.in/)
* Apart from this , you can go to Election Commission's Voter Helpline app or call the toll - free no. - 1950 to know your status.

 
If your name is not there on the list , you have to get it added. Here is the process - 

* Go to this website - [https://voters.eci.gov.in/](https://voters.eci.gov.in/)
* Fill upt the form 6.
* If you've shifted your address and want to modify it , you can fill up the form 8.
* Submit your application.

This voters list fraud is happening today because most of the people do not check before the elections.

 
Everyone , pls check whether your name is there on the final voter list or not and ensure that your democratic right cannot be snatched away from you .

Making people aware is the only way.
