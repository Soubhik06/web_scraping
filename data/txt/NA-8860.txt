The learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that the petitioner is not

accused in Crime No.2460 of 2020 and is no way concerned with the loan

transactions. However, the learned Assistant Government Pleader for Home

submitted that the petitioner account is involved in disbursing/collecting the

loans using the instant loan apps by following harassment/extortion/abusing

process for collecting the loans from the debtors. Petitioner account linked loan

app Indeed services is involved in Cr.No.2460 of 2020 and as a result, the

account was freeze and intimated the same to the learned XII Additional Chief

Metropolitan Magistrate, Nampally. 2. It is contended by the learned Assistant Government Pleader for Home

that the petitioner has got effective alternate remedy under Section 451 of Cr.P.C.

Copy of the written instructions of Sub-Inspector of Police, Cyber Crime Police

Station, CCS, DD, Hyderabad received by the learned Assistant Government

Pleader for Home are furnished to the learned counsel for petitioner. 3. As it is reported that the petitioner's account linked loan app Indeed

Services is involved in Cr.No.2460 of 2020 on the file of Cyber Crime Police

Station, this Court is not inclined to entertain the writ petition. However,
 petitioner is given liberty to avail alternate remedy under Section 451 of Cr.P.C.

before the trial Court for defreezing of his bank account. On such application

being made, the trial Court shall dispose of the same within a period of two

weeks thereafter. 4. The writ petition is accordingly disposed of. The miscellaneous

applications, if any, shall stand closed. There shall be no order as to costs. ______________________
