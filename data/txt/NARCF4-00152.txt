--- POST TEXT ---

Even though authorities have reiterated that we should stop giving out our mobile numbers at shopping malls, I believe customers are still being forced to do so in one way or another.

Two weeks ago, after shopping at Decathlon, I was asked for my number. When I inquired why, the staff at the counter explained that the invoice for the purchase would be sent to the provided number—they’ve essentially linked the billing process to mobile numbers. I faced the same issue at Zudio as well, where the bill was sent to my mobile number.If this continues, customers have no option but to give their mobile numbers at various places, which raises serious concerns about privacy. 

With scammers getting more sophisticated every day, this practice of 'billing to mobile numbers' poses a significant threat to individual privacy.I strongly believe there should be stringent laws in place to prevent data breaches on their end.

The only solution I see to this problem is QR-based invoice generation. After shopping, there should be an option to scan a code that is shown only after payment is made. For those who don’t have the means to scan a QR code, a physical copy of the invoice should be provided. I don’t see any other way around this issue.

What do you think?
