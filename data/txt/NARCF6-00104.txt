Hi,

I wanted to share a small incident that happened to me recently. I took a shared cab from Shillong to Guwahati. Along with me, there were a girl and two other passengers in the cab. When we reached Guwahati, the girl informed the driver that she could only pay via UPI, which the driver refused to accept. She then asked me if I could pay the driver in cash, and she would transfer the money to me via PhonePe. Out of courtesy, I paid the driver, and she attempted to pay me through UPI multiple times, but the transaction failed each time. She assured me she would complete the payment later, but that never happened.

Please be aware of such scams. If you come across a girl named Ayesha with the phone number 6900 295732, be cautious.

Stay safe and vigilant.
