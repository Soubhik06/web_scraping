Hello guys! I work in a PSU bank as a Branch Manager. Recently I have noticed a surge in cyber crimes related to ANYDESK app. As a banker I wanted to let people know about the app and the cyber crime happening through it.

In most of the cases I have been told from scammed customers , they have been searched about their financial issue on Google. Mostly the first link you see is a scam and it provide you with contact number of these fishy people. 

They will win your trust and then ask you to install this app. ANYDESK app is a remote access software and gives you the option to operate someone else's device through your own device. 

I have not gone in much detail and it's all okay to install the app for your help or purpose of accessing different devices but I repeat never on the advice of a fellow stranger or any other person install this app. 

Please also inform your parents and grand parents about this as they are the ones most vulnerable to this scam occuring. 

I have even seen young people fall for this scam. The scammers offer you lucrative offers and then will ask you to pay a meagre amount of say Rs. 5 or 10 and in an instant all of your balance will be gone. Please beware.

P.S. I'm not against the app or anything. I'm just letting people know that the app is used for duping people.
