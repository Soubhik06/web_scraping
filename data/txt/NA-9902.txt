 In the matter of: Bishal Dhanuk
 ... petitioner

 Mr. Angshuman Chakraborty,
 Mr. S. S. Saha. ...for the petitioner. Ms. Subhasree Patel,
 Mr. Debadrita Mondal. ..for the State. Report filed on behalf of the State is taken on record.

 Learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioner submits

 that the petitioner has been falsely implicated in this case. He has no

 clue about any fraud that might have gone on. The petitioner's PAN

 Card and Aadhar Card were misused by one Anup Biswas, a co-

 accused for opening a Demat Account and that the said Anup Biswas

 has already been granted bail on the basis of a compromise

 application. Learned counsel appearing on behalf of the State relies on the

 case diary and the report and submits as follows. The present

 petitioner is directly involved in the money fraud. The petitioner and

 his associates are operating a scam. There are other victims who had

 made complaints in this regard. In connection with the present case,

 a total amount of Rs.22,88,656/- belonging to an elderly person was

 allegedly defalcated. Out of the total sum, Rs.3,90,000/- was

 transferred to the bank account of the present petitioner. Upon 2 enquiry, it was revealed that the bank account contains the e-mail ID

of the present petitioner and the phone number of the father of the

petitioner. Considering the above and the other incriminating materials

available in the case diary, I do not consider this to be a fit case for

granting anticipatory bail to the petitioner. The application for anticipatory bail of the petitioner is, thus,

rejected. Urgent photostat certified copies of this order may be delivered

to the learned Advocates for the parties, if applied for, upon

compliance of all formalities. (Jay Sengupta, J.)
