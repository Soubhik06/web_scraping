Their complaint in the US district court sought statutory damages of up to $150,000 per infringed work.

Earlier, in March 2024, when various countries, including the UK, France, Germany, and Australia, mandated that Meta pay news publishers for their news content, Meta completely removed the news section from Facebook. Therefore, this deal with News Corp. and several other media publications marks a significant shift in how Meta plans to pay news publishers, particularly for AI training.
