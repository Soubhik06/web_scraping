How many friends is still unknown. The group of friends and Vijay blackmailed Sathwika, threatening to send her videos to her family, unless she setup secret cameras in girls hostel washrooms. She did as they asked and started sending the videos to them, for 2 years. 
 
This was two years ago. She was caught last Saturday (24 Aug) by her roommate, while sending the videos. All the girls worried sick, complained to the college management. The Principal said and I quote "This incident has nothing to with the institution. You can settle this out of the campus premises." Sathwika, afraid that she is in danger, informed her father about this. He is a politician with significant amount of power. He threatened the principal that, if Sathwika's name comes out about this incident, he will file a drug distribution case on college. So being afraid, the college management kept silent and provided protection for Sathwika. Destroyed any CCTV evidence, conversation and call logs.

All while Vijay and his friends roamed freely.

Yesterday night, all students male and female protested midnight. Vijay and a few of his friends locked themselves up in a hostel room. Around midnight 12, Sathwika came out of her room, showed every one middle finger and said "No one can even touch me. Shout all you want." Students have been protesting since 2 hours at that time. No police showed up.

Meanwhile, Vijay and his friends got beat up by other guys in hostel. The broke the room door and beat up everyone. That's when college faculty arrived to calm down the situation. No police still. Around 1:30 AM police arrived and all the boys except the criminals were thrown out of hostel. The news blew up this morning in local channels.
