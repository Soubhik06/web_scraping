--- POST TEXT ---

I have been actively looking for work-from-home opportunities, and recently I received a message on WhatsApp regarding a non-voice process job. The message asked me to reply with "yes" if I was interested, which I did. After that, I was informed that a virtual interview had been scheduled for me, with the basic requirements being a stable Wi-Fi connection, a laptop, and at least a 10+2 qualification. It sounded pretty straightforward.

The Google Meet interview was set for 10 AM, and I joined on time. However, no one responded for a long time, and when I called the number provided, it was switched off. The meeting finally started at 10:40, and the HR lady apologized, citing technical issues. The interview had around 72-80 other candidates.

Here's where things got weird: The HR's English seemed quite basic, almost like a 4th or 5th grader’s level. They asked very general questions, like "Tell me about yourself," and then told everyone, "You’re good. Send your email to the same WhatsApp number, you’re selected." The communication skills of many candidates weren’t great either, but still, almost everyone seemed to get selected. The same thing happened with me.

I received an offer letter with a promised net salary of 20k. The offer letter was well-structured, but one thing bothered me: instead of my name, it said "Hey Candidate." When I asked the HR about it, they said I would receive an offline offer letter at home after completing the training, along with a laptop kit.

They said the actual training would begin the next day, but instead of a proper training session, they just sent me YouTube video links every day (which were private, accessible only via the link). The videos had basic customer service info and were very general. This went on for three or four days, with no real explanation about what I’d actually be doing in the job.

The weirdest part is the HR only keeps their phone on for 15-20 minutes a day, then switches it off after sending me the daily links. I checked the company’s website, and it was created just last month, and honestly, it doesn’t look genuine.

Now, here’s my main concern: today, the HR asked me to send sensitive documents like my Aadhar card, PAN card, passport-sized photo, education certificates, and even my parents’ names and address for creating a salary account and registering a laptop they’re supposedly going to send to my house. This has set off alarm bells for me, and I’m really worried this might not be legitimate.

I’ve tried asking the HR multiple times about the company’s products and services, or what exactly I’m supposed to do, but they haven’t responded.

What’s also making me nervous is that I already told my family I’ve got this work-from-home job, and they’re really happy about it. If this turns out to be a scam, I’m going to feel embarrassed in front of everyone.

Has anyone experienced something similar? Should I be worried? Any advice on how to proceed?
