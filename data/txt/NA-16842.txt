I applied for a HDFC house loan three months back and got approved at 8.15% intrest rate. Since we had money to continue construction, we asked them to disburse after two months.

And after two months when I contacted for disbursement, they revised the rate to 9%. Inbetween repo rate has been increased once, but still 0.85% increase was unacceptable. In addition, my CIBIL is still the same, so it is not the reason for such an increase.

We were taking only 15L and we thought 9% is very high and decided not to continue. Then HDFC person contacted us and said they can provide 8.75%. We still were not pleased and said no.

Then they asked us to come to the branch to discuss. We already spent a lot of time to get the necessary documents, so just thought of discussing. They agreed for 8.45% provided we pay 1850 rs as "some" fees. Finally we agreed.

After that everything went fast. We completed the signing on Feb 28 and got the first installment money on March 1 morning. All good.

Now today, I recieved a mail stating that they are increasing the lending rate and as a result my interest rate is increased to 8.70%. Now I feel, what the hell? I got the money yesterday and you've increased the rate today!

When I did some research online, I saw that there is something called intrest reset date and it's usually one or two years. If that's true, then HDFC just cheated me.

Do anyone have information regarding this? 

I'm really confused by all this. Actually my family has been doing all our banking with HDFC for many years. We have their so called "Premium" accounts for long. So I want to make sure they are not cheating us with this change.
