This happens to start with a text message from my friend

"Oye ek event aaya hai karega"

So I was like okay it's not a big deal since I have done a lot of events like Taking Surveys, Advertisements For Reliance Mart etc so I said

"Haaa kyu nhi"

And there he goes explaining about this event like how and where it will be at first I thought it'll be a normal event but later as I went deep in the discussion I came to know that people will get interviewed before the event to get selected for it and I was like

"Aisa bhi kuch hota h kya"

Then he told me about the name of the event which is
NMACC (Nita Mukesh Ambani Cultural Centre) and I was shocked so I thought it might be some sort of scam so I asked upfront from my friend

"Bhai koi scam wam toh nhi h na"

Then he told me

"Bhai muje bhi nhi pata but muje bas ek paragraph based message aaya toh maine tuje bhej diya"

I was like okay well the requirements for the events was 40 Boys and 20 Girls and all the people should be fair enough with fluent English as my friend has a company for supplying for manpower so we started gathering people like from evening till night I attended like 150+ calls and texting all the criteria and the Mam even said that they wanted clean shave since it was a high profile event and she was the one who was going to interview us and from my side I gathered among 5 Boys and from his side 2 Girls and 1 Boy since a lot of people at the end started giving us "TOPA" i hope yk what I mean by that

Well we all were told to come sharp at 11am at the Jio World Convention Centre with a proper dress code which was formals and trust me I woke up around 8:30 am and got freshen up and since yesterday was Monday I couldn't shave my beard at the salon so I had to trim it by the trimmer at home and got ready

And by 9 am I took a rickshaw and reached Bhayandar station to take the train from there to Bandra it was Dadar Fast and all my friends were on their way I was nervous after 45 mins I reached Bandra station by 10:14 am and I saw a lot of TC's on the bridge I was afraid that i might got caught so I took a ticket for Bhayandar which costed my around 10 ₹ lucky enough I didn't got caught right after that I got some calls from my friends and this morning sun was making me sweat alot

I met one of my friends who had already reached there and the rest were on their way as we got off we were trying to find the rickshaw and it was 10:30 am and from the station to the destination was around 5 kms and we were yet to find any vehicle since no one was ready to stop and some uncle told me that

"Capital Tower bolo JWC bologe toh koi nhi rukega" so we said Capital Tower and found one it barely covered any distance and charged us 30 ₹ per seat because it seemed very near from the station since we were running late we didn't think of it and we were trying to find Gate No 20 which was kinda confusing for us since it was my first time being in Bandra and I saw those huge infrastructure of ONGC, Indian Oil, NSE, it was so porsche that i felt very nervous and excited at the same time it was very amazing tbh those vibes were unreal

Later we reached our destination which was Gate No 20 by 11:10 am there was a lot of security and guards outside and before coming we received a text from the event management filling all the details we would get a code and by scanning it you can enter the premises we scanned the QR code and were waiting for the rest to come along we waited till 11:40 am since one of my friend got caught by the TC and got charged around 720 ₹ the situation got tensed since we were late after that we entered the building among with the rest of my friends and it was soothing and beautiful like I never imagined that my first even interview would be at such a place

We got down by the escalator as instructed in the basement where our interview would be conducted and so we settled down on the chairs which were kept down but we saw there were no assistant or any one from their team we waited for 1 hour sitting and exploring the basement going in and out of the washroom after that 1 lady came she was the assistant she called out by our name 1 by 1 after some time the main head the lady who was gonna interview she gave her introduction and gone in the room and called 5 people in a group to take our interview we were all tensed up talking and all sort of stuff

After 15 mins my turn came and there were 3 Girls and 2 Boys 1 including me my heart was pounding as we entered the room we gave our introduction 1 by 1 well the lady she was trying to break the ice between us by talking about our goals and aim we discussed about various stuff and while interviewing the girl she asked

"What brings you here?" 

She said

"I'm an actor and i like singing too"

The interviewing lady was making faces and said

"Oh really can you sing one song?"

She said

"Do you really want me to sing the song?"

Lady said

"Yes why not?"

Then she started singing one song I don't know which song it was but after listening to that song i can say she brought the lady in a very good mood and she was being very sweet to us and the time came where I stood up and gave my introduction

"Good afternoon mam my name is x and I've completed my SSC and HSC from this school and college and I'm pursuing my career in VFX And Animation from this college aside from that I edit reels, videos and I see event management as my second career option and I have done other Promotion Collaboration, Surveys etc"

After that i requested that if i can show my video work to her she said

"Of course go ahead"

She liked my work and after that we greeted her and left the room with a relief we were told to sit behind and we'll be getting the confirmation that you are selected or not after that we started to interact with other people and well they were friendly and it was really nice to get to know them their work how and where they resided and how they were brought up in here after 15 mins the assistant lady came up and started taking name 1 by 1 I was like okay they might be going in the room where she took out my name suddenly my heart skipped a beat and i heard those three magical words "YOU ARE SELECTED"
