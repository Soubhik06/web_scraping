Tl;dr - Got an elaborated scam call from a person posing to be a FedEx agent for sending illegal items to Taiwan which of course I hadn’t. 

Hi guys, I’m not sure whether the scam I’m about to mention is new but I faced it for the first time and the oh god, the Ankit of detailing they went on to is crazy. 
So here goes - I got a call from an IVR number saying that they are calling from FedEx and my package could not be delivered. If I want more information, press 1. In confusion of which package they are talking about I pressed 1 and then it connected me to a person who claimed he is a person from the FedEx team. He said that there is a package that was going from Mumbai to Taiwan and it has been held by the Mumbai customs department because it contained some illegal items. I informed him that I had sent no such package and neither had I any information regarding this, He said that someone must have given your phone number and they must be trying to frame you so I need to report to the Andheri police station in Mumbai regarding this. He even provided all the details regarding the package from tracking number to the phone number of the Taiwanese individual. He said that he is going to transfer my call to Mumbai cyber cell department and that’s when I got a little suspicious. I said I shall visit the police station myself and sort it out and cut the call. Post which I called the official FedEx customer care number and asked them about this and they said all the details and numbers provided are fake and it is a scam call. 

Nevertheless, I though I should post it here so you guys are also aware if you receive any such call
