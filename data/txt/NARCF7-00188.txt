So my dad’s iPhone was stolen &amp; my mom immediately put a lock on it via Find My &amp; had put my mom’s mobile number as the contact in case they find it. 

The thieves sent a phishing text message to my mom’s number saying the phone was temporarily switched on &amp; to click the link to see. In the excitement, my mom clicked it entered the Apple ID. The thieves then removed the phone from the account &amp; now we can’t even trace the phone. 

Apple Support can’t even help due to privacy and security reasons, once the phone is gone off the account, they won’t track it. 

The police are still confident of finding it - how I don’t know 🤷‍♂️

Just a heads up to those who lose their iPhones be very very aware of what you’re clicking.
