Heard counsel for the parties. The applicant has been arrested on 13.02.2015 in
 connection with Crime No.390/2011 registered with Police
 Station Gopalganj, Sagar - commonly known as VYAPAM
 Examination Scam cases - for the offences punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 472 & 474 of IPC and
 Section 3 (d) (2)/4 of the M.P. Recognized Examination Act,
 1937. The role ascribed to the applicant by the prosecution, is
 that of a "beneficiary". The applicant contacted with Prabhat
 Choudhury - racketeer, who arranged for impersonator to
 appear in the PMT Exclamation, 2009 for the applicant. It is fairly stated by the learned counsel for the State, on
 instructions, that custodial interrogation of the applicant is no
 more required. The applicant is not involved in any other crime
 of similar type. Charge-sheet has also been filed against the
 applicant on 11/03/2015. Further, the investigation against the
 applicant is complete in all respects as of now and, therefore,
 the presence of applicant is no more required. In that sense, the
 link between the applicant and the co-accused persons is
 already complete. Besides, the applicant through counsel
undertakes to appear before the Investigating Officer as and
when called for further investigation including against him as
also to punctually appear before the trial Court to ensure early
disposal of the criminal trial. In the interest of justice, taking overall view of the
matter, we deem it appropriate to grant bail to the applicant on
following strict conditions:- The bail application stands disposed of accordingly.
