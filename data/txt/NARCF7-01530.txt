I recently applied for an entry-level Social Media Coordinator role through LinkedIn, posted by a company called "The Golden Rise." The listing looked legitimate, but after applying, I received an email asking me to reply via WhatsApp because my resume was supposedly missing—despite the fact that I had already submitted it through LinkedIn and the company's website.

After replying on WhatsApp, I was offered a very attractive salary: ₹45,000 for full-time and ₹25,000 for part-time. However, things started to get suspicious. They sent me an audio clip and an unlisted YouTube video claiming they provide "job assistance," which completely contradicted the original LinkedIn job listing. 

What followed was even more alarming—they tried to recruit me as a "Recruiter" right away, asking me to recruit more people like this and pay ₹199 for a session. This is clearly a multi-level marketing (MLM) scam.

I just wanted to share this experience to warn others. Don't fall for this scam! Be cautious about job offers that deviate from what was originally posted, especially if they involve upfront payments or recruitment schemes.
