My buddy just got scammed by a company pretending to be LightStream. Some Indian dude who used the name "Peter Smith" he was looking to find loans online and that was one of the first Google results. They "approved" him for 4k. They said because he has bad credit they needed a $150 deposit for "loan insurance" He actually sent the money (🤦🏻‍♂️) 

Now, they called him back, and I happen to be with him and he put it on speaker phone. That's when I found out Peter Smith was actually an Indian man. They said something about USA loans being used for drugs and money laundering (???) And just to ensure that his loan is legit they need another $350 for a "business tax" 

I tried warning him against it as soon as I could. As soon as he told me the company name and I googled it and the word scam and found out about the impersonation being done. Seems like the actual company is legit. Dude totalled his vehicle and is trying to get a loan for a new car. I feel bad for him, wish he was more privy to information regarding scams. He's the trustworthy type. I warned him against it as soon as he told me he paid $150. As far as I'm aware, no loan company charges you money to receive a loan lol. That's never once been my experience but I've only gotten a loan through my bank. 

Just wanted to give you guys a heads up even though I'm sure everyone on this sub would've seen through that bullshit.
