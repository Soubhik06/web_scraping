Hi everyone. Good morning. 

I came to Hyderabad for work and was searching for an apartment. I was using websites like housing, 99acres. One owner I contacted asked for id and Rs. 2000 (refundable) to visit his property and explained it is a visiting card and he is doing this because it is a secure community apartment. The pictures of the place looked fancy too so I thought it might be true, I don't know how things work in Hyderabad. On reaching the location the watchman told me that this same person has taken money from many people and the property he is advertising does not exist here. Then this 'owner' contacts from another number and says pay Rs. 2010 more to see the flat and Rs. 4000 will be refunded "on call" and Rs.10 is GST. Kind of too low an amount for GST and I felt something is now surely wrong. I told I do not want to see the flat and to please refund the initial Rs. 2000. 

All this happened at about 3pm and since then I have been trying to call and message him. He is asking for more money in the name of GST. First he asked for Rs 269 saying it is GST and the refund transaction is stuck because I have not given GST. JUST IN CASE dumb me thought and gave it too. Then he started asking Rs. 261 more. Since then I have not given any money to him.

Also this looks like a scam that is widespread in Hyderabad. 4 other places I contacted are doing the exact same scam.
