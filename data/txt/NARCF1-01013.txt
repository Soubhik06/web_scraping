Is this the right step? We all know how often data breaches occur on Indian websites, sometimes it's the data from NEET exam forms, other times it's from JEE. The question is, when the government is unable to safeguard even such small-scale data, then if a unified portal like this is created, won't it become a golden opportunity for hackers and scammers to exploit people's personal information and make money from it?
