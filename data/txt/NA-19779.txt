# 1. Stagnant Income Growth &amp; Rising Inequality

* **World Inequality Lab (2024):**
 * The top **1%** now controls **40% of India’s wealth**, while the bottom **50%** owns just **13%**.
 * Real wages for middle and lower-income groups have **stagnated or declined** in key sectors.
 * *Source:* [WID World Report](https://wid.world/www-site/uploads/2024/03/WorldInequalityLab_WP2024_09_Income-and-Wealth-Inequality-in-India-1922-2023_Final.pdf)
* **Oxfam India (2023):**
 * The bottom **50%** of Indians earn only **13% of national income**, while debt burdens surge due to inflation and low wage growth.
 * *Source:* [Oxfam Inequality Report](https://www.oxfam.org/en/india-extreme-inequality-numbers)

# 2. Demonetization &amp; GST: Disrupting the Informal Economy

* **CEDA, Ashoka University (2021-22):**
 * Demonetization (**2016**) and GST rollout (**2017**) **crippled small businesses**, forcing many into debt or closure.
 * Informal sector employment **collapsed**, pushing workers into precarious gig jobs.
 * *Source:* [CEDA Publications](https://ceda.ashoka.edu.in/full-version-are-manufacturing-jobs-growing-or-declining-heres-what-the-data-shows/)
* **IMF Working Paper (2021):**
 * Demonetization caused a **liquidity shock**, forcing low-income households to rely on **predatory lenders**.
 * *Source:* [IMF Study](https://www.imf.org/en/Publications/WP/Issues/2021/03/12/India-Demonetization-Through-the-Lens-of-New-Keynesian-Models-50168)

# 3. Credit Boom Without Income Growth: A Debt Trap

* **RBI Reports (2023-24):**
 * Household debt has **doubled** in a decade, now exceeding **40% of GDP**, driven by unsecured loans (credit cards, personal loans).
 * *Source:* [RBI Trend &amp; Progress Report](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/AnnualPublications.aspx?head=Report+on+Trend+and+Progress+of+Banking+in+India)
* **SBI Research (2023):**
 * Despite **high credit growth**, real wage growth remains **weak**, increasing default risks.
 * *Source:* [SBI Research](https://www.sbiresearch.in/)

# 4. Unemployment &amp; Predatory Lending Crisis

* **CMIE (2024):**
 * Unemployment remains **8-10%**, with youth joblessness at **alarming levels**.
 * *Source:* [CMIE Data](https://www.cmie.com/)
* **Livemint (2023):**
 * Low-income households increasingly rely on **high-interest loans** due to job losses and stagnant incomes.
 * *Source:* [Livemint Report](https://www.livemint.com/economy/indias-household-debt-crisis-is-worse-than-you-think-11674905123462.html)

# 5. Overstated GDP Growth &amp; Data Manipulation

* **Economic Analysts:**
 * India’s **slowest GDP growth** in decades occurred under Modi’s second term (pre-COVID avg. **\~4.5%**).
 * *Source:* [Time Magazine](https://time.com/)
* **Fraud in Public Accounts:**
 * **Withheld Consumption Data (2018):**
 * Leaked **Household Consumption Survey** showed **3.7% decline** in real MPCE (2011-12 to 2017-18).
 * *Source:* [MSE Report](https://www.mse.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/Ind-exp.-dec-13-Mind-the-statistics-gap.pdf)
 * **Manipulated MPCE Report (2023):**
 * Government **inflated data** by including freebies, making historical comparisons **misleading**.
 * *Source:* [The Wire](https://thewire.in/economy/modi-governments-record-on-data-is-marred-with-misestimations-and-delays)
 * **Actual Growth Just \~4%:**
 * Even after manipulation, **11-year MPCE growth** was **&lt;3% p.a.**, implying **real GDP growth of only \~4%**.
 * *Source:* [PIB Release](https://pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?relid=98126)
