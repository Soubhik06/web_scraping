This happened around May last year. I just remembered suddenly after reading similar post here, so sharing now. 
This is about a shop named "**bikerzclub**" near RK ashram metro station. Don't rent from those assholes! 
So the scene was, I along with my friend, rented a motorcycle (Royal enfield bullet) on May 3, 2022 for one day which had a rent of 750/day (as told by Rajeev from bikerzclub). We reached their office around noon and got the bike by paying 1750(1000 as security amount and 750 as rent) via UPI. We were told that the security amount would be returned in the evening when the bike would be returned. We returned the bike around 8.30 pm. After the documentation, Rahul from bikerzclub(They guy who gave us the bike in the afternoon, Rajeev wasn't on the shop then) told us that the bike would be checked for any challan and the security would be returned to the same account by next morning 11am i.e, May 4, 2022. Till today the security amount has not been returned. We are making calls to Rajeev and he says that they would make the transfer but haven't done yet. A few months back I was told that by mistake they have sent our amount to someone else, now they are saying that they have lost my bill. Haven't contacted them since, because now I knew I was scammed. 
Those MFs haven't returned our money yet. 
We were students back then and counted each penny before spending and getting ripped of 1000 is just like pocket money of 15 days gone. The shop was decent and they seemed to have enough money to not scam two students for 1000Rs. But what to do! 
I even complained in the [National consumer helpline (NCH)](https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/) but no help :'(
