If you google “Expedia change flight” you get a fake phone number, which actually appears on a “blog” page of this dentist’s website (www dot) firstpointdental (dot) com/blog/can-i-change-my-flight-on-expedia-for-free/#:~:text=Q:%20How%20do%20I%20change%20my%20reservation%20on%20Expedia?,booking%20according%20to%20your%20preferences. - telling you how to cancel your Expedia booking. Why on earth would that be on a dentists website? Turns out it’s a fake dentist. The whole point of the dental website is for that random page to (somehow) manipulate googles AI search results to push the phone number that the page spits out as the Expedia phone number.

I called the dentist, it’s just Indian scammers. And of course the Expedia number was also Indian scammers. My wife was in a rush before work to cancel her booking, so she called the number (which has since already changed) and they had her pay $200 to cancel her $900 booking. On the night of the reservation the hotel said she would be charged the full amount since she was a no show.
