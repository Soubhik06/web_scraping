(19.02.2020) 1. The issues under consideration arising out of the second appeal dated
10.04.2018 include non-receipt of the following information raised by the
appellant through his RTI application dated 17.01.2018 and first appeal dated
27.02.2018:- I, Region-I, Bhavnagar, seeking aforesaid information. The CPIO replied on
08.02.2018. Dissatisfied with the response of the CPIO, the appellant filed first
appeal dated 27.02.2018. The First Appellate Authority disposed of the first appeal
vide order dated 23.03.2018. Aggrieved by this, the appellant has filed a second
appeal dated 10.04.2018 before this Commission which is under consideration. 3. The appellant filed the instant appeal dated 10.04.2018 inter alia on the
grounds that the reply/information given by the CPIO was incomplete. The
appellant has requested the Commission to direct the CPIO to provide the complete
information immediately. 4. The CPIO vide letters dated 08.02.2018 and 27.02.2018 gave point-wise
reply to the RTI application. The FAA vide his order dated 23.03.2018 agreed with
the views taken by the CPIO. 5. The appellant remained absent and on behalf of the respondent Shri Manoj
Kumar Jha, Assistant General Manager, State Bank of India, Bhavnagar, attended
the hearing through video conference. 5.1. The respondent while defending their case inter alia submitted that they had
already provided point-wise reply/information to the appellant vide letter dated
08.02.2018 and 27.02.2018. They also stated that some of the information was
sought in the form of queries based on a situation which was also properly
responded by them. 6. The Commission after adverting to the facts and circumstances of the case,
hearing the respondent and perusal of records, notes that due reply has already
been provided by the respondent vide letter dated 08.02.2018 and 27.02.2018.
Further, the appellant was not present to controvert the averments made by the
respondent and further agitate the matter. Hence, the submissions of the respondent
are taken on record. The Commission further feels that there is no public interest in
further prolonging the matter. Accordingly the appeal is dismissed. Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties. Sd/- (Suresh Chandra) (सुरेश चं ा)
