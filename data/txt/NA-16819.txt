The company being investigated in the 2G scam was [Reliance Communications](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reliance_Communications).

&gt;Reliance Communications Ltd. (commonly called RCOM) is an Indian Internet access (commonly called "broadband") and telecommunications company headquartered in Navi Mumbai, India.

&gt;Established in 2004, it is a subsidiary of Reliance *Anil Dhirubhai Ambani* Group.

Ravishankar Prasad was on retainer with Fine Tech Corporation Private Limited, which is a subsidiary of [Reliance Industries](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reliance_Industries).

&gt;Reliance Industries Limited (RIL) is an Indian conglomerate holding company headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.

Whose Chairman and MD is [*Mukesh Ambani*](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mukesh_Ambani).

It's not even the same group of companies!

Edit: This is in response to AAP supporters bringing up Mr Prasad's tenure on the JPC investigating the 2G scam as proof of wrongdoing.
