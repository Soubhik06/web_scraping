This is majorly for Canada and the US 

About a week ago I thought I got the job of personal assistant at a company named Shebar Finanical Services LLC. 

 
NOTE \*this may be a real company in the US, a simple google search would confirm that, HOWEVER, the real Shebar at least from my POV is not doing Crypto\* 

At first, I was contacted by a woman named Taylor, now on our first phone call, she called from an Alberta area code. The phone call quality was let's say... horrible, I could barely hear this woman for the life of me however what she failed to hide was her Indian accent \*Red flag #1\*, now that is VERY rude to say, but the quality of the phone call and her name and the profile picture with the company, it didn't strike me as someone with an Indian descent at 1st. 

But we live in 2022, and I was not going to be an asshole so I decided to let that slide. 

Fast forward to today, they had me write a couple of letters and forge a document on a fund there doing for the homeless. 

My second red flag is I accidentally googled their web exchange instead of using the link they provided me and wasn't completely surprised that their website is broken (They suggested I bookmark it, so I did. Used to work for a call center and booked marked a TON of things) 

 [Shebar Financial Services – Shebar Financial Services](https://shebarfinancial.com/) &lt;--- Google link 
 [Shebar Financial Services – Shebar Financial Services](http://shebarfinancial.com/) &lt;---- There link 
For anyone wondering there, the link doesn't include the s in HTTPS. 

Fast forward today where I get a new \*Task\* 

My new task was to download a bitcoin wallet, cool done. 

then proceed to go to my bank withdraw 3000 bucks and put it into a bitcoin ATM. Ya no not happening, I've been personally involved with bitcoin for a while and the second I saw this request I was like no. 

However, unfortautntly these types of scams are still being done and people are desperate for a job they will comply with something like this. Just wanted to bring attention to it.
