Got to take a flight at the end of july for a family emergency. I'm in norway and family lives in mexico. In the urgency of the situation I used flightscanner and momondo. Ended up stumbling onto what i thought was a great deal. Absolute idiot :( booked, payed and confirmed the transaction on "travel2be". Immediately when i got the email confirmation something was off. The payment was confirmed but the tickets were not... ohshit.jpg. I got scammed. Checked reviews online. Total scam, why didn't I check before!? Lost 2k USD D: called their customer support and managed to get someone to answer after staying 40 min on the line. Not Indian tech support but still, such an obvious scam. Now my contact info and name are probably making their rounds through the darkweb for other scammers to pick up. Bank can't cancel transaction because it was manually approved. I'm fucked :(
