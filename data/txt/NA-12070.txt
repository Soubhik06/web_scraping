1. The present application is filed under Section 439 of the
Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973, for regular bail in connection
with FIR being C.R.NO. 11195003240067 of 2024 registered
with Amirgadh Police Station, Banaskantha for offence under Sections 406 , 420 , 465 , 467 , 468 , 471 and 114 of the Indian
Penal Code, 1860 (herein after referred to as the " IPC "). 2. Learned advocate appearing on behalf of the applicant
submits in present case, the investigation is over and charge-
sheet is filed. The offence alleged against the present applicant
are triable by the Court of Magistrate and having regard to the
punishment prescribed for the same, the applicant may be
enlarged on regular bail. He further submitted that the applicant
has been falsely implicated in the present offence since the

scam on a large scale involving the parcels of land belonging to

application. 4. Heard learned advocates for the parties and perused the
material available on record. From the record, it appears that the
present offence pertains to a scam of a sizable magnitude,
wherein various parcels of land belonging to government are
involved. The record indicates that the present applicant had
approached various persons, who were illiterate and had assured
them that he would get a parcel of land allotted to them and had
collected huge amounts from those persons, and thereafter, had
handed over them forged orders of Mamlatdar, Amirgadh
allotting various parcels of land in their favour. The present
applicant was also found in possession of various rubber stamps
in the name of Mamlatdar, Amirgadh. It is believed that the
present applicant had handed over such forged orders to at least
50 persons and had collected huge amounts from those illiterate
persons. NEUTRAL CITATION
