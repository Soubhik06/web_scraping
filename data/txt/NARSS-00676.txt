Dear all, 
Wanted to highlight a new scam that is happening on WhatsApp. It just happened on my number and I have mitigated it. So warning others of the same. 

You may receive a WhatsApp saying your number is being portrait to a different device. 

When when you open WhatsApp it will show a code. You will also receive a text sms.

Then someone you know will message you saying that they have accidentally entered your number and you should share the OTP with them so they can get access back to thier account.

The minute you do that all your old messages including potential bank details, photos and other secrets backuped on cloud would be downloaded on to the person's laptop.

And then starts the blackmail.

So beware my fellow citizens.
