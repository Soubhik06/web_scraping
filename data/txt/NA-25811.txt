I need some advice on this. I have taken loans from 2 to 3 apps, and my EMI is delayed by 20 days. Someone accessed my contacts and started calling everyone. They claim that I have taken a loan of 35k, but none of my loans are for that amount. I asked them about the app's name, but they insist on not telling me and instead started abusing me.

I used to have a good reputation in my family, but now I don't know what to do. Please help me!

Edit: So, this is a scam. I have spoken with the police (SI) and a lawyer. First, these scammers call and try to intimidate the victim. If the victim falls for it, they will send account details or UPI to pay some amount. One of my relatives went to his friend, who happened to be a police officer (SI), and he called them. Once he confirmed his identity, they hung up and stopped calling me and my relatives.

Here are a few things I have learned:

1. They only called contacts whom I saved with a relationship name like mom, dad, akka, bava.
2. Even if we didn't use loan apps, if we install any app that asks for contacts permission, we should not install that app.
3. If we receive any calls like this, we should ask for the app name, loan ID, EMI, and loan amount. In my case, they didn't reveal anything.
