(Order of this Court made by T.S.Sivagnanam,J.)

	 The petitioners who were the residents of Avadi had filed this writ petition designed as Public Interest Litigation and pray for a direction to forbear the second respondent from constructing a garbage dumping yard. 2.In the affidavit filed in support of the writ petition, the petitioners would state that the proposed garbage dumping yard is to be located in the midst of residential locality which is thickly populated and the public of that area agitated and conducted a road roko which was widely reported in the press. Earlier one Mr.Vijayakumar filed a writ petition in W.P.No.26929 of 2017 which was disposed of by the Division Bench of this Court by order dated 20.10.2017, directing the Commissioner, Avadi Municipality to consider the representation within a time frame. The petitioners' grievance is that in spite of such direction, the Municipality has not initiated any action. In any event, the allegation being nuisance to the public, in the fitness of things, it would be better to direct the first respondent to consider the representation and take a decision on merits and in accordance with law. 3.Accordingly, the writ petition is disposed of by directing the petitioners to submit a fresh representation to the first respondent along with a copy of this order as well as the earlier order which shall be considered by the first respondent on merits and in accordance with law within a period of two weeks from the date on which a fresh representation is submitted after issuing notice to the Commissioner, Avadi Municipality to take an appropriate decision in the matter. The first respondent/District Collector can depute one of his officials to cause inspection of the area and then take a decision. No costs. Consequently, connected miscellaneous petition is closed.
