Heard learned counsel for the parties. Having regard to the averments made in this writ Patna High Court CWJC No.8915 of 2013 (3) dt.18-07-2014 2 application that the petitioners have no concern, inasmuch as they

 are neither loanee nor mortgagee nor even guarantor and yet their

 land are sought to be put on auction sale, this Court having regard

 to pure question of fact would direct the Debts Recovery Tribunal

 to consider the grievance of the petitioners and pass a reasoned

 order on the claim of the petitioners. Learned counsel for the Bank has informed that earlier

 the same notice as contained in Annexure 11 has not been acted

 upon and a fresh valuation has been made, whereafter steps are

 likely to be taken for auction sale. Thus, if the petitioners file their application before the

 Debts Recovery Tribunal within a period of two weeks from

 today, the Tribunal will ensure that the final order on such

 application of the petitioners is passed in next two weeks and till

 then status quo, as existing today, shall be maintained. With the aforementioned observation and direction, this

 application is disposed of. (Mihir Kumar Jha, J)
