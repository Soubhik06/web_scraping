Seeking the Court to enlarge the petitioner/accused

No.2 in Crime No.200 of 2024 on the file of the Central Crime

Station, Hyderabad, on bail, the present Criminal Petition is

filed. The offences as alleged against the petitioner are under

Section 403, 406 and 420 read with Section 120-B of Indian

Penal Code (for short ' IPC '). 2. The brief facts of the case are that the complainant

alleges that DKZ Technologies/Dikaro Solutions Private

Limited, its directors (Ashfaq Rahil and Ighal), managers

(Umair, Moiz, Aslam, Nazeer, and Bilal), and employees

(Bhagya, Fatima, Sabiya, Sunita, Rubi, Khaled, Salman, and

Mohammed Abdul Waheed) cheated investors, including

himself, of approximately Rs. 25-100 crores. The company,

established in 2018, promised 8-12% monthly profits through

B2B and B2C businesses, partnerships with Amazon and

BOULT, and hired influencers to promote its legitimacy.

However, after consistent payouts, the July 2024 payment

was delayed, and the company cited NBFC certification 2 SKS,J Crl.P.No.13423 of 2024 processes as the reason. Investors suspect fraud after the

office suddenly closed, and phones went unanswered. The

complainant requests prompt action under Sections 403 , 406 , 420 , and 120-B IPC, citing investors' significant losses,

including Abul Jaish (Rs. 2.72 Cr), Md. Athaur Rahman (Rs.

1.74 Cr), and Syed Salma (Rs. 84 Lakhs). The case (Cr. No.

200/2024) has been registered and handed over to Inspector

E. Jahangeer Yadav, EOW.2. 3. Heard Sri Mohammad Imthiyaz Pasha, learned counsel

for petitioner and Sri Syed Yasan Manoon, learned Additional

Public Prosecutor, appearing for respondent. 4. Learned counsel for petitioner submitted that the

petitioner resigned from the post of director of Dikazo

Solutions Pvt. Ltd. on March 2024. He further submitted that

the petitioner is in judicial custody since 10.10.2024, causing

undue hardship to his family. He also submitted that

interrogation is completed and confession statement is

recorded, and further detention of the petitioner is

unnecessary. Therefore, he prayed the Court to grant bail to

the petitioner by allowing this criminal petition. 5. On the other hand, learned Additional Public Prosecutor

opposed the submissions made by the learned counsel for the 3 SKS,J Crl.P.No.13423 of 2024 petitioner stating that the investigation is not yet completed,

therefore, granting bail to the petitioner, at this stage, does

not arise. Hence, he prayed the Court to dismiss the criminal

petition. 6. Having regard to the submissions made by both the

parties, it is apparent that the petitioner is a voluntary

director of Dikazo Solutions Pvt. Ltd. and basing on his words,

number of the victims invested amounts. Later, recently the

petitioner resigned from the post of the director on 01st March,

2024. It is pertinent to note that major part of the

investigation is not yet completed. Hence, considering the

facts and circumstances of the case, this Court is of the

considered opinion that the petitioner is not entitled for the

bail and the Criminal petition is liable to be dismissed. 7. Accordingly, the Criminal petition is dismissed. Miscellaneous applications, if any pending, shall stand
