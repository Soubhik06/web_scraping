Beware

Refer image regarding the scam message

When you'll call the number it will appear busy with recording played in Bengali. Few minutes later a call will be made to you about the paytm kyc being expired and they want to do it online. The scammer will direct you to install a mirroring app and will try to steal you password and other info. He got a little suspicious and hanged up on me when i asked for details. If you want to know how these scams work, feel free to call. Please let others know, these guys prey on the elderly and people who are not aware of how phishing scams work.

Have fun, Bye!
