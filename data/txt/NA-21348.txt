I've noticed a lot of you discussing cyber crimes and private investigators lately. Just wanted to share some insights.

Firstly, private investigators with technical expertise are not really a widespread thing in India, except in matrimonial &amp; corporate scenarios. So, if someone claims to be one, take it with a grain of salt.

Lawyers often have contacts with individuals who handle these matters on a smaller scale. That might be your go-to option when needed.

For cyber crimes, after filing a complaint with the police, consider bringing in a cyber security expert, especially if a significant amount of money is involved. Police response can be slow, and retrieving your funds is not always guaranteed. An expert can help you understand and navigate the technical side of things.

Now, when it comes to issues like porn or extortion-related crimes, even if you're hesitant to involve the police, consider getting a lawyer and a cyber security professional on board. They can work together to address the problem discreetly and minimize the damage.

Stay safe out there!
