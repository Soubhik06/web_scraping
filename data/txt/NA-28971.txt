In 2025, India’s personal loan disbursements hit ₹5.3 lakh crore, growing 9% year-on-year according to CRIF High Mark data, with digital platforms handling over 70% of applications. Today, you can easily secure funds through your smartphones in hours, not even days. Understanding each step of the loan application process helps reduce delays, and minimise unnecessary charges.
Check Credit Score
A strong credit score opens doors to lower rates and higher limits. Lenders reject 40% of applications due to scores below 700, so verification comes first.
You can access your CIBIL score for free once a year through the official CIBIL website or mobile app. Many salaried borrowers find unexpected errors on their reports, such as payment defaults on old accounts or utility bills that were never updated. Before applying for a loan, review your credit report carefully for such discrepancies or for closed loans still shown as active. Correcting even a small error can significantly improve both your approval chances and the interest rate you are offered.

Determine Loan Amount and Tenure
Based on your requirements, understand how much you should borrow. Borrow only what you genuinely need, not what you are offered. Lenders may approve higher limits based on income, but excess borrowing increases long-term interest outgo.

Compare Lenders and Interest Rates
Interest rates on personal loans will vary across lenders. Online marketplaces like Bajaj Markets make comparison easier. Personal loan interest rates depend on credit score, employer profile, income stability, and existing liabilities.
While comparing lenders, look beyond headline rates. Check processing fees, prepayment charges, foreclosure rules, and interest calculation methods.
Also review lender reputation, customer service responsiveness, and transparency in communication. A smooth repayment experience matters as much as quick approval.

Step-by-Step Guide to Applying For a Personal Loan Online
The personal loan online application can be processed through the following steps:
Determine Funding Requirement:
First, you must evaluate how much you need to fulfill your funding needs.
Eligibility Check:
Across different lenders, assess your eligibility.
Shortlist a Lender:
Next, select a lender based on interest rates, loan amounts, tenure, and flexibility.
Visit Online Portal:
Visit the lender’s official online portal.
Fill Application:
Provide personal and employment details, specify the loan amount, and choose the loan purpose. Aadhaar‑based auto‑fill can streamline the process on many platforms.
Upload Required Documents:
You must attach necessary documents for your loan application by scanning them online.
eKYC Verification:
Verify your identity through Aadhaar OTP authentication and a short video KYC call. DigiLocker integration can automatically fetch documents where supported.
Sanction Letter:
A provisional sanction letter is typically issued within 2–24 hours via email, detailing the approved amount, interest rate, tenure, and terms. You can accept it digitally.
eNACH Setup:
Authorise automatic EMI deductions through an eNACH mandate. Once completed, the loan amount is usually disbursed on the same day or the next working day, depending on the terms and conditions.
Common Mistakes to Avoid
Applications may be rejected due to some pitfalls mentioned below:
Submitting multiple loan applications within a short span can trigger several hard enquiries, which may reduce your credit score by 10–20 points. It is advisable to space out applications by at least 30 days.
Hiding gaps like job switches raises red flags. You must disclose such gaps with explanation letters.
Always check processing fees. Ignoring may inflate costs. For example, a 2% processing fee on a ₹3 lakh loan amounts to ₹6,000 paid upfront.
Opting for the longest tenure may lower your EMI but significantly increases total interest outgo. Choose a tenure that balances affordability with overall interest payable.
Avoid choosing lenders solely based on fast disbursal. Speed should not replace clarity, transparency, and fair pricing.
Conclusion
Online personal loans transform urgent needs into structured repayments when approached methodically. Strong credit, precise amounts, and vigilant comparisons can potentially unlock best terms. Funds can be accessible swiftly, but discipline ensures they empower rather than burden finances long-term.
