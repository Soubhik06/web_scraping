Fraud Complaint Against ExporterIndia.com Subject: Fraudulent Business Practices and Fake Promises by ExporterIndia.com I would like to file a formal complaint against ExporterIndia.com, an online B2B website that claims to offer export and buyer connection services. The company and its employees made several false promises regarding guaranteed business leads, verified buyers, and export opportunities after purchasing their package. After payment, no genuine leads or services were provided. Their representatives refuse to respond properly, give misleading information, and keep making excuses. It is clear that both the website and the employees are running a fraudulent operation by making fake commitments to collect money from innocent businesses. ExporterIndia.com appears to be a scam website, and I strongly advise everyone not to purchase any package or service from them. The employees are even more deceptive than the company itself — their behavior and communication show complete dishonesty. I request the concerned authorities to investigate this company for fraud, fake promises, and deceptive business practices. All victims of similar scams should also report this website to the National Cyber Crime Portal (https://cybercrime.gov.in) and Consumer Helpline (https://consumerhelpline.gov.in).
