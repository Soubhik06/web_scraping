It's 00:40 here in the UK and I'm up late because it's my day off tomorrow. I just tried to log into Vinted and Vinted requested a login code, but I didn't notice it because my phone immediately began ringing from an 020 London number.

The call itself was flagged as a suspected spam caller on my phone so I ignored it, but a call this late at night in the UK? I figured it was originating from India or something and just blocked the number. At this point I realised that the login code for my Vinted login had expired, so I requested a new one. My phone immediately begins ringing again, this time from a new 020 number.

Again, the number is flagged as spam, so I ignored it, but then I noticed that I'd received a voicemail on my phone. Curious, I listened to it. It was a pre-recorded voice reading out a 4-digit login code following by "*This code expires in 5 minutes*", and I immediately know what's up.

The comments on its Who Called Me page largely indicate that it is a scam call with the caller claiming to be from **Vinted trying to deliver a fake login code**. Somebody is attempting to gain access to my account. They know what my mobile number is, and they know the exact times that I attempted to log into the account on both occasions.

Can anybody explain to me how this is possible? I consider myself pretty internet savvy, I grew up on the internet and have never fallen for a scam in my life, but I can't wrap my head around how this has occurred. How can scammers have known the exact time I tried to log into Vinted, and how have they been able to gain knowledge of the number tied to my Vinted account? Does this indicated that Vinted is selling user's personal data, and what does this mean for my security?
