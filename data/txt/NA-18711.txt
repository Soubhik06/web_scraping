Got a phone call from someone claiming to be from a major insurance company. They said I had signed up for a scheme in 2010 and paid only the initial premium, after which no annual premiums were paid. The amount has currently more than doubled, but I "cannot claim it" and it will go into "government fund" unless I pay 2x the premium amount "for 2011 and 2012".. and then I will apparently be able to immediately withdraw almost 5x the original amount.

Obviously this seems like a scam. The call came from a mobile number. The same number sent an SMS and told me to contact "branch manager" with another mobile number, and to keep in touch directly not through a call centre. The SMS was in all caps with really bad spelling. 

The insurance company's website did not let me log in with the quoted policy number and DOB, saying it did not match their records. I don't know how to find out if such a policy exists or if I can cross-reference it.

Why would such a big company not contact a policy holder for 10 years if they are not paying premiums? Other companies start hounding with calls and messages months before the payment date. Also my family takes policies together for all of us, and only I got this call. None of us have any memory or documentation of any history with this company. Why are they asking only for two years of premiums and not the entire policy period?

I do not have any record of paying this premium in 2010 (but maybe someone else took it out in my name?). 

So this seems like an obvious scam. However the caller had my full name and lots of personal info. Is there any chance this is genuine?
