 Civil Application. 3. Mr. Atul Sharma, learned counsel for the respondent NSE
 submits that under the Rules and Regulations of the NSE, there
 is no provision to re-constitute the Tribunal in case of a
 remand. He submits that the respondent NSE is only a facilitator
 for adjudicating the disputes. He, therefore, submits that an
 appropriate order may be passed. 4. Considered the submissions on behalf of both the parties
 and perused the documents on record. It is admitted that the
 dispute initiated by the petitioner on 07.10.2021 is not yet
 adjudicated. The award of the Arbitral Tribunal so constituted to
 adjudicate the dispute had rejected the claim of the petitioner
 holding it to be time barred as beyond the period of 4 years. That
 thereafter in an appeal to the Appellate Tribunal, the said award
 has been set aside and it has been directed that the parties to
 avail remedy in accordance with law. It has also been further
 directed by the award dated 26.08.2022 that the respondent
 NSE shall take further steps in the matter in accordance with
 law. Since the dispute raised by the petitioner remains
 unadjudicated and the petitioner has invoked adjudicatory
 mechanism of the respondent NSE, it is directed that the
 respondent NSE shall constitute a fresh Arbitral Tribunal to
 adjudicate the claim of the petitioner within a period of 4 weeks
 from the date of receipt of this order. Such Arbitral Tribunal
 shall decide the dispute on its own merits and in accordance
 with law within a period of 6 months from the date of 1 st hearing
 of such arbitral proceedings. Since the award dated 06.07.2022
 passed by the Appellate Tribunal has become final and there is a
 direction to the respondent NSE to consider the refund of fees
 paid by the appellant, the decision on the same shall be taken by
 the respondent NSE within a period of 4 weeks from the date of
 receipt of this order. The writ petition is allowed to the aforesaid extent. No
 order as to costs. (ANIRUDDHA P. MAYEE, J.)
