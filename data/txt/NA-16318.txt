--- POST TEXT ---

I had ordered a pair of shoes for my dad on 7th march. On 8th afternoon, I received a call from someone claiming to be from Amazon. 

He knew my registered name, phone number, address. And he asked me if I knew about the offer on the product. I asked him, which product? And he knew the exact brand and details of the shoes I had purchased. 

Earlier it had happened as well, but it was after delivery so I thought local delivery agency might be selling data. But this time it was immediately after ordering. 

Basically they were saying I've won a draw and can chose from three items. I won't have to pay delivery but will have to buy another item worth 5k from Amazon. 

Not surprisingly, they asked to transfer the amount via UPI. 

So...I know if my mom or some family member had received such call, it would be easy for them to believe that it's from Amazon. And they won't be wrong to do so. 

There's definitely a leak from Amazon's side, but I feel they're not taking it seriously. Even after an extensive phonecall, they dismissed that data leak is possible from their side. 

Even complained to cyber cell and the dsp also dismissed it as something like my ID must've been hacked or something. 

These people cannot make money on such small amount of data. Unless they have 100s of data sets, it's not feasible. 

Anyways, just FYI. Try and inform every one you can about such scams and why one shouldn't fall for it. 

I had warned my staff about this but even then one fell for it. 

Easy money is too tempting for most people. Hence actively reiterating that it's all a scam.
