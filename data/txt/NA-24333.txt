This is an awareness post for those in the already annoying process of house hunting. 

There was a listing in 99 acres (still active) of a 2bhk with attractive rent and location and was unsold since 2 weeks (already a red flag) 

When i contacted them the "owner" immediately picked up saying it was available etc.
He said he is working out of town in the Indian army he also sent some fake id card and random photo.

When i said i would like to visit he said there would be a visitor card since its a gated society and i would need to pay a registration fee off 2000 which would be refunded later. I immediately called bullshit and asked why he scams people out of their hard earned money like that. He cussed for a bit and cut the call.

If you're house hunting, don't pay a single rupee for anything or to anyone before you actually visit and sign a contract.
