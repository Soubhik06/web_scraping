--- POST TEXT ---

On July 21 , I ordered a few products on amazon using gift cards. After redeeming gift card in my amazon account, i got few notifications saying "someone is trying to login using your mail id" in amazon app which I eventually denied.

But the next day (July 22) morning, I received notifications/mails saying all my orders are cancelled and refund is processed through the same gift card. When I tried to login into my account, it showed that there is no account associated with the mail id or number. But continued receiving mails about declined transactions on unknown websites including GOIBIBO

When I tried contacting amazon customer service, event they responded the same. After some time, I received a mail from amazon stating that there is some suspicious activity and that i have to reset my password. Till that time i had my mail id associated with the amazon account. After few hrs the hacker has changed my mail id too.

**3 days later……..**

my account was recovered but when i checked into the transactions, i noticed 2 unauthorised transactions on some other third-party website which was paid through my amazon wallet balance. Before this, even though the support team has temporarily blocked my account - out of all 4 attempts first 3 transactions were declined. whereas fourth transaction was approved since it is a third-party website.

Finally, after multiple follow up with customer care, my account was recovered and able to login into it.

**Backstory of the infiltration:**

There is a group of people targeting other’s amazon account with pre-loaded amazon pay wallet (through vouchers/Gift Cards/ UPI) and changing login details of the account (still not sure how they did it). They use this balance to pay some random insurance premiums, loan repayment, or use it as a third-party payment method in some other platform. 
 
I‘ve registered a cyber-crime complaint with some details of his name, mail id, mobile number, insurance policy account and loan account that infiltrator had left in my amazon pay account. All accounts route to different names and number.

**What to do if it happens to you?**

In case if this type of incident happens to you or your friends, please do this

1. Report incident immediately to [ofm@amazon.in](mailto:ofm@amazon.in) or [amazonpay-grievance-officer@amazonpay.in](mailto:amazonpay-grievance-officer@amazonpay.in) or [ecr@amazon.in](mailto:ecr@amazon.in) or [investigate@amazon.in](mailto:investigate@amazon.in)
2. Call 1800-1200-9009 or 1800-1200-1637,

* Incase both your mail or mobile has been changed, manually authenticate your account by telling your saved addresses or Order Ids.
* Ask the support agent to redirect you to Account Specialist team and request them to temporarily block your account
* After investigating the activity, your compromised mail id will be restored back. (This may take 24hrs to 72hrs). Keep following up with customer support (no other way).

Please enable 2 factor authentication for your accounts if at all you use any kind of wallet. Also be cautious of the websites that you use to buy your digital gift cards.
