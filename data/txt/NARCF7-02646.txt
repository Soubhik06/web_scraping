We just wanted to understand what is the real estate scene across Thane(broker websites weren't helping much) and visited one of the ongoing projects with the ad - Stop Thane on Tuesday. It was just 510 sq. ft for 2 bhk and the project was to be handed over in dec 2027( Their 1 bhk project will be done by dec 2025). We overall liked the project and it's amenities, but felt the house was small for our needs. Sales guy called me on Thursday, I told him that it is not suiting our needs and we are looking for a bigger house and thanked him for his services.

Today I received calls from 5 different builders advertising the building, all the calls were during the nap time.
The first question I asked them is how you got my number and all gave me the same answer, that you visited a site and we got it from them.

Simliar thing happened with KIA showroom in Manpada which made us buy the car from Powai showroom instead.

Data privacy is really a joke in India and working in Data protection sector, it really hurts how PII is misused to such a level.

In future, if am going to visit any such builders, will end up giving them a fake number or a worst enemy's number(even they shouldn't be put through this).
