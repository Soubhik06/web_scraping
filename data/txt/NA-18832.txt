CBDT Chairman basically said that if one buys in Crypto they have pay TDS on it..

Not only that if one receives it as gift they have to pay Tax on the market price of that Digital Asset (which might be around 30-35%) right away even if that person don't liquidate/sell that gift!!

In stock/securities perspective it is like saying Capital Gain Tax before selling your assets (just for holding them?)

Is govt trying to make Dealing in Digital Decentralized Assets so unattractive indirectly that people would refrain from transacting in them?

I know the prices of Bitcoin and Similar Crypto-Currencies are at absurd high levels. Also the Tx Charges on Blockchain are so high that low value transactions (say in BTC) are impossible. In short they are becoming more like an discretionary asset (if you can call it that since it neither has intrinsic value nor it can be realible store of value because of extreme volatility) and less of a Currency.

Also there are many conmen trying to fool laymen in India with ponzi schemes in Cryptos and NFTs etc..

But the issue here is Tax (il)logic of the Govt.

1) If govt wants to receive Tax before sell of Digital Asset by receiver/holder at current market price, then is Govt going to recoup losses too?

2) If govt want Tax before sell and that too at market price and in INR, then why not just accept paying Tax in Crypto itself?
Otherwise for the sake of tax payment one need to sell the asset itself which will flood the supply and drive down the prices near ITR Deadline.

What this all situation clearly highlight is desperation of Govt for Revenue.
