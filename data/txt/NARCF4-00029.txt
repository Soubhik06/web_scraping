I swear, this has to be one of the most frustrating things that’s ever happened to me. Went to refuel my vehicle at a filling station in CSpur area today, used one of those self-service bays where you scan the QR code, pay online, and the machine dispenses fuel accordingly. Simple, right? Yeah, NO. 
So I scanned the QR code and paid ₹500. All good. But apparently, some dude ahead of me pulled a fast one. This guy tells the attendant that he paid ₹500—when in reality, that was my payment! Meanwhile, I’m standing there wearing my helmet, completely unaware of what’s happening. By the time I get to my turn, the machine only shows ₹100 worth of fuel left for me. I obviously questioned the attendant, who looked just as confused as I was. Then it all clicked—this random guy had actually paid only ₹100 but claimed to have paid ₹500, and the clueless attendant just let him take my share of fuel. So this guy basically scammed his way into a free ₹400 worth of petrol at my expense.
I was already running late for the office, and honestly, I didn’t have the time or energy to argue about it. So I just accepted my fate and moved on. But damn, this whole thing still pisses me off. It ain't a huge amount, but these silly scams frustrate me a lot. It’s not about the cash—it’s about the sheer audacity of people who pull this crap and get away with it.

Has this happened to anyone else?!
