In December of 2017 I had a tonsillectomy and sinus reduction surgery. I was on my family's insurance at the time because I was in between college and my new job and they had already met their deductible. It went through our insurance, Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield at the time just fine. Our surgeon's office made sure to check that all parts of the surgery were in network before we went through with it. In April I received a call from a Palm Beach, Florida number. Maureen, a lady who sounds like she's in her 50's or 60's, asks for my dad's name. I let her know that this is his son, u/ziggyakeebu. She says she's from Acc-Q-Data and spouts off some insurance stuff that I didn't really understand so I give her my Mom's number because I'm a millennial and she has handled these kinds of things in the past. My mom gave them our insurance info and that was that.

Fast forward to July and I get a call from 516-612-7025, a Palm Beach, Florida number. I don't pick up since I'm at work and they leave the following message:

&gt;Yes u/ziggyakeebu this is Maureen from Acc-Q-Data. The reason I'm calling is because you had surgery on 12/26/2017 at (name of surgery center) and I had talked to you back in April you were gonna contact your parents to get me the correct insurance information so I could bill this claim because the initial information we had, the policy had been canceled. So can you please give me a call regarding this. My number is 555-555-5555 extension 555. Thank you.

I decided not to call back since something about the whole thing seemed a little fishy. Yesterday I was called again (same number???) and they asked for my dad's name again. I let them know that this is his son, u/ziggyakeebu, again and that I had already shared these details with them before. They said, "oh we don't see any of your parents information in our notes." I let them know that my mom would again be the best person to talk to so I shared her number.

When they talked to my mom they said that we owe some $1400 dollars on the equipment that was used to constrict my legs and keep my blood pressure up during the surgery (sorry I forget what those straps with the air pumped into them are called). They said it was out of network and that's why we owe the money. They said that once they file it, the insurance company will send us a check which we can sign and then send to them.

I then started doing my own internet investigation of their company and wanted to share what I've found with you and see whether you all can confirm my suspicions that this is a scam.
