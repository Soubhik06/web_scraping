and many more which I haven't linked here, are very much visually similar to original BHIM app launched by National Payments Corporation of India. And can be easily confused with original app, so people might end up giving up their important financial info to these app. Some of these apps might be harmful right now and some of these may not, but it's a matter of time before fraudulent things start happening through apps from playstore. And more over these apps are coming in top results when you search for BHIM app on playstore. 

So please help me in reporting these apps and others which can be confused with original BHIM app. It's not necessary to get them blocked, just convince playstore people to let developers know to change the logo and visual identity of the app so that they aren't confused with the original one.

https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2853570?visit_id=0-636192105100952098-3227449997&amp;p=report_content&amp;rd=1
