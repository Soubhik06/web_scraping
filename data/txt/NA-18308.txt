Young girls and boys posing as social workers and stuff from Delhi and apparently working as volunteers at child relief NGOs have been calling my number for at least the last seven months asking for donations. I know they're fake, and was glad to see this was [discussed on Reddit in relation to National Child Relief Services NGO five months back](https://www.reddit.com/r/india/comments/5og4s9/got_a_call_from_person_claiming_to_be_volunteer/), where most people called it fake.

But the guy who called me today from http://www.nationalcrs.org/ tried to make me feel guilty about doubting their legitimacy, and for refusing care to a child. I couldn't roll my eyes far back enough. These pricks need to be exposed already, and discussing on Reddit or putting it on complaint forums isn't enough. 

Has anyone looked into the address they mention on their website? -- 1160, Ram Nagar colony, N.I.T Faridabad, pincode-121006. The guy today said: "Yes, you can come visit and please come with school books, you'll find us." 

Who's backing these jerks? And, I mean, I can understanding setting up a website and stuff and putting up fake photos of needy kids, but do they actually have a fake office too? What's a way to dig deeper into this? Ideas please! 

Would be great to hear especially from people familiar with how NGOs are supposed to be set up and the rules and regulations they are supposed to comply with.
