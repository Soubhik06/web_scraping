So I have been using an Airtel number for the last 10 years. Purchased a new phone this May and duly got a new offer with that phone. A double data recharge offer. Just to confirm it, I called up their unintelligent customer care and asked the details. Nonchalantly, they said that I will get a double data offer for any recharge over the value of 250. Happily, I got my first recharge done on 19th June (1 GB, 3G pack) and duly got the double data benefit. As everyone knows, their data packs are now valid only for 28 days. At the end of the period, with my data validity expiring, I did another recharge, this time on 16th July (2 GB, 3G/4G pack). Lo and behold, I didn't get any benefit!

Fast forward to this day, after umpteen number of calls, dealing with their useless, unintelligent and misinformed customer care executives, and hearing out a plethora of invalid reasons, they actually deactivated my double data offer. The reason given is that I did not recharge it *in continuity* and hence I cannot avail this offer anymore. And just to please the customer, they offered 200 bucks as a refund, because of *goodwill* and miscommunication. 

So, in short, they saved the data for the next 6 months (the actual time I received for the offer) for just 200 bucks. Nice way to scam folks, yeah, Airtel!
