This is first bail application filed under Section 439 CrPC on behalf of

 applicant in connection with Crime No.232/2022, registered at Police Station
 Govindpura, District Bhopal (M.P.), for the offences punishable under Sections
 389 , 365 and 201 read with 120-B of Indian Penal Code . Learned counsel appearing for the applicant submitted that applicant has
 not been named in the F.I.R. He went to police station to supply meals to
 accused persons and he has also been falsely implicated in the case. It is
 submitted that applicant is a young man of 21 years. Investigation is complete
 and charge sheet has been filed, therefore, applicant be enlarged on bail. Learned Govt. Advocate appearing for the respondent/State opposed the
 prayer for grant of bail. It is submitted that applicant alongwith other family
 members are committing organized crime and they have extorted money by
 threatening the complainant that they will implicate him in case of rape.
 Relatives of applicant called the complainant on his mobile number and had
 striked friendly talks with complainant. Thereafter when she was going in car
 alongwith complainant, photographs and videos were made and systematic
 extortion was being made from complainant. It is further submitted that offence
 is punishable with Life Imprisonment. It is also submitted that bail application
 of co-accused persons has also been dismissed. Considering the nature of

 offence and amount which is involved in the case, bail application may be
 rejected. Heard the learned counsel for the parties. Considering the facts and circumstances of the case, bail application filed
 by applicant is dismissed. (VISHAL DHAGAT)
 JUDGE
