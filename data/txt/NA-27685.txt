So I met a person who claims to send candidates to foreign countries (UK, Europe, Dubai, etc) for job. He runs a recruitment firm for that. I feel it is a scam based on my observations and conversation with this guy, but I would like to know your thoughts and inputs. 
 
Why I feel it is a scam :

1. The process goes like this --&gt; First submit all identification docs, then 2 rounds ( assessment and personal interview)

2. The job role - Senior Market Study Analyst does not exist on the JP job portal

3. They charges a fee for this referral (They have someone in the director level whose referral would be leveraged)

4. The role provides free accommodation, 1 time meal and pick and drop --&gt; Too good to be true

5. For a sales role in Amsterdam, (part of their current vacancy) the company they mentioned is not a registered sponsor. When asked about 30% tax ruling, they had no idea about it. (Dutch govt provides a scheme for internationals, where 30% of your salary is tax free)

6. The role in Amsterdam also provides accommodation - Twin sharing. But regardless, having lived in NL for a year, I know that housing there is super difficult to get and that no company provides free/subsidised housing (At least to my knowledge)

7. About the documents, they wanted full length photo of the candidate (me), which is a bizarre requirement, imo. 

 
Overall, I think it is too good to be true and I know that if something feels that way, it probably is. I dont want miss an opportunity due to my consciousness and might be wrong too. Which is why I ask you guys for your thoughts. Thank you so much in advance and I appreciate your inputs.
