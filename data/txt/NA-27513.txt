I want to point out a couple things. One, I could see the same active chat on both websites. Two, the icon of "Lin" was a generic smiley face. And three, the fishiest part of all this, is that when I had talked to him, his name was "Nate." And, according to the chat records, he was the same person who talked to me in December, under the name "Nina." I called the number, but didn't say anything. I heard the voice, and it was either someone with an Indian accent or someone faking one. I am trying to get to the bottom of this, and I will update you with more info soon.
