I was enjoying myself with my gf at a theme park this weekend when i got a very convincing call from us bank regarding a fraudulent charge for $600 in texas. I am in ohio so i said it wasnt me. Then they ask the usual questions, which i stopped answering when they wanted my full ssn lol. They had my email, phone, address, only weird thing was that in order to stop the charge, i needed to repeat a text code back to them. You know, the one that says in the message specifically to not repeat it to anyone contacting you. Course the indian said that im supposed to share it with him but nobody else. Cause duh right? Obviously i didnt give it to him, just used him to pass time in line for a rollercoaster mostly, but i thought it was a pretty believable scam if it were an older person. If you have any older family who use us bank, might be worth telling them about this.

The next day after locking my cards, i tried logging back in and suprise suprise, my account was locked due to too many login attempts. Wonder why. Filthy indian scam centers is why. Theyre getting clearer and even use the first 3 digits a lot of us bank centers use. 

If these people call you make sure to lead them on for as long as possible, then start insulting them in any way you see fit. Theyre scammers, nothing is below them besides violent criminals. 

Us bank will never ask you to repeat a 6 dogit code. Fraud dept, if you call them, will ask you for debit number, last 4 of ssn and maybe one more number, but theyll never ask you to repeat a code. Those are only for password resets i bekieve.
