This petition is filed by the petitioners-accused Nos.1 to 3

under Section 482 of Cr.P.C. for quashing the FIR in Crime

No.96/2022 registered by Malleshwaram Police Station,

Bengaluru for the offences punishable under Sections 384 , 386 , 389 , 420 , 497, 506(B) read with Section 34 of IPC. 2. Heard the arguments of learned counsel petitioners,

learned High Court Government Pleader for the respondent

No.1-State and learned counsel for respondent No.2. 3. The case of the prosecution is that respondent No.2

filed a complaint on 15.07.2022 alleging that the petitioners

and respondent No.2 are family friends from long back.

Respondent No.2 and his wife are said to be an architects and

builders. While constructing the house, he came in contact with

petitioner No.1 and thereafter they continued their relationship

by telephonically. Later the petitioner said to be shifted to

Hyderabad and thereafter they continued to speak in the

telephone and whenever she requires some financial help, she

used to receive money Rs.2,00,000/- to Rs.3,00,000/- from the

respondent No.2 as and when she required as her husband said -4- NC: 2024:KHC:8917 CRL.P No. 7528 of 2022 to be not getting any income and he was a drunkard. The

petitioner No.2 is the daughter of petitioner No.1, she also

sought help for getting admission in a good pre-university

college, where the respondent denied earlier, later he used to

pay the money for the purpose of education. Subsequently the

petitioner No.1 also obtained Rs.25,00,000/- from respondent

No.2 for sending petitioner No.2 for abroad for education

purpose which was paid through online. Thereafter, once again

petitioner No.1 used to demand money from respondent No.2

as and when required, he has continuously paid and later it is

also revealed in the complaint that the petitioner No.1 used to

come to Bengaluru and used to ask respondent No.2 to reserve

a room at five star Hotel and accordingly, he used to stay and

both of them were stayed together and received money from

the complainant and went back. Therefore, she said to be

demanded Rs.1.5 crores for the purpose of marriage of the

petitioner No.2 and this fact came to be know by the

respondent's family, thereafter, the petitioner No.1 said to be

blocked by the respondent No.2 and demanded Rs.50,00,000/-

and she said to be recorded some conversation held between

them and if the amount is not paid, she would reveal the same -5- NC: 2024:KHC:8917 CRL.P No. 7528 of 2022 to the public. Therefore, the complaint came to be filed. Being

aggrieved with the FIR, the petitioners are before this Court. 4. The learned counsel for the petitioners has

contended that petitioners obtained financial help from

respondent No.2 and repaid the same. In spite of the same, the

respondent No.2 troubled the petitioners' family and when the

marriage of petitioner No.2 was proposed, the family members

of respondent No.2 came and troubled and the marriage

proposal was also broken down. The respondent damaged the

reputation of the petitioner. Therefore, a complaint was filed to

the Commissioner of Police. A case was registered in Crime

No.69/2022 against respondent No.2, now pending in the

Malleshwaram Police for investigation. There was CCTV footage,

everything was given to the Police and civil suit also filed

seeking damage from respondent No.2, Hence, prayed for

quashing the FIR. 5. Per contra, learned High Court Government Pleader

as well as learned counsel for respondent No.2 objected the

petition and contended that the matter requires for

investigation. The act of the petitioner No.1 is nothing but -6- NC: 2024:KHC:8917 CRL.P No. 7528 of 2022 honey trap and blackmailing the respondent No.2 for

demanding money and hence, she has received more than

Rs.2.5 crores from the respondent No.2 and by making the

videograph and mobile conversation, they blackmailed

respondent No.2 and accepted money from respondent No.2.

Therefore, prayed for dismissing the petition. 6. Having heard the arguments and on perusal of the

records and on perusal of the lengthy complaint, the gist of the

complaint reveals, petitioner No.1 came in contact with

respondent No.2 while constructing the building-an apartment

where the friend of the petitioner No.1 one Vimala was

introduced to petitioner No.1 and they also sought help for the

education and respondent No.2 said to be paid money and

continuously the respondent No.2 paid amount up to Rs.2.5

crores. Of course, the petitioner counsel submits that it is

financial help for the purpose of education and it was repaid to

the respondent No.2, but, the petitioner also filed a complaint

against respondent No.2 and the case was registered against

him in Crime No.69/2022 for various offences. She also filed

suit for defamation and damages in a Civil Court. On the other

hand, respondent No.2 complains that honey trap has been -7- NC: 2024:KHC:8917 CRL.P No. 7528 of 2022 made by the petitioner for extorting money and more than

Rs.2.00 crores have been extracted. Considering the facts and

circumstances of the case, I am of the view, whether the

petitioner received only a financial help or she was trying to

extract the money by recording the conversation between them

and nothing but he is in honey trap or not required to be

investigated by the police. Therefore, I am of the view, there is

cognizable case made out against the petitioners for

investigating the matter at this stage and the Police also

registered another case against respondent No.2 on the

complaint filed by the petitioners to the Commissioner of Police.

Such being the case, both the matters should be investigated

by the Police and at this stage, FIR cannot be quashed. 7. Accordingly, the petition is dismissed.
