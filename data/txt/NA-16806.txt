I see that Boycotting facebook is not a practical thing for most of our friends. 

*What's Free basics about?*
Facebook is promoting free basics only so that they can collect enough data on Indian market BEFORE Google so that they can sell you more-targeted ads after a year or so when Indians get access to the real internet. 

They are masquerading it as philanthropy and they are portraying the net neutrality activists as villains who're trying to take away the something that the poor would get. 

We need to educate people on why Free Basics is such a scam and I believe a #BoycottFacebook campaign would be best way to achieve that level of awareness. 

I see many of you asking others to stop using Facebook altogether. While that may be practical for us redditors, it might not be very practical for many of our *less saavy* friends who rely on their facebook feed for their daily giggles and gossips. 

Facebook makes money not from your selfies, memes, gossips, rants but from selling targeted ads. There's an option in their settings which you can use to disable targeted-ads. 

 settings --&gt; adverts --&gt; 'No' for the first option and 'No one' for the second. 

While this WILL NOT prevent them from collecting data on you, if enough of us Indians can disable targeted ads, no rational business would want to list their business on facebook if it wants to target Indians, thus killing it's intentions completely. The data they're collecting will be useless as it cannot be used to sell ads to us, their most lucrative users 

If we can get enough international media attention on this campaign, their stock prices will fall and they'll be forced to withdraw and refrain from any such thing in the future. 

I don't think this is a very far fetched goal as it might sound, considering the impact those ["Honest Free basics ads"](https://imgur.com/a/ltOfM) from /u/snorlaxusedrest made. 

TL;DR: You don't need to disable your facebook account, You can simply turn off the targeted ads, make your friends do the same, get media attention and make FB withdraw.
