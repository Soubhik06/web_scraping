Like I said, I thought I was paying Rs 50k (added the 1 later) for an iPhone 5S, iPhone 6, iPad 4 and Macbook Pro (Non Retina) but ended up getting just a brick in a bag. I feel stupid and screwed up, can't talk about this with friends so hello internet.

[EDIT]

Lot of people were getting confused so I think ill post a detailed synopsis of the event.

I was just pulling into my office in the morning when I was approached by two people right next to my car offering to sell me iPhones which were seized at customs, I told them to wait outside while I park my car. I returned outside and he took out an iPhone 6, 5S, iPad 4 and an Macbook Pro from his bag, the iPhone's had their box and all with them. I confirmed everything was working, and then confirmed their serial numbers and warranty status online. Then googled for the serial to make sure it was not a stolen thing.

Post that he started asking for 50k for everything together, insisting that he needs to quickly leave and pay off the customs guy, and that he deals in customs seized goods. I said fine, keep the things here at my office security gate and me and a college will go with you to an ATM and hand you the money. In this time I was more worried that he will try and pull a fast one on me at the ATM so I wanted to keep the goods and then give him the cash. We went to the ATM and I withdraw the money and hand it to him, he starts asking for more money I tell him that I cannot withdraw more money as the daily limit is Rs 50k and show him that I just have 1k in my wallet (which he took). He even started eyeing my watch and then I got pissed and told him that take the money or take your stuff back. He then said he will meet me back at office someday when he has more things and fucked off.

In the meanwhile I asked a colleague to pick up the bag and bring it inside office (was worried that he would try and go to the office watchman and claim that he left his bag there and take it back). I got back to office a little while later and saw the bag latch was locked, pried it open to find a big brick inside. 

Me and my college who were with me were very surprised because he was with us the entire time, guess his accomplice switched the contents of the bag, or had another bag which they passed onto us. And since we had just seen the contents moments before he handed the bag to us I never thought about opening it before keeping it with the watchman. So yeah, thats what happened. I was more worried that I might get shot or something but this definitely beats that. I guess I should not feel bad about this, since I was greedy. Just want to make sure others know of the scam.
