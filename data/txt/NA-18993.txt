The one topic that has piqued everyone’s interest is the Harshad Mehta Scam. Before Vijay Mallya, Nirav Modi, and Mehul Chowksi, it was this very man who took the world by surprise with the biggest financial scam in the Indian Stock Market in the year 1992. 

Two projects revolving around Harshad Mehta are all set to enthrall the audience. On one hand we have Abhishek Bachchan playing the ‘The Big Bull’ in a biopic and on the other we have National Award-winning filmmaker Hansal Mehta recreating everything that went down in his web-series called ‘Scam 1992’. It is now streaming on Sony Liv. The Abhishek Bachchan movie is out on Oct 23.

Which one did you like better?
