Ltd(Which is a legitimate Indian company, the scammer is merely pretending to be them.) Ten minutes after I applied, they emailed me telling me that I have an interview in the morning via Google Hangouts chat, no camera or voice.

They said that they will provide all the money needed for me to buy office equipment via a check.

"*The funds for the softwares and office equipment will be provided for you by the company via check..Make sure you use them as instructed for the software and i will refer you to the vendor you are to purchase them from okay.
