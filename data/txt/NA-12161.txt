1. The instant petition has been preferred by petitioners under Section
482 of the Cr.P.C. seeking quashment of FIR registered at Crime 2 No.21/2022 at Police Station Dinara District Shivpuri for offence
punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 469 , 490 , 471 of IPC and
25/27 of the Arms Act . 2. Through this petition, petitioners sought following relief:- 3. It is the submission of learned counsel for the petitioners that they
have been falsely implicated in the case. As per contents of the FIR, on
17.01.2022, Sub-Instpector, Police Station-Dinara District Shivpuri,
received information and searched/raided the premises where three
persons were involved in preparation of forged Adhar Cards. They were
taken into custody and from the possession of petitioner No.1 Kaushal
Singh 39 Adhar Cards and 3 mobile phones, from petitioner No.2 Satish
Tomar, 1 Desi Lodaded Katta, 27 Adhar Cards 70 Sims Cards and photos
and addresses of different unknown persons and from Mithun Jha, one
laptop, one printer two data cables and one mobile charger were seized.
These persons were selling forged Adhar Cards and Sim Cards to 3 different persons. 4. As submitted by learned counsel for petitioners, present petitioner
No.1, Kaushal Singh Bais and petitioner No.2 Satish Tomar were
implicated in which name of petitioner No.1 is referred by Satish Tomar,
in his memorandum under Section 27 of the Evidence Act. From the
possession of present petitioner No.1, one mobile of Realme Company,
one mobile of Vivo Company and one mobile of Mi Company, 39 Adhar
Cards were found. Some of the prosecution witnesses have already been
examined and especially seizure witnesses have not supported the story of
prosecution. Alternatively, learned counsel submitted that vide orders
dated 12.12.2023, applications preferred by State of Madhya Pradesh
under Section 439(2) of Cr.P.C. were allowed and bail of petitioners was
recalled vide M.Cr.C. Nos.43144/2023 and M.Cr.C. No.43152/2023 in
respect of petitioner No.1 and 2 respectively. On these grounds, learned
counsel for petitioners prayed that petitioners may be given liberty not to
surrender and police may inquire from them whenever required. 5. Learned counsel for respondent/State opposed the prayer and
submitted that it is the case where hundreds of forged Adhar Cards and
Sim Cards have been recovered. Those Sims cards have been sold to the
different persons in Haryana, Delhi and in other States. Further
Investigation is still going on and incidentally one co-accused Pramod
destroyed the Adhar Cards and Sim Cards where were found in his
possession just to make the case difficult for investigating officer to
complete the chain. Many prosecution witnesses are yet to be examined
and this is not a case which can invoke jurisdiction under Section 482 of 4 the Cr.P.C . 6. Heard counsel for the rival parties and perused the documents
appended thereto. 7. This is a case where allegations against the petitioners/accused
as well as other co-accused are serious in nature. Some of the co-
accused used to prepare forged Adhar Cards and those Adhar Cards
were sold to different persons and on the basis of those Adhar Cards,
Sim Cards were taken by the accused persons from service provider
and those Sim Cards were sold to different persons of different States.
Particulars of Adhar Cards/Sim Cards/Articles seized from possession
of different accused persons are as under: Mithun Jha : Dell Lap Top, Printer, Data Cable, one
 Katta and one Black Charger. Himanshu Sharma: 30 Adhar Cards, 13 Sim Cards, 2 Mobile
 Phones and Rs.6,000/-. 8. Independent role of the petitioners/accused may vary but
collectively their role appear to be challenging and having wider
ramifications in respect of national security and cyber crime. These 5 Adhar Cards and Sim Cards can be used in NDPS offences, Terrorism
 related Activities, Cyber Frauds for kidnapping and ransom purposes
 and for offence of grievous denominations. 9. At present, further investigation in respect of some other co-
 accused persons is still going on. Some of the prosecution witnesses have
 been examined and some of them may have deposed in a different
 fashion, but that aspect is required to be seen by the trial court holistically
 after hearing both the parties finally. This case has its sub-layers to the
 extent where Anti-National/Anti-Social Activities and its wider
 ramifications cannot be ruled out. Under exceptional jurisdiction vested
 under Section 482 of the Cr.P.C., this is not a fit case for interference.
 Trial shall unfold the truth. 10. In the cumulative analysis, petition sans marit is hereby dismissed. 11. A copy of this order be sent to the trial Court concerned for
 compliance and information. Certified copy as per rules. (ANAND PATHAK)
 JUDGE
