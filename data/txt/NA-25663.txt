Hello recently i have seen a jump in People falling for online scams, once transaction is done you have only few ways to get your money back. 

1. Approach home branch asap, as my friends work in backend of private banks, banks they have the ability to stop the transaction and raise a dispute. This worked for some connected people.

2. Head to https://cybercrime.gov.in within 6hrs, raise a complaint, this blocks the transaction chain by blocking the the account of beneficiary. After online complaint you will get a call from the police station you have given in complaint.

-Police will take a written complaint from you with all relevant details. You can always check your complaint status in portal. If you are lucky (time constraint) you will get your money through court in 6 months of time.

3. Credit card charge back dispute resolution. 

4. UPI Dispute redressal mechanism - through payment operator like phonepe. 

Pro tips.

- Lock your Aadhar biometrics.

- Don't share OTP with anyone, check for words like Authorization, Payment before proceeding to sharing otp.

- Don't share any personal information over phone. 

- Identity cloning is real, SM profiles are being cloned, so never trust a message, always confirm by calling before sending money.

- Always check the deceptive banking pages and its url. 

-Use multiple bank accounts one for day to day transactions and keep the other one isolated and secured.

- Never use windows for logging into internet banking. Always check for 🔒 ssl connection.
