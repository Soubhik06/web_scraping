Hi all,

I had created profile in Monster and Naukri. I got a call from Lady who was speaking in Hindi and some broken English(Red flag 1) about job offers. 

Lady : I am from Shine(.)com. We have an offer for you in IT

Me: I never made a profile on shine. What kind of offer in IT?

Lady : Shine, Monster and Naukri are all interlinked. We 3 companies share our profiles. Software Development in IT.

Me: Which Language?

Lady: IT

Me: IT is not a language

Lady: HTML, C++ Java etc

Me: ok which company

Lady: HCL and many others. Then she went on and tried to pretend that the offer was genuine and after 3mins of blabbering about job came to the point of depositing Rs 1850 for interview because they are answerable to companies regarding Candidates. If Candidate does not attend interview or gets better package somewhere else. We will send you email with bank details and we will contact via Gmail and not website or apps as Gmail is so secure.

Me: I cannot deposit any money. (I knew it was fake she talked like she had no clue what she was talking about).

Lady :Why don't you want to pay? etc 

I was shocked how they have all my information. So just beware of these job offers who ask you to pay. If you have lost your job or are looking for one never pay any amount to anyone. In any condition. Data theft is real. I wonder if Shine(.)com is real culprit or not. But this is so shocking. It's so heart breaking to get calls like these a people play on your hopes of getting jobs when you are looking for one. 
Stay safe
