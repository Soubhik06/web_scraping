 The Sub-Inspector of Police, Jayanagar Police Station,

Bengaluru has filed Police Report against the above named

accused persons alleging that they have committed the

offences punishable under Sections 420 , 465 and 468 R/w Section 120B of the Indian Penal Code. 2. It is the case of the Prosecution that, the Manager of

Syndicate Bank has filed this information before the

Jayanagara Police Station that the accused availed loan for

the purchase of residential site bearing No.1118/4 from 3 C.C.No.21047/2013 B.K.Shani. It is said that the said B.K.Shani was the owner of

the property. The Agreement of Sale was executed between

the parties on 24.08.2006 and also the Sale Deed was

registered in the name of accused on 29.08.2006. This

accused has taken loan of Rs.30,00,000/- by executing all

the relevant documents. The accused and the surety agreed

to pay the loan. Later the accused has not paid the loan and

the loan was termed as NPA. The Bank has issued notice to

the accused, but the accused has not repaid the loan. The

Bank verified the records in respect of the said property

wherein they have found that the accused has taken loan by

furnishing fabricated documents. The property was sold by

the BDA in the name of B.K.Shani but however the Sale Deed

executed in favour of the accused by alleged B.K.Shani was

created and forged his signature. The accused even has

introduced one person by name B.K.Shani to open the Bank

Account. The outstanding amount was of Rs.43,78,661/- on

the date of issuing of Notice dated 31.10.2009. The accused

along with the surety K.Kamalesh Rao entered into a criminal

conspiracy to cheat the Bank and fraudulently taken loan of 4 C.C.No.21047/2013 Rs.30,00,000/-. Soon after the incident, the Manager of the

Bank had been to the Police Station and lodged the

information against the accused. 3. Based on the complaint, the crime was registered in

Crime No.96/2010 at Jayanagar Police Station. The accused

persons were arrested and produced before this Court and

enlarged on bail. Since the presence of accused No.2 and 3

could not be secured, the case against them was ordered for

Split Up as per order dated 27.06.2017. Accused No.4 was

reported dead. Hence, as per order dated 19.12.2019, the

case against him was Abated. On completion of the

investigation, the Police Inspector of Jayanagar Police

Station, Bengaluru City filed Police Report against accused

No.1 alleging that he has committed the offences punishable

under Sections 420 , 465 and 468 R/w Section 120B of the

Indian Penal Code. After taking cognizance of the said

offences, the process was issued to him. The copies of the

Police Report and other prosecution papers are furnished to

accused No.1 under Section 207 of Cr.P.C. After hearing, 5 C.C.No.21047/2013 since there were grounds for presuming that accused No.1

has committed the offences triable by this Court, charges for

the offences punishable under Sections 420 , 465 and 468 R/w Section 120B of the Indian Penal Code has been framed

and read over to him in Kannada language. He has pleaded

not guilty and claims to be tried. 4. To prove the charges framed against the accused

persons, the Prosecution has produced the oral evidences of

PW1 to PW6 and the documentary evidences in Ex.P1 and

Ex.P2. After completion of the Prosecution evidences, for the

purpose of enabling accused No.1 personally to explain any

circumstances appearing in the evidences of the Prosecution

against him, examined accused No.1 under Section 313 of

Cr.P.C. He has submitted that he has no defense evidence. 5. Heard the arguments of learned Senior Assistant Public

Prosecutor and the learned counsel for accused No.1.

Perused the materials available on record. 6. The points for determination are; 1. Whether the Prosecution has proved beyond all
 reasonable doubts that accused No.1 has
 committed the offences punishable under Sections 420 , 465 and 468 R/w Section 120B of the Indian Penal Code? 2. What order or sentence? 7. My answers to the above points are as follows: 8. POINT No.1 :- It is the case of the Prosecution this

accused without any title deed and all the accused in

colluding with each other by furnishing forged and fabricated

documents obtained a loan of Rs.30,00,000/- and has not

repaid the loan. 9. To prove the guilt of the accused, the Prosecution has

examined CW1/PW1 by name K.Divakaran, wherein he has

deposed that he was the Manager of the Syndicate Bank. This

accused has taken loan of Rs.30,00,000/- for the 7 C.C.No.21047/2013 construction of his building. Accused No.2 is the surety to the

said loan. Accused No.1 and 2 have furnished that the

accused No.3 is the owner to the property. Later, the accused

has not repaid the loan and they have found that the

signature of the original owner and the signature in the

document submitted by the accused is totally different. He

has created the document to obtain the loan. They have

cheated the Bank by forging the documents. 10. To prove the guilt of the accused, the Prosecution has

examined CW2/PW2 Suresh who has deposed that he has

issued Legal opinion to the Bank after verification of all the

documents to release the loan amount in the name of

accused No.1. Later, he came to know that the accused has

forged the document. 11. To prove the guilt of the accused, the Prosecution has

examined CW3/PW3 who is a Retired Engineer who has

deposed that as per the request of the Bank, he had been to

the site No.118/A and issued valuation Certificate. 8 C.C.No.21047/2013 12. To prove the guilt of the accused, the Prosecution has

examined CW9/PW4 Krishna Kumar who has deposed about

the registration of FIR. 13. To prove the guilt of the accused, the Prosecution has

examined CW4/PW6 Savitha Lakshmi has deposed about the

investigation conducted by her. 14. It is the case of the Prosecution that this accused No.1

for the purchase of Site No.118/A has taken loan of

Rs.30,00,000/- from the Bank. The accused has produced

the Agreement of Sale and Sale Deed at the time of taking the

loan from the Bank. It is the case of the Prosecution that the

said property belongs to B.K.Shani and the signature in the

Sale Deed produced by the accused is not at all the signature

of B.K.Shani. B.K.Shani was the owner of the property. But

however this accused by creating and forging the document

availed loan of Rs.30,00,000/- and has not repaid. 15. The Prosecution has relied the evidence of CW1/PW1

who is the informant and argued that this CW1 has taken 9 C.C.No.21047/2013 loan of Rs.30,00,000/- by introducing accused No.3 by name

Shani as owner to the property. But infact, the signature in

the original Sale Deed standing in the name of B.K.Shani was

forged by this accused No.1 to 3 in the Sale Deed alleged to

be executed in the name of accused No.1. The original owner

B.K.Shani has not at all signed the Sale Deed of accused

No.1. The Prosecution has also relied the evidence of CW2

who is the Retired Chief Manager of the said Bank has

deposed that after verification of all the documents he has

released the amount in favour of this accused No.1. The

evidence of PW1 and PW2 is corroborated with the evidence of

PW3 who has deposed that he has verified all the records and

valued the property to sanction the loan in favour of accused

No.1. 16. I have carefully perused the entire documents and have

found that the main argument of the Prosecution that the

property was standing in the name of B.K.Shani and this

accused No.3 who was introduced by the accused No.1 as

B.K.Shani to the Bank is not at all the original owner. But 10 C.C.No.21047/2013 however the Investigation Officer has not at all taken any

pain to send the said documents to FSL to confirm whether

the Sale Deed produced by the accused No.1 to the Bank at

the time of taking loan was forged and created by him. The

Prosecution has produced the Sale Deed. On perusal of the

Sale Deed it looks that the Sale Deed was standing in the

name of B.K.Shani. It is also the case of the Prosecution that

this accused No.1 has not repaid the loan and committed

fraud to the Bank. But, the main argument of the learned

Senior APP that the accused No.1 has introduced accused

No.3 as owner to the property. The Bank cannot claim the

said property as it was not standing in the name of accused

No.3. Further, I also carefully perused the evidence of CW2

wherein he has deposed that he has verified all the

documents at the time of sanctioning of loan to accused No.1.

When such being the case, how this Prosecution is claiming

that the accused has forged the documents for availing the

loan. Further, CW4 has also deposed that he has valued the

property by personally going through all the documents and

had been to the said property. The Prosecution even have not 11 C.C.No.21047/2013 proved that this accused has furnished false and forged

documents to avail the loan. Further, this matter is pending

since 2013 and the accused is regular to the Court. The only

evidence available before this Court is the evidence of CW1 to

CW3. But, to corroborate the evidence of CW1 to CW3, there

are no evidence to prove the guilt of the accused beyond all

reasonable doubts. The Prosecution in spite of issuing NBW

and even proclamation is unable to secure the witnesses. The

accused is also a Senior citizen and accordingly this Court

finds that the Prosecution failed to prove the guilt of the

accused beyond all reasonable doubts. Hence, I answer Point

No.1 in the Negative. 17. POINT No.2 :- For the reasons stated in Point No.1, the

prosecution has not proved the guilt of accused No.1 for the

offences punishable under Sections 420 , 465 and 468 R/w Section 120B of the Indian Penal Code beyond all reasonable

doubts. Therefore, accused No.1 is not found guilty for the

offences punishable under Sections 420 , 465 and 468 R/w 12 C.C.No.21047/2013 Section 120B of the Indian Penal Code. In the result, I

proceed to pass the following:- His bail bonds and surety bonds
 executed will be canceled after appeal
