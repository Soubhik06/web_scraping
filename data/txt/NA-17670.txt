I could not reply to all your comments and support, but I have read each and everyone of them. I sat up all night yesterday and finally decided to man up. This morning, I called him and asked T to meet me.

He met me, but T was a different person altogether. The first question he asked me was about what my decision to convert was. I gave him a long long pep talk about how he can't see that religion is destroying friendship. A long long friendship. He told me (again) that he wants us to be friends and he wants me to be "alive" and hence is safeguarding me by asking me to convert. He told me I will understand why he is doing what he is doing after some years. All he did was ignore the huge peptalk and ask me if I am ready to convert or no and the reason he gave was that he can not see his best friend die.

I was furious that he is telling this. I went and told this to my mom and my mom told this to my uncle. I spoke to T and while on the call, I made him say everything he told me face to face. I told him I'd get back to him by the evening. My uncle approached the cops and they agreed to help. But, I did not want a complaint on his name as that would ruin his future. He is a bright student and tops the class mostly. The cops called him to the police station and they called me there too.

Initially, he tried to keep himself away from this and told the cops I was lying. But when I showed him the proof, he accepted it. The cops threatened him with an arrest warrant because where I live, it is a Hindu-dominated area and any conversion bullshit is not tolerated. He confessed everything. Here is a gist of what he told :

&gt;He was a fairly religious person but not as religious as he is now. He believed in science first, religion then. 6-7 guys from the mosque he visits, knew about my friendship with him and they were furious about this. They also knew he was smart. They told him a fake plan about how the Hindu people will be dominated by the Muslim in 3-4 years and that Allah has written this and that they are planning to execute their mission soon. They also offered him money, offered to sponsor his education and they were basically ready to give him whatever he asks if he converts people to Islam. He declined this offer. But, He wanted to get me converted because in case the guys were telling the truth, he wanted my family and me to be safe.

The 6 guys from the Mosque were called by the cops and the same procedure repeated. All the 6 guys twisted the story fully and told the cops that it was T who initiated all these things and that T was planning something huge. T was not able to find any proof against the 6 people because all the interactions they had were face to face and never on social media. T's parents were called. T's mom immediately began crying and telling the cops about how she knew her son was at fault, but T's dad did not even regret it. He had the guts to say that he would never like his son to be friends with people who murder Muslims and that it is a part of their religion to spread their religion. He also told my family that if we could not accept their religion's ways, we had no right to keep contact. (Irony).

So, end of the story, T's dad decided it was because of me (idk how) that T turned out to be like this and that T should not talk to me ever. Yes, it hurts. But it feels good that I told my mom everything. I've never opened up so much to anybody. I felt that much connection with my mom. My mom appeared to be my best friend and nobody else mattered/matters.

I would like to extend my thanks to everybody who supported me with wonderful comments. I might not have replied to all, but I have surely read all the comments and the sub-comments too. I'm consulting a psychiatrist for help to forget all this and lead a new life. I love you guys! You all will be my best friends! :)

Thanks.
