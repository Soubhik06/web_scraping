A government which allegedly is all about performance, speed and governance was not able to appoint these key constitutional authorities in last 1 year. So the BJP government has actually spent 20% of it's tenure without any vigilance authorities watching over it.

Also, government has silently amended Prevention of corruption act so that now CBI has to take govt's permission to prosecute even retired public servants. Plus, the criminal misconduct part of it which stopped public servants from taking decisions benefiting a particular entity (Think allotting land to a particular business ) has been diluted saying it will be considered criminal only if the officer was directly taking money for it (This leaves out nepotism + most of the bribe now a days is given through convoluted means, i.e. distant family members etc.)

So, Transparency is receding, laws are being diluted. How do we know corruption is not happening in government level, for all that we know country is still scam ridden and not we cant even know about it.

PS: Only constitutional authority working today is CAG, which in his first report has targeted union transport minister's company

PPS: This government came on the promise of corruption free India.
