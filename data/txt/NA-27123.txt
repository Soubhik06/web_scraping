--- POST TEXT ---

Someone on reddit helped me build a parts list for a custom pc I want to build. As thanks for his services, I sent an electronic gift card to him on Amazon and I found it strange that I haven't gotten a notice that the person I was sending it to received it. About an hour later, I got a phone number from someone claiming to be Amazon. It was a woman with an Indian accent that was calling to confirm that I made an Amazon purchase. Please note that I only added my phone number to this amazon account 5 minutes prior to making the order. I was suspicious so I asked her to tell me how much the order was for and the email it was sent to. She told me the correct info. After I told her I made the order, she said "ok thank you for your time" then hung up. Immediately after I got a confirmation email that the person received the gift card. I was still suspicious so I checked the number online. Reviews of the number showed that it was a scam phone number. I immediately deleted my card info on Amazon, and changed my passwords for both my Amazon account and bank account. Is there anything else I should do to protect myself?
