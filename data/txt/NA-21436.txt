Times have evolved, and so have scammers!

In India, there is a relatively new scam that is doing rounds in the news. This is how it happens:

1. The scammer will pretend to be a woman on dating apps such as Tinder, Bumble, Hinge, etc and wait for matches. 
2. Once matched, the scammer will proceed towards building trust and talking to you like a genuine woman on these apps would.
3. Slowly, the conversation will turn intimate, and the scammer will ask for your Instagram and if you can talk on a video call.
4. If you say yes, you will receive a video call from the scammer, where he plays an objectionable video of a naked woman as if she is the one on call.
5. As soon as your face is visible on the screen, the scammer starts recording the screen.
6. Before you realize it, you have been recorded on an objectionable video call watching a naked woman!
7. The scammers will now threaten to leak your video to your contacts on social media unless you pay up a good sum of money to them.
8. Once you pay, the extortion will continue.

There are not many legal recourses available, as these video calls are made from remote areas of the country, where enforcement is not feasible (see: Jamtara). However, these are the best practices to follow:

1. Never pick up video calls from unknown numbers. Always verify their identity first.
2. If you have been a victim of such a scam, contact the nearest cyber crime branch. They will facilitate the removal of those accounts which are spreading/leaking the fake video.
3. Don't pay up. If you pay once, they will ask for more.

Stay careful!
