I'm getting sick of these scammers calling me, pretending to be from FedEx! They keep claiming there's some parcel sent from Mumbai to Taiwan with illegal stuff in it. Even after blocking their numbers, they just call from new ones. I have no idea how to stop this madness.

I finally bought Truecaller Premium just to get some peace. And here's the kicker – one scammer actually admitted it was a scam, promised to delete my name from their database, and then didn't do it! Seriously, what's it going to take to make this stop?
