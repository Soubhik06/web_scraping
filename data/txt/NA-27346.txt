Here's a list of **10 well-known cases** where **phone scammers** (via calls, SMS, or voice phishing) **stole users' personal data or funds**, often leading to widespread losses and high-profile investigations:

# 1. IRS Scam (USA, 2013–2018)

Scammers impersonated IRS agents, threatening victims with arrest over unpaid taxes.

* **Tactic**: Voice phishing (vishing)
* **Victims**: Over 15,000 reported, mostly elderly
* **Losses**: $36 million
* **Notable**: Run from call centers in India; many arrests were made internationally.

# 2. Microsoft Tech Support Scam (Ongoing)

Victims receive calls from someone pretending to be Microsoft, claiming their computer is infected.

* **Tactic**: Fake tech support via phone
* **Victims**: Hundreds of thousands globally
* **Losses**: Millions annually
* **Notable**: Victims are tricked into giving remote access to their devices.

# 3. Wangiri Scam (Japan-originated, global)

Victims receive a missed call and, when they return it, are charged high international rates.

* **Tactic**: Missed call scam
* **Victims**: Thousands in the UK, US, and Asia
* **Notable**: "Wangiri" means "one ring and cut" in Japanese.

# 4. SIM Swap Scam – Jack Dorsey (Twitter CEO, 2019)

Attackers hijacked Dorsey's phone number via SIM swap, gaining access to his Twitter account.

* **Tactic**: Social engineering and SIM swapping
* **Impact**: Verified Twitter account used for scams
* **Notable**: Highlighted vulnerability in telecom systems.

# 5. The "Can You Hear Me?" Scam (US, 2017)

Victims are tricked into saying "yes" on a recorded line, which is later used to authorize charges.

* **Tactic**: Call recording manipulation
* **Victims**: Thousands reported
* **Notable**: Exploited human reflexes in phone interactions.

# 6. Mumbai Call Center Scam (2016)

Impersonated US officials demanding money for fake legal issues.

* **Tactic**: Impersonation and threats
* **Victims**: Over 6,400 in the US.
* **Losses**: Estimated $300 million
* **Notable**: One of the largest vishing scams ever prosecuted.

# 7. Santander Bank Scam (UK, 2023)

Fraudsters impersonated bank security staff and tricked users into approving transactions.

* **Tactic**: Spoofed caller ID and social engineering
* **Victims**: Thousands in the UK
* **Notable**: Callers had accurate personal and banking details.

# 8. HMRC Phone Scam (UK, 2018)

Callers pretended to be UK tax authorities, threatening arrest for unpaid taxes.

* **Tactic**: Threatening calls
* **Victims**: 60,000 reports in one year
* **Notable**: Prompted a nationwide warning by the UK's HMRC.

# 9. "Bank of America" Voice Scam (US, 2021)

Users received automated calls mimicking BoA's fraud department, asking for login details.

* **Tactic**: Phone spoofing + fake verification
* **Victims**: Thousands reported
* **Notable**: Combined phone and phishing tactics.

# 10. Crypto Investors SIM Swap Attacks (2018–2021)

High-profile investors and influencers lost millions via phone number hijacking.

* **Tactic**: SIM swapping to bypass 2FA
* **Victims**: Dozens, including YouTuber Ian Balina
* **Losses**: Over $100 million in combined cases
* **Notable**: Popular among criminals targeting crypto wallets.
