• The ED initiated its probe into the Mahadev Book App scandal when Shubham Soni hosted an extravagant wedding valued at approximately Rs 250 crore in the UAE this February.

• Soni and his accomplice had a classic rags-to-riches journey before being apprehended.

&amp;#x200B;

The Mahadev betting app scam has resurfaced in the news after the government issued blocking orders against 22 illicit betting apps and websites, including Mahadev Book Online and Reddyannaprestopro. These restrictions coincide with an ongoing investigation led by the ED into the Mahadev betting app case.

&amp;#x200B;

Recently, the ED filed its initial chargesheet in the Mahadev app betting case, accusing app promoters Shubham Soni and Prem Soni. The central agency alleges that the app operated by franchising 'panel/branches' to known associates at a 70-30 profit ratio. The chargesheet claims that the firm utilized the online betting app to recruit new users, generate IDs, and launder money through a network of benami accounts.

&amp;#x200B;

The proprietors of the Mahadev Book app, arrested under Section 19 of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), are currently in custody, facing charges for the offense of money laundering under section 3, punishable under section 4 of PMLA, 2002.

&amp;#x200B;

Despite the current incarceration of the Mahadev betting app owners, Shubham Soni and his associate Prem Soni had a conventional rags-to-riches journey before their apprehension. Here's a glimpse into Shubham Soni, his partner in crime Prem Soni, their connection to the D-Gang, and the Mahadev betting app fraud.

&amp;#x200B;

About Shubham Soni and his ‘Man Friday’ Prem Soni

Shubham Soni, originally from an ordinary family, operated Mahadev Juice Centre in Chhattisgarh's Bhilai until 2018. During that period, he engaged in betting using online apps, incurring losses of around Rs 10-15 lakh. His close friend and accomplice, Prem Soni, also from a modest background, held odd jobs and similarly lost around Rs 10 lakh through gambling.

&amp;#x200B;

In Dubai, the duo undertook odd jobs, pooling funds to launch the Mahadev Book App, named after Chandrakar's Mahadev Juice Centre in Chhattisgarh's Bhilai. Developed by Indian and European coders, the app debuted during the pandemic lockdown in 2020. The business expanded significantly, with Soni boasting around 2,000 centers.

&amp;#x200B;

How was the Fraud Conducted?

The Mahadev app's software was initially developed in Hyderabad, followed by further contributions from foreign developers. Soni and Prem traveled from Hyderabad to Bhilaspur, where IDs and software created in Hyderabad were provided to panel operators for betting activities.

&amp;#x200B;

Subsequently, the betting activity was presented in a video game format, depicting winnings as points rather than money to obfuscate the betting nature, according to the Enforcement Directorate's chargesheet in the case. Users were later sent a link to claim their winnings.
