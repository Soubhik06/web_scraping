 dispensing with the notice on the private respondent. 3. It is the case of the petitioner that he is engaged in painting works and

 has three daughters. In 2020, he came across a newspaper advertisement

 regarding a job opportunity offered by one Mahalingam. He subsequently met

 him, and Mahalingam informed him that a private job was available in Aadi

 Global Private Limited. To secure the job, Mahalingam demanded a sum of

 Rs.50,000/-, which the petitioner paid, believing his representations. 4. After six months, Mahalingam introduced the 3rd respondent to the

 petitioner, stating that he was an influential person who could arrange a Central

 Government job, demanding Rs.3,00,000/- as an initial amount. Believing the

 same, the petitioner paid a total sum of Rs.8,00,000/- to the 3rd respondent. 5. Further, the 3rd respondent informed the petitioner that there were

 additional vacancies and requested him to introduce his relatives and friends

 who were interested in Central Government jobs. Believing this, the petitioner

 introduced 13 persons between 18.06.2021 and 31.12.2022, and the 3rd

 respondent collected a total sum of Rs.1,62,10,876/- from them. 2/5 https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis ( Uploaded on: 16/12/2025 06:28:24 pm ) W.P.Crl.(MD)No.2357 of 2025 6. After it came to light that the appointment orders received by them

 were fake and forged, it was discovered that the 3rd respondent is involved in a

 job scam and several criminal cases had already been registered against him. In

 this regard, a complaint was lodged before the 1st and 2nd respondents on

 19.11.2025. However, no action has been taken till date. Hence, the present

 petition. 7. Mr.A.S.Abul Kalaam Azad, learned Government Advocate (Criminal

 Side), on instructions, submitted that they have not received any complaint in

 this regard. 8. Heard the learned counsel on either side, and have perused the

 materials available on record. 9. Without going into the merits of this case, this Court hereby directs the

 petitioner to submit a fresh complaint before the 1st and 2nd respondents. On

 receipt of the same, the 1st and 2nd respondents are directed to consider the

 petitioner's complaint and deal with in accordance with law and conclude the

 enquiry within a period of two (2) weeks from the date of receipt of a copy of

 this order. 3/5 https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis ( Uploaded on: 16/12/2025 06:28:24 pm ) W.P.Crl.(MD)No.2357 of 2025 10. With the above directions, this Writ Petition is disposed of.
