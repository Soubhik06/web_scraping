 provisions of law germane for the adjudication of present

 same to be stolen property, shall be punished with
 imprisonment of either description for a term which
 may extend to three years, or with fine, or with both. 13.To better appreciate the controversy at hand, the facts of the case
 to be briefly recapitulate it. It is the case of the Complainant that
 on 8th of February 2013, he had found his mobile phone stolen from
 his godown . It is also the case of the Complainant that he had some
 mobile tracking application installed in his mobile phone pursuant
 to which 4th of May 2013, he received a message that his mobile
 phone has been activated through SIM No. - 8239776628.
 Thereafter on 16th of May 2013, he received the message that is
 mobile was activated on another SIM number 9983569947 He also
 states that he received the missed call from the mobile number
 9772965375 on his mobile phone. It is pertinent to note that despite
 the theft having been committed allegedly on 8th of February 2013, Cr. Case No. 426914/2016 State vs. Untrace(Ramesh) Page 5 of 9 the Complainant made the first complaint to the police authorities
 on 4th of May 2013, i.e., after considerable delay of around 2
 months, unexplained by him throughout the proceedings. 14.Investigation was commenced but an Untrace report was filed
 wherein it was noted that after due enquiries from the telecom
 authorities for any SIM activation under the above said to IMEI
 numbers 911148850214839 and 911148850214847, belonging to
 the mobile phone of the Complainant for the period in question, all
 the mobile phone companies had reported NIL, i.e., no SIM
 activation had taken place under the two IMEIs during the said
 period. However, interestingly, on a perusal of the CDR of the
 mobile number of the Accused, Ramesh, i.e., 8239776628 it was
 seen that the said SIM card was found to have been activated under
 one of the IMEI belonging to the mobile phone of the Complainant,
 i.e., IMEI - 91114885021484(71). Noticing this ambiguity in the
 two reports, i.e., the CDR on one hand and SIM Activation on the
 other, the Ld. Predecessor Court had, on 18th of March 2017, that
 further investigation into the matter pursuant to which the instant
 chargesheet came to be filed against the present Accused, Ramesh. 15.A perusal of the instant chargesheet reveals that under the garb of
 further investigation, nothing material has been done by the police
 authorities but only to trace out, interrogate and arraign the present
 Accused on the basis of only an alleged SIM activation appearing
 on the CDR supplied by the Vodafone. No investigation was done
 with respect to the other corresponding SIM activations reported
 by the Complainant nor any effort was made to procure the CDR
 of the other two mobile numbers, i.e., 9983569947 and 1 The last digit of IMEI was considered as reserved/spare digit Cr. Case No. 426914/2016 State vs. Untrace(Ramesh) Page 6 of 9 9772965375 and it appears that either due to inadvertence or by
 way of design, CDR for the wrong periods was procured for the
 said two mobile numbers - for reasons best known to the
 investigating agency. 16. Needless to say, the initial ambiguity that existed and which
 formed basis of filing the Untrace report, i.e., on the one hand the
 telecom operators reporting NIL SIM activation under the two
 IMEI in the relevant period when the Complainant stated that he
 had received messages on his mobile number regarding SIM
 activation and on the other hand, the CDR of the Accused Ramesh
 showing SIM activation under one of the said the IMEI number
 belonging to the mobile phone of the Complainant. 17.It is a matter of record that the said stolen mobile phone was never
 recovered in the present case and the Accused has been arraigned
 as the receiver of stolen property only on the basis of one SIM
 activation which is occurring on 4th of May 2013 and having
 reference to the IMEI number of the stolen mobile phone. There is
 absolutely nothing on record to explain as to how the SIM
 activation under the IMEI number belonging to the Complainant is
 appearing on the CDR of the mobile number of the Accused
 whereas the mobile phone operators when called upon for reporting
 all India SIM activation for the same period, have reporting NIL
 activation, i.e., no SIM was activated under each of the said IMEIs
 during the period when the mobile remained stolen and beyond the
 possession of the Complainant. This very fact is sufficient to secure
 an acquittal to the Accused in the present case on the ground that
 when on the basis of the evidence, two views are possible, the view
 favouring Accused has to be taken. 18.There is also another factor which doubts the case of the
 Complainant and that is the unexplained delay on the part of the
 Complainant in reporting the theft and the non-reflection/non-
 collection of the alleged screenshot/messages of the mobile
 application which had informed the Complainant about SIM
 activation in his stolen mobile phone, which was a crucial piece of
 link evidence and the absence of which, in addition to the
 unexplained delay in reporting leaves the possibility open that the
 Complainant could have, in fact, sold the mobile phone to the
 Accused or any other person and thereafter, reported it to be stolen
 for reasons best known to him only. 19.Even if this case is examined from the standpoint that the IMEI
 number and SIM activation of the Accused as borne out from the
 CDR be taken as gospel truth, still, the CDR/SIM activation by
 itself cannot be used to implicate the Accused in the present matter.
 The possibility still left open that the IMEI number of the mobile
 phone belonging to the Complainant could have been in fact cloned
 and transferred by some different and distinct criminal
 intermediary, to another mobile phone of different make and model
 and passed off to the Accused - this possibility is left open solely
 due to the fact that the alleged make and model of the mobile phone
 reported by the Complainant was never recovered by the
 investigating agency and the IMEI itself cannot be used as sole
 measure of identity of the stolen property. Hence, it can never be
 said with any measure of certainty that the IMEI number appearing
 on the CDR was actually activated against the SIM of the Accused
 inserted in the make and model of the mobile phone reported stolen
 by the Complainant. All this is in addition to the ambiguity already Cr. Case No. 426914/2016 State vs. Untrace(Ramesh) Page 8 of 9 created by the NIL SIM Activations report provided by the telecom
 operators which has gone unexplained in the course of the trial. 20.As the Accused herein has been chargesheeted for the offence on
 the basis of his own CDR which is reflecting the activation of the
 SIM under the IMEI belonging to the Complainant, which has
 already been shown to be ambiguous in nature, the testimony of the
 Complainant and the investigative witnesses even when taken in
 totality, do not take the case of the prosecution any further,
 especially in the absence of any recovery from the Accused. Thus,
 the Accused Ramesh is acquitted of the charge under section 411 IPC. 21.File be consigned to record room after due compliance.
