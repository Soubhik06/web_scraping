I've recently been getting a lot of Job Offers through text and email and they leave a message usually in an accent, my first indicator is the fact that i recently moved from the state that the offers are located and haven't signed up for any job in that state in 8 years, the next indicator is I've been getting a lot of them, as someone who's been in the market for jobs before, no one's that eager to get me to work for them, I have Asperger's and when they talk to me, they are immediately against hiring me due to my weird way of speaking to people and answering questions. The third red flag is they all have accents, in my experience, India and Africa is where a lot of scams originate and the people that have been calling have been exclusively from India based on the accent. I'm guessing they're trying to Phish personal info from me to either sell it or use it to steal my identity or even use it to call me from a different "company" pretending to have dealt with me in the past so they can gain my trust so I reveal more info to steal my money or Identity. We can discuss this but I'm mostly posting this as a warning and to let people know that may be also getting this that they're not alone and it is probably a scam.
