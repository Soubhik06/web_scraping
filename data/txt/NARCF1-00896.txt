--- POST TEXT ---

I moved out of Delhi 4 years ago and am registered as a voter in another state. Yet, during the 2024 Lok Sabha elections and now ahead of the February Assembly elections, I’ve been bombarded daily with 5-6 spam calls. These include automated messages asking me to press 1, 2, etc., based on who I’m voting for, and recordings of candidates urging me to vote for them. There is no way we can block these numbers as it’s not a single centralised unique number that’s calling you. 

The bigger issue here is the complete disregard for data privacy. Data Protection is a joke in this country and it feels like data leaks could easily lead to financial fraud or other crimes, yet no one seems to care or is held accountable. 

Is there something I (we) can do to stop these calls and hold someone responsible for this blatant misuse of personal data? I am sure this must be happening in other states also ahead of their Assembly elections.
