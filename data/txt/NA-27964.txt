mine recently visited Gurgaon. When she arrived, she got a call from a number with the extension +99. She didn’t answer at first, but after several calls, she picked up. The caller said her phone number would be deactivated in two hours due to a cybercrime report claiming her number was used for scams.

Scared, she asked for details. They gave her a case file number and said they would connect her to the cybercrime team in Mumbai. The person claimed to be DCP Pravin from the Mumbai police. She Googled the name and found someone with that title, thinking it was serious, so she kept talking to them.

They called her on WhatsApp video and told her there was an arrest warrant against her. They showed her some court documents, which made her panic. They eventually asked her to transfer money, and she did. After hanging up, she realized it might have been a scam. She called the police, who confirmed she had been cheated and advised her to file a complaint with cybercrime and inform her bank.

Now, she’s worried because the fraudsters have her Aadhaar card details and her current address, which she shared on WhatsApp. She fears for her family's safety since they live together. Another friend mentioned that since she was on video call for an hour, the fraudsters might have recorded her image and could misuse it, morph it upload on porn sites or blackmail her in future 

What can she do?
