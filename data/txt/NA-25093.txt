--- POST TEXT ---

With recent advancements in AI, your voice can be easily cloned by scammers. They've started deploying these deepfakes in phone scams, tricking you into hearing the distressed voice of someone you know. Even a brief phone call with a suspected scammer can be risky as they might be just collecting more voice samples.

Older adults are especially vulnerable to these scams. Consider having a secret passcode with them like your nickname or something, so that even if they hear your voice asking for money or OTP over the phone, they can verify it's truly you. 

Additionally, create two accounts, and enable internet banking on only one of them, account with your savings should be totally disconnected from internet and other account with internet banking you can keep for daily transactions.
