So, I got a message on Telegram from a random number. The guy claimed to be offering a "simple online job" where I could earn money just by liking YouTube videos and writing reviews. Sounded like a scam, but I decided to play along.

He gave me a small "task" first—liking a YouTube video and sending a screenshot. A few minutes later, he actually sent me 50 on Paytm. That’s when I knew what they were up to hook people in with small payments, then convince them to "invest" larger amounts to earn more.

I kept going. He gave me another task—rating a hotel on Google. 100 credited. Then another—writing a fake review.150 credited. By now, I had made 300, and he was convinced I was a gullible target

Then came the big scam. He said, "Sir, you’ve completed the free tasks. Now you can earn 5000 daily! Just invest 1000 in our VIP tasks."

I acted super interested but told him I only had around 800 left in my account so if we could give me one more task for 200 rupees I will send 1000 to him.
And he fell for it 🤣
So he gave me another stupid fake review task and I completed it. And he actually sent the 200.

That’s when I ghosted him. Blocked him on Telegram and WhatsApp, and walked away with 500.

Scammer ko scam karna ka maza hi alg hai 🤣
