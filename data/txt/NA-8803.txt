This is the second bail application under Section 439 Cr.P.C. filed on
behalf of the applicant. First application was dismissed as withdrawn vide order
dated 06.06.2022 passed in M.Cr.C.No.26376 of 2022. The applicant is in custody since 09.04.2022 in connection with Crime
No.23/2022 registered at Police Station Crime Branch, District Gwalior (M.P.)
for the offences punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 34 of IPC and Section 66-
D of IT Act and added Sections 467 , 468 , 471 of IPC. As per prosecution story, the applicant and other co-accused persons are 2 involved in overseas fraudulent dealings through call centers. Styling themselves
as agent of one lending Club Finance Company, they made calls to residents of
United States of America as if there were the local agents alluring them with the
offer of loan of 10000-20000 dollars and in that practice used to obtain their
social security numbers and other bank details. To reinforce confidence some
partial amount used to be deposited in their account. Forged documents were
also prepared and passed on to them camouflaging as loan documents.
Thereafter, misusing their social security numbers and bank details, they used to
withdraw the amount from their account. On the basis of the aforesaid, crime
has been registered. Learned counsel for the applicant submits that applicant is innocent and
he has been falsely implicated. He has no criminal antecedents. Applicant has
recently joined the said call center and was not aware of their working. He was
working merely a trainee. Instant case is that of big scam of fraudulent activities
of call center. The applicant is resident of Ahemdabad. His case is different
from that of rest of the accused person. It is also submitted that co-accused
Monika Dixit has been granted benefit of bail by this Court vide order dated
25.01.2023 passed in M.Cr.C. No.2550 of 2023 and the applicant deserves
parity in treatment. The applicant shall abide all the terms and conditions this
court deems fit and proper. On such grounds, prayer for grant of bail is made. Per contra, learned Public Prosecutor opposes the bail application and
prays for its rejection. U p o n hearing learned counsel for the rival parties but without
commenting upon the rival contentions touching merits of the case, regard being
had to the fact that the applicant is in custody since 09.04.2022 having no
criminal antecedents, he is held entitled for enlargement on bail. Consequently, the application of the applicant filed under Section 439 of
the Criminal Procedure Code, 1973 is hereby allowed. It is directed that the
applicant be released on bail on furnishing personal bond in the sum of
Rs.1,00,000/- (Rupees One Lakh only) with one solvent surety in the like
amount to the satisfaction of the learned Trial Court and on the condition that
he shall remain present before the Court concerned during trial and also comply
with the conditions enumerated under Section 437 (3) of Criminal Procedure
Code, 1973 with following further conditions: Observations made on facts touching merits of the case are only for the 4 purpose of deciding the bail application and shall not have any bearing on the
 pending trial. Certified copy as per rules. (ROHIT ARYA)
