Asking people without children, not just the ones who chose to be childfree but also the ones who couldn't have children. I am not talking about finances. From age 60-70 you will most likely become weak physically and mentally. How do plan to live without having anyone who'll check up on you regularly? Not talking about full care, just calling you once in a while, taking care of your documents and finances, making sure you are not answering scam calls etc. 

I know it's easy to say I'll just kill myself, but if it were that easy we won't have so many old people living miserable lives. 

Please only people who have made a concrete decision to stay childfree i.e. the ones in their late 30s/ early 40s answer. Because I have seen many young people below age 20, say that they won't get married or not have kids few years ago, but now almost all of them are married or planning to get married and having kids. 

Asking because - I am a woman and will be in my late 20s when I begin my career(mental health issues, COVID etc.). I don't think it will be possible to date, don't want an arranged marriage because I don't think I can fulfil the expectations of a family in my community. So the most likely scenario is I'll be single. Even if I do get married, having kids in my mid 30s will be extremely difficult. 

I was kind of okay with not getting married because I have never felt any dating fomo. But being old and having no one to check up on me sounds scary.
