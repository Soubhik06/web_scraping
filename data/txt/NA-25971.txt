Lemme preface by saying, I knew this was a scam from the start. I was just indulging them to have some fun. Inspired by Kitboga, ofc.

They started by asking me to like and share 2 videos on YouTube and screenshoting them on whatsapp as seen here. 
Then they ask to download Telegram and share a contact of a (Receptionist) who will give you the remaining video and pay you RS.150 oce you share the last screenshot. 
After you share it, they ask your personal details and so on. 
I shared some fake personal details but looked like the person caught wind of it and stopped replying. So, that's the end. 

BTW, the whatsapp number is from a business account. Always checking the name of the business is a good practice as this account name made no sense. It was just randomly generated letters.

Please be aware of this type of scam and share with your elders.
