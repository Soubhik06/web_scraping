So recently, I have been going over MFUtilities due to all the buzz its generating. During my first visit, I noticed the website looks very primitive and unimpressive. I had to double check to ensure this was the same platform that's hosted by the congregate of all major MF platforms in India.

Recently, they have allowed filling in all your personal and online details for getting a CAN number [online](https://www.mfuindia.com/CANFormFill). Not only is this primitive looking website taking and saving all your personal and financial details such as PAN number, bank accounts, date of births, it can all be also retrieved by simply entering one's email address. This seems like a low hanging fruit for identity theft.

Also if a data theft happens on this site, who is held responsible? (unlike an AMC's website where the AMC is liable)

If anybody has contacts with MFUtilities, please urge them to address these concerns.
