I've been receiving a lot of scam calls and messages since the past few days. Sim card scam, KBC scam, Wrong number scam -- all of them. 

But today I recieved a new scam. This once is an electricity scam: You'll probably receive a message on WhatsApp mentioning that your electricity is about to get disconnected tonight because you have a pending bill. Then the messages asks you to call on the number and have the issue resolved. Obviously, when you call them, they'll scam you. 

Issued on public interest.
