from them. Thereafter, at about 1:00 AM, the aforesaid car was found parked

in the parking area near the lake, where both the aforementioned accused

were apprehended by the police team. Upon searching the car, 1.012 kg of

heroin was recovered from behind the music system installed in the said car. Further, during the course of investigation, a disclosure

statement of the arrested accused - Vikramjit Singh @ Vicky was recorded

on 29.03.2024 while he was in custody, and on the basis thereof, two more

his possession. Besides, two mobile phones were also recovered. Thereafter, on 31.03.2024, accused - Lovepreet Singh

and while refining the allegations, it was stated that, in fact, Gurjot Singh,

who had been arrested from Ludhiana on 29.03.2024, was the person who
