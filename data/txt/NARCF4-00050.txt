People posing as scam delivery guys are asking for COD payments from house-to-house in exchange for trash products (araroot biscuits, trash perfumes and whatnot) in delivery boxes. They say they are from amazon, as of now. The order ID is fake and when you call their manager, he is avoiding call. 

If anyone sees them and cares about others, report them to police.

I know a lot of people are smart here but spread the news with family and relatives. Stay safe.
