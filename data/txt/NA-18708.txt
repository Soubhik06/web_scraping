Let me tell you about a story how Flipkart scammed me.I purchased a laptop for my brother on 18th of April 2022. At the time the price tag was ₹56,990 (mobile screenshot attached). It was showing a offer for instant discount of ₹2000 on Credit Cards and Debit Cards (screen shot attached. Yes, the price is different. It was updated the second after I purchased the item).I purchased the product and noticed that the offer was not applied to the product. When I asked the customer support, they asked me to wait for 3 days and promised my issue will be resolved within 21st of April 2022. On 21 April their so called customer support gave me a missed call(cellphone was literally on my hand) and sent an email and a SMS saying they were unable to contact me. I requested a call back and was informed that\*\*"The card I used to purchase - SOUTH INDIAN BANK does not support the instant ₹2000 offer"**.**This is not showing in any where in the product page or in payment page. There is no way for a customer to check if his bank is supporting a offer in Flipkart. Even their customer care executives agree with this. Till day 20, I was getting calls from their Customer care executives saying**"My bank is not supporting the offer".** I know my bank is not supporting the offer. But why are you not showing the list of banks that supports the offer *(They are only showing* *ICICI Bank* *offers)*. There is no way for a customer to know which other banks supports the offer.They're literally robbing people with this scam.Yesterday morning I received an email from Flipkart team saying**"We tried to get in touch with you on &lt;my\_mobile\_number&gt; to discuss about the refund issue but were unable to reach you."**No they didn't, they tried the same missed call and SMS trick I mentioned above. This time they didn't even bothered to call *(I have missed call alerts activated).* I requested a call back and that call got connected without an issue. The Customer rep that took the call at the time said "he had requested a call back and they will call back in an hour". In the evening they tried the same missed call and SMS trick and when I asked about this they said it must be some network issues blah blah. But how is it that when their customer care "executives calls to talk about the refund" they face the network issue? It's day 21. They still haven't added the option for a customer to check if the bank offer they show is supported by their bank and Flipkart is still continuing to scam people like this.
