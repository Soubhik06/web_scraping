 acquitted 8. Date of order 24.09.2022 3 C.C.No.16966/2013 The Sub-Inspector of Police, Jayanagar Police Station,

Bengaluru City has filed Police Report against the accused for

the offences punishable under Sections 120B, 420 , 465 , 468 and 471 R/w Section 34 of the Indian Penal Code. 2. The informant has filed this information against the

accused persons on the ground that the informant is the

Manager of Deutsche Postbank Finance Limited. They are

lending loans for purchase of home and plots on mortgage of

the properties. The accused No.1 by name Mohan Achar had

been to their Bank and obtained a loan for the purchase of

two sites through one Mr. Shivaraj. The accused No.1 has

told them that he is self employed and Proprietor of

M/s.Maruthi Industries situated at Rajaji Nagar, Bengaluru

with nature of business in manufacturing industrial tools. He

has produced several documents in support of his loan to the

Bank. During the month of September 2009, they have

sanctioned two loan of Rs.13,50,000/- each for purchase of

two sites from Mr.Saleem Pasha and Mrs.Shanaz Begum. The 4 C.C.No.16966/2013 accused after obtaining the loans was default in payment of

installments. They doubted regarding genuineness of the

documents submitted by the accused and tried to locate the

address of accused No.1. He had furnished wrong address.

They have visited the sites and found that the said sites were

not at all in existence. The Bank doubted even the business

of accused No.1 and verified the Form No.16 and after

verification, they have found that some documents were

forged by the accused in obtaining the loan. They have

personally visited the property and came to know that the

said sites were belongs to a third party. Accused No.1 has

cheated the Bank and obtained the loan. Immediately, the

informant had been to the Police Station and lodged the

information against accused No.1. 3. Based on the First Information of CW1, the crime was

registered in Crime No.65/2010 at Jayanagar Police Station,

Bengaluru City. During investigation, the accused persons

have appeared before the Court and enlarged on bail. On

completion of the investigation, the Police Sub-Inspector of 5 C.C.No.16966/2013 Jayanagar Police Station, Bengaluru City filed Police Report

against the accused persons alleging that they have

committed the offences punishable under Sections 120B , 420 , 465 , 468 and 471 R/w Section 34 of the Indian Penal

Code. After taking cognizance of the said offences, the

process was issued to the accused persons. Accused No.2 is

left out from charge-sheet. Accused No.4 and 6 were arrested

and produced before the Court. They were also released on

bail. Other accused Nos.1, 3, 5, 7 to 9 were split up. The

copies of the Police Report and other prosecution papers are

furnished to accused No.4 and 6 under Section 207 of Cr.P.C.

After hearing, since there were grounds for presuming that

accused No.4 and 6 have committed offences triable by this

Court, charges for the offences punishable under Sections

120B , 420 , 465 , 468 and 471 R/w Section 34 of the Indian

Penal Code have been framed and read over to them in

Kannada language. They have pleaded not guilty and claims

to be tried. 4. To prove the charges framed against No.4 and 6, the

Prosecution has produced the oral evidences of PW1 to PW7

and the documentary evidences in Ex.P1 to Ex.P66. After

completion of the prosecution evidences, for the purpose of

enabling accused No.4 and 6 personally to explain any

circumstances appearing in the evidences of the prosecution

against them, examined accused No.4 and 6 under Section

313 of Cr.P.C. They have submitted that they have no defense

evidence. 5. Heard the arguments of learned Senior Assistant Public

Prosecutor. Perused the materials available on record. 6. The points for determination are; 1. Whether prosecution has proved the offences
 charged against accused No.4 and 6 for the
 offences punishable under Sections 120B , 420 , 465 , 468 and 471 R/w Section 34 of the Indian
 Penal Code beyond reasonable doubts? 2. What order or sentence? 7. My answers to the above points are as follows: 8. POINT No.1 :- To prove the guilt of accused No.4 and 6,

the Prosecution has examined CW1/PW3 Lisa Mohanta, Head

of Operation Sales, Muthoot Finance, wherein he has deposed

that he was the Manager of the said Bank from 2005 to 2008.

During the year 2019, accused No.1 had approached therir

bank and applied for two loans of Rs.13,50,000/- each to

purchased two sites. The Bank on verification of the entire

documents have sanctioned the loan. Later, accused No.1 has

not paid the installments. He has verified the records and

found that the documents are forged. Immediately, he had

been to the Police Station and lodged the information against

the accused persons. 9. The Prosecution had examined CW3/PW2 Muninanje

Gowda, wherein he has deposed that he is the resident of

Doddamaralavadi. One Smt.Mehaboobi had executed an 8 C.C.No.16966/2013 Agreement of sale in respect of Survey No.99 to the extent of

30 guntas of land. The said Ramaiah has converted 30

guntas of land into non-agricultural land and she has

purchased Site No.13 and 14 from the said Ramaiah. The

said Ramaiah has executed the said Sale Deed based on the

GPA executed by Mehaboobi. Later, his wife has sold the said

site No.13 to Ramaiah and Site No.14 to Krishnamurthy. The

said properties in Site No.13 and 14 is in the possession of

Ramaiah and Krishnamurthy. Later, he was called by the

police and informed that the said Mehaboobi again has sold

the Site No.14 to one Mr.Achar. 10. The Prosecution had examined CW3/PW5 Gayathi Devi,

wherein she has deposed that Smt.Mehaboobi had executed

an Agreement of sale in favour of Ramaiah in Site No.99 to

the extent of 30 guntas of land. The said Ramaiah has

converted 30 guntas of land in to a non-agricultural land.

She has purchased plot No.13 and 14 from Ramaiah. This

Ramaiah has executed the Sale Deed as per GPA of

Mehaboobi. Later, they have sold the said Site No.13 and 14 9 C.C.No.16966/2013 in favour of the Ramaiah and Krishnamurthy. The property

bearing Site No.13 and 14 is in possession of Ramaiah and

Krishnamurthy. 11. The Prosecution had examined CW5/PW4

Krishnamurthy M.B., wherein he has deposed that he has

purchased Site No.13 and 14 from Gayathri Devi and he is in

possession of the said property. He has not all alienated to

any one. His statement was not recorded by the Police. 12. The Prosecution had examined CW5/PW5 Rajendra,

wherein he has deposed that accused No.1 has obtained the

loan from the Bank by forging the documents. He was

directed by the Police to produce the documents and he had

produced the entire relevant documents in respect of the said

case. 13. The Prosecution had examined CW6/PW5 Sheshadri,

Retired Sub-Registrar, wherein he has deposed as per the

directions of the Police, he has produced the certified copies

of the Sale Deed. 14. The Prosecution had examined CW7/PW6

Chikkapeddanna, wherein he has deposed about the

furnishing of the documents. 15. It is the case of the Prosecution that accused No.1 has

created and forged documents to obtained two loans of

Rs.13,50,000/- from the complainant Bank. Based on the

information the case was registered against the accused

Nos.1 to 9. Accused No.2 was left out from the charge-sheet.

The Prosecution failed to secure accused Nos.1, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8

and 9 and case was split up against them. 16. During the course of argument the Learned Senior APP

has contended that accused No.1 and 2 have played fraud by

creating the documents and obtained the loan of Rs.

27.00.000. He argued that accused No.7 being the owner of

the property site No.14 has executed G.P.A in favour of CW 3

on 10.09.1999. This accused No.7 has already alienated site

No.15 in favour of G.T Ramaiah. But however, the said site

Plot No.15 was again alienated by accused No.7 and 8 11 C.C.No.16966/2013 infavour of accused No.4 and got registered and got registered

the sale deed. The Site No.14 was alienated in favour of

accused No.5. The accused No.4, 5, 7 and 8 through accused

No.3, 6 and 9 have alienated both site No.14 and 15 in favour

of accused No.1. Accused No.1 in colluding with accused No.3

has produced the forged documents in the name of his

mother Renuka and the mother of accused No.3 Sukanya and

had obtained the loan of Rs.27,00,000/-. Accused No.4 and 5

had alienated site No.14 and 15 in favour of the accused

No.1. After obtaining the loan it was enjoyed by all the

accused. He argued that they have hide the property already

alienated in favour of G.T.Ramaiah and obtained the loan.

The Site No.14 is in possession of CW 5 and Site No.15 is in

possession of Mr.Ramaiah. 17. On perusal CW 1 as per information Ex.P.3 he has

contended in his information that the accused No.1 and

accused No.2 by name Renuka have played fraud and

obtained the loan of Rs.13.50.000/-each for the purchase of

two sites from accused No.4 Saleem Pasha and from accused 12 C.C.No.16966/2013 No.5 Shahanaz Fathima. But however, the Investigation

Officer has dropped accused No.2 by name Renuka on the

ground that she was innocent and evidence were not

available against this Renuka for the said offences. Further

on perusal, this informant as per Ex.P.3 has filed information

against all the accused No.1 to 9. But however this informant

has not alleged any role of accused No.3 to 9 in his

information. The case was only against accused No.1 and 2.

But however, as said supra accused No.2 was left out in final

report and accused No.4 to 9 were declared absconded. The

case is only against accused No.1 on the ground that he has

obtained the loan of Rs.27,00,000/- But however, this

accused No.2 has taken the loan of Rs.13,50,000/- as per the

case of the prosecution. The entire burden was shifted on

accused No.1 including the loan of Accused No.2. The

prosecution has not furnished any details why the case was

charged against the accused No.1 only. 18. Further the prosecution has examined CW 1/PW 3

wherein he has only deposed that the accused No.1 has 13 C.C.No.16966/2013 applied for two loans of Rs.13,50,000/- by creating and

forging the documents. This witness has not at all deposed

anything against the present accused No.4 and 6 nor the

prosecution has cross examined this CW 1 in respect of the

role of accused No.4 and 5. As said supra the accused No.1 to

3, 5, 7 and 9 were already split up. The trial was conducted

against accused No.4 and 6. Further this CW 3/PW 2 and

CW 5/PW 3 have deposed only that this site No.13 and 14

standing in the name of Ramanna and Krishnamurthy. Site

No.14 was again alienated in favour of accused No.1 as per

the say of the police. He has also not deposed anything about

the role of accused No.4 and 6. CW 5/PW 4 has turned

hostile to the case of the prosecution. CW 5/PW 5 Senior

Manager of the said Bank has deposed only that the

documents was created by accused No.1 and obtained the

loan. Further on perusal of the evidence of a sub-register

officer CW 6/PW5 wherein he has deposed that the said sale

deed was duly registered before the sub-register officer. He

has not deposed anything about the alleged forging of 14 C.C.No.16966/2013 documents. It is on record that accused No.1 has produced

the original registered sale deed. It is not the case of the

prosecution that the accused has not at all registered the

said sale deed executed in his favour. The said sale deed was

duly registered in the name of accused No.1 and same was

confirmed by this witness CW 6/PW 5 being sub-register

officer. This CW 7/PW6 has also deposed that the said sale

deed was duly registered. When such being the case the

question of the forging the documents how would arise? The

prosecution absolutely failed to prove the role of the accused

No.4 and 5 in obtaining the loan from the Bank. Under these

circumstances this court finds that the Prosecution has not

proved the guilt of accused No.4 and 6 for the offences

punishable under Sections 120B , 420 , 465 , 468 and 471 R/w Section 34 of the Indian Penal Code beyond reasonable

doubt. Hence, I answer Point No.1 in the Negative. 19. POINT No.2 :- For the reasons stated in Point No.1, the

prosecution has not proved the guilt of accused No.4 and 6

for the offences punishable under Sections 120B , 420 , 465 , 15 C.C.No.16966/2013 468 and 471 R/w Section 34 of the Indian Penal Code beyond

all reasonable doubt. Therefore, accused No.4 and 6 are not

found guilty for the aforesaid offences charged against them.

In the result, I proceed to pass the following; accused No.4 and 6 is hereby acquitted for
 the offences punishable under Sections
 120B , 420 , 465 , 468 and 471 R/w Section
 34 of the Indian Penal Code. Their bail bonds and surety bonds
 executed will be in force till appeal period
