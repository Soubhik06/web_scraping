There are multiple controversies currently regarding release of various datas. Leaked NSSO report on employment made news. NCRB data of 2016 has still not been released (It includes politically sensitive data of farmer suicides), data about Demonitisation was delayed considerably.

As a citizen, I need to be aware about authentic data before I decide to vote, or even otherwise. There should be law which mandates timely release of data. Having a law will allow concerned citizens to go to court and have that implemented, if the govt is dilly dallying.

I will vote for any party which can promise such a law or an enforceable framework in their manifesto. But, will any party do so and take away its own power to manipulate things?
