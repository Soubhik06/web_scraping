I don't think anyone here needs to know the cons of our central govt. But does that mean we should completely ignore the good they have done or have at least tried to? Here I will try to list some of the positives of BJP/NDA?Modi govt. being as factual/logical as I can. You think I am from BJP IT cell. Cool, I might be. You never know. But try to prove my points wrong based on facts and I'll be very open to change.

I myself was apolitical for long. Never even went to vote, kind of apolitical. Every single one of them is corrupt and nothing's gonna change anyway, kind of apolitical.

Recently, though, politics have piqued my interest. I don't like Modi as a person much as he often does some cringe stuff like that Man vs Wild show and the Peacock video. But why should that take away any good he has done for the country?

So, below I'll list down some points I think were done by Modi/BJP and that no one before him did. They said they will but did not. We have always heard the saying that "*Paida karne waale se paalne wala badaa hota hai*". And I hope there is no disagreement there.

Similarly, for politics, I would like to say - "***Bhaashan dene waale se implement karne wala badaa hota hai***". Would you agree?

**Why I support Modi? Find below.**

1. **Aadhar** \- Bhashan by Congress. Implementation by NDA.
2. **GST** \- For 17 years it sat in the pipeline of Congress. Was part of their manifestos. Who implemented it? Modi. Yes, the implementation could have been much better but better than nothing. Isn't it?
3. **Insolvency and bankruptcy code (law) 2016** \- The "Kaala Dhan" saga. Earlier, any business would declare itself bankrupt and flee taking with them everything the investors invested in them. This law is brought to curb that. Again, it might have flaws but still is better than Presidency Town Insolvency Act (1909), Provisional Insolvency Act (1920), Companies Act, the Limited Liability Partnership Act, Securitisation Act, etc.
4. **370/35A Removal** \- This I would attribute to Modi/Shah and not NDA. Hand to your heart. Tell me one politician who would've shown the guts to take such a step. Once you read these vile articles in detail, you'll be horrified and ask why wasn't this done much before.
5. **Swachh Bharat** \- India is a filthy country and will be filthy in foreseeable future too. But at least villages now have toilets. I live in one of the NCR cities and go to work in another. Areas that were covered with junk are regularly cleaned now and have dustbins installed there.
6. **Ujjwala Yojana** \- Providing free LPG cylinders to poor households to make them get LPG connections. The goal was 8 crore connections and this number has already been surpassed. This would help in reducing reliance on coal, wood, etc. in those areas. Much cleaner fuel. Caveat? LPG costs a ton. Hope LPG gets cheaper soon.
7. **Ram Mandir/Babri Masjid (Ayodhya)** \- Hindutva. Gau mutra. This and that. Whatever. This case is finally closed and don't tell me it happened on its own. I'm not happy with the full verdict but at least this got closed now.
8. **Covid-19** \- Can't compare this with any previous government but you'd agree India did a tremendous job. Even international media, medical bodies like WHO, governments are praising India. Especially on the vaccine distribution. A home run in international diplomacy. Did we handle it perfectly? No. Did any country handle it perfectly? New Zealand and Taiwan. That's it. Credit where credit due. We did very well in this area. Could've been better but still appreciable efforts by every department involved.
9. **China** \- I can bet you won't find a single person who had the guts to call a spade a spade when it came to China. Leave India, not many world leaders had the audacity to meddle with the dragon. India called for a boycott of Chinese goods and apps. Now, I don't fully agree with this move but completely stand behind the action taken on the border standoffs. For the first time since 1979 (after losing to Vietnam), did China move back from a territory that it claimed as its own. Internationally, it has been ridiculed for this.
10. **Surgical Strikes** \- "56 inch ki chaati waala ka hawabaaz" was what I used to call Modi. But not after the surgical strikes. I respect that move. **A lot**. And before someone says, but he said "Ek k badle 10, 20 maarenge", come on. Be realistic. These kinds of statements are obviously made to gain votes. At least we took revenge rather than talking of peace every time our soldiers were killed mercilessly. Today, Pakistan itself came crawling to us asking for a ceasefire.
11. **Make in India/AatmNirbhar** \- An almost failed policy revived partly due to Covid. After long talks, they managed to bring Tesla to India. Many other companies like Samsung, Apple have either increased their production in India or have vowed to. Let's see how it goes.
12. **Ease of Doing Business** \- Massive focus of this government, right from inception, has been to bring businesses to India. India was 130th position in the "Ease of Doing Business" ranking in 2016. Now? 63!Still, a long way to go, but at least we are on the way. Bigger businesses are given a Single Point of Contact (SPOC) who ensures a smooth ride and businesses don't have to run from pillar to post bribing every babu they encounter on the path.
13. **Farm Laws** \- And I'll be stoned to death now. After analyzing both sides, talking to farmers in whatever capacity I can. My "saggey chachas" are farmers in Haryana **who are sitting in protest**. So didn't have to go far to talk to a farmer. Yet, I fully support farm laws because I actually fricking read them and would consider this a massive achievement of the ruling government.
14. **CAA/NRC** \- Same as above. Kill me for supporting this. But I do and consider this as an achievement. This has a scope for improvement. Yes. Implementation, as usual, isn't going to be perfect. They should've used the word minority instead of actually naming the religions. Though there is another reason (a very strong one) for not using the minority term. But I fully understand the need for these laws.
15. **DRDO/Ordnance Factory/HAL** \- What changed that all of a sudden, these organizations, who needed to force their own army to buy products manufactured by them are now ready to export Tejas and Brahmos? All these institutions were littered with zero-productivity-running-private-businesses-on-the-side kind of unionized babus and workers. Getting them to work and making to develop such technological marvels in such a short span of time. Isn't that incredible?
16. **Digital India** \- Even vegetable vendors have bank accounts and use UPI now. The digital economy move is also one of the steps towards curbing black money and easier taxation in the future.
17. **Use of Aadhar to Get Govt. Benefits/Licenses etc.** \- True power of Aadhar is being utilized now. For example - [Get your driving license online through your Aadhaar card](https://english.jagran.com/auto/now-get-your-driving-license-online-through-your-aadhaar-card-check-details-inside-10024124). Also - [Parivar Pehchan Patra Family ID Card](https://www.cschry.com/parivar-pehchan-patra-family-id-card-haryana-ppp.php). Isn't this awesome? And please don't start about govt. taking our data and stalking us crap. Don't you already have ration cards, passports, driving license, birth, marriage, death certificates? This is just a centralized and digitalized version of all that. Every developed country has a version of it implemented.

All the points mentioned above are gigantic in nature. Any government, if they managed to achieve even 5 of the aforementioned points, would've blown their own trumpet for eternity. And here we don't even acknowledge them? Besides these big milestones, there are numerous schemes that people don't even know of. Like:

* The law related to triple talaq
* Law for strict punishment against child abuse
* Law empowering transgender persons
* Chit fund scheme fraud prevention law
* National Medical Commission Act
* Appointment of Chief of Defence Staff

and several others.

And the next big thing? **Uniform Civil Code**. France is bringing it to their country. Many other countries have it for decades. No foreign media will oppose it. But, mark my words, when Modi will try to bring that to India, you'll hear chants of "Islamophobic", "Hindutva", "Hindu Rashtra" etc.

What the fuck?And then the control on the Internet. And whatnot.

Then the mystery of fuel prices. Won't ever understand the logic behind that. Why can't we have cheaper fuel ever? At least now there are talks of bringing fuel under GST which might reduce the prices massively. Also, a big focus is being given to electric vehicles.

This year's budget was decent but all the prior ones were borderline idiotic. This govt. has proven that they are economically clueless. They have shown signs of improvement though. I sincerely hope they do well going forward.
