**Need your help &amp; thoughts.** We can create startups, which create jobs &amp; earn using the blockchain ecosystem. It's a win-win situation for everyone.

**Why use the blockchain?**

Blockchain is trustable, reliable, secured, transparent, can record tangible &amp; intangible assets, track &amp; trade, reduce risk &amp; cost. Power will be in the hands of everyone. Not one person or a group of people.

**How does the ecosystem work?**

The system will take in your basic details &amp; verify. It's private &amp; secured.

We need a forum to discuss &amp; vote like Reddit. Assign a leader/moderator for groups. Set the rules. Option to Remove the wrong moderator by vote &amp; assign a new moderator by vote. Give digital rewards. Toggle between being anonymous or open. When your profile levels up by positive works or testimony, you gain points. The higher points you gain, the higher level you get, the more reliable you will be seen in the digital public.

Let's create startups

**Problem with existing startups?**

\- Need to spend for registration &amp; setup of the company(Expense)- Find a good dedicated mentor(Very rare)- need to do own accounts or hire(Time &amp; Expense)- raise capital on your own(Expense)- open a bank current account with Rs20k &amp; if not maintained then will be charged a fine of Rs1k a month(Expense)- do RnD or work on your product(Time &amp; Expense)- hire employees, sales &amp; marketing(Time &amp; Expense)- As per the 2018 survey, more than 80% of startups fail the first year. Insufficient financial resources, wrong team, Strong competition &amp; lots of unexpected situations, time &amp; extra cost of running

We need to change the way startups work. All the above startup problems can be solved using this new method

**Few examples of startups we can create**

\- Create Wework/Coworking/workbench space(space to use Tools, machinery, electronics to build stuff)- Eateries &amp; joints, Clubs &amp; Hangouts- Rentals (Tools, Machinery, vehicles, house, formal clothes, costumes, jewelry, etc)- Retirement (home nurse, home cook, care help, transportation)- Nursery (Play, Hands-on Experience &amp; learn)- Education (Practical, Work, Hands-on Experience &amp; Learn)- Entertainments, media, etc- Sustainable Agriculture, Vertical farming, etc- Easy public transportation- &amp; many more

**Who will invest in these startups?**

The public can invest in the form of shares, assets &amp; tools. If an idea is good &amp; approved by moderators. Then the blockchain system will generate a fixed amt of shares for that idea. The public can purchase the shares. The shares can be publicly bought &amp; sold via this system. Depending on the startup, shareholders earn a small percentage of profit monthly/yearly. The moderators &amp; investors can monitor the spending &amp; growth of the idea into reality.

By using this method where the public is involved in giving ideas, discussing, raising capital, funding, developing, growing &amp; sustaining the startup, it'll always tend to remain healthy &amp; everyone who is involved in it makes income from it. Moderators are bridges between the public &amp; startups, they are mentors for startups &amp; report to the public the health of the startup. Moderators are rewarded for the success of the startup, so they will be more involved in succeeding it. We can solve a lot of problems &amp; make an income from it. It's a win-win situation for all.

**Worst case scenario:** To do fraud &amp; siphon off money is difficult bec all data is public, &amp; even if done. If caught, moderators can mark negative in fraudster's profile &amp; won't be accepted in any groups. You will lose more than gain. It's not worth stealing.
