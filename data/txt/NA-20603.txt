I got scammed a few days ago by SBI, and i think it is a standard modus operandi, so describing the incident here so others don't fall for it.

I have an SBI Elite card, and it is up for renewal in a few months. It costs 5k annually, and i don't think I'm going to renew it unless they waive the fee.

So i got a call from SBI who said they will downgrade the elite card to a basic one, with a ₹500 annual fee. They said it would carry the same limit as my elite card, which is in the 3-4L range. That sounded off, but i dont need a large limit anyway, so i was fine with even a smaller one.

Then he opened an entirely new card for me.

The old card still existed, and the new one had a miniscule 10k limit. 

This is clearly a scam to boost their total cards, and i had to waste a few hours getting this cancelled and refunded. Someone from the cancellation department even tried to pull this one off again. This ticked me off so much, that I'm cancelling the elite card also.
