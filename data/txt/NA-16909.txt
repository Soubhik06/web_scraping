--- POST TEXT ---

The people of Kolkata always complain about the dismal state of the real estate sector. I was not an investor in the sector, but a recent experience with brand name developers and real estate brokers, has made it clear to me why regular people tend to stay away from this sector.

In August 2019, I had come to purchase a unit in a Srijan group project, Nirvana. This was pre-Covid times and we were all financially in a different place than we are today. The Srijan sales group had even managed to upsell us an extension to the base unit, so we had a total unit with cost of base unit, Rs. 56.5L plus Rs. 6.02L. The sales team told us that if we decided to sell the unit in the future, then the extension would be an attractive feature.

Then Covid happened and we decided to put this unit for resale in March 2020. There was a one year lock-in period, and we expected that the sale would happen after Covid issue settled down. I am new to real estate, and my whole family is in service, so we contacted several brokers and agents, along with posting on the websites like magicbricks and 99acres. In this way, I contacted NK Realtors. I spoke with Mr. Vikash (I will share his real name if anyone asks over DM) of NK Realtors and he assured me that the sale would happen, we spoke at length about the process and then he told me to sign a resale agreement letter. I have shared the letter here. It states that I would have to pay NK Realtors 3% commission even if the sale happened with a client not provided by them. I honestly had no issue with this, in fact, Mr. Vikash told me that if I got clients from outside, he would coordinate with them and I would not have to worry. I signed the agreement with them on 9 October 2020.

The letter has no stamp, it is an e-document and has no letterhead, so the legality of the agreement aside, my issue is with the quality of service provided by them.

In the end of November I was contacted by a buyer through 99acres, Ms. Sharma (name withheld). She was a genuine buyer and she came to my house with her husband the next Sunday to discuss. On Sunday we contacted Mr. Vikash together from my house, but he did not entertain us. He was apparently travelling, so I also shared Ms. Sharma’s phone number with him over Whatsapp and told him to contact her as soon as possible. But he never contacted them. Over December, I told him over phone to call Ms. Sharma, but he kept pushing his own client Mr. Beria (name withheld). Mr. Beria always seemed fraud to me, but apparently had contacted Mr. Vikash directly and they had worked out a deal and I would receive a downpayment on 24 Dec 2020.

During this time, Mr. Vikash shared with me a copy of an agreement, this agreement was not made specifically for me. It was some old deal they had done, had other people’s names on it and even the prices mentioned were not calculated for my property. I did not understand the point of this, and I told Mr. Vikash that I do not understand what I am to do with someone else’s agreement, but he told me Mr. Beria is fine and it will not cause issues. Mr. Vikash also told me that I would not have to pay the outstanding last demand letter I had received because the resale would go through. I believed him because I know NK Realtors is directly working with Srijan so it made sense there would be an understanding.

On 24 Dec 2020, I received a message on Whatsapp from Mr. Beria, telling me he was unhappy with the terms of the deal and so was backing out. I did not show any temper to Mr. Vikash, simply asking him if he has other clients and if I should clear the outstanding demand. He told me he was busy and would call me later. He never called me again. I checked my entire call history. He did not even contact Ms. Sharma.

Over January, I worked tirelessly with Ms. Sharma and Srijan to work out the resale. I had to get the extension cancelled, payoff the demand, get the ledger updated etc. I have so many emails with Srijan, that I have lost count. My own work had taken a back seat to this resale. Finally, last week, the deal Ms. Sharma was finalised and I cashed their booking cheque.

Then yesterday, 30 Jan 2020, I received a message from Mr. Vikash telling me to read the resale agreement and that I owe NK Realtors money for doing absolutely nothing because I typed my name on a word document. How can these people, after a month of radio silence, suddenly appear and start demanding money? I am fighting this extortion tooth and nail but I wanted to let my fellow Kolkatans know about the unscrupulous business practices that take place. Even with agreements, companies should be held to a certain standard of service at which they practice. People should not be victimised by these scam artists posing as valid business. The scam culture has to end. I am sharing the Whatsapp conversation.

My point is, if Reddit can take on hedge funds, then we together can always fight against these scams. I am open to advice on how to proceed and I hope others can take something from my own experience.
