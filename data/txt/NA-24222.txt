My brother is interviewing for a position in an Indian IT major, and he cleared 2 rounds. Just before the HR round, the company said that they have a background verification process and for that my brother will have to provide his login/password for the ITR and EPFO portals to a 3rd party. The company HR claims that this contractor will pick up "only" form 26AS from ITR and the employment history from EPFO. The company HR also says this is mandatory to get an offer.
I have many questions about this ridiculous ask:
1. How can he trust this 3rd party not to look at his other financial data including all the bank account numbers, assets, liabilities, capital gains, foreign assets and income? This 3rd party can easily sell off this data or misuse this for anything
2. The 3rd party is also asking for a liability waiver before asking for the credentials, so they're not liable if the data leaks
3. The company HR has a simple solution: just change the password after providing it to the 3rd party. What's the use of changing the password after they have accessed the data and copied it to their servers?
