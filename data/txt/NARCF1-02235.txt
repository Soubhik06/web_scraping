A few months ago, I saw a post going viral on Instagram, where 1-2 young girls were filming in the Maidan. They were kissing, and those two girls were mocking them, filming that, and with so much audacity, posted it online. It was dark, so the couple wasn’t visible enough to be identified. I felt so disgusted, so much anger just from knowing and seeing that. Every freaking person out there knows what happens in those parks, but we don’t know what the couples are up to or even if they’re a couple or not it’s none of anyone’s business. And they were just kissing, not even doing anything inappropriate in that video! I felt so sad that in this society or city, where two people loving each other and sharing a gesture isn’t allowed, but open harassment and open crime are and actually, no one around at that time will ever make a video of that or try to stop it :) After that video, that Kalighat Metro kissing photo also got posted! This is so terrible. Just let people love it's the only good thing we can do.
