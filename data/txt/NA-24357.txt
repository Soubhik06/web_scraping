Alright, Reddit, buckle up because I’m about to vent my fucking guts out. I’ve been living and working in Bangalore for 4 years now, and let me tell you, the police here are the most greedy, hostile, and corrupt pieces of shit I’ve ever encountered. I’m a 26-year-old North Indian guy working as a software engineer, and I’ve had enough of this bullshit. I’m using a throwaway account because I don’t want these assholes tracking me down. 

**Incident 1:** 
This happened when I first moved here. I was in my car with my girlfriend, and things got a little heated. We pulled over in a secluded spot, started kissing, and out of nowhere, some guy on a Splendor bangs on my car door. I roll down the window, and this motherfucker yanks my car keys out, hits me in the nose, and breaks the keys. Turns out, he’s a cop. He tells me to go to the hospital and come to the police station the next day with a “donation” to settle things. I was bleeding, scared shitless, and ended up booking a flight back home to avoid dealing with this psycho. Later, I found out he came to my society in uniform, causing a scene and demanding money. Permanent scar on my nose, broken car keys, and a damaged door – all because I kissed my girlfriend. 

**Incident 2:** 
I was driving, crossed an intersection when the light was green, but it turned red midway because of traffic. A cop stops me and demands 5k. I argued, but he claimed he had “proof” from some camera. I didn’t want the hassle, so I ended up paying 2k. No e-challan, no receipt, just straight-up extortion. 

**Incident 3:** 
A cab driver hit me from behind on the highway, and my car spun 180°. The cab driver called his buddies, and they demanded 1L for damages. I called my friends, and we decided to involve the traffic police. The traffic police arrived, took both our car keys, and impounded our vehicles. My friends and I resolved the matter among ourselves – I agreed to pay 10k since I took a turn and was partially at fault. But when we went to the police station to get our car keys back, the cops demanded an extra 5k. For fucking nothing. 

**Incident 4:** 
This one takes the cake. Here’s the full, detailed breakdown of what went down: 

It was evening, and I got a call from an unknown number. The guy on the other end says, “There’s a card delivery for you, but I only have your hometown address.” He spelled out my hometown address correctly, so I thought it was legit. He asked for my current address, and I sent it to him on WhatsApp. He then asks if I’ll be home in the next 10 minutes, and I say yes. 

Half an hour later, he still hasn’t shown up, and it’s my gym time. I leave for the gym, which isn’t far from my place. About 20 minutes into my workout, I get another call from the same guy saying he’s at my gate. I tell him to come near the gym instead. I go down and wait for 15 minutes, but he doesn’t show. Finally, he calls me from across the street, and I go over to meet him. 

He asks for my address proof, so I open DigiLocker on my phone to show him. Meanwhile, I ask him to show me the card he’s delivering because I don’t remember expecting any such thing. Suddenly, out of nowhere, 5-6 men surround me and start pushing me toward a car. They say they’re from the police and need to take me in for some details. 

I’m immediately suspicious because there’s no police car – it’s just a random yellow-plate cab – and none of them are in uniform. I start shouting, “Bachao bachao!” and call a friend who’s still in the gym. One of the guys shouts, “Eh phone nahi kholna!” and “Don’t call anyone!” 

Even though I’m shouting for help, no one on the street comes forward. There are hundreds of people around because it’s peak traffic time, but everyone just watches. I’m resisting, trying not to get pushed into the car, and eventually, they back off a bit because of the scene I’m making. They take me inside some random shop on the roadside instead. 

By this time, my friend from the gym arrives. He calms me down and talks to these guys. They say someone has disappeared from my circle, and they happen to know I’m one of his friends. I ask them who it is and tell them I’ll help if they just tell me what’s going on. But they refuse to give me any details. Instead, they demand I hand over my phone. 

I say no, I won’t show my phone. I tell them to ask me whatever they want, and I’ll answer, but I’m not giving them my phone. They’re adamant, though, and keep insisting I unlock it. The only “proof” they’ve shown me so far is some random blue-colored laminated piece of paper that looks totally fake. 

My friend, trying to de-escalate, says, “Okay, let’s just show them what they need.” Reluctantly, I unlock my phone and start searching for the numbers they’re asking for. None of the numbers they mention show up in my contacts, except for one guy I sent a “Hi” to back in 2022. 

They ask me who this guy is, and I’m like, “Bro, I don’t even remember what I had for dinner yesterday. How am I supposed to remember someone I haven’t talked to since 2022?” They say this guy is a peddler and ask how I have his number. I tell them a lot of people use my phone, and I have hundreds of contacts – it could be anyone. I even joke, “I don’t ride a bicycle, so I haven’t peddled in a long time.” 

They clarify, “No, ganja, heroin, drugs – that kind of peddler.” I straight-up tell them, “If you think I’ve done something wrong, let’s go get blood tests done or take me to the police station in handcuffs. I don’t smoke ganja. All I do is work and go to the gym. You can even come to my house and check.” 

I keep insisting that whatever they do, they should do it legally and not in this rowdy, kidnapping way. Slowly, the situation cools down because they can’t find any evidence on my phone. I tell them, “If you don’t have anything, I’m leaving.” One of the guys says, “Tumhe Bangalore me hi rehna hai na? Dekh lunga mai tumhe. Bahut ud raha hai tu.” 

This last incident was the fucking worst. I don’t even know what to do anymore. Bangalore’s police force is a joke – they’re just thugs in uniform, extorting money and terrorizing people. If you’re living here, be careful. These assholes will ruin your life for no reason.
