My brother purchased an iPad for me from Reliance Digital store in August 2020. 
He paid using debit card since the website and app of Reliance digital was not user friendly and upi didn't work then.
 
Then, in March 2021, he got an SMS that 50k was debited from his account. He immediately blocked his debit card and informed SBI, his bank. The bank took it up and cyber police filed a case. 
SBI, being SBI, always claimed they were busy with other such issues and cases and weren't very helpful.
He also registered a case at a nearby police station

Last month, we got to know the fraud happend from Reliance store itself. Somebody had forged a duplicate debit card and did a Point of Sale transaction and bought an iWatch for 50k! 
The police, after informing us this, washed their hands off and said they are helpless in getting us back the money. 
There is no lead from the bank either. Neither are they cooperating with us to get back the money. 

Aren't banks provided with insurance to reimburse customers whose accounts/ debit cards are misused?

My brother has lost all hope. I'm very guilty since the fraud happened after he bought the iPad for me.

Any chance to get the money back?
Will approaching the bank ombudsman expedite the case for us?
Any other ways to move forward? Should we sue the bank in a consumer court and Reliance since their website was not secure enough in the first place?

Advices, solutions would be very much appreciated. Thanks.
