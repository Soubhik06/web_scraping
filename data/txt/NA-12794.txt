 indicate any direct incriminating material against the applicant. The
 prosecution case is primarily documentary in nature, resting upon bank
 records, account opening forms, and death certificates, which are already in
 custody of the concerned authorities and are not liable to be tampered with
 by the applicant. It also appears that the complaint itself is anomalous in
 nature and has not been independently verified in a fair and impartial
 manner. Thus, in the considered view of this Court, the applicant deserves
 the benefit of anticipatory bail. Accordingly, the application filed by the
