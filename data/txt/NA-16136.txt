--- POST TEXT ---

I got a fake call from someone claiming to be from an RBI call centre. He told me he was calling me from my bank. I asked him which bank. He said SBI, PNB, etc etc all banks' calls are being made from there. 

He told me I was being called due to my failure to submit my Aadhaar card to the bank for which the deadline was 31st July. He asked me to share my Aadhaar card number with him or else my account would be freezed. 

I told him I'll share any details I need to with my bank and not to some random person calling me. He said I can't submit it in the bank any longer, since the deadline has passed. Fucker couldn't tell me which bank account I hadn't linked my Aadhaar with. 

While I'm sure most Randians are aware enough to not share fall for such a trap, I'm sure many around us are not. So please ask people you know to be aware and careful.
