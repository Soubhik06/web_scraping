* I am not from Hyderabad. 

Back in the 2000s, CTC was my tech paradise. Sure, shopkeepers were pushy, but you could find anything here. Fast forward to 2025: this place isn't just outdated – it's a death trap wearing a tech market costume.

Why I'm furious:

Service scams everywhere: Went to fix my laptop and the guy said "First I'll check if it's repairable, then quote a price." Next thing I know, he's charged ₹600 without even asking me! This happens all the time – ARN Computers did this to someone who paid ₹2500 upfront, then they damaged his motherboard and demanded ₹7000 more!

Narrow death traps: Except for the ground floor, those corridors in front of shops are so narrow you can barely walk. And what's the point of the spacious ground floor when the parking is such a mess?

Lifts from the Stone Age: Those rusty lifts still have an operator pressing buttons like it's 1995! Half don't work, and the ones that do creak like they'll snap any second.

Parking Mafia: Bought a ₹6000 worth of stuff? Still pay ₹40 parking or else. They've been fined ₹50k TWICE (in 2022 and again in January 2024), but they're still scamming people!

Shops = Garbage dumps: Paan stains, hanging wires, crumbling walls..

The irony? Hyderabad's building "Future City" with fancy tech parks, but CTC looks like it survived a bombing. Even the underground parking is now a cluttered dark warehouse – because who needs customer convenience?

I want to love CTC, but why risk it when Amazon delivers in 10 hours? Unless they renovate, sack the scammers, and fix those death-trap corridors, this place will die – and they'll blame "online shopping", not their own greed.

Has anyone else been scammed recently? Or found a shop that's actually honest?
