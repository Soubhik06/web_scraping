Hello! First time poster on /r/scams

We are first time travellers to India and had a bad experience today. The amount of people trying to get money from you is crazy and we got caught up in what appears to be a scam.

We were approached in a coffee shop by a nice young man who said he was attending the local university. After an hour or so of conversation, he suggested we change our plans and head over to a local tourist tour shop. 

We were sold a $450/person tour package. When we left, we looked up reviews for the place and they were all horrible. Hotels switched, high pressure upselling, hidden fees, delays, etc.

We called the agency maybe 2 hours after leaving and asked for a refund. To our surprise, they readily agreed. Both the front desk and the rep who sold us the package confirmed the refund verbally.

But when we asked for email confirmation, they emailed us back that they wanted us to go back to the office to take care of the paperwork. We alerted our CC companies to the issue. 

Now, the question at this point is, what is next best step? The tour was going to start tomorrow but we don't want to go. We're anxious and having a tough time at the moment.
