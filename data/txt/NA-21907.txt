Hello,

My elderly parents were scammed out of Rs. 6,00,000, in the pretense of booking an international flight via a bogus travel agency that they found via JustDail website.

The scammer collected an initial deposit of Rs. 70,000 via UPI and the rest via bank transfer RTGS, through different transactions between 15/10/24 and 18/10/24. They then deposited the amount to the airlines to get a booking confirmation, which they sent to us and then later cancelled to withdraw the entire Rs. 6,00,000 to their bank account. 

Upon realizing the scam, my parents have contacted Cyber Cell 1930 and local police station on 19/10/24. I wanted to weigh my options about recovering the scammed amount and convicting the scammers. I am worried that the slow response of authorities so far is diminishing their chances of recovering the scammed amount.

Thanks!
