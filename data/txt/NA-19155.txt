Fellow Indians,

i had put an ad to rent out my property. after some days of waiting i received a good lead. His Name was **Aniket Vijay Kalbhore.** we had our initial conversation over whatsapp. But i smelled something fishy.

1. He agreed to price (i had inflated the price on the ad)
2. He agreed to pay deposit without visiting the property because he was getting transferred from Assam to Odisha. he worked for AAI. My property is on the opposite side of city where the airport is located.
3. His hometown on CISF ID card did not match his hindi accent.
4. He quickly produced a photo of all his ID proof. Adhaar, CISF ID and a family photo.
5. Even when i insisted that my place was far from the airport he was eager to take it.
6. Did a search for his profile on social media. could not find anything other than recently created Facebook account with the same photo he had shared on whatsapp

So i said my state requires that i fill out a form with all tenants and their adhaar details and submit it to the nearest police station and asked to share Adhaar of wife and kid. At that point time he said to just his details and deferred from sending details of his family members.

I was certain that he was scammer and someone had fallen to identity theft. I blocked him and named reported the number on true caller. in the next few days i received multiple request from property listing websites with the same name. Even today i received another call with the same modus operandi.

I blocked him on whatsapp and on true caller stating as scammer. If you happen to receive a lead with that name , be certain that he is a scammer.

Stay safe India. Use a watermark while sharing digital copies stating the purpose and name of recipient. you can do that for free with Adobe scanner of Microsoft office android application.
