Got a call from this number 097691 08518

It was an IVRS call stating that my phone number has been used for illegal activities and all phone number registered under my name will be deactivated in 2 hours. Then I had the option to press 9 and speak to someone in the department.

I pressed 9. Meanwhile I checked the number on Truecaller and it showed the name Mahendra Mishra. He told me that my number was registered for doing some illegal activies on dark web along with my name and aadhaar card. This was flagged by Crime Branch of BKC Mumbai to Telecom Department. 

He mentioned another number that was registered under my name (I have never used that number) that was unreachable and then my current number. He told me that if I don’t file my complaint within 2 hours in person at BKC Mumbai then my number will be deactivated and a case will be filed against me and I wont be able to get any number on my name and or use UPI etc. Then he told me my name from his records. That name was the fake name I use on Truecaller. And hence I came to know it was a scam. Then he confirmed if I can go physically within 2 hours to BKC. I told him ‘yeah sure’. He confirmed a few more times that I can go physically. I told yes. I told him that you can confirm in my aadhaar that my address is Mumbai. He said yes. And that’s when I disconnected.

I am more concerned about our parents who may fall gullible to all these scams.
