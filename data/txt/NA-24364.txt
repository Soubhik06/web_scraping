Hi,

Looking back now I'm 99% sure this is a scam but unsure as to how would it work? How would he benefit and what would be the perp's next step?

Was waiting for my office cab ~ 10 AM. 2 Hindi speaking gentlemen show up in a scooter gauge if I knew Hindi or not.

They claim they have one iPhone 15 and a MacBook Air M2 for sale. They are on the way to the delivery truck and they want to sell them right away. He is ready to sell both for even Rs 60,000. Manager will take it for free, I'd rather get some money out of it.

Brand new pieces. He uses them, shows me the specs. Shows me his Aadhar card and is ready to give me a photo of it as well. He also shows a bill form them (just hand scribbled)

I ask him to wait till the evening, he says he can't. Needs to sell right now because of the waiting truck and being new to the city, he can't keep them anywhere till the evening.

Even let's mebpay Rs 20,000 on the spot and then the remaining amount in the evening once I've used them.
Swears on his mother that he's a genuine guy, shows pictures of his family.

I resist the temptation and let it go. I tell him that I'll call him in the evening, if it's gone it's gone. It's okay. He keeps reminding me I'm missing out on a great deal.

Now I'm aware replicas of Apple Air Buds exist in the market. But could this Mac Book have been fake? Or was it a stolen piece and it'd get me into trouble with the police eventually?
