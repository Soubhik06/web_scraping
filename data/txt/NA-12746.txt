Heard the learned counsel appearing for the parties. 2. The petitioner is absconding since 4th July, 2023 and has not responded

 the notice issued under Section 41A Cr.P.C and it is not denied by the

 petitioner. Prima facie, it appears from the case diary that it is a case of

 money laundering and bank fraud in which various persons are involved

 and prima facie, it shows that Aadhaar Card of the petitioner has been

 used for transaction. 3. Considering the materials available in the case diary, the nature and extent

 of complicity of the petitioner in the commission of the alleged offence

 and there had been transaction in the bank account of the petitioner for

 which he could not offer any explanation, we are not inclined to grant

 anticipatory bail to the petitioner. 4. Accordingly, the prayer for anticipatory bail of the petitioner is rejected

 and the same is accordingly disposed of. 5. All parties shall act on the server copies of this order duly downloaded

 from the official website of this Court. (Soumen Sen, J)
