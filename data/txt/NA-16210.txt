There was a very recent post about this, and probably many will come again. 

The execution is quite similar in many cases. You get a call, especially at odd hours informing you about re-linking of aadhaar, need to pay a big fine unless you reverify, better card upgrade etc. All you need to do is give them your card details and the OTP which was sent to your phone. After that, you'll get a transaction SMS of money being withdrawn and the number will appear switched off.

My cousin works in a bank, and he says these people come to complain about these type of fraud a lot. Bank won't do anything since it was a negligence from your side. Police too almost never helps in these kind of cases. Basically, the money is gone permanently.

The best thing to do is to inform your parents about how these scam works, unless they already know. Do this to your cook, maid, driver, whoever else you think can be taken advantage of. 

A small step from you will help someone prevent experiencing a traumatic experience. Losing money feels bad, getting cheated is much worse.

Tikona internet looted Rs.5000 from me by faking a call from police station that I have been summoned. This was years ago, but I still feel sad/hurt about that incident.
