We are from India but staying outside from last 5yrs so not well aware of the Instagram shopping much in India. My wife found some interesting trench coats for 1599 INR and wanted to give it a shot by opting for Cash on delivery. Without checking the reviews(my mistake but 99.99% of times usually I check reviews on every website), i have placed the order and paid cash ignorantly without checking the product. 
After opening the box which should be a trench coat, found some cheap night dress co-ords set(last pic) which is hardly 300-400rs. Then wanted to chat with support via their WhatsApp support number and turned out that it doesn’t have WhatsApp even. Read the insta reviews on their posts and found that it’s a scam website and selling some fake products like this. 

This is just a rant and awareness post so that others don’t fall for these kinda trap/sketchy pages on Instagram.
