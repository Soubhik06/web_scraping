New Scam from Kotak Bank:

They deactivate your mobile app saying SMS verification is not taking place. All their conditions are met - you have entered your CRN and MPIN correctly, you are using your registered mobile number (which is the primary/only SIM on your phone). 

Still they say cannot be verified and to call phone banking.

Their phone banking number is a paid number. Once you connect they keep playing ads - and remember you are getting billed for the call. Then they ask you to enter your MPIN. Even the if you enter your correct MPIN they say you have entered it wrong, and exceeded maximum possible attempts.

So they ask you to a phone banking offer. Promptly you get a message saying all our officers are busy now...

Moral of the story: you have spend several minutes - again I reiterate, you are getting billed for the call - listening to their promotional drivel

Bravo Kotak Bank. While others pay to advertise to their customers, you make your customers pay to listen to your promotional crap.
