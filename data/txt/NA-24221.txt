My brother is interviewing for a job at an Indian IT company (80k+ headcount). Before the HR round, the company sent out an email asking to provide the login/password for the ITR portal and EPFO portal to a 3rd party BGV provider.

I have serious privacy concerns about this as this 3rd party can steal his life's financial information including assets/liabilities, capital gains, dividend/interest income, foreign assets and income. The company claims that they will "only access form 26AS", but they are also asking for a liability waiver if the data is leaked or is misused. The company also said that they cannot proceed with the offer without this BGV step.

Is this legal and recommended from a privacy perspective?

The 3rd party is [Digiverifier](https://www.digiverifier.com/) (their website is currently offline, which makes me doubt them even more)
