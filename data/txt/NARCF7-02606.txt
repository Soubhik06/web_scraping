I have been using rapido bikes for quite some time to go to college (for 1.5 month now) . From so many rides , I have encountered 3 rides in which the biker gives his own QR Code of bank and not the one on UI of his Rapido app.
So the scam happens like when we pay the amount , the money deducts from your account but is not received on the Biker's account due to "delay in receiver's acct." . Which 'could be' glitch bypassed by some bikers .Then they ask you for cash of the same amount telling they will return your money on UPI when they receive it on their bank but we all know the reality . You go on to pay double the money . Though , I am not really sure if it really is a scam or just temporary bank issue .

Either way ,Only way to avoid it is to use only Rapido UI QR Code and not personal even if they insist you to .
