ORIGINAL POST BEGINS ----

The first time it happened, I thought the guy must be deaf. I asked him to refill for 500 Rs, he obliged but for Rs. 70. So, I had to ask him to refill again for 430 Rs. I am pretty sure he only filled air the first time.

The second time I went there, another guy refilled for 120 Rs. while I had asked him for Rs. 400. Out of curiosity, I went there again yesterday. Guess what? It was a completely different guy this time who refilled for Rs. 90 while I had asked him to refill for Rs. 500. AGAIN, I am pretty sure he only filled air the first time because it only took 2 or 3 seconds before he said "90." 

The first time it happened, I gave the guy benefit of the doubt. But the second and third time, I was pretty lucid so I know the workers didn't mishear what I had said. The machines are rigged and the owner seems to be involved too. 

These people think I'm a lone, stupid girl and they can scam the shit out of me. I'm planning to go there next week so I can take a video and post everywhere. Let this be a heads-up for anyone from Ahmedabad who lives near S.G. Highway.

**EDIT:** There are two IOCL petrol pumps in Bodakdev. The one I am talking about is next to **Tomato's on S.G.Highway.**
