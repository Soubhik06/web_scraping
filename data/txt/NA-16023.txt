Hey fellow Redditors,

I hope you're all doing good. I wanted to address a pressing issue that many of us have either experienced firsthand or heard about—the alarming increase in poor customer service within the realm of online shopping. It's disheartening to see how this trend has created a negative mindset among consumers, ultimately impacting the overall online shopping experience.

With our population and consumption rates soaring in India, it's no surprise that even prominent giants like Flipkart have faced incidents of fraud and unsatisfactory customer service. This raises a pertinent question: if industry leaders struggle in this regard, what can we expect from small businesses?

Unfortunately, the majority of Indian consumers are not well-versed in the intricacies of legal frameworks and the various options available to address their grievances. This knowledge gap further exacerbates the issue, leaving many feeling helpless and frustrated.

However, I believe that together, we can make a significant difference. It's time to come together and establish a community-driven resource group that provides practical solutions and spreads awareness. We need a collective effort to compile a comprehensive list of options for effectively handling customer service issues.

In addition to platforms like subreddits or Discord channels, I propose the creation of dedicated wiki pages. These pages can serve as a centralized hub of information, offering guidance on how to navigate customer service challenges, providing examples of success stories, and sharing tips and tricks for achieving positive outcomes.

Moreover, we can extend this initiative for addressing issues related to government procedures. Many of us have experienced challenges when it comes to obtaining documents or accessing government schemes. By extending our focus to this area, we can empower each other with the knowledge and strategies needed to navigate these bureaucratic hurdles.

If you are aware of any existing groups or initiatives that align with this cause, please do share them with us. Let's collaborate, consolidate our knowledge, and ensure that our voices are heard.

Remember, change starts with us. By raising awareness and actively engaging in finding solutions, we can foster a more consumer-friendly online shopping environment. Together, we can turn the tide and make a positive impact on the industry.

I greatly appreciate your time and support in this endeavor. Let's make a difference, one step at a time.
