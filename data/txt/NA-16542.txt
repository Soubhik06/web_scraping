I ordered airpods last week on amazon. Or arrival it was all fine. After few hours i noticed static noise on one side. And i was like, its okay theres a 7 days replacement policy. 

I call up amazon, first executive says i have to contact apple directly cery rudely and cuts the call when i asked to transfer to another senior executive. 

Second call, he explains to me multiple times how i have to go to apple first and get a job card and then contact amazon. If i go to apple, they’ll replace the product under warranty for me directly. So this ‘7-day replacement’ is a scam. 

This was an expensive purchase, but still an airpod. What if it was a mac ! So thats it im dont with it. 

Never order anything expensive online. You lose your time and peace of mind for a few thousand bucks discount. Rant over.

EDIT: I think my point isnt conveyed well here because i was ranting. I have also ordered several electronics from amazon including mobile phones. They replaced stuff without any hassle if there were any complaints in the initial 7/10 days. Even mobile phone they offered to replace if it was defective. However theyre telling now that only in case of apple we have to go to the brand. 
This was weird since i dont see anything like that in the replacement policy. At this point they should remove that ‘7 days replacement’ theyve kept just beside the buy button. To me and many folks like me i believe that is one reassuring thing that allows me to peacefully buy things online.
