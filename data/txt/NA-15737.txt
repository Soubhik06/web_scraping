##Do you know that Indian citizens lose more than Rs. 100 Cr every day!

Here is one of the frauds happening over the phone, called Customs fraud that goes something like this: "Emergency! Your parcel is stuck in customs. Dial x number immediately!"

Here is the recording of an actual scam call: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ZSdLN719Z76PsGKs7gOgJT5EJsPZL38C/view?usp=sharing

You should disconnect immediately! In fact, you should never pick a call from an unknown number. Never accept a connect request on Whatsapp from any unknown person.

The attacker usually

Pushes you into a sense of urgency to induce panic on you
Tells you to keep a secret.
Threatens you (electricity disconnection / water supply disconnection / jail etc)
Sens you a link promising unbelievable 'offers' - free gold coin / free lifetime mobile talktime etc
Asks you to install an app (and then takes remote control of your mobile)
Asks you to share an OTP sent to you. 

DO NOT DO ANY OF THE ABOVE

Otherwise the attacker can take out ALL the money in YOUR bank account.

Remember that your mobile no. is linked to your bank ac number. Those who have linked Aadhaar to bank account number are more vulnerable.

You get a call from an unknown number? 

DO NOT PICK

Report the calling number to the telecom operator, including the calling number, date &amp; time of call, nature of scam; and recording of the call (if you have recorded it). The telecom operator will investigate and shut down that number.

Report the calling number to the Cybercrime division of State Police.

Here is a good reference on how to protect yourself and your loved ones from cybercrime.
