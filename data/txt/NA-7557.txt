Heard counsel for the parties. These bail applications have been filed by the applicant,
who has been arrested on 27.04.2014 and is in Jail in
connection with Crime Nos.16/2014, 12/2014, 21/2014 9/2014
and 17/2014 respectively registered with Police Station STF,
Bhopal - commonly known as VYAPAM Examination Scam
cases - for the offence punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 477 and 120-B of IPC and also under Section
3(Gha) (1)(2) / 4 of the M.P. Manyata Prapt Pariksha
Adhiniyam, 1937. The role ascribed to the applicant by the prosecution is
that of a middleman. The applicant facilitated the beneficiary
to commit unfair means during the examination conducted by
VYAPAM. The applicant interacted with one Rinku, who
arranged for impersonator for commission of the offence. It is
stated by learned counsel for the State on instructions that
Rinku has since expired. Further, investigation qua the
applicant is complete in all respects in all these crimes. The
beneficiary as well as impersonator have been arrested and
moreso the co-accused Shiv Kumar Yadav, who interacted
 with the applicant has already been released on bail by this
Court on 01.04.2015, coupled with the fact applicant through
counsel undertakes to appear before the Investigating Officer
as and when called for further investigation including against
him as also to punctually appear before the trial Court to
ensure early disposal of the criminal trial. In the interest of justice, taking overall view of the
matter, we deem it appropriate to grant bail to the applicant on
following strict conditions:- The bail applications stand disposed of accordingly.
