Here's a list of some of the scams that are rampant all over India. This list is not exhaustive so comment out some other scams and their Modus Operandi and how the people can avoid being a victim of these:

1. **Banking frauds:** These involve someone calling from the bank and giving you some story. The story isn't relevant, what is relevant is what they make you do. **Do NOT give out any details about your bank account under any circumstances. No net banking credentials, no card details, no bank account details, no Aadhar number or anything, and Especially not an OTP. DO NOT GIVE OTPs TO ANYONE OVER CALLS.** If you're really concerned if it was really the bank, visit the bank branch and verify. Most cases it won't be, banks don't call usually asking for details because they already have it.
2. **Video calls s\*xtortions and blackmailing:** This involves video calling a person, usually by a girl. The moment the person picks up the call, they screenshot it, when the victim and the girl are clearly in the frame. They blackmail on either sending the video to the cops, or to the family members if they don't pay up. You'd see a lot of these profiles on dating apps where it's just a WhatsApp number and the profiles ask to video call, but you can get some calls without even being on these apps. The best way to avoid this, **don't pick video calls from unknown numbers.** And do not make the mistake of paying because once you do, it'll never stop. Go to the police if you fell victim, but try not falling victim altogether.
3. **Tinder date scams (only in Delhi so far but still beware):** Involves a girl baiting a guy into meeting in person after meeting on a dating app. She'd recommend a shady bar/lounge where you'll be made to pay the bill, which would be 15-20k easily for a few drinks and snacks, and if you don't there would be bouncers to beat you up. Bunch of cases have came from Delhi so be careful. **Meet at a coffeeshop ffs guys.**
4. **Credit apps:** There are apps which give you small loans for personal expenses and what not without much redtape like traditional banks. When you start delaying on payments, their recovery teams (or probably event 3rd party recovery agents) harass you and your family members and friends, since these apps have access to your contacts. Hurling abuses, blackmailing that they'll morph your pictures (anecdotal evidence but won't be surprised if they said that). Sometimes these loans accrue a lot of interest for rather insignificant amounts. **Steer clear from these apps at all costs. Don't even download them if possible.** If you want a credit card, go to your bank and not these apps.
5. **Social Media account hacking:** I was very recently gonna fall prey to this but I avoided it due to quick presence of mind. A friend of mine texted me "I lost my Instagram account, I can't seem to login. So my 3 friends have to verify that I really lost my account. There would be a code on your mail, I'll need it to verify". There indeed was a code but for a password reset, which was funny because his password reset code was in my mail. So I tried logging into my account with that code and it was indeed for resetting my account. Going back to the first point, **DO NOT SHARE OTPs OR ANY PASSWORD LIKE THINGS WITH OTHERS, EVER.**
6. **Package Delivery Scam:** For high value items, amazon and many other companies require you to give an OTP in order for you to receive it. Unless you tell the OTP, the order won't be marked delivered to you. Scammers might try and get you to tell them the OTP, and once you do, keep the package. There are also delivery guys who would break the seal beforehand, probably replace it and then put it back. Amazon has this advisory, don't accept if the seal is broken. If you give the OTP for a broken seal item, it would be assumed that you received the product perfectly fine. So, going back to the last point, **NEVER REVEAL THE OTP WHEN THE SEAL IS NOT INTACT, OR WHEN THE DELIVERY DRIVER HAS NOT ARRIVED WITH THE PACKAGE.** In which case if a scammer might have got a hold of your package but you didn't share the OTP, you can claim refund or the package would be redelivered.

There are many others, but these are the ones I could think of right now. Stay safe everyone.
