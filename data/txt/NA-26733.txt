In Today's India it is very easy to get a Loan. Normally loans help people financially in different situations. But while these loans are used to show off in society it became a burden. Basically today's youth are very active in social media, they like to show their status by using expensive items, which basically they can't afford but Easy Loans fulfill their needs. The Indian loan trap refers to the cycle of debt many individuals fall into due to easy access to loans with high interest rates. Predatory lending, lack of financial literacy, and excessive dependence on credit cards or personal loans often lead to repayment struggles. Many borrowers take new loans to repay old ones, worsening their financial situation. Unauthorized lending apps also exploit borrowers with hidden charges and harassment. To avoid the trap, individuals should borrow wisely, read loan terms carefully, and prioritize savings. Government regulations and financial
awareness programs aim to curb such issues.

So basically financial awareness is very important in Today's India
