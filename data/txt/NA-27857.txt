--- POST TEXT ---

A week ago, I ordered an item from a third-party seller on Amazon for $50. A tracking number was provided, but when the item was listed as having arrived at my house, what I got was nowhere near what I ordered. I received a package that was ordered from Walmart by the seller (it was listed in Walmart's invoice as "Ordered By: [The Amazon Seller]" and "Shipped To: [Me]"). According to the invoice, the item I received had a $4 value, and with free shipping.

I contacted them about the incorrect order, and they gave me a very template response about how sorry they are for the mix-up and how I can return the item to them for a refund. The message was sent at around 3am in Ohio time, and the name on the message was a name of Indian origin which did not match the seller's name, which felt weird considering this just looked like a family-run Ohio business on the outside.

Looking through their customer reviews, I see some questionable things among a lot of generic 5 star reviews. These questionable reviews matching my experience. An incorrect item was shipped, they were asked to return the item. In one case, a reviewer said that an invalid return postage label was given to them, and in other cases, people said the refund was never authorized (though they may have just never followed up after receiving it.)

So this feels like a scam to me. My questions are: What should I do from here, do I just go along with the return process like they want and see what happens? Is there something else I could/should do in this situation? Is this a known type of scam that I've never heard of before?
