This petition is filed seeking regular bail in FIR No. 838, dated

26th October, 2021, under Sections 120-B / 420 / 465 / 467 / 471 of IPC, later

on added Sections 419 and 468 of IPC registered at Police Station Quila

Panipat, District Panipat. 2. The FIR was lodged at the instance of Complainant-Salim. He

alleged that he was known of Savvej, Furkhan (petitioner) and he knew

Ravinder. The complainant went to the shop of the Ravinder for updating

his Aadhaar card where Savvej asked him to give copy of the Aadhaar Card

and several blank papers were got signed for opening his bank account.

Later the complainant gained knowledge that vehicles were got financed in

his name. The role attributed to Ravinder is that from his shop, laptop,

printer and Rs.10,000/- were recovered. Petitioner accompanied co-accused

Savvej and Janisree along with the complainant and he actively connived

with them for managing the scam. 3. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that no active role
