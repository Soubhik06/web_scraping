Beware of AI scammers, people wearing other people's faces, instagram accounts with some real followers, dozens, even hundreds of posts manipulated with AI. Take Jessica for example, https://i.imgur.com/U8qVnbb.jpg

 There was a similar one I found named Iinda (anagram for India, with the lowercase L being the uppercase i) but *he* deleted *his* account before I thought to grab evidence. 

Video faking is just as easy as photo faking.

They will probably mention bitcoins, or some type of stocks or trading. 

You send these people pictures and videos of yourself moving around and talking, you're giving them your face and voice to use on someone else, or to use for some other scam.
