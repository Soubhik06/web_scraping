--- POST TEXT ---

I was initially against watching this one, because all that goes on the TV is "Election, election, election... hurrrr". It's too much. Why would I want to watch a movie on politics/elections when I'm forced to watch election news all the time. 

I kinda had a rough idea of what this film would be like. Cliches, over-acting, under-acting, typical Bollywood over-the-top situations, so on and so forth. But for the first time, I decided to be optimistic about something and look where it got me.

I left with (moderately) high hopes and came back with a splitting headache. I am not saying that the movie is objectively horrible, or you shouldn't watch it. It just happens to be a bit too Bollywood (read "unrealistic") for me.

The story (**Spoiler Alert : Heavy Spoilers**) :

Abhimanyu Kaul (Jackky) is a video game developer who lives in Tokyo with his girlfriend Anwita (Anwita Sharma). I never quite understood what his girlfriend does for a living, but let's move on. One night, Abhimanyu and Anwita come back home dead drunk after a song's worth of partying and are lying on roof passed out. That is when Abhimanyu receives news that his father (Boman Irani), who also happens to be the PM of India, is on his death bed and leaves to Orlando to check on him.

After they meet, Abhimanyu's dad, about to die, asks him to take up the post of interim Prime MInister of the country. Abhimanyu is a bit shocked, but accepts. Anwita, his girlfriend, is upset with him because he had promised to never enter politics and how this will destroy their relationship. Abhimanyu somehow manages to console her and save their relationship.

*at this point, my headache is getting worse, so I'll cut short the highly detailed synopsis I was about to write and focus on the major stuff*

Abhimanyu becomes the PM, He starts off a bit naive, and gets made into a joke by his finance minister. His relationship with Anwita also gets rocky when she gets to face the side-effects of being the PM's consort, but nevertheless tries to support him as much as she can.

Cut to the scene where the couple goes to Agra to visit the Taj (which turns into a song, you guessed it). Next day, his live-in relationship with his girlfriend is exposed by the paparazzi. Abhimanyu, surprisingly, turns really cunning after this attention and successfully silences all his dissenters and opponents by taking total control.

Things seem to get better, but one day, his girlfriend finds out that she is pregnant. Abhimanyu tries to keep this under wraps, but this too, is exposed by one of his fellow partymen, who wants to take the post of PM candidate for himself in the next elections. Abhimanyu resigns as the PM.

Now the acting PM, Abhimanyu causes a sensation by exposing his own party secretary with data from a secret CBI inquiry. After some more backstabbing and manipulative BS, he now has total control over the party.

*cue falana dikhana bullshit campaigning montage*

AND THEN HIS PARTY WINS 272+ SEATS ALL BY THEMSELVES AND FORMS THE GOVERNMENT FUCK YEAH

The movie ends rather abruptly with Abhimanyu and Anwita at a village meet, Anwita deciding to marry him while Abhimanyu is walking towards the podium to address the audience,

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Good :

1. The late Farooq Sheikh. He's the biggest reason I decided to watch this movie. And he delivers as the personal assistant to the PM. His performance makes this movie worth a watch.

2. Neha Sharma. Although many of her scenes have very cheesy dialogues, her acting is nice. The people who wrote the dialogue could have done a much better job for her part.

3. Some of Jackky's scenes. The film has a few juicy scenes like when Jackky talks to his finance minister and some others. But they aren't totally worth watching the movie.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE BAD

- The movie overdoes the whole "Youth Power" thing, making it seem very MTVesque.

- Jackky develops games on a Mac. I might be wrong, but last I remembered most games, even console games, were developed on WIndows. He might totally be running bootcamp, but uh, I forgot what I was going to say.

- The screenplay for some of the romantic scenes. The intro is f*cking annoying. I just wanted to get up and leave. I've lost count of the number of times Jackky and Neha hug each other in the movie. The film literally begins with Jackky hugging her whle they are both in bed goddamnit!

- You get incredibly lame songs thrown at you in quick succession. The music is really bad. The title theme makes me want to puke.

- The background score isn't any better than the music. I was expecting more from Salim Suleiman. They abrupt changes in the musical score immediately makes you know what is going to happen next. The sudden metal guitar riffs to highlight supposed badassery on Jackky's part doesn't help either.

- Boman Irani's appearance is billed as a cameo, but to me it seems something between a cameo and a full-fledged appearance. His incredible talent is still left underused, though

- Neha Sharma's character gets incredibly annoying at times. Wanted to say "Look, lady, your boyfriend is the goddamn PM of India, please don't disturb him for stupid shit just to prove your authority over him." But maybe it is Jackky's fault bringing his work home?

- The movie's statement that "ALL OF INDIA'S YOUTH ARE PROGRESSIVE, URBAN AND LIBERAL". Never heard of a conservative 20-30 year old, guys?

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Eerily Similar (or, the list of real life things/events they stole for the film)

***- The movie takes a clear swipe at Arnab Goswami with a character named Parnab who even says "the nation wants to know!" and gets told by Jackky.***

- The ABKP (Jackky's party) resembles the Congress. Even their election symbol is a fist, similar to the Congress. At the beginning of the film, it is even explicitly stated that the current government is hugely unpopular, a fact that many people would largely agree with for UPA-II.

- Jackky's character, Abhimanyu Kaul resembles Rahul Gandhi in a ton of ways. Son of a PM. Said PM happens to contest LS elections from a UP constituency. The son has to accept responsibility after the father dies. The son used to live abroad before coming back home.

- There is a split within the party due to the demand for the creation of a new state (Telangana).

- There happens to be a Bengali guy who gets made the president by Jackky to win his confidence. (Pranab Mukherjee)

- There is a fighter jet scam. (Chopper scam)

- Jackky's campaign montage features him eating with villagers, preaching to them, *a la* Rahul Gandhi, again.

- The conservative opposition is set to win before Jackky comes along and destroys them with Youth Power. (the NDA)

- YOUUUUUUUUUTH POOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOWER

Does RaGa have a girlfriend? I don't know.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Overall, if you can tolerate the frequent cheesy emotional scenes and all the crying, this movie can prove to be watchable for you. But definitely not worth the 3/5 rating given to it by some local daily I read this morning.
