 employed in the context of a plaintiff under the said sections 134(2) and 62(2) . Thus, in addition to the

 different place, the plaintiff can institute a suit at the place

 the subordinate office. (c) The third case is where the plaintiff has a principal office

 where its subordinate office is located. In this eventuality,
 the plaintiff would be deemed to carry on business at the
 place of his subordinate office and not at the place of the
 principal office. Thus, the plaintiff could sue at the place
 of the subordinate office and cannot sue (under the scheme
 of the provisions of section 134 (2) and 62 (2) at the place
 of the principal office. (d) The fourth case is where the cause of action neither arises
 at the place of the principal office nor at the place of the
 subordinate office but at some other place. In this case, the

 subordinate office. And, consequently, it could institute a
 suit at the place of its principal office but not at the place
 of its subordinate office. 29. Here it is pertinent to mention that one must not
