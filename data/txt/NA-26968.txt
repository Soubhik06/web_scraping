I never thought I’d be writing something like this, but I’m at a point where I have nowhere else to turn. I’m a 20-year-old student, currently preparing for the SSB (Services Selection Board) exam, and yesterday, I got trapped in a Telegram-based scam that left me in debt, emotionally broken, and extremely anxious about what’s next.

The scheme was through a group called Indicoin. At first, it seemed totally legit. They offered quick returns:

I invested ₹1000 and received ₹1200.

Then ₹3000, and got back around ₹4000.

Feeling hopeful, they said I could become a “permanent member” if I invested ₹7000. I believed this was a genuine opportunity to earn some money. I’m a student—I don’t come from a lot. So, I gathered ₹2000 from my savings and borrowed ₹5000 from my elder brother, planning to repay him within 30 minutes from the expected profit.

They added me to a Telegram group with three others. I didn’t know them, but we were all following the same instructions. Then disaster struck. I made a small mistake while following one of their steps, and it led to all of us losing our money. Suddenly, everyone blamed me.

The others were older—25 or 26—and were already earning. I was the youngest, and the pressure broke me.

Then came the “solution”:

Pay ₹21,000 to recover your lost amount.

Or, pay ₹51,000 to get back your lost amount plus ₹51,000 as a bonus.

They all submitted ₹21,000 and got their money back. I didn’t have that kind of money. I was shattered. I had already borrowed ₹5000 from my brother.

So I did what I could—I even went to a gold shop and tried to pawn my earrings, just to collect enough to pay my brother back. I somehow borrowed ₹5000 from a close friend and gave that to him.

Now I owe ₹5000 to my friend, and I have only one month to pay it back. Since then, I’ve been going café to café, shop to shop, trying to find a part-time job. But I barely have any free time—I’m studying full-time and preparing for one of the toughest exams in the country.

I’m emotionally and mentally drained. I made a mistake—but I’m doing everything I can to fix it. I’m not sitting idle. I just need a little help.
