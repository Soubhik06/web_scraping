--- POST TEXT ---

I was visiting my sister in Gurgaon for a day and had ordered some food on Swiggy. The delivery person while delivering the order had tears in his eyes. When asked, he told me his wife has cancer and someone from the family has gone to get medicines but he/she is short on money by 1500 rupees. He was literally crying and that made me feel bad for him. I couldn't tell if he was genuine or not, but since I've experienced my share of scams in Bengaluru, I knew better. I told him I'll be willing to help him rightaway if he can share me a prescription for the medicines he needs. He tried to deviate me from that ask by just crying more and touching my feet. Upon insisting, he tried calling a number and asking him "kaha tak pohach gaye ho?" and then tried to hand me his phone saying "aap inse baat karlo, ye batayenge aapko". I denied speaking to that person and just asked this delivery guy to send me a whatsapp photo of the prescription. He tried to make an excuse that the person who's on the phone has the old kind of phone (not a smartphone).

I then just gave him my phone number, and told him to go right now and send me a photo of the prescription from his phone later. I told him that as soon as I see a photo of that prescription, I'll send the amount via UPI rightaway. After hesitating for a while he went away and I didn't hear back from him.
