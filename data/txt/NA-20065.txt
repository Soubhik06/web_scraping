Is someone getting a copy of my Aadhar card any different than someone getting a copy of my PAN card. 

What can be done with your Aadhar card info that couldn't be done with your PAN card falling in wrong hands?

In fact I feel Aadhar is safer than other IDs since it is possible to verify that you are the same person whose card you have submitted as ID proof. Pan cards, driving licence as well as voter IDs have no such system of verification.

Note: I am not talking about biometric info leak. I am talking about demographic data and aadhar number that is printed on the card.
