19 year old college student here. i got my debit card a few months back, never really used it. idk much about how any bank stuff works and i should probably be aware of it especially after this incident.

earlier this month, i received a message at around 10:52 pm saying my sbi debit card was blocked on my request (which i never requested). a minute later, i got a call which came from a landline number from jaipur (my branch is in chennai). in truecaller it showed as sbi and it was verified but when i later checked in my phone's call logs it showed as junk. a guy with a thick hindi accent and broken english told my name and he seemed to be concerned that if i was the one who requested to blocked my card. i was scared atp so i gave the phone to my mom who is also an sbi account holder. she made him hang up before he could further say anything. but at the end he said "if you don't believe me mam, just contact your sbi branch." 

right after the call i recieved another message saying my debit card was not processed successfully. i rushed to the yono app to ensure if my card was actually blocked and it literally was. my mom berated me saying how foolish i was to get scared of a call from sbi at 11 pm cuz sbi will never do that and she said i should beware of stuff like this, especially as a law student. i felt bad. how did my card get blocked without me requesting it? this makes me question sbi's security and i regret choosing it now. i am yet to call the helpline or go to my branch cuz i get anxious about this. was this actually a scam, did i trust truecaller too much? what do you guys think? has something like this happened to any of you?
