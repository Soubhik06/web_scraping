I was born in Kanpur but didn't get to spend much time there. I always loved visiting it (even though it's was shitty as compared to Bangalore).

But as time flew almost all my cousins left too (they were the reason of me loving the city). 
But now I still visit the city once a year.
Spend a week like a loner. Visit my village. Hear people talk about how expensive my farmland has become. 
Watch my uncles try to manipulate my dad to scam money.

I always wanted to live in Kanpur. But now everything has changed. Infact now I can actually move back too. Dad has retired. I have a remote job. I have been planning on getting into Agri-Tech as well (I know IoT).

I always used to feel that maybe people in kanpur won't understand how my mind functions. Basically I felt I was superior in some way. Which isn't true anymore. Atleast not since Internet got mainstream. You all are damn cool actually. 
I wish I got to spend my childhood in my native.
It was hard growing up in Karnataka. I had to learn to fight back. It's hard being a boy without an elder brother. Without cousins or uncles to watch your back. I had to do it all by myself.

I Don't even know if the City would accept me back anymore. I've been gone for too long. 

I love Kanpur. Always have.
