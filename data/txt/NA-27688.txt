A recruiter emailed me a position. Pretty standard stuff, they wanted me to send them a resume and then later send them answers to a set of questions. I figured this is just pre-screening that would result in some face-to-face interviews. But after just two emails, where I received very little job detail, they're offering me the job. At risk of bragging, it's for a shitload, $150k. For my career and experience, it's not out of possibility. But thats a salary upper management makes and I ain't there yet.

I'm struggling to find how this is a scam since for the past two emails, they've only asked me for information that is easily available on my LinkedIn and resume. On top of that, the email domain seems legit and the position is open on the company's website. The company itself checks out. That being said, something that did pop out to me is that they're going to be sending me a check to buy my own equipment. I've attached the suspicious part below. 

On top of that, there's a bunch of little things that are adding up that are raising some eyebrows 

* They're giving me less than a day for any response, including accepting the job 
* They're using language that's far too formal but very colorful. Lots of 25 cent words. It kind of seems like an AI or translator wrote it. On top of that, they keep using the phrase “please kindly”, which a lot of my Indian clients use. 
* Their insisting this entire exchange should happen over email 
* For a business role, they're asking me to use a macbook. Yes I'm going to mention it again, Because that makes absolutely no sense.
* It's a San Francisco based job but they're exclusively emailing me at 8:00 a.m. EST

I've already asked for a face-to-face via the recruiter and I'm going to be reaching out on the company's official website as well.

In the meantime, any advice is greatly appreciated any advice is appreciated
