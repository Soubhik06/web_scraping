**Background**

You might have read / watched news stories about the revival of Bombay Talkies - the historic film studio of Himanshu Rai and Devika Rani.

Leading newspapers and TV channels raved about a guy (not naming him because of possible Rule #6 violation). The guy claimed that he was "reviving" the historic Bombay Talkies. He also claimed that his grandfather Raj Narayan Dube was a co-founder / co-owner / main financier of the Bombay Takies.

**What really happened?**

The guy found a new company, and misleadingly named it "The Bombay Talkies Studio".

Now, this is not the first time the Indian media has fallen for such a gimmick. A few years back, some guy bought a company founded in 2000, and the Indian media declared "[The East India Company That Ruled Over Us For 100 Years Is Now Owned By An Indian](http://www.mensxp.com/work-life/leadership/27287-the-east-india-company-that-ruled-over-us-for-100-years-is-now-owned-by-an-indian.html)".

But the Bombay Talkies hoax goes way beyond that: This dude created an entire fake history of his grandfather's association with Bombay Talkies and his pivotal role in the Indian cinema. In reality, the grandpa is a nobody, whose name is not found in any source that predates this PR exercise.

**How the hoax was created**

* The guy and his PR team created two websites - one about his grandfather and one about his new company (which he presented as the continuation of the historic Bombay Talkies). Both the websites contain an elaborate history of how Raj Narayan Dube was "the pillar of Indian cinema". Not linking them here, but you can google "The Bombay Talkies Studio" and "Raj Narayan Dube".

* They even changed the [original logo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Logo_of_Bombay_Talkies.jpg) of the Bombay Talkies, replacing Himanshu Rai with [RN Dube](http://i.imgur.com/nlrt24a.png).

* They also created several social media accounts. Can't link here because of the subreddit rules. But an example: the Grandfather's "fan club" on Twitter has 56.7k followers despite having only 3 tweets (2 of which are re-tweets). Obviously, most of the followers are bots / fake profiles.

* They contacted the media, and declared that the grandson of Bombay Talkies' "founder / financier" was reviving the studio. The media took this PR puff at face value, and published the story without verifying it. He and his PR team also created / edited several Wikipedia articles (Bombay Talkies, Raj Narayan Dube etc.), using these news articles as sources. Gradually, several blogs, social media posts and one printed book picked up this hoax.

**Who was fooled?**

IANS, a widely-used news agency picked up the hoax. As a result, it was seen in a large number of publications:

* [Business Standard](http://wap.business-standard.com/article/news-ians/not-ready-to-compromise-with-my-film-abhay-kumar-116011700550_1.html)
* [IBN Live](http://www.ibnlive.com/news/movies/bombay-talkies-studio-all-set-to-be-revived-with-an-anti-gandhi-film-titled-gandhi-vs-aazaad-1185415.html)
* [Indian Express](http://indianexpress.com/article/entertainment/bollywood/bombay-talkies-studio-to-be-revived-with-anti-gandhi-film/)
* [NDTV](http://movies.ndtv.com/bollywood/old-is-gold-revival-time-for-bollywoods-defunct-banners-626535)
* [The National](http://www.thenational.ae/arts-culture/film/why-old-is-gold-in-bollywood) (Abu Dhabi)
* and many more.

Original reports were published in several newspapers. For example:

* [ToI / Mumbai Miror](http://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/hindi/bollywood/news/Bombay-Talkies-revived/articleshow/10299071.cms) / Vickey Lalwani
* [Hindustan Times](http://www.hindustantimes.com/bollywood/bombay-talkies-reopens-after-58-years/story-IxCR0b9vP9iJBCHrYzkxiN.html) / Collin Rodrigues
* [Bollywood Life](http://www.bollywoodlife.com/news-gossip/iconic-studio-bombay-talkies-reopens/) (Zee Entertainment) / Shweta Parande
* [The Sunday Guardian](http://www.sunday-guardian.com/news/bombay-talkies-of-devika-rani-fame-set-to-be-revived) / Mamta Chitnis Sen

Wikipedia was also updated using these sources as citations.

**How was the hoax uncovered**

MelAntipam -- a French Wikipedia editor, who was apparently cross-checking the list of Bombay Talkies films in French Wikipedia article with the English Wikipedia one, found some discrepancies in the list.

As he studied the article more closely, he came across the hitherto unknown "Raj Narayan Dube" and the claim of the studio being "revived".

He did some pretty [solid research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talk:Bombay_Talkies#Rajnarayan_Dube_and_Bombay_Talkies), and discovered that the new company is a completely different entity, and Raj Narayan Dube's association with the historic Bombay Talkies is a hoax.

Unfotunately, the hoax was created during 2010-2013. So, by now, it has been picked up by hundreds of sources that mirror news websites, social media posts and Wikipedia articles. But try using "Custom Range" feature to restrict the search results before 2010, and you'll realize that it's an obvious hoax.

**TLDR**

Some dude created a small-time company named "The Bombay Talkies Studio" and claimed that he was reviving the historic Bombay Talkies. He also created an elaborate hoax that his grandfather was the co-founder/main financier of Bombay Talkies. He spread this hoax through two websites (about his company and his grandfather), social media and some press releases. Indian media picked it up and published this claim without any verification. A French Wikipedia editor uncovered the hoax.
