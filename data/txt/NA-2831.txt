Aspiring actors, models, and child artists often dream of making it big in the entertainment industry. However, there are unscrupulous individuals and companies who prey on these dreams by running scams to make a quick buck. One such company is Cast You, which is notorious for its casting portfolio scam. In this article, we will take a deep dive into their fraudulent practices and provide you with resources to avoid falling prey to such scams. Cast You and Highline Studios - The Scam Exposed Here's a step-by-step breakdown of the casting portfolio scam run by Cast You and Highline Studios: An aspiring actor, model, or child artist comes across an ad on Instagram or Facebook promising opportunities in the film industry. They fill out a form to express interest. The aspiring artist receives a call from a representative of Cast You, who claims that numerous opportunities are available and invites them to meet with a talent head at their office. At the office, the artist is shown fabricated success stories of people who have supposedly landed roles in ads, movies, and fashion shows through Cast You. They are then convinced to pay for a portfolio created by their associated photographer and studio, Highline Studios, starting at ₹15, 000. It's important to note that Highline Studios is owned by the same people behind Cast You. After paying for the portfolio, some artists receive their pictures, while others never do. For those who receive their pictures, when they call Cast You to inquire about the promised work, they are told that no such promise was made. The fine print on their invoice states that there is no guarantee of work, despite being promised otherwise during the meeting with the talent head. The Talent Heads are part of the scam, making false promises to earn commissions on sales. They exploit the emotions of parents, promising their children work, knowing full well they cannot deliver on those promises. The Casting Portfolio Scam has victimised countless aspiring artists, and Cast You and Highline Studios have numerous complaints and police reports filed against them.
