Scammer - " **my father's name**, do you remember me?"

Dad- "i don't, who's this?"

Scammer - "you forgot me already? What are you doing? Are you busy?" 

Dad - "im busy doing my work"

Scammer - "how can you forget me? Guess where you met me"

Dad - "**place where he works** ?"

Scammer - "yes!! You're right, now listen, I've got into a huge problem. My account is not working so a person will send you ₹ 75000 to your account and I need you to send ₹ 75000 through gpay but you can send me ₹50000 for the treatment of an ill child right now. I'll send you a whatsapp message, check it. "

My father tried to check his account if he received any money but the scammer was constantly interrupting him by either calling him or messaging. Eventually my father refused to give him money and the scammer says slurs and then hangs the phone.

My dad was almost about to believe him because of the way he spoke and thought he works in a higher position than him.

Just shared this to let people know. 

My dad said thar scam calls are pretty common but this one seemed legit.
