--- POST TEXT ---

Last week when I was at home I received 10 sms during a span of 3 hours on my mobile about transactions carried out on my credit card. 

I frantically searched for the credit card and thankfully it was still in my wallet. I immediately called the customer care and told them about it. They blocked the card and told me that since the transactions are not yet reflected in my account they can not take any fraud complaint requests and asked me to call back in 48 hours. 

I am not really sure how received sms for the transactions but still those did not reflect in their system. Its possible that I had wrong understanding of what the customer care person said to me. One more thing which i didnt understood is how the total amount of transaction exceeded my available credit limit (credit limit - outstanding billing amount)

I called them back after 2 days **and by then all the transactions reflected in their systems** and hence I registered a fraud complaint for all the transactions. I have been told that I will need to fill up and fax a form to their fraud line after which an investigation will take place which may take about 45 days after which if the findings are in my favor then i dont have to pay anything and the charges will be waived off. 

I am not sure what happens if investigation findings goes against me then I guess I will either have to pay the charges or refuse to pay them and deal with the collection agency and getting my cibil records screwed up. I guess I can also take up a legal action through consumer court.

Initially I assumed that it might have been a glitch in their software as I received sms but nothing reflected in their system. But now after googling on this I think that the my credit card information was stolen by someone and used to make those transactions.

I am now considering making a complaint with cyber crime department of police in my city. Does anyone else have any similar experience to this? Any tips on how I can get out of this mess? 

Edit: Added clarification marked in bold

**Edit2**: Dispute resulted in my favor. Bank has removed the charges from my account and I am applying for a new replacement credit card.
