Ok, background. I have been trying to find training for Hadoop, a big data technology. In my search I found one in Montreal that is supposed to start tomorrow. I've been looking into it for a while and I can't get in contact with the organizer who claims to be a global provider of training. The event is registered with Eventbrite, and after a while of researching the organizer, I've concluded (for myself) that they are a sham. I've contacted the venue and they said they have no events scheduled. The training is $1400. I am unable to contact anyone at the company, the named organizing person, their phone number listed on their site, the contact us stuff etc. Nothing. The company is based out of India, their headquarters is in New York, their site is hosted in the Netherlands, domain registered with GoDaddy and their phone number directs me through Kansas. Yet nobody can be bothered to pick up the phone.

So I spent a while trying to see if I could report the event to Evenbrite. I can't even find any information suggesting anyone has the ability to report any event. So this got me thinking, how much of a cut does Eventbrite get for events? A quick google and it ranges from 5.5% +$0.99 per ticket to as low as 3%. So Eventbrite has an incentive to allow fake events, and given that their refund policy is chosen by the organizer Eventbrite seems to be free of any liability. Has anyone had any experiences like this or find it odd that you can't report a scam given the site's size and popularity? I'm trying to not be paranoid, but I'm also trying to not get scammed which, lets face it, the internet does a lot.
