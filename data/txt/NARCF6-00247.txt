Got Scammed Today at IGI Airport 

I got Scammed Today at IGI Airport 
Totally my fault for falling for the same. Just writing this post to spread awareness.

Today I went to the Airport to pickup someone and was waiting there.
A boy approached me , looked like a College going student. He asked if I had UPI ID, then showed me that his Kotak UPI isn't working due to server issue and he doesn't have much cash.
And needed to send 2000 to cab.
When I questioned a bit, he also told me that I can keep his airpods as a security.

Yes I won't lie , I initially thought it might be a scam, but once I was stuck in a similar situation and a guy helped me with 100Rs (which I returned obviously).
So, I thought of paying it forward and help him.
He took my number , gave a call and promised me that he'll return as soon as his UPI is back up.

Long story short, later the phone was switched off (The number : +919266453523 )

Later, when searching , I came across similar scam stories in this thread.
One cannot imagine that such scams happen even in places like IGI where security forces are spread everywhere.
It was totally my fault for falling into this, Just writing this post to make people aware , so noone else fall for anything similar.
