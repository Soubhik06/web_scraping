--- POST TEXT ---

Yesterday I booked a cab through Uber from T2 of IGI airport in Delhi. The projected price shown on app was 289 Rs. But on reaching my destination, the driver showed me on his phone that the fare was 498. I was shocked to see such an increment in fare. Then I realised that he hadn't ended the ride and he was showing me a screenshot of the fare that had to be given. When I pointed it out, he started getting aggressive and asked me not to touch his phone. I could see on my phone that the ride had not ended. When I showed him that, he started arguing that your status is not updating because of poor internet. I also realised that he didn't move from his location for a long time after accepting the ride because he was creating the data for the screenshot. I argued for a lot of time with the guy, but then realised that this guy is hell bent on committing this fraud. My bus was also about to leave, so I shouted at him and gave him the money.
Fortunately I got the balance money as credits in Uber after I raised a complaint.
So, I am posting this to create awareness and I request you guys to be vigilant about this scam.
