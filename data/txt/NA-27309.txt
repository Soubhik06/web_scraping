I received a call this afternoon from someone named Steve Miller. He said he saw my resume and profile on Monster and asked if I was interested in a position within my career field. I replied yes, and he even asked me what my desired pay would be. The call kept getting cut out, and he sounded Indian (an immediate red flag). I asked if he could e-mail me the details, and he immediately did. What he requested from me right now is in the image below: 

https://preview.redd.it/1ft8z6uidy0e1.png?width=1867&amp;format=png&amp;auto=webp&amp;s=edc60493527629c8f912da548408dbc9949e688f

I have a list of red flags, such as many misspelled words and an iffy phone connection. The only thing that makes me second guess this is a scam is the number from which it comes. This person claims to work for Evolve Esolutions, and the number seems similar (not exactly the same) as their company website's contact information. I checked out their website, and you need to sign up to be reached out by one of their employees. I'm not providing any information until I know whether this is a scam or not.
