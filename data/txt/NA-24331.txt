Hi all,

This has been posted multiple times on this sub but I want to remind everyone again of this scam going on in Bangalore.
 I saw a 1BHK property in a gated community (Celestial Greens) for 13k. Thought that was kind of low but still contacted the owner. He was very sweet and before visiting asked me to pay 1k for visitor ID which he told would be refunded . 
I should’ve known by then what was going to happen but I was so desperate for a place and the pictures were also quite good that I paid the amount. 
After payment another number called me saying that he’s the property manager who will give me my ID. 
I reached the apartment only to find out there’s no one with that name and there are no 1BHKs as well. 
I called him immediately and abused him and he blocked me everywhere.
Please don’t be stupid like me and BEWARE of these scammers.
