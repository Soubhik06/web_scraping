Hi everyone!

Another scam fyi. I'll keep it simple.

Random "poor" guy (PG) with a wife and a kid beside him: (in hindi), Hello sir. I'm from Mumbai. I came here for work. Owner is not paying money and we are stranded. We have to go to Mumbai. But I won't ask money for that. I just want money for feeding my family. Please give Rs. 200 or more.

Me: Sorry, I don't know Hindi (even though I can perfectly understand it)

PG: (makes hands signs for money and food, points to his kid who looks like he just wants to get this over with and go play)

Me: Sorry, I don't have cash

PG: I will take UPI, PhonePe, Google Pay

Me: (wondering how UPI is a boon and a bane as well) Sorry. Ask someone else.

PG: Please help. They say feeding a kid is like feeding god. God will help you in future.

Me: Sorry. No.

PG: Something bad will happen to you tonight. You will go up (pointing to the sky).

Me: WTF. Fuck off.

\_\_\_

Was speaking to my friend about this and she says someone has asked money from her with similar Mumbai immigrant with wife and kid story. Has this happened to you too?
