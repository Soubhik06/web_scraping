Hey everyone,

I wanted to share a horrible experience my friend had with a wedding dress boutique called Kesar Sarees (Address: 19-20, Professor's Colony, Bhawarkua Main Rd, Indore - 452001). I feel like it’s my duty to warn others before they become victims of this scam. My friend had a really unfortunate experience with this store, and I hope this post can help others avoid a similar situation. She booked 2 Lehenga's worth INR 150000 and paid INR 50k in advance.

From the moment my friend placed the order, things started to go downhill. The customer service became almost non-existent, and the lehenga they eventually delivered was of extremely poor quality—not at all what was promised. And as if that wasn't enough, they delivered it way past the agreed date, which caused a lot of stress and inconvenience.

But here's where it gets even worse: after delivering the subpar product, they demanded extra money from my friend. When my friend tried to stand up for themselves, the store's staff turned completely unprofessional. They started blaming my friend and challenging her to file a complaint. They tried to intimidate my friend using CCTV footage and phone call recordings, knowing that their shady store policies were in their favor. The store is run by Suyash and Anuj Gattani, and they were involved in these shady tactics.

What’s even more infuriating is that when my friend and I tried to post a negative review to warn others, we noticed that the store owner has been flooding Google with fake positive reviews! It's pretty obvious too—many of the so-called “happy customers” have the same or very similar surnames. This is clearly an attempt to drown out the negative reviews and maintain a higher rating.

My friend ended up losing 50,000 INR in this scam, and I just can’t stand by and let others be tricked the same way. Please be cautious and think twice before dealing with Kesar Sarees. No one deserves to go through such stress and lose their money to a fraudulent business, especially when planning something as special as a wedding.

Stay safe out there, and don’t hesitate to share this if you know anyone who might be considering this place. Let’s spread the word and protect each other from scams like these!
