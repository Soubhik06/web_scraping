Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and learned

 counsel for the State. Petitioner seeks bail in connection Minapur P.S. Case

 no. 265 of 2020 registered for the offence punishable under sections 414 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 472 , 120B / 34 of the Indian

 Penal Code read with sections 25 (I-b)a, 26 and 35 of the Arms

 Act . Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that

 petitioner is in custody since 046.01.2021 and charge sheet has

 been submitted and is person with clean antecedent. Learned

 counsel for the petitioner submits that the informant in the FIR

 alleges that on secret information, police reached near SBI ATM

 and seeing the police force, four accused on two motorcycles
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.34361 of 2021(2) dt.01-12-2021 2/2 fled away, one accused was apprehended who was in the ATM

 who disclosed his name as Mithun Kumar and also named the

 accused persons who fled away including this petitioner and on

 search one loaded country made pistol with live cartridge was

 recovered from the waist of Mithun Kumar along with one live

 cartridge of 8 MMKF, one ATM cloning machine, mobile with

 sim, 4 ATM cards and one motorcycle. Learned counsel submits

 that petitioner was not apprehended on the spot and has been

 falsely implicated in this case. Learned APP opposes the prayer for bail. Considering the facts that petitioner is in jail custody

 since 04.01.2021, charge sheet has been submitted and is a

 person with clean antecedent, the petitioner is directed to be

 released on bail on furnishing bail bonds of Rs 10,000/- (ten

 thousand) with two sureties of the like amount each to the

 satisfaction of the Chief Judicial Magistrate, Muzaffarpur in

 Minapur P.S. Case no. 265 of 2020. (Satyavrat Verma, J)
