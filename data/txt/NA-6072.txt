 applicant was aware of the registration of the FIR against him so much so he
 filed anticipatory bail application before the Sessions Court. Despite such
 knowledge he did not join the investigation and was declared a proclaimed
 offender. There has been a consistent disobedience of the applicant to join
 the investigation. 17. Keeping in view the law and judicial precedents, anticipatory bail
 shall not be granted to a person who is a declared proclaimed offender
 except in exceptional circumstances. The present case does not form an
 exceptional or extraordinary case where the discretionary powers of the
 Court be exercised. Hence, in light of the cumulative circumstances,
 applicant is not entitled to seek benefit of pre-arrest or anticipatory bail. 18. The present application is therefore, dismissed. RAVINDER DUDEJA, J
