Hey everyone, I’m sharing my story so that nobody else falls for the scam I experienced. Please read this carefully and stay alert.

A few months ago, I came across an article on either ET India or TOI (I can’t seem to find it anymore, even after checking my browser history multiple times). This article led me to a site called Traling (https://www.traling.com/en/home), which claimed to be an automated trading platform. The website looked very professional, so I decided to enter my phone number out of curiosity.

Soon after, I received a call from someone claiming to represent Traling. They seemed extremely knowledgeable about the markets and trading strategies, which made them appear credible. They explained how the platform worked and convinced me to start with a small deposit. On July 4th, I deposited $200. Then, over the next few months, they continued building my trust, persuading me to invest more:

$300 on July 11th

$600 on August 7th

$10,000 on August 13th

$5,316 on September 24th

They assured me that increasing my capital would reduce my risk when opening trading positions. They used polished Excel projection sheets, a professional-looking website, and regular interactions with “Fund Managers” and “Senior Fund Managers” to convince me of their legitimacy. It all seemed so real, and I trusted them completely.

What made me especially vulnerable was my personal situation. I have an autoimmune condition, Psoriasis, which is not curable, and I already had a student loan of ₹18 lakhs. The combination of my financial struggles and ongoing medical expenses made me desperate to earn more money. Their promises of high returns played into this desperation.

Eventually, they convinced me to take out loans to increase my investment. I ended up borrowing an additional ₹10 lakhs to make payments to them, bringing my total loan amount to ₹27 lakhs. I’ve been working since my second year of college to support myself, but now I feel completely trapped.

I now realize this was a well-orchestrated scam. They used psychological tactics to manipulate me, preyed on my vulnerabilities, and made everything seem legitimate. The losses I’ve incurred have been devastating, both financially and emotionally.

I’m sharing this here because I don’t want anyone else to fall for this. These scams are highly sophisticated, and even smart, cautious people can get tricked. If you come across platforms like Traling or similar schemes that require constant deposits and push you to borrow money, DO NOT ENGAGE. Always double-check their credentials, read reviews, and consult a trusted financial advisor before investing.

Stay safe, and if anyone has experienced something similar, please share your story so we can warn others.
