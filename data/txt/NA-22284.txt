Day 0 -

So I have been living in Europe for a little over a year now. My parents, who live in Bengaluru, wanted to send some homemade food items, along with some coconut oil and some clothes. They contacted [parcelchief.in](https://parcelchief.in), a reliable (?) service to send parcels abroad. A company representative got in touch with my dad through phone and they took the parcel from our door to their local hub in Mumbai, from where the parcel would be scheduled to be sent to Europe. They said we only have to pay once the shipment reaches Mumbai. We paid about INR 2000 in advance.

Day 2 -

I ask my father (who is in touch with the representative) to get me a tracking number for the parcel. They sent a [tracking number](https://imgur.com/a/EqdGLUH) which didn't work - and upon asking about this, they just replied very nicely with "[Your package is in transit](https://imgur.com/a/UI6YG0Y)". Shady already, since neither my parents or I were able to track a local shipment and had to rely on e-mail to find the parcel. We paid in total about INR 6000 for the shipping.

Day 4 -

Upon contacting them again, we found out that the parcel had [just reached](https://imgur.com/a/ucpZibd) Mumbai. They said they would check the weight details.

Day 5-8 -

No communication whatsoever.

Day 9 -

This is where the supposed scam happens. They send an email which says "There was a fire accident at our warehouse in Mumbai". [This](https://imgur.com/a/63Sh1fC) is the entire email that they sent, and it's not believable, not a bit.

They also send some whatsapp image attachments, which seem so fake I've never seen anything like this. A quick google search tells you that there was no such incident reported.

My mom is crying as she's emotionally blown away by all this. She prepared a lot of homemade food with love and I've not seen her in almost a year now, and this happened to us. We just had to send one parcel; just one. fucking. parcel. I want some legal advice regarding this, as nothing seems to add up really. What can I do about this?

&amp;#x200B;

Edit: I forgot to mention one thing, they tampered with the package. They said they wouldn't allow coconut oil and that they will take the contents of the package and re-pack themselves for safety purposes. I have not heard of anyone who does that, and my parents don't know a whole lot about it.
