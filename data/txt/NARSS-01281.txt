So my father recently got a w.a call from this number. I was beside him so i obviously said its from Pakistan and its definitely scam call so we didn't pick. Still my father was not 100% convinced its a scam call. He checked the dp and said its look mallu(obviously not😫). So anyway since I know nothing will happen just with a call and my father was in doubt he decided to call back and I agreed and decided to lets see what happens. But the call completely shocked me. After picking he said my father's full name which is kinda not a big thing. But suddenly he said my full name and asked if he is my father. I definitely got shocked. They even know our family details. He asked where am I(where is his son), my father replied why he want to know what's the problem. The scammer became rude and said just don't waste my time and tell me what i asks Iam CBI. I suddenly said Pakistan and hang up the call. Now my parents are asking if I did something online and is police behind me, why did I suddenly hang up, let him speak etc.🥲😫😭 Guys I understand most of the people in this sub would be educated enough to know these scams, but not our parents. Please educate them about these. In our case if I wasn't beside him during this call or he used my brother's name who is suddenly in Mumbai, my parents would definitely be terrified and may got scammed. Please be safe guys!
