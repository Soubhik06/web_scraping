Things you will need: A smartphone, Internet connection, a blank SIM.

1) Firstly target a gullible person whose SIM hasn't been linked with Aadhar card. Now call and tell them to connect with Aadhar using your instructions (see step 2) or their SIM will be deactivated.

2) Tell them to send an SMS to 121 (for Airtel might be different for other carriers). The SMS being "SIM &lt;The number of your blank SIM&gt;".

*From hereon this only works if the person hasn't setup UPI before and the mobile number is linked with the bank account*

3) Now that you have gotten hold of their SIM. Install BHIM or any UPI app and set it up using the OTP you will be getting on the newly conned SIM.

4) Now transfer the funds to accounts of your liking. Preferably conned accounts of other people. Take the money out via ATM (send someone else) and viola you have gotten 1.3 lakhs.

**Edit:** You might also need some bank account details of the victim like debit card number and date for UPI PIN. Better analyze and gather information before proceeding.

**PS:** This is based on the recently posted story of the guy who got conned of 1.3 lakhs possibly like this. The above info is based on the discussion of that thread https://www.reddit.com/r/india/comments/76cw74/aadhaarsim_linking_fraud_how_this_man_lost_rs_13/.
