This is to bring to your attention about a scam going on in Railway stations in Madhya Pradesh. There are people claiming they are from MP Police Department by showing FAKE IDs and Pictures with top officials. So, the scammer first goes to a random person and asks for money citing some emergency and shows the fake IDs and pictures with the top officials of Madhya Pradesh Police and asks for something around 2000 to 5000 rs. This scammer then asks that he will pay in cash but later says that he will ask his friend to pay you, he will try to win you with words and asks for your random details to gain familiarity and even try to gain your trust by saying you can take the money back but won't give it back immediately. And then he will call after 1 hour saying he is stuck in something and will try to pay you later. the later just never comes, the UPI ID is associated with someone named PRYAS SHARMA1, UPI id s702391280@okicici with name as sonam sharma.

My uncle was scammed by this guy (3k rs), Although we filed FIR, there is just little hope for return. Just wanted to let you all know and give your opinions regarding this.
