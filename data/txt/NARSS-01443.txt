I'm from South Kerala and come from a Christian family. My aunt, who is my father's sister, is involved in Zoom meetings with some individuals she refers to as "brothers" and some other housewives with similar backgrounds also join them. These "brothers" encourage them to sing, pray, clap to paise god . Their prayer sessions usually start at 9 p.m. and sometimes go on past midnight. My aunt, a retired government employee, has been living with my family (my dad, mom, brother, and me) since my childhood. She has never been married and worked in the government.

Unfortunately, both of my aunt's parents have passed away, and since her retirement, she has been deeply involved in these nighttime Zoom meetings. Her loud praying can be heard from outside her room, and these meetings are hosted by these "brothers." While my family members are not supportive of her involvement, we've mostly let her be as long as our family life isn't disturbed.

Now, my aunt wants one of these "brothers" to stay in our home temporarily, and my family strongly opposes this request. His audacity for him to ask such a thing of a woman!. These "brothers" seem to take advantage of elderly people, especially those with money and feelings of loneliness. Since my aunt's retirement and the loss of our grandparents, she has been feeling quite lonely, which has escalated her involvement with these individuals.

I've cautioned her against giving them any money, and I've made it clear that I won't provide assistance if she realizes they are fraudsters. While my family and I have faith in God and attend church, we don't share the same level of enthusiasm as my aunt does for these meetings. We've tried talking to her about it, but it hasn't been effective. However, the news that one of these "brothers" wants to stay with us has really upset me.

These individuals seem to be running scams, and I'm wondering if there's any legal action or means to report such activities. It's troubling to see vulnerable people being taken advantage of for financial gain, and I know there are many others who have fallen prey to them. This is a serious concern, as it appears that these people are on the rise. I'm aware of another retired couple with substantial wealth who have also become targets. My advice would be to stay away from individuals like these.
