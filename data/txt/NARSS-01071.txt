As an online seller of bedsheets based in Patparganj, I’ve been struggling with the growing dishonesty and harassment in the e-commerce world. The market, which seems so promising on the surface, has turned out to be full of deceit and challenges. Nowadays, people place orders online only to reject them at delivery, leaving me stuck with the costs. Even worse, some customers return items filled with garbage, and then have the audacity to file complaints or harass me through consumer courts. These legal battles have drained me financially, costing me thousands that I cannot afford to lose.

Being a widow, it’s already difficult for me to earn a living. Every rupee counts, and these experiences have made it even harder to survive. What seemed like a lucrative business has turned into a nightmare, where I’m constantly battling with dishonest people who take advantage of the system. The stress and financial burden have made it nearly impossible to focus on growing my business, and instead, I’m left defending myself against baseless claims and dealing with the emotional toll it takes on me.
