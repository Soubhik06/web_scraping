I know this is an old scam but thought I would share this to alert anyone not aware of this.

Usually you will receive a call from an IVR service with a recorded message that your parcel could not be delivered to the destination and you can choose to press 9 or 0 to speak to a customer service expert.

Once you select the option, a customer service expert will talk to you saying that your parcel meant for Indonesia or Thailand is stuck at customs as it had some narcotic substances. When you say you have not sent any such parcel, the customer service expert will say that your Aadhaar card has been used in the process and you may be subjected to a CBI or police enquiry. I went through this process once and diaconnected the call.

I checked online for similar cases and there were plenty.

From here, they could threaten unsuspecting victims to shell out lakhs of rupees as bribe to escape from the legal action. They could impersonate themselves even as CBI officers and come on video calls, as explained by some victims. 

This happened to me about 8 months back. Surprisingly, I received one such call even recently. It is amazing a specific cybercrime option could go unchecked for 8 months.

Please stay safe.
