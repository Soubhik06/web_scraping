--- POST TEXT ---

I just got a phone call from an Indian woman claiming to be the FBI, who knew my full name and phone number, and was claiming if I had heard from "my local law enforcement" about an identity theft case with my info. They refused to provide any information other than the phone number, which I called back and did come up as the Syracuse NY FBI branch. She then gave me to her "manager", who proceeded to try and BS me for personal information and then started screaming the same thing over and over when I asked for a badge number or ID number. WTF?

Clearly it was a spoofed number and some sort of identity theft. Jokes on them, I worked for DOJ, and the actual FBI Albany NY field office was very interested to hear about this.
