To set some context and to put in an Indian angle to this conversation, let me share a recent &amp; essentially unrelated story:

[A snake was about to devour a deer at Jim Corbett park when a man intervened, disturbed the snake with a branch who then let go of it's grip and the deer scuttled away and the snake slithered away, without a meal](https://twitter.com/_VineetVashist/status/1267718903230332928)

As with every single action that occurs now a days - our desi Twitter-sphere was divided. Some praised the man with the branch for his intervention, some blamed him for intervening in Nature's food cycle, but most importantly - there was a massive debate in the Comments Section. [#wearelikethisonly]

But essentially - a very natural process was disturbed by one man's (possibly misguided) judgement - while another man gained Internet fame by posting it online. 

And i hope this helps to segue into the real matter at hand - and shine the light on Indian keyboard warriors, activists, social media enthusiasts, political figures and - how can we forget - our beloved celebrities - who want to jump on the "Black Lives Matter \ All Lives Matter" bandwagon - with little or no background about what either of these statements stand for - which has resulted in a devastatingly wide variety of results. 

To summarize quickly, the "Black Lives Matter (BLM)" movement started off in &amp; around 2013 (and gained tremendous momentum in 2014) due to the (widely considered unlawful deaths of) African American men - Trayvon Martin, Michael Brown &amp; Eric Garner. 

Three women are widely considered to be the originators of the hashtag &amp; the movement itself - Alicia Garza, Opal Tometi and Patrisse Cullors (who have since worked to build upon the movement with a wide range of enablers). That being said - the movement is not centralized &amp; has no actual hierarchy or structured offices. 

Unfortunately - the movement makes headlines every year since police brutality, government's indifference, downright injustice, blindingly obvious racism against African Americans continues to manifest itself in the form of violence, assault, death &amp; outright murder. The reason why the movement is front &amp; centre again in 2020 while the world is still grappling with a global pandemic no less - is because of the police killing of George Floyd in broad daylight by Derek Chauvin (while being flanked by 3 fellow officers) -and having undeniable video evidence. The act itself follows the highly delayed arrest of 2 white American men (father and son duo) for the daylight shooting of Ahmaud Arbery, who was jogging.

The two cases have polarized &amp; mobilized Americans across the length and breadth of the nation, who complained about the horrendous mismanagement of justice &amp; active violence by the very hands of the police personnel. Arbery's killers were only arrested in May - over 2 months after the actual act of killing - due to bungled police response &amp; indifference in understanding the scale of the matter at hand. It also took for the video of the chase &amp; shooting &amp; eventual collapse of Arbery by the side of the road to be "accidently leaked" on social media for people to completely digest the sheer horror of the racist act. 

[Meanwhile, Floyd's desperate please of "I can't breathe" fell on deaf ears as he called out to his mother &amp; for water - but eventually succumbed to death by traumatic asphyxia - due to Chauvin kneeling on his neck for almost 9 mins.](https://twitter.com/darealtishaaa/status/1268528760036438019/photo/1)

Subsequently Americans all across are flooding social media with posts, statements &amp; their views while using #BlackLivesMatter - and marching on the streets across all states (and even in foreign countries) in record numbers. 

Due to the undeniable horror of systemic police brutality &amp; neglect by the government for African Americans (and indeed other minorities) - the movement has garnered attention like never before. With protests in large numbers - crowd management &amp; policing too is being demanded on an unforeseen scale. 

Since police &amp; government officials are largely in denial of their own role in fuelling these acts of injustice &amp; horror - striking back &amp; doubling down on protestors is the last resort they have chosen to deploy.

Headed by racist in chief Donald Trump's constant rhetoric &amp; history of dog whistling - an already heated situation has escalated into a mammoth social &amp; political epoch - with the upcoming November 2020 elections looming as well.

All this while back home in India - the past few months have seen a tremendous amount of social &amp; political discussion due to the happenings in our own back yard

* the riots in Delhi preceding the Delhi elections.
* the vilification of Jamia &amp; JNU students, violence against them, their capture &amp; arrest by local police.
* multiple instances of atrocities against our Muslim brothers &amp; sisters. 
* the death of Saadhus across multiple states 
* the throttling of civil rights in Jammu &amp; Kashmir (along with access to Internet) - and the various instances of violence against Kashmiri folks.
* the horrible mismanagement of the Corona Virus Lockdown &amp; subsequent financial, healthcare crisis that has been unleashed.
* the indifference towards the migrants, labourers &amp; impoverished - who were left to fend for themselves after being let go of jobs, let go of their houses &amp; having no means of livelihood.

In all the above - the police have played an undeniable &amp; rather violent role. Despite having irrefutable video evidence, footage captured by news channels &amp; actual victims - the ruling party &amp; powers to be are not ready to let go of their vice like grip on the nation's "dissenters &amp; anti national voices". 

Dalit activists were jailed, non Brahmin boys are being beaten up for their refusal to say "Jai Shri Ram", women are being harassed (as in the norm, irrespective of age, caste, creed, religion, financial status) &amp; action by the police against the actual perpetuators of such acts are always delayed &amp; muted - amounting to nothing more than lip service. 

2 other events have added jet fuel to the powder keg of divisive vitriol which already exists in our Indian societal consciousness:

* placing Safoora Zargar in jail for her alleged involvement (as a mastermind, no less) of the above mentioned Delhi riots. Safoora is pregnant; and suffering multiple other medical issues. 
* the death of an elephant in Kerala due to the consumption of firecrackers. the female elephant was pregnant, ingested explosives - which led to the slow &amp; painful death of them both.

Since the elephant's death took place in Kerala - the BJP IT Cell was quick to latch on to the story, add their usual communal &amp; political twist to it, in yet another attempt to turn the sentiment against Kerala - given their relative success with the handling of the Corona virus situation, their general rejection of the Centre's propaganda &amp; their well educated, diverse demographic. 

Within a day - the false narrative and completely fabricated geographic, communal details of the death reached the country's households due to the power of Whatsapp, Social Media &amp; the barking maniacs who are our TV "news reporters". 

By the time facts were brought to the fore &amp; all agenda was stripped bare - the outrage (as usual) was directed &amp; seething from the mouth ensued. Artwork was created, poems were written, religion was invoked - but the truth itself was discarded. Correction &amp; fact checking remained as usual - and afterthought.

Meanwhile, Safoora is Muslim, educated, bearing child &amp; female - hence most unfortunately placed in the cross hairs of India's rabid right wing media, uncles &amp; aunties. At the same time, Sadhvi Pragya continues to plug Hindutva, gau mutra all the while staying in power &amp; staying away from jail under the guise of being "ill". 

[Falsities about the status of her marital state, her pregnancy are only compounding to the muddled narrative - despite the truth being a whole other matter.](https://twitter.com/QuintFactCheck/status/1258388369052061697?s=20)

So obviously being the convenient &amp; insensitive idiots that we are - we lumped this under one single stupid slogan - "All Lives Matter".

Please understand that the very origins of All Lives Matter are mischievous &amp; downright hurtful in essence. The "ALM movement" was perpetuated to simply dilute the very focus of BLM - rather than to enhance it. 

For a lot of Caucasian American people - the very idea of the "focus" being shared &amp; a reminder of their own "White Privilege" is an emotion that worries &amp; scares them. They're enraged that no one is shining a light on their struggles - *while failing to clearly the truth of how exponentially harder it is for people of colour to come up to level, let alone surpass their white counterparts on the basis of talent, merit &amp; hard work.*

[For some background, here's a helpful video from Hasan Minhaj for Patriot Act](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i_FE78X-qdY&amp;feature=youtu.be)

Adding elephants to the mix (and the deer from the first paragraph) doesn't make the All Lives Matter "more inclusive" it trivializes the life of a human being who is in actual hell - to an animal who lives in nature &amp; outside the purview of civilization. It shows insensitivity that you consider a human who has suffered at the hands of a fellow human - is just as essential as a chicken which is being consumed at a local restaurant - and thus both the acts of slaughter should be *equally* treated with contempt. 

Our celebs - who's only gods are money (and fame) - using ALM as some shining beacon of equality. That they had the utter lack of a spine to talk about the atrocities happening in their own motherland to their country men &amp; women is a whole other matter. 

**So if you want to talk about it animals &amp; pets and their lives - feel free to have THAT conversation. Do not lump humans in it.**

**If you want to highlight the plight of Saadhus &amp; Kashmiri Pandits - do so rightly &amp; justly.**

**If you can &amp; want to speak out against the systemic &amp; communist sentiment that exists in the country against Muslims - please do so.**

But when you lump an African American man killed by the police, a pregnant woman jailed under false &amp; draconian charges for "sedition against the nation" &amp; a pregnant elephant who was unfortunate to consume an explosive, toxic which was not even intended for her - under the same "movement" - you're doing a disservice to both - while looking like an complete chutiya at the end who is an ally for no one and just an insufferable attention seeking idiot.
