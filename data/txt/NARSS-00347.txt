I wanted to buy a book and the online delivery was taking more than 3 weeks. So, I thought I'd pick it up from a store. I called a couple of book stores in Delhi to check if they have it before heading out. Oxford book store in Connaught place had a mobile number listed. I called them up and the guy said the book is available but I can't pick it up because the store was shut caz of the pandemic (a whole sob story abt local shops not being able to afford rent, that I believed to be true!) but he can have it delivered home. I gave him the address and confirmed I would give him the money once he delivers. He said he can't send a person unless the payment was made upfront. The site is listed to be a part of higginbothams (which is a major book store chain down south in Chennai). I paid the amount in the merchant link he shared (Bharatpe). He sent me another payment link, saying it's a registration link. But then I realised this guy was just scamming me for money. Some more confrontations lead to the usual scamster response that we see on YouTube. I can't believe I fell for one myself. I cut the call and reported it on cyber crime. But my money seems lost for now. It wasn't much but it doesn't feel too great to lose money to some scammer. 9348880241. Oxford book store in Connaught place. 81, Block N, Connaught Place, New Delhi, Delhi 110001. This guy is a bloody scam!

Hope it's not against the rules to post the number and location. So, more people don't get scammed by the same guy. Doubt anyone would be dumb enough to make an upfront payment seeing a legit site but yeah..
