I had just completed paying the last premium for my tata AIA insurance. Then the call happened.
At least two people claiming to be from tata AIA said they could upgrade me to a 'better plan' and conned me off Rs. 1 lakh. To my dismay I soon realized it to be a fraud and have approached Tata AIA people but they disassociated themselves totally. I resigned myself to my fate although the memory of this incident immediately saddens me to this day. 

Today the fraudsters called me again saying that my 'policy' has lapsed and I can go for 'settlement' if I pay the premium of 2 lakhs. I am not going to fall for this again. I managed to keep calm although my blood was boiling and I told them I would think this over and get back to them tomorrow. What do you think I should do? Any kind of suggestion appreciated. :(

P.S.: I have got their number and it comes from Ghaziabad. How do I go about lodging a police complaint? Will I be required to travel to this place. It is surprising how these scamsters have managed to survive one year after stealing my money.
