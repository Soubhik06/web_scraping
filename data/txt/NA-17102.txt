Sorry for the long post /rant

I am currently working as a BDO in a really backward block as a part of IAS probation. The field realities are nothing close to what I expected and I expected the worst. This might not be applicable to entire country. 

**Schemes Galore!** 
Every government scheme is looted (no big surprise here, but the extent of how rooted the problems are). Be it MGNREGA, IAY, MDM, state level schemes, there are enough avenues to loot for everybody. MDM, PDS are expected siphoned off for open markets. I had two old men come to my office yesterday, they said that the Gram Panchayat head’s husband was threatening them to give Rs. 15,000 from the first allotment of Rs. 17,5000 of Indira Awas Yojana for recommending their names. I asked my staff to write their complaint (as they were illiterate) and took their Left Thumb Impression and their video. 

**NREGA** 
*These are my anecdotes on MGNREGA (IAY is similar, houses of all kinds, mostly invisible)* 

**Workers and Supervisors** 
After 10 years of NREGA, I still cannot see any productive assets from this scheme. Most schemes are land leveling, digging of ponds, wells, etc. Useless infrastructure is the hallmark of NREGA here because of stringent 60:40 wages to material cost ratio and emphasis on agriculture related work (read - land leveling of a farmer’s field). Workers come for 2-3 hours everyday and take money from supervisor and go back. Government tried to take away this discretion from supervisors by linking payments to bank accounts. Now ATM cards are with supervisors. workers want solid cash after a day’s labour not money in account after seven days. Supervisors pay 100 to the labourers and pocket the rest as profit. 

**Administration and panchayat politics** 
Executive Assistant from a GP admitted to me that muster rolls are faked, schemes are made on paper and Measurement Books forcibly signed (willingly also). Gram Panchayat members and chiefs have an interest in running such schemes as they impound job cards of workers. Apart from the planned works they do supplementary plans where roads are constructed and land leveled and the results are only on paper. 

**NGO here - Power without responsibilities** 
NGOs are another group who come for the spoils. There are well networked NGOs directly connected to the state and centre that bypass local authorities. This led to duplication of work and comical results. They claim their work is participative (participative rural appraisal – PRA is in vogue among many CSOs). I admit some NGOs have done good work but my experience of interaction with this particular one was quite bad. A lot of miscommunication and arrogance has led to farcical effects. They could not justify their calculations and budgetary preparations. They complained to the state that the local authorities did not cooperate – I joined after most of the fiasco happened but I can see why the administration is scared of them.

**e-governance** 
Most of work is now monitored online, whether it is NREGA, Swach Bharat Mission, IAY, etc. All that matters to higher authorities is the fact the details are entered on the MIS and money is being utilized. E-governance has only added more work to departments, government departments have an inherent dislike for technology and to add to that lack of enough technical know-how. Computers have not replaced files, registers, and ledgers but only added an e-copy of the hard documents. I am running pillar to post replying every day why my figures are low while I face a volley of questions from people over lack of NREGA work. 

**End notes** 
This is a small percentage of request, complaints, abuses, etc that I get everyday. Understaffed, under-developed and uninterested, the office is a pit stop for me before I take my real charge. I have realized that this office is a disaster management office, I make plans for the entire day and on a good day I can achieve 10% of what I wanted to do. In another month I will be out of here, relieved probably, but knowing a lot more than I did a month ago. 

**TL/DR:** Took my first assignment of probation, saw the ugly side of the welfare state.
