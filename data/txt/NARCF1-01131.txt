He made me wait for 10 days, saying the parts were being sent via BlueDart courier. Later he changed the story — claimed the parts were being sent in a private truck along with his friend’s imported car. I filed a police complaint at Calicut Medical College Police Station, but till now, no action has been taken.

I believe he is currently in Delhi or Noida. He’s known to frequently visit Hashtag Café in Noida.
Please, if anyone spots him or has more information — help me and others he has scammed get justice.
