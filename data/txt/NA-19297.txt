A simple search in the news for Aadhaar card would show that either it is for the new Election Card or payments for NREGA or the opening of bank accounts (Jan Dhan Yojana) or the PAHAL scheme linking Aadhar to banks or the linking of Aadhaar with election card to remove duplicity, it is almost a must to have a Aadhar card. Similar [news today too](http://www.financialexpress.com/article/economy/end-of-paper-trail-in-e-filng-of-income-tax-returns-aadhaar-added/64738/).

All these in a indirect way are linking all our records in one place. That would be a great privacy risk having all your records in one place without any concrete law to protect the citizen &amp; prevent any abuse or misuse of such data. (Remember 66A ?)

It is good to have a system like SSN but without any law &amp; also violating the repeated judgements of the SC ?

My request everyone providing your Aadhaar (linking biometric data with others) would be, to be careful &amp; read the fine lines in the forms they fill up.

What does /r/India think on this ?
