Bought two items from Flipkart , a mini ups and Android phone for my sister , the phone didn't turn on ,so I returned it , the replacement comes , they just repacked the same phone and just send it back , called customer service , they were dodging the questions even going far as to fake bad network , she was like I can't hear u , basically faking signal breaks with her own voice and basically said just go to service centre and repair , yep they wanted me to repair a new bought faulty phone , we were so disappointed as it was supposed to be her birthday gift and this left a sour taste, few days later I was curious, so I just charged for few hours and surprisingly it turned on , perhaps a dead battery ,no idea , I was just happy that I didn't have to go through anyore troubles with Flipkarts CS .

Fast forward few months later mini ups stops working , call for replacement cuz 1 year warranty, new one comes , gives a high pitch noise when connected, the day we'll replace again , take the unit , two days later the same unit comes with new box , I connect it just gives the same beeping noise . 

I just gave up , uninstalled Flipkart , will never buy again .Such a scam these companies are running , so far Amazon hasn't caused any problem , but they do send a lot of faulty products , have to replace for good ones again , atleast they send new ones. I wonder how such a big company can get away with scaming people,so blatantly as well . Indian companies customer service in general has shown to horrible, atleast in my experience
