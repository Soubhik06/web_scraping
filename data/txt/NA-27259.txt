So, this all started when I got a random message from someone saying,
"Give a Google review, and we’ll pay you."

I thought, why not? So, I dropped a review, and then they asked me to move to Telegram. I played along. They asked for my UPI ID, explained the process, and boom—₹200 in my account.

Then today, I get a call:
"Your merchant is waiting on Telegram. Please contact her."

I go back, drop 4 more Google reviews, and they send me ₹100.

Now comes the fun part. They finally sent a "task" that required me to pay first. Classic scam.

So, I deleted my previous messages and reconnected with them, acting clueless:
"Mam, can I start from Task 1 again? Sorry, I was late."

They fell for it. Again. Sent me ₹100. I repeated this twice. Then they caught on and ghosted me.

Total earnings: ₹500.
Entertainment value: Priceless.

Moral of the story: Sometimes, the scammer gets scammed.
