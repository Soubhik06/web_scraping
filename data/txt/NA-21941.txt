Hello Fellow Redditors,

Recently I got a call from some unknown numbers pretending to be from the courier company telling me that my courier has been derouted and needs to be activated or I'll have to wait 45 days for the delivery. Having heard of these scams before I did not fall for it and called the guy right there and then for the same, in response he had the audacity to yell at me when he could get me to pay him 5rs along with my UPI Pin. 

I tried to lodge a cyber cell complaint against them for which I was redirected thrice by the officials and then asked to mail with the same issue to their email Id. To my surprise, I got a response within 10 min saying the cyber cell cannot help me until I have lost more than some particular amount to the scammers. 

Also the email read that if I want to take any action then the local police station is my best shot with physical copies of FIR and all. 

My question is should someone only register a complaint when they have lost the money? If you escape the scam why can't you put an end to it by reporting the same?
