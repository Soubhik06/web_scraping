It's an audio by a lady (from Mumbai if I am not wrong) narrating how she got scammed off 1 lakh rupees through Google pay. She wanted to book a gurudwara so googled up the website and called the number. Guy on the phone told her that only online booking via Google Pay is done. He sent her a link to a Google form to fill and asked for a token amount of 5 rupees. The lady claims the form never asked for any details such as account number, OTP etc. Looked like just another Google form. The guy asked her to stay on hold, meanwhile slowly withdrawing upto 1 lakh.

Apparently her whole phone was cloned in the short time she was on call.

While UPI scam is on the rise, I am just wondering how legit is this story? Is it possible to clone your phone with most insignificant details from our side?
