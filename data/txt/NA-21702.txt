I was recently sent a **scam sms from people impersonating BSNL customer care**, the kind that ambiguously says "Your document verification failed, call XYZ number , your number will be deactivated in 24 hrs".

I called the number as directed (from a public phone) and they attempted to get me to download AnyDesk on my phone and share the access code. When I began asking direct, confrontational questions, because technology is my field of expertise and I understand the remote access scam very well, one of the operators (the one who I was speaking with since he spoke english while the other spoke Hindi) perhaps realised I was not a "bakra" and began to hurl **sexually obscene remarks against my mother and sister** , to be exact : 

&amp;#x200B;

&gt;Your sister is looking so gorgeous &amp; hot &amp; s\*xy 

&amp;#x200B;

&gt; "I will f\*ck your mother and sister"

(sic)

**I had recorded the entire call, as well as documented the SMS**, and immediately submitted these by filing a report @ https://cybercrime.gov.in for offences against women , under the category "Publishing or Transmitting Sexually Obscene material in electronic form" as provided. 

Firstly, does someone involved in criminal cases, have any idea **if this sort of complaint is taken seriously** at all , especially if directly incriminating evidence is filed ? Is there better course of action I can take to bring these fellows to justice ?

Less importantly, as an intellectual exercise , what laws can he be prosecuted against ? As a high school student interested in taking law in college, I have speculated the below from IT act &amp; IPC, would love to know your thoughts : 

1. Section 120, IPC - Criminal Conspiracy . There were at least two individuals recorded pretending to be BSNL call centre employees. 

2. Section 43 (Unauthorised data access) and 66 ( Dishonesty/Fraud to commit offence(s) in section 43) of IT act, for stealing data of BSNL customers to defraud them.

3. Section 66D of IT act and 419 of IPC for impersonation on phone to cheat (or attempt to).

4. Section 420 of IPC for attempting to gain remote access of phone through impersonation and under false pretences.

5. Section 67 (digital transmission of obscenity) of IT act, 509 (Word, gesture or act intended to insult the modesty of a woman ) and 294 (Obscene acts or words in public) of IPC for uttering repeatedly obscene remarks against mother and sister.

6. Section 66E of IT act for accessing personal customer data of BSNL customer without consent , notice nor valid authority.
