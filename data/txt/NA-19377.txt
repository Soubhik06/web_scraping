Today is the last day of our r/India fundraiser. We’ve been able to raise INR 1,79,450, nearly 90% of our 2 lakh goal. We know that it’s been a tough time for the Reddit community and we are so so grateful for the outpouring of support we have received! 

🔗 [https://internetfreedom.in/r-india-for-iff/](https://internetfreedom.in/r-india-for-iff/)

r/India has been and continues to be a core part of the IFF community. Earlier this week, we [wrote](https://www.instagram.com/p/CtbtvFgS9LV/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&amp;igshid=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==) about the changes to Reddit’s API access as thousands of subreddits went dark. Looking to the future, we will continue to play our part in preserving this community that has come to be so integral for our mission to safeguard digital rights. 

We’re in the last 24 hours of our r/India fundraiser, and we are making one final push to reach our target. We will need to raise INR 20,550 to achieve our 2 lakh goal.Your contribution, no matter the size, will make a significant impact on our efforts. By donating today, you help to safeguard our digital rights. 

**Here’s what you’re supporting:**

* This week, we launched a [joint report](https://www.hrw.org/report/2023/06/14/no-internet-means-no-work-no-pay-no-food/internet-shutdowns-deny-access-basic) with Human Rights Watch that documents the harm caused by internet shutdowns by denying access to basic rights and entitlements to marginalised communities. 
* Next week, we’re launching PlugTheBreach, a project by IFF that tracks and records instances of data breaches in India to raise awareness and promote transparency and accountability. Register for the launch [here](https://us06web.zoom.us/meeting/register/tZUuf-qorT0oHd1YgQRDUefPOVR-IcjcTr_y#/registration)!
