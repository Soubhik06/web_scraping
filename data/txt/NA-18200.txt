1. Snack Video :

“The location of storage. We will store your information within the territory of China.”

“You agree that the sources of your personal information from which we collect are you and the connected third-party sites who share your data with us as indicated above in this Policy. We may, where applicable, collect personal information from other sources such as public registers, social medial, publically accessible websites, news agencies.”

“By providing your phone number, you consent to our sending text messages to your phone number containing advertising.”

2. MX TAKTAK : 

The data centers may be based in India (although not mentioned anywhere the app says it’s made in India, for Indians)

Privacy policy doesn’t have anything standing out than the normal data collected by other apps like Reddit and Instagram.

3. Moj by Sharechat: 

This app stores it’s data in the Google Cloud based in California

App is Indian made

Shares user data with third-party for advertising and data analysis

The look of the privacy policy is weird but the content doesn’t have anything which stands out.

4. Josh by DailyHunt: 

Another app made in India

Location of data centers are unknown and not mentioned

The privacy policy site isn’t https secure and the privacy policy is extensive

“Eterno may collect and use Your personal information and other information for business and/or non-commercial purposes. “

5. ShareChat: 

Data stored in AWS and Google Cloud

The rest is same as Moj by ShareChat

6. Zili: 

“Zili may use overseas facilities operated and controlled by Zili to process or back up your personal information. Currently, Zili has data centers in Beijing, United States, Germany, Russia, Singapore and India.”

Uses another site to host its privacy policy

Personal information is used for ads

7. Roposo: 

Nothing out of the ordinary and basic information collection mechanism followed by all apps

Made in India by IIT Delhi alumni

8. Public by Inshorts: 

Data stored in India and app built by a known company Inshorts

Privacy policy not updated since November 2019 and is available in a PDF type format

9. Like Video by Status Saver: 

The privacy policy is of another app and hosted on blogspot

Really shady privacy policy

The privacy policy is fake and this app should not be on Google Play store in the first place

10. Trell: 

Again an Indian app

This app sets a new standard by not selling information to third party vendors. As mentioned “We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer to outside parties your Personally Identifiable Information.”

It later goes on to point out “Information from other sources: We may receive information about you from other sources and add it to our account information.”

Data centers not mentioned and the website is really a copy paste template.

11. MV Master: 

App based in China as it states “The location of storage. We will store your information within the territory of China.”

The privacy policy and the website is not https secure and looks shady

Better to stay away from this one

12. Like.ly: 

Another shady app which impersonates another app

Privacy policy is the copy-paste of another app “King of status” and no efforts have been made to make changes or create an original one

13. UV video status: 

Privacy policy is riddled with grammatical errors which points out the app is a fake or an impersonation. Evidence: “You can exercise any of these rights at any time by contacting us at [●].”

14. Mitron by MitronTv: 

App servers are based in Mumbai

The privacy policy is filled with grammatical errors and is not even aligned

It is believed to be a complete copy of a Pakistan based app

15. Mitron India: 

The privacy policy does not exist (page does not load)
