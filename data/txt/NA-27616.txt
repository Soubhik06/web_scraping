--- POST TEXT ---

I get called about twice a day by someone with a thick Indian accent, offering to lower my credit-card rate. Different person every time, but the script is always the same:

* congratulations, we've been monitoring your payment history and you always pay on time!
* how much money do you owe on your credit card?
* what kind of card?
* would you read me the expiration date?
* now read me the membership number.
* what's the 800 number from the back of the card?
* what's the last four digits of your social security number?
* please hold while we pull up your details ...

At this point they put me on hold while they call the bank, pretend to be me (apparently they already have my name), and find out the balance on the card. If this were to succeed, they would probably get back to me with some overpriced scheme. But of course I've been feeding them fake information all along, so the bank doesn't accept it; and usually at this point they come back to me and ask "is this a new card? are you close to your limit?" and other questions to figure out why it's not going through. And then they ask "do you have another card?" and I can give them more fake data and repeat the process if I want.

But the process is very ... linear. Any deviation at all, and they hang up immediately. If I say, "why are you asking me for all this info when you just said you have my details in front of you and you're looking at my payment history?" they hang up. If I say, "I'm very interested in your service but I don't have my card with me right now, may I have your phone number and I'll call you back?" they hang up.

I'd like to continue to waste their time, but it's difficult to prolong the call, especially when their finger is hanging over the 'disconnect' button. And this makes the calls fairly dull, too.

Any suggestions for how I can keep them on the call longer?
