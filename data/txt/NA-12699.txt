of obtaining Aadhaar Card by practicing fraud cannot
be amicably settled. 7. As the petitioner does not intend to proceed
with the writ petition any further, the same stands
dismissed as 'not pressed'. 8. Dismissal of the writ petition will, however,
not stand in the way of the respondent authority from
taking steps to proceed against the private respondent,
in the event it is found that the Aadhaar Card was
obtained by him after practicing fraud and
misrepresentation. 9. Urgent photostat certified copy of this order
be supplied to the parties, if applied for, as early as
possible. ( Amrita Sinha, J.)
