I have been trying to get BookMyBai to find me a nanny for 2+ months now. Here’s my rant and learning for you all. 

- they promise to give you a maid/nanny/help as per your requirements and if not they say they find a replacement Nd give you 3 free replacements for 6 months. 

- you have to pay BMB fee the first time the first maid comes to your house. NOTE : you don’t get a chance to check how she works. You only get video call or voice call with the maid where they say they do everything 

- once you pay the BMB fee, the maid starts working. You figure out she is incapable. You call BMB for replacements. And that’s where the pawri begins. 

- they will be very slow in finding and giving replacements. Before paying money they will call you every hour and make you talk to maids. After paying money the quantity suddenly reduces. You will hear things like “we are finding”, “we are searching”, “no one is available” and so on. 

- they won’t refund money. Their policy says that “once customer has paid money, and if the customer wants to withdraw for whatever reason the money will not be returned”. 
This is where things get interesting. BMB will never deny providing maids or any service. They will say everyday that they are looking. So this ways the ball always lies in customer court. Even after months if the customer is unable to get maids, they wont be able to get their money back. 

Typically people will not wait forever for house help. They will also look at other places and if they get one, BMB doesn’t even have to find a maid for them. 

- this seems like a pure scam to me. Maybe i am wrong but i have heard similar stories from my friends as well. 

- they dont have any customer care or social media care. You are on the mercy of the person dealing with you from day 1. Their CEO is living under a rock. His social profiles are inactive for a year and he can’t be reached. Their top management team is almost invisible from LinkedIn or Twitter. So there’s no body to complain about. 

They’re not even a proper VC raised startup so not even like you can talk to their investors. Its a self funded 11 years old company that has somehow found a very nice legal way of scamming people. 

I will urge all of you to 
1. Pay to BMB only after at least 2 days of evaluation of your maid. Else your money is lost forever 

2. Stop using BMB if you love your money. 

3. Provide me a list of other agencies you know might work. 

4. If you know somebody from BMB preferably their top managers, please connect with me. 

Thanks for being with me here.
