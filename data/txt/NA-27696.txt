**Summary of Scam:**

In this scam, the scammer claims that you brought your computer in for a repair 2 years ago and they installed a software that autorenews at $699 and that it charged your account already. They claim that the company is no longer operating therefore they would like to refund you that money. In order to do this, they request you to download a remote desktop sharing program so they can "send you the refund form", however the program they have you download allows them to go through your computer while you watch and they start running phoney tests and claiming your computer needs repairs. They then try to sell their services to you instead of refunding your money. There are many variations of this technique.

&amp;#x200B;

Some involve you having a specific web browser such as chrome. Usually when they specify that you need chrome, they will have you log into your bank account while you are connected to them and then they "refund" the money by going into the Chrome "object inspection" and edit the code to appear as if theyve given you a refund in your bank account. They can change the values to appear as if you got a refund, when in reality, there is no refund applied.
