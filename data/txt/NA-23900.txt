--- POST TEXT ---

So I tried to sell an old oven on OLX today. I got a couple buyers right away chatting on the app. Both asked for my number which I gave them. They both spoke in Hindi and tried to negotiate the price. After agreeing to a price, they asked for my location which I denied saying that I need the money first and then location will be sent. They agreed and told me to wait a while. One of them was posing as an army captain which I felt was just horrible. The other guy was posing as an electronics store owner. Both tried the old scan the QR code to recieve money which I saw through right away (Thank God for reddit because I read about the same scam few days ago here). I don't if it's okay to reveal the names they used but one was calling himself Captain Anil Kumar based in Kalyan nagar and the electronics store owner was Manoj Kumar from JP nagar. Please be aware of such people.
