Recently fell victim to what seems to be a longstanding LPG scam. Despite its age, it's shocking that this is still happening. I ordered an Indane LPG cylinder a week ago, and upon delivery, a family member unknowingly accepted it. It wasn't until later that I realized the cylinder's seal was unusually loose. Suspicion kicked in, prompting me to weigh the cylinder at home. To my dismay, it weighed only 27.5 kg instead of the expected 30.1 kg.

After some online research, it appears this is a well-known scam within LPG delivery networks. At a cost of 900 rs for 30 kg, the delivery agent or agency is swindling customers by shortchanging 2.5 kg, equivalent to 75 rs per customer.

As a newcomer to this scam, I'm seeking advice on how to address and prevent such incidents in the future, both before and after delivery. It's been mentioned that weighing the cylinder during delivery and confronting the delivery person may not be effective, as they could be using tampered scales adjusted to show a higher weight.

Have any of you faced similar situations, and how did you handle it? What options do I have, and what steps can be taken to combat this scam? Your insights and experiences would be greatly appreciated!
