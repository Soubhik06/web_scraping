Was travelling to airport on Sunday morning in Ola cab. The fare was 121 but after reaching the airport the driver showed that it's showing 240 as the fare in his phone. It happens sometimes in uber too and though I will get the refund. The amount was not updated on my phone. I told him about it and he said it will get updated, pay 240 and if anything happens, he will refund. I paid him 240 and later after 3-4 hrs called him saying that it's still 121. He said he will refund 120 but not answering to call or message after that. The page which he showed was probably a screenshot which he kept beforehand. I suspected it but just blanked out at the moment. Also forgot to record the call. I acted very dumb, but you guys be careful. I will call the Ola support tomorrow, any other help is appreciated. It's not about the amount, but it's more about the scam he did(which can happen with others too)
