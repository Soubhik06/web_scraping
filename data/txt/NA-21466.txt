**Tldr;** I got conned online with a clever scam - where do I complain?

So I was trying to arrange pickup for my stuff in a town I was working in far from my hometown where I am currently since I am vacating that flat.

I posted an ad on the site - olx to sell an item. One person responded in sometime. So I gave him my number and he called.

He said me to give my number so he can transfer amount for that item after agreeing on price and transporting mechanism.

I told him to send on my same number since I have both PhonePe and Google pay on it.

He sent a QR code saying to scan it. I scan it and see option to make payment from my end.

I tell it is asking to make payment on my end but he says it is merchant account, it works that way. I disagree.. I try keeping call but he is very persistent - continuously calling and messaging

He offers to make trial transaction in 'that' manner - with INR 5 amount

So I take his QR code for that, in PhonePe see the amount along a message ( that I have not come across before ) having written something like - 'accepting this amount from the other account' and make payment from my end for INR 5. So I see INR 5 deducted from my account - and two subsequent INR 5 payments credited to my account.

So he says that this is the way merchant account, all the while hurrying me to complete transaction saying 'his amount is pending'

Now, I am also trying to meet deadline for my job work that I am doing from home amid all disturbances, also previous owner is asking to clear my stuff from house and amid all this I lose focus and do that transaction after him relentlessly pursuing me.

Ofc, the amount gets debited from my end and there is no counter-payment that has come. Now I tell this to him and he says that do one more payment of same amount. Also again shares a screenshot of payment of that amount completed from his end over Whatsapp. 

I hand over phone to my father to tell him to return payment else we will file a cyber crime case ( just to change ownership of handler since he already knows he can fool me now ). My father says that he won't accept this method of payment, either ask some relative to make 'normal' UPI payment but he is persistent. Also on insisting shares his aadhar card ( ofc fake ones ) and one more ID over whatsapp. My father says to return payment deducted from my end and he cuts call.

Scam complete

**Again I would like to commit that it is entirely my stupidity that this happened**

But would also want to acknowledge that this is a very cleverly *engineered* scam. Amid all the people returning back to their home towns they have taken advantage of such people shifting back to their home place. 

And such people are trying to manage a lot of responsibilities and after initial defense are susceptible to being vulnerable when they can easily trust someone. 

I got similar messages from other people too, notably an *army officer* who also asked 'how could I not trust and army person' after sharing ID proofs ( ofc fake ones ) over whatsapp.

Would definitely not want to let this go unreported. Where can I file a complaint for this?
