I saw a couple of posts on Moonlighting here on reddit as well as other platforms.

All the recent news on how corporates frown upon this culture and are hell bent on terminating the employee because it is 'cheating' etc., have stirred up the debate.

&amp;#x200B;

I feel that companies should not be against moonlighting until and unless the employees are vulnerable to divulge confidential information in their second job or hustle. They need to figure out a system to keep the confidential system within the organization. What has teaching ERP online got to do with the super secret procurement of some raw material, right?

&amp;#x200B;

Loyalty is a two way street. When the companies expect the employees to 'stretch' a bit due to 'tight budget' they should also reciprocate with not firing people for 'cost cutting' or to show 'record profits' to appease share holders.

&amp;#x200B;

A side hustle or a second job of sorts will keep us sane from all the turmoil we go through in the primary job. It becomes a life saver during so called cost cuttings or requirement becoming obsolete.
