**How this scam is being advertised and executed:**

Innocent people who barely know anything about cryptocurrencies are pursued to purchase Ethereum from their hard earned money and then create an account on their platform and transfer the Ether to them.

A website by the domain ethertradeasia.com is being run by the scammers and it's promised that the company will double any amount put into their system within approximately 4 months.

The difference here from other such MLM and money doubling promises is that they only accept the crypto currency Ethereum and not Rupees.

When asked how they will manage to double the amount, they say that they put the customer’s ether into trading and earn profits, part of which they return as a trading bonus.

They allow the customer to create an account on their website ethertradeasia.com and transfer the desired ether from their own wallets to the system.

The customer is presented with a fancy looking dashboard which slightly looks similar to other well known trading platforms.

Initially it is promised that the system credits 0.01 Ether every day as a trading bonus which the customer can withdraw on Sundays and when the amount is greater than 0.10 Ether. As withdrawals are on Sundays, bonus of 0.01 Ether will not be credited on Sundays.

Meetings and party gatherings are held to attract and convince investors which is very similar to other MLM processes.

**How the Investors are scammed (How their ether is not given back):**

The first thing they do is to try to block the investors from withdrawing their so-called trading bonus on Sundays. This is done by creating fake excuses like “system is under upgrade for automatic withdrawals” to “high transaction fees on Ethereum blockchain”.

Randomly change withdrawal timings from early morning 6 am to 12:30 pm in afternoon.

Randomly show “Page not found” for the withdrawal link.

Accept the withdrawal request but keep it in pending state for many days without any intimation or announcements.

*Unfortunately, there are no rules and regulations regarding cryptocurrency and crypto is not legally recognizable in India, hence the investors will not be able to raise any legal complaints against these goons.*
