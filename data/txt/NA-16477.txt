Basically the title. Shitty trick that companies, or some managers, rather, play in order to get some hapless candidate to do their homework for them.

I am looking for a job and got a call from a man in the HR dept of a respected company. Said that based on my application for a marketing related job posted on LinkedIn, they would like to schedule an online interview. As scheduled, we connected, and the manager, a lady, was also on the call. First ten minutes was all about them asking standard questions about my career so far, why change etc. Then she said that to evaluate further, I would have to do a deep dive into the potential for a strategic partnership between that company and another company based in the US. I was to revert by end of the week (about 4 days).

So I did what I could. The US company is not a customer facing one, and so they don't have many details about them on the net. Even their own website is filled more with flowery words than with anything of substance. Anyway, I submitted this by mailing this to HR. His was the only email id I had, since this manager lady had just joined the call and logged off after that. Two weeks on, madam has no time to review my presentation, or get back to me. HR man only keep saying we will revert. 

&amp;#x200B;

Frankly, it seems like a shitty scam. Lazy and unethical to get someone else to do your job for you, for free. Shitty, because the candidate is only going to use Google, and finally will have only as much information as you would get yourself. Also, as an industry insider, you would have much more relevant information available to you that a random candidate would not be able to find. I doubt there was anything in my report that they did not know already.
