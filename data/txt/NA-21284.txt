My 56 year old father got added in a whatsapp group where the "investment advisors" claimed to be from Samara Capital Group from Mumbai. The group was daily flooded with random screenshots of people claiming to have made money by investing in their group. My dad was first lured to transfer Rs 10 lac in order to subscribe to an upcoming IPO of DCG Cables and he was told that he was getting the IPO at a discounted rate. He transferred them the money without consulting with me or anyone. Once the IPO was listed, he was told that the IPO has given him a profit of 4 lacs, and they asked him to then reinvest that money into a different IPO rather than withdrawing it. He again agreed to invest all of it in a different IPO. When the other IPO got listed, they today told him that actually they have given him shares worth 33 lacs and he will have to give them 19 more lacs to withdraw all his money along with all his profit. He then got suspicious and told me and then I realised that this is an elaborate scam. When I talked to those people today (they only reply on whatsapp, no surprise!) I told them that we will be reporting them to the authorities, they instead told me that Samara Capital would be taking legal action against my father for violating "trading laws" if we don't give them more money. What should I do next? Whom should I report to? I have already filed a complaint on the cyber crime portal

TLDR: Father got scammed for Rs 10 lacs by people from a whatsapp group claiming to be investment advisors. Need advice on what steps can be taken next. Any advice would be appreciated.
