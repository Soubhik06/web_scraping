Flipkart sale asche.. be warned.. 

Prothome bole rakhi je ami regularly Flipkart and Amazon theke kintam. Apatoto Flipkart e kena ekebarei bondho kore diechi. Very bad experience. 

Last Flipkart sale e ekta phone kinbo bhablam ma er jonyo. 

Price dekhalo.. 15000/- 
Sale price.. 11999/- 
Bank card discount.. 1000/-
Exchange offer.. upto 5000/-

Phone tar condition medium chilo. Body te light scratches chilo. But original Screen guard chilo Tai screen e scratch chilo na. Original box and bill harie gechilo. Exchange value dekhale 2000/- 

Ei porjonto thik chilo. 

Amar price porchilo.. 11999-1000-2000= 8999/- 

Courier er lokta ese sob kechie dilo. Bollo phone beke geche. Tai 1000/- less. Amake nite gele 8999/- noi.. 9999/- die nite hobe. 

Ami obak. Ma obak. Phone take table e rekhe dekhalam.. kothai beka?

Bollo nite hoe nin noeto deny kore din. Ami cancel kore dilam. 

Ei porjonto Tao thik chilo. Actual Scam ta bujte parlam offline market e phone ta kinte gie . . .

Phone ta bikri hocche 8999/- e. Sathe pujo offer e ekta 16gb memory card free. 

Le halua! 

Punoshcho: sob somoi je offline e better hobe ba online e kharab hobe emon kintu noi. Tobe last koekta bochor e Flipkart nijer reputation er ponga marte hat dhue lege poreche seta bojhai jacche. 

P2: ma seshe purono phone tai rekhe diechilo. Notun phone ta kine oi taka die ekta microwave kinechilam.
