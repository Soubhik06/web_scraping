Well nothing new in the world of internet tbh. But yeah. This is something I am experiencing a lot recently and it's getting annoying. There was this one account called u/girl_next_more who texted me after I made a post about feeling confused with life. Now I don't wish to be impolite to anyone for no reason. So I did talk. "She" seemed to be chill so I didn't mind continuing the conversation. Although there were obvious flirting going on which I tried to ignore as much as possible. This also raised doubts in my mind. So I straight up told them that I felt that they are a catfish. They denied. Next thing you know they were asking me for my WhatsApp or telegram. I gave my telegram. And I tried to push for a phone call as much as I could to confirm my belief. When they picked up the call I heard the most blatant voice of a guy pretending to be girl. Anyway. I don't understand people at times. If it was a weird way of making friends then idk what to say. I am happy to talk to anyone as long as they are being real. If it was some plan to scam then that person should be ashamed of themselves
