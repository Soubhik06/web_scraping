TITLE: Indian Coast Guard seizes fishing vessel suspected of drug smuggling Mumbai
AUTHOR: ThePrint Team
DATE: 2024-05-14
URL: https://theprint.in/defence/indian-coast-guard-seizes-fishing-vessel-suspected-of-drug-smuggling-mumbai/2084513/

--- ARTICLE TEXT ---

A fishing vessel suspected of smuggling drugs was seized by the
Indian Coast Guard
(ICG) on May 12, 2024. The boat was apprehended about 27 nautical miles southwest of Mumbai, a PIB press release stated.
The ICG said that 30,000 litres of illicit diesel and Rs 1.75 lakh cash were seized from the vessel. The diesel was found hidden in the fish holds. Interrogation of the crew revealed their intention to sell the illegal fish to unsuspecting fishermen. The ICG said that the suspected vessel was escorted to the Mumbai harbour and was handed over to the authorities of the Department of Customs for proper investigation and legal proceedings.
Other Coastal Security Agencies, including the Police, Fisheries, and Directorate of Revenue Intelligence, were also brought into the fold to ensure a comprehensive action against this maritime menace. The ICG said that the apprehended vessel was carrying four crew members. The names of the crew members were not released by the authorities.
The ICG said that the vessel was seized and other measures were taken to safeguard the coastal waters.
