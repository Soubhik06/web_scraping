% 01.09.2023 1. The present application under Section 438 of the Cr.P.C. seeks
 anticipatory bail in case FIR No. 217/2022 under Sections
 389 / 419 / 420 / 120B / 34 of the IPC and Sections 66C and 66D of the
 Information and Technology Act, registered at P.S. Crime Branch, South-
 East. 2. The case of the prosecution, as per status report dated 26.04.2023
 authored by Inspector Satish Malik, NR-I/Crime Branch is as under:- 3. Learned counsel for the applicant submits that the latter has always
 cooperated with the investigation in the present FIR. It is further pointed out
 that all the recoveries with respect to the present applicant have been made.
 It is further pointed out that out of the 33 persons chargeshseeted in the
 present FIR, only one person was arrested i.e. Kapil Taneja, who has been
 granted bail by this Court vide judgment dated 11.01.2023 in BAIL
 APPLN. 3577/2022, (2023/DHC/000184). It is further submitted that in
 pursuance of the interim protection granted by this Court vide order dated
 17.04.2023, the applicant has joined investigation as and when directed by
 the Investigating Officer and has cooperated with the same. 4. Learned APP for the State submits that applicant has joined
 investigation, however, he did not cooperate with the same. It is submitted
 that his Apple ID was retrieved but no data was found and further request
 has been made to Apple for retrieval of the data. 5. Heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the record. 6. It is an admitted fact that the main chargesheet in the present FIR has
 been filed. The investigation qua the present applicant has been done to the
 extent that his phone as well as laptops have been seized. Relevant pappers
 of property and bank accounts have been supplied to the Investigating
 Officer. 7. In view of the above, the application is allowed, in the event of arrest,
 the applicant is directed to be released on bail on his furnishing personal
 bond of Rs. 50,000/- with one surety of like amount to the satisfaction of the
 Investigating Officer/Arresting Officer, further subject to following
 conditions: 8. The application is allowed and disposed of accordingly. 9. Pending applications, if any, also stand disposed of. 10. Needless to state that nothing stated hereinabove is an opinion on the
 merits of the case. 11. Order be uploaded on the website of this court forthwith. AMIT SHARMA, J
