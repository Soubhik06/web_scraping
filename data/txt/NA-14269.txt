The Ministry of Finance has proposed a draft bill to ban unregulated lending practices, particularly targeting digital lending platforms. Amongst various provisions, it authorises police to conduct warrantless searches, seize assets, and freeze accounts tied to illegal activities.

Penalties include significant fines and prison sentences. The bill also creates a central database of legitimate lenders that will enable public verification and reporting of illegal practices. The draft bill comes after a multitude of reports and increasing regulatory scrutiny about predatory digital lending apps, who trap borrowers in cycles of debt and often use criminal intimidation in order to recover loans.
