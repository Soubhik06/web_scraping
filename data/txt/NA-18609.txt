--- POST TEXT ---

If you are a politically opinionated
person in social media, there is one
skill that you need to acquire to
survive in this vicious world of
facebook and twitter.
That skill is called : The Art of
Trolling the Troll.
I am giving a most recent exchange
to illustrate how it is done.
________________________________
Shakha Historian: Ancient India was
technologically very advanced. 500
years of rule by Muslim invaders
have completely left us backward.
Me: Oh? How did the Muslim
invaders succeed if Indians were
technologically so advanced?

Shakha Historian: They would not
have if Buddhism did not weaken
India.

Me: Oh? You mean Buddhism
destroyed India’s technological
prowess?

Shakha Historian: I didn’t say that.
But Buddhism weakened our Hindu
military prowess.

Me: I see. But why did Indians turn
to Buddhism from superior
Hinduism?
Shakha Historian: Ashoka made it
state religion.

Me: Why did Ashoka make it state
religion?
Shakha Historian: That is not
relevant. Muslims made us
technologically backward.
Me: I see. Is that why they have left
behind a magnificent heritage of
civil engineering works?
Shakha Historian: They only built
tombs.

Me: I see. Have you heard of the
Grand Trunk Road? Any idea who
built it?
Shahkha Historian: How many great
universities did Muslim rulers
build?
Me: How many great universities
did Hindu rulers build?
Shakha Historian: Nalanda was a
great university.
Me: Er…wasn’t Nalanda Buddhist?
And according to you Buddhism
weakened India?

Shakha Historian: All Buddhists are
Hindus only. Got it?
Me: I see. Which means it was
Hindus who weakened India? Since
all Buddhists are Hindus only?
Shakha Historian: Don’t talk like a
communist you fraud, criminal.
Me: I see. I am a criminal like Modi
and Amit Shah?

Shakha Historian: Don’t bullshit. No
court has pronounced them guilty.
Me: Which court has pronounced
me guilty?
Shakha Historian: You are a true
Congressi stooge. I don’t want to
talk to you. I am done.
Me: But, but…you called me a
Communist and criminal? How can I
be both?
Shakha Historian: The whole world
knows you are a criminal.
Me: Accha. Can you explain to me
in which case, why I am in social
media with my own identity but you
have to hide behind a fake one?
Shakha Historian: Being anonymous
in social media is not a crime. You
are a fraud.
Me: Hmmm. Whom have you
robbed that you are in hiding? Is
the police looking for you? Or
people in twitter?
Shakha Historian: That was not our
original argument. You are
digressing.
Me: Well, you started it by calling
me fraud and a criminal and what
not.
Shakha Historian: You are harassing
me. I don’t want to talk to you.
Me: Okay. But but I still didn’t get
my answer: Why did Ashoka turn to
Buddhism?
There has been no response for a
long time now.
