Posting this here in case some one else has faced this issue and can possibly help. Some one used my debit card on this store last night http://www.ebay.in/usr/bidallies. 
Steps taken so far: 
* 1) Reported issue to HDFC bank using thier online form and emailed my 'personal' banker (thier toll free number works only between 9-6 pm ist, transaction took place at 21:30 pm) 
* 2) cancelled debit card online
* 3) changed online hdfc password 
* 4) logged into ebay and reported issue to this seller (their toll free number too does not work, just constant beeping sound) 
* 5) created an account in paypal and registered complaint to them (their toll free number too does not work) 
* 6) Visa does not have any online complaint email id, their toll free number does not work, so posted on their facebook page. 

It would be nice if all of these guys just had a single email id where issues can be reported, instead of me having to create accounts for each and everyone of them, to just report a single issue.

my belief is that the bank should be the one to refund/return my money, however with so many entities involved, does anyone know how to go about getting my money back?

tldr: debit card fraudulently used, suggestions for remediation welcome

UPDATE: In case anyone re-visits this link, i finally got my money back, to do this i had to file a complaint at my cities cyber crime division, this was a simpler process then i thought, you just write down what happened with your card details on a piece of paper, the cyber crime guys make an entry in their register, stamp it and complaint done! This i submitted to HDFC bank with a card reversal form (if such a complaint is done, then the card is cancelled automatically and net access to the account is blocked). Anyway, i got the money back in 12 days which to me is a record. Reporting the issue to PayPal, Visa and Ebay paid no dividends, only PayPal got back they recorded my call and said they would email back and never id, Ebay got back once and then only silence from them. and so ends my saga!
