Hi delhiites! My father was went to the ATM the other day to withdraw some cash. Two young guys came in after he had finished taking money out, and told him he needed to insert the card again to make sure the system 'logged him out'. He is generally savvy but this time he got somehow convinced by them - and gave them his card, and they noted the PIN he put in, and then also with a quick slight of hand, they replaced the card with another fake card. He also couldn't block the card because he doesn't remember the card number and he doesn't have it any more. 
We are shocked as a family but I'm wondering if there is anything else that he can do to mitigate the situation. Also, is there any way the bank can return the money to him through any way? Thanks.
