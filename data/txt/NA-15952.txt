*Hi friends,*

*I have been subjected to an online fraud on* *Amazon.in* *few days ago. Sharing my experience here so that you could take extra precautions while doing online purchases from now on.*

*My name is* *Vishnu Ranjidas* 
*From Kerala, India.*

*I am a prime member in amazon.in and have been using amazon alone as my major e-commerce site for years. I have purchased numerous items through amazon and my experience with amazon was really great all these time.* 
*My trust towards them was solid like many of you until I met with this frightening experience with an order I have placed few weeks back.*

*I ordered a product: ASUS ROG Strix GeForce RTX 3090 OC Graphics Card on amazon.in on 22-01-2022 and paid Rs 1,99,989/- (One Lakh ninty nine thousand nine hundred and eighty nine only) (Nearly USD 2618$) through net banking.*

*(For those who wonders why I brought it, please be informed that I am a 3D/VFX artist, primarily working with* *Unreal Engine 5* *and this product is considered as a 'jet engine' for a professional in my field. It was so much important for my current and upcoming works)*

*As per their online tracking information system the order date was 22 January 2022 and it was shipped on 24th January 2022. As per the description on tracking details the carrier picked up the package at 2.46 PM Monday 24th January 2022 and showed that the package will be arriving between 18th February and 3rd March 2022. But they didn't mention any location details. As per the tracking details the package was shipped with DTDC with tracking ID- MXXXXXXXXX. I enquired about it and got a reply that I will receive the product between Feb 18 and March 03.*

*The Amazon app itself was showing this date for the delivery all the time. So I decided to wait.*

*On 16th February 2022 through Amazon web app, I got information stating that 'your shipment is getting delayed than expected and you can cancel the order and claim for refund if you want'. As per this information I messaged the seller through amazon app messenger service and enquired about the delay and possibility of getting the ordered item. I waited for the reply for one day and did not receive any reply from the seller. I also enquired with the customer service through chat about the shipment on 17 Feb 2022. They informed me that the package is in transit, and is expected to arrive by 3rd March 2022. But as I didn’t get any reply from seller, I decided to cancel the order as Amazon suggested as earlier in the app. I cancelled the order in the app itself on 17th Feb 2022. Customer care informed me that it’s under review, and I need to wait for nearly one week for them to finish.*

*As I was waiting for the refund, after 3 days from the cancellation, on 22 Feb 2022 I got a shocking message from amazon app, showing a delivery receipt in my name along with some papers claiming Proof of delivery of this product and tracking details on behalf of the seller from amazon.in.* 
*It was showing that the product was received by me back on 6th Feb 2022. I checked the document again and found that there was a fake signature put on it towards my name on that slip. I checked the tracking information system again.*

*To my surprise I noticed that the tracking ID of the package has got changed to XXXXXXXXXX from MXXXXXXXX and carrier has got changed to Delhivery Direct from DTDC, which was shown as the courier company in the tracking details of the shipment earlier.*

*Since I have not received any package from Delhivery Direct on this date, I went to Delhivery Direct office at Kalpetta to enquire about this delivery no. XXXXXXXXXX which is now showed by amazon. As per the records in the Delhivery direct website, the package in the above tracking ID is another item which was delivered by them to another address and not in my name or address. That consignee has received it on 6th February 2022. From their delivery details, we have found that this shipment was of another product too, but the same seller.*

*We checked with DTDC too for confirmation and they informed us that they do not have this tracking ID registered with them either.*

*The seller has used another consignment's delivery details to fabricate proofs on my name without sending me the ordered product, and I have raised this issue on amazon through customer care, facebook and E-mails as many times as I could. They told me to wait until March 3rd for the enquiry to finish.*

*Told me they are conducting a high-level enquiry on this, and assured me that they will not allow any customer to lose their money for the mistakes of sellers or the courier companies. They told me that they have escaleted the case as per my request to the social media team.*

*I waited till 3rd March.*

*I had a solid trust on amazon, which was shattered like glass pieces when I came to know that amazon has denied my claim. It was so much easy for the enquiry team to figure this out if they have checked with the courier service and verified the consignment no. and address to which it was sent.* 
*But they didn't.* 
*They just closed the case much easily and took my money without even conducting a proper enquiry.*

*I never imagined a reputed company like Amazon would literally do something like this. Didn't got the item, and lost my money.* 
*Now I am left with no choice, but go public.*

*I have lodged a written complaint on this to Kalpetta cyber police station.* 
*Also, I am moving to consumer court against their unfair trade practice.* 
*As a primary step, my advocate has sent a legal notice towards Amazon and the seller on this matter.*

*I have heard few examples of online fraud through various E-commerce websites like sending Bricks or Sponges instead of the ordered item.* 
*Sad that Amazon didn't even sent me a brick or atleast an empty packet for my 2 Lakh rupees.*

*\[This post is not intended to tarnish Amazon's reputation.* 
*I am just sharing my experience, which is documented.* 
*Order details and consignment no.s of this product are hidden for privacy reasons. Those along with all the proofs I collected towards this case can be shared to any concerned agencies if requested\]*
