--- POST TEXT ---

My and my friend were trying to book a ride to the airport. She found a rickshaw and called him up to make sure he wouldn't cancel. He finally shows up and starts crying about 'return fare nahi milta- mujhe Ghar Jana hai,, tum log ne booking kar ke mujhe scam kiya, pay me double''. I told him drop me to the main road, take 25rs and Ghar ja. We were lucky enough to find a taxi on the app who charged us 220 instead of the rickshaw which was showing fare approx 280. I spoke about this to the cabbie who said rikshaw wala is right 😑. These rickshaw walas did some jhagda few weeks back and are banned from airport premises.
I always take rickshaw from aerocity mal home, without these apps - so it works out.

TLDR: always book cab to the airport and take a rickshaw back home from aerocity mall
