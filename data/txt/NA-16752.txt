Disclaimer : This is not a promotion, just a recommendation.

It's rare that you find an Indian YouTube channel who's content is not outright plagiarized from a similar yet much bigger channel based in a western country. We live in an age where are rural parents are using smartphones on an hourly basis every day, and it begs the need to be careful of what content they are consuming which inevitably dictates their habits and lifestyle. My mother is another one of these parents. She's almost 50 and over the last 5 years, has learnt to interact with her smartphone reasonably well given her religious classes that are held on WhatsApp and zoom. 

Being her son, I feel it is my duty to keep checking up on her every once in a while to make sure she's not consuming misinformation or falling for common scams on the internet. I did so again a few days ago when I found her watching a video on YouTube on a channel called Shape Up Your Life. For the first time in my life, my mother showed me something on the internet that I didn't already know existed, and wasn't bad. I explored the channel a bit and found out that it is run by a quintessential Indian housewife who takes care of her family, cooks, cleans, along with working out, reading and recording her day on YouTube. I was fascinated by this. A vlog channel run by an Indian housewife? Sounds absurd, right?

I ended up watching a lot of her videos and was blown away. She posts recipes, motivational videos, mental health videos, videos encouraging housewives to read and work out regularly, along with many other topics. It's not uncommon for Indian housewives to have undiagnosed mental health issues that go unaddressed pretty much their entire lives, and watching this channel acknowledge them was a breath of fresh air.

Again, this is not a promotion, paid or unpaid. This is simply something intriguing I found on the Internet that I found worth sharing with y'all. If you feel like it's something that your parents might enjoy watching, go ahead.
