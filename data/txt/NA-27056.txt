I just received a call from a Texas number that I’ve not seen before. Because I use my mobile number for business, I have to pick up calls even from unknown number since I do frequently get calls from new contacts.

After I picked up, a couple seconds passed and then I was connected with an Indian-sounding person with a lot of background noise (sounded like a call center). The person knew my name and asked: “is this Mr. ***?” To which I hesitantly replied yes. He then started saying he represents firm *** and is calling me to follow up on a request I made. I told him I don’t recognize his company name and don’t recall doing any business with it. He then asked me if my email address was ***, which I did recognize and hesitantly said yes and he said I filled out an online request for his company’s services. I told him I definitely don’t recall making such a request and he said “ok, we’ll take you off our list then.” And then he ended the call.

This feels weird and I’m not sure if I should be concerned this is the beginning of a more major scam down the line now that they were able to confirm my associated name, phone number, and email address (which they had to begin with).
