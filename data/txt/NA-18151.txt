--- POST TEXT ---

Firstly, if anyone asks for money or details. Lie. Just lie about it.

If you get an OTP, tell them a fake one.

If you are asked for physical location details, tell them fake one. 

If you are asked for money, lie to them you already sent it. 

**Why:**

Scams have been increasing a lot in the past few years with the Scammers knowing about your banking history, Card Variants, Card Numbers, etc.

**How:**

A lot of them call you and ask for some OTPs, or give you some kind of scheme on your Account/Card, ask you for your Mobile Number/Physical Address/etc.

**What if:**

You can get scammed by a lot of you give them even the tiniest of details as there are a lot of ways by which these guys can get Money from you. And each scammer knows something new. 

**Personal Story:**

Each and every single member of my family has reached a point where they are about to get scammed.

* Someone from a Bank calls my Father and offers him some kind of deal on his Account, and ask for *some details*

* My brother was asked if there was an Amazon Delivery which he placed, and that it has won a lottery, please tell us the OTP to confirm. ( The worst part being I ordered a gift for him and the scammer revealed that to him

* I got a WhatsApp Call where someone from KBC said I won 5 Crores. It was actually funny.

* Lastly, my mother got scammed in a really shady way. My mother was talking with someone, and moments later that person's whatsapp account got compromised. They read the chat history and found out this person has medical history, so they asked for money from everyone in Chat, and then asked to send it to an Indian Account. We fell for it. I fell for it. We trusted the person a lot and felt they can't betray us. 

**What should you do when your money is compromised**

I would thank God a Million times because he saved us from this massive loss. A lot of people said I won't get my money back and I personally lost hope as well. But God really heard our prayers and gave us a valuable lesson too! So I am doing my little bit in sharing that with you guys

* If someone asks you for money, call them, message them on other platforms, and confirm it again and again if it is the SAME PERSON.
* Check your phone for any recently installed applications. And uninstall the ones you think you don't know or sound shady
* Enable Two Factor Authentication on all major applications of your family members
* Drop a Tweet to the Bank and put it in their DMs
* Immediately contact the Bank via Customer Care, insist on raising a dispute and try to get the account and Card Blocked
* Visit your Bank's Branch and inform them in person about it
* Write a Mail to the Bank, put every single bit of information about the case, CC it to RBI OMBUDSMAN, and the Banks Branch if possible
* Search for RBI OMBUDSMAN and you'll find a RBI Page for raising complaints. Make a complaint there and explain carefully about what happened
* Make a Cyber Police Complaint about it. Contact them via their Number and make a Complaint on their Site as well (although in my case both of this did not work as the website was broken at the time and no one picked up the Number as it was Bank Holiday)
* Try to call and follow up with the Bank from time to time.
* Some people advised me to make a Police Complaint and FIR, however it maybe tough sometimes as not all Police are wholesome, ready to help, uwu owo mode. Based on some posts and videos, some of these try to charge a huge commission, take the whole money when you receive it via banks efforts, etc.
