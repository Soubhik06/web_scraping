So I applied for an administrative job that was posted online. It's for a tutoring company that's based in a nearby town. Note, I don't strictly have any professional administrative experience, but I'm a college graduate with customer service experience. I conducted an interview with two Indian gentlemen which seemed relatively normal for the most part. However, they did inform me that since the pandemic, they've gone mostly virtual. Which, remote is fine with me, but I was just a little skeptical cuz I've been spurned before. Anyways, they talk about setting me up with a computer to use and connecting my phone so I can answer business calls.

After the interview I was feeling a little concerned so I did some research. The place does have a Facebook page and the little side panel that comes up on Google came up for it. However, all the Google reviews are from Indian names including two of the people I interviewed with. Then I googled the name of one of guys I interviewed with. He showed up in an NBC news article about a murdered family. It wasn't his family, he was interviewed because the kids attended his tutoring academy apparently.

So I'm getting really mixed signals at this point and I just don't even know anymore. So what do you all think?
