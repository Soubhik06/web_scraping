Just a follow-up to my query that I posted here couple of days ago

https://www.reddit.com/r/india/comments/6gs9fc/a_query_about_nri_quota_in_engineering/

So my cousin wanted to apply for his son under the NRI quota data, which TL;DR me paying for his son's education, on paper.

I have been away from India for a number of years, and using my passport to perpetrate a fraud - a victimless crime though - is still an ethical concern for me. People getting admission through this route is definitely a "jugaad", but I somehow find it inappropriate.

However, my biggest concern was an obscure college in Gujarat having a possession of my passport copy. I looked on their website, but there was no information about how the document submitted with the admission form would be protected under the privacy laws.

I would still be okay, if GSEB, who is in charge of centralised admission (I assume - it used to be in past) had access to my passport details. But I just wasn't comfortable with a private college having access to my birth date and passport number with no accountability.

Am I thinking too much? Or is data breach not a big issue in India, where "jugaad" seems to be a norm? I just couldn't stomach a situation where someone could access my passport details whereby I would get into trouble anytime I crossed a country border
