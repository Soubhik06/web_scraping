Is this a potential new scam using fast tag?

Recently I received an email notification that my car's fast tag was used at Brahamarakotlu toll plaza on 15-11-2023 at 12:18:37, when I am at home and the car is parked in my garage. 

Upon contacting the number in the mail, and waiting for 30 mins to speak to the executive, I was told that the complaint is registered and it would take 25 working days to close. 

With shopping malls like the Mall of Asia in Bangalore planning to charge for parking using FastTag, is it really safe to pay using fastag when you can be charged by some random machine 300kms away? 

Even if there is a complaint mechanism, who would compensate for the time list in raising the complaint? How do we safeguard the fast tag from such exploits?
