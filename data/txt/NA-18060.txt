I get to see many videos on YouTube, where foreign vloggers explore the streets of Agra, Varanasi, Delhi or Mathura etc. And they shower praise on silly stuff, like you would know it's not authentic and it's just to farm more views. 

But in the long run, say when 15-20 years have passed and there is a new generation of young internet users, they'll stumble upon this stuff. Fake or not, positive publicity is positive publicity.

So even though annoying, I think there is some positive to it.

But the people who are shooting slum tours and shock value poverty, they, with their schadenfreude, can go to hell. Tourism department needs to take some action regarding this. It is so annoying; I can't wait for these people to get out of my country. 

They'll stay in the cheapest hotels, eat at random obscure street food stalls, travel in the general boggy and then when they'll be burnt out and sick: sad pikachu face-- india bad.

Poor crowded slums are "Real India". We at the city must be a simulation in the matrix developed by whoever the f\*\*\* developed the EPFO website then?
