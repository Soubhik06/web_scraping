I have been getting calls for a while now from a company called Midland Credit Management (when I Google the phone numbers, that's what comes up). They call from various numbers, both 800 and also local numbers. They do not call daily and never leave voicemails, which is different from what I've experienced with debt collectors in the past.

I have answered the calls a couple of times when I haven't screened the call, because I am expecting calls from doctors or job applications. Every time I answer they ask for a slight variation of my name that I do not go by (think of a Jessica being called Jessie). I always say "that's not my name" in response and the caller is either an AI voice or just ignores me because they continue speaking. The few times I've answered, the callers have all been foreign (either sounding Indian or Filipino) and have been men.

My phone doesn't screen these as scam likely. I am wondering if they are scam calls that have some misspelling of my name, or if in the process of one of my medical debts being sent to collections the wrong name was given. Other than the latter being highly unusual/unlikely, I think that would also potentially absolve me of whatever debt they're trying to collect.
