I used OLX today to post something for sale. Within an hour, I received many scam calls. They spoke like genuine buyers, asking about the product and if I used PhonePe. Afterward, they claimed to be army veterans, providing a QR code for payment to receive money and a note of the received amount. Beware of these scams.
