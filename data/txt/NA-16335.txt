Few months back I almost got scammed by a person claiming to be HDFC person.
I had created a HDFC salary account and wanted to get a credit card. I had asked for the credit card and the person said I will get a call regarding this later. When a call did come, they asked me for my Mobile no., email id, date of birth and otp. When they asked for otp I felt something was odd and cut the call. Later from the same number I got a message asking to fill up an online form and sent a link for it. I checked the link before opening it and found it to be genuine HDFC bank link for applying for credit card. They also sent a pic of an ID card ( which I much later found online ). Believing that this might be real, I sent her the final application ID and pan number ( she asked for it). My gut-feeling was ringing alarm bells and I thought I will check the ID card. I found it in an X post and it was about a credit card scam. So, I immediately blocked the number. 
Today, I got a call from a guy claiming to be HDFC bank person asking me about a pre sanctioned credit card. He told me my name, email id, phone number, date of birth, pan number. I said I will think about it and tell later, he pressured me saying this is the last date. Then I said I will only discuss in person with the branch I have an HDFC account with. So, he then told me my account number and the balance in it!
I am now very concerned and unsure what are the right steps to take.
