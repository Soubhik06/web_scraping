 Order No.05/Dated- 12.07.2024

 Heard learned counsel for the parties. 2. The petitioner has been made an accused in connection with
 Jamtara Cybercrime P.S. Case No. 74 of 2022 registered for the offences
 under Sections 413 / 414 / 419 / 420 / 467 / 468 / 471 / 120(B) of the Indian
 Penal Code and Sections 66(B) , 66(C) and 66(D) of the Information
 Technology Act, pending in the Court of learned Additional Sessions
 Judge-I, Jamtara. 3. As per FIR, informant got secret information about cybercrime
 activity committed in village Rampur-Madhopur near Bishnu Kumar
 Mandal's house, a raid was conducted on 19.12.2022 at 2:30 p.m. During
 the raid, four accused persons attempted to flee away, but the police
 apprehended three accused persons while one escaped. Upon search,
 multiple mobile phones with different IMEI numbers, numerous SIM
 cards, bank passbooks and debit cards all allegedly used for
 cybercrimes involving phishing and other digital frauds. Additionally,
 some motorcycles were found. The arrested individuals voluntarily
 confessed, implicating the petitioner as an associate involved in these
 cybercrimes, and identified him as the person who escaped during the
 raid. 4. Learned counsel for the petitioner has submitted that petitioner
 is innocent and has committed no offence at all rather he has been
 falsely implicated in this case only on the basis of confessional
 statement of co-accused. It is further submitted that nothing
incriminating material has been recovered from the physical and
conscious possession of the petitioner. No Test Identification Parade
(TIP) has been conducted. The petitioner is languishing in Judicial
Custody since 20.04.2024 without any rhymes and reasons. Petitioner
undertakes to cooperate in the trial of the case by remaining physically
present as and when required and shall not indulge in any manner in
tampering with the prosecution evidences or influencing the witnesses
of prosecution. It is lastly submitted that other co-accused persons
namely Sanoj Mandal and Sudam Mandal with similar allegation have
already been extended privilege of regular bail by the Co-ordinate
Bench of this Court passed in B.A. Nos. 895 of 2023 and 1125 of 2023
vide orders dated 17.03.2023 and 19.04.2023 respectively. Hence, the
petitioner may be extended the privilege of bail. 5. On the other hand, learned Addl.P.P appearing for the State has
opposed the prayer for bail of the petitioner but so far the matter of
parity is concerned, it is admitted that the case of present petitioner
stands on similar footing with the co-accused persons who have been
granted regular bail in this case. 6. Considering the facts and circumstances of the case, nature of
allegation coupled with the materials available against the petitioner
and period of custody of the petitioner, I am inclined to release the
petitioner on bail. Accordingly, the petitioner named above is directed
to be released on bail on furnishing of bail bond of Rs.25,000/- (Twenty
five thousand) with two sureties of the like amount each to the
satisfaction of learned Additional Sessions Judge-I, Jamtara in
connection with Jamtara Cybercrime P.S. Case No. 74 of 2022 subject to
