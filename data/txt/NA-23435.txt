Another question - 
Most of us are small fish in the market. There's always a systemic risk we undertake when investing - what if there's another Harshad Mehta/Ketan Parekh/Satyam like scam? What if we go to war with a neighboring country or the world plunges into a war (GOD forbid!)? We cannot be prepared for it as investors. Can we?
