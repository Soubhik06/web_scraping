my whole story 6/4/2024 with Screenshot and Photo

I was scammed out of 4,900 rs yesterday at 4 pm at the office no.6 ground floor Hind Rajesthan Building Opposite Chetan restaurant Near by Saraswat bank Dadasaheb phalke road dadar East Mumbai.

Here's how it happened: I arrived for an interview (my first interview) at 2 pm. There were 20+ applicants waiting there for the interview in a room. The interview was from 9 am to 3 pm (later they changed to 3:30), and I was one of the last applicants. Almost half of the applicants were experienced, including some young and middle-aged individuals, and freshers like myself, I talked to some applicants if this was a scam or not, they had no idea.

A lady was at the door sending applicants in groups or one by one into another room where the interview was being held. After almost 2 hours of waiting, I was sent to another room with the remaining applicants. In that room, some middle-aged applicants were waiting around me. As I sat there, the receptionist was registering our resume data on a notebook. Eventually, I was picked and sent to the first cabin. (three sound proof cabin in a room)

As interview begin, The interviewer asked me where I stayed and what position I was applying for. Then, she looked at my resume, wrote my monthly salary after asking my expected salary. This interview wasn't real; the lady didn't ask me anything about my skills or questions related to my job field. Her words were sloppy. Finally, she mentioned the money - 4,900 rupees for software, work from home, 5 days a week. She asked if I had a laptop with me and assured me it's refundable if I don't get selected in the second round of the interview. I asked if this was a scam (it is my own fault for not leaving immediately).

After continuous back and forth for 10mins, she called her boss. A big guy came in the cabin and again tried to convince me it wasn't a scam. like they are not consultant and all and ain't scam people here. Her boss spoke if he had to do a scam, he would do it in lakhs not in measly 4-5k, After trying to convince me this is not a scam, another guy came into the cabin (this guy was around the reception table continuously going in and out of the room checking there were no applicants gathering around at the gate of Hind Rajasthan building after the interview).

This guy and her boss and lady in the office chair, three people were in the cabin with me convincing me that this is not a scam. After a few minutes, I gave in. I UPI'd 4,900, after that, I was sent to the next cabin for the 2nd round, the lady on the chair making a receipt of my payment. She called a guy saying this is some company boss (phone call) and I talk to him. the guy asked me a question about the position I was applying for which I did not give much of an answer, and again I asked if this was not a scam to this lady, she said the same stuff. In the receipt, there are some conditions written clearly saying a job is not guaranteed that I did not even read carefully. she distracted me, told me to sign up this receipt.
