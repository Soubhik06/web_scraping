Given that most posts on this sub, are about scams, here's one that almost happened with me:

I was once almost fooled by a group of 3 college-going-age girls in HSR, who asked me for 500 cash, in return of 500 Paytm transfer. (Apparently, they had to buy earrings and other small things, and didn't have cash) I asked them to transfer me the money first. They scanned my QR code, and showed me a confirmed transfer. I asked them to wait, to check my wallet, but I had received nothing. I asked them to show me their screenshot closely, but they just gave me irritated looks, and walked away without even bothering about the 500 that they had apparently sent me.

Obviously, I never got those 500, and it was most likely a screenshot from one of those fake UPI apps, that she had shown me.

Not sure if they were habitual scammers, or just garden-variety "students from good families", who have a habit of having a grander lifestyle than what their parents can afford them. You know the kind...!! People would sneak and eat in others' weddings, or steal small things from shops, and pretend that it's not a big deal.

Since then, I never pay money to anyone in such scenarios, unless I can see the money credit to my account, and I tell them in advance. If they are scammers, or do not have the patience, they are free to walk away.
