I visited a friend the other day, who said to me while on the phone, Sorry, I'm talking to a person at Microsoft about why my pc screen is locked. As I listened to her end of the conversation, it slowly dawned on me that she was being scammed by a fake tech person. I got closer and could hear the so called Microsoft guy, who was talking SO much, really fast, in an East Indian accent, asking her to open various sites, allow him access to her pc, finding her IP address, etc. Luckily I convinced her to hang up. She said she had no idea it was a scam, as her screen froze and a phone number appeared, for her to call for "help." I suggested she call the real Microsoft tech support number, and her bank, just in case. She had not given out any important info (yet!)...I see these things on my phone and ignore them. Funny thing is that she never "turned her pc off and on again" to try to fix the screen issue! Supposedly turning off your router for 5 minutes resets your ip address (?), so she did that. Now she knows!
