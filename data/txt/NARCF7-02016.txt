Hi everyone, I was nalla napping today when I got a call from a FedEx automated line saying my parcel was returned. I had got some house plants that I have been waiting on and thought it was that. Some lady at customer service was like we have intercepted a parcel from Taiwan with your name, number, and aadhar card details. I was like I haven't gotten anything from Taiwan. She is like okay in that case you have to talk to Mumbai Police Andheri. I was like I will call you back on this number, she tried forcing and asked why I am hesitant if I didn't do anything wrong. I told her I will need to talk to my folks, lawyer, and can go to the commisioner office in Chennai instead. She then said okay and cut the call. 

So ya this is a common and new scam where people call impersonating delivery companies and then connect you to people who impersonate police officers, CBI, narcotics bureau, etc. They will ask you to verify details, bank statements, etc. And seek remuneration which they stress will be refundable before cutting contact. 

Here is a link to an article - https://www.indiatoday.in/technology/news/story/indians-losing-lakhs-to-the-new-courier-scam-what-is-happening-how-to-stay-safe-2399043-2023-06-28

Stay safe, everyone.
