Hi all,

I just realized something shocking on Amazon and something which I think is just blatant daylight fraud and robbery. I think this might have affected others too and I just wanted to spread awareness about this.

It all started when I ordered a sunscreen on Amazon on 5Aug24, it was supposed to be a so called "rush delivery" which would arrive by 5Aug24 between 12pm-4pm and was supposed to be handled by Amazon Fresh. The order never arrived on 5Aug24. Somewhere around 6Aug24 I tried to cancel it via the app but it didn't allow me to cancel. I called the customer care and even they weren't able to cancel. It is already 7Aug24 and still the order is shown as "preparing for dispatch"/"was expected by 5Aug24" and it won't let me cancel it nor will customer care cancel it.

https://preview.redd.it/4tr36xol8ahd1.png?width=1615&amp;format=png&amp;auto=webp&amp;s=2e28d78000c95689f1f72969135a59d28a1140df

Out of curiosity I decided to check my order history and I found another order which was also of the same status. The order status to my shock was "Was expected 21-Sep-23".

https://preview.redd.it/xv7q07878ahd1.png?width=1632&amp;format=png&amp;auto=webp&amp;s=39f0a047e382412a822a51cc98d8db2ffb2d27da

I checked my credit card statement and the amount was already deducted for this order and was never even refunded nor did this order ever come to me. I called the customer care and the guy simply stated "Since this is an order which is more than 90 days old, we can't do anything about it". I lost my cool and shouted at him that am I supposed to keep monitoring Amazon? To that he responded "Humaari thodi galti hai aur aapki bhi".

I was shell shocked at his response. My entire trust on Amazon has eroded. There is no customer care either. It is all a sham with this company.

**If you have ordered anything from Amazon, check you order history, I am sure there is a chance that there will be some such order which will show "Was expected by XXX" and was never refunded nor delivered.**

If someone from Amazon is reading this, highlight this to your management if you guys even care. This is so messed up, there maybe so many people who's money is with Amazon even though they never received the products.
