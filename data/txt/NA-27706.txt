this scam is pretending to be hiring for white orange, an actual software company in india. they texted me posing as a recruiter but upon searching indeed didn’t seem to have any job postings. the red flags for me were the mention of PAYMENT, the way they are reaching out to me for a software job when i have 0 software experience, and this lady’s unprofessional way of talking to me and trying to convince me to do the training… i read about similar experiences like this under different company names so just be cautious. i noticed after being scammed many times the word “kindly” is also often used
