I got an ad on TikTok—don’t even remember what it was about, but I clicked it. It led straight to a Telegram user. I hit them up saying I saw their ad and was looking for work. I knew it was a scam, but I was curious what the angle was.

They told me to give some random restaurant in Mexico a 5-star review—no text needed, just stars and a screenshot. I did it, and they passed me to someone else on Telegram with some weird string of text to send. Did that too.

This new chick—again, profile was some Asian girl (they all seem to do that)—asked if I used some obsecure crypto platform. I said no. It went quiet until I asked if payments were in USDT. She said yes. I gave her my TRC20 wallet, and to my surprise, she actually sent me $5.

Then she gave me another review task for a place in India—same deal, same $5. Then came the real scam: she said I could "learn to trade" and asked me to deposit $50 on aforementioned platform. That’s when I told her to f* off and blocked her.

But hey, I made $10 for almost nothing. Weird little scam, but kind of clever.
