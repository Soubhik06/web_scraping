If anyone of you gets a call from people claiming to be from Mama Earth (they will have your name, number, address everything since Mama Earth company leaks/sells our data after every purchase) please CUT THE CALL. I got scammed and lost a lot of money in thousands as they said its some promotional offer and I was winning laptop/tv/iphone etc if I do shopping of minimum Rs 5000. 

Just start ordering everything COD (cash on delivery) and stop online payments for a while please. Learn from my mistakes. 

And if you HAVE to do any online payment, use paytm or E-wallets. Do NOT link netbanking or UPI.
