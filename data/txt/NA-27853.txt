Tons of scam calls the past two weeks. Calling my burner. I do so enjoy messing with them. In every case it has been the same scam call telling me I'm entitled to a refund and a gift card.

And of course each time I am connected it is always to somebody with a very thick Indian accent calling from a very busy call center as judging by the noise in the background.

They always ask me for my ZIP code and no matter what five digit number I give them they tell me that that number qualifies for the refund and the gift card. But of course in order to get the gift card I have to go grab my electric bill and read them the account number.

The first several times I was able to get away with asking them, "Do you want DN?"

And of course they always ask me, "What is DN?"

Naturally there is only one response to that question:

DEEEEEEEEEEEEEZ NUTS

And this is the point in the conversation where they hang up on me.

Now they are hanging up on me as soon as I asked them if they want DN.

I just wish that once, they'd say, yes, please, I want DN.
