We all know how markets are overpriced right now, anywhere you look you'll will see stocks priced ridiculously to their earnings but that's not the point of this post. What I want to point out is how Nifty Next 50 is now in very concerning state with regards to its recent constituent weights. If you take a look [Top constituents by weightage as of May 31, 2021](https://www1.nseindia.com/content/indices/ind_next50.pdf) you would notice how top positions has been hijacked by Adani stocks.

Stock | Weight
---|---
Adani Green Energy Ltd | 3.59
Adani Enterprises Ltd. | 3.32
Adani Transmission Ltd. | 2.93

If you follow your stock markets you would know that for the past 1 years we have seen exuberant increase in these stock prices without any fundamentals which infact has gone worse now due to piling of up ever increasing debt.

[Sucheta Dalal](https://mobile.twitter.com/suchetadalal/status/1403576980742627333) has pointed out that there's a possibly a scam going on with Adani Related stocks wherein a certain group of FII investors have &gt; 90% of the self worth in these stocks. All the freeloat has been acquired by these mysterious foreign investors. Adani group is now using their increased stock prices to pledge more stocks to get more debt and expand their business.

I know this sub loves index funds including myself but it may seem that we have caught ourselves in bit of dilemma specially with Nifty Next 50. What are your thoughts?
