As someone working at senior levels of Compliance Department of an international Bank overseeing a number of countries including India and UAE, I just wanted to share this article with fellow malayalees. Do not EVER loan your Bank account to anyone to conduct transactions that are not yours or without your knowledge. Also, the article shines light on the dark world of fraud, social engineering scams, laundering funds internationally through crypto rails and thr minions being caught. Please know that well organised, highly resourceful international organised criminal gangs are often behind these elaborate scam themes and gullible (I won't say young or old, as anyone can fall for these) people trying to make a quick buck ends up being caught. 

Please spend some time educating yourself of these risks and be careful when dealing with crypto. If you need to trade, ensure you do so 100% legally, through a properly licensed VASP and Exchange, and only deal with counterparties who have been vetted. Also know the tax implications, both in India and UAE.
