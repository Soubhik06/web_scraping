Been exploring the pros and cons of both ways of buying term insurance. Most online guides just gloss over the usuals:

* Online - ease of buying, no need to visit branch, no need to sustain the sales dialogue of agents, clear CSR, no fraud, etc.
* Offline - better info about riders (but still the fineprint can be difficult to understand for laymen), possible better guidance

But an important concern is the medical check-up:

* How does it happen when you buy online?
* Can you insist on getting a check-up?

ICICI Pru, for instance, seems to provide term plan where there is no difference in the premium when you opt for offline/online. But claims have to be filed offline.

**TL; dr**: What is this sub's opinions on buying insurance online vs offline? Which method would you recommend (or choose) and why?
