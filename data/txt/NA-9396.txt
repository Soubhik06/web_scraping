 The petitioner assails inter alia an order passed by the
 Enforcement Directorate attaching properties of a company
 involved in a Ponzi Scheme under the provisions of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 It appears from orders dated August 12, 2016, August
 18, 2016 and August 31, 2016 passed by the Division Bench in
 MAT 512 of 2015 that the Division Bench had constituted a
 Special Bench for taking up chit fund matters. The Special
 Bench constituted for such purpose had taken up the Ponzi
 Scheme matters and had directed their appearance in Their
 Lordships list. The present writ petition relates to a company
 claimed to be involved in Ponzi Scheme
 In such circumstances W.P. 21551(w) of 2016 is
 released due to lack of determination
 Urgent certified website copies of this order, if applied
 for, be made available to the parties upon compliance of the
 requisite formalities. ( DEBANGSU BASAK, J. )
