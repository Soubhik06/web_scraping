I just had a Fraud Analyst interview with "Capgemini US". Two guys with heavy Indian accent saying they were from Capgemini in Pensilvania, they start off the bat (without introducing themselves) asking me about my CV. I go through it multiple times because they seem to not quite follow what Im saying. Then they ask me some SQL questions, while the job description said only Python is needed. During this whole time they didnt tell me anything about the position (ironically it was for Fraud Analyst position). When they ask me if I have questions I ask them what the day to day tasks are, and they only tell me "you use Python and SQL to create mathematical models to uncover fraud". I was suspicious already, so I ask them to explain to me in more detail what these projects look like, to which they say the same information as before.

At this point I was pretty sure this was a scam, I was worried that I had my cam on all the time during the call. 

Just letting you guys know in case it happens to you. And also, they contacted me through LinkedIn, I will try to report the accounts as fake.
