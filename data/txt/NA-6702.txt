Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and learned

 counsel for the State. Learned counsel for the petitioner is directed to

 remove the defects, as pointed out by the office, within a period

 of four weeks. The petitioner is apprehending his arrest in connection

 with Minapur P.S. Case No. 265/2020 for the offence registered

 under Sections 414 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 472 , 120(B) / 34 of the

 I.P.C. and Sections 25 (1-b)a, 26 and 35 of the Arms Act . The prosecution story, in brief, is that on 20.07.2020

 at about 4.30 P.M. at A.T.M., situated near the State Bank of

 India, Minapur Branch. The informant received information

 that 4-5 persons were standing near A.T.M. and out of whom,

 one was inside the A.T.M. since very long time having revolver
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.35353 of 2021(2) dt.23-05-2022 2/3 and thereafter he alongwith other police forces reached there,

 seeing the police force coming, four persons fled away through

 Motorcycle and one was caught by the police and on search of

 his person, loaded country made pistol, bullet, bag containing

 A.T.M. Cloning Machine and Android Mobile with two Sim

 Cards were recovered. Four different A.T.M. Cards with

 different names were also recovered from the pocket and the

 accused disclosed his name as Mithun Kumar as well as names

 of his associates who managed to escape and he further said that

 they have a five persons gang, whose work is to withdraw the

 money of other persons and they all used to go together having

 country made pistol, knife etc.

 It has been submitted by learned counsel for the

 petitioner that the petitioner has got no criminal antecedent.

 There is no allegation of tampering with the witnesses alleged

 against the petitioner. The petitioner has falsely been implicated

 in the present case. The name of the petitioner has transpired in

 the present case on the basis of disclosure made by co-accused.

 Except for this, there is no other substantive evidence to suggest

 the implication of the petitioner in the present case. There is no

 recovery of any incriminating article from possession of the

 petitioner. The recovery of incriminating articles is from the co-
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.35353 of 2021(2) dt.23-05-2022 3/3 accused Mithun Kumar. On behalf of the State, it is submitted that the

 petitioner is named in the F.I.R. Considering the aforesaid facts and circumstances of

 the case, let the petitioner above named, in the event of arrest or

 surrender before the learned court below within a period of

 twelve weeks from today, be released on anticipatory bail on

 furnishing bail bonds of Rs. 10,000/- (Rupees Ten Thousand)

 with two sureties of the like amount each to the satisfaction of

 the learned C.J.M., Muzaffarpur, in connection with Minapur

 P.S. Case No. 265/2020, subject to the conditions as laid down

 under Section 438(2) of the Code of Criminal Procedure. (Sudhir Singh, J)
