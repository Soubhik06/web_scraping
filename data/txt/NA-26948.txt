Hey everyone,

I’m writing this post because I recently got scammed on Telegram and I want to warn others, as well as get some help identifying these scammers. I lost ₹7,000, and I’m still in shock.

Here’s how it happened:

Earlier today, I was contacted on whatsapp by someone claiming to offer a simple online job. They said I could earn money by completing “review tasks” for restaurants and boosting their ratings. It sounded easy and legit. They asked me to download telegram, join a group where they even showed fake screenshots of “payments” other people had received.

Initially, they asked me to complete a couple of small tasks, and I actually received ₹203 in return. That built my trust. Then, they convinced me to take on a “higher-paying” task that required an upfront payment of ₹7,000 to “unlock” the tasks. They promised I’d get my money back along with additional earnings. After I made the payment, they stopped responding. The Telegram account disappeared, and I was blocked from their channel.
I feel so stupid for falling for this. I’ve want to report this to the Cyber police but I know it’s going to be hard to get my money back.

I wanted to ask:
Has anyone else experienced a similar scam on Telegram?
Do you have any contact details or Telegram usernames of these scammers?
Are there any groups or resources to track and report these scams?
Is there any legal action I can take that could be effective?

Please be careful and don’t trust these “review task” scams. They prey on people who are just looking to make a little extra money. If anyone has any advice or information that could help, I’d really appreciate it.
