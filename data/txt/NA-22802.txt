I was curious, what do you think is happening incase of **growpital**. Around 5,000 investors are left empty handed by what seems to have been a popularly backed ponzi scheme

**What is growpital?** 
Growpital is an alternative investment startup that allowed for investing in its. agriculture produce. It offered upto 12-18% tax-free returns to investors for a span of 3 years

**Where the story gets interesting?**

* It mis-represented the farm acre it operated to investors when seeking investments
* This was backed and awarded by various national awards and media outlets also
* It has worked like any other ponzi scheme, but this one's valuation reached **100+crores**
* SEBI smelled something fishy (as a guideline of 100cr+ investment was breached), and has put the GP funds on hold
* The founder, Rituraj has blamed on SEBI for loss of money and is filing a court case, and is using all possible delay tactics to get the case lengthened
* The investors have recently gotten the right to interject after 5 months of the case going on.
