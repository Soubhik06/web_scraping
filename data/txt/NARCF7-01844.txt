I have come across "free holiday" voucher a few times. Once was by Club Mahindra in Phoenix. They would get our numbers and then randomly call us one day and tell us we were chosen in lucky draw and we are getting a free holiday for three days and two nights etc etc. That we just have to come collect it in person and no other formalities or hidden charges. My parents always shrugged it off as a scam and being in school, I didn't have much of a say in it otherwise. 

Yesterday a colleague of mine was casually telling me about her anniversary trip she has planning for republic day in mahabalipuram. But then she mentioned that this trip was actually from that voucher from prestige group or something and they won it in a lucky draw. It was two vouchers- one day in mahabalipuram and three days two nights in munnar/Ooty/Kodaikanal. The resort she has booked/redeemed seemed very decent as well. Some Country Resort whose online tarrif went upto 7k. Not bad at all. 

So should I pay attention the next time I get a call from a woman in a dingy room telling me I should come collect a holiday voucher? Does anyone else have similar experiences with such vouchers?
