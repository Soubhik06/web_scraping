1. By way of present order, I shall decide a preliminary issue
 framed vide order dated 11.05.2010 to the following effect:- 2. Ld. counsel for the parties argued that the present issue being
 legal issue can be decided without leading evidence of the parties.
 Argument heard. Record Perused. 3. Ld. counsel for the defendant no. 1 argued that defendant no. 1 was discharged from the hospital on 15.07.2005 and final bill
 dated 30.07.2005 is of Rs. 17,323/-, therefore, the suit would have
 been filed within three years from 30.07.2005 but as the plaintiff
 failed to file the present suit within three years from the date of
 cause of action, therefore, the present suit of the plaintiff is barred
 by limitation. Ld. counsel for the defendants no. 2 and 3 argued that
 defendant no. 2 vide its letter dated 27.09.2005 informed the
 plaintiff about the repudiation of the claim of defendant no. 1 i.e.
 Mr. Abdul Khalique . Ld. counsel for the defendant no. 2 and 3
 further argued that defendants no. 2 and 3 came to know from the

 discharge summary issued by plaintiff hospital that the respondent
 no. 1 has been operated for similar symptoms and abscess in
 February and April 2005 which fact was concealed by defendant no. 1. Ld. counsel for the defendants no. 2 and 3 argued that suit of the
 plaintiff is barred by limitation. 4. On the other hand, ld. counsel for the plaintiff argued that suit
 is within limitation by virtue of Section 17 of Limitation Act as the
 fraud engaged in by the defendants in collusion with each other, was
 surfaced on manual check of records by plaintiff's Accounts branch
 officials in the last week of June 2009, therefore, the suit of the
 plaintiff is within limitation period. 5. In brief, the facts necessary for disposal of present issue are,
 that plaintiff filed the present suit for recovery of Rs. 29,795/-
 against defendants on the averments that defendant no. 1 availed
 treatment at plaintiff's establishments by representing that plaintiff's
 bill of expense will be reimbursed by defendants no. 2 and 3 to
 plaintiff in account of defendant no. 1 entitlement covered by
 Insurance policy. On or about 12/13th July 2005, defendant no. 1
 obtained admission as a patient for receiving surgery for ailment he
 suffered and on his signing pre-authorization request form issued by
 defendant no. 2 and based upon estimations provided by plaintiff, it
 was certified by defendant no. 2 that hospital bills up to value of Rs.
 20,000/- would be paid by them to the plaintiff after completion of
 treatment by plaintiff on defendant no. 1. It is case of the plaintiff
 that based upon documents produced by defendant no. 1 and
 authorization granted by defendant no. 2, plaintiff hospital raised bill
 of value Rs. 17,323/- and submitted to defendants no. 2 and 3 for
 payment but defendants no. 2 disowned liabilities to pay amount of

 plaintiff's bill on the reasoning that the information revealed by the
 defendant no. 1 in the pre authorization request form and the
 treatment chart described in the discharge summary given to
 defendant no. 1 at the time of his discharge from plaintiff hospital
 contained discrepancy. It is further case of the plaintiff that ground
 of repudiation is based on fraud played and displayed by the
 defendants in collusion with each other for denying payment to the
 plaintiff and the manual scanning of records revealed that defendant
 no. 1 received treatment at plaintiff's establishments on 13.04.2005
 reflected in plaintiff's final bill dated 14.04.2005 paid by him in cash
 and these records have come to notice in the last week of June 2009
 after failing to obtain response from defendant no. 1 after service
 and delivery of legal notice. It is claimed by plaintiff that suit is
 within limitation by virtue of Section 17 of Limitation Act as period
 of limitation for claim against defendants begins to run from the last
 week of June 2009 when fraud engaged in by defendants in
 collusion with each other surfaced on manual check of records by
 plaintiff's Account Branch officials. 6. The fact that defendant no. 1 availed treatment at plaintiff's
 establishments on or about 12/13th July 2005 and was having
 insurance policy cover issued by defendant no. 3 valid up to
 29.05.2006 is not in dispute. Perusal of record shows that claim of
 the plaintiff bill for treatment of defendant no. 1 under policy of
 defendant no. 3 was repudiated vide letter dated 27.09.2005 as filed
 on record wherein it is mentioned that in the pre-authorization
 request form- Pain Let Gluteal region & fever for 4-5 days and no
 past h/o similar/any other ailment, while in the discharge summary-
 duration of symptoms is one month & past h/o operate for similar

 abscess in Feb. & April 2005 is mentioned. When the discharge
 summary of defendant no. 1 issued by plaintiff itself shows that
 duration of symptom of defendant no. 1 was for one month and past
 history of similar abscess in February & April 2005, it cannot be
 said that defendants no. 2 and 3 played any fraud in collusion with
 defendant no. 1, therefore, the period of limitation will start either
 from the date when the plaintiff raised the final bill to defendants
 vide letter dated 11.09.2005 or from the date when the defendant no.
 2 gave reply to the plaintiff vide letter dated 27.09.2005 informing
 that in the discharge summary duration of symptoms is one month
 & past h/o operated for similar abscess in Feb. & April 2005 and
 thus the claim of the plaintiff was repudiated. The plea of the
 plaintiff that on manual check of their record in June 2009 they
 came to know that defendant no. 1 received treatment on 13.04.2005
 cannot help the plaintiff to extend the period of limitation by taking
 the aid of Section 17 of Limitation Act, due to the reason, that these
 facts as allegedly discovered on manual check in June 2009 was
 already mentioned by defendant no. 2 in the repudiation letter dated
 27.09.2005 and same were also mentioned in the discharge
 summary of defendant no. 1 issued by plaintiff hospital. Therefore,
 the suit of the plaintiff as filed on 02.07.2009 for claim of final bill
 dated 30.07.2005 for Rs. 17,323/- is clearly barred by limitation.
 Accordingly, this issue is decided against the plaintiff and in favour
 of the defendants.
