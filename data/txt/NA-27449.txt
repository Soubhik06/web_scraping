Last week I received a message on LinkedIn from someone claiming to work for a research company doing a study about work from home habits post pandemic. He also advertised an incentive of $100 if you get selected for the study. I looked into his profile, he had a lot of legitimate connections, the company (albeit out of India) seemed legit, and the link he sent me was not malicious (ran it through a couple of checkers). 

The information they had me fill out in the survey was pretty basic stuff, stuff that you could find pretty much anywhere on the internet. The person told me to send them a screenshot of the completion page once I had completed it. I’ve done so, and have not heard back. 

I have received a few spam calls since filling that form out out, and while I don’t think the data collection itself was a way of compromising my credentials, I have a hunch that they used this as a method of collecting data that they either sell to scammers who then try to contact you with the info you provided on the form, or they do it in house but just front as a “research” company.

Any ideas? Am i being paranoid?
