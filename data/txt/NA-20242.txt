My mom bought some jewellery from a small seller's website (Jiwaki). It was delivered by Bluedart, but when she opened it, it was nothing like the product photo on their website. We tried contacting the number and email on the seller's website but the number was invalid and there was no reply to the email.

It was a COD order and she paid the Bluedart delivery guy on Google Pay. So we called Bluedart to ask for help. They said that they can't help because they didn't get the order directly from the seller. They gave us a number and asked us to call there. It was Shiprocket's number. 

We called Shiprocket and they created a ticket to help us with the refund. First they asked to share photos of the shipping details and the product we received. Then they asked to share the Google Pay number and name.

Now they're asking to share bank account details and ID proof. When we asked why, they're saying the seller is not responding to them but will need these details for the refund so Shiprocket is asking in advance. We asked why they needed Google Pay details earlier. They said "we're asking for whatever details the seller is asking us to give". 

Does this look like another scam the seller is trying to pull? Can we trust Shiprocket since there's email proof and a ticket number with Shiprocket?
