Has the following ever happened to you:
- You’re at a red light 🔴
- There is no “No Free Left Turn” sign
- You have a clear path to make a safe free left turn
- And you make the turn, the cops are waiting to catch you and they charge with you running a red light.

Why is this a scam:
- If free turns weren’t a thing, then we wouldn’t have “No Free Left Turn” signs in the first place
- Since there is some ambiguity (if you don’t think about it), the cops take full advantage of this (spouting some law about 3 road junction vs 4 road junction).

Other relevant data:
- In California (drives on the right side of the road), red light cameras are being removed for free safe right turns and past offenders are being compensated/refunded their fines. People had to wake up to the scam and had to file a lawsuit for this to happen though
