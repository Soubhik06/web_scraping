This is a FedEx scam where the person calls and informs you that your FedEx parcel has been cancelled. He then tells you that your Aadhar card, name and number have been misused. Note that he only repeats your phone number and NOT your Adhar card number. He then diligently informs you of all the details and will forward the call to Mumbai cyber crime department because your adhaar card has been misused. 
Parcel details- 
Complaint: Aadhar misuse complaint 
Dept name: FedEx express Mumbai branch 
Parcel date: 4th May, 2024 
Receiver name: Mr. Zhanglin 
Contact number: +88-62737889 
Receiver address: 112-3, Sanbei Road, Taipei City, Taiwan- 104001 
Consignment number: 328932129197 
Parcel content: 5 Traveling passports, 3 bank credit cards, 200gm MDMA, 4kg clothes, 1 laptop, 35000INR cash 
FedEx employee- Rahul Kumar- 58181 (Company ID) 
Pickup address: 208, KP Engineering compound, Near Sumer Plaza, Marul Marushi Road, Mumbai- 400059 
 
Once the call gets transferred to the Mumbai Cyber Crime dept, some fellow asks rudely 'kya dikkat hai. Aise hi complaint nahi karte'. I cut the call after this. I checked the consignment number and there is no such consignment number. 
The phone number from which I received the call- 8143869715 
Be safe. Be aware.
