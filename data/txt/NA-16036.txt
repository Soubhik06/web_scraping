Hello guys ,

So I got a phone call today from a supposedly Bajaj Finserv credit card agent (he called from a personal phone number , not a company number) offering me credit card with a limit of 1,95,000 INR without any documents , on card to card basis he said which means that it will get approved based on the details of my existing credit card.

After that he texted me on WhatsApp and sent a link ([**Bflecares.online**](https://Bflecares.online)) saying that I have to fill my credit card form on this website and make a payment of 1 Rupee from my existing credit card. 
I didn't suspect anything until now , as everything was going well.

While filling the form on that website , it asked for my : name , phone number , email , Aadhar card number , PAN card number , Date of Birth , occupation and address.

I filled everything up and then it asked for a payment of 1 Rupee.

I entered my credit card details and then I got an OTP text from my bank for a transaction of INR 40,644 at Locon Solutions.

My mind was blown here , I just realised that someone was doing fraud with me.

I immediately closed that website.

I told the scammer that I won't enter any OTP on that website. He then asked me to hold the call and said that he needs to contact his senior manager and blah blah.

He then cut the call. I think he realised that he's been caught.

I then just realised that I entered my credit card details on a fraudulent website , so I immediately contacted my bank and blocked the card and placed a request for replacement of my credit card.

I checked details of that website on [scamvoid.net](https://scamvoid.net) and got to know that the website was created only 4 days ago.

I still don't feel comfortable that the fraudulent website has my personal details like my address , Aadhar card number , email and what not.

If anyone can please suggest me what I can do to safeguard myself from any fraud because of the personal information I entered on that website , that'd be appreciated.

Also , has anyone else been contacted for the same fraud ?
