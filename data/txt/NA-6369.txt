 Procedure 1973 for grant of bail filed on behalf of the applicant, who has been
 arrested relating to FIR/Crime No.171/2021 registered at Police Station Cyber
 Cell, district Bhopal, for the offences punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 465 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 201 and 204 of IPC and Section 66-C and 66-D of I.T. Act. 2. Learned counsel for the applicant submits that the applicant is in
 judicial custody since 15.03.2022 and the trial will take considerable time to

 and is ready to furnish adequate surety and shall abide by all the conditions to
 be imposed by the Court. Upon these grounds, it is prayed that the applicant be
 released on bail. 3. Learned counsel for the State opposes the bail application. 4. Heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the case diary. 5. In this bail application it has been argued that the applicant is in
 custody since 15.03.2022 and the trial will take time, the passport of applicant
 has already been seized and resultantly, there is no chance of leaving the
 country, hence, prayer for bail has been made. It has also been submitted that

 the the complainant in para 13 of his statements recorded as PW-1 has
 specifically said that applicant committed no fraud with him. 6. The bail application has been strongly opposed by the State. 7. The Godwin Favour is the co-accused in this case and his act is
 identical to the act of present applicant in the commission of crime. The bail
 application of Godwin Favour has already been dismissed in
 M.Cr.C.No.40982/2023 vide order dated 04.12.2023. The reasons for
 dismissal of said bail application are applicable here also for the applicant is
 involved in a grave financial crime involving a fraud committed to the tune of
 Rs.60,00,000/-. Applicant was using 19 sims and 15 mobiles for perpetration of
 this crime, the bank documents of registered bank account customers were
 obtained and the receipts of crime were being diverted to these accounts, the
 said bank accounts were all over India but the amount deposited therein was
 being redirected to other secondary bank accounts and the money was being
 withdrawn in Delhi; the account holders were Indians but it was found that the
 money was being withdrawn through ATM by a persons of foreign appearance; phone numbers of U.K. and Nigerian Origins were used for committing this
 fraud but the identity was being concealed; it is also established in investigation
 that applicant had closed links with co-accused persons through Whatsapp
 chats. 8. The description of the crime is that innocent persons were allured to
 invest in a fake company being run by applicant and co-accused and
 complainant Manish Patel was one such victim. Although Manish Patel has
 stated that he was not making any payment to applicant but looking to the
 nature of crime, it seems that he was not having the idea of extent and depth of
 this crime. 9. Taking into consideration that the evidence gathered during the
 investigation shows his deep involvement in the crime, this Court is not inclined
 to grant bail to the applicant. 10. Accordingly, this M.Cr.C. is dismissed. (ANURADHA SHUKLA)
