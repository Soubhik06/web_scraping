Was on my way today afternoon from a wedding in Palace grounds. I had a short work near civil court and noticed I didn’t have enough fuel to travel back home.

I went to the petrolbunk near the SBI bank circle . When I reached there, the guys of Indian oil were just standing. I clearly spoke and told them ₹200 in kannada, that guy hear it as ₹50. While I was removing my mobile , he noticed it was an iPhone and started distracting me . But I saw the reading in the machine saying ₹51.23. Later he kept asking about the iPhone when the reading on the machine said ₹151.23. The guy said he added 50 and now 150 which is 200. I scanned the QR and paid ₹150 and said , I’ve paid ₹250 by mistake. Hence he gave me another ₹50 worth petrol. Just thinking , how they think they can scam everyone and believe the can’t get scammed?
