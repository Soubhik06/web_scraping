--- POST TEXT ---

Basically, multiple goalkeeper syndrome is just what it seems. A goalpost is being defended by multiple goalkeeper. Theoretically, the more number of goalkeepers should mean lesser chance of a goal being scored. But, in reality, each goalkeeper isn't playing at his 100% best because he feels /thinks that the other goalkeeper will stop the goal if he misses, so he half asses his work.

Similarly in India, there are multiple organisations often enforcing same laws and regulations. Despite so many organisations like Enforcement directorate, Income tax department, CBI financial wing etc etc, frauds and scams still happen.

The reason being that each organisation is half assing their efforts since they believe that even if they fail, the other organisations will catch the culprit or mistake. Lack of clear demarcation of power is leading to the bucking being passed around to each other.

This is resulting in no organisation working at their level best.

What do you think of this theory?

**Let us take an example**

Banker X is doing some scam. There are three government organisations A,B,C tasked with preventing such scams.

Organization A thinks since B,C haven't raised any alarm, there is most probably no scam

Organization B thinks since A and C do the same work too, hence they don't need to investigate too deeply

Organization C wants to investigate deeply but their elected leaders say there is no need since A, B haven't raised any alarm.

Basically, no one is getting any work done since everyone thinks the other guys are doing it and this leads to crooks getting away easily
