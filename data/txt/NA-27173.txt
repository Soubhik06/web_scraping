--- POST TEXT ---

Some guy who called himself **Jimmy Arthur** called me from this number - **(302) 504-6256** and said that I owe money to IRS because of incorrect filing information in W2 form lines 7, 10 and 14 and that I have there has been funds sent/received from overseas into my checking account. **He gave me his IRS badge number - 6091 0506 90 and IRS case number - 00/100/3636 **

He then said that the IRS had send 2 notices to me on 7th August and 14th August which were not received by anyone. I said that I had recently moved and the current occupants were not there. He then said that there is a case on file against and it will be forwarded to the legal department and they will send the police at my door step and detain me for three days. And he told me to get an attorney 

I panicked because this was the first time I had filed my taxes and I might have made errors and they might have indeed sent notices to my old address. He sensed me panicking and his tone changed and he said he would talk to my case manager and see if something could be done about my case without need for legal action. He put me on hold and then informed that since I had my address changed he could get my time-frame to pay the amount extended but I would need to send him my personal information like **passport**, **drivers license** , **house lease**, **paystubs** via USPS mail to: 
IRS Office
611 South Dupont Highway
Dover
Delaware 19901

And that I need to pay the amount I owe in back taxes immediately, because if the line got disconnected the file would automatically move to the legal department and then I would be arrested. He then proceeded to "verify" my checking account and asked for credit and debit card details. I complied and gave him all that info. He put me on hold and said that cards got declined and he is afraid he can't do anything. In my irrational state I asked if there was any other way, like cashiers check or anything ? He put me on hold again and after "consulting with the accounts department" he said that he could process a Greendot cash card and he told me to withdraw cash and go to 7/11 store and make a cash card and give him the 14 digit code in the back. 

My rational part of the brain must have shut down because I simply complied. I went to the bank, withdrew 620 dollars in cash and made Greendot cash vouchers and gave the money to him. He then said that my case would be cleared and put me on hold to get someone from accounts department to process and give me a reference number. 

After a brief hold some woman who called herself "Victoria" picked up claiming to be from the IRS accounts department and she said that she was able to get clearance from Delaware and Washington IRS offices but she was not able to get clearance from department of homeland security and she would need $1000 more as deposit until they receive my documents and after that they would refund the amount to me. 

I kinda thought that this does not make sense ... ( as if everything prior to this made sense ) but In my panicked state I called a couple of friends asking for the money, because I don't keep money in my checking account and I had already passed my limit widthdrawing from my savings. And thankfully my friends put some sense back in me explained to me that it's gotta be a scam.

At that point I hung up the call. After that Victoria called me again saying because I had disconnected the call previously, my filed had moved on and that now the legal department would call me and I need to conference them into our call with her so that she could explain the situation. I simply hung up again and someone from the number **(302) 504-6257** called and I simply ignored the call. 

I should point out that *Jimmy Arthur* sounded kinda like an eastern European guy. and *Victoria* sounded kinda like she had an Indian accent. 

After coming home I found this notice from the IRS - http://www.irs.gov/uac/Newsroom/IRS-Repeats-Warning-about-Phone-Scams

I also found this new report about the scammer using the exact same name to scam someone else. http://www.channel3000.com/news/man-loses-4320-to-phone-scam/27266178

And this Confirmed that it was a scam. I tried contacting the IRS on the phone numbers provided in the webpage but their mailbox was full on one of the numbers and I left a message on the other. 

I also filed a TIGTA complaint online and reported it to Local Police Department. And I also cancelled all my credit and debit cards and changed my checking account. 

I have tried to give all of you all the information I can from my experience and I would like you to take note of the details here, because if and when they target you next, some of the detail are most likely going to be the same. 

Also I have come to understand that the IRS does not accept payment via cash cards. This should have been a nobrainer really but at that time my brain was fucked. 

I really hope that if and when these scumbags call you tell them to go shove a cactus up their assholes and promptly lodge a complaint to TIGTA here http://www.treasury.gov/tigta/contact_report_scam.shtml (bookmark this link on your browser)

I hope you reverse scam these motherfuckers and trace their number and contact their local police department at Dover and get them arrested. 

And if you happen to read this so scum of the earth scammer, scamming honest, hardworking people... remember that karma is a bitch and you'll get what's coming to you sooner or later you motherfuckers.
