Hello, I have received this text from 2 different phone numbers with a local area code:

Hi there! We'd like your input on the upcoming election. You've been randomly selected for a short survey. Please click here: t.ly/xnAdZ
Thanks!

No I didn't click the link, answer the text or call the numbers. I blocked it because I don't trust it.

First of all, who is "we"? And what business is it of theirs how I feel about "the upcoming election"? Yes I know some people like to answer these questions and talk about their favorite candidates but I have decided that I don't want to be bothered with these polls if that is what this is. I remember my 9th grade civics teacher telling us that the secret ballot is one of the good things about being American, that it is not the case in many other countries and we should be glad we have it and I concur. I did in fact vote this week in a local primary but suspect that is not the "upcoming election" they mean. I have participated in a few live phone polls in the past but I said no to the last one. It's nobody's business who I vote for and in the USA you don't ever have to tell anyone this.

Maybe it is an honest poll but I would be maybe more trusting if the pollsters would identify themselves, none of this "we" shit.

Many elections around the world are scheduled in 2024, not just the USA, there is one in India right now. Plenty of scam opportunities if that is what this is. Has anyone here any theories and/or experience of what would happen if I clicked on the link? Thanks. And this has become one of my favorite subs in which I have been learning a hell of a lot of useful info.
