TITLE: My fellow Randians, Please beware of SBI de-registering your PAN from your account
DATE: 2018-02-28

--- POST TEXT ---

Some context first - my mom has a current account since 2014 and a savings account since 1998, both with SBI. The current account is regularly used for paying online taxes of our clients. Many of them pay in cash, which we deposit in the current account and pay the taxes online. 

Everything was going on fine until today. To avoid waiting at the counter, we normally use a debit card to deposit cash at the CDM operated by the bank. Today, I went to deposit Rs. 95000, but was shocked to see that after counting the notes and selecting "accept" on the CDM, it returned the cash saying "PAN NOT LINKED!!! PLEASE CONTACT BRANCH TO UPDATE YOUR PAN." I went inside the branch immediately and confirmed with the clerk. Both these accounts, infact did not have the PAN linked. I'm 101% sure we have updated the PAN multiple times over the past few years, whenever asked during KYC updation. 

I am unable to understand how SBI has allowed us to run these two accounts without linking the PAN all these years. This might be an employee's honest mistake or a deliberate one, I don't know. With the rate of frauds happening these days, I seriously think there must be something to this. Just think, if someone deletes your PAN, or Aadhaar etc. from your account and routes some transactions through it without your knowledge. These people have forced everyone to open an account, link PAN/Aadhaar etc. but if left unattended, the accounts can be used for fraudulent activity. 

I just wanted to let you know. Please be careful with SBI.
