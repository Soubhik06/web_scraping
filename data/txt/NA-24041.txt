--- POST TEXT ---

So, yesterday I ordered food from Zomato from a famous Bangalore restaurant chain which had terrific reviews on Zomato. Only to find that the food was extremely average and not worth the price at all. I then scrutinized the reviews thoroughly only to find terrible English and the same review content copied and pasted onto other reviews which confirmed that these were fake reviews.

It's just so frustrating that reviews are so easy to fake and give the impression of a good reputation to a bad establishment.

This is not the first time I'm falling prey to the fake reviews scam. 5 years ago, I enlisted the services of a driving school based off of excellent Google reviews only to find them be extremely unprofessional and they had a very lackadaisical attitude towards everything.

For a country that prides itself on rich cultural heritage, spirituality and worships thousands of gods, we Indians sure have abysmally low levels of ethics, morality and integrity.
Heck, I even read an article recently where Indians are leaving multiple bad reviews on American business establishments and deleting them in exchange for Google Play gift cards.

Its reasons like these that give Indians a terrible reputation abroad and I even hesitate to say I'm from India whenever I travel abroad.

Any solutions to the rampant fake reviews scam will be much appreciated.
