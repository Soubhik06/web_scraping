I just got a text from DHL Express (22345) saying a delivery is in transit -- with a legit tracking number, but I never ordered anything.

Supposedly comes from a company in India named ' **SHAILENDRA H PANDEY** ' which I could not find online -- they had my phone number, all the info for the shipment is the right one (address, name, postal code etc), which I find super worrying... I checked my bank account for possible fraudulent activity and everything looks good so far.

I called DHL and they told me it seemed legit, but could not provide any more details as none were available, except that "the company has an instagram account" (I couldn't find it). They offered to cancel the package, but I'm so weirdly curious and puzzled I want to see what's in it; if it actually gets delivered.

Anyone experienced something similar? I saw a couple of posts on here but with usually no follow-up on whether the delivery actually happened or not. Also, what sort of scam is this? What can they get from sending random packages? Is this a strategy to validate someone's infos? 

**UPDATE**: After calling a second time to cancel, the support suggested that I could just select a service point for drop-off as opposed to my home address, which I did. 
What worried me is that, that same person told me "it's definitely someone who knows you given they have all your information" :/
