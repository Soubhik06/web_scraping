Hey everyone,
 
 I wanted to share a recent experience to help others avoid falling into a scam that I unfortunately became a victim of.
 
 I was in a group dedicated to finding deals and where I asked for suggestions for gaming monitors. A user named Aman Jatt reached out to me with an offer after I expressed interest in a deal. After a few days of chatting, Aman suggested we use a payment method outlined by the group: the Admin Method. This method supposedly involved an admin who would hold the payment until the product was received, ensuring a secure transaction.
 
 Aman Jatt created a group with the admin, whom I verified multiple times. This admin, who seemed legitimate and had a profile with 86K members, provided a UPI ID (vivek.admin9956@ybl) for payment. I transferred the amount of ₹27,999 for the monitor plus ₹100 as an admin fee to this UPI ID, after verifying it was correct. I then sent a payment confirmation directly to the admin.
 
 Over the next few days, I tried to follow up on the shipping status with Aman Jatt and the admin, but received no updates. I even reached out to other admins but had no success. Eventually, I contacted Kryptonix Gaming directly and learned that the account used was likely fake.
 
 If anyone encounters a similar situation, be extremely cautious. Always verify the legitimacy of the admin and the payment methods thoroughly before proceeding. Scammers can be very convincing, and it’s crucial to protect yourself by double-checking everything. 
 
 Stay safe out there and watch out for scams! They frequently change their names as seen in the screenshot Beware.
