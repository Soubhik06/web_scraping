(05.07.2023) 1. The issue under consideration arising out of the second appeal dated 18.12.2021
include non-receipt of the following information sought by the appellant through the RTI
application dated 15.07.2021 and first appeal dated 24.09.2021:- 2. Succinctly facts of the case are that the appellant filed an application dated
15.07.2021 under the Right to Information Act, 2005 (RTI Act ) before the Central Public
Information Officer (CPIO), Punjab and Sind Bank, Chandigarh, seeking aforesaid
information. The CPIO did not reply to the appellant. Aggrieved by the same, the appellant
filed first appeal dated 24.09.2021. The First Appellate Authority (FAA) vide order dated
20.10.2021 disposed of the first appeal. Aggrieved by that, the appellant filed second
appeal dated 18.12.2021 before the Commission which is under consideration. 3. The appellant has filed the instant appeal dated 18.12.2021 inter alia on the grounds
that no reply was received from the respondent. The appellant requested the Commission
to direct the CPIO to provide the complete information and take necessary action as per Section 20 (1) of the RTI Act. 4. The CPIO did not reply to the appellant. The FAA vide order dated 20.10.2021
directed the CPIO to reply to the RTI application within 20 working days from the receipt
of FAA's order. 5. The appellant remained absent and on behalf of the respondent Shri Deepak Kumar,
Choudhary, Chief Manager, Punjab & Sind Bank, Chandigarh attended the hearing
through video conference. 5.1. The respondent while defending their case inter alia submitted that they had replied
to the appellant, in compliance of the FAA's order dated 20.10.2021, vide letter dated
05.09.2022 intimating him about the action taken on his grievance. 6. The Commission after adverting to the facts and circumstances of the case, hearing
the respondent and perusal of records, observed that no reply was given by the respondent,
as per records produced before the Commission. During the course of hearing, the
respondent submitted that they had complied with the FAA's order dated 20.10.2021 and
replied vide letter dated 05.09.2022. However, the copy of the same was not uploaded on
the Commission's web portal. In view of the above, the respondent is directed to re-visit
the RTI application and provide comprehensive and para-wise replies, to the appellant
within three weeks from the date of receipt of this order. With these observations and
directions, the appeal is disposed of. Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties. Sd/-
