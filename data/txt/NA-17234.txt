they claim to be calling from icici credit card division and mentioned to me that i have some 7k refund on expired card and they need my new card details for crediting the same. some how they have all my details such as address and DOB. Though I detected it's a fraud call immediately, i played with them by giving random card number, expiry and cvv as my new card. They said for verification they are going to send sms and I should give them OTP. I thought being a random card number that may not work and they will leave it. But to my surprise it went thru and these guys are keep on calling me for OTP. I blocked their number and received about 15missed calls from them :)
