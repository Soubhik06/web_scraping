Shri R.B.Sharma, D.S.P. (S.P.E.), Lokayukt, Jabalpur
 appears in person. None appears for the applicant though represented by
 Advocate - presumably because of the boycott call given
 by the Bar Associations. The Court, however, is obliged to
 proceed with the matter, as observed by the Supreme Court
 in the case of Ex-Capt. Harish Uppal Vs. Union of India
 and others - 2003(2) SCC 45 (Para 35). By way of indulgence and as the litigant should not
 suffer, we direct the Registry to issue SPC to the applicant,
 represented by Advocates to make necessary arrangement
 to espouse his cause and/or to remain personally present in
 Court or through authorized representative, for that
 purpose on the next date of hearing, failing which the
 Court may proceed with the matter appropriately. SPC is made returnable on 23.02.2016. The notice to
 clearly mention that if none appears for the applicant
 on the next date, the Court will have no other option,
 but to proceed with the matter appropriately. No interim relief can be granted as the offence is
 punishable under the provisions of Prevention of
 Corruption Act, 1988 in view of the statutory bar. In any 2 case, interim relief prayer cannot be considered without
 hearing both the sides. Hence, no orders on
 I.A.No.22103/2015 - application for stay. We make it clear that the Trial Court may proceed
 with the trial irrespective of the pendency of this
 proceedings, in accordance with law. Copy of this order be forwarded to the concerned
 Trial Court for information and necessary action.
