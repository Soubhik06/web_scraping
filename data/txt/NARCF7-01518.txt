Hello All- Sharing my experience here to alert unsuspecting victims.

No broker is a semi popular app to find rental properties. Free version has pay walls and a basic plan needs to be purchased to have owners details shared with prospective tenants. 

For a premium of approx 1800 - End users get about 25 owner contacts along with property listing , pics etc.

There are several fraud elements pretending to be home owners in this app. Encountered 2 just today. 

So the modus operandi is some clown from presumably Jamtara or thereabouts sets up a property listing on No broker App pretending to be the Home owner. Rent/deposit amount reasonable luring people in.

Once you contact such folks they either ask for "Visiting Card" to be prepared for visiting property (Apartment complex) Apparently visiting card hlare chargeable lol Also, in some cases they ask for token advance as there are SO many folks contacting them and they need funds to hold the property even before finalizing or visiting the said location 

Just the crass manner in which they talk chewing pan any attentive person will usually identify the Scam. Calling it out specifically through this post here to prevent unsuspecting victims.

Also, No broker App folks have not removed the listing after reporting fraud. Turn around for Fraud cases should be faster than handling regular customer enquiries to prevent additional impact. 

Here is a screen shot of one such scammers. Their DP is of a IAS office from Chennai. (Reverse image search google)

Be aware peeps- If something is too good to be true it probably is. Cheers
