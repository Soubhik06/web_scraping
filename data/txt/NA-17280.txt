--- POST TEXT ---

Received a call from random mobile number (+91 98732 76826) claiming to be from ICICI Prudential Life Insurance (Mumbai office) and stating that my mothers life insurance policy has lapsed so I need to renew immediately. Usually I flag these calls as scams and hang up, but the details he had about us got my attention and I probed further only to find out that “they will send us an OTP to verify”. I immediately called out on his bullshit and asked him not to scam people, what followed was a barrage of abuses from him and his colleagues towards me (which only made my case stronger).

It surprised me how much data they had about us. Please be vigilant to such kind of scams.
