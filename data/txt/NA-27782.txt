Okay, last night, approx 6:50pm, my phone rang and it said Walgreens, but I didn't recognize the number, and thought it might be the number directly into the pharmacy. 

I answer, thinking maybe something is up with a prescription. 

The person on the other end has a foreign accent. They're looking for me. They said something about they had an offer for me, and asked if "she" (me) was available. 

I was quiet for a minute, mainly because I work midnights and they woke me up. I snapped, "no. She's not."

They willingly hung up. 

Here's why I am sketch:

There are no foreigners that work at my towns Walgreens where the call said it originated from. I know all the techs and the pharmacists. The thickest accent you'll find is the lady from up north. 

The phone number was not the Walgreens number I know. It said Walgreens, thus why I answered, but it was an unrecognizable local number.

I'm 99% sure this was not really Walgreens. They've called me before, and it comes in as the number I recognize, and it's always an American at the other end. 

If y'all already know this as a scam, my apologies. It's new to me.

Edit: the accent was an Indian accent trying to sound American. You know, the people who call and say their names are "Harold Douglass MacDougall the third."

EditEdit: I spoke to my pharmacy personally. She said that corporate IS doing something similar to this, to try and set people up on a discount program, BUT that they can do the same thing in store and with all the scam calls out there, it would be best to not trust it, especially if it came from a spoofed number to their store.
