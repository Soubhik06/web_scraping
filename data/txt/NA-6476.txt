Heard. This is first bail application under Section 438 of Cr.P.C. on behalf of
the applicant, who is apprehending his arrest, in connection with Crime

No.81/2021 registered at Police Station P.S. Cyber Crime, Bhopal for the
offence punishable under Sections 420 , 120-B of IPC and Section 66D of
I.T.Act. As per the case of the prosecution that the applicant was involved in
committing fraud alongwith other co-accused persons alleging that Crores of
rupees got deposited in the account of co-accused Dileep Patel. The
complainant made a complaint that through website, the Director of Company
got more than Rs.97 lac deposited in different accounts on the false pretence
of enticing offer to invest amount in the business of spices giving hope of

bumper returns. But, the complainant did not fetch any profit, conversely he
came to know that he has been defrauded and the amount deposited by him
was further transferred in the account of Dileep Patel through one Vijay
Hariyani and Vijay Chhutlani (present applicant) and thereafter Dileep Patel by
his Wazir-X (Crpto-currency exchange) through wallet transferred the same in
the wallet of Vijay Hariyani and Vijay Chhutlani (present applicant). Focusing on the gravity of the offence, learned Panel Lawyer appearing
for the respondent-State informs that it is an international gang operating to
exhort money from the people by cheating and as such Cyber-Cell, Bhopal
investigated the matter and found that fake web-page was hosted by the
accused persons on Alibaba Clouds. The learned counsel for the applicant tries to establish before this
Court that the applicant had already resigned from the Company which was

found involved in alleged fraud and he is no more Director in the said
Company. He also submits that when the applicant has nothing to do with the
said company and if any amount is deposited without his approval in the
account of company, the applicant cannot be held responsible for the same. To combat the said submission made on behalf of the applicant, Shri

Gupta, learned Panel Lawyer submits that when the applicant had resigned
from the company, it was incumbent upon him to accordingly intimate the
respective bank where the company's account exists asking to stop further
transactions in the name of applicant, but that has not been done by the
applicant and during the investigation, which is still going-on, it has emerged
that the applicant is one of the participants in the said crime and has actively
made transactions from his account. He also submits that it is not a sole case,
but other cases have also been registered in which investigation is going-on.
In such circumstances, it would not be proper to grant the benefit of
anticipatory bail at this stage. Counsel for the applicant has placed reliance upon a decision reported
in 2017 (3) MPLJ 356 (Rajpal s/o. Jitram Solanki and others v. State
of M.P.). On going through the said decision, it is gathered that in a petition
under Section 482 of Cr.P.C. the Court has quashed the offence registered
against the petitioner for theft of electricity whereas he was no more Director
of the Company which was unauthorizedly using the electricity and in the said
offence, the company was not party and after perusal of the documents and
record of the Registrar of company, the Court opined that the petitioner had
nothing to do with the said company as he had already left and was no more
Director in the Company, therefore, offence was quashed. However, the facts
of case at hand are altogether different. Merely because the applicant has
resigned from the company yet his bank account is found operative/involved
in the alleged fraud, the benefit of said case is not available to the applicant. On perusal of case-diary, but without expressing any view on merit, I
