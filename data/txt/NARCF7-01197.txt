30th March i was at majestic metro station dropping my family members to bus stand.
And suddenly a guy in mid 30's came to me asking for 200rs as he lost his wallet, and wanted to go to Tumkur.
Firstly cash and when denied he asked for online transaction.

I think its scam based on my observation..you can share your thoughts and be careful if encounter something similar.

1. He was inside metro so must have bought end to end ticket.
2. Why he is not going to metro security if he lost something inside metro.
3. Their is no metro till tumkur.
4. After I denied he immediately went to other person.
5. No luggage, just casual guy in slippers, shorts and shirt.
