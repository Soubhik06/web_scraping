Heard learned counsel for the applicant and learned A.G.A. for the respondent State. Notices to respondent no.2 are dispensed with. Learned counsel for the applicant submits that he has been falsely implicated. He is not named in the First Information Report. Learned A.G.A. has opposed the bail application and submitted that during investigation the complicity of the applicant has been found by the Investigating Officer. There is ample material against him. After due consideration of the charge-sheet the cognizance as well as summoning order has been issued. There is no merit in this application. Perused the record. Prima facie from perusal of the charge-sheet it appears that the Investigating Officer has filed the charge-sheet after considering the statement of the prosecution witnesses, evidence received from the cyber cell, the evidence given by the paytm company and on that basis the account holders including the present applicant have been found to be involved in commission of crime. Considering that learned trial court appears to have taken cognizance and issued summoning order. It is online fraud and amount has been transferred from the account of the complainant to the accused persons and on the basis of the investigation the complicity of the present applicant has been found in commission of the offence. I do not find any illegality in the impugned order. Accordingly, the application being devoid of merit is dismissed. Order Date :- 22.4.2024
