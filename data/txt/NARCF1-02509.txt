My partner and I were in Jaipur for 4 nights and had a very mixed experience. The sites and shopping were great but the constant feeling of being scammed was not. We both understand Hindi and I can speak it a bit and we still faced problems of people trying to take advantage of us. We booked travel through our hotel and even they were trying to scam us. I felt like everyone was so money hungry and you couldn't trust anyone. This made for a horrible experience and it's somewhere I won't be going again. I really wonder how it would be for non-Indian travellers. Again it's a beautiful place but the people were largely not great.
