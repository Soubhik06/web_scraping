--- POST TEXT ---

In hindsight I shouldn’t have been so dumb about it. I was just naive at the time and didn’t know better but it sucks that such a big company chooses to associate with a scam. 

But around the beginning of this year I had gone to BAM! to buy some books for myself and as I was checking out the cashier that was ringing me up told me that I can get 3 free magazine subscriptions for 3 months. My dumbass took it at the time and I regretted it. The first warning sign was when I found a charge for 60 dollars from one of the magazines. Luckily I was able to cancel and get a full refund, however there were still the other magazines. I failed to notice that I was getting charged for the other ones until I recently decided to cancel. I tried to cancel through the magazines directly but I couldn’t because it was going through a third party company. I was given the number to this third party company and this man answers with a heavy Indian accent calling himself something along the lines of John. I immediately knew this was a scam and demanded he cancel it. He asked for my address but nothing else. He said he cancelled them all so I hung up. However I didn’t trust this and immediately canceled my card. I’m just upset that BAM would give my card information to a third party company without my consent. It’s screams shady practices to me. Unfortunately I suffer from anxiety so this made me extremely nervous about hacking and people having my credit card information. Since then I’ve been very careful with my security but I am still panicking. I know to be more secure with my information now but I can’t help but let the panic consume me.
