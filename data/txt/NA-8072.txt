1. Heard learned Counsel for the petitioners and learned

 Additional Public Prosecutor for the State. 2. This application, for grant of anticipatory bail, arises out

 of Roh Police Station Case No. 177 of 2024, dated

 18.06.2024, disclosing offences under Sections

 419 / 420 / 467 / 468 / 471 / 120B / 34 of the Indian Penal Code

 and Sections 66(C) / 66(D) of the Information Technology

 Act. 3. The prosecution case, as per the First Information Report,

 is that on 17.06.2024, in the evening, the police, on secret

 information that the petitioners are scamming/decoying
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.58980 of 2024(2) dt.11-09-2024 2/3 people on phone in order to make money by false

 assurance of jobs, reached the place of occurrence. On

 seeing police party, they started fleeing away, however,

 one person, namely, Pawan Kumar, was apprehended and

 others succeeded in fleeing away. Upon enquiry the

 arrested co-accused disclosed the name of the petitioners 4. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the

 petitioners have not committed any offence in the manner

 alleged and they have falsely been implicated in the

 present case on the basis of confessional statement of

 arrested co-accused. He next submits that the petitioners

 were not present at the place of occurrence. The

 petitioners have got no criminal antecedent. 5. Regards being had to the submissions made on behalf of the

 parties and taking into consideration the fact that police

 recovered a large number of mobile phones and debit cards

 from the accused person having SIM numbers issued from

 different places at Kolkata, and the fact that all the accused

 persons appears to have formed a gang defraud the innocent

 public in the name of providing job and benefits of various

 schemes and the phishing/cyber fraud is rampant in the area

 where the attackers try to trick individuals into providing

 user names, password and other details and are indulged

 in e-mail phishing, voice phishing, SMS phishing,
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.58980 of 2024(2) dt.11-09-2024 3/3 etc., and the case is still under investigation, for which,

 custodial interrogation of the petitioners may be required,

 I not am inclined to grant the petitioners privilege of

 anticipatory bail. 6. This application is, accordingly, dismissed. (Anil Kumar Sinha, J)
