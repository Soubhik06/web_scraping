I haven't looked to see if you guys have discussed this, but I just got a call from what appears to be a widely known IRS scam. The callers seem to be from India and claim that they are investigators for the IRS and tell you that you need to pay up or go to jail. They will tell you that they are sending the police to your house to arrest you if you do not pay. I have found a list of phone numbers that the FTC page (http://www.consumer.ftc.gov/blog/fake-irs-collectors-are-calling) has in it's comment section. These phone numbers are the numbers that show up on caller ID. I won't post them here due to rules, but you can find them in the comment section at http://www.consumer.ftc.gov/blog/fake-irs-collectors-are-calling

What I have found is that many of these numbers are MagicJack phone numbers. While that may be irrelevant, it gives some insight into how they are going about it. Some numbers have a voice mail message and allow you to leave a message. Some numbers are 'not accepting calls'. Some numbers do not connect. I am using my magicjack account to call them to avoid exposing my information. One person returned my call and I pretended to be someone investigating him. He spouted off a bunch of pseudo babble and numbers to make him seem legit. 

I'd like to see if we can give a full force reddit hug of death to these phone numbers. I want them to experience the same harassment that they impose on others. I have no other motivation except that scammers call my house all the time and I want to cause trouble for them. I know that the best action is to hang up and forget about it, but that does not excite me. 

If anyone has information on this scam or some suggestions on how to cause trouble for them, let's hear it!
