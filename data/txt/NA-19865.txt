This is a post to gather any news/info you all may have read on the Indian submarine project.

The point of a submarine is stealth. With modern technology submarines can be tracked based on the amount of sound that they produce due to their internal workings like pumps used to cool the nuclear reactors, bubbles produce by the propeller's and so on.

From what I have read online Indian submarines are noisy which means that they can be tracked. This beats the whole purpose of having a nuclear submarine capable of launching ballistic missiles. The whole point is for it to be the third and more capable arm of our nuclear strike capabilities as they act as the strongest nuclear deterrent. Even if the mainland is attacked, you can retaliate using your subs and launch missiles.

Source that say Indian subs are noisy:

https://thediplomat.com/2015/09/how-china-and-indias-noisy-nuclear-subs-contribute-to-instability-in-asia/

&gt; The Chinese Type 094 Jin-class SSBN–“China’s first credible sea-based nuclear deterrent,” according to the Pentagon—is allegedly easier to detect than Soviet SSBNs from the late 1970s. Conversely, the acoustic signature of India’s Arihant-class SSBN “is not likely to be quieter than China’s Jin-class boats” the study notes.

Have you read anything that contradicts this?
