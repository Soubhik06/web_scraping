 O.A. 128 OF 2018 9 Rout and he had not intimated to his department and thereby

since the applicant had not intimated the department about

since the transaction money exceeds two months basic pay he

should have informed of such transaction. Rule 18 of CCS

inquiry report in the disciplinary proceedings or in CBI charge

sheet it has been stated that the said money was paid to the

applicant by Sri Rout for his alleged involvement in the fraud. 9. This Tribunal is well aware of its limited scope in interfering

 ".........In disciplinary proceedings, the High Court is not and

 High Court can only see whether: 10. Any allegations of misconduct should be substantiated with

documentary evidence. There is absolutely no murmur in the

inquiry report or in the punishment order about omission or

commission of the applicant in the fraud. There is nothing to

show that his act led to the fraud or he had directly or indirectly

involved in anything to help commit the fraud. The whole

conclusion arrived by the inquiry officer, disciplinary authority

and appellate authority is based on conjectures and surmises

and on the assumption that since Sri Ranjan Rout had paid

money to the applicant it is case of misconduct. The conclusion
 O.A. 128 OF 2018 12 is based on no evidence and therefore this Tribunal finds the

action of the respondents illegal and arbitrary. Accordingly the

impugned order under Annexure A/6 and A/8 are quashed and

set aside. 11. The OA stand allowed but with no order to costs.
