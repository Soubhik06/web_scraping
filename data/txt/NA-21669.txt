--- POST TEXT ---

Around 3.5 months back, I received a text message on Telegram regarding an online part-time work opportunity. The initial tasks involved liking YouTube videos, and I was even paid for these small tasks. However, the situation took a dark turn when I was asked to invest a substantial amount of money in prepaid tasks, which were supposedly for crypto trading. 
 
Under the belief that this was a legitimate opportunity, I invested nearly **Rs. 2.5 lakhs** for the prepaid tasks. At first, I was paid back for two tasks, which led me to trust the process. However, when I requested a refund for the profits along with my initial capital, they denied it, citing baseless reasons. To further complicate matters, they attempted to lure me into investing even more money, but I refrained from doing so and later they stopped responding to my messages. 
 
I immediately filed a case with the **cybercrime police station in Whitefield, Bangalore**, providing all the necessary proofs like chat screenshots and bank statements. However, it has been more than three months, and I have yet to receive a satisfactory response from the police. The only updates or actions seem to be initiated when I physically visit the police station, which has become increasingly difficult due to my job location change that led me to leave Bangalore. 
 
I am deeply concerned that my case is not being taken seriously, and I fear that my evidence might be lost or overlooked as I cannot be present physically. I have reached out to the police over the phone and mail and explained my situation, but I have not received any response. 
 
I seek your guidance and advice on how I can escalate this matter and ensure that my case receives the attention it deserves. What further steps can I take to get a resolution at the earliest? Are there any higher authorities I can contact or consumer protection agencies I should approach? 
 
Additionally, I would be grateful for any advice on legal recourse against scammers and steps to prevent others from falling victim to similar scams.
