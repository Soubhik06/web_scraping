During the weekend, I decided to visit my colleague who lives in Thane. Since her apartment was on the 3rd floor, I opted to take the stairs instead of waiting for the lift to come down.

As I made my way down, I had my earphones on and was in a cheerful mood. However, something inside me suddenly kicked in when I reached the first floor. It was like my instincts took over, and I couldn't ignore the feeling. I want to emphasize that I wasn't under the influence of any substances.

What caught my attention was a house on that floor. The door and the surrounding wall were adorned with an abundance of Hindu talismans, unlike the other houses that usually had nimbu mirchi or similar items typically seen in front of homes for warding off evil.

My senses went on high alert, and I couldn't shake off the overwhelming feeling of unease. That house gave me the creeps, and I sensed that something was off about it.

Now, here's the shocking part. Just four days later,i.e today : the police showed up at that very house. It turned out there was suspicious activity taking place inside, and they were conducting an investigation related to a case of fraud. The residents of the house were eventually taken away by the authorities.
