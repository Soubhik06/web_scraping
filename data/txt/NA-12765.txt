This Criminal Petition, under Section 438 of the Code of

Criminal Procedure, 1973, is filed by the petitioners/A.2 and A.3,

for grant of bail in Crime No.37 of 2018 of Tirumala I Town Police

Station, Chittoor District, registered for the offences punishable

under Sections 419, 420, 468, 471 read with 34 of I.P.C. 2. Heard the learned counsel for the petitioners/A.2 and

A.3 and the learned Additional Public Prosecutor representing the

respondent-State and perused the record. 3. The learned counsel for the petitioners would submit

that the petitioners belong to Bangalore, Karnataka State. They

have nothing to do with the alleged seva ticket of A1. They are

software engineers and ultimately, prayed to allow the petition. 4. The learned Additional Public Prosecutor opposed the

grant of bail to the petitioners. 5. As per the material available on record, the

petitioners/A.2 and A.3 are in the habit of fabricating Suprabatham

tickets, etc. When verification is made, 200 tickets were booked

by taking different ID numbers and Aadhaar cards. It goes to

show that fake tickets are being made to have Suprabatham and

Arjitha sevas. It is also alleged that the petitioners are doing the

said business from 2009 onwards. It appears that there is a big

scam of fabricating tickets and having dharshan basing on

fabricated tickets. There are specific and grave allegations against

the petitioners. It requires thorough investigation. Custodial 2 interrogation is also necessary. It is not a fit case to allow the

petition. 6. In the result, the Criminal Petition is dismissed.

 Miscellaneous petitions, if any, pending in this Criminal

Petition, shall stand closed. ____________________
 DR. SHAMEEM AKTHER, J
