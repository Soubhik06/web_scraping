TITLE: Beware of people scamming using fake Apartment listing
DATE: 2023-01-30
SCORE: 21

--- POST TEXT ---

Recently I was searching for 1 Bhk apartment online. I contacted the owner on whatsapp and asked about the details of the flat and other things. He then told me that I need to make a visiting card worth 2000rs to enter the society and see the apartment. If I didn't like the flat then I will get a full refund. I agreed and sent my ID photo and 2000rs on UPI. Then I tried contacting him on the day before the visit and they asked for even more money for some authentication. Then I realized it was a scam. I blocked them and also complained in cybercrime portal. Since they took my ID it can be misused. So please don't share your ID with anyone. And please share this information to as many people as you can so they don't fall in their trap. Stay safe!
