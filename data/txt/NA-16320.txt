--- POST TEXT ---

I have received more than three calls that were attempting to defraud me. First two were of the type where they say you're going to get money and guide you into paying them via UPI. 

Recent one was about winning 14 lac worth car or cash. And giving them 999 for some kind of registration before they send the cash. Would ask for aadhar and PAN details for car. 
I managed to get their account number and two phone numbers. I also have the call recording. 
They were claiming to be from Amazon so complained to them but even they didn't follow up for bank details. 

These scams are simple. Most people won't fall for it but enough people do. I wonder why there isn't a swift way of eliminating such practices when they attempt to do it. Waiting for people to fall for this and then chasing those people seems counterintuitive. 

I personally know 3-4 people who have fallen for such things in different amounts. From 6k- 40k. 

These people should be afraid to call people. They know 90% won't fall for it but don't care because there's no consequences for failure. 

Which is astonishing. 

I had contacted cyber cell the first time, but nothing was done.
