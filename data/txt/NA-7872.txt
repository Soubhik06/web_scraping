CORAM : HON'BLE MR. JUSTICE AMBUJ NATH ------ For the Petitioner : Mr. Jitesh Kumar, Advocate
 For the Opp. Party : Mr. Naveen Kumar Gaunjhu, A.P.P. ----- 3/ 19.12.2025 Heard the parties. 2. Petitioner has been made accused in connection with Cyber Crime
 Case No. 29 of 2024 arising out of M.G.M. P.S. (East Singhbhum) Case No. 80 of
 2024, for the offences registered under Sections 354(C) , 354(D) & 509 of the I.P.C.
 subsequently, Sections 384 & 386 of the I.P.C. and Sections 66(E) , 67 & 67(A) of
 the I.T. Act was added, pending in the Court of learned Special Judge (Cyber
 Crime), East Singhbhum, Jamshedpur. 3. Bail application of this petitioner was earlier rejected by this Court
 vide B.A. No. 10949 of 2024 by order date 05.03.2025. 4. The petitioner was acquainted with the victim. He blackmailed her to
 show her body part on video call and recorded it. Thereafter, he made her obscene
 photograph viral. 5. A report regarding the stage of trial, earlier called for, has been
 received. It appears that charge has been framed on 03.01.2025, but the prosecution
 has not examined any witness. 6. It was submitted that the petitioner has remained in custody since
 06.09.2024 and the prosecution has not examined any witness till date. 7. Considering the period of custody and the fact that the trial is not in
 progress satisfactorily, I am inclined to enlarge the petitioner on bail. Accordingly,
 the petitioner, above named, is directed to be released on bail on furnishing bail
 bonds of Rs. 20,000/- (Rupees twenty thousand) with two sureties of the like
 amount each, to the satisfaction of learned Special Judge (Cyber Crime), East
 Singhbhum, Jamshedpur, in connection with Cyber Crime Case No. 29 of 2024
 arising out of M.G.M. P.S. (East Singhbhum) Case No. 80 of 2024. (Ambuj Nath, J.)
