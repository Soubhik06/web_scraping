“On 17 June 2023, I filed an RTI under RTI Act 2005 to sought information to the Government of India for the following questions to clear doubts on the fake Anglo - Kuki War. There is no such incident happened in the history of India. Yesterday, I got the response from Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India:

1) Please state the list of the names of all Kukis Freedom Fighters from Manipur who fought against British as per records of the Government of India.
Ans: [Refer to MHA Website] There are 62 freedom fighters from Manipur as per Government of India records who received monthly pension from the Ministry of Home Affairs. Out of 62, there are only 4 freedom fighters from Kukis community who received pension scheme of MHA called Samman Pension under SSSY. Their names are 
Haozathang, Letsei Kuki, Letkhothang Lhangum and Lunkhoson Kuki.

2) Please furnish me all the details of the Anglo - Kukis War fought between Kukis and the British in Manipur.
Ans: NO INFORMATION AVAILABLE.

3) Please State when did Anglo - Kuki War happened and exactly where? Please state the name of the exact area or village in India. 
Ans: NO INFORMATION AVAILABLE”
