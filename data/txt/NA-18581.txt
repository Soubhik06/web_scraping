Just a cautionary post - I know you guys are cautious enough but as the title says, please call your families and extended family who are unaware of phone fraud. Usually they call posing as a customer representative and ask for ATM number and pin. If a person is naive enough they get to score the money in the account. I saw it first hand happening and want to share with you guys.
