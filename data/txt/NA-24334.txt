I purchased a refurbished Apple MacBook Pro 2019 (Superb Condition) from Cashify. Unfortunately, the product arrived defective, which prompted me to notify their team for repair services within the 15-day full refund window period offered by Cashify.

However, despite this notice and multiple follow-up attempts on my part, I have received no substantial updates or proper communication regarding the repair or replacement status. They tell me to wait for 24-48 hours when it has been over 2 weeks. It is now over 2 months and there is still an uncertain resolution from Cashify despite regular follow-ups, multiple times throughout the day.

There is no clear timeline for the refund transaction and their employees seem to be unaware of the case since they ask me to explain the scenario every time I follow up. They claim that they have "expedited, escalated and prioritised" this issue but clearly that is not the case here. They have also treated other customers this way - there are numerous reviews on app store against them. It is quite obvious that they are running a scam here which we sadly missed. But hopefully, others can save themselves from falling into this trap by reading my review.

They have mentally harassed me to a point that I was forced to take a legal action against them.
