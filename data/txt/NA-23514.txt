Hello, I'm a new joinee in an IT company. Today morning I received a call saying that my fedex parcel was not delivered and press 1 to know more [ my biggest mistake was to press 1].
I was connected to an "agent" and she told me that my parcel which was supposed to go from mumbai to iran was stuck in customs and it maybe due to illegal items present in it [credit cards, drugs or clothes]. 

as normal I told them it wasn't me and I live in Bangalore. She told me, if it wasn't me then there is high chances my aadhar details have been leaked [told me if I ever lost my aadhar card or if my company has leaked this information] and that she can help through fedex for raising cybercrime complaint or I have to go to Mumbai cyber crime branch to report it.
I told her I can't go to Mumbai and asked her to give me the cybercrime number of Mumbai and I will speak to them.
She said she will help me through fedex and asked to connect with me later.
I was thinking about this call and I knew that there was no way for me to check if there was a parcel in fedex and I was considering calling the bangalore cyber crime and inform them about this.

I did a reddit check and found so many similar frauds like this 

I wanted to know if there was any way I can report this or have any sort of precautionary measures I can take to avoid such calls in the future.
