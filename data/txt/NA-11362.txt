CORAM: HON'BLE MR. JUSTICE RAJESH KUMAR --------- For the Petitioner : Mr. H. K. Shikarwar, Advocate
 For the State : Mr. Sunil Kr. Dubey, A.P.P --------- 1. Heard learned counsel for the applicant and learned
 counsel for the State. 2. The applicant, who is aged about 18 years, is in custody
 since 24.08.2022, has approached this Court for grant of regular
 bail in connection with Barkatha P.S. Case No.147 of 2022,
 registered for the offence under Sections 420 / 385 / 34 of the
 Indian Penal Code and Sections 66(C) / 67 of the I.T. Act. 3. It appears that the applicant has been apprehended along
 with other persons and they have been made accused for the
 offence of sextortion. One of the co-accused namely, Sandip
 Kumar has been granted bail by the Co-ordinate Bench of this
 Court vide order dated 07.12.2022, passed in B.A. No.11231 of
 2022. 4. Innocence of the applicant has been claimed and
 undertaking has been given for participation in the trial. 5. Learned counsel for the State has opposed the prayer for
 bail. 6. Considering the period of custody and the age of the
 applicant, I am inclined to enlarge the applicant on bail.
 Accordingly, the applicant is directed to be released on bail, on
 his furnishing bail bond of Rs.10,000/- (Ten thousand) with two
 sureties of the like amount each to the satisfaction of learned J.M.
 1st Class, Hazaribag in connection with Barkatha P.S. Case No.147
 of 2022, on the condition that one of the bailors will be the father
 of the applicant and the applicant will submit self-attested
 photocopy of his Aadhaar Card and also submit his mobile
 number before the learned court below which he will always keep
 active and will not change it during pendency of this case without
 prior permission of the court. (Rajesh Kumar, J.)
