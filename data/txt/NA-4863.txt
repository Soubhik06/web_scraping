 This application under Section 439 of the Code of Criminal Procedure,
1973 has been filed by the petitioner, namely, Rajen Sidana @ Rajan Sidhana @
Ranjan Sidhana @ Ranjan, who has been detained behind the bar since
31.10.2023 in connection with Chandmari P. S. Case No. 353/2023 under section
306 of the IPC. The gist of the accusation in this case is that on 24.9.2023, one Shipra
Sarkar, SI of Police lodged an FIR before the Officer-in-charge of Chandmari
Police Station, inter-alia, alleging that one committed Suicide on 16.09.2023 at

about 5.10 PM by jumping from the flat which was situated at the 6 thfloor of an
apartment known as Subansiri Apartment which belongs to one Mahendra
Chand. It is also stated that in the FIR that the said Tangchi K. Sangma was
employed in Medicare Call Centre as a service representative and said call
center was involved in a cybercrime in Cyber P. S. Case No. 08/2023 under Section 120B / 419 / 420 / 385 / 467 / 471 / 468 / 507 , of the IPC read with Section
65 / 66 / 66B / 66C and 66D of the Information Technology Act, 2000. It is also alleged in the FIR that the deceased was working in the said
Medicare Call Centre at the instigation of the present petitioner and another co-
accused named in the FIR in order to make more profit and thinking that the
work she has been engaged in is legal. However, when the deceased came to
know about the arrest of the present petitioner in a cyber-fraud case, she
apprehended that she may also be arrested in the said case and also
apprehended that her reputation would be lost and hence, she committed
suicide. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that there is no allegation of

aid or instigation on the part of the present petitioner in the FIR. It is further
submitted by the learned counsel for the petitioner that the deceased Tangchi K.
