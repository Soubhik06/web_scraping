China is using targeted [attacks](https://thehackernews.com/2020/07/chinese-hackers-hong-kong-india.html?m=1) on india and hong-kong. It introduces itself using spear phishing attacks through email and such. Arogya setu app already has major [vulnerabilities](https://youtu.be/Uee8XB2vsoo) and the same with others like chingari or mitron. We can't even trust the NIC. If only govt can access the data, then it's a little understandable but if anyone can access it then it's bullshit. I repeat, there's a difference between govt can access it and anyone can access it. All these Indian app gets breached using simple attacks. It clearly shows the rush in development and no testing phase. If you ever have doubt about a app being ready too early then don't download it. I was already suspicious about arogya setu but I trusted the govt and installed it on my papa's phone. I should have trusted my gut feeling. And you already know about the slow loadings of govt websites. That's cuz devs are copypasting from stackoverflow and using some bullshit server nobody ever heard of. Companies just want the final product, not a good one. India should introduce cybersecurity to the education. But do you know why it doesn't? Cuz if everyone became critical thinkers then the first thing that's gonna die is their bullshit propaganda. If govt give everyone 2 brain cells then everyone will know that whatsapp university and facebook institute is bullshit. But if there will be no country then what will the propaganda be used for? So I want y'all to share the hacker news as much as possible. Maybe make petitions on to focus on cybersecurity in india. Send emails to the ministry of electronics and information technology, NSC or NTRO. But first be aware of these things and learn about it more as much as possible
