--- POST TEXT ---

I was downloading a Minecraft APK when I accidentally clicked on an 18+ site link. Suddenly, my browser was locked, and a government bureau-looking page called "National Records Control Bureau" was opened. The page started playing an alarm, saying, "Your browser has been blocked due to excessive attempts to access restricted pornographic content." There was a lot of made-up technical and legal jargon, and the right side of the page asked me to pay a fine of 29,500, providing a slot to enter my card details, and said my browser could not be accessed if the fine was not paid.

Please do not fall for this scam. Accessing 18+ sites is not a fineable offense in India, and is not illegal if done privately. Even if you access a blocked site, you will never be fined by the government. If this happens to you, just restart the computer and delete the browser, then reinstall it.

Cybercrime and fraud are at an all-time high in our country. Stay alert.
