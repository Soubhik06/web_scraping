**Scam 1** 
 
I arrived in Delhi from Bangalore on August 13 and went to the metro at the airport. I had my metro card from when I lived in Noida/Gurgaon two years ago, but it was almost empty. That is the old blue colour card, and earlier it was not possible to recharge this card online. I checked its balance, and it was 34 rupees only. I asked the metro token guy to recharge my card. I requested him to add a 200 rupee recharge and gave him a 500 rupee note. He stated that only recharge of at least 300 rupees is possible (I suspected scam, but I thought maybe the rule had changed). Alright, I replied. Later, I looked up my balance in that small machine and discovered that he only recharged for 200 rupees and kept 100 in his pocket. I knew I got scammed, but because I was tired and high rush was there, I let it go.

 
**Scam 2:**

As soon as I was returning from the recharge window. A random guy says “hi” to me. He asks for 100 rupees from me, says he has no cash, and he has to recharge his metro card. I asked what about UPI, and he said the Internet is not working. He called my number and said I would return your money through UPI. I was again aware of such scams, but the guy looked genuine. So I gave him 100 rupees, thinking it’s just 100 rupees, and if he’s genuinely in need, it would help him and make me feel better about me. That guy never sent me any money. 

These two motherfuckers didn't take anything significant from me. They just brought down the name of their city and its people. Also, they took away any chances of me helping any other person who may be genuinely in need in a similar situation in the future.
