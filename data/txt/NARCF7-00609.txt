--- POST TEXT ---

I had to reach a hotel in aero city only about 4km and ten minutes away. But since my flight was delayed and it was 3 AM, Uber was showing a high price of ₹349 but I understood the surge, tolls etc and booked the cab. 

Upon arriving at the location, the driver shows a payment page with my Uber username that's shows that I owe him ₹599. 

I was thoroughly confused and argued that for me on my app it still shows ₹349. He very convincingly stated that the initial amount shown to the customer doesn't account for tolls and parking fees and that it will update for me in some time and I could even write an email about it if it did not update. 

I paid by cash and gave in to this coercive driver. After all, the payment page for him was showing ₹599.

After exiting the cab, I looked this up online and turns out a few people have posted about this exact scam on this page. Wish I had come across it soon.

Uber thankfully swiftly put the surplus amount into my Uber wallet after raising a complaint. But the damage has been done and I shan't be trusting Uber for trips to/fro the airport although I've never had an issue with the in city drivers.

Please beware guys!!! Try to pay through UPI so that the correct amount will pop up for you and can't be coerced into paying extra cash through fake screenshots/duplicate apps shown by the driver.
