I am a practising Advocate before Supreme Court and MP High Court.

Recently there have been instances of increasing cyber fraud herein people will hack your WhatsApp and use your WhatsApp to ask your contacts send them money.

So please ensure that your web WhatsApp is not logged in while you are away from your computer/laptop.
