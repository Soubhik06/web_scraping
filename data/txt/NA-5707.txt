 Be that as it may, having given a considerable thought to the

submissions made hereinabove especially to the fact that co-accused of the

antecedents of the petitioner are clean, as he is not involved in any other case,

this Court is of the considered view that there is no valid or cogent reason to

deny the bail to the present petitioner, wherein he has bona fide intentions and

is ready and willing to join the investigation and cooperate for furtherance of

the same so that the final report can be submitted by the Investigating Agency

in time. 5. Decision
