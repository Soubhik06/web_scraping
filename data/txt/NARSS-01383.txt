So we all get that pre call tune thing which says be alert of scams right? I kind of did a mini experiment since theres a debate going whether phone rings during that announcement or not and here's the quick summary:-

1) If the announcement stops when you press any key on your keypad, the phone was NOT ringing.

2) If it doesnt stop then the call is working

P.S If theres any emergency please spam random numbers on your keypad as that 30 seconds is just wasted.
