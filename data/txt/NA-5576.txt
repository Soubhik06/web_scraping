Applicant Smt. Rubi Kumari, is in judicial

custody in FIR No. 21 of 2021, under Section 120B , 420 , 467 and 468 IPC, and Section 66D of the Informant

Technology Act, 2000, Police Station Bhatrojkhan, District

Almora. She has sought her release on bail. 2. Heard learned counsel for the parties and

perused the record. 3. It is a case of online fraud. The informant was

telephonically told that under Pradhan Mantari Mudra

Yojna, he would be given 2% and 40% subsidy.

Telephonically the money was demanded, which the

informant paid through Google Pay. Total Rs.10,7,800/-

were sent by the informant. But, subsequently, the

informant came to know that he has been cheated. 2 4. Learned counsel for the applicant would submit

that the applicant is a woman belonging to the State of

Bihar. Her ATM and Sim was given by her to a Rajni. She

does not know as to what had happened. 5. The Court wanted to know from the learned

counsel for the applicant as to why did the applicant give

her sim and ATM to some other person? Was it under

conspiracy? 6. On the other hand, learned State counsel would

submit that the amount which was forwarded by the

informant was deposited in the account maintained by the

applicant. 7. The record has been filed alongwith the counter

affidavit alongwith the statement of the witness. 8. Online fraud is such a menace which cheats

innocent people. This is one of such case. The informant

was cheated and the amount was deposited in the account

of the applicant. If the applicant is released, the chances of

repeat offences may not be ruled out. 9. Having considered the entirety of facts, this Court

is of the view that it is not a case fit for bail. Accordingly, the

bail application deserves to be rejected. 3 10. The bail application is rejected. (Ravindra Maithani, J.)
