Lately we've been hearing cases of mobile phones put in dustbins (third wave coffee house, bengaluru) and fake ceilings of public places (Anandam restaurant, uttarakhand), coffee shops and some guys even going as far as trying to record women using the washrooms at the theatres too. It's honestly scary and depressing to know that we gotta keep looking out every damn place we go. 

Please be cautious though, when you're going to public places and using their washrooms or restrooms. 
 
&gt; Try **double checking your surroundings**, dustbins, ceilings etc. for suspicious electronic devices. Be it public toilets or the ones in cafes, hotels, restrooms, or malls, clubs. 
 
&gt; Also, when you're using trial rooms at clothing stores, **do check the mirrors to see if they're normal ones (safe) or two way mirrors (unsafe)**. You can do the **fingertip test** (put your fingertip against the mirror and if there's a gap between your tip and the reflections, it's safe and if there's no gap, that means it's a two way mirror and it's not safe.) or the **knocking test.** (A two-way mirror might produce a less solid sound compared to a traditional mirror, due to its different construction and the space typically behind it.)

 
&gt; Keep an eye on your drinks. carry your drinks, prefer bottled stuff if you're outside, buy your own stuff. Get a power bank if you're going out, normally for a couple of hours or on trips and stuff, just in case your phone runs out of juice. share your location/trip/outing details with your parents or atleast two or three friends. Keep updating them about your whereabouts. Share your ride details - cab or bike. carry a pepper spray with you. you can get one off amazon. 

(in case you don't have a power bank and your phone runs out of juice, make sure you try to remember or note down the numbers of your parents/friends in a handy notebook and carry it on you and inform them beforehand through a call - from someone's cellphone at a public place like a cafe or store. if you're outside, pretend like you're on a call with someone. there are apps/audios online that you can download, which have audios that you can use to pretend like you're on an actual call. try googling about it. **do it even if your phone is switched off)**

Most importantly, **trust your gut**. Trust nobody except yourself. If you have wonderful and trustworthy people with you, it's well and good. But it's always better to trust your instincts and keep an eye out.

**If you're a girl reading this, please remember and please tell your friends too. If you're a guy, please tell your mother/sister/cousins/any other female friends that you have.**
