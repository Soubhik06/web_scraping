So I (26F) had my bus from isbt 43 in the morning at 7:30 and I booked an Uber from Mohali. And when the cab arrived, i saw the driver was crying. 
As I sat in the cab, I could see he was talking on the phone via earphones and talking about how his father passed away yesterday and now he’s the sole earner. Tho he was speaking Punjabi during the other conversation but I guess I was able to hear the phrase “my father passed away in hospital yesterday and his dead body is still there” very clearly as he was mentioning it again and again. That’s when my suspicion started to grow and I recalled how someone mentioned in this sub the potential scams of the cab drivers using such stories to lure passengers into giving them extra money and targeting female passengers more.
Maybe idk it was my paranoia or trust issues raised by the society now, I had to keep myself safe as I’ve already been scammed before. So I kept my mom on call till the time I reached my destination. 
As I was about to reach, he suddenly stopped talking on the phone and at drop off, he started crying again while telling me the amount I had to pay after the ride, which I obviously did. 

Idk if the whole situation was serious and true or it was a suspected fraud. I guess you can trust no one that easily nowadays and that post on this sub kind of helped me a lot. 

If it was serious, I feel really sorry for the guy and kudos to him that’s he’s still working hard given the situation he’s facing now. 🙏🏻

But if it wasn’t, I would like to thank that person who mentioned about that scam thing which helped to keep myself safe. ✨
