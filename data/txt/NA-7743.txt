 Code and R.I for three years with a fine of Rs. 25,000/- and default
 sentence under sections 420 , 409 , 467 , 468 , 471 and 477A of the Indian
 Penal Code, vide impugned order of sentence dated 15.02.2022. All the
 sentences have been ordered to run concurrently. 5. Learned counsel for the appellant submits that the role of the
 appellant (A-49), a proprietor of M/s Trishul Enterprises, Ranchi has
 been discussed by the learned CBI Court at para-209 and 210 of the
 impugned judgment. Learned trial court has erroneously convicted the
 appellant for having made fraudulent supplies during the financial year
 1990-91 to 1993-94 through his firm of GNC and fish meal worth
 Rs.42,05,942/- against which an amount of Rs.7,69,760/- was only
 found as fraudulent supplies during investigation. Learned Trial Court, -2- however, considering the lesser gravity of the offence has been pleased
 to award the maximum sentence of three years only under the relevant
 penal provisions and granted provisional bail to the appellant vide order
 dated 15.02.2022, which may be confirmed. 6. Learned counsel for the CBI has opposed the prayer. He submits
 that the case of the prosecution has been successfully established
 through oral and documentary evidence against the appellant. 7. Having considered the submissions of learned counsel for the
 appellant and C.B.I and the facts and circumstances noted above,
 provisional bail granted to the appellant vide order dated 15.02.2022 by
 the Court of learned Special Judge-V, C.B.I. (AHD Scam Cases),
 Ranchi is confirmed, subject to deposit of 50% of fine amount imposed
 by the learned Trial Court and if not wanted in connection with any
 other case. The appellant would not leave the country without
 permission of the learned Trial Court. He would also submit his
 passport, if any, before the learned Trial Court and the appellant and his
 bailors shall not change their address or mobile nos. without permission
 of the learned Trial Court. 8. I.A. No. 2437/2022 stands disposed of accordingly. (Aparesh Kumar Singh, J)
