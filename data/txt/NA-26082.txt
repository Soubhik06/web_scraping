I received a call today from an automated voice saying that my delivery is canceled asking me to click 1 to talk to a agent regarding the delivery. As I was expecting a delivery, I clicked 1. Immediately, a guy came on line and verified my name and phone number and the convo is below: 

Him: Someone used your name and Aadhar to send a parcel from Mumbai to Iran containing some medicines. It was seized by customs as it contained illegal items. Did you send the parcel? 

Me: No. I am not even from Mumbai. 

Him: 87,000 was transfered from a card ending in 17. Is that your account? 

Me: No 

Him: Did you lose your aadhar recently? 

Me: No

Him: This could cause huge problems in future, please file with Mumbai police and get a police clearance certificate. 

Me: Ok. I will file with Chennai police first. 

He cut the line immediately. I was confused and called Tamil Nadu cyber crime call to get some clarity. They asked me whether i transferred any money and asked me to file a complaint online. I did. 

Well, anybody else?
