--- POST TEXT ---

This morning, I encountered what appears to be a Fastag scam at Chennai International Airport. I was dropping off a family member and, while entering the airport campus, I noticed what looked like scanners at the toll plaza. The attendant didn’t say anything—just waved me through—so I assumed my Fastag had been scanned and my entry time recorded. I dropped off my family member and headed to the exit within 10 minutes, which should have been free as per the rules.

However, at the exit toll, the attendant asked, “Entry slip kodukaliya?” (Didn’t you get an entry slip?). I explained that I didn’t receive one because I thought they had scanned my Fastag at the entrance. He then checked my Fastag, did something on his system, and the barrier opened. I left assuming no charges were applied since I exited within the free time window. But later, I received an SMS notifying me of an Rs. 85 deduction. It seems the attendant didn’t properly check my entry time and instead charged me the full base fare, despite my short stay.

This feels like a deliberate scam to exploit unsuspecting people. It’s frustrating to see such practices by private entities operating around the airport, taking advantage of the public with little accountability.
