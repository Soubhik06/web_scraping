***Disclaimer***: I love and respect all religions, races, countries and other cultural groups.Also, there is a 5% chance it might not be a scam. On second thought make that 1%.

Also, Mr Hacker, if you are reading this, then make no mistake, I won't shy away from reaching the authorities or guess what I have already informed them.

***Prologue***: After a very rough year (career-wise) I was desperate for a job. I work on a somewhat niche tool in IT sector in India. I have worked for a couple of years in the UK/US and my LinkedIn profile has all the basic contact details. It also says that I am looking for a job and can be viewed by recruiters. I get a lot of calls from the UK for job offers and occasional calls from Europe, US, Middle East and Australia as well.

&amp;#x200B;

***22 Jan 2019***: I get a mail (from a company's domain not gmail) suggesting a job offer and asking my Skype Id. On replying, I get a call from Philippines. The guy offers me an IT job in Cyprus. He has an Israeli name and said that he is 'Managing Market R&amp;D'. I can't single out his accent though, but he was definitely not a native speaker. Arranges the interview on the very next day (very unusual in my field of work. Usually it takes weeks to get an interview slot).

People conversed today = 1 (R&amp;D guy)

&amp;#x200B;

&amp;#x200B;

***23 Jan 2019***: I get a Skype invite from a Russian guy for the interview. (By Russian I mean a very Russian accent, Russian name and Russian look in the profile picture). The R&amp;D guy introduces him as the software developer, I would be working under and we hit off the interview. The interview happens to be the easiest interview I have ever been through. It was such a cakewalk that at one stage the Russian interviewer exclaimed that "I was giving too much information." We leave on a very happy note.

People conversed today = 2 (R&amp;D guy and Russian developer)

&amp;#x200B;

&amp;#x200B;

***24 Jan 2019***: I get a Skype invite from a lady with an Israeli name. She says that I got selected and I have to come over to Cyprus. Asks for basic info and says "good" when I tell her that I am single. When I tell her my UK salary she even says "Very good". Then she says that her company cannot match my UK salary and offers something close + lodging (free of rent). She also requests that I join them early on tourist visa as business visa will take a month to process. I get overly excited. I thank her and she says "We will have a great time". Then she mails an offer letter (from her company's domain) and requests that I sign it and reply back.*In my entire career I have never had such a smooth sail to the offer letter, despite applying for hundred's of company's and yet I did not have an ounce of doubt*.I get the offer letter on my personal id and this is where the whole thingy starts falling apart.

Call it inception but the very seed of doubt was planted in my mind literally by the company's name in the offer letter. Interestingly, nobody has uttered the company's name so far (or maybe I overheard it). The offer letter mentioned the company's name as \*something\*CON instead of \*something\*COM. I ask her about this and she casually suggests that I can change it myself. The offer letter is missing any logo. It has just one field. Passport number. Also, the regular company rules and regulations about leaves, etc. I fill in the number, sign it and mail it back.

Then I sent her a LinkedIn invite. She immediately accepted it. The profile has over 500+ connections with 20+ endorsements for different HR skills and mentions her as a VP but none of her companies are mentioned.

Clouded with doubt I googled about the company \*something\*COM Cyprus. The official site does not show up. Instead various directory sites show up that have details about the company like address, board of directors, etc.Then I searched "Cyprus job fraud" and [this](https://hci.gov.in/nicosia/?4074?000) shows up.Meanwhile I get a mail from another lady (with the company domain's email), congratulating me on landing the job and asking for a copy of my passport.A panicked me searches the company name in the email. The company does exist and its a big one. It also has an office in Cyprus with 10-15 employees. Many of them are friends with the HR guy and the Skype lady. I send friend requests (or whatever it is in LinkedIn) to everyone who are not friends with them. A couple of them accept the friend request and I immediately enquire them about these guys. Nobody replies. Instead I get a pretty angry mail-

And while I was pretty sure that I have uncovered a scam, a google search with the lady's name uncovers very valid Facebook, Insta, etc. profiles. She is even conducting a seminar with people wearing the company's t-shirt in some of the photos.

People conversed today = 3 (R&amp;D guy + Skype lady + Passport lady)

&amp;#x200B;

&amp;#x200B;

***25 Jan 2019***: I get a Skype message from the R&amp;D guy asking, "Is everything ok ?". At the same exact moment I get another Skype message from the Skype lady saying "i sent you email yesterday. please let me know how you want to proceed." \*Yeah sure! Whatever happened to the bad blood from yesterday!\*I don't reply to any of them. The R&amp;D guy then sends "If you have any concern , this is my LinkedIn profile :" and attaches a LinkedIn profile of a CTO for Philippine based company. *Who said anything about concern to him!*

The Skype lady then drops this final message-I send friend requests to everyone in the company (mostly people from Ukraine). Some of them accept my request. I ask them about these people. While some of them don't know because of different locations, one guy says he knows them and even confirms that they are getting a project in my "niche technology" through some other company (\*something\*COM?!). I keep bugging him about my uneasiness but he is pretty sure that the job is "legit and legal".

Finally , I drop a message on the company's contact-us mailer. No reply. Then, through the company's facebook page I drop a mail to the company's general HR email id. I get a reply from the company's HR.

![img](rzm7iqexrdd21 "The official HR's mail
")

Here I must point out that the bad english has been a common recurrence throughout the conversation. I send her a LinkedIn friend request. She accepts and immediately messages the same exact lines from the mail.

And then it happens!!!She sends me a Skype friend request with the same exact lines.

***BITCH I NEVER gave you my Skype Id*** !!!

This blew the entire scam for me.

People conversed today = 5-6 (Skype lady+R&amp;D guy+Overzealous HR lady+Many legit guys from the company)

&amp;#x200B;

***26-27-28 January:*** Many other guys from the company and the Russian developer who took my interview has accepted my LinkedIn friend request. The Russian developer works for a different firm.

People conversed today = 0

&amp;#x200B;

**Key takeaways-**

1. The single most important one- SOMEONE, SOMEWHERE CARES. Better still, there might be a group of people thinking about me. Yup, me. God, it has done wonders to my self-confidence.
2. LinkedIn profiles can be hacked and many of these are already hacked.

&amp;#x200B;

**More research**\-

1. The Philippine based company has over 500 people and many glassdoor reviews, but their site is not https.
2. The Skype lady is a real person with real job, friends and family.
3. The passport HR does not exist. No LinkedIn, Skype or google results.

&amp;#x200B;

**Possible solutions**\-

1. There is some guy who has hacked the LinkedIn, Skype profiles and most importantly the company's official mail server of all the above mentioned lovely people. Why they are oblivious to it is still a mystery!
2. It is a bigger scam with someone on the inside helping someone on the outside.
3. I have just screwed up some excellent job offer.

&amp;#x200B;

**Open questions**\-

1. WHY ME?
2. What is the purpose of such an elaborate scam? What could the hacker possible get from me? They went on to conduct an interview of my technology which is a closed-source project with possibly 10,000 or less practitioners across the world.
3. How much is TOO much? Apparently the hacker has hacked half the holy LinkedIn.
4. How can he/she call from so many different international numbers?

&amp;#x200B;

Just when I thought it was over- 

***29 Jan 2019:*** I get a call from an HR lady with broken English offering me a job in Denmark. While it appears completely legit the offer is ridiculously same as the Cyprus one. The lady is a real person on LinkedIn. Almost the same salary with accommodation free of rent. The calling number had an STD code of Denmark. She even replies saying that my resume is very impressive. *Who does that?* 
People conversed = 1 (HR lady from Denmark) 

***30 Jan 2019:*** A call from the UK offering contract with a very well reputed firm in Netherlands. Similar pattern of discussion but they did not offer any accommodation free of rent. Very upfront about the money. Also, they wanted an interview on the same evening over WatsApp! *Never had an interview on WatsApp before*. Did not get any call for the interview and repeated enquiries on LinkedIn and mail did not get any reply. 
People conversed = 1 (HR lady from the UK) 

Then everything stops. Zilch. No calls. No mails. No interviews. Nothing. 

***31 Jan 2019***: I get a mail on this id (from which I am logged in for this post) that some HR firm in the UK searched this email id. I have a LinkedIn profile with this id as well.
