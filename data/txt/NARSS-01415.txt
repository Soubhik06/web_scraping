Kerala govt is spending 67% of the 7200+ Cr budget of this project. We only needed like 2k crore more investment. Only for that amount the govt sold out kerala to adani. According to the PPP deal Adani will operate the port and collect revenue for minimum 40 years, eventho all PPP projects are normally for 30 years. That extra 10 years alone will cost kerala govt like 20k crores of user fee. After that they can extend upto 45 more years if they want (I heard theres also a clause that makes it possible for them to avail 3x the extension period). So overall we wouldn't be able to own and operate the port for 40 to upto 85 or even 180 years. 
They also have the rights to build and operate their resorts and facilities permanently in the land bought by GoK. They have also mortgaged the land bought by GoK. Kerala govt will get no share of user fee for first 20 years, eventho adani will get all his investment + interest back after 10 years of operating it. After 20 years, GoK will only get 1% of the revenue. Shockingly bad deal. 
Now coming to the funds GoK is investing now, which is around 5k crores, they are taking loan. For which they'll only start paying after 20 years, which is also dumb cause by then the amount will be compounded to 3x or 4x times. 
I was so excited about the possibilities of vizhinjam port until I read all these details of the deal. I couldnt believe how terrible the deal is for kerala. How did adani scam kerala so easily and why did kerala govt. agree to this deal like absolute idiots? This is probably the worst deal anyone would've ever struck. The CAG criticized this a lot too. Why were the govt so desperate that they couldnt make a better deal or wait for other investor? Or they could've found a way to finance 2k crore more and built and operated the port themselves. They could easily pay it back from the massive revenue they could collect from the port. Did the udf govt. just want to rush and start a statement project themselves for political reasons? 
Central govt is directly funding and building another mothership port at colachel in TN as part of the sagarmala project, which is too close to vizhinjam and will be 4x the size of vizhinjam. Central govt hasnt approved any greenfield expressways or major urban projects in kerala unlike for all other states. I feel like theres a massive lobby/conspiracy against Kerala. 
I just wanted to vent and get your thoughts on this.
