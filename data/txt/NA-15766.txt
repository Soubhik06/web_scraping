Hey everyone, I hope you're doing well. I wanted to share a recent experience that left me feeling frustrated and taken advantage of. About two weeks ago, I came across an Instagram store that appeared to be legitimate. They were offering products at unbelievably low prices that seemed too good to pass up. Intrigued by the offer, I decided to make a purchase and spent Rs 500 on three items.

However, things quickly took a turn for the worse. After waiting patiently for my order to arrive and receiving no updates, I reached out to the store through messages. To my surprise, they not only ignored me but also blocked me on Instagram. It was then that I realized I had fallen victim to cybercrime fraud.

Determined not to let this slide, I'm here seeking advice and assistance. I refuse to let the owner of this fraudulent business get away with it. I'm committed to holding them accountable for their actions, but I'm unsure about the steps to take next. Despite my efforts, I haven't been able to get through to the cybercrime helpline number (1930) as it's always busy.

I'm reaching out to this community in the hope that someone can guide me on the right path. I'm open to suggestions, legal advice, and any information that could help me get justice. Online scams are becoming more prevalent, and it's important that we stand together against such fraudulent activities.

If you've been in a similar situation or have insights to share, please lend your support. Let's spread awareness about the risks of online scams and work together to ensure that those who engage in fraudulent practices are held accountable for their actions. Thank you for taking the time to read this, and I'm looking forward to your suggestions and guidance
