Good day everyone,

Recently, about 2.5 weeks back i recieved a call from a mobile number. On picking up, there was a computerised voice saying my parcel could not be delivered and to press 1 for more information.

Confused, as I hadn't sent any parcel, I pressed 1 and a young man took over the call. He said there was a parcel I had tried to send to Taiwan, which could not be completed due to some issues at custom. 

This rang a bell in my head as I had heard of similar scams before. I said I had not sent such a parcel to which the man replied, "how can that be possible sir, your aadhar number is used here for the same". So I asked them to repeat my aadhar number to me, they said they could not due to privacy reasons.
I just hung up the call and didn't think too much of it other than a poor failed scam attempt.

Fast forward to today, i recieved two similar computerised voice calls asking me to press 1. 
Slightly worried now, I checked the numbers registered to my aadhar card through the online portal, and there were two unknown numbers registered to it. I quickly reported those numbers as not mine.

But now I am worried if my number was indeed used for any illegal activities. What should be my next step? Should I approach the police?

Thanks in advance guys!
