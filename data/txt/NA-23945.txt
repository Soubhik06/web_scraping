Today I had a strange experience while booking a cab on Uber for relatives. First, using Uber go option, the driver did not move an inch for eight minutes was showing five minutes away. So, I went into the vehicle section to cancel the order, but saw another option that let me change my Driver. Don’t remember exact words, but it said something like: not satisfied with your driver? Click here to change… so I went ahead and click that button and another driver appeared and this time he did come towards us However, on providing the OTP, my relative was told that the cab was an Uber excel and that she would need to cancel, rebook with Excel so that she could get him as a driver. Panicked, my relatives called me and unsure about whether this was a crafty way for the driver to get more money… The price was almost double the amount of Uber go, I told my relatives I would not be cancelling. Instead, they booked another cab using another app so wanted to check if anyone else had this experience and to find out whether this might happen again, as well as opinions on whether it’s a scam or just an annoying business decision by Uber.
