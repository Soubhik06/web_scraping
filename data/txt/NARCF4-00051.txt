Be cautious with Uber Parcel in Bhubaneswar – Some riders misuse the system. Just a heads-up for anyone using Uber Parcel in Bhubaneswar:

Recently, I booked a parcel delivery from Jharpada to Bapuji Nagar(~5km) with an estimated fare of ₹58 The rider picked it up and then took a completely illogical detour—via Palasuni, Vani Vihar, Acharya Vihar—before finally reaching the destination. This extended route doubled the fare to ₹116

From tracking the ride, it became clear the rider likely accepted a passenger ride from another app and completed both trips together. When confronted, he first denied it, then admitted he took a longer route and tried to manipulate asking me to pay rs 100 saying ,I will mark the delivery as complete &amp; tried negotiating the fare. Ended up paying 70rs. 

Lesson: Always track your parcel rides and don’t hesitate to call out suspicious behavior. Some riders may misuse the loopholes in fare calculation.
