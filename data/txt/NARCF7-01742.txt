Rapido is a scam company. I was shown an estimate of 283 Rs for a trip, but when the driver finished the trip, I had to pay 298 Rs, which I paid. I usually get these bills reimbursed at work, but they sent me a bill for 283 Rs. I spent 25 minutes chatting with customer service to get a refund, but it was still a scam because they credited it to my Rapido wallet, which I can only use for bike or parcel services—services I never use.

5min to book the ride then 25 min for a refund .Haha
