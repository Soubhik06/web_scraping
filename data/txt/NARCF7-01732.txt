So the other day, my friend and I were going to the market on foot when this couple approached us. The lady started asking for help, claiming that they had been to a nearby temple where her husband's wallet was stolen and needed some money.

Both of us were a little doubtful about the story and apologized and then decided to move on.

An amount of 50/100rs is not much when helping someone but helping a scammer would only encourage these activities which is dangerous for the development of our city.

On our way, we were discussing whether this case was true or not and came across several points:

\- The temple was approximately 2 km from the area where they approached us

\- They could've gone home by auto and paid with the money they had at home

\- If they had mobiles with them, they could've called their friends/family for help

&amp;#x200B;

On another occasion, I was approached by a guy right outside the metro station asking me to transfer 50rs since he had no money and wanted to go back home. Is this a new scam?

&amp;#x200B;

I don't know whether we/I did the right thing or not but the world has come to such a situation where you cannot trust anyone these days.
