I received a phone call from *PhonePe customer care* asking me if I have received a 1000rs cashback yesterday. As it was a ridiculous amount and I hadn't received such reward, I said no. Then he asked me to make a 1rs transaction on the number he called from. That's when I ended the call. Point to note is, I primarily use GPay for my transactions and on the odd day when GPay isn't working I use PhonePe. I haven't made any transaction from PhonePe in about 2 months. My issue is, he knew my name and number. Are the vendors we are paying through UPI apps selling our details to scammers? If so, then brace yourselves for much more scam calls in the future.
