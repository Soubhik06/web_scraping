--- POST TEXT ---

A few days ago, I applied for a Relationship Manager position at ICICI Bank through the National Career Service (NCS) portal. Today, I received a call from someone claiming to be an HR representative. After discussing the job, she clarified that the position was actually for KYC verification, not a target-based sales role, which I found acceptable, so I continued the conversation.

However, things took a strange turn when she informed me that I would need to travel to Kolkata for the interview. I explained that I live in Mumbai and had specifically applied for jobs in Mumbai, so traveling to Kolkata wasn’t feasible. She then offered the option of an online interview but said there would be a ₹299 fee to conduct it. The fee, she explained, would be refundable if I was selected for the job, but if I wasn’t selected, the money would not be refunded.

At this point, the situation started to feel like a scam. She also mentioned that I could be placed at any private bank, such as ICICI, Axis Bank, or another I don’t recall, and that the job location would be within 7-15 km of my postal code.

Is this a legitimate offer, or could this be a scam?
