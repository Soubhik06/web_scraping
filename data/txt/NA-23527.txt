Scammed in Ponzi Scheme and Frozen Bank Accounts

Hi guys, 

My first post here. A friend of mine had fallen victim yo Ponzi scheme, resulting in frozen bank accounts and significant financial loss. 

On August 2023, he discovered that he had become a victim of a fraudulent investment scheme that promised high returns in a short period. He innocently invested a substantial amount of money, naively believing that this would be a lucrative opportunity. However, it has now become apparent that it was an elaborate Ponzi scheme designed to defraud unsuspecting investors like himself.

As a result of this scam, his financial resources have been depleted, and bank accounts have been frozen by my financial institution in connection with an ongoing investigation into fraudulent activities. This freeze has caused immense hardship and has left him unable to access the funds necessary for his daily living expenses, including mortgage payments, utility bills, and even basic necessities such as groceries.

He already reported this incident to his bank and provided them with all the necessary documents and evidence of the fraudulent transactions. He also seek assistance and intervention to expedite the investigation process, recover the funds lost, and help resolve this distressing situation with the police. However after 6 months he received a message that the investigation is completed. He did not receive his money, no information from cyber crime department. 

We all are getting such whtsapp messages on daily basis, luring other people for jobs. But this can lead to such financial and mental torture. So beware!
