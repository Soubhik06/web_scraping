So basically I ordered some food from Swiggy amounting to ₹908. I had put CoD as payment option, which I generally avoid because delivery partners always say they don’t have change and you have to end up paying ₹20-60 more. However, incidentally I had some cash and decided I’ll spend it here.

I had added a note for him to bring ₹100 change but he didn’t.

He came and I asked if he had change, he said he only has no change at all.

I gave him ₹910 with a ₹10 coin and he said that the coin is fake. (I am well versed with law and the fact that denying legal tender is a crime but tbh it’s not worth going there for ₹10).

I asked him if he could UPI or give any change if I gave him ₹1000. He now magically has ₹50 note (perhaps because he saw I have more cash).

I was riled up and angrily went upstairs, my brother keeps his coins in a jar, I took exactly ₹8 from there and paid him ₹908 exactly.

He was not very happy but didn’t say anything knowing he would receive an earful from me.

TLDR: Swiggy delivery guy tried to get some extra cash from me on the pretext of having no change, I gave him exactly the amount in coins.
