the sides, let refer section 354(A) of the IPC to find out the
ingredients of the offence. 11. The FIR alleges the offence punishable u/s 354(A)(1)(iii) of
the IPC. If we read the provisions of law, sexual harassment can
be made out if the woman has been shown pornography against her
will. Apart from the offence u/s 354(A)(1)(iii) of the IPC, the
sexual harassment can be said to have been committed if the
accused contacted a woman physically and advised involving

can also said to have been committed if the demand or request of

ingredients of section 354(A)(1)(iii) of the IPC. The FIR spells that
when the complainant went to the office for tendering her

improbable on the basis of which no prudent person can ever reach
a just conclusion that there is sufficient ground for proceeding
against the accused or if the proceedings founds to be manifestly
attended with mala fide and/or where the proceeding is maliciously
instituted with an ulterior motive to settle the score, such FIR
should not be allowed to culminate into the trial. The present FIR
is of such kind and it does not require trial. 15. For the foregoing reasons, present petition is hereby allowed
