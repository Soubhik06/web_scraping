Been in Bengaluru for 3+ years, first time was stopped by 3 hijras demanding money (10 rs) near Marathahalli. Luckily I had read old posts regarding this scam.

So, I tried avoiding them saying I had no money, to which one of them pointed out my wallet, which could be seen as I was in sports trousers. I said I can only UPI to which they took 1 rupee and perform some rubbish gibberish and said to put it in my wallet. I said no and tried to move forward but one of them pushed me back with both hands. Damn! I was angry and shocked. It's meaningless to confront them. I quickly turned back and started walking away from them, never looking back. They didn't care about chasing me. Later they moved to another side of the road near the spice garden bus stop. Tough day.
