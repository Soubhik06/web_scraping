Step out of your house and go to any shop, you will be fooled and you will not even know.

Hungy? Grab a bite at McD, BK, KFC, Starbucks. You will be asked "Sir/Mam, what would you like on the side/choice of dip/flavour on your coffee" and you will respond with one of the options. Well, that option costs extra.
"Sir/Mam, do you want a regular burger/beverage" You will say "Yes". But there is an option for a small cup/size which is almost the same size as regular. It costs less.
"Sir/Mam, if you take a meal, you get fries too" You say "oh, sure then. Make it a meal. Those fries cost extra.

Hell, if you order it from your table which is how it is in malls now-a-days, you pay extra. It is not a free service.

Then comes the retail outlets who already oversell, depending on the brand. You will find the same quality, even better, at your local tailor. But these high end stores will sell you at 5x and call it sale price.

Is it a fraud? Not really. It is smart. They are just choosing the right word combined with the pressure on the customer to make a quick decision. 

I have saved a lot of money after knowing these gimmicks. Then I splurge it all on irrelevant stationary like an obsessed fool that I am.

Bottomline. Nothing is free in this world. You are a fool to get excited over deals and offers.
