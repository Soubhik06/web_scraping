--- POST TEXT ---

Yesterday my mother was a victim of cybercrime and lost 4 lacs to a scammer. In hindsight she did commit a big mistake but as soon as money started getting debited we started taking action. So the cybercrime helpline did not work. Somehow after we connected they kept asking for transaction id which was missing in the bank messages and emails. They refused to do any work without the id even though we knew the account number. We went to the police station within an hour. They were helpful but at snail pace. Again kept asking for transaction id. They didn’t want the account number even though we had it and they didn’t even mention it in the complaint. Chalu hai. Le kiya hai complaint. Dekhte hai. Process chalu kiya hai. In this time the entire amount was withdrawn by the scammer from his account. We came to know this the next morning when banks opened. All I want to say is, that yes it was foolishness from my mother’s part but aren’t the police supposed to be smarter and faster and help us. I mean we earn money through years of hard work, pay taxes and then lose it in minutes. Can’t the government employ more people for this department, can’t they update their softwares and buy efficient infrastructure to help the common people against such frauds. Feeling extremely disappointed and let down by the police and cybercrime department.
