Mumbai, Jul 11 (PTI) A joint managing director of a prominent stationery product company was duped of Rs 13.26 lakh by unidentified cyber fraudsters, who obtained access to his corporate card, email ID and other vital details and made online purchases, a police official said on Friday.
The incident came to light on July 3 when the assistant of the joint managing director approached the Central Region Cyber Police Station with a complaint of online fraud, cheating and impersonation, the official said.
The joint managing director has an American Express corporate card that is linked with a mobile phone and an email ID and is used by him or his assistant for any kind of purchases, the official said quoting the complaint.

“On July 1, when the joint MD was in the office, his assistant noticed that several online purchases of Rs 11,713, Rs 884, Rs 45,435, and Rs 41,843 were made through the card from the Amazon platform. On being asked, the joint MD told his assistant that he did not make the purchases,” he said.
As the corporate card could also be accessed by the previous assistant and two others, they too were asked but they denied making any purchase, the official said.
During the investigation it came to light that the purchases using the corporate card were made from February this year, when it expired, and was replaced by a new card number, the official said.
“Cyber Police has registered a case against unidentified fraudsters for purchases through unauthorized access to the card and its password. Further probe is underway,” he added. PTI DC BNM
