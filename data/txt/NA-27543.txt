I got a call from a 972 Area code a week ago. Some guy with an average US name (like :John Smith") left a voicemail saying he was trying to collect on some unspecified debt for a client. Since I had no idea what he was talking about I figured it's a scam.

Then he called back yesterday. He left another voicemail saying to have my lawyer contact him, yada yada. I looked up the number on beenverified and it supposedly belongs to a person with a name the I probably couldn't pronounce...maybe Indian, but I don't know. It also has a 65% fraud likelihood. 

I also looked up the Area code and saw that it is used to trick people into calling back and incurring some kind of crazy fee. What the hell is this? If someone thinks I owe them money I don't think they'd go about it this way and I have no idea who this bozo is or who I could owe "a substantial amount" too.
