Justice N.V. Ramana has been recommended to the government by Chief Justice of India S.A. Bobde as his successor. Justice Ramana is now [set to take over as the 48th Chief Justice of India ](https://newsalert.thehindu.com/a/hBgXCy$B9HlHIB9HlJNAANSUMih/link20)from April 24 and will hold that post till August 26, 2022.The day of the recommendation also saw another announcement from the Supreme Court that a [complaint against Justice Ramana](https://newsalert.thehindu.com/a/hBgXCy$B9HlHIB9HlJNAANSUMih/link60) by Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister Jagan Mohan Reddy has been dismissed after due consideration in an in-house proceeding, which will not be on public record. 

The [complaint was filed by Mr. Reddy](https://newsalert.thehindu.com/a/hBgXCy$B9HlHIB9HlJNAANSUMih/link52) in a letter to the CJI in October 2020, in which he said that Justice Ramana was interfering in the workings of the Andhra Pradesh High Court — with regard to the roster of which judge hears which case — to the benefit of the opposition TDP. There were several issues linked to this letter, some of which will be alive even after the complaint has been dismissed. The timing of the letter had been described as suspect, since it came after a verdict by Justice Ramana that fast-tracked cases against Ministers and legislators. One of the cases thus taken up was a disproportionate assets case against Mr. Reddy, who has 31 cases against him. The basis of Mr. Reddy's allegation against Justice Ramana was the Amaravati land scam case in which an official in the previous TDP government and relatives of a sitting Supreme Court justice are said to have benefited. The High Court was accused of stalling the proceedings in the case. Another issue that sprouted from the letter was a defamation case against Mr. Reddy, for having raised allegations against the higher judiciary in public. While the Advocate-General of India has so far not given his consent for the defamation case to move forward, the plea is alive. 

[Justice Ramana](https://newsalert.thehindu.com/a/hBgXCy$B9HlHIB9HlJNAANSUMih/link23) has been part of the Bench in several key verdicts such as those that upheld freedom of speech on the Internet in the context of the [curbs in Jammu and Kashmir](https://newsalert.thehindu.com/a/hBgXCy$B9HlHIB9HlJNAANSUMih/link59), and on women's rights. However, his opinion that the [RTI Act cannot be used as a "surveillance tool"](https://newsalert.thehindu.com/a/hBgXCy$B9HlHIB9HlJNAANSUMih/link55) in the judgement that brought the[CJI's office under the RTI Act](https://newsalert.thehindu.com/a/hBgXCy$B9HlHIB9HlJNAANSUMih/link58), had been termed by transparency advocates as "unfortunate." These background details are what make the news about Justice Ramana's proposed elevation to the highest judicial office in India important.
