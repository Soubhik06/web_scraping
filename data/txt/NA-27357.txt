Hey everyone, just wanted to share a near-miss experience I had with a sophisticated scam that almost got me. This scam starts by showing a fake BBC article and interview with Amitabh Bachchan, claiming he uses an AI trading platform called "Immediate 2.2 Alrex" to make thousands of rupees daily.

The fake article mentions Amitabh Bachchan supposedly revealing this secret during an interview with Nag Ashwin (who’s promoting his Kalki movie), and claims that the Indian government even placed Amitabh under house arrest for "damaging the economy" by sharing this platform with the public. The whole thing looks legit, even using real interview images of Amitabh Bachchan, though he never actually mentioned anything about "Immediate 2.2 Alrex" or being under house arrest. It’s all fabricated to make the scam seem more believable.

I came across it while scrolling through Instagram, and it redirected me to a fake news page that mimicked the BBC website, then to an “Immediate 2.2 Alrex” site, and eventually to a completely unrelated site called "FXroad" to create an account.

It's all just a well-crafted scam trying to trick people into handing over their money by promising huge returns from AI-based trading. I wanted to post this here to make sure no one falls for this. Always verify sources, especially when it comes to financial investments that seem too good to be true. Stay safe!

TL;DR: There's a fake BBC interview circulating on Instagram featuring Amitabh Bachchan, promoting a scam AI trading platform called "Immediate 2.2 Alrex." The scammers used real interview images, but Amitabh never said anything about it and is definitely not under house arrest. It’s a scam – don't fall for it!
