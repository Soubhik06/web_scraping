Title: Wake Up, India! Use Social Media to Hold Politicians Accountable

India has one of the largest social media user bases in the world. We have a powerful tool in our hands—the internet. Yet, many of us are silent while politicians, regardless of party, continue to exploit our country.

It’s time to take action. Whether you support this government or that, it doesn’t matter. What matters is calling out corruption, scams, and misgovernance. If we don’t, who will? If we stay silent, they will continue unchecked.

Earlier, the media and newspapers were controlled, and information was scarce. But today, we have access to everything. We can expose wrongdoing, spread awareness, and demand accountability. But for that, we need to act!

Make Twitter, Instagram, Reddit, and every possible social media account.

Start calling out corrupt politicians and bureaucrats.

Share real issues, expose their failures, and demand better governance.

Don’t say, "I don’t like politics." Politics affects you whether you like it or not.

If we stay naive, one day, we or our families might be the next victims of bad governance. We must unite, speak up, and fight for a better India. The power is in our hands. Let’s use it!
