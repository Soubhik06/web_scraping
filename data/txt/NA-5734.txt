(31.01.2022) 1. The issues under consideration arising out of the second appeal dated 10.07.2021
include non-receipt of the following information raised by the appellant through the RTI
application dated 11.01.2021 and first appeal dated 03.03.2021:- 2. Succinctly facts of the case are that the appellant filed an application dated
11.01.2021 under the Right to Information Act, 2005 (RTI Act ) before the Central Public
Information Officer (CPIO), Punjab National Bank, Shalimar Bagh, New Delhi, seeking
aforesaid information. The CPIO vide letter dated 11.02.2021 replied to the appellant.
Dissatisfied with the same, the appellant filed first appeal dated 03.03.2021. The First
Appellate Authority (FAA) vide order dated 19.04.2021 disposed of the first appeal.
Aggrieved by the same, the appellant filed a second appeal dated 10.07.2021 before the
Commission which is under consideration. 3. The appellant has filed the instant appeal dated 10.07.2021 inter alia on the
grounds that reply given by the CPIO was not satisfactory. The appellant requested the
Commission to direct the CPIO to provide the complete information and take necessary
action as per Section 20 (1) of the RTI Act. 4. The CPIO replied vide letter dated 11.02.2021 and the same is reproduced as
under:- The FAA vide order dated 19.04.2021provided revised information and the same is as
under:- has been marked by the bank on 09.12.2020 in the aforementioned beneficiary
 account. (iii) Information sought at point no-3, The SMS log record of the said transactions has
 already been provided by the CPIO, wherein the time of generation of intimation
 messages regarding the said transactions and time of delivery of said messages
 mentioned therewith." 5. The appellant and on behalf of the respondent Shri R K Pattanaik, CPIO and Ms.
Vijay Bhawani Jhinga, Senior Manager(Law), Punjab National Bank, Delhi, attended the
hearing in person. 5.1. The appellant inter alia submitted that he had requested to hold the payment of
amount Rs.85320/-, which had been withdrawn by making fraud from his account
through 9 transactions. He further submitted that the bank expressed their inability to
stop the payment due to rules/regulation and they didn't take immediate actions to hold
the payment from Shiv Nath Prasad account which was withdrawn from his account.
Accordingly, he requested the bank to deposit fraudulent amount to his account which
was still due to him. 5.2. The respondent while defending their CPIO's reply dated 11.02.2021 and the
FAA's order dated 19.04.2021 inter alia submitted that information pertaining to the
alleged fraudulent transaction was given to the appellant except the aadhar number and
mobile number of the beneficiary account on the ground of third-party information. They
further submitted that the appellant had also lodged an FIR with the police and the matter
was pending. 6. The Commission after adverting to the facts and circumstances of the case, hearing
both the parties and perusal of records, observed that due reply was given by the CPIO's
reply dated 11.02.2021 and the FAA's order dated 19.04.2021. Perusal of the record
further reveals that the appellant had grievance with regard to fraudulent transaction done
from his account through Paytm which may be raised before an appropriate forum.
Since, the information sought had been provided to the appellant except personal
information of third party, there appears to be no public interest in further prolonging the
matter. Accordingly, the appeal is dismissed. Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties. Sd/- (Suresh Chandra) (सुरेशचं ा)
