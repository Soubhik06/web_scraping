This writ petition has been filed by the petitioner seeking to direct the 2nd

 respondent to issue the passport to the petitioner pursuant to petitioner passport

 vide number T8829095 issued dated 19.09.2019 and the petitioner

 representation to the respondent dated 30.01.2025. 2.Learned counsel for the petitioner would submit that the petitioner was

 placed in job in Laos City, Thailand. Due to significant stress and discomfort in

 the workplace, he was unable to continue under the job and subsequently

 resigned and returned to India on 21.02.2024. While so, the petitioner’s friends

 were planning to go to Bangkok for a trip and they approached the petitioner to

 join in the trip. Therefore, the petitioner was about to travel on 21.04.2024 to

 Bangkok and during the immigration check, the 1st respondent seized the

 petitioner’s passport on the ground of passport tampering. Therefore, in this

 regard, the petitioner made a formal representation to the 2 nd respondent.

 However, till date there is no response from the respondents Authorities. Hence,

 the respondents by referring the counter affidavit would submit that the passport

 of the petitioner was seized with the remark that the said passport was tampered

 by removing a Sticker Visa on Page No.22 and the matter has been referred to

 the Superintendent of Police, Villupuram District for conducting enquiry and the

 Superintendent of Police, in his report, has stated that though the

 applicant/petitioner claimed his employer has removed the visa sticker on Page

 No.22 in his passport, his explanation is not convincing. Furthermore, there was

 a caution entry against the Passport Number T8829095 made by Embassy of

 India, Vientiane, Laos stating that the petitioner has been rescued from Golden

 Triangle SEZ in Lao PDR by Embassy of India, Veintiane, in August 2023.

 However, the petitioner again went to Golden Triangle after rescuing.

 Therefore, he cheated Lao Police and Embassy of India. 4.He would further submit that the matter has already been referred to the

 Ministry of External Affairs, New Delhi for inclusion of petitioner’s name in

 Prior Approval category since he has been frequently visiting Lao PDR,

 Thailand and Vietnam “Golden Triangle as part of Cyber Slavery Scam

 network. Hence, he prayed to dismiss the present writ petition.

 strong objection for considering the plea of the petitioner, considering the

 limited prayer sought for in this writ petition, without going into the merits of

 the case, this Court directs the 2nd respondent to consider the petitioner’s

 accordance with law, within a period of one month from the date of receipt of a

 copy of this order. 7.With the above direction, this writ petition is disposed of. No costs. 16-12-2025
