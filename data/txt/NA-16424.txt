I'm reaching out on this really tough day to share a heartbreaking experience that happened to my cousin. She was targeted by a sophisticated online scam that has left us all shaken. I'm sharing this story in the hopes that it might prevent this from happening to someone else.

The scam began with an unexpected phone call asking my cousin to install Skype and receive a further call there. During the Skype call, she was falsely informed that she was implicated in a serious crime involving a minister from Maharashtra related to drugs and smuggling.

The scammer claimed to be an officer from the Crime Branch and even used the Crime Branch's logo during the call to appear legitimate. The call lasted a total of 55 minutes, during which the scammer skillfully manipulated the conversation to create panic and fear. They told her that her involvement had been confirmed, and to avoid severe punishment, she needed to verify her identity and clear her name. Here’s how they tricked her into complying:

1. \*\*Identity Verification:\*\* They asked her to share her Aadhar card details over Skype, which she did, and the scammer took screenshots of it.

2. \*\*Bank Account Info:\*\* Next, they requested her bank account details under the guise of "official verification." She was navigating through the SBI YONO app, which he watched during the call.

3. \*\*Money Transfer:\*\* Finally, they demanded that she transfer money immediately to help "resolve" the case. To check around the frequencies of transactions with her account. She was directed to send ₹49,000 to one Indus Bank account and ₹26,000 to another through Google Pay. Unfortunately, screen recordings failed to capture the Google Pay app but the Yono app allowed it nicely (such a shame).

4. \*\*Personal Family Information:\*\* She also shared detailed information about her relatives, including parents and siblings, their names, and where they reside.

As the call neared its end, the scammer claimed that he needed an additional 15 minutes to verify the information and then he would refund the money and then This never happened.

My cousin, being the gentle and trusting soul she is, complied with all these demands. It was only after the ordeal that she realized she had been scammed and was left devastated, crying throughout the day.
