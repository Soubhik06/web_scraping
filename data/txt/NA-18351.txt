&gt;On 2 July 2014, the Google Security team became aware of fake Google certificates issued by the National Informatics Centre of India.[2][3][4] Google immediately notified the Indian NIC and Indian Controller of Certifying Authorities (CCA) as well as Microsoft. Microsoft revoked the NIC's certificate and published Security Advisory 2982792.[5]
