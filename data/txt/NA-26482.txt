Guys, I recently posted an as for renting out my flat on Magicbricks.com. All of a sudden, I started getting a flurry of calls from prospective tenants all claiming to work for the Indian Army. They asked me the rent and the deposit and agreed immediately on seeing fee photos of the flat. One lady even sent copy of her documents, including her PAN. Now, I work in a Bank and can easily identify whether such documents are genuine and fake. As a matter of caution, I checked out her PAN and was actually surprised to find out it was fake.
I took to the Internet to read about this weird phenomenon where army people are desperately looking for flats. And I unearthed an article from the Hindustan Times where such folks pretend to belong to the armed forces and agree to your rented flat. They ask for your UPI to transfer the amount and gain access to your account. Then, they dupe you.
Fortunately, I had the resources to do some research and avoid getting cheated.
Please spread this awareness among all to NOT share UPI details with anyone, no matter what. Transfer of rent and deposit can be made through normal RTGS and NEFT for which normal account details suffice.
DO NOT SHARE UPI DETAILS AT ALL!

Tomorrow I am going to play along with this guy and give him a piece of my mind. If possible, I will gather my friends and invite him for discussion and humiliate him publicly. Additionally, his PAN and phone number will be shared with Hinjewadi police to start some sort of investigation.

By the way, I have a 2 BHK flat in Hinjewadi, Phase 3. It is very close to TCS building. It's available for rent from April 2021 onwards. Thanks!
