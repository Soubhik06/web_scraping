TLDR: I'm being harassed with calls from scammers pretending to be from Blue Dart and loan recovery agencies. They're targeting my family members as well.

I've been getting a wave of calls from unknown numbers, starting this morning. The callers initially claimed to be from Blue Dart, saying there was a parcel for one of my cousins who's currently out of India. Recognizing the classic FedEx scam, I quickly said fuck off to the caller.

However, the harassment didn't stop there. The calls continued, this time from different numbers, with the scammers now pretending to be from a loan recovery agency. They're claiming my cousin has an unpaid loan and are demanding to speak to him. I confirmed with my cousin and he said he hasn't taken any loan.

The strange part is, they're not asking for any money. Their sole purpose seems to be harassing us and causing distress.

Frustrated and annoyed, I decided to take action. I filed an online complaint with the cybercrime department and even visited my local police station. Unfortunately, the help I received was minimal. The police officer suggested I ignore the calls and block the numbers, or even consider taking a break from using my phone for a few days.

As a last resort, I contacted the Gujarat Cyber Crime Helpline (1930). After several attempts, I finally got through and reported the incident.
While I'm hoping the authorities will take action, the constant calls are truly frustrating.

Has anyone else experienced similar harassment? Any advice on how to deal with these persistent scammers?
