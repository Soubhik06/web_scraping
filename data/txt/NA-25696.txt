Hey everyone, I wanted to share my recent experience with a scam call that could have easily fooled someone less aware, especially older folks like our parents. This happened recently and I hope it helps prevent others from falling into such traps. It started with a WhatsApp call from someone claiming to be a police officer. The call was first answered by my mother, who was pretty scared. Then my sister took over, and finally, I had to talk to him myself. The man claimed to be from The CBI police department and asked if I was \[Ramesh\] (using my father's name). I said yes, just to see where this was going. Then he asked who \[Priya\] (my sister) was. Since I was pretending to be my father, I said she was my daughter. He started asking about my sister, where she was, what she was doing, etc. I gave vague answers, but finally, he dropped a bomb, saying that she had been arrested and was in his custody. He demanded ₹1,00,000, saying she had cheated a lot of people and this was the fine to release her. At that point, I knew it was a scam. When I told him that I would report him to the cyber police, he didn't even panic and hung up after a while. This was clearly a scam, and unfortunately, it's easy for these scammers to scare people, especially those who aren't familiar with such tactics, like my parents. The scammer used a photo of an actual police officer to make it seem real. The number was from Pakistan, and it was clearly a scam.

Please be aware:

Always verify the identity of anyone claiming to be from the police or any authority.

Don’t share personal information over the phone.

Scammers often use fake profile pictures to make their stories more convincing.

If you receive suspicious calls like this, don’t engage—report them immediately to the cyber police.

Stay safe and help spread the word to your friends and family, especially those who may not be as tech-savvy. Let's make sure these scammers don't succeed!
