2. The petitioners have been made accused in connection with
 Deoghar (Cyber) P.S. Case No. 23 of 2024 registered under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 & 120-B of the I.P.C. read with Section 66(B) , 66(C) , 66(D) & 84(C) of the I.T. Act pending in the Court of Learned Additional Sessions
 Judge II-cum-special Judge, Cyber Crime, Deoghar. 3. As per F.I.R., allegation is that the petitioner was found indulged in
 commission of cheating with innocent people by committing cybercrime. 4. Learned counsel for the petitioners has submitted that the
 petitioners are innocent and has committed no offence at all rather they
 have been falsely implicated in this case on the basis of suspicion. From
 conscious possession of the petitioners, one mobile phone has been
 recovered and the SIM cards used in cybercrime has not been connected
 with the petitioners. Other co-accused persons have been granted bail by
 this Hon'ble Court vide order dated 26.06.2024 in B.A. No. 4210 of 2024
 with B.A. No. 4184 of 2024 with B.A. No.4219 of 2024 with B.A. No. 4270 of
 2024 with B.A. No. 4478 of 2024. The petitioners are languishing in jail
 since 28.02.2024 without any rhymes and reasons. Petitioners undertake to
 co-operate with the trial of the case and to remain physically present on
 each and every date till the conclusion of the trial and shall not indulge in
 tampering with the prosecution evidence or influence the witnesses or gain
 over them. Hence the petitioners may be enlarged on bail. 5. Learned Addl. P.P. has vehemently opposed the prayer for bail of
 the petitioners and submitted that there are direct and specific allegation
 against the petitioners showing their involvement in the alleged offence of
 commission of cybercrime. Hence, petitioners do not deserve to be
 enlarged on bail. 6. Considering the facts and circumstances of this case, nature of
 allegation against petitioners couple with materials available on record and
 period of custody of the petitioners, I am inclined to release the above-
 named petitioners, on bail. Accordingly, the petitioners named above, is
 directed to be released on bail on furnishing bail bond of Rs.25,000/-
 (Rupees twenty-five thousand) with two sureties of the like amount each to
 the satisfaction of Learned Additional Sessions Judge II-cum-special Judge,
 Cyber Crime, Deoghar in connection Deoghar (Cyber) P.S. Case No. 23 of
 2024 subject to the conditions:- In case of violation of the aforesaid conditions, the bail of the
 petitioner shall be cancelled and shall be taken into custody by the learned
 Trial Court itself. (Pradeep Kumar Srivastava, J.)
