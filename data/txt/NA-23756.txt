I recently got the following message on WhatsApp.

"Dear Customer, Your Electricity power will be disconnected Tonight 10.30 pm because your previous month bill was not update please call officer number 6202200542".

Your typical online scam. So, I reported it, updated the number as a scam in truecaller.

I brought this up in discussion with a police friend of mine. Here are some of the things he told me. 

1) Most scammers are very well organized. They collect phone numbers in an area with the help of data brokers and look out for Bescom notices. When Bescom has a scheduled outage, they send a message to the phone numbers in that area stating that their power has been disconnected due to late payment. 

2) Apparently, they ask for a bribe if the victim tells them that the payment was already made and this seems to be a mistake. Bribe could range anywhere from 100 to 500 rupees. 

3) They even edit their name in truecaller to "Electricity Board RE XXXX" to add a bit more legitimacy to their scam. 

4) If you are a woman and call these numbers back, along with the scam, they also sell your phone number to some creepos. So, just answering a scammer's call or calling them back could land you in a ton of trouble and months of harassment. 

5) Elderly are the most common victims of these scams. Also, their phone numbers are collected and sold to other scammers.
