It was really strange that I recently came across a scam in the name of providing PS4 and PS5 gaming consoles where the scammer named as Aditya Sharma and Chand Khan is posing under a company name of Logica Infoway Ltd and taking money from the people and not delivering them any products. The scammer is providing forged government documents as a proof and giving fake ids to make you believe them. I had a word with them to confirm everything and was told that the delivery was free of cost. Then they ask you to transfer the money into their accounts. 

 The height of the scamming is that they provide you with a forged bill as well. If you ask them the location of their product then they even share these details with you. In my case I grew suspicious when they asked for some extra amount for the transportation. This is when I tried contacting the guy and he wouldn’t take any calls. I then tried reaching out to the guy whom the payment was made and it turned out that he didn’t even know if any console was being sold. Moreover he was asked to withdraw the money from his account on behalf of his friend thus he was indirectly involved in this).

 So kindly beware of such fraudulent people. I hope this helps other people. I have attached all the screenshots of our conversations and their documents.
