 This is an application seeking regular bail in FIR No. 790/2021 dated
 31.12.2021, under Sections 376(2)(n) / 506 / 323 IPC, registered at Police
 Station - Patel Nagar. It is stated by Mr Khan, learned counsel that there are material
 differences in the statement made in the FIR and under Section 164 Cr.P.C
 He states that in the FIR it is stated that the applicant established physical
 relationship with the complainant on the pretext of marriage and thereafter
 blackmailed her and again established physical relationship. He further
 stated that in the statement under Section 164 Cr.P.C the prosecutrix stated
 that the accused sent her a video on her mobile and thereafter under that
 threat established physical relationship. I am unable to agree with the contentions of the learned counsel.
 In the present case, admittedly, the applicant is a married person with Signature Not Verified Digitally Signed byAMIT ARORA Signing Date:20.04.2023 16:49:53 two children. He could not have made physical relationship on the pretext of
 marriage as he was already married. However, as per the status report, there
 are videos found on the telephone of the applicant containing nude videos of
 the complainant. The fact that she was blackmailed on the basis of the
 videos cannot be ruled out. The victim is yet to be examined. The fact of the
 victim being pressurized and threatened by the applicant cannot be ruled out.
 For the said reasons, I am not inclined to entertain the application. The application is dismissed. JASMEET SINGH, J
