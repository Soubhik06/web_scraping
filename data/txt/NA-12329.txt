 respondent. ---------------------------------------- INDERJIT KAUSHIK, PRESIDING JUDICIAL MEMBER:- 2. Facts in brief are that Sh. Balbir Singh, respondent/

complainant (hereinafter called as "the respondent") filed a complaint

under section 12 of the Consumer Protection Act, 1986 (in short, "the

Act") against the appellants, pleading that he purchased a second hand

tractor bearing No.PB-05-N-6331 which was insured vide policy bearing

No.OG-08-1213-1811-00000071 and the said insurance policy was

transferred in the name of the respondent. 3. On the intervening night of 27/28.01.2009, the said tractor

was stolen from the premises of the respondent. The respondent got

registered the FIR with the Police Station, Sadar, Ferozepur vide FIR

No.22 dated 29.01.2009. The respondent approached appellant no.1

and asked it to reimburse the amount of Rs.2,28,001/- i.e. the amount

of insurance policy, but appellant no.1 put off the matter on one pretext

or the other and ultimately, the respondent received a letter on

13.03.2009 vide which the claim was repudiated. In this letter, it was

mentioned that the respondent has not locked the vehicle at the

material time when the tractor was lost and has not taken reasonable

steps to safeguard the vehicle from the loss or damage. The

respondent tried his level best to trace the vehicle, but it could not be

traced and the respondent has not violated the terms and conditions of

the policy. The act and conduct of the appellants caused lot of mental

tension and physical harassment to the respondent and the respondent

is entitled to be compensated to the tune of Rs.50,000/-. 4. It was prayed that the appellants may be directed to

reimburse the amount of insurance to the tune of Rs.2,28,001/- and to

pay Rs.50,000/- as compensation along with interest @ 18% p.a. First Appeal No.1815 of 2009 3 5. In the written version filed on behalf of the appellants,

preliminary objections were taken that the complaint is false and

frivolous. Complicated questions of law and facts are involved and the

District Forum has no jurisdiction. The respondent has not come to the

District Forum with clean hands and has suppressed material facts. The

contract of insurance is a bi-lateral contract which is reduced into

writing and the parties to the contract are bound by the terms and

conditions of the contract. The respondent violated condition no.4 of the

policy. The respondent is estopped by his own act and conduct from

filing the complaint. 6. The respondent is a consumer of the appellants. The

tractor in question was allegedly stolen in the intervening night of

27/28.01.2009 and at that time, the vehicle was insured in the name of

Raj Singh and the insurance was got transferred in the name of the

respondent on 05.02.2009 i.e. after the date of loss. There was no

insurable interest on the date of loss and the respondent is not a

consumer. The tractor in question was being used for commercial

purpose by the Brick Kiln for transportation of bricks and the complaint

deserves dismissal. 7. On merits, it was submitted that as per the terms and

conditions of the policy, it is the duty of the insured to take all

reasonable steps to safeguard the motor bike from the loss or damage,

but the respondent failed to do so. 8. On receipt of the information of the theft of the tractor in

question, Sh. U.S. Kohli of M's Kohli Surveyors was appointed, who

visited the spot and collected the information regarding the theft of the

vehicle and has also recorded the statements of the witnesses. As per

the statements, the key of the tractor was in the ignition and there was First Appeal No.1815 of 2009 4 negligence of the owner and the claim was rightly repudiated. All other

allegations were denied and it was prayed that the complaint may be

dismissed with costs. 9. Parties led evidence in support of their respective

contentions by way of affidavits and documents. 10. After going through the documents and material placed on

file and after hearing the learned counsel for the parties, the learned

District Forum observed that the insurance of the tractor was admitted

and there is no rebuttal regarding theft of the tractor. The only reason,

as mentioned for repudiation of the claim, is that the key of the said

tractor was in the vehicle at the time of theft. Admittedly, the tractor

was parked in the premises of brick kiln owned by the respondent and a

watchman was employed and there was no negligence by keeping the

tractor in the safe custody and the tractor was parked in the premises of

brick kiln where a regular watchman was employed. The respondent

has taken all reasonable steps and the repudiation was illegal. The

complaint was allowed and the appellants were directed to pay Rs.2.28

lacs along with interest @ 6% p.a. from the date of repudiation till

realization and Rs.1,000/- as litigation expenses. 11. Aggrieved by the impugned order dated 16.09.2009, the

appellants have come up in appeal. 12. We have gone through the pleadings of the parties,

perused the record of the learned District Forum, perused the written

arguments submitted on behalf of the appellants and have heard the

arguments advanced by the learned counsel for the respondent. 13. In the written arguments submitted on behalf of the

appellants, it was submitted that the vehicle in question was registered

on 22.01.2009 in the name of the respondent and the policy was First Appeal No.1815 of 2009 5 transferred in the name of the respondent on 05.02.2009 and, as such,

the respondent had no insurable interest at the time of theft in the

intervening night of 27/28-01-2009 and the provisions of GR-17 were

violated. The District Forum has not considered all these facts and the

order passed by the District Forum is liable to be set aside. 14. On the other hand, learned counsel for the respondent has

contended that the vehicle was registered in the name of the

respondent on 22.01.2009 and on the same day, he applied for transfer

of the insurance in his name and as per the provision of GR-17 issued

under India Motor Tariff, within 14 days the registered owner has to

apply for the transfer of the insurance from the date of registration of

the vehicle in his name. In the present case, even the statutory period

of 14 days had not expired. The appellants were negligent and

sluggish in not transferring the insurance policy in the name of the

registered owner till 05.02.2009, but unfortunately for no fault of the

registered owner, the theft took place in the intervening night of

27/28.01.2009 and the repudiation was illegally made. 15. We have considered the respective submissions of the

parties and have thoroughly scanned the entire record and other

material placed on the file. 16. Admitted facts are that the tractor in question was insured

with the appellants and it was stolen during the intervening night on

27/28.01.2009. The said vehicle was registered in the name of the

respondent on 22.01.2009, that means that at the time of theft, the

respondent was registered owner of the tractor in question. The

respondent applied on the same day for transfer of the insurance in his

name, although 14 days' time is provided as per GR1-7 issued under

India Motor Tariff. The appellants did not transfer the insurance policy First Appeal No.1815 of 2009 6 immediately in the name of the respondent. For the fault of the

appellants or any negligence or slackness on their part, the respondent

cannot be blamed for the same. The duty of the respondent was to

apply within 14 days and he has not wasted even a single day and

applied on the same day when the vehicle was registered in his name

i.e. on 22.01.2009. The relevant provisions of GR-17 issued under India

Motor Tariff are reproduced below:- 17. In view of above discussion, the repudiation of the claim by

the appellants was illegal and the respondent is entitled to the

insurance claim and the District Forum has rightly held so. There is no

ground to interfere with the impugned order passed by the District

Forum which is legal and valid. 18. Accordingly, the appeal is dismissed and the impugned

order under appeal dated 16.09.2009 passed by the District Forum is

affirmed and upheld. No order as to costs. 19. The appellants had deposited an amount of Rs.25,000/-

with this Commission at the time of filing of the appeal and another sum

of Rs.90,000/- vide receipt dated 27.05.2010 in compliance of the order First Appeal No.1815 of 2009 7 dated January 28, 2010 passed by this Commission. Both these

amounts with interest accrued thereon, if any, be remitted by the

registry to the respondent/complainant by way of a crossed

cheque/demand draft after the expiry of 45 days under intimation to the

learned District Forum and to the appellants. 20. Remaining amount as per the order of the District Forum

shall be paid by the appellants to the respondent/complainant within 45

days of receipt of copy of the order. 21. The arguments in this appeal were heard on 10.07.2013

and the order was reserved. Now the order be communicated to the

parties. 22. The appeal could not be decided within the stipulated

timeframe due to heavy pendency of court cases. (Inderjit Kaushik)
