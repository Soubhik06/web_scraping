Service Canada will not call you about your SIN being suspended because of potential fraud investigation and there is not a program with them that will call you about it either.

Please tell your elderly relatives this, as well as your friends who might be new immigrants. We receive calls about this type of thing daily, but I can assure you, if you receive a call that says there is a warrant out for your arrest because of thousands or millions of dollars in fraud related to your SIN, just hang up.

If you have caller ID and an answering service with your provider, just let it go to voicemail if you don't recognize the number. If you get frightened or concerned by the message, be sure to follow up, but it's a lot easier to deal with when you aren't listening to a message in real time trying to press 1 to speak with an agent.

* anyone can say they are calling from Service Canada, Department /Minister of Justice, Border Services, Immigration
* If the call comes from a legitimate government number, say 1-800-622-6232,that is the phone number for 1-800 O CANADA, however, they don't do outgoing calls and can't see your files!
* If they don't ask to speak with you by name, e.g. "Hello this is Angela, looking to speak with Fred..." then don't give them the benefit of the doubt
* If the call says there is a warrant out for your arrest and the police will be dispatched, it's not true (why the hell would they warn you)
* All of these calls operate on fear and the hope that you will be in the right (or wrong) mindset to fall for this
* DO NOT just assume that a call is fraudulent because you hear someone with a strong accent, they could be a legitimate employee/ contracted with the government
* DO NOT confirm your SIN with someone over the phone unless you called the legitimate government number, or you can verify they are a legitimate government program
* DO be wary of calls that come from your local area, especially ones that start with your area code and the first three digits of your phone number
* DO be wary of someone who gives you an employee number and a case file, especially if they do not ask to speak with you BY name
* DO be wary of fluent English speakers, there are a lot of scams being run from within the country, betting that you will be more likely to fall for it because "They don't sound like they are coming from a call center in India"
