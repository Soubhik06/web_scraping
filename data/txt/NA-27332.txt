I am posting this because the people who are running this scam will never be caught. Due to a single mistake, my mother risks losing her identity, is in the embarrassing position of having scam emails sent from her account, and has lost access to a decade’s worth of correspondence. She received an e-mail stating that she was beyond her 3GB limit on her Hotmail account and would lose access to her account if she did not provide her email credentials. She naively did so, and her account was compromised. 

Scammers took control of her account, set up a forwarding address to another email but did not change the password (for some reason?). They also deleted and probably downloaded all her email. 
My wife received an email asking for money to be sent to Thailand. She engaged the scammers and they provided the following information:

* Bank Names:BANGKOK BANK 
* A/C NAME: DANIEL ------- ------- 
* A/C NO 1**********
* Address: 333 Silom Rd, Silom, Bang Rak, Bangkok 10500 
* SWIFT code: BKKBTHBK 

Representatives of Bangkok Bank said they could not do anything, and asked me to e-mail the details. I am not hopeful of any negative outcome for the scammers. My mother is in contact with MS support, but there is small possibility of recovering the emails. 

In case you feel your family is not likely to face this, please know that my mother is well educated, and I did not expect her to fall prey to such a simple ruse. 

I would like your advice on the following:

*	Is there any way to recover the deleted e-mails?
*	Is there any authority that we can report this to? (My mother is in India, I reside in USA)
