original post: 
“Motorcycle dealer scam? I am shopping for a motorcycle and I got approved for a loan, but the down is 30% and the interest is 9.99%. My credit isn’t bad, in the mid 7s and I feel like this is a bad deal.

UPDATE: Just found out that the credit app I submitted online wasn’t directly sent to the financial institution, but to their GM who then edited the info and submitted it to their lender. The GM asked for almost 15000, when the bike I am looking at is closer to 10000. 

Wtf” 

I asked for the notice of approval and they sent me a grainy picture of an approval that was unreadable. The address for the credit reporting agency they ran my credit though was incorrect on the document and 
when confronted they brushed it off. When asked why the submitted an application for almost 5k more then the price of the bike and the sales rep told me that “it was standard and they picked a bike that would ‘fit me better’ and that was the reason for the price difference.” 

Oh and here is another thing, they are advertising a bike online for a specific price, the 2019 Indian Scout, starting at 8,999.00 but apparently the “California price” is at least a grand more, even though they are a California dealer. I am really mad about this and I feel like I was almost scammed. Now my credit had a dent in it from the dealer running it and I have no bike.
