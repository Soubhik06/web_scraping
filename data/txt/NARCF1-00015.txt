Liquor store owner here, in light of the recent liquor store scams that have been taking place in Mumbai, I’ve decided to advice people on what on what to do. 

Our liquor store’s google business page was recently hacked, we have no idea how as only I had the passwords and access to those accounts. The scammers have gone ahead and gotten rid of all our photos and uploaded theirs, they have their number put up on our google business page. They’ve so far managed to create a website with our brand name, address and operating hours but with their phone number. 

I have stayed up the whole night sending multiple emails to google, and the cyber crime department and done everything I can, trying to bring it to their notice as we’re not the only liquor store this has happened to, as there are multiple stores across Mumbai that this has happened to. We have also submitted a complaint with the cyber crime department. 

So far 4 of our customers have been scammed of 15,000-20,000 rupees. 

I would like to make it clear to NEVER EVER PAY IN ADVANCE USING A CREDIT/DEBIT CARD while ordering online or over the phone and NEVER GIVE YOUR CARD DETAILS TO ANYONE OVER THE PHONE. Liquor stores NEVER ask for your card details, not in person nor over the phone, we literally don’t have the technology to even do this. We have very strict protocols laid out by the excise department, which we have to follow. I have time and time repeated this across other platforms but still somehow people end up giving their card details and getting scammed and the liquor store in question is doubted. 

Please be careful and order from your local liquor store by getting their number in person. 

Never drink &amp; drive and always drink responsibly!
