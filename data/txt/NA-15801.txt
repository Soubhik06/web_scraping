--- POST TEXT ---

Intimate relationships might result in the exchange or possession of explicit images with your partner. After separation, a looming threat of leaking such images is impending.

Suppose your partner or ex-partner is threatening to leak or has leaked your private pictures or videos to an external entity, either a person or a website. In that case, you can do the following to escalate action:

1. If your images or videos have been leaked on a website, write an e-mail to the website administrator mentioning that the upload of such media was non-consensual and ask for immediate removal.
2. If the same have been leaked to a social media platform such as Discord or Reddit, contact the platform moderators via e-mail or other means and seek immediate removal.
3. Go to [cybercime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in/), and file a cybercrime complaint against the concerned individual, referencing all possible evidence you might possess linking them to the leak.
4. Write a complaint to the National Commission for Women (**NCW**), detailing your plight and referencing all possible evidence.

The cybercrime complaint will escalate the matter from a criminal law perspective. In contrast, the NCW complaint will help in garnering support and creating pressure on the concerned individual(s) involved in the leak.
