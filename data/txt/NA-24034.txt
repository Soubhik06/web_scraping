Yesterday I was scrolling Instagram and found an ad that was too good to be true. 4kg dry fruits for just 399 rs. 

This was an sponsored ad.

Upon further investigation I found that the Instagram page was imitating Dmart and the "shop now" button redirected the user to a fake website.

The website used the same logo, and layout as Dmart's official ecommerce website. The webpage did not have any "disclaimer", " about us" , "contact us" etc.

I tried reporting on Instagram but their report feature sucks ass.

How do we report such scam websites?
