Hello /r/India,

I had posted here earlier [asking for support and help](https://np.reddit.com/r/india/comments/7jk95z/speakforme_ask_your_mps_to_raise_your_concerns/?utm_content=title&amp;utm_medium=user&amp;utm_source=reddit&amp;utm_name=frontpage) for the #SpeakForMe campaign. So first off, thank you for emailing your MPs, mobile operators and banks with your grievances regarding implementation of Aadhaar. We, the humans behind #SpeakForMe, are happy to inform you that there has been significant activity in Parliament as a result of your participation. 

- A total of 23 MPs have raised concerns regarding Aadhaar in Parliament in the current Winter Session

Here’s an update on what all has happened:

#Answers Given

Based on the questions asked by various representatives, the Government has given a few interesting responses (including the Twitter handles of respective ministries in case you want to tweet to them/seek clarifications):

- Regarding distribution of Welfare, the Social Justice Ministry has said, “Under the provisions of Aadhaar Act, 2016, **Aadhaar is not mandatory** for a beneficiary to avail subsidies, benefits or services under the said schemes. If an Aadhaar number has not been assigned to an individual, **the individual shall be offered alternate and viable means for identification for delivery of benefits**.”(https://twitter.com/MSJEGOI)

- Regarding Data Leaks, the Electronics and Information Technology Ministry has said, “It was found that **approximately 210 websites of Central Government &amp; State Government departments including educational institutes, were displaying the list of beneficiaries along with their name, address, other details and Aadhaar numbers** for information of general public. The concerned departments were instructed to remove the Aadhaar and other personal data from the said websites.”(https://twitter.com/GoI_MeitY)

- Regarding number of schemes where Aadhaar needs to be linked, Electronics and Information Technology Ministry said, “395 schemes from 56 Ministries/ Department have been onboarded on Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) platform. Of these schemes, Aadhaar notifications under Section 7 of the **Aadhaar Act have been issued in respect of 227 schemes till date.**(https://twitter.com/GoI_MeitY)
