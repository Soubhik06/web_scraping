For the first time in my life I made a reservation through booking com. Then a message inside booking (and an e-mail), came from the hotel, saying that I have to send the payment to confirm my reservation. First of all the money did not check out, and the formating of the e-mail seemed odd, besides that I had to go to a different website. I clicked on the link, where my personal information was already there, but my e-mail was wrong. I tried to write into a chat window, but of course no answer came. Everything, like buttons on the site worked, and it really made it feel official. For the payment I only had one choice, where I had to go to a paywall site. I did not go through with it, cause I wasn't sure and I just realized that on booking, it said I do not have to pay anything until aug 06. I very much think it is a scam, and if it is, it looks very real, and its not like the average indian one. I got the message from official booking address, there was a very well made website too, and they got my personal info very closely. I hope you can help me out, did any of you ever made a reservation payment like this, or is this surely a scam?
