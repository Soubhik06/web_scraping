In November 2023, in Bangalore, we were a group of 10 to 12 people working together in the same company. Among us was a colleague who told us his father was in the ICU for heart surgery and that he needed urgent money to cover the operation costs. We all trusted him, as he had been working with us for a year. Despite being freshers with modest salaries, we decided to help him by contributing what we could, using our credit cards and savings. He assured us that he would take care of the EMIs.

A month later, he cleared our credit card dues as promised and then asked for more money, explaining that the total amount needed for his father’s treatment was high. Again, we contributed to help him.

However, in late November 2024, he left for his hometown in Bihar, saying his grandmother was seriously ill. It was the end of the month, and our credit card due dates were approaching, so we tried contacting him for payment. For three days, he was unreachable, not answering our calls, which made us panic. We started discussing the situation amongst ourselves and soon realized that he had taken a total of 18 lakhs from all of us, citing his father’s health issues. He had even borrowed personal loans of 8 lakhs from some of his close friends and another 4 lakhs in savings from another colleague. I personally lent him 64k through my credit card. The total came to 18 lakhs.

When we contacted his family, we discovered he was actually attending his cousin’s wedding, and everything he told us about his father’s condition was a lie. After a month of phone calls, arguments with his sister, brother-in-law, and father, they finally agreed that he would repay us in small monthly installments as we thought of police complaint as per request done by his sitser saying if you take legal action and if he lost his job then you may not get your money back as he is only one earning in his family and his family dont know what he has done with that money. We had already converted our credit card bills into EMIs and cleared the dues. He did send money for five months, but now, almost a year later, he has stopped paying, and his family is no longer responding to us.

One of our colleagues, who gave him a personal loan, lost his job during the layoffs and is now struggling with depression. When we dug into his past, we found out he had been pulling similar scams since his college days.

Now, we’re in a tough spot. The problem isn’t just about the money anymore —it’s about the scam he will likely continue to run on others in the future.
