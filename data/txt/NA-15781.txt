Hello r/india

I see a lot of people fall for blackmailing scams where they get an e-mail saying they have “private” videos of yours from your webcam, and you need to send out the money to get them to delete the video.

Last week I got this [e-mail](https://ibb.co/jrYYq7K) saying the similar thing.

Foremost, don't panic and think. Did you do anything in front of your PC, which the hacker is claiming? If yes, then verify the claims he has mentioned like email receiving date, the things he mentioned, check your computer like do a scan and see if you have unusual high activity in task manager. If you still feel paranoid, PLEASE contact cyber police and log the complaint online on their [website](https://cybercrime.gov.in/).

I am confident that the majority of these claims are fake. They want to scare you, make you panic and get the money.

I received the email which had one of my usernames saying that they know my username, so they have access to my PC. Let's debunk their claim-

See, username/email and passwords get leaked online almost every day. There are breaches all the time. And most people are not very educated about their privacy, so they use the same email everywhere and share that email on each platform.

Hackers get the email and password from these breaches and the various things like brute forcing, sending out fake emails, etc.

My username was from Wattpad website, if you google about Wattpad you'll see that they had a huge data breach back in 2020. So, my username is easily available.

So, they took my username and email from that breach and emailed me.

Secondly, I don't have a webcam on my laptop.

Third, I don't do the things they have mentioned.

Clearly, they wanted me to get feared and send them money.

They have a BTC address in that email and if you go to blockchain and see the transaction history then you'll see they have received two [payments](https://ibb.co/dt0nzcK) worth around 1300 USD and if you go deep then you will see the whole chain of scam going on (for ex just go to the address where this hacker sent the money he received).

JUST BE SAFE. DON'T PANIC. DON'T USE SAME USERNAME AND EMAIL EVERYWHERE.
