There’s an ongoing scam in Hyderabad involving fake vape shops listed on online maps. Recently, I was searching for a vape refill and found a shop that looked legitimate. I visited the location, but there was no store there. I called the number listed in the store photo, and the person claimed the shop was temporarily closed but offered home delivery. He asked for payment in advance, which seemed normal for such services, so I paid via a scanner.

Later, he sent me a WhatsApp number of a “delivery agent” who then asked for a security slip from the shop owner. The owner demanded ₹1,999 for a “computer-generated” slip, promising it would be refunded. When I refused to pay, both numbers blocked me.

I then noticed several similar fake listings, all showing shop images with overlaid phone numbers—a clear pattern of fraud.(search for vape shops near me y’all will be shocked to the number of same shops)
