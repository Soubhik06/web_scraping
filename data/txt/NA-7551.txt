Heard counsel for the parties. This is second bail application filed by the applicant,
who has been arrested in connection with Crime No.5/2014
registered with S.T.F. Police Station, Bhopal - commonly
known as VYAPAM examination scam cases - for the offence
punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 and 120-B IPC and also under Section 3 (D) 1 and 2 / 4 of the M.P.
Manyata Prapt Pariksha Adhiniyam, 1937. The role ascribed to the applicant, by the prosecution, is
that he acted as impersonator for the original candidate
Lokendra Dhakad. It is not in dispute that Lokendra Dhakad
has already been released on bail. Even one middleman -
Manu Singh Patel has been released on bail. Charge-sheet has
already been filed against the applicant on 09.06.2014. Counsel for the State, on instructions, submits that
further presence of the applicant is not required as the
investigation against the applicant is complete as of now.
Further, the applicant through counsel undertakes that as and
when his presence is required by the Investigating Officer, he
shall make himself available and also punctually attend before
the Trial Court during the trial and extend full cooperation for
 conduct of free and fair trial. Moreover, the applicant has not
been named as accused in any other offence of similar type. Since the applicant is willing to abide by all the strict
conditions to be imposed by this Court for grant of bail, in the
interest of justice, this application in connection with Crime
No.5/2014 is allowed on following strict conditions: The bail application stands disposed of accordingly. Certified copy as per rules.
