Why do we call it petrol pump when they also give diesel? LXL

Anyways, I have been thinking a lot and through my intense research I have found that many pumps in India are robbing us. 

Now I am not talking about the "you ask for 500Rs. Fuel and he gives you 100 and then says sorry and then gives you only 400 and you think he'd zeroed it."

I am talking about the quantity you actually get. i.e when you fill 1litre of fuel do you really get 1litre of it?

This conundrum gave me many sleepless nights. I couldn't let my people get cheated in such a bastardly manner.

So I started hanging out near petrol pumps, in my observations, I saw that the average Indian 2wheel commuter never put more than 100-200Rs. of fuel in his bike(sub 100cc mileage king bike/scooty). The average car owner(alto/swift) most of the time never put more than 1000Rs. of petrol. So there's a definite rhythm in the way the average Indian puts fuel in his personal car!

Rental taxi's or those that make leaving ferrying public usually showed up in the mornings and filled it up to the brim. But they didn't go to just any pump but the same one usually.

By now, I was no where near any sort of answer. But then I noticed the trucks/lorries or the Punjab Body trucks that we call them.

Thing is, these trucks have a gigantic fuel tank right next to the body. But they never put fuel in it directly. Usually they would park near or inside the petrol pump(not on the pump itself) and put fuel in the jerrican of 5-10 litre capacity. Now that got me thinking why? Why the extra effort. I saw that some of the drivers then used the fuel from the jerrican and put it in the trucks tank. But why? Not all did this, but there was a definite pattern again. 

One fine day I asked a nice Sikh guy while I too was putting fuel in my Rodeo, about why he does that? He laughed and said that can't trust them! I was like what? There's a meter and it reads out completely what's being delivered. The Sikh guy then told, using the jerrican he can view the exact amount of fuel being pumped. If he says three litre, he can see if he got three litre by just looking into it, but can't see so if he put fuel in the trucks tank.

He explained to me that pumps where they don't have that calculator type thingy, where they type the amount required are even more dangerous. They put fuel by looking into the meter, you see, pulling that nozzle's valve even at **half pressure** will read the same output delivered. And that's how they cheat. Normally you'd expect the guy to press that valve all the way but halfway through, they stop doing so and apply only half pressure. Even at half pressure, the read outs the same i.e - at full pressure 5litres of fuel pumped, then at half pressure too 5litres of fuel pumped but (5l virtually in the meter)

This all gets a bit confusing, but stick with me, the Sikh guy told me because of this trick he is reluctant to even trust the calculator type petrol pumps too. So using the jerrican, he can see for himself.

I needed proof, but unless I was a cop, I wasn't going to get any. Then one day I was putting 100Rs fuel in my rodeo and noticed that the fuel coming out of the nozzle isn't that high pressure. I asked the guy why pressure so low? He laughed and said, "if it's too much, the petrol will just blast out because of force." The pressure wasn't much, about the same if you turned a litre bottle of Bisleri upside down. So I did so, in doing so, I used a stop watch to measure the how much time it took the water to go out the bottle? It was on average 9secs for one litre of water to drain upside down from the bottle. 

I know a friend/hippie who goes around wild on his Royal Enfield Thunderbullet something, he puts shit loads of fuel in his tank when he hits reserve. So one day I hitched a ride with him, praying he does not hit a bus the way he drove. And volla, he hit reserve, he drove 1more kms till he found an HP pump and went right into it. I told him I will pay 200Rs. for his fuel if he let me measure it later. He said, his house was just 6kms away, no need for so much fuel, he'll put full tank later now he just wants to put a 100. I insisted, he said o.k

I got down from the bike, gave 500rs, got 300 back and saw for myself by leaning on top of the bikes fuel tank the fuel of 200Rs. Being pumped. The meter read 3.3L delivered. Hmm.

We sat and the hippie rode his bike to his house at an average speed of 50kmph, not much. Once I got to his house, his mother called us for some tea, but I told him to go get a jerrican, he gave me a 2L empty Pepsi bottle instead (fuckin hipster), I asked him for another bottle and got a 600ml coca cola . I told him, that I will extract 2L+600ml from the tank and at least 400ml of fuel should be there excluding the reserve capacity of 2.5L of his bike. He went bat shit crazy but ultimately he agreed. Now, I took out 2L of petrol from his bike in the Pepsi bottle and 600ml in the coca cola bottle.

So now, there should be 2.5L(reserve capacity of bike) + 400ml from the petrol pump. Excluding the .3litre as we rode 6km.

I ask him to go for a ride till we hit reserve again. He behaved like a baby but agreed, we went out of his drive way, out of his colony, and as we about to enter the main road, the bike coughed. Indicating it hit reserve. My hippie friend said, "behenchod" and turned the bike back to his house. We had barely ridden the bike for half a km and we hit reserve how? 400ml= 1/2km?

Not possible! I put the 2.6L fuel back in his bike, ate lunch and left. 

So, we put 3.3litre of fuel from HP, took out 2.6L of fuel, used .3L of fuel. So where the fuck did the 400ml of fuel go? Bikes mileage is around 40 per litre.

The answer is we never got the 400ml fuel in the first place.

TL;DR :- Cheated for quantity not quality. Is organised like organised crime. I bet it's like coal scam/Vyapam scam. Don't wanna proceed further, may get murdered.
