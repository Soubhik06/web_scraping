I just received call from a mobile number claiming to be from FedEx agent Karan Sharma [from a normal mobile number]. The caller identified me by name and informed me that my ID (Aadhar and Pan Number) was used to register a parcel from Mumbai to Sydney, Australia which seemed to have illegal items (3 passports, 4 SIM cards, 4 atm cards &amp; 2 books). He gave detailed address of the pick-up point at Mumbai ( I haven’t been to Mumbai in over half a year) and also the receiver address (w/ name and Australian contact number). He asked me to register a complaint with Sion Police Station, Mumbai and connected the call (conference) to Sub Inspector (gave me his name also) who explained to me that this could even be connected to terrorist activities. I was worried, collected the SI’s contact who asked me to reach SION station as soon as possible and file a complaint.

A bit later called the SI again and the number was switched off, so I called Sion police station from the listed contact on Google Maps. The (apparent) cop who picked up the call told me that there is no SI by the name I had at the station. He was pretty pissed to even have a convo and informed that he’s been receiving calls regarding the same since morning. I wasn’t sure I was even talking to a cop. 

Now I am paranoid and feel that every call I have made and received are frauds. Help.
