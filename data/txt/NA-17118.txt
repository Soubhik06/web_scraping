I recently visited my cousin who joined as an assistant professor at NISER bhubaneshwar. I was shocked to hear about the extent of fraudulent merchandize being sold at grossly overpriced rates at the Goverment e-marketplace which is used by the central goverment universities as a public procurement platform. As a new faculty my brother is trying to setup his lab and is facing immense diffculty in getting quality equipment. Even simple electronics like computer hardware is faulty and becomes malfunctional after a month or so of use. And the registered companies are selling the electronics under strange names which you haven’t even heard of for standard hardware like monitors, desktop graphics cards etc. Everything comes with the *made in india* logo making an utter mockery of it. What is going on?!! My brother says a masters student just joined his lab and till now they haven’t been able to arrange a desktop workstation for his thesis work because of the utterly faulty stuff being sold at GeM. He works in a field which requires processing of tons of data so a computer is the most fundamental requirement for any research at all. 

If this is the scenario in one of the so called top institutes in the country, what could be the situation in state universities??
