Why is the viraat overlord happy that street hawkers are selling pakoras for ₹200 a day?

Why has the situation in Kashmir deteriorated to such an extent under Modi, even with the ruling party in J&amp;K allied to the BJP? 

Why has our relationship with China (Doklam, trade, etc), Nepal (pro-China stance till now, speculation after ), Sri Lanka (fishermen arrests), Pakistan (need I even say about the weekly ceasefire violations?) and even fucking Maldives (Modi named Anti-Muslim in state newspapers) deteriorated to such an extent? 

Why has feku not addressed a single press conference since taking power?

And last but not the least, why hasn't he given one, single, unbiased and direct interview with a respected and credible journalist/publication since assuming power without running away like a whipped cur?
