So, recently got to know that BMC has a website Bhubaneswar.me. Sharing my experience of using it.

Instead of complaining on Twitter, I shared one concern and after resolving the ticket from their side, all they could send as "proof" was the pdf file icon. To top it off one OAS officer actually approved all it to close the ticket. In reality some road construction work has started (not sure if it was because of the grievance or general cleanup that they are doing for the hockey World Cup) but that's only a part of what I had added in my grievance.

So, the next time they show off that they solved this many tickets on twitter, you can be sure there are tickets that they have obviously faked or includes tickets that do not deserve to be closed whatsoever.

All this while major problems like encroachments, incomplete projects are still haunting the city along with poor drainage because of waterways getting clogged.
