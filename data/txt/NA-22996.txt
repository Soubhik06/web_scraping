Last November, I received a very good job offer from a large multinational corporation, which was cancelled in two weeks for some ridiculous reason. I need to know what legal steps I can take under Indian law. The following is a more detailed explanation: 

* The onboarding process began, and I submitted some of my government documents and salary information.
* The cancellation was not made by the offer's authorised signatory, but by a subordinate of the authorised signatory. 
* I requested the actual reason via email but received no response. 
* I filed a whistleblower complaint, but the investigation team provided no practical solution.
* The investigation team promised me confidentiality on the matter, but they leaked information to some of the company's employees.
* There is no clause in the offer stating that the company has the final right to cancel the offer; however, the termination requires the signature and prior approval of both parties.
* I asked the company to remove my data from the database after not receiving a practical solution, but I can still see it on the Onboarding portal.

Although it had no effect on my current earnings, I did miss out on an opportunity to earn more.
