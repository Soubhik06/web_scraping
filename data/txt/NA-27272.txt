I need advice after getting entangled in what seems to be a sophisticated scam via WhatsApp and Telegram. It began with a message on WhatsApp, offering me a chance to earn money by completing simple tasks. After I completed tasks like liking products on Flipkart and sharing screenshots, they transferred a small sum to me via UPI.

Subsequently, I was invited to join a Telegram group, which posted similar tasks involving investments and cryptocurrency trades. Initially, the returns seemed real, with a small profit of Rs 300. However, as I got more involved, the nature of the tasks changed, requiring larger investments under the guise of group tasks.

After a supposed mistake during one of these tasks, the group administrators said they could not issue refunds unless I paid Rs 8 lakhs to correct my 'credit score.' When I contested, they requested my bank details for a refund. Unfortunately, I mistyped my bank account number, and now they're demanding Rs 7.5 lakhs to 'unfreeze' my funds and are asking for my Aadhar card for 'verification.'

I was given a Telegram user ID and a code to contact someone for the payments. After interacting with this person and following through on their instructions, the demands for money began.

They provided me with a certificate for GI TECHNOLOGY PRIVATE LIMITED as proof of their legitimacy. However, after attempting to verify it on the Startup India website with the provided certificate number, no records were found. To date, I've been defrauded of Rs 7,63,300, and they're pressing for even more.
