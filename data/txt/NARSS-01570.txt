--- POST TEXT ---

Aadhaar data of over 80 crore Indians has been put on sale in dark web by a hacker. The data includes our addresses, phone numbers, names, etc. There are high chances of identity theft and phishing. This is not a joke, some crime could use your identity to do crimes in India and the blame could come on you. Just imagine what would happen if your data comes into hands of a terrorist/sleeper cell. Blocking your Aadhaar would make it unusable. Till the government brings a solution, we should keep our Aadhaars blocked.
