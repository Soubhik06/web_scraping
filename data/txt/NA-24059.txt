I took a house on rent via a broker. Here's the chronology of the events

1. House is finalized. 
2. Broker asks to transfer ₹10K as token amount to owner.
3. Broker tells to submit the brokerage too. Naively, I did it because it was my first time. 
4. Owner meets me in the evening. A little back and forth on the deposit and finalized.
5. On the say of agreement, I enquire with the owner about him receiving the ₹10K from (2). Owner says he didn't receive it. 
6. Turns out the broker made us send the amount in (2) to someone else. He accepted it on call and says that it'll be returned when the owner pays the brokerage too. 
7. Owner is hell bent on not paying any brokerage. And rightly so because the tenant has already paid the brokerage. 
8. Broker is refusing paying back the ₹10K until the other brokerage is paid.
9. Broker has not been answering my/owner's call since yesterday. No replies on WhatsApp either but messages are being delivered.
10. Turns out, he lied about his name too. Owner has a different name, and he told me a different name. 

Now, I think I have the following options

1. Let go of the ₹10K as a lesson.
2. Approach the police and have file a complaint for fraud. 
3. May be send a legal notice? But I don't see this happening because I have nothing other than his phone number. I can dig up my dadhcam footage and check his vehicle number but I won't be surprised if it's someone else's. 
4. File a complaint against the person who received the money and approach the bank to reverse the transaction.

The only reasons I'm trying to avoid approaching police is because it can be time consuming and, given how a lot of people are aggressive, I'm afraid of him paying under the pressure and then showing up at the rented place and creating a mess.
