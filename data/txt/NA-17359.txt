I got a message saying:

Dear Paytm Customer your 
KYC has been suspended. 
Paytm office PH 8207255850
Call Immediately. Your account 
will block with in 24hours Thank you Paytm Team

If you call then the will ask you to download TeamViewer and take control of your phone to perform transactions. I remember reading about how someone else fell for a similar scam and lost a bunch of money in a UPI transaction that was made within seconds.

Also it was a dead giveaway when I called them out on it and they started insulting me. XD

I'm sure if there is any actual issue, the PayTM people will send you an email and give you plenty of time to comply.

These kinds of panic inducing messages are textbook scam stuff. Be careful everyone !
