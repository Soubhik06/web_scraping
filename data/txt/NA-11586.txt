Heard learned counsel for the parties. Petitioner is accused in connection with Ranchi CBI ACB Case No.RC 01

 (A)/2010-R registered under Sections 120-B , 420 , 467 , 468 & 471 of the Indian Penal

 Code and Sections 13(2) r/w 13(1)(d) of the Prevention of Corruption Act which is

 pending in the Court of Sri N.N. Singh, learned Spl. Judge-1 C.B.I./A.C.B., Ranchi. It reveals from the First Information Report that firm M/s Kalawati

 Construction (P) Ltd., Chinia More, Garhwa was given a contract to construct road. It

 was detected that the concerned firm, of which the petitioner happens to be the

 Director, has furnished fake invoices against purchase of bitumen and received

 payment against inferior construction of road. The aforesaid 11 invoices relates to

 224.2548 M.T. of bitumen worth Rs.55,41,979/-. This case has been instituted after

 instruction given by the High Court in a P.I.L.

 It is submitted that the petitioner happens to be the Director of said M/s

 Kalawati Construction (P) Ltd. and he has not been personally benefited with

 production of any such invoice. Learned counsel appearing for the C.B.I. has vehemently opposed the prayer

 for bail and submitted that no accused till the date has been granted anticipatory bail

 in such bitumen scam cases. It is apparent that 11 invoices against purchase of bitumen to the tune of

 Rs.55,41,979/-, furnished by the said firm have been found fake. Considering aforesaid aspects of the matter, I am not inclined to grant the

 petitioner, above named, anticipatory bail. Accordingly, this anticipatory bail application stands rejected. Petitioner is

 directed to surrender in the court below within a fortnight for seeking regular bail

 which will be considered by the Court below without being prejudiced with this order. (D.N. Upadhyay, J)
