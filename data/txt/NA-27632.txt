I just want to write this to cope with the fact that I got scammed by expecting that easy money exists. Maybe it does if you know how to trade or have the knowledge to navigate it. It’s better to rely on myself for trading than to put my trust in others and just pay them.

What initially hooked me into this scam was when I was messaged on Viber. I thought it was just a job application, but I was confused when they made it seem so easy. They tasked me with following a marketing app called Shopee, paying me 80 pesos for each follow. The pattern was: three tasks, then a merchant task with 22 tasks daily. The merchant tasks doubled my pay: 40 pesos for the first task, 80 pesos for the second, and 160 pesos for the third. Without or skipping the merchant tasks would deduct the salary to 50%

To continue, I had to keep paying for these merchant tasks, depending on the tier I was in. As a beginner, I needed to pay 1,200 pesos for each task, but they promised 30% returns, so I'd get 1,500 pesos back (note: 1 point = 1 peso, and I’m from the Philippines). My first merchant task was successful i had to logged in to their trading system etc it's overcomplicatd, and I was so excited that I made 1,000 pesos in a day with such simple tasks. But then, the bad news hit. The receptionist told me that the beginner tier was a one-time option, and I could only keep progressing if I paid for the 3,500 pesos option.

I thought to myself that if I kept paying for the 3,500 pesos option, I’d get 4,500 pesos back with that 30% return. Daily, there were five merchant tasks available. If I kept paying, I’d eventually reach 7,500 pesos in a day. But I didn’t have enough money, so I planned to borrow from people who offered 30-40% cashback on deposits. Looking back, I realize these were probably bots, something I only learned after the fact.

Then, one person messaged me, claiming they could help if I wanted money for the 3,500 pesos deposit. They invited me to another investment, run by an Indian owner. I checked the reviews and thought it was legitimate. I deposited the money I’d made from the previous scam, including my own savings and money I should have used for college. I told my dad I’d lost my wallet. I threw all my money into this gamble, convinced by the owner that I could make a profit from the trading platform, which even showed my name on an account. I was told that to withdraw, I’d need to make another payment.

"All of this motivation came from thinking I could make money, since I had recently made some."

I was incredibly ignorant. How could one person organize all of this? Using fake photos and impersonating different personalities, each with different country codes? Yes, there were some winners, subscribers, and many channels—documents and photos that looked legitimate but couldn't be verified. It turns out, this was a whole organization. I hope they rot in hell. The winners are probably the 1-10% in out of a million subscribers, So there are winners and uses those winners to hooked the masses telling that investment platform is legit by their reviews.

I’m just a naïve 20-year-old. But I’ve learned an invaluable lesson: never invest in this kind of scam. I'm glad I learned this now, before it was too late. If I had kept going, I might have lost everything. And my future career is marine officer in shipping companies, Such a waste giving money or getting scammed to these lowlife rat creatures.
