Went to fill diesel in my car yesterday(tier 2 city in Khandesh region of Maharashtra). I said auto cut. He started filling and stops in between, saying something wrong with the sensor where amount was ₹421. He then went near machine pretending to start it again and started to fill the fuel again and stopped at 3870. 

And while paying he calculated final amount as 3870+421.
I said I was looking at the meter and it did not start from zero. 
He was dening that it cannot happen, machine always starts from zero. 

I said check the camera and print the last receipt, then he said just pray that I should be paying the remaining amount from my salary. 

I gave him my phone number and said call me I ll come but first I will check the cameras, then he let it slip and I didnt receive any call till now.

I dont know if this is a new or old scam but this is the first time happened with me in my life.

PS: I saw the same scam in YouTube video couple of days ago so it clicked me just at the right time.
