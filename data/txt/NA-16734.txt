--- POST TEXT ---

Why do we like to make excuses when faced with genuine questions and criticisms, be it culturally, economically, socially, or politically? My post isn't politically or religiously motivated so treat it as such.

If we want to talk about inflation in India, we are hit with "Pakistan is struggling worse than us. A dozen eggs cost 400rs there."

If someone asks for a rape case justice in, say, UP or Rajasthan, we are hit with "Jharkhand or West Bengal or Bihar has XYZ number of rape cases under ABC party 5years ago, why not talk about it"

If we want to talk about a scam or money laundering case, people say "So what? This and this minister scammed 4000 crores under this and this party"

If we want to talk about one religion being treated unfairly, people say "They did this and this to us 500 years ago so we should retaliate"

Basically, as I saw in an internet clip, our mentality has become "Look at them, their underwear has 4 tears (holes) in them, ours only have 3. We should be more concerned about their underwear. Why should we care about our modesty?"

The examples are endless. My question is, when did we start following this culture of excuses?

If we want to improve a situation, shouldn't we just try to hold people accountable and do something for the better? Why, instead of trying to solve a problem, do we try to bury it under rage and excuses? It has gotten so bad that you can't even have a civil discussion without someone raging out. Even on insta and reddit, if someone doesn't agree with your opinions, they straight up start abusing you.

What is wrong with us?
