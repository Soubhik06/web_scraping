Hello everyone, Developers across India are working towards making data more accessible. It involved updating live data, visualizing stats and open-sourcing APIs. India's IT sector employees count is around 3 Million. This is a very small population when compared to the whole of India. Developers are literally striving hard to educate people through articles, blogs, and websites, etc. However, Indians didn't take it seriously.

When our Prime Minister announced Jantha Curfew, everyone followed it. I am totally surprised by the outreach possible by Television. I feel that the developers are catering posts to the already informed individuals and that they are addressing a very narrow audience. Although the rumors "Clapping kills Virus" and "Duration of Corona is 12 hours" are totally outrageous and fake, they motivated people to abide by the Jantha Curfew. Let me be honest, those fake news have reached my family group as well.

We **should target** the masses. Let us try to make people aware of information like [this](https://medium.com/@tomaspueyo/coronavirus-act-today-or-people-will-die-f4d3d9cd99ca). Please suggest your ideas below. Let's soon start a Slack/Telegram group, depending on the participation levels. 

Giving a few examples:

1. Breaking down articles into a storytelling format (Visual format helps a lot).
2. Translating informative videos into regional languages (Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu etc).
3. Creating informative viral content targetting everyone who's a part of Fake News chain. Let's use the Fake News chain for social good.
