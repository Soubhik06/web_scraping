Today our secretary received a call from someone saying they were calling me back. She didn't know any better and passed the call though to my desk phone. When I get calls at work I assume they are clients so I answered how I would any other call, "Hello, \*My company name\* this is \*First name\* speaking." The person on the other end responded in a thick Indian accent "\*My first name and last name.\*"

I was still in professional mode and replied "Yes." They then hung up. The instant they hung up I knew it had to be someone phishing for information or some sort of scam. I feel uneasy knowing a scammer has confirmed my name and place of work and possibly recorded my voice saying yes.

My questions: 

How worried I should be? 

What can be done with this information?

Are there any accounts I should keep an eye on? (bank, credit card, etc.)

 
Has anyone else experienced this?

I know I will be answering the phone differently from now on.
