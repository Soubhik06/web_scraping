I’ve been getting about two calls a day. I’m from Rhode Island and they always have a local area code. When I answer, it is a robocall saying I’ve overpaid my electric bill and they are offering a refund. I can press 1 to initiate the process. When I do this, I get connected to someone in India (with a heavy accent but usually with a name like Robert or Nancy). Usually, I’m very nice when they ask how I am doing. Then, they ask me if I am the one that pays the bill. I say yes, then they ask me if I have a bill handy. I say I do, and at this point, sometimes they hang up. Sometimes, they ask me for my zip code, but they usually hang up before they even ask for any sensitive info. Yesterday, “Tom” asked me if I had my bill with me, and when I said yes, he told me to roll it up and stick it up my butt. So weird! 

Any idea what this scam is and why they aren’t very good?
