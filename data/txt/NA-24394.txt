So, this just happened. I got a call from a mobile number. When I received the call, it was an AVR, informing me about an international courier. Now, I do get one or two but it's extremely rare. It asks me to press 1 for more information. The call is now transferred to a guy who informs me that it's the help centre of FedEx. I say ok. What is this about? He asks if I am expecting any parcel. I say no, and then he asks my name which i give. He then tells me that he is verifying and then mentions that I have sent a parcel out on which he says he has important information. I now realise this is a scam and I press the record call button. I guess he heard the automatic voice informing that the call is bwing recorded. He promptly abused me and disconnected. 
I can share the number from which I got the call in case it helps.
Tl;Dr : AVR call pretending to be FedEx tried to scam.
