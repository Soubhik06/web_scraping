Chatgpt-

Abe property bechna hai ya circus ka ticket? Sun, seedha kaam hai lekin paperwork mein dimag ka dahi ho sakta hai. Tere jaise “lovable idiot” ke liye step-by-step breakdown de raha hoon, samajh le warna koi fraud tera flat bhi bech dega bina bataye.

Step-by-Step, Ghanta-Busting Guide:

1. Property ke Documents Ready Kar:

Sale Deed (pichla wala)

Khata certificate/Khata extract (local authority se)

Encumbrance certificate (yeh dikhata hai property pe loan-shoan to nahi)

Property tax receipts (saare bhare hone chahiye)

Occupancy certificate (builder se, if flat is new)

NOC from society or builder (agar society hai to)

2. Buyer Dhoond:

Broker laga, online daal (99acres, MagicBricks, NoBroker)

Jo bhi kare, bina likhit agreement ke chomu buyer pe bharosa mat kar

3. Price Finalize Kar:

Market rate dekh le

Broker ki baat pe mat nach, thoda research kar

Black money lene ka sapna chhod de, income tax waale ka danda lagega

4. Agreement to Sell Bana:

Advance le, likhit mein sab likhwa (token amount, time frame, legal clauses)

Stamp paper pe likhwa, lawyer se check karwa (bina lawyer ke chalega to pit jayega)

5. Sale Deed Execute Kar:

Sub-registrar office mein dono party jao

Buyer stamp duty aur registration charges bharega

Tujhe PAN card, address proof, photos leke jaana hoga

6. Possession De &amp; Hawa Hoja:

Hand over keys, electricity bill, water bill, etc.

Possession letter de (ek kaam properly kar le)

7. Income Tax ka Khatta Sambhal:

Agar capital gain hua to tax bharega

2 saal se zyada rakha to long-term capital gain, nahi to short-term
