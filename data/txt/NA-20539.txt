So, a few days ago, I saw an old flash drive for sale and it was priced at 8k. So I messaged the seller (krishnascollection) to know if it were a typo and I'm interested in buying it if they bring down the price. They replied that it's pricey because it's a collector's item and said they'll bring down the price to 4500. Which is ridiculous because we could buy a 1TB hard disk for that price.

http://imgur.com/8xbTE7o
http://imgur.com/kfrHXQn

So I looked at their other products and it was all highly priced. 10k+ for an ordinary SanDisk Cruzer blade (still in production) and 10k for a card reader? Then I realized it was a scam. They are offering to sell the products on EMI. The initial payment in the 12 month installment is close to what that product actually cost. maybe unsuspecting people buy it without realizing it and loose all the money? Either way, what they are doing is illegal.

http://imgur.com/D7JyX7R
http://imgur.com/eaoh548

So being the concerned shopper i am, I emailed them saying that selling at prices above MRP is illegal and that I will approach the consumer court if they don't bring down the prices of all their products. After that, they deleted that flash drive (which I wanted to buy) from amazon and never replied again. You can see those products if you go to their store ( http://www.amazon.in/s?merchant=A10DCGAQSQ8U2F ) 

So I believe the scam works this way. Imagine there is a pendrive being sold on amazon by two sellers. The first one is priced 600 and the second 10k. There's only one 600 rupee pendrive left. So two people have the pendrive to their cart. the first person purchase the only 600 rupee pen drive. When the second person click purchase, the order is placed to the second seller (10k). Either the customer won't notice this or it's purchased under EMI (where the first installment is close to 600 rupees). Either way, the second seller makes money by scamming on people.

There is a possibility that this is a way of money laundering. Not sure. But I bet many people have fallen into their trap and lost money. Amazon does not have a system for reporting such frauds. I looked at many products and found that other people are doing these scams. There is no one to stop them. So please help bring this to the public's attention.

Thanks.

EDIT: From the comments, this sounds like a money laundering scheme. People are paying for purchases in cash (black money) and making it white money. By the looks of it, many people are doing it.
