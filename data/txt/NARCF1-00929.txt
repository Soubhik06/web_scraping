This happened today in South Delhi. I was travelling from Khanpur to Chirag Delhi and was looking for an auto. After some time, an auto-wala stopped, and I told him that I would give him 40 Rs. He agreed, and after 5 minutes, he stopped the auto and started looking at maps and told me that the price I had offered was too low. I asked him why he had agreed to the price in the first place. He then asked me to deboard the auto, I deboarded and found another auto after 2 minutes. After 2 minutes that person started following my auto and asked my auto-wala to stop in the middle of the road and demanded that I pay him some amount. I told him that I didn't have any money, and he started harassing me, calling me a scammer and threatening to call the police. The situation was horrible, and I felt helpless. Finally, I had to give him some money, and he started accusing me of being a fraud and what noy. It was an awful experience. What else could have I done in that situation.
