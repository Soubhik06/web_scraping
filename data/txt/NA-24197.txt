--- POST TEXT ---

After a few chill months, I decided to go back to the gym and use my Cult membership. It was my first day at the Cult gym (like a gym gym with equipment, not the group class one). 

And low and behold, the security guy is like we need you to put your right thumb here.. I mean.. why?! Why do you need sensitive biometric information for something like a gym? 

This feels so unsafe. I know the general responses here would be "well just go to another gym", but that's really not the point. What happens in case of a data breach? And how are they storing our biometric information? How is this okay?!
Prior to this, I had only gone for the group classes where we just had to scan the QR code on our phone, and that seemed alright, I don't really understand why we now need sensitive biometric information to be shared just to use a gym.
