Hi all,

Tl;DR possible Upi confidence scam where the guy denies receiving money, and shows his phone to prove lack of receipt of money. 

Long time lurker, first time poster. I want to share incident which may or may not be a scam. Any replies to clarify would be wonderful. 

My car stopped yesterday in the middle of the Road and threw up a check engine light. My guess was the fuel had run out (yes I was lazy :/). 
I hailed a auto to drive me to the fuel station and drop me back to my car for 150 bucks. The trip completed and I made a PhonePe Upi transaction to him, successfully. 

He waited for a minute and said he has not received it. The deduction was from my phonepe wallet, and I got a success message immediately. He insisted there was no transaction, and showed me the lack of sms of phonepe notification. I did not have time to argue, so I just paid him cash again and got rid of him. There is not reversal of transaction till now. 

He has multiple bank accounts added to his phone pe account, which I saw when he showed me the phone. 

Here are the possibilities:
1. The Upi account linked to the QR code is on some other phone or not with him, and his MO is to deny having received the money by showing the mobile phone with him. 
2. There were multiple accounts added to his phonepe. Does the phonepe transaction history that we see, belong to specific primary bank account, or does is span transaction for all accounts liked to the phonepe account. 

This was a low risk transaction in a tricky situation. It is possible for me to spend more time arguing and finding out the receipt of money. But I let it go. Perhaps he is counting on people to just move on?
