I ordered a pair of boots from Cosoto Italiano and paid ₹4000 as an advance. Initially, I trusted them because of the positive reviews I found online, but later, I discovered these were fake, likely written to mislead buyers. Upon deeper research, I found that many customers complained about them delivering poor-quality products, wrong items, refusing returns, or in some cases, not delivering the product at all.

Realizing this, I canceled my order within an hour and requested a refund. They assured me that the refund would be processed, but it's been some time now, and I still haven’t received my money back. I’ve already raised a complaint with the cyber cell. When I informed Cosoto Italiano about filing a complaint with cybercrime, their response was shockingly dismissive—they simply reacted with a thumbs-up emoji.

If anyone has faced a similar situation or knows how to proceed further, please share your advice. I really need help recovering my money 

Beware these scammers have two websites, Instagram page and facebook page.
