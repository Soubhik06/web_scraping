TLDR in the end.

while i was selling my sofa set one person said they are ready to pay whatever my asking price was (which was a bit high for what and how it was) so we got a little sceptical, they asked for our number and my dad picked up the call and they said they want the sofa and are ready to pay the full amount rn then are there . My dad said there is no hurry you should come and see the sofa yourself and then we will discuss the price. They kept saying the dont have time bec they are business and they will send the worker and take away the sofa. Dad refused to sell them without seeing them or without them checking the sofa bec they do not respect the object. Next day they called and said they will still want to pay, dad being frustrated said "ok how will you pay" they said upi apps (now the real scam starts)They asked for our upi scan or number then they sent us hi message on gpay , then they told my dad he will recieve money accept option and we have to click accept ( for people who dont know about this, you can request for money from someone on upi, **so while you will think they are sending you the money and asking your permission to send you money , actually they are taking money from your account so never ever click accept bec there is no accept or approve or any button to get money on your own account** ) after realising the dude was trying to scam we hung up the call and blocked them.

TLDR:- the buyer sends me money requests thinking i will accept it bec its asking me for permission to get into my account.
