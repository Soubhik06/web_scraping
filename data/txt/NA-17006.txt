Here's the article- [Fraud alert! Scamsters using Google Pay, PhonePe to con you, 81 users lose ₹1 cr | Mint (livemint.com)](https://www.livemint.com/money/personal-finance/fraud-alert-scamsters-using-google-pay-phone-to-con-you-81-users-lose-rs-1-cr-11679113977390.html)

Basically, the scammers send you some money and ask you to return it hoping you would return it in good faith. Once you do all your PAN, Aadhaar details are visible to the scammer through which they can hack your account.

&amp;#x200B;

* I just don't understand how this is technically possible (unless the scammers are some great hackers). I don't know a way through which they can access my PAN, etc. just by receiving money through phonepe. Even if they did, they would need to login into my account, have my phone for OTP to scam me, right?
* Secondly, I also witnessed something similar to this where a guy sent me 5k by mistake (around 6 months back). I returned him his money after deliberating for a day but did take his audio recordings (for proof that he had sent his money by mistake and wanted it back), his aadhaar, etc. Now I think about it he called to thank me but then started asking me about where I lived. I brushed it off thinking he was just trying to make small talk. After a few hours I received a call from a different number from an unknown person who said that the 1st guy had sent me money by mistake which this guy (the one who called later on) was supposed to receive (before he could start I shut him down and told I've proofs of repayment and ID so he then disconnected). I'm wondering why the 2nd guy who called even received my number. Could it have been something similar to above? If yes, why wasn't I "hacked"?

Thoughts?
