I came across this phishing website [https://mangodeal.online](https://mangodeal.online) on twitter promotion. It is selling mangos at an attractive price. There is no COD option. I ordered Raspuri Mangos for 10 kgs online and paid via UPI Rs.528.64. The product should have been delivered in 4-5 days, but it has been more than a week and not updates of the delivery and I have not received the product.. No way to track it. No customer support information. 

Phone number associated to this company is 7250186602. I called the person, and he asked me to install any desk remote access app on my phone.

It is registered under a pseudonym. I have already raised a complaint with the hosting company.

https://preview.redd.it/votkgwuuqdxa1.png?width=779&amp;format=png&amp;auto=webp&amp;s=0d24efcf4c58f7d7ec34c59bb41ba75f77fdb92b

I have also raised a complaint with Indian consumer forum. I see multiple people have raised complaints about this site. It should have been taken down already. Sad to see it is still scamming people.

This is just a warning. Please beware when buying online.
