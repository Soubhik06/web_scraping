 (A.H.D Scam Cases), Ranchi. 3. Learned counsel for the appellant has prayed for confirmation of
 provisional bail granted to the appellant for 60 days vide order dated
 15.02.2022 through I.A. No. 2697/2022. 4. The sole Appellant stands convicted in connection with R.C.
 Case No. 47(A)/1996-Pat vide impugned judgment dated 15.02.2022
 passed by the Learned Special Judge-V, C.B.I (A.H.D Scam Cases),
 Ranchi for the offences under sections 120-B read with sections 420 , 409 , 467 , 468 , 471 and 477A of the Indian Penal Code and Section 13
 (2) read with Section 13(1)(c)(d) of Prevention of Corruption Act and
 has been sentenced to undergo R.I. for three years with a fine of Rs.
 25,000/- and default sentence under section 120-B of the Indian Penal
 Code and R.I for three years with a fine of Rs. 25,000/- and default
 sentence under sections 420 , 409 , 467 , 468 , 471 and 477A of the Indian
 Penal Code. No separate sentence has been awarded for the offences
 under the P.C Act vide impugned order of sentence dated 15.02.2022.
 All the sentences have been ordered to run concurrently. 5. Learned counsel for the appellant submits that the learned trial
 court has discussed the role of the appellant (A-84) at paragraph-262 to
 264 of the impugned judgment. The appellant was the Manager of M/s
 Magadh Chemical Corporation, Bhagalpur whose proprietrix was A-83.
 It is submitted that the learned Trial Court has rendered an erroneous
 finding that the appellant was also in conspiracy with appellant no.
 A-83, his wife involved in fraudulent supplies to the Animal Husbandry -2- Department and availing fraudulent payments. However, learned Trial
 Court upon considering the lesser gravity of the offence has awarded
 maximum sentence of three years under the relevant provisions of Indian Penal Code and has been pleased to grant provisional bail to the
 appellant, which may be confirmed. 6. Learned counsel for the CBI has opposed the prayer. He submits
 that appellant no.A-83 the proprietrix and her husband the present
 appellant were found to have made fraudulent supplies to the Animal
 Husbandry Department and received payments. 7. Having considered the submissions of learned counsel for the
 appellant and C.B.I and the facts and circumstances noted above,
 provisional bail granted to the appellant vide order dated 15.02.2022 by
 the Court of learned Special Judge-V, C.B.I. (AHD Scam Cases),
 Ranchi is confirmed, subject to deposit of 50% of fine amount imposed
 by the learned Trial Court and if not wanted in connection with any
 other case. The appellant would not leave the country without
 permission of the learned Trial Court. He would also submit his
 passport, if any, before the learned Trial Court and the appellant and his
 bailors shall not change their address or mobile nos. without permission
 of the learned Trial Court. 8. I.A. No. 2697/2022 stands disposed of accordingly. (Aparesh Kumar Singh, J)
