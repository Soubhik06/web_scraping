TITLE: Potential post office scam or genuine case? Speed post undelivered (still in transit) and now officials asking for money to reactivate parcel delivery. Courier is already pre-paid.
DATE: 2023-06-06
SCORE: 8
URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/india/comments/1426lw4/potential_post_office_scam_or_genuine_case_speed/

--- POST TEXT ---

A courier was sent to me via speed post from Punjab to Karnataka. It reaches Delhi within a day and was dispatched same day. Then it took 5 days to register and Nagpur and was dispatched. It's 9th day now and no update. Today we received a call from someone claiming to be from Delhi Post office, informing us that our courier has been deactivated due technical glitch and we need to pay the ₹5 to reactivate it. 

Ofc we didn't reactivate it because it was just a few perishables that would have gone bad by now (usually it takes 2-3 day for delivery from start to finish so some perishables can be mailed). We asked at local post office in Punjab and they seemed irritated saying they didn't know why but Nagpur PO seems to be sitting on packages and not passing it along. I have a feeling some internal officials are involved (maybe in Nagpur? Or maybe in collaboration with Delhi PO?) And looking at the scale, charging ₹5 to enough people might add up to a lot of money. 

Has this happened to anyone else here? Is it genuine? Or is this another small scale scam by government officials?

Edit: I received a reply from Indian Mail that the department of posts does not collect money from customers by calling or sending links and that I should avoid getting scammed.

Update: Package received within 24 hours of registering complaint, without paying any fees. It was definitely a scam.
