Buying and selling online has gotten way more accessible — but it's also gotten riskier.

Scammers, fake products, and payment fraud are now serious problems for anyone trying to get a good deal. It's frustrating enough finding the right item - you shouldn't have to worry about getting scammed too.

After personally dealing with a few bad experiences, I decided to build a better way. That's why I created Vault - a platform designed to protect buyers and sellers from online fraud.

Here's how Vault works:
1. Buyer and seller agree on the deal terms
2. Buyer makes the payment
3. Funds are safely held with our payment partner - even we can't touch the money
4. Buyer confirms delivery
5. Funds are released to the seller

✅ If anything goes wrong, the buyer gets a 100% refund.

With Vault, you can secure any deal - no matter what you're buying or selling. Never miss out on great deals just because they seem too good to be true.
