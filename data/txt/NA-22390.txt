Saw a post here of a guy trying to stop his family from getting scammed by a relatives financial fraud- and it suddenly sounded exactly similar to Monson Mavumkal Scam case in Kerala, 
 
Tho this guy Monson Mavumkal was arrested and is getting prosecuted for multiple crimes/scams he conducted one of his scams sounds is getting repeated again and again across India. Why do people keep on falling to copy-cat scams again and again? Ain’t news coverage enough?

For those who don’t know about Monson’s scam:

It is alleged that this Monson guy claimed his fortune of 2,62,000 crores (Yeah you read it correct 2L cores!) was blocked/confiscated for foreign exchange violations or something similar under FEMA Act. He shown them some fake RBI orders, HSBC bank statements etc.. (anybody with basic knowledge of computers can make these stuffs) to gain trust.

The people on whose complaint he has now been arrested say that they gave him around 10 crores to help "release" the blocked money in exchange for % share once the money is released. A typical "Nigerian Prince" scam.

Funny thing is Monson was able to fool many big wigs including DGP of the state, politicians, channel reporters. 

Scammer monson even purchased 7 luxury cars from Bangalore based used car dealer and paid only 500 Rupees it seems!

Reminds me of Victor Lustig who is widely regarded as one of the most notorious con artists of his time, and is infamous for being "the man who sold the Eiffel Tower twice"
