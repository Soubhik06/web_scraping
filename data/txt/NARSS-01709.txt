No cases of cybercrime that I get are cases of high-tech or intrusion of malicious codes. All the cases are those of low-level fraud. 'Conning' will be a better word to describe almost all of cybercrime in Uttar Pradesh, and rest of India.

As a part of my cybercrime research, I came across this documentary from Discovery channel. I've posted relevant snippet for purpose of creating awareness👆🏼

Kindly stay aware and do not respond to fraudulent calls or enticements. Help your family members, especially young adult and senior citizens, stay aware as well. Don't give away your hard earned money to cybercriminals yourself.
