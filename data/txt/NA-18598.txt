TITLE: Seen those people who ask for your contact details for 'offers and lucky draws' at malls? Here's one of the many things that happens next.
DATE: 2015-09-02
SCORE: 24
URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/india/comments/3jc49x/seen_those_people_who_ask_for_your_contact/

--- POST TEXT ---

My mum was at the Mall the other day, where this girl sweet talked her into sharing her contact details for some 'lucky draw and offers'. Yesterday she received a call who claimed that 'You have won free gifts worth Rs. More-than-my-monthly-salary/-. This is not a scam. You just need to come as a couple and collect it."

Mom handed it over to me and I probed him, here's what they said "Malls make extra profit sometimes and they do this for publicity and build patronage. You'll get a dinner set and some shopping vouchers. You don't have to spend a single rupee."

They kept insisting that I handover the phone to my mom so that they can explain the directions to the place. And made it clear that only mom and dad should come because this is for 'couples' only. They did not mention the name of the organization at all.

Now, obviously, me being the reddit-lurking, smarty-pants that I am, I did some sniffing around.

The first red flag was that the number had been marked as spam by about 60 users on truecaller. 

I was still curious though, because the office they mentioned seemed to be a 'well-known' place.

And then I googled around which is when the horror stories about them started to show up.

[Story 1](http://www.grahakseva.com/complaints/18056/country-vacation-fake-company)

TL;DR:- Called for the gifts. Convinced the guy to buy vacation package. Guy agrees to do it on EMI of 5 years. Entire amount charged in one shot. Refund not being processed.

[Story 2](http://www.mouthshut.com/review/Country-Vacations-Hyderabad-review-smunpoqqos)

TL;DR:- After seeing the demo of their services, when this guy refused to buy their services, he was abused and insulted. He declined the 'free gifts' that brought him there in the first place.

[More stories](http://www.consumercomplaints.in/complaints/country-club-india-ltd-victim-of-country-vacation-fraud-scheme-c839087.html)

Now, I am in no way implying that the entire organization is a fraud. They seem to be well connected with branches across the nation. I already have enough troubles of my own.

Just make sure you educate your folks and friends.
