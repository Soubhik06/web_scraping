This petition has been filed under Section 439 Cr.P.C. for grant

of regular bail in case F.I.R. No.0160 dated 04.05.2023 registered under Sections 406 / 420 / 506 IPC ( Sections 467 / 468 / 471 / 120-B IPC added later

on) at Police Station City Mullana, District Ambala. As per the contents of the FIR, it has been alleged as under :- 3 Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the petitioner
was an employee in the firm at a meager salary of Rs.10,000/- living and
paying his accommodation. He was neither a Consultant nor Director. He
further submits that the investigation already stands concluded as the
challan stands presented and thus custody of the petitioner can not be
prolonged as a punitive measure. 4 Learned counsel for the petitioner further relies upon orders
dated 17.08.2023 passed in CRM-M-39827-2023 and CRM-M-39449-2023
whereby co-accused Kuldeep Singh, Raj Kumar & another have been
granted indulgence by noticing that the parties have compromised the
matter. 5 Per contra learned State counsel submits that in fact as per the
prosecution, it is the petitioner who owns the firm, however, he is not in a
position to dispute the fact that the investigation already stands concluded
and challan stands presented. 6 Having heard rival contentions, this Court is of the considered
opinion that keeping in view the incarceration suffered by the petitioner, the
fact that the matter already stands compromised and co-accused already
stand admitted to interim bail by this Court, the present petition is allowed.
The petitioner is ordered to be released on bail on his furnishing bail/surety

bonds to the satisfaction of the Ld. Trial Court/Duty Magistrate, concerned. CRM-M No.53529 of 2023 (O&M) 4: 2023: PHHC:140137

7 Needless to say that anything observed herein shall not be

construed to be an opinion on the merits of the case. (PANKAJ JAIN )
