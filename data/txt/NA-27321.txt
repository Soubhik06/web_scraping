My elderly parents fell for the classic "this is Microsoft Defender your IP address has been hacked and we need to call your bank to move your money into a safe account" scam. They spoofed the bank phone number and then used the remote access my parents gave them to "move the money" i.e. steal it.

I am a lawyer, but I have little experience dealing with the FDIC or FTC directly. Nor do I have experience with being scammed myself. For those of you who have had family, friends, etc fall victim to this before, does a regular big bank (insured by the FDIC, under FTC, etc) have the power to reverse the transaction? Is that money just gone?
