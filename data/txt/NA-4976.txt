 1.0 In the present petition, the petitioners seek quashing of the FIR no.
 0004 dated 10.01.2022 under Section 420 IPC of Cyber Police Station,
 West, Delhi which was registered on the complaint of the respondent no. 2
 who is present in court. 2.0 It is submitted that the petitioner no. 1 was arrested and detained in
 custody for six days and subsequently released on regular bail.
 2.1 It is also submitted that the petitioner no. 2 was enlarged on
 anticipatory bail given by the learned Trial Court. It was the case of the
 complainant that she was initially lured into an online trading scam on the
 ground that whatever amount is invested by the petitioner, would be doubled Signature Not Verified Digitally Signed By:GEETA JOSHI Signing Date:17.08.2022 11:45:08 within 30 minutes; falling for the lure, the respondent no. 2/complainant
 deposited an amount of Rs 8,000/-. The said amount was transmitted to the
 account of the petitioner no. 2. However, despite waiting for many days, the
 respondent no. 2/complainant did not receive any return of what was
 originally promised. The above FIR was got registered on this incident.
 3.0 Mr Dinesh Rohilla, learned counsel appearing for the petitioners,
 submits that they are young adults and their acts may be condoned. He also
 further submits that the matter has been amicably settled between the
 parties, sum of Rs 8,000/- has been repaid to the respondent no. 2 on
 14.02.2022. 3.1 The Settlement Agreement is annexed as Annexure P-5 (Page 33 to 35) of the present petition which is signed by both the parties.
 3.2 Mr Rohilla further submits that an attested affidavit dated 25.04.2022
 of the complainant/respondent no. 2, is at Page 18 to 20 of the present
 petition, whereby the complainant has stated in para 6 as under: 4.0 IO Mahesh Kumar identifies the complainant. Mr Rohila identifies the
 petitioners present in court. 5.0 Looking at the facts and the conduct of the petitioner, this court is
 inclined to direct the petitioner to do some social service to atone for their
 sins. They are also warned not to repeat such actions in the future. 6.0 Accordingly, it is directed that the petitioners will plant 30 saplings at
 forest office DFC, Mandir Lane, near Birla Mandir, New Delhi through the
 Horticulture Department, West District. 6.1 It is further directed that an affidavit of compliance shall be filed by
 the petitioners, within 15 days from today with photographs in order to show
 compliance. 6.2 The affidavit of the concerned officer of the Horticulture Department
 shall also be filed alongwith the status report with photographs.
 7.0 In view of the above, the FIR no. 00014 dated 10.01.2022 under Section 420 Cr.P.C stands quashed. 8.0 Nothing further survives. Matter is disposed of.
