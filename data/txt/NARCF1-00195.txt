Since the fairly decent participation in my last [post](https://www.reddit.com/r/mumbai/comments/xbewh0/spooky_sunday_stories/) for "Sunday Stories", I've decided to have a thematic post every Sunday. I'm hoping the community will enjoy these, everyone will have something they want to share one of these Sundays, or will find it relatable or will just enjoy the threads. 

I got today's idea when i received the well-known "your electricity power will be disconnected" [message](https://imgur.com/a/XoaJnTy) on WhatsApp. It's widely known that these are scam messages. We all are smart enough to avoid such shams. But I'm sure at some point some of us or those we know would have fallen for some or the other scam either via messages or calls or in-person with a swindling "friend/ relative". 

So, let's share stories of how you or someone you know got scammed, what happened afterwards and were you able to reach a resolution or not. This will help some of us become more aware and we may also be able to crack light hearted jokes around it. 

One of my friends had fallen for the Nykaa scam. There was a time when people would get a call from fake Nykaa customer service reps if they have made a recent purchase on their app or website. The caller would know what items have been purchased, would say that they are among the five lucky customers chosen for a special offer wherein if they purchase items for Rs.5000, they would get 40% cashback. My friend ended up paying Rs.5313 and never got any products, found out later that she was scammed but also didn't do make a police complain (yeah, annoyed me a lot) coz she felt it was too much hassle for the amount. 

Disclaimer: This post is not intended to crowd source content for any platform. I simply like reading anecdotes, experiences and people's stories. I do not intend to offend anyone nor trivialise someone's experience.
