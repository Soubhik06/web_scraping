I took a cab from Bangalore Airport this weekend. I was in hurry to get the next connecting train and I didn't realise what happened. But this is the gist -

&gt; When the cab driver arrived, he asked if I need to take the toll road and that would be charged extra. I agreed to pay that amount as a tip not releasing that the toll amount was already included in the fare.
.
&gt; The trip was a 2 stop trip but the map the driver was using directed the cab to the final stop itself. I realised this when the driver said I reached my destination while The map that I had on my mobile displayed something else. This is when I realised the cab was at the last stop.
Was this a big of uber? Because the from the airport, on the way to the first stop - the last stop also falls along the place so did the uber app mess up? 
Or did the driver did some mistake and clicked the route to the final route?
.
&gt; Now since we had reached the final destination directly (Train station), I now had to ask the driver to go to the first station first and then come back here. I had a connecting train very soon and the only way I could do it was request him. He was all like, I usually won't agree but to this and no driver would agree to this. I offered to pay him extra cash if he could do the extra travel back and forth.
.
I was in an crunch situation and really had no time to argue with the driver or call uber support. 
.
So now, I think it was such a scam-
1. The driver scammed me for the toll gate fees.
2. The mistake on part of uber app or the uber driver made me get to last stop first
3. I had to pay extra amount of cash to the driver.
.
I have sent a ride support message to Uber. Any ideas how can I get Uber to refund me any extra cash I paid ( payment made by Gpay) and also reverse the tip. I am not yet charged but the app keeps nudging me to complete it.(this was the amount he asked for toll.)
