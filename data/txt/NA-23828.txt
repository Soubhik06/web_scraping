I often encounter some North Indian laborers claiming they have been cheated by their employer and have no money to return home. They often ask for money and even offer a UPI number for payment. The first time I believed their story, but now I am suspicious, as I have encountered multiple people like this for over a year, especially around Yeshwantpur Railway Station and near Jalahalli. Is this a scam, or have they genuinely been cheated?
