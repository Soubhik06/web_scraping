Recently I have come across a lot of posts about audio/videos/photos created using AI. Most of them harmless, e.g. cover of a song using voice of an artist who never actually sang the song, deepfake videos of artists appearing in scenes which were never filmed on them. Am I the only one who thinks as this tech becomes accessible, public scams would become uncontrollable, given a lot of personal data is already available on internet. Photos, videos, audios,. We will soon start seeing people getting phone calls from their relatives where you can feel it's the relatives voice but it is a snippet created by scammer using AI. What can be done to keep ourselves safe from such scams?
