What are the scams/cons you or people you know are aware of ? Let's enlighten each other. I am not including scams by corporations but feel free to add them if you feel like warning people. Here's some I know. Will add more if they come to mind. 

1. KYC scam (usually OTP based)

2. UPI scam. (OTP based)

3. "Kya aapko hindi aata hai ?" Con. A north Indian family lost in a south indian state without any money. (Have seen so many of them in Bangalore)

4. Loan recovery scam. You get a call claiming someone you know took a loan and gave your name as guaranteer. 

5. Courier tracking. You need to pay money for more detailed courier tracking. Again OTP based. A friend of mine lost 10k. 

6. You won a prize scam. Pay fees to claim your prize. 

7. WhatsApp call/message from KBC

8. NRI needing money for illness. (Met one on Delhi)

9. A stranger at railway station claiming his friend brought him into the city and stole all his belongings. Need money to go back to home town/village. (3 of my friends have met them on different occasions)

10. (OG) Nigerian prince.

11. Someone claiming you bumped into them and broke their stuff and want money. (Happened to me in guwahati) 

12. Future predictions. 

13. Hand over a flower (forcibly) and then ask for money because you "bought" them. (Common in London)

14. Catfishing. 

15. Chit funds.

16. OLX
