In the month of august my mother contacted this person on facebook who claimed to run a Saree boutique out of Shantipur and showed interest in ordering some sarees as Durga pujo was near. So I made the payment for 5 sarees and to our expectation we did receive 4 before durgapujo. But afterwards he started making excuses about the 5th one that it will be delayed due to some imaginary issues. At that momment we were fine as it can happen and we waited the durga pujo out. Afterwards he blocked my mother on facebook fearing she might join the page and post comments on it. Though he didn't block on other social media. Whenever we tried calling him, he was either busy or not picking up the call. Even tried to call from third party number he only picks up the call for the first time and never again ( typical scammer behaviour).

Recently when maa tried to reach out he mocked her in a sarcastic way about getting the saree. 
I'm moving forward with lodging a compain in cyber crime and consumer forum.

Any advice on this is welcome and if anyone of you are from Shantipur, kindly provide some info on this dude.
