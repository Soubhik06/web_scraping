 becomes incomparable with the whole world. Such an approach
 is not only fallacious but also against the provisions of the
 Income Tax Rules 1962. Each Comparable needs to be tested on
 its own strength and weakness for comparability study. On this
 basis, we proceed to test the challenge of the assessee to various
