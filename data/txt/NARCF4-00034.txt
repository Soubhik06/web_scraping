So yesterday I was travelling around the KIIT to Chandrasekharpur route and this happened basically (trying to remember details)

this guy approached me kind of panicking and said something about his dad being in the hospital and he needing 5 lakhs and pulled out a digital copy of a medical report, then told he was a student from KIIT aswell and started saying “aap KIIT se ho? Mujhe dekhke pata lag gaya tha. Aapko toh pata hai KIIT mei sablog ameer hai mai pata nahi kyu yaha aagaya mai lower middle class family se belong karta hun” and all that emotional baggage.

Now I’ve seen similar scams happening in NCR but I was in a hurry and wondered if he was acting like he wqs because he was nervous or something and the good of my heart kicked in so I just UPId him a 100 and went on my way.

Anyone else who had this experience?

Some things I should’ve done that I forgot but suggesting you guys to do:

1. Click a picture of his face and the documents for proof. (I didn’t even check the document properly mujhe laga 100 rupaye hai kya hi hojayega but I should’ve stayed vigilant atleast for everyone else’s safety).

2. Take the number and then pay instead of just scanning otherwise G-Pay will censor it.

3. Ask his name branch and section in detail and you could tell him to meet you outside his own class to collect the donation (if you want to donate)

4. Compare the names in the medical report and UPI and if sounds vastly different (surnames don’t match/middle names) question him about it.

5. Be inquisitively questioning without being rude, in case he’s actually a guy in need :)
