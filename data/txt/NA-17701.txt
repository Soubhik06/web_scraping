Atul Subhash Case
The tragic suicide of Atul Subhash, a Bengaluru techie, has sparked nationwide debates on the misuse of dowry and domestic violence laws. Atul left behind an 81-minute video and a 24-page suicide note accusing his estranged wife, Nikita Singhania, and her family of filing multiple false cases to extort money. He also alleged judicial corruption, claiming a judge demanded a bribe for case settlement⁴. This case underscores the need for reforms to prevent the misuse of protective laws and ensure genuine cases are taken seriously.

Exam Scams
India has witnessed several exam-related scams, where corruption and malpractice have undermined the integrity of educational assessments. One prominent example is the Vyapam scam in Madhya Pradesh, where officials, politicians, and middlemen were involved in manipulating entrance exams for medical colleges and government jobs. Despite numerous arrests and investigations, the case highlighted systemic corruption and the challenges in ensuring accountability.

Adani Solar Electricity Case
The Adani solar electricity case involves allegations of over-invoicing of equipment imports to inflate project costs and siphon off funds. Despite evidence presented by whistleblowers and international scrutiny, the Indian government did not take significant action until the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) began investigating the matter. This case raises concerns about regulatory oversight and the influence of powerful corporate entities on the justice system.

Other Suppressed Cases
- Sahara India Pariwar Scam: The Sahara group was accused of raising billions of dollars through illegal bonds. Despite a Supreme Court order to refund the money to investors, the process has been slow, and many believe the case has been suppressed due to the group's political connections.
- 2G Spectrum Scam: This high-profile case involved the allocation of 2G spectrum licenses at throwaway prices, causing a massive loss to the exchequer. Although the case led to the resignation and arrest of several politicians and officials, many believe the full extent of the scam was never uncovered.
- Coal Allocation Scam: Known as Coalgate, this scam involved the allocation of coal blocks to private companies without a transparent bidding process. The case highlighted the nexus between politicians and business interests, but many accused were acquitted due to lack of evidence.

These cases illustrate the challenges within India's justice system, where corruption, political influence, and systemic inefficiencies often hinder the delivery of justice. They call for urgent reforms to ensure transparency, accountability, and fairness in the legal process.
