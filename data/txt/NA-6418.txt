CORAM: - HON'BLE MR. JUSTICE AMBUJ NATH ----- For Petitioner : Mr. Ankit Kumar, Advocate
 For Opp. Party : Mr. Shekhar Sinha, Spl.P.P --- 03/16.11.2021 The case diary earlier called for has been received,
 perused the same and heard the parties. Petitioner has been made accused in connection with
 Deoghar (Cyber) P.S. Case No. 54 of 2021, registered under Sections
 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 and 120B of the Indian Penal Code and Sections 66(B) , 66(C) 66(D) and 84 (C) of the Information
 Technology Act, pending in the Court of learned Additional Sessions
 Judge-II, Deoghar. It is alleged that on the basis of the confidential
 information that some cyber criminals had assembled into the house of
 the Md. Sabab Raja at village Rangasirsa, the police party raided his
 house from where several mobile phones and other e-gadgets were
 recovered, which were used in the phishing of money from the bank
 accounts of unsuspecting account holders. On the basis of his
 confessional statement this petitioner was also arrested, from his
 possession several mobile phones alongwith Allahabad Bank ATM
 Card, SBI Passbook, Bank of India Passbook and a black color laptop
 were recovered. During the course of investigation it transpired that
 the several e-wallets were created using SIM used in the recovered
 mobile phones. From perusal of the case diary it appears that
 witnesses have stated that call was made to them from the mobile
 phone recovered from the possession of the petitioner, enticing them
 to disclose their account details but being suspicious the details were
 not shared. There is specific allegation against the petitioner for
 contacting different bank account holders for committing fraud. Regards being had to the facts and circumstances of the
 case, I am not inclined to enlarge the petitioner on bail, hence the
 prayer for bail of the petitioner stands rejected. (Ambuj Nath, J.)
