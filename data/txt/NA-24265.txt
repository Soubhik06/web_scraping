Me and my brother booked an auto with namma yatri from basawangudi, the driver started the conversation saying thank you for booking the ride and he doesn't have even 10₹ in his pocket. I gave a friendly response to which he went on to tell me how he's been having a shit day. His kid is hospitalized and due to lack of funds he is not able discharge the kid. And how no one is helping him for even ₹1k, even though he had helped them in the past. He even mentioned that his dad died recently due to which his savings got depleted. During all this only I was responding to him, brother didn't get involved. As the ride was about to end, I doubled the fare to ₹130 and paid him. To which he was surprised, I said keep it. I know it's not a lot. When we got off, we both waved at him and went ahead. We both noticed that his eyes were very red. Brother said that he might telling the story to make extra to buy alcohol. With all the scams that have been going around its hard to trust people in genuine need in this city. I want know if someone here experienced something similar. I think that this was genuine. While my brother was a little bit 🤨.
