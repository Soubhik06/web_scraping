DATE: 2017-10-06

--- POST TEXT ---

For the last few weeks, I have received fraudulent scam calls many times from people who claim they represent a bank. The first time a woman called me about replacing my expiring credit card and asked for my date of birth and last four digits of the CC number. I realised what was happening and asked them why they needed such things. The woman immediately transferred the call to a man, who claimed to be the manager and pressured me into divulging the details.

Thankfully I knew something was off and told them I will deal directly with the branch. When I called the bank, they had little to help me with, so I just gave them the phone number from which I received the fake call from. The Customer care executive had no answer when I asked them on how to identify if the person is really from the bank.

I again received a similar call a week later from another woman claiming she was from Axis bank. I had no card or account with them, so I shut them up immediately.

Why do banks put the onus on their customers? I have noticed that almost all communications from phonebanking, cards or loans come from unidentified landlines and mobile numbers. There is simply no way to keep track of them without resorting to a service like Truecaller.

IMO such scams can be easily stopped if all departments of the banks communicated from the official and easily identifiable phone number. Not everyone wants or can use a service like Truecaller or other privacy intrusive services.
