CORAM: HON'BLE MR. JUSTICE GAUTAM KUMAR CHOUDHARY ----- The petitioner named above has been made accused in connection with
 Dhanbad Cyber Crime P.S. Case No. 07 of 2022 (Cyber Case No. 05 of 2023) for
 the offence under Sections 419 , 420 , 34 of the Indian Penal Code and Section
 66(C) and 66(D) of I.T. Act, pending in the court of Addl. Sessions Judge-II-
 cum-Special Judge, Cyber Crime, Dhanbad. As per the prosecution case, cyber fraud was committed with the informant
 in which by five transactions he was defrauded of Rs.3,69,989/-. During
 investigation, the name of the petitioner has come up as out of defrauded amount
 of Rs.64,999/- some purchases were made by the platform of flipcart by using the
 mobile of this petitioner in the delivery point. It is submitted by learned counsel for the petitioner that the petitioner is
 not involved in the offence. The petitioner has no criminal antecedent and by
 cloning Adhar number, SIM card was issued in his name which was used for
 taking delivery of goods purchased from flipcart. Learned counsel for the State has opposed the prayer for bail.
 Considering the facts and circumstances of the case, the petitioner, named
 above, is directed to be released on bail on furnishing bail bond of Rs.25,000/-
 (Rupees Twenty Five Thousand) with two sureties of the like amount each, to the
 satisfaction of learned Court below. (Gautam Kumar Choudhary, J.)
