However, I continue to receive promotional emails from the service provider.

These service providers (referred to as 'data fiduciaries' in the act) are just online marketplaces which I signed up for going through some deals and how they give extra discount when you enter your email. It’s incredibly frustrating, and if the DPDP Act can help put an end to this, I’d like to know how to use it effectively.
