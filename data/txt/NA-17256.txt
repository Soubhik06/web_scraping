**My Experience with a Potentially Deceptive Delhivery Practice** 

I recently had a concerning experience with Delhivery that raises serious questions about their delivery practices. I ordered an item scheduled to arrive on May 28th. Upon arrival, the delivery agent refused doorstep delivery, despite scorching temperatures exceeding 50°C in my city. He demanded I travel 10km to collect the package.

When I insisted on doorstep delivery, he threatened to cancel my order. Following his refusal, I filed a complaint through their app. However, I received no response. Shockingly, the next day, the order was marked as delivered without any OTP or notification.

Further attempts to contact Delhivery through their app and Twitter, with daily complaints for a week, yielded no response. Finally, I reached out directly to the delivery agent. He claimed he returned the parcel to the nearest hub for next-day delivery.

**Uncovering a Potential Scam**

The agent then revealed a disturbing practice. He explained that for distant deliveries, they sometimes mark packages as delivered and store them at the hub. If the customer complains, they receive their item. Otherwise, the package is supposedly held for a month before being claimed by the delivery personnel. But the issue is you can't raise a complaint as it's useless because no action will be taken. 

**A Widespread Issue?**

This revelation was deeply concerning. If true, this practice could be impacting many customers, especially those unaware of complaint channels. Many in my area lack the technical knowledge to navigate apps like Twitter for redressal.

**Urging Delhivery for Transparency**

This potential scam raises serious questions about Delhivery's business practices. I urge Delhivery to investigate this matter thoroughly and ensure transparent and ethical delivery procedures. Additionally, I encourage fellow Redditors to share similar experiences and hold delivery companies accountable.
