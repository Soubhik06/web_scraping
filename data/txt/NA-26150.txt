--- POST TEXT ---

I'm from Bangalore and I come to Chennai for some work often and I reached my limit. I reached the metro station near my accomodation and tried to book an auto from Rapido, Ola and Uber. After waiting for about 10 mins I start asking running autos and the one person who did stop asked me to pay 200 for a simple 3-4 km ride. I think this price quote might be because I didn't ask him in Tamil? But nonetheless I ended up waiting another 15 mins near the metro station for an auto from the online apps before another running auto agreed to take me for 150. 
I told him my accomodation was near a college and after reaching the college, I guided him and after about 100 meters he says something in Tamil and all I understood was 200. 
I asked him to stop as I didn't want to pay more than 150 and handed him 2 notes of hundred rupees. This man then proceeds to flash a smile and tells me sir do you really want 50 rupees? So far you made me come. 
I insisted and he handed me 20 rupees and left. 

The time was about 9 PM and I just can't understand why the auto drivers don't want to use the easy apps like Rapido and Ola and even want to pull these scams. 
Not just that but also why does everyone want to be paid cash? It is so common to pay via phone now. Cash causes so many problems and even when I have gotten Ola autos before, they have picked fights with me when I try to pay via Gpay. And I cannot even imagine what Ola money Postpaid would be like for these people. 

I feel like Chennai is a great developed city but such things spoil the whole experience and just want to understand why cities like Bangalore can ensure smooth Auto rides even at odd times when Chennai struggles to do so at normal times too.
