State. Heard counsel for the parties. MCRC No.4931/2015 & MCRC No.6267/2015

 Both these applications are filed by two different
accused named in Crime No.22/2014 registered with
Police Station - S.T.F., Bhopal commonly known as
VYAPAM Examination Scam cases - for the offence
punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 120-B of I.P.C. and Section 3(Gha) 1 , 2 read with Section 4 of
M.P. Manyata Prapt Pariksha Adhiniyam, 1937. The role ascribed to the applicant in the first
application is that of a middleman and to the applicant in
the second application of racketeer. According to the prosecution, the applicant - Hemant
Kumar Shakya was responsible to facilitate commission of (2) offence for 14 candidates appearing in the VYAPAM
examination. The applicant - Virendra Singh Shakya acted
as a middleman between the impersonator and the
racketeer - Hemant Kumar Shakya. It is stated by the learned counsel for the State that
the investigation in this crime is still incomplete. The name
of impersonator disclosed by the applicant - Virendra
Singh Shakya, who acted as a middleman, has been found
to be misleading. The phone number disclosed by the
applicant - Virendra Singh Shakya pertains to some other
person who claims to have no connection with VYAPAM
examination or any of the members of conspiracy. Suffice it to observe that the investigation of Crime
No.22/2014 is still incomplete. The impersonator is yet to
be identified and arrested. No doubt, the beneficiary -
Rajesh Jatav has been released on bail, but, that cannot be
the basis to release the present applicants on bail, who are
the prime offenders and Rajesh Jatav was only the
beneficiary, having taken advantage of the situation for
consideration paid to the applicants herein. As the investigation is still incomplete and the
involvement of the applicant - Hemant Kumar Shakya is
noticed in more than one crime of similar type and that (3) Virendra Singh Shakya having disclosed incorrect name of
impersonator and the impersonator is yet to be identified
and arrested, the question of granting bail to these
applicants, does not arise. At this stage, counsel for the applicants requested not
to record further reasons and instead permit the applicants
to withdraw the respective applications with liberty to the
applicants to apply for bail afresh before this Court after
filing of the final charge-sheet in Crime No.22/2014. Accordingly, we dismiss these applications as
withdrawn with liberty to the applicants, as aforesaid,
leaving all questions open to be considered in the proposed
bail applications on its own merits in accordance with law. MCRC No.5427/2015 & MCRC No.6268/2015
 Both these applicants have been named as accused in
Crime No.266/2012 registered with Police Station -
Gopalganj, Sagar commonly known as VYAPAM
Examination Scam Cases - for the offence punishable
under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 472 , 474 , 120-B of
I.P.C. and Section 3(Gha) 1 , 2 read with Section 4 of M.P.
Manyata Prapt Pariksha Adhiniyam, 1937. The role ascribed to the first applicant - Prabhat
Choudhary is that of a racketeer and to the second (4) applicant also as racketeer. The second applicant, as
mentioned in the companion application, is involved in
more than one crime of similar type and has been so far
found to be responsible to have facilitated commission of
offence by helping 14 candidates for that purpose. The applicants through counsel submit that even
these applications be allowed to be withdrawn with liberty
to the applicants to file fresh bail application after filing of
the final charge-sheet in Crime No.266/2012 without
recording any reasons so that the fresh bail application can
be considered on its own merits in accordance with
law. Accordingly, both these applications are allowed to
be withdrawn with liberty to file fresh bail application
after filing of the final charge-sheet in Crime No.266/2012,
leaving all questions open to be considered in the proposed
bail applications on its own merits in accordance with
law. The note of the gist of investigation done in the
respective crimes i.e. Crime No.266/2012 and Crime
No.22/2014, which is prepared on the basis of
information available in the case diary, tendered for
our perusal, by the learned counsel for the State, is (5) ordered to be kept in sealed cover in the safe custody of
 the Registrar (Judicial).
