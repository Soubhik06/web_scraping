This is first application filed under Section 439 of Cr.P.C. on behalf of
 applicant, who is in jail since 22.07.2023 in connection with Crime
 No.0223/2023, registered at Police Station Awadhpuri, District Bhopal (M.P.)
 for the offence punishable under Sections 419 , 420 & 201 of the IPC. 2. As per prosecution story, it is alleged that complainant filed a written
 complaint at police station that on 2/09/2022 an unknown person came to his
 pani puri stall and said that he is employee of Paytm and loan has been
 sanction on his Paytm app. The applicant took the complainant's bank details. On 3/09/2022 applicant made UPI transaction of Rs. 4600/- thereafter on
 5/09/2022 another UPI transaction was made using complainant's bank account
 details from applicant's mobile phone. By using the complainant's bank detail,
 applicant has committed fraud of Rs. 69,600/- by way of Paytm app. On the
 basis of above, aforesaid offence was registered against the present applicant. 3. Learned counsel appearing for the applicant submitted that applicant
 is in custody since 23/12/2023. It is submitted that applicant is ready to deposit
 amount of Rs. 70,000/- under protest. If said amount is not deposited then
 applicant may be arrested again and sent to jail. In these circumstance,
 applicant may be enlarged on bail. 4. Learned Panel Lawyer for the State has opposed the bail application of
 the applicant. 5. Heard learned counsel for the parties. 6. Considering the aforesaid submissions made by counsel for the
 applicant, bail application filed by the applicant is allowed on condition that
 applicant will deposit Rs.70,000/- (Rupees Seventy Thousand Only)
 under protest before the trial Court. 7. It is directed that the applicant shall be released on bail on
 depositing Rs.50,000/- (Rupees Fifteen Thousand Only) before the trial
 Court under protest on his furnishing personal bond in the sum of Rs.
 50,000/- (Rupees Fifty Thousand Only) with one solvent surety in the like
 amount to the satisfaction of the trial Court and rest of the amount i.e.
 Rs.20,000/- will be deposited within a period of one month. If the said
 amount is not deposited by the applicant then bail granted to him shall stand
