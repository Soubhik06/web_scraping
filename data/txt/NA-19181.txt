Hey! Let's talk about something very important. 

Recently there have been reports of multiple data breaches of the Aadhar database. On January 4th, a report by The Tribune came out claiming that they were sold the adminstartor privileges of the Aadhar system for ₹500. This was followed by a report in The Quint pointing out an inherent loophole in the Aadhar mechanism where one administrator can grant administrator access to anyone else. Essentially: our names, addresses, photograph and our biometric details are so insecure within the Aadhar system that almost any quack can gain unlimited access to it for as low as ₹500. On top of that, the lack of any sort of data protection law only makes the matter much worse. This opens a Pandora's box of possible identity theft, privacy invasion and other sorts of bad stuff.
 While the UIDAI is in perpetual denial of the loopholes of this very flawed system there is something you can do about it. Follow the link below to send a pre-written E-mail to your local MP to bring this issue in the upcoming Parliament session. The website also allows you to send an E-Mail in your local language. I urge all of you reading to please send an E-mail.
