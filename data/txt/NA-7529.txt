Heard counsel for the parties. The applicant has been arrested on 16/11/2014 in
connection with Crime No.22/2014 registered with Police
Station S.T.F., Bhopal - commonly known as VYAPAM
Examination Scam cases - for the offences punishable under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 and 120-B of IPC and
Section 3 (Gha) 1, 2/4 of M.P. Recognized Examination Act,
1937. The role ascribed to the applicant by the prosecution, is
that of a "beneficiary". The applicant contacted middlemen,
namely, Hemant Shakya and Virendra Shakya. The said
middlemen arranged for an impersonator, who appeared in
place of applicant in the Forest Guard Recruitment
examination 2013, conducted by VYAPAM. The applicant
succeeded in the said examination by committing unfair
means. It is fairly stated by the learned counsel for the State, on
instructions, that custodial interrogation of the applicant is no
more required. The applicant is not involved in any other crime
 of similar type. Charge-sheet has also been filed against the
applicant. Further, the investigation against the applicant is
complete in all respects as of now and, therefore, the presence
of applicant is no more required. In that sense, the link
between the applicant and the co-accused persons is already
complete. Besides, the applicant through counsel undertakes to
appear before the Investigating Officer as and when called for
further investigation including against him as also to
punctually appear before the trial Court to ensure early
disposal of the criminal trial. In the interest of justice, taking overall view of the
matter, we deem it appropriate to grant bail to the applicant on
following strict conditions:- The bail application stands disposed of accordingly.
