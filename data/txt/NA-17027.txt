Today, that is on 18th february, 2019 I got a call at 10:30 AM from alleged paytm employees from their call centers saying I had won a lucky draw and I can choose any out of five, very expensive products, which were a refrigerator, AC, LED TV, a dell laptop and a iphone 7 . The very first thing that came in my mind was that, this has to be a scam. But the alleged paytm employee whose name she says as 'Pooja' managed to not only tell me my registered mobile number which I had used to create an account on paytm, but also my entire residential address !

&amp;#x200B;

But, I was still suspicious, so I humored her for a bit and then told her I was unsure which product I wanted and was it possible to call them afterwards, she obliged by saying yes, but told me to call in an hour. In that time, I decided to google recent paytm call center scams, and this one resembled my experience word for word :- [https://consumercomplaintscourt.com/fraud-call-in-the-name-of-paytm-mall/](https://consumercomplaintscourt.com/fraud-call-in-the-name-of-paytm-mall/)

&amp;#x200B;

I then called them back, recorded the entire conversation this time, wherein they also said that they are not giving the product completely for free, but I have to first login to my paytm account and then buy a 5000 rupees product, and then I can have my desired gadget.

I hope you spread this message, especially to our older, not so tech- savvy population\[mostly, I guess\] as nobody should fall for these deplorable scams.

I do not know whether this is a paytm leak, or they sold customer information willingly to third parties, but this is just disturbing, to say the least .
