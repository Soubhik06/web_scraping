So there was a previous post about someone getting a text message about someone else's covid test results. I noticed most of them saying it could be because of mismatch numbers or intended to someone else. But less awareness about smishing attack which targets mobile. These messages make the target (you) to make you click links in the sms. DON'T OPEN OR CLICK ON THE LINKS if you think it's not intented to you. 
Smishing attacks works in a way that the attacker crafts a text making the target confuse or grab their attention to make them click on the links by which they can install a backdoor Trojan Or malware. Be careful. Pls don't become a victim.
