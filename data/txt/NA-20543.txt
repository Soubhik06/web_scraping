Eye opener - How does Amazon India handles fraudulent charge case and how does it's customer support team replies.

The Story - 

Fraudulent transaction happens via victim's debit card on Amazon. Amazon India alerted within 2 hours of incident with transaction ID from bank records. By the end of the day, necessary documents furnished. After 16 days, Amazon replies with "no such charge received, contact bank".
Case filed with bank closes with status - "Merchant confirmed transaction as successful".
Amazon again denies receiving any charges and asks to contact bank.
Again, when contacted, bank's fraud management team gets in touch with payment gateway, which in turn gets revert from Amazon that "Since the services has been utilized, hence we are unable to issue refund".

https://archive.org/details/AmazonsAmazingWayOfHandlingFraudDisputeCaseWithDualReplies

What's the point of having a risk and fraud management team, who do not work. And why two types of replies?
