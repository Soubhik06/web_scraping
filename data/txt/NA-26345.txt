Online fraud incidents are increasing daily, with cybercriminals using various methods to deceive citizens financially. Recently, they have adopted a new technique involving PDF files. They send a Portable Document Format (PDF) file from an unknown mobile number, and as soon as citizens open it, money from their bank accounts is automatically transferred to an unknown account. This scam often uses the Union Bank of India’s logo and name.
