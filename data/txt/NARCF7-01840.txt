--- POST TEXT ---

After returning to Chennai from living in Europe for almost three years, I'm surprised by the frequent need to share my phone number for seemingly unnecessary reasons. From entering parking at a mall to individual stores and even a small coffee vendor, I was asked for my number. While some claim it's for "eco-friendly" paperless billing, some said they were instructed to do so and there is no other option.

Also, as soon as I activated my Indian SIM, I started receiving spam calls from various countries like Russia and Kazakhstan. This leads me to believe that our data might be vulnerable due to this constant phone number sharing.
I don’t want to sound like an overreacting NRI, but considering the prevalence of online scams, I find it concerning that there seems to be little effort to curb unwanted data sharing. Is this a recent phenomena ? Do you folks notice this ? Thoughts ?
