--- POST TEXT ---

I've been getting automated calls from FedEx saying my courier is yet to be dispatched due to clearance issues. I was ignoring it for couple of weeks because I wasn't expecting any FedEx courier. Today I decided to pick up the call. Automated voice prompted me to press 1. I did, and the call was directed to an agent.

The agent(with a heavy MTI, but good English) informed me that it was a parcel for me from Taiwan, awaiting clearance at Mumbai Customs Office.

I said it's not mine. The agent said, you might have given your Aadhar or Pan somewhere. Your name and phone number are here. I said yes, maybe, a lot of places take these details. And then he went on to say your details might have been misused, do you need any help with this. I said no need, not my parcel, not my headache. He was dumbstruck. I said good day and cut the call.

*A quick Google search made me realize that this particular scam is going on for a while in India. Just posting it here for people who are unaware.*

**TLDR**: Received persistent automated calls from FedEx/Customs regarding a courier, spoke to an agent about a Taiwanese parcel, denied ownership, and declined assistance.
