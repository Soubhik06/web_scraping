 We have gone through the affidavit-in-opposition filed by Sri Jayant Singh, Deputy
Commissioner of Police, Crime Branch, Guwahati sworn on 3.2.2020. As per the affidavit in
regard to Dispur PS Case No.1816/2011 registered under Sections 120 B/420/406/468/409 IPC , Final Report was filed by mistake in the Court on 19.6.2014 but the Court did not accept
the Final Report and ordered further investigation. The affidavit discloses that there are allegations of a scam of Rs.270 Crores involving 67
works in connection with implementation of irrigation scheme. The affidavit further discloses
that further investigation is still going on. 2. It is most unfortunate that the case registered in 2011 indicating embezzlement of a
huge amount of public money by public servants is still pending investigation. 3. We hereby direct Sri Jayant Singh to himself take over the investigation and conduct
investigation on day to day basis and file the investigation status report on or before
24.3.2020. We also direct Sri Jayant Singh to remain present in Court on the next date of listing to
explain as to under what circumstances, a crime reported in 2011 remains inconclusively
investigated, although huge sum of public money is involved and the matter should have
assumed utmost urgency. It appears to be complete failure of the investigating and
prosecuting agency. 4. Let copies of this order be conveyed not only to the said officer but also to the Director
