Although the title says "scam", I'm not really sure if it was a scam or a honest mistake on part of the auto driver.

Either way, I booked an auto through Namma yatri. Very uneventful trip and the total amount at the end was 150rs. The driver told me that he has to pay Namma Yatri 90rs for something idk, and asked me to 90 to Namma Yatri on his behalf and the rest of the 60rs to him directly.

I thought this was weird but agreed either way. He showed me a QR code from his Namma Yatri app which I paid through paytm.

Here's the kicker, what he showed me was a auto payment/mandate setup that deducts 90 rs on demand and credits it to Namma Yatri. Paytm showed me the auto payment setup immediately and I realized what was going on. I showed this to the driver and he said he doesn't know what it was (I want to give him the benefit of the doubt here because he seemed not used to tech or payment apps because he was struggling with it for a bit). 

At the end I paid 90 and 60 and walked away. Paytm has an option to pause and then cancel auto payment setups which I did immediately afterwards.

TL;DR - Be careful and have a second look when you're scanning QR codes and after payments. You could be given a auto payment setup that the other party can use to deduct money whenever.
