I am 24 years old and I came to Delhi from Mathura for a job. I was working at a PNB MetLife call center in Laxmi Nagar for quite some time. It was a small room where a few of us worked, making calls using MTS phones to try and sell insurance policies. The call center owner used to withhold our salaries if we didn't meet our targets. I would receive a salary of 10,000 rupees, but often, due to not meeting the targets, they paid me only 7,000 or 8,000 rupees.
I'm not financially strong, and my family isn't either, so I would send 3,000 rupees back home and pay 4,000 rupees for room rent. One day, when I went to the office, I found it locked, thinking they might open it later, but no one showed up. Those people were fake and scamming customers. They had held onto my salary for almost two months. I haven't paid the room rent for two months, and that's why I can't leave, and my landlord has taken my bag today, which contains all my documents and clothes.
I've been staying at the Bangla Sahib Gurudwara for many days, but now, even there, they are starting to recognize me, and due to the G20 summit, they aren't allowing me to stay there loudly. I eat the food provided at the Gurudwara, which is free, but I feel my health deteriorating because of it. I've spoken to several people on Reddit, and one of them has offered me a job, but I only have 23 rupees right now, which means I can't even afford to travel for an interview. I am currently at my room, and I had to eat a 10 rupees Parle-G biscuit dipped in water. I don't have a bike, or else I could have done food delivery, which pays daily, but I can't afford the 1,700 rupees weekly rent for an electric bike. Life is really tough for me right now.
