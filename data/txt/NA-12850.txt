These applications are arising out of the same P.S.

 Case i.e., the Gardanibagh Patna P.S. Case No. 50 of 2025,

 therefore, they are being heard together and are being decided

 by a common order. 2. Heard the learned counsel for the petitioners and

 learned APP for the State. 3. The petitioners seek regular bail in a case registered

 for the offence under Sections 318(4), 338, 336(3), 340(2),

 112(2) of the BNS and Sections 10/11 of the Bihar Public

 Examination (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act. 4. The petitioners are accused of impersonation. The

 allegation against the petitioners is that with the help of

 middleman, the petitioners got their written examination for

 recruitment of Bihar Police cleared by playing fraud by way of
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.18773 of 2025(3) dt.12-05-2025 3/4 coming out of the examination center after photography and

 biometric. 5. It has been submitted by the learned counsel for the

 petitioners that the petitioners are innocent and have not

 committed any offence. They are meritorious students and have

 falsely been implicated in this case. The petitioners are the

 candidates who were to appear in the examination and someone

 else has impersonated them and appeared on their behalf in the

 examination. 6. So far as petitioners namely Raushan Kumar and

 Subodh Kumar are concerned, they are in custody since

 18.01.2025. 7. So far as petitioners namely Munna Kumar and

 Bhupendra Kumar are concerned, they are in custody since

 19.01.2025. 8. Considering the facts and circumstances of the

 case, this application for regular bail is allowed. 9. Accordingly, let the petitioners, namely Munna

 Kumar, Bhupendra Kumar, Raushan Kumar and Subodh Kumar,

 (above named) be released on bail on furnishing bail bonds of

 Rs. 10,000/- (Ten Thousand) each with two sureties of the like

 amount each to the satisfaction of the learned concerned Court
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.18773 of 2025(3) dt.12-05-2025 4/4 below, Patna in connection with Gardanibagh P.S. Case No. 50

 of 2025. 8. These applications for regular bail stand allowed. (Sandeep Kumar, J)
