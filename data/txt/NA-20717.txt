One package got a fake status update as "Attempted delivery, party not at home". I called the call center, and they must have some experience with this sort of complaint, since they quickly agreed this was likely a fake update.

The second package took 5 days for Flipkart to ship from the date of the order (Slow Billion sale indeed), and it's been sitting at the Delhivery center in Bangalore for the next 5 days. The Delhivery call center says it will likely take 2 more days to reach Chennai. Chennai is a 5 hour train journey from Bangalore.

I've had this experience in the past with Delhivery too, so if I see FK or Amazon are shipping using Delhivery I definitely roll my eyes. I groan less when I have a package shipped by the Indian Postal service - and those guys are unionized workers who don't normally give a shit about service.

If the online retailers would let me choose the courier company at checkout I think I'd pay extra for that.

COD seems best - both Amazon and Flipkart use their own courier services to collect cash for large items. But if you prepay then they stick you with a service like Delhivery.
