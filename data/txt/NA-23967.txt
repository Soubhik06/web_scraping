--- POST TEXT ---

I needed a new SIM so I registered for a new prepaid sim on JIO site on Friday. However I bought it from 10digi instead on Saturday and didn't need it anymore. Yesterday, I was assigned a service representative who would provide me the SIM. I received an sms that had an OTP which I was supposed to provide the rep. after activation of the number was done. 

The message had this - "Please share this with our representative only after receiving SIM delivery and doorstep activation". 

I told the rep. to cancel the order as I didn't need it anymore. He told me that he will need the OTP to do that. I told him that I won't share it because the message said so. He called me thrice, then changed numbers to call me. I told him that sharing the OTP meant I acknowledged activation and he can mark it delivered then, and even worse use it, so I won't do that. In the evening, he calls with new number and tells me that he is near my home. I refused to meet him and told him that I'll send a cancel request directly to JIO. 

&amp;#x200B;

Not sure if the message gave wrong information or if the rep. was out to scam me. But, be careful guys. Have heard similar stories about courier delivery reps as well. 

Although, I hope it is not me just being paranoid, because it seems like these guys have some crazy quotas otherwise.
