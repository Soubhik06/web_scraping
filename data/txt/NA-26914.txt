I want to train you how this new ELABORATE financial crime works. These snakes are even dragging your bank into it: 

They were inches away from fleecing my husband! 

First a little fraud charge for $1.25 charged to Samsung Pay. He has Apple phone so the legit Bank of America called to alert us of suspicious activity and told us they will overnight a new card.  THIS ASPECT IS LEGITIMATE 

Well then a few hours (from scammers)  you get a text that some suspicious activity took place on your card and ask to text back yes or no if you made those charges. They were for Walmart and for quick pay Zelle. 

So then you text them NO. The problem is it is not your bank texting you. 
So then the scammers call you on the phone !!!and caller ID says Bank of America!!!! and tell you to log into your bank. Well the guy started coaching and coaxing my husband on how to set up Zelle to send them $3500 on quick pay to help with this credit card claim. "To make things right" is their rebuttal. 

Thankfully he does not understand online banking and asked my help. Well the guy sounded like an echo chamber from India. 
I walked upstairs and he was getting ready to send strangers (((thinking it was the bank)))  $3500 thinking it was to help stop fraud! 

So I demanded he hang up. 
If you ever get fraud alerts only call the bank directly. Do not deal with inbound texts and phone calls. Legit bank problems will ask you to contact them. 

Be careful. They rig the caller ID!
