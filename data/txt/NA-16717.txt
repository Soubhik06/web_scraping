Yesterday, I went in to order something and completed a successful transaction via UPI . Money debited etc, and yet Swiggy’s app insisted “we’ve placed your order, but you need to pay”. 

“Pay again?”I thought. 

I suspected some tech failure at their end. If the amount is debited and they haven’t received it, shouldn’t they just… wait. 

Here I am sitting with the debited amount, thinking wait… this has happened before and I’ve never had a refund come through. Something is surely fishy the way they say wait for 4-6 days and your amount will be credited back. Does it? Do we follow up on that? 

Anyway I had a conversation with the customer care who would not listen that it’s not normal for us to pay twice (remember, my bank told me the transaction had completed). Weird. But okay, maybe she meant transfer the chat. 

The very next second I receive a message saying ‘A refund has been initiated’. 

If there was no transaction, how can a refund be initiated? Anyway I stopped getting answers and I requested in chat that my order is cancelled. 

No response. I manually initiated a cancel. Got a message saying it would cost me $$ next time. That’s okay. 

I’d briefly read about Swiggy one being a scam (guess what I’m signed up for it), so I went in to cancel it this morning. I learnt that you can only cancel it after chatting to a sales rep. 

The message flashing on my perfectly functional phone “Your Internet is a little wonky.” 

The same Internet that’s posting this. 

I get that Swiggy is trying to push sales, but this is a new low.
