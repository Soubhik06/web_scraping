1. Small and Medium Sized Enterprises (SMEs) are the lifeblood of
India's economy. To thrive and grow, SMEs often require financial support
and this is where the banking sector steps in. SME loans are tailored
solutions that cater to the specific needs of SMEs, enabling them to prosper
and contribute to India's economic growth. These loans foster growth and
resilience of India's SME sector by financing them with working capital,
expansion or technology upgrades. However, the flip side is when some
unscruplous borrowers misuse the benefits extended to them by intentionally
defrauding the banks and thus contract credit supply, distort allocation of
credit, worsen market confidence and slow economic growth. But as they say,
"As you sow so shall you reap", the end of malice can never be fancy as future

conspiracy is hatched in private or in secrecy, it is rarely possible to establish a
conspiracy by direct evidence and usually both the existence of conspiracy and
its object have to be inferred from the circumstances and conduct of the
accused. 21.2 In fact, the essence of criminal conspiracy is the unlawful
combination and ordinarily the offence is complete when the combination is
framed. The purpose of law making conspiracy a crime is to curb the zeal to
commit mischief which is gained by combination of minds. The
encouragement and the support which conspirators give to one another

CBI No. 26/2019 (Namrita Aggarwal)
 Spl. Judge (PC Act) CBI-21/23.02.2024 114 making the criminal act possible, which if left to an individual act or effort
would not have been possible, makes the conspirators liable for punishment.
Thus, conspiracy is an agreement between two or more persons to do an
unlawful act by unlawful means. 21.3 In this case, A-1 and A-8, being members of one family had a
common intention of defrauding Corporation Bank, Aali Gaon Branch and
therefore furnished forged and fabricated documents in order to avail loan
under CVPOD scheme with an intention never to repay it as the collateral
security which was offered for availing the said loan was also not under the
ownership of the guarantor. In fact, it was Shiv Kumar Verma whose brain
child it was to cheat the bank and in order to give colour to this illegal
intention he conspired with his son(A-8) who in turn furnished false, forged
and fabricated documents and affidavits to Corporation Bank as the borrower
and the guarantor to secure the loan. It was not possible for any one of these
accused persons to cheat the bank without the help of other and thus there is a
clear cut conspiracy between them in order to defraud the bank. Further, A-2
Advocate Sandeep Sharma also conspired with A-1 and A-8 to get this loan
