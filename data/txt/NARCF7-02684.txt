In the latest episode of our podcast, we brought together voices from cities that resonate with so many of us—Bhopal, Bhubaneswar, Bangalore, and Delhi—to uncover the raw truths of social media's dark side. This episode features the courageous Ayushi, Rumani Sharma, and Hazel, who represent these cities and have bravely shared their personal battles with cyberbullying and slut shaming.

Ayushi, from Delhi, shared how she confronted her abuser online, turning her pain into a powerful stand against harassment. Anushka, a model from Bhubaneswar, spoke about the unsolicited explicit content she receives, and how she used that violation to make a bold statement. And Hazel, with roots in both Bangalore and Bhopal, opened up about the relentless hate and slut shaming she's faced, and how she's been fighting back on social media.

What makes this episode so powerful is that these stories aren't just from strangers—they're from people who walk the same streets, face the same challenges, and live in the same cities as we do.

One moment that really struck me was when Ayushi said, "We are expected to stay silent, to endure, but I refuse to let them win." Her words are a rallying cry for everyone who's ever felt cornered by the dark side of social media, especially in cities where these conversations are often swept under the rug.
