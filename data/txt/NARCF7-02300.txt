New latest scam in the market. I'd call this as modern day begging. People will come to you and they'll make scenarios wherein they say that they lost their wallet or lik they broke mobile or say forgot cash. Basically any reason to create an very urgent need of cash to reach back home or to eat. Then after they are done telling their very sad and unfortunate story they'll ask you for immediate help of just ₹50 - 100 and even promise that they'll return it after reaching home through online payment. 
After hearing their sad unfortunate story a person like me will think all this is genuine and definitely try to help them. But I realised this is a new type of scam when this happened 4 time in the past 5 days with me. At different locations, different age people and a slightly different story.

It's never about the ₹100, instead it's about the losr trust on this society.
We all know "The boy who cried wolf" story.
 
Some things that differentiate these modern day begger/scammers:

1) Very decent/normal clothing: you will not be tell the clothes that this person is indirectly cheating for just 100. 

2) New and Creative story everytime

3) Relatable factor and intelligence too

4) They portray that they need a favour and not help( promise to return the money back) 

 I have met a engineer, student, labourer, househelp and had decent knowledge about their fields like engineer demanded that he wanted to go to Hinjewadi, student guy was specifically roaming nearby the college area. The maid aunty warned cash to pay for bus fair. Etc etc

I don't know if they are genuinely in need or really smart scammers, I'm not more gonna help them
