Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and learned

 Additional Public Prosecutor for the State. 2. The petitioner seeks regular bail in connection with

 Gardanibagh P.S. Case No. 09 of 2025, registered for the

 offences punishable under Sections 318(4), 338, 336(3), 340(2)

 and 112(2) of the B.N.S., 2023 and Section 10/11 of the Bihar

 Public Examination (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024. 3. The prosecution case is to the effect that during the

 Central Selection Board Examination being conducted for the

 appointment to the post of Constable, the petitioner was found

 to be committing fraud and he had used another person in their

 place in order to secure job and during the biometric

 examination because of the differences found in the photo and

 thumb impression, the petitioner was apprehended.
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.15415 of 2025(2) dt.02-05-2025 2/3 4. The learned counsel for the petitioner submits that

 the petitioner is innocent and he has falsely been implicated in

 this case as he is a bona-fide student of the examination which

 was being conducted for appointment to the post of Constable.

 The petitioner has clean antecedent and he is in custody since

 04.01.2025. 5. Learned APP for the state vehemently opposes the

 prayer for bail of the petitioner and stated that the petitioner by

 such conduct is trying to defame the state as well as it affects the

 other candidates adversely and hence he should not be released

 on bail. 6. Considering the facts and circumstances of the

 case and taking into account the fact that the petitioner is

 students who is appearing on the examination conducted for the

 appointment to the post of Constable and taking into account

 that the petitioner has clean antecedent and he is in judicial

 custody since 04.01.2025, let the petitioner, above named, be

 released on bail on furnishing bail bond of Rs. 10,000/- (Ten

 thousand) with two sureties of the like amount each to the

 satisfaction of the court of learned Sub Judge-X-Cum-

 Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate-XIII, Patna in connection

 with Gardanibagh P.S. Case No. 09 of 2025, subject to the
