Beware of this new scam targeting Rapido captains! Here's how it unfolds: You accept a ride, and then you receive a call from the customer. They ask if you accept Google Pay, and if you say yes, they request your Google Pay number. After you provide it, they'll mention your banking name. Later, they instruct you to call them when you reach the pickup spot.

When you call, they'll claim they're giving you 800 rupees, with 200 for the ride and 600 to hand back to the rider, who is their friend(non existent customer at pickup location). If you agree, they send you a fake message stating that 800 has been credited to your account, along with a Photoshopped screenshot of a PhonePe money transfer. Don't fall for it! The SMS you receive is a duplicate, not from your bank, and the Google Pay transfer screenshot is fabricated using the banking name they obtained from your number. Stay vigilant!

JiYour a/c no. XXXXXXXX2187is credited for payment bank Rs.800.00 05-10-2023:07.19(UPI Ref no 235244663347)
