This Writ Petition is filed questioning the action of the

3rd respondent in issuing the notice under Section 91/160(102) of

Cr.P.C. dated 20.01.2022 with respect to FIR No. 1946 of 2021

and directing the 4th respondent to freeze the accounts of the

petitioner bearing Account No. 055181300002134 and wallet

accounts as arbitrary, illegal, mala fide and violative of Articles 14,

19 and 21 of fundamental rights guaranteed under the

Constitution and also Section 102(3) Cr.P.C. 2. Sri M. Sai Chandra Haas, learned counsel for the

petitioner submits that the account of the petitioner is freezed

basing on the instructions given by the respondents and by virtue

of the same, right from January, 2021, they are facing lot of

hardship. He submits that the petitioner is not an accused and

while freezing the account, the respondent / police failed to follow

the procedure under Section 102 Cr.P.C. According to the learned

counsel, no notice is given and it is not even informed to the

Magistrate forthwith as contemplated under Section 102(3) Cr.P.C.

Learned counsel submits that basing on a complaint with regard

to a loan App where on allegation of involvement of Rs.3,000/-, the

entire account of the petitioner is freezed. He submits that the 2 petitioner is a public limited company and a borrower from 1998.

He further submits that from January 2021, the petitioner was

continuously requesting the respondents to defreeze the account

and they are submitting all the relevant documents as requeseted

by them and in spite of that, no action has been taken. Learned

counsel submits that the action of the respondents in

indiscriminately freezing the accounts for a long time is contrary to

the judgment of the Apex Court in Red Carpet tech. Pvt. Ltd. v

State of TS (Manu/TL/0520/2021). He submits that by this

action of the respondents, the petitioner is put to lot of hardship

and injustice. 3. Learned Assistant Government Pleader for Home Sri

A. Manoj Kumar submits that a complaint was received on

11.12.2021 about the irregularities in loan App and basing on the

same, the police have registered Crime No. 1946 of 2021 under Sections 66(C) , 66 read with Section 43 of IT Act and under Sections 384 , 354 (D) , 506 , 509 IPC. on the file of Cyber Crime

Police Station. It is submitted that while conducting investigation,

the respondent police have sent notices to all payment gateways

with a request to furnish the beneficiary / settlement accounts for

the transactions involved in the loan App fraud cases and one of

such account is of the petitioner company - Pinnacle Capital

Solution in Kotak Mahindra Bank and Axis Bank. It is submitted 3 that as such, the respondent police sent notices to both the banks

for freezing the accounts. It is also submitted that as

contemplated under Section 102(3) Cr.P.C., on the same day ie. on

11.01.2022, the police have informed the same to the Magistrate

and the petition is numbered as SR No. 162 of 2022. Learned

Assistant Government Pleader submits that in respect of a crime,

as per Section 102 , when the police have freezed the accounts, the

petitioner has an effective alternative remedy of filing an

Application under Section 451 Cr.P.C. According to the learned

Assistant Government Pleader, without availing such remedy, the

petitioner has come up before this Court by filing this Writ

Petition. He submits that the Writ Petition itself is not

maintainable. 4. The arguments advanced by the learned counsel for

the petitioner are that 1) the respondents failed to comply with Section 102(3) Cr.P.C. and inform about freezing of accounts to

the Magistrate; 2) for a small amount of Rs.3,000/-, the accounts

are freezed by which the petitioner is put to lot of inconvenience;

and 3) right from January, 2021 till now, for such a long time, the

accounts cannot be freezed by the respondents. 5. As far as complying with Section 102(3) Cr.P.C. is

concerned, it is already submitted by the learned Assistant

Government Pleader that police have brought to the notice of the 4 XII Additional Chief Metropolitan Magistrate, Nampally on

11.01.2022 about freezing of accounts of the petitioner. Even

such defect will not go to the root of the matter and per se the

whole proceedings cannot be found fault for non-compliance of the

same. Apart from this, it is an admitted fact that so far, the

petitioner is not arrayed as an accused. Still in such a case also,

the petitioner can make an Application under Section 451 Cr.P.C.

as the said provision does not say that only the accused can make

an Application for interim custody. The petitioner's effective

remedy is to file an Application under Section 451 Cr.P.C. before

the learned Magistrate where all the material is available for

consideration. 6. The Writ Petition is accordingly, disposed of giving

liberty to the petitioner to avail the alternative remedy. There shall

be no order as to costs. 7. The Miscellaneous Applications, if any shall stand

automatically closed. ----------------------------------- LALITHA KANNEGANTI, J
