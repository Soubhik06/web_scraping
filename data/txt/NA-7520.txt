Heard counsel for the parties. The applicant has been arrested on 12.12.2014 in
connection with Crime No.390/2011 registered with Police
Station Gopalganj, District Sagar - commonly known as
VYAPAM Examination Scam cases - for the offences
punishable under Sections 417 , 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 , 472 , 474 of IPC and Section 3 (Gha) (2) read with Section 4 of
Manyata Prapt Pariksha Adhiniyam, 1937. The role ascribed to the applicant is that of a beneficiary.
The applicant with the help of racketeer/middleman - Gyan
Singh Jatav resorted to unfair means during the examination
conducted by VYAPAM. The applicant directly interacted with
racketeer/middleman - Gyan Singh Jatav, who, in turn,
facilitated commission of unfair means by the applicant during
the examination by engaging impersonator to appear on behalf
of the applicant. The said racketeer/middleman - Gyan Singh
Jatav has expired. It is, fairly, stated by the learned counsel for the State, on
instructions, that custodial interrogation of the applicant is no
more required. The link between the accused persons is already
 complete. Charge-sheet has also been filed against the
applicant on 11/3/2015. Further, the investigation against the
applicant is complete in all respects as of now and, therefore,
the presence of applicant is no more required. Further, the
applicant is not involved in any other crime of similar type.
Besides, the applicant through counsel undertakes to appear
before the Investigating Officer as and when called for further
investigation including against him as also to punctually
appear before the trial Court to ensure early disposal of the
criminal trial. In the interest of justice, taking overall view of the
matter, we deem it appropriate to grant bail to the applicant on
following strict conditions:- The bail application stands disposed of accordingly.
