I am from India, I got a message from an foreign number that they are providing some services to the top companies like google. The will give a first task, Just by searching a keyword in google or by review any hotel in google maps. And they will also credit you some fund 150 - 200 rupees. Once after that they will ask you to join a telegram group where they post other tasks. 

Some old days scams are asking people to pay some initial deposit to start the earning. Now they came with some smarter idea by gaining the trust by a very minimal amount. And they will ask you to pay a bulk amount in one of the task very randomly.
