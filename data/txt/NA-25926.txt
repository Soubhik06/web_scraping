So my friend's dad was diagnosed with HCC(hepatocellular carcinoma) stage 4 almost a year ago, which has very poor prognosis. Doctors gave him 5-6 months at max.

The treatment options were very limited, just a couple of chemotherapy drugs and immunotherapy. After multiple sessions of immunotherapy, there was no improvement; in fact, his condition worsened. That’s when the family started looking into alternative medicines like Ayurveda and some diet. I was against these, but in such situations, people just want to try everything and hope for a miracle.

1. Punarjan ayurveda - Claims he had done more than 25 years of cancer research and can cure cancer without chemotherapies or radiation. He gives some 5-6 bottles of medicines with some herbs and all( calls them inmunoboost).

2. Naati vaidya narayan murthy - He is so famous, 1000s of people visit this guy. they send some powder to make a soup kind of thing and drink everyday.

3.**VRK** (The worst of all)- This person is a complete fraud, giving false promises and spouting random bullshit that many people just blindly believe. He even told my friend that the immunotherapy made him worse and claimed he should’ve come to him earlier and followed his diet from the start (audacity!).

There was another patient complaining that his illness hadn’t improved despite following all the instructions. His reply was baffling. He accused the patient of not strictly adhering to his diet. When the patient insisted he had followed everything except for maybe missing one or two days a month, this fraudster said, *"That’s why your illness hasn’t reduced. You must follow it every single day without fail."*

His treatment includes random medicines and *COCONUT OIL*, and he charges over ₹50,000.

4. **A Quack in Parigi (Vikarabad District)**\- Someone told my friend about this man who supposedly cures any disease. There were literally hundreds of people waiting to see him, some from different states. This quack also said we should’ve come to him earlier. He then wrote down names of few items on a slip and asked to get them from the shop outside . You wouldn’t believe what it included: cow dung (to mix with milk and drink) and a cow urine tonic.

These quacks thrive by exploiting the suffering of others. Most of the people who visit them are poor and from rural areas, which makes it even worse because they’re being robbed of both their money and access to real medical treatment, all while being fed false hope.
