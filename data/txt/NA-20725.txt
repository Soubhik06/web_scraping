UPDATE:

It took some searching (yay bureaucracy!), but since they kindly provided the tariff code 98049000, I looked it up on the [CBIC website](https://www.cbic.gov.in/htdocs-cbec/customs/cst2021-300621/cst-idx) and found [Chapter 98](https://www.cbic.gov.in/resources//htdocs-cbec/customs/cst2021-300621/chap-98.pdf) where it shows the duty cannot be more than 35%.

Now that we know it is a lie, is there any avenue to just lose the tariff completely? Or will he still have to pay that 35%? 
For reference it is an exercise device to be held in the hand to exercise the wrist. Like a finger strengthener used by musicians, but round.
