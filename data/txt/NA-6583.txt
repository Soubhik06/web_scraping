1. The petitioner is seeking the concession of bail under Section

439 of the Cr.P.C. in case FIR No.174 dated 26.04.2024 under Sections

420 , 467 , 468 , 120-B of the IPC registered at Police Station Sector 17,

HUDA, Jagadhri, District Yamuna Nagar. 2. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the petitioner

has been in custody since 26.04.2024 in a magisterial trial wherein false

allegations have been levelled against him that he had tampered and forged

some stamp papers. It has been further submitted by the learned counsel

that the case at hand rests on documentary evidence and the investigation in

the present case is complete as even challan stands presented. Hence,

further incarceration of the petitioner would serve no useful purpose as

none of the 11 prosecution witnesses have been examined so far. 3. Per contra, learned State counsel, while opposing the prayer

and submissions made by learned counsel for the petitioner, has not
