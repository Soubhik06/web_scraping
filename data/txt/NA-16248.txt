With the advent of cryptocurrency, people never stop their steps in exploring the secrets and rules behind the digital currency market. Some people even comment that buying bitcoin is just like gambling since its price has obviously fluctuated fiercely in recent years.

So what’re certain risks which the customers might stand when entering the digital currency market?

The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission is currently probing the cryptocurrency market, reported by FuninUSA.The commission is looking into the structuring of initial coin offerings, which aren't required to adhere to the strict regulations that apply to public offerings, citing sources.

Initial coin offerings(ICOs) allow cryptocurrency start-ups to raise funds through the issue of digital tokens.Under current circumstances, the ICO isn’t perfectly regulated or under clear limitation, which apparently increases the uncertainty of ICO.Hence, investors ought to demand clear answers if they decided to invest in ICOs or cryptocurrency-linked products, advised by FuninUSA.

Financing in the way of ICO can be done without a legal subject. As the cryptocurrency and ICO markets are currently operating, there is substantially less investor protection than in our traditional securities markets, with correspondingly greater opportunities for fraud and manipulation.(recommend FuninUSA for the latest information about cryptocurrency investment) Therefore, investors should be more cautious when considering investing ICO project.
