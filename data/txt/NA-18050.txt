I recently started getting manga ads on my insta and got one from:-

 https://www.bookishadda.com

Anyone who is planning on purchasing manga from this or the others listed below (also added links to ones that offer original ones) please note that these are not original manga’s. These websites are selling one volume of manga for 200-300 rupees, Berserk Vol 1-11 Set for 1400 😂, that’s the cost of original Vol 1 &amp; 2 combined (Approx)

1. Even in Japan (Japanese version) the manga cost $3.5 - $4. Here they are selling Translated one (English Version) even below that which is not possible due to licensing fee and translation fee, the cost increases and it’s not possible to.

2. There are indiscrepancies in the page no., ISBN numbers.

3. The conditions of the books in the images provided by them on the listing pages aren’t great, edges are roughed out.

Next one is just blatantly katna logo ka 😂
4. Solo leveling (not an exception) in all these cheap manga websites is in black and white, even tough as a manhwa it should be in color, their are no official black and white editions as these websites list them. They have this edition cause black and white is cheaper and that makes it feasible to them to sell you at these cheap rate.

5. Just like last example of solo leveling, in their product listing, they have images of pages, which are in black &amp; white btw 😂, the images quality and the text character quality is literally so bad that is screams fake just by the looks of it. (have seen same things in others too on their site, for consistency using Solo leveling again for example for consistency)

I don’t want to demean anyone who is choosing for these options, as to be real, 600-800 per volume, and then going on to keep a collection of all volumes costing you from 16K - 30K is a big price, so someone who can’t spend that much and still want to use these for collection, it’s your choice no hate I understand.

BUT, 
Promotes these fakes and takes that money away from the original creator.

These ones hold no value as a collector item if let’s say you maintain them and re sell them years down the line as they are fake. 
(In terms of collector value, English Ones are of less value compared to Japanese Ones)
