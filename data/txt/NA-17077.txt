RBI has come up with new directions for Credit &amp; Debit cards. These will apply from July 1 2022.

Some notable additions:

1. The issue of unsolicited cards/upgradation is strictly prohibited. In case, an unsolicited card is issued/existing card upgraded and activated without the explicit consent of the recipient and the latter is billed for the same, the card-issuer shall not only reverse the charges forthwith, but also pay a penalty without demur to the recipient amounting to twice the value of the charges reversed.

2. Card-issuers shall seek One Time Password (OTP) based consent from the cardholder for activating a credit card

3. If a credit card has not been used for a period of more than one year, the process to close the card shall be initiated after intimating the cardholder

4. No charges shall be levied on transactions disputed as ‘fraud’ by the cardholder until the dispute is resolved.

5. Any credit amount arising out of refund/failed/reversed transactions or similar transactions before the due date of payment for which payment has not been made by the cardholder, shall be immediately adjusted against the ‘payment due’ and notified to the cardholder.

The last two seem to be really helpful to deal with disputes/fraud as right now we are forced to pay charges and wait for resolution.
