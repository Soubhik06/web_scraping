I almost fell into a scam...

Around 2:30 this morning, I was woken up by my watch and phone vibrating; an 888 number was calling me. I pressed decline. Immediately, they called again and I declined. They called again, declined and then I finally picked up the next time because I was worried something happened to someone I know and it was a toll free number from a hospital or something.

It was an automated recording of a woman's voice saying my AfterPay account was hacked and I need to enter the six-digit verification code that was sent to me. Sure enough, I get a text about a code. I enter it into the phone (keep in mind I'm half asleep and sort of freaked out from repeated late night calls); it hangs up and then they start calling again and again.

 
I realize that this was definitely phishing and I log on to AfterPay and change my password. The calls stop. I look at my text messages and emails and find that they logged into my account from India (I didn't see this on the texts originally because it wasn't in the notification preview) and they changed my password and requested verification codes three times. 

Thankfully my AfterPay account has a unique password from my other accounts. I last used AfterPay two years ago and I guess they erase your payment information after a while because my card wasn't listed so I doubt they would have been able to buy anything anyway although they could probably do other things.

 
Not sure if calling late at night was intentional or just because of time zone differences but they almost got me because I was half asleep and my brain wasn't fully functioning.
