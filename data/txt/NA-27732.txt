I received a call earlier today from California. The number that called me was a 626 area code. They claimed to be an agent with the CBP and said that 3 packages were flagged coming out of Mexico and Columbia and that the had my name as the recipient. They said that the receiving addresses of the packages were a location in El Paso Texas and then my home address in NJ. They proceeded to tell me that the packages contained illegal substances and that my identity could be a part of drug trafficking and that I could be a victim of identity theft. They then went over my banking information. (No I didn’t give anything personally out to them other than that I use chase as my primary bank) and they claimed there have been accounts with other banks opened in my name. Now this all seemed very real at first as they provided a case number along with a badge number. However I was transferred back and forth between this agent and a “US Marshall” whose number showed up unknown. I did notice that both of them sounded like they were calling from a call center based on the background noise and they both did have slight Indian accents from what I could tell. They then claimed that I will get a visit from the local Marshalls office tomorrow morning and will have to meet with them. Now the issue is that they didn’t directly ask for any money but I was getting the vibe they were going to ask for my social so I hung up and they never called back. I am usually pretty good about identifying scam callers but have never had someone pretending to be from the CBP. I looked it up on their website and saw there’s an issue with people calling pretending to be agents. Has this happened to anyone else? Is this what happened in my case today?
