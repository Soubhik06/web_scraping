So I am looking for a 2BHK to rent, and got in touch with a "property owner" at DLF Oakwood Estate, Phase 2. The property seemed nice, the rent was perfect. I contacted him, and asked him if a visit could be scheduled, he insisted that it should be within the next few days as a lot of people are looking at it, and if I am interested I can alternatively hold the unit by paying some amount. I told him I will visit on Monday.

He asked me to send him my details, because a visitor's pass needs to be issued without which I cannot enter the society, and then told me that I need to pay Rs.2000 as surety, which would be refunded to me. I told him that the site through which I found his contact specifically warns not to pay any visitation charge, he said it's society rules falana dhimkana. 

This is a scam right?
