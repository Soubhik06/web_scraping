--- POST TEXT ---

Booked an Uber to go to howrah station yesterday night around 9pm. Driver asked fare, i told him ₹340, he asked cash or online, I told cash, so he agreed.

Now i had some luggage and had 2 friends too. We boarded the cab, i informed him the PIN and he started the ride.

Now here is the scam, he took the PIN, but didn't enter in his app. Also later i realized he didn't put his phone on the dashboard so we could see. I reached howrah station and paid him ₹340 only.

Then after 5 mins i started getting a call from Uber driver that he is waiting for me at my pickup location, I informed but my ride is already over, that's when it stuck me that the earlier driver didn't start the ride and upon reaching the destination he must have transferred it to other driver.

So be careful out there guys, because if anything happens during the ride, Uber will not take responsibility as its not an official ride.
