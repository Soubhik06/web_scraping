--- POST TEXT ---

My father recently retired and moved back to our hometown. He received a call claiming to be from Gail Gas, the gas pipeline company, around 10 am. The caller said they needed a small fee (Rs. 5) to resume the gas pipeline connection process my parents had recently applied for. The scammer had access to my parents' customer ID details related to their gas pipeline application, which immediately made them seem legitimate. The scammer convinced my father to install an app via a WhatsApp link. My father didn't know .apk file are for app and installed it. Inside the app, he asked my father to fill in their card details to pay this small fee, as scammer claimed it to be a legitimate/official website. He also tricked my father into enabling call forwarding asking for father to dial a specific sequence (\*\* followed by &lt;his number&gt; #, probably, not sure). This allowed the scammer to receive all OTPs (one-time passwords). The scammer managed to steal 29,000 INR. There were further attempted transactions of 50k and 29k, and more for next 2 hours which thankfully failed, as my parents contacted relatives for suggestion, to block cards and UPI. My parents have blocked their cards and UPI accounts. They've also filed a cybercrime complaint. (They sent me a screenshot of acknowledgement ID of complaint).

 
My questions:

Has anyone encountered a similar Gail Gas-related scam in India recently? 
 
What are the chances of recovering the stolen funds? 
 
What other preventative steps can I take to protect my parents from future attacks? I have told them to take relevant photo of Whatsapp communication, using other phoe, factory reset their phone, and change, Gmail, FB, etc, social media passwords. 
 
Are there any additional reporting bodies I should contact beyond local cybercrime?

Any other advice or insights would be greatly appreciated.
