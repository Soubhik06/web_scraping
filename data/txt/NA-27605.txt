Hello, I read the rules and I hope my post does not violate any of them. I came home for a break from work and my mother was on the phone with someone whose voice I didn't recognize. It sounded like this person was in India or Pakistan. I was worried by the sound of the phone call and went into her room to tell her to make sure she is not giving any information to someone that she doesn't know over the phone or the internet. She said that's not what she's doing and so I left and went to lie down. She came in a few minutes later to ask for my help.

She had turned on her computer, clicked on a link in this email, and allowed the guy on the phone to have access to her computer. I'm not sure if she downloaded anything to let him do this. She is on the phone with her bank now and I have to go back to work.

I told her to turn off her computer and call her bank and change all her passwords and have her accounts locked for now. I told her not to turn the computer back on until my brother can come over and run scans for viruses and malware. 

The email was one of those fake confirmation emails. It had a fake order number and a receipt for what looked like some TVs. The return address was not Amazon, it was a personal account. Is there anything else I should be telling her to do to protect herself? She says that she did not give the guy any personal information except her cell phone number, and of course, let him on to her computer.

Thanks for your help.

EDIT: I just heard from my brother. He says they went in a tax folder on her computer and lifted her SSN.
