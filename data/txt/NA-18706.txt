So, this happened yesterday:

A person called my dad at midnight. He said he transferred money by mistake to the wrong phone number as he was in a hurry at some hospital. He had to pay the bill and requested to transfer the money immediately to his phone number to pay off the bill.

He also shared the UPI screenshot with an arrow mark the screenshot. The arrow was pointing towards my dad's number. The UPI id was something like ```bharatpe&lt;phone_number&gt;@yesbankltd```. My dad could not understand what was happening as he usually relies on bank SMS for confirmation.

So, they played a trick using custom UPI id + urgent situation + late night call + elderly people.

I'm sure most people here can easily find out about the scam, but I'm sharing it here so that you can educate elderly people in your family.
