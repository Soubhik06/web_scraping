Mahesh Peri, who was publisher of Outlook and now runs "Career 360", reports that Modi *kaka*'s close associates and buddies seem to run the portal and Gujarat based company that "process" these loan applications to the banks.

The "59 minute loan" scheme for MSME, announced by Modi *kaka* recently, works this way -

1. You have to register with a website.
2. You have to submit your loan application.
3. **You have to pay an application processing fee of ₹1000 + tax.**
4. You will immediately receive a "letter of approval" by email.
5. Then the bank will call you and ask you to submit documents.
6. In 5 weeks the bank will tell you if you got the loan.

The portal loan application reply is from an email sent by **CapitaWorld**.

- Capitaworld Platform Private Limited is a company registered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat in March 2015, and the authorised signatory are Jinand Shah and Vikas Shah.
- The company's total revenue till March 2017 was ₹15,000.
- One of the director, **Vinod Modha**, is strategic advisor for corporates including Nirma and Mudra (earlier Owned by **Anil Ambani**).
- After March 2018, the company inducted 4 new directors. One of them, **Akhil Handa** ran Modi's 2014 campaign.

**Further, if your loan application is approved 0.35% of it will be deducted from you as payment for further "processing" fee!**

Scroll.in points out further shadiness:

&gt; The tender went on to define eligibility requirements. To qualify, consultants needed to have earned a fee of at least Rs 50 crore from management consulting during the three preceding years. *“The consultant should have been in existence in India since April 01, 2012,”* it stipulated.

&gt; Once a consultant was shortlisted, said the tender, a new legal entity would be formed, in the form of a company with SIDBI and other banks as shareholders.

&gt; **However, a closer look at psbloansin59minutes shows that the company behind it does not quite meet the tender stipualtions. The website is not run by a new legal entity as envisioned by the SIDBI tender. Instead, it is run by a Ahmedabad-headquartered fintech company called CapitaWorld Platform, which was set up in 2015.**
