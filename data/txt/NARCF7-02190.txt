--- POST TEXT ---

A short while ago, my father received an SMS, prompting me to step in and make a call to address the situation. The individual on the other end inquired about payment methods and customer IDs, which I didn't disclose. In response, they became upset, threatened to impose a fine of Rs 7000, and even threatened to disconnect my electricity service. I cross-checked the number using Truecaller, which indicated it was from the Electricity Bill Department. Subsequently, I contacted the CESC customer care, who confirmed that it was indeed a scam. The way these scammers operate makes it challenging for the elderly to deal with such issues.
