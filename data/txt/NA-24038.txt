Posting this here because I believe this concerns freshers and working professionals from most industries. 

Due to a few traumatic past experiences with some of the world's shittiest HR professionals (most of them unfortunately women), I am highly concerned about these kind of workplace review websites and what amount of my information is available on these websites. 

I recently signed up to Ambition Box because I was trying to look at the reviews of a dream company I am applying to. I used Google to sign up to it and so my Ambition Box profile now shows my profile picture. However, it STILL didn't show me any reviews until I provided reviews of all the companies I worked for. (Ofcourse, at this moment I am tempted to provide some fake reviews of some fake companies and get by but my profile picture is STILL on the website and I have no clue if any of this is public information or viewable by some paid accounts (?))

i then discovered there is NO WAY to delete your information on Ambition Box's entire website. How is this even possible? Isn't this illegal?
Moreover, why would I share such deeply personal information about my previous work experience with a website with such shady privacy practices?

Request everyone here to be wary of such websites.
