**The Wire Report**: [Assam: A Flyover Inauguration, a Tender and a Web of Companies Connected to CM’s Family](https://thewire.in/government/assam-a-flyover-inauguration-a-tender-and-a-web-of-companies-connected-to-cms-family) || [Also a Dec 2021 report in the similar context](https://thewire.in/government/assam-government-land-himanta-biswa-sarma-rbs-realtors)

What **The Wire** reports is quite interesting. TLDR: *Himanta Biswa Sharma's family members (wife, mother, mother-in-law, maternal uncle of wife, and of course old aides) are involved in a series of companies that have strange Kolkata connections.*

&amp;#x200B;

1. The (three) companies have got sketchy business activities related to government tenders (details in the report but the main allegation is nepotism)
2. **Speaking about Kolkata, these companies use some addresses in the notorious areas of Barabazar, but none of this three companies can be traced there. No office, no business activity, no physical presence, yet the companies share the same Barrabazar-Kolkata address.**
3. **Also, these three companies keep getting money pumped in into them from as many as 51 companies based in Kolkata under unclear circumstances!**

&amp;#x200B;

I have written time and again in this subreddit about the strong presence of shell companies in Kolkata. Wherever a scam is unearthed in India, the money trail somehow happens to pass through a bunch of shell companies in Kolkata. From perfume seller of Kanpur to Mamata Banerjee's trusted lieutenant and the former of education minister of Bengal --- the picture remains same.

The fake call centers and Infotech frauds of Sector V continue to draw our attention, of course because of the media buzz, but shell companies in Kolkata comprise a different beast and have been operating for a much longer period of time. My personal guess is that it chews a much, much bigger pie compared to the fake call center industry of Kolkata/Siliguri. The usual money laundering route is Kolkata shell companies -&gt; Bangladesh -&gt; Middle East and then the flood gate opens.
