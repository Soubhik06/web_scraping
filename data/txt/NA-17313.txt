TITLE: To people who use their debit cards for international transactions : You can avoid online theft by having two cards joined to two accounts, one which has international activated and one which doesn't. Keep all your money in the domestic account and transfer it to the international one when needed.

It'll cost you double what you used to pay for the card, but at least now you never need to worry about online theft, because even if someone gets your domestic debit card details, they can't use it without generating an OTP, which they simply can't get. Sure, with credit cards, you can dispute the fraudulent transaction, but there's always a chance that your bank will be uncooperative and you'll still have to deal with stress.
