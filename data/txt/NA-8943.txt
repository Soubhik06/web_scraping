: The present petition has been filed under Section 482 of the Code of
 Criminal Procedure for quashing the entire criminal proceeding in connection with
 Sonahatu P.S. Case no. 7/2014, corresponding to G.R. No. 832/2014 for the
 offence punishable under Sections 409 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 and 34 of the Indian
 Penal Code, pending in the court of learned Judicial Magistrate, 1st Class, Ranchi. Learned counsel for the petitioner by referring Annexure-1 to this petition,
 pointed out that the present FIR has been instituted on 11.2.2014 in respect of the
 alleged incident occurred on 30.6.2010. Thus it was filed at a belated stage i..e. after
 the lapse of about 3 ½ years. It is also submitted that the complainant took the loan
 from the Bank where the petitioner is discharging his duty as Computer Terminal
 Operator. Since the complainant has become defaulter, a proceeding was also
 initiated against him before Debt Recovery Tribunal. It is lastly submitted that a false
 and fabricated case has been instituted against the petitioner, who is the Bank
 employee, with a view to undue harassment. Learned APP appearing for the State submitted that since the present case is
 arising out of originally complaint case, notice may be issued to the complainant. In view of the above position, let notice be issued upon the complainant, for
 which, requisites etc. under registered cover with A/D as well as ordinary process
 must be filed on or before 28th November, 2014, failing which, the interim relief(s)
 granted by this Court shall stand vacated without making further reference to this
 Court . Put up this case on 06th January, 2015. In the meantime, counter affidavit, if any, must be filed.
 Having regard to the above submissions, till the next date of listing, no
 coercive steps shall be taken against the petitioner in connection with Sonahatu
 P.S. Case no. 7/2014, corresponding to G.R. No. 832/2014, pending in the court of
 learned Judicial Magistrate, 1st Class, Ranchi. Let this order be communicated to the court below concerned through FAX
 at the cost of the petitioner.
