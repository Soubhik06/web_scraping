Be aware if you're planning on getting some cheap hosting.

They will sell your details to anyone who will buy them within a few hours of registering.

You will get calls from India, but the numbers will vary globally (one minute it's Indonesia, the next minute it's the USA, but it's always an Indian person on the other end)

The actual scams are pretty basic, the Microsoft Windows "we detected a problem with your computer" scam, all the way through to "fantastic investment opportunity".

The numbers change constantly, and some are too long or too short or a mixture of letters too, so this makes blocking them next to impossible.

I had to get a new number because there were upward of 60 calls a day.

1and1 customer support denied any involvement, but it's ridiculously obvious.

You have been warned.
