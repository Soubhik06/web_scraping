That price was for a check-in bag too, but I told them i don't have a check in bag, I just need a carry on and the most I should pay for a carry on is $50. Don't trust any good reviews you see about this company. I spoke to Indian agents. Any good reviews are likely fake using their army of Indian agents. They get aggressive and rude when you confront them about their scam. Do not use this company!!!
