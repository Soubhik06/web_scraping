Every month or so, I get visited by a young college kid asking for donations on behalf of NGOs. On every brochure there is a mention of Support India Private Limited. Seems they are some kind of outsourcing company for collecting donations and the kids are working on their behalf. But the kids say they are from XYZ NGO.

Also when I ask them to give me address of their regional office so that I can visit there and donate directly, they start giving some bullshit explanations for not collecting donations directly or online.

I am pretty sure Support Direct charges some money for collecting donations. Why the fuck do NGOs tie up with them? If they don't, then is this some kind of a scam?
