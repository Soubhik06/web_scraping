 In the matter of: It is submitted by Mr. Dastoor, learned Additional

Solicitor General that he also endorses the same view 3 regarding expeditious disposal of the criminal case. But

he requires to get certain instructions from the

Investigating Officer who is now stationed in Bombay. Moreover, in order to dispose of the instant

revision this Court will have to come to a finding in

view of the fact that in this case some M.L.A.s and M.P.s

are also arrayed as accused persons. Along with them

there are individual accused persons. The offence for

which the accused persons were charge sheeted are all

triable by the Court of the learned Magistrate. In view of such matter and when Special Courts

for trial of M.L.A.s and M.P.s. have been set up, the

cases are required to be sent to the Court of the learned

Special Judge responsible for hearing of the cases

against the M.L.A.s and M.P.s and the remaining cases

shall be tried and disposed of by the learned Judicial

Magistrate, First Class. In view of such circumstances, there may be

conflicting decisions in the same case by two different

Courts. Mr. Dastoor also submits that the Investigating

Officer may be directed to be present in Court on the

next date of hearing. The matter is heard-in-part. The Investigating Officer, C.B.I. is directed to

remain present on 12th May, 2021. 4 The learned advocates at the Bar are earnestly

requested to come not with the problems but with the

solutions on the next date of hearing. (Bibek Chaudhuri, J.)
