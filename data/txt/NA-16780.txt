The backdrop - BJP bags over 12-times the donation of all other parties in aggregate (source - [https://www.moneylife.in/article/bjp-bags-over-12-times-the-donations-of-all-other-parties-aggregate-report/56135.html](https://www.moneylife.in/article/bjp-bags-over-12-times-the-donations-of-all-other-parties-aggregate-report/56135.html))

So BJP introduced an anonymous way of campaign financing with the introduction of electoral bonds. What are they?

&gt;The EB scheme allows any person (including HUF), company, firm, charitable trust or unincorporated body of individuals to buy EBs from the designated branches of SBI during each window of sale that the government announces from time to time. EBs are in the nature of promissory notes and carry no rate of interest. **The identity of the buyer is not recorded on the EB.**

I was reading about the the "robber baron" days of the US where crony capitalism found it's way into the functioning of the government and how fortunes were built through regulatory capture.

I think this is an inevitable phase in the evolution of a nascent democracy. People are now totally outraged over outright scams and theft of public property like the 2G and Coal scam.

So what is the next stage?

Politicians get cozy with businessmen, relatives of politicians and bureaucrats sprout up businesses related to public sector infra projects or in industries with heavy regulation and licensing. Laws get bent, policy decisions are made before an Indian business heavyweight enters a sector. Why will anybody indulge in outright theft of public resources anymore when they can acquire wealth through cronyism?

Consider this happens on 27th Dec 2018: New FDI guidelines shake up the E-Commerce Biz in India (source - [https://tech.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/internet/the-new-fdi-guidelines-likely-to-shakeup-the-e-commerce-biz-in-india/67262264](https://tech.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/internet/the-new-fdi-guidelines-likely-to-shakeup-the-e-commerce-biz-in-india/67262264))

And this happens on 18th Jan 2019: Reliance Retail, Jio to launch new e-commerce platform (source - [https://www.livemint.com/Companies/6NOjBdCIogiGboMvYSP7ZN/Reliance-Retail-Jio-to-launch-new-ecommerce-platform.html](https://www.livemint.com/Companies/6NOjBdCIogiGboMvYSP7ZN/Reliance-Retail-Jio-to-launch-new-ecommerce-platform.html))

So the crux of the matter is that the Govt. imposes restrictions on Amazon and Flipkart (because they are foreign-owned) from "influencing" the market place with discounts and cashbacks. But the same restrictions will not apply to Reliance Jio because it is not foreign-owned. So, Amazon is being hated on for using AWS money to lure customers in India but Reliance Jio is alright for using it's petroleum refining monopoly money in India to lure customers.

I don't want to get into the Rafale thing because it's outside my circle of competence to understand but there seems to be some nudging to get Anil Ambani on his feet in a new lucrative sector.

You can be damn sure the Electoral Bond Scheme plays a role in these sort of situations. It's just common sense.

And there are numerous examples if you observe your local MLA. He will probably have a relative or son doing seemingly very well in business. A simple glance at a site like www.myneta.info throws up all sorts of data of how assets of politicians increase after their term. It is right in our faces that politicians and their relative's wealth increase if they are in office. You can see it in the cars they travel in, their houses in prime real estate locations etc.

So what do we do about it? Well, US history tells us that the barons do get rich and their heirs become an elite masked in foundations and trusts and go on to live a relatively carefree life. This will happen in India as well. The best you can do for yourself is start a business and not really think about all this because you are just a small cog in a huge machine. And maybe talk about it with as many people as you can. Who knows, maybe the internet will the differentiating factor in history.
