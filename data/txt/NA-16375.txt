Online delivery scams are pretty common. Found a new one today. I am sure it has happened to others but first time for me. My family has started calling it, " Tu mera bhala kar, mein tera karunga scam." 

Ordered a laptop under exchange. My exchange laptop itself is almost 10 years old and we were getting a pretty good exchange rate on this ( Around 6200.)

First laptop got delayed twice. From Friday to Saturday, and then to Monday. Finally, the delivery person came and ran some tests. And here the scam starts...

They modified the inputs on whatever app they were using to increase my laptops worth. My i3 3rd Gen processor turned into i3 8th gen. My broken trackpad was now working and my dead battery was now working great. I am not sure how is this even allowed. But anyway, the exchange value increases by almost Rs. 2800. , as per Flipkart Exchange Summary. 

The delivery person then asked for Rs. 3000 for all this. Ofc he was not straightforward about this and kept on beating around the bush all the while claiming this is offical and how Flipkart processes things. Asked for reciept, bill or any official summary. Nothing to show that this is anywhere close to official.

It was pretty clear what all this was about.He would help us fudge the return value and take some "fees" for it. When we refused to pay him, he took back the laptop and left. Probably fuming we refused to be part of this 'scam.'

In the end, we are cancelling the order. It's not about money but principles. Have also raised a complaint but not sure where this will go.

Have ordered several things with exchange from Amazon, and have never faced any such issues.

The whole thing has left a pretty bad taste in mouth and I will be staying away from Flipkart for a while.
