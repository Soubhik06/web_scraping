Hey All,

I just got scammed trying to set up a new Canon printer online and want to inform everybody. Apologies if it's already been posted. New here just to post this.

So...I typed in the website address to set up the new printer. The site I went to said it couldn't be set up as there was an error code. I called the help line on the website. A man answered with an Indian accent. He told me to go to a certain website that would help him figure out the problem. I believe it was "[support.me](https://support.me)". I did so and he gained access to my computer, like remotely took it over. A box popped up and said his name was Shawn Walker. (Starting to get suspicious). He took over my mouse and I noticed I had a couple windows open (my budget and my email). When I tried to take over my mouse to close them he insisted he couldn't see anything and would close them himself which he did super slowly. When I tried again to close them myself he got snarky and yelled "I will close them". (OK, red flags everywhere).

He pulled up a window and showed me that I had the Trojan virus and this was why I couldn't hook up the printer. Then he called me by my name. I hadn't mentioned it. (Freaking out!) I turned off the computer and then changed all my passwords.

When I tried to set up the printer again I saw my mistake. I had typed ij.start.canon but then clicked a link that took me to ijstartcanon.

I am so upset that I got scammed, that I didn't clue in sooner. I think I can usually smell a scam a mile away. Looks like all of my banking stuff, Rev Can, etc is ok. I changed my passwords for everything including my computer's start up. Is there anything else I should do?

Thanks Reddit
