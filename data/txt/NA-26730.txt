I'm seeing lot of posts where people are stuck in bad debt due to personal loans taken from NBFC and Chinese loan apps with ridiculous interest and unethical recovery method. 

OGs of these sub may know this method , alternative of these apps could be government approved co operative society, talk to you father and uncles chances are they might be associated with some society where they help their members financially by giving loans at a very less interest (around 8% or less) . This is less than any bank or financial institute .

This is method is also helpful to those who have less or no cibil score. The only downside is you cannot get huge amount ( maximum 2 lakh depending on the size of society) but better than taking loans from these fraud apps. 

Also some society also provide housing loans at a very low interest ( my father took one) but getting into that society is tough. 

You cannot join the society on your own. You need some to recommend you as that person will be the guarantor for your admission. But talk to the uncles of your society, pay your dues on time and you good to go. 

If you default and don't pay the recovery method is still bad but better than those goons.
