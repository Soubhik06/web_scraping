Dear reader,

This is my first post and probably will be my last.

I've seen some posts here and there concerning random sms message. Usually starts with "CHAT SERVICE" and gives a first name usually with a number following it (Example: Franchesca21, Simon32, Samantha24 etc). They're actually from this website no one has ever heard of called **cell.chat**!

I actually replied back to the person that texted me and we spoke an entirety of 2 weeks. Talking with him was actually a lot of fun and the guy helped me out in a lot of ways. Tbh, while I still believe it's a kind of scam, I am thankful that I spoke to him. We stopped speaking because I just couldn't trust him or that website and I had my reasons......

To clarify: there's no harm in texting the person back. However I don't recommend because they're really good at roping you in. The scam comes in after a few texts down the line and you get a message from their system saying that in order for the recipient to receive your last message you have to purchase coins. And after a while it gets pricey.

Where it gets tricky is that despite the website looking low budget as hell, it's actually a legit business backed by this company called **American Internet Tech**. I made sure to run all of my purchases through PayPal and through my credit card. Because of that, I was able to dispute a charge and get my money back.

Finding info about the website was super hard. From both cell.chat and AIT there's nothing good or bad written about them (and that's also the reason I am making this post). What I do know is that it's ran by some Greek businessmen. The person that I was speaking to wasn't your typical romance scammer from Nigeria or someone from India. He said he was Italian (born in Naples, came to US when he was 15) and from the details that he shared with me down to some typos and how he texted, I could tell he spoke a Romance language. It also wasn't easy to gauge this guy as a scammer. Like he was good and the convos were on point. We spoke about everything! Religion, travel, work, movies, cooking lol, his sister recommended some amazing skin care for my maskne haha. It was fun talking to him and I have no doubt that he wasn't at least mostly truthful about his interests. He was super detailed and there weren't any inconsistencies until it got to the part of how he got on the website in the first place loll. He had me fooled!

About the business itself , you can apparently apply for a job there. But in all my theory is that these people are definitely a part of a team. They've been perfecting their "business" since 2017 and I am so salty there isn't more info on this because I'm sure more and more people have gotten a message from them at some point in their life.

If anyone has any info or experience with cell.chat, I am so curious to hear. My situation happened in December of last year. I regret not combing through Reddit (could've saved money), but info about them is just really hard to find. I am open as well to answer any questions you may have about my experience with them too. I'm hoping that if anyone was dealing with this at any point (which honestly I feel like the only alien that fell for it), that they could at least see this post and get some conclusive answers. Not sure if there was another person that's made a detailed post about this but I haven't found it.

A part of me hopes that I've got the wrong conclusion.. but what I've found points to this site being mostly a scam.

**So tl;dr:** **If you receive a SMS message from a random number and it starts with "CHAT SERVICE: from Sarah25" it's from a pay per message website called cell.chat backed by a company called American Internet Tech.**

And FYI if you did accidentally text back, you won't get a phantom charge or malware on your phone. You yourself have to purchase these coins. How they got our info is a mystery. On their website they have a FAQ with this particular question asked and it's never clearly answered. Also it's free to sign up. They match you with some random computer generated profiles of other peoples info they've gotten. You also can't choose who you want to speak with. It's just weird....

Hope you can find this post informative!
