Got a random person messaging me from LinkedIn offering a "service desk" job, the company is a ghost. It's got a site, but it's not popping up on any search at all so it must be new. Company claims it's operating out of Germany, but domain is registered in Iceland of all places.

They claim to be a cryptocurrency middleman, the job offer claims 4 hour days, 100% work from home, and $3,000 monthly after your one month free training is finished and on completion of the training you'll be paid for that month.

I was curious enough to give them a Google voice number just to see what the scam was. I got a call from a woman with a shitty call center connection and an Indian accent who claimed her name was a very generic English name. She was really desperate to get me to fill out the form she sent. I thought that'd be the hook, but nope. Name, address, three references, etc.

So what's the hook?
