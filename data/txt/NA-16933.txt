The "sanskari friend" sparked something in me. My parents are the most sanskari people I know. They follow every stupid ritual as much as they can. Nothing nasty like child marriage or stuff but every inane superstition you've ever heard of. Here is a list of all that I can remember:

**On food**

* No beef (obviously)
* No meat on Tuesdays
* No meat on the 5th of every lunar calendar month (This happens twice every month FYI)
* Do not touch food with your left hand
* lol @ alcohol, smoking (They'd probably get a heart attack if I told them I smoked weed in college)

**On personal hygiene**

* No haircut on Mondays
* No haircut on day of birth (i.e. if you were born on Sunday as I was and have a 6 day working week, then fuck you. Your hair will keep growing forever and you get castigated by the same parents for not cutting your hair.)
* Put oil in your ears after a haircut
* Don't cut nails on a Saturday
* No shower after lunch
* Have to wear that thread and pull it over my ear while taking a dump. (It's easier to just wear the damn thread than to argue with my mom why I shouldn't)

**God and religion**

* Light a diya at home every fuckin' day (Dad wakes me up every morning because he can't do that shit silently)
* A death in the family is the only time you don't light a diya at home (3 days if the death was on mom's side, 11 if on dad's side)
* Don't spend money while the diya is lit
* Don't wear slippers in the room with god's photos
* Every new purchase has to be shown to god
* Inter-caste marriage? You're not allowed in the inner sanctum of the temple. Fuck you.
* Have shani (saturn) trouble? Go to the Hanuman temple every Saturday and pour oil over the idol
* You have to wear a lungi (sans underwear) while performing puja
* Turn and pray to every temple that they cross on the road
* Don't pray to Sai Baba. He's Muslim

**Stereotypes they believe**

* People from Delhi are all rapists/thieves
* Baniyas are frauds
* Mallus are bad with money
* Bongs are lazy
* Lower caste people are greedy
* Why do girls want to study so much? They will not find a guy if they're overqualified
* Muslims are all terrorists. They should be sent back to Pakistan or Saudi Arabia
* Christians just want to convert you
* US has no culture. Indian kulcha best kulcha
* India is a Hindu country (TBF this is new. They never voted BJP till last election. This makes me think if the propaganda works better on people who are new to the smartphone +social media ecosystem)

**Relationships and family**

* How dare you speak back to your dad like that? We give you too much freedom, my parents would have whacked me by now
* lol. What is a girlfriend?
* Marriage conditions: Arranged, in-caste, in-gotra, matching kundalis (this has led to more failed marriages than when the couple chose to get married themselves. At least among my relatives)
* If nothing works, it's OK to marry your cousin (my grandparents were cousins)
* Damn those weddings are so full of cringe-worthy functions. I didn't even bother trying to remember

**Miscellaneous crap**

* Why are you working in a startup? No one will marry you
* Don't wear black (my dad doesn't wear anything black at all)
* Buy everything from Patanjali (seriously, we have a toilet cleaner that is made of Gomutra)
* Touch feet of every guest who is older than you
* Don't speak of excretion while eating
* Why have teachers in schools stopped hitting kids? Look at us, we turned out great

There's probably more but I can't keep track of everything. It would have been amusing if they did it themselves. But it's absolutely fucked up that I have to follow these too. Or argue for hours and spoil my mood.

Does everyone else's parents also believe any of these or do my parents just make up stuff on the fly? 

/rant

Edit: Just to be clear, I don't mean to say my parents are like evil incarnate or bad people at all. Just that they believe in way too many silly things and try to enforce them. It's a challenge when every tiny thing has to conform to some inexplicable rules but not the worst thing in the world.
