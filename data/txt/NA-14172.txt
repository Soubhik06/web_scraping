Update: Story updated on 22/11/2024 with responses sought from Zomato.

After social media users raised concerns about the presence of ‘odd listings’ of restaurants offering single items on Zomato, the food delivery platform clarified its stance. Taking to X, the company stated that all such restaurants have been identified as “potentially fraudulent” and consequently delisted from the platform.

Zomato also claims to have launched investigations into similar restaurants offering very limited menus comprising prohibited items. “We have further strengthened our fraud checks to proactively identify such cases to avoid similar incidents in the future”, the company added.

Recently, social media platforms went abuzz with conversations about new restaurants popping up on Zomato offering only single items. A Reddit user who noticed this trend on the platform in Chandigarh stated that these “so-called restaurants” only provide single products at “absurdly high” prices. The user also claimed that many restaurants lack real reviews and display fake or nonexistent addresses. Later, after the user tried placing an order, it was automatically cancelled after a while and the restaurant displayed a closed status.

The restaurants offering products like “Naughty Strawberry”, “Blue Adventure”, “Kiwi Delight Mojito”, and “Citrus Punch”, were soon identified as vape flavours by Reddit users, who claimed that the pricing also resembled typical vape product costs.

Some users speculated the restaurants to be a front for money laundering or drug delivery.

Firstly, it is important to note that e-cigarettes are popularly known as “ vapes “.

In 2019, the Indian government passed The Prohibition of Electronic Cigarettes Act , banning the manufacture, import, export, transport, sale, distribution, storage, and advertisement of electronic cigarettes in the interest of public health. The law also stipulates that any person found to be violating the aforementioned provisions shall be liable to face up to a year of imprisonment or a fine up to Rs 1 Lakh or both in case of the first offence. As per the law, electronic cigarettes (e-cigarettes) denote “electronic devices that heat a substance, with or without nicotine and flavours, to create an aerosol for inhalation”.

In addition to removing these restaurants, Zomato disclosed that all restaurants listed on the platform have a Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) license. Furthermore, the company claimed they actively block items like alcohol, cigarettes, cigars, and vapes from being sold on their platform.

However, a MediaNama report revealed that a different version of its quick commerce platform Blinkit, namely ‘ Blinkit Lit ‘ made these tobacco products available for sale on Android phones.

On Apple devices, the Blinkit Lit feature isn’t available for download owing to App Store guidelines rejecting apps “encouraging the consumption of tobacco and vape products, illegal drugs, or excessive amounts of alcohol”. However, Blinkit Lit can be accessed via a search browser on these devices.

Within the platform, such products are also sold by solely displaying their names without any photos, which could be deemed as a strategy to circumvent regulations stipulated by the Cigarettes and Other Tobacco Products (Prohibition of Advertisement and Regulation of Trade and Commerce, Production, Supply and Distribution) Act (COTPA), 2003.

Additionally, the COTPA also prohibits the sale of cigarettes to persons under the age of eighteen and in an area within one hundred yards of any educational institution. However, as a mechanism for age verification, Blinkit Lit asks users to confirm if they are over eighteen, warning them that they will report the user’s account if they are deemed a minor or near an educational institution.
