Avoided a potential rental property scam recently.

Found some properties on 99acres &amp; housing &amp; started talking to the owners. Mind you, "owners", not agents/brokers. Found a good place with a reasonable rent in ST Bed Layout. Started talking to him &amp; he mentioned he has the keys &amp; lives in Mysore &amp; would only be coming to show me the house if I pay a token amount \[which can be refunded as per him\].

As per him, if I don't show up, it would be an unnecessary trip for him. Doesn't make sense to me of course because that'd be a shit prank to pull on somebody.

Anyway, I just went to see the apartment building directly just to check it out &amp; started talking to the guard who mentioned that there's no property available here \[Yeah, I know they lie sometimes\]. Got curious &amp; looked at more flats &amp; found a pattern. A lot of these listings had the same photos, same apartments \[a lot of times\] &amp; different "owners". Talked to a couple more of them &amp; everybody wanted me to send them a token amount beforehand.

So, just letting y'all know.
