important to establish proof of direct or indirect acts of
instigation or incitement of suicide by the accused, which must

but to commit suicide. 13. Abetment involves a mental process of instigating a
person or intentionally aiding a person in doing of a thing.

aid in committing suicide, conviction cannot be sustained. Observation of the court: 14. Proceedings in the present case were initiated under
