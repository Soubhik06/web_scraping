--- POST TEXT ---

My father bought a flat in Rajarhat area in 2019. It was a 3BHK flat at Sanhita (Simoco) Township Project https://g.co/kgs/ZaibWNh . At the time he booked the flat, it was still undergoing construction. However, there were already a few other buildings in the same complex that were completely furnished and handed over to people, so he assumed it would be done soon. He has already paid 90 percent of the total price. During covid, work was stopped on the project. They failed to pick up calls, didn't reply to emails and completely cut off communication. At some point, dad just accepted that it was a scam and forgot about it.

A couple of months ago, someone from that project called my father and said that the work on his flat is done, and he sent pictures and a demand notice. Now according to this guy, after the rest of the money (along with interest that has accumulated over this many years) has to be paid for the handover, and then registration will be done. My father is suspicious of them. He wants the registration to be done first, and then hes gonna pay because he doesnt trust them with his money anymore.
