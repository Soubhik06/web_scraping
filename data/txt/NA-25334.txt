I wanted to share a frustrating and concerning experience I recently had with Swiggy, hoping it serves as a warning for others.

I ordered from Pizza Hut via Swiggy (Order ID: #190654889245920). The delivery executive arrived, and I paid him ₹515 in cash. After receiving my order, I called Swiggy's customer support to confirm that I had received it, as I was facing some network issues on my phone.

After the call, I put my phone on silent and started eating my meal. To my shock, when I later checked the app, my order was marked as "Cancelled," and a penalty of ₹515 (the same amount I paid the delivery guy) was imposed on my account.

This was clearly a case of fraud by the delivery executive, who probably exploited my network issue to claim the order was canceled, despite my confirmation with Swiggy’s support team.

I’ve attached proof of payment (the bill) and pictures of the food packaging in a formal complaint to Swiggy, but I feel compelled to warn others about this. This incident is not only frustrating but also feels like a breach of trust from both the delivery executive and the system in place.

I’ve ordered thousands of times from Swiggy, and this is the first time I’ve faced such an issue. I’ve requested Swiggy to:

1. Remove the unfair cancellation penalty from my account.

2. Take strict action against the delivery executive for fraudulent behavior.
