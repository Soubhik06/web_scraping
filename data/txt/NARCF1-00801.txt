Imagine a respected professional like *Doctor G*, recently scammed out of a significant sum of money. The scammer, pretending to be a law enforcement official, convinced her she was "digitally arrested" and gained her trust by reciting her *exact bank account details*.

As a concerned citizen, I have questions that deserve answers. I’m not a cybersecurity expert, nor an investigator. I’m just a regular person questioning the system’s integrity. Here's what I need to know:

# 1. How Do Scammers Access Bank Account Details?

Are Indian banks failing in their duty to protect customer information? Are privacy and security becoming a joke? Scammers seem to have easy access to sensitive data—so where are the loopholes? Is it insider involvement, weak cybersecurity measures, or sheer negligence?

# 2. Are Banks Conducting Proper KYC?

Banks claim to follow stringent KYC (Know Your Customer) protocols. Yet, when scammed money is traced to specific accounts, why are these account holders not immediately apprehended? Are loopholes in KYC being exploited, or is it a lack of enforcement?

# 3. Is Police Action Only Triggered Under Pressure?

Why do victims often feel ignored unless they bring public or political pressure? Shouldn't timely updates and assurances be standard practice? Trust in law enforcement erodes when victims are left in the dark.

# 4. Can Victims Directly Lodge an FIR Against the Scammer’s Account Holder?

Why isn't there a transparent process allowing victims to file a First Information Report (FIR) directly against the scammer’s account holder? Does bureaucracy shield scammers, or is it a gap in the legal process?

# 5. What If the Police Aren’t Helping?

When authorities collect details but remain inactive, what’s the next step? Are citizens supposed to wait indefinitely? Should victims turn to higher authorities, media, or even public protests to get justice?

# 6. Why Can’t Media Cover Scams Like They Cover Elections?

If media outlets can dedicate entire days to political events, why can’t they spare 10 minutes daily to expose financial scams? Raising awareness would not only educate citizens but also push authorities to act more decisively.
