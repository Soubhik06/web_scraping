Recently I received a call from a person claiming to be a CISF trainee , as they're looking for oral prophylaxis treatment for 33 members of their crew.
Previously During Elections, a bunch of Indian army members posted in nearby election location camps visited my clinic for treatments.
So, i didn't think anything out of the ordinary and I said, I will accomodate their request.
I even cleared some of my appointments to accomodate them only because they're from Army and I have respect for them.
Later on, this person connected me to his supposed "Captain" and said, we need to provide "gate pass" for these members and we would pay you the half amount upfront.
I told them, it is private clinic, we don't take payment upfront, instead send the patients, and I will take payment after completing the treatment of each patient.
But this person insisted that, it's a Indian army policy, and they have to give the amount upfront cause they get some sort of army discount.

I was reluctant, but finally agreed only for him to say that he wants to get on a video call and then wants me to scan a QR code through video call to receive the advance amount lol.

I immediately suspected fould play and fraud, so I took a risk of unpaid work and said, you don't have to pay anything, I will do the whole treatment for free as a service to the country. Just send the patients to my clinic. And as expected they said that's not possible and they're cancelling the appointment.

These phone scammers have become so audacious that they're trying to defraud people with the name of the army. 

The only small problem is now my assistant had to recall and reinstate the older appointments.

Please be careful if you're a practicing dentist or have any private service business.
