Listen to the learned Doctor from Lucknow, about her ordeal with cybercriminals.

Cyber criminals from various parts of India place almost one thousand calls per minute, aiming to defraud and loot unsuspecting citizens. Educational achievements, job title, prestige and age has nothing to do with being victim of a cybercrime. Anyone can fall prey to online fraud and other forms of cybercrime.

There is only one way to beat cybercrime - Awareness.

Stay vigilant, stay safe.
