Title: Need to investigate fake call centre issue in Mumbai. DR. KIRIT SOMAIYA ( MUMBAI NORTH EAST ): There is a need to take action against Meera Road, Bhayander area call centres scam in Mumbai. The employee of these call centres posing themselves as IRS officer used to threaten the US citizens to instantly settle the tax default by making payment to them. This is a well planned organized gang’s activities to dupe US citizens. Statements of employees and people working in the surroundings have been with local police, chargesheet has been issued. The matter is very serious. The reputation of our country and that of  IRS officers is maligned. The Ministry of Home Affairs should order investigation and take strict action against the persons behind this scam.
