My father got an sms just a while ago, and eventually I had to step in to call this person to get this sorted.

He was asking me about payment modes and customer ids which I didn't share, to which he got offended and threatened me to fine Rs 7000 and also to disconnect my electricity connection. 

I checked the number on Truecaller and it showed me the number is from Electricity Bill Department. 

Later I did call cesc customer care and they confirmed that it is indeed a scam.

The way these people are planning the scams, it will be very hard for the elderly to handle all these issues.
