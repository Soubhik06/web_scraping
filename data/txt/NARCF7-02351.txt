So today I got a call from Priya Sharma [7209622895] asking if I needed a job. As a jobless graduate I immediately agreed and asked her about the details. She said her company connects freshers to IT companies.

She then texted me a website [shinework.in] and asked me to register on the website and pay Rs. 10/- as registration fee. *Further she also told me remain on call while I did all this*. I clicked on Register here and started filling in my details. The UI looked poorly designed, but I didn't care much. 

The OTP I got from my bank stated that the Transaction was for Rs. 4910/- and not just Rs. 10/-. I thought I'd made a mistake so I filled the form again and double checked the Rs. 10/- option. But I get the same message from my bank.

So I cut the call and started paying more attention. 

Went back to the website homepage and realised it's a barebones website, where nothing works at all and just the [Register Here] option works. 

Initially I wasn't going to make this post but then I get another call at 4pm and this time another woman [6287502910] repeats the same casette. Sends me a text on Whatsapp via [9661729130]

There are some old videos on YT about a company called shine.com running a similar scam.
