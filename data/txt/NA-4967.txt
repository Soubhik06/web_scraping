This Criminal Original Petition has been filed seeking modification of

 the condition imposed by the learned Principal Sessions Court, Puducherry, in

 Crl M.P. No.130 of 2026 dated 27.01.2026. 2.The petitioner is alleged to have committed an online scam by

 inducing two victims to part with a sum of Rs.14.5 lakhs. He was arrested and

 remanded to judicial custody on 27.12.2025. Thereafter, the petitioner

 approached the learned Principal Sessions Court, Puducherry, seeking bail. Upon considering the case on merits, the learned Sessions Judge was inclined

 to grant bail, subject to certain conditions, including the production of property

 documents worth Rs.15 lakhs as security or furnishing one surety. 3.The petitioner seeks modification of the said condition on the ground

 that it is onerous. He submits that he has not made any statement agreeing to

 deposit any documents for the purpose of bail. The learned counsel for the

 petitioner further contends that the condition imposed is excessive and that the

 petitioner is ready and willing to co-operate with the trial proceedings. He

 therefore prays that this Court may modify the said condition and grant

 appropriate relief. __________

 condition directing the petitioner to produce property documents as security has

 been imposed by the Sessions Court in view of the nature and gravity of the

 allegations. However, imposing a condition requiring deposit of property

 documents without any offer or undertaking from the petitioner appears to be

 onerous, particularly when such a condition may effectively deprive him of the

 benefit of bail if he is unable to comply due to financial constraints. 5.In view of the above, this Court is of the opinion that the learned

 Sessions Judge shall reconsider the bail condition in the light of the

 observations made herein. Accordingly, the matter is remitted to the trial Court

 for passing appropriate orders in accordance with law. 6.Consequently, this Criminal Original Petition is disposed of. 12-02-2026
