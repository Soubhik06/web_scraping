1. Heard learned senior counsel Mr.Yogesh Lakhani appearing
with learned advocate Mr.Dhruvik K.Patel and learned A.P.P.
Mr.J.K.Shah appearing for respondent No.1 -State and learned senior
counsel Mr.N.D.Nanavaty appearing with learned advocate Mr.Meena
for respondent No.2-State Bank of India. 2. The present application is filed under Section 439 of the Code
of Criminal Procedure, 1973 seeking bail in connection with FIR being
C.R.No. I - 98/14 registered with Police Station, Sector 7, Gandhinagar
for the offences punishable under Sections 406 , 420 , 465 , 467 , 471 ,
506 (2), 120 (b) read with Section 114 of the Indian Penal Code. 3. We cannot ignore the judgment in the case of Sanjay
Chandra versus Central Bureau of Investigation reported in
(2012) 1 SCC 40 while dealing with such matters. 4. However, before controverting, it would be appropriate to refer
the rival contentions. Learned senior counsel Mr.Yogesh Lakhani has
pointed out that practically present petitioner is not signatory of any Page 1 of 4 1 of 6

that there is no signature of the present applicant while sanctioning
loan. Learned senior counsel Mr.N.D.Nanavaty for respondent No.2
has also confirmed that in fact there is no role of any of the bank
officer so far as commission of alleged offences is concerned. It is
further submitted that, on the contrary, since the bank has filed a

the present applicant in the alleged offence, at present there is no
necessity of custody of the petitioner. Therefore, relying upon Sanjay
Chandra 's case (Supra), it would be appropriate allow the petition.
