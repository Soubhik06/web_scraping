Things are going south. People are clanging plates and going for jaadhas.

Why not stay at home, and have a nice hearty singalong of Ekantha Chandrike next Sunday at 5pm?I am not claiming it will send invisible energy waves to kill the Corona Virus. But It might kill other virues, you never know.

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ijJYgmxMgSw&amp;t=6s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ijJYgmxMgSw&amp;t=6s) 

Here is something that you can forward to friends and fam. 

=========

What: Sing Ekantha Chandrike #ekanthachandrike

When: this Sunday ( 29th March 2020), 5 PM IST.

Where: Balconies and Muttams. Atleast a few meters away from another human being

Why: Just for a horror.

Guys... The prime ministers appeal to applaud the Health and other service workers who are risking their lives to keep the virus at bay is quiet appreciable. But, many were reluctant to take part due to multiple reasons, a major one being that they felt it seamed fake and manufactured, as opposed to the natural response seen in other countries. 

In such a situation, lets come forward ourselves and do some thing that everyone, irrespective of religion, cast or political affiliation can join in without any inhibition. This sunday (29th March 2020), at 5 Pm, lets all go out to our balconies or windows and sing "Ekandha Chandrikee" from the movie "In Harihar Nagar", but unlike in the movie, we should do it individually, keeping atleast 1m distance between each other. 

Don't go out and celebrate, just stay indoor and sing out loud to show our appreciation, not just to the health workers, but to all who are working tirelessly to make our life as seamless as possible. Sing your heart out for the Nurses, the Doctors, the hospital Attenders and Staff, the Drivers, the Delivery boys, the Shop keepers, The Housekeepers, the district administrators, the Police, the Researchers, the State and Central level bureaucrats and each and everyone who is quarantining themselves to prevent the spread. Spread the word, not the virus. #ekanthachandrike #coronoottyvittoda
