Hi everyone,

Today my sister was defrauded of 55k by a person claiming to be from naukari premium. He called and described about various job opportunities available for her profile only if she avails premium subscription. Then he shared this link naukrionlineservice.com and asked her register to make a payment of 100 rupees for activation of premium service. On the website she put her credit card details and proceeded with the otp. But instead of 100 rupees 20k got deducted and she got a sms for that. 

On confrontation the naukari guy said due to server overload extra has been deducted he is arranging the refund and asked my sister to not to block the card. 

My sister called and informed me about this and i immediately got her card blocked. But by then 55k has been deducted. The fraudster has loaded the amount in PhonePe wallet and sent to various beneficiaries.

Since I am living in Mumbai, and my family stays at kolkata My father and sister have gone to police station to file an FIR.

1. My first Request to all to be very vigilant. I work in a bank and regularly counsel my parents and sister about such scams. I have seen 20 lakh been siphoned off from accounts. Still this happened, my sister is shocked beyond words 

2. We have blocked the card and raised a dispute with the credit card via call. They said they will be investigating this.
Please suggest anything else can be done to get the money back.
