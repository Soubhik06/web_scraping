The brakes are once more becoming sloppy. The accelerator cable was eventually cut off.I was in the middle of the street. I spent two hours trying to reach customer service. The mechanic finally showed up after two hours and drove me to the other hub. After some time, they promised a thorough repair and requested me to test drive and rent the scooter. How can I trust them, really? They made an effort to scam me to ride the same broken scooter. I asked how these people could try to rent a broken scooter. Also there were a lot of people yelling them for giving a broken scooter.
What if they have an accident? They assured me that I would receive a full refund. On July 2, everything happened. I'm still begging them for my refund. I think I've been scammed. Customer service consistently say that refund requests are being rejected. Do not rent from these cheap company, please.
