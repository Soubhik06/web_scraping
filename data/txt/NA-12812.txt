 offences punishable under Sections 318(4), 338, 336(3), 340(2)

 Central Selection Board Examination being conducted for the

 appointment to the post of Constable, the petitioners were found
 Patna High Court CR. MISC. No.14606 of 2025(3) dt.02-05-2025 2/4 to be committing fraud and they had used another person in

 their place in order to secure job and during the biometric

 examination because of the differences found in the photo and

 thumb impression, the petitioners were apprehended. 5. The learned counsel for the petitioners submits that

 the petitioners are innocent and they have falsely been

 implicated in this case as they are the bona-fide students for

 examination which was being conducted for appointment to the

 post of Constable. The petitioners have clean antecedent and

 they are in custody since 04.01.2025. 6. Learned APP for the state has vehemently opposed

 the prayer for bail of the petitioners and stated that the

 petitioners by such conduct are trying to defame the state as

 well as it affects the other candidates adversely and hence

 should not be released on bail. 7. Considering the facts and circumstances of the

 case and taking into account the fact that the petitioners are

 students who are appearing on the examination conducted for

 the appointment to the post of Constable and taking into account

 that the petitioners have clean antecedent and they are in judicial

 custody since 04.01.2025, let the petitioners, above named, be
