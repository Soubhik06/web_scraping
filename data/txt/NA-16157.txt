--- POST TEXT ---

1. Stack all your money in a bank and don't use it for online transaction, pos shopping. Use it's card only in atm and for emergency. Get a spending account for all the spending. ie: another account in another bank, transfer money from main account and keep balance under 10k, or better get a credit card.

2. Don't use pirated apps on your android, it could be infected with adwares and spywares, yeah android sandboxes them all but better be safe than sorry. 

3. Stop giving out your phone number and email id. Just give a fake phone number to any salesman. 

4. TrueCaller: Unlist from that shit. 

5. Use a stronger, different password for netbanking with a mix of capital letters, numbers and special characters. 

6. Get a safer chip based card. (Sbi is issuing all new cards with chips), if you don't want to then cover your existing card's cvv, validity date with a black tape.

7. Always update your browsers, apps and antivirus definitions on your pc.

8. Don't share your email id, birth date on social media. If you have fb, then hide them. 

9. Be cautious using debit card for international transaction, they don't ask for otp your money will be gone in seconds, and it's real money means your savings account money, you'll have a tough time convincing bank about the fraud transaction. Since it was real money from your account you'll be broken. In credit card you can delay the payment but not in case of debit card payment. So credit card is safer choice for international transaction.

10. Do change your netbanking password, atm pin regularly, always go for large ones and with all the combinations.
