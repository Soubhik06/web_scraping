--- POST TEXT ---

Recently, I purchased a laptop on Flipkart. Unfortunately, the delivery was delayed, and despite reaching out to customer support multiple times, I received no updated information or assurance regarding my order. Frustrated, I decided to express my concerns publicly on X (formerly Twitter) in hopes of resolving the issue. Shortly after, I get a call from "Flipkart Support"(Claimed by Truecaller) aka the scammer. He had obtained my contact information from a previous tweet directed at my state's electricity board(boomers still want my contact number to raise a complaint).

The individual posed as a Flipkart representative and informed me of a payment issue that allegedly caused the delay in my order. He then requested screenshots and urged me to switch to a WhatsApp call for screen sharing to resolve the issue. During the call, he instructed me to make a minimum purchase using the same card I used for the laptop order. By this point I was a lil sceptical but moved forward considering it was Flipkart and how bad and outdated their services are. While on the screen share, I followed his instructions, and as a result, several OTPs were generated during the payment process which he used to login into my account and place orders. It later turned out that he used these OTPs to purchase gift cards totaling 35,000 INR, including three cards worth 10,000 INR each and one worth 5,000 INR(acc had 6K so only this went through), all sent to his email through my Flipkart account. And that's how the scam took place. 

Reflecting on the incident, I realize the importance of being cautious with personal information shared on social media platforms. Despite being aware of potential scams, I fell victim to this deceptive scheme. It serves as a reminder to exercise vigilance and discretion when engaging in online interactions, especially involving sensitive details.
