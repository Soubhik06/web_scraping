7 First defence of the accused is that there is difference in the last 
digit of IMEI number as stated in Ex.PW6/A and Ex.P1. It is argued that 
since there is difference in the last digit of the IMEI number, it is evident 
that call was not made from the mobile of the accused. Admittedly, all the 
digits of IMEI number as stated in the call details Ex.PW6/A is the same as 
that of mobile phone Ex.P1 which has been seized vide memo Ex.PW2/A FIR no. 202/03 10.09.12 4/6 except the last digit. The fact that above said mobile number is in the 
name of accused is not disputed. Hon'ble Supreme Court in case of State(NCT Of Delhi ) vs. Navjot Sandhu, MANU/SC/0465/2005 has 
discussed the issue regarding the difference in the last digit of IMEI 
number and has held that last digit of IMEI number is spare digit and 
according to GSM specification it should be transmitted as "0". At this 
stage, Ld. Defence counsel is critical about the fact that IMEI number in 
Ex.PW6/A has been transmitted as "8" from "0". It is clarified that IMEI 
number contains 14 decimal digits plus a check digit. The last number of 
IMEI number is a check digit which is calculated according to LUHN 
formula. The check digit shall not be transmitted to the network. The 
check digit is a function of all other digits in the IMEI number . The 
purpose of the same is to help guard against the possibility of incorrect 
entries to the CEIR and EIR equipment. Hence, the difference in the last 
digit which is the check digit of IMEI number is not material and does not 
goes to the root of the case of prosecution. Admittedly, the mobile 
number from which the call is made is in the name of accused and 
mobile phone has been recovered from the possession of accused. The 
fact that said mobile set belongs to the accused is admitted by accused 
himself. All the 14 digits of IMEI number as Ex.PW6/A and Ex.P1 are 
same. The question regarding the difference in the last digit has been 
clarified above. 8 Second defence of the accused is that someone else might have 
cloned the sim of the mobile of accused and made these calls. No 
evidence in this regard has been lead by accused. Moreover, there is no FIR no. 202/03 10.09.12 5/6 suggestion in this regard in the entire cross examination of either of 
prosecution witness. Even if it is assumed, that the sim was cloned, the 
fact that the IMEI number is the same cannot be ignored. Hence, it is 
established that call was made from phone number 98118412739 which 
belongs to accused on 16.06.03 as is evident from Ex.PW6/A.

9 PW3 Ct. Sunita has categorically deposed that she received the 
phone call at about 1.08 pm from mobile number 98118412739 and the 
caller insulted her by using obscene words whether she was wearing bra 
and panty or he should he get same for her. The fact that said mobile 
phone is in the name of accused and the set was recovered from the 
possession of accused is established. The minor difference in the time of 
the call as per testimony of PW3 is not fatal to the case of prosecution. A 
person cannot be expected to be too specific about each and every 
minute and second of the incident. 10 It is further argued by Ld. Defence counsel that accused had no 
intention to insult any body's modesty. It is further argued that in the 
absence of intention on part of the accused, he is entitled for benefit of 
doubt and be acquitted accordingly. The ultimate test to ascertain 
whether modesty has been insulted, is whether, the action of offender is 
such as can be perceived as one which is capable of shocking sense of 
decency of a woman. The woman's reaction is most relevant in deciding 
the question of guilt. In the present case, complainant immediately 
informed her seniors about the said indecent call and filled the form 
mark P3­1 at 1.15 pm. DD entry Ex.PW4/A was duly lodged and on the FIR no. 202/03 10.09.12 6/6 basis of same FIR was lodged on the same day. Ld. Defence counsel has 
failed to show any motive of false implication by the complainant. Even, 
if it is presumed that accused had no such intention, considering the age 
of accused, he must be attributed with knowledge, that such words would 
insult the modesty of any normal female. Knowledge that the act would 
likely insult the modesty of a female is also punishable u/s 509 IPC. The 
accused knowingly called at number 100 and still dared to use such 
obscene words. Hence, in the given facts and circumstances, on the basis 
of evidence on record, I am of the view that prosecution has proved the 
offence u/s 509 IPC against the accused beyond the pales of reasonable 
doubt. Accused is thus convicted for the offence u/s 509 IPC.
