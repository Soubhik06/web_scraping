I see so many posts about insurance fraud or people realizing later that they or their loved ones (especially older or financially less literate folks) have been mis-sold a policy. I haven't seen many posts discussing this particular option for help, so sharing it here in case it's of help to someone. 

There's an Insurance Ombudsman platform in the country as an alternate grievance redressal solution for affected policyholders. 

One of my very close relatives (retired government servant, will refer to this person as they/them for privacy) has served as an Ombudsman, so I've had an opportunity to witness how this platform allows the Ombudsman to apply his/her discretionary judgment fairly liberally (and with empathy) within the ambit of certain rules, and order insurance companies to provide relief which can include return of premium or conversion of the mis-sold policy to another one that can be of some benefit to the policyholder in case of years-old cases.

Before registering complaints with the Ombudsman office, complainant should've exhausted available option i.e. should've complained to the insurance company, and either received no response or unsatisfactory response.

The case is dealt with through a hearing (usually a single 30-45 min hearing) in front of the Ombudsman -- the best part is since COVID, most Ombudsmen allow virtual attendance if in-person attendance is difficult for complainant (this may be confirmed, my information may be outdated here).

Complaints can be registered and more information about the process can be found by searching for 'India Insurance Ombudsman' (www dot cioins dot co dot in).

Hope this helps at least someone!
