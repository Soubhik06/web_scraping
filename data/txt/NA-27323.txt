Someone charged 18 gift cards to my debit card on Amazon. The total was $275. The purchase only shows up under transactions, not orders on my account, so I can’t even claim that my problem is with a certain purchase. Has anyone heard of this before? They ordered 18 gift cards (even sent an e-gift card to me from me, the others were all print outs) and purchased them all at one time. Amazon says that they see it but gives me the runaround that a department will look into it. They originally told me that I’d hear from them within 24 to 48 hours via email. Didn’t hear a thing. They refunded me $25 on the one e-gift card. That’s it. Not the other 17 ($250). I know it’s call center in India. I know they’re reading from a script. I’m at the end of my rope. The entire thing is both maddening and creepy. My account already had a 2 step verification in place. I changed the password and removed my debit card on file after this happened over the weekend. I contacted my bank. They closed the card. I now have a new one. I’ve never heard of this type of scam before. I’ve heard of fake gift cards but not someone hacking into your account and buying them. Even creepier, they saw a gift card that I purchased back in February and copied and pasted the message I wrote in the message box. They pasted this message in 17 of the print-out gift cards they bought. It’s insane. No, no one has access to my card nor my account. 

Could this be an inside job? It’s not as if someone copied my card from an ATM because the person who did this actually logged into my account and looked at my purchase history and copied a message from a past gift card I had sent.
