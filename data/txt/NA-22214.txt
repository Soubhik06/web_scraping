(Google Maps Review Scam / Task Scam)

Yesterday, I recieved a random message on whatsapp asking me to participate in a 'part-time job'.

They asked me to rate some places on Google Maps and I would recieve 200rs in return.

I did and then I was redirected to telegram where they asked for my bank details for payment. I did not share them.

I gave them my secondary UPI id on paytm and recieved 200rs.

Seeing that they actually sent the money, I took to Google and Reddit to find more about what just happened. I found out that it is a 'Task Scam'.

The scammers give you money for doing some mundane tasks to gain your trust and them ask you for more money(fees) to up the level of the task or to give you access to more task. Once you give them money, they disappear.

What did unsettle me though was that some posts mentioned that my bank account could be frozen because the money I received is scammed money.

Is it true? The scammers only have my UPI id and I can change it.

**What are the legal repercussions I could face for recieving such money?**

Info about me scammers have -
1. My first name.
2. My phone number.
3. Screenshots of reviews on Google Maps.
4. My UPI id.
5. Screenshots of receiving money from them. _(Screenshots don't show anything related to me. Just the transaction.)_

_(I didn't actually review any places on Google Maps, I just sent them the screenshot of filling the review.)_
