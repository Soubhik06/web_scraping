So a few days before ppl posted about Airpods scam around Madhapur, Hitech Area and having faced it myself, i also shared my experience. 

Today while leaving office building, one sardarji smiled and waved and very politely handed pic of Guru Nanak and started saying something about langar. I thought he was generally telling me about langar and inviting me. So i smiled and politely thanked him. Then he said something more and in few seconds i realised he is requesting for donation. He started showing me credentials etc. At that point i realised something is wrong because I vaguely recollected that Sikhs don't collect donations for langar, i don't know where i have read/heard/seen this. Ppl can correct me if i am wrong. But i left from there, because it seemed shady that someone would wait near exit gates of office building at 9:15 p.m and seek donations for langar. For all you know the scammer was faking to be a Sikh. 

Quick search on reddit did show that there have been similar scams in Bangalore. 

Bringing to notice so that people are careful.
