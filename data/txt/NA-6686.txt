Heard the petitioner in person and learned counsel

 for the respondents. Although at the initial stage, the petitioner had filed

 this application seeking directions of this Court in connection

 with several cases, however, subsequently the instant

 application was confined to Hawai Adda P.S. Case no.217 of

 2020, registered on 19.12.2020, under sections 379 and 420 of

 the Indian Penal Code and sections 65 , 66B , 66C , 66E , 67 , 72 and 72A of the I.T. Act. A supplementary counter affidavit has been filed on

 behalf of the respondents sworn by the Assistant Superintendent

 of Police, Sadar and In-charge of Deputy Superintendent of

 Police, Sachivalaya, District- Patna, wherein it has been stated

 that the Superintendent of Police, Central having perused and

 considered the material collected during course of investigation,

 directed the Investigating Officer to submit final form as 'Lack

 of Evidence'. Accordingly, Final Form no. 01 of 2023 dated

 17.1.2023 (Annexure-A to supplementary counter affidavit) has
 Patna High Court CR. WJC No. 2430 of 2018(22) dt.12-04-2023 4/4 been prepared by the Investigation Officer. In the opinion of the Court, final form having been

 submitted, in connection with Hawai Adda P.S. Case no.217 of

 2020, nothing remains for the petitioner in the instant

 application. The petitioner will be at liberty to raise his remaining

 grievances in the learned trial Court where the respective case(s)

 are pending which, if filed, will be decided by the learned trial

 Court in accordance with law. This application stands disposed of. (Partha Sarthy, J)
