Hi everyone. Kindly beware of this OLX scam. We recently posted an ad to buy an old fridge and suddenly received an influx of messages interested in buying the fridge. I forwarded my dad's number to them. They called my dad and were interested in buying the fridge without negotiating. Then, they said that they would make the payment now and would send a worker to collect the fridge. First he was like I'll send you a payment of 1 rupee to this number but instead sent us a QR code on WhatsApp. My dad immediately noticed that he didn't receive an sms about receiving 1 rupee and said he'd call again later. The person was adamant that my dad stay on the call. My dad found this fishy and cut the call. My dad called the person and said he hasn't received 1 rupee yet and the person was like why did you cut the call and started abusing my dad. My dad dealt with him and we reported the person's username to OLX and his account was suspended after awhile. If you're looking for a genuine buyer on OLX, kindly wait for atleast an hour before responding as OLX tends to suspend most scammer accounts pretty swiftly and please ask interested buyers to come to see the product and then make the payment. This would deter most scammers. Stay safe everyone
