So I got a Whatsapp message asking me if I would like to earn money online. All I had to do was leave good Google Reviews on the businesses/shops they shared. Also one could earn by making comment and liking posts, following instagram pages. 
They pay like 20-50 rupees for each Tasks, call them a tasks cause everyday we get around 20 tasks (activity like engaging on social media post or sharing google review ) . 
For this they add the people on Telegram group and all the communication is done there. 
Now comes the scam part, hidden objective of this is to lure people into Bitcoin trading platform where one is gauranteed income of 7000 on investment of 5000 or maybe 35k on investment of 30k. 
They ask you to make account on Bitcoin platform like WazirX But once u send them money ( on Google pay or NEFT ) they block you. 

Dont know how long this has been going on.. but be careful.
