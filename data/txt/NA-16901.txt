I am trying to book some travel in India from outside. The issues I am facing are just outrageous and I wonder how e-commerce is working in India.

* Booking flights with Indigo airlines. First day the site didn't work, couldn't search flights. Next day it worked. I booked flights with my international credit card. Got confirmation email, 4 days now and no payment transactions have happened in my card. It also hides charges until last page, this kind of hidden charges are banned in Australia but I guess Indian customers have to suffer.
* I made account with Indigo giving by foreign mobile no, got OTP and created account with password. Today I can't login. It says my password is invalid and I am not getting any OTP either.
* Booking hotels in Puri. The hotel I liked (#3 in Tripadvisor) has a good enough website but when I fill the form and click on the payments button the payment gateway is timing out. I have tried twice. Waste of my time, hello don't you want to take money from me.
* Another luxury resort hotel in Puri has all links broken when you got their website, Check yourself [https://www.mayfairhotels.com/mayfair-puri/](https://www.mayfairhotels.com/mayfair-puri/)
* Edit: This hotel has a different website and phone number on google maps. Somebody has fraudulently squatted their name. At least they have a fraud alert on the real website. [https://hotelgoldenpalacepuri.in/#contact](https://hotelgoldenpalacepuri.in/#contact) 

I had no issues with Booking an international brand 5\* hotel in Bangalore from their global website. But Indian brands have left a disappointing impression on me.
