Possible scam performed on my mother who has a cat, by a resort near Hyderabad.

Context- she messaged them on their website weather the resort is per friendly. They said yes. Popular sites on Google suggest they accept pets.
 https://snouters.com/pet-friendly-resorts-in-hyderabad/

On going 50km and spending 8k rs for a room they said they don't accept pets, checked us in after seeing the pet and then denied refunding.

Learnings- Please read the terms and conditions and do not really on the words of their employees and website chat. Do not use random Google website links to come to conclusions.

Maybe customer ignorance or a well thought of plan to lure people to pay money and use terms and conditions to deny refund and entry of pets, regardless as humans we need to find solutions for our the benefit of each other.

My mom had a bad time. Sucks.
