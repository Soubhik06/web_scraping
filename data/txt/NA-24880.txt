Then they told me unka group join kar le usme isi tarah ke 15-20 task hote h, where you have to like videos and for every task you will going to get 50rs, ye group telegram pe tha, isme 90 members ke karib the. Aur agar tumne sare task consecutively nhi Kiya toh it will reduce 25rs for every task. Aur har task ke baad wo kahte the ki jisne bhi 1-10 lagatar task Kiya usey 500rs ka incentive and who completed all the 20 task, they will going to get additional 1200rs for that. maine group join kar liya, there were task of video likes, aur har do teen video task ke baad kuch alag sa task hota tha, jisme we have to give a certain amount of money to a merchant. And for every thousand we will gonna get 30% profit. Maine try Kiya because, itna risk mai le sakta tha. Maine try Kiya 1000 se, and after unke steps ke 15-20 min ke andar, mujhe 1300 credit ho gye account mai. Again same thing 2-3 video task and 1 merchant task. But unhone kaha, ab 1000rs nhi laga sakte kyoki, that were for newbies. 3000 se starting thi maine le liye but i didn't understand what's the meaning of group task. Jab Kara toh pata chala ki 3000 wale mai 2-4 task ho sakte h. Aur agar tumne ek baar daal diye, toh task pura karne ke baad hi tumhe wapas milenge commision ke saath. Isi ke andar unka 2nd task included tha, 3000 again to another merchant. Mai chod nhi sakta tha kyoki my 3000 were on stake. Maine kahi se jugad kare aur de diye. 3rd task jisme 0 payment ka option tha maine choose Kiya aur settle karwa liya. Then fir se mujhe code Mila Jo Maine apne senior ko diya, after sometime (10-15 minutes) my accoun got credited by 7800. Fir se wahi video likes and same process. Now the merchant task was of 5000, unhone kaha 3000 wala abhi koi merchant task nhi aaya h, mere Mann tha mai na lagau, but lalach(greedy) toh aa gya tha, aur Maine laga diya. Ye bhi group task hi nikla, 5000 ke baad, mujhe next payment 20000 (30% profit) ki mangi, ab mujhe pata chal gya tha ki ye loop type ka hi game h aur mai fass gya, ab mai give up karta hu toh mere 5000 chale jayenge, toh maine 20000 kahi se kare friends wagera se, aur ye chale gya. Ab bas mai hope kar rha tha ki next task 0 payment ka ho, but life bakchod toh h. Next task was of 65000 (30% profit), and 4th task 0 payment ka hone wala tha. But ab mai 65000 nhi kar sakta kahi se bhi. Toh ab Maine unse request wagera ki ki mai nhi kar sakta paise wapas de do and all. But they are just saying merchant task complete hone ke baad hi merchant code deta h settlement ka tabhi mujhe mere pure paise with 30% profit wapas milenge, warna ye ab bhi mil rhe. There were 2-3 more guys with me, unse Maine pucha ki tumhe task complete hone ke baad settlement Hui ya nhi unme se ek ne hi reply Diya and kaha ki usey paise wapas mil gye h. Ab samajh nhi aa rha kya karu, unhone kaha ye task 48hrs tak ke liye ho valid h.
