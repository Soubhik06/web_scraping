Please take a look at this app in playstore. They also have one in appstore.

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=virtualnumber.secondphone.virtualphone

It offers phone numbers for rent for certain payment and the claimed the will provide the verification code on their app. I wanted to start freelancing and thought using these for WhatsApp communication for privacy.

But when I tried using these numbers most of them are used and have existing whatsapp accounts and also never got any verification codes in the app. I got suspicious and removed my account. 

My guess is they are holding some phone numbers from every country and claiming to provide verification codes..but they never do. All reviews seem to be generated from chatgpt...

I am surprised to see this is standing in top 10 apps in business...

Also it occurred to me later that the previous user could have done scams using those numbers and luckily I didn't OTP for any of the numbers and haven't logged into WhatsApp .. So I hope I will not be liable for any activities done using those numbers because I never logged into them...
