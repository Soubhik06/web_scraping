Consumers have rights under various data protection laws, including the Information Technology Act and the Data Protection and Privacy (DPDP) Act, in India.

These laws require companies to notify customers about breaches and to take necessary steps to safeguard their data.

Sharad Mathur, MD and CEO at Universal Sompo General Insurance, said, “Customers can file complaints with their insurance companies and escalate matters to the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) if they are unsatisfied with the response.”

He added that if individuals experience financial harm due to a breach, they may seek legal recourse, including compensation claims.
