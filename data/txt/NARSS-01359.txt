Got a call today from someone claiming to be from FedEx, using the name Swathi Sarma and even mentioning an employee ID. They spun a story about an illegal package sent under my ID from Mumbai, now stuck with Mumbai Customs.

I agreed to file a complaint and was redirected to what was supposed to be a police station. However, when I mentioned this to a friend via text, they warned me that it's a new scam. I immediately hung up and learned that this scam is becoming increasingly common, with several similar incidents reported on Instagram.

Has anyone else experienced something like this?
