Between January and December 2023, telecom companies received 1.22 million spam complaints against unregistered telemarketers, Minister of State for Communications and Rural Development, Dr Pemmasani Chandra Sekhar said in response to questions raised by MPs Vijaykumar Alias Vijay Vasanth and Maddila Gurumoorthy on July 31.

The MPs asked the Indian government whether it had taken cognizance of telemarketing companies using employees’ personal phones to call customers. They also asked for details of the efforts that the government is taking to curb scams/spam calls.

Chandra Sekhar mentioned that TRAI has allocated a separate number series (140-number series) to telemarketers. Despite this, telemarketers continue to use 10-digit numbers to make calls. In such cases, telecom companies give telemarketers warnings, put them under usage caps, and also disconnect services in case of repeated violations.

Of the total 1.22 million complaints registered in 2023, telcos imposed usage caps during the investigation on 0.25 million telemarketers. Usage caps limit a telemarketer to making only 20 outgoing voice calls per day and sending only 20 messages per day. Further, telcos sent warning notices to another 0.39 million telemarketers, imposed usage caps after an investigation on 57, 565 and disconnected 27,043 connections.
