Hello Redditians,

In the upcoming Republic Day Sale, Flipkart is providing "Buyback Guarantee" to Mi 10T mobile i.e., within 12 months if this mobile is exchanged with another mobile, flipkart would provide a guaranteed exchange price of 25k rupees.

However a couple of youtube videos suggested that this "Buyback Guarantee" is a scam. They mentioned that at the time of exchange, the delivery person would find one or another fault to reject the phone by default like even a small scratch on the backside or in case of no scratch the phone would be rejected with the reason that a small white dot is present on the screen (even-though the white dot doesn't exist).

If anyone has any idea/experience on this "Buyback Guarantee Scheme" please share your opinion 🙏🙏

Thanks!
