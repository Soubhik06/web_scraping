The company offers 4d5n for 5 years for rs 50K +8000 annual fee and offer 4 + star accomodations anywhere in India or abroad (this is just for that day on the spot offer) 

Right of the bat seems like a scam i.e. the company might not be delivering such promises for every holiday. 

Just want to check if anyone uses this? Or has any insights does any know anything about this company? 
 
Things if found suspicious are 
1. Website is not mobile friendly and doesn't open the webpage in proper formatting ( being 6years as a company it should have a proper mobile friendly website)
2. Website doesn't have a feedback/ customer complaints page it returns 404
3. Website domain is registered in 2023 and the promotion that they are doing is of 6th anniversary.

It seems fishy with all this I did not take any offer and left from there but wasted 1 hr 😑
