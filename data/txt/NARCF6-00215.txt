Hey! 
Received an automated call from FedEx- saying that my parcel has been undelivered due my unavailability at my residence. To know more press 1. 

Thanks to multiple threads on Reddit, I didn’t press 1 and immediately disconnected the call, because I knew I hadn’t ordered anything nor was expecting any orders.
But this got me thinking… 
1) what if it was my birthday? And maybe I was expecting a parcel and would have got scammed via this call. 
 2) How does one differentiate a scam call and a real one? 
3) Do we have strong systems in place to provide people who got scammed relief and put these scammers behind bars ? 

I have myself reported two numbers in the cyber cell, but haven’t been of no help. Scammers just use new numbers, once their old ones get reported. 
I tried searching FedEx number on the net to verify the number, but there no fixed number that the company use.
