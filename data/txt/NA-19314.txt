Yesterday's judgement has declared that folks who don't file ITR and don't avail govt subsidies don't need to have Aadhaar. 

But doesn't this mean that a person who has a lot of black money and hides his income from Govt, is now also hidden from the Aadhaar system.

Wasn't the entire point of the exercise to unearth those who commit fraud / tax aversion?

For ppl paying tax, all our bank accounts &amp; major assets like jewelry / car / house are already tied by PAN, so what new did they gain in further tagging us with Aadhaar?
