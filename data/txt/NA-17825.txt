Last year in December, I enrolled in Byju's Tablet learning program for UPSC and for a few months, used it thoroughly only to later realise that all those videos in the tablet are available on their YouTube channel for FREE as well (Slow clap for my dumbness). All the books might be worth 5-6K and the Samsung tablet is worth 15K, and they charged me 85K for this package (please clap again). I feel like betrayed now.

When I called them in February this year multiple times when I started to realise that these videos were on YouTube, they were actually pretty good at convincing me that it's not the case. Their mentorship program is also useless as they cannot help you with solving even simpler doubts.

One good thing is they have weekly sessions on Zoom for Daily affairs but 'World Affairs' and 'StudyIQ IAS' provide that for free on YouTube and their professors are equally good. 

The books can be purchased directly from Amazon or local vendors. All Byju's does is change the cover of the textbook with their brand and resell. You can simply go for original books which would be even cheaper.

I'm a working IT professional so it's even more painful for me as I had to bare the lose from my salary. 

It's a humble advice to any aspirant, please don't fall for Byju's scam. You can get all the learning materials for free on YouTube and books for cheap from second hand store near you. Get a good WiFi connection at home and it would be hardly 5K investment for you. Don't pay Byju's even a penny.
