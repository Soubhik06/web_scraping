Call for the lower court records in connection with R.C. Case
 No.47(A)/1996-Pat from the court of learned Special Judge-V, C.B.I., (A.H.D.
 Scam Cases), Ranchi. Learned counsel for the appellant has prayed for confirmation of
 provisional bail granted to the appellant vide order dated 15 th February 2022 by
 learned CBI Court, for which I.A. No. 2225 of 2022 has been preferred. Sole appellant stands convicted in connection with R.C.-
 47(A)/1996-Pat vide impugned judgment of conviction dated 15.02.2022 and
 order of sentence dated 21.02.2022 passed by the learned court of Special
 Judge-V, CBI, (A.H.D. Scam Cases), Ranchi for the offences under Section
 120B read with Section 420 , 409 , 467 , 468 , 471 and 477A of the Indian Penal
 Code and under Section 13(2) read with Section 13(1)(c) & (d) of the Prevention of Corruption Act and was sentenced to undergo rigorous
 imprisonment for 3 years (three years) with a fine of Rs.10,000/- (ten
 thousand) and a default sentence under Section 120B ofthe I.P.C.; a common
 sentence for all other IPC offence rigorous imprisonment for 3 years (three
 years) with a fine of Rs.40,000/- (forty thousand) and a default sentence. All the
 sentences were directed to run concurrently. Learned counsel for the appellant submits that appellant was
 Proprietor of M/s. Ashok Kumar & Brothers, Ranchi. He has been accused of
 having received Rs. 6,08,710/- for supply of items like Gur, Besan, Mustered
 oil etc. during the financial years 1990-91 and 1991-92. However, the learned
 trial court has, on the basis of certain CNC Bills and vehicle registration
 number verified from the concerned DTO, arrived at a finding that payments 2. have been received by the appellant by fraudulent supply because registration
 no. of one truck was found to be that of scooter during investigation. However,
 considering the amount of fraudulent withdrawal, lesser sentence of three years
 have been imposed upon him by the impugned judgment for the offences under I.P.C with fine amount. Learned trial court has granted provisional bail to the
 appellant on consideration of the aforesaid facts and lesser sentence granted to
 the appellant, which may be confirmed. Learned counsel for the CBI has opposed the prayer. He submits
 that the appellant, who is a supplier has been instrumental in receiving
 fraudulent payment of more than Rs. 6.00 Lakhs, though such supplies were
 actually not made as established through the documentary evidence as well as
 prosecution witness nos. 441 and 451, clerk in the District Transport Officer,
 Ranchi. However, learned counsel for the CBI does not dispute that the
 appellant has been awarded maximum sentence of three years. Having considered the submissions of learned counsel for the
 appellant and the C.B.I. and the facts and circumstances noted above,
 provisional bail granted to the appellant vide order dated 15th February 2022 by
 the court of learned Special Judge-V, C.B.I. (AHD Scam Cases), Ranchi in R.C
 Case No. 47(A)/1996-Pat, is confirmed, subject to deposit of 50% of the fine
 amount in the court below and if not wanted in connection with any other case.
 The appellant would not leave the country without permission of the learned
 trial court. He would also submit his passport, if any, before the learned trial
 court and the appellant and his bailors shall not change their addresses or
 mobile nos. without permission of the learned Trial Court. I.A. No. 2225 of 2022 stands disposed of accordingly. (Aparesh Kumar Singh, J)
