I have encountered this 3 time until now. An individual or a family approaches. They say that they dont know kannada and are from Maharashtra. The contractor asked them to come and work in Bangalore but did not give them money after work and now they dont have money to go back home. 

I am asking about this scam because the family that I encountered had kids as young as 3 years. I was tempted to help after seeing the kids but decided not to do so. And the entire day I tussled with the guilt of not helping those kids.
