Salesperson from a car company from which I purchased a car had contacted me multiple times stating that he has become the branch manager now and could provide me with good discount if I renew it through him as he has special offer on his company ID. I even asked him for a draft policy before making the payment, but he just told me that the policy will directly get generated after the payment is made. Somehow I believed him and made the payment. Initially he sent me an OTP and later he told me that the KYC is done, he also told me that I can pay some extra amount and get insurance for longer under this offer. Don't know why, but I agreed and paid him additional amount. After all this the person told me that I will receive my insurance policy by morning. In the morning he started giving me reasons like he is going to the office and will check the status there. The person turned off his mobile later. When I sent him an SMS to check, he told me that he has taken this money for some of his personal expenses he also told me that he will return it in sometime. When I contacted the branch of the car company, I was informed that the employee had previously also done such scam with other customers that is why he was removed from the organization. The company shared his aadhar card, phone number and photo with me and told me that there is nothing more they can do and just asked me to file a police complaint. When I visited the police station, I am being told that it is a civil case and I will need to visit bandra court. The amount of fraud is 23,000.
