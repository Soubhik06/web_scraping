This Criminal Petition is filed under Section 482 of Bharatiya

Nagarik Suraksha Sanhitha, 2023 (for short 'BNSS') seeking

anticipatory bail to the petitioner/accused No.1 in Crime No.8801 of

2024 on the file of Cyber Crime Police Station, Cyberabad registered

for the offences punishable under Sections 204, 318(4), 335, 336(3),

338 and 340(2) of BNS and Section 66-C of IT Act, 2000-2008. 2. The case of the prosecution is that on 26.11.2024, the

complainant lodged a complaint stating that he received a call from

someone claiming to be from FedEx, alleging misuse of their Aadhaar

Card, and was then connected via Skype to a person posing as a

Mumbai Cyber Police officer, who coerced them into sharing bank

details under the threat of legal action, demanding Rs.12,77,847 for

police clearance. Basing on the same, Crime No.8801 of 2024 was

registered. 3. Heard Mr.A.Ravinder Reddy, learned Senior Counsel

representing Mr.M.Sateesh, learned counsel for the petitioner and 2 Mr.Syed Yasar Mamoon, learned Additional Public Prosecutor for the

respondent No.1-State. 4. Learned Senior Counsel for the petitioner submitted that the

petitioner has not committed the offence and he was falsely

implicated in the above crime, basing upon the confession statement

given by accused Nos.2 and 3 only. Even according to the allegations

made in the complaint the ingredients of alleged offences are not

attracted against the petitioner and the amount belonging to the

complainant was not deposited in the account of the petitioner and

accused Nos.2 and 3 received the forged amount from the

complainant. He further submitted that accused Nos.2 and 3 were

already enlarged on bail and entire investigation is completed, except

filing of charge sheet. He further submitted that accused No.3 filed

application in Crl.M.P.No.130 of 2025 on the file of the I Additional

Junior Civil Judge-cum-XII Additional Judicial Magistrate of First

Class, Ranga Reddy District at Rajendranagar, seeking bail and the

learned Magistrate while granting bail recorded that investigation

was completed and charge sheet was also filed and custodial

interrogation of accused No.3 is no more required and he is entitled

for grant of bail. He further submitted that accused Nos.2 and 3 are 3 habitual offenders and they are accused in other crime No.2546 of

2024. He further submitted that accused Nos.2 and 3 have given

bank account in favour of one Maddirala Sudheer and 5 other

persons. Hence, question of giving bank account in favour of the

petitioner does not arise. He further submitted that the petitioner is

ready to cooperate with the investigation and also ready and willing

to abide by the conditions which are going to be imposed by this

Court. Hence, the petitioner may be enlarged on anticipatory bail. 5. Per contra, learned Additional Public Prosecutor submitted

that petitioner has committed grave offence and there are specific

allegations against the petitioner that at his instance complaint

sustained a huge loss of Rs.12,77,847/- and investigation is under

progress. Hence, at this stage, the petitioner is not entitled for grant

of anticipatory bail. 6. Having considered the rival submissions made by the

respective parties and after perusal of the material available on

record, it reveals that the complainant lodged a complaint on

26.11.2024, wherein she specifically stated that she sustained a huge

loss of Rs.12,77,847/- in the hands of petitioner and other accused. It 4 also reveals accused Nos.2 and 3 in their statements specifically

stated that they have given their bank accounts in favour of

petitioner/accused No.1 and accordingly petitioner paid 5% of

commission to them. According to the learned Additional Public

Prosecutor investigation is under progress and accused Nos.2 and 3

have already given their bank accounts in favour of one Maddirala

Sudheer and 5 other persons in Crime No.2546 of 2024, and very

same grounds were mentioned in the present crime and the same has

to be revealed during investigation and the same is pending. Taking

into consideration the gravity of the offence, this Court is not inclined

to grant anticipatory bail to the petitioner, at this juncture. 7. Accordingly, this Criminal Petition is dismissed. Miscellaneous applications, pending if any, shall stand closed. ____________________
 J. SREENIVAS RAO, J
