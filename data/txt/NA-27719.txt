Sooooo here’s the deal. Nothing grinds my teeth more than people trying to make fast money, especially when it involves scamming others (as it usually does!!) so today I was home sick from work and so bored - until I got an IRS as an phone call! 
It started out great I was having fun just calling playing little funny tricks (like seeing how long they’d stay on the phone if I limited my convos to salutations only). They stopped answering my phone number so I hid my number (my thinking being hey, I’ve got time to spare and a bone to pick maybe it will prevent them from scamming someone else today). 
The last phone call was him admitting it was a scam. He was not the IRS. Jackpot I thought! Woah! So I pushed further, ‘why are you doing this? Why are you scamming people’ and he gave me a sob story about his dad in the ICU and they couldn’t afford it/things are very corrupt in India. My heart sank immediately (but no I didn’t give him any money or contact info or anything of the sort!!) 
Let’s assume that he’s actually telling the truth. Is this a good enough reason to scam other people? Do external circumstances validate shitty behavior? Is right v wrong fuzzier than we think? HELP!
