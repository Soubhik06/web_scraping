Two days ago my maternal granny received an SMS stating your "electricity power will be disconnected at 9:30 pm as previous bill payment wasn't updated. Please call on no. \*\*\*\*". 

My mother usually receives updates on her bills, but she wasn't alert enough, so she called the number. The person asked her to go to the website [rechargecube](https://rechargecube.in/) and make the payment of Rs10 which will be refunded. As soon you enter the information you lose your max card limit. We took the necessary action against them and everything is done. The person had the audacity to call back again and has still not been blocked. Ludo supreme is the name where the money was transferred, a famous cyber hack crime gang in Kolkata. They have multiple callers to trick people. I wasn't present during the whole incident.

I just want to inform people that this is the latest scam in the market and older generations aren't alert. This can happen with anybody, just be careful and in such cases inform the bank first, take the transaction id details and go to a cyber crime cell in your local area. Please spread this information as much as possible. There are various such scams happening the name of loan apps, matrimony sites and job scams.

TDLR: electricity will be cut message is the latest scam, please don't engage in such messages and websites.
