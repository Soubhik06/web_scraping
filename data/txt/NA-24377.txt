**Etu Edhiretu - Fantasy - 3.5/5 - Available on YouTube**

Etu edhiretu is a weird little movie to describe, it involves a battle of a couple of tantriks in a modern setting &amp; can only be described as a dark fantasy movie. A throwback to the effects rich movies that involves magic. Movie stars Srinath, Sundarkrisna Urs &amp; Lakshmi in lead roles. Shockingly, Lakshmi already a well established &amp; a well known actress than both the leads has a very little screen time throughout the movie but will sear into your memory in the climax with her mannerisms when possessed by spirit. Real highlight of the movie is SundarKrishna Urs as Ugrayya, an evil tantrik who terrorizes the village via dark magic, Srinath an educated man, who returns to his village goes toe to toe with Ugrayya to expose him but failing every-time. The dark magic &amp; a "hero's journey" storyline to learn the art of magic to avenge his defeat is always an appealing plotline especially when you are invested to see an evil character like Ugrayya fall. Sundarkrishna urs oozes menace in this movie &amp; as a despicable villain its easy to be invested in the storyline to see Urs's personification of evil to be defeated, although the main face-off is a bit underwhelming. Movie mixes mysticism, mythology &amp; dark magic &amp; does it without the end product being cheesy &amp; the acting being over the top. It seems much more concise as it concentrates on main storlyline &amp; unlike other movies doesn't deviate from it there are no parallel story threads, or an out of the place romantic storyline to dilute the theme, which allows for an invested viewing experience a rare thing for a kannada movie. Overall a compact movie that does a good job building its mystical world &amp; offers an engaging story &amp; a villain we can all agree to hate.

**Halli gombe, Belli rambe - Comedy - 2/5 - Available on Youtube**

Malaashri was an icon in the 90s who could pretty much elevate any sub par movie &amp; in this is one such example. Movie stars Gurudutt, Malashri, Girija lokesh &amp; Chi Uday Shankar in lead roles. Gurudutt stars as a momma's boy who sides with his mom Girija lokesh as she rules the house with an iron fist causing Malaashri's sister, marries to Gurudutt's brother, to return to her home as a punishment for standing against her mother-in-law. So Malaashri comes up with this brilliant, elaborate &amp; undoubtedly senseless scheme that is so 90s to pose as an innocent village girl, with a fake family &amp; then woo Gurudutt &amp; enter his home as his wife &amp; teach a lesson to her demanding mother-in-law. Movie is full of outdated comedy bits that is more grating than gratifying, Gurudutt as a lead is unbearable &amp; in contrast to Malaashri's searing screen presence everything else fades away. This Movie is completely owned by Malaashri's oomph factor &amp; charisma. Chi Uday Shankar's one liners does get a chuckle out of you now &amp; then but rest of the comic relief seems tasteless &amp; most of the comedic gags falls flat.

**Hello doctor - Educational?/Parody - 0.5/5 - Available on Youtube**

This one was recommended by a friend &amp; its safe to say i wouldn't be asking him for any recommendation in future. This movie borders on being a soft porn/educational/drama movie &amp; stays in limbo without being categorized into any, it wont be a stretch to call it a dark comedy though. Movie starts of in a worse possible way where a bunch of men are having nightmares &amp; killing themselves for some reason. That reason being they are all infected with HIV while they got drunk &amp; gangraped a handicapped girl. After this grand opening movie tries hard to empathize with these HIV infected rapists while as a viewer i was just wondering what kind of sociopath the director is &amp; what kind of head injury did he suffer to conclude unapologetic rapists needs sympathy as well. After this tone-deaf start movie starts to throw AIDS into any person it wants to &amp; pretend its a social drama, all the while it uses multiple shower scenes of a horny wife as a filler &amp; surprise, surprise even this marriage drama has an AIDS angle. It even has a scene with an actual doctor who tries to educate viewers that AIDS is transmitted ONLY via homosexual relation &amp; with that it fails at what little "education" it has to offer. The only thing i remember from this poor excuse for an "AIDS awareness programme" is the segment involving a clueless husband, who himself looks like a diseased thriller manju, handling his horny wife with an lackadaisical dialogue delivery. Only thing I would recommend is to only watch the soulless acting of the "thriller manju" lookalike doctor &amp; his dialogue delivery &amp; acting is a thing of beauty &amp; would have been a meme treasure in right hands. While the horny wife is acting her ass off the clueless actor just stands around like a tree repeating the dialogue &amp; goddamn its hilarious levels of bad acting I have ever seen &amp; I have seen some bad bad movies.

**27 Maavalli circle - Thriller - 3.5/5 Available on Youtube**

This movie in an unofficial remake of Audrey hepburn's "Wait Until Dark". Movie stars Avinash, who dons a questionable wardrobe throughout the movie, Umashree &amp; Devaraj in his debut role. T.N. Narasimhan who is the director/producer also plays a lead role in the movie. Movie follows Narasimhan as a master thief who hires Devaraj &amp; Somashekar rao to swindle a blind woman to collect a lost or possibly misplaced doll in her home. Tn Narasimhan, Devaraj, Somashekar form a gang where they impersonate multiple characters as they try to terrorize a blind woman into recall where she has misplaced the doll, which has valuable diamonds hidden in it. The elaborate schemes &amp; counter schemes are fun to watch &amp; after the initial 20mins of movie which is all over the place it settles into its own tune. It embraces its play like structure &amp; allows each character to walk in flex their acting muscle &amp; go back &amp; make room for the rest. Adapting a very strong source material with brilliant acting talent &amp; it gives each character their own space to express themselves which as a whole is a treat to watch, right story, right actors &amp; decision to shoot it like a play localized in a home gives it an air of freshness. Overall a strong source material, coupled with fine acting makes this work although it seem more like a radio play enacted on screen, it works as it has the cosy feeling of a quality &amp; an earnest radio play.

**Madhu chandra - Drama - 1/5 - Available on YouTube**

Madhu chandra stars Shankarnag, Jayamala &amp; Ramakrishna in its lead roles. Movie starts off in a picturesque beach-side resort where Shankarnag &amp; his wife Jayamala are on their honeymoon trip. The movie spends its entire first half showing how idyllic their relationship is &amp; as predicted the couple drift apart from each other. While Shankar nag is effortless in his portrayal as a devoted husband, Jayamala falls short in her role. When the couple starts to drift from one another Jayamala fails to match the intensity of a sudden bitterness in the relationship, that comes naturally to Shankarnag. Movie also tries to hint a much more with a ton of foreboding scenes with Ramakrishna which is left open for interpretation throughout the movie. As we slog through the borefest of a movie wondering whether there is any sort of plotline to this movie which so far has bored us with scenes after scenes of lead pair playfully enjoying their vacation &amp; then continues to bore with their sudden distrust for each other. If you are still awake then movie thrusts in a murder out of the blue to raise the stakes &amp; then finds the murderer &amp; haphazardly closes all the paper-thin plot-lines before viewer can complete a yawn. Movie is all over the place &amp; is an inexcusable mess of a movie &amp; pairing a weak actress like Jayamala alongside Ramakrishna &amp; Shankarnag who are honestly trying &amp; wasting their time is actually a crime.

**Gagana - Drama - 3/5 - Available on YouTube**

Gagana a product of Dorai-Bhagawan comes with all the bells &amp; whistles their production is known for. Starring Anant nag, Kushboo, Srinath, Leelavati the movie boasts of quality leads &amp; a quality supporting cast. Its a light-hearted family drama with no serious stakes &amp; is watchable due to its charismatic leads in Kushboo &amp; Anant nag. The first half of a movie has a 3 stooges feel to it with Slapstick comedy intermixed with melodrama, the friction between Kushboo's character &amp; Anantnag is a fun watch, Kushboo as a happy-go-lucky lead in first half is a breezy watch, however the second half tones down on this as movie enters its melodramatic phase &amp; loses the steam. Plot wise it talks about how a widower Anant nag overcomes his barrier to accept Kushboo(titular Gagana) as his soulmate. First half of the movies is much better &amp; serves to reel in its audience with a lovable &amp; bubbly character in Kushboo &amp; slapstick office shenanigans, while second half tries to balance the tone the sudden shift from a playful character to a devoted lover is jarring, but thanks to the talented cast none of it gets in your way for enjoying a well crafted lighthearted family drama.
