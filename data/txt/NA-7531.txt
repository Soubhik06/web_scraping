Heard counsel for the parties. This bail application has been filed by the applicant,
who has been arrested on 06.11.2014 and is in Jail in
connection with Crime No.05/2014 registered with Police
Station S.T.F., Bhopal - commonly known as VYAPAM
Examination Scam cases - for the offence punishable
under Sections 419 , 420 , 467 , 468 , 471 and 120-B of IPC
and also under Section 3(Gha) 1 , 2 / 4 of the M.P. Manyata
Prapt Pariksha Adhiniyam, 1937. The role ascribed to the applicant by the prosecution
is that of a middleman. He obtained an amount of
Rs.3,50,000/- from beneficiary - Bhura Rawat and out of
which paid Rs.3,00,000/- to one Ramveer Rawat to arrange
for an impersonator to appear in place of beneficiary -
Bhura Rawat during the examination conducted by
VYAPAM. It is not in dispute that the beneficiary Bhura
Rawat has been released on bail. It is fairly stated by the learned counsel for the State
on instructions that custodial interrogation of the applicant
is no more required. Charge-sheet has also been filed
against the applicant. Further, the investigation against the
applicant is complete in all respects as of now and,
therefore, the presence of applicant is no more required.In
that sense, the link between the accused persons is already
complete. The applicant is not involved in any other
criminal case. Besides, the applicant through counsel
undertakes to appear before the Investigating Officer as
and when called for further investigation including against
him as also to punctually appear before the trial Court to
ensure early disposal of the criminal trial. In the interest of justice, taking overall view of the
matter, we deem it appropriate to grant bail to the applicant
on following strict conditions:- The bail application stands disposed of accordingly.
