I moved into a 2bhk builder floor (top floor) last month after recently getting married. The owner has individual electricity meter setup in ground floor and we pay bill to him by checking the monthly units. He in turn pays the bill for complete building. 

The problem is he has been charging Rs 10/unit where I got to know yesterday that the electricity price in the area is based on tier of usage and highest slab is 6 Rs/unit and that too post 500 units of consumption. He has documented this is the rental agreement as well that we'll pay Rs 10/unit to him. We never really felt that it is a scam since I was paying Rs 12/unit in a society where there was 24 hr power back up. Here we don't even have that.

When I confronted him yesterday, he says this is a standard practice and that's why he doesn't charge any maintainance fees for cleaning common area. But the problem is my March bill was 430 units which is 4300 Rs in a setup where we didn't even use ACs. Now if we start using AC this will go to 12-1500 units on just 1 single AC. 

I have to just pay this exorbitant amount in peak summers because of my own negligence. Sharing this here so you can negotiate while moving in. Also if there's any anonymous way to complain would be really helpful because I like the flat and it is in my budget and don't want poor terms with owner. He also owns 50+ properties in Gurgaon so I am assuming he is well connected.
