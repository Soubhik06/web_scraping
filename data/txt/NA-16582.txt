Has anyone else noticed that all the major e-commerce websites in India have changed their return policies. In essence all the companies have mostly removed the ability to return products with only a few exceptions.

I remember that almost all the products available on flipkart or Amazon which were fulfilled by them had the option of returning the product. But now it's either a 7 day or a 10 day replacement policy with only a few exceptions.

In my opinion, i think a part of online shopping was the ability to return the products if they were not upto your expectations, as we cannot judge the product beforehand like we can do in offline shopping. So if we didn't like the product or if the product quality was not up to the mark, we can't even return it now. All we have is a replacement. And why would i want a replacement if I didn't even like the product first, or if the quality of the product is bad.

Online shopping is not the same anymore, all we get are those continuous fake sale scams. Sad.
