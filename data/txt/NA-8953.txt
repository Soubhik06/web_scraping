➢ We do not find anything illegal or contrary to the law in issuing such

 Respondent No. 1 and contained details required proof of debt and default

 along with various acknowledgements by the Promoters and the

 Respondent No. 1. ➢ Thus, we hold that the Recall Notice dated 17.01.2018 was valid. -108- Comp. App. (AT) (Ins.) No. 1287 & 1291 of 2022

 ➢ We have already noticed the details of additional loan agreement dated

➢ At this juncture, we would like to take into account the fact that this
