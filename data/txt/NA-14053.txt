MobiKwik reported consolidated revenue from operations of Rs 270.2 crore for the quarter ended September 30, 2025 (Q2 FY26), a decline of about 7% year-on-year (YoY) from Rs 290.6 crore in Q2 FY25, according to its unaudited financial results. Including other income of Rs 9.1 crore, total income stood at Rs 279.3 crore, compared with Rs 293.7 crore in the same period last year.

Total expenses were largely stable at Rs 285.7 crore, versus Rs 286.8 crore a year earlier, as higher payment-processing and financial-guarantee costs offset lower employee and lending-operations expenses. The company posted a net loss of Rs 28.6 crore for the quarter, widening sharply from a loss of Rs 3.6 crore in Q2 FY25. Its EBITDA loss stood at Rs 6.4 crore, compared with a positive Rs 6.8 crore last year.

Despite the reversal into loss, MobiKwik maintained a steady revenue base across its payments and financial services segments. The company’s consolidated assets stood at Rs 1,398 crore, while its equity share capital totalled Rs 15.7 crore. The results, reviewed by B S R & Co LLP, mark the company’s second quarterly disclosure since its December 2024 stock-market debut.

MobiKwik reported robust operational growth in its payments business during the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The company’s gross merchandise value (GMV) for payments rose to Rs 43,216.7 crore, marking a 53% YoY and 13% quarter-on-quarter (QoQ) increase, its highest-ever quarterly GMV. This expansion was driven by strong platform engagement and higher transaction volumes across its wallet and UPI offerings.

The fintech also reinforced its position in India’s digital payments landscape, ranking among the top three fastest-growing UPI apps in the country, with UPI transaction volumes rising 3.5 times YoY. Its user base expanded to 18.35 crore, while its merchant network reached 47.1 lakh during the quarter, reflecting continued ecosystem adoption.

In the payments segment, revenue rose 11% YoY, supported by increased monetisation and lower payment gateway costs. The company maintained a net payment processing margin of 14 basis points, while achieving an all-time high gross margin of 29%, up 71% YoY.

MobiKwik’s financial services operations continued to expand steadily in the quarter ended September 30, 2025, driven by growth in its credit products and improved cost efficiencies. The company’s ZIP EMI business recorded a GMV of Rs 807.1 crore, reflecting a 16% QoQ increase as lending demand recovered to pre-slowdown levels. The segment’s gross margin rose to 42%, highlighting improved portfolio performance and stronger unit economics.

According to the company’s filings, gross profit from financial services climbed 231% QoQ, supported by a sharp decline in lending-related expenses. These costs fell to 4.4% of GMV from 7.3% in the previous quarter, aided by tighter risk management and lower default rates among lending partners.

MobiKwik attributed the improvement to operational discipline and the growing confidence of its partner network. The company said the segment’s profitability trajectory reflected a strong comeback in lending margins, positioning it for further growth in digital financial products.

MobiKwik has broadened its product suite to strengthen its position in India’s digital finance ecosystem and support its next phase of growth. The company continues to operate a diverse range of offerings across payments and financial products, including the MobiKwik Wallet, UPI, Pocket UPI, and Zaakpay, its payment gateway solution for merchants. These products together enable seamless digital transactions for both consumers and small businesses.

On the financial services side, MobiKwik offers ZIP EMI and Merchant Cash Advance (MCA) products that provide short-term credit to individuals and merchants, respectively. It has also expanded into wealth and savings products, facilitating investments in fixed deposits, mutual funds, and digital gold through its platform. Additionally, the company operates Lens.AI, a proprietary analytics tool designed to enhance risk assessment and credit decisioning.

Chairperson and Chief Financial Officer (CFO) Upasana Taku noted that the company is “gearing up to accelerate our play in UPI and digital lending to drive the next phase of our growth,” adding that the gains in contribution profit and EBITDA reflect “disciplined cost optimisation and steady gains across both payments and lending.”
