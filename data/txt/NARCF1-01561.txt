Police have arrested a gang making fake sauces with acetic acid in Shamshabad. They raided a factory run by the name of Shree Balaji Industries in the Shamshabad area and seized the sauces that were made with deadly chemicals and synthetic colors. Also, it was found that expired sauce packets are being sold in the market with stickers with new dates. 772.13 liters of sauce, 30 liters of acetic acid and 13 kg of corn flour were seized. Officials said that the value of the seized cargo is Rs 3.50 lakh. The police have registered a case and are investigating the incident.
