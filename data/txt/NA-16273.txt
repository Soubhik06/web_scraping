# JEE and NEET: The Backbone of Indian Education

* **JEE (Joint Entrance Examination):** With over **15 lakh aspirants every year**, this exam opens doors to India’s premier engineering institutions, including the IITs and NITs. 
* **NEET (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test):** A staggering **24 lakh students** vie for admission to top medical colleges, dreaming of becoming doctors and serving the nation.

These exams aren’t just tests; they’re lifelines. For millions of students and their families, they represent hope, opportunity, and a path out of poverty. Parents, especially from rural areas, sacrifice everything—savings, comfort, and even their own health—to pay for their children’s coaching fees.

But lurking behind this facade of meritocracy is a **sinister mafia** that sells these dreams to the highest bidder.

# The Paper Leak Scam: A Blight on India’s Future

Every year, an organized network of scammers leaks JEE and NEET question papers weeks or even days before the exams. These papers are sold at exorbitant prices to wealthy students and their families, ensuring that money—not merit—determines success.

Here’s what I uncovered while crawling the **dark web** last month:
