Petitioner asserting to be an injured suffered in a RTA

filed petition under Section 166 of M.V.Act claiming

compensation from the respondents who are the owner and

insurer of the Motorcycle bearing No.KA-52-U-5675

asserting that on 25.01.2023 at about 17.30 p.m. when

standing on the footpath to cross the road at Bhudihal

gate, Bhudhihal - Mandigere road, Nelamangala Taluk,

Bengaluru, rider of Motorcycle bearing No.KA-52-U-5675,

rode the same negligently from Bhudihal towards

Bhudhihal gate, dashed the same to the petitioner, thereby

caused accident, caused injuries to the petitioner. 2. Petitioner took treatment at J.P.hospital,

Nelamangala and as on the date of accident petitioner

being aged about 29 years, used to earn Rs.25,000/p.m.

by working as Tailor. 3. Regarding accident Nelamangala Rural police

registered crime No.11/2023 against the rider of the

Motorcycle bearing No.KA-52-U-5675. 4. In spite of service of notice, respondent No.1 -

owner of the motorcycle remained absent. 5. In response to the notice, respondent No.2-

insurance company appeared through his Advocate, filed

written statement, denying the allegation regarding reason,

mode of accident, age, occupation, income, nature and

consequence of the injuries suffered by the petitioner. 6. On the basis of the pleadings on 21.08.2024, this

Authority has framed the following issues : 7. On 22.04.2024, 27.09.2024 petitioner deposed as

PW1 and produced 13 documents at Ex.P.1 to P.13. On

25.06.2024, 29.10.2024 petitioner produced the evidence

of Dr. Nagaraj B.N. as PW2 and get produced 2 documents

at Ex.P.14 and P.15. 8. I heard the arguments submitted on behalf of both

contesting parties and perused the records. 9. On the basis of the record, my findings on the

above issues are as under: 10. Issue No.1 :- In the evidence petitioner stated

that on 25.01.2023 at about 17.30 p.m. when standing on

the footpath to cross the road at Bhudihal gate, Bhudihal -

Mandigere road, Nelamangala Taluk, Bengaluru, rider of

Motorcycle bearing No.KA-52-U-5675, rode the same

negligently from Bhudihal towards Bhudhihal gate, dashed SCCH-1 5 MVC No.3915/2023 the same to the petitioner, thereby caused accident, caused

injuries to the petitioner. 11. In the written statement respondent No.2-

insurance company denied the allegation regarding reason

and mode of the accident. 12. In the cross-examination petitioner/PW.1 denied

the suggestion that when sitting on the pillion of the

motorcycle bearing No.KA-52-H-8805 rode by her husband

fell along with the motorcycle, suffered injuries. Petitioner

denied the suggestion that motorcycle bearing No.KA-52-

H-8805 neither dashed to her nor concerned to the injuries

might have been suffered. During recording of evidence of

PW.1 husband of the petitioner who is in the court hall

shown his Driving Licence No.KA06 2014000000385 dated

30.12.2013. 13. In the evidence PW.2- Dr. Nagaraj B.N. stated

nothing regarding reason and mode of the accident. 14. Ex.P.1 is the copy of the FIR with complaint

pertaining to crime No.11/2023 registered by Nelamangala

Rural police against rider of Motorcycle bearing No.KA-52-

U-5675 for having committed offence punishable under SCCH-1 6 MVC No.3915/2023 Section 279,337 of IPC, Sec.187 of MV Act regarding the

accident taken place on 25.01.2023 at 5.30 p.m. resulting

in the injuries to the petitioner, on the basis of the

statement of petitioner recorded in the hospital. Ex.P.2 is

the crime detail form. Ex.P.3 is the motor vehicle seizure

mahazar. Ex.P.4 is the IMV Report. Ex.P.5 is the wound

certificate in which reason for the injuries is noted as

alleged RTA on 25.01.2023 at 5.30 p.m.. Ex.P.6 is the copy

of the charge sheet filed by Nelamangala Rural Police in

crime No.11/2023 against Manu M. S/o Mahadeva rider of

Motorcycle bearing No.KA-52-U-5675 for having committed

offence punishable under Section 279, 338 of IPC and

Sec.187 of MV Act. 15. In this case respondents have not produced any

type of evidence to disbelieve the evidence placed by the

petitioner regarding reason, mode and consequence of

accident. Hence, I have concluded that on 25.01.2023 at

about 17.30 p.m. when petitioner was standing on the

footpath to cross the road at Bhudihal gate, Bhudihal -

Mandigere road, Nelamangala Taluk, Bengaluru, Manu M.

S/o Mahadeva- rider of Motorcycle bearing No.KA-52-U- SCCH-1 7 MVC No.3915/2023 5675, rode the same negligently from Bhudihal towards

Bhudhihal gate, dashed the same to the petitioner, thereby

caused accident, caused injuries to the petitioner. Hence, I

answer issue No.1 in the affirmative. 16. Issue No.2: In the evidence petitioner stated that

in the accident she has suffered injuries to face i.e., facial

bone fracture, right maxilla (anterior wall) orbital floor,

interior orbital floor, interior orbital margin fracture and

zygomatic arch, right little finger MP x anterior dislocation

at PIP joint with Zone 3 extensor tendon injury, took

treatment at J.P. hospital, Nelamangala, as inpatient from

25.01.2023 to 30.01.2023 by spending Rs.2,00,000/-, as

on the date of accident used to earn Rs.25,000/-p.m. by

working as Tailor. 17. In the written statement, respondent No.2-

insurance company denied the allegation regarding nature

and consequence of the injuries suffered by the petitioner. 18. In the cross-examination, petitioner denied the

suggestion that in the alleged accident she neither suffered

grievous injuries nor underwent surgery nor spent

Rs.2,00,000/- for treatment. Petitioner admitted that bill SCCH-1 8 MVC No.3915/2023 produced at Sl. No.9 worth Rs.5,000/- is advance bill.

Petitioner denied suggestion about the fabrication of the

bill for the purpose of the case. 19. In the evidence affidavit PW.2 Dr. Nagaraju B.N.,

Orthopedic Surgeon at Sai Ortho and Dental Center stated

that on 01.06.2024 when clinically examined the petitioner

noticed that petitioner suffered permanent physical

disability at 27% to hand which is about 9% to the whole

body. In the evidence PW.2 produced OPD card and X-ray

at Ex.P.14 and P.15. In the cross-examination PW.2

admitted that he neither treated the petitioner, nor verified

inpatient/followup treatment record of the petitioner nor

collected opinion of the doctor who treated the petitioner.

PW.2 stated that as on 01.06.2024 fractures of the

petitioner are united and Metacarpal fracture of the

petitioner has been treated conservatively. In this case

petitioner has not produced any documentary evidence

regarding quantum of the income. 20. Wound certificate, discharge summary,

prescriptions, medical bills, X-ray and other medical

records shows that in the accident petitioner suffered SCCH-1 9 MVC No.3915/2023 swelling over right side of the face, step deformity at infra

orbital margin, depressed fracture of right zygomatic arch,

anterior dislocation of right PIP joint of little finger,

abrasions over dorsum of right hand and right forearm,

facial bone fractures, right maxilla (anterior wall), took

treatment at J.P. Hospital, Nelamangala as inpatient from

26.01.2023 to 30.01.2023. 21. In the Aadhaar card marked at Ex.P.8 the date

birth of the petitioner is noted as 24.05.1993. The medical

bills shows that the petitioner spent approximately

Rs.1,06,182/- for treatment. The bill produced at serial

No.9 marked at Ex.P.9 is with respect to implant charges

worth Rs.5,000/-. In the file there are no documents

regarding either decrease in the income of the petitioner or

loss of income of the petitioner subsequent to the

treatment taken for the injuries suffered in the accident.

Hence, I have inferred that the oral evidence of PW.2

Dr.Nagaraj B.N. is not sufficient to award compensation

under the head loss of future income(Permanent Physical

Disability). 22. Considering the nature of injuries suffered by the

petitioner, length, place and nature of treatment taken by

the petitioner, lack of documents regarding quantum of the

petitioner regarding Rs.5,000/- spent towards implants as

noted in the bills produced at Sl.No.9, I award

Rs.1,00,000/- towards pain and suffering, Rs.1,06,182/-

towards medical expenses, Rs.10,000/- towards

attendant, conveyance and nourishment charges,

Rs.80,000/- (Rs.16,000 x 5) towards loss of income during

treatment period and Rs.75,000/- towards loss of

amenities and future happiness. Hence, I come to the

conclusion that the petitioner is entitle to receive

compensation at Rs.3,71,182/- with current and future

interest at 6% per annum. 23. Hence, I came to the conclusion that the

petitioner is entitle for Rs.3,71,182/- with current and

future interest at the rate of 6% p.a. as compensation.
