Hey folks, just a heads-up about a scam I recently encountered on a dating app in Gurgaon. I matched with someone who seemed genuine. We had great conversations, shared interests, and she even suggested meeting up for coffee. However, things took a weird turn when she brought up a "side business" they were running and insisted on introducing me to it. I played along for a while, she then sent me a link to an investment scheme that promised huge returns. When I hesitated she started pushing hard, even claiming she had already made a lot of money. A quick Google search revealed it was a pyramid scheme. I unmatched immediately. A friend later shared that this is a common scam, posing as genuine matches to lure people into shady investments.

Always rely on your instincts and avoid sharing personal information or sending money to anyone online!
